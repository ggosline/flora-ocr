# *Gouania longipetala* Hemsl.

**Family**: [[Rhamnaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Gouania]]

## Description

Woody climber using axillary tendrils. Leaves alternate and toothed; flowers small, 5-merous, in branched inflorescences, with petals longer than in related species and a conspicuous disc. Fruit dry, three-winged and separating into mericarps.

## Distribution

Lower Guinean and western Central African forest from Nigeria to Angola, including Gabon.
