# *Bulbophyllum strobiliferum* Kraenzl., Mitth. deutsch. Schutzgeb. 2 : 155 (1889).

**Genus**: [[Bulbophyllum]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Megaclinium imbricatum (Lindl.) Rolfe, FTA 7 : 37 (1897)

## Description

vophyllum stenorhachis Kraenzl., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 22 : 25 (1895).

– Bulbophyllum laurentianum Kraenzl. in Durand & De Wild., Mat. Fl. Congo 3 : 50 (1899).

– Bulbophyllum kamerunense Schltr., Westafr. Kautschuk-Exp.: 281 (1901).

– Megacinium congolense De Wild., Et. Fl. B. et Moy. Congo 1 : 21 (1903).

– Megaclinium hebetatum Kraenzl., Vierteljahr. Naturf. Ges. Zurich 68 : 423 (1923).

– Bulbophyllum linderi Summerh., Kew Bull. 4 : 202 (1935).

Pseudobulbs 0.7-7 × 0.8-4 cm, ovoid or narrowly ovoid, 1-2(3)-leaved. Leaves petiolate, 4.5-25 × 0.6-3.7 cm, narrowly lanceolate to linear, obtuse. Inflorescence rather lax, 5-60 cm, glabrous, 26-120-flowered. Flowers small, sepals and petals cream, orange, yellow to greenish, often with dark red to almost entirely dark purple marbling, lip white to pale greenish, often with purple dots. Dorsal sepal glabrous or papillose, oblong-ovate to lanceolate-ovate, acute; lateral sepals obliquely elliptic-triangular with apex recurved acute or rounded, glabrous or papillose. Petals falcate, linear-lanceolate, acute. Lip broadly triangular in its basal half, ligulate above, apex obtuse, margins fimbriate toward the base, entire toward the apex, thick, recurved, glabrous or nearly so. Pl. 76, p. 155.

## Distribution

Sierra Leone, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Equatorial Guinea, São Tomé, Gabon, Congo (Brazzaville), Congo (Kinshasa)

## Ecology

alt. 0-800 m. Epiphytic; lowland to submontane forest, sometimes in coastal forest, plantation and secondary forest
