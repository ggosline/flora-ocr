# *Begonia scapigera* Hook.f. subsp. scapigera

**Genus**: [[Begonia]]
**Family**: [[Begoniaceae]]

## Description

Plate 12 (1–3, 5, 6) in Oliv., Fl. trop. Afr. 2 : 572 (1871). Plant reaching 35 cm in height. Leaf peltate; petiole (2.5)6–30(–36) cm, without or rarely with a few long hairs; blade broadly elliptic-ovate to circular, (4.5–)6–20(–25) × (3–)4–14(–18) cm, apex briefly acuminate or sometimes acuminate-caudate, margin entire to slightly dentate, upper surface glabrous. Inflorescence: peduncle 2–19.5 cm, with 2–4(–12) ♂ flowers and 1 ♀ flower with yellow tepals; bracts 1–4.5 mm in length. ♂ Flower: tepals 8–17.5 × 7–23 mm; androecium with 20–34 stamens. ♀ Flower: pedicel elongating in fruit to 7 mm; tepals 9–19 × 7.5–25 mm; ovary narrowly ellipsoid to almost linear, 8–30 × 1–4.5 mm, without long hairs, without wings; styles (3–)4. Fruit ellipsoid to very narrowly ellipsoid, 16–38 × 2–7.5 mm.

Asonganyi 444; 2, 3, 5: van Veldhuizen 1398, plant cultivated in greenhouse; 6: Sanford 6318). – B. scapigera subsp. australis: 4. ♀ Flower (× 1). (van Veldhuizen 1395, plant cultivated in greenhouse). Drawing by W W, National Herbarium of the Netherlands – Wageningen branch. IL ESSEL

## Distribution

east of Nigeria, west of Cameroon and north of Gabon

## Ecology

on slopes, river beds or rocky cliffs in primary forest, often at the edge of watercourses and rapids; 560–1250 m in altitude. B: Hutchinson, Dalziel & Keay (1954), Sosef (1994). BIBLIOGRAPHY
