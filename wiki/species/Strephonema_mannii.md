# *Strephonema mannii* Hooker

**Genus**: [[Strephonema]]
**Family**: [[Combretaceae]]

## Description

in BENTHAM & HOOKER, Gen. Pl. 1 : 782 (1867) ; KEAY, FWTA, ed. 2, 1(1) : 264 (1954) ; LIBEN, Fl. Cam. 25 : 3 (1983) ; JONGKIND, Review of the genus Strephonema. Ann. Miss. Bot. Gard., 82(4) : 536 (1995). TYPE : Nigeria. Old Calabar (fl., Feb.), Mann 2293 lecto-K ; iso -BR (fragments).

Trees, height ≤ 30 m ; trunk, sometimes with buttresses or stilt roots mimicking buttresses, diameter ≤ 20 cm.

Leaves alternate ; petiole ≤ 2 cm ; blade oblong-elliptic to oblong-obovate, ≤ 30 × ≤ 9 cm, coriaceous, glabrous, often furnished with marginal glands ; young leaves wine-red in aspect glabrous despite very numerous minute bifid hairs on the lower face ; major lateral nerves 7–15 pairs ; apex of blade acuminate.

Inflorescences subumbelliform, axillary to leaves or subcauliflorous ; rachis ≤ 2.5 cm but generally much shorter, subglabrous ; bracts 1–2 mm long, persistent.

Flowers, pedicel 4–7 mm. Receptacle, height 3 mm and diameter 4 mm, nearly glabrous, pale green to green-brown. Calyx : lobes ciliate. Petals white, orbicular, diameter 3 to 4 mm, ciliate with very small hairs, elsewhere glabrous. Stamens exserted 4 mm, anthers 1.5 mm long, not septate, pale yellow. Style white, exserted 5 mm (Pl. 28, B, C, p. 98).

Fruit, diameter ≤ 7 cm, brownish, pustulate. – Pl. 29, A, C, p. 99 & Pl. 30, A, B, p. 101.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Congo (Brazzaville), Congo (Kinshasa)

## Ecology

riparian and swampy forests in evergreen zones ; altitude ≤ 600 m ; species sometimes very common and even dominant
