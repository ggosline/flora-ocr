# *Scadoxus pseudocaulus* (Bjørnstad & Friis) Friis & Nordal

**Family**: [[Amaryllidaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Scadoxus]]

Norw. Journ. Bot. 23: 64 (1976).

## Synonymy

- *Haemanthus pseudocaulus* Bjørnstad & Friis, Norw. Journ. Bot. 19: 219 (1972).
- *Haemanthus pseudocaulus* subsp. *prorumpens* Bjørnstad & Friis, l.c.: 221 (1972).

## Description

Herb reaching 80 cm in height, with a short rhizome. Leaf base forming a false stem 15–45 cm long. Blade broadly lanceolate, acuminate and attenuate to truncate, 15–45 × 5–12 cm. Leaves and flowers appearing simultaneously.

Inflorescence scape 30–65 cm long, central among the leaves growing from the middle of the false stem (in material from Nigeria, plants are found whose peduncle has broken through the false stem and appears lateral). Inflorescence conical to globose, composed of 20–50 flowers. Pedicels approximately 1.5–2.5 cm long (4–5 cm in fruit), drooping as the fruit develops. Perianth tube 10–18 mm long; segments ± lanceolate, 1.2–2.6 cm long, 2–4.5 mm wide, with 5 veins, the inner segments somewhat wider than the outer. Stamens somewhat longer than the segments at maturity. Corolla, stamens and style pale red.

Berries red at maturity, 1.5–2 cm in diameter.

## Distribution

**Range**: Rainforest zones around the Bight of Biafra, from Nigeria to Gabon.

**Nigeria**: type locality (Richards 3417).

**Gabon**: Lambaréné area; Bélinga.

## Notes

The type specimen is Richards 3417 from Nigeria, held at BM. The subspecies *H. pseudocaulus* subsp. *prorumpens* is listed as a synonym but no distinction is made in the description provided. The inflorescence position varies: in Nigerian material the peduncle penetrates the false stem and appears lateral, whereas in material from other localities it remains central.
