# *Thaumatococcus daniellii* (Benn.) Benth.

**Genus**: [[Thaumatococcus]]
**Family**: [[Marantaceae]]

## Synonyms

- Phrynium Danielli Benn., Pharm. Journ. 14 : 161 (1855).
- Monostiche Danielli Horan, Monogr.: 18 (1862).

## Description

— Benth. et Hook. f. Gen. Pl. 3 : 652 (1883). — G. Mangenot, Ic. Pl. Afr. I.F.A.N., 4 nº 95 (1957).

Petioles reaching 2 to 3 m tall, callous portion of ± 10 cm, separation from the midvein marked by a prominent beak on the upper surface. Lamina ovate, asymmetric, briefly acuminate, rounded to truncate at the base, glabrous, of large size (60 × 40 cm or even larger).

Raceme of about ten centimeters long, simple or ramified at the base. Rachis pubescent, articles of 4–8 mm long, bracts imbricate, caducous of ± 2 cm long. Flowers whitish lilac or purplish reaching the same length. Ovary pubescent, fruit red, of about 3 cm diameter with 3 prominent meridional ribs. Seeds triangular, irregularly pyramidal, rugose.

Species known from Sierra Leone to the Central African Republic and in the equatorial forest regions of Congo, Gabon and Cameroon. Understory of non-swampy forests. The leaves are used for the making of roofs and as packing material.

Vernacular names (after Walker and Sillans): Ototo (Mitsogo); Mutotolo (Bavili, Banzabi); Dutura (Eshira, Bavarama); Duturu (Bavungu, Bapunu); Mokwangalaga (Apindji).
