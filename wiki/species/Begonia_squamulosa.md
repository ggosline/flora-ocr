# *Begonia squamulosa* Hook.f. Planche 51

**Genus**: [[Begonia]]
**Family**: [[Begoniaceae]]

## Description

in Oliv., Fl. trop. Afr. 2:579 (1871). Epiphyte; stem decumbent, creeping, (3–)5–8 mm in diameter, reaching 2 m in length. Stipules ± persistent, narrowly triangular, 15–30 × 5–11 mm. Leaves: petiole (3.5–)6–23 cm; lamina subsy­mmetric, narrowly ovate to ovate, 11–23(–26) × 3.5–11 cm, base obtuse to subcordate, apex acute, acuminate or cuspidate, margins entire to weakly sinuous and loosely denticulate. Inflorescence unisexual, the two sexes generally disjunct in time, the ♂ in strongly contracted cymes, 5–60-flowered, with peduncle (2–)3–6(–8.5) cm; the ♀ 1(–3)-flowered, with peduncle reaching 3 mm. Male flower globose and erect in bud; pedicel 3–8 mm; tepals fleshy, the outer obovate, 8–12 × 6–9 mm, the inner obovate to elliptic, 6–7 × 3–4 mm; androecium zygomorphic, stamens 20–50, fasciculate, free parts of filaments 0.1–1 mm, anthers dehiscent by 2 short apical slits. Female flower subsessile; outer tepals 10–17 × 8–10 mm, inner ones 8–13 × 4–6 mm; ovary slightly curved, 15–17 mm in length; styles (3–)4(–5), bifid at apex, stigmatic zone horseshoe-shaped spiralled at the ends. Infructescence with 1(–3) fruits. Fruit sausage-shaped, sometimes apiculate, reaching 3 cm in length, dehiscent by 4 longitudinal slits.

## Distribution

Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo
