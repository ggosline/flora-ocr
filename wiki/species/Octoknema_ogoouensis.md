# *Octoknema ogoouensis* Malécot & Gosline

**Family**: [[Octoknemaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Octoknema]]

Described as new in 2011. **Endemic to Gabon**, known only from the Lopé Reserve, and from a single collection.

## Diagnosis

Compared with [[Octoknema_aruwimiensis|O. aruwimiensis]], from which it differs
in its **shrubby, multi-stemmed habit to 4 m** (not a tree to 12 m), **narrowly
elliptic** leaves (not elliptic), and lax male inflorescences **to 3 cm bearing
few solitary flowers** (not 4 cm or more, bearing numerous fascicled flowers),
with sparsely rather than densely pubescent pedicels.

One of the three species the authors describe as shrubs, with
[[Octoknema_genovefae|O. genovefae]] and *O.* sp. C.

## Distribution

**Gabon only** — Lopé Reserve, Ogooué-Ivindo Province, by the Ogooué.

## Habitat and ecology

Gallery forest, 200 m elevation.

## Conservation

The authors note that the Lopé Reserve is relatively well studied, so a single
collection indicates a genuinely rare species.

## Source

Gosline G. & Malécot V. (2011). A monograph of *Octoknema* (Octoknemaceae — Olacaceae s.l.). *Kew Bulletin* **66**: 367–404.
