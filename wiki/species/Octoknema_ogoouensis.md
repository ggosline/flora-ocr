# *Octoknema ogoouensis* Malécot & Gosline

**Family**: [[Octoknemaceae]] *sensu lato* · **Current placement**: Erythropalaceae · **Genus**: [[Octoknema]]

Kew Bull. **66**: 397 (2011). Species 13 of 14 in the monograph.

IPNI: `urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:77114311-1`

Described as new in 2011. **Endemic to Gabon**, known only from the Lopé
Reserve and from a single collection.

## Diagnosis

*Latin diagnosis*: similar to *O. aruwimiensis* but differing in its shrubby,
multi-stemmed habit to 4 m (not a tree to 12 m); leaves narrowly elliptic (not
elliptic); male inflorescences lax and to 3 cm bearing few solitary flowers (not
4 cm or more, bearing numerous fascicled flowers); pedicels sparsely (not
densely) pubescent; and flowers with glabrous, buff petals remaining incurved
(not pubescent, brown, and recurved at anthesis).

## Description

**Habit**: scrambling shrub to 4 m tall with multiple trunks (c. 20), each
c. 5 cm diam. Old branches glabrous; bark grey. Young twigs very sparsely
pubescent. Indumentum of simple light-brown stellate hairs. Terminal bud
pubescent.

**Leaves**: narrowly elliptic, 8–25 × 3–7 cm; base rounded to obtuse; apex
acute, the acumen triangular, to 1 × 1 cm. Dull green, drying darker on the
upper surface. Primary vein prominent on the lower face; veins contrasting
paler below; midrib very sparsely pubescent below. Secondary veins in 6–7
pairs, forming an angle of 45° with the primary vein, ascending, **not
anastomosing**. Tertiary veins visible but not prominent. Lower surface
glabrous except at the base.

**Petiole**: 1–4 cm long, sparsely pubescent, flat on the adaxial side; apical
pulvinus minimal.

**Male inflorescence**: solitary, axillary, racemose, 2.5–3 cm long, densely to
sparsely pubescent; floral bracts distinct; peduncle 5–10 mm long; flower
clusters 2–5 mm apart on the rachis, with 3 or fewer flowers per cluster.

**Male flowers**: bud 2 mm diam. Pedicel longer than the flower, 3.5–5 mm long,
thin (0.5–1 mm diam.), sparsely pubescent. Sepals absent. Petals 5, convex,
barely fused at the base, sparsely pubescent outside, the inner surface smooth
with a few filiform hairs. Stamens 5, epipetalous, the filament as long as the
anther. Pistillode truncate, pyramidal.

**Female inflorescence and flowers**: unknown. **Fruits**: unknown.

## Types

**Holotype**: Gabon, Lopé Reserve, Ogooué — hippo dip, 17 Sept. 1993,
*White, L. 1046* (WAG); **isotype** MO.

## Distribution

**Gabon only** — the Lopé Reserve, Ogooué-Ivindo Province, by the Ogooué.
Map 4 in the monograph.

## Habitat and ecology

Gallery forest; 200 m elevation.

## Conservation

**VU D2** (IUCN 2001 criteria), assessed in the monograph. The Lopé Reserve is
relatively well studied, so a single collection indicates genuine rarity; the
authors note there are no known threats within the protected area, but that any
threat to the reserve, or failure to find further localities, would require
reassessment as **CR B2ab(iii)**.

## Specimens examined

**Gabon**

- Ogooué-Ivindo Province: Lopé Reserve, Ogooué — hippo dip, 17 Sept. 1993,
  *White 1046* (holotype WAG; isotype MO)

The only known gathering.

## Notes

Known from male material only — the female flowers and the fruit have never
been seen, so the species cannot be assessed against the fruit characters that
carry much of the weight elsewhere in the genus.

Its nearest relative on the authors' account is
[[Octoknema_aruwimiensis|O. aruwimiensis]], a widespread Congo-basin tree.
*O. ogoouensis* is one of the three species the monograph treats as shrubs,
with [[Octoknema_genovefae|O. genovefae]] and *O.* sp. C.
