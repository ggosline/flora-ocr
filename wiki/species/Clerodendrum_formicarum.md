# *Clerodendrum formicarum* Gürke Planche 6 ; Figure 2(A, B)

**Genus**: [[Clerodendrum]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 18 : 179 (1893)

## Description

C. triplinerve Rolfe var. sulcatum (B.Thomas) Moldenke, Phytologia 3 : 409 (1951). C. triplinerve Rolfe var. grandiflorum Moldenke, Phytologia 4 : 177 (1953). Climbing herb, with stem 5–6 m, or liane or spreading shrub 1.5–4.5 m tall; stem hollow, generally with 5 to 8 pale distinct ridges, sometimes only striate, brownish-purple, shortly pubescent with curly hairs, glabrous later, generally with pseudo-spines of (3–)6–10(–18) mm, often harbouring ants. Leaves generally in whorls of 3 or 4, rarely opposite; petiole 0.3–2.5(–3.5) cm; blade oblong or narrowly elliptic to elliptic or ovate-oblong, (1.2–)6–9.5(–14.5) × (0.8–)4–6(–6.5) cm, broadly cuneate at base, shortly to ± long acuminate over up to 1.8 cm at apex, entire, often wrinkled, pale green, punctate, shortly pubescent, glabrous or pubescent on the nerves below, distinctly 3-nerved. Inflorescence terminal, with primary axis strongly reduced, (0.5–)3–4 × (4)–7–13(–19) cm, broadly paniculate, with length/width ratio 0.2–0.5; cymes with (4 to)10 to 24(to 36) flowers, dichasial, very condensed, ± 2 × 4 cm, distant 1–5 mm apart, the lower ones sometimes distant 3 cm; peduncle generally horizontal, (1–)2–4(–6.5) cm, densely pubescent; bracts and bracteoles linear, 2–3(–5) mm long, the 2 or 3 lower bracts often foliaceous, reaching ± 5 × 3 cm. Flower: pedicel 0.5–2 mm; calyx shortly pubescent, accrescent, with ovoid tube, 2–3 mm long, with triangular lobes, 1.5–2 mm long at anthesis, ± 5 × 7(–9) mm and broadly triangular in fruit, apiculate; corolla pale green, greenish cream, white or cream-white, with tube (3–)6–8 mm long, with oblong lobes, 2–4 × 1.5 mm; stamens exserted 5–7 mm. Fruit 8–9(–10) × 5–7 mm, dark green or black with red flesh.

## Distribution

Egypt, tropical West Africa, Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Central African Republic, Angola, north-east Africa and tropical East Africa; in Gabon: Ngounié, Ogooué-Ivindo and Woleu-Ntem. Conservation status: Evaluated as Least Concern (LC) by IUCN and BGCI (2020b)
