# *Adenopodia scelerata* (A. Chevalier) Brenan

**Genus**: [[Adenopodia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull. 41 (1): 76 (1986)

## Description

239 (1920); TORRE, CFA 2 (2): 258 (1956); KEAY, FWTA, ed. 2, 1 (2): 490 (1958); IRVINE, Woody Pl. Ghana: 345 (1961).

– Entadopsis scelerata (A. CHEV.) GILB. & BOUT., FCB 3: 204 (1952).

– Entada ivorensis A. CHEV., mss.

- Acacia ivorensis A. CHEV., mss.

Woody liane capable of reaching a length of 80 m with a diameter of 10 cm at the base. Bark section reddish with whitish cambial layer. Pale yellow wood releasing for a very long time a strong odor of rotting cabbage. Mature branchlets pubescent, greyish-brown when fresh and dark brown with lighter lenticels when dry; angular, obscurely angular to cylindrical, grooved, spinose on the ridges or on longitudinal ridges. Young branchlets pubescent, pentagonal angular, ± distinctly spinose on the ridges. Stipules ± sparsely pubescent, linear, about 8 mm long, apex very acute, early caducous. Petiole pubescent, 3-9 cm long; ± pentagonal in section; spines on the median, inferior and lateral ridges; large gland, prominent, oblong, cupuliform near the base; upper face plane marked by 2 lateral ridges. Rachis pubescent, 6.5-12 cm long; ± pentagonal in section; upper face grooved with 2 prominent longitudinal ridges with 1-2 very prominent cupuliform glands between the apical pinnae, median ridge of the lower face with spines; apex of lower face with pubescent mucro, ± 2 mm long, with acute apex. Pinnae 6-11 pairs. Pinnae axis pubescent above and ± sparsely pubescent to glabrous below; 1.9-7 cm long; upper face ± plane marked by a strong wing-like keel, bearing 1-2 prominent glands between the terminal leaflets, lower face hemispherical; mucro as previously at the apex. Stipels 2, very short and acute at the base (rarely remaining as leaflets). Leaflets 7-14 pairs. Petiolule glabrous to sparsely pubescent, sometimes more densely on the upper edge, flattened, 0.5 mm long. Blade ± pubescent to glabrous above, appressed pubescent to glabrous below; dark green to pale green above and pale green below when fresh, greenish-brown to brownish-green above and greenish to pale green below, matte on both faces when dry; asymmetrically oblong to lanceolate oblong (for the apical leaflets), reaching 1.5 × 0.8 cm; base asymmetric: proximal edge rounded and distal edge attenuate to attenuate-rounded; margin ± pubescence ciliolulate, apex rounded ± asymmetric very shortly mucronulate. Primary vein ± diagonal, obscurely concave to prominent above and prominent ± pubescent below. Secondary veins 5-6 pairs, ascending, most often straight, prominent on both faces. Veinlets in very lax reticulation, practically invisible above, prominent below.

Inflorescences in 1-4 racemes very slightly supra-axillary or axillary, 3-6.5 cm long. Axis densely pubescent, longitudinally grooved. Bracteole caducous, ± pubescent on the midline of the outer face, lanceolate, incurved; apex acute. Pedicel glabrous, ± 0.30 mm long. Floral bud ellipsoid-oblong, stamen with short and straight filament, style short and folded back. Flower ♢, pentamerous, white to yellow when fresh. Calyx obconic, glabrous, with fused sepals 0.75-1.5 mm long; free lobes triangular, 0.25-0.50 mm long, apex acute. Petals 5, ± strongly fused to the stamens at the base, glabrous, narrowly elliptic to narrowly lanceolate, 2-2.25 × 0.50-0.75 mm, apex acute. Stamens 10, very long-exserted after anthesis, ± 4.5-7 mm long, bases shortly fused to each other and to the disk; filament white when fresh; anther yellow when fresh, narrowly oblong, 0.50-0.75 mm long, with connective prolonged by a small globular gland shortly and finely pedicelled. Disk fleshy intrastaminal fused to the stamens. Pistil with glabrous, slender stipe ± 2 mm long; ovary widely protruding beyond the flower, ± pubescent especially on the ventral face and apex, ellipsoid-oblong, 0.75-1 mm long, with 9-12 ovules; style glabrous, slender, ± 2.25-3.50 mm long, with scarcely visible stigma.

Lomentum chartaceous-papyraceous, very sparsely pubescent to glabrous, light brown ± glossy, with sparse and scarcely visible reddish pruina; 12-20 × 3-3.5 cm; base attenuate subcuneiform; sutural ridges cylindric; transverse venation sparse, concave; apex obtuse apiculate. Seed elliptic oblong, triangular, lanceolate, 5.5-7 × 3.75-6 mm, central areole. - Pl. 21, p. 85.

## Type

## Discussion

This scandent species is found in thickets of fallow land, or in windthrow areas in dense forest; also on rocky domes and in periforest savannas. Its range extends from Côte d'Ivoire to Zaïre (Central forestier) and Cabinda.
