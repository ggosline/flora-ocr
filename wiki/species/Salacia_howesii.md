# *Salacia howesii* Hutchinson & Moss

**Genus**: [[Salacia]]
**Family**: [[Celastraceae]]
**Protologue**: FWTA 1: 453 (1928), nomen ; Kew Bull. : 22 (1929) ; BRENAN, Kew Bull. : 221 (1950) ; HALLÉ, Mon. Hippocr. Af. occid., Thèse : 198 (1958) ; Mém. IFAN 64: 202 (1962) ; IRVINE, WPG : 459 (1961)

## Description

, nomen ; Kew Bull. : 22 (1929) ; BRENAN, Kew Bull. : 221 (1950) ; HALLÉ, Mon. Hippocr. Af. occid., Thèse : 198 (1958) ; Mém. IFAN 64: 202 (1962) ; IRVINE, WPG : 459 (1961).

Moss : 10, node ; 11, bud ; 12, flower, diam. 6 mm ; 13, 14, sepals c. 1 mm ; 15, petal 2.3 mm long ; 16, 17, disk, diam. 2 mm ; 18, 19, stamens ; 20, biovulate locule ; 21, fruit, diam. 2.5 cm (10, 21, Emwiogbon FHI 46772, Nigeria ; 11-20, Keay FHI 25514, Nigeria).

Shrub of 1.5 m or more, normally a liane exceeding 3 cm in diameter. Bark greyish fissured. Young internodes grey-olivaceous with very fine erect blonde pubescence. Petiole channelled 5-12 mm, blonde hispid, with finely wrinkled rugose tegument when dry. Blade grey-olivaceous light when dry, glabrous (except on the lower surface in juvenile forms), 7-15 × 2.5-8 cm, with acute or obtuse base, short or rostrate acumen, finely bordered margin, loosely dentate or entire. Secondary veins 6-9 pairs, the longest ascending being the median ones.

Inflorescences pluriflorous fasciculate subcorymbiform axillary with divided rachis often towards the base (2-4 main branches exceeding 1 cm) ; axils elevated as well as the pedicel articulations ; axes micropubérulent. Pedicels 6-11 mm and ovoid bud 2.3 mm in diameter, micropubérulent. Expanded flower rotaceus 4-5-6 mm in diameter. Sepals 1 mm, ovate to subacute, ± broad, pubérulent. Petals 5 micropubérulent on the 2 surfaces and the margin, approximately 2.3 × 1.8 mm. Disk truncate-conical 1.8-2 mm in diameter and 0.7 mm thick. Filaments exceeding 1 mm ; anther bright orange, 1 mm broad, microapiculate. Style pyramidal somewhat pubérulent 1 mm. Ovary with 3 locules each containing 2 superposed ovules.

Fruit globose or even somewhat wider than long, 25-30 mm in diameter, smooth and red at maturity, reddish-brown and with thin teguments, 1 mm or less, when dry. Seed ellipsoid $ 18 \times 11 $ mm in a monosperm fruit. - Pl. 38, fig. 10-21, p. 137; pl. 41, fig. 6, p. 143.

## Type

Howes 974, Ghana, Anaje, 7 miles NW Sekondi, 1.10.1925 (holo-, K !, iso-, P !)

## Discussion

Ivory Coast, Ghana, Nigeria (Emwiogbon 46772), Gabon. To be sought in Cameroon. Juvenile sterile forms more or less sarmentose are recognizable : blade attenuate towards the base which is narrowly cordate ; petiole and young internodes very densely clothed with small blonde hairs ; apex of blade rostrate ; lower surface of blade soft to the touch when dry ; midvein weakly depressed above when dry.
