# *Cassipourea plumosa* (Oliv.) Alston Planche 30.2

**Genus**: [[Cassipourea]]
**Family**: [[Rhizophoraceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1925 : 266 (1925)

## Synonyms

- Weihea plumosa Oliv., Fl. trop. Afr. 2 : 411 (1871).

## Description

Tree or shrub 4–10 m tall; branches glabrous, initially yellow-grey then grey. Stipules caducous, narrowly lanceolate, 3–4 mm long, pilose. Leaf: petiole 5 mm; blade elliptic, 7.5–10.5 × 2.5–4.5 cm, base rounded to cuneate, apex abruptly and longly acuminate, margin entire, glabrous; lateral veins (3–)4(–5) pairs, joined in arches before the margin (bochidodromous venation). Inflorescence axillary, 1–5 flowered, compact, subsessile; bracts minuscule. Flower with pedicel almost absent, stipe 2 mm; calyx narrowly campanulate, 7–9 mm long, with 4 lobes straight or slightly curved, deltoid, obtuse to acute, 4–5 mm long, glabrous or sparsely pilose externally, densely covered with yellow hairs internally; petals spatulate, 10 mm long, with filiform languettes reaching 7 mm and provided with long soft hairs, dirty white; stamens ± 30, glabrous, mixed with rigid hairs, filaments 4 mm, anthers oblong-ovate, 1 mm long, distinctly apiculate; ovary subglobose, 2-locular, densely covered with appressed hairs; style 7 mm, pilose, stigma truncate. Fruit not seen. Seeds not seen.

## Distribution

very rare species, known from 4 localities in Gabon (Estuaire) and one in République du Congo

## Ecology

mangrove on sandy soil, secondary forest; at low altitude. Floral buds in February, flowering in April and July. b: Breteler (2008). Bibliography
