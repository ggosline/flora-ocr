# *Habenaria schimperiana* A.Rich.

**Genus**: [[Habenaria]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]

## Description

PL. 16. – Habenaria buntingi Rendle: A, flower; B, spur, ovary, pedicel and bract; C, labellum; D, dorsal sepal; E, petal; F, lateral sepal; G, gynostemium, lateral view; H, gynostemium spread out (A, ex TALBOT 1913, modified; B-H, Le Testu 6480, P).

PL. 18. – Habenaria schimperiana A.Rich.: A, spur, ovary, pedicel and bract; B, labellum; C, dorsal sepal; D, petal; E, lateral sepal; F, gynostemium, lateral view; G, central part of gynostemium, spread out, anterior view (A-G, Kiss 35, P).

Stem 30–100 cm, glabrous. Leaves 7–28 × 1–2(–3.7) cm, linear to linear-lanceolate, acute. Inflorescence 6–35 cm, 4–30-flowered. Flowers green, centre white, unpleasant smell. Sepal oblong-elliptic, obtuse, glabrous; lateral sepals obliquely or broadly obovate with a lateral apicule. Petals bipartite almost from the base, fairly thick. Labellum trilobed, undivided base 2–3 mm, fleshy, glabrous: median lobe linear, obtuse, the lateral ones linear-lanceolate, acute, curved upward. Spur bent forward, geniculate near the base, subrectilinear, twisted several times near the middle, swollen at the 1/3 or upper 1/2, apex blunt. –Pl. 18, p. 45.

## Distribution

Gabon, Congo (Kinshasa), Ethiopia, Sudan, Kenya, Tanzania, Malawi, Zambia, Zimbabwe

## Ecology

alt. 0–2550 m. Marsh, damp grassland on poorly drained soil

## Specimens examined

Le Testu 1524a, Leuga (fl., Déc.), BM!, BR!.
