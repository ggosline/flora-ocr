# *Impatiens letestuana* N. Hallé

**Family**: [[Balsaminaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Impatiens]]

## Description

Gabonese endemic with alternate leaves and axillary pedunculate pink flowers. Lip very deep, flower about 5 cm, spur rather long and worm-like; inflorescence peduncle under 8 cm. Capsule fusiform; seeds about 2.1 mm.

## Distribution

Mayombe, Gabon.
