# *Securidaca welwitschii* Oliv. Planche 26

**Genus**: [[Securidaca]]
**Family**: [[Polygalaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. trop. Afr. 1 : 135 (1868)

## Description

Prickly scrambling shrub or liana 10–15 m long; young branches with fine appressed pubescence, becoming glabrous rapidly. Leaf: petiole 5–8 mm, pubescent; blade broadly oblong-elliptic, 5–15 × (1.5–)2–5.5 cm, subcoriaceous, cuneate to obtuse, ± acuminate, upper face shiny and glabrous or nearly so, lower face glabrous. Inflorescence: axillary or terminal racemes or simple panicles, 2–10(–20) cm long, often on short spinescent branchlets, multiflorous; peduncle and rachis pubescent; bracts and bracteoles early caducous. Flower pink or purple, sometimes streaked with white, with ± sweet fragrance; pedicel 5–8(–10) mm, pubescent; upper sepal ovate, 4–5 × 3–4 mm, ciliate, winged sepals suborbicular, (4–)5–9(–11) mm in diameter, lower external sepals suborbicular to broadly ovate, 4–4.5 mm in diameter; upper petals narrowly elliptic or oblong-obovate, (4.5–)5–6(–7.5) × 2–3 mm, ciliate, keel 7–10 mm × 5 mm, with a small lobed appendage of ± 1 mm long; staminal tube (3–)4–8 mm long, ciliate on the upper margin. Fruit narrowly elliptic, 3–5 × 0.8–2 cm, with one wing slightly obliquely curved, and a second imperfect and rudimentary wing. Seed subglobose, 8–10 mm in diameter, weakly rugose or smooth.

## Distribution

widely distributed, from the western part to East Africa: Guinée-Bissau, Libéria, Sierra Leone, Côte d'Ivoire, Gabon, Guinée Équatoriale (Bioko), République Démocratique du Congo, Angola, Zambie, Tanzanie, Ouganda and Kenya; in Gabon: Woleu-Ntem, Moyen-Ogooué, Ogooué-Maritime, Nyanga, Ngounié, Ogooué-Lolo and Ogooué-Ivindo

## Ecology

primary evergreen dense forest or old secondary dense forest, forest galleries; in Gabon at 50–500 m elevation, reaching 1650 m in evergreen forest of East Africa
