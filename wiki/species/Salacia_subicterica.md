# *Salacia subicterica* Hallé, sp. nov.

**Genus**: [[Salacia]]
**Family**: [[Celastraceae]]

## Description

Liana S. pallescenti Oliver affinis sed petiolorum marginibus crispulatis, pedicellis perbrevibus, floribus subflavescentibus, disco pentagonali 1.5 mm diam., sulca perigyna conspicua, filamentis brevibus, antherarum rimis fractis, ovarii loculis (3)4(5)-ovulatis, differt.

## Type

Le Testu 7163, Gabon, Lastoursville, 10.4.1929 (fl. « d'un jaune incertain ») (holo-, P !)

Bos 4860, Cameroun (holo-, WAG ! ; iso-, P, BR)

Baumann 489 p.p., Togo (B, probablement perdu)

## Discussion

Liana with opposite or subopposite leaves, sometimes alternate, lacking rubber. Internodes ± marked with 4 fine ridges. Petiole (3)4–6(7) mm, with a narrow channel with undulate margins. Blade brownish when dry, elliptic, thin, 8–18 × 3.5–6.5 cm, briefly cuneiform to subobtuse, with acuminate apex, with dentate margins, teeth reduced and spaced. Midvein forming when dry a fine prominent ridge in a narrow groove. Secondary veins 8–13 pairs.

Floral glomerules axillary, sessile, many-flowered. Pedicels short, 1–2 mm. Bud briefly ovoid, 2 mm in diameter. Flower rotate, yellowish, 5 mm in diameter. Calyx 2.5 mm in diameter, with 5 unequal rounded sepals, 0.5 to 1.1 mm wide, somewhat denticulate. Petals 5, ovate, 1.6–2.2 × 1.5–1.8 mm, with multinerved flabellate venation. Disk spreading pentagonal, in profile cushion-like, 1.5 mm in diameter. Perigynous groove well marked. Stamens 3, short, twisted on themselves at anthesis; anther 0.5 mm wide, with 2 contiguous locules in chevron, the dehiscence line forming an angle greater than 90°. Pistil pyramidal, approximately 1 mm. Locules not clearly immersed, with (3)4(5) ovules each, arranged rather in 2 rows.

Fruit unknown. – Pl. 22, fig. 1–9, p. 85.

Florence 1724, Boka Boka, slope of Mt. Bengoué, 700 m, 4.3.1979 (fl.): the perigynous groove is not clear, the ovules are 5 or 6 per locule.

14 bis. Salacia letouzeyana Hallé, sp. nov.

S. whytei Loes., affinis, sed petiolis majoribus, inflorescentiis minimis subsessilibus, floribus minoribus, pallidioribus, disco mediocriter pulvinato, vix intus sulcato, filamentibus haud conjunctis differt. Antherae transversae thecis confluentibus rimis dehiscentibus arcuatis. Pistillum pyramidale subulatum; loculi semi-immersi 4(-5)-ovulati; ovulis biseriatis. Incolit in Cameruno Nigeriaque.

Tangled liana 2.5–6 m high, or ± twining and drooping 8–10 m, glabrous and lacking rubber. Young internodes smooth, pale ochraceous when dry; bark becoming ± reddish then grey. Leaves opposite or subopposite. Petiole 5–15 mm, with channel margins straight or somewhat undulate, often pinched. Blade olivaceous subconcolour, rather thin, 6–14(–17) × 2–6(–7) cm, with ± briefly acute base, with acuminate or subcaudate apex, with subentire to denticulate margins. Secondary veins 7–10(11) pairs, somewhat yellowish when dry beneath the blade as well as the midvein; veinlets visible on both faces.

Inflorescences uni- or pauciflorous, axillary, comprising a ± divided and subsessile glomerule, approximately 1–3 × 1–3 mm. Bud yellow when dry, ovate, less than 2 mm. Pedicel 2–3.7 mm. Flower 3.5–4.8 mm in diameter, yellow, yellowish, salmon-pink or pale orange. Sepals somewhat unequal, 0.9–1.7 mm wide, subsemicircular, entire or few-denticulate. Corolla somewhat reflexed; petals ovate or somewhat rhombic, 2–2.7 × 1.4–2 mm, with for much of their extent subparallel veins. Disk yellowish or whitish, in a circular cushion 1.5–2.1 mm in diameter and 0.4 mm thick; perigynous groove weak to absent. Stamens less than 1 mm, with filaments enlarged at the base but not connivents; anthers 0.4–0.6 mm wide, with confluent locules and an arcuate transverse slit. Pistil apparent over 1–1.5 mm in height, pyramidal attenuate; ovary semi-immersed with 4(–5) biseriate ovules per locule.

Fruit reddish-orange, subglobose, $ 4 \times 3 \times 3 $ cm, with a smooth pericarp; whitish sweet pulp containing 2 seeds $ 15 \times 10 $ mm with cotyledons well fused together (Letouzey 10769). - Pl. 22, fig. 10–20, p. 85.

Cameroon and Nigeria. To be searched for in northern Gabon. Forest liana rare or poorly collected (the flowers are very modest) capable of persisting in post-cultural scrub. The species is dedicated to the eminent botanist R. LETOUZEY.

14 ter. Salacia togoica Loesener

Bot. Jahrb. 44: 181 (1910); HUTCH. & MOSS, FWTA 1: 452 (1928); HALLÉ, Monogr. Hippocr. Afr. occid., Thèse: 179 (1958); MÉM. IFAN 64: 181, tab. 61 (1962); KEAY & BLAKELOCK, FWTA, ed. 2, 1 (2): 632 (1958); WILCZEK, FCB 9: 229 (1960); IRVINE, WPG: 461 (1961).

Liana entirely glabrous, lacking rubber, with smooth or nearly smooth internodes. Leaves opposite or subopposite. Petiole channelled, 2.5–9 mm long. Blade elliptic, 6–14.5 × 2.5–5.5 cm, with cuneiform base, with attenuate acuminate apex; margins entire or weakly denticulate. Midvein forming above a fine ridge when dry; 6–9 pairs of secondary veins.

Floral fascicles axillary, sessile, 1–3-flowered. Pedicels 2–4 (–12) mm (up to 12 mm according to LOESENER and WILCZEK: see note below). Bud 2.5–4 mm in diameter, shorter than broad. Flower yellow, ochraceous-yellow or ± coppery, 6–9(10) mm in diameter, rotate with corolla somewhat concave above. Calyx with unequal sepals, the outer sometimes subacute, generally obtuse or widely rounded, 1–1.6 mm wide, entire or microdenticulate or bearing, laterally toward the base overlapping, some cilia. Petals suborbicular, 3.5–4.6 mm, with slightly attenuate base, with numerous flabellate veins. Disk 2.5 mm in diameter, helmet-shaped and fleshy, projecting 0.4 mm over the base of the petals, with orbicular outline with 5 small interpetalar angular prominences; a strong perigynous groove is occupied without intervals by three broad staminal filaments. Stamen 0.8 mm high; anther transverse with two distinct but confluent locules, suberect in the bud. Pistil short, 0.6–1 mm; 3 semi-immersed locules with 2 superposed ovules each.

Fruit globose, orange, reaching 3–5 cm, nearly smooth with 3 weak verruculous ridges. - Pl. 23, fig. 1–9, p. 89.

Sierra Leone, Ivory Coast, Ghana, Togo, Dahomey, Nigeria and Cameroon then Zaire (see reservations below). Species riparian or mangrove-edge. To be searched for in Gabon.

There is discordance between all the materials dissected by us where we observed 2 ovules per locule, and those described by WILCZEK for Zaire having 6–8 ovules per locule. The original diagnosis of LOESENER specifies 6 ovules sub-biseriate in each locule. Our initial determinations of this species took into account those of HUTCHINSON & DALZIEL (FWTA, ed. 1) which were also accepted by WILCZEK. The Zaire materials (Yangambi: Louis 15030, Evrard 1984 and Germain 4969) will need to be reviewed. It remains probable that the species presently described will require the use of a new designation. The length of the pedicels in the type, 6–12 mm and that of the stamens, 2 mm, raise doubts, in addition to the number of ovules, about our determination. Note that at the time of collection of the type there was a mixing of two species and the second part of the same number is the type of S. baumannii Loes.

MATERIAL STUDIED (Cameroon):

Leeuwenberg 6193, Riv. Boumba, 8 km SW Madjwé, 36 km road Yokadouma-Moloundou, 470 m, 20.7.1965 (fr. and very young buds). Letouzey 14822, Missellele, 10 km NE Douala, 4.5.1976 (fl.). mangrove edge.
