# *Baissea subrufa* Stapf Planche 22

**Genus**: [[Baissea]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Wiss. Ergebn. Deut. Zentr.-Afr. Exped. 1907-1908, Bot. 2 : 539 (1913)

## Description

Liane reaching 20 m in height, with dark pubescence; branches densely puberulent. Leaf: petiole 2–9 mm, generally with colleters above; lamina obovate to oblong, 1.6–6.5 times as long as wide, 0.5–12 × 0.3–7 cm, rounded to cuneate at base, obtuse to acute at apex, glabrous above, puberulent or glabrous below; midvein and secondary veins generally red, the secondary ones (3)5 or 6(8) pairs distinctly arched; tertiary venation scalariform, forming right angles with the midvein; domatia present. Inflorescence axillary and terminal, lax with dense cymes, pluriflorous; peduncle 0.7–7.3 cm. Flower fragrant; pedicel 1.5–3 mm; calyx with lobes 0.7–2.2 times as long as wide, 1.5–2.5 × 0.8–1.5 mm, puberulent on the exterior, ciliate, glabrous within. Biodiversity Center (©), reproduced from Hawthorne & Jongkind (2006).

Idem, dorsal surface (× 20). – (1: Breteler 2092; 2: Leeuwenberg 7479; 3-8: Leeuwenberg 7479). Drawing by Yuen Fang Tan, Naturalis Biodiversity Center (©), reproduced from van Dilst (1995).

On the interior and with colleters at the base, corolla white to yellow, with cylindrical tube, slightly campanulate, 1.1–2 mm long, puberulent on the exterior, with tufts of hairs on the interior, otherwise glabrous, with lobes ± patent, ovate to oblong, 0.7–1.4 times as long as the tube, ± times as long as wide, 1.2–2.5 × 0.8–1.2 mm, diversely puberulent on the right side, glabrous or partially puberulent on the exterior on the left side, glabrous on the interior; stamens inserted 0.3–0.4 mm above the base of the tube, exserted by 0.1–0.7 mm; anther 0.4–0.6 mm long, puberulent and or papillose on the exterior, ovary pubescent at the apex. Fruit: follicles reaching up to 4.2 cm × 0.7–0.9 mm, puberulent. Distribution: Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon: Ngounié, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo and Woleu-Ntem. Ecology: primary and secondary forest in Gabon, up to 700 m altitude, elsewhere up to 800 m. B: van Dilst (1995). BIBLIOGRAPHY
