# *Dalbergia obliquifoliolata* O.Lachenaud, sp. nov. Planche 47

**Genus**: [[Dalbergia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]

## Description

Leaflets conspicuously asymmetric, inflorescences and flowers very similar to D. bakeri and D. adiantifoliae, but differing from the former in leaflets emarginate at apex and lamina above glabrous with midvein only sparsely pubescent (not lamina on both surfaces shortly appressed-pilose), from the latter in leaflets conspicuously larger (upper ones 5.2–6.4 × 2.1–3.2 cm, not 1.5–2.7 × 0.9–1.3 cm) and sparsely appressed-pilose beneath (not glabrous); from both distinguished by longer calyx, 4–5.5 mm, with acute lobes 1–1.3 mm (not calyx 2.5–4 mm, lobes 0–1 mm). Type (and sole known material): Mcpherson 16286 (holo.: BR!; iso.: MO), Gabon, Forêt des Abeilles, E of Lopé Reserve, in NSG forestry concession, c. 0°27' S 11°45' E, 2 December 1993 (fl). Liane with pronounced aromatic fenugreek odour when dry, apparently without spines or prehensile branches; bark dark brown, ± longitudinally fissured, with lighter lenticels; young branches with short hooked appressed hairs. Stipules unknown, very caducous. Leaf imparipinnate; petiole 0.4–0.6 cm; rachis 4–8.1 cm, with short semi-erect hairs; leaflets 6 to 8, alternate and clearly unequal, with petiolules of 0.7–1.3 mm, blade ± rhomboidal, distinctly asymmetric except in the terminal leaflet, 5.2–6.4 × 2.1–3.2 cm in the upper leaflets and 0.2–2.1 × 0.5–1.4 cm in the lower ones, acute or obtuse at base, obtuse and slightly emarginate at apex, papyraceous, olive-green to olive-brown above and very glaucous beneath when dry, upper surface glabrous except for the midvein shortly and very sparsely pubescent, lower surface with short sparse appressed hairs; lateral veins 5 to 9, scarcely more prominent than the veinlets, oblique and joined near the margin, veinlets in a network prominent and concolorous on the lower surface, with clearly elongate meshes. Inflorescence in a raceme or fairly short panicle, axillary or terminal on leafy branches, 1.8–4.3 cm long, with golden-brown appressed hairs; bracts persistent at flowering, broadly elliptic, ± 0.5 × 0.5 mm, pubescent. Flower: pedicel 3–4.5 mm; bracteoles persistent at flowering, broadly elliptic, 0.7–1 × 0.5–0.7 mm, pubescent; calyx with sparse appressed hairs, tube fairly narrow, 3–3.5 × 2–2.8 mm, teeth valvate ± equal in length, triangular, acute, 1–1.3 mm long, the upper ones somewhat wider and shortly connate; corolla white, petals with claw equalling or exceeding the calyx, standard bent at right angle, with claw 3 mm and elliptic limb 4 × 3 mm, with bilobed apex, wings equalling the keel, with claw 3 mm and limb 4.5 × 2.3 mm, smooth externally, with auriculate base, keel strongly curved, with claw 3 mm and limb 4.5 × 2.3 mm, with auriculate base; stamens in 2 phalanges of 5, filaments ± 7 mm, connate at ¾ of their length, straight at base and curved in their free part; ovary 2.5 × 0.7 mm, glabrous, with 2 ovules and stipe 4 mm; style linear, 2 mm, with punctiform stigma. Pod unknown.

## Distribution

endemic to Gabon, collected only once in the Forêt des Abeilles

## Ecology

primary forest; at about 200 m altitude. Flowering in December

## Source note

This species is very closely related to D. adiantifolia and D. bakeri. It differs from D. bakeri in its leaflets slightly emarginate at the apex and glabrous on the upper surface (except on the midvein), as well as in the longer calyx with very marked teeth. From D. adiantifolia, it is distinguished by the distinctly larger leaflets, with very glaucous and sparsely pubescent lower surface, as well as by the longer floral pedicels and calyces. The type of D. obliquifoliolata was previously referred to D. heudelotii Stapf (Sosef et al. 2006), a surprising confusion since the two species scarcely resemble each other
