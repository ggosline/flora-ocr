# *Dracaena glomerata* Baker Figure 2(D)

**Genus**: [[Dracaena]]
**Family**: [[Dracaenaceae]]
**Protologue**: J. Bot. 12 : 166 (1874)

## Synonyms

- Pleomele glomerata (Baker) N.E.Br., Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1914 : 278 (1914).

## Description

Shrub up to 3.5 m tall; trunk generally simple. Leaves ± uniformly distributed, leaving the stem visible between the sheaths or cataphylls; sheath long, clasping the stem for distinctly more than the circumference, over a length of < 1.5 cm; pseudpetiole shorter than the blade, up to 5 mm wide, slightly winged, grooved, green, base passing abruptly into the sheath; blade elliptic to broadly elliptic, up to 22 × 10 cm, base cuneate, apex cuspidate to mucronate reaching 2 mm, coriaceous, upper surface bright green, midvein sulcate, lower surface pale green, midvein prominent over more than ¾ of the length. Inflorescence erect or pendulous, in a spiciform thyrse, up to 30 cm long, or more rarely bearing up to 5 lateral branches up to 5 cm long; axes often zigzagged with several glomerules, smooth, each generally with distinctly more than 15 flowers; bracts subtending the glomerules deciduous, broadly ovate to narrowly ovate, up to 2.5 × 1.5 cm, decreasing in size toward the apex, scarious; floral bracts obscure. Flower: pedicel up to 4 mm, articulate up to 2 mm from the base; perianth 18–21 mm long, with lobes as long as the tube, reflexed, white to cream with longitudinal red-violet striae on the exterior; stamens inserted near the throat, up to 1 mm shorter than the lobes, filament white, anther up to 1.5 mm long, white; ovary oblong-ellipsoid, up to 3 × 2 mm; style exsert up to 3 mm, white, stigma ± 1 mm in diameter. Fruit spheric to obloïde, ± 14 × 20 mm, entire to 2- or 3-lobed, fleshy, bright yellow to orange; receptacle up to 4 mm long. Seed ellipsoid, ± 11 × 8 mm, smooth.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué, Ngounié, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

understory of primary and secondary forests, humid places; in Gabon as elsewhere at 5–500 m altitude. Flowering recorded in July, August and November

## Source note

D. soyauxiana, D. gabonica and D. buettneri represent the same species, collected from the same locality (environs of Sibang) and represent only different developmental stages. B: Baker (1898), Damen et al. (2018). Bibliography
