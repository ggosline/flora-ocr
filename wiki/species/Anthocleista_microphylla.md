# *Anthocleista microphylla* Wernham

**Genus**: [[Anthocleista]]
**Family**: [[Loganiaceae]]

## Description

in Cat. Talbot's Nig. Pl. : 67 (1913). — FWTA ed. 1, 2 : 18. — LEEUWENBERG, 1. c. : 32, fig. 14, carte 11. — FWTA ed. 2, 2 : 37.

Liane or shrub without spines; small branchlets sometimes somewhat angular when dry.

Leaves shortly petiolate; petiole up to 18 mm long; blade elliptic or narrowly obovate, 2–3 times longer than wide and 7–20 × 3–9 cm, shortly acuminate or apiculate at the apex, rounded or cuneate at the base, or decurrent on the petiole; veins indistinct.

Sepals orbicular, 4–8 mm long, rounded or subtruncate at the apex, spreading or not when dried, the inner ones generally larger, up to 1.5 times longer than the others and 8 mm long, often torn by the development of the corolla. Corolla in the mature bud 3–5 times longer than the calyx, 22–25 mm long and, as in the young bud, cuneate at the apex, white; tube 1.5–3 times longer than the calyx and 1–1.2 times longer than the lobes; 9–12 oblong lobes, spreading.

Berries subglobose, 20–30 × 15–20 mm, apiculate, tuberculate or not, thin-walled.

## Type

Talbot 304, Oban, Calabar, Nigeria (holotype : BM)

## Discussion

Nigeria (Calabar), Fernando Po, Principe and São Tomé, presumably also occurring in Cameroon. Secondary forests. Altitude 400–1 000 m and possibly also lower.
