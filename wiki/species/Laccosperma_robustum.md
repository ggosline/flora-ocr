# *Laccosperma robustum* (Burret) J.Dransf. Planche 12, Figure 3A-C

**Genus**: [[Laccosperma]]
**Family**: [[Arecaceae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull. 37 : 457 (1982)

## Description

, nom. illeg . Neoancistrophyllum robustum (Burret) Rauschert, Taxon 31 : 557 (1982). Robust liane ; stem up to 45 m, 30–50 mm in diameter . Leaf : sheath moderately to abundantly armed with very narrowly triangular spines, with black apex, directed upward, juveniles abundantly armed, mature ones with dense brown-black pubescence, more rare on young sheaths ; ochrea 20–30 cm in length, in longitudinal fragments and disintegrating, armed like the sheath ; petiole 5–12 cm , with brown-black pubescence, particularly on the upper surface, slightly concave above, rounded below, margins armed with spines up to 1.4 cm and with black apices ; rachis 1.5–2 m, transverse section trapezoidal becoming triangular distally, armed like the petiole, spines becoming scattered distally, with scattered light brown pubescence ; lamina with 45 to 65 pairs of equidistant leaflets, opposite to alternate, pendulous, very narrowly elliptical , 30–60 × 1.2–2.8 cm, apex broadly to finely acuminate, margins armed with inequidistant spines of 5–8 mm ; cirrus up to 1.5(–2) m, weakly armed with spines with bulbous black apices, with spine-leaflets of 4.5–5 cm . Inflorescences 6 to 12 on the distal part of the stem ; peduncle 12–20 cm ; prophyll ± 15 cm in length ; rachis up to 50 cm, perpendicular to the main axis, with bracts of 1.5–1.8 cm in length, decreasing distally, dry, covered with dense brown-black pubescence ; floriferous branches 18–25 cm, pendulous, with campanulate-cylindric bracts, ± 5 mm in length, with broadly acuminate triangular apex, dry ; flowers by 2(–3). Flower 10 × 2.5–3 mm ; calyx with tube 4 mm in length, lobes 3.5 × 3 mm, slightly striated, apex rounded to triangular, rarely acuminate ; corolla tubular for 2 mm, lobes 7 × 2 mm, apex acuminate ; stamens with filament 3 × 1 mm, fleshy, anther 3 × 1 mm ; ovary ± 1 mm in diameter ; stigma up to 5 mm in length. Fruit ovoid, 1.2–1.5 × 0.8–1.2 cm at maturity, with 17 to 20 rows of scales, with 1 or 2 seeds. Seed ovoid, 0.8–1.3 × 0.6–0.8 × 0.5 cm, smooth, with a slight depression on one side.

Spine-leaflets. – 6. Infructescence. – 7. Fruit. Scale bar : 1 = 80 cm, 2, 3 = 5 cm, 4 = 1 cm, 5 = 6 cm, 6 = 5 cm, 7 = 1 cm. (1-5 : Sunderland 1757 ; 6, 7 : Sunderland 1791 ). Drawing by Lucy T. Smith, reproduced with permission from Sunderland (2012).

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola (Cabinda) ; in Gabon, present in Nyanga, Ngounié and Ogooué-Ivindo. Conservation status : Least Concern (LC)

## Ecology

secondary forests, in forest clearings, very common along roads and trails, on upland or periodically flooded soil ; in Gabon between 100 and 500 m altitude, elsewhere between 50 and 500 m

## Vernacular names

asperge (fr., nom forestier) ; pendulous-leaved African rattan (angl.) ; aka, nkan (fang pour le stipe nettoyé). Usage : Les cannes sont fortement prisées pour la vannerie.
