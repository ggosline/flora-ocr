# *Laccosperma robustum* (Burret) J.Dransf. Planche 12, Figure 3A-C

**Genus**: [[Laccosperma]]
**Family**: [[Arecaceae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull. 37 : 457 (1982)

## Synonyms

- Ancistrophyllum robustum Burret, Notizbl. Bot. Gart. Berlin-Dahlem 15 : 746 (1942), nom. illeg .
- Neoancistrophyllum robustum (Burret) Rauschert, Taxon 31 : 557 (1982).

## Description

Robust liane; stem up to 45 m, 30–50 mm diameter. Leaf: sheath moderately to abundantly armed with very narrowly triangular spines, black-tipped, directed upward, juveniles abundantly armed, mature ones with dense brown-black indumentum, rarer on young sheaths; ochrea 20–30 cm long, in longitudinal strips and disintegrating, armed like the sheath; petiole 5–12 cm, with brown-black indumentum, particularly on the upper surface, slightly concave above, rounded below, margins armed with spines up to 1.4 cm with black tips; rachis 1.5–2 m, transverse section trapezoidal becoming triangular distally, armed like the petiole, spines becoming sparse distally, with scattered light brown indumentum; blade with 45 to 65 pairs of equidistant leaflets, opposite to alternate, pendulous, very narrowly elliptic, 30–60 × 1.2–2.8 cm, apex broadly to finely acuminate, margins armed with unequally spaced spines 5–8 mm; cirrus up to 1.5(–2) m, weakly armed with spines with bulbous black tips, spine-leaflets 4.5–5 cm. Inflorescences 6 to 12 on the distal part of the stem; peduncle 12–20 cm; pre-leaf ± 15 cm long; rachis up to 50 cm, perpendicular to the main axis, with bracts 1.5–1.8 cm long, diminishing distally, dry, covered with dense brown-black indumentum; flowering branches 18–25 cm, pendulous, with campanulate-cylindric bracts, ± 5 mm long, with broadly acuminate triangular apex, dry; flowers 2(–3) per node. Flower 10 × 2.5–3 mm; calyx with tube 4 mm long, lobes 3.5 × 3 mm, slightly striate, apex rounded to triangular, rarely acuminate; corolla tubular for 2 mm, lobes 7 × 2 mm, apex acuminate; stamens with filament 3 × 1 mm, fleshy, anther 3 × 1 mm; ovary ± 1 mm diameter; stigma up to 5 mm long. Fruit ovoid, 1.2–1.5 × 0.8–1.2 cm at maturity, with 17 to 20 rows of scales, 1 or 2-seeded. Seed ovoid, 0.8–1.3 × 0.6–0.8 × 0.5 cm, smooth, with a slight depression on one side.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola (Cabinda); in Gabon, present in Nyanga, Ngounié and Ogooué-Ivindo. Conservation status: Least Concern (LC)

## Ecology

secondary forests, in forest gaps, very common along roads and trails, on firm ground or periodically flooded land; in Gabon between 100 and 500 m altitude, elsewhere between 50 and 500 m

## Vernacular names
