# *Craterosiphon montanum* Domke

**Genus**: [[Craterosiphon]]
**Family**: [[Thymeleaceae]]
**Protologue**: Domke, Notizbl. Bot. Gart. Berlin, 11 (105) : 353 (1932)

## Description

Small woody liana, more or less regularly ramified, with opposite divaricate branches, twisted near their point of differentiation, straight and slender at their apex. Bark glabrous, rugose, dark reddish-brown, somewhat longitudinally furrowed-reticulate, covered with numerous pustulous lenticels, grey.

Leaves often subalternate, with petiole rather thick, 1 mm thick by 3–5 mm long, somewhat canaliculate. Lamina more or less coriaceous, scarcely thick, 4 to 6 cm long and 3 to 4 cm wide, ovate-oblong, regularly attenuate towards the base, with greatest width situated slightly below the midpoint, fairly abruptly narrowed distally into a narrowly triangular acumen of 10–15 mm, sometimes slightly caudate when dry. Primary vein impressed on the upper surface, very prominent on the lower surface, as are the secondary veins; the latter, approximately 20 pairs, subparallel, anastomosing very close to the somewhat thickened margin of the lamina.

Flowers most often solitary, very shortly pedicellate; pedicel approximately 1 mm covered with 4–10 minute scaly bractlets, sometimes slightly ciliate at the margins. Floral buds acute at the apex. Perianth tube very narrow, 0.5 mm at its base, 1 mm at the apex, infundibuliform, 12–15 mm long, glabrous; lobes glabrous, somewhat fleshy, much longer than wide, approximately 4–6 × 1 to 1.5 mm, narrowly rounded at the apex. Zone of differentiation of the external parts scarcely marked by weak turgescence, without differentiation of a notable thickening. 10 stamens, with filaments inserted at the throat of the tube approximately on a single circle; filaments of the episepalous stamens approximately 1 mm long, filaments of the alternisepalous stamens less than 0.5 mm; anthers very small, nearly 0.5 mm, scarcely prominent at anthesis. Ovary subsessile; style filiform short, scarcely 10 mm long; stigma small (0.5 mm), globulose or discoid, slightly papillose. Hypogynous disc cupuliform, 0.2 mm.

Fruit unknown.

Type from Cameroon: Ledermann 5423 (holotype B probably destroyed; merotype BR! f. fl.); other original material Ledermann 2747.

The only Gabonese material referred to this species possesses characters falling exactly within the description given by Domke; this latter author further notes that the tube is greenish externally, yellowish internally and that the stamens are distinctly yellow (according to Ledermann's notes).

This plant appears well characterized by its leaves with long fine and narrow acumen, and would easily be distinguished from C. beniense Domke by its flowers without petaloid thickenings, from C. pseudoscandens Domke by its very long perianth lobes, from C. scandens Engl. et Gilg by its much smaller flowers.

This plant resembles C. beniense greatly and could exist in the Congo basin.
