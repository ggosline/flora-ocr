# *Rhynchosia preussii* (Harms) Taub. ex Harms

**Genus**: [[Rhynchosia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: R. cyanosperma sensu Baker, non Benth. Fl. W. trop. Afr. éd. 1, 1 : 401 (1928), pro parte

## Description

in Engl., Pflanzenw. Afr. 3, 1 : 671 (1915). R. cyanosperma sensu Baker, non Benth. Fl. W. trop. Afr. ed. 1, 1 : 401 (1928), pro parte. Robust climbing herb; branches longitudinally furrowed, with white pubescence. Stipules caducous, ovate. Leaf: petiole 6–18 cm; rachis 2–3.5 cm; petiolules 5–6 mm; stipels linear, 3–4 mm long; leaflets with elliptic-rhomboidal blade, 6–11 × 5–8 cm, apex with acumen ± 1 cm long, glabrous above, with short whitish to yellowish velvety pubescence below, especially on the veins. Inflorescence in simple or sometimes branched pseudoracemes, 10–40 cm long; peduncle 5–20(–25) cm, floriferous part very dense. Flower: pedicel 9–12 mm, pubescent; calyx with tube 2–3 mm long, with mixed-length brown pubescence, upper lobes obovate, 20–25 × 19–20 mm, lateral ones oblong, obtuse, short, 6–8 × 3 mm, lower one elliptic, ± 18 × 10 mm; corolla yellow streaked with red or pink, standard elliptic, blade 12–15 × 6–8 mm, with claw 2 mm, with 2 auricles and 2 linear glands, wings obovate, blade 10 × 2 mm, with claw 3 mm, auriculate, keel falcate, blade 10 × 3.5 mm, with claw 3 mm; stamens 15 mm, free part ± 5 mm; ovary covered with short dense hairs, extending over 2/3 of the style. Pod constricted between the 2 seeds, ± 15 × 5 mm, with dense grayish pubescence. Seed globose, 3 mm in diameter, bright blue when fresh, black in herbarium.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué, Ngounié, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo, and Woleu-Ntem.

## Ecology

Evergreen forest, secondary forest, forest edge, roadside; from low altitude to 975 m. Flowers in January, March and April and from August to November; fruits in April, May and November. B: Hauman (1954c), Pellegrin (1949). Bibliography
