# *Cyperus brevifolius* (Rottb.) Hassk. Planche 52

**Genus**: [[Cyperus]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Cat. hort. bot. bogor. : 24 (1844)

## Description

Slender perennial herb; rhizome horizontal creeping, up to 15 cm × 1–2 mm, covered with brownish scales, generally bearing stems regularly spaced along its entire length, rarely rather crowded; stems 5–30(–40) cm × 0.3–1.2 mm, glabrous, trigonous and striate; base not swollen, but enclosed in brownish sheaths without lamina, and usually curved down to the rhizome. Leaf: lamina flat or canaliculate, usually 2–3 per stem, the largest 3–10(–15) cm × 1–2 mm, very scabrous on the margin and central vein. Inflorescence a globose or hemispherical head 4–8 mm wide, generally with 1 spike, but sometimes also with short lateral spikes; involucral bracts 1–4, but usually 3 arranged in a cross (one erect and two spreading), rarely (mainly in robust forms) all spreading or reflexed, the main one usually 1–5 cm long; spikes usually with numerous spikelets, but some stunted plants have spikes with only 5–10 spikelets. Spikelet 2–3.5 mm long, with 1(–2) flower; glumes 2–3.5 mm long, often unequal with the apex acuminate and sometimes incurved, often golden-brown, with 2–3 lateral nerves, with central vein greenish, glabrous; old glumes often darker, dull brown or bronze, the colour persisting on dried plants. Achene elliptic to obovate, about 1.2 × 0.8 mm, blackish with small papillae.

## Distribution

widely distributed in tropical regions; rare or poorly known in Gabon, found in Estuaire and Woleu-Ntem (2 collections)

## Ecology

in grassy areas or open wooded zones; in Gabon, at 0–700 m altitude, up to 2900 m in East Africa. B: Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al. (2010), Hooper & Napper (1972), Bibliography Lye (1997)
