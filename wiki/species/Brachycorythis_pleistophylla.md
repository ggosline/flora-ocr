# *Brachycorythis pleistophylla* Rchb.f.

## Description

Terrestrial herb. Roots thick, fleshy, cylindrical, woolly. Stem 25–100 cm. Cauline leaves 10 × 1.5 cm, lanceolate to narrowly lanceolate, glabrous. Inflorescence 15–35 cm, dense, <45-flowered. Flowers rather large or medium-sized, lilac or various shades of purple, often with a white or yellow centre. Dorsal sepal elliptic to ovate, ± cochleate, subacute; lateral sepals semi- to obliquely orbicular, acute. Petals semi-orbicular to obliquely orbicular, acute. Labellum elliptic to orbicular, thickened only along the midline, trilobed at the apex with median lobe absent or tooth-like and much smaller than the 2 lateral lobes entire, rounded or acute. Spur absent.

This species comprises two varieties:

a. var. pleistophylla
