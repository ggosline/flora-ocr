# *Brachycorythis pleistophylla* Rchb.f.

**Genus**: [[Brachycorythis]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Otia Bot. Hamburg : 104 (1881)

## Description

– Brachycorythis briartiana Kraenzl., Bull. Soc. Bot. Belg. 38 : 219 (1900).

– Brachycorythis pulchra Schltr., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 53 : 485 (1915).

– Brachycorythis perrieri Schltr., Beih. Bot. Centralbl. 34 (2) : 296 (1916).

– Brachycorythis macclouniei Braid, Kew Bull. 9 : 357 (1925).

Herbaceous terrestrial. Roots thick, fleshy, cylindrical, woolly. Stem 25–100 cm. Cauline leaves to 10 × 1.5 cm, lanceolate to narrowly so, glabrous. Inflorescence 15–35 cm, dense, ± 45-flowered. Flowers fairly large or medium-sized, lilac or various shades of purple, often with a white or yellow center. Dorsal sepal elliptic to ovate, ± cochleate, subacute; lateral sepals semi- to obliquely orbicular, acute. Petals semi-orbicular to obliquely orbicular, acute. Lip elliptic to orbicular, thickened only along the midvein, three-lobed at the apex with median lobe absent or tooth-like and much smaller than the 2 lateral entire lobes, rounded or acute. Spur absent.

This species comprises two varieties:

a. var. pleistophylla

## Ecology

alt. 1100–2200 m. Grassland, open woodland, savanna, forest edge

b. var. leopoldii (Kraenzl.) Geerinck

Bull. Jard. Bot. Nat. Belg. 52 : 141 (1982) ; FAC 1 : 37 (1984). – BASIONYM: B. leopoldii Kraenzl., Orchid. Gen. Sp. 1 : 542 (1898). – TYPE: Laurent s.n., Congo (Kinshasa), holo-BR!

It is distinguished from the typical variety by its lip broader than long and its dried flowers, pale or medium brown.
