## Description

Wageningen Agric. Univ. Papers 94(4) : 13 (1994). Baphiastrum brachycarpum Harms, Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 49 : 435 (1913). Baphia boonei De Wild., Bull. Jard. Bot. État Bruxelles 5 : 16 (1915). Baphiastrum boonei (De Wild.) Vermoesen ex De Wild., Pl. Bequaert. 3 : 310 (1925). Liane; stem up to 8 cm in diameter; branches densely pubescent-rusty, the same hairs on the stipules, inflorescences, pedicels, calyx, pods and, less densely, on the leaflets beneath. Stipules early caducous, 5–9 × 1.5–6 mm. Leaf: petiole (0.5–)1–2(–9) cm; leaflet with oval-oblong to oblong-elliptic blade, (8–)15–22(–31) × (3–)5–9(–12) cm, rounded to subcordate at the base, 0.5–1(–2) cm acuminate at the apex, papyraceous to coriaceous, glabrous above; lateral nerves in (7–)8 to 13 pairs. Inflorescence in raceme or panicle, up to 15 cm in length; peduncle 0–2.5 cm; bracts and bracteoles oval-triangular, 3–6 × 2 mm. Flower: pedicel 9–20(–26) mm; calyx ± spatulate, generally with two slits and ± partially reflexed, densely covered with tomentose hairs; papilionaceous corolla, 15–20 mm in length, with a greenish spot inside, petals hairy or not on their apical margins—

## Distribution

Cameroon, Central African Republic, Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, known only from Estuaire, Ogooué-Ivindo and Ogooué-Lolo

## Ecology

primary and secondarized forest; in Gabon at 5–600 m in altitude, elsewhere up to 700 m. b: Breteler (1994a), Toussaint (1953). ibliography
