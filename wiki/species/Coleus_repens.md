# *Coleus repens* Gürke

**Genus**: [[Coleus]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 19 : 213 (1894)

## Synonyms

- Solenostemon repens (Gürke) J.K.Morton, J. Linn. Soc., Bot. 58 : 272 (1962).
- Plectranthus epilithicus B.J.Pollard, Kew Bull. 60 : 145 (2005).

## Description

Perennial creeping herb, generally epilithic, 0.05–0.15(–0.25) m tall; stem rooting in its lower part, 0.2–0.3 m long, pubescent with spreading or downward-directed hairs, provided with reddish sessile glands. Leaf: petiole 15–30 mm; blade broadly ovate to subcircular, 1–2.5 × 1–2.5 cm, broadly cuneate to rounded at the base and attenuate on the petiole, rounded at the apex, crenate, often marked with violet or brown, sparsely furnished with reddish sessile glands below. Inflorescence terminal, very short, composed of 1 or 2 pairs of opposite cymes; cymes lax, with 5 to 7 flowers, shortly pedunculate; bracts inconspicuous. Flower: pedicel 3–5 mm; calyx shortly tubular, 2–3 mm long and 5–7 mm in fruit, pubescent and with reddish sessile glands, 5–7 mm in length, upper lip obovate, rounded to mucronate at the apex, lateral lobes oblong to ovate-elliptic, lower lobe entire, linear, parallel to the upper lip, not closing the throat, with short narrowly triangular teeth; corolla white, upper lip 2–3 mm long, violet, lower lip 5–6 mm long; stamens with filaments fused at the base. Fruit: ovoid achenes, 1 mm long, dark brown.

## Distribution

Tropical Africa of west-central and western regions, including: Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea (Bioko), Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon: Estuaire, Haute Ngounié, Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo and Woleu-Ntem. Conservation status: Widely distributed; probably Least Concern (LC).

## Ecology

The species is a lithophyte, rarely epiphytic, which grows on humid rocks at the edge of streams in understory or on humid and shaded rocky walls; in Gabon at 450–900 m altitude, up to 2000 m elsewhere. B: Morales (2011), Morton (1963), Pollard (2005), Vande weghe et al. (2016). Bibliography
