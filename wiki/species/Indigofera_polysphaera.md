# *Indigofera polysphaera* Baker

**Genus**: [[Indigofera]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1895 : 65 (1895)

## Description

Annual herb, erect, 80–130 cm in height, with dense, brownish hispid indumentum. Stipules ovate-lanceolate to falcate, 4–10 × 1–2 mm. Leaf simple; petiole 0–2 mm; stipels absent; leaflet with narrowly lanceolate blade, 2.5–7 × 0.3–0.8 cm, apex mucronate, densely hairy on both faces. Inflorescence composed of numerous terminal and axillary panicles, condensed to subcapituliform, each 2 × 1.5 cm at maximum; bracts linear, 2–4 mm. Flower: pedicel 1–2 mm; calyx with tube 0.5 mm in length, lobes linear, ± 3 mm in length; corolla red, standard ± 5 mm in length, apex subulate, hispid on the outside, wings ± 4 mm in length, glabrous, keel ± 4 mm in length, hispid; stamens and ovary 4 mm in length. Pod cylindric, 3–5 × 1.5–2 mm, with 1 to 3 seeds, brown, densely hispid. Seed reniform, ± 1 × 0.7 mm, brown.

## Distribution

from Côte d'Ivoire to Uganda, Tanzania, Zambia and Angola; in Gabon, probably in the Estuary (Libreville), but its presence remains to be confirmed

## Ecology

savanna; elsewhere up to 1800 m in altitude. Use: The species is probably a good forage

## Source note

This species has complicated synonymy, and is sometimes confused with I. geminata Baker. b: Cronquist (1954), Gillett (1971a). Bibliography
