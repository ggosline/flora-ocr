## Description

Flower with 3 stamens; style with 3 branches. Achene elliptic, 1.4‒1.9 × 0.4‒0.8 mm, trigonous, reddish-brown, with finely papillose surface.

## Distribution

widely distributed in tropical and subtropical regions of Africa, Asia and Australia, probably introduced in America; in Gabon, very frequent, found in Estuaire, Haut-Ogooué, Ngounié, Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo and Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology

in disturbed habitats, often a weed of cultivations, roadsides and forest paths, more rarely in grasslands and savannas; in Gabon, at 0‒500 m altitude, up to 2400 m in East Africa. Taxonomic note: very variable species, particularly regarding robustness and number of flowers per spikelet. This very common species is recognized by its "umbellate" anthela of fairly short spikes composed of numerous very densely packed small spikelets. Although frequently with 2‒3 flowers, the spikelets often produce only a single achene. The various subspecies and varieties described are often difficult to separate, and Hoenselaar et al.
