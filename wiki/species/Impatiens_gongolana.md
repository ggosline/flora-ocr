# *Impatiens gongolana* N. Hallé

**Family**: [[Balsaminaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Impatiens]]

## Description

Gabonese endemic with alternate leaves, acute unappendaged bases and puberulent-scabrid upper blades. Pink flowers are spaced along a terminal portion of the pedunculate raceme; lip white and cup-shaped, spur long and narrow; posterior lobe of the lateral petal acute, triangular and much shorter than the anterior lobe.

## Distribution

Monts de Cristal, Gabon.
