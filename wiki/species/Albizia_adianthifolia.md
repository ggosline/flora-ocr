Tree 15-20 m tall, reaching up to 36 m. Trunk practically without buttresses, usually short, attaining a diameter of 96 cm. Bark smooth, rarely finely fissured, light brown to reddish-brown, red, brown and white, weakly exuding a clear gum. Crown flat with some major spreading branches. Old branchlets glabrous, brown with lighter lenticels. Young branchlets pubescent yellow or rusty. Stipules broadly ovate to lanceolate pubescent on the outside, apex obtuse; caducous. Petiole thinly pubescent yellow to rusty, 1.5-7.5 cm long, upper surface flat to weakly concave, bearing in the lower third a prominent gland. Rachis pubescent yellow, 3-12 cm long; upper surface channelled to progressively keeled channelled bearing 1-2 elliptic prominent glands at the level of insertion of the terminal pinnae. Pinnae (3) 5-10 pairs, elliptic to oblong-elliptic. Pinna axis pubescent yellow to rusty, 6-13 cm long; upper surface keeled bearing 1-4 glands at the level of insertion of the terminal leaflets; lower surface terminated by a mucro narrowly triangular with acute apex, early caducous. Leaflets 5-14 pairs, sessile. Limb pubescent shortly stiffly haired on both surfaces (more abundantly below); green to black above and green ± dark below when dry; rhomboid to elliptic obovate for the apical leaflets; reaching 1.4 × 0.7 mm; base asymmetric: proximal margin attenuate subperpendicular to the pinna axis to weakly rounded, and distal margin attenuate subparallel or at a very acute angle with the pinna axis; apex obtuse to acute, weakly mucronate. Primary vein diagonal, sparsely pubescent to glabrous above and pubescent below, prominent on both surfaces. Secondary veins ascending, slightly curved, glabrous above and pubescent below, prominent on both surfaces; 1 basal secondary vein parallel to the proximal margin sometimes 1 additional very short secondary in the proximal half.

Inflorescence in capituliform spikes, axillary, solitary or fascicled, or in terminal panicle. Peduncle densely pubescent, 2-6 cm long. Inflorescence bract similar to stipules. Bracteole entirely pubescent, very narrowly oblanceolate, ± 3.5 mm long, apex obtuse. Floral bud claviform. Basal flowers ♂, white to violet on the living plant, with pubescent pedicel, 0.5-1.75 mm long. Calyx narrowly obconic, pubescent on the outside (more densely at the apex), pale green on the living plant, 2.75-4.25 mm long; tube 2.5-4 mm long; free lobes triangular, 0.25-1 mm long. Corolla pubescent on the outside, white on the living plant, 9-12.5 mm long; corolla tube 6.75-9 mm long; free lobes narrowly oblanceolate to narrowly elliptic, 1.5-4 mm long, apex acute. Stamens 30.5-36.5 mm long; staminal tube longly exsert, 25-31 mm long; free filament 5.5-7 mm long; anther ellipsoid, 0.25 mm long. Pistil subsessile or with stipe reaching 0.25 mm; ovary glabrous, narrowly ellipsoid, ± laterally compressed, 2.2-75 mm long, containing 6 ovules; style slender 29.5-31.5 mm long, terminating in a capitate plateau. Terminal flower sessile. Calyx cylindric, pubescent, 5 mm long; tube 4 mm long; free lobes narrowly triangular, 1 mm long. Corolla pubescent on the outside, 9 mm long; tube 6.5 mm long; free lobes elliptic 2.5 mm long, apex acute. Stamens 13.5 mm long; staminal tube 7 mm long, apex free, reflexed, 6.5 mm long; anther as previously. Central pistillode.

Pod dehiscent, chartaceous, finely densely pubescent, elliptic-oblong to oblong, 10-17.5 × 2-3 cm; base distinctly long or shortly stipitate; sutural edges thickened ± keeled; lateral faces with dense very prominent venation, weakly to distinctly ascending, forming a dense network with polygonal meshes; apex broadly obtuse often very asymmetrically very shortly apiculate. Seeds black-brown, ± orbicular, 6.5-8 × 6.5-8 mm; central areole 5-5.5 × 3-3.5 mm.

Seedling with cotyledons ± oblong, fleshy, flattened, 8 × 5 mm, erect, emarginate at the base; early caducous. Hypocotyl pubescent, 2 cm long then 5-6 cm. Epicotyl short, 1 cm long. Leaves 1 and 2 opposite, bipinnate compound, with one pair of pinnae. Petiole pubescent long, 2 mm; upper surface terminated by a gland; lower surface terminated by a mucro. Pinna axis pubescent, 1.8-2 cm long; lower surface terminated by a mucro. Leaflets 5 pairs (basal pair with one reduced leaflet and one stipellary). Limb asymmetric, 8 × 4 mm, base truncate; primary vein pubescent (After G. de la Mensbruge GPEACI, 1966).

## Type

Thonning s.n., Ghana, Bligusso (C)

## Discussion

This species is very variable as to the size of the leaflets, the density of pubescence of the stem and leaves, the shape of the stipules and inflorescence bracts.

It is quite close to A. gummifera (Gmel.) C.A.Sm. but differs from it by the absence of auricles at the base of the leaflets and the pubescence on both surfaces of the limb.

A. adianthifolia var. adianthifolia is widely distributed throughout intertropical Africa and Madagascar. Its range extends from Gambia to Kenya and South-West Africa and Mozambique towards the South. It is generally found in fallows and in zones of secondary vegetation.
