# *Desmodium velutinum* (Willd.) DC.

**Genus**: [[Desmodium]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Prodr. 2 : 328 (1825)

## Description

D. lasiocarpum (P.Beauv.) DC., Prodr. 2 : 328 (1825). Herb or suffrutex, 1–1.8(–3) m tall; stem cylindrical, dark red, uncinate-puberulent. Stipules persistent, narrowed from the base into a slender point, 2.2–6 × 1–3.5 mm, greyish. Leaf: petiole 0.3–2.2 cm; stipels subulate, 1.5–4 mm long; leaflets 1(–3), with rhomboid-ovate to elliptic blade, 4–12 × 2.3–8 cm, base cuneate to obtuse, apex obtuse and mucronate, coriaceous, with fairly dense and semi-appressed pubescence above, erect below; venation reticulate below. Inflorescence terminal and axillary, in slender pseudoracemes 3–10(–15) cm long, dense, with 3 to 5 flowers per node, with abundant uncinate pubescence mixed with straight pointed hairs; bracts lanceolate-pointed, 1.5–4 × 0.5–1 mm, finely hirsute. Flower: pedicel 1.5–3 mm; calyx bilabiate, strigose, with lobes 2–3 mm long; corolla rose-purple to violet, standard and wings 4–5.5 mm long, keel exceeding the other petals. Pod sessile to shortly stipitate, dorsal suture slightly undulate, ventral suture emarginate-rounded, denticulate, with 2 to 6(–7) articles 2.5–4 × 2.5–5.5 mm and uncinate-pubescent. Seed ovate-subreniform, ± 2 × 1.5 × 1 mm, fairly flattened, yellow or brown; hilum ± central, black.

## Distribution

Tropical Africa, Madagascar, Indonesia, Malaysia and Caribbean; in Gabon, known from Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Maritime, Ngounié, and Nyanga

## Ecology

fairly common in humid savanna and open forest, roadsides and ruderal; in Gabon up to 250 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1300 m. Use: Outside Gabon, this species has medicinal uses

## Source note

The specimen Mouandza-Mbembo 210 from Loango National Park has trifoliolate leaves, which is very rare (cf. Schubert, 1971). Bibliography: De la Estrella et al. (2010), Hepper (1958), Mackinder et al. (2001), van der Maesen (2006a), Pellegrin (1949), Raponda-Walker & Sillans (1961), Schubert (1954, 1971)
