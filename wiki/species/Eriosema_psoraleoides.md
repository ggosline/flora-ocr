# *Eriosema psoraleoides* (Lam.) G.Don Planche 62

**Genus**: [[Eriosema]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Gen. hist. 2 : 348 (1832)

## Description

' Crotalaria psoraleoides Lam., Dict. 2 : 201 (1786), as psoraloides . E. cajanoides (Guill. & Perr.) Hook.f. ex Hook., Niger Fl. : 314 (1849). Suffrutex 1–1.5(–3.6) m tall, with a strong taproot; woody base; branches angular, striate, tomentose, with dense appressed grey-brown hairs. Stipules triangular to lanceolate, 3–5 mm long, scarious, striate, pubescent outside, glabrous inside. Leaf: petiole 2–6 mm; rachis 3–6 mm; leaflets 3, petiolules 2–3 mm, blade oblong-elliptic, 2–9 × 0.8–4 cm, with cuneate base, apical part obtuse, true apex rounded to acute, mucronate or sometimes emarginate, green and appressed-silky above, grey and appressed-tomentose below; lateral veins in 9 to 16 pairs, with brownish hairs. Inflorescence axillary and terminal, in spiciform racemes 3–15 cm long including the peduncle of 2–3 cm; bracts very caducous, lanceolate, 3–4 mm long, brown-pubescent outside, glabrous inside. Flower pendulous; pedicel 1 mm; calyx with tube 2 mm long, teeth triangular, 2–3 mm long, densely pubescent greyish-yellow; corolla yellow, standard suborbicular, 6–9 × 4–7 mm, with apex slightly emarginate, with 2 rounded auricles, without gibbosities, claw 2–3 mm, wings glabrous, keel 7–10 mm long, glandular. Pod elliptic, reaching 1.5 × 1 cm, with 2 seeds, densely pubescent golden-yellow. Seed oblong, ± 5 × 3.5 × 2 mm; hilum surrounding the upper and lateral sides.

## Distribution

Widely distributed, from Senegal to Mozambique, in South Africa and the Mascarene Islands; in Gabon, known from Haut-Ogooué and Nyanga

## Ecology

savanna, roadside; in Gabon at 90–150 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1700 m. Flowering: February, May, August to October. Uses: Cited by Raponda-Walker & Sillans (1961) without mention of use. In the Democratic Republic of the Congo, it is medicinal or applied as ichthyotoxic (Hauman 1954b)

## Vernacular names

ngwè-yígongo (mpongwè). b : De la Estrella et al. (2010), Ern (1984), Hauman (1954c), Hepper (1958), Mackinder ibliographie et al. (2001), van der Maesen (2006a), Pellegrin (1949), Raponda-Walker & Sillans (1961), Verdcourt (1971c), Watt & Breyer-Brandwijk (1962)
