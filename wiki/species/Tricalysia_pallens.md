# *Tricalysia pallens* Hiern

**Genus**: [[Tricalysia]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Hiern, F.T.A. 3 : 121 (1877)

## Description

a. — var. pallens

Woody shrub or small tree 0.50–3 m tall. Juvenile branchlets slender and ochraceous pubescent. Stipules pubescent, 2–4 (7) mm long, with a subulate acumen varying from 1–3 (6) mm. Petiole 3–7 mm, pubescent, with no distinct channel. Blade grey-ochraceous or olivaceous when dry, glabrous, elliptic, 4–12 × 1.5–4.5 cm; base acute, more rarely subobtuse; apex acuminate, sometimes lanceolate or subobtuse. Midvein presenting a fine pubescent groove on the upper surface, briefly pubescent beneath. 6–8 pairs of secondary veins; fine network moderately distinct. Domatia axillary, presenting a ciliolate ostiole.

Inflorescences axillary in cymes or fascicles of very contracted cymes, few- to many-flowered; the most floriferous forms present spherical inflorescences approximately 2 cm in diameter at each node. Axes and pedicels absent; bracts pubescent, united into briefly naviculate cupules. Apex of bud acute. Flower white with green and pubescent calyx. 4–5 calycine lobes generally very short, approximately 0.5 mm or less, exceptionally 1 to 3 mm; sometimes with minute intercalary denticles; calyx tube approximately 1 mm tall. Corolla tube 2.5–4 mm long, ± glabrous externally, ± pubescent internally. 5 (6) lobes 3–4 × 1.5–2 mm, oblong, moderately acute, distinctly pubescent on the external surface and at the apex. Anthers oblong, 2.5 × 0.5 mm, briefly apiculate, somewhat pubescent (N. Hallé and L. T. 262) to glabrous (N. Hallé 3501), inserted toward the lower third. Filament glabrous, exsert 1–2 mm. Style pubescent, with a bilobed, exsert, white stigma. Annular disk.

Ovary sometimes glabrous, sometimes pubescent under the bracteal cupule, with 2 locules bi- or triovulate.

Fruit small, globose, 3–4 mm in diameter, pubescent or glabrous, with a persistent pubescent calycinal limb. Seeds not observed in the Gabonese material.

b. — var. gabonica (Hiern) N. Hallé, comb. nov.

= Tricalysia gabonica Hiern, F.T.A. 3 : 122 (1877).

Differs from the typical variety by the pubescence of the internodes, which is somewhat stronger and more hirsute. The leaves of the type are somewhat smaller, obovate, cuneiform at the base. Fruit with 2 seeds (Klein 4).

Gabonese material (attributed with hesitation):

Klein 4, Cape Esterias (fl. and fr. July).

Thollon 1138, Mayumbe forest (buds July).

c. — var. dundensis (Cavaco) N. Hallé, comb. nov.

= Tricalysia dundensis Cavaco Fl. de la Lunda : 149 (Lisboa 1959).

This non-Gabonese variety is distinguished by the lower surface of its leaves clothed with fine hairs of an almost woolly appearance; it is very close to the preceding one, notably in the pubescence of its internodes.

It is necessary to clarify that this plant has no affinities with Tricalysia griseiflora K Schum. (Type: Weilwitsch 3128, isotype P!) which is a synonym of Tr. angolensis A. Rich., the type species of the genus. The confusion arises because the numbers Welwitsch 3131 and 3132 (P. ex Esc. Polyt.) were cited by Hiern (Cat. Welw. 2 : 469, 1898) as T. griseiflora whereas they actually belong to Tr. pallens.
