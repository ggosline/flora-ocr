A large tree reaching a height of 45 m and a circumference of 3.60 m. Slender trunk, tall, furnished at its base with winged buttresses extending 3 m or more, often branched. Brownish bark, smooth, finely fissured on old trees; cut surface cream-colored becoming reddish towards the interior. Crown flat and broad with fine foliage. Young branchlets pubescent tomentellous to glabrous. Old branchlets glabrous dark brown. Stipule caducous, densely pubescent, linear, 5.5–9 mm long; apex acute. Petiole pubescent tomentellous, 0.7–3 cm long, flat at the base, channelled towards the apex. Rachis pubescent tomentellous tawny, 2.5–11 cm long (reaching 22 cm in young leaves), channelled above; prominent ridge below. Pinnae 10–12 pairs alternate to rarely opposite. Pinna axis pubescent with longer hairs on the margins of the upper surface at the level of the leaflet insertion, 1–4.5 cm long; semicircular in section, upper surface flat. Leaflets 26–60 pairs, sessile. Blade glabrous on both surfaces; black above and brown below when dry; rhombic asymmetric linear ± falcate, 1.5–7.5 mm long; base asymmetric: proximal margin auriculate and distal margin linear to weakly rounded; margin ciliate; apex obtuse. Primary vein in the distal half of the blade, straight, invisible above and very weakly prominent to concave when dry, below. Secondary veins invisible above and almost indistinct below. Veinlets not visible.

Inflorescence in a dense terminal panicle. Axis densely pubescent tomentellous. Bracteole pubescent tomentellous externally, lanceolate, 0.50–1 mm, apex acute, early caducous. Pedicel pubescent to puberulent tawny, ± 0.75 mm long, with apical abscission zone. Floral bud glabrous, ellipsoid fusiform, narrowed at the apex. Flowers ♢, pentamerous, yellow, yellowish or white in life. Calyx cupuliform, glabrous on both surfaces, 0.75 mm long; lobes free broadly triangular, 0.25 mm long; apex obtuse sometimes very shortly ciliate. Petals white to yellow when fresh; glabrous; adnate to the stamens; 2.5–2.75 mm long, apex acute. Stamens 10, red when fresh; adnate to each other by the filament and to the disc, over 0.75 mm; free filament slender 3–4 mm long; anther ellipsoid oblong, 0.25 mm long, connective terminating in a globose sessile gland early caducous. Intrastaminal fleshy disc present; adnate as a cushion to the stamen filaments. Pistil glabrous, red in life; with a stipe 2.75–3.50 mm long; ovary 1.50–2 mm long with 9 ovules; style slender 2.75–3 mm long, terminated by a weakly dilated and hollow stigma.

Legume dehiscent by a single slit, chartaceous sublignenous, flattened, straight or incurved, linear, 12.5–36 × 1.6–3.2 cm; base attenuate with a stipe 0.5–0.8 cm long; apex ± obtuse apiculate; transverse venation prominent little to moderately abundant. Seed winged with a membranous wing, shiny brown when dry, oblong, 3–9.5 × 1.3–2.5 cm; elongate along the fruit axis; funicle linear, inserted on a long face.

Seedling with hypocotyl reaching 3 cm, ribbed and pubescent to tomentose. Cotyledons epigeal, weakly fleshy, transversely elliptic, with an emarginate base. Epicotyl pubescent rusty grooved. First leaf alternate compound pinnate with a petiole 3–4 mm long, pubescent rachis 3–5 cm long, with 30–50 pairs following in pseudo-whorls of compound pinnate leaves as previously. Roots with moderately abundant nodosities. – Pl. 14, p. 59.

## Discussion

The range of this species extends from Senegal (Casamance) to Sudan and Uganda in the East and Angola in the South. It is found in forest groupings. Flowering and fruiting take place during the rainy seasons.

1. BRENAN, in FTEA, l.c., records flowers with shorter styles.

## Uses

The wood, yellowish brown, rather tender, is used in carpentry. A decoction of bark is said to have abortive properties; it is also used in gargles against toothache.
