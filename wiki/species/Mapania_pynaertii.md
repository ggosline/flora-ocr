# *Mapania pynaertii* De Wild. Planche 113(1-4), Figure 19b

**Genus**: [[Mapania]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Ann. Mus. Congo Belge, Bot., sér. 5, 3 : 166, tab. 28, 12–17 (1910)

## Description

Hypolytrum pynaertii (De Wild.) Nelmes, Kew Bull. 10 : 81 (1955). H. gabonicum Cherm., Bull. Soc. Bot. France 77 : 277 (1930). H. gabonicum Cherm. var. plicatum Cherm., Bull. Soc. Bot. France 83 : 494 (1936). Robust or slender herb, with thick woody rhizome 5–20 mm in diameter, erect or oblique; 1–5 lateral stems, often arising below the living lower leaves, 10–70 cm long, triangular–rounded, green, sometimes becoming brownish with age, nearly smooth, basal portion covered by 3–5 nearly tubular and greenish sheaths. Leaves all basal, numerous; sheath indistinct; blade linear, flat or folded, the largest 30–120 × 0.5–3 cm, green with prominent lateral veins, margins distinctly scabrid, gradually narrowed to an acute apex. Inflorescence terminal, in globose to hemispherical head, 1–3 cm broad, composed of 10–60 crowded spikes; involucral bracts ± developed, generally shorter than the inflorescence, green sometimes becoming brown with age; spikes ovoid to cylindric, 6–10 × 3–4 mm, brown to light brown, usually composed of 20–30 crowded spikelets; spikelet bracts ovate–triangular, approximately 3 × 2 mm, brown to light brown, apex rounded. Flower: floral bracts 2, linear, 2.5 × 0.5 mm, brown, keel hispid; style with 2 branches. Achene obovoid to turbinate, 3.5–4.5 × 1–1.5 mm, straw-yellow to pale brown with darker reddish-brown spots, with distinctly cuneate base and rostrate apex, surface weakly wrinkled.

## Distribution

From Cameroon to Congo; fairly widespread in Gabon, found in Ngounié, Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem (9 collections).

## Ecology

Swamp forests, sometimes along watercourses and at pond margins; in Gabon, at 300–600 m altitude, in Cameroon up to 700 m. Taxonomic note: very close to M. secans, differing from it by its green involucral bracts, its larger achenes with a different colour pattern. Bibliography: Nelmes (1955a), Raynal (1968b).
