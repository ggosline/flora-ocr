# *Bulbostylis pilosa* (Willd.) Cherm. Planche 15

**Genus**: [[Bulbostylis]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Bot. France 81 : 266 (1934)

## Description

Abildgaardia pilosa (Willd.) Nees, Linnaea 9 : 289 (1835). B. aphyllanthoides (Ridl.) C.B.Clarke in Durand & Schinz, Consp. fl. afric. 5 : 611 (1894). Fimbristylis africana Durand & Schinz, Consp. fl. afric. 5 : 601 (1894), non Fimbristylis pilosa (Poir.) Vahl (1805). Perennial herb, in dense tufts, with stems closely packed along a thick creeping rhizome; stems 20–70 cm × 0.5–2 mm, angular, scabrous at least above, and finely hairy on the slightly swollen part below the inflorescence, glabrous or hairy below. Leaf: sheath brown or reddish-brown, hairy and with very long white hairs at the collar; blade 5–25 cm × 1–3.5 mm, flat, finely hairy. Inflorescence terminal, formed of a fascicle of 3–10 spikelets; main involucral bracts foliaceous, 1–2 cm long, finely hairy and bordered towards the base with long white hairs. Spikelet ovate, 8–15 × 3–8 mm, compressed, with at least the lower glumes distichous; glumes 6–8 mm long, ovate to triangular, golden or reddish-brown (sometimes with golden inflorescences and reddish-brown inflorescences on the same plant) with usually a green scabrous central nerve ending in a point, otherwise almost glabrous or with a hairy margin. Flower: style with 3 branches. Achene 1.7–2 × 1.2–1.5 mm (including the reddish-brown base, usually persistent, of the style), obovate with a fusiform, triangular base, greyish; surface transversely ridged.

## Distribution

very widespread in tropical Africa from Senegal to Kenya and southwards, to Zimbabwe; rare in Gabon, found in the Haut-Ogooué, Moyen-Ogooué and Woleu-Ntem (3 collections)

## Ecology

in grassy savanna and hills; in Gabon, at 100–750 m altitude, up to 1200 m in East Africa
