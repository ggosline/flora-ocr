# *Renealmia polyantha* K. Schum.

**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Renealmia]]

## Description

Creeping-rhizomed herb with leafy shoots 0.5–1 m. Petiole 3–5 cm; narrow glabrous blades to c. 40 × 4 cm. Glabrous inflorescence as tall as the leafy shoot, with green narrow bracts each subtending 4–6 flowers. Flowers white; calyx 6 mm. Red globose capsule c. 8 mm diameter, normally with 8–9 seeds.

## Distribution

Humid forest understorey in Cameroon and Gabon.
