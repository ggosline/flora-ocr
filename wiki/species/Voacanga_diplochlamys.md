# *Voacanga diplochlamys* K.Schum.

**Genus**: [[Voacanga]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]

## Description

in Engl. & Prantl, 1DW3ÀDQ]HQIDP 4(2) : 149 (1895). Shrub or small tree reaching 6 m in height; branches glabrous. Leaf: petiole 1–2 cm, limb elliptic to narrowly oblong, 6–8 × 2–3 cm, cuneate at base, progressively acuminate at apex, glabrous above, densely puberulent with colorless hairs on the principal veins beneath; secondary veins 6 to 16 pairs, tertiary venation discrete. Inflorescence pendant, 8–12 cm long, with flowers densely puberulent, pedicels 2–4 cm, ramifications absent or up to 4 cm, bracts generally persistent at anthesis, 6–8 × 3–6 mm. Flower: pedicel 4–8 mm; calyx persistent, often even under the fruit, pale green, not enclosing the corolla tube, with campanulate tube, 5–6 mm long, with a discontinuous band of colleters towards mid-height, with lobes spreading or generally reflexed at anthesis, elliptic to obovate, 3–4 × 1.5–2 mm, rounded; corolla with tube 10–12 mm long, somewhat longer to much longer, somewhat shorter to much shorter than the calyx, retracted up to the throat, strongly twisted especially in the upper half, glabrous on the outside, with a dense band of short appressed pubescence on the inside, with yellow lobes, spreading or reflexed at anthesis, obovate, 3.5–7 × 2.5–4 mm, 1–2 times as long as wide, rounded, forming at the apex in bud an ovoid or short conical head; anther 1 mm long, arriving just (apex included) at the corolla throat; pistil 7–9 mm long. Fruit with distinct carpels, obliquely obovoid, 3–4 × 2.2–2.5 cm, rounded at the apex (on the dry state). Distribution: southeastern Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Rio Muni and Gabon, in Gabon known by a single collection (Le Testu 9399) from the Woleu-Ntem.

## Ecology

Primary evergreen forest, secondary regrowth; in Gabon at ± 600 m altitude, elsewhere at 150–760 m

## Source note

In the revision by Leeuwenberg (1985), V. diplochlamys was included in V. bracteata, considered here as endemic to West Africa. The specimens from Gabon assigned to V. bracteata by Leeuwenberg (1985) belong here to V. diplochlamys, V. lanceolata and V. micrantha. B: Jongkind & Lachenaud (2022), Leeuwenberg (1985).
