# *Ranalisma humile* (Kunth) Hutch.

**Genus**: [[Ranalisma]]
**Family**: [[Alismataceae]]
**Protologue**: Alisma humile Kunth, Enum. pl. 3 : 154 (1841)

## Synonyms

- Alisma humile Kunth, Enum. pl. 3 : 154 (1841).
- Echinodorus humilis (Kunth) Buchenau, Pringsh. Jahrb. 7 : 28 (1868).
- Sagittaria humilis (Kunth) O.Ktze, Rev. gen. pl. 3 : 326 (1891).

## Description

Herb 2–6 cm if emergent on mud or reaching 15 cm if submerged; roots reddish-brown; stolons short. Leaves with petiole 1–10 cm long; blade of emergent leaves ovate to ovate-lanceolate, 1–3 × 0.5–1.5 cm, base rounded to long-attenuate, apex acute, that of submerged leaves linear-lanceolate, up to 10 × 0.5 cm; main veins 3(–5). Inflorescence 1–2-flowered, on a scape as long as the petioles. Flowers with imbricate sepals, ovate, 4–4.5 × 2.5–3 mm, green; petals 6–6.5 × 4–5 mm, white to pink, very delicate; stamens with filament 2.5 mm long, anthers ovoid, less than 1 mm long, yellow; carpels up to about 70; style 1 mm long. Achenes arranged in a globose head 4–10 mm in diameter, 2–2.5 × 1.5–2 mm, laterally compressed, with persistent and rigid style. Seed with thin tegument, nearly smooth.

## Distribution

Species with wide distribution, known from Mauritania, Senegal, Mali, Niger, Nigeria, Chad, Sudan, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Gabon, Tanzania, Zambia and Angola. In Gabon, the species is known only from the North-East, in the provinces of Woleu-Ntem and Ogooué-Ivindo

## Ecology

Springs and watercourses, pools, marshes, mud banks, and gallery or swamp forests; in Gabon at ± 500 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1500 m. BIBLIOGRAPHY: Carter (1960), Hepper (1968b), Symoens (1984), Symoens & Billiet (1975)
