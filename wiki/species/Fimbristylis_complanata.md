# *Fimbristylis complanata* (Retz.) Link Planche 89

**Genus**: [[Fimbristylis]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Hort. Berol. 1 : 292 (1827)

## Synonyms

- Scirpus complanatus Retz., Obs. Bot. 5 : 14 (1788).

## Description

Perennial herb, in tufts, but sometimes appearing annual; short creeping rhizome, often branched, woody and densely clothed with stems 30–120 cm × 1.5–3 mm, flattened, glabrous except the margin which is usually scabrous just below the inflorescence, bases of stems protected by rigid, split scales. Leaf: sheath closed, glabrous on the surface, hairy on the margin near the ligule which is formed of a dense row of hairs; blade 1–35 cm long (always more than 5 cm for the largest leaf), flat, with scabrous margin, surface glabrous or weakly hairy below. Inflorescence in complex anthela with groups of spikelets of order 3 and 4; primary bracts 1–2 cm long, foliaceous with scabrous margin and glabrous surface. Spikelet approximately 5–8(–12 at fruiting) × 2 mm, dark brown; primary bract of spikelet approximately 1.5–2 mm long, brownish, usually with the central nerve prominent and scabrous; fertile glumes approximately 3 mm long, apex acute or with the central nerve slightly prominent, dark brown with a paler margin, generally frayed. Flower with 3 stamens, filaments flattened; style with 3 branches. Achene approximately 0.8–1 × 0.7 mm, brownish, triangular, with cells in longitudinal rows and scattered papillae (young fruits sometimes white and smooth), epidermal cells rectangular or transversely oblong.

## Distribution

in most tropical regions; uncommon in Gabon, found in Estuaire, Haut-Ogooué and Ogooué-Ivindo (4 collections)

## Ecology

in humid grasslands, on river banks in forest, at the edge of swamps and pools; in Gabon, at 0–450 m altitude, up to 1700 m in East Africa. B: Goetghebeur & Coudijzer (1984a), Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al. (2010), Hooper & Napper (1972), Lye (1995, 1997)
