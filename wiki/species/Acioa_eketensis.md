# *Acioa eketensis* De Wildeman

**Genus**: [[Acioa]]
**Family**: [[Scytopetalaceae]]
**Protologue**: BJBB 7 : 214 (1920); KEAY, FWTA, ed. 2, 1 : 433 (1958)

## Description

This species is to date known only from the type specimen Talbot s.n. from Nigeria (and from a specimen Talbot 3239 which is presumably only part of the former); moreover, it is not mentioned by KEAY, ONOCHIE & STANFIELD, Nig. Trees 1 : 320 (1960). A Gabonese specimen (Chevalier 27127) can seemingly be attached to it, whose characters are given below, with the different or additional specific characters of Acioa eketensis De Wild. also indicated in parentheses, as they result from the diagnosis and observation of the holotype.

Branchlets smooth, sparsely lenticellate, very sparsely puberulent when young. Stipules linear (falcate-lanceolate), 6 mm, furnished with short appressed hairs. Petiole 3–4 mm, glabrous. Limb oblong (elliptic), 4–5.5(–8.5) × 10–14.5(–17) cm; base rounded subcordate (cordate); apex obtuse with acumen, 1 cm, curved downward; a few very rare short hairs, scattered beneath the midvein; lateral veins 7–8 pairs, inclined at 45° to the midvein and curved near the margin; limb

glabrous; 2 large circular or elliptic glands at the extreme base of the limb beneath, sometimes 1 or 2 higher up, circular glands along the midvein below the apex and acumen, rarely elsewhere.

Inflorescences cymose terminal or subterminally axillary, reaching 10 cm in height, rather open, 1, 2(–3) times branched ± dichotomously; peduncle approximately 3 cm, furnished with a few lanceolate or deeply tricuspid bracts, puberulent; all axes and pedicels likewise puberulent; secondary axes 15–20 mm, tertiary axes 8–10 mm, at the base of the axes oval acute bracts, 5 mm, tomentellose on the outside and inside; portion of the pedicels below the articulation 5–8 mm bearing at the apex 2 opposite oval acute bracteoles, 2 mm (4–5 mm?), tomentellose also on both surfaces. Young receptacle pedicellate (3–4 mm), then narrowly obconic 6–7 mm, tomentellose; sepals 5–6 mm, tomentellose on the outside (sometimes with lateral glands); petals oblong with subcordate base, 5 mm in height; androecium (35–40–45 mm (recorded as briefly pubescent fide KEAY, l.c. : 433), approximately 20 filaments.

Fruit and infructescence unknown. — PL. 7, p. 29.
