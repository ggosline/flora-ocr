# *Scleria interrupta* Rich. Figure 26a

**Genus**: [[Scleria]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Actes Soc. Hist. Nat. Paris 1 : 113 (1849)

## Description

S. hirtella sensu Napper, Fl. W. trop. Afr. éd. 2, 3(2): 344 (1972), non Swartz (1788). Annual herb, slender, with fibrous roots; stem 20–60 cm, hairy or more rarely glabrous. Basal and cauline leaves; sheaths hairy especially below the throat; blade 5–20 cm × 1–3 mm, pubescent or glabrous. Inflorescence a simple "spike", 3–20 cm long, with 4–15 glomerate spikelets erect or spreading, never pendulous. Spikelet 3–4 mm long; glumes brown at the base, but generally more reddish-brown towards the apex, ± hairy, with white or brown hairs at least on the margin and central nerve. Achene obovoid to almost globose, 1.2–1.8 × 1–1.5 mm, tuberculate to trabeculate, grey, on a triangular pedicel.

## Distribution

Widely distributed in tropical Africa, also in South Africa, Madagascar and South America; rare in Gabon, found only in Nyanga and Ogooué-Maritime (2 collections)

## Ecology

Humid depressions of grasslands and forests, often near watercourses; in Gabon, at 100–200 m altitude, at 0–1160 m in East Africa. Taxonomic note: close to S. melanotricha and differs from it by its glomerate spikelets erect or spreading, never pendulous. b: Haines & Lye (1983), Hooper & Napper (1972), Raynal (1976), Robinson ibliography (1966)
