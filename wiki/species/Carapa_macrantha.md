# *Carapa macrantha* Harms

**Genus**: [[Carapa]]
**Family**: [[Meliaceae]]
**Protologue**: Notizbl. Bot. Gart. Berlin 65(7) : 228 (1917)

## Description

Notizbl. Bot. Gart. Berlin 65(7) : 228 (1917). C. oreophila Kenfack, Harvard Pap. Bot. 16(2) : 214 (2011), syn. nov. Small tree 6–20(–25) m tall; bark brown, scaly; interior cream-coloured, discolouring brown (upon oxidation?). Leaf paripinnate: petiole 12–38 cm, relatively narrow at base, 3–7 mm in diameter above the pulvinus, brownish, glabrescent; rachis 7–32(–40) cm; petiolule (0.5–)1–1.5(–2) cm; leaflets 6–12(–22), opposite or subopposite, elliptic to narrowly oblong or obovate, (13–)14–23 × (4–)6–9 cm, base obtuse to broadly cuneate, apex obtuse and moreover very shortly acuminate or mucronate, coriaceous, glabrous; primary nerve prominent and glabrous beneath, secondary nerves 7–12 pairs. Inflorescence with pellicular rachis reaching 25 cm in length, branches reaching 5 cm in length, glabrous. Flower: pedicel 2–4(–5) mm, glabrous; calyx patellifom, deeply 5-lobed, lobes (sepals) almost free, broadly ovate, 2–3 × 2.5 mm, nearly glabrous, ciliate; petals 5, obovate, (5.5–)7–9 × 4–5 mm, white to pink, glabrous; staminal tube urceolate, 5.5–7 mm in length, fairly deeply 10–11-lobed at apex, lobes subtruncate 1.5 mm in length, glabrous, with 10 ovate anthers 1 × 0.5 mm; annular disc, 1–2 mm in length; ovary narrowed in male flowers, longitudinally deeply 5-ridged in functionally female flowers, glabrous, 5-locular, with (3–)4 ovules per locule; style reaching 2.5 mm, glabrous. Fruit subglobose, 7–12 cm in diameter, brownish, surface strongly mammillose. Seeds up to 4 per locule, 4–5 cm in diameter, surface smooth, brown.

## Distribution

Nigeria and Cameroon; presence in Gabon remains to be confirmed.

## Ecology

Primary forests and old secondary forests; up to 2450 m in altitude. Taxonomic notes: 1- Given the diagnosis of Carapa oreophila, validly published by Kenfack (2011), based on the collection of S.N. Saigne 1260 (YA, MO) of Cameroonian origin, the majority of the characters presented are compatible with the description of C. macrantha Harms (1917). Although the distribution of C. oreophila as defined by its author is limited between 1100 m and 2500 m in altitude, in the mountain forest zone, the entity described by Kenfack is considered as an altitudinal variation of C. macrantha Harms. 2- The concept of C. macrantha Harms by Kenfack (2011) is in contradiction with the original description of this species by its author which specifies 4–11 pairs of leaflets for the leaves, a calyx length of 2 mm and petals 7–8 mm in length. In Kenfack, it is respectively (10–)12–22 pairs of leaflets, calyx 0.8–1.2 mm in length and petals 3.5–5.5 mm in length. Kenfack & Issembé's choice of 2068 collected in Gabon as neotype of C. macrantha is therefore debatable. Moreover, Carapa herbarium specimens from Gabon examined do not present the combination of characters prescribed by Harms for C. macrantha. Consequently, the presence of the species in Gabon remains to be verified. Bibliography: Harms (1940), Kenfack (2011).
