# *Baissea atrobrunnea* O.Lachenaud Planche 14

**Genus**: [[Baissea]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Candollea 77 : 18 (2022)

## Description

Vine reaching 4 m in height, covered on most of its parts (except the corolla and the upper surface of adult leaves) with a short and dense felt of dark brown ± dendritic hairs (Plate 14.3). Leaf elliptic to ± oblong-elliptic, 1.5–2.3 × 2.3–3.5 cm, acute at the base, acute or shortly acuminate at the apex, strongly coriaceous, at first entirely and densely felted brown on both surfaces, then only the upper surface soon glabrescent; secondary veins 4 to 6 pairs, widely spaced and strongly curved, tertiary veins scarcely distinct; domatia absent. Inflorescence axillary, loosely paniculate, 4–5 cm long, with flowers spaced 0.3–0.5 cm apart, main branches 0.8–2.3 cm long; bracts narrowly triangular, < 0.5 mm long. Flower pedicel 0.5–1 mm; calyx with ovate imbricate lobes, 0.5 mm, with ± 5 small colleters at the base; corolla yellowish white, with widely infundibuliform tube, 3–4 × 1–1.5 mm, glabrous on the exterior, with an annulus of deflexed interior hairs, situated above the stamens, with narrowly elliptic to ovate lobes, 1–1.5 × 0.5–0.7 mm, obtuse at the apex, glabrous; stamens inserted 0.3 mm above the base of the tube, anther 2 × 0.4 mm, glabrous except for the puberulent connective; ovary densely villous at the apex. Fruit: follicles narrowly subcylindric, 3–4 × 0.3–0.5 cm, densely felted with brown, forming between them a very obtuse angle. Distribution uniquely known from the type locality in central Gabon, Ngounié province, in Waka National Park. Ecology habitat not specified but presumably evergreen forests. B : Jongkind & Lachenaud (2022). BIBLIOGRAPHY
