# *Neuropeltis velutina* Hallier f.

**Genus**: [[Neuropeltis]]
**Family**: [[Convolvulaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Herb. Boissier 5 : 374 (1897)

## Description

Large liane reaching the upper stratum of the forest; branchlets, petioles, lower surface of leaf blades and inflorescences with tomentose, reddish hairs. Leaf: petiole 6–40 mm; blade ovate to elliptic or obovate, 5–13 × 3–7 cm, widely rounded, subcordate to cordate at the base, shortly acuminate for 3–10 mm and long-mucronate for 2–8 mm at the apex, with tomentose, reddish hairs when young and bearing depressed punctations above; lateral veins, 6–8 pairs. Inflorescence in multiflorous racemes, solitary in the axil of the upper leaves and much shorter than 20 cm, or sometimes in large terminal panicles and up to 45 cm in length; bracts 3–6 mm in length, ± subulate. Flower: pedicel 1–2.5 mm; calyx with ± circular sepals, 1.5–2.5 mm, hairy externally; corolla ± 5 mm in length, with lobes hairy externally; stamens glabrous, shorter than the corolla, anthers, 1 mm in length; pistil 4.5–5 mm in length, included. Fruits ovoid, 7–8 × 5 mm, glabrous; bract enlarged, widely elliptic, 5–5.5 × 4–4.5 cm, rounded to cordate at the base, rounded, obtuse to emarginate at the apex, sparsely pubescent. Mature seeds not seen.

## Distribution

S.E. Nigeria, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Angola (Cabinda) and Gabon where it has been collected in the provinces of Estuaire, Ogooué-Ivindo and Ogooué-Lolo

## Ecology

primary and secondarized forest; up to approximately 550 m altitude. B: Breteler (2010), Lejoly & Lisowski (1984). Bibliography
