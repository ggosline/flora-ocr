# *Neuropeltis velutina* Hallier f.

**Genus**: [[Neuropeltis]]
**Family**: [[Convolvulaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Herb. Boissier 5 : 374 (1897)

## Synonyms

- Neuropeltis sanguinea R.D.Good, J. Bot. 67, suppl. 2 : 115 (1929).

## Description

Large liane reaching the upper stratum of the forest; branchlets, petioles, lower surface of leaf blades and inflorescences with russet tomentose hairs. Leaf: petiole 6–40 mm; blade oval to elliptic or obovate, 5–13 × 3–7 cm, broadly rounded, subcordate to cordate at the base, shortly acuminate over 3–10 mm and with a long mucro over 2–8 mm at the apex, with russet tomentose hairs when young and with impressed punctations on the upper surface; lateral veins, 6–8 pairs. Inflorescence in multiflorous racemes, solitary in the axils of the upper leaves and much shorter than 20 cm, or sometimes in large terminal panicles and up to 45 cm long; bracts 3–6 mm long, ± subulate. Flower: pedicel 1–2.5 mm; calyx with ± circular sepals, 1.5–2.5 mm, hairy on the outside; corolla ± 5 mm long, with lobes hairy on the outside; stamens glabrous, shorter than the corolla; anthers, 1 mm long; pistil 4.5–5 mm long, included. Fruit ovoid, 7–8 × 5 mm, glabrous; bract enlarged, broadly elliptic, 5–5.5 × 4–4.5 cm, rounded to cordate at the base, rounded, obtuse to emarginate at the apex, sparsely pubescent. Mature seeds not seen.

## Distribution

S.E. Nigeria, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Angola (Cabinda) and Gabon where it has been collected in the provinces of Estuaire, Ogooué-Ivindo and Ogooué-Lolo

## Ecology

primary and secondarized forest; up to approximately 550 m altitude. B: Breteler (2010), Lejoly & Lisowski (1984). Bibliography
