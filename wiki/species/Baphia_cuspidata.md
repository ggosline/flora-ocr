# *Baphia cuspidata* Taub.

**Genus**: [[Baphia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 23 : 176 (1897)

## Description

Liana, rarely shrub or lianescent shrub; branchlets pubescent, glabrescent. Stipules narrowly triangular, 2–4 mm long, sparsely pubescent. Leaf: petiole with separate pulvini, (5–)8–20(–25) mm, sparsely pubescent, glabrescent; leaflet with ± elliptic blade, (3–)5–10(–12) × (1.5–)2–5(–6.5) cm, rounded to subcordate at base, apex acuminate over 0.5–1.5(–3) cm, papyraceous to coriaceous, glabrous or very rapidly glabrescent; lateral veins in (4–)5 to 7 pairs. Inflorescence in simple axillary fascicle or sometimes compound and terminal, pubescent; bracts early caducous, narrowly ovate-triangular, 1–3(–5) mm long. Flower: bracteoles situated 1–4 mm below the calyx, opposite or alternate, ± reflexed, ovate-elliptic, 1–4(–6) mm long; pedicel 10–20 mm, puberulent; calyx spatulate, 9–10 × 10 mm, sparsely pubescent; standard ± circular, 13–17 mm in diameter, with claw 2.5–3 mm, wings falcate, 15–16 × 5–6 mm, with claw 2–3 mm, keel petals ± falcate, 13–15 × 4–5 mm, with claw 2 mm; stamens 9–12 mm long, anthers 1–1.5 mm long; pistil 10–12 mm long, glabrous or rarely with a few hairs, ovary with 3 to 5 ovules. Legume oblong, 4.5–8 × 1–1.5 cm, ± rounded at base, with 1 to 3 seeds. Mature seed not seen.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea and Gabon; in Gabon, known from all provinces except Ngounié and Ogooué-Maritime.

## Ecology

Primary or secondarized forest; in Gabon at 300–500 m altitude, elsewhere at 0–400 m. Bibliography: Pellegrin (1949), Raponda-Walker & Sillans (1961), Soladoye (1985).
