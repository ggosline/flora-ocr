# *Macrotyloma biflorum* (Schumach. & Thonn.) Hepper var. biflorum Planche 84

**Genus**: [[Macrotyloma]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull. 26 : 565 (1972)

## Synonyms

- Glycine biflorum Schumach. & Thonn., Beskr. Guin. Pl. : 345 (1827).
- Dolichos chrysanthus A.Chev. var. occidentalis (Harms) R.Wilczek, Fl. Congo Belge 6 : 324 (1954).
- Kerstingiella biflora (Schumach. & Thonn.) J.A. Lackey, Phytologia 38(4) : 229 (1978).

## Description

Perennial herb; stem trailing or sometimes climbing, slender, up to 2 m long. Stipules ovate-lanceolate, 2–4 × 1.2 mm. Leaf: petiole 0.5–2.3 mm; rachis 1–2 mm; stipels filiform, 1.2 mm; leaflets 3, with blade ovate to oblong, 1–4 × 0.5–2 cm, base rounded, apex rounded or rarely acute. Inflorescence ± sessile, in fascicles with 1 to 4 flowers; bracts ± 2 mm long. Flower: pedicel very short or absent; bracteoles linear, 2.5–4 mm long; calyx with erect pubescence, tube 2 mm long, lobes lanceolate, 3–5.5 mm long, upper lobes connate for half their length; corolla pale yellow to white or green, standard 11–16 × 8–14 mm, apex emarginate, base subauricate, pink inside, with linear appendages 4–6 mm, wings oblanceolate, 12–14 × 2 mm, keel slightly curved, 11–13 × 3 mm, apex obtuse, base subauricate; ovary ± 6.5 mm long, with 4 to 10 ovules, margin puberulent. Legume linear, 3.5–4.7 cm × 3.5–4.5 mm, acuminate, with 4 to 10 seeds, glabrous to densely pubescent. Seed 3–3.5 × 2–2.5 × 0.9 mm, reddish-brown with or without black stippling, with terminal elaiosome.

## Distribution

widely distributed in West and Central Africa; in Gabon, on the Plateaux Batéké (Haut-Ogooué)

## Ecology

Kalahari sands, shrubby or wooded savannas, inselbergs; in Gabon at 630 m altitude, elsewhere up to 900 m. Flowers in December

## Source note

The second variety, var. apprime-puberulum Verdc., from Guinea to Cameroon, in Central African Republic, in Democratic Republic of the Congo and in Sudan, has not yet been recorded from Gabon. Its indumentum is appressed rather than spreading, the rachis is longer and the leaflets are smaller. Bibliography: Mackinder et al. (2001), Verdcourt (1982), Wilczek (1954).
