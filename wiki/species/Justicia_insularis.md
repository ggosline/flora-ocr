# *Justicia insularis* T. Anders.

**Genus**: [[Justicia]]
**Family**: [[Acanthaceae]]
**Protologue**: T. Anderson, Journ. Linn. Soc., Bot. 7 : 40 (1863)

## Synonyms

- Justicia rostellarioides Lindau, Bot. Jahrb. 20 : 69 (1894); Engl. et Prantl, Natürl. Pflanzenfam. IV 3 B : 349 (1895).

## Description

, excl. syn. J. Galeopsis T. Anders. ex C. B. Clarke, l. c. : 196 (1900).

≡ Adhatoda (Tyloglossa) diffusa Benth., in W. J. Hooker, Fl. Nigrit.: 483 (1849), non Justicia diffusa Wild. (1797).

J. Galeopsis T. Anders. ex C. B. Clarke (F.T.A. 5 : 196, 1900) is not included in the present synonymy as is the case in the two editions of F.W.T.A.; the plants cited under this name are not entirely conspecific with Justicia insularis T. Anders.

Herb reaching up to 80 cm high, sometimes prostrate or lying on the ground, ascending and with rooting nodes, stems ± branched, subquadrangular, striate, distinctly narrowed at the nodes, ± pubescent. Petiole ± pubescent 0.5–2.5 cm long; the upper leaves are often subsessile or very shortly petiolate. Leaf blade ovate to ovate-lanceolate, acuminate-cuspidate, with apex obtuse or rounded at the tip, acute at the base, 4–7 × 1.5–3.5 cm, ± hairy on both surfaces. 4–5 pairs of lateral veins.

Inflorescences axillary, with 1–3 flowers, almost sessile; peduncles up to 2 mm long; bracts elliptic, acuminate at the apex and acute at the base, ± hairy, ciliate at the margins, 4–5 × 6–7 mm; bracteoles very small, subulate-lanceolate, about 1 × 0.2 mm, ciliate. Sepals 5, linear-lanceolate or elliptic, 4 × 1 mm, acuminate, hairy. Corolla pink, purple or light violet, funnel-shaped, reaching 13 mm long; relatively little open at the throat; tube 6 × 4 mm; upper lip slightly emarginate 5 mm long, 4 mm wide at the base and 1 mm wide at the apex; lower lip 6 × 6 mm, briefly trilobed. Stamens glabrous, with filaments 4 mm; anther lobes 1 mm, the inferior one with a spur of about 0.5 mm; anther slightly bifid at the base. Disc rather small; ovary 1.5 mm long, glabrous; style 10 mm, hairy towards the base.

Capsule reaching 9 mm long, glabrous, with base briefly stipitate. Seeds subtriangular-globose, 2 × 1.7 mm with greyish-brown testa, rugulate-verrucose.

## Type

Vogel s. n., Fernando-Po (K, holotype!)

## Discussion

J. insularis was originally described under the name Adhatoda (Tyloglossa) diffusa Benth. and the description based on the specimen of Vogel, from Fernando-Po; the epithet of this binomial could not be retained because of Justicia diffusa Willd. (Sp. Pl. 1 : 87, 1797), which has nothing to do with this taxon. The oldest name for a species of this group (of which the species described to date are in all likelihood only infraspecific taxa) is Justicia ladanoides Lam. (Tabl. Encyel. Illustr. 1 : 42, 1791 = J. lithospermifolia Jacq., Hort. Schoenbr. 1 : 3, t. 4, 1797; see H. Heine, l. c.: 427, 1963.)

This species belongs to section Calophanoides, a polymorphic African group that is rather difficult to delimit. It is distributed, in the strict sense, in Sierra Leone, Ghana, southern Nigeria, Cameroon, Fernando-Po, Central African Republic, Gabon and Congo.
