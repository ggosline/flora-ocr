# *Eleocharis atropurpurea* (Retz.) Presl Planche 80

**Genus**: [[Eleocharis]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Reliq. haenk. 1 : 196 (1828)

## Synonyms

- Scirpus atropurpureus Retz., Observ. bot. 5 : 14 (1789).

## Description

Annual herb, low, with very slender root system and numerous very crowded stems; stems 1–12 cm × 0.2–0.3 mm, angular, often incurved. Leaf: two sheaths above the prophyll, the basal one usually violet and truncate, the upper one paler and terminating in a narrow lanceolate lobe. Inflorescence in a single spikelet, without bracts, with all glumes fertile. Spikelet ovoid to broadly lanceolate, 2–4 × 1–2 mm; glumes very spreading at maturity, exposing the achenes, approximately 1 mm long, dark reddish-brown (sometimes the lower ones very pale), with a very distinct green central nerve terminating at the rounded apex, with or without a narrow pale margin. Flower: perianth usually with 4–5 bristles, sometimes absent, shorter than the achene, furnished with recurved spinose teeth; stamens 2; style with 2 branches. Achene broadly obovate, 0.5 × 0.4 mm, flattened, smooth and very glossy, yellowish-white or pale brown becoming black at maturity; appendage grayish, small, flattened.

## Distribution

widely distributed in tropical regions of Africa, America and Asia; very rare or poorly known in Gabon, found only in Estuaire (1 collection)

## Ecology

on bare mud of mangroves near watercourses, in swamps and freshwater pools; in Gabon, at sea level, up to 1700 m in East Africa. Taxonomic note: The fertility of all glumes is an exception to the usual description of the genus giving the 2 lower glumes empty. B: Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al. (2010), Hooper & Napper (1972), Bibliography Lye (1995, 1997)
