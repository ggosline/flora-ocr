# *Cyphostemma adenopodum* (Sprague) Descoings

**Genus**: [[Cyphostemma]]
**Family**: [[Vitaceae]]
**Protologue**: Descoings, Not. Syst. 16 : 120 (1960); Natural. monspel. 18 : 218 (1967)

## Synonyms

- Cissus adenopoda Sprague, Kew Bull.: 247 (1906).
- Cissus Bakeriana Planchon, DC. Monogr. Phaner. 5 : 599 (1887).
- Cissus Rowlandii Gilg et Brandt, Engl. Bot. Jahrb. 46 : 523 et 538, f. 16 (1912).

## Description

Herbaceous climbing plant, reaching several metres in length, with swollen roots. Stems fairly densely pubescent, bearing some glandular hairs, sometimes glabrescent, cylindrical, 2–3 mm in diameter; internodes 7–12 cm long. Tendrils cylindrical, deeply striated, bifurcate, sparsely pubescent; bracts ovate deltoid 1–2 mm long, glabrous or somewhat pubescent. Stipules ovate deltoid, attenuate toward the apex, more or less falciform, 7–10 mm long, 3–4 mm wide, with long and more or less dense pubescence. Leaves compound 3-foliolate. Petiole and petiolules bearing very dense pubescence with in addition a few rare glandular hairs; petiole approximately 1 mm in diameter and 3–6 cm long; petiolules 4–8 mm long. Blade of leaflets obovate to elliptic, 6–10 cm long, 4–7 cm wide, with base cuneate to obtuse, apex rounded to cuneate terminated by a broad acumen; lateral leaflets often asymmetric, more developed in the lower part, with oblique base; margins presenting broad deltoid indentations; on both surfaces, veins more or less densely and long pubescent with some long glandular hairs, blade more or less sparsely pubescent becoming often glabrescent; venation pinnate.

Inflorescences 6–20 cm long, in loose corymbiform composite cymes; peduncle 5–10 cm long; peduncle and axes more or less cylindrical with fairly lax pubescence; bracts and bracteoles linear to deltoid, 1–2 mm long, glabrous. Flowers 2.5–5.5 mm long, greenish. Pedicel bearing more or less long glandular hairs, cylindrical, 1.5–3 mm long, loosely pubescent or glabrescent. Calyx glabrous cupuliform, approximately 0.5 mm in height. Corolla more or less cylindrical, scarcely constricted in the median part, presenting at the apex four strong rounded tubercles, glabrous except for a tuft of long hairs; petals oblong rounded at the extremity, approximately 3 mm long. Stamens approximately 2 mm long; anthers 0.8 mm long. Ovary glabrous.

Berries glabrous, globular approximately 5 mm long. Seed more or less globular, 4–5 mm long, 3.5 mm wide and thick; rostrum triangular well marked; foveate impression narrow; median ridge narrow slightly prominent; lateral lines distinct emitting ventral and dorsal radial lines; testa with finely granular surface.

Type from Uganda: Dawe 224, Buriro (K).

Climbing liane with tuberous roots growing in the understory of primary and secondary humid forests, forest galleries and forest fallows. It is widely distributed in tropical Africa, from Togo to Uganda and Congo.

C. adenopodum may be considered an ornamental plant by its yellow red purplish foliage, its pink or dark red pubescence, the purple spots that sometimes cover the lower face of the leaves, and finally by its greenish or yellowish-green flowers marked with red. Furthermore, it is used by Gabonese peoples who consume its leaves, or use them to wrap fish.
