# *Strychnos densiflora* Baillon

**Genus**: [[Strychnos]]
**Family**: [[Loganiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia 12 : 369 (1879)

## Description

— FTA 4 (1) : 528. — FWTA ed. 1, 2 : 24, p.p. (excl. syn. S. jollyana A. Chev. et Chevalier 16351, 16360 et 16395). — A. CHEVALIER, Rev. Bot. Appl. 27 : 372 (1947), p.p. (excl. syn. S. martreti A. Chev., S. fleuryana A. Chev. et S. nigritana Bak., et pl. 16A, which are S. nigritana). — AUBRÉVILLE, Fl. For. Soud.-Guin. : 440, pl. 96, 8-9 (1950). — FWTA ed. 2, 2 : 43, p.p. (excl. syn. S. martreti A. Chev.). — LEEUWENBERG. 1.c. : 104. fi. 19. phot. 1. map 15.

— S. suaveolens GILG in ENGLER, Bot. Jahrb. 17 : 566 (1893).

— S. chlorocarpa GILG in ENGLER, Bot. Jahrb. 28 : 128 (1899).

— S. hirsutostylosa DE WILD., Bull. Jard. Bot. Brux. 5 : 43 (1915).

Liane reaching a length of 20-35 m or more and a height of 20 m; stem 7-12 cm in diameter; bark pale grey, lenticellate; wood cream; branches pale grey, often pale brown when dry, lenticellate; branchlets glabrous, distinctly lenticellate; tendrils in pairs.

Leaves with petiole glabrous, 4-10 mm long; blade coriaceous or weakly coriaceous for sun-exposed leaves, papyraceous or weakly coriaceous for shaded leaves, even on living material, elliptic or narrowly elliptic, 6.5-18 × (1.5-) 2.5-7.5 (-9.5) cm, apiculate or acuminate to subcaudate at apex, especially for shade leaves, cuneate or rounded at base, glabrous on both surfaces; one pair of secondary nerves curved along the margin; principal veins impressed above, prominent below; tertiary venation not distinctly reticulate, prominent or not prominent; margin scarcely or not reflexed.

Inflorescences axillary, usually several grouped together, small, rather contracted, 1.5-4 × 1.5-3 cm, pauciflorous; peduncle, branches and pedicels with scattered pubescence with ± seriately arranged hairs, or glabrous. Flowers 5-merous. Sepals pale yellowish green, free, broadly ovate, orbicular or broadly orbicular, 2-3.5 × 2.5-4 mm, rounded, finely ciliate, glabrous outside, with fine appressed pubescence at base or sometimes glabrous inside. Corolla, in mature bud, 7.5-8 mm long, white or with white greenish tube and yellowish green lobes, glabrous outside, inside furnished at the throat, at the base of the lobes, with a ring resembling a brush of white hairs; tube 1.5 times as long as the lobes, 4.5-5 × 2-3 mm; lobes narrowly triangular, 3-3.5 × 1.6-1.8 mm, very thick, especially at apex, acute, spreading. Stamens slightly exserted, inserted at corolla throat, with very short filament, glabrous; anther oblong, glabrous or sometimes with a few hairs at base. Pistil hairy in middle, 6-7 mm long; ovary ovoid or oblong, glabrous or hairy at apex, 2.5-3.5 × 1.5-2 mm, two-locular; style thick, hairy except at apex, 3.5-4.2 mm long; stigma capitate; ± 50 ovules per locule.

Fruits large, yellow, nearly ripe pale glaucous, globulous, 3-10 cm in diameter and containing 8-25 seeds; wall hard, 3-5 mm thick. Seeds irregularly tetrahedral or elliptic, rugose, ± 25 × 15 × 7 mm. Pl. 29, 1-5, p. 136.
