# *Adenia poggei* (Engl.) Engl. Planche 25.7

**Genus**: [[Adenia]]
**Family**: [[Passifloraceae]]
**Protologue**: Veg. Erde 9, Pflanzenw. Afr. 3(2) : 603 (1921)

## Synonyms

- Ophiocaulon poggei Engl., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 14 : 386 (1891).

## Description

A. dewevrei (De Wild. & T.Durand) Engl., Veg. Erde 9, Pflanzenw. Afr. 3(2) : 603 (1921). A. apiculata (De Wild. & T.Durand) Engl., Veg. Erde 9, Pflanzenw. Afr. 3(2) : 603 (1921). Herbaceous to woody vine, reaching 20 m in length; stem at base reaching up to 7 cm in diameter. Stipules persistent, reniform with crenate-laciniate margins, ± 1 mm in length. Leaf with petiole reaching 4 cm; lamina broadly ovate to ovate-elliptic, up to 11 × 8.6 cm, with rounded base, the more basal laminae sometimes with cordate base, apex shortly acute to distinctly acuminate and mucronate, papyraceous, discolorous, the undersurface distinctly lighter, a single pair of additional lateral veins; a single broadly spatulate gland 3 mm in diameter at the base of the lamina, laminar glands 0; sterile tendrils little branched, up to 15 cm. Inflorescence: the male comprising up to 40 flowers, the female up to 10 flowers; tendril present or absent; peduncle slender, up to 5 cm (male inflorescence) or 2.5 cm (female inflorescence); bracts and bracteoles triangular, 1 mm in length, acute, margin dentate. Male flower with pedicel almost absent and stipe 5–20 mm, ± campanulate, 5–8 mm in length; hypanthium cupuliform, shallow, 0.5–1.5 mm, without partitions; sepals free, oblong-elliptic, 5–7 mm in length, obtuse to sub-acute at apex, without punctations; petals narrowly oblanceolate, 5–7 mm in length, sub-obtuse to acute; corona and glands absent; stamens inserted at the base of the hypanthium, filaments 0.5–1.5 mm, free, anthers 4 mm; pistillode 0.5 mm. Female flower as the male, but with pedicel 1–2 mm and stipe 1–2.5 mm, 5–6 mm in length; hypanthium 1 mm in height; sepals narrowly ovate, 3–4 mm in length, acute; petals linear, 3–4 mm in length, acute, margin entire; annular corona, 0.2–0.5 mm in height, glands of the hypanthium absent; staminodes 0.5 mm; ovary ellipsoid, 2–2.5 × 1.5 mm, gynophore 0.5–0.7 mm; style

connate, 1–1.5 mm, stigmas sub-reniform, 1–1.5 mm in diameter, laciniate-papillose. Fruits 1–4 per inflorescence, ovoid to globose, 1.5 × 1 cm, with smooth, scarious pericarp, gynophore 1–2 mm. Seeds 15–25 per capsule, oblong-ovoid, 3–4 × 2–2.5 mm, with 7–9 pits on a meridional line.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Central African Republic, Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Democratic Republic of the Congo; very common in Gabon, known from Woleu-Ntem, Ogooué-Lolo, Haut-Ogooué and Ngounié

## Ecology

rainforests, swamp forests, forest galleries, clearings, roadsides; in Gabon, up to 1000 m altitude. Flowers from September to January; fruits in February. Uses: Used as cord for tying various bundles. b: De Wilde (1971), Robyns (1995). ibliography
