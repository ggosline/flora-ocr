# *Porterandia nalaensis* (De Wild.) Keay

**Genus**: [[Porterandia]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Protologue**: R. W. J. Keay, Bull. J. B. Brux. 28 : 24 (1958)

## Synonyms

- Randia nalaensis De Wild., Ann. Mus. Congo 5, 2 : 347 (1908).
- Randia Adolf-Friederici K. Krause in Mildbr., Wiss. Ergebn. Deutsch. Zentr.-Afr. Exped. 1907-8, 2: 320 (1911). Synonymie vérifiée : Mildbrød 4139! (HBG), Molundu, Cameron.
- Randia Sereti De Wild., Ann. Mus. Congo, Sér. 5, 2 : 164 (1907).
- Gardenia Sereti (De Wild.) De Wild., loc. c. : 348 (1908).

## Description

Tree 4-5 (...) m tall; 10 to 20 cm in diameter; wood soft whitish-yellowish. Vegetative apparatus fairly strongly pubescent. Stipules ovate or oblong 7-26 mm long, briefly acute or obtuse at the apex. Petiole 8-25 mm. Blade puberulent to pubescent above, distinctly pubescent below, obovate 17-50 × 7-20 cm, with acute attenuate base, with ‡ acute lanceolate apex, sometimes obtuse. 15-26 pairs of secondary nerves.

Inflorescences ± villous, cymose, with peduncle 8-12 mm; bracteoles ovate caducous. Flowers pubescent ± numerous with pedicel 3-8 mm. Calyx tubular for 3-4 mm, with triangular teeth of ca. 2-3 mm. Corolla similar to that of P. cladantha. Anthers 7.5 × 1 mm. Style 15 mm with furrowed club.

Fruit broadly elliptic 20-25 × 12-20 mm, plus the cupule. The accrescent pedicel reaches up to 15 mm. Epidermis pubescent ribbed fairly coarsely. Seeds angular 1.5 mm, very numerous.

## Type

Seret 805 (holotype BR!), Nala, Congo

## Discussion

The type is not among the most pubescent forms of this species which sometimes distinguishes itself with difficulty from P. clandantha.

Cameroon, Gabon, Congo, from Ouesso (Pobeguin 94) and Sibiti (de Néré 1831) to Yangambi, and Angola (Gossweiler 715). Degraded forest with semicaducous tendency.
