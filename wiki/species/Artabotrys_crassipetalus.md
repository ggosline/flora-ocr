# *Artabotrys crassipetalus* Pellegrin

**Genus**: [[Artabotrys]]
**Family**: [[Annonaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc.Bot.Fr.94 : 257 (1947)

## Description

Liane with almost glabrous black branchlets. Leaves with petiole 5–6 mm long, canaliculate on the upper surface; blade coriaceous, elliptic to subovate, 10–17 cm long by 5–8 cm wide, with acute base and abruptly acuminate apex; upper surface glabrous, lower surface glabrous except on the appressed-pubescent midvein. 8–10 pairs of ascending oblique veins, united in arches near the margin; network of veinlets irregular, visible on both surfaces.

Inflorescences 1–2-flowered, oppositifoliate, with axis and peduncle hooked, 1–3 cm long, very sparsely appressed-pubescent. Flowers ± reddish, with pedicel approximately 1 cm long, sparsely appressed-pubescent. Sepals semi-orbicular, approximately 3 mm long by 6–7 mm wide, almost glabrous. Petals covered with short appressed hairs, the outer ones very thick, coriaceous, broadly ovate, 18–20 mm long, 8–12 mm wide, concave, rounded at the base, the inner ones thick, lanceolate, almost as long as the outer ones, 4–6 mm wide, with concave base dilated into a spoon-shaped structure, strongly united into a kind of crypt allowing the reproductive organs to appear. Stamens numerous, 5-seriate, 1–1.5 cm long, with linear extrorse anthers, triangular connectives, dilated above the anthers and turned outward. Carpels 6–10, 3–4 mm long, ovaries ovoid, stigmas voluminous, recurved outward above the stamens. 2 basal ovules.

Fruit unknown.

## Type

Le Testu 8ooo,région de Lastoursville,Gabon (holotype P!; iso-type BM!)

## Discussion

Species known only from Gabon, in dense humid forest, remarkable for its particularly fleshy flowers with very thick petals.
