# *Octoknema klaineana* Pierre

**Family**: [[Octoknemaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Octoknema]]

Bull. Soc. Linn. Paris **2**: 1293 (1897). — VAN TIEGHEM, Journ. de Bot. **19**: 46 (1905). — "*O. gabonensis* Pierre", in HUTCHINSON, Fam. Fl. Pl.: 240, tab. 184 (1926), nom. nud.

## Description

**Habit**: tree or shrub 12 m tall. Leafy branchlets grey or yellow, weakly longitudinally striate, with yellow or russet stellate hairs.

**Leaves**: petiole pubescent with stellate hairs, 1–2.5 cm long, enlarged and wrinkled at apex, flattened and finely striate to striate beneath. Blade glabrous on upper face, densely pubescent on lower face, yellowish-green above, brown beneath, elliptic, rarely narrowly obovate, 9.5–27 × 4.5–11.5 cm; base rounded or weakly cordate; apex slightly acuminate. Midrib concave above, prominent pubescent beneath. Secondary nerves 9 pairs, ascending, arcuate, anastomosing 2 mm from the blade margin, concave on upper face, prominent pubescent on lower face. Nervules scalariform in sparse network, obscurely prominent above, prominent beneath.

**Male inflorescence**: dense axillary raceme of multiflowered fascicles. Rachis pubescent, 5–11 cm long.

**Male flowers**: yellow. Pedicel pubescent. Calyx absent. Petals pubescent externally, glabrous internally, connate, fleshy, elliptic or lanceolate; internal margins with abundant small verrucous digitations; apex bearing a large fleshy excrescency. Stamens 5, epipetalous, free, 1.5 mm long; filament short and broad; anther with 2 basifixed ellipsoid locules. Disc with 5 lobes. Pistillode with persistent style.

**Female inflorescence**: formed by a pauciflorous axillary raceme, 2.5 cm long. Inflorescence rachis broadly pubescent.

**Female flowers**: pentamerous, 8 × 4 mm, white. Calyx absent. Petals connate, pubescent externally, glabrous internally, bearing numerous verrucous fleshy excrescencies on the internal face margins. Staminodes 5, epipetalous. Ovary inferior with 4 uniovulate locules; placentation axile; style short and broad, terminated by a stigma with 3 digitate lobes.

**Fruit**: globose pubescent drupe. Exocarp soft; endocarp woody, emitting 8–10 meridional laminae towards the albumen, reducing to 5 towards the apex. Albumen abundant. Embryo apical with radicle towards the apex of the seed.

## Types

SYNTYPES: *Klaine 776, 809*, Gabon.

## Distribution

**Gabon only**: known from between Libreville and Sibang, and Sibang.

## Notes

**Type species (2011).** This is the **type species of [[Octoknema]]** — Pierre described the genus on this single species, collected in Gabon by Klaine. Gosline & Malécot (2011) restrict it to **Gabon near Libreville** and designate the lectotype (*Klaine 636*, P).

Revision: Gosline G. & Malécot V. (2011). A monograph of Octoknema (Octoknemaceae — Olacaceae s.l.). Kew Bulletin 66: 367–404.


In Hutchinson's *Families of Flowering Plants*, a figure by the author represents an "*Octoknema gabonensis* Pierre". No trace of this binomial has been found in Pierre's publications on Octoknemaceae. This plate undoubtedly represents *O. klaineana* Pierre.
