# *Warnecka cauliflora* Jacques-Félix

**Genus**: [[Warnecka]]
**Family**: [[Melastomataceae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia, sér. 2, 19 (3) : 262, tab. 3 (1980)

## Description

Shrub 1 to 2 m tall; branches robust, 4 mm in diameter in the still leafy part, distinctly 4-angular-winged. Leaves ample, subsessile, subcoriaceous; petiole robust, 1-3 mm; blade elliptic to ovate-elliptic, up to 11 × 28 cm, broadly rounded, then narrowly cordate on the petiole, obscurely acuminate at the apex; veins impressed above, strongly salient below; the principal convergent ones distinctly supra-basal, arching only toward the apex; 1 to 2 pairs of additional arching veins, the submarginal pair evanescent before the apex; 6-8 principal transversals; intercalary veins loosely reticulate.

Cymes fascicled by 12-15 on the nodes of the old wood, 3-4 cm long, stipites 5-10 mm long; 2 to 3 times ramified, branches 5-8 mm; bracts scarious, 1-2 mm long, long-persistent. Flower with robust pedicel, 10-12 mm; floral bud ellipsoid, 4.5 × 6 mm, corolla conical, equal to half the height; hypantho-calyx campanulate, 5 × 4 mm; calyx sinuate, or lobes very obtuse, 3 × 1 mm, imbricate. Petals fleshy, longitudinally plicate (by contact with the stamens in the bud), oblong-triangular to elliptic, base truncate without claw, apex obtusely triangular, 3 × 4.5 mm. Anthers 1.8 × 1.2 mm, with convex thecae; connective obtusely conical, slightly saddle-shaped by the gland equal to one third or half the length; filament 1.7 mm. Epigynous chamber grooved; style 4-5 mm. Ovary 2-locular, 6-8 ovulate.

Fruit unknown. Pl. 56, p. 155.

## Type

Le Testu 7776, Gabon (P)

## Discussion

Recognizable from other cauliflorous species by its young branches distinctly 4-winged. Also occurs in Congo.
