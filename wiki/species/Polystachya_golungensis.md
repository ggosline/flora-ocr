# *Polystachya golungensis* Rchb.f.

**Genus**: [[Polystachya]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Flora 48 : 185 (1865)

## Description

– Polystachya coriacea Rolfe, Kew Bull. 9 : 340 (1913).

Pseudobulbs 2–6 × 0.4–0.7 cm. Leaves 5–28 × 0.3–2.5 cm, ligulate. Inflorescence 6–40 cm, 50–200-flowered. Flowers yellow, yellow-green or cream-brown, with unpleasant scent. Dorsal sepal glabrous. Petals somewhat falcate. Lateral sepals obliquely ovate-triangular or oblong-triangular. Lip three-lobed in the middle, with a callus in the basal third covered with multicellular powdery hairs; median lobe almost orbicular to elliptic, emarginate, sparsely pubescent; lateral lobes obliquely obovate to oblong, obtuse, sparsely pubescent. Spur sacciform-conical. - Pl. 120, p. 273.
