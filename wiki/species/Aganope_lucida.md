# *Aganope lucida* (Welw. ex Baker) Polhill

**Genus**: [[Aganope]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull. 25 : 266 (1971)

## Synonyms

- Ostryocarpus lucidus (Welw. ex Baker) Dunn, Bull. Misc. Inf. Kew 1911 : 363 (1911).
- Ostryoderris lucida (Welw. ex Baker) Baker f., Leg. trop. Afr. : 562 (1929).

## Description

Sarmentose shrub or liane, reaching 30 m in length; young branchlets cylindric, striated, glabrescent. Leaf: petiole 4–10 cm; rachis 8–10 cm, glabrous; stipels absent; leaflets in 2 to 5 pairs, with glabrous petiolule 4–11 mm, limb obovate-oblong, (4–)6–13 × (3–)4–8 cm, slightly attenuate toward base, apex shortly acuminate, acumen sometimes twisted, glabrous, shining, coriaceous; secondary nerves in 6 pairs, ascending, reticulation distinct. Inflorescence ± 30 cm in length; axes 3–12 cm, with short, russet pubescence; bracts caducous, narrowly lanceolate, 2 mm in length. Flower: pedicel 1–2 mm, velvety, articulate at right angle to calyx; calyx 4–5 mm in length, with deltoid teeth 0.5–1 mm in length, brown-villous on both faces; corolla whitish, ± 1 cm in length; stamens 8 mm in length; ovary 10 mm in length, ± sessile, with 3 to 5 ovules. Pod elliptic-oblong, 10–15 × 2.5–4(–5) cm, base and apex attenuate, reddish-green or with red wings, with 1 or 2 seeds, wings 3–7 mm in width. Seed lenticular.

## Distribution

Côte d'Ivoire, République centrafricaine, Cameroun, Gabon, République du Congo, République démocratique du Congo and Angola; in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Ngounié and OgoouéLolo

## Ecology

Primary and secondary forest, riparian forest; in Gabon at 200–470 m altitude. Bibliography: Hauman (1954d).
