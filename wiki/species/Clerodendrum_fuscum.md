# *Clerodendrum fuscum* Gürke Figure 2(C, D)

**Genus**: [[Clerodendrum]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 18 : 175 (1893)

## Description

C. umbellatum Poir. var. gossweileri (Cavaco) Moldenke, Revista Sudamer. Bot. 10 : 231 (1956). Vine reaching (0.5–)3(–4) m or climbing shrub up to 1.5 m tall; stem cylindrical, generally hollow, often dark reddish-brown, pubescent with violet hairs, spreading in youth, without pseudospines, not reported as myrmecophilous. Leaves opposite or occasionally subopposite; petiole 0.5–2(–7.5) cm, pubescent; blade ovate to suborbicular, (3.5–)6–10(–19.5) × 4–7(–15.5) cm, cordate or rarely subauricate at base, abruptly acuminate at apex, entire or slightly undulate, sparsely hairy above, more densely below, 3- to 5-nerved. Inflorescence terminal, lax, paniculate, up to 30 × 18 cm, with length/width ratio of 1.5–2; cymes 5–8(–10) × 5–8(–14) cm, distant 4–11 cm apart, with (5 to)8 to 20(to 30) flowers, densely scaly or pubescent; peduncle 5–9(–11) cm; foliaceous bracts, 5.5–11 × 4.5–8.5 cm; bracteoles linear, (0.5–)1 cm long. Flower: pedicel slender, (0.5–)1.5–2 cm; calyx yellow or cream (generally bright lemon-yellow when dry), pubescent, with campanulate tube, slightly swollen below, ± 5 × 10 mm, with equal lobes, narrowly elliptic-ovate, (7–)15(–22) × (4–)6–7(–10) mm, strongly acute; corolla yellowish-white, reddish-violet on the outside and marked with reddish-violet at the base of the 3 upper lobes, with very narrow tube, (1.3–)2–2.5(–3) × 0.1–0.2 cm, puberulent, with ovate, narrowly ovate to obovate or narrowly elliptic-obovate lobes, (5–)10 × ± 5 mm, obtuse or subacute at apex; stamens exserted 1.5–3(–4) cm. Fruit ± 12 × 12–13 mm, black, glossy.

## Distribution

Equatorial Guinea (first record here), Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Angola and tropical East Africa; in Gabon: Estuaire, Haut-Ogooué, Ngounié, Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem. Conservation status: widely distributed and preliminarily assigned to Least Concern (LC).

## Ecology

Primary and secondary forests, roadsides, forest-savanna margins, dried swamps, swamps with dying trees, forests on sandy soil; in Gabon at 5–900 m altitude, elsewhere up to 2350 m.

## Source note

Two specimens at K identified as C. sp. ? nov. aff. C. umbellatum and from Equatorial Guinea (Mann 1757 and J.N. Davies 239) clearly represent C. fuscum; this is the first record of this species for this country. Bi: Vande weghe et al. (2016), Verdcourt (1992). Bibliography
