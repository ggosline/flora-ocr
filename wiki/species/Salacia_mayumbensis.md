# *Salacia mayumbensis* Exell & Mendonça

**Genus**: [[Salacia]]
**Family**: [[Celastraceae]]
**Protologue**: Bol. Soc. Brot., ser. 2, 26: 225, tab. 3 (1952); CFA 2(1): 24, tab. 5 (1954); WILCZEK, FCB 9: 211 (1960)

## Description

Shrub glabrous and devoid of rubber, 0.5–3.5 mm tall, erect, sometimes subsarmentose. Juvenile internodes with two pairs of fine ridges. Leaves opposite or weakly subopposite, in vivo slightly varnished above, dry subconcolorous. Petiole (3)–4–11 mm, with straight margins, with channelled groove towards the base. Limb oblong elliptic, 10–24 × 3–9 cm; base acute, sometimes subobtuse, apex with a narrow rostrum 1–1.5–2.5 cm long, rounded at the extremity; margins with regular teeth, often 1 mm high and spaced 5 mm apart. Midvein often forming a fine double ridge above; 9–14 pairs of secondary veins rather little ascending; fine reticulation visible under magnification when dry on both faces.

Flowers 1–4(7) per axillary sessile glomerule. Pedicel 7–13 mm; bud subglobose, 2.5 mm in diameter. Sepals 5, very unequal, quincuncial, 2 small ovate ones 0.9 mm wide, two large suborbicular ones 1.8–2 mm. Corolla rotate, yellow or orange, 5–7 mm in diameter; 5 suborbicular petals 2–3 mm, closely contiguous. Disc brown (upon rehydration), 1.6–1.8 mm in diameter and 0.3–0.4 mm thick, circular, adorned with radiating striae above extending to the outer margin which is rather erect than collapsed. Perigynous groove well marked. Stamens 0.9–1.4 mm, with reniform anther 0.6–0.8 mm wide, with locules apically confluent. Style pyramidal, pale yellow spotted with brown (upon rehydration), 1 mm tall; ovary locules sunken, each containing 2 superposed ovules.

Fruit short or long, often somewhat verrucose, often with 3 prominent longitudinal ridges, slightly attenuated at the base or not, often apiculate, orange or red, vivid and shiny at maturity. Dimensions: 49–65 × 26–30 mm for certain forms from NE Gabon; 22–40 × 15–20 mm, forms from Cameroon; 80 × 40 mm, another form from Cameroon. 2–4 seeds per fruit, with white cotyledons fused. – Pl. 20, fig. 1–9, p. 77.

## Discussion

The plate by EXELL & MENDONÇA, published twice, contains several erroneous details: absence of perigynous groove, incorrect anther shape, incorrect arrangement of ovules. The radiating grooves of the disc are missing; they are also lacking from WILCZEK's description.

Cameroon, Gabon, Congo, Zaire and Cabinda. In understory of moderate altitude and up to 1,300 m. Dense forests.

This species differs from S. loloensis Loes. by a somewhat thinner texture of the limb which is moreover more sharply toothed; the fruit is often more verrucose and more elongate. The disc appears to be less expanded and with margin less collapsed.
