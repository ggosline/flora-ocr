# *Tabernaemontana psorocarpa* (Pierre ex Stapf) Pichon Planche 118 ; Figure 13(F, G)

**Genus**: [[Tabernaemontana]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Notul. Syst. (Paris) 13 : 253 (1948)

## Synonyms

- Gabunia psorocarpa Pierre ex Stapf, Fl. trop. Afr. 4(1) : 137 (1902).

## Description

Climbing vine, reaching 20 m in height; branches glabrous. Leaves in one pair, equal or sometimes distinctly unequal (but of similar form), glabrous; petiole 3–11 mm; lamina narrowly elliptic to strictly elliptic, 6–17 × 2.5–8 cm, cuneate to rounded at the base or decurrent on the petiole, acuminate at the apex, much paler dull and glaucous beneath, lateral nerves in 4 to 10 pairs, often obscure, more visible on the larger leaves, tertiary venation scarcely visible. Inflorescence with 4–12 flowers; pedicel 2–3 mm, glabrous, bracteate with squamiform bracts. Flower with sweet perfume; pedicel 5–16 mm; calyx with lobes 2–4 × 2–3 mm, pale green, glabrous on both faces, ciliate, without colleters inside; corolla white or cream, with tube often greenish, waxy, mature bud 12–23 mm long, with terete or ovoid to subglobose corolla, relatively broad and gaping, glabrous or weakly pubescent on the lobes and sometimes also on the upper half of the tube on the outside, pubescent or hairy inside the base of the lobes towards the base; tube subcylindric, 10–15 mm long, enlarged at the level of the anthers to 3–4 mm, widened above becoming abruptly enlarged at the throat, sometimes twisted 1/4 turn at the level of the anthers, lobes oblique and narrowly oblong, falciform, 0.5–1 times as long as the tube, 1–1.5 times as long as wide, 9–17 × 3–5 mm, slightly auriculate on the left side of the base, partially ciliate at the base; stamens included, inserted near the base of the tube, anther 5–6.5 mm long; pistil 4.5–6 mm long, glabrous; style 1–2 mm. Fruit: distinct mericarps, obliquely ellipsoid, 55–60 × 20–62 × 10–35 mm, pale glaucous green, bumpy and with longitudinal ridges; exocarp fleshy 10–12 mm thick at maturity. Seed 7–11.5 mm long, densely papillose and longitudinally striate. Distribution: southeastern Nigeria, Cameroon and Gabon, in Gabon: Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué, Ngounié, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem. Ecology: lower level of primary and secondary forest in Gabon, up to 800 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1,200 m.

## Source note

In the revision of Tabernaemontana (Leeuwenberg 1991), the distribution range of T. psorocarpa showed a large disjunction between Ghana and eastern Nigeria. It is now clear that the specimens from the west represent a different species with slightly larger flowers and smooth orange fruits. The fruits of T. psorocarpa as presented here are strongly bumpy and pale green at maturity (Fig. 13G).
