# *Polygala gilletiana* E.M.A.Petit Planche 23(5)

**Genus**: [[Polygala]]
**Family**: [[Polygalaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Jard. Bot. État. Bruxelles 25 : 334 (1955)

## Description

Annual herb, up to 50 cm; stem erect, slender, simple or weakly branched, striated, glabrous. Leaves alternate, subsessile; blade linear, 5–22 × 1–1.5 mm, cuneate at base, acute at apex, glabrous. Inflorescence: simple racemes, mainly axillary, up to 14 cm long, laxly flowered, glabrous; bracts linear, 0.75–1 mm long, ciliate at base, early caducous; bracteoles similar, but 0.5 mm long. Flower pale yellow to purplish blue; pedicel 2–3.5 mm, glabrous; upper sepal linear-lanceolate, 1.25–1.5 × 1.25 mm, glabrous, rarely ciliate, winged sepals elliptic, 3 × 1–1.5 mm, trinerved at base, glabrous, persistent in fruit, lower outer sepals linear-lanceolate, 1 × 0.75 mm, glabrous, rarely ciliate, connate; upper petals obliquely obovate, 2–2.5 × 1–1.5 mm, keel 3 × 2–2.5 mm, crest filiform, 0.75 mm long; stamens 8. Fruit flattened, ellipsoid-obovoid, 2.5 × 2 mm, emarginate, very narrowly winged, glabrous. Seed ellipsoid, 2–2.5 × 1–1.25 mm, with silky white pilosity; caruncula symmetric, reniform, with scarcely marked appendages.

## Distribution

Chad, Central African Republic, Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon: Ogooué-Ivindo and Nyanga

## Ecology

gramineous formations with humid season; in Gabon at 600–650 m altitude. B: Petit (1958), Paiva (1998). i Bibliography
