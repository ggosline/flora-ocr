# *Platostoma denticulatum* Robyns Planche 21(4-6)

**Genus**: [[Platostoma]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Jard. Bot. Belg. 17 : 22 (1943)

## Description

Perennial herb, aromatic, up to 1 m tall; stem prostrate or ascending, rarely erect, generally rooting at the nodes, branched, ± pubescent, sometimes glabrous towards the base, with hairs spreading or directed downwards, sometimes bluish on the inflorescence axis. Leaf: petiole 5–20 mm; blade ovate to broadly ovate, 1.5–5 × 1.2–4 cm, attenuate at the base, acute at the apex, dentate to crenate, with 20 to 40 teeth, with scattered hairs on the veins of the lower surface and sessile glands or glandular-punctate below. Inflorescence lax; verticillasters with 8 to 16 flowers, spaced 0.5–1.5 cm apart or almost contiguous towards the apex; bracts narrowly ovate to elliptic, 2–7 mm long, often white or brownish at the base. Flower: pedicel 2 mm; calyx funnel-shaped, 1–2 mm long at anthesis and 2.5–4 mm in fruit, lower lip with 5 small teeth or emarginate at the apex, pressed against the upper lip and closing the throat at maturity, with the median lobe slightly cucullate; corolla 2–3.5 mm long, pink, blue or white.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Rwanda, Burundi, Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania and Angola; in Gabon: Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem. Conservation status: Widespread; probably Least Concern (LC)

## Ecology

marshy soil in grassland or open forest; in Gabon at 150–700 m elevation, elsewhere up to 1500 m. B: Morton (1963), Paton (1997b), Paton et al. (2009, 2013). Bibliography
