# *Abrus canescens* Welw. ex Baker Planche 1

**Genus**: [[Abrus]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]

## Description

in Oliv., Fl. trop. Africa 2 : 175 (1871). Twining suffrutescent herb, reaching 2 m in length. Stipules small. Leaf: petiole 3–7 mm; rachis 4–5 cm, terminated by a point of (0–)1–2 mm; leaflets in 10 to 14 pairs, with oblong blade, (6–)10–20(–30) × 2–7(–10) mm, green above, greyish-green below, with grey or white pubescence on both faces. Inflorescence generally terminal, with flowers in separated fascicles; bracts lanceolate, equalling or slightly exceeding the calyx, 4–7 mm in length. Flower subsessile; calyx (3–)4–6 mm in length; corolla 10–15 mm in length, generally purple. Pod rectangular-flattened, ± 4–6 × 0.8–1.2 cm, the beak recurved, with 6 to 9 seeds. Seed ovoid, ± 4–6 × 3–4 mm, pale brown to black, shiny.

## Distribution

Tropical Africa, from Gambia to Angola in the South and Kenya and Tanzania in the East; in Gabon, known from the Nyanga and Ogooué-Ivindo

## Ecology

climbing on trees and shrubs, in saxicolous and grassy savanna, often in marshy places; in Gabon, at 0–800 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1300 m. Flowers and fruits most of the year (March to May, July, August, November). b : Boutique (1954), Breteler (1960), Pellegrin (1949), Verdcourt (1970, 1971b). Bibliography
