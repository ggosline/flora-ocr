# *Trachystigma mannii* C.B. Clarke

**Genus**: [[Trachystigma]]
**Family**: [[Bignoniaceae]]

## Description

in DE CANDOLLE A. & C., Mon. Phan. 5 (1) : 131, tab. 14 (1883) ; BAKER C.G. & CLARKE C.B., FTA 4 (2) : 503 (1906) ; BURTT, Fl. Cam. 27 : 24, tab. 4, fig. 2-3 (1984).

– Roettlera mannii (C.B.Cl) K. FRITSCH, in ENGL. & PRANTL, Nat. Pflanzenfam. 4 (3B): 148 (1894).

– Didymocarpus mannii (C.B.CL.) WONISCH, in STIZUNGSBER, Akad. Wiss. Wien, Math. Nat. Kl. 118: 483 (1909).

Herb unifoliate; hypocotyl ("stem") somewhat fleshy, up to 8 mm in diameter. Leaf highly variable in size, from 6 × 6 cm to 35 × 20 cm, ovate to oblong, apex normally withered, base cordate, pubescent near the base above; glabrescent toward the apex, very shortly and finely pubescent beneath, more rarely shortly pilose; lateral veins widely spreading.

Inflorescence forming in acropetal series, at the apex of the hypocotyl and on the base of the midvein, without bracts at the base; peduncle 3 to 5 cm long below the first branches, glabrous, flowering portion of the inflorescence 4 to 10 cm long; bracts 5 to 7 × 2 mm, hairy. Calyx 4 to 6 mm long, divided almost to the base into 5 linear to lanceolate segments, glabrous or pubescent, or with stiff hairs near the apices of the segments. Corolla white with a tube and violet throat, or entirely violet, 1.5 to 2 cm long, broadly tubular in its lower portion, widening above, 5-lobed, glabrous or more rarely hairy externally; lobes all subequal or forming 2 lips, the upper one with 2 lobes, ± 3 × 3.5 cm, the lower one with 3 lobes, ± 7 × 5 mm, united for 2 mm at the base. Disc unilateral. Stamens 2, filaments inserted just above or just below the middle of the corolla tube, ± 5 to 6 mm long, usually with a well-marked thickening in the middle, rarely rather filiform, showing at the apex a sharp tooth; anthers coherent, face to face, thecae widely divaricate, ± 1 to 2 mm across. Staminode one, filiform, 2 to 3 mm. Ovary cylindric, ± 5 mm long, glabrous or sparsely glandular, narrowing into a style of similar length, stigma sometimes thicker than the style, wrinkled, bifid.

Capsule ± 3 cm long, held almost horizontally in the ± persistent calyx.

## Type

## Discussion

GESNERIACEAE

Only a single species can now be recognized, but the small amount of material clearly shows considerable variation in floral detail. Testu 7200 has a calyx pubescent over the entire external surface, upper and lower corolla lobes strongly unequal, filaments distinctly widened and flattened in their middle, with a well-marked tooth on the back of the anther (but not in apical position) and an ovary shortly glandular. Testu 5590 has a calyx linear with glabrous segments, a well-marked apical projection on the anther and a very shortly glandular ovary. Hallé N. & Cours G. 6082 has some short, stiff hairs at the apex of the calyx lobes, otherwise glabrous; this is to date the only material with a corolla hairy externally, the filaments are almost filiform and there is an apical projection, but no downward-directed tooth on the connective.

Are the variations constant within populations and will a geographical distribution eventually emerge? This cannot yet be said. More collections and more careful field observations are necessary before the taxonomic significance of this variability can be judged.

Individuals of Trachystigma mannii are variable in size, which is usual in these Gesneriaceae, with a single accrescent cotyledonary leaf; they can grow as low epiphytes or on mossy rocks in dense forest.
