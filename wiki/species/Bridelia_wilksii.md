# *Bridelia wilksii* Breteler Planche 4

**Genus**: [[Bridelia]]
**Family**: [[Phyllanthaceae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia , sér. 3, 33(2) : 232 (2011)

## Description

Adansonia, ser. 3, 33(2): 232 (2011). Shrub or small tree up to 7 m tall; branches often spiny; branchlets glabrous or sparsely pubescent, with hairs ± appressed. Stipules early caducous, not seen. Leaf: petiole channeled above, (8–)9–12 mm, glabrous or sparsely pubescent, with hairs ± appressed; lamina obovate-elliptic, more rarely ovate-elliptic or oblong, (6–)9–14(–25) × (2.5–)4.5–7 cm, rounded to acute at base, apex ± abruptly acuminate over (5–)10–15(–20) mm; lateral veins (8–)9–12(–14) pairs, glabrous or with hairs ± scattered and appressed on the midvein and lateral veins on both faces, glabrescent. Inflorescence in multiflorous glomerules; bracts with a few apical hairs, the outer ones 1–1.5 × 2–3 mm, the inner ones narrowly triangular and approximately 1 mm in length. Male flower not seen. Female flower with glabrous pedicel; sepals 1–1.5 mm in length. Fruit immature, ellipsoid, glabrous.

## Distribution

endemic to Gabon, known only from two localities, one in the Cristal Mountains near Tchimbélé, the other in Waka National Park

## Ecology

secondary dense forest; at 400–600 m altitude. b: Breteler (2011b). Bibliography
