# *Sporobolus pyramidalis* P. Beauv.

**Genus**: [[Sporobolus]]
**Family**: [[Gramineae]]
**Protologue**: P. BEAUV. Fl. Owar. et Bénin, 2 : 36, t. 80 (1807)

## Synonyms

- Agrostis owariensis Schult.
- Vilfa pyramidalis Steud.

## Description

Perennial herb, reaching 1 m tall or more. Leaves clustered at the base, sheaths glabrous except for the margins, the lower ones more or less compressed. Blades linear, 10–30 × 0.2–0.4 cm or more, generally involute, glabrous, smooth.

Spiciform panicle elongate, up to approximately 40 cm tall; branches solitary erect, irregularly disposed on the rachis. Pedicels short, spikelets olive-green, 2 mm long with similar glumes, the lower one obtuse and often denticulate, reaching 1/3 the length of the spikelet, without nervures; the upper one more or less acuminate, somewhat longer, with 1 nervure. Lemma lanceolate, acuminate, with 1 or 3 nervures, palea similar, somewhat shorter. 3 stamens; seeds brown with thin pericarp.

Common species throughout tropical Africa; it is a ruderal that presents scarcely any practical interest.
