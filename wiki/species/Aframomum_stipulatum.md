# *Aframomum stipulatum* (Gagnep.) K. Schum.
**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Aframomum]]
## Description
Stoloniferous savanna herb to 1.5 m. Sessile blades c. 20 × 4.5 cm; bifid ligule c. 1 cm. One or two basal few-flowered spikes; flowers pink to pale mauve at ground level. Red elongate fruit with smooth black seeds.
