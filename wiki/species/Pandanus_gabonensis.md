# *Pandanus gabonensis* Huynh

**Family**: [[Pandanaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Pandanus]]

## Description

**Habit**: Tree 5 m tall. Trunk supported by stilt roots; bark grey and spiny,
the spines to 5 mm long and 3 mm in diameter at the base.

**Leaves**: Dark green and glossy adaxially, 140–150 × 4.4–5 cm at the blade
base, gradually narrowing to the convergence of the lateral folds. Lateral
folds unarmed and visible almost to the blade base; tail c. 10 cm. About 106
longitudinal veins at mid-blade, visible on both surfaces; transverse veins
visible adaxially and barely visible or locally invisible abaxially. All
marginal spines antrorse: lower spines to 2.8 mm deep and 20 mm apart, median
spines to 1.8 mm and 18 mm apart, upper spines to 1.6 mm and 10 mm apart; on
the lower 8 cm of the tail minute and to 5 mm apart, becoming dense above.
Costal spines begin almost at the blade base and are generally more widely
spaced than marginal spines at the same level; lower ones retrorse and usually
shorter, upper ones antrorse and equal to or slightly shorter than marginal
spines, those on the tail slightly longer and broader at the base.

**Male structures**: Unknown.

**Infructescence**: Monosyncarpic. Mature syncarp pendent, oblong, to 26 × 13
cm, triangular with rounded angles in cross-section. Core triangular, c. 2.6
cm per side, solid above but partly hollow below when dry. Rachis slightly
curved, c. 47 cm long and c. 1.5 cm thick apically, becoming thinner toward
the base; bearing about 11 bracts separated by internodes to 9 cm, and c. 14
cm below the basal leafy bract.

**Drupes**: Usually (3.5–)4(–4.2) cm long, to 1.5 cm wide (usually 0.9–1.2 cm)
and 1.2 cm thick (usually 0.8–1.1 cm), 5–6-angled, usually 1-locular with a
U-shaped stigma c. 1 × 1 mm, sometimes 2-locular with two closely associated,
opposed stigmas. Equator slightly above the endocarp; supra-equatorial part c.
1 cm, conical or narrowly conical. Pileus slightly shorter, sometimes armed
with prickles below the stigmas. Most pileal hypodermal cells unlignified;
some lignified cells to 165 µm and parallel to the drupe axis. Upper and lower
mesocarp rich in spongy matter when dry. Endocarp c. 1.1 cm along the seed
locule axis and 0.2–0.8 cm peripherally, slightly shouldered. Seed locule c.
0.8 × 0.4 cm, its centre slightly supramedian; intralocular fibre c. 1.8 mm
wide. Endosperm straw-coloured. Germination tube c. 1.7 mm below the locule,
free from the lateral fibres or rarely fused only in its upper part.

## Type

Gabon, banks of the Komo near Mbel, 12 January 1968, *Hallé N. & Villiers
J.-F. 4332* (holotype and isotype P).

## Distribution

Known in the treatment only from the type locality near Mbel on the Komo,
Gabon.

## Specimens examined

**Gabon** — *Hallé N. & Villiers J.-F. 4332*, near Mbel, banks of the Komo
(leaf, infructescence rachis and fruit, Jan.).

## Notes

The species resembles *P. candelabrum* in its c. 4 cm drupe and the position
of the upper mesocarp base near the roof of the seed locule, but differs
clearly in having a single syncarp rather than 3–5.

The OCR lost the printed species heading and part of the Latin diagnosis on
image-heavy pp. 14–17. The complete French description, type, comparison and
specimen statement were retained and are the basis of this page.
