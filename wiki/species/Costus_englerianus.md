# *Costus englerianus* K. Schum.
**Family in Vol. 9**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Current placement**: Costaceae · **Genus**: [[Costus]]
## Description
Creeping, node-rooting herb bearing a single erect dark-green elliptic leaf c. 15 cm long. The short terminal spike of three white flowers sits in the sheathing leaf base. The labellum is c. 5 cm, obovate with crisped margins, white with yellow near the base; ovary glabrous.
## Distribution
Rainforest understorey from Sierra Leone to Gabon, often forming stands. Type: Preuss 461, Cameroon. Gabon collections are from Fougamou, Makokou and Abanga in the Monts de Cristal.
