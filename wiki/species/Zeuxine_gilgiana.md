# *Zeuxine gilgiana* Kraenzl. & Schltr.

**Genus**: [[Zeuxine]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 38 : 150 (1906)

## Description

– TYPE: Zenker 1625, Cameroon, B del., lecto-K, isolecto-P! (designated here) – FCAM 1: 258 (1998).

Stem < 26 cm, with numerous glands above the leaves. These, clustered at the lower part of the stem, 3.3–5.5 × 1.2–2.1 cm, obliquely ovate-lanceolate, acute. Inflorescence 8–10 cm, 20–30-flowered. Perianth segments pale green and pink, labellum bright orange to orange-red. Sepals thin, fragile with fairly dense glands on the external side; dorsal sepal ovate, subobtuse; lateral sepals obliquely oblong-ovate. Petals obliquely ovate-lanceolate, subacute. Labellum sessile, constricted at the apical 1/3: lower part saccate with 2 large fleshy processes on the internal side, thickened toward the top, overall oblong-ovate form, involute margin, and apical part reniform, sometimes with small apicule. – Pl. 29, p. 67.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon, Fernando Po, Annobon, Congo (Brazzaville), Congo (Kinshasa)

## Ecology

alt. 300–650 m. Terrestrial plant in forest gallery and rainforest
