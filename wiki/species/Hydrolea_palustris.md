# *Hydrolea palustris* (Aublet) Raeuschel Planche 9

**Genus**: [[Hydrolea]]
**Family**: [[Hydroleaceae]]
**Protologue**: Nomencl. éd. 3 : 76 (1797)

## Description

Herb up to 60 cm high; stem erect to decumbent, branched or simple, succulent, glabrous, unarmed. Leaves sessile or on a petiole up to 6 mm long; blade lanceolate or rhomboidal to narrowly rhomboidal, 4–12 × 0.7–2.5 cm, glabrous, base cuneate to decurrent on the petiole, apex acuminate to acute, margin entire or sinuate. Inflorescence axillary, with 1–5(–7) flowers in glomerate or short racemose arrangements. Flower with 2 bracteoles; calyx lobed almost to the base, glabrous, lobes 5–7.5 × 1.5–3 mm, acute; corolla lobed almost to the base, blue, lobes 5–7 × 2–3 mm; stamens 4–6 mm long; ovary glabrous; styles 1.5–2 mm long. Fruit ± globose, 3.5–5 × 3.5–4.5 mm, glabrous. Seeds ovoid, 0.4–0.6 mm long; testa longitudinally striated.

## Distribution

African species known from Senegal, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Benin, Togo, Nigeria, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Gabon, Congo (Brazzaville), Democratic Republic of the Congo, southern Sudan, and Madagascar; in Gabon poorly collected, probably not rare

## Ecology

hydrophytic herb of generally open wet stations, savannas or swampy fallows, along rivers or small lakes, but also in wet openings in forest and gallery forest; up to 800 m altitude. B: Davenport (1988), Erbar et al. (2005), Hepper (1963b). Bibliography
