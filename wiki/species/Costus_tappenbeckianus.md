# *Costus tappenbeckianus* J. Braun et K. Schum.
**Family in Vol. 9**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Current placement**: Costaceae · **Genus**: [[Costus]]
## Description
Small creeping-rhizomed herb with leafy stems only a few decimetres tall. Leaves cluster above, c. 10 × 4.5 cm, sparsely hairy above and red beneath with fine appressed hairs. A separate 1–3 cm stalk carries a two- to four-flowered spike. Corolla white; labellum c. 6 cm, pink with a white median zone.
## Distribution
Humid forest understorey in Cameroon and Gabon. Type: J. Braun s.n., Cameroon. Gabon material from Mougouyanga, Maboumi and Tsande near Lastoursville.
## Source
Koechlin, J. (1964). Zingibéracées. *Flore du Gabon* 9: 74–76.
