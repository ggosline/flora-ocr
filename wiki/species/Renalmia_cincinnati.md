# *Renalmia cincinnati* (K. Schum.) Bak.

**Genus**: [[Renalmia]]
**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]]

## Synonyms

- Ethanium cincinnatum K. Schum., Bot. Jahrb. 15 : 424 (1892).

## Description

F.T.A. 7:312 (1898).

Herbs with leafy stems reaching one meter in height or more; leaves with petioles $ 4\ \text{to}\ 7\ \text{cm} $ long and with narrowly white blade, glabrous, purplish on the lower surface, shortly acuminate at the apex, attenuate at the base and unequally decurrent along the petiole, $ 15\text{-}40\ \times\ 4\text{-}7\ \text{cm} $. Ligule truncate, $ 1\ \text{mm} $ long. Inflorescence paniculate, lax, reaching $ 1\ \text{m} $ long, of which approximately half for the peduncle bearing $ 4\ \text{to}\ 6\ \text{leafless sheaths} $ glabrous or slightly pubescent towards the apex. Axis of the inflorescence slightly zigzag, finely pubescent. Bracts oblong, lanceolate, green, erect and remaining appressed against the flowers, of variable length according to position ($ 0.8\ \text{to}\ 3\ \text{cm} $ or more) and each axilling a subsessile secondary inflorescence of $ 4\ \text{to}\ 6\ \text{flowers} $ or more; bracteoles scarious, enrolled in a tube not split to the base, puberulent, approximately $ 8\ \text{mm} $ long. Floral pedicels approximately $ 6\ \text{mm} $ long, up to $ 10\ \text{mm} $ during fruiting. Calyx irregularly dentate, $ 7\ \text{mm} $ long. Flower white, corolla $ 16\ \text{mm} $ long, labellum $ 9\ \text{to}\ 10\ \text{mm} $. Fruit red, dehiscent, $ 12\text{-}13\ \times\ 5\text{-}6\ \text{mm} $, with about twenty light brown seeds with yellow laciniate aril becoming white cottony in appearance when dry.
