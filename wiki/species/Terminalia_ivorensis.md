# *Terminalia ivorensis* A. CHEVALIER

**Genus**: [[Terminalia]]
**Family**: [[Combretaceae]]
**Protologue**: Vég. Util. 5 : 152 (1909) ; GRIFFITHS, Journ. Linn. Soc. Lond., Bot., 55 : 876, tab. 20 (1959) ; LIBEN, Fl. Cam. 25 : 68 (1983). TYPE : Côté-d'Ivoire, Cercle des lagunes, Macouguie (fr.) Chevalier 16153 (lecto-K, cf. GRIFFITHS (1959), voir aussi LIBEN, Bull. Jard. Bot. Nat. Belg. 54 : 290 (1984))

## Description

TYPE: Côte d'Ivoire, Cercle des lagunes, Macouguie (Fr.) Chevalier 16153 (lecto-K, cf. GRIFFITHS (1959), see also LIBEN, Bull. Jard. Bot. Nat. Belg. 54: 290 (1984)).

Trees, height ≤ 40 m, diameter ≤ 1 m. Bark slice yellow glossy.

Leaves alternate; petiole ≤ 2.5 cm; blade obovate, ≤ 10 × ≤ 4 cm; major lateral veins, 5–7 pairs, domatia barely visible; base attenuate, apex shortly acuminate.

Inflorescences in axillary spikes ≤ 12 cm, bracts linear, 4 mm long.

Flowers 5-merous, buds acuminate. Receptacle with silvery reflections due to the presence of an indumentum; the lower 1.5–3 mm long, the upper ≤ 6 mm wide, including the calyx lobes which are long, triangular and acuminate. Stamens 5 mm long and anthers, 0.4 mm. Disk hidden beneath long hairs. Style 4 mm long.

Fruit with two marginal wings, ≤ 9 cm × ≤ 2 cm, ± rectangular, pubescent, apex rounded or emarginate; stipe ≤ 15 mm. – Pl. 32, EH, p. 107.

## Ecology

Dense humid forests

## Distribution

species of West Africa, planted for its timber in Gabon
