# *Pteris burtoni* Baker (P.L. XVIII)

**Genus**: [[Pteris]]
**Family**: [[Adiantaceae]]

## Synonyms

- Pteris Johnstoni Baker, loc. cit.
- Pteris aethiopica Christ, Ann. de Bot. 22 : 21 (1909).
- Pteris atrovirens var. Cervonii Bonaparte, Bull. Mus. Paris 19 : 388 (nomen).
- Pteris Burtoni var. aethiopica (Christ) Tard., Mém. IFAN 28 : 72, t. 10, f. 8 (1953).

## Description

— Bak., Ann. Bot. 5 : 218 (1891). — Tard., Mém. IFAN 28 : 72, t. 10, f. 6-7 (1953).

Rhizome short, with fronds in tufts. Petiole 10-18 cm long, straw-coloured to reddish at the base and sometimes along its entire length, canaliculate, smooth. Lamina with lanceolate outline, 20-25 cm long by 15-17 cm wide, with 3-7 pairs of lateral pinnae of fairly variable form, either entire with entire apex, tapered, crenate, decurrent, the lower ones bearing a basal lobe, the middle ones with entire apex, pinnate at the base. Rachis broadly winged at the apex, smooth; terminal pinna similar to the lateral ones; presence of a bud at the base of the terminal pinna. Costae and costulae smooth (very rarely with a few spines). Veins forming two rows of areoles between the margin and the costa. Fertile pinna often more dissected than the sterile, no paraphyses.

## Type

Burton, sans n., Ghana

## Discussion

From Guinea to Nigeria, Fernando Po, Congo and southern Angola. River margins.
