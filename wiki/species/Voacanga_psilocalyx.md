# *Voacanga psilocalyx* Pierre ex Stapf Planche 125

**Genus**: [[Voacanga]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. trop. Afr. 4(l) : 159 (1902)

## Synonyms

- Voacanga zenkeri Stapf, Fl. trop. Afr. 4(l) : 159 (1902).

## Description

Shrub or small tree reaching 3 m in height; branches glabrous. Leaf glabrous; petiole and blade elliptic to narrowly elliptic to obovate, 0.5–10 × 1–6 cm, highly variable in form and size, even on the same plant, long attenuate at the base, acuminate at the apex; secondary nerves 5 to 14 pairs. Inflorescence: peduncle 3.5–20 cm; bracts reaching approximately the size of the calyx. Flower: pedicel 5–20 mm; calyx 11.5–17 mm long, with tube bearing a ring of internal colleters, with erect lobes, oblong, 2–4 times as long as the tube, 2–4 mm wide; corolla white, tube 12–18 mm long, distinctly twisted, progressively narrowed toward the throat, with lobes ± patent and incurved toward the apex, oblong to ovate-oblong, 10–16 × 3–5 mm, with a distance of 1–2 mm between the throat and the base of the lobes, forming in the bud an ellipsoid or elongate conical head; stamens inserted above the middle, anther 2–3 mm long; pistil 8–11 mm long. Fruit: carpels free, obliquely ovoid or ellipsoid, 1.5–3 cm long (in dry state), with a short beak.

## Distribution

south-east Nigeria, Cameroon and Gabon; in Gabon: Estuaire

## Ecology

understory of primary and secondary forest; in Gabon, below 100 m altitude. Notes: Additional field work is necessary to confirm that V. psilocalyx and V. zenkeri represent indeed the same species. The form of the corolla lobes overall is more slender toward the northern part of the distribution range (Cameroon and Nigeria). Several specimens cited in the revision of Leeuwenberg (1985) under V. psilocalyx represent here newly described species, V. bidaultii and V. wieringae, or else V. micrantha.
