# *Magnistipula cupheiflora* Mildbraed

**Genus**: [[Magnistipula]]
**Family**: [[Scytopetalaceae]]
**Protologue**: Not. Bot. Gart. Mus. Berlin 8 : 57 (1921); WHITE, BJBB 46 : 289 (1976)

## Description

— Hirtella cupheiflora (MILDBR.) A. CHEV., Bull. Mus. Nat. Hist. Nat. Paris, ser. 2, 3 : 195 (1931); HAUM., BJBB 21 : 184 (1951); KEAY, FWTA, ed. 2, 1 (2) : 430 (1958).

Shrub reaching 10 m in height and 20 cm in diameter. Trunk somewhat fluted and sinuous; branches erect, twisted; crown densely leafy. Bark fairly tender, granular; oblique section pinkish-red with spots similarly ± dark. Wood yellowish-pink towards the exterior then reddish-brown towards the heart, very hard. Branchlets lenticellate, glabrous. Young branchlets with sparse appressed puberulence (found also on stipules and petioles), then glabrescent. Stipules linear to lanceolate, acute, 0.5–1 × 4–5 mm. Petiole 2–5 mm, glabrescent then transversely wrinkled beneath. Blade elliptic to lanceolate, 3–4(–6.5) × 6–8(–16) cm (up to 18 cm in length in Sierra Leone, fide KEAY, l.c. : 430); base cuneate to acute, sometimes obtuse; apex acute, sometimes obtuse, acumen elongate (0.5 × 1 cm), with obtuse apex; midvein channelled puberulent then glabrescent above, with sparse appressed hairs then glabrescent below; lateral nerves 5–7(–9) pairs; blade glabrous; sometimes small elliptic or circular glands clearly above the base of the blade below near the midvein, sometimes minute circular glands below the acumen.

Racemes axillary, often grouped in pairs or threes, slender, scarcely exceeding 2–3 cm (up to 8 cm in Sierra Leone, fide KEAY, l.c. : 430) and pauciflorous (about 10 flowers), covered with silvery-golden or golden-brownish tomentellum extending into the interior of the sepals; peduncle very short; on the raceme axis, at the base of each pedicel, an oval concave bract, 2 mm long, and two similar reduced bracteoles; pedicel slender reaching 5–6 mm in length to the base of the receptacle. Flower 8–10 mm long (without the petals early caducous); receptacle yellowish, straight, very distinctly ventricose at the base, bearing perpendicularly, from its middle, a conical button formed by the 5 petals, the interior of the receptacle lined with white appressed hairs directed downward across the throat, the gibbous part being golden and glandular internally; sepals greenish, triangular, 3–4 mm tall; petals white with purple spot at the base, 5 of which 3 larger ± orbicular (3 mm), margin villous; stamens 5, white, curved, 4 mm in length (the median and external ones the longest), filaments widened at the base and connate for almost half their height; staminal rim formed of 5–7 (–8) triangular glandular teeth, purple, scarcely exceeding 0.5 mm in height; ovary globular (1 mm), covered with hispid white erect hairs, reaching 1 mm in height; style white, 4 mm long, somewhat curved.

Young infructescence with fruiting peduncle proper fairly robust, reaching 1 cm in length, terminated by the reflexed sepals, and bearing a young erect drupe, with golden tomentellous surface and sparse yellowish hispid hairs. Mature infructescence and fruit unknown. (According to specimen Fox 31 from Sierra Leone, associated with this species: Drupe globular, 3.5 cm in diameter, with golden tomentellous surface and sparse yellowish hispid hairs). — PL. 25, p. 85.

## Type

Mildbraed 8307, Cameroun (holo-, B delet.; iso-, K!)

## Discussion

Magnistipula cupheiflora Mildbr. is known on the one hand from Sierra Leone and Ivory Coast (Geerling & Bokdam 760, WAG!), on the other hand from Cameroon and Gabon.

Breteler 2619, 6 km Bertoua on road Batouri–Bétaré Oya (fl., j. fr., Feb.) (K, P, WAG). Letouzey 4418, 15 km S Djouo, 20 km E Somalomo on the Dja (fl., Feb.). Mildbraed 8307, near Nanga Eboko, 130 km NE Yaoundé (fl., Feb.) (K).
