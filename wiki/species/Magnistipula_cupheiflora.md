# *Magnistipula cupheiflora* Mildbraed

**Genus**: [[Magnistipula]]
**Family**: [[Scytopetalaceae]]
**Modern family**: [[Lecythidaceae]] subfam. Scytopetaloideae — *Scytopetalaceae* as circumscribed here is superseded; see [[Scytopetalaceae]]
**Protologue**: Not. Bot. Gart. Mus. Berlin 8 : 57 (1921); WHITE, BJBB 46 : 289 (1976)

## Synonyms

- Hirtella cupheiflora (MILDBR.) A. CHEV., Bull. Mus. Nat. Hist. Nat. Paris, ser. 2, 3 : 195 (1931); HAUM., BJBB 21 : 184 (1951); KEAY, FWTA, ed. 2, 1 (2) : 430 (1958).

## Description

Shrub attaining 10 m in height and 20 cm in diameter. Trunk slightly grooved and sinuous; branches erect, tortuous; crown densely leafy. Bark rather tender, granular; oblique section pinkish-red with flecks somewhat darker. Wood yellowish-pink toward the outside then reddish-brown toward the heartwood, very hard. Branchlets lenticellate, glabrous. Young branchlets with sparse appressed pubescence (found also on stipules and petioles), then glabrescent. Stipules linear to lanceolate, acute, 0.5–1 × 4–5 mm. Petiole 2–5 mm, glabrescent then transversely rugose beneath. Lamina elliptic to lanceolate, 3–4(–6.5) × 6–8(–16) cm (up to 18 cm in length in Sierra Leone, fide KEAY, l.c.: 430); base cuneiform to acute, sometimes obtuse; apex acute, sometimes obtuse, with an elongated acumen (0.5 × 1 cm), apex obtuse; midvein grooved and pubescent then glabrescent above, with sparse appressed hairs then glabrescent beneath; lateral veins 5–7(–9) pairs; lamina glabrous; sometimes small elliptic or circular glands distinctly above the base of the lamina beneath near the midvein, sometimes minute circular glands beneath the acumen.

Axillary racemes, often grouped 2–3 together, slender, scarcely exceeding 2–3 cm (up to 8 cm in Sierra Leone, fide KEAY, l.c.: 430) and pauciflorous (about 10 flowers), covered with a golden-silvery or golden-brownish tomentellum extending to the interior of the sepals; peduncle very short; on the raceme axis, at the base of each pedicel, an ovate concave bract, 2 mm long, and two similar bracteoles more reduced; pedicel slender attaining 5–6 mm in length to the base of the receptacle. Flower 8–10 mm long (excluding the petals early caducous); receptacle yellowish, straight, very distinctly ventricose at the base, bearing perpendicularly, from its middle, a conical bud formed by the 5 petals, the interior of the receptacle furnished with white appressed hairs directed downward across the throat, the gibbous part being golden and glandular within; sepals greenish, triangular, 3–4 mm tall; petals white with purple spot at the base, 5 of which 3 larger ± orbicular (3 mm), margin villose; stamens 5, white, curved, 4 mm in length (the median and external ones the longest), filaments enlarged at the base and fused for nearly half their height; staminodial margin formed of 5–7(–8) triangular glandular teeth, purple, scarcely exceeding 0.5 mm in height; ovary globose (1 mm), covered with white hispid hairs, erect, attaining 1 mm in height; style white, 4 mm long, somewhat curved.

Young infructescence with the fruiting peduncle proper rather robust, attaining 1 cm in length, terminated by the reflexed sepals, and bearing a young erect drupe, with golden tomentellous surface with scattered yellowish hispid hairs. Mature infructescence and fruit unknown. (According to specimen Fox 31 from Sierra Leone, related to this species: drupe globose, 3.5 cm in diameter, with golden tomentellous surface with scattered yellowish hispid hairs). — PL. 25, p. 85.

## Type

Mildbraed 8307, Cameroun (holo-, B delet.; iso-, K!)

## Discussion

Magnistipula cupheiflora Mildbr. is known on one hand from Sierra Leone and Côte d'Ivoire (Geerling & Bokdam 760, WAG!), on the other hand from Cameroon and Gabon.

Breteler 2619, 6 km Bertoua on road Batouri-Bétaré Oya (fl., j. fr., Feb.) (K, P, WAG). Letouzey 4418, 15 km S Djouo, 20 km E Somalomo on the Dja (fl., Feb.). Mildbraed 8307, near Nanga Eboko, 130 km NE Yaoundé (fl., Feb.) (K).
