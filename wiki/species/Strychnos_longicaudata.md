# *Strychnos longicaudata* Gilg

**Genus**: [[Strychnos]]
**Family**: [[Loganiaceae]]

## Description

in ENGLER, Bot. Jahrb. 17 : 570 (1893) (« longecaudata »). — FTA 4(1) : 527 (« id. »). — FWTA ed. 2, 2 : 44. — LEEUWENBERG, l. c. : 153, fig. 22, map 24.

— S. bequaerti De Wild., Rev. Zool. Afr. 10 (1), Suppl. Bot. : 5 (1922, non 1915).

— S. brevicymosa De Wild., l. c. : 6.

Climbing shrub or liana reaching a height of 4–30 m and a length of 10–60 m; stem 2.5–15 cm in diameter; bark dark brown, with large lenticels; wood pale dirty yellow or cream; branchlets non-lenticellate; small branchlets glabrous, with hairs arranged in lines below the stipular line or, less often, with dense or sparse pubescence, cylindrical; tendrils solitary, at the axils of ordinary leaves.

Leaves with petiole pubescent or glabrous, 2–7 mm long; limb papyraceous to subcoriaceous, even when fresh, thinner and often larger for leaves situated in shade, elliptic or narrowly elliptic (and often ovate for leaves situated on the main axis), 5.5–14 (–17.5) × 2–6 (–8) cm, acuminate or, especially in shade, caudate at the apex, cuneate or rounded at the base, glabrous on both faces or with some stiff hairs on the main veins below; one pair of secondary veins curved along the margin; tertiary venation reticulate, scarcely distinct.

Inflorescences axillary or exceptionally terminal, solitary, ± contracted, 1.5–4 × 1.5–3.5 cm; peduncle and first branches rather elongate and, like the other branches and pedicels, pubescent with hairs sometimes arranged in lines, or glabrous. Flowers 5-merous. Sepals pale green, fused for 1/3 or 2/5 of their height, broadly orbicular to broadly obovate, (1–) 1.2–1.5 × (1–) 1.2–1.7 mm, obtuse, ciliate, glabrous on both faces. Corolla, in the mature bud, 4 (–5) mm long, whitish-green to white, or pale green outside and white inside, campanulate or broadly cylindric, glabrous outside or sometimes bearing sparse pubescence but with the base glabrous, inside white-penicillate at the throat; tube 1.3–1.7 times longer than the lobes, 2.2–2.5 (–3) mm long, with a fine cavity just above the insertion of the stamens; lobes thick, triangular, 1.5–2 × 1–1.5 mm, suberect. Stamens included, inserted at 3/5 of the corolla tube, with very short filament, glabrous; anthers cordate, ciliate with rather stiff hairs at the base and often also on the outside. Pistil glabrous, 2.1–2.4 mm long; ovary globular or nearly so, 0.9–1.2 × 1–1.2 mm, two-locular; style short, thick, 1–1.4 mm long; stigma capitate; 9–18 ovules per locule.

Fruits orange or yellowish-green, small, soft at maturity, ellipsoid, generally somewhat longer than wide and often indistinctly laterally compressed, 14–17 × 11–17 × 11–15 mm, containing one seed and with smooth skin; wall thin, 1 mm thick. Seed flattened, ± plano-convex, elliptic to almost ovate, not curved, 11–15 × 8–12 × 3–5 $ mm, with very short pubescence, smooth. Pl. 27, p. 134.
