# *Canscora alata* (Roth ex Roem. & Schult.) Wall. Planche 22

**Genus**: [[Canscora]]
**Family**: [[Gentianaceae]]
**Protologue**: Numer. List : 4363 (1831)

## Synonyms

- Exacum alatum Roth ex Roem. & Schult., Syst. Veg. 3 : 159 (1818).
- Pladera decussata Roxb., Fl. Ind. 1 : 418 (1820).
- Canscora decussata (Roxb.) Roem. & Schult., Mant. 3 : 229 (1827).

## Description

Herb 10–50 cm tall; stem ± branched. Leaf: blade narrowly ovate, 7–40 × 2–10(–15) mm, base obtuse to briefly attenuate, apex acuminate to acute. Inflorescence lax, 1- to 7-flowered, rarely with solitary flower. Flower: pedicel 3–20 mm, 4-winged; calyx 8–10 mm long, tube 5–9 mm long, lobes 1.5–2 mm long; corolla 10–12 mm long, white, tube 6–9 mm long, lobes obovate to elliptic, 3–5 mm long; fertile stamen with filament ± 2 mm and anther ± 1 mm long, the three sterile ones somewhat smaller; ovary cylindric; style 3–4 mm. Fruit 5–8 mm long. Seed ± 0.4 × 0.2 mm.

## Distribution

from eastern Guinea-Bissau to Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea (Annobón), Gabon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Sudan, Kenya, Tanzania, Mozambique, Zambia, Zanzibar and Madagascar; also in tropical Asia, from India to China; in Gabon, known from Nyanga and Ogooué-Ivindo

## Ecology

savannas; in Gabon at low altitude, elsewhere at 0–2500 m

## Source note

C. alata, also known from tropical Asia, is the unique species of Gentianaceae in Gabon with zygomorphic flowers. With its dichasia inflorescence it resembles species of the genus Centaurium Hill.
