# *Gladiolus gregarius* Welw. ex Bak. var. gregarius

**Genus**: [[Gladiolus]]
**Family**: [[Iridaceae]]
**Protologue**: Trans. Linn. Soc. London , Bot. 1 : 268 (1878)

## Description

Herb 25–120 cm tall, glabrous; corm 10–40 mm in diameter; stem 2–4 mm in diameter; cataphylls often foliaceous, up to 8 cm long. Leaves 4–7, cauline, imbricate, linear to elliptic, the lower ones up to 60 cm × 10–20 mm, the upper ones more reduced. Inflorescence erect, 4–25-flowered; bracts imbricate, green but becoming brownish, 18–70 mm long. Flowers zygomorphic; perianth distinctly bilabiate, light to dark purple, with flared and incurved tube 12–15 mm long; lobes unequal, the upper median one arched, elliptic, 18–30 × 12–18 mm, the other 5 united at the base for 4–6 mm of which the 3 lower ones unguiculate and elliptic, united at the base for about 2 mm, 10–20 × 4–6 mm, ornamented with a ± diamond-shaped macule, dark purple; stamens arched beneath the dorsal tepal, exserted 5–10 mm, filament 9–15 mm long, anther 6–12 mm long; ovary 3–5 mm long; style 20–25 mm long, stigmas about 2–5 mm long. Fruits narrowly ellipsoid, 12–15 mm long; valves 3–10 mm wide. Seeds subelliptic or angular, scarcely to broadly winged, 5–7 mm long.

## Distribution

Senegal, Mali, Guinea-Bissau, Guinea, Sierra Leone, Burkina Faso, Ivory Coast, Ghana, Togo, Benin, Niger, Nigeria, Cameroon, Chad, Central African Republic, Gabon, Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Angola, Sudan, Uganda, Burundi, Tanzania, Zambia, Malawi, Mozambique; fairly common in Gabon

## Ecology

savannas; between 250 and 1320 m altitude

## Source note

Only the variety gregarius is recorded from Gabon. The variety angustifolius (Van Bockstal) Geerinck, with narrower leaves, smaller bracts and flowers, has a more eastern and southern distribution. BIBLIOGRAPHY: Baker (1892, 1898), Geerinck (2005), Goldblatt (1993, 1996a, 1996b), Hepper (1968c)
