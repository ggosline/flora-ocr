# *Pausinstalia macroceras* (K. Schum.)

**Genus**: [[Pausinystalia]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Beille, Act. Soc. Linn. Bordeaux 61 : 130 (1906)

## Synonyms

- Corynanthe gabonensis A. Chev., Bois du Gabon : 222 (1916).

## Description

Pierre ex Beille (PL. 10)

Tree 15-40 m tall with a bole 10-15 m and 0.40-0.60 m diam. Wood pale yellow washed with pink. Extremities entirely glabrous. Petiole 5-20 mm. Blade brown-russet or black when dry, 7-25 × 2.5-7.5 cm, with base ± attenuate or cordate more often acute, the two forms possibly occurring on neighbouring nodes; apex acuminate. 9-16 pairs of secondary nerves.

Inflorescences subapical generally opposite in pairs, entirely glabrous, 8-12 cm long. Flowers white grouped in multifloral spherical pseudoumbels, sessile or with pedicel 0.5 mm. Ovary entirely glabrous (a few rare hairs in Thollon 124 n. s.), ca. 1 mm tall; calyx tubular for less than 0.5 mm, with glabrous lobes 0.5-0.9 mm, acute and suberect. Corolla glabrous with slender tube for nearly 1 mm then widely inflated for 1 mm, very briefly divided into (4)5(6) erect lobes bearing a filiform apical appendage, 6-15 mm long. Anthers sessile sagittate acute 0.8 mm. Style 1-1.5 mm, with bilobed stigmatic apex.

Capsular fruit 15-20 mm long and 5-7 mm wide in sicco, pedicelled for 3-8 mm, dehiscing its seeds through the partly opened apex of the two valves. Seed 7-10 × 1.5-2 mm.

## Discussion

This species appears fairly polymorphic; the leaves are ± long and ± nigrescent. We attribute to it variationally numerous specimens that have been examined by Schumann, De Wildeman, Chevalier, Raymond-Hamet and Pellegrin. Opinions and identifications often diverged over slender or poorly constant morphological data. The abundance of material currently known is at the source of our viewpoint. The typical variety groups all specimens with entirely glabrous calyx ¹.

1. Pausinstalia macroceras (K. Schum.) Pierre ex Beille forma brachythrysum (K. Schum.) N. Hallé stat. nov.: = Corynanthe brachythyrus K. Schum., Notizbl. Bot. Gart. Berlin 3: 95 (1901); = P. brachythyrus (K. Schum.) De Wild., Ann. Soc. Sc. Brux. 42. 1: 175 (1922). The type: Zenker 1746 (P!), Bipinde, Cameroun, pertains to a specimen essentially characterized by the reduction of its inflorescences; it is cited alone without other reference specimen. The pubescence of the ovary, whereas the calyceal lobes are nearly glabrous externally, gives this form an intermediate position between P. macroceras and its variety Bequaertii. One can consider that this form should be subordinated to variety Bequaertii rather than to the typical variety. By recommending the rank of form to this taxon represented by a mediocre type, one can avoid having to reject for reason of priority the name Bequaertii founded on a type in very fine condition and which presents in its pubescence characters more pronounced than Schumann's plant. Our viewpoint is in agreement with article 60 of the International Code of Nomenclature.

South Nigeria, Cameroun, Rio-Muni, Gabon, Mayombe and lower Congo.

Bark with aphrodisiac properties. Fine cabinet wood.

2 bis. Pausinstalia macroceras (K. Schum.) P. ex B. var. Bequaertii (De Wild.) N. Hallé comb. nov. (PL. 10, 9).

= Pausinystalia Bequaertii De Wild. = Corynanthe Bequaertii De Wild., Ann. Soc. Sc. Brux. 42 : 117 (1922). Both names are presented together as new: the author wished to guard against the risk of seeing his new species recombined by another, at a time when generic positions gave rise to divergent opinions; this procedure is illegitimate. = Pausinystalia brachythyrsa (K. Schum.) Brandt, according to F. Pellegrin, Fl. du Mayombe 3 : 7 (1938).

Tree exceeding 20 m tall. Seems to differ from the typical variety only in the ± abundant pubescence of the calyx at the level of the ovary and on the lobes. Axillary domatia are glabrous: this character emphasized by De Wildeman, is peculiar neither to the variety nor even to the species.

Vernacular names (probably applicable to the whole species): Ngantsa (Bavungu); Musuli (Eshira).
