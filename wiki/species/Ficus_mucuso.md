- F. sidifolia HIERN, l.c.: 1018 (1900); LEBRUN & BOUTIQUE, l.c.: 14 (1948). LECTOTYPE: Welwitsch 6407, Angola, Golungo Alto, near Sange (BM; iso-, P).

- F. corylifolia WARB. ex DE WILD. & DUR., Ann. Mus. Congo, Bot., sér. 3, fasc. 2: 215 (1901), descr. gall.; WARB., Ann. Mus. Congo, Bot., sér. 6, 1: 27 (1904), descr. lat.; MILDBR. & BURRET, l.c.: 193 (1911). TYPE: Dewèvre 681, Zaïre, Coquilhatville (holo-BR).

- F. corylifolia WARB. ex DE WILD. & DUR. var. glabrescens WARB., l.c.: 28 (1904); MILDBR. & BURRET, l.c.: 193 (1911). TYPE: Gillet 1259, Zaïre, Kisantu (holo-, BR).

Trees up to 30 (-40) m tall, often with buttresses. Leafy branchlets 3-8 mm thick, glabrescens, with long white or brown hairs at the nodes, reddish-brown when dry, periderm flaking in small scales. Leaves spirally arranged, tending to be distichous. Blade suborbicular to elliptic (to obovate), 6-17 cm long, 4-15 cm wide, papyraceous to subcoriaceous, apex shortly acuminate to obtuse, base cordate to sometimes truncate, margin entire to crenate; upper surface scabrid to scabrous, hirsute or with scattered long, weak hairs only on the main veins, lower surface pubescent white to brownish or hirsute, long stiff brown hairs with an echinulate base on the main veins; veins almost flat above, prominent below; 3-6 pairs of lateral veins, including 1-2 pairs of basal veins, glandular dots in the axils of the basal veins. Petiole (1-) 2-9 cm long, 1-2 mm thick, pubescent white mixed with stiff brown hairs with an echinulate base. Stipules free, 1-2 cm long, pubescent mixed with long appressed white or reddish-brown hairs, with glabrous margins, caducous.

Figs solitary or in pairs on the nodes of leafless ramified branchlets (bearing only stipules up to 8 mm) and on the main branches and twigs of the tree, or also on the trunk; peduncle 1.5-2 cm long, 1-2 mm thick, apex (often thickened to 6 mm), pubescent, periderm flaking in small scales; basal bracts 3, broadly ovate to semicircular, 3-5 mm long, 3-6 mm wide, basally fused or free, ± densely pubescent; receptacle globose (depressed) to obovoid, 2.5-4 cm in diameter when fresh, 2-3 cm when dry, ± densely pubescent, yellow, orange, reddish, or reddish-brown at maturity.

Pistillate flowers with 2-4 tepals 1.5-2.5 cm long, irregularly cleft or very narrow, free or basally fused, stigmas free; seed flowers sessile or with a pedicel up to 1.5 mm, style 1.5-2.2 mm long, stigma elongated; gall flowers with a pedicel up to 3 mm, style ca. 1 mm long, stigma shorter and ± bottle-shaped. Fruits globose, ellipsoid, oblong or (sub) ovoid, 1-1.5 mm long; gall fruits ellipsoid, oblong or (sub) ovoid, ± distinctly stipitate, 2-3 mm long. Staminate flowers sessile or with a pedicel up to 0.5 mm, subtending bracts 2 (or 3), 2-3 mm long, sometimes cucullate, perianth 1.5-2.5 mm long, stamens 2, filament up to 0.5 mm, anther 1.3-1.5 mm long. - Pl. 36, p. 133.

Pollinators: Ceratosolen sp. (Ivory Coast).

## Type

Welwitsch 6416, Angola, Golungo Alto, riv. Cuango (iso-, B, K, P)

## Discussion

This species extends from Guinea-Bissau to Tanzania and Angola via Cameroon and Gabon; it is also present in Fernando Po and São Tomé. It is found in regions of dense humid evergreen forest, dense humid semi-deciduous forest, and in forest galleries.
