# *Rhynchospora holoschoenoides* (Rich.) Herter Planche 123, Figure 23a

**Genus**: [[Rhynchospora]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Revista Sudamer. Bot. 9 : 157 (1953)

## Description

Perennial herb, stoloniferous, glabrous, base covered by fibrous remains of old leaves and stems at the tip of short scaly stolons; stem 30–140 cm × 1.5–4 mm, triangular. Leaves numerous, mostly basal, but some in the upper two-thirds

a b

## Distribution

Tropical America and Africa, including South Africa and Madagascar; in Gabon, found in Estuaire, Ngounié, Nyanga and Ogooué-Maritime (10 collections)

## Ecology

wet habitats, often on sandy soils near rivers and marshes, seasonally wet or flooded grasslands, also in brackish waters; in Gabon, near sea level (less than 20 m), up to 1800 m in East Africa. Taxonomic note: easily distinguished from other Gabon species by its inflorescence formed of several spherical heads of tight spikelets. It is sometimes confused with Actinoschoenus filiformis, a plant that grows only on inselbergs. b: Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al.
