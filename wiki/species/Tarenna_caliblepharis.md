# *Tarenna caliblepharis* N. Hallé, sp. nov.

**Genus**: [[Tarenna]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]

## Description

(PL. 25, p. III)

Shrub of moderate size, very hirsute. Stipules acute with pilose margins. Petiole hirsute. Leaf blade elliptic, discolorous, nitidulous, hirsute along the midrib, scarcely pilose above, the lower surface provided with sparse white hairs. Secondary nerves 8-16 pairs, arcuate; reticulation inconspicuous. Inflorescences villose with subulate bracts, hirsute. Sepals 5, linear, acute, circa 3 mm long, piloso-ciliate, glabrous above. Ovules 6-7 in each locule, immersed in the placenta. Related to T. lasiorachis (K. Schum.) Bremek. but differing chiefly in larger leaves with very hirsute pubescence and longer sepals.

Shrub 1.5-2 m high, tortuous, somewhat decumbent, with young branches subterete, densely velutinous-hirsute, with ochraceous or whitish hairs in dried material, approximately 1 mm long. Stipules deltoid, somewhat elongate, with a very acute apex, 5-10 mm long, nigrescent, glabrous with pubescent margins to the apex. Marked or strong anisophylly. Petiole 8-18 mm, densely hirsute. Leaf blade discolorous; upper surface brownish, somewhat glossy, glabrescent or with sparse hairs and the midrib pubescent; lower surface light rust-colored with a loose covering of white hairs and the nerves and margins hirsute; elliptic in shape; dimensions 6-21 × 2.2-8.5 cm; base acute, scarcely asymmetric; apex with an acumen 8-14 mm long. 8-16 pairs of secondary nerves; fine reticulation not distinct.

Inflorescences velutinous, terminal, 4-6 cm wide, with narrow acute velutinous bracts, 2-3 mm long. Pedicels 3-6 mm, velutinous. Calyx strongly velutinous (hairs 1 mm), turbinate for 2 mm, tubular for 1 mm, with 5 sublinear acute lobes 2.5-4 × 0.9 mm, velutinous on the outer surface and curved extrorsal. Corolla and androecium unknown. Ovary with 2 (3) locules and (5) 6-7 ovules per locule, immersed in a fleshy placenta.

Fruits whitish, black when dry, globose, 5-7 mm in diameter, moderately pubescent, with a very finely wrinkled surface, with persistent or somewhat degraded ± caducous calyx, with a very thin corneous endocarp. 5-6 seeds per fruit, rust-colored, smooth, reaching 3-4.5 mm, convex opposite the hilum which is broadly cave-like and 2-3 mm in diameter.

## Type

N. Hallé 4032, Bélinga, 22 juin 1966 (P), infrutescence juvénile

## Discussion

On humic rocky blocks, very shaded streamside.
