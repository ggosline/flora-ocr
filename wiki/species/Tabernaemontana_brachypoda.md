# *Tabernaemontana brachypoda* K.Schum. Figure 12(E)

**Genus**: [[Tabernaemontana]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 23 : 223 (1896)

## Synonyms

- Gabunia brachypoda (K.Schum.) Stapf, Fl. trop Afr. 4(1) : 137 (1902).
- Gabunia latifolia Stapf, Fl. trop Afr. 4(1) : 137 (1902).
- Tabernaemontana latifolia (Stapf) Pichon, Not. Syst. (Paris) 13 : 253 (1948).

## Description

Vine, flowering and fruiting at the top, with white ramifications, glabrous, smooth. Leaves of one pair equal or unequal, glabrous; petiole 4–20 mm; ochreae generally inconspicuous; limb broadly to narrowly elliptic to obovate, 6–11 × 4–8 cm, cuneate-decurrent on the petiole at the base, acuminate at the apex, with numerous minute black dots scattered beneath; lateral nerves in 5 to 8 pairs, generally inconspicuous. Inflorescences often in pairs, non-ramified, condensed, with 3–15 flowers; pedicel to 5 mm, glabrous. Flower fragrant; bracteoles less than 1 mm long; pedicel 4–12 mm, glabrous; calyx with lobes 1.5–2 × 1–1.5 mm, fimbriate on the margin, without colleters inside; corolla completely white or greenish near the base, in mature bud with rounded apex, widely ovoid or subglobose, sometimes the length of the bud, to tube subcylindric, long 3.2–5 cm, broad 1.5–1.8 mm, often twisted throughout or especially near the base, reflected at the level of the anthers and widening near the throat, the exterior glabrous or almost glabrous, the interior somewhat pilose, with lobes ± 2 cm × 3–5 mm; stamens with sessile anther, narrowly triangular, long 4–5 mm. Fruit: pericarp distinct, up to 2 × 1.5 cm, yellow or orange, smooth, with winglike crests, with several seeds. Seed with shallow longitudinal furrows, with a minute honeycomb-like structure. Distribution: Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Rio Muni, Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola. Cabinda in Gabon, Ngounié, Nyanga, Ogooué, Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo, Maritime and Woleu-Ntem. Ecology: primary and secondary forest in Gabon, to 800 m altitude, elsewhere to 1100 m.

## Source note

In the revision of Tabernaemontana (Leeuwenberg 1991), T. brachypoda is treated as a synonym of T. eglandulosa. Better knowledge of the fruits of T. eglandulosa s.l. has made comprehensible the re-establishment of T. brachypoda as a distinct species. B: Boiteau & Allorge (1981), Stapf (1902). BIBLIOGRAPHY
