# *Combretum marginatum* Engler & Diels

**Genus**: [[Combretum]]
**Family**: [[Combretaceae]]
**Protologue**: Mon. Afr., Pfl., 3 : 18 (1899) ; LIBEN, FZ : 46 (1968) ; Fl. Cam. 25 : 44 (1983). TYPE : Congo (Kinshasa), s.l. (fl.) Duchesne s.n. anno 1893 (holo-BR)

## Description

TYPE: Congo (Kinshasa), s.l. (fl.) Duchesne s.n. anno 1893 (holo-BR).

– C. obanense (BAKER FIL.) HUTCHINSON & DALZIEL, FWTA, ed. 1, 1: 221 (1927); KEAY, FWTA, ed. 2, 1(1): 272 (1954).

– C. paucinervium ENGLER & DIELS var. obanense BAKER FIL., Cat. of the plants collected by Mr. and Mrs. Talbot: 38 (1913). TYPE: Nigeria, Oban (fl.) Talbot 1676 (holo-BM; iso-K, Z) – syn. nov.

PL. 16. – Combretum marginatum Engler & Diels: 1, flowering branch; 2, entire fruit and cross-section with seed pole; 3, flower, profile and disc view (1,3,5), Bréteret & Jongkind 10339; 2, Tisserant 2260). – Combretum scandens Liben: 4, flowering branch; 5, infructescence (fragment); 6, flower, disc view (4, 6, Bréteret & de Wilde 547; 5, Léonard 930). – Combretum demeusei De Wildeman: 7, apex of leafy branch; 8, entire fruit and cross-section of distal region (7, Bréteret, Jongkind & Dibata 8949; 8, Evrard 4370). – Original drawings by JOANNE DAVIES – Department of Plant Taxonomy, Agricultural University, WAGENINGEN (Netherlands).

Liana ≤ 15 m. Young branches glabrous and somewhat shiny.

Leaves opposite; petiole ≤ 2 cm; blade oblong-elliptic, ≤ 13 × ≤ 7 cm, coriaceous, margin narrow but distinctly cartilaginous, glabrous or pubescent only beneath the midvein; major lateral veins 6–10 pairs; blade apex glandular (often grazed by animals), base attenuate.

Scales, diameter 100 μm.

Inflorescences: panicles with bracts 1–2 mm.

Flowers 4-merous, sessile, yellow-white, fragrant. Lower receptacle 3 mm tall, upper receptacle patelliform, diameter 3 mm. Calyx: lobes, length ≥ upper receptacle tube. Petals white, 2 × 0.5 mm, glabrous, white. Stamens 3–4 mm long; filaments white; anthers 0.5 mm long, yellow. Style 4 mm long.

Fruit ellipsoid, sessile, cross-section angular, length ≤ 5.5 cm, diameter ≤ 1.5 cm, adapted for water dispersal. – Pl. 16, 1–3, p. 59.

from Liberia to Gabon and Congo (Kinshasa)
