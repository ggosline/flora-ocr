# *Spathodea campanulata* Palisot de Beauvois

**Genus**: [[Spathodea]]
**Family**: [[Bignoniaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. Oware 1: 46, tab. 27 (1805); SPRAGUE, FTA 4 (2): 529 (1906); HEINE, FWTA. ed

## Description

2 : 386 (1963) ; GENTRY, Fl. Cam. 27 : 44, tab. 13 (1984).

- Bignonia tulipifera Thonn. in SCHUMACH., Dansk. Vid. Selsk. 4: 47 (1829). TYPE : Thonning s.n., Guinea (P-JUSS (?), specimen without label "e Guinea").

– Spathodea tulipifera (Thonn.) G. Don, Gen. Syst. 4: 223 (1838).

– Spathodea nilotica SEEM., J. of Bot. 3: 333 (1865). TYPE: Speke & Grant Exped. s.n., Guinea (not seen).

- S. danckelmaniana BÜTTN., Verh. Bot. Ver. Brandenb. 31: 87 (1890). TYPE: Büttner 376, Zaire (not seen).

Tree at least 25 m tall and 50 cm in diameter. Leaves imparipinnate, usually with 9–15 leaflets, leaflets ± elliptic, acute to acuminate, asymmetrically subtruncate or broadly cuneate at base, 3–16 × 1.5–9 cm, completely glabrous to densely puberulent below, usually puberulent at least along the principal veins, subsessile or petiolulate, up to 3 mm long.

Inflorescences in terminal raceme. Pedicels mostly about 4 cm long, the lower ones longer and curved upward giving a flattened apex appearance. Flowers with a spathe-like calyx, tapering to a recurved point that curves away from the corolla, 4–5.5 cm long, puberulent to hairy, sometimes with a few prominent longitudinal ridges. Corolla glabrous red-orange with a yellow margin, broadly campanulate above a cylindrical base, large, 8–15 × 4.5–7 cm at the mouth of the tube ; tube 6–8 cm long ; lobes 8.5–10 cm long. Stamens 4, subexsert, held against the base of the tube ; anther thecae divaricate, thin, 7–8 mm long. Pistil 6–7 cm long, ovary narrowly oblong, 4–5 mm long, 2–2.5 mm wide, very finely papillose ; ovules multiseriate in each locule. Disc cupular, 2–3 mm long, 4–5 mm wide.

Capsule narrowly oblong-elliptic, narrowed at the ends, 17–27 × 3.5–7 cm, dehiscent on each side ; valves navicular. Seeds about 1.5 cm long and 2 cm wide, thin, winged, wings broad and hyaline membranous, distinctly demarcated, completely surrounding the minute cordate seed body. - Pl. 9, p. 41.

## Type

Palisot de Beauvois s.n., Nigeria (non vu)

## Discussion

This species is somewhat variable, especially in the pubescence of the leaf and calyx. In East Africa, it has uniformly hairy calyces and densely puberulent leaves below, whereas in West Africa, the leaves are uniformly glabrous to glabrescent and the calyx puberulent with shorter hairs. These differences have been cited (for example DEGENER, Phytologia 28: 419, 1974) as sufficient to recognize two species. However, in geographically intermediate Cameroon, both extremes as well as intermediate cases occur, suggesting that the differences in pubescence are at best variable.

COMMON NAMES : Gabon tulip tree ; evong-evong (Fang) ; evong-vong ; ichiogo.
