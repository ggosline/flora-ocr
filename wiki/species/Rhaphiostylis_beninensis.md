# *Rhaphiostylis beninensis* (Hooker fil. ex Planchon)

**Genus**: [[Rhaphiostylis]]
**Family**: [[Icacinaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. Nigrit. : 259, tab. 28 (1849)

## Description

— Apodytes beninensis PLANCH., Hook. Ic. Pl. 8; tab. 178 (1848). — OLIVER, FTA 1:355 (1868) pro parte, excl., var. β. — TH. DURAND & SCHINZ, Acad. Roy. Belg. mém. 52, 4:91 (1896). — DE WILD. & TH. DURAND, Syll. : 98 (1909). — DE WILD., Bull. J. B. Brux. 15:153 (1914).

— R. heudelotii PLANCHON ex MIERS, Contrib. Bot. 1, tab. 6 (1851-1861).

— R. stuhlmannii Engl., Bot. Jahrb. 17 : 72 (1893).

— R. latifolia Pierré, Bull. Soc. Bot. Paris 2 : 1325 (1897).

— R. scandens ENGL., Bot. Jahrb. 43 : 183 (1897).

— R. zenkeri Engl., l. c., (1897), syn. nov.

Liane 10-12 m long with glabrous branchlets finely striated longitudinally. Petiole glabrous 3-6 mm long, canaliculate plicate above and transversely plicate below. Blade glabrous on both faces, dark brown on the upper face, pale brown or reddish on the lower face, narrowly elliptic, elliptic or lanceolate, 6-15 × 2-7.5 cm; base rounded or attenuate; apex acuminate obtuse or attenuate. Midvein concave above and ± prominent striated below. Secondary nerves 5-7 pairs, ascending, arcuate, anastomosing 3-6 mm from the blade margin, weakly prominent on the upper face, prominent on the lower face. Venules in lax network obscurely prominent above, prominent below.

Inflorescence in a multiflorous axillary fascicle. Numerous bracts at the base of the pedicels. Floral bud ± ellipsoid 7.5 × 1 mm, enlarged at the base. Pedicel glabrous 6-10 mm. Flower pentamerous. Sepals fused, glabrous except for the upper ciliolate margin, very broadly ovate, 0.75 × 1 mm, ± spreading. Petals valvate, free from each other, linear, ± enlarged at the base, glabrous, 7 × 1 mm, apex acute. Stamens 5 free 6 mm long; filament enlarged at the base bearing short villosity on its external face, progressively narrowing toward the apex; anthers with 2 ellipsoid locules. Pistil 5 mm long; ovary glabrous except at the apex, ellipsoid, 1.5 mm long; style asymmetric, 3.5 mm long, glabrous, often twisted, terminating in a globose stigma.

Drupe red when fresh, black when dry, 1.3 × 1-2 × 1 cm, ± reniform, reticulate, with a prominent rib on one face and two on the other, pedicellate. Endocarp woody. Seed strongly reniform, 7-10 × 8-15 × 3-5 mm, with apical embryo. PL. 8, 1-10", p. 35.

## Discussion

We were able to compare R. beninensis and R. zenkeri ENGL.; finding no differences between these two taxa, we recognize their synonymy; R. beninensis is the correct name.

Rhaphiostylis beninensis sometimes shows at the base of the blade, on the upper face, two small blackish spots corresponding to two glands. This character is not visible on all leaves but, when present, permits rapid determination of this species in the absence of flowers or fruits.

The specimen Breteler 5795, collected in Gabon, near Lambaréné, presents differences from other specimens of the species. The two basal secondary nerves are very ascending and sometimes extend up to 3/4 of the blade height. The acumen is long and very obtuse. The size of the floral buds does not permit easy examination of the various floral parts; however the pubescence of the ovary is that of R. beninensis.

This species occurs in the evergreen forests of upland and swampy areas, in riparian formations and in forest patches from Senegal to Tanzania in the East, to Angola in the South.
