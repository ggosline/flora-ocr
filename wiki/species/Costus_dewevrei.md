# *Costus dewevrei* De Wild. et Th. Dur.
**Family in Vol. 9**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Current placement**: Costaceae · **Genus**: [[Costus]]
## Description
Robust herb to 3 m with narrow-lanceolate leaves to 30 × 8 cm, long brown hairs beneath, and a thin hairy ligule to 4 cm. The cylindrical terminal spike has hairy bracts extended into leafy tips. Calyx hairy and splitting laterally; corolla 5 cm and labellum pink, c. 7 cm.
## Distribution
Former Belgian Congo and Gabon, in relatively light forest understorey. Type: Dewèvre s.n., Belgian Congo. Gabon material spans Nyanga, Haute-Ngounyé, Makokou, Mayombe and Massima near Etéké.
