# *Vahadenia laurentii* (De Wild.) Stapf Planche 120 ; Figure 14(C, D)

**Genus**: [[Vahadenia]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. trop. Afr. 4(1) : 30 (1902)

## Description

Fl. trop. Afr. 4(1): 30 (1902). Liane reaching 50 m in height; bark smooth, 2–3 mm thick; branchlets dark green, turning brown when fresh. Leaf elliptic to elliptic-obovate, sometimes as long as broad, 6–11 cm, cordate to cuneate at base, apiculate with an obtuse point, sometimes obtuse, truncate or even retuse at apex, petiole nil or reaching 7 mm, glossy, glabrous, with yellowish midvein; secondary veins 6 to 10 pairs clearly visible, dark and slightly reddish below. Inflorescence with 1 to peduncle 3.6–20 cm. Fragrant flower; pedicel 3–19 mm; calyx with ovate to obovate or broadly ovate to obovate lobes, 1–2 times as long as broad, 4–12 × 3–9 mm, apex rounded, truncate, sometimes emarginate or subacuminate, generally hyaline at margin; corolla white, reddish at base, tube 1–2 times as long as calyx lobes, 1–2 times as long as corolla lobes, 11–21.3 × 2–5 mm, enlarged to 3–8 mm at level of stamens, generally not constricted toward throat, lobes 1.2–2.5 times as long as tube, 1–2 as long as broad, 4–8 × 3–5 mm, apex rounded or sometimes slightly apiculate; stamens inserted 8.5–13.7 mm from base of tube, long 13–22 mm, apex at throat level or included up to 1 mm, filament 10–16 mm, anther long 3–5 mm, pistil long 24–34 mm, ovary cylindrical, narrowly retuse in style, pale green, glossy; style 6–11 mm, glabrous. Fruit globose, pyriform or obovoid, 3–10 × 3.5–10 cm, smooth, yellow to orange, sometimes with black spots, glabrous, with 10 to 40 seeds; wall 4–11 mm. Seed 11–18 × 8–12 × 6–8 mm; endosperm 3–4 mm thick. Distribution: Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Central African Republic and Angola, in Gabon all provinces except the Estuaire. Ecology: Primary and secondary forest in Gabon, up to 1400 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1 m. Use: The roasted fruits and seeds are edible.
