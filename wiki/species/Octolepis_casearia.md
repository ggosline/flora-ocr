## Description

— Pearson, F.T.A. 6 (1): 254 (1910). — Hutchinson et Dalziel, F.W.T.A., ed. 1 (1): 149 (1927). — Pellegrin, Flore du Mayombe, 2: 57 (1928). — Keay, F.W.T.A., 2nd ed. 1 (1): 174 (1954). — Walker et Sillans, Plantes utiles du Gabon: 414 (1961).

Shrub or small tree with erect branches, 2-5 m tall, with white or greyish bark, finely reticulate or longitudinally furrowed, often somewhat pustulous; young branches with very pronounced angles. Numerous fibres, closely packed, silvery at the fracture.

Leaves with petiole 8-12 mm, thick (2-3 mm), pubescent-greyish or very wrinkled. Blade quite variable in dimensions and texture, generally 15-20 cm long and 5 to 8 cm wide, reaching more than 25 cm by 3 to 9 cm, sometimes smaller, 10-12 cm by 2-4 cm, most often with greatest width at the level of the distal third and almost always in the distal half, broadly ovate-lanceolate and regularly attenuate-cuneate towards the base, rarely somewhat rounded or even subcordate, narrowed and more or less longly acuminate at the apex; apex often very acute; margins somewhat revolute. Blade somewhat pubescent-greyish or glabrous, with slightly discolorous faces, with veins well visible on both faces. Veins yellow or brown, fairly prominent on the lower face, the lateral ones 10-15 (sometimes 20) pairs, alternate or subopposite, at first fairly straight and making an angle of approximately 45° with the main vein, curved near the margin with anastomoses; tertiary network clearly visible forming a reticule with large meshes; quaternary network very fine, clearly visible in transparency.

Inflorescences pauciflorous, very short, axillary or situated on nodular excrescences of the leafless parts of the stems, silky pubescent. 1-10 flowers shortly pedicellate (on 2-4 mm when young, 5-8 mm at the time of fruiting). Floral bud globose, somewhat tetragonal. No differentiated tube; 4 sepals (rarely 5), joined only at the base, broadly triangular or triangular-lanceolate, 3-6 mm long, by 1-3 mm wide, more or less densely silky-pubescent externally, covered with shorter and felted pubescence internally. 4 (rarely 5) petals, all deeply bifid and forming inside the calyx a circle of 8 squamiform pieces, oval or lanceolate, lamellar, white, 2-3 mm, bristling at their apex with long stiff, whitish hairs. 8 (rarely 10) stamens, alternipetalous, with very fine and long filaments (up to 5 mm) somewhat pubescent-ciliate and widened at the base, very exserted; anthers small, globose-elongate. Ovary densely ciliate-hirsute, ovoid, with 3-5 locules, each bearing a pendulous ovule.

Fruit dry, capsular, dehiscent, with 2-4 valves, 10-15 mm long by 8-10 mm in diameter. Seeds oblong-attenuate, convex on the external face, flat and marked with a longitudinal depression on the internal face, and ornamented with a strophiole (arillate appendage) with the consistency of cork (when dry). Testa brown or black, smooth.
