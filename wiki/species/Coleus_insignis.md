# *Coleus insignis* (Hook.f.) A.J.Paton

**Genus**: [[Coleus]]
**Protologue**: Phytokeys 129 : 60 (2019)

## Synonyms

- Plectranthus insignis Hook.f., J. Proc. Linn. Soc., Bot. 7 : 210 (1864).

## Description

Robust herb or shrub, to 4 m tall; stem erect, glabrous to sparsely pubescent at base, becoming densely pubescent on the inflorescence axis, with non-glandular colored hairs. Leaf ascending, sessile, but lamina with base long and narrowly decurrent (resembling a narrowly winged petiole), falling before fruiting; lamina elliptic, 0.5–11.2 × 1.5–3.0 cm, acuminate at apex, crenate to dentate, with base long-decurrent, decurrent portion 5–15 mm long, often auriculate and clasping the stem, nearly

Inflorescence terminal, lax, with whorled cymes distant to 20 mm; cymes sessile, 3-flowered; bracts narrowly elliptic-ovate to ovate, reaching 5 mm long, entire. Flower: pedicel 4 mm; calyx 4–5 mm long and 11–20 mm in fruit, tubular, slightly curved upward, with sparse pubescence, with pale or brownish sessile glands, upper lip ovate, mucronate at apex, decurrent, lower lip with narrowly triangular lobes, divided to the base, long-subulate at apex, the median ones narrowly triangular slightly curved upward; corolla 12–20 mm long, bright yellow, sometimes reported as having a crimson or brownish throat, nearly glabrous but with pale sessile glands, tube sigmoid, 7–10 mm long, lower lip 6–10 mm long, deeply cucullate and enveloping the stamens; stamens with filaments connate at base. Fruit: achenes ovoid, slightly compressed, 1.5 mm long, brown, finely reticulate due to the pattern of epidermal cells.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea (Bioko), Gabon and Central African Republic; in Gabon: Woleu-Ntem. Conservation status: Considered vulnerable in Cameroon due to regression of forest habitat (Onana & Cheek, 2011). The species is not endemic to Cameroon, as these authors suggest, and should be evaluated across its entire distribution range. A Near Threatened (NT) category may be appropriate due to the decrease in extent and quality of habitat.

## Ecology

secondary vegetation; in Gabon at 600 m elevation, in Cameroon mainly at 700–1200 m, but up to 2500 m on the highlands of Bamenda.

## Source note

Similar to C. decurrens and often confused with it in herbaria. Unlike C. decurrens, the leaves of C. insignis fall after flowering and the species does not flower every year (every 7 or 9 years in Cameroon). The base of the leaf is wider and the fruiting calyx longer than in C. decurrens and the corolla bright yellow rather than orange-yellow. B: Morton (1963), Onana & Cheek (2011). Bibliography
