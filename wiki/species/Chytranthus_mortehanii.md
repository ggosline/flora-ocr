# *Chytranthus mortehanii* (De Wildeman) De Voldere ex Hauman

**Genus**: [[Chytranthus]]
**Family**: [[Sapindaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. Congo 9:358 (1960)

## Synonyms

- Pancovia mortehanii DE WILD., Bull. Jard. Bot. Etat Bruxelles 4 : 373 (1919); RADLK., in ENGL., Pflanzenreich, Sapindaceae : 809 (1932).
- Chytranthus fouilloyanus PELLEGRIN, Mém. Soc. Bot. France 102 : 73 (1955). Syn. nov.

## Description

Monocaulous shrub 2–5 m tall (25 m in Congo?) and 4–5 cm in diameter. Apex glabrous with leaves in a bundle. Leaf rachis 75 cm long, of which 31 cm below the first pair of leaflets, cylindrical, 5 mm in diameter, finely striate longitudinally when dry; 5–7 pairs of leaflets opposite or subopposite, with petiolules 5–12 mm; blade glabrous, discolorous when dry, olivaceous above, reddish-brown below, oblong obovate, with acute unequal base and abruptly acuminate apex, 15–40 × 7–14 cm; midvein glabrous in a slight depression on the upper surface; 14–18 pairs of secondary nerves in relief, as well as the fine reticulum, on both surfaces when dry.

Inflorescences cauline, distributed at all heights on the trunk or only between 0.50 and 1.70 m; slender racemes 9–24 cm long, with puberulent rachis bearing unequally spaced floriferous cymules approximately 2–8 mm apart and pedunculate for 1–2 mm; bracts are recaulescent; buds and flowers greenish with pedicel 2–4 mm; calyx puberulent, approximately 5 mm high with 5 lobes, one narrower; 4 elliptic petals, pubescent, with 2 superposed ligules, 5 × 2.5 mm; 7 (8) curved stamens of which two somewhat more robust, 10 mm long; filament hairy and anther glabrous; aborted pistil of the ♂ flower pubescent with 3 carpels, elongate with style arcuate in the ♂ flower; disk somewhat longer than wide, approximately 2 × 1.8 mm.

Infructescence observed with 1–3 fruits; they would be reddish in the immature state (Tisserant 513); smooth, green, fleshy succulent at maturity, triangular in section, 58 mm long, 60 mm wide; 3 locules each containing one brown seed 21–34 × 14–27 × 6.5–8 mm whose cotyledons appear to be fused. PL. 21, p. 97.

## Type

Mortehan 462, Dundusana, Zaïre (holo- BR)

## Discussion

In certain ombrophilous forests of Cameroon, Gabon, Central African Republic (Tisserant 432, 513, 553, Boukoko), and Congolese Mayumbe in Bas-Katanga. We know of no collections from the Brazzaville Region.

USE: The fruits are cited as edible in the Congo.
