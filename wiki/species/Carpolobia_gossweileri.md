# *Carpolobia gossweileri* (Exell) Petit Planche 21

**Genus**: [[Carpolobia]]
**Family**: [[Polygalaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. Congo Belge 7 : 281 (1958)

## Description

Tree or shrub attaining 30 m in height; branchlets sparsely puberulent, glabrescent or glabrous. Stipules generally absent or reduced to a dark glabrous glandular spot. Leaf: petiole (2–)3–4(–5) mm, glabrescent to glabrous; blade ovato-elliptic to obovate, (5–)7–14(–19) × (1.5–)2.5–6.5(–7.5) cm, papyraceous to finely coriaceous, rounded to cuneate at base, generally caudate-acuminate at apex, glabrous or with a few hairs on the midvein beneath, with (3–)5–7(–10) pairs of secondary veins. Inflorescences 1–4 per axil, up to 1.5(–5) cm in length, (2–)4–9(–50)-flowered; peduncle and rachis ± pubescent; bracts and bracteoles suborbicular to elliptic, concave, 1–1.5(–2) mm in length, pubescent at least on the margin, the basal ones subulate-oblong, up to 3(–5) mm in length, glabrous. Flower: pedicel (0.5–)1–3 mm, puberulent; sepals suborbicular to broadly ovato-elliptic, 1.5–5 × (1–)1.5–4 mm, the largest up to 2–2.5(–3) times larger than the smallest, ciliate, glabrous or with a few short hairs on both faces; petals subequal, narrowly obovate-elliptic, (4.5–)6–9(–13) × 1.5–2.5(–3) mm, glabrous externally except the margin and apex ciliate; stamens 5–7(–9) mm in length; staminodes 0–2. Fruit subglobose, up to 1.8 cm in diameter, orange at maturity, generally lobed, puberulent-tomentellous, the visible surface between the hairs. Seed ellipsoid-ovoid, ± flattened, 8–11 × 5–8 mm, covered with erect hairs, gray to yellowish brown.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon, Congo (Brazzaville), Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola; in Gabon: Woleu-Ntem, Estuaire, Nyanga and Ogooué-Lolo

## Ecology

primary or secondary evergreen forest, forest margins behind mangrove; at 0–650 m altitude. Bibl.: Breteler & Smissaert-Houwing (1977), Petit (1958). Bibliography
