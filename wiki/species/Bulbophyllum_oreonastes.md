# *Bulbophyllum oreonastes* Rchb.f.

**Genus**: [[Bulbophyllum]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Otia Bot. Hamb. 2 : 118 (1881)

## Description

Pseudobulbs 0.4–3.5 × 0.4–1.2 cm, suborbicular to narrowly ovoid or ellipsoidal, 2-leaved. Leaves shortly petiolate, 0.6–8.2 × 0.4–2 cm, broadly elliptic to linear-lanceolate, obtuse. Inflorescence rather lax, 1.5–17.5 cm, rachis glabrous, somewhat swollen, zigzag or not, 5–36-flowered. Flowers small, yellowish, yellow or shining orange, generally streaked with dark red. Sepals acute, thin and glabrous; dorsal sepal oblong-ovate; lateral sepals obliquely oblong-triangular, sometimes apiculate. Petals linear, obtuse, thin and glabrous. Lip yellowish, brownish to purple, oblong or elliptic, apex rounded and very thick, base somewhat concave, glabrous, recurved. – Pl. 90, p. 181.

## Distribution

From Guinea to Cameroon, Central African Republic, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Congo (Kinshasa)

## Ecology

alt. 160–750 m. Epiphyte, 2–10 m above ground, often collected on dead branch in lowland to montane forest, sometimes Brachystegia forest or thicket, sometimes lithophyte
