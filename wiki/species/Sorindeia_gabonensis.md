# *Sorindeia gabonensis* Bourobou & Breteler Planche 8, 11(B), 12(C)

**Genus**: [[Sorindeia]]
**Family**: [[Anacardiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Jard. Bot. Nat. Belg. 66 : 342 (1997)

## Description

Shrub or small tree reaching 3.5 m in height, sometimes somewhat lianescent; branchlets glabrous or sometimes sparsely puberulent when young. Leaf: leaflets 1 to 5, petiolule 4–10(–15) mm, with elliptic limb, (7–)15–20(–25) × (3–)4–7(–10) cm, cuneate or sometimes rounded at base, with acumen of (4–)7–20(–30) mm at apex, papyraceous to coriaceous, glabrous; midvein and 11 to 16(–18) pairs of lateral veins prominent on both surfaces, oblique vein fine, ± distinct below. Inflorescence axillary or pseudoterminal, narrow, up to 23 cm in length, puberulent; peduncle (2–)4–6(–8) cm. Male flower: pedicel 1–2 mm, basal part puberulent, upper part glabrous; calyx 1 mm in length, with deltoid lobes, 0.5 mm in length; petals valvate, narrowly elliptic, 2.5–3 × 1 mm, glabrous; stamens 6 to 10(–13), free or nearly so, 1.5–2.5 mm in length, glabrous, filaments 1 mm, anthers 0.7–1.5 mm in length. Female flower: pedicel 1.5–2.5 mm, basal part puberulent, upper part absent or very short and glabrous; calyx as in male flower, but 1.5–2 mm in length; corolla ± as in male flower; staminodes 5, ± 1.5 mm in length; pistil 2.5–3 mm in length, glabrous; style 1.5–2 mm, stigma 3-lobed. Infructescence pendulous, with fruits erect, orange-red at maturity, ovoid-ellipsoidal, 10–11 × 5–7 mm, glabrous.

## Distribution

endemic to Gabon, known from Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo and Woleu-Ntem
