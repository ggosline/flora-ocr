# *Laccosperma laeve* (G.Mann & H.Wendl.) G.Mann & H.Wendl. Planche 11(1–5)

**Genus**: [[Laccosperma]]
**Family**: [[Arecaceae]]
**Protologue**: Ancistrophyllum laeve (G.Mann & H.Wendl.) Drude, Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 21 : 111 (1895), nom. illeg

## Description

in De Kerchove de Denterghem, Palmiers : 35, 249 (1878). Ancistrophyllum laeve (G.Mann & H.Wendl.) Drude, Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 21 : 111 (1895), nom. illeg. Neoancistrophyllum laeve (G.Mann & H.Wendl.) Rauschert ex J.Dransf., Kew Bull. 37 : 456 (1982). Slender liane; stem up to 13 m, 8–15 mm in diameter. Leaf: sheath moderately to weakly armed with slightly spreading or recurved spines, caducous black pilose hairs present on mature sheaths; ochrea 8–20 cm long, armed like the sheath; petiole up to 18 cm, flattened above, rounded beneath, margins armed with spines of ± 1.3 cm, spreading or recurved; rachis 60–90 cm, distinctly trapezoidal in transverse section at the base, distally rounded in section, armed like the petiole, spines decreasing distally and regularly reflexed; limb with 10 to 12 pairs of leaflets, generally subequidistant towards the base and subopposite distally, sigmoid, (15–)20–30 × (3.5–)6–10 cm, margins lacking spines; cirrus up to 70 cm, armed like the rachis, with spine-like leaflets 2.5–2.8 cm long. Inflorescences 4 to 8 distally on each stem; peduncle ± 8 cm; prophyll ± 3.5 cm long; rachis with bracts 1.5–2 cm, narrowed to a short triangular lobe; flowering branches 8–12 cm, with imbricate bracts ± 3 mm long. Flower 6–8 × 2–3 mm; calyx 4–4.5 mm, tube 2.5–3 mm, lobes triangular, 2–2.5 × 2 mm; corolla with tube 0.8–1 mm long, lobes 6–7.5 × 2–2.5 mm, apex broadly acuminate, cream-white or pale; stamens with filament ± 3 mm, anther 3 × 1 mm; ovary ± 1 mm long; stigma up to 4 mm long. Fruit ovoid, 0.8–1 × 0.6–0.8 cm at maturity, with 14 to 18 rows of scales, monospermatous. Seed ovoid, 0.6–0.8 × 0.4–0.6 × 0.3 cm, flattened on one side, with a linear depression, smooth.

## Distribution

Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Angola (Cabinda); in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo and Ogooué-Maritime. Conservation status: Least Concern (LC)

## Ecology

understory of dense humid forests, coastal forests; in Gabon between 90 and 500 m elevation, elsewhere between 50 and 600 m

## Vernacular names

munyengi (éshira) ; ndele (fang) ; tèkè (mitsogo). B : Faye et al. (2014), Pajarón et al. (2015), Raponda-Walker & Sillans (1961), Sunderland i B liographie (1998, 2007, 2012)
