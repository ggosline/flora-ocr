# *Dracaena braunii* Engl. Planche 6, Figure 2(A)

**Genus**: [[Dracaena]]
**Family**: [[Dracaenaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 15 : 479 (1892)

## Description

Pleomele braunii (Engl.) N.E.Br., Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1914 : 277 (1914) . D. litoralis Mwachala & Eb.Fisch., Novon 22(4) : 444 (2013). Stoloniferous herb 10–30 cm tall, forming broad dense mats, sometimes forming small shrubs up to 50 cm tall; roots bright orange-red; stem short, simple, if a shrub then stem ± ascending, grey. Leaves equitant; sheath embracing the stem over clearly more than the circumference, sheathing the stem over a length of > 1 cm; pseudopetiole variable, absent, short or long, even on the same plant, green, channelled to grooved; blade narrowly ovate, up to 15 × 4 cm, base cuneate, gradually narrowed to acuminate to caudate apex, with mucro of ± 1 cm, smooth, papyraceous-coriaceous, upper surface dark green to bright pale green, lower surface paler and dull. Inflorescence erect, in spiciform thyrse, up to 30 cm long, axis smooth, green becoming violet towards the apex, with flowers aggregated on the distal 5–7 cm, of 2 to 4 bracts rapidly caducous, narrowly ovate-ligulate, up to 50 × 4 mm, green with violet base; fascicles with 1 to 3 flowers, each subtended by narrowly triangular bracts, ± 1 mm long, scarious and rapidly caducous. Flower: pedicel 1–2 mm; perianth 12–19 mm long, white to cream, with lobes longer than the tube, reflexed, tinged pink-violet; stamens inserted near the throat, as long as the lobes, filament filiform, white, anther 0.8 mm long, cream; ovary ovoid, up to 2 ×

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, in Estuaire, Nyanga and Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology

Edges of coastal forests and mangrove-to-savanna transition, in shade, lateritic to sandy soils, also on rocks, tolerating salt spray; in Gabon at 0–20 m altitude, elsewhere at 0–70 m. Flowering recorded from January to April, June, October and December. Use: Plants are browsed by the forest buffalo (Syncerus caffer nanus). B: Baker (1898), Damen et al.
