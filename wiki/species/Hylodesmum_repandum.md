# *Hylodesmum repandum* (Vahl) H.Ohashi & R.R.Mill Planche 68

**Genus**: [[Hylodesmum]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Edinb. J. Bot. 57 : 185 (2000)

## Synonyms

- Desmodium repandum (Vahl) DC., Prodr. 2 : 334 (1825).

## Description

Podocarpium repandum (Vahl) Y.C.Yang & P.H.Huang, Bull. Bot. Lab. N.E. Forest. Inst., Harbin 4 : 13 (1979). Trailing herb becoming subshrub, reaching 1 m in height; stem with small uncinate hairs and long straight hairs in variable density. Stipules persistent, oval-triangular, 10–15(–20) mm long, brown. Leaf: leaflets 3, with oval to rhomboidal lamina, 4–11 × 3–8 cm, the lateral ones smaller than the terminal one, with margins slightly sinuate, upper surface pubescent with straight, setaceous, scattered hairs, lower surface with very small glandular hairs and deciduous uncinate hairs. Inflorescence in pseudoracemes 15–30 cm long, very lax, sometimes branched, with flowers in pairs; bracts deciduous, narrowly oval, 5–10 mm long. Flower: pedicel 1.5–3 cm, slender; calyx 3–4.5 mm long, puberulent, lobes narrowly oval-triangular; corolla bright orange, standard 8–13 mm long, keel exceeding the other petals. Legume stipitate, 1.5–4.5 cm long, dorsal suture straight to slightly undulate, ventral suture deeply incised, isthmus very narrow, ± 0.5 mm wide; articles (1–)2 to 5, ± semicircular, 5.5–8 × 2–3 mm, with uncinate hairs. Seed reniform, 5–8 × 2.5–4 mm, brown.

## Distribution

very widespread; from Sierra Leone to Ethiopia, toward southern Africa and Madagascar, in Asia, in Yemen, in India, in Bhutan, in China and in Malesia; not yet recorded from Gabon, but present in Cameroon, in Democratic Republic of the Congo and in Equatorial Guinea (Bioko)

## Ecology

humid and shaded forest; at 0–3000 m altitude. b: De la Estrella et al. (2010), Léonard (1954b), Lewis et al. (2005), Ohashi & Mill (2000), Bibliography Pellegrin (1949), Verdcourt (1971d, 2000)
