# *Lasiodiscus fasciculiflorus* Engl.

**Family**: [[Rhamnaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Lasiodiscus]]

## Description

Shrub or small tree distinguished by flowers densely fascicled on short axillary axes. Leaves alternate, toothed and pinnately veined. Flowers small, 5-merous, with hooded petals and a hairy disc; fruit drupaceous.

## Distribution

Cameroon and Gabon.
