# *Chytranthus imenoensis* Pellegrin

**Genus**: [[Chytranthus]]
**Family**: [[Sapindaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Bot. France 102 : 226 (1955); Mém. Soc. Bot. France : 71 (1955)

## Description

The type and only known material of this species is a collection by Le Testu (no. 8606, Iméno-Poubi, 90 km WSW Lastoursville, 21 Dec. 1930); it consists of leaves and detached inflorescences for which there is a slight possibility of doubt as to the homogeneity of the collection. On one hand the inflorescences and buds are very close to Ch. macrobotrys with very weak differences, on the other hand the leaves are very different from those of this species. These are therefore the distinctive characters that we will content ourselves with highlighting pending other collections.

Leaves with thin blade, with fine golden-yellow hairs on both surfaces and veins; midvein clearly pubescent above; petiole pubescent, short, only 3-4 mm; leafy rachis densely pubescent golden-rust with erect hairs, 0.8-1.5 mm long; 7 pairs of subopposite leaflets.

Bud 6.5 mm in diameter, ♂ with 5 sepals, 4 petals, 11-12 stamens, 7 carpels; appendages of petals absent or reduced not exceeding the apex of the ligule; anther with connective slightly swollen and pustulous in appearance on the margins, with surface texture less finely papillose than in Ch. macrobotrys but showing in section the same palisade anatomical structure; ovary entirely aborted, glabrous, very weakly raised on the receptacle.
