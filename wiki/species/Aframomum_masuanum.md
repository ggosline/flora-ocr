# *Aframomum masuanum* (De Wild. et Th. Dur.) K. Schum.
**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Aframomum]]
## Description
Herb 1–1.5 m; glabrous narrow-lanceolate blades to 30 × 6 cm and thin bifid ligule 1–1.5 cm. Few-flowered head on a robust peduncle to 40 cm with large bifid mucronate bracts. Flowers mauve.
