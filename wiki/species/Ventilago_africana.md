# *Ventilago africana* Exell

**Family**: [[Rhamnaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Ventilago]]

## Description

Woody climber with alternate petiolate leaves, blades elliptic to oblong, finely toothed, and small flowers in axillary or terminal spike-like panicles. Flowers 5-merous with a conspicuous disc and partly inferior ovary. Fruit a samara about 6 cm long, the basal seed-bearing body extended into a long apical wing.

## Distribution

Western Central Africa, including Gabon; treatment material includes Lastoursville-region and other Gabon collections.
