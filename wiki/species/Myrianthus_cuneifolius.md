# *Myrianthus cuneifolius* (Engler) Engler

**Genus**: [[Myrianthus]]
**Family**: [[Moraceae]]
**Protologue**: Mon Afr. Pfl. 1, Moraceae: 40 (1898); RENDLE in HUTCH. & RENDLE, FTA 6 (2): 236 (1917); DE RUITER, BJBB 46: 494 (1976)

## Description

RENDLE in HUTCH. & RENDLE, FTA 6 (2): 236 (1917); DE RUITER, BJBB 46: 494 (1976).

- M. serratus (Trécul) Benth. & Hook. var. cuneifolius Engl., Bot. Jahrb. 20: 150 (1894).

Shrubs or small trees reaching 2 (-3.5) m in height; leafy branchlets thick 0.4-0.6 cm; internodes 2-6 cm long; bark of leafy branchlets brown-red (when dry), made scabrous by the presence of stiff, recurved hairs, 0.2-0.5 mm long. Blade simple and entire to compound with 5 sessile leaflets, coriaceous to chartaceous, reaching 35 × 40 cm in compound leaves, elliptic to oblanceolate in simple leaves, reaching 30 × 8 cm, acute to acuminate; base truncate; margin subentire to serrate; upper face glabrous, except for some hairs on the main nerves; lower face with stiff recurved hairs on the main nerves; areoles arachnoid-tomentose; nerves prominent beneath; 12-18 pairs of secondary nerves. Petioles (0.5-) 2.5-24 cm long, sulcate and ribbed, brown-red, covered with stiff recurved hairs. Stipules subpersistent, 1-1.4 cm long, with appressed hairs, grey to greyish-yellow.

Male inflorescences (except the peduncle) 2-7 × 2-5 cm; primary axes 2-5, each branched 4 to 6 times; peduncle 0.5-3 cm long, 1 mm thick, with short erect, dense hairs, grey to greyish-yellow. Flowers crowded on the distal axes or those of lower order, up to 1.5-3.5 cm from the tips of the distal axes. Perianth 0.6-1.2 mm high, with cucullate tepals with a thickened apex, ciliolate. Stamens 0.9-1.4 mm long, with filaments often completely fused, and anthers 0.3-0.5 (-0.6) mm long. Interfloral bracts 0.4-1 mm long.

Female inflorescences 1-2 cm in diameter, subsessile or shortly pedunculate (up to 1 cm in length). Flowers 25-40; perianth ± longly conical, 6-7 mm in length and 2-3 mm in diameter; stigma 2-2.5 mm long. Interfloral bracts deltoid, covered, like the perianth, with appressed, firm hairs, brown-red to yellowish.

Infructescences reaching 3 (or more ?) cm in diameter; perianths few in the fruit (3-5), ovoid; pyrene 1.1 × 1 cm, smooth. - Pl. 81, p. 259.

## Type

Soyaux 381, Gabon (holo-, P ; iso-, K)

## Discussion

This species, which proves to be very close to M. serratus, is virtually endemic to Gabon. Most collections come from the NW of that country.
