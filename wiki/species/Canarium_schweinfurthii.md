# *Canarium schweinfurthii* Engl.

**Family**: [[Burseraceae]] · **Genus**: [[Canarium]]

## Description

Large tree to 35 m and over 1.5 m diameter. Leaves clustered at thick branch ends, with 8–15 pairs of leaflets; rachis to 45 cm, broadened and subwinged at base, bearing scars of a caducous pair of lamellar reduced leaflets. Leaflets variable, oblong to lanceolate, 18 × 6 cm, base rounded to cordate, with 15–25 pairs of veins. Dioecious; panicles to 20 cm. Flowers greenish, 3-merous; petals 10–11 mm; six stamens united on a stipitate cup-shaped disc. Fruit violet, ellipsoid, 3–4 cm, with a very thick three-locular stone and one seed.

## Distribution and ecology

Widespread from Guinea-Bissau to Ethiopia, Tanzania and Angola. A light-demanding secondary-forest tree, deciduous or semi-deciduous in the dry season. Aromatic resin is used for torches; pale light timber is an okoumé substitute.
