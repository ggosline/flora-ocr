# *Orthopichonia cirrhosa* (Radlk.) H.Huber Planche 85(1-4)

**Genus**: [[Orthopichonia]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull. 15 : 437 (1962)

## Description

Liane reaching 35 m in height. Leaf: petiole 4–14 mm, blade 23–48 × 10–25 cm, oblong to obovate ± obovate, cuneate at base, with acuminate apex 6–17 mm, glabrous on both faces; secondary nerves 21 to 44 pairs, alternating with less visible nerves. Inflorescence axillary, often apparently terminal, solitary or in pairs, rather lax to compact, reaching 3.5 cm in length, with flowers sometimes terminal then in the form of a tendril; peduncle of the axillary inflorescence reaching 8 mm, that of the terminal one up to 5 mm. Flower: pedicel 2–4 mm; calyx with lobes 1–2 times as long as broad, 0.7–1.2 × 0.4–0.8 mm; corolla 13.5–19 mm long in mature bud, forming an oblong and rounded head of 0.26–0.37 of bud length, with white lobes, sometimes pink, with reddish or yellowish-green tube with pale green base, exterior glabrous or sometimes puberulent on a large part of tube, tube 9–15 mm long, of maximum width 1.2–1.8 mm at 2.5–3.8 mm above base, then contracted just above anthers, interior with a pilose band 0.3–0.6 mm wide at 0.4–0.8 mm above base of tube, throat glabrous or nearly so; lobes oblong to elliptic, 1–2 times as long as tube, 1–2 times as long as broad, 3.2–7.4 × 1.5–3 mm, rounded at apex, glabrous on exterior, ± ciliate, more so at base, interior glabrous or finely pilose at base; stamens inserted 1.5–2.5 mm from base of tube, anther 0.7–0.9 mm long; pistil 1.8–2.9 mm long, with head situated 1–2.4 mm below apex of anthers, ovary glabrous. Fruit pyriform to subglobulous, 24–46 × 18–35 mm, attenuate at base, truncate at apex, verrucose. Seed irregularly ellipsoid to reniform, 6–10 mm long.

Distribution: Liberia, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Congo below, and Angola. Cabinda in Gabon. Estuary, Moyen-Ogooué or Ngounié, Nyanga Ogooué, Ivindo Ogooué, Maritime and Woleu-Ntem.

Ecology: Primary and secondary forest in Gabon, up to 650 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1000 m.

Notes: In the revision by Vonk it is indicated incorrectly that the type specimen Soyaux 183, came from Gabon: it was collected in Angola (Cabinda).

B: Beentje (2021), Vonk (1989).
