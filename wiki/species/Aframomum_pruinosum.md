# *Aframomum pruinosum* Gagnep.
**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Aframomum]]
## Description
Slender pendent leafy shoots to 5 m, sheaths and stems white-pruinose. Strongly asymmetric blades 30–50 × 4–10 cm. Inflorescence axis 50–100 cm with up to about twelve one-flowered lateral spikes. Mauve flower with 6–7 × 5.5 cm labellum; fruit 70 × 12 mm.
