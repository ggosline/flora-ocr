# *Costus afer* Ker Gawl.
**Family in Vol. 9**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Current placement**: Costaceae · **Genus**: [[Costus]]
## Description
Robust herb 2–3 m tall with glabrous lanceolate blades to at least 20 × 6 cm. A raised line, not a complete ciliate collar, marks the short ligule. The terminal globose spike has green, red-tinged bracts, each subtending two flowers. Corolla white; 5–6 cm labellum white, pink-violet edged with a yellow median band. Capsules 1–1.2 cm. Separate basal flowering shoots occur exceptionally.
## Distribution
Common in forest understorey across tropical Africa. Type from Sierra Leone. Gabon collections cited from Libreville, Mitzic and unlocalized material.
