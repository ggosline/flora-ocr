# *Impatiens irvingii* Hook. f. ex Oliv.

**Family**: [[Balsaminaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Impatiens]]

## Description

Common wet-place herb, often growing with its base in water. Flowers axillary, rose to carmine; petiole bears appendages and leaf base is very acute. Lip shallow, white and cup-shaped, with a long narrowly tapered spur; lateral-petal lobes equally rounded and resembling the standard.

## Distribution

The broadest-ranging Gabon balsam, from Guinea across Lower Guinea and the Congo region to Uganda.

## Vernacular names

*Mbongo* (Kota).
