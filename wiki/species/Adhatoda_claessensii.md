# *Adhatoda claessensii* (De Wild.) Heine, comb. nov.

**Genus**: [[Adhatoda]]
**Family**: [[Acanthaceae]]

## Description

≡ Justicia Claessensii De Wild., Bull. Jard. Bot. Etat Brux. 5 : 11 (1916).

≡ Duvernoya Claessensii De Wild., l. c. (1916), in syn., nom. illeg.

Liane, weak shrub or suffrutescent plant; stems cylindrical, glabrous or somewhat pubescent; indumentum, especially in the inflorescence, brownish or ferruginous. Petioles 1-6 cm, glabrescent or, when young, covered with the same indumentum. Leaf blade ovate, 6.5-23 × 4-11 cm, ± oblong or elliptic, acuminate, acumen obtuse, up to 2 cm long; base rounded or subcordate. 6-8 pairs of lateral veins, sparsely and lightly pilose beneath.

Inflorescences axillary or terminal, simple or sometimes with a branch at the base; spikes 8-13 × 6 cm with flowers in full bloom. Flowers axillary, solitary or fasciculate, pedicellate or subsessile; pedicel reaching 4 mm at maximum; bracts foliaceous at the base of the inflorescence; upper bracts lanceolate, acuminate 12-25 mm approximately; bracteoles lanceolate, 4-6 × 1 mm. Calyx up to 14 mm long, with ± free sepals acuminate, lanceolate, 10-12 × 1-4 mm, villous and ciliate. Corolla whitish inside, violet outside and veined with purple, 3.5 cm long, somewhat villous outside. Corolla tube 11-12 × 5-8 mm; upper lip erect 22-25 mm long, bilobed at apex, with rounded lobes, 2-3 mm long; lower lip trilobed, 20 × 21-22 mm, with central lobe approximately 10 × 4 mm, obtuse; filaments 2 cm, glabrous; anther thecae somewhat divergent, 4-5 mm long, obtuse. Ovary 4 mm high, densely villous, indumentum golden or yellowish sulphur; style approximately 20 mm; stigma slightly bilobed.

Capsule 30 × 7 mm, finely pubescent, 4-seeded, seeds glabrous, yellowish, 6 × 4-5 mm.

## Type

F. Claessens 643, Yambata, Congo 1910 (BR. holotype!)

## Discussion

Congo, Upper Sangha, Gabon.
