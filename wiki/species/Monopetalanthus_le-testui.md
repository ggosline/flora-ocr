# *Monopetalanthus le-* Testui Pellegrin

**Genus**: [[Monopetalanthus]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: F. Pellegrin, Bull. Soc. Bot. Fr. 89 : 120 (1942); Lég. Gabon : 75 (1948); de Saint Aubin, For. Gabon : 53 (1963)

## Description

de Saint Aubin, For. Gabon : 53 (1963). — Letouzey et Mouranche, Ekop Cam. : 46 et pl. 10 (1952).

Trees. Leaves paripinnate, rachis pubescent 10–15 cm long; 17–20 pairs of leaflets somewhat falciform, 20–25 mm long, 5–6 mm wide, linear-oblong, with base truncate parallel to the rachis, the posterior lobe being prolonged by a distinct auricle, apex rounded, subemarginate, the anterior lobe exceeding the posterior lobe, glabrous except for a few fine cilia on the margins. Midvein primary, secondary veins pinnate. On each node of the branches trace of a very enveloping scar left by the fall of the bud scales.

Inflorescences in terminal panicles, 5–6 cm long, formed first of scaly glomérules, rusty-pubescent, composed of imbricate bracts. Flowers sessile. Bracteoles oblong, pubescent, 5 × 4 mm. Receptacle shallow, 2 mm deep. Calyx reduced to 2 very small pubescent teeth, on either side of the sole petal. Petal obovate, emarginate at the apex, shortly unguiculate. Stamens 8 with filaments briefly connate at the base. Ovary subsessile, hirsute.

Pod flat terminated by a point. The sutural edge is prolonged on each side by a prominent crest. The surface is marked by a strong longitudinal vein. 1–2 seeds approximately 2 cm in diameter.

## Vernacular names

Mayo

Andoung, Eko-andoung, Evel-andoung (Fang); Mayo (nom pilote d'origine camerounaise)

## Type

Le Testu 9314, Gabon (P)

## Discussion

Large tree capable of attaining large diameters. Crown powerful umbellate, with light canopy. Trunk cylindrical and elevated furnished at the base by a buttress formed by the junction of the large roots which can extend on the ground fairly far from the bole. Bark smooth, approximately 1 cm thick. Fracture hard, fibrous, pale ochre in colour. Wood light rose-coppery, tender and light.

This Andoung appears distributed throughout Gabon, it is particularly abundant in the Cristal Mountains. It grows in groups of a few trees in mixture with other Andoungs. Also in Cameroon and Spanish Guinea.
