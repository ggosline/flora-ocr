# *Sphenoclea zeylanica* Gaertn.

**Genus**: [[Sphenoclea]]
**Family**: [[Sphenocleaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fruct. 1 : 113, t. 24, fig. 5 (1788)

## Description

Herb reaching 1.5 m in height; roots numerous and long; stem fistulose, often much branched. Leaves with petiole reaching 1.5(–2) cm in length; blade lanceolate to narrowly ovate or elliptic, 2.0–12.5 × 0.4–5.0 cm, attenuate at base, acute or subacute at apex. Spikes cylindric, reaching 12 cm in length and 1 cm in diameter, bearing only a few flowers open at any one time; bracts and bracteoles oblanceolate-spatulate, embracing the flower but spreading during anthesis. Flowers angular from compression; calyx with lobes 1–1.5 mm in length; corolla 2–3 mm in length, white to whitish-green, with ovate-triangular lobes; anthers approximately 0.5 mm in length; ovary obovoid, 1.5–2 mm in length. Capsules 4–5 mm in diameter. Seeds oblong, ± 0.5 mm in length, yellowish-brown.

## Distribution

Species with wide distribution, almost pantropical, but probably introduced to Asia and America

## Ecology

Species of humid places such as ponds, marshes, river banks and tidal creeks, at 0–1500 m altitude; in Gabon collected only once, but probably fairly common. Taxonomic notes: The West African species, S. dalzielii N.E.Br., is distinguished from S. zeylanica by the sessile, smaller leaves rounded at the apex, the shorter and oblong spikes, and the pink corolla. B: Airy Shaw (1968), Hepper (1963a), Thulin (1973, 1983). IBLIOGRAPHY
