# *Renalmia cabraei* De Wild. et Th. Dur.

**Genus**: [[Renealmia]]
**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]]

## Synonyms

- R. erythroneura Gagnep., Bull. Soc. Bot. Fr. 4° sér. 2 : 31 (1902).

## Description

— Bull. Soc. Bot. Belg. 38: 145 (1899).

Slender herb, less than 1 m tall. Leaves with petiole 1 to 4 cm, blade narrowly lanceolate, attenuate at both ends, glabrous, 25–35 × 4–5 cm.

Panicle arising directly from the rhizome, sometimes appearing at the tip of the leafy stem (?), very lax, up to 25 cm long, on a peduncle 60 to 70 cm and bearing generally 3 leafless sheaths distant from one another. Bracts spaced at least 6 cm apart from one another, at least towards the base of the axis, numbering approximately 8, lanceolate, 1 to 2 cm long, eventually spreading away from the axis and the flowers. Secondary inflorescences with 2 or 3 flowers, shortly pedicelled. Flowers with tridentate calyx, corolla and labellum white, capsular fruit approximately 2 × 1.2 cm.

## Type

Cabra, Bas-Congo, s. n. (BR)

## Discussion

This species appears very close to R. congoensis and more abundant material would be necessary to verify whether there is good reason to separate these two species.

Plants of the understory, known from Cameroon and Congo.

Probable presence in Gabon.
