# *Renealmia cincinnati* (K. Schum.) Bak.

**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Renealmia]]

## Description

Leafy shoots 1 m or taller. Petiole 4–7 cm; narrow glabrous blade 15–40 × 4–7 cm, violet beneath. Loose panicle to 1 m, about half peduncle; green bracts subtend 4–6 or more flowers. Flowers white, corolla 16 mm, labellum 9–10 mm. Red dehiscent fruit 12–13 × 5–6 mm, with about twenty pale-brown seeds and a yellow laciniate aril.

## Distribution

Humid forest understorey from Côte d’Ivoire to Cameroon and Gabon, probably Congo.
