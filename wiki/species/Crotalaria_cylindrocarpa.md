# *Crotalaria cylindrocarpa* DC.

**Genus**: [[Crotalaria]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Prodr. 2 : 133 (1825)

## Description

Perennial herb, 0.4–0.6 m tall; stems several, their base suffrutescent, striated, branched from the base. Stipules falciform, 4–10 mm long, with acute apex. Leaf: petiole 1–5 cm; leaflets 3, with lamina narrowly elliptic-obovate to elliptic-oblong or obovate-oblong, 2–7 × 0.4–2.3 cm, acuminate and mucronate at apex, pale green, glabrous above except on the nerves, scarcely pubescent below. Inflorescence in generally terminal racemes; racemes 4–23 cm long; bracts caducous, filiform, 2–5(–8) mm long. Flower: bracteoles situated at the base of the calyx; pedicel ± 3 mm; calyx 6–8(–10) mm long, ± rounded at the base, not reflexed against the fruiting pedicel, appressed-puberulent, with triangular-subulate lobes, longer than the tube; standard ovate, 10–15 mm long, yellow, striped with brown-red or orange, almost glabrous, wings exceeding the keel, keel 10–15 mm long, geniculate-erect, with a straight beak, upper margin woolly. Pod with stipe of 1–4 mm, cylindric, 2.3–4 × 0.8–1 cm, appressed-puberulent, with 18 to 26 seeds. Seed oblong-reniform, ± 4 mm long, surface with minute granules, pale yellow to brown; strophiole minute.

## Distribution

on the periphery of the Guineo-Congolian forest; in Gabon, known only from Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology

estuaries, grasslands and savannas, ruderal stations, persisting as a weed; under abundant rainfall and in pyrophytic situations; in Gabon at 120 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1200 m. Flowers and fruits in September. Use: Ameliorating plant, used as green manure

## Vernacular names

Gambia pea (angl.). b : Burkill (1995), Pellegrin (1949), Polhill (1982, 2003), Raponda-Walker & Sillans (1961), ibliographie Wilczek (1953)
