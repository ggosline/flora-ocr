# *Rhaphiostylis fusca* (Pierre) Pierre

**Genus**: [[Rhaphiostylis]]
**Family**: [[Icacinaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Linn. Paris 2 : 1324 (1897)

## Synonyms

- Chelonocarya fusca Pierre, 1. c. : 1261 (1897).

## Description

Liane with glabrous adult branchlets and puberulent ramilles with stellate hairs, obscurely longitudinally striated. Petiole 3–5 mm long, glabrous, canaliculate, wrinkled above, very strongly wrinkled below. Blade glabrous on both faces, dark brown on the upper face, dark brown on the lower face, narrowly oblong to narrowly elliptic, 6–11 × 1.5–4 cm; base rounded to subcuneiform; apex acuminate-acute. Midvein concave above, salient pubescent below. Secondary veins 4–6 pairs, ascending, arched, anastomosing 1.5–2.5 mm from the blade margin, concave on the upper face, salient on the lower face. Veinlets in loose reticulation, obscurely salient on the lower face.

Inflorescence in multifloral axillary fascicles. Floral bud ellipsoid, 10 × 1.5 mm, with valvate petals and ± erect sepals. Flower white.

Pedicel densely pubescent to tomentose with stellate hairs, 3 mm long, bearing bracteoles. Sepals connate, pubescent on the outer face and glabrous on the inner face, broadly elliptic, 6 × 1 mm, spreading. Petals free, pubescent on the outer face, glabrous on the inner face, linear or narrowly elliptic, 6 × 1 mm. Stamens 5, free, gynobasic, 6 mm long; filament broadly oblong, 0.8 mm long, abruptly narrowing at the apex; anther with 2 oblong cells; connective mucronate at the apex. Pistil 6 mm long; ovary ellipsoid pubescent; style glabrous with globose stigma.

Drupe reniform glabrous flattened with strong ornamentations of ribs and points, 2.7 × 2.2 × 1.5 cm. Endocarp woody. Seed reniform with apical embryo. PL. 9, 1–11, p. 39.

## Type

Klaine 158, Gabon (P!)

## Discussion

This species is encountered in forests, from Gabon to Kasaï.
