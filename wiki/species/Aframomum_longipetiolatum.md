# *Aframomum longipetiolatum* J. Koech.
**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Aframomum]]
## Description
Herb 2–3 m, distinctive in its c. 4 cm petiole and inflorescence peduncle to 1 m. Blades c. 25 × 5.5 cm; few 1–4-flowered spikes cluster near the peduncle apex. Flowers unknown; smooth red fruit 7 cm, topped by persistent 4 cm calyx. Known from Etéké, Gabon.
