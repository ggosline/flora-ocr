# *Philoxerus vermicularis* (L.) P. Beauv.

**Family**: [[Amaranthaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Philoxerus]]

## Description

Fleshy, much-branched seashore herb with opposite narrow leaves and compact white flower heads. Five scarious tepals surround a staminal cup; the one-locular ovary has a single ovule and matures into a dry indehiscent one-seeded fruit.
