# *Strychnos fallax* Leeuwenberg

**Genus**: [[Strychnos]]
**Family**: [[Loganiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Meded. Landbouwh. 69 (1) : 117, fig. 17, carte 17 (1969)

## Description

Liane; stem 3-10 cm in diameter; branches dark brown, lenticellate, not grooved when dry; branchlets glabrous, lenticellate when bearing developed leaves, cylindrical or nearly so, often somewhat grooved when dry, tendrils in pairs.

Leaves with short petiole, glabrous, 1-4 mm long; blade thin-coriaceous, elliptic, 3-11 × 1.5-5 cm, acuminate at apex, cuneate or rounded at base, glabrous on both faces; one pair of secondary nerves curved along the margin; midvein and often also main secondary nerves impressed above; tertiary venation reticulate, prominent below and generally also above.

Inflorescences terminal, rather lax, 3-4 × 2-3 cm, reaching up to 9 × 9 cm during fruiting: peduncle, branches and pedicels glabrous or with fine pubescence. Flowers 5-merous. Sepals united to one-third their height, ovate to triangular, 1 × 0.8 mm, acute, finely ciliate, with short pubescence outside, glabrous inside. Corolla, in the mature bud, 3 mm long, white (?), with fine pubescence outside, inside furnished with a pilose ring at the throat; tube short, 1 mm long; lobes twice as long as the tube, 2 × 1.2 mm, acute, spread. Stamens slightly exserted, inserted at the throat of the corolla, with glabrous filament, 0.5 times as long as the anther; anther oblong, bearded at the base. Pistil pilose, 1.5 mm long; ovary ovoid, 1 × 0.8 mm, 2-locular; style 0.5 mm long; stigma capitate; 5-6 ovules per locule.

Fruits orange or yellowish-orange, small, with smooth skin, tender, ovoid, laterally compressed, 19-25 × 18-23 × 14-16 mm, containing 2-3 seeds; wall thin, when dried 0.3 mm thick. Seeds flattened, ± plano-convex, obliquely ovate or elliptic, often irregularly curved, 12-15 × 10-12 × 4-5 mm, acute at one end, with very short thick erect hairs, rather rugose; testa chartaceous, not adhering to the pulp. Pl 31, 1-8, p. 138.

## Type

Koechlin 5507, Région de Pointe Noire, Congo-Brazzaville (holotype : WAG, isotype : IRSC)

## Discussion

Gabon, Congos. Humid forests (Gilbertiodendron); often on riverbanks or in forest galleries. Altitude: 0-1,000 m.

## Specimens examined

Hallé N. 4147, Bélinga (fr. non murs, juin) P, WAG.
