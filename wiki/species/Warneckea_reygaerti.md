# *Warneckea reygaerti* (de Wildeman) Jacques-Félix

**Genus**: [[Warneckea]]
**Family**: [[Melastomataceae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia, sér. 2, 18 (2) : 234 (1978); Fl. Cam. 24 : 170 (1983)

## Description

Adansonia, sér. 2, 18 (2): 234 (1978); Fl. Cam. 24: 170 (1983). — Memecylon reygaertii DE WILD., Bull. Jard. Bot., Brux. 4: 425 (1914).

Shrub 1.50 m tall; branches robust, subangular then rounded. Leaves large, glossy on the upper surface; petiole 3–5 mm; blade broadly elliptic acuminate, 5–8 × 10–17 cm, broadly cuneate at the base, acumen approximately 1 cm long; veins impressed above, raised below; the principal convergent suprabasal veins not sinuate; the additional veins well visible below, sinuate 1 mm from the margin; approximately 10 principal transverse veins very prominent below, often divided and supplemented by reticulate intercalary veins, relatively dense, finely raised on both surfaces.

Axillary cymes, branched but compact and globose, 1.5 cm long; bracts triangular, 1 mm long, tardily caducous. Flowers with pedicel 2–4 mm; hypantho-calyx cupulo-patellar, 3 × 2 mm; calyx lobes triangular to semi-ovate. Petals transversely elliptic, 3.5 × 2.5 mm. Stamens with anther 1.8–2 × 1 mm, thecae convex; connective conical, saddled by a gland occupying more than half the length; filament 2 mm. Epigynous chamber spreading, thick-walled; style 4 mm. Ovary 6-ovulate.

Fruit globose, 10–15 mm in diameter.

## Type

Reygaert 1311, Zaïre (holo-, BR!)

## Discussion

Distinguished by its compact cymes of relatively large flowers and its globose fruits. Sterile specimens may be confused with W. membranifolia, but the leaves are generally larger, smooth (without sclerites), the branches thicker and early rounded. Species of the forests of Cameroon, Zaïre, Gabon and Cabinda.

## Specimens examined

Le Testu 7213 (avr.); 8538 (nov.), Lastoursville.

Espèces a rechercher

Trois espèces, dont les caractères distinctifs sont cités dans la clé, n'ont pas été récoltées au Gabon, et ne sont pas décrites, leur existence étant incertaine.

— Warneckea yangambensis (A. & R. FERN.) JACQ.-FÉL., Fl. Cam. 24 : (1983). Observé au Zaïre et au Cameroun.

— Warneckea cinnamomoides (G. DON) JACQ.-FÉL., Fl. Cam. 24 : (1983). Surtout connue dans les bosquets d’Afrique occidentale.

— Warneckea jasminoides (GILG) JACQ.-FÉL., Fl. Cam. 24 : (1983). Surtout connue du Zaïre à l'Afrique orientale, a été également observée au Cameroun et en Angola.
