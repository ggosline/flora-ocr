# *Utricularia arenaria* A.DC. Planche 33, Figure 9(A, B)

**Genus**: [[Utricularia]]
**Family**: [[Lentibulariaceae]]

## Description

in DC., Prodr. 8 : 20 (1844). Terrestrial herb; rhizoids and capillary stolons, numerous at the base of the floral scape. Leaves numerous at anthesis, spaced along the stolons, narrowly obovate-elliptic to obovate-spatulate, 2–15 × 0.3–2 mm, 1-nerved. Utricles numerous, stipitate, ovoid, 0.6–1 mm in length; terminal opening, with upper lip ± 2 times as long as the lower, the 2 lips ornamented with pectiniform rows of glandular hairs. Inflorescence erect, 2–16 cm in length, with 1 to 5(–8) flowers; peduncle smooth in the upper part, ordinarily papillose at the base; bracts basifixed, ovate-elliptic, ± 1 mm in length, acute at the apex, ≤ 2 times as wide as the bracteoles. Flower: bracteoles similar to the bracts, but narrower; pedicel cylindric, 0.5–1 mm, erect or spreading at fruiting; calyx with subequal lobes; corolla 3–7 mm in length, white or lilac with a yellow spot on the palate, upper lip narrowly oblong, rounded, truncate to emarginate at the apex, the lower one orbicular, palate prominent, ornamented with 2 smooth or transversely tuberculate crests, spur conic-subulate, ± 2 times as long as the upper lip. Fruit globose, 1.5–2.5 mm in diameter, dehiscent by 2 longitudinal slits, one abaxial, the other adaxial, with thickened margins, with numerous seeds. Seed conic-truncate, angular, ± 0.2 mm in length; testa smooth, with indistinct and oblong cells.

## Distribution

Senegal, Mali, Sierra Leone, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Togo, Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Burundi, Sudan, Ethiopia, Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, Zambia, Mozambique, Malawi, Zimbabwe, Angola, Namibia, South Africa (Transvaal, Natal), Madagascar, India (Madhya Pradesh); in Gabon, observed in the Nyanga and Ogooué-Maritime
