# *Dacryodes klaineana* (Pierre) Lam

**Family**: [[Burseraceae]] · **Genus**: [[Dacryodes]]

## Description

Tree with comparatively few broad leaflets, glabrescent blades marked beneath by many minute whitish stomatal points and appressed scales; petiolules relatively long and often swollen at both ends. Flowers 3-merous; disc glabrous. Fruit small and globose, initially scaly; endocarp with the shield characteristic of the genus.

## Distribution

Treated from Gabon; the account distinguishes it from *D. macrophylla* by its leaflet and fruit characters.
