# *Calpocalyx klainei* Pierre ex Harms

**Genus**: [[Calpocalyx]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Bot. France, mém. 8 : 156 (1911) ; HEITZ, forêt Gabon ; 135, tab. 41, fig. 1 (1943) ; PELLEGRIN, Légum. Gabon : 24 (1948) ; RAPONDA-WALKER & SILLANS, Pl. ut. Gabon : 240 (1962) ; VILLIERS, BMNHN 6 (3) : 307, tab. 3, fig. 7-8 (1984)

## Description

Tree 7-10 (-25) m tall, with short, weakly fluted trunk reaching 30-40 cm in diameter. Parasol-shaped crown with diffuse, regular, rather sparse foliage. Bark grey-light, marbled with white or greenish-grey ± dark, weakly rugged, finely and regularly longitudinally fissured; inner bark pale brown, 0.5 cm thick, weakly fibrous. Young twigs angular, dark brown with lighter crests shortly pubescent. Petiole ± shortly pubescent pubérulent, 0.7-1.8 (-4) cm long, apex thickened bearing a broad, flat gland on its upper surface. Rachis of the pinna clearly pubérulent russet or tawny, 21-41.5 cm long, upper surface flat to ± canaliculate bearing glands at the level of almost all pairs of leaflets. Leaflets 7-11 pairs, the lowest often reduced to an external leaflet. Petiolule densely pubérulent, 2-6 mm long, canaliculate to flat above. Blade glabrous on the upper surface and glabrous below, dark brown above and beige-brown below on the dry state; elliptic or oblong at the base to oblanceolate at the apex, decreasing in size from apex to base, reaching 16 × 7 cm; base asymmetric: proximal margin attenuate and distal margin rounded to subcordate for basal leaflets, unequally attenuate for terminal ones, apex clearly acuminate mucronate. Main midvein weakly prominent to concave ± pubescent above and prominent pubescent below. Secondary veins ascending, very arched, ± prominent to concave above, prominent pubescent below. Veinlets in rather dense reticulum, little visible above and prominent below.

Inflorescence a panicle, axillary or terminal. Axis pubescent like the stem. Lateral bracts at the base of the branches, acicular, 0.3-1 cm long. Median bract at the base of the branches thick, densely pubescent, with broad glandular patch at the apex of the adaxial surface, 4-8 mm long, apex acuminate, sometimes with two other lateral points. Interflortal bracts thick, pubescent, lanceolate, 1 mm long, apex very acute apiculate. Floral buds oblongoid. ♂ and ♀ flowers yellow to ferrugineous yellow in life, yellowish-brown on the dry state, sessile. Calyx ± cylindric, pubescent, 2.5-2.75 mm long, lobes free weakly campanulate, triangular, 0.50-0.75 mm long, apex acute. Petals fused together, pubescent on the outside, ± 5.5 mm long, free lobes ± narrowly lanceolate 1-1.25 mm long, apex acute. Stamens 10, free from one another; filament 9.5-10 mm long; anther widely ellipsoid, 0.5 mm long; connective prolonged by a pedicellate gland. Pistillode of ♂ flowers substipitate, pubescent, 0.75 mm long. Pistil of ♀ flowers substipitate; ovary pubescent, obovoid, ± 1.50-1.75 mm long, containing 4 ovules; style 4 mm long terminated by a pointed stigma.

Woody dehiscent pod, narrowly obtriangular, somewhat sinuous, ± pubérulent, 21 × 5 cm, base very attenuate; dorsal sutural margin ± weakly thickened; lateral surfaces striated; apex somewhat reflexed obtuse. - Pl. 26, Fig. 5-6, p. 101; Pl. 28, Fig. 7-8, p. 107.

## Type

Klaine 1035, Gabon (holo-, P ; iso-, P)

## Discussion

This species, by its habit and the general aspect of the foliage, may be confused with C. dinklagei. However the pubérulence of the leaves and the form of the fruits allow easy recognition.

The range of C. klainei is currently limited to southern Cameroon and the coastal zone of Gabon.

## Vernacular names

misise, misizé, mississé (Fang)
