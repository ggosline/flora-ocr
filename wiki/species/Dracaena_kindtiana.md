# *Dracaena kindtiana* De Wild. Planche 13

**Genus**: [[Dracaena]]
**Family**: [[Dracaenaceae]]
**Protologue**: Ann. Mus. Congo belge, Bot., sér. 5, 2 : 119 (1907)

## Description

Pleomele kindtiana (De Wild.) N.E.Br., Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1914 : 278 (1914). Shrub up to 1.5 m tall; stem generally simple; rhizome woody, long-creeping, bright orange. Leaves spiral, radiating, leaving the stem visible between the sheaths or cataphylls; sheath embracing the stem over clearly more than the circumference, over a length of > 1 cm; pseudipetiole somewhat shorter than the lamina, green, rounded in section, grooved above, base gradually passing into the sheath; lamina broadly elliptic to ovate-elliptic, up to 26 × 10 cm, base rounded, apex acuminate to caudate and recurved toward the ground, mucro reaching 1 cm, papyraceous-coriaceous, slightly undulate longitudinally, upper surface medium green to dark green uniform, lower surface paler. Inflorescence erect, spiciform or sometimes weakly branched at the base, up to 35 cm long, lax, axes smooth, green, with flowers aggregated on the upper half of the spike; bracts quickly caducous, narrowly ovate-ligulate, up to 5 × 0.2 cm, becoming smaller toward the apex; fascicles with 2 to 4 flowers, subtended by bracts quickly caducous, narrowly triangular, ± 2 mm long, scarious. Flower with fragrance of Scilla or Endymion; pedicel 1–2 mm; perianth 20–30 mm long, pink-white to brownish-green, tinged with violet, lobes longer than the tube, reflexed; stamens inserted near the throat, as long as the lobes, filament white, filiform, anther 3 mm long, cream-coloured; ovary oblong, up to 2 × 1 mm; style exsert up to 2 mm, white, stigma ± 0.5 mm in diameter. Fruit subsessile, entire to deeply 2- or 3-lobed, ± 10 × 5 mm, orange to red, lobes ellipsoid, divergent. Seed ovoid, ± 7 × 5 mm.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Uganda, Angola; in Gabon, known from Ngounié and Ogooué-Ivindo

## Ecology

Understory and brush, often at water's edge; in Gabon at 150–650 m altitude, elsewhere at 0–1830 m. Flowering recorded from November to April and from June to September
