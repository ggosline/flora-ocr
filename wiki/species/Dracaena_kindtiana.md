# *Dracaena kindtiana* De Wild. Planche 13

**Genus**: [[Dracaena]]
**Family**: [[Dracaenaceae]]
**Protologue**: Ann. Mus. Congo belge, Bot., sér. 5, 2 : 119 (1907)

## Synonyms

- Pleomele kindtiana (De Wild.) N.E.Br., Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1914 : 278 (1914).

## Description

Shrub up to 1.5 m tall; stem generally simple; rhizome woody, long-creeping, bright orange. Leaves spiral, radiating, leaving the stem visible between the sheaths or cataphylls; sheath embracing the stem over clearly more than the circumference, over a length of > 1 cm; pseudpetiole somewhat shorter than the blade, green, terete, furrowed above, base gradually passing into the sheath; blade broadly elliptic to oval-elliptic, up to 26 × 10 cm, base rounded, apex acuminate to caudate and recurved towards the ground, mucro reaching 1 cm, papyraceous-coriaceous, slightly longitudinally undulate, upper surface medium green to dark green uniform, lower surface paler. Inflorescence erect, spiciform or sometimes weakly branched at the base, up to 35 cm long, lax, axes smooth, green, with flowers aggregated on the upper half of the spike; bracts rapidly caducous, narrowly oval-ligulate, up to 5 × 0.2 cm, becoming smaller towards the apex; fascicles with 2 to 4 flowers, subtended by bracts rapidly caducous, narrowly triangular, ± 2 mm long, scarious. Flower with scent of Scilla or Endymion; pedicel 1–2 mm; perianth 20–30 mm long, pale pink to brownish-green, tinged with violet, with lobes longer than the tube, reflexed; stamens inserted near the throat, as long as the lobes, filament white, filiform, anther 3 mm long, cream; ovary oblong, up to 2 × 1 mm; style exsert to up to 2 mm, white, stigma ± 0.5 mm in diameter. Fruit subsessile, entire to deeply 2- or 3-lobed, ± 10 × 5 mm, orange to red, lobes ellipsoid, divergent. Seed ovoid, ± 7 × 5 mm.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Uganda, Angola; in Gabon, known from Ngounié and Ogooué-Ivindo

## Ecology

Understory and scrubland, often at the water's edge; in Gabon at 150–650 m altitude, elsewhere at 0–1830 m. Flowering recorded from November to April and from June to September
