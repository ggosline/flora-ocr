# *Begonia mannii* Hook. Planche 49

**Genus**: [[Begonia]]
**Family**: [[Begoniaceae]]
**Protologue**: Curtis Bot. Mag. 90, sér. 3(20), tab. 5434 (1864)

## Description

Curtis Bot. Mag. 90, sér. 3(20), tab. 5434 (1864). Epiphytic or subepiphytic; stem erect, pendulous or creeping, reaching 5 m in length. Stipules ± persistent, narrowly triangular, (10–)20–40 × 6–12 mm. Leaves: petiole (0.5–)1–6.5(–8.5) cm; blade slightly asymmetric, narrowly elliptic to ovate or obovate, (7.5–)10–22 × (3.5–)4–10.5 cm, base subauricular to obtuse, apex acute to acuminate, margins slightly dentate; midvein and (5–)6–8 pairs of secondary veins distinctly marked above, prominent and often red below. Inflorescence unisexual; the ♂ in short dichasial cymes, 5–7(–15)-flowered, peduncle 3–10(–20) mm; the ♀ (1–)3(–7)-flowered, robust peduncle 1–10(–20) mm. ♂ Flower: pedicel (5–)10–15(–20) mm; outer tepals elliptic to obovate, 7–12 × 4–8 mm, inner ones narrowly obovate, 7–12 × 2.5–3.5 mm; androecium conical, actinomorphic, stamens (15–)18–27, column 2 mm long, anthers nearly sessile with longitudinal dehiscence. ♀ Flower sessile; outer tepals 10–16(–20) × 5–9 mm, inner ones narrower; ovary (15–)20–30(–35) × 1.5–4 mm, circular in section, sometimes the base narrowed; styles 4(–5), simple, subulate, stigmatic zone linear, 1.5–3.5 mm. Infructescence with 2 or 3 fruits. Fruit sausage-shaped, 40–55(–80) × 5–7 mm, opening by 2 or 3 valves.

## Distribution

Guinea, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea (including Bioko), Gabon; to be sought in Ivory Coast and Ghana
