# *Xyris angustifolia* De Wild. & T.Durand

**Genus**: [[Xyris]]
**Family**: [[Xyridaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Bot. Belg. 38, 2 : 149−150 (1889)

## Description

Perennial herb, in dense tuft. Leaf: sheath widening toward the base, brown, slightly wrinkled externally, margin pilose; blade linear, 10–15 cm × 0.5–1 mm, flattened, furrowed on both faces, weakly rugose between the furrows, apex acute or apiculate, slightly asymmetric. Inflorescence: peduncle 20–25 cm × 0.2–0.4 mm, smooth, spiraled; spike ellipsoid, 3−4 × 2−3 mm, pale brown; lower bracts nearly circular, ca. 1.5 × 1.5 mm; upper sterile bracts broadly elliptic, 2 × 1.5 mm; fertile bracts comparable but longer, 3 × 1.5 mm, apex rounded, without dorsal mark. Flower: lateral sepals ca. 3 × 0.6–0.7 mm, keel with scattered obtuse teeth; corolla described as white, but more probably yellow, lobes broadly obovate, ca. 3 mm in length; stamens ca. 1 mm in length; styles and ovary not observed. Fruit unknown.

## Distribution

Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon known only from Ogooué-Ivindo

## Ecology

humid grasslands, often burned; in Gabon at ca. 300 m altitude. Taxonomic note: Perennial prairie species with narrow leaves are poorly known and require additional taxonomic work. B: Lisowski et al. (2001). Bibliography
