# *Voacanga lanceolata* (Stapf) Jongkind Planche 124 ; Figure 15(F, G)

**Genus**: [[Voacanga]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Candollea 77 : 50 (2022)

## Synonyms

- Voacanga bracteata Stapf var. lanceolata Stapf, Fl. trop. Afr. 4(1) : 160 (1902).

## Description

Shrub or small tree reaching 4 m in height; branches glabrous. Leaf glabrous; petiole of ±1–2 cm; lamina elliptic to narrowly obovate of ±8–15 cm, cuneate at the base, acuminate to almost caudate at the apex, secondary nervures ±8 pairs, tertiary venation discreet. Inflorescence drooping; peduncle of 2–8 cm; bracts of 5–9 × 1.5–6 mm. Flower: pedicel of 2–8 mm; calyx 8–12 mm long, pale green, with campanulate tube, with a dense band of internal colleters near the base, with ± erect lobes, slightly shorter and about as long as the tube, ±2 times as long as broad, corolla with tube ±2 times as long as the calyx, 8–10 mm long, white to pale, constricted near the throat, strongly twisted especially in the upper half, glabrous on the outside, with a dense band of short appressed hairs on the inside, with lobes spreading, then reflexed, obovate, <1.5 times as long as broad, of 5–7 × 4–5 mm, at first yellow to white, then brown, forming an ovoid head in bud; stamens included, with apex reaching just the throat, anther 1.5 mm long. Fruit with distinct carpels, sometimes only one developed, obliquely globulous, ±4 × 3 cm, pale green to ivory at maturity, with numerous seeds. Distribution southeast of Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon: Haut-Ogooué, Ogooué, Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo and Woleu-Ntem. Ecology undergrowth of primary and secondary forest in Gabon up to m of altitude; elsewhere up to 1200 m.
