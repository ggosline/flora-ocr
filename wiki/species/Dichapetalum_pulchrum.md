# *Dichapetalum pulchrum* Breteler

**Genus**: [[Dichapetalum]]
**Family**: [[Dichapetalaceae]]
**Protologue**: MLW 81 (10) : 86-90 (1981) ; AUWP 86 (3) : 12 (1986). TYPE : Gabon, 25 km au N.E. d'Asok, Breteler & de Wilde 100 (holo-, WAG)

## Description

MLW 81 (10) : 86-90 (1981) ; AUWP 86 (3) : 12 (1986). TYPE : Gabon, 25 km N.E. of Asok, Breteler & de Wilde 100 (holo-, WAG).

Large liane, shrub or small tree with lianescent apex. Entire woody cylinder, wood dense and hard. Bark of stem and older branches fairly smooth, whitish, fine and corky. Young branches with dense and often pale brown velvety to villous pubescence, long-persisting, also present on petioles and stipules but more appressed on the latter.

Stipules very persistent, narrow and triangular, pennatilobed to dentate or entire, (4) 8-17 (22) mm long.

Leaves. Petiole subcylindric, 3-8 (12) mm long. Blade narrow and obovate-elliptic, (2) 2.5-3.5 (4) times longer than wide, (15) 20-35 (42) × (6) 7-11 (14) cm ; base rounded to cordate ; acumen present, rounded to subacute or even mucronate, 0.5-2 (3) cm long ; young blade cottony above, early glabrescent, except generally at the basal part of the midvein which is impressed ; lower surface with ± persistent indumentum (early caducous on Congo material), villous-velvety, on the venation most often very prominent, denser on the midvein and to a lesser degree on the (10) 11-14 (16) pairs of principal secondary veins ; glands generally present but only below, small and fairly inconspicuous, sometimes not very well developed, well distributed or more concentrated along the midvein.

Inflorescences condensed, villous-velvety, often multiflorous. Bracts and bracteoles ovate-triangular and generally narrow, ≤ 3 mm long ; external pubescence appressed, interior glabrous. Pedicel ≤ 7 mm, appressed pubescence, without visible articulation.

Flowers persistent. Sepals erect, ovate-elliptic to oblong, 3-5 × 1-3 mm, appressed pubescence externally, glabrous or with a few hairs at the apex of the inner surface. Petals erect with narrow and obovate outline, 4.5-8 mm long, split for 0.5-2 mm, base free, outer surface glabrous or with a few hairs. Stamens erect, 4.5-7.5 mm long, glabrous; anthers, diameter ≈ 0.5 mm. Staminodes subquadrate to oblong, 0.7 × 0.7 mm, glabrous; apex obtuse to bilobed. Pistil 3-merous, 5.5-9 mm long; ovary velvety; style glabrous terminated by 3 very short lobes.

Fruits 1-2 (3?)-seeded, ovoid-ellipsoid to obovoid, ≤ 4 × 1.5-2 cm, velvety to villous ; exo- and mesocarp together, 1-2 mm thick ; endocarp chartaceous, smooth and shiny inside. Seeds ovoid-ellipsoid, laterally compressed, ≤ 28 × 11 × 7 mm ; testa smooth brown, 0.5-0.7 mm thick. - Pl. 46, p. 159.

## Discussion

This species is very close to D. glomeratum Engl. The two species show the same tendency toward arborescent habit and both have an entire woody cylinder and very dense wood. The indumentum of young branches and leaves is of the same nature. The flowers also are fairly similar and differ only slightly in their size and by the absence of a distinct articulation of their pedicel. These species can be distinguished as follows:

Stipules always entire, 1-5 (6) mm long ; leaves (4) 10-16 (24) × (1.5) 3.5-6 (12) cm with 8-12 (16) pairs of principal secondary veins ; sepals 1.5-2.5 mm long and petals (3) 4-6 mm .....
