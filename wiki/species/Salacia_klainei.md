# *Salacia klainei* Pierre ex Wilczek

**Genus**: [[Salacia]]
**Family**: [[Celastraceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Jard. Bot. Etat Brux. 27: 690 (1957); FCB 9: 197 (1960)

## Description

Liane containing rubber sometimes scarcely abundant in the leaves. Internodes grey when dry, smooth, with 4 fine keels, well-salient to aliform. Petiole 5-8 mm, with wide channel with straight margins. Blade elliptic or oblong, sometimes obovate, rust or rusty-coloured above when dry; base rounded to acute; apex briefly to moderately acuminate, with toothed margin sometimes poorly, often distinctly micropustulous below between the veins, 8-23 × 4-11.5 cm. Secondary veins 6-10 pairs, no very fine reticulum apparent.

Glomerules 1-6-flowered multibracts axillary sessile. Pedicel 9-12 mm. Bud ovoid 2-3 mm in diameter. Flower rich in rubber, (8)10-13 mm in diameter, pale yellow-green. Sepals 5 unequal, obtuse at apex, with ciliolate glandular margin, 0.8-2.4 mm wide with the cilia. Petals 5(7) elliptic 4-6 × 2-3 mm, spreading or somewhat reflexed; disc pulley-shaped 3 mm in diameter and 1.2 mm thick. Stamens 3 spaced with filament nearly 3 mm, not coiling in spiral, with base slightly dilated but furnished with a salient external axis. Anther 1.4 × 1.4 (-1.8) mm, with 2 longitudinal non-confluent thecae, with apex of connective furnished with a remarkable mucro. Style pyramidal 3 mm with the apex of the ovary; 3 locules each having 3 superposed ovules.

Fruit oblong ellipsoid 6-8 × 2.5-3 cm, with 3 longitudinal crests; base attenuate or not, apex attenuate to apiculate; wall 1 mm thick when dry. Seeds, 4 in a fruit, the largest 30 × 14 mm. - Pl. 50, fig. 18-28, p. 173.
