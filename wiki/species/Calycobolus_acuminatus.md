## Description

Liane or sometimes lianeous shrub; stem/trunk up to 7 cm in diameter; branchlets with appressed hairs ± dense to ± ferrugineous-villous or sometimes ± hispid, generally glabrescent. Leaf: petiole (2–)5–13(–15) mm, hairy as the branchlets; lamina obovate-elliptic, (6–)8–18(–20) × 2–7(–9) cm, rounded or narrowly cordate at the base, acuminate over 0.5–2 cm at the apex, with ± scattered, short, brown, ± appressed hairs on both surfaces when young, generally finely pustulous above, with (6–)9–11(–12) pairs of lateral veins. Inflorescence in axillary to (sub)terminal racemes, with axis up to 20 cm in length, sometimes flowers in fascicles or rarely solitary; bracteoles opposite, (1–)2–12 mm in length, attached ± at the middle of the pedicel. Flower: pedicel (5–)10–15(–30) mm; sepals: the two outer unequal, 6–20 × 5–15 cm, cordate at the base, rounded to acute at the apex, the three inner circular to ovate-triangular, (1–)5–9 × 1–3 mm; corolla infundibuliform, sometimes somewhat campanulate, (2.5–)3.5–5(–6) cm in length, white, hairy at the apex; stamens ± equal, (6–)14–30(–33) mm in length, anthers pendulous, 3–5(–6) mm in length, the slits with long hairs; pistil (10–)15–33 mm in length, glabrous; styles united for ± half their length. Fruit ellipsoid, 15–19 × 10–12 mm, glabrous, the two outer sepals strongly enlarged, ± circular, the larger 4–7(–9) × 4–7(–9) cm, the smaller 3–6 × 2.5–3.5 cm.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Angola and western Tanzania; in Gabon, known from all provinces

## Ecology

dense humid evergreen and semi-deciduous forest, gallery forest; in Gabon, at 0–600 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1050 m. B: Breteler (2013). Bibliography
