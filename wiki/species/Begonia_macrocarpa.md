# *Begonia macrocarpa* Warb.

**Genus**: [[Begonia]]
**Family**: [[Begoniaceae]]

## Description

in Engl., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 22(1) : 43 (1895). Herb 9–100 cm tall, glabrous, pubescent when young. Stipules rather quickly caducous, ovate to narrowly ovate, 3–9 × 1–4.5 mm, acuminate, dentate-ciliate at apex. Leaf: petiole (2–)7–40(–55) mm; blade ovate to elliptic, asymmetric, (2–)5–15(–19) × (1–)2–6(–7) cm, base oblique, apex acuminate, margin entire to denticulate, glabrous on both surfaces. Inflorescence with (2–)5–17 ♂ flowers and 1 ♀ flower; peduncle 5–19 mm; bracts early caducous, obovate to circular, 4–10 × (1.5–)3–10 mm. ♂ Flower: pedicel 4–12 mm; tepals circular to broadly obovate, 5–9.5 mm diameter, white, veined with pink or entirely red at the base; stamens (9–)12–20(–23). ♀ Flower subsessile; tepals 8–13 mm diameter; ovary ellipsoid to obovoid, 7–15 × 8.5–18 mm, (including the 3 unequal wings); styles 3, 4–6.5 mm, stigmas subreniform. Fruit solitary, pendulous, on a peduncle 12–40 mm and curved at base, broadly elliptic to obovate, 10–30 mm diameter, with rostrum reaching 1.5 mm.

## Distribution

West and central Africa, from Guinea to Angola

## Ecology

Primary and secondary dense humid forests, preferably in somewhat disturbed stations; also in riparian forests, in periodically flooded stations; up to 1000 m altitude. Use: eaten as spinach

## Vernacular names

oseille de la brousse (Fr). B : Wilczek(1969a). IBLIOGRAPHIE
