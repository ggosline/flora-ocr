# *Campylosiphon congestus* (C.H.Wright) Merckx ex Maas Planche 3

**Genus**: [[Campylosiphon]]
**Family**: [[Burmanniaceae]]
**Protologue**: Burmannia densiflora Schltr., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 38 : 141 (1906)

## Description

Burmannia congesta (C.H.Wright) Jonker, Meded. Bot. Mus. Herb. Rijksuniv. Utrecht 51 : 94 (1938). Burmannia densiflora Schltr., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 38 : 141 (1906). Herb 2–30 cm tall; stem simple, rarely branched, white, rarely purple. Leaves narrowly triangular to linear, 1–6 mm long. Inflorescence multiflorous to pauciflorous, rarely uniflorous, often contracted; bracts 2–6 mm long. Flower 5–10 mm long (ovary included), without or rarely with 6 ridges 0.2–0.3 mm wide; pedicel often absent, sometimes up to 5 mm long; perianth with tube 3.5–5 mm long, white or rarely purple, lobes white to yellow, outer lobes triangular, 1.5–2 mm long, inner lobes linear, 0.5–1 mm long; ovary obovoid, 2–3 mm long; style 3.5–5 mm long. Fruit ellipsoid to subglobose, 2–4 mm long. Seeds ellipsoidal, 0.4–0.5 × 0.2–0.3 mm.

## Distribution

Ghana, Liberia, Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola; in Gabon present in almost the entire country

## Ecology

primary forests; in Gabon between 50 and 460 m altitude, elsewhere at 0–200 m. Taxonomic note: see the appendix. B: Geerinck (1970), Hepper (1968), Jonker (1938), Merckx (2008). Bibliography
