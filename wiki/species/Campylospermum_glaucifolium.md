# *Campylospermum glaucifolium* Biss.

**Genus**: [[Campylospermum]]
**Family**: [[Ochnaceae]]
**Protologue**: Blumea 58 : 5 (2013)

## Description

Small tree reaching 2 m in height; branches with whitish bark. Stipules caducous, 2–3 mm long. Leaf: petiole 7–15(–20) mm, canaliculate above; blade narrowly elliptic to narrowly elliptic-obovate, 13–25(–30) × 4–7(–8) cm, ratio 3.5–4.6, cuneate at base, acuminate at apex, coriaceous to strongly coriaceous, with serrulate margins, dark green but becoming blue-green to greyish when dry above; primary vein generally prominent on both faces, lateral veins in 14 to 16 pairs, slightly prominent above, tertiary venation scalariform, perpendicular to primary vein, indistinct above, distinct below. Inflorescence terminal, pendulous, branched or unbranched, lax, main axis (9–)15–33 cm; racemes 0 to 2(–3), 3–6(–10) cm; gemmate scales persistent; cymes distant 0.5–1(–1.5) cm apart, 1- to 4-flowered; bracts caducous, triangular, approximately 2–3 mm long. Flower: pedicel 3–12 mm, articulate 1–3 mm from base; sepals ovate, 6–7 × 2–3 mm in flower, 8–9 × 3–4 mm in fruit; petals obovate, 7–9 × 4–5 mm, shortly unguiculate, rounded at apex;

stamens: anthers 5–6 mm long; style 6–7 mm. Fruit: receptacle approximately 3 mm wide; drupelets 1 to 4 well-developed per receptacle, reniform, 7–10 × 6–8 mm; cotyledons accumbent, equal.

## Distribution

Equatorial Guinea (Rio Muni), Gabon and southwestern Republic of the Congo (Niari); in Gabon, quite rare, known from Estuaire, Ngounié, Moyen-Ogooué, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué Maritime and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

primary and secondary forest, stream margins and marshy places; in Gabon, at 50–750 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1000 m. Taxonomic note: This species resembles C. calanthum (Gilg) Farron, but differs from it by its blade with blue-green to greyish upper surface (when dry), its longer petiole and its pendulous inflorescence, with 0 to 2(–3) racemes. b: Bissiengou (2014), Bissiengou et al.
