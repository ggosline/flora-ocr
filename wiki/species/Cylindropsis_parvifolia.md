# *Cylindropsis parvifolia* Pierre Planche 29

**Genus**: [[Cylindropsis]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Linn. Paris , sér. 2, 1 : 39 (1898)

## Description

Liane with hard woody stems up to 8 cm in diameter, at least branched, with numerous lenticels; ramification smooth, generally glabrous, rarely with some glandular hairs. Leaf: petiole 2–10 mm; lamina narrowly ovate, sometimes narrowly elliptic to narrowly obovate – sometimes as long as wide, 5–15 × 1–6 cm; at the interior with arranged rows of colleters; 7–15. Pistil 1.7–4.7. Fruit 10–14. Rameau fructifère 8–12. Seeds one of them between each open; Leeuwenberg 5080; 2-3, 6: J. Louis 13786; 4-5: R. Germain 4764; 7, 9: Tisserant 2170; 8: A.M. Louis 1348). Drawing by Piet Kostense, Naturalis Biodiversity Center (©), reproduced from van der Ploeg (1985).

Open corolla, showing the stamens (× 8). – 5. Calyx with style and stigma (× 8). – 6. Pistil (× 8). – 7. Fruit 10–14. Seeds with and without pulp; Luja 156; 2: Le Testu 4713; 3-6: Reitsma 3214; 7: Breteler 6853; 8, 10: Klaine 103bis). Drawing by Yuen Fang Tan, Naturalis Biodiversity Center (©), reproduced from Haegens (1994).

Base cuneate to rounded, apex apiculate, sometimes obtuse or even retuse; acumen null or up to 2 mm, violet-red when it is young; secondary nerves 7 to 14 pairs, alternating with finer and shorter interstitial nerves; tertiary venation reticulate. Inflorescence axillary, sometimes at the same time terminal, contracted, sometimes slightly loose, with numerous flowers; pedicel null or up to 3 mm, glabrous. Flower. Sepal null or up to 3 mm. Calyx 5-lobed – sometimes as long as wide, 1.5–2.8 × 1.5–2.3 mm; hyaline margin ciliate, without colleters; corolla completely white or only the tube pale green, glabrous on the outside, or rarely with a pubescent band at the level of the stamens, on the inside with scattered hairs above the insertion of the stamens and often pubescent at the base of the lobes, subcylindric tube, 2.2–4.3 mm long, 0.4–0.8 mm wide, not thickened above the anthers, with lobes 0.3–0.9 times as long as the tube, 1.3–2.8 × 0.5–1.1 mm, ± ciliate; stamens with apex at or 0.5 mm below the throat, anther 0.5–0.9 mm long; pistil 1.7–4.7 mm long, with ovary variously pubescent in the upper part, otherwise glabrous, gradually narrowed into a 0.7–3 mm style, glabrous. Fruit globose to ovoid, 11–14 mm long, orange; pericarp smooth, pulp creamy viscous parenchyma 1.5–2 mm thick. Seed 13–23 mm long; cotyledons 2–3 mm thick. Distribution: Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola; Cabinda in Gabon. Estuary, Haut-Ogooué, Nyanga, Ogooué, Ivindo-Ogooué, Maritime and Woleu-Ntem. Ecology: Forest in Gabon and elsewhere up to 800 m altitude. Use: The fruits are edible. B: Beentje (2021), Haegens (1994). BIBLIOGRAPHY
