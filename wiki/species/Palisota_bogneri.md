# *Palisota bogneri* Brenan Planche 15, Figure 3(K, L)

**Genus**: [[Palisota]]
**Family**: [[Commelinaceae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull. 39 : 829 (1984)

## Description

Acaulescent herb; taproot ± 40 cm with fibrous roots. Leaves in rosette, highly variable; pseudopetiole ± 2–5 cm, densely woolly toward the base, with stiff white hairs; limb obovate-elliptic, 15–20(–40) × 5–6(–10) cm, base cuneate, gradually narrowed toward the pseudopetiole or more or less rounded, apex obtuse to rounded, apiculate, margins with a dense line of white hairs, shorter toward the apex, with obscure pubescence on the upper surface, white woolly on the lower surface. Inflorescence lateral; peduncle appearing among the leaf sheaths, decumbent, 4–10 cm, woolly; thyrse broadly oblongoid to subspheric, dense, 1.5–2.5 × 1.5–2.5 cm; bracts 2 or 3, ovate, ± 1–2.5 × 0.5–1 cm, apex acute, dark red; floral bracts narrowly triangular, up to 5 mm in length, margins with brown-russet hairs. Flower: pedicel ± 3–8 mm, covered with white woolly pubescence; sepals equal, oblong-elliptic, ± 4.5–6 × 2–2.5 mm, apex obtuse and very concave, white or greenish, distinctly pubescent with rigid hairs often retrorse; petals equal, elliptic, concave, ± 5 × 2.5 mm, apex cucullate-rounded, white or slightly pinkish, glabrous; stamens yellow, with filament 3 mm, unequal, the 2 upper ones with basifixed anther, narrowly oblong, ± 1.5 × 0.5 mm, flat, that of the lower one dorsifixed, robust, reniform, strongly curved, 1.75 × 0.75 mm; staminodes 3 mm in length, composed of a filament with long fan-shaped hairs on the upper half, white; ovary 0.75 mm in diameter, provided with erect, rigid hairs, 1.75 mm in length; style 3 mm, stigma depressed, somewhat wider than the style. Fruit ovoid to oblong-ovoid, 2–2.5 × 0.8–1 cm, acute to distinctly acuminate at the apex, slightly pubescent, orange to scarlet red, on a spiraled pedicel, with up to 7 seeds. Seed subspheric to rounded pyramidal, ± 4 mm in diameter, finely ribbed with ± 40 superficial ribs, light grayish-brown.

## Distribution

Gabon and Republic of the Congo; in Gabon in the Nyanga, Ogooué-Lolo and Ogooué-Maritime regions.

## Ecology

Mature humid lowland forests; in Gabon from 10 to 530 m in altitude. Flowering from September to December; fruiting in March.

## Source note

A poorly known species, apparently highly variable in its size, in the base of the limb more or less rounded to gradually narrowed, in the length of the peduncle, etc. It seems to us that plants with leaves having a rounded base of the limb and glabrous petiole are close to the type specimen. The more robust plants with limbs gradually narrowed at the base are more distant from the type and could even represent a mixture of several undescribed species, some of which could approach P. alboanthera. More herbarium material and field observations are necessary in order to resolve the taxonomic complex that this species represents. Although mentioned in the Flora of Equatorial Guinea (Cabezas et al. 2014), this species has not yet been recorded there. The specimen cited (Senterre & Ngomo 896) actually represents P. plicata.
