# *Mucuna occidentalis* (Hepper) T.M.Moura & G.P.Lewis

**Genus**: [[Mucuna]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull. 69 : 9544, 5 (2014)

## Synonyms

- Mucuna poggei Taub. var. occidentalis Hepper, Kew Bull. 1956 : 127 (1956).

## Description

Liane, stem reaching 30 m in length, up to 20 cm in diameter; slender branchlets, striate and covered with rust-coloured urticating hairs. Stipules caducous, lanceolate, small. Leaf: petiole 10–14 cm, sparsely hairy; rachis comparable, 3–4 cm, hairy; stipels filiform, 1–4 mm; leaflets chartaceous, petiolule 6–8 mm, villous, the terminal one with subrhomboidal lamina, the lateral ones very asymmetric, ± ovate, 8–16 × 7–12 cm, subcordate base, obtuse apex, hairs sparse above, loose, short, appressed, grey-silvery hairs below. Inflorescence in a raceme with numerous flowers; peduncle 25–40 cm, villous, knotty. Flower: pedicel 0.5–2 cm, villous; calyx campanulate, externally covered with short, whitish, persistent hairs and longer, rare, rust-coloured, urticating and ± caducous setae, tube ± 1 cm in length, with ± triangular teeth, the upper one 3 mm in length, the lower ones ± 1 cm in length; corolla yellow or white tinged with green, standard ± 5 cm in length, wings ± 8 cm in length, pubescent at the base, keel 8.5–9 cm in length, acute. Pod oblong, 5–20 × 3–4 cm, attenuate at the base, apex with an oblique mucro, 2 or 3-seeded, coriaceous, with undulate margins, with rust-coloured urticating hairs. Seed ± 2 cm in diameter, ± 0.5 cm in thickness; hilum covering 3/5 the length of the seed.

## Distribution

from Sierra Leone to Cameroon, Gabon, Central African Republic and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, known from Ngounié

## Ecology

stream banks, forest margins of humid forests, savannas and periodically flooded areas; in Gabon at 600 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1250 m

## Source note

The varieties of the Mucuna poggei complex have been elevated to species level (Moura & Lewis 2014). The true M. poggei, with nerves on the lower surface of the leaflets densely hairy with long hairs and stipels 5–6 mm in length, is found in Angola, the two Congos, Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania and Zambia. Use: Dye plant of local importance

## Vernacular names

mucuna de Pogge (fr.) ; buffalo bean (angl.). b : Pellegrin (1949), Hauman (1954e), Jansen (2005b), Mackinder et al. (2001), Moura & ibliographie Lewis (2014), Verdcourt (1971c), Watt & Breyer-Brandwijk (1962)
