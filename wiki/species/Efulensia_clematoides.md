# *Efulensia clematoides* C.H.Wright Planche 33

**Genus**: [[Efulensia]]
**Family**: [[Passifloraceae]]
**Protologue**: Hooker’s Icon. Pl., ser. 4, 6 : tab. 2518 (1897)

## Description

Deidamia clematoides (C.H.Wright) Harms in Engl. & Prantl, Nat. Pflanzenfam., 3, 6a, Nachtr. 1: 254 (1897). Liana reaching 40 m; stem 1–5 cm in diameter at base; axillary tendrils present or absent. Stipules reduced to a raised line or absent. Leaf with petiole 2–8.5 cm, with a pair of auricate glands at base; blade trifoliolate; leaflets with petiolule 1–3 cm, obovate to elliptic, 3.5–12 cm × 2.7–7.5 cm, base attenuate to obtuse, apex obtuse or rounded, sometimes shortly acuminate or mucronate, both surfaces glossy. Inflorescence 10- to multiflorous. Flower with very short pedicel and stipe 1–2 cm; whitish to greenish; hypanthium patelliform, ± 5 mm in width; sepals ovate, 6–10 mm in length, apex obtuse to sub-acute; petals oblong, 5–8 mm in length, apex obtuse to acute; corona white, 4–8 mm in length, with tube 1–1.5 mm and filaments 3–7 mm; stamens 5, filaments 6–10 mm, united at base into a tube 1–1.5 mm, anthers 2–4 mm, with apicule ± 0.5 mm; gynophore 0.5–1 mm; ovary ellipsoid, 2–3 × 1.5–2 mm; styles ± 2.5 mm, united for about half their length, stigmas globose. Fruits 1–6 per inflorescence, with gynophore 2–10 mm, depressed-globose, 1.5–2.5 cm in diameter; valves (2–)3(–4), yellow to orange. Seeds 4–12 per capsule, 7.5–9.5 × 5–6.5 mm, blackish, coarsely foveolate, with ± 5 pits along the length.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon and the Democratic Republic of the Congo; very common in the western half of Gabon, apparently absent from Woleu-Ntem, Ogooué-Lolo, Haut-Ogooué and most of Ogooué-Ivindo.

## Ecology

Fallows, forests (swampy or not); in Gabon up to 1000 m in altitude. Flowering in April, May, October, November. Use: The roots yield a hunting poison used to impregnate arrows. b: De Wilde (1974a, 1974b), Robyns (1995). Bibliography
