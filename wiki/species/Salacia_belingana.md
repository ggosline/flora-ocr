# *Salacia belingana* Hallé, sp. nov.

**Genus**: [[Salacia]]
**Family**: [[Celastraceae]]

## Description

S. eleganti Welw. ex Oliv. affinis, sed ramis lenticellosis, foliis majoribus integribus, nervis secundaribus paucis et longe arcuatis, sepalis carnosis obtusis, ciliato-laciniosis, petalis excurvatis semi-urceolatis, staminibus brevibus, antheris apiculatis, stylo trifurcato, ovulis 2 juxtapositis in quoque loculo, fructo globoso ruguloso, ligno cum striis concentricis haud serratis, differt. Incolit in Gabonia.

## Type

Hallé 3121, Gabon (holo-, P !)

## Discussion

Liana exceeding 10–20 m, glabrous, rich in rubber filaments. Branches early lenticellate ± reddish-brown. Wood 35 mm in diameter with concentric parenchymatous striations not scalloped. Leaves almost all subopposite. Petiole 6–9(10) mm with straight margins of the channel. Lamina green when dry, glossy above, discolorous, elliptic, 8–14 × 3.5–6 cm, with acute or slightly obtuse base, with entire margins slightly bordered, with medium or short acumen. Secondary nerves 5–7 pairs regularly arched ascending; fine venation apparently not very dense.

Inflorescences in axillary glomerate clusters subsessile or sessile and few-flowered; on the glomerate cluster the small bracts are ciliate. Small globose buds (seen swollen). Pedicel green 3–4 mm, accrescent at fruiting time to 6–8 mm. Flower ochre-yellow 4 mm in diameter, semi-urceolate. Calyx 3 mm in diameter, with almost equal sepals, 1.2–1.4 mm broad, obtuse, ciliate or ± laciniate, fleshy in the middle. Petals curved above, ovate, about 1.7 × 1.5 mm. Disk narrow, low and very weakly cupuliform. Stamens short; anther 0.6 mm broad in chevron at right angles with a terminal apicule. Pistil somewhat knobbly and trilobed, with short style and trifurcate stigma; 2 ovules juxtaposed per locule; at least 1 to 3 ovules knobbly in the juvenile fruit.

Fruit green globose, rugose, possibly exceeding 22 mm in diameter, not seen at maturity, with ± persistent floral parts. Seeds 6 in one of the studied fruits. – Pl. 42, fig. 16–27, p. 147.

Endemic to Gabon, in dense forest of the Bélinga massif.
