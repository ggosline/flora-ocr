# *Stanfieldiella imperforata* (C.B.Clarke) Brenan Planche 27, Figure 6(D, E)

**Genus**: [[Stanfieldiella]]
**Family**: [[Commelinaceae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull. 14 : 284 (1960)

## Synonyms

- Buforrestia imperforata C.B.Clarke in A.DC. & C.DC., Monogr. phan . 3 : 233, t. 234 (1881).
- Buforrestia minor K.Schum. in Engl., Pflanzenw. Ost-Africas C : 136 (1895).

## Description

Herb erect or ascending, rarely decumbent; stem 15–20(–30) cm, internodes with fine pubescence extending to the upper sheath. Leaves spaced on the stem, then concentrated at the apex; sheath 1–2 cm long, with a line of dense pubescence on the suture and apex densely or sparsely covered with long pubescence (sometimes absent); pseudpetioles often ciliate; blade narrowly elliptic to elliptic, (3.5–)5–13(–18) × (1–)1.5–4(–4.5) cm, base cuneate, sometimes long-ciliate, apex acuminate, acute to attenuate, margins scabrous at the apex, faces discolorous, the lower tinged with purple, generally glabrous, rarely pilose. Inflorescence in a compound terminal thyrse, subsessile, sometimes with 1 to 3 additional axillary thyrses, moderately dense, ovoid to broadly ovoid, ± 3–5 cm wide, generally with up to 10 cincinni (rarely many more), each with up to 10 bisexual flowers; peduncle (of an individual inflorescence) up to 3.7 cm. Flower: pedicel 3–8 mm, strongly declined during fruiting, glabrous or with glandular pubescence 0.5–1 mm long; sepals narrowly elliptic-ovate to narrowly elliptic-oblong, 3.5–5 × 1–1.8 mm, glabrous or with glandular pubescence, sometimes reddish purple; petals elliptic to narrowly elliptic-obovate, 4–5 × 1.5–1.8 mm, white or rarely pink; stamens with filament ± 3.5 mm, anther ± 0.5 mm long; style 2.5–3 mm, curved at the apex. Fruit cylindric or slightly trigonous, (4.5–)6–9(–10) × 1.5–2 mm, clearly longer than the calyx, glabrous or with glandular pubescence, dark brown shining, sometimes becoming dull grey-brown after dehiscence, with 5 to 10 seeds per locule. Seed compressed dorsiventrally, cubic to transversely ellipsoidal, 0.7–1.3 × 0.7–1 mm; testa white or greyish-pink, smooth, farinose or not; hilum linear-oblong.

## Distribution

Sierra Leone, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, São Tomé and Príncipe, Equatorial Guinea (Bioko and Río Muni), Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Central African Republic, Uganda, Ethiopia, Tanzania and Angola; in Gabon in Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué, Ngounié, Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

Dense humid forest, often in open situations such as clearings and roadsides, often along streams, sometimes in rocky places; in Gabon from 15 to 800 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1275 m. Flowering from March to May, November and December; flowers opening in the morning. Plant very common

## Source note

Brenan (1960) distinguished var. glabrisepala (De Wild.) Brenan as having glabrous sepals and pedicel, whereas var. imperforata shows glandular pubescence on these parts. Some specimens collected in Gabon have glabrous sepals and pedicels, others with considerable glandular pubescence, but we have also observed intermediates. We therefore choose not to distinguish the two varieties. A complete revision of the genus at the African scale remains necessary, and could lead to the synonymization of var. glabrisepala.
