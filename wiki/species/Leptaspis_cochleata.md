# *Leptaspis cochleata* Thwaites.

**Genus**: [[Leptaspis]]
**Family**: [[Gramineae]]
**Protologue**: Thwaites, Enum. Pl. Zel.: 357 (1864)

## Synonyms

- L. conchifera Hack.
- Lepidaspis conchifera A. Chev. (par erreur).

## Description

Perennial plant with prostrate stem at the base and rooting at the lower nodes, reaching up to 1 m tall. Leaves with tessellate venation, sheaths longer than the internodes, imbricate and shortly pubescent on the dorsal axis. Ligule membranous, short, ciliate at the apex. Blades oblong-lanceolate, acute at both ends, distinctly petiolate, 10–20 and sometimes up to about thirty centimetres long and 3 to 6 cm wide. Secondary veins oblique, approximately 5 main veins on each side of the primary vein. Inflorescence paniculate, branches subverticillate, except toward the apex, the lower ones reaching up to about twenty centimetres long. Axes of the inflorescence and racemes shortly pubescent. Male spikelets 3 to 4 mm long, glumes acute, 1.5 mm long, without apparent veins. Lemma with 5 tessellate veins, lanceolate and finely pubescent like the glumes. Palea linear, with 2 veins; 6 stamens. Female spikelets finely pubescent, hairs of the lemma curved into a hook. Glumes ovate, with a single vein, abruptly acuminate. Lemma swollen, coriaceous, reaching 5 mm long, with 5 prominent veins converging on the ventral surface around a small circular opening from which emerge the 3 feathery branches of the style. Palea linear, narrow. Species frequent in understory in forested zones of Africa. Also in Malaysia and in the Pacific.
