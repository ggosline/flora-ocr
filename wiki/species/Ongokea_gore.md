# *Ongokea gore* (Hua) Pierre

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Ongokea]]

Bull. Soc. Linn. Paris **2**: 1314 (1897).

## Description

**Habit**: dioecious tree, 8–40 m tall, with cylindrical and straight stem, without buttresses, sometimes lobed at the base. Crown narrow. Rhytidome grey to reddish-brown, cracked, peeling into small plates on older specimens. Bark 1–2 cm thick, pulverizing in section, yellowish to ochre on cross-section becoming reddish-brown when dry, peeling from the sapwood through a surface that proliferates in the wood by longitudinal ridges. Branches with 2 wings, compressed, grey or greenish.

**Leaves**: petiole glabrous, 5–10 mm long, canaliculate above and wrinkled beneath. Blade glabrous on both faces, dark green ± glossy above, lighter green dull beneath, elliptic, 4–11 × 2–5 cm; base cuneate or narrowed, apex shortly acuminate, obtuse. Midrib concave above, strongly prominent and striated beneath. Secondary nerves 6–10 pairs, ascending, little or not arched, anastomosing 1–2 mm from the leaf margin, effaced or little prominent above, little prominent beneath. Nervules in a very lax network, little prominent beneath.

**Inflorescence**: raceme of coryumbs or umbels, many-flowered, axillary (subtending leaf quickly caducous). Inflorescence rachis 5–15 cm long, lateral branches 1–3 cm long; umbel peduncle 0.5–0.7 cm long, in the axil of a large bract. Flower bud green, cylindrical (sometimes square in section), swollen at the apex, 1–2.5 mm long.

**Flowers**: tetramerous, yellow or green. Calyx cupuliform with 4 glabrous sepals on both faces, fused at the base. Petals linear, glabrous on both faces, valvate with linear claw, reflexed and rolled, 3 × 0.5 mm; apex acute. Glands 4, alternipetalous, fleshy, yellowish-green. Stamens 4, epipetalous, fused in a tube by the filaments and anthers; stamen tube 3 mm long; anthers with 2 locules opening by annular dehiscence slits, valves bending back towards the base of the locules. Ovary superior, glabrous, unilocular with 1–2 pendulous ovules from a central erect placenta. Style slender, 2.5 mm long, ending in a pointed stigma.

**Fruit**: solitary, enclosed in an accrescent calyx surrounding the drupe almost entirely except at the apparent apex. Calyx opening by 2–3 valves. Exocarp smooth, yellow, areolate at the apex. Mesocarp fleshy, covering the endocarp except at the apex. Endocarp hard, 2 mm thick, longitudinally striated. Seed solitary, globose or ovoid, yellow. Albumen white and oily. Embryo apical or subapical, elongate.

## Type

HOLOTYPE: *Dybowski 103*, Achouka, Central African Republic (P!).

## Distribution

**Range**: from Côte d'Ivoire to Zaire (Democratic Republic of the Congo). **Gabon**: Estuaire (Libreville region, Île de l'Éléphant at Ipassa near Makokou); Haut-Ogooué (Lastoursville); Nyanga (Tchibanga, Pera); Ogooué-Ivindo (Donghila). Also recorded from an unspecified locality and the Mondah forest.

## Habitat and ecology

Dense evergreen forest and inundated forest. Flowering from December to June; fruits ripening from January.

## Properties and uses

The wood is yellowish, hard and homogeneous; used for construction, joinery and carpentry. The bark contains 16% catechol tannin; used as a purgative or to facilitate childbirth. The fruit contains 60% of a dichroic, drying oil with a fishy odour (potentially having uses similar to linseed oil); children use it as a spinning top. The seed is edible in the fresh state; used as bait for hunting porcupines and palm rats.
