# *Oncinotis hirta* Oliv. Planche 78(3), 82

**Genus**: [[Oncinotis]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Hooker’s Icon. Pl. 13 : 25, t. 1232 (1877)

## Description

Vine reaching 25 m in height; branches with dense reddish-brown pubescence. Leaf: densely covered above with ramified hairs with several orders; below elliptic to obovate, 1–8 times as long as wide, 0.5–2 cm, rounded to cuneate at the base, generally acuminate at the apex, covered with diversely ramified hairs most densely along the nerves and the margin; secondary nerves 5 to 9 pairs, tertiary venation rather discrete; domatia absent or discrete, formed of a few hairs. Inflorescence of 3.1–10.7 × 1–5.5 cm; peduncle and axes with very dense rust-brown tomentum; bracts long of 1–1.4 mm. Flower: pedicel of 1–2.5 mm; calyx with lobes 1–1.5 times as long as wide of 1.2–1.8 × 0.8–1.1 mm with dense brown tomentum on the exterior; corolla in mature bud long of 4.1–5.9 mm, yellow to yellowish-green, tube ellipsoid, 1–1.5 times as long as the lobes of the calyx of 1.5–2.2 mm pubescent

## Distribution

from Cameroon to Uganda and Angola; in Gabon: Ngounié, Nyanga and Ogooué-Lolo. Ecology: old forest and secondary forests near rivers; in Gabon up to 750 m altitude; elsewhere up to 1000 m. Use: The latex is said to be a cicatrizant for wounds. B: de Kruif (1985), Omino (2002), Raponda-Walker & Sillans (1961). BIBLIOGRAPHY
