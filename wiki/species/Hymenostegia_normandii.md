# *Hymenostegia normandii* Pellegrin

**Genus**: [[Hymenostegia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Pellegrin, Bull. Soc. Bot. Fr., 89 : 246 (1942); Lég. Gabon; 89 (1948)

## Description

Small trees. Leaves 4-5 jugate. Rachis 10-20 cm. Stipules persistent, very well developed, 3-4 cm long; lanceolate acute, very unequal at the base, one side of which is extended into a volute. Leaflets obovate oblong or obovate elliptic, acuminate, with very unequal base, externally auriculate and rounded, internally acute, glabrous, 5-9 cm long by 2.5-4.5 cm wide. Secondary nerves and nervules forming a finely prominent network on both faces.

Axillary racemes of violet flowers, lax, 8-9 cm. Axes somewhat pubescent. Pedicels ca. 1 cm, somewhat pubescent. Bracts lanceolate, acute, glabrous, 15 mm, caducous. Bracteoles opposite, ovate, somewhat cordate at the base, violet, glabrous, 1-1.5 cm, inserted 1-2 mm below the calyx tube. Calyx with 4 elliptic lobes, obtuse, 5 mm, somewhat ciliate; tube funnel-shaped, 5 mm. Petals 3, narrow, spatulate, 10 × 4 mm, glabrous. Stamens 10, inserted at the margin of the calyx tube. Ovary fixed at the base of the calyx tube by a short stipe, somewhat villous on the margins.
