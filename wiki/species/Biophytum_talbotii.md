# *Biophytum talbotii* (Baker f.) Hutch. & Dalziel

**Genus**: [[Biophytum]]
**Family**: [[Oxalidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fl . W . trop . Afr. , éd. 1, 1 : 140 (1927)

## Description

Subshrub 8–15 cm tall; stem simple, pubescent, bearing leaf scars at the apex. Leaves 2.5–7 cm long, arranged in a ± dense rosette at the apex; petiole and rachis pubescent; leaflets 14–18(–20) pairs, subsessile, with papyraceous blade, obliquely rectangular to falciform, 6–10 × 2–3 mm, proximal side oblong to obovate, distal side triangular-oblong, glabrous above, sparsely puberulent below, base truncate, apex rounded to mucronulate; lateral veins 8–12(–16) pairs, finely prominent, forming an acute angle with the midvein. Inflorescence: peduncle 0.5–1 cm long, pubescent; bracts lanceolate, (3–)4–8(–12) mm long, pubescent, 1-nerved, apex acuminate for 1–5 mm. Flower: pedicel 5–10(–12) mm, articulate above the insertion, pubescent; sepals lanceolate, 3–4(–5) mm long, pubescent above, 3–7 nerved, apex acuminate; petals 5–6 mm long, connate at the base, pink to white, retuse at the apex; filaments in one long cycle of 5 mm, one cycle of 3 mm. Capsule oblong, ± 2 mm long. Seeds 3–5, flattened, 1.5 mm long.

## Distribution

Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon and the Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, collected in the provinces of Ogooué-Ivindo and Nyanga

## Ecology

river margins in riparian forests, forest galleries, sand banks periodically inundated. B: Hutchinson & Dalziel (1927, 1954), Wilczek (1958). BIBLIOGRAPHY
