# *Tricalysia idiura* N. Hallé, sp. nov.

**Genus**: [[Tricalysia]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]

## Description

Small slender shrub with juvenile branchlets puberulous. Stipules tubulous for less than 1 mm, attenuate for 1-2 mm. Petiole slender, puberulent, 2-3 mm long. Lamina thin, olivaceous, glabrous, 5-10 × 1.9-3 cm, acute at base, strongly acuminate at apex, margin slightly bullate. Midrib glabrous and sulcate above. 5-6 pairs of secondary nerves. Fine reticulum moderately apparent. Nerve axils glabrous.

Inflorescences axillary, pauciflorous, minute, 3-6 mm, glabrous. Buds green, acute. Calyx sessile, glabrous, with thin cupule 0.5-0.8 mm high, truncate, 7-8 denticulate. Corolla white, with tube 6 mm long (5), with 4 acute lobes 3 × 1.3 mm, distal part of upper surface pubescent. Stamens exsert, with very short filament, submedifix. Style almost glabrous, white, bifid, 4 mm long. Disc thick, truncate-conical, glabrous. Ovary glabrous, smooth, turbinate, covered by a deep bracteolar cupule, 1 mm high. 2 locules; each with 2 (?) ovules.

Type and only known material: N. Hallé and J.-F. Villiers 5133 (P), Nkan NE of Méla, Monts de Cristal (flowers, 9 Feb. 1969).

Note: The insufficiency of material does not permit us to affirm the relationship of this species with T. Gossweileri.
