# *Elatostema paivaeanum* Wedd. Planche 25

**Genus**: [[Elatostema]]
**Family**: [[Urticaceae]]
**Protologue**: E. gabonense H.Schröt., Repert. Spec. Nov . Regni Veg. 47 : 217 (1939)

## Description

in DC., Prodr. 16(1): 178 (1869). E. gabonense H.Schröt., Repert. Spec. Nov. Regni Veg. 47: 217 (1939). Perennial herb with creeping rhizomes, sometimes flowering probably as an annual; stem erect, up to 50(–100) cm tall, generally simple, pubescent in the upper part. Stipules weakly dimorphous, those of normal leaves somewhat shorter and broader than those of reduced leaves, linear-lanceolate, (5–)6–9(–12) mm long, with apex long-acuminate with a filiform apex, glabrescent, brownish, transparent when dry, often with traces of two median nerves. Leaf: petiole absent or rarely up to 5 mm; lamina obliquely and broadly obovate to elliptic, (3.5–)7–12(–14) cm long, laterally asymmetric, proximal side 0.8–1.5 cm wide, distal side 1–2.5 cm wide, base asymmetrically cuneate or distal side subcordate, apex acute to acuminate, margin serrate on the distal side, with (12–)16 to 22 teeth, fewer on the proximal side, upper surface glabrous or sometimes sparsely hispid, with numerous linear cystoliths, clearly visible above, at least when dry, lower surface pubescent to strigose on the nerves; lateral nerves 5 or 6, prominent on the wider (distal) side, 3 or 4 on the proximal side, basal lateral nerve on the distal side not reaching half the length of the lamina. Male and female inflorescences frequently on the same stem, axillary, sessile or subsessile; the male ones up to 1.5 cm in diameter, with broadly ovate bracts, 3–5 mm wide, acute to acuminate, ciliate; the female ones 1–1.2 cm in diameter, with numerous flowers, with lanceolate bracts, 1–2 mm wide, ciliate. Male flowers: pedicel ± 2 mm; bracteoles lanceolate, ± 1 mm wide, ciliate; perianth ± 1.5 mm in diameter, 4-merous; tepals glabrescent, with a subapical appendage of ± 1(–2) mm long. Female flowers: pedicel short, accrescent during or after anthesis; bracteoles linear-lanceolate, densely pilose, giving the whole inflorescence a greyish appearance; perianth reduced to 3 or 4 small ciliate lobes; staminodial scales approximately as long as the achene; ovary ± 0.8 mm long; stigma penicillate. Fruit ± 1 mm long, with pedicel of ± 1 mm, pale brown, smooth or with slight discontinuous longitudinal sculptures or longitudinal lines.

## Distribution

widely distributed in tropical Africa, from Guinea to Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon and Republic of the Congo, recorded with doubt from the Democratic Republic of the Congo (Hauman 1948b, Letouzey 1968); also known from eastern tropical Africa, Tanzania and Malawi; in Gabon, known from Ngounié, Ogooué-Ivindo, and perhaps from Nyanga (see Notes)

## Ecology

humid lowland forests, altitudinal transition forests, forming on the ground a part of the humid soil cover, often along rivers; in Gabon at 650–700 m, elsewhere up to 1600 m

## Source note

The type specimen of E. gabonense, Thollon 1261, shows on the label: "Gabon, forests of Mayombe" and "Congo". It is not possible to specify further the locality of the type in relation to current political boundaries. In fact, it is more likely that the plant was collected in Republic of the Congo, and thus this single record for Nyanga remains doubtful.
