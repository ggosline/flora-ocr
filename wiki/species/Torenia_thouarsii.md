# *Torenia thouarsii* (Cham. & Schltdl.) Kuntze Planche 50, 54(M–N)

**Genus**: [[Torenia]]
**Family**: [[Scrophulariaceae]]
**Modern family**: [[Linderniaceae]] — *Scrophulariaceae* as circumscribed here is superseded; see [[Scrophulariaceae]]
**Protologue**: Rev. gen. pl . 2 : 468 (1891)

## Description

Annual herb; stem prostrate to ascending, with numerous ramifications (paraclades), 40 cm in length, glabrous. Leaf: petiole reaching 4–9 mm; lamina oval to lanceolate, 23–30 × 9–14 mm, acuminate at apex. Inflorescence: each floriferous node bearing 2 flowers. Flower: pedicel 7–9 mm; calyx 5–6 mm in length, with acuminate lobes 1 mm in length; corolla with tube 6 mm in length, white to pink with pale purplish-violet spots, upper lip entire and 3–4 mm in length, lower lip tripartite and 4 mm in length; filaments of anterior (abaxial) stamens 3–4 mm, with indistinct appendage in the form of a gibbosity, filaments of posterior (adaxial) stamens 1.5–2 mm; ovary 3–4 mm in length; style 6–7 mm. Fruit elongate, ovoid, 7–8 mm in length.

## Distribution

Senegal, Mali, Guinea-Conakry, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Togo, Nigeria, São Tomé and Príncipe (Príncipe), Cameroon, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Rwanda, Burundi, Sudan, Ethiopia, Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, Angola, Zambia, Malawi, Zimbabwe, Mozambique, Botswana, South Africa, Madagascar and Mauritius; also in India, Burma and South America; in Gabon, observed in Estuaire, Haut-Ogooué, Moyen-Ogooué, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

frequented places, fields, Cyperus marshes, secondary ombrophilous forests; in Gabon, at 0–530 m altitude, in Rwanda, up to 2000 m. B: Fischer (1992, 1995, 1999), Ghazanfar et al. (2008), Hepper (1963), Philibliographie cox (1990)
