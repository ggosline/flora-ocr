# *Begonia subscutata* De Wild. Planche 52

**Genus**: [[Begonia]]
**Family**: [[Begoniaceae]]
**Protologue**: Ann. Mus. Congo , sér. 5 ( Bot. ) 2 : 322 (1908)

## Description

Ann. Mus. Congo, sér. 5 (Bot.) 2: 322 (1908). Epiphyte; woody stems, reaching 7 mm in diameter and 2.5 m in length. Stipules ± persistent, 9–19 × 2–5 mm. Leaves subpeltate; petiole 3–25(–35) mm; lamina symmetric to asymmetric, fleshy, narrowly elliptic to ovate or obovate, 2–12 × 1–7 cm, base subcordate to cuneate, apex acute to acuminate or sometimes cuspidate, margins entire, undulate or loosely crenulate, lower surface dull, with dense tomentose indumentum of tawny or beige hairs, veins scarcely distinct. Inflorescence unisexual, rarely bisexual; the ♂ 3–7(–19)-flowered, peduncle 5–25(–30) mm; the ♀ 1–3(–7)-flowered, with peduncle 1–3 mm; the bisexual ones similar to ♀ but with 1 or 2 male flowers. ♂ flower: pedicel 7–14 mm; outer tepals elliptic to obovate, 5–8(–10) × 4–6 mm, the inner ones 4–8 × 1–2 mm; androecium nearly actinomorphic, stamens 6–12, column 1 mm, anthers subsessile with longitudinal dehiscence. ♀ flower sessile; tepals similar to those of ♂ flowers; ovary 10–20 × 2 mm, circular in cross-section, base narrowed; styles (2–)3(–4), free parts subulate, rarely bifid, stigmatic zone terminal, linear, entire, 1.5–2 mm. Infructescence generally with 1–3 fruits. Fruit 20–35 × 3–4 mm, opening by a longitudinal slit.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Congo (Brazzaville) and Democratic Republic of the Congo

## Ecology

evergreen forests and semi-deciduous forests, primary and secondary; up to 1100 m in altitude

## Vernacular names

sanga-batsema (Bakota). B : Wilczek (1969a), de Wilde (2002). IBLIOGRAPHIE KE EWALD Pays-Bas – Wageningen branche
