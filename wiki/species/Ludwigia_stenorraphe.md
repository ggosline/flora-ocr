# *Ludwigia stenorraphe* subsp. *stenorraphe* (Brenan) H.Hara

**Family**: [[Onagraceae]] · **Genus**: [[Ludwigia]]

J. Jap. Bot. **28**(10): 294 (1953).

## Description

Shrub or sometimes herb, branched and woody at base, to 2(–3) m, pubescent to densely hirsute with tawny or grey hairs. Leaves subsessile; petiole 0–4 mm; blade linear-lanceolate to narrowly obovate-elliptic, 2.5–13 × 0.3–3 cm, lower surface glaucous grey-green and long-haired, sometimes nearly woolly; lateral veins 9–15 pairs. Flowers solitary; pedicel 1–10 mm; sepals 4, 4–10 × 1.5–3 mm, hirsute; petals 4, 7–13 × 4–12 mm, notched and yellow; stamens 8; ovary 8–10 × 0.8–1 mm; style 2–4 mm. Fruit cylindrical, 19–30 × 1.5–3 mm, reddish or purple, eight-ribbed and hirsute on dehiscence lines, with several seed rows. Seed 0.7–0.8 × 0.4 mm, finely reticulate; raphe about one-fifth of seed width.

## Distribution

Continental tropical Africa from Senegal to Sudan and south to Angola and Zambia. In Gabon recorded from Estuaire, Nyanga and Ogooué-Ivindo.

## Habitat and ecology

Marsh grassland, floodplains, stream margins and savanna; to 250 m in Gabon and 1200 m elsewhere.

## Notes

One of four recognized subspecies in an African polyploid complex; the other three occur in East Africa. The plant is reportedly used in veterinary medicine.
