# *Aeschynomene lateritia* Harms Planche 5

**Genus**: [[Aeschynomene]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 26 : 292 (1899)

## Synonyms

- Bakerophyton lateritium (Harms) Hutch. ex Maheshw., Taxon 16 : 238, 464 (1967).

## Description

Annual herb; stem erect, prostrate or trailing, 0.3–1 m in length, rather slender, base suffrutescent; indumentum finely pubescent, hairs erect, yellowish. Stipules elliptic-ovate to narrowly elliptic-ovate, non- or unilaterally appendiculate, 3–9 × 1–3 mm, with serrate-ciliate margins. Leaf: petiole ± 3 mm; rachis 1–3.5 cm; leaflets in 7 to 20 pairs, with oblong lamina, 3–8 × 1–2 mm, base obtuse to rounded, upper part denticulate-ciliate, glabrous. Inflorescence in axillary or pseudoterminal pseudoraceme, slender; axis 1.5–8(–10) cm, markedly zigzag; bracts trifid to tripartite, 2–3 × 1.5–2 mm, teeth serrate-ciliate. Flower: pedicel erect then recurved, filiform, 3–8(–10) mm; bracteoles lanceolate, 3–3.5 × 0.5–0.7 mm; calyx bilabiate, tube almost absent, upper lobe with 2 teeth, lower lobe with 3 teeth, lobes 3 × 1–1.5 mm, ciliate; corolla glabrous, yellow-orange to rose-mauve or cream, standard oblong-rectangular, 2.5–3 × 1.5–2.2 mm, veined, wings obovate to elliptic, 2–3 × 1 mm, keel petals subrombic, 2.5–3 × 1 mm; stamens alternately long 1.5 mm and short 1.2 mm, with anthers respectively large sub-basifix and minute dorsifix; ovary ovate, glabrous, with 2 ovules, on stipe 0.5 mm; style 1 mm, persistent. Legume with 1 (or 2) suborbicular articles 1–1.5 × 1–1.2 mm, densely verrucose at maturity, included in the persistent bracteoles and calyx, indehiscent.

## Distribution

Ghana, Côte d'Ivoire, Bénin, Nigéria, Cameroun, République du Congo, République démocratique du Congo, Burundi, Angola; to be searched for in Gabon

## Ecology

arboreal savannas, flooded savannas, roadsides, cultivated land, fallows, inselbergs, often on sand; reaching at least 700 m. b: Léonard (1954b). Bibliography
