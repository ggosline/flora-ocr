## Description

Bull. Jard. Bot. Etat Brux. 4: 386 (1914). – Boutique, Fl. Congo Belge 2: 311 (1951). – Tisserant et Sillans, Not. Syst. 15: 330 (1958).

Sarmentose shrub or liana reaching 20 m in height, 3–5 cm in diameter at the base, with circular section. Rhytidome smooth, bark with black section then orange, granular and hard. Twigs and branchlets hirsute reddish-brown. Leaves with petiole 3–6 cm long, hirsute; lamina papyraceous, elliptic-oblong to obovate, 8–23 cm long, 3.5–11 cm wide, rounded to subcordate at the base, acuminate at the apex; upper surface glabrous, lower surface sparsely appressed-pubescent with long stiff hairs. Midvein impressed above, prominent and hirsute below. Lateral nerves 9–12 pairs, obliquely ascending, joined in loops well away from the margin, pubescent and prominent below. Nervules forming a second row of smaller loops near the margin.

Inflorescences extra-axillary or opposite the leaves, in cymes generally uniflorous, with axis and peduncle curved in a hook shape, hirsute, 0.8–1.8 cm long. Flowers ochre yellow, with short pedicel, 3–3.5 mm long, hirsute. Sepals triangular acute, 5–7 mm long, 3–4 mm wide, hirsute. Petals subequal, 13–20 mm long, longitudinally canaliculate, reddish-brown hirsute on the outside, appressed-pubescent with short hairs on the inside; the outer ones lanceolate acute, approximately 4 mm wide, with enlarged rounded base, concave and glabrous on the inside; the inner ones lanceolate subulate, narrower, 2 mm wide, with enlarged concave base and ± unguiculate, free in the upper 2/3, fused just above the base, forming a cavity that partially covers the reproductive organs. Stamens very numerous, approximately 70, 5-seriate, cuneiform, 1.5–2 mm long, anthers extrorse, connectives dilated and truncate above the anthers. Carpels 8–10, ± cylindric, 1.3–1.5 mm long, glabrous except at the base, styles oblong, 1–1.2 mm long, glabrous, curved toward the outside. 2 basal ovules. Receptacle hirsute.

Fruits red at maturity, with pedicel 3–4 mm long, sepals persistent; mericarps ellipsoid to ± fusiform, 1.2–1.5 cm long, 0.5–0.6 cm wide, apiculate at the apex, glabrous. Seeds ellipsoid-oblong, 1–1.2 cm long, 0.5 cm wide, with thin testa.

## Type

Malchair 274,Likimi, Congo-Kinshasa (holotype BR!)

## Discussion

Species of dense humid forest, frequent at the edge of streams. Widespread in Cameroon, Gabon, Central African Republic and Congo-Kinshasa.

This species is very close to A. rupestris Diels, but in the East African species, the leaves are coriaceous, ovate with rounded base, the sepals shorter, the petals thicker, cylindric blunt, the style longer.

Use: water liana.
