# *Sauvagesia africana* (Baill.) Bamps Planche 26(7–11)

**Genus**: [[Sauvagesia]]
**Family**: [[Ochnaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. Afr. centr., Ochnac. : 58 (1967)

## Synonyms

- Vausagesia africana Baill., Bull. Soc. Linn. Paris 2 : 871 (1890).

## Description

Perennial herb to subshrub, rhizomatous, 10–40(–60) cm tall; stem erect, slender, simple or sometimes branched. Stipules 3–8 mm long. Leaf: petiole 0–2 mm; blade narrowly elliptic-obovate to narrowly elliptic-obovate, 2–4(–5) cm × 2–12 mm, progressively narrower and more elongate toward the stem apex, attenuate to cuneate at the base, obtuse to acute-apiculate at the apex, margin crenate-serrate; secondary veins prominent above. Flowers axillary, at the axil of reduced leaves, clustered at the stem apex, in unipary cymes 4–10 cm long or sometimes solitary; pedicel (3–)6–10 mm, articulate at the base; sepals narrowly elliptic-ovate, 3–6 × 1–1.5 mm, acuminate; petals obovate, 5–8 × 3–4.5 mm, pink or reddish, with white or yellow base; stamens with filament 0.2 mm, anthers oblong, 1.5–2 mm; staminodes 5, in 1 whorl, petaloid, narrowly elliptic-ovate, 3–5 × 1 mm, slightly connate at the base; ovary ovoid, 1.5–2 mm long; style 2–3 mm. Fruit ovoid, 4–6 × 4–5 mm, with acuminate valves. Seeds ellipsoid, 0.8 × 0.5 mm, foveolate, brown.

## Distribution

Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Uganda and Zambia; in Gabon, rare and known only from Haut-Ogooué (Plateaux Batéké).

## Ecology

Swampy savannas, esobé, marshes, lake and river margins; in Gabon, at 400 m altitude, elsewhere at 350–1500 m. b: Bamps & Farron (1967), Robson (1963), Sastre (1971), Verdcourt (2005). ibliography
