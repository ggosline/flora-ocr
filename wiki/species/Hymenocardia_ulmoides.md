# *Hymenocardia ulmoides* Oliv. Planche 9(A-E)

**Genus**: [[Hymenocardia]]
**Family**: [[Phyllanthaceae]]
**Protologue**: Hooker’s Icon . Pl . 12(2) : t. 1131 (1873)

## Description

Hooker's Icon. Pl. 12(2): t. 1131 (1873). Shrub to small tree up to 8 m tall; bark longitudinally fissured; branchlets hairy, glabrescent; young shoots and lower surface of very young leaves with numerous glandular discs. Stipules linear to narrowly elliptic, 4–6 mm long. Leaf: petiole 5–11 mm, hairy; blade ovate-elliptic, 1.5–6.5 × 1–3 cm, acute or obtuse at base, acuminate at apex, glabrous or ± hairy along the midvein above, often bearing tufts of hairs at the axil of the 4–8 pairs of lateral veins and with very rare or absent glandular discs below. Inflorescence axillary, solitary, 1–3 cm long. Male flower: (4–)5(–8)-merous; calyx emarginate or divided to the middle, lobes ciliate; pistillode ± bilobed and ciliate at apex, ± 1 mm long. Female flower: pedicel 1 mm, reaching 4–7 mm in fruit, glabrous; sepals (4–)5–6(–7), slightly connate at base, 2 × 0.5 mm, ciliate; ovary suborbicular, truncate or retuse at apex, entirely winged, 1 mm diameter, glabrous; styles 5 mm, accrescent to 1.5 cm. Fruit samaroid, indehiscent, obovate-elliptic, (1.2–)1.8–2.5 × (1–)1.5–2.2 cm, winged all around the margin, emarginate at apex; wings 3.5 mm wide. Seeds semi-ovate, (5–)7–8 × 2–3 mm.

## Distribution

from Cameroon, the Central African Republic, Gabon and the Republic of the Congo to Tanzania to the east and the South African Republic (Transvaal, Natal) and Angola to the south; in Gabon, known from the northeast, in the provinces of Haut-Ogooué, Ogooué-Ivindo and Woleu-Ntem.

## Ecology

montane forest, often saxicolous, forest galleries; in Gabon, at 400–900 m altitude. Uses: The bark is used for tanning hides. It is also used in decoction for its astringent properties. The wood serves for indigenous construction.

## Vernacular names

lesser heart fruit, red heart fruit, small red-heart (angl.). b : Leonard & Mosango (1985), Pax & Hoffman (1922). ibliographie
