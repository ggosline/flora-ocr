# *Tetraberlinia bifoliolata* (Harms) Hauman

**Genus**: [[Tetraberlinia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Hauman, Mém. Ac. Roy. Belg. 23 : 51 (1952)

## Synonyms

- Berlinia bifoliolata Harms, Engl. Bot. Jahrb. 30 : 83 (1904); Pellegrin, Lég. Gabon : 62 (1948); de Saint-Aubin, For. Gabon : 62 (1963).
- Julbernardia bifoliolata (Harms) Troupin.

## Description

Ekaba (Pl. 75, p. 297)

— de Saint-Aubin, For. Gabon : 62 (1963). — Letouzey et Mouranche, Ekop Cam. : 17, pl. 1 (1952).

Trees. Leaves unijugate, with oblong leaflets, crescent-shaped, acuminate more or less shortly, subsessile, 10-16 cm long, 4.5-5.5 cm wide, glabrous. Midvein arched. About ten secondary veins, joining in loops ± far from the margin; reticulate veinlets conspicuous. Petiole very short, 3-4 mm.

Dense terminal panicles, velvety. Flowers sessile. Bracts suborbicular. 2 valvate bracteoles velvety, keeled, 9-10 mm long. Receptacle short. Sepals 4, subequal, one of them broader, ovate, often emarginate, the others lanceolate, 6 mm long. Petals 5: one large shortly clawed; blade 13-14 mm in length; the 4 others linear, spatulate. Stamens 10; 9 slightly connate at the base, 1 free. Ovary stipitate, with hirsute margin; 4 ovules.

Pod flat, light brown, woody, 10-12 × 5-6 cm with thickened sutural border, prolonged on each side by a ridge; marked by a longitudinal furrow toward the middle and by transverse furrows.

## Type

Zenker 2275 (isotype P)

## Discussion

Large tree able to reach 1.5 m diameter and 20 m tall under branches. Base of trunk simply thickened by 3-4 slightly ascending buttresses. Trunk straight, cylindrical.

Bark smooth, grey, with brittle fracture, reddish to orange-yellow. Crown of older trees more or less umbrella-shaped.

Occurs in Cameroon in the Kribi region where it is sometimes very abundant. It is widespread throughout Gabon, sometimes in stands. It is also found in Mayumbé. Wood semi-hard and semi-heavy, light rosy brown.

## Vernacular names

Eko (Fang); Mondoungou (Bapounou); Ndala grandes feuilles (Bavoungou); Kogo (Mitsogo); Mukuru (Eshira); Pavola (Apindji); Ribi (nom pilote camerounais)
