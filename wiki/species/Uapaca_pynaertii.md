# *Uapaca pynaertii* De Wild. Planche 33E

**Genus**: [[Uapaca]]
**Family**: [[Phyllanthaceae]]
**Protologue**: Ann. Mus. Congo Belge, Bot. , sér. 5, 2 : 274 (1908)

## Description

Tree up to 40 m tall; trunk up to 70 cm diameter, with stilt roots at least to 4 m tall; branchlets glabrous. Stipules absent. Leaf glabrous; petiole cylindric, (1–)2–5(–13) cm; blade obovate, (14–)16–23(–42) × 6–8(–14) cm, cuneate at base, obtuse to retuse or acuminate to sometimes 1.5 cm at apex, very coriaceous, ± rigid when dry; lateral veins (7–)8–12 pairs, obscure above, prominent below. Inflorescence (1.5–)2–4 cm diameter; peduncle 3–5(–7) cm, glabrous; involucral bracts ± 10, elliptic, 1.5–2.5 × 1–1.5 cm, concave, thick, glabrous. Male flower: sepals ± free, unequal, narrowly oblong, 1–1.5 mm long, pubescent to glabrous; stamens 4–6(–7), ± 6 mm long, filaments pubescent to glabrous; pistillode obconic, lobulate at apex, 1–2 mm long, pubescent. Female flower unknown. Fruit with peduncle 1–2.5 cm, globose, 3–4 cm diameter, with 5 seeds, glabrous; fruiting calyx glabrous.

## Distribution

from Guinea to Ghana, and thereafter from Nigeria to Cameroon and Central African Republic to the east, Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo to the south; in Gabon, known from all provinces except Estuaire, Ngounié and Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology

primary and secondarized forest, sometimes in riparian forest; in Gabon, at 0–800 m altitude. Use and vernacular names: as in U. guineensis. b: Breteler (2012a, b), De Wildeman (1936), Pax & Hoffmann (1922). Bibliography
