# *Scleria melanomphala* Kunth Planche 136

**Genus**: [[Scleria]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Enum. pl. 2 : 345 (1837)

## Description

Robust perennial herb, with thick rhizome 4–6 mm; stem 60–180 cm × 2–6 mm (10 mm at the level of the sheaths), triangular, glabrous, scabrous on the angles. Leaf: sheath triangular, scabrous on the angles, the lowest reddish, without or with a very short blade, often densely pubescent, forming a ligule up to 10 mm long; blade 20–60 cm × 7–18 mm, flat, glabrous or pubescent, scabrous on the margin and the nerves. Inflorescence formed of a terminal panicle and 5–9 lateral ones, often pendulous; panicles lanceolate, 2–10 × 1–3 cm; peduncles up to 25 cm, usually 2–4 at the lower node of the panicle, flattened and winged, finely scabrous and often hairy above; bracts of the spikelets and of the groups of spikelets resembling the glumes, but

Male spikelet 10–13 mm long, with glumes straw-yellow usually with the margin dark reddish-brown and hairy, and a central green scabrous nerve exceeding the glume. Female spikelet 10–12 mm long, with glumes similar to those of the male spikelets. Achene ovoid, 3.7–4.2 × 2.7–3.2 mm, smooth, glabrous, bright white, but usually blue-blackish and with a small depression at the apex; hypogynium whitish or yellowish-brown, of irregular shape and without distinct lobes.

## Distribution

widespread in tropical Africa and South Africa, also present in Madagascar and South America; very rare or poorly known in Gabon, found only in Ogooué-Ivindo (1 collection)

## Ecology

marshes, lake and river banks, often forming large floating stands; in Gabon, at 100 m altitude, at 0–1800 m in East Africa. Taxonomic note: recognized by its numerous, narrow and dark panicles and the smooth ovoid achene; no closely related species in Gabon. Bibliography: Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al.
