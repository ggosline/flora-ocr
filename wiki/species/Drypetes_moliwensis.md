# *Drypetes moliwensis* Cheek & Radcl.-Sm.

**Genus**: [[Drypetes]]
**Family**: [[Putranjivaceae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull. 55: 895, 897, fig. 1 (2000)

## Description

Plate 48; Figure 10(C, D)

Small tree, up to ±9 m tall, sometimes tree up to ±28 m tall; trunk up to 21 cm in diameter, not fluted; bark yellowish-brown, with numerous lenticels, with strong mustard-oil odor; branchlets grayish, smooth, the youngest puberulent, glabrescent, with yellowish hairs; buds puberulent. Stipules caducous, narrowly triangular, ±3.4 mm long, puberulent. Leaf: petiole (7.3–12–17.5–(22.5) mm, strongly canaliculate above, slightly swollen, glabrescent; lamina elliptic-obovate to elliptic-ovate, (15.5–)24–29.5(−35) × (3.7–)8–10(−13.5) cm, coriaceous, with oblique base, cuneate, shortly acuminate at apex, with margin subentire to obscurely serrulate towards apex, plane to slightly revolute, sometimes undulate, glabrous on both surfaces, shiny and dark green above, paler below; venation weakly reticulate, midvein yellowish, secondary veins 7 to 14, ascending, slightly looped and anastomosing near margin, impressed above and slightly raised below. Male inflorescence in leaf axils or below leaves, with up to ±8 flowers; bracts ovate to triangular, (0.5–)0.6–0.8(−1) mm long, sparsely puberulent. Male flower with unpleasant odor; pedicel robust, (1.5–)2–3(−6) mm, finely hairy; sepals (4 or 5(or 6), imbricate, ovate to oblong, (2.6–)3.3–4(−4.5) × (1.3–)2–3.8(−4) mm, obtuse at apex, pale green to yellowish-green, sparsely hairy outside, hairy inside, with minute marginal cilia; stamens (8 to)10 to 13(to 18), arranged in a whorl around the edge of the disc, filament (0.7–)1–1.7(−2.8) mm, anther 1.1–1.4 mm long, disc glabrous 2–3(−3.6) mm in diameter, slightly convex, rugose, sometimes foveolate, with undulate margin, slightly salient between filaments, glabrous; pistillode up to 0.9 mm tall or absent. Female inflorescence like the male, with 1 or 2 flowers; bracts similar to males. Female flower pedicel 2.9–3.5 mm, robust, finely hairy; sepals 5, 4.3–5.7(−7) × 4.5–5.5(−7) mm, yellowish-green, similar to those of males; disc annular, (3–)3.4–6.3 mm in diameter, with slightly undulate margin, glabrous; ovary globose, 1-locular, glabrous; style up to 1.2 mm, unbranched to pulvinate-capitate stigma, rounded to oblong, 2.3–2.6(−3.5) mm in diameter. Fruit globose to ellipsoid, (17.5–)18–19.5(−20.5) × 13–14.5 mm, reddish when ripe,

glabrous, with persistent sepals and stigmas, 1-seeded; pedicel (4.6–)5.2–7(−8) mm, robust, shortly hairy, glabrescent. Seed (12.5–)13–15.5(−17.5) mm long.

## Distribution

Cameroon, mainland Equatorial Guinea and Gabon; in Gabon: Estuaire, Ogooué-Ivindo and Ogooué-Lolo

Habitat: evergreen forests, riparian forest; in Gabon at 250–460 m altitude, elsewhere up to 970 m.

## Source note

This species was mainly collected in central-eastern Gabon in the Ogooué basin. It has large elliptic-obovate to narrowly elliptic-ovate leaves, with margin obscurely serrulate towards apex, with base mainly cuneate and with main and secondary veins yellowish, often becoming blackish with drying. It resembles D. arborescens and D. similis (both with "open" sepals), and D. aylmeri (imbricate sepals smaller in smaller flowers, with fewer stamens), all with a unilocular ovary and capitate stigma. The petiole is generally long, robust, slightly swollen and strongly canaliculate above. In D. moliwensis, the stigma is sessile or subsessile and does not have a regular circular form seen from above, which is the case in all the aforementioned species, visible in the female flower and fruit.
