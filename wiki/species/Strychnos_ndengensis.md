# *Strychnos ndengensis* Pellegrin

**Genus**: [[Strychnos]]
**Family**: [[Loganiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Mus. Hist. Nat. Paris 33 : 267 (1927); Mém. Soc. Linn. Norm. n. ser. 1 : 37 (1928)

## Description

Climbing plant; branches with lenticels; small branches glabrous, somewhat lenticellate especially at the base, often somewhat furrowed when dry; tendrils solitary.

Leaves with petiole glabrous, 5-10 mm in length; blade coriaceous, elliptic or narrowly elliptic, 6-12 × 3-6 cm, briefly acuminate at the apex, cuneate at the base, glabrous on both faces; one pair of secondary nerves curved along the margin but not reaching the apex, and one pair less distinct submarginal; midvein at the base often somewhat impressed, but otherwise prominent above, as are the main secondary nerves; tertiary venation reticulate, scarcely distinct.

Inflorescences axillary, several grouped together, lax or rather contracted, 1.5-3 × 1.5-2 cm; peduncle, branches and pedicels glabrous. Flowers 4-merous. Sepals united to one-quarter their height, suborbicular, 1-1.2 mm in length, obtuse or rounded, finely ciliate, glabrous on both faces. Corolla, in the mature bud, 5.2-6 mm in length, white, glabrous outside, densely pilose inside but glabrous at the base of the tube; tube 2-3 mm in length; lobes 1-1.6 times longer than the tube, oblong, 3-3.3 × 1-1.2 mm, acute, recurved. Stamens well exserted, inserted at the throat of the corolla, with filament glabrous, 1-1.5 times longer than the anther; anther oblong, glabrous. Pistil glabrous, 4.5-6 mm in length; ovary ovoid, 1 × 0.6-0.9 mm, 2-locular; style slender, 3.5-5 mm in length; stigma small, capitate, as thick as the style; 5-6 ovules per locule.

Fruits unknown, presumed small and similar to those of S. floribunda. Pl. 34, 1-5, p. 141.
