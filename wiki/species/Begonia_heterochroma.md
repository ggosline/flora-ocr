# *Begonia heterochroma* Sosef

**Genus**: [[Begonia]]
**Family**: [[Begoniaceae]]
**Protologue**: Wageningen Agric. Univ. Papers 91-4 : 102 (1992)

## Description

Plant reaching 14 cm in height. Leaf peltate; petiole 1.5–16 cm, hirsute; blade often reniform, circular to broadly elliptic-ovate or elliptic-obovate, 3.5–13 × 2.5–7.5 cm, apex rounded, margin entire or sometimes denticulate, upper surface sparsely to densely hirsute. Inflorescence: peduncle 1–6(–10) cm, with 3–6 ♂ flowers and 1 ♀ flower with yellow or white tepals; bracts 1–4.5 mm. ♂ Flower: tepals 6.5–11.5 × 5–10.5 mm; androecium with 15–20 stamens. ♀ Flower: pedicel elongating in fruit to 1 mm; tepals 6–12 × 7–14 mm; ovary broadly obtriangular to obtriangular depressed, 3–10 × 6–14 mm, without long hairs, with 3 wings 1.5–5 mm wide; styles 3. Fruit broadly obtriangular to very broadly obtriangular-obovoid, 7.9–13 × 2.9–6.9 mm.

## Distribution

Cameroon (South), Gabon (Cristal Mountains)

## Ecology

primary and secondarized forests, sometimes in very shaded habitat, on slopes or flat ground, often along stream margins; 80–360 m altitude. B: Sosef (1992a), Sosef (1994). BIBLIOGRAPHY

ESSEL branch.
