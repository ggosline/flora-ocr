# *Manilkara le-* Testui Aubréville et Pellegrin.

**Genus**: [[Manilkara]]
**Family**: [[Sapotaceae]]
**Protologue**: Notulae Systematicae. Vol. XVI. Fasc. 3-4 : 233 (1960)

## Description

Leaves oblong elongate, attenuate at the apex, cuneiform at the base, 25–30 cm long, 6.5–8.5 cm wide, coriaceous, glabrous. About twenty secondary nerves, very prominent beneath, joining very near the margin. Network very apparent of parallel nervules perpendicular to the secondary nerves, and of veinules. Long petiole, ca. 4 cm.

Flowers in fascicles on older branchlets. Pedicels long, ca. 2 cm, and tomentose. Sepals 6, tomentose externally, ca. 7 mm long. Corolla with short tube 1.7 mm long. Lobes 6, each furnished with 2 appendages as long as the lobes, 6–7 mm long. Stamens 6 and staminodes 6 (sometimes 4–5), inserted at the height of petal insertion. Stamens with short filaments somewhat pubescent, bearing long anthers somewhat pubescent. Staminodes triangular laciniate on the margins and pubescent.

Ovary hirsute, locules 6 (4–5). Fruit unknown.

## Type

Le Testu 9322, Ngon, entre l'Ogooué et le Cameroun (fl. oct.)

## Discussion

Remarkable species, but very imperfectly known from a single specimen of Le Testu.
