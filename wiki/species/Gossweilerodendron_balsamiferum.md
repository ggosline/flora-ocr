# *Gossweilerodendron balsamiferum*

**Genus**: [[Gossweilerodendron]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Harms, Notizbl. Bot. Gart. Mus. Berlin 9 : 459 (1925)

## Synonyms

- Pterygopodium balsamiferum Vermœsen, Man. Ess. for. Congo Belge : 233 (1923).

## Description

(Vermoesen) Harms

Tola or Agba (Pl. 33, p. 149)

— de St-Aubin, For. Gabon : 68 (1963). — Hutch. et Dalz., F.W.T.A., 2e : 466 (1958).

Large resiniferous trees. Leaves with 6–10 alternate, oblong elliptic leaflets, with obtuse or obtusely acuminate apex, approximately 4–9 × 2–4 cm. Limb bordered by a marginal vein, rather papyraceous, riddled with translucent points, marked characteristically by a network of numerous secondary veins and anastomosing veinlets, traced to the margin, well-meshed and finely prominent on both surfaces. Petiolules sometimes slightly twisted, 3–4 mm.

Paniculate spiciform racemes of very small, whitish, subsessile flowers, slightly pubescent. Bractlets minute. Sepals 2 mm, with ciliate margins. Stamens with filaments 3 mm, slightly villous at base. Ovary villous, stipitate.

Samaroid pods, oblong, very briefly stipitate, marked by a loose network of veinlets, 10–14 cm in length by 3.5–4.5 cm in width. Seeds 2–3 cm in length.

## Type

Vermoesen, 1681, Temvo, Mayumbé (BR, isotype P)

## Discussion

Very large trees, with straight and cylindrical trunk to the ground. Bark longitudinally fissured, forming ± rectangular scales. Fibrous section, red. Sapwood fissured, secreting a greenish resin. Wood soft, light, pale yellowish brown, non-resinous, used in construction, joinery, and canoe manufacture.

The species is widespread from southern Nigeria to Congo.

In Gabon it occurs only east of Ndjolé. Tola is found

often in very fine stands of large trees, especially in the Mayombé.

## Vernacular names

Mbogou (Fang)

## Specimens examined

de St-Aubin : 1943, Makokou; 2031, Haut Obangué.
