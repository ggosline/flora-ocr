# *Olax triplinervia* Oliver

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Olax]]

FTA 1: 349 (1868).

## Synonymy

- *Neurolax autriana* Pierre, Bull. Soc. Linn. Paris 2: 1295 (1897)
- *Olax denticulata* Engl., Notizbl. bot. Gart. Berl. 2: 286 (1899)

## Description

Shrub 0.5–2.5 m tall. Branches greenish, ± strongly winged, ridged, striated. Leaves alternate, ± decussate. Petiole glabrous, 0–2 mm long, concave above, wrinkled below. Blade glabrous on both surfaces, dark green mat on upper surface, paler green on lower surface, oblanceolate, elliptic, oblong-elliptic or oblong, 8–20 × 3–7.5 cm; base cuneiform, narrowed, rounded; apex acuminate, apiculate, sometimes mucronate. Midvein concave above, prominent striated below. Secondary veins 4–6 pairs, ascending, arcuate, anastomosing far from the blade margin; the two basal veins anastomosing with the next pair at 1/2 or 3/4 of the blade length, concave above, prominent below. Venules forming a loose network, scarcely visible on upper surface, prominent on lower surface.

**Inflorescence**: Short many-flowered axillary racemes or fascicled 2–3 together. Rachis flattened, 0.6–2 cm long; flowers on the broad faces. Bracteoles 1 mm long, ovate or suborbicular, with midvein terminating in an apical point on the upper surface. Pedicel short, 1–1.5 mm long. Floral bud ellipsoid, 3.5 × 1.8 mm; petals valvate. Flower pentamerous, yellow-green, yellow-orange or red, 3 × 2 mm.

**Calyx**: Formed by sepals fused together in a glabrous cupule with undulate or entire upper margin, 0.75 × 2 mm.

**Petals**: Glabrous on both surfaces, linear-elliptic, 3 × 1 mm, fleshy, apex ± obtuse.

**Stamens**: 5, epipetalous, 2 mm long; filament strongly fused to petals, thickening from base to apex, oblong, wider than anthers, 1.25 × 0.60 mm; anther with 2 ellipsoid lobes, with longitudinal dehiscence slits.

**Staminodes**: 3, alternipetalous, 3 mm long; filament narrow, fused to lateral edges of petals, 2 × 0.5 mm, terminated by 2 blades fused at base, 1 mm long, inflated at apex or acute.

**Disc**: Surrounding base of ovary.

**Ovary**: Superior, glabrous, ovoid, 1.5 × 1.25 mm; base furrowed; 1 locule containing 3 pendulous ovules from an erect central placenta.

**Style**: Squat, 2.5 mm long, terminated by an obscurely trilobed capitate stigma.

**Fruit**: Calyx strongly accrescent surrounding the drupe except at apex with an opening 2–5 mm in diameter. Calyx globose, 11–16 mm in diameter, yellowish or reddish in colour with darker veinules. Drupe subglobose, 10 × 14 mm. Exocarp thin, yellow to reddish-brown. Endocarp hard, lignified, brown with meridional striae. Seed solitary, globose, depressed at apex; surface ± wrinkled in lower part; albumen abundant.

## Distribution

**Range**: West and central Africa.

The species is recorded from Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, and the Central African Republic. Specimens have been collected from localities including Ambam, Bipindi (Cameroon); Libreville, Mayumba, Makokou, Monts de Cristal, Atsié, Lastoursville (Gabon).

## Habitat and ecology

Shrub of primary forest and wooded savanna. Grows along riverbanks. Flowers November to June; fruits mature from January.

## Notes

The holotype is Mann s.n. from Mount John, Kongui River, Equatorial Guinea (K). Three synonyms are cited in the source; *Olax denticulata* is noted as having been used by both Engler and Sleumer.
