# *Olax triplinervia* Oliver

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Olax]]

FTA **1**: 349 (1868). — Engler, Pflanzenfam., ed. 1, Nachtr. 1: 146 (1897); Pflanzenw. Afr. 3: 78 (1915). — Sleumer, Pflanzenfam., ed. 2, 16b: 24 (1935). — Michaud, Mém. IFAN 75: 215, tab. 6 (1966).

## Synonymy

- *Neurolax autriana* Pierre, Bull. Soc. Linn. Paris 2: 1295 (1897)
- *Olax denticulata* Engl., Notizbl. bot. Gart. Berl. 2: 286 (1899); Pflanzenfam., l.c.

## Description

**Habit**: shrub 0.5–2.5 m tall. Branchlets greenish, ± strongly winged, ribbed, striate.

**Leaves**: alternate, ± decussate. Petiole glabrous, 0–2 mm long, concave above, wrinkled beneath. Blade glabrous on both faces, dark green, dull on the upper face, paler green on the lower face, oblanceolate, elliptic, oblong-elliptic or oblong, 8–20 × 3–7.5 cm; base cuneiform, narrowed, rounded; apex acuminate, apiculate, sometimes mucronate. Midrib concave above, prominent and striate beneath. Secondary nerves 4–6 pairs, ascending, arcuate, anastomosing far from the margin; the two basal nerves anastomosing with the next pair at 1/2 or 3/4 the length of the blade, concave above, prominent beneath. Nervules in a loose reticulum, scarcely visible on the upper face, prominent on the lower face.

**Inflorescence**: short, many-flowered axillary racemes or fascicled by 2–3. Rachis flattened, 0.6–2 cm long; flowers on the wide faces. Bracteoles 1 mm long, ovate or suborbicular, the midvein ending in an apical point on the upper face. Pedicel short, 1–1.5 mm long. Floral bud ellipsoid, 3.5 × 1.8 mm; petals valvate.

**Flowers**: pentamerous, yellow-green, yellow-orange or red, 3 × 2 mm. Calyx formed by sepals fused together into a glabrous cupule with undulate or entire upper margin, 0.75 × 2 mm. Petals glabrous on both faces, linear-elliptic, 3 × 1 mm, fleshy, apex ± obtuse. Stamens 5, epipetalous, 2 mm long; filament strongly fused to the petals, widening from base to apex, oblong, wider than the anthers, 1.25 × 0.60 mm; anther with 2 ellipsoid locules, with longitudinal dehiscence slits. Staminodes 3, alternipetalous, 3 mm long; filament narrow, fused to the lateral margins of the petals, 2 × 0.5 mm, terminating in 2 blades fused at the base, 1 mm long, swollen at the apex or acute. Disc surrounding the base of the ovary. Ovary superior, glabrous, ovoid, 1.5 × 1.25 mm; base furrowed; 1 locule containing 3 pendulous ovules from an erect central placenta. Style stout, 2.5 mm long, terminating in a capitate stigma obscurely trilobed.

**Fruit**: calyx strongly accrescent, surrounding the drupe except at the apex which is pierced by an opening 2–5 mm in diameter. Calyx globose, 11–16 mm in diameter, yellowish or reddish in colour with darker veinlets. Drupe subglobose, 10 × 14 mm. Exocarp thin, yellow to reddish-brown. Endocarp hard, lignified, brown with meridional striations. Seed solitary, globose, depressed at the apex; surface ± wrinkled in the lower part; albumen abundant.

## Types

**Holotype**: *Mann s.n.*, Mount John, Kongui river, Equatorial Guinea (K).

## Distribution

**Range**: from Cameroon to the Central African Republic. **Gabon**: collected at Libreville and its surroundings, Mayumba, Makokou, 3 km N of Makokou, Monts de Cristal (rocher Mboû), the Ogooué (lac Zilé near Atsié), and Lastoursville.

## Habitat and ecology

Primary forest and wooded savanna. Grows on riverbanks. Flowers from November to June; fruits mature from January.
