# *Atroxima liberica* Stapf Planche 18

**Genus**: [[Atroxima]]
**Family**: [[Polygalaceae]]
**Protologue**: J.. Linn. Soc., Bot. 37 : 85 (1905)

## Description

Woody liane; stem reaching 6 cm in diameter; branches smooth. Stipules absent or glandular and dark in colour. Leaf: petiole 3–5(–7) mm; lamina elliptic or oblong-elliptic to ovate-elliptic, (5–)7–14(–20) × (2–)3–7(–9) cm, ± coriaceous, obtuse to subcordate at the base, acuminate or rarely rounded at the apex, with (4–)5–8(–10) pairs of secondary nerves. Inflorescences 1–2(–4) per axil, up to 5(–7.5) cm in length, 25(–40)-flowered; peduncle 3–10 mm, sometimes puberulent like the rachis; bracts and bracteoles ovate-triangular, 0.5–1(–3) mm in length, ciliate. Flower: pedicel 3–6 mm, glabrous or sparsely puberulent; sepals fleshy, the 3 outer ones oval to suborbicular, concave, 2–6.5 × 1.5–6(–7.5) mm, the largest up to 1.5–3 times longer than the smallest, glabrous or nearly so on the outside, finely puberulent on the inside, margin ciliate; petals subequal in length, 7–11(–14) mm in length, cream, keel 3–4 mm in width, the others 1.5–3 mm in width, all puberulent on the inside at the apex, the upper ones ± puberulent on the inside; stamens (4–)5–7(–9) mm in length, tube puberulent on the inside. Fruit subglobose, usually flattened and weakly 3-lobed, up to 4(–5) cm in diameter, sometimes shortly apiculate, smooth, bright orange at maturity, glabrous. Seeds flattened, ellipsoid, up to 18 × 8–13 mm, yellow to orange, hairs up to 2 mm in length, disappearing when dry.

## Distribution

West and Central Africa: Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Cameroon, Gabon, Congo (Brazzaville), Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola; in Gabon, known from Woleu-Ntem, Estuaire, Ogooué-Maritime, Ngounié, Ogooué-Lolo, Ogooué-Ivindo and Haut-Ogooué

## Ecology

primary and secondary evergreen forest, semi-deciduous forest, forest edges of back-mangrove; at 0–500 m altitude. B: Breteler & Smissaert-Houwing (1977), Keay (1954). Bibliography
