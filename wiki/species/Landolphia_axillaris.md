# *Landolphia axillaris* Pichon Planche 47

**Genus**: [[Landolphia]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Mém. Inst. Franç. Afrique Noire 35 : 46 (1953)

## Description

Large liana; branches densely provided with brown-orange lenticels, glabrous or weakly pilose. Leaf: petiole 0.5–2 cm; limb elliptic to obovate, sometimes also as long as wide, of 8–16 × 5–11 cm, acute rounded or rounded at the base and decurrent on the petiole, acuminate at the apex, glabrous or puberulent at the base of the midvein below or with a few hairs on the margin; secondary nerves 9 to 15 pairs, more or less straight, with 1 to 3 less visible nerves between two secondaries, tertiary nervation reticulate. Inflorescence terminal and axillary, 12 mm, glabrous to pubescent; bracts oval to triangular, 1.1–1.3 mm long. Flower: pedicel 0.6–2.8 mm, glabrous to pubescent; calyx with broadly oval lobes, 0.7–1 times as long as wide, 0.8–1.3 mm long, ciliate, puberulent to slightly pubescent at the exterior principal axis; J.J. Mature fruit unknown. Distribution known only from four specimens from Equatorial Guinea, Rio Muni and Gabon, Haut-Ogooué and Ngounié.
