# *Polygonum lanigerum* var. *africanum* Meisn.

**Family**: [[Polygonaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Polygonum]]

## Description

Robust whitish perennial. Shortly petiolate lanceolate leaves reach 24 × 7.5 cm and have a pale whitish indumentum; ochrea cylindrical, membranous and shortly ciliate. Moderately dense spikes 4–9 cm are paired or form a terminal panicle. Flowers pink, c. 4 mm, with five perianth lobes and usually six stamens. Achene lenticular, black and glossy.

## Ecology

Riverbanks; reportedly cultivated for making indigenous salt.
