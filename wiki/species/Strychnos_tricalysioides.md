# *Strychnos tricalysioides* Hutch. & M. B. Moss

**Genus**: [[Strychnos]]
**Family**: [[Loganiaceae]]
**Protologue**: FWTA ed. 1, 2 : 24; Kew Bull. : 334 (1937)

## Description

Climbing shrub or vine 6–40 m tall and reaching a length of 50 m or more; stem 3–9 cm in diameter; bark dark brown, with large lenticels resembling warts; wood pale brown; branches without lenticels, cylindrical; branchlets glabrous, cylindrical, not or scarcely furrowed when dry; tendrils in pairs.

Leaves with petiole glabrous, 5–6 mm long; blade subcoriaceous when dried, elliptic or narrowly elliptic, 7–14 × 2.5–6 cm, caudate at apex (acumen 10–15 mm long), cuneate at base, glabrous on both surfaces; 2 pairs of secondary veins curved along the margin and reaching ± the apex, the first one weakly marked and submarginous; primary veins impressed above, even for fresh leaves; tertiary venation reticulate, indistinct.

Inflorescences axillary, several grouped together, resembling a fascicle, 1–1.5 × 1–1.5 cm, rather contracted, few-flowered; peduncle, branches and pedicels short, glabrous. Flowers 4-merous. Sepals fused for one quarter of their height, suborbicular, 1.2–1.3 × 1.2–1.3 mm, rounded, finely ciliate, glabrous on both surfaces. Corolla, in mature bud, 6 mm long, cream, glabrous outside, densely pilose within, but with base of tube and apex of lobes glabrous; tube 2 mm long; lobes oblong, 4 × 1.4 mm, acute, recurved. Stamens well-exserted, inserted at the throat of the corolla, with glabrous filament, 2.5 times longer than the anther; anther oblong, glabrous. Pistil glabrous, 4–4.5 mm long; ovary ovoid, 0.8–1.2 × 0.8–1 mm, 2-locular; style slender, ca. 3.5 mm long; stigma small, capitate, as thick as the style; ca. 10 ovules per locule.

Fruits orange, rather small, rather hard, subglobose or ellipsoid, 2.5–4 × 2–3.5 cm and containing ca. 1–10 seeds; wall 2 mm thick when dry, brittle for ripe and fresh fruits, hard when dry. Seeds flattened, elliptic, 21–24 × 16 × 6–8 mm, with one side ornamented with a deep depression and the other with a boss surrounded by a groove and appearing papillose; false papillae composed of curved hairs. Pl. 40, 6–14, p. 147.
