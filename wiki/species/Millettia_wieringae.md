# *Millettia wieringae* Adomou, sp. nov.

**Genus**: [[Millettia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]

## Description

Millettiae griffonianae Baill. affinis. Tree of moderate size. Stipels caducous or inconspicuous. Inflorescence large paniculate. Calyx deeply lobed.

## Distribution

endemic to Gabon, known from Haut-Ogooué and Ogooué-Ivindo

## Ecology

shrubby savanna, evergreen forest; at 200–550 m altitude. Flowers and fruits in August, fruits in December
