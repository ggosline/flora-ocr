# *Utricularia appendiculata* E.A.Bruce Planche 32

**Genus**: [[Utricularia]]
**Family**: [[Lentibulariaceae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull. 1933 : 475 (1934)

## Description

Terrestrial herb; rhizoids and capillary stolons numerous at the base of the flowering scape. Leaves in rosette but absent at anthesis, linear, 30 × 1.5–3 mm, 1-nerved. Utricles numerous, stipitate, ovoid, 0.8–1 mm in length; lateral orifice, oblique, with upper lip —

Inflorescence voluble, reaching 60 cm in length, with 1 to 8 spaced flowers; pedicel glabrous in the upper part, finely papillose towards the base; bracts medifixed, narrowly lanceolate, acute at both ends, ± 3 mm in length. Flowers: bracteoles similar to the bracts; pedicel erect, 2–3 mm; calyx with very unequal lobes, the upper one broadly rhombic, ± 3 mm in length, the lower one oval or oval-oblong, truncate at the apex, much narrower than the upper one; corolla 5–9 mm in length, cream or pale yellow, upper lip oblong, truncate or emarginate at the apex, the lower one orbicular, entire or emarginate at the apex, palate prominent, obscurely furnished with 2 bosses, spur narrowly cylindric, curved, ± 2 times as long as the lower lip. Fruit globular, ± 2.5 mm in diameter, obliquely and dorsiventrally compressed, dehiscent by a single abaxial longitudinal slit, with very numerous seeds. Seed narrowly cylindric; testa longitudinally striate and furnished with numerous short, rough and conical papillae.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Burundi, Uganda, Tanzania, Zambia, Mozambique, Malawi, Zimbabwe, Madagascar; in Gabon, observed in Estuaire, Haut-Ogooué, Ngounié and Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology

marshy savannas often on Kalahari sand, small ponds; in Gabon at 5–420 m altitude, in the Central African Republic, reaching 700 m, in East Africa, mainly between 1500 and 1860 m. B: Taylor (1964, 1972, 1989). Bibliography
