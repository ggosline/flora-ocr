# *Salacia tuberculata* Blakelock

**Genus**: [[Salacia]]
**Family**: [[Celastraceae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull. 1956 : 246 ; HALLÉ, Monogr. Hippocr. Afr. occid., Thèse : 182 (1958) ; Mém. IFAN 64 : 185 (1962) ; WILCZEK, FCB 9 : 225 (1960) ; IRVINE, WPG : 461 (1961)

## Description

1. Flower fascicles up to 25-flowered; 2–6 ovules per locule ... 25a. var. tuberculata. 1'. Flower fascicles up to 40-flowered; 2 ovules per locule ..... 25b. var. ngaziensis.

25a. var. tuberculata

Glabrous liane, devoid of rubber. Young branches olivaceous when dry, subcylindrical, quadrangular or subalate, with finely rugose epidermis. Leaves opposite or subopposite. Petiole 4–13 mm, with narrow canal margins slightly undulate. Limb concolorous ± olivaceous when dry, subcoriaceous, elliptic, 5–18 × 2.5–8 cm, with base ± acute to rounded, apex acuminate, margins denticulute. Secondary nerves 6–10 pairs.

Flower fascicles axillary sessile pauci- to multiflorous. Bud ovoid approximately 2–3 mm in length and width. Pedicels smooth 5–10 mm. Flowers yellow ± green or ± orange, 4–9 mm in diameter. Sepals somewhat unequal, pauciciliolate or nearly entire, rounded, 1.1–1.7 mm wide, outer ones smaller. Petals spreading or slightly reflexed, oblong or somewhat angular, approximately 2.2–4 × 1.4–3 mm. Disk cushion-shaped low patellifom with subpentagonal outline, 1.6–2.7 mm in diameter. Stamens spaced without perigyne groove, 1–2 mm long, anther 0.7 mm wide, dehiscent in a very obtuse chevron. Pistil pyramidal 0.8–2 mm in height, with punctiform stigma; locules half-immersed each containing 2–6 ovules ordinarily biseriate.

Fruit globose 2–5 cm in diameter, with thick exocarp, smooth or ± ornamented with irregular tubercles, with 3 or more seeds. - Pl. 32, fig. 1–15, p. 119.

## Discussion

Sierra Leone, Liberia (Baldwin 912), Côte-d'Ivoire (?) (Robert 17751, G), Ghana (Hall & Abbiw 45154), Nigeria (Talbot 3186) Gabon and Zaïre. This species apparently always rare should be found in Cameroon. It grows in forests, but not the wettest ones, in gallery forests or forest margins.
