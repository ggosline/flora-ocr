# *Rhizophora harrisonii* Leechm. Planche 38.1

**Genus**: [[Rhizophora]]
**Family**: [[Rhizophoraceae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull. 1918 : 8 (1918)

## Description

Small tree or shrub 2–5 m tall, glabrous. Stipules rapidly caducous, 3.5–5.5 cm long, green or tinged with pink. Leaf: petiole 1.5–2.5 cm, robust, most often green or tinged with pink; blade elliptic to obovate, (5–)11–15 × (1.5–)3.5–5(–7) cm, base cuneate, apex subacute to obtuse, coriaceous, with entire margin; primary vein most often green or tinged with pink beneath, ± 10 pairs of secondary veins. Inflorescences abundant, axillary, in biparous many-flowered cymes, branched (2–)4–5(–6) times at an angle of ± 30–60°; peduncle 2–5.5(–8) cm. Flower: pedicel 3–11 mm; bud narrowly ovoid, apex acute, often slightly curved, 6–9 × 4–6 mm; intermediate bracteolar cupule, often two-lipped; calyx with sepals connate at base, oval-elliptic, 6–9 × 2–4 mm, woody; petals linear, 6–7 × 1.5 mm, with numerous flexible hairs 1.5 mm on the inner margin; stamens 8, subsessile, anthers 5–6 mm long, mucronate at apex, pollen sacs numerous, ± isodiametric, 0.15–0.2 mm in diameter; disk 8-lobed; ovary invisible; style conical at base, stigma bifid. Fruit indehiscent, ± curved, 2.5–3.5(–6) × 2–2.5 cm, coriaceous; cotyledonary cylinder not or scarcely emerging from fruit; hypocotyl 11–26 cm long; fertilization less efficient than the two other species.

## Distribution

West Africa, from Senegal to Angola and the Atlantic coast of South America; in Gabon, in the Estuaire, Ogooué-Maritime and Nyanga

## Ecology

mangroves, very salt-tolerant, at the seaside and at sea level. Flowers and fruits germinating throughout the year. Use: as R. racemosa

## Vernacular names

pareils au ceux de R. racemosa . Note taxonomique : L’espèce est intermédiare entre les deux autres et pourrait représenter leur hybride (Breteler 1969). b : Breteler (1969, 2006), Keay (1953, 1954). ibliographie
