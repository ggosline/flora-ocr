# *Eugenia gabonensis* Amsh.

**Genus**: [[Eugenia]]
**Family**: [[Myrtaceae]]
**Protologue**: Amshoff, Act. Bot. neerl. 7 : 54 (1958); t.c. vol. 9 : 409 (1960)

## Synonyms

- Eugenia Miegeana Aké-Assi, Bull. Jard. Bot. Bruxelles 30 : 15 fig. 1 (1960).

## Description

Subshrub 3-6 dm tall. Young branches finely pubescent, then scaly. Leaves subsessile, lanceolate or spathulate, attenuate toward the base, the base itself subcordate or rounded, acuminate at the apex, glabrous, 17-28 cm long and 5-8 cm wide; 12-15 pairs of lateral nerves. Petiole very short and thick.

Flowers fasciculate, axillary. Pedicels 2-6 mm long, finely pubescent. Sepals ciliate, up to 3 mm long. Petals 5 mm long. Ovary finely pubescent. Stigma very small.

Fruit subglobose, 1-1.5 cm in diameter, red or orange, monospermic. Embryo with a short lateral fissure (always?).

## Type

Klaine 2827 (Gabon)

## Discussion

Species long confused with E. Klaineana, yet clearly distinct by the form and dimensions of the leaves.

First known and described from Gabon, this species was subsequently found in Côte d'Ivoire and in the former Belgian Congo.
