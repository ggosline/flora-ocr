# *Indigofera paracapitata* J.B.Gillett

**Genus**: [[Indigofera]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull., Addit. Ser. 1 : 28 (1958)

## Description

, pro parte. Annual herb, erect, reaching 1 m in height; branchlets angular. Stipules attenuate-deltoid, 5 mm long. Leaf: petiole 4–10 mm; rachis angular, reaching 5 cm; stipels absent; leaflets 5 to 7, opposite, with oblanceolate blade, 10–16 × 2–4 mm, the upper ones rolled, appearing narrower. Inflorescence in condensed panicle, terminal or axillary, subcapituliform, brown. Flower: pedicel ± 1 mm; calyx 5–8 mm long, split almost to the base, sparsely pilose; corolla red, standard 4 × 1.7 mm, apiculate. Pod ovoid, 3–4 mm long, with (1–)2 seeds, glabrous. Seed broadly cylindrical, 1.5 × 1 × 1 mm, pale brown; hilum central.

## Distribution

Tropical Africa, from Nigeria to Gabon, to the Republic of the Congo and the Democratic Republic of the Congo, to northern Angola and Zambia; in Gabon, known from Haut-Ogooué, Moyen-Ogooué and Nyanga

## Ecology

savannas, gallery forest, ruderal stations, on paths and sandy soils; in Gabon at 130–430 m altitude, elsewhere around 1250 m. Flowering almost year-round

## Source note

This species resembles I. capitata very closely – to the point of sometimes having been confused with it – but the branchlets of I. capitata are cylindrical and the pod silky then glabrescent, ± 2.5 mm with 1(–2) seed. In the Democratic Republic of the Congo, there exist plants with leaflets having a linear blade, only 0.5 mm in width. Bibliography: Gillett (1958, 1971a).
