# *Afrothismia saingei* T.Franke Planche 15

**Genus**: [[Afrothismia]]
**Family**: [[Thismiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Syst. Geogr. Pl. 74 : 28, fig. 1 (2004)

## Description

, syn. nov. Herb 0.5–2 cm in height; stem up to 6 cm in length; rhizomes 1.6–6 mm in length, with spheres 3.5–6 mm in diameter, each composed of 20–70 tubercles; stem white to yellowish or pale brown. Leaves narrowly triangular to triangular-ovate, 2–5 mm in length. Inflorescence with 1–3 flowers; bracts 7–10 mm in length. Flower strongly zygomorphic; perianth tube whitish, tinged with dark rose, the base urceolate, the middle curved at 55–90°, the apex cylindric, length from base of tube to opposite wall, 7–16 mm, maximum width 4.5–10.5 mm; throat of tube circular, surrounded by a yellow ring approximately 1 mm in height; inner rim 6-lobed, approximately 0.5 mm in width; perianth lobes strongly unequal, arranged in 3 pairs, yellow to orange or brown, linear, inflated at the apex, 1.5–7.5 cm in length, each with 2 white filiform appendages at the base; stamens incurved, anthers 1.2–1.4 × 0.8–1 mm, adnate to stigma; ovary subglobose, 2–3.5 mm in length, whitish; style 1–1.6 mm in length, stigma trilobed horseshoe-shaped, 1.2 × 2.3 mm in length. Fruit obconic, 5.5–6 × 1.5–2 mm, placental column presenting placentae with seeds agglomerated above the persistent base of fruit. Seeds narrowly ellipsoid, 0.7–0.8 × 0.3 mm.

## Distribution

Cameroon (South-West Province, Mount Kupé) and Gabon (Estuary, Pongara National Park; Ngounié, Waka Valley)

## Ecology

Primary forests or old secondary forests, in Gabon up to 500 m in altitude, elsewhere up to 1000 m. Taxonomic notes: A. gabonensis is placed in synonymy with A. saingei for the first time. In the literature, the presence of Afrothismia winkleri and Afrothismia insignis is reported from Gabon. This is simply an incorrect identification of the single specimen Wilks 1179 which indeed represents A. saingei, as published by Franke (2004). This Afrothismia is provided with perianth lobes distinctly unequal. 3 B: Dauby et al. (2007), Franke (2004). Bibliography
