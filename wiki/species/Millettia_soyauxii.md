# *Millettia soyauxii* Taub.

**Genus**: [[Millettia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 23 : 185 (1896)

## Description

, syn. nov. Liana or climbing shrub, reaching 25 m in height; stem compressed, reaching 20 × 4 cm at the base, with 4 or 5 longitudinal ridges rising in a helix; branchlets hairy, with spreading fulvous or reddish pubescence formed by long and very short hairs mixed together. Stipules persistent, lanceolate or linear, 8–20 × 1–2.5 mm, hairy. Leaf 6.5–30 cm in length; petiole 2–10 cm; rachis 3–10 cm; stipels absent; leaflets 7 to 13, with hairy petiolule of 2–4(–6) mm, blade oblong, 2.5–10(–15) × 1.3–6.5 cm, rounded at the base, acuminate at the apex, glabrous on the upper surface (except on the midvein), densely hairy with reddish long hairs (0.5–1 mm) erect or semi-erect on the lower surface, becoming olive-brown to olive-green when dry; veins slightly sunken on the upper surface, the lateral ones in 7 to 11 pairs, well-marked, strongly ascending and joined at ± 1 mm from the edge in very faint arches, venules in a dense but generally very faint reticulum. Inflorescence terminal and axillary, in pseudoracemes of 11.5–25 cm in length, hairy; brachyblasts ± globose, 1–3 mm in length; bracts linear, 8–12 × 0.5–3 mm, hairy, exceeding the calyces. Flower: pedicel 1–2 mm; bracteoles inserted at the base of the calyx, linear, 2–5 × 0.3–0.5 mm, hairy on the outside; calyx hairy with long fulvous semi-erect hairs, tube 2–3 mm in length, triangular teeth 0.5–1 mm in length; bicoloured corolla, standard brown to violet, broadly elliptic, 9–14 × 7–9 mm, with claw of 2 mm, outer surface densely covered with appressed golden hairs, wings and keel white to pale yellow, oblong-oval, wings 9–10 × 2.3–3 mm, with truncate base and claw of 3.5 mm, glabrous, keel 10.5–11 × 3–4 mm, with truncate base and claw of 3.5 mm, pubescent on the outside with a glabrous zone on the upper edge; stamens monadelphous; disk null; ovary linear, 10 mm in length, pubescent, with 4 or 5 ovules; style curved, 2 mm, glabrous. Pod narrowly oblanceolate, flattened, 8–12 × 1.5–2 cm, attenuate towards the base, apex with a curved beak, with 2 or 3 seeds, with poorly salient sutures, with very dense velvety brown pubescence. Seed oblique-rounded, flattened, 10 × 12 × 2 mm, brown.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Central African Republic and Angola (Cabinda); in Gabon, recorded in Estuaire, Haut-Ogooué, Moyen-Ogooué, Nyanga and Ogooué-Ivindo

## Ecology

primary and secondary forest, sometimes at the edge of rivers or in swampy terrain; in Gabon at 10–500 m altitude, elsewhere up to 600 m. Flowers from September to December, fruits in October and December

## Source note

M. soyauxii and M. bicolor do not show, after examination of the material, notable differences. The character of the length of the calyx teeth, invoked by Pellegrin (1949) to distinguish them, is not reliable. The two species are therefore considered as synonyms; the name M. soyauxii is the oldest. The type specimen of M. soyauxii, Soyaux 96, comes from Cabinda (Angola) and not from Gabon, as indicated by Pellegrin (1949). The specimen Wieringa et al. 6258 has inflorescences similar to those of M. soyauxii and M. comosa, but cauliflorous. As it bears no leaves, it cannot be determined whether this is one of these two species or a third as yet undescribed (van der Maesen, 2013). b: Pellegrin (1949). Bibliography
