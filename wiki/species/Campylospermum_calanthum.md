# *Campylospermum calanthum* (Gilg) Farron Planche 5

**Genus**: [[Campylospermum]]
**Family**: [[Ochnaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Jard. Bot. État Bruxelles 35 : 394 (1965)

## Description

Monelasmum zenkeri Tiegh., J. Bot. (Morot) 16 : 200 (June 1902) (non Campylospermum zenkeri (Tiegh.) Farron ≡ C. oliveri (Tiegh.) Farron). Ouratea calantha Gilg, Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 33 : 259 (March 1903). Ouratea sibangensis Gilg, Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 33 : 267 (March 1903). Monelasmum sibangense (Gilg) Tiegh., Ann. Sci. Nat., sér. 8, Bot., 18 : 36 (June 1903). Ouratea nigroviolacea Gilg ex Bak. in Rendle et al., Cat. pl. Oban : 124 (1913). Tree reaching 10 m in height; branches with dark brown bark. Stipules persistent, narrowly to very narrowly triangular, 4–12(–25) mm long. Leaf: petiole 2–10 mm, stout, channelled above; lamina elliptic to elliptic-ovate or rarely obovate or narrowly elliptic-obovate, (8–)17–20(–37.5) × (3.5–)6–10(–12.5) cm, ratio (2.25–)3–4, cuneate or sometimes attenuate, rarely rounded, at the base, apex acuminate or rarely acute, coriaceous, slightly to distinctly bullate, margin serrulate, sometimes slightly or rarely entire; midvein plane above, prominent below, lateral nerves in 11 to 22 pairs, plane to slightly prominent on both surfaces, tertiary venation scalariform perpendicular to the midvein, indistinct above. Inflorescence terminal, branched, lax, main axis (8–)15–35(–38) cm; gemmate scales persistent, sometimes foliaceus, 15–20(–30) × 4–10 mm; racemes 2 to 7, slender, (5–)8–19(–23) cm, ascending or rarely spreading; bracts persistent, triangular, 3–15(–30) mm long; cymules distant 0.5–2 cm, 1- to 6(–15)-flowered. Flower: pedicel 5–9 mm, articulated 2–4 mm from the base; sepals ovate, 6–8 × 3–4 mm in flower, 7–10 × 4–6 mm in fruit; petals obovate, 3–9 × 3–6 mm, truncate at the base, rounded or slightly emarginate at the apex; stamens: anthers 5–6 mm long; style 4–5 mm. Fruit: receptacle 4–6 × 4–5 mm; 1 to 4 well-developed druplets per receptacle, reniform, 8–11 × 6–8 mm; cotyledons accumbent, ± equal.

## Distribution

Guinea, Liberia, Ivory Coast, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola (Cabinda); in Gabon, common and present throughout the country

## Ecology

primary and secondary forest, gallery forest, coastal, littoral and swampy forest, forest edges; on sandy and silvery soil; in Gabon, at 30–850 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1100 m. Taxonomic note: The distinction between C. calanthum and C. dybovskii is sometimes difficult. The former generally has much broader laminae with lateral nerves prominent below, but some individuals of C. calanthum have rather narrow laminae, while some individuals of C. dybovskii have lateral nerves slightly prominent below. In these cases, C. calanthum can be identified by its persistent stipules and leaves that are slightly to distinctly bullate (stipules caducous and leaves smooth in C. dybovskii). b: Bamps & Farron (1967), Bissiengou (2014), Exell & Mendonça (1951), Bibliography Farron (1963, 1985), Hawthorne & Jongkind (2006)
