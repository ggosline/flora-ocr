# *Polygala welwitschii* Chodat

**Genus**: [[Polygala]]
**Family**: [[Polygalaceae]]
**Protologue**: Mém. Soc. Phys. Genève 31(2) : 341, tab. 27, fig. 46 (1893)

## Description

Mem. Soc. Phys. Genève 31(2) : 341, tab. 27, fig. 46 (1893). subsp. pygmaea (Gürke) Paiva Plate 23(7) Fontqueria 50 : 189, tab. 34, fig. g (1998). Polygala pygmaea Gürke in Engler, Pflanzenw. Ost-Afrikas 2/3, C : 234 (1895). Dwarf annual herb, 3–9 cm; stem slender, generally simple or branched at the base, sometimes pseudo-di- or trichotomously branched, with crisp pubescence. Leaves alternate, longer than the proximal inflorescence; petiole 1–3 mm, with crisp pubescence; blade lanceolate to oblong-elliptic, 15–60 × 1–10 mm, cuneate at the base, subacute to rounded at the apex, glabrous or very finely pubescent on both faces. Inflorescence: terminal racemes strongly condensed, often subglobose, 8–25 mm long, rachis with crisp pubescence; bracts lanceolate, 1–1.5 mm long, generally pubescent, persistent; bracteoles similar, but 1 mm long. Flower whitish, pinkish or lilac; pedicel 1.5–3 mm, with crisp pubescence; upper sepal keeled, 1.5–2 × 0.75 mm, pubescent or glabrescent, winged sepals elliptic to suborbicular, 2–3 × 2–2.75 mm, pubescent toward the base, lower external sepals keeled, 1–1.5 mm long, free; upper petals obliquely elliptic, constricted in the basal half, 2.5 × 1.5–2 mm, pubescent, keel 2.5 × 1.25 mm, crest 1.5–2 mm long, fimbriate; stamens 8. Fruit ellipsoid to subglobose, 2–2.5 × 2–2.5 mm, glabrous, with wings 0.25 mm wide. Seeds flattened and ellipsoid to flattened and ovoid, 1.5–2.2 × 0.6–0.9 mm, with silky white pubescence, rarely puberulent; caruncle subsymmetric, 0.4–0.6 mm long, with three small appendages 0.2–0.3 mm long.

## Distribution

Widely distributed in southern tropical Africa: Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Angola, Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi, Tanzania, Zambia, Zimbabwe, Namibia and South Africa (Transvaal); in Gabon: Estuaire

## Ecology

Woody savanna and burned grassland formations; in Gabon at 0–100 m altitude, elsewhere up to 2000 m. Taxonomic note: The type subspecies, endemic to Angola, is an erect herb reaching 18 cm, with stems generally pseudo-di- or trichotomously branched, winged sepals 3–4 × 2–3 mm, seeds with brownish silky pubescence. By contrast, subsp. pygmaea, widely known throughout tropical Africa, is a dwarf herb reaching 9 cm, with stems simple or branched from the base, sometimes also pseudo-di- or trichotomously branched, winged sepals 2–3 × 2–2.75 mm, seeds with whitish silky pubescence. At a few stations in Angola, it is not easy to separate the two. B: Exell (1960), Paiva (1998, 2007), Petit (1958). Bibliography
