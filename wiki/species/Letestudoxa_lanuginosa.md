# *Letestudoxa lanuginosa* Le Thomas

**Genus**: [[Letestudoxa]]
**Family**: [[Annonaceae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia,ser.2,6,1 :145,tab.1, fig.1-7 (1966)

## Synonyms

- Letestudoxa bella Pellegrin,Bull. Soc.Bot.Fr. 95 :137 (1948),pro parte.

## Description

Vine with thick, velvety, ferruginous branchlets. Leaves with thick petiole, 4–5 mm in diameter, 7–10 mm long, densely rust-pubescent, woolly; blade coriaceous, oblong to obovate-oblong, 14–25 cm long, 5.5–12 cm wide, rounded to subcordate at the base, rounded, sometimes emarginate at the apex; upper surface glabrous, lower surface densely pubescent-woolly with persistent pubescence. Lateral nerves 15–18 pairs, slightly oblique, ascending especially toward the margins where they join in arches, impressed above, prominent below; nervilles very lax, prominent below.

Flowers solitary, dark pink, supra-axillary, with thick pedicel, 8–12 mm long, rust-pubescent; bracts very broadly ovate to suborbicular, rounded at the apex, 10–15 mm in diameter, densely rust-pubescent. Floral bud subglobose, slightly flattened, rounded at the apex, rust-tomentose. Calyx forming a hood around the floral bud, splitting transversely into irregular lobes. Outer petals very broadly ovate to suborbicular, 4.5–5.5 cm long, 3.5–5 cm wide, distinctly unguiculate at the base, silky on both surfaces; inner petals narrower, ovate-oblong, 4–5 cm long, 1.5–2.5 cm wide, silky. Stamens subsessile, prismatic, approximately 5 mm long, thecae linear extrorse, connectives dilated above the anthers and puberulent. Carpels very numerous, pressed together, approximately 10 mm long, ovaries ± cylindric, 3 mm long, with base sunken in the receptacle, stigmas hairy. Ovule 1, erect.

Fruit unknown.

## Type

Le Testu 932o, Ncout, environs de Mitzic,fl. (holotypc Pl; isotype BM!)

## Discussion

This red-flowered vine appears to be more robust than L. bella; the branchlets and pedicels are thicker, the leaves and flowers larger. It is known only from two collections made in dense humid forest in northern Gabon.
