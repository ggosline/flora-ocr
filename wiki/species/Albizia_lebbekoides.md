# *Albizia lebbekoides* (A.P. De Candolle) Bentham

**Genus**: [[Albizia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Hook. Lond. J. Bot. 3: 89 (1844)

## Synonyms

- Acacia lebbekoides DC., Prodr. 2: 467 (1825).

## Description

Tree 10–15 m tall, reaching 20 m. Trunk reaching 80 cm in diameter. Older branchlets grey when dry, strongly wrinkled longitudinally. Young branchlets dark brown to blackish, wrinkled longitudinally. Bark fissured, a few light brown lenticels. Petiole glabrous, 2.5–6 cm long, upper surface bearing a small globose gland, most often between the middle and apex. Rachis weakly pubescent above, 2.7–12 cm long, flattened transversely; upper surface ± channelled bearing 1–3 prominent globose glands at the insertion level of the terminal pinnae. Pinnae 2–5 pairs. Pinnae axis glabrous to pubescent on the keel, 5–15.5 cm long; upper surface keeled bearing 1–3 elliptic glands at the insertion level of the terminal leaflets. Leaflets 11–27 pairs, sessile or subsessile. Blade glabrous on both surfaces; grey-green to blackish above and greenish to greyish below when dry; triangular-oblong, sometimes slightly falcate, reaching 25 × 8 mm; base asymmetric: proximal margin truncate to rounded cordate and distal margin attenuate-rounded; margin sometimes slightly ciliate; apex acute. Primary vein off-centre toward the distal margin, weakly prominent above and strongly prominent below. Secondary veins ascending, curved; prominent above and strongly prominent below; up to 5 basal secondary veins divergent in the proximal half, one very long. Rest of venation not visible.

Inflorescence in a panicle of capitate spikes, axillary or terminal. Panicle axis ± glabrous to pubescent, angular. Spike peduncle pubescent, 2.5–14 mm long. Bracteoles pubescent externally, obovate, 0.5 mm long, apex acute. Floral bud narrowly obovoid. Flowers ♂¹, white to yellowish when fresh, sessile. Calyx obconic, pubescent externally, pale green when fresh, 1.25–2 mm long; lobes triangular, 0.15–0.30 mm long, apex acute. Corolla narrowly obconic, pubescent externally except at the base covered by the pale green calyx when fresh, 3.5–4.5 mm long; lobes free triangular-ovate, 0.75–1 mm long, apex acute. Stamens 7.5–10.5 mm long; fused in a tube for 3.5–5 mm; anther broadly oblong, 0.15–0.25 mm long. Pistil sessile to very shortly stipitate; ovary glabrous, narrowly ellipsoid, 1–1.30 mm long, containing 10–12 ovules; style slender 8–10 mm long, with slightly capitate stigma.

1. There is no very marked floral heteromorphy in this species. Only the size of the apical flowers is somewhat increased.

Legume dehiscent, chartaceous, brown when fresh and when dry, glabrous, narrowly elliptic to narrowly elliptic-oblong (3.5) 6.5–16.5 × 1.5–2.5 cm; base not to very weakly stipitate; sutural margins weakly thickened; lateral faces with transverse primary venation, ± perpendicular to sutural margin, not abundant, weakly prominent, rest of venation ± concave not very visible; apex obtuse very shortly apiculate. Seeds light brown to blackish, ovoid to oblong-elliptic, 4.5–7 × 3.5–4 mm; central areole elliptic, 2.25–3.5 × 1.25–1.50 mm.

## Type

Decaisne s.n., Timor (holo-, G-DC ; iso-, K)

## Discussion

This plant is found in Indochina and in the eastern part of the Malesian range. It was introduced to Gabon; but it is known there only from a specimen from a botanical garden.

## Specimens examined
