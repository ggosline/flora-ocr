# *Albizia lebbekoides* (A.P. De Candolle) Bentham

**Genus**: [[Albizia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Hook. Lond. J. Bot. 3: 89 (1844)

## Description

- Acacia lebbekoides DC., Prodr. 2: 467 (1825).

Tree 10–15 m high, reaching 20 m. Trunk reaching 80 cm in diameter. Old branches grey when dry, strongly ridged longitudinally. Young branches dark brown to blackish, ridged longitudinally. Bark fissured, a few pale brown lenticels. Petiole glabrous, 2.5–6 cm long, upper surface bearing a small globose gland, most often between the midpoint and the apex. Rachis weakly pubescent above, 2.7–12 cm long, flattened transversely; upper surface ± channelled bearing 1–3 prominent globose glands at the insertion of the terminal pinnae. Pinnae 2–5 pairs. Axis of the pinna glabrous to pubescent on the keel, 5–15.5 cm long; upper surface keeled bearing 1–3 elliptic glands at the insertion of the terminal leaflets. Leaflets 11–27 pairs, sessile or subsessile. Blade glabrous on both surfaces; grey-green to blackish above and greenish to greyish below when dry; triangular-oblong, sometimes somewhat falcate, reaching 25 × 8 mm; base asymmetric: proximal margin truncate to cordately rounded and distal margin attenuate-rounded; margin sometimes slightly ciliate; apex acute. Primary vein eccentric toward the distal margin, weakly prominent above and strongly prominent below. Secondary veins ascending, arched; prominent above and strongly prominent below; up to 5 secondary basal veins divergent in the proximal half of which one very long. Remainder of the venation not visible.

Inflorescence in a panicle of capituliform spikes, axillary or terminal. Axis of the panicle ± glabrous to pubescent, angular. Peduncle of the spike pubescent, 2.5–14 mm long. Bracteoles pubescent externally, obovate, 0.5 mm long, apex acute. Floral bud narrowly obovoid. Flowers ♂¹, white to yellowish in life, sessile. Calyx obconic, pubescent externally, pale green in life, 1.25–2 mm long; lobes triangular, 0.15–0.30 mm long, apex acute. Corolla narrowly obconic, pubescent externally except at the base covered by the pale green calyx in life, 3.5–4.5 mm long; free lobes triangular-ovate, 0.75–1 mm long, apex acute. Stamens 7.5–10.5 mm long; fused into a tube for 3.5–5 mm; anther broadly oblong, 0.15–0.25 mm long. Pistil sessile to very shortly stipitate; ovary glabrous, narrowly ellipsoid, 1–1.30 mm long, containing 10–12 ovules; style slender 8–10 mm long, with slightly capitate stigma.

1. There is no very marked floral heteromorphy in this species. Only the size of the apical flowers is somewhat increased.

Legume dehiscent, chartaceous, brown in life and when dry, glabrous, narrowly elliptic to narrowly elliptic-oblong (3.5) 6.5–16.5 × 1.5–2.5 cm; base not to very weakly stipitate; sutural margins weakly thickened; lateral faces with principal transverse venation, ± perpendicular to sutural margin, not abundant, weakly prominent, remainder of venation ± concave scarcely visible; apex obtuse very shortly apiculate. Seeds pale brown to blackish, ovoid to oblong-elliptic, 4.5–7 × 3.5–4 mm; central aril elliptic, 2.25–3.5 × 1.25–1.50 mm.

## Type

Decaisne s.n., Timor (holo-, G-DC ; iso-, K)

## Discussion

This plant is found in Indochina and in the eastern part of the Malesian range. It has been introduced to Gabon; but it is known there only by a specimen from a botanical garden.
