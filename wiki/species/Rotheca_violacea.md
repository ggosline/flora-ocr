# *Rotheca violacea* (Gürke) Verdc. subsp. violacea Figure 4(I)

**Genus**: [[Rotheca]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull . 55 : 154 (2000)

## Synonyms

- Clerodendrum violaceum Gürke, Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 28 : 303 (1900).

## Description

, syn. nov. Liane up to 5 m tall or climbing shrub reaching 2 m in height; stem generally solid or rarely hollow; young branches ± rounded, quadrangular and/or finely striated, with short pale ferruginous pubescence or glabrescent, the older ones generally quadrangular; lateral leafy shoots often with ± shortened internodes. Leaves opposite or rarely ternate; petiole 0.2–2.5(–3) cm; blade ovate to elliptic or oblong-elliptic, 3–13.5(–16) × 1.5–7(–10) cm, rounded or subcordate to narrowly cuneate or weakly attenuate at the base, shortly or longly acuminate over 0.5–2 cm at the apex, entire to ± slightly undulate or slightly to coarsely serrate with apiculate teeth, membranous to coriaceous, releasing an unpleasant odor when rubbed, ± glabrous to slightly pubescent above and on the venation below or rarely more densely pubescent/tomentose. Inflorescence terminal, lax, paniculate, 8–20 cm long; cymes 4(–8) × 2(–4.5) cm, distant by ± 1–4.5(–7) cm, with 2 to 6 flowers; peduncle reaching 2.5(–4) cm, glabrous to pubescent; bracts sessile, foliaceous, generally cuneate at the base, the lower ones ovate, 3–5 × 2–3 cm, acuminate, the upper ones ± narrowly elliptic-ovate, 5–10 × 1–4 mm. Flower: pedicel 4–9(–12) mm; calyx pink, reddish or purple, with cupuliform tube, 3–6 mm long, with lobes broadly rounded to ovate, sometimes reflexed, 3–5 × 2–5 mm; corolla zygomorphic, pale blue, violet, variegated with violet and white, or greenish, with tube 4–10 mm long, with lobes 8–16 × 5–5.5 mm, central lobe often darker than the others; stamens exserted 1–3.5 cm. Fruit depressed-globose, 8 × 11 mm, red, with 3 or 4 seeds.

## Distribution

Tropical West Africa, Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Angola (Cabinda), Burundi, tropical southern Africa to South Africa (Natal); in Gabon: Haut-Ogooué and Woleu-Ntem. Conservation status: Preliminarily assigned to Least Concern (LC) by Pollard in Cheek et al. (2004), under the name C. violaceum

## Ecology

primary and secondary forests, forest margins; in Gabon at 700 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1700 m

## Source note

Several authors have recognized Clerodendrum carnosulum as a distinct species. The key of Huber et al. (1963) separated C. carnosulum and C. violaceum on the basis of phyllotaxy (ternate vs. opposite; the type of C. carnosulum, Welwitsch 5702 (K) being ternate), pubescence, leaf texture and the depth of the calyx lobes. All these characters are variable within Clerodendrum. Some species exhibit both opposite and ternate leaves even on the same plant. Pubescence and leaf texture can vary considerably in the genus, particularly according to altitude. Calyces can also show considerable variation in the size of the lobes, depending on the degree of accrescence during fruit maturation. These characters do not seem to us sufficiently robust to recognize two distinct species and C. carnosulum is therefore considered here as a synonym of C. violaceum. The subspecies C. violaceum subsp. kigeziense Verdc. is present in Uganda. Bibliography: Fernandes & Verdcourt (2000), Figueiredo & Smith (2008), Huber et al. (1963), Paton et al. (2010), Verdcourt (1992).
