## Description

Herb reaching 60 cm in height, with a short rhizome bearing leaves arranged roughly distichously. Petioles winged, 13–32 cm long, not forming a false stem. Leaf blade lanceolate to ovate, acute to obtuse and attenuate to truncate, 18–35 × 5–11 cm. Leaves and flowers appearing simultaneously.

Floral scape tinged reddish, 15–42 cm tall, central among the leaves. Inflorescence conical to globular, with approximately 20–80 flowers, pale red. Pedicels 1.5–3.5 cm long, drooping when fruit develops. Perianth tube 0.3–1 cm long; segments roughly lanceolate, 1.4–2.8 cm long and 2.5–7 mm wide, with 5 nerves, the inner segments somewhat wider than the outer. Anthers yellow.

Berries orange, 1–2 cm in diameter.

## Distribution

**Range**: Western and central Africa, from Sierra Leone east to western Uganda and northern Angola.

**Gabon**: Bélinga, Lonkandou, Djengila, Nyanga region, Lastoursville, Mayabi, Saint-Martin, Nzeng Bour road region, Ngounié.

## Habitat and ecology

Restricted to humid forest zones. Mainly in riverine vegetation but can extend into semi-deciduous forest zones. Found in primary, secondary and degraded vegetation. Tolerates deep shade but can also grow in clearings. Generally found below 700 m altitude, but reaches 1300 m on Mount Cameroon.

## Notes

The type specimen (plant sent from Gabon to Decaisne) has been lost; the lectotype is designated as plate 1195 in *Flore des Serres*, series 2 (Bjørnstad & Friis, Norw. Journ. Bot. 19: 208, 1972).
