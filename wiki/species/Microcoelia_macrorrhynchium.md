# *Microcoelia macrorrhynchium* (Schltr.) Summerh.

**Genus**: [[Microcoelia]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: FTWA, ed. 1, 2 : 454 (1936) ; Jonss., Symb. Bot. Ups. 23(4) : 131 (1981) ; Cribb,

## Description

– BASIONYM : Angraecum macrorrhynchium Schltr., Syst. Bot. Jahrb. 38 : 22 (1905).

– TYPE : Schlechter 12785, Cameroon, holo-B, delete., neo-BM!, drawing-K.

– Encheiridion macrorrhynchium (Schltr.) Summerh., Bot. Mus. Leafl. Harvard Univ. 11:162 (1943).

Stem < 1.5 (−2.9) cm. Scaly leaves < 0.4 (−0.7) cm. Roots generally few in number, < 7 cm. Inflorescence dense, < 10 (−17) cm, −18 (25)-flowered. Flowers horizontal, dorsal sepal pale brown, lateral sepals white with a brownish midvein, petals white, lip white with a yellowish-green blotch at the base, spur pale brown to beige-orange at the apex. Dorsal sepal cucullate, ovate, acute. Petals obliquely ovate, erose. Lateral sepals obliquely ovate, acute. Lip 4.5–6(−7.3) × 3.6–5.8 mm, trilobed; midlobe spatulate, semicircular, distinctly dentate with finely erose margins; lateral lobes erect, oblong. Spur < 11 mm, pendant. - Pl. 167, p. 369.

PL. 166. – Structure of the gynostemium of the genus Microcoelia Lindl. : A, front view ; B, lateral view ; C, rostellum with tegula and viscidium ; D, remainder of the rostellum ; E–H, tegula and viscidium, various views ; I, pollinia, various views (A–I, ex SZLACHETKO, 2003).

## Distribution

Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Zaire, Uganda, Zambia

## Ecology

alt. 200–1,100 m. Epiphyte usually on exposed stem and branch, in dense forest, riparian forest, dense woodland, often in secondary forest and plantation, observed on Citrus, Coffea, Terminalia superba and Theobroma cacao

## Specimens examined

Le Testu 8801, Haute Ogooué, Tomisimba (fl. Mai) (fide Summerhayes 1943). Pobeguin s.n., Ashonka, P!.

Sous-genre Microcoelia

Labelle sans auricules à la base du labelle ni lobes latéraux ou avec ceux-ci peu développés. Parties libres du rostellum plus courtes que le gynostème. Tegula et viscidie de longueur variable, viscidie générale-ment plus courte que la tegula.
