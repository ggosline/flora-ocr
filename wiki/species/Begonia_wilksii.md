# *Begonia wilksii* Sosef

**Genus**: [[Begonia]]
**Family**: [[Begoniaceae]]
**Protologue**: Wageningen Agric. Univ. Papers 91-4 : 109 (1992)

## Description

Plant reaching 7 cm in height. Leaf peltate to subpeltate; petiole 0.5–4.4 cm, hirsute; blade ± spreading when fresh, not bullate but rugose because of sunken veins, symmetric or nearly symmetric, narrowly to very narrowly elliptic-ovate, 4–16 × 0.5–2.5 cm, apex gradually attenuated, margin entire to dentate or denticulate, upper surface glabrous or furnished with a few long hairs. Inflorescence: peduncle 1.5–6.5 cm, with 1–3 ♂ flowers and 1 ♀ flower with white tepals; bracts 0.5–3.5 mm in length. ♂ Flower: tepals 5.5–11.5 × 4.5–7 mm; androecium with 8–13 stamens. ♀ Flower: pedicel elongating in fruit to 4.5 mm; tepals 4–9 × 5–7 mm; ovary (including the wings) broadly obtriangular-obovoid to obtriangular depressed, 3–5 × 2.5–5.5 mm, hirsute to sparsely hirsute, with 3 small obovate to obtriangular depressed wings; styles 3. Fruit broadly obovoid or obtriangular depressed to nearly globulous, 3.5–7.5 × 4–7.5 mm; rostrum to 1.5 mm.

## Distribution

endemic to Gabon (Monts de Cristal and Massif du Chaillu); rare species

Inflorescence pauci- or pluriflorous, bisexual, cymes biparous or in part uniparous, capituliform, with peduncle furnished at the apex with 2 large persistent bracts forming a flattened cupule enclosing the inflorescence, with slender branches furnished with smaller persistent bracts. Flowers with 2 white or pink tepals. ♂ Flowers: androecium zygomorphic, stamens numerous with unequal filaments, united at the base. ♀ Flower: tepals united at the base in a column overtopping the ovary bearing the free parts of the perianth; ovary obovoid, apterous, 4(–5)-locular; placentation axial; styles 4(–5), united at the base, bifid at the apex, stigmatic band horseshoe-shaped, the extremities spiraled.
