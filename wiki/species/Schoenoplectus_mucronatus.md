# *Schoenoplectus mucronatus* (L.) Palla ex Kerner Planche 126, Figure 24a

**Genus**: [[Schoenoplectus]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Sched. fl. exs. austro-hung. 5 : 91 (1888)

## Description

Herb relatively robust, perennial or rarely annual; rhizome erect or horizontal, with 3–10 fascicled stems; stem 40–80 cm × 3–8 mm, triquetrous, with indistinct longitudinal striations. Leaf: leaf sheath 4–15 cm long, pale or brownish, often ending in a triangular point or short mucro; blade absent. Inflorescence apparently lateral, in a head formed of 4–25 sessile spikelets aggregated (mature spikelets appearing stipitate after the fall of lower glumes); main inflorescence bract erect or inclined backward, resembling the stem, 15–25 mm long, triquetrous with concave faces. Spikelet ovoid to cylindric, approximately 5 mm long at anthesis, but reaching 30 × 6 mm at fruiting, pale brown; glumes more or less circular, 3–3.5 mm long, strongly concave, pale brown with numerous longitudinal striations almost as distinct as the midvein, ciliate at the margin especially toward the apex, terminated by a point. Flower: perianth of 6 brown bristles furnished with retrorse spinous teeth, often longer than the achene; stamens 3, with flattened filaments persistent on the mature achene; style with 2–3 branches. Achene 1.5–2.5 × 1.2–1.4 mm, trigonous, blackish with fine transverse wrinkles.

## Distribution

widely distributed in tropical and temperate regions; rare in Gabon, found only in Ngounié and Nyanga (4 collections)

## Ecology

margins of open waters such as pools or streams, marshes and river beds; in Gabon, at 500–750 m altitude, up to 1370 m in East Africa. Taxonomic note: This species is immediately identified by its triquetrous stems and its short bract resembling the stem. b: Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al. (2010), Hooper & Napper (1972).
