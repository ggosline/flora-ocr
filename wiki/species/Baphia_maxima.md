# *Baphia maxima* Baker

**Genus**: [[Baphia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]

## Description

in Oliv., Fl. trop. Afr. 2 : 250 (1871). Liane, reaching at least 30 m in length, or lianescent shrub or rarely small tree 8 m tall and trunk 10 cm in diameter; branchlets densely villous. Stipules narrowly triangular, 7–12 × 1–2 mm. Leaf: petiole with separated pulvini, (1–)1.5–4(–10) cm, tomentose; leaflet with blade ovate to obovate-elliptic, (5–)7–15(–19) × 3–7(–11.5) cm, rounded to subcordate at base, apex emarginate to rounded or acuminate for 0.5–1 cm, tomentose, early glabrescent above, tardily so below; lateral veins in 7 to 10 pairs. Inflorescence axillary but often terminal by abscission of axillary leaves, solitary or in fascicles of 2 to 6 flowers; bracts oblong, ± 6 × 4 mm, tomentose. Flower: bracteoles situated at the apex of the pedicel against the calyx, obliquely ovate, 4–9 × 6–7 mm, tomentose; pedicel 12–18 mm, tomentose; calyx spatulate, 11–16 × ± 12 mm, tomentose outside, glabrous inside; standard 15–18 × 16–22 mm, wings elliptic, 15–17 × 5–7 mm, with claw 1–1.5 mm, keel petals 15–22 × 5–7 mm, hairy on the dorsal margin, with claw 1 mm; stamens 10–15 mm, anthers 3–3.5 mm long; pistil 15 mm long, ovary densely hirsute, with 2 or 3 ovules. Pod subligneous, 10–12 × 1.5–2 cm, hirsute, becoming glabrous, with 1(–2) seeds. Seed 12–20 × 17–25 mm, brown.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, known from all provinces except Estuaire, Nyanga and Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology

Primary and secondarized forest; in Gabon at 30–500 m altitude, elsewhere up to 600 m

## Vernacular names

disoni (echira), musopu-ndjiyaa (bavangu) (= boyau de gorille)

## Source note

In the key of Soladoye (1985), the keel petals are described as glabrous, but they are at least partially hairy on the dorsal margin, as in B. le-testui and B. megaphylla. b : Pellegrin (1949), Soladoye (1985). ibliography
