# *Olax staudtii* Engler

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Olax]]

Notizbl. bot. Gart. Berl. 2: 286 (1899).

## Description

Shrub 0.6–1.5 m tall. Branches green with 4 ridges or 4 longitudinal wings, with both longitudinal and transverse striations. Leaves alternate or weakly decussate. Petiole absent or very short. Blade glabrous on both surfaces, dark green above, lighter green or yellowish-green below, elliptic, 7.5–12.5 × 3–4 cm; base narrowed or rounded, apex strongly acuminate acute mucronate or attenuate, ± obtuse or apiculate obtuse. Midrib weakly concave above, prominent striated below. Secondary nerves 6–9 pairs, ascending, arched, anastomosing 2–4 mm from the blade margin; the basal pair of nerves is often more ascending than the others; concave above, prominent below. Nervules in a very loose network, slightly prominent on the upper surface, little or not prominent on the lower surface.

Inflorescence in racemes solitary or clustered 2–3, axillary, multiflorous. Rachis quadrangular, flattened, 6.2 cm long; flowers on the broad faces. Bracteoles oval carinate dorsally, apiculate at the apex. Floral bud ellipsoid slightly or not constricted in its median part, 3 × 2 mm. Female flower pentamerous, glabrous, reddish-brown or pinkish. Calyx cupuliform with the upper margin entire. Petals glabrous on both surfaces, free between themselves to the base, 3 × 1 mm, erect in the opened flower; apex apiculate acute. Stamens 6 epipetalous, 2.75 mm long; filament flattened, oblong, abruptly narrowed at the apex, 2 × 0.75 mm; anther introrse, ellipsoid with 2 longitudinal dehiscence slits. Staminodes 3 epipetalous, 3 mm long; filament similar to that of the stamens, 2 × 0.75 mm, terminating in undulate laminae perpendicular to the base, fused to the base, 1 mm long, apex perpendicular dilated. Ovary superior, surrounded at the base by a thick glabrous disc, ovoid, 1.25 × 1 mm; 3 uniovulate locules; central erect placenta. Style short, thick, 1.5 mm long, terminating in a trilobed stigma.

## Distribution

**Range**: Cameroon to Central African Republic.

**Cameroon**: Edéa, Mabam, Yaoundé, Nkomo near Ngoase, Monts Kala, Lolodorf.

**Gabon**: Mayumba, Ndjolé, Monts de Cristal, Libreville, Ogoué.

## Notes

This species is very closely related to *Olax latifolia* Engl. and can be distinguished chiefly by floral structure. In the absence of flowers, the form of the primary nerve on the upper surface of the blade can distinguish them: it is much less concave or not concave at all in *O. staudtii*. The source notes anomalies in floral structure: some flowers possess 6 petals instead of 5, and the number of staminodes is variable—in some cases they are absent while in others they are replaced by fertile stamens.
