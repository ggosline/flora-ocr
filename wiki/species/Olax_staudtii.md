# *Olax staudtii* Engler

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Olax]]

Notizbl. bot. Gart. Berl. **2**: 286 (1899); Pflanzenfam., ed. 1, **1**: 146, nom. nud. (1897). — Sleumer, Pflanzenfam., ed. 2, **16b**: 24 (1935). — Michaud, Mém. I.F.A.N. **75**: 219, tab. 7, a–c (1966).

## Description

**Habit**: shrub 0.6–1.5 m tall. Branchlets green with 4 ribs or 4 longitudinal wings, longitudinally and transversely striated.

**Leaves**: alternate or weakly decussate. Petiole absent or very short. Blade glabrous on both faces, dark green above, lighter green or yellowish-green beneath, elliptic, 7.5–12.5 × 3–4 cm; base narrowed or rounded, apex strongly acuminate acute mucronate or attenuate, ± obtuse or apiculate obtuse. Midrib weakly concave above, prominent and striated beneath. Secondary nerves 6–9 pairs, ascending, arcuate, anastomosing 2–4 mm from the blade margin; the pair of basal nerves is often more ascending than the others; concave above, prominent beneath. Nervules forming a very lax reticulum, slightly prominent on the upper face, little or not prominent on the lower face.

**Inflorescence**: solitary or fascicled in groups of 2–3, axillary racemes, many-flowered. Rachis quadrangular, flattened, 6.2 cm long; flowers borne on the broad faces. Bracteoles ovate, carinate dorsally, apiculate at the apex. Flower bud ellipsoid, slightly or not constricted in its median part, 3 × 2 mm.

**Flowers**: female, pentamerous, glabrous, reddish-tinged or pinkish. Calyx cupuliform with upper margin entire. Petals glabrous on both faces, free to the base, 3 × 1 mm, erect in the open flower; apex apiculate acute. Stamens 6, epipetalous, 2.75 mm long; filament flattened, oblong, abruptly narrowed at the apex, 2 × 0.75 mm; anther introrse, ellipsoid with 2 longitudinal dehiscence slits. Staminodes 3, epipetalous, 3 mm long; filament similar to that of the stamens, 2 × 0.75 mm, ending in wavy blades fused at the base, 1 mm long, apex expanded. Ovary superior, surrounded at the base by a thick glabrous disc, ovoid, 1.25 × 1 mm; 3 uniovulate locules; placenta central erect. Style short, stout, 1.5 mm long, ending in a three-lobed stigma.

## Types

TYPE: *Staudt 364*, Cameroon (holotype B, deleted; isotype P).

## Distribution

**Range**: from Cameroon to the Central African Republic. **Gabon**: Mayumba; C.E.F.A. Ayem, 10 km SW Ndjolé; Mont Méla, Monts de Cristal; Libreville; Ogoué.

## Notes

This species is very close to *Olax latifolia* Engl. Only the structure of the flower permits clear separation. In the absence of flowers, the form of the primary nerve on the upper face allows them to be distinguished: it is much less concave or even not concave in *O. staudtii*.

Anomalies have been noted in the structure of the flowers; flowers with 6 petals instead of 5 are found. The number of staminodes is variable: in some cases their disappearance is observed, in others this disappearance is accompanied by the appearance of new fertile stamens.
