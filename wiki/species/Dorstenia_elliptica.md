# *Dorstenia elliptica* Bureau

**Genus**: [[Dorstenia]]
**Family**: [[Moraceae]]

## Description

in DC., Prodr. 17 : 271 (1873) ; ENGL., Mon. Afr. Pfl. 1, Moraceae : 16 (1898) ; RENDLE in HUTCH. & RENDLE, FTA 6 (2) : 31 (1916).

- D. frutescens ENGL., l.c. : 12 (1898). LECTOTYPE : Preuss 384, Cameroon (B ; iso-, K).

Plants reaching 1.5 m in height, with a creeping rhizome and erect or ascending stems with woody base, unbranched or (sometimes ?) with spreading branches ; small leafy branchlets 1.5–4 mm thick, sparsely to rather densely puberulent, in part with uncinate hairs ; internodes 0.5–4 cm long. Leaves spirally arranged (at least on the stem). Blade (elliptic to) oblong-lanceolate, broader at the middle or most often in the upper half, (3–) 7–28 × (1–) 2.5–9 cm, chartaceous, acuminate, or sometimes obtuse or acute ; base attenuate to acute ; margin entire to obscurely sinuate or dentate ; blade subglabrous or sparsely puberulent, especially at the base, with uncinate hairs, glabrescent ; midvein prominent, the others (nearly) flat above, ± prominent below ; (6–) 10–16 (–19) pairs of secondary veins without parallel tertiary veins. Petiole (2–) 5–12 (–20) mm long, c. 1.5 mm thick, puberulent with uncinate hairs. Stipules subpersistent, subulate, 3–17 mm long, sparsely puberulent with stiff hairs, to subglabrous.

Inflorescences solitary or grouped by 2, sometimes more, in the leaf axils, generally brown to purplish, broadly turbinate to discoid, 5–11 mm in diameter ; peduncle 4–6 mm long (reaching 10 mm in fruit), puberulent in part with uncinate hairs, as well as the receptacle. Bracts ovate to lanceolate to subulate, reaching 3–5 mm, scattered on the outer face of the receptacle and roughly grouped in 2 rows on the margin. Flowers with perianth 2, 3 or 4 times divided, 0.2–0.8 (–1) mm high, puberulent with papilla-like hairs, small, globose to oblong ; stamens (2–) 3 (–4) with filaments 0.4–1.6 mm long, glabrous, swollen at the lower part ; anthers 0.1–0.25 × 0.1–0.25 mm with narrow connective ; pistillode reaching 0.8 mm, ± brush-like. Flowers ♀ (1 ? –) 3–7 ; free part of the perianth tubular, 0.4–0.8 mm high, entire to weakly 3–4-lobed, puberulent with papilla-like hairs, small, globose to oblong ; style c. 1.5 mm long ; stigmas 2, 0.2–1 mm long.

Infructescences broadly turbinate to discoid-convex, 1–2 cm in diameter. Fruit ellipsoid, c. 8 × 6 mm ; endocarp subglobose c. 4.5 × 5–6 mm, with crustaceous (or coriaceous ?) endocarp, smooth ; seed c. 5 × 4 mm, with testa provided, under the hilum, with a thickened but not distinctly vascularized part ; embryo with rather thin cotyledons, folded, unequal, the inner almost entirely covered by the outer ; radicle rather long, curved (remains of albumen in the median fold). - Pl. 10, p. 39.

## Discussion

On average the perianth of the ♂ flowers and the staminal filaments are longer in specimens from Gabon than in those collected in Cameroon.

This species extends from W Cameroon to Angola-Cabinda, also to Fernando Po. It is a species of the forest floor in humid forest ; it grows usually in wet places, often along watercourses. It is locally abundant and gregarious.
