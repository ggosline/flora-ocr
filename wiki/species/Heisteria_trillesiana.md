# *Heisteria trillesiana* Pierre

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Heisteria]]

Rev. Cult. Colon. **11**: 258, tab. 1–3 (1902); **13**: 1, tab. 1 (1903). — A. Chevalier, Vég. ut. Afr. tr. fr. **9**: 136 (1916). — Sleumer, Pflanzenfam., ed. 2, **16b**: 16 (1935). — Michaud, Mém. I.F.A.N. **75**: 182, tab. 2, h–i (1966).

## Description

**Habit**: shrub or small tree 3–5 m tall, or small tree 10–15 m tall, with a trunk lacking branches. Bark ash-grey with a reddish base, scaly on the surface. Branchlets striate and weakly bi-winged longitudinally.

**Leaves**: petiole glabrous, 5–15 mm long, canaliculate above, striate below. Blade glabrous on both faces, shiny dark brown above, dull below, oblong, oblong-elliptic, elliptic or lanceolate, 10–18.5 × 3.5–7.5 cm; base cuneate, apex obtusely acuminate. Midrib weakly concave or weakly prominent above, prominent below. Secondary nerves 6–8 pairs, ascending, arcuate, anastomosing 1.5–2 mm from the blade margin, weakly prominent on the upper face, prominent on the lower face. Nervules forming a fairly lax reticulum, obscurely visible on the upper face, weakly prominent on the lower face.

**Inflorescence**: axillary many-flowered fascicle. Floral pedicel 3–4 mm long. Floral bud globular with valvate petals. Flower pentamerous.

**Flowers**: calyx with 5 glabrous sepals on both faces, triangular, apex acute marked by a black spot sometimes absent. Petals glabrous externally, pubescent in the middle of the inner face, 2.5 mm long, oblong-lanceolate, thicker at the base, slightly fused, apex weakly obtuse and apiculate. Stamens 10 in two cycles; filament of alternipetalous stamens flat, narrowed at the apex, 1 mm long; filament of epipetalous stamens fused to the petals, narrowing at the apex, 0.5 mm long; anther with 2 locules with longitudinal dehiscence slits. Disc short, surmounting the ovary, lobed. Ovary superior, globular, glabrous, with 3 incomplete uniovulate locules. Central placenta erect. Style short, ending in a trilobed stigma.

**Fruit**: solitary drupe surrounded by the strongly accrescent calyx, forming a collar with 5 triangular lobes wider than long, apex obtuse or somewhat acute, red in colour. Drupe ovoid, black and wrinkled. Endocarp hard and lignified, 1 mm thick. Seed solitary with abundant albumen. Embryo apical.

## Types

LECTOTYPE: *Trilles 2227* (= 6633 in herb. Pierre) (P).

## Distribution

**Range**: Gabon and Cameroon. **Gabon**: Estuaire (Libreville, Komo basin, Mboro on the upper Ramboué, Nhingé-Nhingé plantation near Kango post, Komo edges at Mbel), Ogooué-Maritime (50 km SE Lambaréné, Remboué), Ogooué-Ivindo (vicinity of Adouma on the Orimbo, tributary of the Ogooué, towards the Udzémé river near Monts de Cristal), and Woleu-Ntem (Ivilis country, near the Samba cataracts in the Ngoumé).

## Habitat and ecology

Occurs in mountainous regions and along streams and rivers. Flowering from July to November; fruits mature from November to March and fall and rot immediately.

## Vernacular names

*kamba* (Ivilis); *nsine nsidé* (Fang, Libreville); *vias* (Fang).

## Properties and uses

The wood is pale yellowish-white washed with pink, firm and splits easily. The seed has a hazelnut flavour and is eaten raw, but most often is consumed boiled. It contains numerous oily inclusions yielding a fluid oil that can be consumed but becomes rancid very rapidly, then acquiring an unpleasant taste and potentially causing colic. The seed oil yield is 63%.

## Notes

This species appears intermediate between *H. parvifolia* Sm. and *H. zimmereri* Engl. It is very difficult to distinguish from these two species in the absence of fruit.
