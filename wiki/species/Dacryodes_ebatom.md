# *Dacryodes ebatom* Aubr. et Pellegr.

**Family**: [[Burseraceae]] · **Genus**: [[Dacryodes]]

## Description

Imperfectly known tree, about 30 cm diameter. Leaves with 5–6 pairs of opposite oblong-elliptic leaflets; blades acuminate, base obtuse or rounded, about 12 × 5.5 cm, glossy above and nearly glabrous beneath except for traces of scales; petiolules about 5 mm; secondary veins 6–8 pairs. Flowers unknown. Fruit small and globose; stone subspherical, 1.3 × 1.5 × 1.2 cm, convex on two faces and with two apical pores.

## Distribution

Known only from the holotype, S.R.F. 1280, Akoré near Lambaréné, on muddy river-margin ground.
