# *Salacia mannii* Oliver

**Genus**: [[Salacia]]
**Family**: [[Celastraceae]]
**Protologue**: FTA 1 : 376 (1868) ; HUTCH. & MOSS, FWTA 1 : 450, 453 (1928) ; HALLÉ, Monogr. Hip-pocr. Afr. occid., Thèse : 163 (1958) ; MÉM. IFAN 64 : 167 (1962) ; WILCZEK, FCB 9 : 226, tab. 25 (1960)

## Description

- S. soyauxii LOES., Bot. Jahrb. 19: 241 (1894); Pflanzenf., ed. 2, 20B: 227 (1942); HALLÉ, Monogr. Hippocr. Afr. occid., Thèse: 161 (1958); Mém. IFAN 64: 165 (1962); WILCZEK, FCB 9: 208 (1960). TYPE: Soyaux 124, Gabon (iso-, K!; P!).

– S. cuspidicoma LOES., Bot. Jahrb. 44: 175 (1910). SYNTYPES: Staudt 523, 610; Zenker 3732.

PL. 15. – Salacia mannii Oliv.; 1, 1', buds; 2, 2', 2", flowers, diam. 4, 6–7, 3–5, 6 mm; 3, calyx seen from below, 4 mm; 4, 4', young discs; 5, 5', discs in oblique view; 6, 6', discs in cross-section; 7, 7', 8, 8', stamens; 9, 9', locules with few or two-ovulate; 10, 10', ripe fruit 38 × 30 mm; 11, seed 16 × 10 mm (1, 2, 6, Hallé N. 802; 1', 4, 8, Le Testu 7600; 2', 5, Letouzey 10124; 2", 5', 8', Letouzey 14985; 3, 7, 7', Hallé N. 2678; 4, 9', Bouquet & Sita 2501, Congo; 6', Soyaux 124; f. soyauxii: 10, 10', 11, Hallé N. 3287).

Shrub 0.80–6 m tall, sometimes sarmentose, or liana 3 to more than 15 m (Bos 4178), lacking rubber. Internodes rugose, often reddish-brown when dry with whitish lenticels. Leaves opposite or subopposite. Petiole 3–10 mm with straight-edged canal margins. Blade discolorous, upper surface russet to olivaceous-grey when dry and dull or uniformly matte, elliptic, 8–23 × 2.5–9 cm; base acute or rounded; apex acuminate; margin entire or moderately crenate. Secondary veins 5–8 pairs; fine reticulation not visible.

Inflorescences in sessile axillary glomules, few- to pluriflowered (approximately 10 flowers at most). Pedicel (5)8–20(24) mm. Bud subglobose, somewhat thickened at base, 2–3 mm in diameter. Flowers yellow, yellowish, orange, or more rarely reddish, sometimes with unpleasant odour. Calyx with unequal sepals, the smaller ones subdeltoid, 0.9–1 mm wide, the larger ones semicircular, 1.3–2.5 mm wide, ± erect or spreading. Petals rotate above the base, subungulate, 2–5 × 1.3–5 mm, obovate or oblong, base sometimes remarkably fleshy with a callus entire or marked by a weak longitudinal groove; sometimes 2 small calli reduced to absent. Disc reduced, cupulate-pentagonal, compressed by the petal calli, 0.9–1.5 mm in diameter and 0.25–0.7 mm tall, with or without a small horizontal rim. Stamens 3, 0.6–1.2 mm, anthers ellipsoid, 0.4 mm, transversely dehiscent, exserted and curved. Style subpyramidal, exceeding ± the disc and weakly trilobulate at the apex; 3 locules sunken at the level of petal insertions, each containing either 2 ovules, juxtaposed or superposed, or 3–4 ovules.

Fruit ovoid or ellipsoid, sometimes slightly attenuate at the extremities, 4–7 × 2.8–3.5 cm, containing 2–6 seeds 10–22 mm, with fused yellowish cotyledons. Exocarp smooth or almost so, approximately 1 mm thick. - Pl. 15, p. 55 and pl. 41, fig. 1, 2, p. 143.

## Distribution

Nigeria (Brenan 8426), Fernando Po, Cameroon, Muni (Tessmann 960, K!, 985, K!), Gabon, Congo (Bouquet & Sita 2501, fl. 5.5 mm diam.) and Zaïre

One can sometimes distinguish under the designation of forma soyauxii (Loes.) Hallé, stat. nov. (ref. above), the flowering materials whose corollas exceed 8 mm in diameter. In fact the size of flowers is highly variable and does not appear to be linked to sexual differences despite the following remark by LE TESTU on his collection no. 7600: "There are flowers of two kinds, some with a large corolla appearing ♂, others with a narrower, greenish corolla, certainly having an ovary, but perhaps rudimentary stamens, Lastoursville, 4.11.1929".

This species, with extremely variable floral characters, appears very close to S. kivuensis Wilczek (Zaïre and Kivu): the latter differs by its more reniform anthers, by its ovules numbering 4–6 per locule, and by its petal bases always lacking calli. With these characters one might think that it is perhaps only a subspecies of S. mannii. One can also relate to S. rufescens Hook. f., with reniform anthers, 4 ovules per locule, absence of calli, but especially with an annular cushion-like disc; this species, difficult to place taxonomically, is known only from the type, from Sierra Leone: Rositer ex Vogel s.n. (K!).
