# *Costus letestui* Pellegr.
**Family in Vol. 9**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Current placement**: Costaceae · **Genus**: [[Costus]]
## Description
Slender, sometimes epiphytic herb with 50–75 cm stems spiralling near the apex. Narrow oblanceolate glabrous blades are 9–15 × 2–5 cm; the reddish tubular ligule reaches 3 cm. One to three lateral few-flowered spikes bear pinkish-white flowers dotted red; the 5 cm labellum has deeply wavy edges, red-brown spots and a double yellow stripe.
## Distribution
Gabon and Cameroon. Type: Le Testu 1683, near Mouila, Gabon; also recorded from Abanga in the Monts de Cristal.
