# *Eriosema griseum* Baker var. griseum

**Genus**: [[Eriosema]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]

## Description

in Oliver, Fl. trop. Afr. 2 : 228 (1871). Perennial herb to subshrub, reaching 50 cm in height; rhizome woody, very thick; stems branched, woody, with grey villosity especially when young. Stipules 8–12 × 2–3 mm, with acute apex, scarious. Leaf: petiole 2–3 mm; rachis 1–2 mm; leaflets 3, petiolule ± 1 mm, blade elliptic-oblong, 2–3(–4) × 0.5–1 cm, base cuneate, apex acute to rounded, mucronulate, grey-green and with scattered hairs above, greyish-white and densely hairy below; lateral nerves in 5 to 6 pairs. Inflorescence mainly terminal, in dense and subglobose racemes, ± 3 cm in diameter; peduncle 1–2 cm, hairy; bracts 9–12 × 3 mm, covered with long white hairs. Flower: pedicel 2 mm; calyx gibbous at base, 14–18(–25) × ± 2 mm, tube 4–5 mm in length, teeth subequal; corolla yellow to pale yellow, standard obovate, 11 × 9 mm, auriculate, with claw of 3 mm, apex glandular, external surface pubescent, wings 11 mm in length, with claw of 3.5 mm, keel ± 5 mm in length, very glandular, with claw of 5 mm; ovary with long white hairs. Pod oval, 13–16 × 8–9 mm, mucronulate, black, with long white hairs. Seed ovoid, flattened, ± 5 × 3 × 2 mm, brown speckled with black; hilum central, whitish.

## Distribution

Tropical Africa, common in West Africa, from Guinea to Angola; in Gabon, known from Ogooué-Ivindo

## Ecology

herbaceous or shrubby savanna, roadside; in Gabon at 100 m altitude, elsewhere up to 900 m. Flowers in November. Use: The plant is reported to be vermifuge

## Source note

In our zone, this is var. griseum. In West Africa, one also finds var. togense (Taub.) Jacq.-Félix which differs from it in the bracts abruptly caudate, not lanceolate. b: Ern (1984), Hauman (1954c), Hepper (1958), Jacques-Félix (1971), van der Maesen ibliography (2006a), Raponda-Walker & Sillans (1961), Verdcourt (1971c)
