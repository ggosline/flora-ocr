# *Salacia coronata* Hallé, sp. nov.

**Genus**: [[Salacia]]
**Family**: [[Celastraceae]]

## Description

S. miegei Hallé and S. ekokae Louis ex Wilczek affinis, but differing by green leaves when dry, entire, with sessile inflorescences in a capitulate glomerule, slender pedicels, narrowly oblong flower buds, subacute sepals with ± marginate sides, oblong petals, truncoconical disk raised and micropapillate and crowned with small denticulae, stamens separate, slender filaments, anther thecae confluent sometimes microapiculate, subulate style not lobulate, ovules 2 in each locule mostly superposed. Inhabits Gabon.

PL. 44. — Salacia coronata Hallé: 1, bud 2.3 mm long; 2, flower, diam. in spread position 5.5 mm; 3, 4, sepals; 5, 6, petals 2 and 2.4 mm long; 7-9, disk, diam. 0.9-1.2 mm; 10-13, stamens; 14, 15, biovulate locules (1-3, 6-10, 13, 14, Le Testu 6397; 4, 5, 11, 12, 15, Klaine 917). — S. ekoka Louis ex R. Wilczek: 16, bud 2 mm long; 17, flower, diam. 4 mm; 18, calyx seen from below; 19, sepal; 20, 21, petals 2.5 and 2.3 mm long; 22-25, disks, diam. 1-1.8 mm; 26-27, stamens; 28-29, biovulate locules (16, 18, 21, 23, 24, 27, 28, Le Testu 5828; 17, 25, Louis 2155, Zaire: 19, 22, 26, 29, Le Testu 8868; 20, Robyns 871, Zaire).

## Type

Le Testu 6397, Gabon (holo-, P!; iso-, BM, BR)

## Discussion

Glabrous liane with rubber filaments; young branches smooth olivaceous, compressed at the approach of nodes. Leaves opposite, subopposite or nearly alternate. Petiole 6-13 mm with straight margins of the canal. Blade olive-green or yellowish-green when dry, elliptic, 9-16 × 4.5-7 cm, with acute base, with mediocre short or narrow acumen, with entire or subcrenulate margins confusedly. Secondary veins 7-10 pairs; reticulum very fine, visible under magnification on both surfaces of the blade.

Inflorescences axillary multiflorous on sessile capitulate glomerules 2-3(4) mm in diameter, and forming clusters of which the most abundant are spherical. Pedicels slender 5-9 mm. Flower buds oblong, 2.2 × 1.2-1.6 mm, somewhat turbinate when dry. Flowers yellow, reddish at the end of anthesis, 3-5 mm in diameter. Calyx with short slightly unequal sepals, with acute or obtuse apex, entire or marginate and dentate laterally, 0.6-1 mm wide. Petals oblong 2-3 × 1-1.2 mm, with proximal part slightly enlarged, with distal part spoon-shaped, sinuous or reflexed, with 3 main subparallel veins. Truncoconical disk micropapilliferous, 0.7-0.8 × 0.8-1.1 mm, crowned around the androecium by 6 to 9 small prominent denticulae: 3 are opposite the ovary locules, 3 others sometimes doubled oppose the filaments. Stamens 1.4-2 mm, with slender ± contorted filaments, spaced at the base; anther about 0.5 mm, with connivate or confluent locules, with chevron-like slits slightly sinuous of about 90°, with emarginate or microapiculate apex. Style 0.8-1.2 mm, pyramidal subulate, with punctiform stigma. Locules sunken more than halfway, with 2 superposed or sometimes juxtaposed ovules.

Fruit unknown. – Pl. 44, fig. 1-15, p. 155.

This rare liane, presumably of very humid dense forest, was collected without ecological notes. Endemic to Gabon.
