# *Dacryodes edulis* (G. Don) Lam

**Family**: [[Burseraceae]] · **Genus**: [[Dacryodes]]

## Description

Medium tree. Leaves 7–8-jugate; leaflets oblong to narrowly oblong, acuminate, asymmetrically rounded at base, to 20 × 6 cm; 9–12 pairs of looped secondary veins; stellate pubescence caducous, sometimes simple hairs remaining. Panicles terminal and ferruginous-tomentose. Flowers grouped by threes, 3-merous; sepals and petals 5–6 mm, six stamens, disc and ovary glabrous. Fruit ellipsoid, to 7 × 3.5 cm, pink then violet, with thin flesh and a very thin fragile endocarp.

## Distribution and uses

Probably native near the Gulf of Guinea and widely planted from southern Nigeria to Uganda, Congo and Angola. Gabonese forest trees may mark abandoned villages. Fruits are cooked and eaten salted.

## Vernacular names

*safoutier*, *safou*; *atanga* (Mpongwe); *odou* (Fang); *issanga safou* (Bayaka); *diganga* (Vove); *osségou*.
