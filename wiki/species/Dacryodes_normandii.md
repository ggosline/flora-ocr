# *Dacryodes normandii* Aubr. et Pellegr.

**Family**: [[Burseraceae]] · **Genus**: [[Dacryodes]]

## Description

Large canopy tree. Leaves clustered at thick branch ends, 6–9-jugate; rachis about 40 cm, initially covered with caducous long-stalked branched rust-coloured hairs. Leaflets subsessile, oblong, acuminate, asymmetric, about 16 × 5 cm; 8–15 pairs of lateral veins; glossy and glabrous above, initially densely rust-pubescent beneath. Female flowers on 3 mm pedicels; sepals 3 mm, petals 5.5 mm; stamens 6 with simple and glandular hairs; disc glabrous; ovary and short style tomentose. Fruit obovoid, about 3.5 cm; stone strongly grooved with a shield-like mark and two short pore-ridges.

## Distribution

Known only from Gabon, around Libreville and the Ngounié basin. Holotype: S.R.S.F. 140, Ntoum-Rogolié. Fruits edible boiled; pale wood used for veneer and interior joinery.
