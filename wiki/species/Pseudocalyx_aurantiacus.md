# *Pseudocalyx aurantiacus* R. Benoist

**Genus**: [[Pseudocalyx]]
**Family**: [[Acanthaceae]]
**Protologue**: R. Benoist, Bull. Soc. Bot. France, 85 : 678 (1938); Notul. Syst. 11 : 149 (1944)

## Description

Liane, with young cylindrical stems somewhat pubescent, then glabrous, with greyish bark. Petioles 2 cm. Leaf blade 10–15 × 5–6 cm, glabrous, entire. 8–9 pairs of lateral nerves.

Flowers in elongated racemes 3.5–9.5 cm, axillary, with approximately 2–6 flowers; pedicels and bracts covered with very dense, felted, orange-brown stellate hairs; pedicels 4–4.5 cm; bracts oval, rounded at base and apex, 26–27 × 9–10 mm. Calyx lobes unequal, reaching 4.5 mm in length, the two anterior ones triangular with fairly obtuse apex, the two lateral ones resembling the preceding but slightly larger; posterior lobe very short; all covered with small stellate hairs. Corolla tube covered externally with stellate hairs, limb bilabiate; the 4 stamens are inserted towards the middle of the tube; basal portions, for approximately 4 mm, of the inner side of the tube, covered with simple, hyaline, refracted hairs, swollen at the base and approximately 1.5 mm long. Anthers elongate, connective covered with small stiff, papillose hairs. Annular disc surrounding the base of the ovary. Ovary densely covered with stellate hairs, bilocular, with parallel locules, biovulate. Style filiform, glabrous, elongate, slightly bilobed at apex.

Mature fruit unknown; immature fruit elliptic-acuminate.

This plant, known only from the type, resembles very much Ps. saccatus Radlk. (= Ps. africanus S. Moore), but its inflorescences are axillary and laxer, with much longer peduncles; the bracts are larger and broader; the calyx above all is very remarkable for its 5 lobes, unequal, triangular and well individualized. This character brings the species closer to Ps. heterochondros Mildbr. and to Thunbergia rufescens Lindau (Bot. Jahrb. 17 : 96, 1893, from Nigeria and Cameroon; see on this subject H. Heine, F. W. T. A., ed. 2, 2 : 402, 1963). The calyx has the form of a ring almost invisible in P. saccatus type species of the genus Pseudocalyx. The delimitation of the genus with respect to the genus Thunbergia becomes therefore, with the inclusion of Ps. heterochondros and Ps. aurantiacus, somewhat uncertain and would need to be studied by a monographer of African and Malagasy Thunbergia.

The preceding description is liable to modification in the dimensions of the reproductive apparatus: the type specimen, sole example of the species known up to the present, with a single floral bud presents no fully open corolla.
