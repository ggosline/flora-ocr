# *Anthonotha pynaertii* (De Wild.) Exell et Hillcoat

**Genus**: [[Anthonotha]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Exell, Bol. Soc. Brot. 2,29 : 39 (1955)

## Synonyms

- Macrolobium Pynærtii De Wild., Ann. Mus. Congo, Bot., 5,3 : 192 (1910). -- Pellegrin, Lég. Gabon : 50 (1948).
- Macrolobium Malchairii De Wild., Bull. Jard. Bot. Brux., 7:255 (1920).
- Macrolobium Bonnivairi De Wild., Bull. Jard. Bot. Brux., 7 : 256 (1920).

## Description

Trees. Leaves 3-5 jugate. Leaflets oblong elliptic, caudate acuminate acute, appressed pubescent ferruginous and ± lustrous beneath, 4-20 cm long by 2-9 cm wide.

Racemes very short fasciculate, axillary or on old wood, forming together small densely flowered globules.

## Type

Pynaert 1226 (BR)

## Discussion

This species is very close to A. macrophylla from which it is separated by the condensed inflorescences.

It is distributed from Gabon to Cabinda and Angola.

## Specimens examined

Le Testu : 1737, Tchibanga (fl. mai); 8759, Koulamoutou (fl. avr.); 9564, Oyem (fl. mai).
