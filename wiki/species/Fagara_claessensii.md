# *Fagara claessensii* De Wild.

**Family**: [[Rutaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Fagara]]

## Description

More or less climbing shrub to 2 m or a small tree, with recurved brown prickles about 3 mm long. Leaves are pari- or imparipinnate, to 30 cm, with 5–6 pairs of petiolulate leaflets; blades are 5–8(–12) × 2–4(–5) cm, subcoriaceous to coriaceous, entire or obscurely crenate-dentate and narrowly acuminate. Many-flowered terminal and subterminal panicles reach 20 cm; flowers are 5-merous. Fruit was unknown to the treatment.

## Distribution

Known from the central forest district of the former Belgian Congo and the Lastoursville region of Gabon.
