Tree capable of reaching 20 m in height and 40 cm in diameter; trunk fluted and sinuous; rhytidome light grey to light brown, ornamented with sinuous veins; tender bark with pale yellow section. Branchlets glabrous. Leaves with petiole 2–7 mm long, glabrous; blade membranous to papyraceous, elliptic-ovate to oblanceolate, 6–20 cm long, 2.5–7.5 cm wide, cuneate to ± rounded at base, acuminate at apex, glabrous on both faces. Lateral veins 9–14 pairs, prominent on both faces.

Flowers solitary, axillary or extra-axillary, pendulous, with pedicel 3–7 cm long, furnished towards the middle with a broadly ovate bracteole, attenuate at apex, ciliate on the margins, 0.6–2 cm long, 0.5–1.5 cm wide. Sepals membranous, greyish-brown at centre, dark red towards the exterior, reflexed, ovate to ovate-lanceolate, attenuate at apex, 1–3 cm long, 0.5–1.7 cm wide, undulate-crisped on the margins. Outer petals whitish, spotted with red and brown, ovate-lanceolate, undulate-crisped, 4–7 cm long, 2–3 cm wide, glabrous; inner petals whitish spotted with pink, cochleariform, elliptic to narrowly oblong, 1.5–3.5 cm long, 0.6–1.5 cm wide, obtuse at apex, unguiculate at base, concave in the median part whose margins bear two oblong, pubescent appendages. Receptacle convex. Stamens cream-white, broadly oblong, 0.75–1 mm long, sessile, anthers linear extrorse, connectives enlarged-truncate above the anthers, puberulent. Ovary ± ovoid, 1.5–2 mm long, 1–1.25 mm wide, glabrous, stigmas pale yellow.

Fruits with pedicel 3.5–7 cm long, glabrous; syncarp pendulous, globular, smooth, green spotted with white when fresh, 4–8 cm in diameter, with woody pericarp 5 mm thick. Numerous seeds, enveloped in a resinous coating, 10–15 mm long, 8–10 mm wide, ornamented with a prominent circular rim, with brown, smooth testa.

## Discussion

Species of dense humid forest and secondary woodland, widely distributed, from Guinea to Angola and Fernando Po.

Flowering occurs on the denuded tree, before the emergence of young leaves.

The seeds, with aromatic odour, are used as an antihaemorrhagic and wound-healing remedy.

Species of dense humid and degraded forest, widely distributed in tropical Africa, from Sierra Leone to Uganda and Angola, at Fernando Po, São Thomé and the Prince Islands.

The fruits are used as a vermifuge by the inhabitants of Gabon; the seeds serve as condiments.
