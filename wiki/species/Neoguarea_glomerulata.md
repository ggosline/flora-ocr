# *Neoguarea glomerulata* (Harms) E.J.M.Koenen & J.J.de Wilde Planche 15

**Genus**: [[Neoguarea]]
**Family**: [[Meliaceae]]
**Protologue**: Pl. Ecol. Evol. 145(2) : 233 (2012)

## Description

Pl. Ecol. Evol. 145(2) : 233 (2012). Guarea glomerulata Harms, Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 23 : 159 (1896). Guarea ngounyensis Pellegr., Bull. Soc. Bot. France 86 : 151 (1939). Shrub reaching 5(–8) m in height. Leaf: petiole 6–17 cm; rachis 20-38 cm; petiolule 2–5(–7) mm, shortly pubescent; leaflets (7–)9–17(–22), elliptic to narrowly rectangular or sometimes obovate, (4.5–)9–17(–24) × (1.5–)3.5–5.5(–6.5) cm, the upper ones usually larger than the lower ones, base obtuse to cuneate or narrowly cuneate, apex acute or caudate, glabrous above but puberulent in the channel of the midvein, below bearing orange or brown punctations and some short indumentum on the venation; lateral veins 12–30(–50), fine. Inflorescence reaching 150 cm in length, with more or less numerous branches, sometimes 30 cm in length, with flowers solitary or fascicled by 3 or more, with axes shortly puberulent to orange glandular hairs. Functionally male flower: pedicel 1–2 mm; calyx 2(–3.5) mm in height, densely covered with glandular trichomes; petals narrowly ovate, 11–13(–15) × 2–2.5 mm; staminal tube 8–10(–13) mm in length, glabrous, anthers 1.3–1.8 × 0.5–0.8 mm; gynoecium (pistillode) 9–10(–14) mm in length, including the gynophore of 0.5–2 mm, densely hairy. Functionally female flower similar; ovary globular, 1.5–2 mm in diameter; style 3–4 mm, hairy, stigma 0.8–1 mm in diameter. Fruit subglobular, with apical depression, 1.3–1.8 cm in diameter, vermillion to dark red. Seed 1.3–1.5 × 0.5–0.9 cm.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, fairly common in all provinces

## Ecology

understory of primary humid forest and old secondary forest; in Gabon, at 250–1020 m elevation, elsewhere up to 1750 m. B: Koenen & de Wilde (2012), Staner (1941). Bibliography
