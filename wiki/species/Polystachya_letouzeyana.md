# *Polystachya letouzeyana* Szlach. & Olsz.

**Genus**: [[Polystachya]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: FCAM 2: 520 (2001)

## Description

Pseudobulbs 6.5 cm × 0.15 cm. Leaf 9 × 0.8 cm, oblong-ligulate. Inflorescence < 1 cm, pauciflorous. Flowers variegated with green. Dorsal sepal ovate to elliptic-ovate, apiculate. Lateral sepals obliquely rectangular, elliptic, weakly saccate at the base. Labellum channeled, fairly thick, sparsely pubescent, trilobed in the upper third; callus positioned beneath the base of the median lobe, thick, fleshy, obtuse; median lobe circular-ovate, very thick, fleshy, finely and densely papillose; lateral lobes triangular, ciliate. - Pl. 115, p. 261.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon

## Ecology

alt. 0-300 m. Epiphyte at 2 m height, hilltop bathed in moisture
