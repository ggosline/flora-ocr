# *Vigna multinervis* Hutch. & Dalziel

**Genus**: [[Vigna]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. W. trop. Afr., éd. 1, 1 : 409 (1928)

## Description

Perennial herb, climbing, very sparsely pubescent except sometimes on the petiolules and peduncles; stem rather slender, 0.5–2 m in length. Stipules ovate-lanceolate, 2–4 mm in length, cordate at the base. Leaf: petiole 2–7.5 cm; rachis 1–5 mm (leaflets sometimes appearing digitate); stipels ovate, 1 mm or less in length; leaflets with a wrinkled petiolule of ± 1 mm, limb lanceolate to linear, 3–10(–15) × (0.2–)0.5–1.3 cm, base rounded to cordate, apex rounded to acuminate, mucronate, rather thick and glabrous; secondary veins perpendicular to the main vein. Inflorescence pauciflorous; peduncle 1–10 cm; rachis 1–1.5 cm, furnished with hollow glands between the pedicels. Flower: pedicel 1.5–3 mm; bracteoles caducous, ovate-lanceolate, 1–1.5 mm in length; calyx glabrous or sparsely pubescent, with tube 2 mm in length, lobes deltoid, 0.7–2 mm in length; standard wider than long, 8–10 × 8–12 mm, apex emarginate, bright yellow within, exterior pink or brownish and glabrous, wings falcate, yellow, base white with pink striae, keel whitish-green, beak short and nearly straight. Legume linear-cylindric, compressed, 3–6 × 0.3–0.5 cm, glabrous or nearly so, dark brown. Seed ellipsoid, 2.8–3 × 1.5–2 × 1.2 mm; strophiole well-developed, yellowish, eccentric.

## Distribution

from Guinea to Sudan, to Uganda, to Zambia and to Angola; in Gabon, known from Haut-Ogooué, Nyanga and Ogooué-Ivindo

## Ecology

on annually burned savannas, grasslands, often on rocky soil, flat and hilly relief, shrubby savanna, near marshes; in Gabon at (0–)100–650 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1350 m. Flowers in June, July, October and November; fruits in July

## Vernacular names

haricot sauvage (fr). B : Pasquet (2001), Verdcourt (1971c), Wilczek (1954). i B liographie
