## Description

Kew Bull. 7(2) : 186 (1952). Commelina simplex Vahl, Enum. pl. 2 : 177 (1805). M. stictosperma Brenan, Kew Bull. 7 : 187 (1952). Erect or ascending herb, up to 120 cm in height; stems in tufts, unbranched or sparsely branched, 2–5 mm in diameter, base sometimes bulbous; roots hard, not tuberized. Leaf: sheath 4 cm in length, pubescent to glabrescent, ciliate at apex; basal blades narrowly elliptic-ovate to linear, 7–40 × 0.4–1.7 cm, the cauline ones shorter, often conduplicate, narrowing strongly toward the apex of the plant, the apical ones bracteolar, faces glabrous to densely pubescent. Flower up to 16 mm in diameter; pedicel 4.5–8 mm, erect during fruiting; sepals narrowly elliptic-ovate to elliptic-ovate, 4.5–7 × 2–3.5 mm, green, glabrous, translucent; petals spreading to reflexed, broadly ovate-orbicular to ovate-elliptic, 5.5–11 × 4.5–8 mm, blue, purple, lilac to pink or mauve; fertile stamens 2, filament curved to one side (in bisexual flowers) or divergent (in male flowers), 5–7 mm, with long spreading flexuous hairs, violet or purple, anther 1–1.3 mm in length, dark blue, with white pollen; staminodes 4, 3 opposite to petals and one opposite to the lower sepal, antherodia with 3 subglobose lobes, light yellow, or sometimes absent; ovary 1.5–2.5 mm in length; style filiform, curved to one side, 4–6 mm, stigma punctiform. Fruit oblong, 4–5(–7.5) × 2.5–4 mm, with apiculate apex, glabrous, opening in 3 valves; locules with 2(–3) uniseriate seeds. Seed transversely ovate-elliptic to rectangular, often flattened on one side, (1.1–)1.4–1.8 × 1.2–1.7 mm; testa covered with a dull, brown, scrobiculate to alveolate layer, generally provided with small white tubercles arranged radially between the depressions.

## Distribution

from Senegal to Ethiopia, extending to southern Africa, Madagascar and also in Asia; in Gabon in Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué, Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

coastal savannas and other damp sandy places; in Gabon up to 500 m in altitude, elsewhere up to 1700 m. Flowering from mid-afternoon until 6 o'clock in the evening.
