# *Afraegle paniculata* (Schum.) Engl.

**Family**: [[Rutaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Afraegle]]

## Description

Shrub or small tree to 15–20 m and 40 cm diameter, often branching near ground level; wood hard, pale yellow, branches usually with slender straight axillary spines to 2 cm. Leaves trifoliolate, rarely reduced; petiole 1.5–4 cm, leaflets obovate or oblong-oblanceolate, 4–15 × 2–7 cm and slightly crenate above. Few-flowered axillary panicles are 4–6 cm. Fragrant white flowers have four 10–13 × 4–6 mm petals, usually 16 stamens and an 8–10-locular ovary.

## Distribution

Humid-forest West Africa; the treatment says it is not certainly known east of Nigeria and regards sterile Cameroonian material as doubtful. It does not confirm a Gabon occurrence.

## Source

Letouzey, R. (1963). Rutacées. *Flore du Gabon* 6: 106–109.
