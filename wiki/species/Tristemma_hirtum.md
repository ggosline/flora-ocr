# *Tristemma hirtum* Palisot de Beauvois

**Genus**: [[Tristemma]]
**Family**: [[Melastomataceae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. Oware et Bénin 1 : 94, tab. 57 (1806); JACQ.-FÉL., Fl. Cam. 24 : 68, tab. 19 (1983)

## Description

Perennial herb, hirsute; branches generally quadrangular-winged, sometimes subangular. Leaves with petiole 1–3 cm, flattened, hirsute; lamina ± 4 × 10 cm, lanceolate to ovate-lanceolate, rounded at the base, acute to subacuminate at the apex; bristles dense appressed above, strigose below, especially on the veins, mixed with short hairs; 5 (7) veins.

Glomerules voluminous; axial bracts foliaceous, floral bracts lanceolate, as long as the flowers, hirsute on the back, except the innermost glabrescent on the compressed portions. Flower sessile; hypanthium oblong-campanulate, 2–3 membranous-ciliate rings, membrane 1 mm on the upper ring, narrower or absent on the underlying rings; often ciliate fringes without membrane towards the base; sepals triangular-acuminate, ± 3 × 5 mm, distinctly ciliate, sometimes with a few dorsal bristles. Petals pink, obovate, 6 × 16 mm, ciliate. Stamens relatively long; anther 8 mm, subulate; podoconnective short, curved, anterior lobes of equal length, erect, linear-obtuse; filament 7 mm. Apex of ovary convex, densely covered with stiff bristles, taller than the glabrous peristyle ridge; style relatively long, linear, 1.5 cm.

Fruit oblong-ellipsoid, 8 × 13 mm. — Pl. 17, p. 49.

## Type

Palisot de Beauvois, Nigéria (holo-, G; iso-, P)

## Discussion

Species very close to T. mauritianum; generally more hirsute; with narrower leaves and shorter petiole; with longer subulate stamens. Replaces T. mauritianum towards West Africa; the populations from Gabon are more clearly distinct.
