# *Drypetes gabonensis* Pierre ex Hutch.

**Genus**: [[Drypetes]]
**Family**: [[Putranjivaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. trop. Afr. 6(1): 680 (1912)

## Description

Plate 44

Tree up to 25 (-35) m tall; trunk up to 37.5 (-75) cm in diameter, straight or leaning, slightly grooved; bark smooth, pale greenish-brown to pale brown, with a remarkable heavy odor; branchlets cylindric to slightly flattened, distinctly slender, very sparsely puberulent, glabrescent; buds finely hairy. Stipules caducous, broadly ovate, ±1.8 (-3) mm long, puberulent. Leaf: petiole (4.2)-7.5-9.5 (-13) mm, canaliculate above, not very wrinkled, glabrous; lamina elliptic-ovate to broadly oblong, (10.3)-12.5-18 (-21.5) × (3.5)-4.5-6 (-9) cm, coriaceous, remarkably asymmetric at the base, less often oblique, acute to slightly obtuse, sometimes the two inserted at different levels, short and abruptly acuminate at the apex, with curved apex, margin subentire to slightly and obscurely crenate or serrulate, plane to slightly recurved, frequently undulate, shiny and dark green above, much paler and dull below; venation narrowly reticulate, slightly raised above and below, midvein glabrous, secondary veins 6 or 8 (to 9), slightly prominent above, ascending, slightly looped and anastomosing well before the margin. Male inflorescence in the axils of leaves or below the leaves along the branches, with up to ±50 flowers, often much fewer; bracts ovate, 0.2-0.4 (-1.3) mm long, densely puberulent. Male flower remarkably fragrant, pedicel slender, (2)-4.5 (-6.5) mm, puberulent, glabrescent; sepals (3 or) 4 (or 5), imbricate, ovate to oblong, (1.2)-1.9-2.3 (-2.9) × (0.3)-1.8 mm, obtuse and slightly cucullate toward the apex, greenish to bright yellow, slightly puberulent on the outside, glabrous on the inside, with minute marginal cilia; stamens 3, in a whorl around the margin of the disk, filament (0.8)-1.5-3.4 (-4.2) mm, anther (0.4)-1.0-1.1 (-1.4) mm long, glabrous; disk slightly concave to concave, (0.3-) 0.5-0.8 (-1.2) mm in diameter, fine, glabrous, with slightly lobed margin, the lobes slightly prominent between the filaments. Female inflorescence on the branches and cauliflorous, with up to ±7 flowers; bracts 0.6-0.9 mm long, as in the males. Female flower: pedicel 1.9-3.2 mm, robust, puberulent, glabrescent; sepals 5, imbricate, ovate to suborbicular, 2.9-3.9 × 2.5-3.1 mm, puberulent on the outside, glabrescent, glabrous on the inside, with short marginal cilia; disk cupuliform, up to ±2.8 mm in diameter, glabrous; ovary globose, depressed at the apex, with 3 (or 4) locules, glabrous; style 0.7–1.1 mm, unbranched and columnar, then 3-branched, arising from an apical depression of the ovary, with spatulate to obotrapezoidal stigma, 0.8–1 × 1–1.7 mm. Fruit subglobose, 19–21 × 20–23.5 mm, reddish, glabrous, with caducous sepals and stigmas, with (1 or) 2 or 3 (or 4) seeds; pedicel 2–4.5 mm, thick, glabrous. Seed up to ±17 mm long.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Angola; in Gabon: Estuaire, Ngounié, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo and Ogooué-Maritime

Habitat: primary and secondary evergreen forests, riparian forests; in Gabon at 10–660 m altitude, elsewhere up to 850 m.

## Source note

D. gabonensis has been collected mainly in the Estuaire and central Gabon. The male flowers of D. gabonensis are smaller and different from the female flowers, which bear suborbicular sepals. Its branches display a somewhat flexible appearance in herbarium material and its leaves are generally discolorous, the upper surface very shiny. Specimens with female flowers of D. gabonensis appear to have been collected very rarely
