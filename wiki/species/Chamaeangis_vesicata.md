# *Chamaeangis vesicata* (Lindl.) Schltr.

**Genus**: [[Chamaeangis]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Beih. Bot. Centralbl. 36 : 109 (1918) ; FTWA : 264 (1968) ; Cribb, FTEA, Orchid. 3 : 547 (1989) ; FAC 2 : 598 (1992) ; FCAM 3 : 760 (2001)

## Description

– Listrostachys vesicata (Lindl.) Rchb.f., Flora 48 : 190 (1865).

Stem 1–5 (–8) cm. Leaves 3–10, 20–40 × 0.7–1.6 cm, linear, falcate, margins revolute, very unequally bilobed, generally twisted at the base to lie in the same plane. Inflorescence dense, 7–30 cm, < 100-flowered. Flowers small, lemon-green, yellow, orange, fragrant during the day, 2 at each node. Dorsal sepal ovate to elliptic-ovate. Petals lanceolate, oblong-ovate. Lateral sepals obliquely ovate, shortly apiculate. Labellum 2.5–3 × 1–2 mm, narrowly triangular, oblong-ovate to narrowly ovate, with a small callus at the spur throat. Spur 7–12 mm, incurved, narrowly cylindric, abruptly dilated in the apical third and then reaching 3 mm in diameter. — Pl. 202, p. 431.

## Distribution

Guinea, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Togo, Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Congo, Central African Republic, Zaire, Rwanda, Burundi, Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania

## Ecology

alt. 0–2,200 m. Epiphytic in riparian and montane forest, observed 5 m above ground on Bridelia sp., Juniperus procera, drought-tolerant
