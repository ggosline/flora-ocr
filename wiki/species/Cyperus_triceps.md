# *Cyperus triceps* Endl. Planche 62

**Genus**: [[Cyperus]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Cat. horti. Vindob . 1 : 94 (1842)

## Synonyms

- Kyllinga tenuifolia Steud., Synops. pl. glum . 2 : 69 (1855).
- Kyllinga triceps Rottb., Descr. icon. rar. pl . : 14, t. IV, 6 (1773), nom. illeg.

## Description

Perennial herb, tufted, with the base of stems slightly swollen, bulb-shaped; old bulbs persistent; stems 5‒30 cm × 0.5‒1 mm, angular, glabrous, with longitudinal ridges, base covered by old sheaths. Leaf: blade 5‒15 cm × 1.5‒3 mm, flat or incurved, densely scabrous on the margin and central nerve, often with dark violet dots or lines. Inflorescence in an irregular and dense head, white or greenish, often triangular in outline, 4‒9 mm wide, composed of a few distinct spikes or more commonly, an ovate central spike with a few smaller spikes at the base; involucral bracts generally 3‒6, foliaceous, reflexed or spreading, the largest 2‒8 cm long. Spikelet 2‒2.5 mm long, one-flowered, with glumes separating slightly when the achenes reach maturity; glumes elliptic, 1.5‒2.2 mm long, whitish with brown dots near the glabrous and not winged keel, with 3‒4 prominent lateral nerves, central nerve of the upper glumes terminating in a short point. Flower with 1‒3 stamens; style with 2 branches. Achene ellipsoid to obovoid, approximately 1.5 mm long, flattened, brown, finely papillose.

## Distribution

distributed in the tropical regions of Africa and Asia, except in the most humid regions; common in Gabon, in the Woleu-Ntem, particularly around Oyem, but not found elsewhere (8 collections)

## Ecology

seasonally humid grasslands and shallow soils of inselbergs; in Gabon, around 500‒700 m elevation, up to 2100 m elsewhere. Taxonomic note: resembles C. inselbergensis, but distinguished from it by its triangular inflorescence formed of an ovate to cylindrical central spike, often accompanied by a few smaller lateral spikes. It is also distinguished by its glumes with a glabrous keel (neither scabrous nor winged). B: Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al. (2010), Hooper & Napper (1972), Bibliography Lye (1997). CYPERUS L. subgenus PYCNOSTACHYS C.B.Clarke Fl. Brit. India 6: 597 (1893). Perennial herbs or more rarely annuals; stems usually 20‒80 cm long. Basal leaves numerous, rare or absent. Anatomy of stems and leaves of eucyperoid type (that is, C3-type anatomy). Inflorescence in a lax anthela (rarely compact) with groups of digitate spikelets and generally large foliaceous involucral bracts. Spikelet many-flowered, 2‒15 mm long, slightly compressed; axis persistent (spikelets not falling as a single unit); glumes 0.5‒3.5 mm long, obtuse, acute or with central nerve prolonged by a straight or curved mucro. Flower with 1‒3 stamens, connective often producing an apical appendage; style caducous, with 3 branches. Achenes sessile, trigonous, 0.4‒1.5 mm long, surface usually tuberculate or finely papillose. Medium-sized subgenus, comprising approximately 150 species from marshes and forests, usually in zones with high rainfall, growing mostly in tropical and subtropical regions; in Gabon 8 species.
