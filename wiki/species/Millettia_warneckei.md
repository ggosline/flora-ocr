# *Millettia warneckei* Harms var. warneckei

**Genus**: [[Millettia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 30 : 87 (1901)

## Description

Large vine or small tree up to 8 m in height; branchlets tomentose to glabrous, with whitish lenticels; reddish exudate. Stipules ± persistent, narrowly ovate to ovate, 3–6 × 1–4 mm, with spreading pubescence externally, ciliate margins, with 4 to 8 nerves. Leaf: petiole 1.5–5 cm, channelled above, with spreading pubescence; rachis like the petiole, 5–15 cm; pulvinus 3–10 mm, silky to pubescent; stipels subulate, 1.5–3(–4) mm, slightly pubescent or glabrous; leaflets 7 to 13, petiolule 1–4.5 mm, blade elliptic to narrowly ovate or obovate, 2–9 × 1–4 cm, base attenuate to rounded or slightly cordate, apex acute to acuminate, mucronulate, acumen up to 8 × 4 mm, glabrous above, margins slightly revolute, silky below; lateral nerves prominent, in 5 to 16 pairs. Inflorescence terminal or sometimes axillary, in panicle 12–24 cm in length, brown tomentose; peduncle 2.5–7 cm; bracts persistent or caducous, ovate, ± 2 × 1 mm, silky externally. Flower: pedicel 2–5 mm, silky; bracteoles at base of calyx, ovate, 1.5–3 × 1.5–3 mm, base truncate to cordate, apex acute, silky externally, ciliate margins, glabrous internally; calyx silky yellowish to dark brown, with tube 3–5.5 mm in length, lower lobes 1–1.5 mm, upper lobes connate except at apex for less than 0.5 mm; corolla cream white, with pale greenish spot at base of standard, sweet-scented, standard with claw 3–5 mm, blade broadly elliptic to suborbicular, 11–19 × 9–17 mm, apex emarginate, auricles inflexed, wings with claw 2–4.5 mm, blade elliptic, 9–18 × 3–6 mm, apex obtuse, auricle 1–1.5 × 0.5–0.8 mm, lateral pocket present, keel with claw 2–7 mm, blade elliptic-falcate, 9–21 × 4–6 mm, apex obtuse, auricle 0.5–1 mm in length, lateral pocket present; stamens with staminal tube 3–14 mm, free part of filaments 2–9 mm; ovary 6–10 mm in length, with spreading and dense pubescence, stipe 0.5–1 mm, with 3 to 6 ovules; style 4–14 mm, with dense pubescence except apex for 1–2 mm. Pod oblong,

flattened, 4–12.5 × 1.5–4 cm, base attenuate to cuneate, apex pointed, with 1 to 3 seeds, valves contorted, yellowish brown; rust to dark brown, tomentose, glabrate with age. Seed broadly elliptic, 6–10 × 4–6 mm.

## Distribution

from Guinea to Nigeria and Equatorial Guinea; expected in Gabon and Cameroon

## Ecology

forest, clearings, near rivers, disturbed forests; at 240–450 m altitude. Use: The wood is used for case construction
