# *Cyclocotyla congolensis* Stapf Planche 28

**Genus**: [[Cyclocotyla]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1908 : 260 (1908)

## Description

Liane reaching 40 m in height; exudate abundant, white; branches with numerous lenticels, ramification ternate, glabrous, medium green. Leaf glabrous: petiole 1–2 cm, with 1 row of axillary colleters; limb narrowly elliptic, sometimes narrowly obovate, (3–)5–7(–8) × (1–)1.5–2.5(–3) cm, cuneate at base, acuminate with an obtuse point at apex; secondary nerves more than 20 pairs, dense. Inflorescence terminal and at the axil of apical leaves, sometimes axillary, lax, 3.5–8 × 5.5–12 cm, glabrous; bracts 1–2 mm long; peduncle 0.7–3.5 cm. Flower fragrant; pedicel 1.5–7 mm; calyx pale green to pale red, glabrous on the outside, fleshy, with unequal lobes, the largest up to 3 times as long and also as wide as the smallest, 3–6 × 4–7 mm, membranous margin with 1 to 3 rows of erect internal colleters, of increasing length from the axis, 1–3 mm in total, up to 5 mm per lobe; corolla white or cream, red-wine or rose with reddish longitudinal bands at the base, 8.5–10 mm long, glabrous on both surfaces, with almost cylindrical tube, 6–9 mm long; lobes spreading and with revolute border, obliquely ovate, 1.9–3 × 3.5–4.5 mm, auriculate on the right side; stamens included, anther 4–5 mm long; disk covering the ovary; pistil 6–8 mm long. Fruit pyriform, 12–22 × 12–25 mm, smooth, pale green. Seed oblong, 8–10 mm long, pale brown, shiny. Distribution: Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Central African Republic, and Democratic Republic of the Congo; Gabon: Ngounié, Nyanga, Ogooué, Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo, Ogooué-Maritime, and Woleu-Ntem. Ecology: Forests, often near rivers, in Gabon up to 650 m altitude; elsewhere up to 700 m. B: Beentje (2021), van der Ploeg (1985). BIBLIOGRAPHY
