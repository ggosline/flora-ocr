# *Inversodicraea boumiensis* (C.Cusset) Cheek Planche 9

**Genus**: [[Inversodicraea]]
**Family**: [[Podostemaceae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull. 71(4) : 55, 2 (2016)

## Description

Kew Bull. 71(4) : 55, 2 (2016). Ledermanniella boumiensis C.Cusset, Bull. Mus. natl. Hist. nat., sér. 4., Adansonia 5(4) : 370 (1983). Plant with basal thalloid foliate portion; stem erect, robust, dichotomous branching, 2.5–3 cm, with scattered foliate scales toward the base, becoming increasingly dense toward the apex of the branches. Foliate scales entire, at the base of the stem oval to subrhomboidal with acute apex, mucronulate, becoming progressively elliptic toward the apex of the branches with obtuse to rounded apex, 0.8–0.9 × 1–1.2 mm. Leaves caducous, recurved by water current, 2- to 5-pinnate, vigorous, 2–6 cm long. Inflorescence terminal on the branches, subsessile, bearing (2–)3 to 5 flowers; bracts oval with attenuate base, entire, irregularly denticulate at apex, grouped at the base of the spathe; spathes ellipsoid, mucronate, tearing irregularly at anthesis at the apex. Flower: pedicel 4–5 mm after anthesis; tepals linear, 0.4 mm long; androecium longer than ovary, stamens 2, filament ± 2 mm, fused for approximately half its length, anther 0.6 × 0.4 mm, pollen in dyads; ovary 1.6–1.8 × 0.4 mm, gynophore 0.1 mm long; stigmas linear, 0.5 mm. Fruit ellipsoid, 2 × 0.6 mm. Seed 0.3 × 0.2 mm.

## Distribution

South Cameroon and Gabon; in Gabon, known from a single collection from Mbigou (Ngounié). B: Cheek et al. (2017), Cusset (1983, 1987a), Sosef et al. (2006). Bibliography
