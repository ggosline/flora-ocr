# *Eriosema pulcherrimum* Taub. Planche 63

**Genus**: [[Eriosema]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]

## Description

in Engl. & Prantl, Nat. Pflanzenfam. 3(3) : 375 (1894). Perennial herb, 30–40(–60) cm tall; root tuberculous, fusiform, up to 20 × 1 cm; stem erect, angular, velutinous-woolly, white or silvery. Stipules persistent, lanceolate, 1.5–3 cm long, hairy. Leaf: petiole and petiolule together 3–7 mm long, with white appressed to woolly hairy indumentum; leaflet 1, lamina ovate-elliptic, 8–18 × (3–)6–8.5 cm, base subcordate, apex rounded or slightly acute, glabrescent above when mature; midvein prominent below; lateral veins in 10 to 12(–15) pairs, ascending, ± straight. Inflorescence mainly terminal; racemes elongate, very dense, 5–12(–14) cm long; bracts filiform, 15–17 mm, acute, with long white hairs. Flower pendulous, subsessile; calyx gibbous, campanulate, sparsely furnished with long white woolly hairs, tube ± 3 mm long, teeth lanceolate, 3–4 mm long, very acute; corolla purple to violet, standard oblong, 9–10 × 7–8 mm, apex emarginate, slightly hairy externally, claw 2–3 mm, with 2 acute uncinate auricles, without gibbosities, wings oblong, oblique, ± 8.5 × 2–3 mm, claw 2.5 mm, with 2 unequal auricles, keel oblique, obtuse, auriculate at base, whitish; ovary with long hairs; style glabrous. Pod rounded-rectangular, 10 × 7–11 mm, 2-seeded, with grey silky hairs. Seed 4–5 × 1.5–3 mm, reddish-brown, smooth.

## Distribution

Ghana, Togo, Benin, Nigeria, Sudan, Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, known from Haute-Ogooué and Ngounié

## Ecology

savannas, sometimes as a pyrophyte; in Gabon at ± 450 m altitude, elsewhere at 250–680 m. Flowers in December. Bibliography: Ern (1984); Hauman (1954c), Hepper (1958), van der Maesen (2006a), Pellegrin (1949).
