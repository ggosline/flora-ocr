# *Cyathula prostrata* var. *pedicellata* (C. B. Clarke) Cavaco

**Family**: [[Amaranthaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Cyathula]]

## Description

Creeping forest herb about 60 cm long, rooting at nodes; only the c. 30 cm flowering branches ascend. Leaves resemble the typical variety but reach only 5 × 3 cm. Slender inflorescences to 10 cm; cymules remain upright against the axis after flowering, on c. 2 mm pedicels, with widely spreading glochids. Fertile flowers nearly glabrous.

## Distribution

Sierra Leone to the former Belgian Congo, Uganda and Tanzania. Gabon: 7 km east of Mitzic, Jeffrey 35.
