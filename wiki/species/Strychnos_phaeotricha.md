# *Strychnos phaeotricha* Gilg

**Genus**: [[Strychnos]]
**Family**: [[Loganiaceae]]

## Description

in ENGLER, Bot. Jahrb. 36: 105, fig. 3 (1905). — FWTA ed. 2, 2: 41.

— S. thyrsiflora GILG in MILDBRAED, Wiss. Ergebn. deutsch. Zentr.-Afr. Exped. 1907-08, 2: 532, t. 74 (1914).

Liane reaching a length of 35-50 m and a height of 4-40 m or more; stem 2-7 cm in diameter or more; bark pale grey, lenticellate; branches lenticellate or not; branchlets brown-hirsute, not lenticellate; tendrils in pairs.

Leaves with short petiole, brown-setulose, 2-4 mm long, with a few large glandular hairs at the base; blade papyraceous, even when fresh (young leaves membranaceous when dry), narrowly obovate, narrowly elliptic or sometimes narrowly obovate (and ovate for leaves on the main axis), (2.5-) 5-16.5 × (1.4-) 2.5-8 cm, reaching, for leaves exposed to shade, up to 22 × 8 cm and often herbaceous, acuminate at the apex, cordate or subcordate at the base, brown-setulose on both surfaces, especially or only on the midrib; one pair of secondary veins curved along the margin; tertiary venation forming characteristic "mussel-shell" patterns beneath.

Inflorescences terminal, partially monochasial from the branch apices, lax, multiflorous, 5.8 × 5-8 cm; peduncle and branches slender, brown-hirsute as the pedicels. Flowers 5-merous, sessile or subsessile. Sepals green, fused at the base, linear or nearly so, 1.8-3 × 0.2-0.6 mm, subulate, brown-hirsute outside, glabrous inside. Corolla, in the mature bud, 2.8-4 mm long, white, cream or yellow, brown-hirsute outside on the lobes, or sometimes entirely glabrous, inside with a pubescent tuft of thick hairs, just above the base of the lobes; tube urceolate, very short, 0.5-0.8 mm long; limb 6.5-7.5 mm in diameter; lobes narrowly oblong, bearing the tuft of hairs mentioned above just above the base and, from this point, recurved, 3.3-5 times longer than the tube, 2.6-4 × 0.7-1 mm (in the bud 2.3-3.2 × 0.7-1 mm), acute. Stamens very exsert, inserted at the throat of the corolla, filament bearing internally toward the base a bilobed pubescent tuft, then curved at the insertion of this tuft, for the rest glabrous, 2-2.5 mm long, elongated during flowering; anther suborbicular, glabrous or with a few small stiff hairs at the base. Pistil slightly pilose in the middle, or completely glabrous, 2.8-3.2 mm long; ovary ovoid, 1-1.5 × 0.6-1 mm, 2-locular; style curved at the apex during flowering, straight afterwards, 1.5-2 mm long; stigma capitate; 5-6 ovules per locule.

Fruits orange (?), ellipsoid, quite large, soft, 25-30 × 18-27 × 18-25 mm, containing 3-7 seeds, glabrous at maturity but brown-setulose previously; wall thin, about 1 mm thick when dry, brittle. Seeds flattened, ± plano-convex, obliquely ovate or elliptic, generally irregularly curved, 15-23 × 10-15 × 3-6 mm, smooth; testa very thin, adhering to the pulp. Pl. 35, p. 142.
