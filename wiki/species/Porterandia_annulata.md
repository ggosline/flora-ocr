# *Porterandia annulata* (K. Schum.) Keay

**Genus**: [[Porterandia]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Keay, Bull. J. B. Brux. 28 : 24 (1958)

## Synonyms

- Randia annulata K. Schum., Engl. Bot. Jahrb. 23 : 435 (1897).
- Randia Letestui Pellegr., Bull. S. B. Fr. 82 : 467 (1936).

## Description

Liane with young internodes clothed in tawny pubescence. Stipules lanceolate 8-10 mm long, pubescent at the juvenile stage, connate tubular at the base for approximately 3 mm, caducous, leaving a circular scar adorned with a fringe of tawny hairs on the inner margin. Petiole pubescent 9-20 (30) mm. Blade pubescent then glabrescent above, brownish when dry, obovate-oblong 8-18 × 3-7 mm; base cordate sometimes rounded, often asymmetric; apex briefly acute or obtuse. 10-17 pairs of secondary veins pubescent on the lower surface.

Inflorescences 2-8-flowered with peduncle 5-12 mm; axes pubescent tawny; bracts opposite deltoid approximately 3 mm, persistent. Pedicel 3-10 mm long; flower purple-violet or deep pink inside, yellowish outside. Ovary densely tawny pubescent 7 × 4 mm; calycine limb tubular slightly flared 9 × 8 mm, with 5 (6) deltoid acute or attenuate teeth 3-4 mm high. Corolla funnel-shaped with tube 20-25 (30) × 15-20 mm, densely ochraceous hairy when dry, with 5 ovate or suborbicular lobes with ± acute apex, 10-15 mm long, with outer surface ochraceous hairy on the overlapping half; strong dense ring of hairs over 4 mm in the narrow part of the corolla tube. Anther 11 × 2 mm, apiculate for 0.5 mm, emarginate at the base, subsessile, inserted toward the lower quarter. Style 22 mm with furrowed stigma 8 × 2.5 mm; column very weakly inflated and finely furrowed in its lower half; disk glabrous 3 mm in diameter.

Fruit elliptic 4 × 2 cm plus the calycine limb. Very numerous seeds approximately 2 mm.
