# *Calliandra surinamensis* Bentham ¹

**Genus**: [[Calliandra]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Hook. Lond. Journ. Bot. 3: 105 (1844)

## Synonyms

- Inga fasciculata Willd., Spec. Pl. 4 (2): 1022 (1806).
- Mimosa fasciculata POIR., Dict. suppl. 1: 46 (1810).
- Anneslia fasciculata (WILLD.) KLEINH., Fl. Suriname 2 (2): 322 (1940).

## Description

Shrub reaching 3 m, branching low. Mature branches glabrous; smooth when fresh and wrinkled when dry, with grey rhytidome bearing fairly light brown lenticels. Young branches glabrous or pubescent, greyish black. Stipules glabrous, ciliate margins, narrowly triangular asymmetric, reaching 22 mm; asymmetric base: adaxial margin attenuate rounded and abaxial margin rounded cordate; dense parallel venation, prominent on the exterior; apex very acute. Leaves often clustered on short branches. Petiole sparsely pubescent with thickened apex, 2.5–10 mm long; upper surface canaliculate; lower surface terminating in a glabrous, triangular mucro, ± ribbed, with very acute apex. Pinnae 1 pair. Pinnae axis sparsely pubescent ± hirsute, flattened transversely, 1–8 cm long; upper surface canaliculate; lower surface terminated by a glabrous mucro, folded in a gutter, with

acute apex. Leaflets 4–16 pairs, sessile to subsessile. Blade glabrous on both faces; dark green to glossy green above and green to brown below when dry; oblong, asymmetric oblong elliptic, elliptic for the apical ones, ± weakly falciform, of decreasing size from apex to base, reaching 22 × 8 mm; asymmetric base: proximal margin ± cordate to strongly cordate and distal margin ± rounded; margin sometimes ciliate; apex acute ± apiculate. Main vein eccentric toward the distal margin, prominent on both faces. Secondary veins ± ascending, weakly curved or straight, prominent on both faces; up to 3 basal divergent secondary veins in the proximal half, one dominant. Veinlets in rather loose network, little visible above and ± prominent below.

Inflorescences in capitate spikes, erect above the branches when fresh, axillary, solitary or fascicled. Inflorescence peduncle pubescent, 10–20 (35) mm long, ribbed or angular. Bracts 1–3 on the peduncle, glabrous or sparsely pubescent, lanceolate, 1.25–2 mm long, ciliate margin, acute apex. Bracteoles glabrous to sparsely pubescent, lanceolate, 1–2 mm long, ciliate margin, very acute apex. Floral bud obovoid or claviform, green when fresh. Flower 8 or 9 through abortion, sessile or subsessile. Basal flower 8 or 9. Calyx cylindric, ± pubescent externally with erect hairs, green to pale green when fresh, 2.25–3.25 mm long; free lobes 5–6, lanceolate to elliptic lanceolate, 0.25–0.60 mm long, acute apex. Corolla tetramerous, pubescent externally above the calyx on the tube, green to pale greenish yellow when fresh, broadly obconic, 7.5–13 mm long; free lobes broadly ovate, 1.25–3.5 mm long, acute apex. Stamens white at the base then pink violet to red toward the apex when fresh, 28.5–60 mm long; narrow tube 12–33 mm long, sometimes slightly thickened at the base; anther oblong, 0.25 mm long, connective strongly granulose. Pistillode ellipsoid or pistil sessile to subsessile, ovary puberulent, ellipsoid, 1.5–2 mm long, containing 6–7 ovules, style slender 22.5–55.5 mm long, stigma filiform. Central flowers always 9. Calyx as previously for form, pubescence, colour, 2.5–4 mm long; free lobes 0.25–1 mm long, acute apex. Corolla as previously for form, pubescence, colour, 8.5–13 mm long; free lobes broadly ovate, 1–3.5 mm long, acute apex. Stamens of same colour as previously, 33–60 mm long; fused into a tube for 17.5–33 mm, broadly flared at apex; anther as previously. Intrastaminal disk fleshy, 0.5 mm high, free, with lobulate margin. Pistil sessile to subsessile; ovary puberulent with some long hairs on the lateral faces, ellipsoid, 1.25–3 mm long, containing (4) 7–9 ovules; style slender, 24.5–55.5 mm long, with capitate or filiform stigma.

Legume dehiscent by 2 dehiscence slits, woody, brown to blackish when dry, ± pubescent, asymmetrically long and very narrowly lanceolate, 7.7–9 × 0.8–1.1 cm, attenuate at base; sutural ridges very thickened; venation of lateral faces, oblique relative to the dorsal ridge, ascending, prominent becoming very much attenuate toward the central sutural margin, forming a dense network, apex subacute apiculate. Seeds 5–7, longitudinal, very light brown, ovoid ellipsoid to oblongoid, 7–10 × 4–8.5 mm; no visible aril. – Pl. 41, Fig. 1–3, p. 173.

## Type

Horstman 171, Surinam (iso-, P)

## Discussion

Native to tropical America. Introduced elsewhere as an ornamental plant.
