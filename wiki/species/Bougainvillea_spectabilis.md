# *Bougainvillea spectabilis* Willd.

**Family**: [[Nyctaginaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Bougainvillea]]

## Description

Climbing ornamental shrub with robust axillary spines to 2 cm. Three-flowered axillary and terminal clusters are surrounded by three persistent, coloured, ovate-acuminate leafy bracts c. 3 cm long. Tubular perianth 2–2.5 cm, green below and yellow, 5-lobed above; stamens ten or fewer; fruit 5-ribbed.

## Distribution

Brazilian species widely cultivated as an ornamental in tropical Africa; represented by a Libreville herbarium collection.
