# *Waltheria indica* L..

**Genus**: [[Waltheria]]
**Family**: [[Sterculiaceae]]
**Protologue**: LINNÉ, Sp. Pl. 2 : 673 (1753)

## Synonyms

- W.americana L.

## Description

— PELLEGR., Sterc. Gab. Mém. S. B. Fr. : 45 (1950-1951). — F. W. T. A. 2° ed. I : 319 (1958).

Herb erect or with the habit of a shrub. Branch tips clothed with erect, stellate yellowish hairs. Stipules tapered, 4.6 mm long, pubescent. Petiole hairy, 1–2 cm long. Blade 4–12 cm long by 2–6 cm wide, rather elongately ovate, with rounded or subcordate base, with obtuse or subacute apex, with irregularly denticulate margins, clothed with stellate hairs on both faces. Lateral veins 6–8 pairs, ascending and straight, the lower pair basal, terminating with their ramifications in the margin teeth.

Inflorescences in compact, subsessile glomerules, sometimes arranged in small terminal spikes. Glomerules 5–10 mm in diameter, of 12–20 sessile flowers. Bracts hairy, lanceolate, nearly as long as the flowers. Calyx 4–5 mm long, with 10-nerved tube, with 5 acute teeth, very hairy except inside the tube. 5 yellow petals, 3–4 mm long, narrow and glabrous. Androecium glabrous with 5 stamens fused into a collar. Anthers extrorse with 2 parallel locules. Ovary with a single biovulate locule, clothed, including the style, with stellate hairs; stigma brush-like.

Capsular fruit pubescent, 2.5 mm, included in the calyx. Valves unequal. Solitary seed, ovoid, blackish, not ornamented, 2 mm long.

This species widely distributed in the tropics, as a ruderal or in savannas, is known from Mauritania to South Africa and also to Madagascar. In Gabon it is infrequent.
