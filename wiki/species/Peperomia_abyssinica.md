# *Peperomia abyssinica* Miq. Planche 25.2

**Genus**: [[Peperomia]]
**Family**: [[Piperaceae]]
**Protologue**: London J. Bot. 4 : 419 (1845)

## Description

Perennial herb, epiphytic, glabrous, up to 40 cm tall. Leaves alternate; petiole 4–5 mm; blade elliptic, 1.5–10 × 0.5–4 cm, with cuneate base, acute to obtuse or rounded apex, thick and often fleshy, with 5 to 7 palmate veins. Inflorescence with 1 spike per node, terminal and/or axillary, 1.5–12 cm × 1–3.5 mm, greenish to yellowish; peduncle 10–20 mm. Fruit obovoid, ± 1 mm in diameter, papillose.

## Distribution

East Africa, Gabon and possibly Cameroon (see Notes); in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Ogooué-Ivindo and Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology

epiphytic on the base of trunks, in dense coastal forest, upland forest, riparian forest, submontane forest and montane forest, sometimes in rocky places; in Gabon at 10–610 m altitude, elsewhere up to 3150 m

## Source note

P. abyssinica is reported principally from East Africa. Its resemblance to P. vulcanica Baker & C.H.Wright, described from Cameroon, is striking. However, it differs by its elliptic leaves, with non-cordate base. The presence of the species in Cameroon is probable, but a revision of all African material is necessary.
