# *Maesobotrya griffoniana* (Baill.) Pierre ex Hutch.

**Genus**: [[Maesobotrya]]
**Family**: [[Phyllanthaceae]]
**Protologue**: Pierardia griffoniana Baill., Adansonia 4 : 136 (1864)

## Description

in Dyer, Fl. trop. Afr. 6(1.4) : 669 (1912). Pierardia griffoniana Baill., Adansonia 4 : 136 (1864). Maesobotrya bipindensis (Pax) Hutch. in Dyer, Fl. trop. Afr. 6(1.4) : 667 (1912), syn. nov. Tree 4–14 m tall; trunk up to 14 cm in diameter; branchlets beige to pale grey, glabrous. Stipules early caducous, narrowly triangular to subulate, (2–)3–4(–5) mm long. Leaf: petiole canaliculate above, 0.5–7 cm, ± glabrous; blade elliptic to obovate, (9–)12–17(–21) × 3–7(–10) cm, cuneate to rounded at base, acuminate over (0.5–)1–2(–2.5) cm, ± glabrous, glands present beneath but inconspicuous; lateral veins 4–6 pairs. Inflorescence on trunk, large branches or axillary, fasciculate or not, 2–4(–20) cm long, densely puberulent to almost glabrous. Flower solitary or in groups of 2 to 5, 5(–6)-merous. Male flower: pedicel c. 1 mm, puberulent; sepals ± ovate, c. 1 mm long, puberulent outside; stamens 4–5, c. 2 mm long, glabrous; disk glabrous to puberulent. Female flower: pedicel 1–1.5 mm, puberulent; sepals as in male flowers; disk annular, puberulent; pistil c. 2 mm long, ovary 3-locular, with short erect hairs; styles c. 1 mm. Fruit indehiscent or tardily dehiscent, obovoid-ellipsoid, ± angular when dry, 11–16 × 10–13 mm, red, with short, scattered and appressed hairs. Seeds 1(–2); sarcotesta white.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea and Gabon; in Gabon, observed in western provinces: Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué, Ogooué-Maritime, Ngounié and Nyanga

## Ecology

primary and secondarized forest; in Gabon, at 0–600 m altitude

Taxonomic note: This species was also known, especially in Cameroon, under the name M. bipindensis (Pax) Hutch. This name is placed in synonymy with M. griffoniana here for the first time. On female inflorescences, bisexual flowers, and often with petals, were observed. Bibliography: Léonard (1994).
