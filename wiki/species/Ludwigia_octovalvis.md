# *Ludwigia octovalvis* (Jacq.) P.H.Raven

**Family**: [[Onagraceae]] · **Genus**: [[Ludwigia]]

Kew Bull. **15**(3): 476 (1962).

## Description

Annual or perennial herb, sometimes woody at base, to 3(–4) m, much branched and variably white-pubescent. Leaves subsessile; blade linear to narrowly elliptic-ovate, 0.5–10 × 0.2–0.6(–1.5) cm, cuneate, attenuate and pubescent to hirsute. Flowers solitary; pedicel 1–3 mm; sepals 4, 3–6 mm; petals 4, obovate, 3–8 × 2–4.5 mm, notched, golden yellow; stamens 8; ovary about 10 × 0.75 mm; style 1.5–3.5 mm. Fruit subcylindrical, 17–40 × 2–5 mm, grooved below and four-angled above, reddish and often puberulent, with several seed rows. Seed subglobose, 0.5–0.7 mm, longitudinally striate; raphe swollen and about as broad as the seed.

## Distribution

Pantropical and widespread across tropical Africa to Madagascar. Recorded from five Gabonese provinces.

## Habitat and ecology

Very wet grassland, river and lake margins, marshes, sandy soils and other wet places; 30–100 m in Gabon and to 2200 m elsewhere.

## Notes

Extremely variable, with over 70 published synonyms. The treatment follows specialists in recognizing one broad species; under an infraspecific scheme, Gabonese material would belong to subsp. *brevisepala*.
