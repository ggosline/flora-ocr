# *Garcinia epunctata* Stapf

**Genus**: [[Garcinia]]
**Family**: [[Clusiaceae]]
**Protologue**: J. Linn. Soc., Bot 37 : 86 (1905)

## Description

G. klainei Pierre ex Engl., Veg. Erde 9, Pflanzenw. Afr. 3(2) : 514 (1921). G. nyangensis Pellegr., Bull. Mus. Hist. Nat. (Paris) 27 : 194 (1921). G. kuluensis Spirlet, Bull. Jard. Bot. État Bruxelles 29 : 328 (1959). G. obscura Spirlet, Bull. Jard. Bot. État Bruxelles 29 : 328 (1959). Dioecious tree, up to 30(−35) m tall; branchlets rounded. Leaves opposite, glabrous; petiole 3−15 mm; blade ovate to obovate or sometimes narrowly ovate to narrowly obovate, 6−18 × 2.5−9 cm, cuneate to obtuse at base, long-acuminate at apex, papyraceous; midvein grooved above, prominent and rounded below, lateral nerves numerous (15−25 more pronounced), at an insertion angle of 45−80°, translucent secretory canals parallel to lateral nerves but indistinct, numerous black canals subparallel to the midvein, reticulation visible when dry. Flowers unisexual, tetramerous. Male flowers in axillary cymes or on branchlets, few-flowered or with up to 15 flowers, glabrous; pedicel 1−3 mm; sepals suborbicular, outer ones 1−2.5 mm long, inner ones 3−4 mm long; petals obovate, 6−10 × 3−5 mm, white to yellowish or yellow-greenish; stamen bundles 3−7 mm long, with 4−15 sessile and oblong anthers per bundle, not locellate; disk conical, 2−2.5 mm diameter. Female flowers solitary, axillary or terminal; pedicel 1−4 mm; stamens absent; ovary 4-locular, cylindric to 4-angular, 2.5 × 2.5 mm; stigma subsessile, 4-lobed. Fruit subglobose, 2−4 cm diameter, smooth, green to orange, glabrous. Seeds 2−4, subreniform, 13−20 × 7−11 mm.

## Distribution

Sierra Leone, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola; in Gabon, fairly common and known from all provinces

## Ecology

Primary and secondary forest, evergreen or semi-deciduous, swamp forest, riverine or gallery forest; in Gabon, up to 800 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1000 m

## Vernacular names

balala, botata, masikidi, koworo. Note taxonomique : voir sous G. preussii. Bi : Bamps (1970a), Hutchinson & Dalziel (1954), Pellegrin (1959). B liographie
