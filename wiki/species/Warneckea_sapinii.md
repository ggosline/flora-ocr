# *Warneckea sapinii* (De Wildeman) Jacques-Félix

**Genus**: [[Warneckea]]
**Family**: [[Melastomataceae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia, sér. 2, 18 (2) : 234 (1978); Fl. Cam. 24 : 166, tab. 53, 3-6 (1983)

## Description

Adansonia, sér. 2, 18 (2) : 234 (1978); Fl. Cam. 24 : 166, tab. 53, 3-6 (1983).

— Memecylon sapinii De Wild., Comp. Kasai : 378 (1910).

Shrub to small tree 8 to 10 m; branches obscurely 4-angular, then rounded. Leaves subcoriaceous, dark green above, paler below, smooth; petiole 5 mm; lamina elliptic-lanceolate, approximately 4 × 10 cm, cuneate at the base, acumen imprecise, acute, 8 to 10 mm long; veins prominent below; the main convergent veins slightly suprabasal, not coarctate, evanescent in the acumen, the submarginal veins fine, arcuate or obscure; 5 to 6 main transversal veins, often bifurcate in loose primary reticulum, secondary reticulum obscure.

Cymes copiously fascicled on the leafless nodes and on the probably polycarpic nodes of old wood, totalling about sixty flowers per node; 1 to 2.5 cm long and 3.5 cm broad; slender stipites 2 to 7 mm, little and briefly, or not, branched and each bearing 3 to 9 flowers with long pedicels; scarious bracts, tardily caducous. Flower with slender pedicel 3 to 10 mm; hypantho-calyx campanulate to cupulopatellate, approximately 3 × 2.5 mm, lobes short, broadly triangular. Corolla conical in bud; petals oblong, with triangular, acuminate apex, approximately 1.5 × 2.5 mm. Stamens with frontal thecae 0.8 mm; connective conical-acute, straight with punctiform gland situated towards the apex; filament 2.5–3 mm. Epigynous chamber shallow, without partitions; style slender 7 mm. Ovary 2-locular, 6-ovulate.

Fruit subglobose, 7 × 7.5 mm.

## Type

Sapin s.n., Zaïre, juil. 1907 (BR)

## Discussion

Forest species from Cameroon, Gabon, Angola, and extending to Zambia towards the East. Remarkable for its multiflorous cymes.

## Specimens examined

Le Testu 7704, Liyanga (nov.).
