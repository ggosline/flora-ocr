# *Fagara bouetensis* Pierre ex R. Let.

**Family**: [[Rutaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Fagara]]

## Description

Shrub or small tree 5–15 m tall. Young branches have pale raised lenticels and blackish, straight or slightly recurved prickles 3–5 mm long. Leaves cluster near branch tips, 30–60 cm long, with 4–6 leaflet pairs and a terminal leaflet; blades are papery, finely crenate, densely translucent-punctate and sparsely hairy beneath. Male panicles reach 18 cm. The branched infructescence bears glandular, glabrous, pink then partly red follicles about 8 mm in diameter; the seed coat is metallic dark blue.

## Distribution

Known only from Gabon in the treatment.
