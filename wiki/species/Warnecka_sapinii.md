# *Warnecka sapinii* (De Wildeman) Jacques-Félix

**Genus**: [[Warneckea]]
**Family**: [[Melastomataceae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia, sér. 2, 18 (2) : 234 (1978); Fl. Cam. 24 : 166, tab. 53, 3-6 (1983)

## Description

Adansonia, ser. 2, 18 (2): 234 (1978); Fl. Cam. 24: 166, tab. 53, 3-6 (1983).

— Memecylon sapinii De Wild., Comp. Kasai: 378 (1910).

Shrub to small tree of 8 to 10 m; branchlets obscurely 4-angular, then rounded. Leaves subcoriaceous, dark green above, paler below, smooth; petiole 5 mm; blade elliptic-lanceolate, approximately 4 × 10 cm, cuneate at the base, acumen poorly defined, acute, 8 to 10 mm long; veins prominent below; the main convergent veins slightly suprabasilar, not coarctate, evanescent in the acumen, the submargilanal veins fine, arcuate or obscure; 5 to 6 principal transverse veins, often bifurcate into a lax primary reticulum, secondary reticulum obscure.

Cymes copiously fasciculate on the defoliate nodes and on the probably polycarpic nodes of old wood, totalling about sixty flowers per node; 1 to 2.5 cm long and 3.5 cm wide; slender stipites 2 to 7 mm, slightly and briefly, or not, branched and each bearing 3 to 9 flowers with long pedicels; scarious bracts, tardily caducous. Flower with slender pedicel 3 to 10 mm; hypantho-calyx campanulate to cupuliform, approximately 3 × 2.5 mm, lobes short, broadly triangular. Corolla conical in bud; petals oblong, with triangular apex, acuminate, approximately 1.5 × 2.5 mm. Stamens with frontal anther cells 0.8 mm; connective conical-acute, straight with a punctiform gland situated toward the apex; filament 2.5-3 mm. Epigynous chamber shallow, without septa; style slender 7 mm. Ovary 2-locular, 6-ovulate.

Fruit subglobose, 7 × 7.5 mm.

## Type

Sapin s.n., Zaïre, juil. 1907 (BR)

## Discussion

Forest species of Cameroon, Gabon, Angola, and extending to Zambia toward the East. Remarkable for its multiflorous cymes.

## Specimens examined

Le Testu 7704, Liyanga (nov.).
