# *Clerodendrum leucobotrys* Breteler Planche 7

**Genus**: [[Clerodendrum]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia , sér. 3, 27(2) : 319 (2005)

## Description

Adansonia, ser. 3, 27(2): 319 (2005). Lianescent shrub ± 1 m tall; branchlets sparsely pilose, probably becoming hollow, armed with pseudospines 1–2 mm. Leaves opposite; petiole subcylindric, grooved above, 0.4–1.1 cm, glabrous or very sparsely pilose; blade narrowly elliptic, ± 3 times longer than wide, (5–)7–11 × (1.5–)2.5–3.5 cm, cuneate to slightly rounded at base, acute-acuminate for 0.5–1.5 cm at apex, papyraceous, glabrous, finely punctate dark green to black beneath, midvein and 6 to 8 pairs of secondary veins flat to impressed above, prominent below. Inflorescence (sub)terminal, thyrsoid, pendulous, on a peduncle 1.5–2.5 cm, with dense white villosity of ± appressed hairs (when dry) 2–5 mm; cymes bearing 8 to 10 (to 16) flowers, lax, dichasial, branched, on a peduncle 1–1.5 cm; bracts and bracteoles very narrowly elliptic-obovate, up to 6 mm long. Flower: pedicel 2–4 cm, villous; calyx ± campanulate, 5-lobed, sparsely villous, with tube 1.5–2 mm long, with lobes erect, triangular, ≤ 1 mm long; corolla with tube 9–11 × 1 mm, distinctly enlarged in lower part over 1–1.5 mm, slightly in upper part, glabrous to base externally, glandular-pubescent in middle, villous toward apex, glabrous within, lobes dark yellow; stamens reaching 1 cm long, elongate, long-exserted, glabrous. Fruit unknown.

## Distribution

Endemic to northwestern Gabon (Woleu-Ntem). Conservation status: known only from the type locality – a logging site – and continuous threat can therefore be inferred; preliminarily assigned to Critically Endangered (CR).

## Ecology

Forest road margins; at ± 200 m altitude. B: Breteler (2005). Bibliography
