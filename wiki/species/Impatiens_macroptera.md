# *Impatiens macroptera* Hook. f.

**Family**: [[Balsaminaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Impatiens]]

## Description

Herb with alternate leaves and long-pedunculate pink or mauve inflorescences. Lip broad and hood-shaped with a moderate curved, sometimes swollen spur; wings conspicuously large; peduncle over 8 cm.

## Distribution

Lower Guinea, including Gabon.
