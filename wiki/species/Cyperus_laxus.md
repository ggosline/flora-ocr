# *Cyperus laxus* Lam. subsp. buchholzii (Boeckeler) Lye Planche 67

**Genus**: [[Cyperus]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Nordic J. Bot. 3 : 232 (1982)

## Description

C. diffusus Vahl subsp buchholzii (Boeck) Kük. in Engl. & Diels, Pflanzenr. IV. 20(101) : 210 (1936). C. buchholzii Boeckeler, Beitr. Cyper . 1 : 3 (1888). Perennial herb, medium-sized, in tufts, with a short woody rhizome; stems 25–50 cm × 1.5–3 mm, trigonous, glabrous, lower part covered by numerous leaves. Leaf: sheath purple to reddish-brown; blade 5–40 cm × 3–8 mm, flat, scabrous on the margin and central vein. Inflorescence in an anthela rather open and often somewhat triangular, 3–7 cm × 5–10 cm; main branches 2–5 cm long, bearing an umbelliform fascicle of digitate spikelets; prophyll tubular at the base of each branch violaceous, but often hidden by the involucral bracts; fascicles composed usually of 3–5 sessile spikelets; involucral bracts 5–8, foliaceous, erect or spreading, the principal ones 20–45 cm long, far exceeding the inflorescence. Spikelet linear, 4–6 × 1.5–2.5 mm, light brown, with 5–10 fertile flowers; glumes ovate, 1.2–1.6 mm long (including the mucro), strongly nerved, light reddish-brown with a broad green central nerve prolonged by a slightly curved mucro. Flower with 3 stamens. Achene oval to obovate, 1.2–1.3 × 0.7–0.8 mm, reddish-brown, almost smooth to finely and irregularly alveolate or wrinkled.

## Distribution

in the forest regions of tropical Africa; locally common in Gabon in the Estuaire, Ogooué-Ivindo and Woleu-Ntem, less in Ngounié, Ogooué-Maritime and Ogooué-Lolo (23 collections)
