# *Okoubaka aubrevillei* Pellegrin & Normand

**Family**: [[Octoknemaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Okoubaka]]

Bull. Soc. Bot. Fr. **91**: 25 (1944); l. c. **93**: 139 (1946). — NORMAND, Agro. Trop. **1**: 371, tab. 4 (1946). — KEAY, FWTA, ed. 2, **1**: 656 (1958). — AUBRÉVILLE, FFCI ed. 2, **1**: 114, tab. 20, 1–7 (1959).

**Synonym**: *Octoknema okoubaka* Aubr. & Pellegr., Bull. Soc. Bot. Fr. **84**: 390, tab. 1 (1937).

## Description

**Habit**: tree reaching 25 m, with straight bole 15 m high and 80 cm diameter without fluting at the base. Branches ± horizontal. Crown dense, hemispherical. 

**Bark**: rhytidome yellowish-brown, scaly, 3–8 mm thick. Inner bark showing, after a clear cork zone, yellowish-orange and orange spots. Sapwood yellowish becoming white towards the interior.

**Branchlets**: pubescent, russet, flattened with 4 ribs, leaves ± decussate.

**Leaves**: petiole 1–1.2 cm long, pubescent, enlarged and canaliculate above. Blade pubescent, sparsely pubescent or glabrous on both faces, black above and dark green below, ovate-elliptic or lanceolate, 2.5–8.5 × 1.5–6 cm; base rounded to cuneiform, sometimes cordate; apex obtuse. Midrib concave above, prominent and pubescent to glabrous below. Secondary nerves 5 pairs, very ascending, arcuate, anastomosing 2 mm from the margin, prominent on both faces. Nervules in very lax reticulum, scarcely visible on both faces.

**Inflorescence**: multiflowered panicles on older branches and branchlets. Rachis pubescent, ochre, 8 cm long, reaching 20–40 cm in length. Floral buds very broadly ovoid.

**Male flowers**: pentamerous. Pedicel short, pubescent. Petals shortly and densely pubescent externally, pubescent internally with or without a tuft of simple white hairs, triangular or broadly lanceolate, ca. 2.5 × 2.5 mm; apex obtuse. Disc with very strongly undulate border, pubescent internally, cupular. Stamens 5, epipetalous, free from each other, fertile, situated in depressions of the disc border, 0.75 mm long; filament short, very strongly enlarged at the base, rapidly narrowing towards the apex, 0.25 mm long; anthers introrse, basifixed, with 2 lobes ± free from each other at the apex. Ovary inferior, with 1 locule containing a placental column bearing aborted ovules. Style short, 1 mm long, pubescent at the base, terminating in 4 points.

**Female flowers**: larger than male flowers, pentamerous. Petals pubescent on both faces, internal face with long white hairs, triangular or broadly lanceolate, ca. 2.5 × 2.5 mm; apex obtuse. Stamens 5, sterile. Disc cupular, pubescent, with very undulate upper border. Ovary inferior, unilocular with central erect placenta bearing 4 pendulous ovules. Style short, 1 mm long, pubescent at the base, terminating in a plateau-like stigma with 4 lobes.

**Fruit**: drupe green becoming shiny yellow, glabrous, 15–16 × 9–10 cm, weakly longitudinally ribbed. Mesocarp golden yellow, 1.5–2 cm thick. Endocarp woody, 3–5 mm thick, very strongly ribbed externally, smooth internally. Seed 7 × 4.5 cm when fresh, adhering to one side of the endocarp. Albumen white.

## Type

**LECTOTYPE**: *Aubréville 603*, Yapo, Ivory Coast, female flowers (P).

## Distribution

**Range**: Ghana, Ivory Coast, Cameroon, and Zaïre (Democratic Republic of the Congo). The species is quite rare, found only as isolated trees. In Zaïre, it is known by a variety *glabrescentifolia* J.Léonard. According to some authors, it appears to cause the disappearance of other tree species in its vicinity.

**Not recorded from Gabon** — the treatment gives no Gabonese material, and the
range as stated stops at Cameroon.
