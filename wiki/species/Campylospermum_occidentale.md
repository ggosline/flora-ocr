# *Campylospermum occidentale* Biss.

**Genus**: [[Campylospermum]]
**Family**: [[Ochnaceae]]
**Protologue**: Blumea 58 : 6 (2013)

## Description

Shrub reaching 4 m in height; branches with whitish bark. Stipules caducous, triangular, 2–3 mm long. Leaf: petiole 2–5 mm; lamina narrowly elliptic to narrowly obovate, 6–16 × 1.5–4.5 cm, ratio 3–4, cuneate at base, slightly acuminate at apex, papyraceous, margin entire to serrulate; main vein slightly prominent above, prominent below, lateral veins in 7 to 11 pairs, prominent on both surfaces, tertiary veins scalariform, perpendicular to main vein, very distinct on both surfaces. Inflorescence terminal, branched, lax, main axis slender, 4–8(–12) cm; racemes 1 to 4(–6), 1–7(–11) cm, spreading, often branched two or three times, giving a corymbiform appearance to the inflorescence; bud scales absent; bracts rapidly caducous; cymules distant by (5–)10–15 mm, 1- or 2(or 3)-flowered. Flowers: pedicel 4–10(–15) mm, articulate at 1–6 mm from base; sepals ovate, 5–7 × 2–3 mm in flower, 6–7 × 3–3.5 mm in fruit, orange; petals obovate, 5–11 × 4–7 mm, cuneate at base, rounded to emarginate at apex; stamens: anthers 3–4 mm long; style approximately 4 mm. Fruit: receptacle approximately 3 mm thick; drupels 1 to 3 well developed per receptacle, ellipsoid, 8 × 5–6 mm, red-orange at maturity; cotyledons incumbent, equal.

## Distribution

endemic to Gabon, found only in the Atlantic region (provinces of Ogooué-Maritime and Estuaire)

## Ecology

primary and secondary forest, humid forest, near flooded areas; on sandy soil; at 5–30 m altitude. Taxonomic note: C. occidentalis is close to C. nutans (Hiern) Biss., but distinguished by the branched racemes which give the inflorescence a corymbiform appearance. The sole observation of material in fruit (apparently at mature stage) shows that the drupels, as well as the sepals, are orange, contrary to black which is the normal color. Further observations are necessary to confirm this trait. b: Bissiengou (2014), Bissiengou et al. (2013). Bibliography
