# *Maesopsis eminii* subsp. *berchemioides* (Pierre) N. Hallé

**Family**: [[Rhamnaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Maesopsis]]

## Description

Fast-growing tree 15–25 m in Gabon. Leaves elliptic or oblong, 7–14 × 2.5–6 cm, base obtuse to subcordate, margins with spaced rounded gland-tipped teeth; 7–10 secondary-vein pairs with basal acarodomatia. Axillary dichasial cymes 1–5 cm. Flowers green, 3.5–5 mm; sepals 5, petals 5 and hooded over subsessile anthers; ovary one-locular and one-ovuled; stigma peltate, 10-lobed. Drupe 2.2–3 cm, yellow then red to black-violet, with a hard two-ridged endocarp and usually one oily seed.

## Distribution and uses

Western subspecies from Liberia through Gabon; the typical form begins around Ubangi and Congo and extends eastward. Colonises secondary bush and savanna edges. Wood is used for cheap interior work; bitter bark is used as a purgative, diuretic and remedy for colic.
