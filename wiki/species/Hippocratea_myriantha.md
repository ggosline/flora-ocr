## Description

Bull. IFAN, sér. A, 21 (3) : tab. 74 (1959) ; Pollen & Spores 2 (1) : 8 (1960) ; Mêm. IFAN 64 : 127, fig. 4c, 6b et c, tab. 46 et 47 (1962) ; WILCZEK, FCB 9 : 170, tab. 21 (1962).

– Hippocratea pachnocarpa LOES. ex FRITSCH, Bot. Centralblatt, Band 11, Beih. 5: 342, fig. 5 (1901). TYPE: Zenker 1072, Cameroon (K!, P!).

Tall liane exceeding 15-45 m in length, with young lateral branchlets cylindrical glabrous, ± clothed with a white-bluish pruina. Mature branchlets ± lenticellate. Cork covering a fine orange layer. Old wood with 4 grooves reaching 20 cm in diameter, with grayish bark somewhat rugose. Stipules on a fine transverse ridge, sometimes ± confluent or even reduced to a single pair per node. Petiole 5-13(-20) mm. Blade chartaceous to subcoriaceous, discolorous often brown when dry, ovate or obovate, sometimes obdeltoid, 4-15 × 2.5-8(-10) cm ; base cuneate or obtuse ; apex acuminate or truncate ; margin entire (except in juvenile forms). Secondary veins 4-7(8) pairs.

Inflorescences in large cymes abundantly provided with supplementary branches, sometimes arranged in panicles, with cyme peduncles 3-8 cm long ; axes ± puberulent whitish-yellow, with 7-8 levels of cylindrical ramifications ; ultimate ramifications dichotomous ; small bracts acute. Pedicels puberulent 1-2.5 mm. Buds globose 0.8-1.2 mm in diameter, with valvate prefloration. Flowers very numerous 1.5-4 mm in diameter, white or pale yellow. Calyx with 5 equal deltoid lobes, about 0.4 mm, and puberulent. Petals ± suberect deltoid, 1-1.5 mm long, puberulent on the outside, hairy on the inside except at the base. Disc white or green, narrowly annular rather than cupuliform. Stamens inserted in a perigynous groove, with short filaments, with puberulent anthers ; pollen pale yellow. Ovary glabrous, 2/3 sunken and trilobed ; 4-8 ovules in each of the 3 locules. Style very short ; stigma with very small lobes opposite the stamens.

Juvenile fruits with bluish pruina ; mericarps suberect reaching 5-9.5 × 1.7-3 cm, elongate-oblong or attenuate, emarginate or not at the apex. Seed containing rubber, (3)4-6.8 × 1.2-1.8 cm, with dark brownish-black testa and pale reddish-salmon wing. Cotyledons adherent, difficultly separable, often long and narrow. Funicular sheaths 2.2-5 mm long. - Pl. 73, p. 259.

According to WALKER & SILLANS (Les Plantes Utiles du Gabon, Paris : 202, 1961) the flowers would be very fragrant with a violet perfume.

## Discussion

Guinea, Liberia, Sierra Leone, Côte-d'Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Central African Republic, Congo, Zaire, Angola. Humid forests sometimes flooded, sometimes degraded or refuge stations : gallery forests rocks etc. Young stems sometimes harvested as ties.
