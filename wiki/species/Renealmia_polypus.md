# *Renealmia polypus* Gagnep.

**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Renealmia]]

## Description

Robust herb to 2 m. Petiole 13–25 cm; glabrous caudate-acuminate blade 50–60 × 10–15 cm. Usually two subopposite basal panicles, each 15–20 cm on a c. 20 cm sheathed peduncle; persistent bracts subtend 2–6 flowers. Corolla white or pink; ellipsoid capsule black at maturity, 10–12 × 5–7 mm, with about fifteen long yellow-laciniate-arillate seeds.

## Distribution

Forest understorey in Cameroon and Gabon.
