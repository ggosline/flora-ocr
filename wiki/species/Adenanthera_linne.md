# *Adenanthera linne*

**Genus**: [[Adenanthera]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Sp. Pl.: 384 (1753)

## Description

- Gonsii ADANS., Fam. 2: 318 (1763).

- Pongelion ADANS., Fam. 2: 319 (1763).

– Stachychrysum BoJ., Hort. Maurit.: 114 (1837).

Tree with bipinnate multijugate leaves. Inflorescences elongate, in spikes or panicles, terminal. Flowers ♢ or polygamous, pentamerous, white or yellow. Calyx campanulate. Petals free or connate to each other. Stamens 10, free, anthers with connective prolonged by a caducous gland. Ovary sessile. Legume linear, curved or falciform, flattened or turgid around the seeds, with 2 valves, often coiled at maturity, divided between the seeds by a septum. Seeds thin with hard seed coat, red or bicolorous, enclosed in a thin pulp.

TYPE SPECIES: A. pavonina L.

This genus contains 11 species from the tropical and subtropical regions of Asia to northern Australia and Polynesia. In Africa one introduced species is known.

Adenanthera pavonina Linné

FZ 3 (1): 11 (1970); LIBERATO, Fl. Guiné Port., Mimosaceae: 14 (1972); FL. S. Tomé e Principe, Mimosaceae: 10 (1975); GUNN FSGM: tab. p. 51, fig. F, J-K (1984).

1. A specimen bearing the same number is determined as Calpocalyx heitzii.

Tree 6–7 m tall. Crown globose fairly voluminous. Branchlets glabrous, dark brownish, with brownish lenticels. Petiole glabrous, 2.5–11 cm long, weakly canaliculate above. Rachis glabrous, (2.5–) 9.5–30 cm long, canaliculate above. Pinnae 3–5 pairs. Axis of the pinna sparsely glabrescent to glabrous, 5.5–15.5 cm long, upper surface carinate to pubescent canaliculate. Leaflets 9–15, alternate. Petiolule weakly pubescent above, 1–1.5 mm long, twisted. Blade glabrous appressed above and pubescent below; dark greenish mat above and dull green below on the dried specimen; elliptic oblong, 1.4–3.7 × 0.9–1.9 cm; base asymmetric: proximal margin attenuate and distal margin subcuneiform; apex rounded. Midrib somewhat pubescent and plane above; prominent below. Secondary veins ascending, rectilinear, weakly prominent on both faces. Veinlets scarcely or not visible.

Inflorescence in a terminal panicle of multiflorous racemes. Axis of the raceme ± pubescent. Bract ± pubescent, very narrowly triangular-linear, ± 1.25 mm long, apex acute. Pedicel glabrous, 3.25–4 mm long. Flower ♂, yellow in the living state. Calyx campanulate, glabrous or very sparsely pubescent externally with hairs reflected towards the base, 0.75–1 mm long, lobes free 5, very short, apex ± acute. Petals free valvate, glabrous, narrowly lanceolate, 4–4.5 × 1 mm, reflexed in the open flower, base long attenuate, keel sinuate on the upper 1/2 of the inner face, apex very acute. Stamens 10, some strongly appressed to the inner face of the petals, free; filament ± 4 mm long; anther oblong, 0.75 mm long, with 2 locules, connective terminating in a stipitate gland. Pistil with stipe ± 0.10–0.30 mm long; ovary glabrous, narrowly ellipsoid, ± 2.75 mm long, with 10–12 ovules; style ± 2 mm long, terminating in a tapering point.

Legume glabrous, black externally on the dried specimen, reaching 23 cm, with sinuate margin, compressed between the seeds, base attenuate stipitate, arched then becoming contorted at maturity. Seed white then red, lustrous, orbicular, ± 1 × ± 1 × ± 5 mm, areole scarcely visible. - Pl. 15, p. 63.

## Discussion

This tree is planted in all tropical regions as an ornamental and shade plant.
