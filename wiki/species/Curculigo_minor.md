# *Curculigo minor* E. Guinea

**Family**: [[Hypoxidaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Curculigo]]

Ens. Geobot. Guinea Cont., Espan.: 257 (1946); Anal. Jard. Bot. Madrid 6: 471 (1946); HEPPER, FWTA, ed. 2, 3: 173 (1968).

## Description

Similar to *C. pilosa* in all characters, except for narrower leaves reaching a width of 8 mm and smaller flowers with tepals approximately 7–8 mm long.

## Distribution

**Range**: Equatorial Guinea and Gabon.
**Equatorial Guinea**: Rio Muni (type locality).
**Gabon**: collected at Ngoré, Djoua, and Tchibanga.

## Specimens examined

**Gabon**

- Dybowski 84, Ngoré, P.
- Duparquet s.n., s. loc., P.
- Eckendorff 149, Djoua (fl. sept.), P.
- Le Testu 1072, Tchibanga (fl. juill.), P.

## Notes

The type specimen (Guinea E. s.n., Rio Muni, 1.VII.1945) was not examined by the author. The source notes some uncertainty about whether *C. minor* is truly distinct from *C. pilosa* or merely a small form thereof.
