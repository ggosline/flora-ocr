# *Eleocharis variegata* (Poir.) C.Presl Planche 87

**Genus**: [[Eleocharis]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Isis (Oken) 21 : 269 (1828)

## Synonyms

- Scirpus variegatus Poir. in Lam., Encycl. 6 : 749 (1804).

## Description

Perennial herb, stoloniferous, with stems in small tufts, but sometimes with a thick erect rhizome in older plants; stolons approximately 30 cm × 1.5‒2.5 mm, straw-yellow, internodes with grey-black scales, 5‒15 mm long; stems of highly variable appearance, 25‒90 cm × 1‒5 mm, rounded, quadrangular, with rounded angles or more distinctly triangular but not winged, with ± distinct longitudinal striae. Leaf: often 3 sheaths above the prophyll, terminating in greyish triangular lobes. Inflorescence in a single spikelet; bracts green below and greyish above, much shorter than the glumes. Spikelet cylindric, 10‒55 × 2.5‒5 mm; glumes 3‒5 mm long, apex rounded but often very frayed, violaceous with often a broad central greenish zone and a translucent margin in the upper part, central nerve scarcely distinct. Flower: perianth usually with 7‒8 reddish bristles, much longer than the achene, densely furnished with curved spiny teeth, sometimes reduced or absent; stamens 3; style with 2‒3 branches, variable on the same plant. Achene biconvex and almost urceolate, 1.5‒1.9 × 1.3‒1.5 mm, whitish to yellowish becoming brownish at maturity, surface finely ribbed and reticulate between the ribs (more visible on achenes before maturity); appendage darker, conical.

## Distribution

widely distributed in the tropical regions of Africa, America and Asia; rare in Gabon, found only in Haut-Ogooué and Ogooué-Ivindo (2 collections)

## Ecology

in humid zones of swamps such as pools and muddy holes; in Gabon, around 100‒300 m elevation, up to 1200 m in East Africa. Bibliography: Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al. (2010), Hooper & Napper (1972).
