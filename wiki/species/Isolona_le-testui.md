# *Isolona le-* Testui Pellegrin

**Genus**: [[Isolona]]
**Family**: [[Annonaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull.Mus.Nat.Hist.Nat.Paris 26 :657(192o)；Bull. Soc.Bot.Fr.94 :387 (1947)

## Description

Bull.Mus.Nat.Hist.Nat.Paris 26:657(1920); Bull. Soc.Bot.Fr.94:387 (1947).

Tree 3–4 m tall with furrowed trunk and numerous basal branches; twigs and branchlets glabrous, dark brown. Leaves with petiole 2–3 mm long; blade papyraceous, elliptic to oblongo-obovate, 6–15 cm long, 3–5.7 cm wide, cuneate to subrounded at base, ± longly acuminate at apex, glabrous on both surfaces. Midvein prominent on both surfaces; lateral veins 10–11 pairs, oblique-ascending, joined in arches 2–5 mm from the margin; reticulum lax, clearly visible.

Flowers solitary, axillary, with slender pedicel, 1.3–1.4 cm long, furnished at base with 4 very small ovate bracteoles. Sepals, connate at base, broadly ovate, 2–2.5 mm in diameter, mucronate at apex, ciliate on margins. Corolla green, red at base of petals; tube 6–10 mm long, longitudinally striate within; lobes thin, linear acute, 4–9 cm long, c. 2 mm wide. Stamens subsessile, 1–1.5 mm long, thecae oblong lateral extrorse, connectives dilated above the anthers and extended towards the centre of the flower in the innermost ones. Ovary ovoid, longitudinally ribbed; stigmas sessile, capitate-lobulate.

Fruit unknown.

Type and only Gabonese material studied: Le Testu 1252, Tchibanga, fl. Nov. (holotype P!; isotype BM!).

Species of dense humid forest, endemic to Gabon, particularly remarkable for its long linear petals.
