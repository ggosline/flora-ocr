# *Antrocaryon nannanii* De Wild. Planche 2

**Genus**: [[Antrocaryon]]
**Family**: [[Anacardiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Rev. Zool. Afr. 14, Suppl. Bot. 4 : 7, t. 2 (1926)

## Description

Deciduous tree, up to 40 m tall; trunk straight, up to 1 m in diameter; milky exudate, becoming brownish-yellow. Leaves in tufts at the tip of branches, tomentellous to pubescent when young; petiole and rachis together 20–40 cm; leaflets 11 to 19, with very small petiolules. Female flower: pedicel, calyx, petals and disc as in the male flower; ovary 1–2 mm in diameter, glabrous; styles 0.3–0.4 mm. Fruit subglobose, laterally compressed, 4.5–5.5 × 3.5–4.5 × 4.5–5.5 cm, yellowish-green, with fibrous pulp, 0.5–1 mm thick; stone broadly obovoid to subspheric, 3.5–4.5 × 3–3.5 × 3.5–4 cm, with thick osseous wall, with 5 opercula. Seeds 1 to 3 per stone, unequal, curved, compressed, 1.8–2.5 × 0.6–0.7 cm, with shiny testa, light brown. Seedling with hypocotyl 8–11 cm; cotyledons linear, 4–6 cm long; epicotyl 7–14 cm; the first leaves opposite, imparipinnate, with 2 or 3 pairs of leaflets.

## Distribution

Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, rare and only occasionally recorded in the East, notably in Ogooué-Ivindo and Ogooué-Lolo

## Ecology

Primary and secondarized forest; in Gabon, up to 300 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1200 m. Usage: The oleaginous seeds are edible and the wood is used for veneer peeling and joinery in the DRC

## Vernacular names

Onzabili (nom pilote)
