# *Crotalaria dewildemaniana* Wilczek subsp. dewildemaniana

**Genus**: [[Crotalaria]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Jard. Bot. État Bruxelles 23 : 190 (1953)

## Description

Herb 1–3 m tall; stem erect, branched, furrowed when young, subcylindrical, with scattered appressed hairs. Stipules absent. Leaf: petiole canaliculate, 1–5 cm, puberulent; petiolules 2–3 mm, tomentose; leaflets 3, with elliptic to lanceolate blade, 3–7(–10) × 1–2(–3) cm, base cuneate, apex mucronate, glabrous above, pale green and appressed-puberulent below, hairs golden. Inflorescence in terminal racemes 10–60 cm long, dense, multiflorous; bracts and bracteoles persistent. Flower: calyx becoming truncate at the base and reflexed against the pedicel in fruit, ± 8 mm long, with acuminate lobes longer than the tube; corolla bright yellow, streaked with red, standard broadly obovate, 10–14 × 10–12 mm, keel falciform-curved, with ciliation on claw 5 mm, ventral and dorsal margins tomentose, beak with apex slightly twisted; stamens with filaments fused for ± 8 mm, free for 3–4 mm; ovary oblong, ± 6 mm long, tomentose especially on the suture, with > 100 ovules; style curved, 10–12 mm. Pod on a stipe of ± 3 mm, oblong-cylindric, 2.8–4 × 0.5–0.6 mm, black at maturity, appressed-tomentulose to puberulent, with 60–75 seeds. Seed oblique-cordiform, flattened, ± 2.5 mm in diameter, orange.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon, East Democratic Republic of the Congo, Rwanda, Burundi, Uganda and Tanzania; in Gabon, known from the Estuaire

## Ecology

cultivated areas, fallows, herbaceous and saxicolous savannas; in Gabon at 30 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1300 m. Flowers and fruits in September
