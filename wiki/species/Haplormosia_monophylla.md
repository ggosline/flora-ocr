# *Haplormosia monophylla* (Harms) Harms Planche 67

**Genus**: [[Haplormosia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: H. ledermannii Harms, Repert. Spec. Nov. Regni Veg. 15 : 23 (1918)

## Description

in Engl., Veg. Erde 9, Pflanzenw. Afr. 3(1): 533 (1915). Ormosia monophylla (Harms) Harms ex Stapf, in Engl. & Prantl, Nat. Pflanzenfam., Nachtr. 3: 158 (Nov. 1906) & in Johnston, Liberia 2, Appendix: 598 (1906). H. ledermannii Harms, Repert. Spec. Nov. Regni Veg. 15: 23 (1918). Tree reaching 30 m; trunk irregular, up to 80 cm in diameter; bark scaly; wood fibrous and yellowish, with a banana-like odour; branches drooping, purplish, glabrous, slightly ridged. Stipules circular to triangular. Leaf bright red when young; petiole 2–4 cm; stipels pubescent or caducous; leaflet with petiolule 3–4 mm, wrinkled, blackish, blade elliptic to oblong, 6–20 × 3–9 cm, base rounded, apex with short acumen, coriaceous, shiny above; lateral veins 6 to 8 on each side. Inflorescence: peduncle 7–12 cm; bracts triangular, slightly pubescent. Flower rather large; pedicel glabrous or shortly hairy; bracteoles pubescent at the apex; calyx reddish or green streaked with purple or black, tube ± 5 mm long, lobes oblong-ovate, ± 5 mm long, margins with short hairs; corolla purple, pale purple to pink, sometimes white, petals with green spots at the base, standard 13–15 × 18–20 mm, glabrous, wings oblique; stamens ± 16 mm long, white or violaceous. Pod 5–8 × 4–5 cm, brown to dark brown. Seed 4–5 × 2–3 × 1 cm; hilum in a short fissure perpendicular to the margin. Distribution: Sierra Leone, Liberia, Ivory Coast, Nigeria, Cameroon and Gabon; in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Nyanga and Ogooué-Maritime.

## Ecology

Evergreen humid forest, near rivers and in swamps, lagoon margins, on sand; in Gabon and elsewhere at 0–100 m altitude. Flowers and fruits from March to May and from November to December. The seeds are sometimes floating. Uses: The wood is used for making house posts, charcoal for forging and in construction (beams); it is resistant to termites.
