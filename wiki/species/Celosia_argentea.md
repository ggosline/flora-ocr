# *Celosia argentea* L.

**Family**: [[Amaranthaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Celosia]]

## Description

Glabrous erect annual 0.4–2 m. Leaves usually lanceolate, 6–12 × 1–1.5 cm. Dense terminal spikes to 15 × 2 cm are white, pink or pinkish white. Perianth c. 7 mm, sometimes 10 mm; five stamens with purple anthers. Capsule ovoid with usually 4–8 glossy black reniform seeds.

## Ecology and uses

Very common in savanna; cultivated as a leafy vegetable.
