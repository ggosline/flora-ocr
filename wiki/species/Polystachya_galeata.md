# *Polystachya galeata* (Sw.) Rchb.f.

**Genus**: [[Polystachya]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Walp. Ann. 6 : 637 (1864) ; FTWA : 225 (1968) ; PODZORSKI & CRIBB, Kew Bull. 34 : 164 (1979) ; FAC 2 : 404 (1992) ; FCAM 2 : 521 (2001)

## Description

– TYPE : Afzelius s.n., Sierra Leone, holo-S, iso-BM!, W-R!.

– Bulbophyllum galeatum (Sw.) Lindl., Gen. Sp. Orch. Pl. : 57 (1830).

– Polystachya grandiflora Lindl. ex Hook., Bot. Mag.: t. 3707 (1839).

Pseudobulbs 6-14 x 0.2-0.4 cm, cylindrical. Leaf 8-27 x 1-3.6 cm, ligulate to narrowly oblong. Inflorescence 4-6 cm, < 6-flowered. Flowers rather large, green, yellowish-green, yellow, pink, or pure white, more or less spotted with violet or brown or violet within. Lateral sepals obliquely triangular-ovate, apiculate. Lip, the basal three-quarters oblong-obtriangular, rather thin, canaliculate, sparsely pubescent, trilobed in the upper quarter; central callus rather obscurely trilobed; midlobe square or rounded or briefly apiculate, finely papillose and sparsely pubescent. – Pl. 114, p. 259.

## Distribution

Sierra Leone, Liberia, Guinea, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Zaire, Angola

## Ecology

alt. 400-1000 m. Epiphyte in dense humid lowland or submontane forest
