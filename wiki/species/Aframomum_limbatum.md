# *Aframomum limbatum* (Oliv. et Hanb.) K. Schum.
**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Aframomum]]
## Description
Herb to 5 m. Blades c. 40 × 10 cm, linear-lanceolate, midrib and margins pubescent beneath; ligule 3 mm. Short 2–3-flowered spikes arise at ground level. Flowers pink-purple; labellum 4.5–5 cm across. Fruit smooth.
## Source
Koechlin, J. (1964). Zingibéracées. *Flore du Gabon* 9: 39–40.
