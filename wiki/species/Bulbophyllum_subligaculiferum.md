# *Bulbophyllum subligaculiferum* J.J. Verm.

**Genus**: [[Bulbophyllum]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Orchid Monogr. 2 : 30 (1987)

## Description

– TYPE : de Wilde, Arends, Louis, Bouman & Karper 183, Gabon, holo-WAG!

Pseudobulbs 3.2 × 3 cm, orbicular to ovoid, 1-leaved. Leaf petiolate; 9.5 × 2.5 cm, lanceolate, obtuse. Inflorescence dense, 95 cm, 45-flowered, flowers all open, bracts, sepals and petals yellowish, labellum with purple hairs. Sepals ovate-lanceolate, somewhat recurved, acute, glabrous; lateral sepals somewhat oblique. Petals linear-falciform, acute, glabrous. Labellum linear-lanceolate, subacute, lower half contracted, with a deltoid tooth, dorsoventrally flattened very near the base, margins with fairly thick hairs of variable length, short on the adaxial face, long on the abaxial face, those of the margins of the apical half much longer <6 mm. - Pl. 71, p. 147.

## Distribution

Species currently known only from Gabon
