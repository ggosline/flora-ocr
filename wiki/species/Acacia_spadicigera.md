# *Acacia spadicigera* Schlechtendal & Chamisso

**Genus**: [[Acacia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Linnaea 5: 594 (1830)

## Description

Shrub or small tree. Mature branches grey, finely furrowed, with numerous paler lenticels. Stipules fused at the base, elongately conical, woody, hollow, spinescent, straight to strongly recurved, 3–9 cm long, apex very acute. Petiole puberulent, 0.7–1.5 cm long; bearing a broadly conical gland flattened or narrowly ellipsoid, with ± cupular apex, enlarged at the level of the gland, channeled above; base fused to the stipules. Rachis very shortly puberulent, 3.4–13.5 cm long; glands present (similar to those of the petiole) or absent on the upper deeply channeled face; apex terminated below by a linear triangular mucro 2.25 mm long, with acute apex, caducous. Pinnae 4–10 pairs. Axis of the pinna finely puberulent at least on the keel, 2.5–5.2 cm long; base channeled, then prominent dorsal keel; apex bearing below a narrowly triangular mucro, 0.75 mm long, recurved, acute apex, caducous. Leaflets 9–22 pairs, sessile. Blade glabrous on both faces; brown above and green below when dry, oblong, 1–2 × 6–11 mm, base very asymmetric: proximal margin broadly rounded to weakly

subcordate and distal margin shortly rounded attenuate; apex rounded to subtruncate, weakly retuse. Midvein ± median, prominent on both faces. Secondary veins ascending slightly curved to curved; weakly prominent above and prominent below. Veinlets in an indistinct network, weakly prominent below.

Inflorescences in dense axillary spikes, solitary or geminate. Peduncle sometimes with small spines at the base, finely shortly and thickly pubescent, numerous fine keels; bearing towards the base broadly ovate bracts, finely pubescent and ciliolate on the margin. Bracteoles numerous, peltate, 1.5 mm long; base sparsely pubescent; elliptic plateau, ± pubescent above. Flowers ♂, sessile, yellow in life. Calyx oblongoid flattened, finely pubescent puberulent in the upper 1/2 and on the ribs, 1.5 mm long; tube ribbed; lobes scarcely discernible. Corolla tubular flattened, 1.75 mm long; tube glabrous; lobes finely puberulent; imbricate after anthesis; lanceolate, 0.25 mm long. Stamens numerous, 2.5 mm long; fused into a tube 1.5 mm long; anther broadly oblong, ± 0.1 mm long. Pistil without a stipe; ovary glabrous, ellipsoid, 0.75 mm long, with 15 ovules; style 1.75 mm long.

Pod very sparsely pubescent to puberulent; yellow, yellowish greenish reddish when dry; ellipsoid to weakly obovoid fusiform, ± woody, 7–9 × ± 1.5 cm; base stipitate and apex elongately apiculate acute. Seeds 12, black ± shiny, enclosed in yellow pulp in life and brownish russet when dry, oblongoid, 9 × 5 mm; areole as long as the seed but narrower.

## Type

Deppe & Schiede s.n., Mexique : près de la Lagune verte

## Discussion

In America, the hollow stipular spines serve as a refuge for ants.

This species is native to the arid regions of Mexico. It has been introduced to other tropical regions as a fence plant. In Gabon, it is known only from a specimen from a garden.

Apertures généralement de type poré, situées soit distalement et aux angles, soit proximalement, mais jamais (ou presque) équatorialement.

Cette tribu regroupe environ 17 genres pour 950 à 1 000 espèces, essentiellement tropicales. Au Gabon, 2 genres, peut-être 4 sont reconnus.

Certains genres comme Albizia, Cathormion, Samaneda, etc. sont assez difficiles à reconnaître en l'absence des parties florifères ou fructifères. Par contre l'appareil végétatif seul, s'il reste difficilement utilisable pour définir les genres, permet une distinction assez aisée de chacune des espèces de la tribu.

C'est pourquoi nous proposons à ce niveau une clé des espèces par l'appareil végétatif et l'appareil florifère.
