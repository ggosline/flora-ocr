# *Oricia lecomteana* Pierre

**Family**: [[Rutaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Oricia]]

## Description

Known in the treatment from fruiting Gabon material with five leaflets and polyembryonic seeds containing two large and two small embryos. Its generic placement is explicitly uncertain: Letouzey considered either expanding *Oricia* to include it or making it the type of a related new genus.

## Distribution

Gabon: Nioumvoux and Mayumba.

## Specimens examined

Lecomte C8; Chevalier 11364.
