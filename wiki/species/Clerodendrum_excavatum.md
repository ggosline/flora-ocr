# *Clerodendrum excavatum* De Wild. Figure 1(E, F)

**Genus**: [[Clerodendrum]]
**Protologue**: Ann. Mus. Congo Belge, Bot., sér. 5, 3 : 132 (1909)

## Description

, nom. illeg. C. globuliflorum B.Thomas, Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 68 : 63 (1936), syn. nov. Shrub or small tree erect or climbing up to 5 m tall or liane 3–10 m long; stem angular when young, becoming subcylindric, hollow, sometimes myrmecophilous, glabrous, generally with pseudospines of (2–)7–14(–30) mm. Leaves opposite or subopposite; petiole thick, 1–3.5(–10) cm, glabrous; blade broadly elliptic to oblong or obovate, (8.5–)14–23.5(–33) × (3.5–)6–9.5(–14.2) cm, rounded to cuneate and often slightly oblique or markedly asymmetric at base, acuminate over 0.7–1.4(–2.2) cm at apex, generally entire, sometimes (sub)undulate, rarely denticulate, clearly 3-nerved. Inflorescence often cauliflorous, near the ground on leafless branches, rarely on leafy lateral shoots, very dense, ± globular, 4.5–8 cm in diameter (without corollas), pubescent, becoming glabrous, borne on a very reduced peduncle, almost absent; cymes in dense whorled clusters; bracts linear, ± 1 cm long. Flower: pedicel up to 12 mm; calyx with tube 3–4 mm long, lobes ovate, 1–2.5(–3) × 0.6–1.1 cm, acuminate at apex, purple, red-violet, violet to brownish-violet, pink, white or whitish-green; corolla white, sometimes tinged purple, with tube (3.5–)5–8(–16) cm, with lobes oblong-elliptic or obovate, 5–16 × 4–8(–10) mm; stamens exserted 3–4(–5) cm. Fruit ± 10 × 15 mm, becoming black when mature.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea (Bioko), Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Central African Republic; in Gabon: Haut-Ogooué, Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo and Ogooué-Maritime. Conservation status: Pollard in Cheek et al. (2004) evaluated C. globuliflorum preliminarily as Least Concern (LC) and C. grandifolium as Near Threatened (NT). These two species are considered synonyms of a broader species concept of C. excavatum (see taxonomic notes below) to which is therefore preliminarily assigned Least Concern (LC).

## Ecology

undergrowth, primary and secondary tropical forest, riverbanks; in Gabon at 60–380 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1500 m.

## Source note

Authors have recognized C. grandifolium and C. globuliflorum as distinct species, especially on the basis of calyx and corolla length. Pollard in Cheek et al. (2004), had already expressed doubts about their distinction. Several specimens have been observed which present within the same inflorescence corolla tube lengths that cover the range specified for both species and C. grandifolium and C. globuliflorum are therefore considered here as conspecific. C. grandifolium Gürke is the earlier name, but it is illegitimate, and C. excavatum is the oldest available valid name. Bi: Cheek et al. (2004), Huber et al. (1963), Thomas (1936), Vande weghe et al. (2016). Bibliography
