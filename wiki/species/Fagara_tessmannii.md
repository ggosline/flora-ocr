# *Fagara tessmannii* Engl.

**Family**: [[Rutaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Fagara]]

## Description

Tree with comparatively short, oblong-ovate pinnate leaves and a petiole and rachis that are little armed. The petiolulate leaflets are characteristically unequal at the base—cuneate on one side and rounded on the other—flexible, drooping and curved on the petiolule, though the treatment stresses overlap with *F. macrophylla*. Flowers are normally 5-merous; male flowers have a 5-lobed disk and a conical rudimentary carpel. Fruit consists of glandular follicles with black shining seeds.

## Distribution

West-central African forest region, including Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon and the Congo basin.
