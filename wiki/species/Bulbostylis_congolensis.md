# *Bulbostylis congolensis* De Wild. Planche 9

**Genus**: [[Bulbostylis]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Pl. bequaert. 4 : 194 (1927)

## Description

B. pusilla (A.Rich.) C.B.Clarke subsp. congolensis (De Wild.) R.W.Haines, Sedges & Rushes E. Afr., App. 3 : 1 (1983). Abildgaardia pusilla (A.Rich.) Lye subsp. congolensis (De Wild.) Lye, Nordic J. Bot. 3 : 239 (1983).

Annual herb in tufts with the base of stems slightly swollen; stem 10–50 cm × 0.5 mm approximately, densely hairy and with prominent longitudinal ridges. Leaf: sheath straw-yellow or pinkish-brown, hairy, with long whitish hairs at the collar; blade 10–40 cm × 0.2–0.4 mm, densely hairy. Inflorescence an open anthela 3–5 cm long, with 3–9 primary branches of different lengths and 10–40 spikelets; involucral bracts 4–5, 5–15 mm long (including the awn), narrow, triangular, covered with short reddish-brown hairs at the base, with the central nerve prolonged by a green awn. Spikelet ovate or ovate-lanceolate, 3–5 × 1.5–2 mm; glumes 1.6–2 mm long, reddish-brown, ovate, acute, but muticous, covered with short hairs; main nerve trinerved. Flower: stamens 3; style approximately 1.5 mm long with 3 stigmas 0.6–0.8 mm. Achene 0.8–1 × 0.7–0.8 mm, light brown to olive, obovate or cordate, triquetrous, transversely wrinkled.

## Distribution

Tropical Africa, from Senegal to Ethiopia and southwards to the Democratic Republic of the Congo and Tanzania; rare in Gabon, found in Haut-Ogooué, Ogooué-Ivindo and Woleu-Ntem (3 collections)

## Ecology

in grasslands and open forests, often in seasonally humid habitats; in Gabon, at 400–700 m altitude, up to 1500 m in East Africa. Taxonomic note: Close to B. pusilla subsp. yalingensis (Cherm.) R.W.Haines, but distinguished by its very hairy stems. B: Goetghebeur & Coudijzer (1984b), Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al. (2010), Hooper & Napper (1972), Lye (1997)
