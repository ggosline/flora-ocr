# *Citropsis articulata* (Willd. ex Spr.) Sw. et Kell.

**Family**: [[Rutaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Citropsis]]

## Description

Spiny shrub or small tree with articulated compound leaves and winged petiole and rachis segments. The 1–5 leaflets are gland-dotted and crenulate. White flowers are borne in axillary pseudoracemes, usually with four petals and eight stamens. The berry-like fruit has a glandular rind and few large seeds.

## Distribution

Tropical African forest region, including Cameroon, Gabon and the Congo basin.
