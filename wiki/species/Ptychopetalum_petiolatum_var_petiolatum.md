# *Ptychopetalum petiolatum* var. *petiolatum*

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Ptychopetalum]]

## Description

**Habit**: woody shrub or tree up to 30 m tall. Branches greenish or black,
weakly two-winged, weakly ribbed.

**Leaves**: petiole short or absent (0–4 mm), glabrous, furrowed above, wrinkled
beneath. Blade glabrous on both faces, coriaceous, dark matt green above,
lighter green beneath, lanceolate, oblong, elliptic or oblanceolate, 7–19 ×
3–7 cm; base cuneiform or rounded, apex acuminate, obtuse. Midrib concave on
the upper face, prominent on the lower face. Secondary nerves 4–8 pairs
(sometimes 10 pairs), ascending, arcuate, anastomosing 5 mm from the margin or
more, concave above, prominent beneath. Nervules forming a loose reticulum,
concave on the upper face, prominent on the lower face.

**Inflorescence**: short axillary panicled raceme. Inflorescence rachis 5–12 mm
long. Flower bud 4.5 × 1.5–2 mm, constricted in its middle part, at the axil
of a strong bract.

**Flowers**: female, pentamerous. Pedicel striated, 2–3 mm long. Calyx absent.
Petals erect, reflexed at the apex, glabrous externally, pubescent on the edges
of the inner face, narrowly elliptic, free to the base, rounded at the apex.
Stamens 5–7, hypogynous, epipetalous in 1 or 2 cycles, fused to the petals by
the filament, alternately large or small; filament slender, 2.25–3 mm long;
anther dorsifixed with 2 locules with longitudinal dehiscence slits, ellipsoid,
0.5 mm long for a long filament and 0.75 mm for a short filament. Ovary 2–2.5
mm long, weakly conical, constricted at the upper third, with 6 weak ribs,
flattened at the apex, with 1 locule containing 2–3 pendulous ovules from the
apex of the locule. Style 1.5 mm long, slender, ending in a globose stigma.

**Fruit**: drupe red at maturity, ellipsoid, 17–21 × 12–14 mm. Exocarp surface
mamillate, often with 6 mamillae. Endocarp with 6 longitudinal ribs on the
inner face. Seed solitary, apiculate at the apex, covered with a greyish-yellow
felt. Embryo apical.

## Types

SYNTYPES: *Mann 1746* (iso-, P!); *Mann 1793*, Gabon.

## Distribution

**Range**: from Nigeria to Gabon. **Gabon**: Muni and Kongui rivers; Mont
Mvélakéné, 5 km W Méla; 42 km SE Lambaréné, 1°S and 10°28'E.

## Habitat and ecology

Forest understorey in humid dense forest. Recorded up to 800 m altitude.

## Notes

The author compared the types of *P. acuminatissimum* Engl. and *P.
petiolatum* Oliv. and found that differences, particularly in the length of
acumens, showed intermediates between the extreme cases described in the
literature. The number of secondary nerves varies from 3–4 pairs in *P.
acuminatissimum* to 4–8 pairs in *P. petiolatum*, characters that are thus
not determinative. Consequently, the author treats these two taxa as synonymous,
with *P. petiolatum* Oliv. having priority.
