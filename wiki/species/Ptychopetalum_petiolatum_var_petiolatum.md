# *Ptychopetalum petiolatum* var. *petiolatum*

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Ptychopetalum]]

## Description

Woody shrubby plant or tree up to 30 m tall. Branches greenish or black, weakly bi-winged, weakly ribbed. Petiole short or absent (0–4 mm), glabrous, furrowed above, wrinkled below. Lamina glabrous on both faces, coriaceous, dark matte green above, lighter green below, lanceolate, oblong, elliptic or oblanceolate, 7–19 × 3–7 cm; base cuneiform or rounded, apex obtusely acuminate. Midvein concave on the upper face, prominent on the lower face. Secondary nerves 4–8 pairs (sometimes 10 pairs), ascending, arched, anastomosing 5 mm from the limb edge or further, concave above, prominent below. Nervilles in loose network concave on the upper face, prominent on the lower face.

Inflorescence in a short pauciflorate axillary raceme. Inflorescence rachis 5–12 mm long. Floral bud 4.5 × 1.5–2 mm, narrowed in its median part, at the axil of a strong bract. Female flower pentamerous. Pedicel striated, 2–3 mm long. Calyx absent. Petals erect, reflexed at the apex, glabrous externally, pubescent on the edges of the inner face, narrowly elliptic, free to the base, rounded at the apex. Stamens 5–7, hypogynous, epipetalous in 1 or 2 cycles, fused to petals by the filament, alternately large or small; filament slender, 2.25–3 mm long; anther dorsifix with 2 locules with longitudinal dehiscence slits, ellipsoid, 0.5 mm long for a long filament and 0.75 mm long for a short filament. Ovary 2–2.5 mm long, weakly conical, narrowed at the upper third, with 6 weak ribs, flattened at the apex, with 1 locule containing 2–3 ovules pendant from the apex of the locule. Style 1.5 mm long, slender, terminated by a globose stigma.

Drupe red at maturity, ellipsoid, 17–21 × 12–14 mm. Exocarp surface mamillate, often with 6 mammillae. Endocarp with 6 longitudinal ribs on the inner face. Seed solitary, apiculate at the apex, covered with a yellowish-grey tomentum. Embryo apical.

## Distribution

**Range**: From Nigeria to Gabon.

**Cameroon**: Collected at Banga, Kumba region, Sangmélima area, Badjob area, Makak region, Nyong river, Ebolowa, Lolodorf, Bipindi region, and Yaoundé area.

**Gabon**: Lambaréné region, Mont Mvélakéné area, Muni and Kongui rivers.

## Habitat and ecology

Understory plant of dense humid forest, occurring up to 800 m altitude.

## Notes

The treatment notes that *P. acuminatissimum* Engl. and *P. petiolatum* Oliv. were compared, and the authors found intermediates between extreme forms with respect to acumen length. Secondary nerve counts varied from 3–4 pairs in *P. acuminatissimum* to 4–8 pairs in *P. petiolatum*, so these characters were not considered determinative. The authors consequently accepted synonymy between the two taxa, with *P. petiolatum* Oliv. having priority.
