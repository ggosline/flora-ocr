# *Taeniorrhiza gabonensis* Summerh.

**Genus**: [[Taeniorrhiza]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Mus. Le afl. Harvard Univ. 11:166 (1943)

## Description

Stem < 2 cm. Roots < 40 cm × 0.6–0.9 cm, ribbon-like, flattened, with a prominent midvein, green, silvery on the lower surface and yellow-green on the upper surface when dry. Inflorescence 1 cm, 1-flowered. Flower rather large, perianth white, but labellum lilac. Dorsal sepal ovate-lanceolate, acuminate. Petals lanceolate-ligulate. Lateral sepals obliquely ovate-lanceolate, acute to apiculate. Labellum 17–18 × 19–19.25 mm, suborbicular-ovate, truncate with a short apicule, widened just above a very narrow and unguiculate base, entire, without callus. Spur 14 mm, narrowly conical, slightly curved upward, subobtuse. – Pl. 176, p. 383.

## Distribution

currently known only from Gabon and Zaïre

## Ecology

epiphytic in forest
