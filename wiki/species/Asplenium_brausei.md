# *Asplenium brausei* Hieronymus

**Genus**: [[Asplenium]]
**Family**: [[Aspleniaceae]]

## Synonyms

- Asplenium nigritianum Tardieu (pp., non Hk.), Mém. IFAN 28 : 196 (1953), p.p.

## Description

— Hier., Engl. Bot. Jahrb. 46 : 359 (1911). — Alston FWTA : 57 (1959).

Rhizome shortly creeping or ascending, bearing grayish-brown scales, broad, cordate at the base, with dentate, ciliate margins. Frond blade 30-50 cm long; petiole reaching 1/4-1/3 of the blade length, channeled, scaly at the base, brownish. Rachis blackish, bearing the same broad scales as the rhizome. Blade lanceolate, narrowed at the base, bipinnate to tripinnatifid, pinnatifid at the apex. 20-25 pairs of alternate pinnae, sessile, oblong or linear-oblong, the longest approximately 7 cm long by 1.5 cm wide. Ultimate segments with superior base cuneate-truncate, inferior base cuneate, trapezoidal-rhomboidal, with superior margins distinctly dentate, teeth acute. Texture subcoriaceous. Surfaces glabrous. Veins bifurcate. Sori with blackish indusium.

## Type

Yaoundé, Zenker et Staudt 527 (type B, isotype P), Cameroun

## Discussion

This species was confused by me with A. nigritianum Hk. from Fernando-Po, from which it differs by the broad grayish scales on its rhizome and rachis, and by its highly dentate segments. It is also rather close to A. Schnelli Tardieu from Guinea, which differs from it by its smaller size, its black scales, its glabrous rachis in the adult, its ultimate segments cuneate, very obscurely lobed. The scales of A. nigritianum are very narrow, almost filiform, and cover the rachis.

On hillside on "inselberg" denuded, in peleohygrophilous forest, rare.
