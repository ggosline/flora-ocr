# *Carpolobia gabonica* Breteler Planche 20

**Genus**: [[Carpolobia]]
**Family**: [[Polygalaceae]]
**Protologue**: Edinburgh J. Bot. 67(2) : 343 (2010)

## Description

Edinburgh J. Bot. 67(2) : 343 (2010). Shrub 2–3 m tall; branchlets with appressed pubescence, becoming glabrous early. Stipules subulate, sometimes falcate, 2–3(–4) mm long, glabrous or with a few hairs. Leaf: petiole 2–3(–5) mm, sparsely pubescent to glabrous; lamina elliptic to obovate-oblong, (11–)14–20(–25) × (3.5–)6–8.5 cm, papyraceous, broadly cuneate to rounded at base, with acumen 1–1.5 cm, with (7–)8–11(–15) pairs of secondary veins, prominent on both faces as are the tertiary veins, midvein and secondary veins glabrous or sparsely pubescent. Inflorescence reaching 2 cm long, 10(–15)-flowered; peduncle 0.5–1.5(–2) mm, pubescent like the rachis; bracts and bracteoles ovate, concave, 1–2 mm long, tomentose. Flower: pedicel 2–3 mm, tomentose; sepals ovate-orbicular to elliptic, concave, 2.5–5 × 2.5–3 mm, the 3 inner ones smaller, ciliate, sparsely pubescent externally, glabrous internally; petals whitish, 10–11 × 5 mm, pubescent in the part adnate to the staminal tube with ciliate lobes; stamens with filaments 4–5 mm, glabrous; staminodes 3–4, minute. Fruit ovoid, weakly trigonous, 10–12 mm in diameter, slightly apiculate, densely and shortly velutinous, the surface obscured by hairs. Seeds ellipsoid to ovoid, with dense appressed hairiness.

## Distribution

south-western Gabon (Nyanga) and Congo (Brazzaville)

## Ecology

primary evergreen forest or old secondary forest; at 220–300 m altitude. B : Breteler (2010). Bibliography
