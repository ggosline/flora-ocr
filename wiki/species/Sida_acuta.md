# *Sida acuta* Burm.f. subsp. acuta Planche 23

**Genus**: [[Sida]]
**Family**: [[Malvaceae]]
**Protologue**: S. carpinifolia L.f., Suppl. pl. : 307 (1782)

## Description

Fl. indica: 147 (1768). S. carpinifolia L.f., Suppl. pl.: 307 (1782). S. acuta Burm.f. subsp. carpinifolia (L.f.) Borss.Waalk., Blumea 14: 188 (1966). Perennial herb or suffrutex, much branched, up to 1 m in height, with distichous glabrescendent branches. Stipules subpersistent, linear-acute, 1–1.5 cm, 1–3-nerved, often unequal, one with 1 nerve, the other with 3, with a few hairs on the margin. Leaves distichous, roughly equal in size to the end of the stem; petiole 0.5 cm; limb narrowly elliptic to ovate, of very variable dimensions, 2–6(–10) × 0.5–1.5(–3.8) cm, acute at apex, with dentate-serrulate margin, 3-nerved, underneath with long hairs, especially on the nerves, fine venation clearly visible between the main nerves. Flower axillary, most often solitary or sometimes a few grouped at the stem apex; pedicel 5–12 mm; sepals fused for about 2/3 of their length, 8 mm in length, foliaceous, acuminate, glabrous, becoming brown in fruit; petals 12 mm in length, yellow-orange; staminal tube 2 mm in length, filaments free for most of their length, anthers very numerous; ovary ± globose, with (6–)10 carpels; style divided into (6–)10 branches of 3 mm, slightly exceeding the anthers. Fruit with trigonous mericarps, indehiscent, 2–2.5 mm in length, with 2 awns of 1–1.5 mm.

## Distribution

Pantropical species, in Africa, from Senegal to Ethiopia, southward to Angola and Mozambique; in Gabon known from Ogooué-Maritime, Ogooué-Lolo, Ogooué-Ivindo and Haut-Ogooué.

## Ecology

Savanna, new fallows, ruderal stations; in Gabon at 100–450 m altitude, elsewhere up to 2200 m. Flowers and fruits from March to December. Uses: The leaves are used for the treatment of pulmonary affections and diseases of the liver and spleen. The plant has antiseptic properties; the powder of stem charcoal is applied against leprous ulcerations.

## Vernacular names

sida aigu (fr.) ; broomweed, common fanpetals (angl.). Note taxonomique : Une espèce très variable, parfois divisée en sous-espèces. Au Gabon, on ne trouve que des formes qui appartiennent à la sous-espèce acuta . La sous-espèce carpinifolia (L.f.) Borss. Waalk. est mentionnée par Sosef et al. (2006), mais la différence avec la sous-espèce acuta n’est pas évidente. De plus, la sous-espèce carpinifolia n’est connue que des Mascareignes, de l’Asie et du Pacifique. B : Akoègninou et al. (2006), Berhaut (1979), Exell & Meeuse (1961), Haui B liographie man (1963), Verdcourt (2009)
