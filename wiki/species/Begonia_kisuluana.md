# *Begonia kisuluana* Büttner Planche 44

**Genus**: [[Begonia]]
**Family**: [[Begoniaceae]]
**Protologue**: Verh. Bot. Vereins Prov. Brandenburg 32 : 45 (1890)

## Description

Epiphyte; stem subligneous, reaching 1(–2) m in length; indumentum marked by scaly hairs. Stipules narrowly triangular, 7–30 × 5–8 mm. Leaves: petiole 0.7–6 cm; blade slightly asymmetric, ovate to elliptic, 2–11 × 0.8–6.5 cm, base obliquely cordate to rounded, apex generally obtuse, margins entire, reddish, upper surface dull green scattered with dentate-laciniate hairs, whitish, veins red. Inflorescence unisexual, the ♂ in dichasial cymes up to 15(–31)-flowered, peduncle 5–30 mm; the ♀ 1–3-flowered, peduncle 1–5 mm. ♂ Flower: pedicel 2–12 mm; outer tepals obovate, 5–8 × 3–5.5 mm, the inner narrowly obovate, 3.5–8 × 1–3 mm; androecium flattened, zygomorphic, stamens 10–19, free portions of filaments unequal, anthers with longitudinal dehiscence. ♀ Flower sessile; tepals similar to those of ♂ flowers but longer; ovary 7–23 mm in length, circular in section, 1.5–3 mm in diameter; styles 3(–4), simple, stigmatic zone linear 1–1.5 mm. Infructescence with 1–3 erect fruits. Fruit slightly curved and with narrowed ends, 12–45 mm in length, opening by a longitudinal fissure.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Congo (Brazzaville), Democratic Republic of the Congo, Uganda, Angola

## Ecology

hygromesophilous epiphyte in dense humid semi-deciduous forests, also in the submontane forest layer, between 150 m and 1500 m altitude. B: Plana et al. (2006), Wilczek (1969a), de Wilde (2002).
