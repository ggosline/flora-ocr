# *Ledermanniella boloensis* C.Cusset Planche 15

**Genus**: [[Ledermanniella]]
**Family**: [[Podostemaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Mus. natl. Hist. nat., sér. 4., Adansonia 6(3) : 272 (1984)

## Description

Bull. Mus. natl. Hist. nat., sér. 4., Adansonia 6(3) : 272 (1984). Plant with a basal thalloid portion; stem unbranched (except rarely), ± 15 cm in length. Stipules absent. Leaf unknown. Inflorescences situated on one side of the stem, sessile or ± longly pedicellate; bracts 8–10 mm in length, linear, 1–2-dichotomous; spathelle pedicellate, obovoid, 6–8 mm in length, tearing irregularly at the apex at anthesis. Flower: pedicel longly exsert, ± 15 mm after anthesis; tepals 0.2–0.3 mm in length; androecium longer than the ovary, stamens 2 or 3, filaments 2–2.5 mm, connate for approximately half their length, anther oblong, 0.8–0.9 × 0.4–0.5 mm; ovary 2 × 0.7 mm, gynophore ± 1 mm in length; stigmas very short, subglobose. Fruit with caducous valves.

## Distribution

Endemic to Gabon, known from a single collection from the Bolo waterfall (between Bitam and Minvoul, Woleu-Ntem)

## Ecology

at ± 600 m altitude. B: Cusset (1984), Sosef et al. (2006). Bibliography
