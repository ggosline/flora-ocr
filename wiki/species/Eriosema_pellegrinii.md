# *Eriosema pellegrinii* Tisser.

**Genus**: [[Eriosema]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Mus. natl. Hist. nat. Paris , sér. 2, 2 : 316 (1930)

## Description

Geophytic herb, (5–)15–20(–30) cm in height; napiform tuber, 4–6 × 3–4 cm, rather deep in the soil; stems with long silvery-white hairs, glabrescent. Stipules lanceolate, 8–20 mm in length, brown, striated, sparsely hairy. Leaf: petiole 3–5 mm; leaflet 1, with oval blade, 4–10 × 2–5 cm, dark green and glabrous above, with silvery-white woolly hairs below; lateral veins in 10 to 12 pairs. Inflorescence terminal or axillary; racemes 1–2.5 cm in length; peduncle 1–2 cm, hairy; bracts linear, 10–15 × 1–2 mm. Flower subsessile; calyx 7–8 mm in length, teeth lanceolate, 5 mm in length, very acute, subequal, hairy; corolla yellow, standard obovate, 5 × 3–4 mm, auricled, claw 4 mm, wings and keel 8–9 × 3–4 mm, dorsally densely hairy, claw 4–4.5 mm; ovary hairy; style pubescent at base. Legume elliptic, ± 9 × 6 mm, with 1 or 2 seeds, with long white hairs. Seed ± 4 × 3 × 2 mm, pale brown; hilum covering half of the seed.

## Distribution

Mali, Burkina Faso, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Benin, Niger, Central African Republic, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola; in Gabon, known from Haut-Ogooué (Batéké Plateaus)

## Ecology

herbaceous and shrubby savanna, sandy soil, open forest; in Gabon at ± 600 m altitude, elsewhere up to 650 m. Flowers in May and September

## Source note

Species recently discovered in Gabon, with several related species in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Bibliography: Hauman (1954c), van der Maesen (2006a), Vandewegh (2008), Walters et al. (2011).
