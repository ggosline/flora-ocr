# *Strychnos retinervis* Leeuwenberg

**Genus**: [[Strychnos]]
**Family**: [[Loganiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Meded. Landbouwh. 69 (1) : 228, fig. 37 (1969)

## Description

Tree (?); small branchlets glabrous, not lenticellate.

Leaves with petiole glabrous, 8-11 mm long; limb coriaceous, elliptic or narrowly elliptic, (6.5-) 12-17.5 × (3-) 4-6 cm, acuminate at apex, cuneate at base, glabrous on both faces; 1-2 pairs of secondary veins curved along the margin and anastomosing with the other secondary veins, which are no less distinct; tertiary venation reticulate and prominent on both faces.

Inflorescences axillary, small, contracted, many-flowered, 1.5-3 × 1.5-3 cm. Flowers 4-merous. Sepals connate at base, suborbicular, 1.2-1.4 × 1 mm, obtuse, glabrous on both faces, finely ciliate. Corolla, in mature bud, 7.5 mm long, cream (?), glabrous outside, inside densely pilose at base of lobes and at throat (few hairs in tube); tube cylindric or nearly so, 1.5 times longer than the lobes, 4.5 mm long; lobes oblong, 3 × 1 mm, recurved. Stamens well-exserted, inserted at throat of corolla, filament glabrous, at least 2 times longer than anther; anther oblong, glabrous. Pistil glabrous,

8.5 mm long; ovary ovoid, 1 × 0.7 mm, 2-locular; style slender, 7.5 mm long, well-exsert; stigma capitate, small; approximately 5 ovules per locule.

Fruits unknown, supposed to be small and containing one seed. Pl. 36, 1-4, p. 143.

## Type

Le Testu 7943, Gabon (holo-, P; iso-, BR, WAG)

## Discussion

Known only from the type. Forests. Low altitude.
