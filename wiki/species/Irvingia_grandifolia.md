# *Irvingia grandifolia* (Engl.) Engl.

**Family**: [[Irvingiaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Irvingia]]

## Description

Large tree with a long straight cylindrical bole and thick ascending buttresses to 3 m. Stipules caducous, 2.5 cm. Blade obovate-elliptic or elliptic, to 35 × 18 cm, apex obtuse with a broad pointed acumen, base rounded and slightly cordate, papery, glabrous, glossy above and matt beneath; about 20 pairs of secondary veins and a double parallel veinlet network.

Terminal panicles to 15 cm; flowers subsessile, whitish, glabrous and solitary along axes. Sepals about 1 mm; petals about 3 mm; stamens 10, alternipetalous filaments 3.5–3.75 mm, others 2.5–3 mm; disc thick, greenish and papillose; ovary deeply immersed, style short. Fruit fleshy, golden-yellow, mango-like, ovoid and slightly flattened, 6 × 5.5 × 4.5 cm, with fibrous pulp and one seed.

## Distribution and ecology

Cameroon–Gabon and Congolian forest domains. Widespread in Gabon, especially low wet ground and riverbanks. Leaves turn bright red before falling; wood very hard.

## Specimens examined

Holotype: Zenker 3348, Bipindi, Cameroon. Gabon: Fleury 26672 (Lake Fernan Vaz); Le Testu 1122, 1185, 1609, 1628, 8914. Baudon material from Ouesso is also cited.

## Vernacular names

*opaness*, *olène*, *okouévess*, *esson* (Fang); *olinda* (Nkomi); *hendo* (Bumwali); *moulenda* (Bayaka).
