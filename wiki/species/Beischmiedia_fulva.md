# *Beischmiedia fulva* Rob. et Wilcz..

**Genus**: [[Beilschmiedia]]
**Family**: [[Lauraceae]]

## Description

— Robyns et Wilczek, Bull. jard. bot. Ét. Brux.: 469 (1949).

Tree 25 m tall with a bole reaching 18 m long and 30 cm in diameter; rhytidome light whitish, more or less scaly, bark 1 cm thick, brown-sepia. Young branchlets fawn tomentellous. Leaves subcoriaceous with numerous translucent points visible under magnification, discolorous; upper surface olivaceous, glabrous; lower surface with a fawn-rust tomentum formed of curly hairs separating into flakes. Young fruit ovate.

## Type

Michelson 889 (BR) Congo ex-belge : rivière Belanzavi (alt. 660 m) forêt ombrophile primaire équatoriale, sol argileux lourd. Bois tendre, blanc-rosé, cœur brunâtre ; écorce à odeur fine de térébenthine

## Discussion

The tree studied here is known only from a branchlet with leaves, without flowers, collected in 1920: Sargos 135 (P) Kouilou region (Todo) near the southern border of Gabon. It is also known from Cameroon.
