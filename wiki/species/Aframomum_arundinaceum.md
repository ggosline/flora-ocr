# *Aframomum arundinaceum* (Oliv. et Hanb.) K. Schum.
**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Aframomum]]
## Description
Slender often pendent herb 1.5–6 m; blades c. 30 × 5 cm, midrib pubescent beneath. One-flowered spikes arise on the creeping rhizome. Mauve flower with broad yellow-throated labellum c. 7.5 cm across. Fruit 7 × 4 cm.
