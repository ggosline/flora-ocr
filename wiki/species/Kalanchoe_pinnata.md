# *Kalanchoe pinnata* (Lam.) Pers. Planche 9

**Genus**: [[Kalanchoe]]
**Family**: [[Crassulaceae]]
**Protologue**: Syn. pl. 1 : 446 (1805)

## Synonyms

- Bryophyllum calycinum Salisb., Parad. Lond. : t. 3 (1805).

## Description

B. pinnatum (Lam.) Oken, Allgem. Naturgesch. 3 : 1966 (1841). Perennial herb, up to 1(–2) m tall, glabrous; stem erect, branched, slightly woody at base. Leaves: basal ones simple, upper ones 3- or 5-foliolate; petiole 2.5–10 cm, semi-amplexicaul; simple lamina: ovate to elliptic, 4–9 × 3–5 cm, with cordate to rounded base; pinnatifid lamina: leaflets orbicular to oblong-ovate or oblong-spatulate, 2.5–8 × 1.5–4 cm, base cuneate, apex obtuse, margin crenate. Inflorescence lax, glabrous, 10–80 cm long. Flower pendulous; pedicel 18–25 mm; calyx cylindric-urceolate, tube 22–30 × 8 mm, lobes ovate-triangular, acute, 7–11 mm long; corolla cylindric, green at base, red at apex, sparsely pubescent-glandular throughout towards base, tube 25–40 mm long, constricted at 8–10 mm from base, lobes ovate-triangular, acuminate, 9–14 × 4–6.5 mm, recurved; stamens inserted below the constriction of the tube, filaments 25–27 mm, anthers exsert, 3 mm long; nectariferous scales 2–2.5 mm long; carpels oblong-ovoid, up to 14 mm long; style 25–30 mm. Seeds oblong-ellipsoid, approximately 0.5 mm long.

## Distribution

present in almost all tropical countries but of uncertain origin; introduced as an ornamental plant in gardens and regularly escaped; presence in Gabon mentioned only by Raponda-Walker & Sillans (1961) but not yet confirmed by a herbarium specimen

Kew (©), from Wickens (1987).

## Ecology

anthropophile species, escaped from gardens and cultivation, in dry, rocky stations or along streams, also in shade; in Africa up to 1600 m altitude. Uses: regularly cultivated by villagers for the same uses as the preceding species. Elsewhere, the leaves are regularly used as a poultice in the treatment of burns of all kinds

## Vernacular names

life plant, floppers (Angl.). Pour les noms gabonais, voir l’espèce précédente. b : Descoings (2003), Raponda-Walker & Sillans (1961), Toussaint (1951), ibliographie Wardah & van Valkenburg (1999), Wickens (1987)
