# *Cyperus iria* L. Planche 35

**Genus**: [[Cyperus]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Sp. pl . 1 : 45 (1753)

## Synonyms

- Chlorocyperus iria (L.) Rikli, Jahrb. Wiss. Bot . 27 : 564 (1895).

## Description

Annual herb (or perhaps sometimes perennial), in tufts, with numerous short roots; stems 8–60 cm × 0.6–3 mm, trigonous with acute edges, glabrous, green. Leaf: sheath green to reddish brown; main blades 4–25 cm × 1–5 mm, flat, scabrous on the margins and main veins. Inflorescence an open anthela of 1.5–20 × 1–20 cm, with groups of sessile spikelets or on primary rays 0.5–15 cm long; involucral bracts foliaceous, the largest 5–30 cm × 1–6 mm; spikelets sessile or nearly so, of irregular shape and length. Spikelet elliptic, 2–10(–15 with the naked rachis) × 1.5–2 mm, flattened, with 5–20 flowers, golden to yellowish green; glumes obovate or circular, 1.3–1.6 mm long, golden brown with a colourless margin and a greenish central nerve slightly exceeding them. Flower: style short with 3 branches. Achene obovate, 1.1–1.4 × 0.6–0.7 mm, trigonous and apiculate, dark brown to almost black at maturity, with an almost smooth surface.

## Distribution

pantropical and subtropical, widely distributed, very common in Asia; rare in Gabon or poorly known, found only in Moyen-Ogooué and Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology

in seasonally humid habitats, such as temporary pools, marshes and drainage ditches, also often a weed of rice fields; in Gabon, at 0–100 m altitude, up to 1600 m elsewhere

## Vernacular names

grasshopper’s cyperus, rice flat sedge, umbrella sedge (angl.). Usage : a plusieurs applications médicinales en dehors de l’Afrique. Note taxonomique : se reconnaît facilement à ses petites glumes circulaires et dorées et par son inflorescence d’épis et d’épillets assez lâches. B : Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al. (2010), Hooper & Napper (1972), ibliographie Lye (1997)
