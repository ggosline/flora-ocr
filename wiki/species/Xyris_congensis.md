# *Xyris congensis* Büttner Planche 28

**Genus**: [[Xyris]]
**Family**: [[Xyridaceae]]
**Protologue**: Verh. Bot. Vereins Prov. Brandenburg 31 : 71–72 (1890)

## Description

Perennial herb in tufts, often with the base formed by the bases of persistent leaves. Leaf: sheath red-brown, shiny, glabrous, gradually widening at the base, split, persistent and becoming silky with age; ligule acute, approximately 1 mm long; blade linear, reaching 30 cm × 1–6 mm, glabrous, often spiralled, apex indurated, acute. Inflorescence: peduncle 25–50 cm × 1–1.5 mm; spike ellipsoid-oblong, obtuse, approximately 9 × 6 mm at first flowering, widening with age to approximately 20 × 6 mm or longer; sterile bracts 4–6, suborbicular, approximately 3–4 mm, coriaceous, shiny, uniformly brown, generally without dorsal marking; fertile bracts comparable, numerous. Flower: lateral sepals arched, 3.5–4.5 mm long, coriaceous, brown, keel irregularly fimbriate-dentate on the upper half; corolla yellow, lobes broadly obovate, apex laciniate, 4.5 ×

## Distribution

very widespread in tropical Africa: from Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea and Gabon eastward to Uganda and southward to South Africa, also in Madagascar; in Gabon known from the provinces of Estuaire and Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology

wet grasslands, flooded or floodable marshy stations; in Gabon at low altitude, elsewhere up to 2300 m. B: Lewis (1981), Lock (1999, 2010). Bibliography
