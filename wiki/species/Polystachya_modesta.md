# *Polystachya modesta* Rchb.f.

**Genus**: [[Polystachya]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Flora 50 : 114 (1867)

## Description

– Polystachya shirensis Rchb.f., Otia Bot. Hambrug. 2 : 112 (1881).

– Polystachya kindtiana De Wild., Ann. Mus. Congo, Bot. Ser. 5(1): 21 (1903).

Pseudobulbs 0.6–10 × 0.3–1.5 cm, conical. Leaves 7–22.5 × 0.7–2.5 cm, lanceolate. Inflorescence 5–23 (–40) cm, 20–200-flowered. Flowers yellow, yellow-green, pink, violet, labellum yellow. Dorsal sepal elliptic, acute. Lateral sepals obliquely triangular. Labellum rather thin, trilobed in the middle, pubescent in the centre, with a cushion of hairs in the basal third or half; midlobe orbicular to semi-elliptic, emarginate, bullate-tessellate; lateral lobes obliquely ovate-triangular to ovate-oblong, obtuse, somewhat falcate. Spur conical-sacciform. –Pl. 123, p. 281.

## Distribution

Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Togo, Nigeria, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Zaïre, Rwanda, Burundi, Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, Angola, Malawi, Zambia, Zimbabwe, Mozambique, South African Republic

## Ecology

alt. 500–1,200 m. Epiphytic or rarely lithophytic in riparian forest, woodland, thicket

MATERIAL EXAMINED (Map 54):

Florence 1719, Boka-Boka, 15 km W. of Mékambo, intraforest savanna of Mt. Bengoué (fl. March), P!.
