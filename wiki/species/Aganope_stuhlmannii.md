# *Aganope stuhlmannii* (Taub.) Adema

**Genus**: [[Aganope]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Thai Forest Bull. Bot. 28 : 10 (2000)

## Synonyms

- Ostryoderris stuhlmannii (Taub.) Dunn ex Harms , Pflanzenw. Ost-Afrikas 3(1) : 644 (1915).
- Xeroderris stuhlmannii (Taub.) Mendonça & E.C.Sousa, Bol. Soc. Brot., sér. 2, 43 : 273 (1968).

## Description

Tree reaching 6–9(–27) m in height; young branchlets, petioles, rachis, petiolules and inflorescence axes with dense brown pubescence. Stipules caducous, linear to lanceolate, 6–12 mm long. Leaf: petiole 5–7 cm; rachis 15–17 cm; stipels absent; leaflets in 5 to 7 pairs, with petiolule 2–3 mm, limb oblong-ovate, 3–6(–13) × 2–3(–9) cm, apex subcordate and slightly emarginate, glabrous and smooth above, with ferruginous to silvery pubescence below, glabrescent; midvein prominent, secondary nerves in 4 to 7 pairs. Inflorescence 7–18(–22) cm long; slender branchlets, ± 3.5 cm, with short reddish pubescence; bracts very caducous, linear-lanceolate, ± 1 mm long. Flower: pedicel 2–5 mm, velvety, articulated at right angle with calyx; bracteoles similar to bracts; calyx 3–5 × 4 mm, with deltoid teeth, 0.5–1 mm long, brown-villous on both faces; corolla whitish, 10–15 mm long; stamens 8 mm long; ovary 10 mm long, ± sessile, with 3 to 5 ovules. Pod linear-oblong, (6–)10–14(–18) × 2.5–3.5(–5) cm, base and apex attenuate, greyish, with 1 or 2(–5) seeds, wings 2–3 mm wide. Seed lenticular, ± 12 mm long, brown-red.

## Distribution

fairly widespread in West Africa, from Senegal to Nigeria and in East Africa, from Kenya to Zimbabwe; to be sought in Gabon. Its presence in Central Africa remains doubtful.

## Ecology

primary and secondary forest; elsewhere at 50–600 m altitude. Flowering at the end of the dry season, just before new leaves develop. b: Arbonnier (2000), Hauman (1954d), Hepper (1958), Lemmens (2008f). ibliography
