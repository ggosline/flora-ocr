# *Aganope stuhlmannii* (Taub.) Adema

**Genus**: [[Aganope]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Thai Forest Bull. Bot. 28 : 10 (2000)

## Description

Ostryoderris stuhlmannii (Taub.) Dunn ex Harms, Pflanzenw. Ost-Afrikas 3(1): 644 (1915). Xeroderris stuhlmannii (Taub.) Mendonça & E.C.Sousa, Bol. Soc. Brot., sér. 2, 43: 273 (1968). Tree reaching 6–9(–27) m in height; young branchlets, petioles, rachis, petiolules and inflorescence axes with dense brown pubescence. Stipules caducous, linear to lanceolate, 6–12 mm in length. Leaf: petiole 5–7 cm; rachis 15–17 cm; stipels absent; leaflets in 5 to 7 pairs, with petiolule of 2–3 mm, with oblong-ovate lamina, 3–6(–13) × 2–3(–9) cm, apex subcordate and slightly emarginate, glabrous and smooth above, with ferruginous to silvery pubescence below, glabrescent; midvein prominent in relief, secondary veins in 4 to 7 pairs. Inflorescence 7–18(–22) cm in length; branchlets slender, ± 3.5 cm, with short, russet pubescence; bracts very caducous, linear-lanceolate, ± 1 mm in length. Flower: pedicel 2–5 mm, velvety, articulate at right angle to the calyx; bracteoles as the bracts; calyx 3–5 × 4 mm, with deltoid teeth, 0.5–1 mm in length, brown-villous on both faces; corolla whitish, 10–15 mm in length; stamens 8 mm in length; ovary 10 mm in length, ± sessile, with 3 to 5 ovules. Pod linear-oblong, (6–) 10–14 (–18) × 2.5–3.5 (–5) cm, base and apex attenuate, greyish, with 1 or 2(–5) seeds, wings 2–3 mm in width. Seed lenticular, ± 12 mm in length, brown-red.

## Distribution

fairly widespread in West Africa, from Senegal to Nigeria and in East Africa, from Kenya to Zimbabwe; to be searched for in Gabon. The presence in Central Africa remains doubtful

## Ecology

primary and secondary forest; elsewhere at 50–600 m altitude. Flowering at the end of the dry season, just before new leaves develop. b: Arbonnier (2000), Hauman (1954d), Hepper (1958), Lemmens (2008f). ibliography
