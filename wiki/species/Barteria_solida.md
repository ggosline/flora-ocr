# *Barteria solida* Breteler Planche 32(1–8)

**Genus**: [[Barteria]]
**Family**: [[Passifloraceae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia, sér. 3, 21 : 316 (1999)

## Description

Tree reaching 13 m in height; trunk up to 13 cm in diameter; young branches plagiotropous provided with appressed hairs or not; fertile lateral branches solid, not swollen and without myrmecodomes. Leaf with petiole of (1–)3–7(–10) mm; blade of variable form and dimensions, elliptic to obovate-elliptic, (4–) 10–15 (–18) × (2–)4–5(–6.5) cm, generally cuneate or rarely rounded at the base, (long) acuminate at the apex, coriaceous, smooth above, scattered appressed hairs below; lateral nerves in 5–9(–11) pairs. Inflorescence with flowers solitary or in pairs, situated on the upper side of the branches; bracts in 3–4 rows, ovate-orbicular, up to 8 mm in length, rounded, emarginate or shortly apiculate, brown, tearing little (in the middle). Flower subsessile; sepals with base fused for 0.5 cm, oblong, 3.5(–5) × 1–1.5 cm, apex acute to acuminate, silky-silvery on the outside, white; petals similar to the sepals, fused to the sepals for 0.5 cm and fairly large, glabrous, white; outer corona membranous, laciniate, ± 1.5 cm in height, glabrous, the inner one thick, 2–4 mm in height, shortly plurilobed, glabrous; stamens approximately 30, arranged in one row, filaments ± 2 mm, fused on the basal quarter, anthers 5–7 mm in length; ovary subglobose, 3–5 mm in length, glabrous; style 2–2.5 cm, stigma capitate, ± 7 mm in diameter, pale yellow. Fruit subglobose, 1.5–2.5 cm in diameter, apiculate, pale green. Seeds about ten per capsule, obovate, 4–5 × 3–3.5 × 1.5–2 mm, laterally compressed.

## Distribution

southeastern Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, rare in the Ogooué-Ivindo, fairly common in the Ngounié

## Ecology

dense forests; in Gabon at 550–950 m in altitude, elsewhere up to 1600 m. Flowers in March, November and December; fruits in November. b: Breteler (1999), Peccoud et al. (2012). Bibliography
