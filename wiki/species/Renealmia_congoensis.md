# *Renealmia congoensis* Gagnep.

**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Renealmia]]

## Description

Creeping-rhizomed herb, leafy stem about 60 cm or taller. Petiole 3–6 cm; narrow oblanceolate glabrous blade c. 30 × 7 cm. Extremely loose panicle 20 cm or longer, with lower secondary clusters 4–6 cm apart and each bearing usually two flowers. Calyx red, 7–10 mm; corolla and labellum whitish yellow, the latter yellow-spotted below. Red ovoid fruit c. 12 mm, seeds yellow-arillate.

## Distribution

Humid forest understorey in Cameroon, Gabon and Congo.
