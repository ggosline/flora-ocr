# *Impatiens pellegrini* N. Hallé

**Family**: [[Balsaminaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Impatiens]]

## Description

Gabonese endemic with alternate small leaves bearing filiform appendages at the base. One or two dark-pink flowers arise from a peduncle with one bract opposite a small tooth. Lip boat-shaped, spur unswollen and short.

## Distribution

Mayombe, Gabon.
