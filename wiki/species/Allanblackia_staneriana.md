# *Allanblackia staneriana* Exell & Mendonça Planche 1(6,7)

**Genus**: [[Allanblackia]]
**Family**: [[Clusiaceae]]
**Protologue**: J. Bot. 74 ( Suppl. ) : 20 (1936)

## Description

J. Bot. 74 ( Suppl. ) : 20 (1936). Tree up to 30 m tall; trunk straight, smooth or fluted; bark reddish-pink in section; latex yellow to pale yellow. Leaf glabrous; petiole 10–15 mm; blade elliptic to elliptic-oblong or oblanceolate, 10–20 × 3.5–7 cm, base cuneate to obtuse, apex acuminate, subcoriaceous; lateral veins approximately 25–30, secretory canals superposed to lateral veins or oblique. Male flower: pedicel 8–10 cm; sepals suborbicular, the outer ones 6–10 mm in diameter, the inner ones 11–15 mm in diameter; petals obovate, 20–22 × 12–15 mm, white to whitish-greenish; stamen fascicles 12–15 mm long, with anthers arranged on both faces; disk glands smooth. Female flower: pedicel 5–6 cm; sepals and petals similar to those of the male flower, petals white; stamen fascicles 4–5 mm long; disk glands smooth; ovary 5-ribbed, with 4–5 ovules per locule; stigma 5-lobed. Fruit ovoid to ellipsoid, 5-ribbed, 15 × 7 cm. Seeds 4–20, ovoid or ellipsoid to suborbicular, 2–2.5 × 1.5–2 cm.

## Distribution

rare species from southern Cameroon, Gabon, western Democratic Republic of the Congo (Mayombe) and Angola (Cabinda); in Gabon known only from Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology

primary forest, forest edges; in Gabon at 30 m altitude, elsewhere up to 300 m. Taxonomic note: In the literature, the epithet of this species name is often spelled as "stanerana". Exell clearly indicates that he named the species after Dr. P. [Pierre] Staner. If one follows recommendation 60C.1d of the Code of Nomenclature, it is necessary to correct the spelling as "staneriana". Bi: Bamps (1969, 1970a). Bibliography
