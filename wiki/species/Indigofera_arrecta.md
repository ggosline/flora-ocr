# *Indigofera arrecta* Hochst. ex A.Rich.

**Genus**: [[Indigofera]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Tent. Fl. Abyss. 1 : 184 (1847)

## Description

Branched shrub, 1–2(–3) m tall; branches erect, somewhat striate, with fine appressed pubescence. Stipules caducous, setaceous. Leaf 6–8 cm long; petiole 4–16 mm; stipels subulate, 1 mm long; leaflets (7–)11 to 25, opposite, with oblanceolate to narrowly elliptic-oblong blade, (7–)10–15(–22) × (3–)5–10 mm, glaucous and finely pubescent, drying black. Inflorescence in axillary racemes, sessile, 10–25(–50) mm long, with 6 to 12(–20) flowers; bracts ± 1 mm long. Flower sessile; calyx 1–1.5 mm long, finely villous on the external face, with appressed, silvery hairs, lobes as long as the tube, the upper ones deltoid, the lower ones lanceolate; corolla yellow to reddish, 4–5 mm long. Pod generally reflexed, straight, cylindric to slightly 4-angular, 12–30 × 2–3 mm, with 6 to 8 seeds, brown. Seed rectangular, ± 1.5 × 1.5 × 1.2 mm, strigulose, brown to brown-olive; hilum central.

## Distribution

Tropical Africa, South Africa, Madagascar and South Arabia; to be sought in Gabon as not yet recorded, but present in neighbouring countries

## Ecology

Savannas and abandoned cultivations; at 300–2700 m altitude

Use: The roots have various medicinal applications. The leaves produce a blue dye. Bibliography: Botton & Hallé (1957), Cronquist (1954), Gillett (1958, 1971a), Lemmens & Cardon (2005), Lewis et al. (2005), Raponda-Walker & Sillans (1961), Watt & Breyer-Brandwijk (1962).
