# *Achyrospermum ciliatum* Gürke

**Genus**: [[Achyrospermum]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 36 : 128 (1905)

## Description

Herb 0.2–0.6 m tall; stem quadrangular, ascending, sometimes woody at base, arising from a fibrous root system, densely villous toward the apex, with spreading whitish hairs and retrorse hairs and sessile glands in the lower part. Leaf: petiole 10–20 mm; blade obovate to broadly elliptic, 10–15 × 5–6 cm, cuneate or sometimes rounded at base, acute to apiculate at apex, sometimes obtuse on lower leaves, serrate with mucronate teeth and ciliate, upper surface uniform in color, with scattered stiff hairs 1–1.5 mm, lower surface pubescent on the veins and with sessile glands. Inflorescence terminal, cylindric, 4–9 × 1.5–2 cm; opposite cymes, condensed, with 6 to 8 flowers; bracts ovate, 5–8 mm long, apiculate. Flower: calyx 7–8 mm long, ± regularly 5-lobed, with pubescent tube, triangular lobes, long-acuminate-caudate, ± 3 mm long, long-ciliate (cilia ± 1 mm); corolla 6 mm long, scarcely exceeding the calyx, white, lower lip 2 mm long. Fruit: achenes ovoid, 2 mm long, brown, pubescent, bearing near the apex linear, flattened scales, 2 mm long.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon: Moyen-Ogooué, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem. Conservation status: Widely distributed; probably Least Concern (LC)

## Ecology

banks and open habitats in forest, 140–580 m elevation in Gabon; elsewhere up to 900 m. B: Bruce (1936), Sosef et al. (2004). Bibliography
