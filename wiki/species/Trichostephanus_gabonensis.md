# *Trichostephanus gabonensis* Breteler

**Genus**: [[Trichostephanus]]
**Family**: [[Flacourtiaceae]]
**Modern family**: **Achariaceae** — *Flacourtiaceae* as circumscribed here is superseded; see [[Flacourtiaceae]]

## Description

TYPE: De Wilde & Jongkind 9541, Gabon, 40 km NNW Doussala (holo- and iso-).

Shrub 2.5–4 m tall.

Leaves with linear ascending stipules, pubescent to glabrescent, 1.5–3.5 × 0.5 mm, ± persistent; petiole glabrescent, (1)–2–4–(5) mm long; lamina elliptic to elliptic-obovate, (3)–7–13 × 2–4 cm, glabrous, chartaceous to coriaceous, cuneate at base, acuminate at apex; secondary veins 5–6 pairs.

Inflorescences in few-flowered glomerate clusters; bracts triangular, 2 mm long; pedicel 2–3 mm long, not articulate, puberulent to tomentose. ♂ Flowers cream white; sepals elliptic-oblong, 4.5–5 × 1.5–2 mm, glabrescent to tomentellous; stamens 7–8; filaments flattened, pubescent towards the lower half, on the inner face, and united to each other towards the lower third; anthers subovoid; receptacular disc in cupuliform ridge; abortive ovary pubescent. ♀ Flowers cream white; sepals glabrescent, 5–5.5 × 1.5–2 mm, elliptic-oblong; stamens (7–8) sterile, filaments flattened, pubescent towards lower 2/3, on the inner face; receptacular disc in cupuliform ridge; pistil glabrous or sparsely pubescent; ovary with 3–4 multiovulate placentas; styles 4–5; stigmas slightly papilliferous.

Immature fruits ellipsoid, 20 × 10 mm, glabrous, with styles 2–3 mm long. – Pl. 3, 7–10, p. 15.
