# *Bulbostylis oritrephes* (Ridl.) C.B.Clarke Planche 14

**Genus**: [[Bulbostylis]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Trans. Linn. Soc. London, Bot. 4 : 54 (1894)

## Description

Abildgaardia oritrephes (Ridl.) Lye, Bot. Not. 125 : 497 (1974). Slender perennial herb, with stems growing in rows along a horizontal rhizome; stems 3–40 cm × 0.3–0.5 mm, lower part thickened and almost bulbous, lower portion with numerous spreading white hairs 0.2–0.4 mm, upper part almost glabrous. Leaf: sheath greyish to reddish or golden-brown, glabrous or hairy, with ± flexuous hairs at the collar; blade 20–60 × 0.2–0.5 mm, plane or canaliculate and generally densely hairy. Inflorescence formed of one sessile spikelet and 1–3(–9) pedicelled spikelets; main involucral bracts generally shorter than the sessile spikelet (but sometimes up to 2–3 times longer than the latter). Spikelet 4–8 × approximately 2 mm; glumes dark reddish-brown with a narrow paler main nerve, hairy; basal glumes subulate, the upper ones acuminate; margin only with short hairs. Achene approximately 1.2 × 1 mm, pale brown, with transverse undulations.

## Distribution

Tropical and subtropical Africa, from Guinea to Kenya and southwards to South Africa; rare in Gabon (or overlooked), found only in the Ngounié and Ogooué-Ivindo (2 collections)

## Ecology

on relatively bare ground, in open herbaceous savannas or in disturbed places, often in intensely grazed or trampled habitats; in Gabon, at 0–200 m altitude, up to 2100 m in East Africa. Taxonomic note: distinguished from all other species by its stems with bulbous base, in horizontal rows. It can sometimes be confused with B. filamentosa, but the latter has an achene with fine papillae and without transverse undulations. B : Goetghebeur & Coudijzer (1984b), Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al. (2010), Hooper & Napper (1972)
