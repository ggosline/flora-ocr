# *Bulbostylis oritrephes* (Ridl.) C.B.Clarke Planche 14

**Genus**: [[Bulbostylis]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Trans. Linn. Soc. London, Bot. 4 : 54 (1894)

## Synonyms

- Fimbristylis oritrephes Ridl., Trans. Linn. Soc. London, Bot. 2 : 155 (1884).
- Abildgaardia oritrephes (Ridl.) Lye, Bot. Not. 125 : 497 (1974).

## Description

Perennial herb, slender, with stems growing in a row along a horizontal rhizome; stems 3–40 cm × 0.3–0.5 mm, lower part thickened and almost bulbous, basal part with numerous spreading white hairs 0.2–0.4 mm, upper part almost glabrous. Leaf: sheath grey to reddish or golden brown, glabrous or hairy, with hairs ± flexuous at the collar; blade 20–60 × 0.2–0.5 mm, plane or canaliculate and generally densely hairy. Inflorescence formed of one sessile spikelet and 1–3(–9) pedicellate spikelets; main involucral bracts generally shorter than the sessile spikelet (but sometimes up to 2–3 times longer than the latter). Spikelet 4–8 × approximately 2 mm; glumes dark reddish brown with a narrow paler main nerve, hairy; basal glumes subulate, the upper ones acuminate; margin only with short hairs. Achene approximately 1.2 × 1 mm, pale brown, with transverse corrugations.

## Distribution

Tropical and subtropical Africa, from Guinea to Kenya and southward to South Africa; rare in Gabon (or overlooked), found only in Ngounié and Ogooué-Ivindo (2 collections)

## Ecology

on relatively bare ground, in open grassy savannas or in disturbed places, often in intensely grazed or trampled habitats; in Gabon, at 0–200 m altitude, up to 2100 m in East Africa. Taxonomic note: distinguished from all other species by its stems with bulbous base, in horizontal rows. It can sometimes be confused with B. filamentosa, but the latter has an achene with fine papillae and without transverse corrugations. B: Goetghebeur & Coudijzer (1984b), Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al. (2010), Hooper & Napper (1972)
