# *Augouardia le-* Testui Pellegrin

**Genus**: [[Augouardia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: F. Pellegrin, Bull. Soc. Bot. Fr. 71 : 310 (1924); Fl. Mayombe 1 : 109 pl. 10 (1924); Lég. Gabon : 92 (1948)

## Description

Trees. Leaves with 5 pairs of opposite leaflets, oblong or oblong-lanceolate, long-acuminate acute, obtuse or rounded at the base, coriaceous, glabrous, 10-12 cm long, 3.5-4.5 cm wide. Midvein somewhat depressed above, prominent below. About ten secondary veins, some anastomosing, others extending to a marginal vein. Network well-meshed, visible on both faces, of veinlets and venules almost as finely prominent as the secondary veins. Rachis about 20 cm. Petiolules 5-6 mm, articulate on the rachis.

Terminal or axillary panicles densely flowered; axes pubescent. Small flowers sessile or nearly so. Basal bract suborbicular, concave, glabrous, with fimbriate margins, caducous, 2 mm. Bracteoles orbicular, concave, free, imbricate, persistent, glabrous, 2 mm. Sepals 4, ovate oblong, concave, subequal, glabrous, 2-2.5 mm. Petals 0. Fertile stamens 3 fused at the base into a shield 2 mm high, glabrous externally, hairy internally; filaments 5 mm; anthers 2 mm, emarginate at the apex, cordate at the base. Staminodes 4, filiform, glabrous. Ovary hairy, 2 ovules. Stigma capitate. Fruit unknown.

## Type

Le Testu 2295, Gabon (P)

## Discussion

Small tree little known, which appears endemic to Gabon and Mayombé, but little widespread.

## Specimens examined

Aubréville : 150, Nzilé (fl. juin). Le Testu : 2228, Sindara ; 2295, Sindara (fl. oct.).
