# *Drypetes arborescens* (Oliv.) Hutch.

**Genus**: [[Drypetes]]
**Family**: [[Putranjivaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. trop. Afr: 6(1): 680 (1912)

## Description

Plate 39; Figure 7(D, E)

Tree up to 21 m tall; trunk up to ±28 cm in diameter, slightly fluted; bark smooth, with fine horizontal lenticels, pale grey to pale brown; slash with sweet and fruity odour; branchlets cylindric to slightly flattened, glabrous; buds glabrescent. Stipules caducous, narrowly elliptic-ovate, up to ±5.8 mm long, with pubescent apex and margin. Leaf petiole (5.2–7.5–11.4(−13.9) mm, slightly fluted above, slightly wrinkled, glabrous; lamina elliptic-ovate to oblong, (7.5–14.5–17.5(−24.9) × (2.5–5.4–6.7(−9) cm, papyraceous to finely coriaceous, with oblique base, acute, rarely slightly obtuse, with apex slightly acuminate, with margin subentire to slightly and obscurely crenate or serrulate, often slightly recurved, dark green glossy above, pale green below; midvein yellowish, glabrous, secondary nerves 4 or 5(−6) on each side, ascending, looped and anastomosing well before the margin, impressed above, prominent below, yellowish, frequently drying blackish, tertiary venation weakly reticulate. Male inflorescence in the axils of leaves or below leaves along the branches, with up to ±40 flowers; bracts broadly ovate to ovate, 0.5–0.7(−1.3) mm long. Male flower with unpleasant fragrance; pedicel slender, (2.2–3),5–5.7(−7.9) mm, finely hairy; sepals (4–5(−6), not touching or scarcely touching during anthesis, narrowly oblong to ligulate, (1.2–1.4–1.8(−2.2) × 0.3–0.6(−0.8) mm, with obtuse apex, with slightly toothed margin, pale greenish to yellowish, slightly pubescent outside, glabrous inside, with a few scattered minute cilia; stamens 3 or 4, in a whorl around the disc margin, filament (0.4–1),2–3.2(−5.3) mm, anther (0.3–0.7–1(−1.3) mm long, glabrous; disc (0.6–1),1–1.5(−1.8) mm in diameter, flat to slightly convex, with somewhat undulate margin, glabrous, with or without a central conical projection reaching 0.3 mm. Female inflorescence like the male, with up to ±10(−15) flowers; bracts 0.4–0.7(−0.8) mm long, similar to male ones. Female flower pedicel (2.2–3.8–4.8(−6.1) mm, slender, finely hairy; sepals 5(−7), (1.2–1),4–2.5(−2.7) × 0.8–1.5(−1.7) mm, similar to those of males; disc (2.2–2.4–2.5(−2.7) mm in diameter, annular, glabrous; ovary globose-ellipsoid, 1-locular, glabrous; style up to 0.6(−0.8) mm or absent, robust, simple; stigma pulvinate-capitate, (0.7–1),1–1.2 mm in diameter, rounded. Fruit ellipsoid to globose, (10.5–11–14.5 × (8–9–10(−12.5) mm, greenish to ivory white at maturity, glabrous, with persistent sepals and stigmas, 1-seeded, pedicel (5.5–10.5–18.5(−20) mm. Seed (7–7.7–9.9(−11.4) mm long.

## Distribution

Cameroon, continental Equatorial Guinea and Gabon; in Gabon: Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué, Ngounié, Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem

Habitat: primary and secondary evergreen forests, submontane forests, coastal forests on sandy soils, swamps, riparian forests; in Gabon at 5–915 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1200 m.

## Source note

D. arborescens is a forest tree often observed and collected in central and south-western Gabon, whereas the number of records decreases significantly towards the regions furthest north and east of the country. Its leaves do not reach the large dimensions of those of D. similis or do not correspond to the larger and often elliptic-obovate ones of D. moliwensis, another species with subsessile capitate stigmas and a 1-locular ovary.
