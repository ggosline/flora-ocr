# *Cyathula prostrata* (L.) Bl.

**Family**: [[Amaranthaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Cyathula]]

## Description

Herb erect below then trailing and often rooting at lower nodes; stems usually 30–40 cm. Leaves broadly elliptic to obovate, 4–9 × 2.5–5 cm. Terminal spikes 10–20 cm bear 3-flowered units with one fertile flower and two sterile flowers reduced to hooked bracts and tepals. Fertile flowers to 3 mm; fruit 2–3 mm, seed yellow.

## Ecology and uses

Humid forest understorey and cultivated ground; leaves used as a vegetable.
