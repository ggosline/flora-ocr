# *Polystachya odorata* Lindl.

**Genus**: [[Polystachya]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: J. Linn. Soc., Bot. 6 : 130 (1862)

## Description

– Polystachya usambarensis Schltr., Notizbl. Bot. Gart. Berlin-Dahlem 2 : 250 (1898).

– Polystachya striata De Wild., Ann. Mus. Congo, Bot., Ser. 5,3 : 58 (1909).

Pseudobulbs 2-5 (-18) × 0.6-1.5 cm, subglobose to narrowly conical. Leaves (6-) 13-30 × 1.5-4.5 cm, elliptic-ovate. Inflorescence 10-30 cm, 25-200-flowered. Flowers fragrant, white, dull red-brown, pale green, washed or striped with red or violet on the sepals, labellum white, midlobe spotted with pink, callus yellowish toward the apex. Lateral sepals triangular-ovate, apiculate. Labellum trilobed, more or less pubescent at the centre with an oblong callus in the lower half; midlobe transversely elliptic, with crenulate-undulate margins; lateral lobes 2-3 mm, oblong-ovate to oblong-triangular, acute to subobtuse, falcate, thin. Spur saccate. Pl. 124, p. 283.

## Distribution

Côte d'Ivoire, Burkina Faso, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Central African Republic, Zaire, Rwanda, Uganda, Tanzania, Angola
