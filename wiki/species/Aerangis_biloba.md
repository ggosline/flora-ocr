# *Aerangis biloba* (Lindl.) Schltr.

**Genus**: [[Aerangis]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Die Orchideen : 598 (1914) ; Beih. Bot. Centralbl. 36 : 114 (1918) ; FTWA : 265 (1968) ; Stewart, Kew Bull. 35(2) : 281 (1979) ; FAC 2 : 513 (1992) ; FCAM 3 : 846 (2001) .- BASIONYME : Angraecum bilobum Lindl., Bot. Reg., Misc. 69 (1840). - TYPE : Bowdich s.n., Ghana, holo-K!

## Description

Stewart, Kew Bull. 35(2): 281 (1979); FAC 2: 513 (1992); FCAM 3: 846 (2001). – BASIONYM: Angraecum bilobum Lindl., Bot. Reg., Misc. 69 (1840). – TYPE: Bowdich s.n., Ghana, holo-K!.

– Angraecum apiculatum Hook., Bot. Mag., t. 4159 (1845).

– Angraecum campyloplectron Rchb. f., Bonplandia 3: 226 (1855).

– Angorchis biloba (Lindl.) O. Kuntze, Rev. Gen. Pl. 2: 651 (1891).

– Angorchis campyloplectron (Rchb. f.) O. Kuntze, Rev. Gen. Pl. 2: 651 (1891).

– Listrostachys biloba (Lindl.) Kraenzl., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 22: 28 (1895).

– Rhaphidorhynchus bilobus (Lindl.) Finet, Mem. Soc. Bot. France 54(9): 32 (1907).

– Aerangis campyloplectron (Rchb. f.) Garay, Bot. Mus. Leafl. Harvard Univ. 23(4): 157 (1972).

Stem < 20 cm. Leaves 4–10, < 18 × 3–6 cm, obovate to oblong-obovate. Inflorescence 10–40 (–60) cm, 8–20-flowered. Flowers medium-sized, resupinate, white, often tinged with pink or brown on the pedicel and spur. Dorsal sepal narrowly oblong-lanceolate, acute. Petals narrowly lanceolate to oblong-lanceolate, acute, falcate. Lateral sepals linear-lanceolate to oblong-lanceolate, acute, falcate. Lip 15–25 × 5.3–8 mm, lanceolate to oblong-oblanceolate, cuspidate. Spur (30–) 50–60 (–75) mm, pendant, acute. – Pl. 196, p. 421.

## Distribution

Senegal, Guinea, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Togo, Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon

## Ecology

alt. 0–700 m. Epiphytic on forest edge and thicket, woodland and forest, collected in subsistence cultivation and village trees
