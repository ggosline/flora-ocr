# *Strychnos aculeata* Solereder

**Genus**: [[Strychnos]]
**Family**: [[Loganiaceae]]

## Description

in ENGLER, Bot. Jahrb. 17 : 556 (1893). — FTA 4 (1) : 520. — FWTA ed. 1, 2 : 22. — A. CHEVALIER, Rev. Bot. Appl. 2 : 363 (1947). — FTEA, Loganiaceæ : 20. — FWTA ed. 2, 2 : 43. — LEEUWENBERG, l. c. : 49, fig. 1, carte 1. — SANDBERG, LUNELL & RYRBERG, l. c. : 82 (1969).

— S. mortehani De Wild., Bull. Jard. Bot. Brux. 5 : 50 (1915).

Large vine reaching a height of 40 m and a length of at least 100 m; stem 2.5-22 cm in diameter, spiny; bark pale brown or pale greyish-brown, weakly fissured, not lenticellate; branches not lenticellate, dark green or pale brownish; small branches often furrowed when dry, variably spiny like the stem and large branches (exceptionally some unarmed), glabrous, dark green and glossy when fresh; tendrils in 1-3(-4) pairs.

Leaves with petiole glabrous, 2-12 mm long; limb glossy on both surfaces, coriaceous to thick-papyraceous when dry and equally so when fresh, elliptic, oblong or narrowly elliptic, 1.7-3.5 times as long as wide, (5-8)-18 × (3-3.5-)8 cm and measuring up to 24.5 × 10 cm for shade leaves, apiculate or acuminate at apex, rounded at base or, in apical leaves, cuneate, glabrous on both surfaces; one pair of secondary veins at or nearly at the base, curved along the margin, and often a second pair less distinct submarginal; main veins impressed above and prominent below when fresh.

Inflorescences axillary, 1.5-7 × 0.8-3 cm, compressed, appearing umbellate; peduncle 1-3 times longer than the rest of the inflorescence, glabrous like the branches and pedicels. Flowers pentamerous. Sepals pale green, united for one-third or one-half of their height, broadly orbicular or nearly so, 1.5-1.8 mm long, rounded or obtuse, finely ciliate, glabrous on both surfaces. Corolla, in mature bud, 2.4-5 mm long, white or pale green, glabrous outside, with a brush of stiff hairs inside each lobe; tube 1-2 times as long as the lobes, 1.2-3.2 mm long; lobes thick, ovate to triangular, erect. Stamens included, inserted in the corolla tube at mid-height or somewhat higher, with very short filaments; anther ciliate with strigose hairs all around, or only at the base. Pistil glabrous, 2-2.5 mm long; ovary ovoid, 1.5-2 × 0.8-1.5 mm, 2-locular; stigma subsessile, oblong; numerous ovules per locule.

Fruits large, very hard, like balls, subglobose, laterally compressed, dark green, yellowish-green or sometimes yellow, 10 × 10.5 × 9 to 12 × 12.5 × 11 (-16 × 18.5 × 14.5) cm, and containing (20-)50-90(-145) seeds; wall thick, very hard and ivory-coloured when fresh, somewhat bumpy inside, the thickness varying from 8 to 15 mm, even within a single fruit; pulp cream-coloured, containing saponins. Seeds flattened ± plane-convex, obliquely ovate, elliptic or rhomboid, generally irregularly curved, 20-35 × 16-25 × 4-8 mm, scabrous, with thick erect hairs, very short. Pl. 15, p. 124.

West and equatorial Africa. Scattered in forest; not riparian. Altitude 0-700 m.

Uses: Fruits used to kill fish, pounded and thrown into water.
