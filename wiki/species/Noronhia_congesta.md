# *Noronhia congesta* (Baker) Jongkind, comb. nov. Planche 6

**Genus**: [[Noronhia]]
**Family**: [[Oleaceae]]
**Protologue**: Linociera congesta Baker, Fl. trop. Afr. 4 : 20 (1902)

## Synonyms

- Linociera congesta Baker, Fl. trop. Afr. 4 : 20 (1902).
- Chionanthus mannii (Soler.) Stearn subsp. congesta (Baker) Stearn, Bot. J. Linn. Soc. 80 : 201 (1980).

## Description

Shrub 0.5–2(–4) m tall. Leaf glabrous or nearly so; petiole channelled above, 4–8 mm; blade elliptic to elliptic-oblong, 7–25 × 2.5–8.7 cm, with attenuate base, acuminate over often more than 1 cm at apex; main vein grooved above, prominent below, lateral veins in 6 to 10 pairs looped; domatia absent. Inflorescence axillary, dense, ± 2 cm long; bracts 1–2 mm long, glabrous or sparsely armed with minute appressed hairs. Flower fragrant, subsessile or with short pedicel; calyx with lobes 1–2 mm long and wide, glabrous or with a few scattered appressed hairs; corolla 9–20 mm long, with lobes 1–1.5 mm wide; ovary glabrous. Fruit ellipsoid, 1.5–2 × 0.7–1 cm, glabrous, red.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea (Rio Muni), Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Ngounié, Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo, Ogooué-Maritime, Moyen-Ogooué and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

understory of primary or secondarized forest, limestone cliff, coastal forest, riparian forest, forest margins, mangrove, on sandy soil; in Gabon at 0–1020 m altitude

## Source note

This species is by far the most common Noronhia in Gabon. Stearn (1980) had reduced N. congesta as a subspecies of N. mannii, stating that the characters overlapped. In our view, he had reached this conclusion because he had included in his concept of N. mannii specimens from other species; N. mannii s.s. and N. congesta are well distinct. B: Liben (1973), Stearn (1980). Bibliography
