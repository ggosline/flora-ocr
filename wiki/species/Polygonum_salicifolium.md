# *Polygonum salicifolium* Brouss. ex Willd.

**Family**: [[Polygonaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Polygonum]]

## Description

Slender annual about 1 m. Nearly sessile lanceolate, sometimes falcate leaves are 9 cm or longer and c. 1 cm wide, minutely prickly on midrib and margins; ochrea with long apical bristles. Several loose slender spikes 6–8 cm occur per stem. Pink flowers have a 2–3 mm 5-lobed perianth, 6–8 stamens and usually three styles. Achene normally trigonous, black and glossy.
