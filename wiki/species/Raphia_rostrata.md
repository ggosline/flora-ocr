# *Raphia rostrata* Burret Figure 4G

**Genus**: [[Raphia]]
**Family**: [[Arecaceae]]
**Protologue**: Notizbl. Bot. Gart. Berlin-Dahlem 12 : 307 (1935)

## Description

Cespitose palm with 6 to 10 stems up to 3 m in length, up to 48 cm in diameter, covered with old leaf sheaths and long pendant fibres around the stem (these loops are not as dense as in R. hookeri) and some straight and pointed ones 1 cm in width, black. Leaves 11 to 20; sheath ± 1 m in length; petiole 4.7–13 m; rachis 4–10 m, armed with spines on the upper surface; margins and midrib armed with spines; blade with 95 to 140 pairs of leaflets in irregular arrangement in several planes, 23–155 × 1–4 cm. Inflorescences (3–)4, pendulous, 95–170 cm in length; prophyll 40–70 cm in length; peduncle 15–20 cm, with tubular bracts; rachis 75–160 cm, covered with numerous bracts; first-order flowering branches 31 to 50, 14–41 cm in length, those of the second order, 20 to 37, 10–20 cm in length and > 1 cm in diameter, thick and robust, not breaking easily, longer in basal position than in apical position and directed towards the apex. Male flower: corolla with lobes 8.5–15 × 3.1–5.5 mm; stamens 15 to 20. Female flower: corolla with lobes 13–15 × 6.5–7 mm. Fruit oblong, 6–11 × 4–4.5 cm, with rostrum 1–1.5 cm, with 11 to 12 rows of scales, orange-brown to dark red-brown. Seed oblong, 2–3 cm in diameter.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon and Angola; in Gabon, collected along the Lara River in the Woleu-Ntem. Conservation status: Data Deficient (DD)

## Ecology

Species confined to the margins of rivers with strong currents; between ± 450 and 500 m in altitude

## Vernacular names

esa (beti)

## Source note

R. rostrata is very rarely collected in Gabon and often confused with R. hookeri. It is recognizable by its presence along rivers with strong currents, its cespitose stem covered with a network of pendant fibres, the very rounded aspect of its crown composed of numerous leaves (11 to 20), its robust second-order flowering branches and its fruit generally rostrate.
