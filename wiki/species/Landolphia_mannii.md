# *Landolphia mannii* Dyer ex Dewèvre Planche 59

**Genus**: [[Landolphia]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Ann. Soc. Sci. Bruxelles 19(2) : 148 (1895)

## Description

Ann. Soc. Sci. Bruxelles 19(2): 148 (1895). Liana; stem able to reach 100 m; branchlet glabrous or with pilose hairs often brown-orange. Leaf: petiole 4–13 mm; blade narrowly ovate to ovate or obovate, 1.2–4.3 times as long as wide 2.5–25 × 2–8.5 cm, generally rounded or acute at the base, ± distinctly acuminate at the apex, glabrous or weakly pilose on the midvein and secondary nerves up to tomentose and violet when young; secondary nerves 6 to 18 pairs, tertiary venation reticulate, clearly visible on both faces. Inflorescence terminal, 2–5 cm long, or sometimes in whorls and longer, to flowers multiple; peduncle reaching mm pilose to glabrous.
