# *Zeuxine stammleri* Schltr.

**Genus**: [[Zeuxine]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 38 : 151 (1906)

## Description

– TYPE : Stammler s.n. « anno 1900 », Cameroon, holo-B del. – Annet 349, Cameroon, neo-P ! (designated here) ; FAC 1 : 19 (1984) ; FCAM 1 : 254 (1998).

– Hetaeria stammleri (Schltr.) Summerh., Kew Bull.: ? 208 (1934).

Stem 14-40 cm, erect, fragile, glandular above the leaves clustered near the base, 3-8 × 1-3.5 cm, ovate to elliptic, asymmetric, acute. Inflorescence 2.5-10 cm, < 25-flowered. Perianth pink-greenish and white. Sepals with scattered glands on the outer side; dorsal sepal ovate to oblong-ovate, subobtuse; lateral sepals obliquely ovate, subacute. Petals: lower part linear, upper part oblong-elliptic, obtuse. Labellum sessile, contracted near the apex: lower part deeply cochleate, distinctly thickened toward the apex,

PL. 30. – Zeuxine stammleri Schltr.: A, B, labellum ; C, detail of the hypochile apex viewed from below ; D, dorsal sepal ; E, petal ; F, lateral sepal ; G, leaf (A, Farron 4492, P; B-F, Hallé 4175, P).

inner side with fleshy projections near the base, overall oblong-ovate form, margins of the base involute, those of the apical 1/2 very revolute; epichile transversely rhombic. – Pl. 30, p. 69.
