# *Angylocalyx vermeulenii* De Wild.

**Genus**: [[Angylocalyx]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Ann. Mus. Congo Belge, Bot. sér. 5, 1 : 251 (1906)

## Description

A. schumannianus Harms var. vermeulenii (De Wild.) Yakovl., Adansonia sér. 2, 8(3): 331 (1968). Shrub 3–5 m tall. Leaf: petiole 4–13 cm; rachis 7–15 cm; leaflets 5 to 7, with ovate blade, 12–25 × 8–12 cm, cuneate at base, with long acuminate apex, thick and subcoriaceous; lateral veins ± 5 pairs, sometimes with less prominent veins between them. Inflorescence cauline, with axis 1–3 cm, velutinous. Flower: pedicel 8–10 mm; receptacle cylindric, 5 mm long (subsequently enlarging to 7–9 mm), gibbous; calyx with cupule 4–6 mm long, forming an angle of nearly 90° with the receptacle; petals white, tinged with mauve, or violet, standard oval to subcircular, 12 mm in diameter, with claw 8 mm, wings narrowly lanceolate, 17 × 3 mm, keel 17 × 2.5 mm; stamens with filament 8–10 mm; ovary shortly stipitate, ± 4 mm long, densely silky or rarely glabrous, with 8 to 10 ovules. Legume torulose, up to 20 × 1.5 cm, shortly pubescent, with dry pericarp, narrowed at base, acute at apex. Seed oblong, up to 2.5 × 0.8 cm.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, known from Haut-Ogooué, Moyen-Ogooué, Ngounié, Nyanga and Ogooué-Ivindo

## Ecology

gallery forest; at 80–330 m altitude. B: Pellegrin (1949), Povydysh et al. (2010), Toussaint (1953), Yakovlev (1968). Bibliography
