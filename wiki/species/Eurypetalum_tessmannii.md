# *Eurypetalum tessmannii* Harms

**Genus**: [[Eurypetalum]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Harms, in Engl. Bot. Jahrb. 45 : 295 (1910); Engl. Vég. Erde 9, Pflanz. Afr. 3,1 : 437 fig. 240

## Description

Small tree. Species very close to E. Batesii and perhaps identical. The apex is more briefly and sometimes obtusely acuminate. One large white petal 6-7 × 12-13 mm, and 4 small ones approximately 1 mm.

## Type

Tessmann 978, Guinée espagnole

## Specimens examined

Le Testu : 2236, Ndougou, Ngounié (fl. oct.); 7657, 8537, Lastoursville (fl. nov.).
