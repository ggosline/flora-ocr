# *Ottelia ulvifolia* (Planch.) Walp. subsp. ulvifolia Planche 24

**Genus**: [[Ottelia]]
**Family**: [[Hydrocharitaceae]]
**Protologue**: Ann. bot. syst. 2(3) : 510 (1852)

## Description

Ann. bot. syst. 2(3): 510 (1852). Boottia abyssinica Ridl., J. Linn. Soc., Bot. 22: 239 (1886). Ottelia lancifolia A.Rich. var. fluitans Ridl., J. Linn. Soc., Bot. 22: 238 (1886). Ottelia plantaginea Welw. ex Ridl., J. Linn. Soc., Bot. 22: 238 (1886). Ottelia vesiculata Ridl., J. Linn. Soc., Bot. 22: 237 (1886). Ottelia abyssinica (Ridl.) Gürke in Urban & Graebner, Festschr. P. Ascherson: 540 (1904). Ottelia brevifolia Gürke in Urban & Graebner, Festschr. P. Ascherson: 543 (1904). Ottelia latifolia De Wild., Pl. nov. horti then. 1(5): 153, t. 33 (1905). Annual or perennial herb, submerged; stem contracted, cormiform. Leaf: petiole sometimes well-defined and 6–30(–100) cm, sometimes not differentiated from a long-attenuate blade; blade elliptic-ovate to linear-elliptic, (3.5–)8–40(–45) × (1–)2–4.5(–11.5) cm, cuneate to long-attenuate at base, acute to broadly obtuse at apex, thin and often translucent, pale green to brownish-green, often marked with irregular purple streaks, margins undulate, smooth or very finely denticulate; primary veins (5–)7 to 13. Inflorescence: peduncle triquetrous or tetragone, 14–50(–60) cm, smooth, spiralling and retracting after anthesis; spathe surrounding 1 (or 2) flowers, elliptic-oblong to ovate or sometimes almost orbicular, compressed, (15–)25–45(–60) × 4–20(–30) mm, apex most often 2-lobed at anthesis, sometimes deeply cleft at maturity, green becoming brown, unarmed or bearing very small teeth, with 2 undulate wings reaching 4 mm in width. Hermaphrodite flower, emerging just above water; sepals narrowly elliptic to linear, 8–15(–20) × (1.5–)2–3(–4) mm, translucent, pale green to pale pinkish-brown, apex darker; petals obovate, (10–)12–22(–30) × 8–10 mm, yellow; stamens (3–)6, 4–8 mm in length, anthers oblong, yellow; ovary narrowly ellipsoid, pale green; styles (3–)6, yellow-orange. Fruit ovoid to cylindric, 20–40 × ± 12 mm, with irregular dehiscence; hypanthium exsert 3–5(–10) mm above spathe. Seed oblong to fusiform, 2–2.5 × 0.5–0.7 mm; testa dark brown.

## Distribution

From Senegal to Ethiopia, in East and southern Africa, to South Africa and Madagascar; distribution in Gabon unknown (see Notes below)

distribution area narrower than that of subsp. ulvifolia and limited to the rainforest regions of Cameroon, Central African Republic, Equatorial Guinea (Bioko), Gabon, Democratic Republic of Congo and Angola; in Gabon, known with certainty from Ngounié, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo and Woleu-Ntem; probably also present in Nyanga (see Notes under the typical subspecies)

## Source note

Populations from savanna regions have yellow flowers and belong to subsp. ulvifolia, those from forest regions or certain biotopes with acidic waters in savanna regions have white or white petals with yellow base and belong to subsp. lancifolia (see below). If the colour of petals was not mentioned on the herbarium label by the collector, it is impossible to assign the material to one of the two subspecies. Of the 13 specimens studied, flower colour is mentioned only five times and it is always white. The presence of subsp. ulvifolia in Gabon thus remains to be confirmed. Two collections from the Médouneu region (Woleu-Ntem), Jeffrey 149 and 195, represent plants smaller than O. ulvifolia with floating(?) leaves, and could represent a new taxon (subspecies or species), but more material is necessary to make a decision on their taxonomic status.
