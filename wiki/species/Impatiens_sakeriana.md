# *Impatiens sakeriana* Hook. f.

**Family**: [[Balsaminaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Impatiens]]

## Description

Wet-place herb distinguished by whorled leaves and pedunculate racemes of red flowers. Lip funnel-shaped, over 15 mm, with scarcely oblique prognathous opening; spur short, swollen and abruptly bent at the base; wings narrow.

## Distribution

Cameroon and Gabon.
