# *Heteranthocia guineensis* (Franch.) Robyns.

**Genus**: [[Heteranthoecia]]
**Family**: [[Gramineae]]

## Synonyms

- H. isachnoides Stapf.
- Dinebra guineensis Franch.
- D. tuēnsis Vanderyst.

## Description

Culms slender, rooted at the lower nodes, more or less branched and erect up to approximately thirty centimetres tall. Leaves lanceolate, 12-20 × 2-5 mm, glabrous or slightly pubescent with tuberculate hairs at the base.

Panicle 2 to 7 cm long, generally with about ten secondary branches, the longest ones, towards the base, approximately fifteen millimetres. Axis of the racemes terminated by a naked point, approximately 1/2 to 2/3 of a millimetre wide. Spikelets approximately 2 mm, glumes 1 mm or slightly more, lower flower the size of the spikelet, the upper one measuring approximately half.

Small plant found in dense patches in marshy habitats, devoid of practical interest.

## Specimens examined

Griffon du Bellay : sables de Bouando, recouverts d'eau.
