# *Cyanotis arachnoidea* C.B.Clarke

**Genus**: [[Cyanotis]]
**Family**: [[Commelinaceae]]
**Protologue**: C. lanata Benth. var. lanuginosa K.Schum., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 36 : 209 (1905)

## Description

in A.DC. & C.DC., Monogr. phan. 3 : 250 (1881). C. lanata Benth. var. lanuginosa K.Schum., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 36 : 209 (1905). Perennial herb, with dense white arachnoid pubescence; stem decumbent or prostrate, internodes fairly thick, covered by the sheaths; fibrous roots at the base of the plant and more rarely at the nodes. Basal leaves arranged in a rosette then generally more than 10 distichous leaves on the flowering stem; sheath 1(–1.5) cm long; blade conduplicate or flat, that of basal leaves narrowly elliptic-ovate, 3–7.5 × 0.5–1.2 cm, with acute apex, sometimes mucronate, margins densely ciliate, that of cauline leaves narrowly elliptic-ovate to ovate, 1–4 × 0.4–1.4 cm, rounded base, acute to acuminate apex, generally mucronate, margins ciliate, at least towards the base, lower surface more densely pubescent than upper surface. Inflorescences: flowering stems several per plant, from the periphery of the rosette, ascending or decumbent, 6–25 cm long; terminal and axillary inflorescences, on 1 to 5(–8) terminal nodes; cincinni sessile or stipitate, solitary or sometimes paired, 0.5–1.2(–1.5) × 0.7–1.7 cm; floral bracts of same length as inflorescences or slightly longer, often strongly reflexed at apex, covered with same pubescence as leaves; bracteoles biseriately arranged, 3 to 5 for each series, ovate to narrowly elliptic-ovate, falcate, 5–9(–11) × 1–3(–3.5) mm, acuminate apex, margins ciliate, with pubescence identical to cauline leaves. Flower: sepals 5–6.5 mm long, fused at base for 1.8–3.5 mm, lobes ovate to subulate, 2–4 × 1.5–1.8 mm, keeled, acute apex, with dense arachnoid pubescence; corolla 7–9 mm long, tube 5–6 mm long, lobes broadly ovate, 2–3 × 3–3.5 mm, with rounded apex, blue, purple, mauve; stamens: filaments free, 8–9 mm, densely bearded for 3–3.5 mm, with a terminal thickening, anther 0.8–1.1 mm long; ovary oblong-ellipsoid, 1–1.4 mm long, with dense appressed pubescence; style 7–8 mm, densely bearded at apex below a glabrous terminal thickening, stigma small. Fruit oblong-ellipsoid, 2.5–3 × 1.6–2 mm, light or medium brown, sometimes with darker spots, with dense white pubescence at apex; locules with 1 or 2 seeds. Seed ovoid to ellipsoid, (1.1–)1.4–2(–2.2) × 0.8–1.3 mm; testa uniformly brown or with sometimes lighter, raised and slightly and irregularly tuberculate striae.

## Distribution

Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, Angola and Zimbabwe; also in India, Thailand and China; in Gabon in the Ogooué-Ivindo and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

rocks, cliffs, in full sun; in Gabon from 300 to 750 m altitude, from 1500 to 2600 m in East Africa, elsewhere from 400 to 1830 m. Flowering throughout the year, except March and November

## Source note

C. arachnoidea is a highly variable species, also known from India from where the type specimen comes. Collection sites in East Africa are at altitudes above 1520 m. Specimens collected in Gabon were from notably lower altitudes, for this reason they could represent a different and not yet described species. Lacking sufficient collections, we provisionally retain the name C. arachnoidea. B: Brenan (1968b), Cabezas et al. (2014), Faden (2012), Sosef et al. (2006), Vandeweghe et al. (2016)
