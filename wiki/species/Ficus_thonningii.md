Shrubs or epiphytic trees (stranglers) up to 15 (30 or more ?) m tall, often heavily branched. Leafy twigs 1.4-7 mm thick, glabrous except on the stipule scars or sparsely to densely white to brown pubescent with ± wrinkled hairs, grey-brown, reddish or blackish when dry, periderm mostly persistent. Leaves arranged in spirals, sometimes tending to be opposite. Blade oblong to elliptic or lanceolate, widest at the middle or above, 3-12 (-18) cm long, (1-) 1.5-6 (-7) cm wide, subcoriaceous, brown to grey when dry, mostly horizontal or with the margin slightly revolute, or in young leaves sometimes folded or with the margin involute; apex shortly acuminate to subacute to obtuse or rounded, base subacute to rounded, sometimes subcordate in large leaves, margin entire; both surfaces glabrous or the lower surface with short pubescence, principally on the main veins or on the midvein; veins coarsely prominent to plane, ± prominent below; 5-12 (-16) pairs of lateral veins, including 1-2 pairs of basal veins; glandular dot normally visible. Petiole 0.4-4 (-6) cm long, about 1-2 mm thick, glabrous. Stipules free, 0.3-1 (-1.5) cm long, white to brownish (shortly) pubescent, sometimes only in the middle, stipules always ciliolate, caducous or occasionally subpersistent.

Figs usually in pairs, sometimes below the leaves, and then sometimes (crowded) on old branches, sessile or pedunculate; peduncle up to 1 cm long, about 1 mm thick, sparsely puberulent to white pubescent; basal bracts 2-4 (-split) basally fused, broadly ovate to semicircular, about 3-4 mm long, often ± split, sparsely to densely white to yellowish puberulent to pubescent; receptacle (sub)globose, sometimes ellipsoid, 0.4-0.8 mm diameter when fresh, 0.3-0.8 mm when dry, almost glabrous to densely puberulent or pubescent, yellowish, reddish or brownish at maturity, often with paler spots, wall thin, often thickening towards the apex, ostiole two-lipped, sometimes open, especially if the apex is somewhat umbilicate.

Pistillate flowers with a perianth about 1 mm long, (bi)tri(tetra)-partite; seed flowers sessile, style about 1.5 mm long; gall flowers pedicellate up to 1 mm, style 0.5 mm long. Fruits ellipsoid to subglobose, about 1-1.5 mm long; gall fruits ellipsoid to subglobose, about 1.5-2 mm long. Staminate flowers with pedicel reaching 1 mm, perianth 1-1.2 mm long, tripartite, filament very short to almost absent, anther about 1 mm long. Interfloral bracts narrow, about 1.5 mm long. - Pl. 46, p. 163.

Pollinators: Elisabethiella stuckenbergi Grandi (South Africa, Zambia, Zimbabwe-Rhodesia) ; Alfonsiella brongersmai Wiebes (Zambia, Zimbabwe-Rhodesia) ; Alfonsiella longiscapa Joseph (Zambia).

The species extends from the Cape Verde Islands, across Cameroon and Gabon to Ethiopia and the Republic of South Africa, also in Fernando Po, São Tomé, Annobon and Príncipe; usually as shrubs or small trees in (wooded) savanna regions or as large trees in mountain forests up to an altitude of approximately 2150 m.
