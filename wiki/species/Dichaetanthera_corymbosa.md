# *Dichaetanthera corymbosa* (Cogniaux) Jacques-Félix

**Genus**: [[Dichaetanthera]]
**Family**: [[Melastomataceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. bot. Fr. 102 : 38 (1955); Fl. Cam. 24 : 56 (1983)

## Synonyms

- Barbeyastrum corymbosum Cogn., Mon. Phan. 7, Melast. : 376 (1891).

## Description

Shrubs or small trees up to approximately 10 m; all young parts, including the hypanthium, densely scabrous to setulose; branches 4-angulate-obtuse, setae longer on slightly thickened nodes.

Leaves coriaceous, with rounded-canaliculate petiole, 1–2.5 cm long, densely scabrous to strigose; blade elliptic, base rounded and sometimes narrowly cordate, apex obtuse, rarely lanceolate, varying 4–8 × 9–13 cm; hairs with thick base and fine pointed tip bristly above; longer below, especially on the veins; 3 prominent veins below ± 2 submarginal more slender ones, transverse venation parallel, visible only below; margins entire but scaberulous.

Panicles grouped into a terminal head on the branches and their branchlets; principal axillary nodes with normal leaves; bracts naviculate, ± 3 × 4 mm, early caducous; rachis, bracts and pedicels densely strigose. Flowers with pedicel 2 mm; hypanthium ± 6 × 8 mm, regularly strigose; calyx waved by the broadly triangular, obtuse or rounded lobes, spreading after anthesis. Corolla large; petals dark red, obovate, 9 × 14 mm. Stamens slightly unequal; outer ones with anther 11 mm; connective stalk 3.5 mm, anterior appendages 3.5 mm, linear, apex swollen then acute; filament ± 12 mm; inner ones with anther 10 mm; connective stalk ± 3 mm, appendages 3 mm; filament ± 11 mm. Ovary apex strigose; style 25 mm long.

Capsule ellipsoid, 8 × 12 mm; ovary truncate-grooved, not prominent; peristyle collar 4-lobed, setulose, variously developed, sometimes thick and short, sometimes the 4 lobes more developed and bifid, erect or spreading. Seeds 2 mm in total length, papillose; lateral expansion of raphe, short funicle.

## Type

Brazza J. de 45, Congo (P)

## Discussion

Heliophilous species of fresh to marshy soils at moderate altitude, absent from large forest massifs; occurs up to Uganda.

## Specimens examined

Le Testu 7081, 7361, Lastourville (mars, mai).
