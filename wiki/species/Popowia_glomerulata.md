# *Popowia glomerulata* Le Thomas

**Genus**: [[Popowia]]
**Family**: [[Annonaceae]]
**Protologue**: Ada nsonia,ser. 2,3 : 287-89,tab.1, fig. 8-12 (1963)

## Description

Liane with puberulent branchlets, branchlets tomentose brownish-red. Internodes 1.5-3 cm long. Leaves with thick petiole, 0.5-0.7 cm long, tomentose brownish-red; blade subcoriaceous, elliptic-oblong to obovate-oblong, 11-20 cm long, 4.3-7.5 cm wide, subrounded at base, obtusely attenuate or very shortly acuminate at apex; upper surface glabrous except at base of midvein, lower surface pubescent with short, silky and appressed hairs. Midvein prominent beneath. Lateral veins 11-14 pairs, ascending, terminating in arcs near the margin; venules well visible and parallel to each other.

Inflorescences cauliflorous, arising on branchlets of several years old, in dense glomerules, 2.5-3.5 cm in diameter. Flowers with thick and short pedicel, 0.7-1.2 cm long, tomentose, bearing one or two very small triangular-ovate bracteoles, pubescent externally. Sepals broadly triangular-ovate, 2.5-3 mm long, 3-4 mm wide, tomentose externally. External petals fleshy, concave, broadly ovate, 5-6 mm long, 6-7.5 mm wide, tomentose externally, puberulent internally; internal petals much smaller, obovate, narrowed at base, 2 mm long, 0.7 mm wide, puberulent on both surfaces. Carpels very numerous (80-95), 2 mm long, approximately 0.4 mm wide, ovaries ellipsoid-oblong, villous, stigmas linear. Ovules 6-7 lateral.

Flowers and fruits unknown.

TYPE AND ONLY MATERIAL STUDIED:

Le Testu 8700, Lastoursville region, fl. March (holotype P!; isotype BM!).

This species known only from Gabon is very close to P. cauliflora Chipp, by the form and pubescence of its leaves, its cauliflorous flowers, but it differs essentially from it by its inflorescences in glomerules.

Liane of dense humid forest.
