# *Popowia glomerulata* Le Thomas

**Genus**: [[Popowia]]
**Family**: [[Annonaceae]]
**Protologue**: Ada nsonia,ser. 2,3 : 287-89,tab.1, fig. 8-12 (1963)

## Synonyms

- Ada nsonia,ser. 2,3 : 287-89,tab.1, fig. 8-12 (1963).

## Description

Liane with pubérulent branchlets, branchlets tomentose brown-russet. Internodes 1.5–3 cm long. Leaves with thick petiole, 0.5–0.7 cm long, brown-russet tomentose; blade subcoriaceous, elliptic-oblong to obovate-oblong, 11–20 cm long, 4.3–7.5 cm wide, subrounded at the base, obtusely attenuate or very shortly acuminate at the apex; upper surface glabrous except at the base of the midvein, lower surface pubescent with short, silky, appressed hairs. Midvein prominent beneath. Lateral veins 11–14 pairs, ascending, ending in arches near the margin; veinlets well-visible and parallel to each other.

Inflorescences cauline, originating on branchlets of several years, in dense glomerules, 2.5–3.5 cm in diameter. Flowers with thick and short pedicel, 0.7–1.2 cm long, tomentose, furnished with one or two very small triangular-ovate bracteoles, pubescent externally. Sepals broadly triangular-ovate, 2.5–3 mm long, 3–4 mm wide, tomentose externally. External petals fleshy, concave, broadly ovate, 5–6 mm long, 6–7.5 mm wide, tomentose externally, pubérulent internally; internal petals much smaller, obovate, narrowed at the base, 2 mm long, 0.7 mm wide, pubérulent on both surfaces. Carpels very numerous (80–95), 2 mm long, approximately 0.4 mm wide, ovaries ellipsoid-oblong, hairy, stigmas linear. Ovules 6–7 lateral.

Fruits and flowers unknown.

TYPE AND SOLE MATERIAL STUDIED:

Le Testu 8700, région de Lastoursville, fl. March (holotype P!; isotype BM!).

This species known only from Gabon, is very close to P. cauliflora Chipp. by the form and pubescence of its leaves, its cauline flowers, but it differs essentially from it by its inflorescences in glomerules.

Liane of dense humid forest.
