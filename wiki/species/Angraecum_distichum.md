# *Angraecum distichum* Lindl.

**Genus**: [[Angraecum]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Edw. Bot. Reg. 21 : tab. 1781 (1836)

## Description

Garay, Kew Bull. 28(3): 507 (1973); Cribb, FTEA, Orchid. 3: 494 (1989); FAC 2: 480 (1992); FCAM 3: 884 (2001).

– Mystacidium distichum (Lindl.) Pfitzer in Engl., Nat. Pflanzenfam. 2(6): 216 (1889).

Stem < 25 cm. Leaves 5–11 × (2.5–) 3–6 (–7) mm, falcate, oblong-elliptic, rounded at apex, with a groove on the upper surface. Inflorescence shortened, 1-flowered. Flower small, pure white with a gynostemium slightly yellowish to greenish. Dorsal sepal oblong-elliptic to elliptic, obtuse. Petals oblong or oblong-elliptic, falcate, obtuse. Lateral sepals oblong-ovate to oblong-elliptic, obtuse. Labellum (1.7–) 3–5 × 2–4 mm, weakly trilobed, cochleate; median lobe triangular, acute, short; lateral lobes rounded, small. Spur (3.2–) 5.5–7 mm, shorter than the pedicel and ovary, straight, narrowly cylindrical with apiculate apex. – Pl. 220, p. 461.

## Distribution

Guinea, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Benin, Nigeria, São Tomé, Príncipe, Cameroon, Gabon, Congo, Central African Republic, Zaire, Rwanda, Uganda, Angola, Mozambique

## Ecology

alt. 800–1,750 m. Epiphyte in semi-deciduous forest with Celtis and Sterculiaceae, in tall wet dense forest, in mangrove swamp, on trunk and branch often covered with moss, 1 to 30 m above ground in shade and high humidity, on branch above water, on Lophira alata
