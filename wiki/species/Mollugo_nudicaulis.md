# *Mollugo nudicaulis* Lamk.

**Family**: [[Aizoaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Mollugo]]

## Description

Glabrous erect annual. Spatulate leaves form a ground rosette, 2–6 × 1–1.5 cm, rounded above and long-attenuate below. Numerous trichotomous scapes to 15 cm end in loose cymes collectively forming an umbel-like inflorescence to 37 cm across. Greenish flowers have five 2–3 mm tepals and included stamens. Ovary 3-locular with six ovules per locule and three stigmas; seeds reniform and black.

## Distribution and ecology

Common on sandy soil; widespread in tropical Africa south to the Transvaal, Arabia, Madagascar and tropical America. Recorded in Gabon.
