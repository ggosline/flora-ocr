# *Acanthus latisepalus* C. B. Clarke

**Genus**: [[Acanthus]]
**Family**: [[Acanthaceae]]
**Protologue**: C. B. Clarke, F.T.A. 5:108 (1899)

## Description

Plant approximately 1 m in height, quite robust, resembling the preceding species, with young shoots and inflorescences pubescent. Petioles 6–7 mm or leaves subsessile. Leaf blade 20–36 × 5.5–10 cm, entire or somewhat sinuous-undulate and of quite variable form, narrowly elongate lanceolate-obovate, obovate or elliptic, very acuminate; acumen approximately 1–2 cm long; 8–9 (11) pairs of lateral veins, pubescent or glabrescent beneath.

Inflorescences terminal, 7–10 cm, with pubescent rachis, obovate bracts 25 × 2 cm with 5 very prominent longitudinal nerves bearing approximately 13 very elongate spines; among these spines fairly long cilia, 1–2 and up to 10 mm long; linear lanceolate bracts 35 × 1–1.5 mm, entire with long cilia in the upper portion; upper calyx segment linear, 44 × 9 mm, with 5 longitudinal nerves which, like the margins, are ciliate above the segment with hairs reaching 2 mm long, rounded at the apex, emarginate to approximately 1 mm depth; lower segment linear, 30 × 4 mm, bearing two very elongate acumens as spines, approximately 9–11 mm, ciliate; lateral segments lanceolate 11–12 × 2–2.5 mm, with spinose apex 7–8 mm long. Corolla 4 cm long, lilac (Thollon 868), densely pubescent within, glabrous without, with lobes more pronounced than in the preceding species; central lobe 4 × 14 mm, rounded; lateral lobes slightly broader but shorter. The other floral characters differ little from the preceding species. Corolla tube 7 mm long. Stamens inserted at the throat on a pilose internal ring; filaments 11 and 12 mm long; anthers 3 mm. Style glabrous, even at the base, 2 cm long.

Capsule approximately 2 cm long.
