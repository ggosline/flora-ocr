# *Bulbophyllum fuscum* Lindl.

**Genus**: [[Bulbophyllum]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Reg. 25 : misc. 3 (1839)

## Description

– Bulbophyllum ogoouense Guillaumein, Bull. Mus. Paris 30 : 522 (1924).

Pseudobulbs 1–5 × 0.4–1.4 cm, somewhat flattened, 2-leaved. Leaves generally shortly petiolate 1.2–5.5 (9) × 0.4–2 cm, broadly elliptic to linear-lanceolate, obtuse. Inflorescence rather dense 4–12 cm, rachis sometimes somewhat flattened, glabrous, 6–20-flowered. Flowers small. Sepals yellow, greenish to dark red or reddish-brown, acute to acuminate, rather thick, glabrous; dorsal sepal ovate-lanceolate; lateral sepals obliquely triangular to elliptic-triangular. Petals yellow or dark red, linear, subfalcate, obtuse, glabrous. Labellum yellow, orange or dark red, elliptic, apex rounded, very thick, rather straight, not recurved, with 2 distinct lateral lobes on the basal 1/2, generally denticulate, thin and curved upwards. – Pl. 88, p. 178.

## Distribution

Guinea, Sierra Leone, Côte d'Ivoire, Nigeria, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Gabon, Angola, Congo (Kinshasa)

## Ecology

alt. 0–1000 m. Epiphytic in lowland and foothill forest, collected at 2 m above ground on branch of Dacryodes edulis
