# *Petchia africana* Leeuwenb. Planche 86 ; Figure 9(C)

**Genus**: [[Petchia]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Wageningen Agric. Univ. Papers 97(2) : 56 (1997)

## Description

Wageningen Agric. Univ. Papers 97(2): 56 (1997). Shrub 0.3–2.5 m tall. Leaves all opposite; petiole 4–10 mm; blade narrowly elliptic to narrowly ovate, 3–7 times as long as wide, 7–15 × 1.5–3 cm, cuneate at base, abruptly caudate with a linear appendage 0–2 mm at the apex; secondary veins 12 to 20 pairs, rather straight, forming various angles with the midvein; tertiary venation reticulate. Inflorescences terminal, solitary or grouped by 2, 6–8 cm, with 3–6 flowers; pedicel 1–2 mm; bracteate like the calyx lobes and sometimes as long as them. Flower: pedicel 4 mm; calyx with lobes 2–3.5 × 0.5–1.5 mm, long acuminate, persistent under the mature fruit; corolla pale yellow, with a pilose band under the insertion of the stamens, 5 mm wide, tube 13–15 mm long, lobes spreading, 6–11 × 3–4 mm, rounded at apex; stamens with apex 1–2 mm below the throat, inserted 9–10 mm from the base of the tube, anther 2.2–3 mm long; pistil with apex ± 2 mm below the base of the anthers. Fruit: follicles 14–27 × 1 cm, torulous to almost moniliform, almost cylindrical, yellow to orange, with a stipe of ± 5.5 cm and a beak of ± 4 cm, with 6 to 11 seeds. Seed ellipsoid, 7.5–8.5 × 2–2.5 mm, winged at both ends. Distribution: Cameroon and Gabon; rare in Gabon, Moyen-Ogooué, Ogooué-Ivindo and Woleu-Ntem.
