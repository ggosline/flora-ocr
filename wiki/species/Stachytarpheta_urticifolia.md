# *Stachytarpheta urticifolia* Sims

**Genus**: [[Stachytarpheta]]
**Family**: [[Verbenaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Mag . 43 : t. 1848 (1816)

## Description

Herb or slender shrub 0.5–2 m tall; stem ± erect, much-branched, glabrous or very sparsely hairy. Leaf: petiole 0.5–2 cm; blade broadly elliptic to ovate, 4–12.5 × 2–7 cm, attenuate at the base, distinctly acute at the apex, margin regularly and strongly dentate, entire at the base, glabrous on both faces, soft to the touch. Inflorescence: spike 14–45 cm long, elongate, slender, glabrous, with numerous flowers; axis ± 2.5 mm in diameter; bracts narrowly elliptic-ovate, 7 × 2 mm, long-acuminate at the apex, green with whitish margins, ± glabrous. Flower: calyx cylindric, 7 × 2 mm, with 5 equal teeth; corolla blue or mauve, sometimes dark violet, with throat paler or white, tube 7–8 mm long, slightly curved, with oblong lobes, 2–3 × 3–5 mm, rounded at the apex; stigma exsert. Fruit: mericarps cylindric, 3–5 × 1 mm, black at maturity, with reticulate ribs.

## Distribution

Native to tropical America and introduced and often naturalized in (sub)tropical Africa, including São Tomé and Príncipe, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Central African Republic and Angola; in Asia and the Pacific; in Gabon: Estuaire, Ngounié, Ogooué-Lolo and Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology

gardens, roadsides, disturbed areas; in Gabon at 5–600 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1200 m. B: Fernandes & Diniz (2005), Verdcourt (1992). i Bibliography
