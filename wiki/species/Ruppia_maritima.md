# *Ruppia maritima* L.

**Family**: [[Ruppiaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Ruppia]]

*Species plantarum* **1**: 127 (1753).

## Synonymy

- *Ruppia rostellata* W.D.J.Koch ex Reichenb., *Iconogr. bot. pl. crit.* **2**: 66, t. 174/306 (1824).
- *Ruppia maritima* subsp. *rostellata* (W.D.J.Koch) Asch. & Graebner, *Syn. mitteleur. Fl.* **1**(4): 357 (1897).

## Description

Submerged herb; stem filiform, to 50 cm and freely branched. Leaf sheath 2–10 cm × 0.4–1.5 mm, flattened; blade filiform, (2–)5–15 cm × 0.2–0.9 mm, margins denticulate near the apex.

Inflorescence a spike of two alternate flowers; peduncle reflexed after anthesis and elongating to 2(–6) cm but not spiralling. Anther thecae subglobose, 0.6–0.7 mm in diameter; carpels 4–7, on stipes elongating in fruit to 0.5–1.5(–3) cm. Fruit obliquely ovoid, 2–3 × 1.5–2 mm, with a rostrum about 0.2 mm obliquely attached at the apex, reddish-brown to almost black; endocarp with a distinct oval window.

## Distribution

Nearly cosmopolitan and present along African coasts. In Gabon it has been observed only at Ngowé Lagoon in Loango National Park, Ogooué-Maritime, but is very probably present elsewhere along the coast.

## Habitat and ecology

Coastal brackish water, including lagoons, and inland salt pools; at sea level in Gabon and to 800 m elsewhere.

## Vernacular names

- Rupelle maritime (French).
- Beaked tassel-pondweed; wigeon grass (English).
- Meeres-Salde (German).

## Notes

Most modern authors treat the extensive morphological variation as continuous within one species rather than recognizing numerous infraspecific taxa. The Gabon record is based on a diagnostic photograph, not an herbarium collection. An earlier ORSTOM report is of uncertain origin.
