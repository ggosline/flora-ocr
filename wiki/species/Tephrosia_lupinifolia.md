# *Tephrosia lupinifolia* DC. Planche 122

**Genus**: [[Tephrosia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Prodr. 2 : 255 (1825)

## Synonyms

- Lupinophyllum lupinifolium (DC.) Hutch., Gen. flow. pl. 2 : 626 (1967).

## Description

Annual or perennial herb, (10–)30–80 cm tall, with yellowish pubescence; stems prostrate to volubile. Stipules linear-subulate, reaching 6 mm. Leaf digitate; petiole 1–10 cm; stipels near the apex of the petiole, subulate, 2–5 mm long; leaflets 3 to 5(–7), with blade linear-elliptic to linear-oblanceolate, reaching 8 × 1.5 cm, apex often twisted, glabrous above, pubescent below; midvein and secondary veins prominent, yellow. Inflorescence with normal flowers in very lax terminal pseudoracemes opposite to the leaves, 25 cm long, some racemes axillary and shorter, with cleistogamous flowers and geocarpic pods; bracts fairly persistent, 1–2 mm long. Flower (normal): pedicel 3–4 mm; calyx with white pubescence, tube 1.5–2.5 mm long, lobes acute, almost equal, the lower one longer than the tube; corolla pink, standard suborbicular, 8–10 mm long, apex emarginate, with white pubescence, wings and keel obovate, 8–10 mm long; stamens with tube 6–7 mm long; ovary ± 9 mm long; style glabrous, stigma capitate. Pod linear, ± straight, reaching 37 × 5 mm, apex curved downward, with 5 to 8 seeds, briefly and loosely hairy, straw-colored; pod from a cleistogamous flower 7–10 × 3–4 mm, and with 1 to 3 seeds. Seed broadly ellipsoid, ± 4 × 2.5 × 1.6 mm, yellowish; hilum almost central.

## Distribution

Tropical Africa, from Senegal to Sudan, Tanzania, Angola and southern Africa; in Gabon, known from Haut-Ogooué, Nyanga and Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology

open savanna, on sandy soil; in Gabon at 15–800 m elevation, elsewhere up to 1400 m. Flowers and fruits in February, May, August and September. B: Brummitt et al. (2007), Burkill (1995), Cronquist (1954), Gillett (1971d), Pellegrin (1949) Bibliography
