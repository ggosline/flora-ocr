# *Amaranthus hybridus* subsp. *cruentus* var. *paniculatus* (L.) Thell.

**Family**: [[Amaranthaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Amaranthus]]

## Description

The Gabon treatment represents *A. hybridus* by this cultivated infraspecific taxon: an erect annual with alternate petiolate leaves and terminal, branched, often reddish spike-like panicles. Small unisexual flowers occur in contracted cymules; the dry one-seeded fruit opens transversely.

## Notes

Older literature frequently misapplied the name *A. caudatus* to this plant.
