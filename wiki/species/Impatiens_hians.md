# *Impatiens hians* Hook. f.

**Family**: [[Balsaminaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Impatiens]]

## Description

Moist-forest herb with alternate leaves and pedunculate red flowers. Lip deep, straight and tube-shaped, over 15 mm long, its opening strongly oblique; spur shorter, abruptly bent at the base. Wings linear-lanceolate and scarcely exceeding the lip opening.

## Distribution

Lower Guinea, including Gabon.
