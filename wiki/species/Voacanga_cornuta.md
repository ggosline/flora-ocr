# *Voacanga cornuta* Jongkind Planche 123

**Genus**: [[Voacanga]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Candollea 77 : 46 (2022)

## Description

Small tree (?); branches glabrous. Leaf elliptic or oblong, 7–18 × 1.5–6 cm, attenuate at base, acuminate at apex, glabrous above, with numerous minute and erect, colourless and discrete hairs below; secondary nerves 7 to 9 pairs, tertiary venation discrete. Inflorescence terminal and in pairs, pauciflorous; peduncle slender, 0–1 cm with minute scattered hairs; bracts reaching 2 mm, glabrous. Flower pedicel 0–1 mm, glabrous or nearly so; calyx 1–1.5 mm long, with scattered hairs inside the tube, lobes ± 5 mm in length and width; corolla with tube ± 12 × 4 mm, the lower half clearly twisted back to the throat, glabrous on the exterior, on the interior with a pubescent band from a few mm above the base to the insertion of the stamens; lobes elliptic, 5–6 × 3.5 mm, rounded, glabrous, forming in bud an almost conical head, 1.5 times as long as wide; stamens included, but apex near the throat, anther subsessile, ± 5 mm long; ovary ± 1 mm in height, glabrous, with free carpels; style ± 5 mm, glabrous. Fruit with free carpels, narrowly ellipsoid, 3–5 × 0.6–1 cm, acute at apex, with numerous seeds, glabrous. Seed obliquely ellipsoid, 4–5 × 2 mm, dark brown, finely tuberculate. Distribution endemic to Gabon, known only from two localities in Woleu-Ntem.

## Ecology

dense evergreen forest understorey; at 500–600 m altitude. B: Jongkind & Lachenaud (2022). BIBLIOGRAPHY
