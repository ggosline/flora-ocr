# *Bulbophyllum oxychilum* Schltr.

**Genus**: [[Bulbophyllum]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 38 : 18 (1905)

## Description

– TYPE: Schlechter 13042, Cameroon, holo-B del. – NEOTYPE: Bos 2336 – Liberia, WAG (chosen by Vermeulen, 1987). – FAC 2: 339 (1992); FCAM 2: 453 (2001).

- Bulbophyllum buntingii Rendle, Cat. Talb. S. Nig. Pl.: 99, 146 (1913).

– Bulbophyllum ellipticum De Wild., Rev. Zool. Afr. 9: suppl. bot. 29 (1921).

Pseudobulbs 0.6–2.3 × 0.5–1.6 cm, ovoid to suborbicular, somewhat flattened, 1-leaved. Leaf petiolate, 1.6–12 × 0.7–2 cm, elliptic to oblong-oblanceolate, obtuse to acute. Inflorescence 6.5–31 cm, fairly dense, rachis glabrous, 15–100-flowered. Flowers small. Sepals yellow to white or entirely pink, acute, to shortly acuminate, thick, glabrous; dorsal sepal oblong-ovate, thick; lateral sepals broadly ovate to ovate-oblong, subequal. Petals white, rhombic or spatulate, acute or obtuse, finely papillose towards the apex. Lip yellow or white, ovate-cordate to hastate, apiculate to acute, thick, glabrous to very finely papillose. – Pl. 100, p. 201.

## Distribution

Liberia, Ivory Coast, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Central African Republic, Congo (Kinshasa), Uganda

## Ecology

alt. 0–500 m. Epiphytic on branch and tree trunk; lowland and hilly forest, relict forest in savanna, collected on Euphorbia
