# *Trichoscypha imbricata* Engl. Planche 21(E)

**Genus**: [[Trichoscypha]]
**Family**: [[Anacardiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 1 : 426 (1881)

## Description

Shrub, often lianescent; branchlets puberulent, with appressed hairs. Leaf up to 20 cm in length; petiole 1.5–7 cm, puberulent; rachis puberulent; leaflets (1–)3 to 7(–9), petiolules 2–10 mm, with elliptic blade, 5–11(–23) × 4–10 cm, rounded at base, acuminate over 0.5–1.5 cm at apex, coriaceous, glabrous and finely pustulous above and generally hairy on the impressed midvein, glabrous to sparsely puberulent below, with 6 to 9 pairs of lateral veins. Inflorescence in narrow panicle, puberulent, with flowers grouped in small compact glomérules. Flowers (sub)sessile, 4-merous. Male flower: calyx 1–1.5 mm in length, puberulent; petals slightly imbricate, ± erect, elliptic, 1–1.5 mm in length, glabrous; stamens as long as or slightly longer than the petals, glabrous, with anthers 0.5 mm in length; disk velvety; pistillode absent. Female flower: calyx 1.5 mm in length, puberulent; petals erect, ovate-elliptic, 2.5 mm in length, glabrous; staminodes slightly shorter than the petals; disk velvety; ovary 2 mm in length, with short, appressed hairs; stigmas 4, shortly (≤ 0.5 mm) stipitate. Fruit subglobose to ellipsoid, ± 1.5–2.5 × 1.2–1.5 cm, with short, scattered and appressed hairs, dark red at maturity; mesocarp juicy. Seed: cotyledons white in section.

## Distribution

Coastal species, endemic to Gabon, known from Estuaire, Ogooué-Maritime and Nyanga

## Ecology

on sand near the sea; at 0–10 m elevation. B: Breteler (2004). Bibliography
