## Description

Engl., Monogr. Afr. Pflanzenf. 6: 73 (1901). – Pellegr., Bull. Soc. Bot. Fr. 94: 256 (1947). – Tisserant et Sillans, Not. Syst. 15.3: 330 (1958). – R.E. Fries in Engl. et Prantl, Nat. Pflanzenf. ed. 2, 17 A.2: 123 (1959). – Le Thomas, Adansonia, ser. 2, 5.3: 447 (1963).

Liane with glabrous branchlets, green when young and fresh, with short pale green longitudinal lines. Branchlets sparsely appressed-pubescent, russet. Leaves with petiole 3–5 mm long, appressed-pubescent to glabrous, channelled above; blade coriaceous or subcoriaceous, elliptic-oblong, 7.5–10 cm long, 2.5–6 cm wide, with rounded to cuneate base, attenuate or acuminate apex; upper surface glabrous and shining, lower surface glabrous except for the appressed-pubescent midrib. 10–12 pairs of lateral veins, oblique, ascending, united in loops near the margin.

Inflorescences few-flowered, with peduncle curved like a hook, 1.5–2 cm long, sparsely appressed-pubescent. Flowers with pedicel 7–10 mm long, appressed-pubescent. Sepals triangular-acute, 3–5 mm long, 2–3 mm wide, appressed-pubescent on the outside. Petals initially white or pale pinkish-green, becoming yellow-orange, reddish at the base, tomentose on both faces; outer ones ovate-lanceolate, 15–30 mm long, 4–9 mm wide, with rounded and concave base, attenuate at the apex; inner ones lanceolate-linear, narrower than the outer ones, almost as long, narrowed towards the base which is very thickened and concave, recurved in its upper part over the reproductive organs. Numerous subsessile stamens, rectangular or cuneiform, about 1 mm long, with extrorse thecae and truncate connectives, widened above the anthers. Carpels 8–10, ovoid, about 1.5 mm long, 0.5 mm wide, styles cylindric, puberulent, curved outward. Receptacle hirsute. 2 basal ovules.

Fruits red at maturity, with pedicel 7–13 mm long; mericarps few, ellipsoid-oblong, 15–20 mm long, 7–13 mm wide, rounded at the ends.

## Discussion

Liane of rainforest, islands, waterfalls, rocky thickets in rivers, along banks, with Uapaca Heudelotii and Cathormion altissimum.

This species, fairly abundant in humid soils, is distributed throughout the equatorial forest, in Gabon, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Congo-Kinshasa and Congo-Brazzaville.
