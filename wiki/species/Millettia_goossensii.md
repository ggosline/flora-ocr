# *Millettia goossensii* (Hauman) Polhill

**Genus**: [[Millettia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull. 25 : 260 (1971)

## Synonyms

- Lonchocarpus goossensii Hauman, Bull. Jard. Bot. État Bruxelles 24 : 223 (1954).

## Description

Shrub, up to 4 m high; branchlets glabrous. Stipules not peltate, triangular-ovate, 2–4 mm long, finely striate. Leaf: petiole reaching 5 cm; rachis up to 18 cm; stipels subulate, 0.5–3 mm; leaflets 5 to 9, with petiolules up to 5 mm, limb oval, the lateral ones 3.5–8.5 × 2.5–4.3 cm, the terminal reaching 15 × 7 cm, base rounded to subcordate, apex acuminate, glabrous, coriaceous; lateral nerves in 9 to 14 pairs, scarcely more prominent than the nervules. Inflorescence axillary, in pendulous and simple pseudoracemes, 7–16 cm long; bracts persistent, triangular, ± 1 mm long. Flower: pedicel slender, ± 4 mm; bracteoles very caducous; calyx ± 6 mm long, margin scarcely undulate, with short appressed hairs; corolla yellow, spotted with violet at the base, standard obovate, ± 16 × 8 mm of which 4 mm for the claw, apex emarginate, wings 17 mm long of which 5 for the claw, keel at the base furnished with a very pronounced diverticulum; stamens 17 mm long, the vexillary one free; ovary with 4 ovules, with white appressed hairs; style 6 mm, glabrous. Young pod narrowly lanceolate, 18.5 × 1.6 cm, apex acute, very finely puberulous. Mature seed not known.

## Distribution

Western Democratic Republic of the Congo and Gabon; in Gabon, known only from Ogooué-Lolo

## Ecology

primary forest on ridge, secondary forest; in Gabon, at approximately 550 m altitude, elsewhere, at low altitude

## Source note

M. goossensii is very close to M. griffoniana and it has only recently been discovered in Gabon. Bibliography: Hauman (1954a).
