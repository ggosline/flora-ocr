# *Millettia dinklagei* Harms

**Genus**: [[Millettia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 26 : 287 (1899)

## Description

Liana or lianescent shrub with stem reaching up to 12 m, sometimes a tree; branchlets appressed-pubescent, brown, with concolorous lenticels. Stipules caducous. Leaf 15–25 cm in length; petiole 2–5 cm, canaliculate above, pubescent, pulvinus 4–10 mm; rachis 5–12 cm, velutinous; stipels absent; leaflets (7–)9 to 11, petiolule 3–5 mm, yellowish, velutinous, canaliculate above, blade elliptic to ovate or obovate, 5.5–11 × 1.5–4 cm, base cuneate, rounded to subcordate, apex acute to acuminate, with acumen of ± 1.5 cm, glabrous above, densely silvery-silky and glossy below; lateral veins in 8 to 12(–16) pairs, almost plane above, prominent below. Inflorescence axillary or terminal, in panicle, (2.2–)10–24 cm in length, densely brown-yellow tomentose; peduncle 2–8 cm; bracts often persistent, ovate, densely brown-velutinous. Flower: pedicel 1.5–2 mm, densely velutinous; bracteoles at the base of the calyx, ovate to suborbicular, ± 1 × 1 mm; calyx with tube 3–4 mm in length, upper lobes 2–3 × 1.5 mm, lower lobes 3.5–5 × 1.5–2 mm, brown-yellowish, velutinous golden-glossy; corolla white to pale yellow, standard elliptic 9–11 × 6–7 mm, apex emarginate, auricles minute, callositites not prominent, with claw of ± 2 mm, wings with elliptic blade, 10–11 × 2 mm, lateral pocket present, exterior sculptured, with claw of 3–3.5 mm, keel elliptic-falciform, 10 × 4.5 mm, with claw of 3 mm; stamens: staminal tube 8–9 mm in length, free part 3–4 mm in length; ovary 3–4 mm in length, brown to yellow tomentose, stipe ± 0.5 mm, with 3 or 4 ovules; style 10–11 mm, flattened, curved part glabrous, stigma capitate. Pod obovate-oblong, flattened, 6–7.6 × 2–2.5 cm, base attenuate to obtuse, apex rostrate, with 2 to 4 seeds, chocolate brown, with dense brown-yellowish to rust-tomentose pubescence. Seed reniform, 13–15 × 7–9 mm, surface dull or sometimes punctate; hilum 1.8–2 mm.

## Distribution

Guinea, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Ivory Coast, Equatorial Guinea (Rio Muni) and Gabon; in Gabon, known from Estuaire and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

primary forest, swamp forest, forest edges, coastal scrub; in Gabon at low altitude, elsewhere up to 1325 m. Fruits in August. b: Hepper (1958). Bibliography
