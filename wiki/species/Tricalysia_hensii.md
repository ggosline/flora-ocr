# *Tricalysia hensii* De Wild.

**Genus**: [[Tricalysia]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Protologue**: E. De Wildeman, Ann Mus. Congo, 5, 2 : 167 (1907)

## Description

Shrub reaching 4–5 m in height. This species is very close to the preceding one, differing from it by its stipules somewhat more elongately attenuate reaching 3–7 mm, by its leaves somewhat smaller with a more distinctly acute base and a more distinctly acuminate apex. The secondary veins are 5–7 pairs in number; there are pilose axillary domatia.

Inflorescences similar; flowers somewhat smaller; bud more narrowly acute with apex somewhat pubescent. Placentas 3–6 ovulate.

Fruits similar, red at maturity.

## Type

Habitat: riparian forests of the Congo and the Ogooué.
