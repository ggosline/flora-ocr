# *Samanea saman* (Jacquin) Merrill

**Genus**: [[Samanea]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Journ. Wash. Acad. Sci. 6 (1): 47 (1916); KEAY, FWTA, ed. 2, 1 (2): 504 (1958);

## Description

Tree reaching a height of 25 m and a diameter of 35 cm. Crown ample and dense. Bole short, tortuous, with stout branches from the base. Bark greyish-brown, with rhytidome peeling in long fibrous bands, deeply fissured, with pink to yellowish section and fibrous toward the interior, exuding brown gum. Branchlets shortly velvety with brown or yellow hairs, finely wrinkled longitudinally. Petiole velvety to subglabrous, 4.5-5.5 cm long, with 2 lateral longitudinal sulci very marked on young leaves; groove of the upper surface very distinct on young leaves; prominent gland at the base. Rachis pubescent velvety, 5.5-9 cm long, upper surface plane or with prominent crest; lateral longitudinal sulci distinct on young leaves; prominent gland always present at the insertion of the first pair of pinnae; present or absent for the others. Pinnae 5 pairs, obtriangular. Axis of the pinna like the rachis, increasing in size from base to apex. Leaflets 3-7 pairs, decreasing in size from apex to base, petiolulate. Petiolule pubescent, 0.5-1 mm long. Blade shortly finely pubescent above and densely pubescent velvety below; more or less shiny brown above and yellowish-brown below when dry; broadly elliptic to lanceolate (basal leaflets), more or less distinctly oblong to falcate (middle leaflets), asymmetrically elliptic (apical leaflets), reaching 3.5 × 5 cm, base asymmetric: proximal margin rounded and distal margin attenuate to attenuate-acute; apex obtuse to rounded retuse more or less mucronate. Principal vein rectilinear to subarched, arched for apical leaflets; prominent or not above; strongly prominent pubescent below. Secondary veins ascending, scarcely or not arched, prominent or not above, prominent pubescent below. Veinlets in moderately dense network prominent on both faces.

Inflorescences in short capitate spikes, axillary or terminal, solitary or fascicled. Floral peduncle pubescent, sulcate, 1.5-4 cm long. Bracteole pubescent tomentose externally and pubescent internally except at the base, lanceolate to asymmetric oblanceolate, approximately 4 mm long. Flowers ♂, pentamerous, sessile, 2 types. Basal flower with obconic calyx, densely pubescent tomentellous externally, 5.5 mm long. Corolla with petals united into a tube, 6-8 mm long, free lobes pubescent externally on the median keel, 1 mm long. Stamens numerous, 2.8 cm long, united into a tube 6 mm long not exsert, anther ellipsoid, 0.25 mm long. Pistil obscurely stipitate, ovary glabrous or pubescent toward the apex, very narrowly ellipsoid, 2.25 mm long with 20 ovules, style slender, 2.5 cm long, terminating in a capitate stigma. Apical flower with calyx finely pubescent tomentous externally, cylindric, 5 mm long. Corolla with petals 9-10 mm long, united into a tube at the base, free lobes pubescent tomentous at the apex and on 1 median keel of the external face, narrowly lanceolate, 2.5-3 mm long. Stamens numerous, 2.2 cm long, united into a tube 1.2 cm long subsequently dividing into phalanges and more or less adnate to the corolla tube. Annular disc more or less lobed wrinkled. Pistil obscurely to shortly stipitate, ovary glabrous, 1 mm long, style slender 7 mm long, sinuous at the base, very weakly claviform toward the apex.

Fruit indehiscent, glabrous, flattened, narrowly ellipsoid 10-20 × 1.25-1.75 cm, margins thickened. Mesocarp fleshy. Seeds elliptic, 9 × 5 × 4 mm, areole elliptic, central.

## Type

Jacquin, Fragm. Bot.: 15, tab. 9 (Mimosa saman Jacq.)

## Discussion

This plant, native to America, has been introduced into all tropical regions as an ornamental and shade tree.

To be sought in Gabon.
