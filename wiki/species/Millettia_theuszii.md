# *Millettia theuszii* (Büttner) De Wild. Planche 92

**Genus**: [[Millettia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Ann. Mus. Congo Belge, Bot., sér. 5, 1(2) : 140 (1904)

## Description

Ann. Mus. Congo Belge, Bot., sér. 5, 1(2) : 140 (1904). Lonchocarpus? theuszii Büttner, Verh. Bot. Ver. Brandenb. 32 : 51 (1891). M. chevalieri Harms, Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 40 : 35 (1907). Tree up to 15 m tall or robust liana, with stems up to 25 m ; branches ferrugineous-tomentose, glabrous with age. Stipules ovate, 5–7 mm long, pubescent. Leaf 15–25 cm long ; petiole 3–8 cm ; rachis 12–14 cm ; stipels setaceous, ± 1 mm ; leaflets 9 to 13, petiolule 4–5 mm, blade oblong to obovate, 8–10 × 3.5–4.5 cm, with short acumen, subcoriaceous, glabrous and glaucous above, with appressed and silky-golden or silvery hairs, but glabrescent below, midvein prominent below ; lateral veins in 7 to 10 pairs, arched, ascending, evanescent near the margins, veinlets fine but well visible below. Inflorescence robust, 30–40(–70) cm long ; main axis thick, reaching 1 cm in diameter ; branches rare and short, spreading, with reddish pubescence ; brachyblasts globose, each with 4 or 5 flowers. Flower splendid, ± 2.5 cm long ; pedicel 10–12 mm ; bracteoles at the midpoint, caducous ; calyx narrowly ellipsoid, brown-red, pubescent externally, tube 6–8 mm long, lobes triangular, 3–4 mm long, upper lobe sometimes slightly incised ; corolla bright red or brick red, standard with claw 2 mm, blade suborbicular, 20 mm diameter, with cordate base, silky-pubescent on the outside, wings with oblong blade, ± 20 × 6 mm, obtuse, base auriculate, pubescent on the outside, keel oblong, ± 20 × 10 mm, with folded margins ; stamens, ± 25 mm, free part 5–8 mm, anthers brown ; ovary linear, 15 × 2 mm, silky, with 6 to 8 ovules ; style 10–12 mm, pubescent. Pod on pedicel of 12–18 mm, narrowly oblong, 17–22 × 2–4.5(–5.5) cm, with 5 to 7 seeds, reddish-brown, shiny black with age, with caducous hairiness. Seed flattened, ± 2 cm diameter, brown.

## Distribution

Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola ; in Gabon, known from Haut-Ogooué and Ogooué-Lolo

## Ecology

heliophilous liana ; primary and secondary forest, gallery and riparian forest, forest edges, savannas, fallows ; in Gabon and elsewhere at 260–650 m altitude. Flowers in May, June and September to January ; fruits in February, May and September. Use: The species has potential ornamental value

## Vernacular names

ntubungu (Djambala) ; ópipà (Batéké)
