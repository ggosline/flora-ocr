# *Coleus inselbergi* (B.J.Pollard & A.J.Paton) A.J.Paton Planche 12

**Genus**: [[Coleus]]
**Protologue**: Phytokeys 129 : 59 (2019)

## Synonyms

- Plectranthus inselbergi B.J.Pollard & A.J.Paton, Kew. Bull. 61 : 225 (2006).

## Description

Annual or short-lived perennial herb, 0.4–0.7(–1.2) m tall; stem erect or ascending, rarely creeping, sparsely to densely pubescent with curved or variously oriented white hairs, with scattered sessile reddish glands. Leaf: generally sessile or very rarely with petiole (0.5–)1(–2.5) mm; lamina narrowly obovate to elliptic-ovate or more rarely narrowly or broadly ovate, (0.7–)1.5–4(–6.5) × (0.3–)0.6–1.5(–2.6) cm, slightly succulent, cuneate to attenuate at base, obtuse at apex, distal part slightly dentate, entire toward base, almost glabrous to densely pubescent with sessile reddish glands beneath. Inflorescence generally condensed toward apex, lax toward base with verticillasters distant 5–15 mm in fruit; cymes sessile or sometimes shortly pedunculate, most often compact, rarely increasingly distant and loosely branched in fruit, generally with 5 to 9 flowers; bracts erect in distal part, slightly cucullate, 5 mm long. Flower: pedicel 2–5 mm; calyx 1–2 mm long, 4–5 mm in fruit, with spreading white hairs and a few scattered sessile red glands, upper lip ovate, acute and strongly reflexed at apex, lower lip with incurved lateral lobes, narrowly triangular, apex acute, median pair fused, forming an ovate-oblong, emarginate lobe, curved upward at maturity and closing the throat; corolla 6–12 mm long, mauve or violet, entire surface uniformly furnished with sessile red glands, tube 3–6 mm long, slightly to distinctly sigmoidal, upper lip erect or reflexed, lower lip 3–6 mm long, cucullate and enclosing the stamens; stamens with fused filaments. Fruit: achenes ovoid, 1 mm long, brown, spotted with reddish.

## Distribution

Equatorial Guinea and Gabon; in Gabon: Woleu-Ntem and Ogooué-Ivindo. Conservation status: Distribution is very limited, populations are severely fragmented and habitat is under pressure; probably Endangered (EN)

## Ecology

Inselbergs or exposed rocks; at 500–750 m altitude. B: Morales (2011), Pollard et al. (2006), Vande weghe et al. (2016). Bibliography
