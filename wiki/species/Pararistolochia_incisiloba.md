# *Pararistolochia incisiloba* (Jongkind) M.E.Leal, comb. nov. Planche 3(2, 3)

**Genus**: [[Pararistolochia]]
**Family**: [[Aristolochiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Aristolochia incisiloba Jongkind, Bull. Jard. Bot. Natl. Belg. 60 : 147 (1990)

## Description

Liane with rectangular-elliptic stem in section. Leaf: petiole 12 mm; blade rectangular-elliptic, 7–11 × 4–6 cm, truncate to obtuse at base, apex with acute acumen 3 mm long. Inflorescence: rachis 12–40 mm, bearing 2–3 flowers. Flower: pedicel (excluding ovary) up to 2 cm; perianth grey-cream to pale green, with purple veins, utricle 0.5–1.2 cm long, interior pubescent and with 2 reniform glandular zones, tube 4–6.5 cm long, lobes 2, unequal, spreading, reddish, upper lobe incised, approximately 3.5 × 4 cm, lower lobe triangular, approximately 3.5 × 4.5 cm; gynosteme 13–20 mm long, with 9 pale brown stamens and 9 stigmatic lobes; ovary up to 3 cm long. Fruit unknown.

## Distribution

endemic to Gabon, known only from Mont Sono in the Massif du Chaillu

## Ecology

montane forest, at 1020 m altitude. B: Jongkind (1990). Bibliography
