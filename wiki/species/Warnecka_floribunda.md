# *Warnecka floribunda* Jacques-Félix

**Genus**: [[Warneckea]]
**Family**: [[Melastomataceae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia, sér. 2, 19 (3) : 264, tab. 4 (1980)

## Description

Small tree; branchlets rounded. Leaves somewhat coriaceous, dark green and glossy above, pale green and dull below; petiole slender, 5–8 mm long; limb elliptic, 4.5 × 9 cm, narrowly cuneate on the petiole; acumen poorly defined, obtuse; veins impressed above, prominent below, the main convergent ones coarctate with the 5–8 main transverse ones; additional ones fine, submariginal and coarctate; reticulum finely prominent below.

Cymes fasciculate in groups of 8–12 on the nodes of old wood, 3 cm long, with a stipe of 8–10 mm, then 1–2 tiers of short branches bearing long-pedicellate flowers; bracts long-persistent, scarious, 1–1.5 mm long. Flower with pedicel 12–18 mm; floral bud 4 × 4.5 mm, corolla conical, well-exserted; hypanthium cupulo-patellar, calyx lobes incised to the base, triangular-obtuse, 2 × 1 mm. Petals thick, semi-oval, 2.5 × 3.5 mm, obscurely unguiculate. Anthers 1.3 × 1.2 mm, thecae somewhat convex; connective short, divergent especially at the base, deeply saddle-shaped by the gland occupying 2/3 of the length; filament 1.5 mm. Epigynous chamber smooth; style 4 mm. Ovary with 2 more or less complete septa, 8-ovulate.

Fruit unknown. — Pl. 58, p. 159.

## Type

Le Testu 7710, Gabon (P)

## Discussion

This species is manifestly close to W. sapinii DE WILD.; and was collected in the same region. However, we have distinguished it from that species by its various organs being larger, and by a different stamen conformation. The question is to be studied in situ.
