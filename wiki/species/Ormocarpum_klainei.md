# *Ormocarpum klainei* Tisser.

**Genus**: [[Ormocarpum]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Ormocarpum spec. A. Hepper, Fl. W. trop. Afr. éd. 2, 1 : 577 (1958)

## Description

Bull. Mus. Natl. Hist. Nat. sér. 2, 3 : 334, 335 (1931 ). Ormocarpum spec. A. Hepper, Fl. W. trop. Afr. éd. 2, 1 : 577 (1958). Shrubs, 1–1.5 m tall; stems and branchlets glabrous. Stipules triangular, 5 mm long, striate, glabrous. Leaf: rachis 6–18 cm, slender, sulcate above, glabrous or with short and sparse glandular setae; leaflets (5–)7 to 15, petiolule 2 mm, blade elliptic, 4–7 × 1.5–3 cm, cuneate at base, acuminate at apex, mucronate, glabrous, the terminal one often obovate, the basal ones often reduced. Inflorescence in racemes of (2–)6–18 cm long, bearing short and sparse setae; bracts trifid, glabrous, embracing the pedicel. Flower: pedicel 5–10 mm, ± hairy; bracteoles 1.5 mm long, near the calyx; calyx narrow, 5 mm long, hairy on the nerves; standard obovate, 12 mm long, wings obovate, 10 mm long, keel 15 × 4 mm, subacute at apex. Pod strongly chambered, thickened at apex, the sutures bearing glandular hairs with swollen base, glabrescent, sometimes sparsely verrucose, with 2 or 3 elliptic articles, 18–20 × 5 mm, attenuate. Seed not seen.

## Distribution

Western Cameroon, Gabon and Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, rare and known from the Estuaire

## Ecology

Dense forest, in the understory; in Gabon at low altitude, elsewhere up to 450 m. Flowers and fruits in October

## Source note

As Pellegrin (1949) and Gillett (1966) had already mentioned, the differences between O. klai nei and O. megalophyllum are not convincing. In Cameroon, there are specimens with intermediate characters and a thorough taxonomic study is desirable. B : Gillett (1966), Pellegrin (1949). Bibliography
