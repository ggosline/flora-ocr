# *Calycobolus heineanus* Lejoly & Lisowski

**Genus**: [[Calycobolus]]
**Family**: [[Convolvulaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Jard. Bot. Nat. Belgique 55 : 40 (1985)

## Description

Vine, at least up to 15 m in length; branchlets appressed-pubescent, early glabrescent. Leaf: petiole 6–12(–16) mm, glabrous; limb ovate-elliptic, (5.5–)9–12 × (1.5–)4–6 cm, rounded to cuneate at the base, acute to acuminate over 0.5 cm at the apex, glabrous; lateral nerves 7–10 pairs, prominent beneath. Inflorescence in a contracted, few-flowered raceme, puberulent; bracts ovate-triangular, 1–2 mm in length; bracteoles elliptic, 3–4 mm in length. Flower: pedicel 15–20(–22) mm, puberulent; sepals: the two outer ones ± equal, ovate, 5–6 × 4–5 mm, acute at the apex, tomentellous on both faces, the inner ones ovate, ± 3 mm in length; corolla campanulate, 2.6 cm in length with rounded lobes and pubescent on the outside; stamens with erect anthers; pistil 19–20 mm in length, glabrous; styles subequal, fused on the half. Fruit unknown.

## Distribution

endemic to Gabon, known only from a single locality in the Estuary

## Ecology

dense humid forest, at low altitude. B: Breteler (2013). ibliography
