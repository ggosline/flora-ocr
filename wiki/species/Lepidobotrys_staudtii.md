# *Lepidobotrys staudtii* Engler

**Genus**: [[Lepidobotrys]]
**Family**: [[Lepidobotryaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. 32 : 108 (1902)

## Description

Tree 3–15 m tall and 20–30 cm in diameter, glabrous. Leaves alternate, articulate; stipules broadly ovate, ciliolate, caducous, $ 1 \times 0.5 $ mm; stipels ovate to elliptic, ciliolate, caducous, with bilobed apex, $ 3.5–4.5 \times 2 $ mm; petiole 5–8 mm; petiolule canaliculate-rugose, 3–5 mm. Blade oblong-rectangular, $ \pm $ acute, obtuse to rounded at base, long-acuminate at apex, $ 5–15 \times 2–7 $ cm. Veins arcuate, anastomosing, prominent on lower surface, forming a very dense network.

Male inflorescences in multiflorous racemes, 10–20 mm. Flowers articulate; bracts $ \pm $ subquadrangular, rounded at apex, glabrescente, ciliolate, thick at base; pedicels 5–8 mm, clothed with short hairs. Male flowers with glabrous sepals, oblong-rectangular, 4–5 × 1–2 mm. Petals obovate, 4 × 5.5 mm. Stamens 10, glabrous, alternately long and short; filaments 5–7 mm long and wider at base for the episepals stamens, and 4–6 mm long for the epipetalous stamens; anthers approximately 1 mm long, ovate, versatile, introrse, dehiscing by 2 longitudinal slits. Rudimentary ovary, approximately 1 × 1 mm, with atrophied ovules; styles 3, very short.

Female inflorescences in pauciflorous racemes, approximately 1 cm long. Flowers with sepals and petals identical to those of male flowers. Stamens included, not fertile. Ovary 3-lobed, 2–2.5 × 1.5–2 mm; ovules 2 per locule, collateral, pendulous; styles 1.5 cm long.

Capsule grayish-violet, ellipsoid, approximately $ 2.5 \times 1.5 $ cm, with septicidal dehiscence, monospermic. Seed black, ellipsoid, $ 1–1.5 \times 0.8 $ cm, covered on two-thirds of its length by a red aril. PL. 10, p. 41.

## Type

Staudt 911, Cameroun (holo-, B, delet.)

## Discussion

Upland forests, more rarely swampy formations. Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Central African Republic and Zaire.

## Specimens examined

Le Testu 9331, Mitzic (fl., oct.).
