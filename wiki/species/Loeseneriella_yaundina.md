# *Loeseneriella yaundina* (Loesener) Hallé ex Wilczek

**Genus**: [[Loeseneriella]]
**Family**: [[Celastraceae]]
**Protologue**: FCB 9:149 (1960)

## Synonyms

- Hippocratea yaundina LOES., Bot. Jahrb. 34: 117 (1904); EXELL & MENDONÇA, CFA 2(1): 19 (1954).
- Loeseneriella yaundina (LOES.) HALLÉ, Monogr. Hipp. Afr. occid. Thèse, Paris : 98 (1958) ; Bull. IFAN, sér. A, 21 (3) : tab. 67 (1959) ; Pollen & Spores 2 (1) : 11 (1960) ; nom. nud.

## Description

Liane 5–10 m tall with dark brown or greyish bark. Stipules reduced on a thin transverse rim. No rubber. Petiole 6–12 mm. Blade entire or confusedly crenulate, subcoriaceous, reddish-brown, varnished above when dry, somewhat margined, (5)7–16×(2.5)3–8 cm; base acute or rounded; apex with acumen 3–9 mm. Secondary nerves 6–8 pairs; nervilles unequally or scarcely apparent when dry.

Inflorescences branched from the base or fasciculate with variable cymes, (2)3–10 cm; main axes almost glabrous; branches reddish-brown pubérulent. Pedicel ± pubérulent 2–6 mm. Bud glabrous, oblong ovoid, rounded at apex, petals quincuncial imbricate but not cucullate at apex. Flowers greenish, whitish, yellow, yellowish or orange, 10–12 mm in diameter. Sepals brown-green suborbicular, broadly imbricate, ± scabrous toward the base, somewhat unequal, 2–2.5 mm wide, microciliolate. Petals oblong with thinned margins, green to yellowish-brown apex, ± decumbent 5–7 × 2 mm, micropubérulent or almost glabrous above, indistinctly ciliolate. Disc in narrow cupule somewhat concealed at the base of the globose fleshy androgynophore, 2.8 mm in diameter and 1.4 mm in height, glabrous. Filaments ribbon-like 1.5 mm; anthers yellow with subapical extrorse slit, 1 mm wide. Visible part of pistil 2 mm; style pyramidal; locules half-immersed containing 9–16 ovules each.

Mericarps elliptic 8–10 × 3–4 mm, glabrous. Seed reaching 66 × 18 mm, with light reddish-brown wing; cotyledonary part 30 × 5 mm; cotyledons fused; funicular sheaths 3 mm. - Pl. 70, fig. 1–16, p. 245.

## Type

Zenker & Staudt 129, Cameroun (iso-, BM! BR! K!)

## Discussion

Cameroon, Gabon, Central African Republic (Tisserant 297, 1218; Le Testu 4355), Congo (Descoings 8418), Zaïre from Mayombe to Kivu (Troupin 9348) and Cabinda (Gossweiler 7923, BM).

Large heliophilous liane frequently encountered in central Africa in gallery forests and in ± swampy stations.
