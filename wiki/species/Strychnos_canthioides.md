# *Strychnos canthioides* Leeuwenberg

**Genus**: [[Strychnos]]
**Family**: [[Loganiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Meded. Landbouwh. 09 (1) : 79, fig. 5 (1969)

## Description

Liane; branches cylindrical, not lenticellate, as are the small branchlets which are glabrous and often sulcate when dry; tendrils in 1-3 pairs.

Leaves with petiole 2-4 mm long, glabrous; blade ± subcoriaceous, generally narrowly ovate to narrowly elliptic, but often ovate for the leaves situated on the central axis, 3-11 × 0.8-3.7 cm, acuminate at the apex, cuneate or rounded at the base, glabrous on both faces; one pair of secondary nerves curved along the margin; primary nerves slightly impressed above; tertiary venation reticulate, not very distinct.

Inflorescences axillary and terminal, rather lax, pauciflorous, 1-5 × 1-3 cm; peduncle, branches and pedicels glabrous. Flowers 4-merous, fragrant, all pedicellate. Sepals pale green, connate at the base, suborbicular, 1-1.2 mm long, rounded, glabrous on both faces, finely ciliate. Corolla, in the mature bud, 5-7 mm long, white, glabrous outside, inside densely pilose except at the base of the tube; tube 1-1.4 times longer than the lobes, 2.5-4 mm long; lobes oblong, 2.2-3.2 × 1-1.2 mm, acute, recurved. Stamens exserted, inserted at the throat of the corolla, with white, glabrous filament, 2.4-3 times longer than the anther; anther oblong, glabrous. Pistil glabrous, 4.5-6.2 mm long; ovary often broadly ovoid, 0.5-1 × 0.5-0.6 mm, two-locular, pale green; style filiform, white, 4-5.2 mm long; stigma very small, often scarcely bilobed; 2 ovules per locule.

Fruits unknown, no doubt small. Seeds presumably similar to those of S. barteri. Pl. 17, 9-15, p. 126.
