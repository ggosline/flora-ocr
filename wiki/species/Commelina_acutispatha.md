# *Commelina acutispatha* De Wild.

**Genus**: [[Commelina]]
**Family**: [[Commelinaceae]]
**Protologue**: Pl. bequaert. 5 : 164 (1931)

## Description

C. clarkeana K.Schum., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 24(3) : 343 (1897), non C. clarkeana De Wild. & T.Durand, Ann. Mus. Congo Belge, Bot. sér. 3, 1(2) : 245 (1901). C. thomasii Hutch., Kew Bull. 1939 : 243 (1939). Perennial herb; stem creeping or erect, rooting at the nodes, 1.5–5 m in length, sometimes more, glabrous to puberulent. Leaves distichous; sheath closed, 1.5–2.5 cm in length, with a pubescent line along the suture, faces glabrous to puberulent or distinctly pubescent, ciliate or ciliolate at the apex; pseudopetiole absent; blade narrowly elliptic-ovate to narrowly ovate-oblong, (3.5–)5.5–10.5(–13) × (0.9–)1.2–1.7(–2) cm, base oblique, with one side cuneate, the other rounded, apex attenuate, margins scabrous, sometimes ciliate at the base, faces pubescent at least on the nerves or upper face glabrous. Inflorescence with a solitary spathe; peduncle 1.5–3 cm, puberulent at least along one longitudinal line; spathe (2.5–)3–6.2 × 1–1.6 cm, slightly falciform, base cordate, free margins, scabrous and ciliate, apex attenuate, pubescent on both faces; upper cincinnus generally present, enclosed or shortly exsert, with 1 or 2(–3) male flowers, sometimes bisexual, the lower one with 4 to 7 bisexual and male flowers. Flower ± 15 mm in diameter; sepals 3.5–4.5 mm in length, the 2 lower ones apparently free, pale greenish-white transparent; upper petals spatulate, 7–9 × 5 mm, of which 3–4 mm for the claw, whitish, mauve or pale blue, the lower one broadly ovate, 3–4 × 2.5 mm, concolorous or else white; lateral stamens with filament 5–5.5 mm, white or blue, anther 1.2–1.5 mm in length, yellow, with sharp connective, the median one with filament 4.5–5 mm, anther 1.8–2.3 mm in length, connective with a broad blue spot; staminodes 2, the median one apparently absent; style 5–7 mm, white. Fruit 3-locular, obovoid-ellipsoidal, 5.5–6 × 4 mm, bivalved, light brown or light greenish-brown, often with some brown spots, dorsal locule indehiscent, with 1 seed, ventral locules with (1–)2 seeds. Seed transversely oblong-ellipsoidal, ± compressed dorso-ventrally, 2–2.5 × 1.5–1.9 mm; testa smooth to finely alveolate, reddish-brown and unspotted to dark brown and weakly or distinctly spotted with light brown, not farinose; hilum straight; embryotegium circular, slightly sunken.

## Distribution

Sierra Leone, Liberia, Ghana, Togo, Benin, Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo; rather rare in Gabon, known from Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem.

## Ecology

Forest edges, rocky outcrops (inselbergs), Cynometra forest (East Africa), secondary forest, humid places; in Gabon, on rocky outcrops and inselbergs, shrubby thickets, reported as floating, from 30 to 680 m in altitude. Flowers opening in the morning; in Gabon, observed fertile in February, May and September.
