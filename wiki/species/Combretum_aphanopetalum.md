# *Combretum aphanopetalum* Engler & Diels

**Genus**: [[Combretum]]
**Family**: [[Combretaceae]]
**Protologue**: Mon. Afr. Pfl., 3 : 76 (1899). TYPE : Gabon, Rio Muni (fl.) Mann 1758 (holo-B delet. ; LECTOTYPE désigné ici : K ; iso-P)

## Description

TYPE: Gabon, Rio Muni (fl.) Mann 1758 (holo-B delet.; LECTOTYPE designated here: K; iso-P).

Liane, length ≤ 50 m, diameter ≤ 23 cm: young branchlets pubescent.

Leaves opposite; petiole up to 7 mm long; blade elliptic, ≤ 14 × ≤ 4 cm, with pellucid dots; upper surface glossy, lower surface with red scales, otherwise glabrous or nearly so; major lateral veins 6–12 pairs, domatia scarcely visible; blade base subcordate and apex shortly acuminate.

Scales circular, diameter 70–100 µm.

Inflorescences in axillary spikes up to 8 cm long; bracts scarcely visible.

Flowers 4-merous, sessile, protogynous. Receptacle pubescent on both surfaces; the lower one 1 mm tall, the upper one 3 mm tall and funnel-shaped. Calyx, lobes triangular. Petals absent. Stamens exserted 2–3 mm; anthers apiculate, 0.5 mm long. Style exserted 2 mm.

Fruit, subcircular in profile, diameter 5–8 cm, with papyraceous wings; stipe ≤ 2 cm. Young fruits viscous.

from Liberia to Gabon
