# *Costus maculatus* Ker Gawl.
**Family in Vol. 9**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Current placement**: Costaceae · **Genus**: [[Costus]]
## Description
Robust herb with glabrous, long-acuminate lanceolate blades c. 25 × 7 cm and a short wavy ligule. The usually separate 30–50 cm flowering stalk bears an ovoid spike; each bract subtends two flowers. Corolla white, labellum c. 5 cm and white with pink near its apex.
## Distribution
Known from Sierra Leone, Cameroon and Gabon. Gabon collections: Klaine 3559, Mont Bouet near Libreville; Le Testu s.n., Mayumba.
