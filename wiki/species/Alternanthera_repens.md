# *Alternanthera repens* (L.) Link

**Family**: [[Amaranthaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Alternanthera]]

## Description

Prostrate, rooting herb with opposite leaves and compact axillary heads. Persistent pointed tepals enclose the small, dry, one-seeded fruit. The treatment separates it from *A. sessilis* by its more conspicuously woolly, sharply bracted flower heads and creeping habit.

Cavaco, A. (1963). Amaranthacées. *Flore du Gabon* 7: 43–44.
