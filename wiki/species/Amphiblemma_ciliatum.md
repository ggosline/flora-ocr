# *Amphiblemma ciliatum* Cogniaux

**Genus**: [[Amphiblemma]]
**Family**: [[Melastomataceae]]
**Protologue**: Mon. Phan. 7, Melast.: 528 (1891); JACQ.-FÉL., Fl. Cam. 24 : 100, tab. 32 (1983)

## Description

Shrub little branched, erect up to 1 m. Leaves petiolate, membranous; petiole 5 to 8 cm, puberulent or glabrous; limb ovate-cordate, 5-9 × 8-15 cm, apex acute or moderately acuminate; short hairs, scattered on both surfaces, the aspect remaining glabrescent; 3-5-nerved, the transverse nerves parallel; margins serrulate-ciliate.

Terminal cymes, subsessile, verticillate in groups of 3 to 5, each bearing 10 to 15 flowers biseriate, erect, contiguous; sometimes preceded by axillary cymules on the uppermost nodes. Flower with pedicel 2 to 3 mm; hypanthocalyx campanulate, 3-5 mm long, pubescent, often with caducous glandular hairs, leaving a verrucose scar; lobes tooth-like, 2 mm long, bearing on the back soft, glandular, spreading hairs. Petals pink, 10-12 mm long, ciliate toward the apex. External stamens with anther 7-8 mm; podoconnective 5 mm long, with claviform appendage 1 mm; filament 5 mm. Internal stamens with anther 4 mm; connective with obtuse appendage on either side; filament 4 mm. Ovary deeply included, adnate over almost its entire height; apex concave by the epigynous crown, with scarious, loosely ciliate margin; style 10 mm; placentas sessile.

Fruit short, 5 × 7 mm; locules short; perigynous scales accrescent, reaching the margin of the hypanthium. Seeds ellipsoid; 1 mm long, lateral membrane progressively dilated into an apico-frontal vesicle. — Pl. 28, fig. 1-4, p. 79.

## Type

Thollon 439, Gabon (P)

## Discussion

Species scattered from Angola to Central Africa; in forest clearings and gallery forests.

## Specimens examined

Le Testu 5210, haute Ngounié, entre Mouila et Kembélé (fév.). Pobéguin s.n., Lambaréné (an. 1913). Thollon 245, Ogooué (mars, avr.); 439, Lambaréné, en sous-bois (mars).
