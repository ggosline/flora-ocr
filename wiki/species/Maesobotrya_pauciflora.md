# *Maesobotrya pauciflora* Pax Planche 15

**Genus**: [[Maesobotrya]]
**Family**: [[Phyllanthaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot . Jahrb . Syst . 33 : 281 (1903)

## Description

Shrub 0.35–2(–3) m tall; branchlets with short, ± scattered and ± appressed hairs, glabrescenct. Stipules ± oblong, (0.5–)1–2.5(–3.5) mm long, with appressed hairs. Leaf: petiole 0.5–3(–5) cm, hairy like the branchlets; blade obovate-elliptic, (3–)7–15(–21) × (1.5–)3–8.5 cm, cuneate at base, acuminate over 0.5–2 cm, entire-margined, with short, ± scattered and ± appressed hairs below (and sometimes above) especially on the veins, early glabrescenct, small glands sometimes present below; lateral veins 5–8 pairs. Inflorescence puberulent, the male ones 0.5–4(–5) cm long, the female ones 0.5–2.5 cm long, up to 3 cm in fruit. Male flower: pedicel 1.5–3 cm, the upper part glabrous; sepals 5, ± spreading, ≤ 1 mm long, glabrous or only ciliate; stamens 5, 1–2 mm long, glabrous; disk 5-lobed, glabrous; pistillode glabrous. Female flower: pedicel 0.5–1 mm, glabrous; sepals ± as in male flowers; disk cupuliform, glabrous; pistil 1–1.5(–2) mm long, glabrous or with a few hairs; ovary 2-locular. Fruit dehiscent by a lateral slit, ovoid, 10–15 × 5–7.5 mm, glabrous, red at maturity. Seed 1; sarcotesta white.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, known from all provinces except Nyanga

## Ecology

primary and secondarized forest; in Gabon, at 350–600 m altitude. Bibliography: Lachenaud & Breteler (2011), Léonard (1994).
