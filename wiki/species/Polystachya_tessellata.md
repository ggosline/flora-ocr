# *Polystachya tessellata* Lindl.

**Genus**: [[Polystachya]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: J. Linn. Soc., Bot. 6 : 130 (1862)

## Description

– Polystachya rufinula Rchb. f., Gard. Chron. 1 : 41 (1879).

– Polystachya buchananii Rolfe, Kew Bull. 82: 335 (1893).

– Polystachya lehmbachiana Kraenzl., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 28 : 166 (1900).

– Polystachya latifolia De Wild., Not. Pl. Util. Inter. Fl. Congo 1 : 138 (1903).

– Polystachya gracilis De Wild., Not. Pl. Util. Inter. Fl. Congo 1 : 136 (1903).

– Polystachya praealta Kraenzl., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 36 : 118 (1905).

– Polystachya lettowiana Kraenzl., Repert. Sp. Nov. Regni. Veget., Beih. 39 : 54 (1926).

– Polystachya lepidantha Kraenzl., Kew. Bull. 7 : 290 (1926).

Pseudobulbs < 15 × 0.5–0.7 cm. Leaves (3–) 10–30 × 0.8–6 cm, lanceolate to elliptic. Inflorescence 10–50 cm, 20–200-flowered. Flowers cream, yellow, pale green, pink, dull red, labellum white with pink midlobe. Dorsal sepal oblong-elliptic. Lateral sepals obliquely triangular-elliptic, acute. Labellum narrowing towards the base, trilobed at the middle, pubescent, callus ridge-shaped, fleshy, more or less pubescent in the lower half; midlobe elliptic to subcubicular, truncate to rounded at the apex, margins crenulate-undulate; lateral lobes triangular to oblong, falcate. Spur saccate-conical. – Pl. 127, p. 289.

PL. 127. – Polystachya tessellata Lindl.: A, flower and floral bract; B, labellum; C, dorsal sepal; D, petal; E, lateral sepal; F, leaf. – Polystachya adansoniae Rchb.f.: G, flower and floral bract; H–K, labellum, various views; L, dorsal sepal; M, petal; N, lateral sepal; O, leaf (A–F, Le Testu 9274, P; G–O, Schlechter 12409, P).

## Distribution

Guinea, Sierra Leone, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Togo, Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Annobon, Equatorial Guinea, Congo, Central African Republic, Zaire, Rwanda, Burundi, Sudan, Ethiopia, Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, Angola, Zambia, Malawi, Zimbabwe, Mozambique

## Ecology

alt. 0–1,700 m. Epiphytic in dense humid forest, wooded savanna, observed notably on Crudia harmsiana, Coffea sp., Elaeis guineensis, Garcinia punctata, Mangifera indica, Millettia laurentii, Scorodophlaeus zenkeri, Uapaca heudelotii
