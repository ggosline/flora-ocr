## Description

Pseudobulbs < 15 x 0.5–0.7 cm. Leaves (3–) 10–30 x 0.8–6 cm, lanceolate to elliptic. Inflorescence 10–50 cm, 20–200-flowered. Flowers cream, yellow, pale green, pink, dull red, lip white with median lobe pink. Dorsal sepal oblong-elliptic. Lateral sepals obliquely triangular-elliptic, acute. Lip narrowing towards the base, trilobed at the middle, pubescent, callus shaped like a fleshy crest, more or less pubescent in the lower half; median lobe elliptic to subquadrate, truncate to rounded at the apex, margins crenulate-undulate; lateral lobes triangular to oblong, falcate. Spur sacciform-conical. – Pl. 127, p. 289.
