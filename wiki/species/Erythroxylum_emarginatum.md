# *Erythroxylum emarginatum* Thonning

**Genus**: [[Erythroxylum]]
**Family**: [[Erythroxylaceae]]

## Description

— E. monogynum auct. non RoxB. : Sim., For. Fl. Port. E. Afr. : 21 (1909), p.p.

— E. monogynum Roxb. var. caffrum (Sond.) Eyles, Trans. Roy. Soc. S. Afr. 5 : 38 (1916).

— E. perrotii A. Chev., Bull. Soc. Bot. Fr. Mém. 8 : 142 (1912).

Tree reaching 20–25 m. Leaves alternate, penninerved; stipules intrapetiolary, fused, triangular, acute, with membranous persistent margins, approximately 1 mm long. Petiole 3–5 mm. Blade coriaceous, elliptic to obovate, cuneate at the base, most often narrowed but always ± emarginate, rarely rounded at the apex, 2.5–7 × 1.5–3.5 cm; secondary nerves ascending, distinct on both surfaces, tertiary veins scarcely apparent.

Flowers solitary or in fascicles, axillary or clustered on young leafless branchlets with short internodes. Pedicels slender, 2–6 mm. Sepals valvate, triangular, fused at the base, obtuse at the apex, 1–2 × 1 mm. Petals very shortly unguiculate at the base, rounded at the apex with a nectariferous appendage inserted at the lower quarter, 3–4 × 1–1.5 mm. Stamens subequal, 4–5 mm in flowers with short style, unequal in flowers with long style, those opposite the petals 3–3.5 mm, those opposite the sepals 2–3 mm; filaments fused at the base, forming a tube 1.25–2 mm high, long persistent; anthers 0.5–0.75 mm, ellipsoid, basifixed, dehiscing by 2 longitudinal slits. Ovary 1.5 mm long, most often with a single fertile locule and a single pendulous ovule. Styles 3, free, 1–1.5 mm in short-styled flowers and 3–3.5 mm in long-styled flowers; stigmas club-shaped.

Drupe ellipsoid, approximately 10 × 5 mm. PL. 12, 1–9, p. 51.

## Type

Thonning s.n., Ghana (holo-, C)

## Discussion

Leaf dimensions and form are highly variable at the scale of the African continent, but a series of intermediate forms links the narrow or small leaves to the typical leaves.

Forests or thickets, rocks, sandy soils or dunes. From Guinea to Kenya, Tanzania and South Africa.
