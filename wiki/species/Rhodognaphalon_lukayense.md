## Description

Tree 15–20 m tall; bole straight, 60 cm in diameter at the base; crown dense. Rhytidome pale grey, longitudinally fissured. Leaves clustered at the tip of the branchlets, digitately compound with 5–7 leaflets, most often in even number; stipules triangular, glabrous, acute at the apex; petiole 5–22 cm long, glabrous; petiolules glabrous, 0.8–2.5 cm long, canaliculate, with a strong dorsal keel. Blade glabrous on both faces, coriaceous, very broadly obovate, 9–23.5 × 3.8–8.7 cm, narrowed cuneiform at the base and apex shortly acuminate obtuse or subacute, mucronate. Midvein prominent above and very strongly prominent below, secondary nerves 8–14 pairs, slightly ascending, very slightly arched, anastomosing in arcades approximately 5 mm from the margin of the blade. Tertiary nerves forming a very loose network or a single visible tertiary nerve on both faces, quaternary nerves indistinct.

Flowers solitary axillary or fascicled, at the tip of the branchlets, below the terminal bud, actinomorphic, 15.5–17.5 cm long; pedicel glabrous, approximately 1 cm long. Floral bud ± linear, yellow at the base and reddish at the apex on the living plant. Receptacle 0.5 cm long, bearing linear glands forming a bright red ring on the living plant, glabrous. Calyx campanulate, irregularly lobed, ciliolate or not at the apex, external face puberulent and silky internally. Petals 5, ribbon-like, yellowish at the base, becoming reddish at the apex on the living plant, densely puberulent externally and shortly pubescent internally, 15–17 × 1.4–1.6 cm. Stamens approximately 360, 10.8–14.5 cm long, united into a staminal tube 2.8–3.5 cm long, brownish-red on the living plant, with 5 rows shortly pubescent alternating with 5 rows glabrescent to pubescent, dividing into 5 phalanges; filaments 8–11 cm long, brownish-red to bright red on the living plant; anthers 3–4 mm long, red to purple on the living plant. Ovary glabrous, style 12–15 cm long.

Capsule oblong-ovate, with ± marked furrows, acute at the apex, 12.5–15 × 5–5.5 cm, brownish-red, glabrous, dehiscent on the tree. Seeds angular to subglobose, 1.6–2.5 × 1.3–1.8 cm; testa dull brownish-rust, one broad face bearing a densely tuberculate patch. Kapok sparse, rust-coloured. PL. 9, 5, p. 35; PL. 12, p. 45.
