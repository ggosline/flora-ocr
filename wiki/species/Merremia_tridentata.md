# *Merremia tridentata* (L.) Hallier f. subsp. angustifolia (Jacq.) Ooststr. Planche 22

**Genus**: [[Merremia]]
**Family**: [[Convolvulaceae]]
**Protologue**: Blumea 3 : 323 (1939)

## Description

Xenostegia tridentata (L.) Austin & Staples, Brittonia 32(4): 533 (1980). Xenostegia tridentata (L.) Austin & Staples subsp. angustifolia (Jacq.) Lejoly & Lisowski, Fragm. Flor. Geobot. 38(2): 379 (1993). Perennial herb with woody rootstock; stems slender, up to 1.5 m long, prostrate or twining, ± angular and sometimes slightly winged when dry, glabrous. Leaf: petiole 0–3 mm; blade of variable shape, linear to lanceolate or oblong, (0.5–)1–6 cm × 1–3 mm, cuneate, truncate, hastate or auriculate at base, acute and mucronate at apex; midvein ± prominent beneath, depressed above, lateral veins obscure. Inflorescence in cymes generally uniflorous; peduncle 1–2 cm; bracteoles narrowly triangular, 1–2 mm long. Flower: pedicel 4–6 mm; sepals ovate to lanceolate, 4–5 mm long, acuminate to cuspidate at apex; corolla barely 5-lobed, 10–15 mm long, yellow, glabrous; stamens included, glabrous; pistil included. Fruit subglobose, 4–8 mm in diameter. Seeds triquetrous, dark greyish-brown to brown, glabrous.

## Distribution

Tropical Africa, tropical Asia, Australia; in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Ogooué-Maritime, Nyanga and Haut-Ogooué.

## Ecology

Sandy beaches, sand savannas, ruderal stations; in Gabon, up to about 500 m, elsewhere, up to 1550 m. Taxonomic note: Another subspecies, subsp. alatipes (Dammer) Verdc. (= Xenostegia tridentata (L.) Austin & Staples subsp. alatipes (Dammer) Lejoly & Lisowski) is not present in Gabon. B: Deroin (2001), Gonçalves (1987), Heine (1963), Verdcourt (1963). Bibliography
