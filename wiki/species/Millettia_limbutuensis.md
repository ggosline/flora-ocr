# *Millettia limbutuensis* De Wild.

**Genus**: [[Millettia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bol. Soc. Brot. 28 : 18 (1920)

## Description

Tree 4–15 m tall; branches yellow. Stipules triangular, 3 mm long, with acute apex. Leaf entirely glabrous; petiole 4–5 cm; rachis 12–16 cm; stipels absent; leaflets 7 to 9, petiolule 3–5 mm, blade elliptic, 7–14 × 3–6 cm, the terminal one the largest, base cuneate, apex with acumen 1–2.5 cm; lateral veins in 6 or 7 pairs. Inflorescence with slender to robust axis 12–40 cm, of which the peduncle 4–9 cm, with very fine and very short appressed white hairs, extending to the pedicels, bracteoles and calyces; flowers in fascicles of 5 or 6, floral buds with grey pubescence; bracts caducous, minute. Flower: pedicel 3–4 mm; calyx almost truncate, 4–5 mm long; corolla cream-white, standard with claw 2 mm, pubescent externally, blade wider than long, 10–15 × 15–20 mm, apex slightly emarginate, wings with claw 5–6 mm, blade obovate, 13–17 × 3 mm, keel with claw 6–7 mm, blade ± rectangular, 13–17 × 6 mm; stamens with tube ± 10 mm, strongly curved, free part ± 5 mm; ovary linear; style glabrous, at right angle to the ovary. Pod linear, attaining 10 × 1.7 cm, with scattered short hairs, valves coiling at maturity. Seed ovoid, ± 12 × 10 × 5–6 mm, brown to black; hilum excentric, ± 3.5 mm long.

## Distribution

Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Haut-Ogooué, Ogooué-Ivindo and Ogooué-Maritime.

## Ecology

Low riparian forest, primary forest; at 0–400 m altitude. Flowers in April and October; fruits in January and May.
