# *Impatiens palpebrata* Hook. f.

**Family**: [[Balsaminaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Impatiens]]

## Description

Herb with alternate leaves and pedunculate pink or violet flowers. Lip shallow and funnel-shaped; spur sinuous and abruptly narrowed over its terminal 1–2.5 mm; leaf margin has deep sinuous teeth.

## Distribution

Cameroon and Gabon, including the Ngounié basin.
