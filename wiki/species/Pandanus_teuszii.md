# *Pandanus teuszii* Warburg

**Family**: [[Pandanaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Pandanus]]

Pflanzenreich 4 (9): 67 (1900).

## Description

Vegetative and male apparatus unknown.

Drupes club-shaped, approximately 3 cm long, approximately 1.3–1.5 cm wide, approximately 0.9 cm thick, 1–2-locular; apex tabulate; equator (circumference at the level of the greatest transverse diameter of the drupe) fairly visible, in the vicinity of the base of the upper mesocarp; supraequatorial part approximately 1/3 of the length of the drupe. Hypodermal epidermal cells mostly not lignified, the others lignified with a single distinct layer of lignin, some very elongated (up to 195 µm long) and parallel to the axis of the drupe. Upper mesocarp rather poor in spongy material in the dry state, with a base around the ceiling of the seed locules or slightly below, with axial fibres from bottom to top much thicker than the thickest extraaxial fibres, sometimes dissociated into 3–4 fibrils of unequal thickness; lower mesocarp rather poor in spongy material in the dry state, its thickest fibres slightly thicker than the thickest extraaxial fibres in the upper mesocarp. Endocarp approximately 1.5 cm long on the axis of the seed locules, 0.4–0.7 cm at the periphery, not shouldered. Upper wall of the seed locules very prominent (reaching the median level of the upper mesocarp) and strongly thickened on their axis, measuring approximately 3.2 mm in thickness. Seed locules approximately 0.8 × 0.4 cm, with centre clearly supramedian. Albumen dark brown. Germinal tubes approximately 3.3 mm long beneath the seed locules, entirely free of fibres emerging laterally from the lower part of the seed locules.

## Distribution

**Gabon**: locality unknown. The type specimen was collected by Teusz from the coastal zone, though the original label bears no locality or collection date information.

## Notes

The holotype at Berlin, consisting only of drupes, was destroyed during World War II. An isotype exists at Florence (FI), collected by Teusz from Gabon. The specimen bears the number "71" on the original Berlin label, which refers not to a collecting number but to the 71st species in Warburg's monograph in *Das Pflanzenreich*. Three drupes were preserved on this isotype: one entire with a large epicarp fragment retained; one entire but with epicarp lost; and one with epicarp lost and sectioned longitudinally, showing two seed locules (bilocular). The treatment emphasizes the remarkably thick "columellae" (axial fibre plus the prominent apical part of the endocarp above each locule) as a distinctive character of this species, particularly notable for their short, squat appearance relative to other West African Pandanus species with 1–2-locular drupes.
