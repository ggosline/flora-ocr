# *Cayratia debilis* (Bak.) Suesseng.

**Genus**: [[Cayratia]]
**Family**: [[Vitaceae]]
**Protologue**: Suessenguth, Naturl. Pflanzenf. 20 D : 278 (1953)

## Synonyms

- Cissus debilis (Bak.) Planch., DC. Monogr. Phaner. 5 : 569 (1887). — F.W.T.A. ed. 2, 1 : 679 (1958). — L. Willems, Fl. Congo belge et R.-U. 9 : 550 (1960).
- Vitis debilis Baker, F.T.A. I : 403 (1868).
- Cissus repens A. Chevalier, Bot.: 146 (1920), nomen.

## Description

Herbaceous or subligneous vine, highly branched, climbing. Stems more or less cylindrical, 1-3 mm diam., glabrous, sometimes slightly pubescent; internodes 5-10 cm long. Tendrils more or less cylindrical, slender, glabrous, several times bifurcate; bracts about 1 mm long, glabrous. Stipules oblong deltoid, obtuse, 2-3 mm long and 1.5-2.5 mm wide, glabrous or weakly pubescent, with irregular and ciliolate margins. Leaves compound pedate, 5-foliolate. Petiole and petiolules striate, about 1 mm in diameter; petiole 4-12 cm long, glabrous; petiolules canaliculate, more or less sparsely pubescent especially on the margins of the canalicule, 0.5 cm long. Blade of leaflets obovate, ovate or ± elliptic, 5-12 cm long and 2-5.5 cm wide, with cuneate or obtuse base often oblique, with cuneate or obtuse apex terminated by an acumen; margins presenting more or less prominent deltoid indentations, terminated by a thick point; upper surface of blade glabrous with the principal veins scattered with coarse whitish hairs, lower surface entirely glabrous; venation pinnate, 4-6 pairs of secondary veins along the midvein; texture thin.

Inflorescences 10-25 cm long, in lax cymes more or less corymbiform. Peduncle cylindrical, slender, 8-20 cm long, glabrous or slightly puberulent; axes glabrescent or finely but sparsely puberulent; bracts deltoid to oblong ovate, obtuse, 0.3-0.5 mm long, densely pubescent. Flowers 1-2 mm long. Pedicel more or less cylindrical, 1.5-3 mm long, sparsely puberulent. Calyx cupuliform, very thick, 0.3-0.4 mm in height, with entire or slightly lobed margin, sparsely puberulent. Corolla glabrous globose, flattened on top; petals deltoid, about 1.5 mm long, very thick. Stamens about 0.5 mm long; anthers more or less ovate. Ovary glabrous.

Berries globose, flattened on top, 4-5 mm in diameter and 3 mm in height, glabrous. Seeds about 2.5 mm long and wide, deltoid cordate, attenuate at the base; rostrum strong triangular; oval deep pits; on the dorsal surface short median relief, pronounced radial reliefs; testa with finely irregular surface.

Types from the islands of the Gulf of Guinea: San Thomé, Welwitsch 1503, and Fernando-Po, Mann 412 (syntypes).

Cayratia debilis is a slender herbaceous vine with whitish greenish or yellowish green flowers; sylvicole, it grows in dense ombrophilous forests, secondary forests, old regrowth areas and forest galleries. It is widely distributed in the forested regions of equatorial and tropical Africa, from Côte d'Ivoire to the region of the Great Lakes and southward to Congo (Léopoldville) and Angola.
