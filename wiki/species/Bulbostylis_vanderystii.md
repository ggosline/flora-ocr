# *Bulbostylis vanderystii* Cherm. Planche 18

**Genus**: [[Bulbostylis]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Rev. Zool. Bot. Africaines 24 : 299 (1934)

## Synonyms

- Abildgaardia vanderystii (Cherm.) Lye, Nordic J. Bot. 1 : 758 (1982).

## Description

Perennial herb, fairly robust, with few or many stems arising from a short woody rhizome; stems 20–40 cm × 0.5–0.7 mm, trigonous, glabrous. Leaf: sheath reddish-brown, truncate, with long hairs at the collar; blade 5–15 cm × 0.3–0.5 mm, canaliculate, almost glabrous to very scabrous at least along the margin. Inflorescence in a dense head 5–10 mm wide, formed of few or many closely packed spikelets, dark reddish-brown to almost black; involucral bracts often shorter than the inflorescence, resembling glumes, reddish-brown, sometimes with the central nerve terminating in a green mucro up to 2 cm long. Spikelet ovoid to lanceolate, approximately 5–7 mm long; the largest glumes 3.5–4.5 mm long, but the upper ones often shorter, finely pilose or scabrous at least near the apex, dark reddish-brown with the central nerve terminating at the apex or slightly exceeding it. Achene 1–1.2 × 0.7–0.8 mm, obovate to obtriangular, pale brown, topped by the persistent base of the dark reddish-brown style; surface of the achene almost smooth.

## Distribution

Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo; very rare in Gabon, found only in Haut-Ogooué (3 collections)

## Ecology

savannas and savanna/forest margins; in Gabon at approximately 380–600 m altitude. Taxonomic note: somewhat resembles B. scabricaulis, but differs from it in the almost smooth stem and much darker spikelet, almost black. B: Goetghebeur & Coudijzer (1984b). Bibliography
