Inflorescence reaching 49 × 12 cm, with approximately 10 cymes; peduncle 9.5–14 cm, puberulent or glabrescenr, bracts persistent, sepals with dense pubescence, long 1–1.5 mm. Flower: pedicel null or reaching 2 mm; calyx with lobes 1.2–2 mm long, densely pubescent externally, ciliate margins without or up to collateral, alternating with those of corolla white, 20–30 mm long in mature bud, densely pubescent externally, tube long ± mm, fairly thickened, broadened at the level of anthers, thickened above them, abruptly widened at the throat with an internal pilose band between the anthers up to above them or at the base of the lobes, pubescence less dense towards the lobes, with narrowly elliptic to narrowly obovate lobes, sometimes as long as the tube, mm long, densely pubescent externally on uncovered portions of the bud, glabrous to pilose externally on covered portions; stamens inserted on approximately 20% of the tube length, anther 1.2–1.4 mm long; pistil ± 2.5 mm long, ovary densely puberulent; style glabrous. Fruit globose to pyriform, yellow to reddish, velvety, with 4 to 20 seeds. Distribution Cameroon and Gabon: Estuaire, Ngounié, Nyanga, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem. Ecology Primary and secondary forest in Gabon, up to m altitude, elsewhere up to m.
