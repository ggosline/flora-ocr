# *Campylospermum monticola* (Gilg) Cheek, Pl. Mount Cameroon, Conserv. Checklist :

**Genus**: [[Campylospermum]]
**Family**: [[Ochnaceae]]

## Description

92 (1998).

Fruiting branch. Drawing by Sabine Bousani in Neuchâtel (©) and reproduced with permission.

Tree reaching 15 m in height; branches with brownish bark. Stipules caducous, triangular, 4–5 mm long. Leaf: petiole 3–8 mm; lamina elliptic to narrowly elliptic or narrowly elliptic-obovate, (7–)13–26(–45) × (3–)5–8(–9) cm, ratio 2–4, cuneate at base, slightly acuminate to obtuse at apex, coriaceous, plane, margin densely spinescent-serrate to spaced teeth; main vein plane to slightly prominent on upper surface, prominent on lower surface, lateral veins in 11 to 25 pairs, tertiary venation scalariform, perpendicular to main vein, distinct above, very distinct below. Inflorescence terminal, branched or sometimes simple, rather dense, main axis (7–)10–20(–31) cm; gemmate scales absent; racemes 0 to 5, (3–)6–18(–21) cm long, more or less spreading, the lower one sometimes branched; cymules distant 0.5–1 cm, (1–)3- to 10-flowered. Flower: pedicel (5–)7–15(–17) mm, articulate 1–4(–5) mm from base; sepals narrowly ovate, 6–7 × 2–3 mm in flower, 6–7 × 3–5 mm in fruit, acute at apex, spreading in fruit; petals obovate, 5–10 × 4–8 mm, cuneate at base, rounded or slightly emarginate at apex; stamens: anthers 5–6 mm long; style about 5 mm. Fruit: receptacle 5 × 5 mm; drupels 3 or 4 well developed per receptacle, ellipsoid to broadly ellipsoid, 6–10 × 4–6 mm; cotyledons incumbent, unequal, with small external cotyledon.

## Distribution

from Guinea-Bissau to Cameroon, to Central African Republic, Gabon, northern Republic of the Congo and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, rare, known only from Ogooué-Ivindo and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

primary and secondary forest, swamp forest, dry forest, or gallery forest, coastal savanna; on sandy, lateritic and clayey soil; outside of Gabon at 15–2200 m altitude. Taxonomic note: This species is generally easy to recognize by its leaves with spinulescent-serrate margins and the branched inflorescence with comparatively small flowers, in rather closely spaced cymules.
