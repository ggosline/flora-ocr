# *Eremospatha hookeri* (G.Mann & H.Wendl.) H.Wendl. Planche 7

**Genus**: [[Eremospatha]]
**Family**: [[Arecaceae]]

## Description

in De Kerchove de Denterghem, Palmiers : 244 (1878). Cespitose liana; stem cylindrical, up to 30 m, up to 25 mm in diameter. Leaf: sheath longitudinally striated, glabrous or covered with black pilosity; ochrea entire, with obliquely truncate apex; knee present, 1.5–3 cm in length; juvenile leaves with petiole 8–10 cm, with bifid blade, or with naked rachis (without leaflets), up to 80 cm; mature leaves without petiole; rachis up to 1.5 m, concave above, rounded below, margins armed with spines; blade comprising up to 20 pairs of unequidistant leaflets, pointed forward or rarely backward, of very variable forms, obovate-elliptic or narrowly obovate-elliptic to almost rhomboidal, 12–22 × 3.5–5.5 cm, apex narrowly to very broadly premorse, margins armed with spines, the basal ones narrower than the others, linear to ovoid, reflexed around the leaf sheath; cirrus 50–70 cm, margins armed with spines, spine-leaflets 2–2.5 cm in length. Inflorescence up to 40 cm in length; peduncle 12–18 cm; rachis 18–22 cm, straight, with triangular bracts 0.5–1.5 mm in length; floriferous branches in 8 to 10 pairs, 8–12 cm in length, decreasing distally in length. Flower unknown. Fruit ovoid to cylindric, 2.5–3 × 1.5–1.7 cm at maturity, covered with 20 to 21 rows of scales, with 1 or 2 seeds. Seed 2 × 1.2 × 0.6 cm, compressed, margins slightly undulate.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea and Gabon; in Gabon, present in the Estuaire, the Ngounié and in the north of Woleu-Ntem. Conservation status: Least Concern (LC)

## Ecology

humid tropical forest, common under the canopy, but also along forest edges; in Gabon between 10 and 500 m in altitude, elsewhere between 10 and 600 m

## Vernacular names

alua-nlong (fang) ; gigorula (éshira). B : Faye et al. (2014), Cosiaux et al. (2018), Pajarón et al. (2015), Raponda-Walker & Sillans i B liographie (1961), Sunderland (2007, 2012)
