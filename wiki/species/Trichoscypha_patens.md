# *Trichoscypha patens* (Oliv.) Engl. Planche 22(C)

**Genus**: [[Trichoscypha]]
**Family**: [[Anacardiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 1 : 425 (1881)

## Description

Tree reaching 22 m in height, or liane (see Notes); trunk up to 40 cm in diameter; branchlets glabrous. Leaf up to 80 cm in length, glabrous; petiole 5–12 cm; leaflets 7 to 11(–15), petiolule ± 7 mm, with elliptic to obovate or oblong blade, (5–)10–18(–26) × (2–)3–7(–9) cm, cuneate or sometimes rounded at the base, often caudate-acuminate over 1.5(–2) cm at the apex, coriaceous, midvein plane or prominent above, lateral veins in (5–)7 to 11(–14) pairs. Inflorescence (sub)terminal, generally pendulous in lax panicle, up to 60 × 30 cm, glabrous or very sparsely puberulent. Male flower: pedicel 1–2 mm, glabrous; calyx ± 0.5 mm in length, scarcely lobed, glabrous; petals slightly imbricate, yellow, spreading to reflexed, ovate-elliptic, 1.5 mm in length, glabrous; stamens 1 mm in length, distinctly shorter than the petals, with anthers 0.3 mm in length; disk lobulate, glabrous. Female flower: pedicel and calyx as in the male flower; petals somewhat larger than in the male flower; staminodes ≤ 0.5 mm in length; disk glabrous; pistil glabrous, 1 mm in length; stigmas 3, shortly (< 0.5 mm) stipitate. Fruit ellipsoidal, laterally compressed or not, up to 4 × 3 cm, red at maturity, smooth, glabrous.

## Distribution

southeastern Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea (Rio Muni), Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué, Ngounié, Ogooué-Ivindo and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

Primary and secondarized forest; in Gabon at 150–600 m altitude, elsewhere up to 650 m

## Source note

Most collectors reported this species as a tree, but a few as a liane or as a liane with a question mark. B: Breteler (2004), Van der Veken (1960a). Bibliography
