# *Trichomanes erosum* Willdenow (P.L. X)

**Genus**: [[Trichomanes]]
**Family**: [[Hymenophyllaceae]]

## Synonyms

- Trichomanes muscoides Hooker (non Swartz), Syn. Fil.: 75 (1866) (pp.).
- Microgonium erosum (Willd.) Copeland, Phil. Journ. Sc. 67 : 62 (1958).

## Description

— Willd., Sp. Pl. 5 : 501 (1810). — Tard., Mém. IFAN 28 : 41, pl. 4, f. 3-4 (1953) (pp.). — Alston, FWTA : 30 (1959).

Rhizome extensively creeping, filiform, bearing black, caducous hairs. Petiole always shorter than the blade, winged at the apex. Blade obovate-oblong, 1-4 cm long, entire or irregularly lacerate-lobed. Margins plane. Coloration very dark green in the fresh state. Venation pinnate. False marginal vein present, false lateral veins anastomosing with it. Sori chiefly in the upper part of the frond, with campanulate, included indusia.

## Type

Flügge, royaume d'Oware et du Bénin (S Nigeria). Afrique tropicale et orientale, Angola, Rhodésie

## Discussion

On rocks near rivers, or epiphytic.

## Specimens examined

N. Hallé : 629, La Nkoulounga entre les riv. Mondah et Noya; 2216, Abanga, monts de Cristal.
