# *Monopetalanthus pectinatus* A. Chev.

**Genus**: [[Monopetalanthus]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: A. Chevalier Rev. Bot. appl.: 593, pl. 14 (1946)

## Description

Leaves paripinnate with 20-25 pairs of opposite leaflets. Rachis short, 6-8 cm, villous. Leaflets oblong, linear, 7-14 × 2-3.5 mm, with apex somewhat falciform, terminated by an acute point. Primary nervure forming the anterior margin. Base truncate, parallel to the rachis, attached at the summit of the anterior angle, prolonged into a posterior auricle. Stipules persistent, very large, enveloping, 2 cm × 1.5 mm fused into an intrapetiolary blade striated and furnished at its base with veined auricles.

Inflorescences in short axillary racemes of 2 cm, densely flowered, with reddish-villous axes. Flowers white or whitish-pink surrounded at first by bracts of 7-8 mm, reddish-villous, striated, caducous. Bracteoles 6 mm, villous. Calyx absent or reduced to small villous teeth. One large petal, unguiculate, ovate, 6-8 mm long. Stamens 10; 9 briefly fused at the base, 1 free. Ovary briefly stipitate, hirsute. Ovules 2-3. Fruit unknown.

## Type

Le Testu 6069, Gabon (P)

## Discussion

Species close to M. microphyllus Harms and M. compactus Hutch. from Sierra Leone from which it differs by its large persistent stipules.

## Specimens examined

Le Testu : 6069, Comi Mavaco, Haute-Ngounié (fl. sept.).
