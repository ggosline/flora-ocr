# *Dictyophleba lucida* (K.Schum.) Pierre

**Genus**: [[Dictyophleba]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Linn. Paris , sér. 2, 1 : 93 (1898)

## Description

Liane with ramifications smooth and glabrous to scabrous or hirsute. Stipules quickly caducous, 1.5–3 mm long, triangular, with axillary colleters. Leaf: petiole 0.5–1 mm; limb obovate to elliptic or narrowly obovate to narrowly elliptic, rarely ovate, 3–17 × 2–8 cm, acuminate to retuse at apex; secondary veins 6 to 13 pairs. Inflorescence 3–23 × 1–5 cm with alternate cymes with several flowers; peduncle 3–11 cm. Flower slightly fragrant: pedicel reaching 1.3 mm; calyx with erect, oval to oblong lobes, 1.5–3 times as long as wide, 1.5–3 mm long, ciliolate; corolla white to pale yellow or pinkish, often with darker tube, in bud yellow, red or violet, 6–8 mm long, with tube 1.5–2 times as long as calyx, 6–8 mm long, with lobes 1.5–2 times as long as wide, 2–4 mm long, with 0.5–1 mm hairs on the margin; stamens with anther 1.7–2.1 mm long; style 5.5–10 mm. Fruit globose depressed, 12–50 × 10–35 mm, red, orange or yellow, with acidulous pulp, with 1 to 29 seeds. Seed ellipsoid, flattened, 6–8 × 4–5 × 2–3 mm, smooth.

## Distribution

from Nigeria to the Comores; in Gabon: Ogooué-Ivindo. Ecology: rainforest in Gabon up to 600 m altitude, elsewhere up to 800 m. Notes: At the time of preparation of the taxonomic revision by de Hoogh, this species was not known from Gabon. Today there are still no known flowering specimens from Gabon, but the specimen Wieringa et al. 3641 in fruit appears to correspond to it.
