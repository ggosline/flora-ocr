# *Fimbristylis ferruginea* (L.) Vahl Planche 93

**Genus**: [[Fimbristylis]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Enum. pl. 2 : 291 (1805)

## Synonyms

- Scirpus ferrugineus L., Sp. pl. éd. 2, 1 : 74 (1762).

## Description

Perennial herb with a short rhizome forming dense tufts up to 60 cm wide; stems 40–100 cm × 1–2 mm, compressed, glabrous or scabrous below the inflorescence. Lower leaves reduced to sheaths, the upper leaves progressively bearing a blade; sheath greyish, hairy, somewhat coriaceous; blade up to 10(–30) cm × 1.5–2 mm. Inflorescence usually in a simple anthela with few or many shortly pedunculate spikelets, or almost all spikelets sessile or subsessile, the inflorescence then appearing almost capitate; involucral bracts short (4–5 mm at most), green and foliaceous. Spikelet 4–18 mm long, acute, brown with a greyish tinge; glumes numerous, 3–4 mm long, reddish-brown, the upper part with dense fine greyish-white hairs. Flower with 3 stamens; style fringed, compressed, 2-branched. Achene obovoid lenticular, approximately 1.2 × 1 mm, almost smooth; epidermal cells small, isodiametric.

## Distribution

in all tropical regions with saline soils, especially near sea level; in Gabon, found only in Estuaire and Ogooué-Maritime (7 collections)

## Ecology

bordering mangroves and sandy beaches near the sea, rarely in muddy pools in inland areas; principally in saline habitats

## Vernacular names

West Indian fimbry (angl.). Note taxonomique : se reconnaît facilement à ses glumes densément pubescentes sur la partie supérieure. B : Goetghebeur & Coudijzer (1984a), Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et ibliographie al. (2010), Hooper & Napper (1972), Lye (1995, 1997)
