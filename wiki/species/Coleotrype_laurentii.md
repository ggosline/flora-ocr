# *Coleotrype laurentii* K.Schum. Planche 5, Figure 1G

**Genus**: [[Coleotrype]]
**Family**: [[Commelinaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 33 : 377 (1903), comme « Coleotripe »

## Description

, as "Coleotripe". Decumbent herb, up to 1.2 m tall, glabrous; fibrous roots; stem with a slight white exudate. Leaves well-spaced along the stem; sheath reaching 3.5 cm long, glabrous, apex ciliolate; blade elliptic to narrowly elliptic, 15−22 × 3−6 cm, base attenuate into an almost winged pseudopetiole, apex subcaudate, margins ciliolate toward the base, scabrous toward the apex, lustrous, upper surface with an inframarginous band of very short hairs, otherwise glabrous, lower surface glabrous. Inflorescence 2−3 × 2−3.5 cm, dark green, containing 3 to 8 pluriflorous cincinni; external bracts ± reniform, 1−1.5 × 1.2−2 cm, widely attached at the base, sparsely hairy or glabrous, the internal ones ovate to oblong, keeled at least at the apex, 0.5−2 × 0.4−0.8 cm, glabrous. Flower: bracteoles similar to the internal bracts; sepals very narrowly obovate to narrowly obovate-elliptic-oblong, 12−15(−16 in fruit) × 1.5−3 mm, ciliolate at the apex, apex cucullate, keeled, hairy on the keel, the external one strongly cucullate with a broad tuft of hairs; corolla white, tube 10−12 mm long, with 3 oblong-elliptic lobes, strongly recurved, 6−9 × 2−3 mm, apex rounded, margins recurved; stamens: filament with free portion of 3−4 mm, glabrous or with a few hairs at the base, anther ± 1−1.5 × 1 mm, introrse, cream-colored; ovary 3-locular, 2−3 × 1−1.5 mm, apex densely hairy, locules with 2 ovules; style 13.5−16 mm, exceeding the corolla by 5.5−7.7 mm, equal to or longer than the stamens, curved to one side, stigma capitate. Fruit sessile, cylindric-trigonous, ± 11 × 4.5 mm, base and apex rounded, apex hairy, persistent style base, locules with 1 or 2 seeds. Seed ellipsoid, 2.4−2.8 × 1.6−1.9 mm (excluding the aril appendage); aril brown, drawn out into an ± linear prolongation, of 0.8−1.4 mm, situated at the base or apex of the seed.

## Distribution

Ivory Coast, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Central African Republic, Uganda, Tanzania and Angola; in Gabon in Ogooué-Ivindo and Ogooué-Lolo

## Ecology

forests and sometimes abundant in clearings; in Gabon at 300 m altitude, elsewhere from 300 to 1050 m. Flowering in Gabon in January. Flowers opening from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. in West Africa (Brenan 1986b). B: Brenan (1968a, 1968b), Faden (2012), Sosef et al. (2006). Bibliography
