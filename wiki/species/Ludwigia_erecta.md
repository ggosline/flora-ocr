# *Ludwigia erecta* (L.) H.Hara

**Family**: [[Onagraceae]] · **Genus**: [[Ludwigia]]

J. Jap. Bot. **28**(10): 292 (1953).

## Description

Glabrous, much-branched herb, sometimes woody at base, 0.5–3 m high; stems four-angled, fleshy, hollow, green to reddish. Petiole 1–12 mm; blade narrowly ovate-elliptic to elliptic, 2–13 × 0.3–3.5(–4.5) cm, narrowly cuneate and acute to acuminate; lateral veins 16–27 pairs. Flowers solitary in upper axils; pedicel 0.5–1 mm; sepals 4, triangular, 3–5 mm; petals 4, obovate-oblong, 4–6 mm, golden yellow; stamens 8; ovary about 8 × 1.5 mm; style 0.5–1 mm. Fruit subsessile or on a pedicel to 2 mm, four-angled, slightly curved, 10–19 × 2–2.5 mm, glabrous, with several rows of free seeds. Seeds 0.3–0.5 × about 0.2 mm with a narrow raphe.

## Distribution

Native to Central and South America and widely introduced. In Gabon it is known from Sibang in Estuaire and Lastourville in Ogooué-Lolo.

## Habitat and ecology

Wet places, watersides, coastal sand, mud and wet fallow; to 325 m in Gabon and 1200 m elsewhere.

Recognizable by the subsessile, glabrous, four-angled capsules with flat faces. Reported as new for Gabon in this treatment.
