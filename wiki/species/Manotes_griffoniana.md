# *Manotes griffoniana* Baillon

**Genus**: [[Manotes]]
**Family**: [[Connaraceae]]
**Protologue**: BAILLON, Adansonia, sér. 1, 7: 244 (1867); BAKER, FTA 1: 460 (1868); SCHELLENBERG, PR 103 (4, 127): 58 (1938); TROUPIN, FCBR 3: 74 (1952); EXELL & MENDONÇA, CFA 2: 140 (1954); JONGKIND, in BRETELER (éd.), AUWP 89 (6): 302 (1989). TYPE: Gabon, Pyrat Griffon du Bellay 292 (holo-P)

## Description

SCHELLENBERG, PR 103 (4, 127): 58 (1938); TROUPIN, FCBR 3: 74 (1952); EXELL & MENDONÇA, CFA 2: 140 (1954); JONGKIND, in BRETELER (ed.), AUWP 89 (6): 302 (1989). TYPE: Gabon, Pyrat Griffon du Bellay 292 (holo-P).

- M. tomentosa GILG, EBJ 14: 331 (1891). TYPE: Gabon, Sibange Farm, near Libreville, Soyaux 432 (holo-B delet.; lecto-K; iso-Z).

Very large vine reaching up to 30 m in length or shrub. Young branches with dense indumentum of long hairs and lenticels.

Leaves. Petiole 2.5–8.5 cm long; rachis 4–14 cm long pubescent; petiolules 1–4 mm long; leaflets 7–13, ovate to elliptic, pubescent, base cordate to acute; apex acuminate; young leaflets pink; terminal leaflet 4.3–12 × 1.5–5 cm; lateral leaflets 1.9–12 × 0.9–4 cm.

Inflorescences ≤ 40 cm long, pubescent. Pedicel, length above articulation 0.5–2 mm.

Flowers. Floral bud, at calyx opening 1–1.2(–1.7) × 1–1.2(1.5) mm. Sepals lanceolate (1.3–1.7–2(–2.5) × (0.7)1–1.2 mm, grayish-white to dark red but generally pink. Petals 5.5–7.5 × 0.8–1.3 mm, reflexed, at first white often with pink, then yellow. Sepals and petals frequently bearing 3 types of hairs.

Stamens, the long ones 3–6.5 mm, the short ones 1.5–4 mm, filaments with glandular hairs and generally as many spherical hairs. Pistil 2–5.5 mm long, not equal to the stamens, ovary entirely covered with long hairs; style with glandular and spherical hairs (already present in the bud, Pl. 29, p. 97).

Fruit, follicles 1–5 of ≤ 20 × 10 mm, orange-brownish to red-brownish, with long spherical hairs and sometimes as many glandular hairs. For the indumentum of the carpels and follicles, see Pl. 29, p. 97.

Seeds ≈ 10 × ≈ 5 mm; testa for the most part fleshy and red, dorsal part thin and black.

Epigeal germination; hypocotyl strongly elongated with dense pubescence; first pairs of leaves opposite and often unifoliolate, membranous. – Pl. 30, 1, 2, 4–9, 11–13, p. 99.
