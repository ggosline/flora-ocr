# *Lasiodiscus contumax* N. Hallé

**Family**: [[Rhamnaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Lasiodiscus]]

## Description

Gabonese species described as new in Vol. 4, separated in the treatment key by its leaf indument and inflorescence characters. Flowers small and 5-merous; disc hairy; fruit drupaceous.

## Distribution

Known from Gabon.
