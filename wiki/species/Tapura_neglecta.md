# *Tapura neglecta* N. Hallé & Heine

**Genus**: [[Tapura]]
**Family**: [[Dichapetalaceae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia, sér. 2, 7 (1): 47 (1987); Breteler, AUWP 86 (3): 67-69 (1986). TYPE : Gabon, Mandji (Iboundji), Le Testu 6090 (holo-, P ; iso-, BR, WAG)

## Description

Adansonia, ser. 2, 7 (1): 47 (1987); Breteler, AUWP 86 (3): 67-69 (1986). TYPE: Gabon, Mandji (Iboundji), Le Testu 6090 (holo-, P; iso-, BR, WAG).

Shrub (or small tree?). Young branches brownish with short appressed hairs initially, soon glabrescent.

Stipules early caducous, narrow and triangular to lanceolate, approximately 3 mm long.

Leaves. Petiole canaliculate above, (4) 6-9 mm long, glabrous to furnished with short and scattered hairs. Blade coriaceous, obovate-elliptic, 7-12 × 2.5-5 cm; base cuneate; acumen short, 0.5-1 cm, rounded at the apex; margin often somewhat revolute; primary vein I and the 4-7 pairs of main secondary veins II, flat above and prominent below; upper surface glabrous; lower surface with subappressed hairs scattered on vein I and the main lateral veins, sometimes also with arachnoid hairs between these major veins; glands fairly small only beneath the blade, especially in its lower half.

Inflorescences axillary, not fused to the petiole, sessile or nearly so, generally condensed and with rather indistinct branching, composed of capitula, from a few to numerous, shortly stipitate to sessile, and ≤ 10 flowers; inflorescence axis very short or somewhat elongated, with subappressed pubescence. Pedicel ≤ 1.5 mm; lower portion ≤ 0.5 mm with short and appressed hairs; upper portion 0.5-1 mm, glabrous.

Flowers shortly pedicellate, 4-5 mm long. Sepals unequal, appressed or nearly so, thick, concave, ovate-elliptic, 2-3 × 1.5-2 mm; margin ciliate and outer side sometimes bearing a few hairs. Petals 5, fused to the 5 stamens in a tube 2.5 mm high; free apical portions 1-1.5 mm long; exterior of tube with short and scattered hairs in the upper region; free portions glabrous; inner surface with a tomentum especially in the upper region of the tube and extending somewhat onto the free portions; 2 large petals with bicuculate apical portion, 1.5 mm long and split for 0.5 mm; 3 small petals with entire, narrow and triangular to lanceolate apical portion. Stamens, 3 fertile and 2 without anthers; the fertile ones as long as the large petals, the 2 lateral ones (adjacent to the small petals) furnished with a small subacute lobe (adjacent to the neighboring small petal), anthers approximately 0.5 mm long, connective distinct and slightly muricate; free portion of sterile filaments narrow and triangular to lanceolate, pubescent at the apex. Staminode, 1, opposite the large petals, semiannular, 0.7 mm high, glabrous. Pistil 3-merous, approximately 5 mm long; ovary depressed with short dense velvety pubescence; style generally curved or inclined, glabrous toward the base and villous in the middle and upper portions, apical portion attached to the tube opposite the large petals and terminated by 3 short glabrous exsert lobes. Fruits unknown. - Pl. 65, p. 213.
