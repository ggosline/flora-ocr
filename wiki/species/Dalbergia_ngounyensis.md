# *Dalbergia ngounyensis* Pellegr.

**Genus**: [[Dalbergia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Bot. France 92 : 92 (1945)

## Description

Liane climbing high into trees, inodorous, apparently without spines or prehensile branches; bark dark brown, with light lenticels often poorly visible; young branches with semi-erect hairs ± hooked, short and dense. Stipules caducous, elliptic ± falciform, 2.3–3.5 × 1.3–1.5 mm, obtuse, shortly pubescent. Leaf imparipinnate; petiole 0.6–1.7 cm; rachis 4.5–11.2 cm, pubescent as the branches; leaflets 4 to 11, alternate and unequal, with petiolule of 1.5–4 mm, limb elliptic or slightly obovate, symmetric, 4.5–7.1 × 2.2–3.3 cm in the upper leaflets and 2.7–4.5 × 1.7–2.9 cm in the lower ones, rounded or acute at the base, acuminate at the apex, coriaceous, browning when dry, upper face glabrous or with very short hairs on the midvein only, lower face with dense and persistent golden hairs, appressed, very short and unidirectional; lateral veins 7 to 10, poorly visible to very prominent, veinlets in a slightly prominent and ± scalariform network. Inflorescence in a short panicle of unilateral racemes, axillary and terminal on leafy branches, 1.5–2.5 cm in length, with brown-golden hairs similar to those on the branches; bracts persistent at flowering, broadly ovate, ± 0.6 × 0.8 mm, pubescent. Flower: pedicel 1–2.5 mm; bracteoles persistent at flowering, broadly ovate, ± 0.7 × 0.4 mm, pubescent; calyx with appressed dense brown-golden hairs, tube 1.5–2 × 2–2.5 mm, truncate or with ± equal valvate teeth shortly triangular of < 0.4 mm; corolla white, petals with claw much exceeding the calyx, standard bent obliquely, with claw of 2.5–3 mm and suborbicular to obcordate limb of 4.5 × 4.5–5 mm, with rounded base and bilobed apex, wings equal to or slightly exceeding the keel, with claw of 3 mm and elliptic limb of 4–4.5 × 1.5–1.8 mm, smooth externally, with rounded or truncate base, keel distinctly curved, with claw of 2.5–3 mm and limb of 3.5–4.5 × 1.5–2 mm, with rounded basal auricle; stamens in 2 phalanges of 5, filaments ± 6 mm, fused on the ¾, straight at the base and curved in their free part; ovary 1.5–2 × 0.7 mm, glabrous and very finely verrucose, with 2 ovules and straight stipe of 3–3.5 mm; style linear, 1.3–1.5 mm, with punctiform stigma. Pod unknown.

## Distribution

endemic to Gabon, known only from two collections in Ngounié and Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology

lowland forest, at ± 250 m. Flowering in May and June

## Source note

D. ngounyensis is considered here as endemic to Gabon. The Congolese specimens referred to this species by Cronquist (1954) differ from those of Gabon by their non-verrucose ovary, and their generally larger leaflets with more prominent veinlets; they probably belong to a new taxon. The description of the fruit by Cronquist (1954) refers to this material; the fruits of the true D. ngounyensis are still unknown. D. ngounyensis is very close to D. lastoursvillensis, from which it differs by the characters noted in the key; we do not share Cronquist's (1954) opinion that these two species are synonymous. It is also very close to D. pachycarpa, but is distinguished from it by the golden leaflets on the lower face and by the verrucose ovary. Bibliography: Cronquist (1954), Pellegrin (1949).
