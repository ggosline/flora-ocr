# *Dalbergia lastoursvillensis* Pellegr.

**Genus**: [[Dalbergia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Bot. France 92 : 92 (1945)

## Description

Vine (?) inodorous, apparently without spines or prehensile branches; bark dark brown with clearly visible pale lenticels; young branches with short and dense ± appressed pubescence. Stipules early caducous, elliptic, ± falciform, 3.5–6 × 1.3–2 mm, obtuse, with short appressed and scattered hairs. Leaf imparipinnate; petiole 0.6–1.8 cm; rachis (0.9–)1.9–7 cm, with short and dense hairs similar to those of the branches; leaflets (3–) 5 to 7, alternate and unequal, with petiolule of 3–6 mm, blade elliptic to slightly obovate, symmetric, 4.2–10.8 × 2.3–5.2 cm in the upper leaflets and 2–4.4 × 1.5–3 cm in the lower ones, rounded or obtuse at the base, acuminate at the apex, coriaceous, browning when dry, with upper surface glabrescent (with very scattered appressed hairs, eventually caducous) and lower surface covered with very dense and persistent copper-coloured hairs, appressed and ± unidirectional; lateral veins 6 to 12 pairs, well marked, clearly ascending and reaching almost the margin of the blade, veinlets in a dense and concolorous reticulum, prominent on the lower surface. Inflorescence in a short and dense panicle of unilateral racemes, axillary and terminal on the leafy branches, 1.5–3.5 cm in length, with dense golden-brown hairs; bracts persistent at flowering, ovate to broadly elliptic, ± 1 × 0.7 mm, pubescent. Flower: pedicel 2–3 mm; bracteoles persistent at flowering, broadly elliptic, 0.8–1.2 × 0.4 mm, pubescent; calyx with short and very dense golden-brown hairs, tube 2.5 × 2–3 mm, teeth valvate ± equal and broadly triangular, 0.3–0.7 mm in length; petals with claw shorter than the calyx or barely exceeding it, standard bent at a right angle, with claw of 1.7–2.3 mm and suborbicular blade of 3.3–4.5 × 3.7–5 mm, slightly cordate at the base and bilobed at the apex, wings somewhat longer than the keel, with claw

Pod and seed unknown.

## Distribution

endemic to Gabon, known only from two collections in the Ngounié and Ogooué-Lolo

## Ecology

habitat unknown, probably in lowland forest. Flowering in May and August

## Source note

D. lastoursvillensis resembles D. ngounyensis closely by its copper-coloured leaflets on the lower surface, but differs from it by the floral characters indicated in the key. The synonymization of the two species by Cronquist (1954) therefore appears to us unjustified. b: Cronquist (1954), Pellegrin (1949). Bibliography
