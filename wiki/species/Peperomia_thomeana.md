# *Peperomia thomeana* C.DC. Planche 26.2

**Genus**: [[Peperomia]]
**Family**: [[Piperaceae]]
**Protologue**: P. vacciniifolia C.DC., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 19 : 226 (1894)

## Description

in Henriques, Bol. Soc. Brot. 10 : 154 (1892). P. vacciniifolia C.DC., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 19 : 226 (1894). Perennial herb, epiphytic, up to 40 cm tall, glabrous. Leaves alternate; petiole 4–10 mm; blade elliptic, 1.5–4 × 1–2 cm, with obtuse to rounded-emarginate apex, with 3 palmate nerves. Inflorescence in spikes, several but rarely more than 5, terminal or subterminal, 1–3 cm × 1–2.5 mm; peduncle 10–15 mm. Fruit globose, ± 1 mm in diameter.

## Distribution

Cameroon, São Tomé, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Tanzania and Madagascar; in Gabon very rare and known from a single collection from Estuaire

## Ecology

epiphytic or terrestrial in dense submontane and montane forest; in Gabon at 490 m altitude, elsewhere up to 2500 m

## Source note

Species with disjunct distribution, apparently very frequent in Cameroon. B : Düll (1973). Bibliography
