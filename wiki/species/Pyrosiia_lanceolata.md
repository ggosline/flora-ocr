# *Pyrosiia lanceolata* (L.) Farwell

**Genus**: [[Pyrosiia]]
**Family**: [[Polypodiaceae]]

## Synonyms

- Acrostichum lanceolatum L., Sp. Pl.: 1067 (1753).
- Polypodium adnascens Hk., Syn. Fil.: 349 (1868) (pp. non Sw.).

## Description

— Farwell, Am. Midl. Nat. 12: 245 (1931). — Tard. Mém. IFAN: 224 (1953). — Alston, FWTA: 46 (1959).

Rhizome very extensively creeping, with fronds very widely spaced, bearing lanceolate scales, attenuate, with peltate base, margins furnished with long hyaline projections (especially at the apex of the scale), formed of cells with thin walls, colourless lumen. Frond very shortly petiolate (petiole at most 1 cm long), lanceolate or linear-lanceolate, 6–10 cm long by 1–1.5 cm wide, cuneate at the base, with rounded apex. Texture coriaceous. Lower face bearing stellate scales, greyish, with 6–8 broad, equal, obtuse branches; upper face bearing similar scales but caducous. Fertile frond slightly longer than the sterile, reaching 10–15 cm by 0.8–1 cm wide, with lower face covered with grey scales; sori situated in the upper part of the frond, immersed in the furrows.

in Herb. Hermann (1, fol. 3) (BM). Ile du Prince, Congo, Afrique orientale, sud de l'Asie. Épiphyte sur Elaeis
