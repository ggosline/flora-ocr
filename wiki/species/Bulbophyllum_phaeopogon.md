# *Bulbophyllum phaeopogon* Schltr.

**Genus**: [[Bulbophyllum]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 38 : 157 (1906)

## Synonyms

- Bulbophyllum gentilii Rolfe, Gard. Chron, ser. 3, 35 : 266 (1904).
- Bulbophyllum schinzianum Kraenzl. var. phaeopogon (Schltr.) J. J. Verm., Bull. Jard. Bot. Nat. Belg. 56 : 229 (1986).

## Description

Pseudobulbs 1-6 × 0.8-3 cm, orbicular to narrowly ovoid, 1-leaved. Leaf petiolate; 7.5-32 × 1.4-6.5 cm, oblong, rounded, sometimes subacu­te. Inflorescence dense to sublax, 28-102 cm, 11-102-flowered. Flowers medium-sized, glabrous, incomplete opening. Dorsal sepal glabrous, oblong-elliptic, subacute; lateral sepals obliquely oblong-elliptic, subacute to subobtuse. Petals linear falcate, acute to acuminate. Lip rather thick, lower part linear, ovate to elliptic, apex rounded, margins covered with dense and thick hairs, short with large papillae on the adaxial surface and less than 4 mm long on the abaxial surface. – Pl. 68, p. 141.

## Distribution

Côté d'Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroun, Gabon, Congo (Kinshasa)

## Ecology

alt. 0-500 m. Epiphyte on branches of large trees in primary forest and in forest relicts in savanna
