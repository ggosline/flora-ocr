# *Fimbristylis scabrida* Schumach. & Thonning Planche 98

**Genus**: [[Fimbristylis]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Beskriv. Guin. pl . : 32 (1828)

## Description

Perennial herb, in tufts, with the base swollen covered by the fibrous remnants of disintegrated old leaf sheaths; stems 35–50 cm × 0.7–1 mm, rounded or slightly compressed above, deeply striated, scabrous along the entire length. Leaf with pale sheath with thin greyish wings; ligule absent; blade approximately 10 cm × 1–2.5 mm, plane or slightly canaliculate, maintaining its width to the apex, with very scabrous margin, apex somewhat oblique, acuminate or tooth-like, often with numerous spinous hairs. Inflorescence in an open anthela; peduncles rather long and slender; main involucral bract foliaceous, 1–3 cm long. Spikelet lanceolate and cylindric, approximately 6–14 × 2 mm; glumes approximately 3 mm long, rather triangular, reddish-brown with pale margin, shiny surface, glabrous or scabrous near the central nerve always prolonged by a point. Achene obovoid, 1.2–1.3 × 1.2 mm, brownish, trigonous, but without distinct ribs separating the faces, surface with numerous warts.

## Distribution

Sierra Leone, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Togo, Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Uganda; very rare in Gabon, found only in the Lopé National Park (Ogooué-Ivindo) (1 collection)

## Ecology

in well-drained grassy and sandy areas of savannas, especially in heavily grazed places; in Gabon, at approximately 150 m altitude, up to 950 m in East Africa. Taxonomic note: only Gabonese species with wide leaves and very scabrous stems; somewhat resembles F. dichotoma, but it has a more fibrous base and very different fruit.
