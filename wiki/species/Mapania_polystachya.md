# *Mapania polystachya* (Cherm.) Lye Figure 18a

**Genus**: [[Mapania]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Lidia 7 : 90 (2011)

## Synonyms

- Hypolytrum polystachyum Cherm., Bull. Soc. Bot. France 80 : 507 (1933).

## Description

Robust herb, with a short erect rhizome up to 2 cm thick; central stem, solitary, 60–130 cm × 2–10 mm, triangular, green to brownish, smooth below, distinctly scabrous above. Leaves numerous, mostly basal, but some arranged along the stem, shorter or longer than the stem; sheath green, glabrous; blade flat or slightly folded, the largest 60–150 × 3–4.5 cm, gradually narrowed to an acute apex, margins and principal ribs scabrous to finely dentate. Inflorescence in a terminal, lax panicle, 12–40 × 12–20 cm, light brown, with 6–15 primary branches 2–10 cm long; involucral bracts foliaceous, often spreading, linear, green, apex acute; the lower one generally 20–40(–80) × 2–2.5(–3.5) cm; spikes cylindric, 6–8 × 1.5–2 mm, brown variegated with light brown, apex obtuse; spicoid bracts very crowded, oblong to obovate, approximately 3 × 1.5–2 mm, pale in the lower third, becoming gradually darker brown towards the middle, and abruptly pale brown again in the upper third, glabrous, apex obtuse. Flower: floral bracts 2, oblong, approximately 3 mm long, keel not winged and scabrous, apex obtuse. Achene unknown.

## Distribution

endemic to Gabon and found only in the region of Lastoursville in Ogooué-Lolo (2 collections)

## Ecology

humid forests, sometimes along rivers and watercourses; at approximately 250 m altitude. Taxonomic note: M. polystachya is recognized by its spicoid bracts with characteristic stripes, pale brown with a central band of medium brown. b: Nelmes (1955a). ibliography
