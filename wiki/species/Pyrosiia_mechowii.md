# *Pyrosiia mechowii* (Hier.) Alston

**Genus**: [[Pyrosiia]]
**Family**: [[Polypodiaceae]]

## Synonyms

- Cyclophorus Mechowii Hier., Engl. Bot. Jahrb. 46 : 395 (1911).
- Pyrrosia Schimperiana var. Mechowii (Hier.) Schelpe, J. S. Afr. Bot. 18 : 129 (1952). — Tard., Mém. IFAN 28 : 225, pl. 43, f. 5 (1953).

## Description

— Alston, Contr. Contr. Fl. Mog. 12 : 37 (1955); FWTA : 46 (1959).

Rhizome creeping, bearing lanceolate scales, peltate at the base, with entire margins, formed of cells with thin walls, those at the apex of the scale thickened and dark. Frond outline linear, gradually narrowed on the short scaly petiole (1-3 cm long), 15-20 cm long by 1.5-2 cm wide, with acuminate apex. Texture coriaceous. Upper surface of frond punctate, glabrous, with midvein scarcely evident. Lower surface bearing greyish scales formed of numerous equal branches. Fertile frond similar to the sterile. Sori localized to the upper part of the frond.
