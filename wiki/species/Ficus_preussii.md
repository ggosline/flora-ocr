# *Ficus preussii* Warburg

**Genus**: [[Ficus]]
**Family**: [[Moraceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. 20 : 156 (1894) ; MILDBR. & BURRET, Bot. Jahrb. 46 : 237 (1911) ; HUTCH. in HUTCH. & RENDLE, FTA 6 (2) : 152 (1916) ; LEBRUN & BOUTIQUE in HAUMAN, FCB 1 : 162 (1948) ; KEAY, FWTA ed. 2, 1 : 607 (1958)

## Description

HUTCH. in HUTCH. & RENDLE, FTA 6 (2) : 152 (1916) ; LEBRUN & BOUTIQUE in HAUMAN, FCB 1 : 162 (1948) ; KEAY, FWTA ed. 2, 1 : 607 (1958).

Epiphytic shrubs (strangler) or tree up to 10 m. Leafy branchlets 8–10 mm thick, ± densely stiffly hairy white to puberulent, red-brown to greyish-brown when dry, bark ± detaching, solid. Leaves arranged in spirals. Blade oblong to (sub)ovate, sometimes sub-panduriform, 19–26 (–45) cm long, 8–13 (–17) cm wide; coriaceous, apex acuminate, base obtuse to truncate to subcordate or sometimes cordate, margin entire, upper surface glabrous, lower surface glabrous or with white to brownish, short, rather stiff hairs on the lower part of the midvein; veins plane above, prominent below, 6–8 (–9) lateral veins, including 1 (–2) pairs of basal veins; glandular dot (rather) visible. Petiole 2.5–8.5 cm long, approximately 4 mm thick, glabrous to brownish stiffly hairy, periderm detaching. Stipules free or basally fused, approximately 2 cm long, glabrous (or white strigulose at the base), persistent.

Figs solitary or in pairs in the leaf axils, sessile or subsessile; basal bracts 2, broadly ovate, approximately 5 mm long and 5 mm wide, glabrous; receptacle globose, 2.5–5 cm in diameter when fresh, approximately 2–3 cm when dry, rather densely yellowish-white strigulose to hirtellous, ± distinctly verrucose, greyish-green with yellowish blotches at maturity, wall approximately 5 mm thick when dry, ostiole prominent up to 0.5 mm, bilabiate.

Pistillate flowers with 4 ± unequal tepals, 2.5–4 mm long, (sub)acute, free; seed flowers sessile or pedicellate up to 0.5 mm, style 3.5–4 mm long; gall flowers pedicellate up to 1–5 mm, style 2.5–3 mm long. Fruits oblongoid to subobovoid, 2–2.5 mm long; gall fruits obovoid to ellipsoid and ± longly stipitate or almost subobovoid, 3–3.5 mm long; fruits distinctly bicolorous. Staminate flowers few, pedicel 0.5–1.5 mm long, perianth with 4 tepals 3–4 mm long, subacute, their upper parts finely membranous, filament 0.3–1 mm long, anther approximately 1.5 mm long, apiculate or not. Interfloral bracts approximately 4 mm long. – Pl. 69, p. 231.

## Type

Preuss 454, Cameroun, Barombi (= Kumba) (holo-, B ; iso-, BM, K)

## Discussion

F. preussii is rather distinct, but sometimes the characters can be close to those of F. pringsheimiana. Sterile specimens can be confused with F. abscondita.

The species ranges from Nigeria to Zaïre and Uganda through Cameroon and Gabon. In riparian forests or secondary vegetation in dense humid evergreen forest zones.
