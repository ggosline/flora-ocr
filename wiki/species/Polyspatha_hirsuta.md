# *Polyspatha hirsuta* Mildbr.

**Genus**: [[Polyspatha]]
**Family**: [[Commelinaceae]]
**Protologue**: Notizbl. Bot. Gart. Berlin-Dahlem 9 : 256 (1925)

## Description

Herb 15–30(–60) cm tall; stem with internodes bearing hooked hairs and uniseriate hairs. Leaves alternate, spaced along the stem and some concentrated at the apex; sheath 1–1.5 cm long, pilose-puberulent, long-ciliate at apex; pseudopetiole ± 7 mm; blade elliptic to oblong-elliptic, (4–)6–12.5 × (1–)1.7–4.2 cm, base cuneate, apex acuminate, margins ciliolate, scabrous, upper face pilose-puberulent, lower face generally similar and often with ± appressed hairs along the midvein. Inflorescence terminal, sometimes with a few at upper leaf-axils, the latter never piercing the sheath, with up to 12 simple thyrses, each with a short peduncle and an elongated axis distinctly zigzag, with reflected pubescence; spathes broadly ovate-falciform, up to 10 per thyrse, at first erect, then deflexed, (5–)6–8.5(–11) × 4–6 mm, apex rounded to obtuse or rarely acute (the lower ones), pilose-puberulent, long-ciliate, green; cincinni 2- to 5-flowered. Flower: pedicel 1.5–2.5 mm, with a few scattered hairs; sepals 2.5–6 × 1.5–3 mm; upper petals spathulate, 3.5–7 × 2.5–5 mm long (of which 1.5–3 mm for the claw), lower petal nearly linear, 2.5–5 × 1 mm; stamens subequal, filament 2.5–5 mm, yellow; staminodes filament 1.5–3 mm, white; ovary ovate, slightly flattened laterally, white translucent. Fruit oblong-ellipsoidal to ellipsoidal-globose, 2.5–3.5 × 2.3–3 mm, apex truncate to emarginate. Seed ellipsoidal, 1.8–2.3 × 1.4–1.6 mm; testa superficially ribbed-reticulate, neither tuberculate nor transversely interrupted, covered by a brownish layer.

## Distribution

Guinea, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Ivory Coast, Ghana, Togo, Benin, Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of Congo, Democratic Republic of Congo, Uganda, Sudan, Tanzania and Angola (Cabinda); in Gabon mainly in Ogooué-Maritime, also in Ogooué-Ivindo and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

humid terrain, humid lowland dense forest, sometimes locally abundant and covering large areas; in Gabon up to 590 m altitude, from 700 to 1000 m in East Africa, elsewhere from 80 to 700 m. Flowers and fruits in May, July, October and November. Flowering in the afternoon. Bibliography: Brenan (1968b), van der Burg (2006), Faden (2012), Morton (1967), Sosef et al. (2006), Vandeweghe et al. (2016)
