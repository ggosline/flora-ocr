# *Mapania senegalensis* (A.Rich.) Lye

**Genus**: [[Mapania]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Lidia 7 : 92 (2011)

## Synonyms

- Hypolytrum senegalense A.Rich. in Pers., Syn. pl. 1 : 70 (1805).

## Description

Robust herb, with a thick erect woody rhizome approximately 1 cm in diameter; stems 1–4, lateral, arising from the axil of lower leaves (often decomposed), 8–40 cm × 0.8–2 mm, green to purple, trigonous to almost cylindrical, glabrous. Basal leaves often 15–20, very tightly arranged in two opposite rows; sheath poorly distinct, green or straw-yellow; blade linear, 60–80 × 1–2 cm, flat, scabrous on the margins and main veins, at least toward the apex. Inflorescence terminal, in a contracted anthela, 2 cm wide (up to 4 cm in West Africa), blonde to pale brown, lateral branches visible or not; involucral bracts 2–5, glumaceous to foliaceous, 5–20 × 2.5–3 mm, shorter to slightly longer than the inflorescence, greenish or violaceous with numerous prominent veins; spikes ovate–elliptic, 4–7 × 2–2.5 mm at anthesis, often elongating to 9–10 mm at fruiting, with 20–25 flowers; spicoid bracts very tightly packed, ovate–elliptic, 2–3 mm long, blonde to brown with blonde margins. Flower: floral bracts 2, lanceolate, 1–3 mm long, light reddish-brown, keel not winged and scabrous, apex acute to almost rounded; style with 2 branches. Mature achenes not seen.

## Distribution

Guinea Conakry, Sierra Leone and Gabon; in Gabon, only at Minkébé in the Woleu-Ntem (2 collections)

## Ecology

Riparian forest and open forest with Gilbertiodendron; in Gabon, around 500 m elevation. Taxonomic note: highly variable in West Africa as in Gabon. It is very close to M. heteromorpha, but differs by its paler spikes and more compact inflorescences. As the plants collected in Gabon are immature, the identifications should be considered as preliminary. Bibliography
