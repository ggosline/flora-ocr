# *Microcharis remotiflora* (E.G.Baker) Schrire

**Genus**: [[Microcharis]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bothalia 22 : 167 (1992)

## Synonyms

- Indigofera welwitschii Baker var. remotiflora (E.G.Baker) Cronquist, Fl. Congo Belge 5 : 172 (1954).

## Description

Annual herb, with scattered, spreading and appressed hairs; stem rooting, up to 40 cm. Stipules subulate to narrowly triangular, 1.5–5 mm long. Leaf with 3 to 5(–7) leaflets, but often 1-foliolate or simple towards the base; petiole 1–12 mm; leaflets of simple or 1-foliolate leaves with elliptic to suborbicular blade, 4.5–15 × 2.5–8 mm, terminal leaflet of upper leaves with elliptic to oblong-lanceolate blade, 5–26 × 2–6 mm, with bifid hairs above. Inflorescence in racemes 2–12 cm long, including the peduncle of 0–2 cm, with 3 to 12 lax flowers; bracts linear, up to 1 mm. Flower: pedicel 2–5 mm; calyx 1–1.5 mm long, with narrowly triangular lobes, ± as long as the tube; corolla pink or reddish; stamens 2–2.5 mm. Pod straight or slightly curved, 7–20 × 1–1.5 mm, ± glabrous, with 4 to 8 seeds. Seed rectangular, ± 1 mm long.

## Distribution

Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria, République centrafricaine, Cameroun, Gabon, République démocratique du Congo, Tanzania, Angola, Zimbabwe, Malawi and Mozambique; in Gabon rare, known from a single collection from the Lopé Reserve (Ogooué-Ivindo)

## Ecology

herbaceous and wooded savanna, rocky stations, forest edge, riverside and roadside; in Gabon at approximately 150 m altitude, elsewhere up to 2100 m. b: Cronquist (1954), Gillett (1971a), Schrire (1992, 2012). ibliography
