# *Tessmannia lescrauwaetii* (De Wild.) Harms

**Genus**: [[Tessmannia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Harms, Engl., Pflanzenw. Afr. 3, I : 457

## Description

= Baikixea? Lescrauwætii De Wild., Ann. Mus. Congo Belge, Bot. Sér. 5,2 : 141 (1907).

Trees. Leaves with 4–9 pairs of alternate leaflets. Rachis 7 to 15 cm. Stipules lanceolate, caducous or subpersistent. Leaflets lanceolate-oblong, imperceptibly attenuate at the apex which is slightly emarginate, asymmetric at the base, 1.5 to 6 cm long, 6 to 20 mm wide, glabrous. Dense reticulum of veins and veinlets very visible on both surfaces. Lamina riddled with translucent dots.

Axillary racemes, solitary or geminate, 3–9 cm in length. Axes densely velutinous and riddled with nail-like hairs, glandular. Pedicels 6–19 mm, velutinous and glandular. Sepals 4, coriaceous, with thin margins, somewhat imbricate in bud, lanceolate, 12–14 mm long, one of them wider than the others, velutinous-glandular without, silky velutinous within. Petals 5, white-pink, spatulate-oblong, with crisped margins, long-clawed, glabrous except on the midvein without, 23–30 mm long, 3–10 mm wide. Stamens 10, of which 9 fused and velutinous at the base; one free. Ovary hirsute and glandular.

## Type

Lescrauwaet 415, Kasai (BR)

## Discussion

Large tree with a rectilinear bole, without buttresses.

Species which according to Léonard would inhabit the forests of the southern border of the Congo basin (Kasai, Maniema). It has been found again in eastern Gabon and in eastern Cameroon.
