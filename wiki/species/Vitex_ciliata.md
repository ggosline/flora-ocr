# *Vitex ciliata* Pierre ex Pellegr.

**Genus**: [[Vitex]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Mus. Natl. Hist. Nat. Paris 33 : 268 (1927)

## Description

V. lutea Exell, non A.Chev., J. Bot. 69 (Suppl. 2) : 145 (1931), syn. nov. Shrub or tree reaching 20 m in height; branchlets reddish to brown, with fine golden-brown hairs spreading. Leaf: petiole 3–13 cm, sparsely pubescent to subglabrous; leaflets (3 or)5, with petiolules 0–2(–3) mm, the median one with narrowly obovate blade, distinctly spatulate, having its greatest width in the upper third or quarter, progressively narrowed in the lower two-thirds, 6–18 × 3–6.5 cm, apex rounded or truncate, cuspidate, entire or sometimes slightly crenulate toward the apex, upper surface dark green shining, somewhat strigulose (stiff hairs ± 0.3 mm directed toward the apex), with a fringe of long fine brownish to golden hairs on the main vein, with reticulation somewhat prominent, lower surface punctate with yellow glands, almost glabrous except the veins with yellowish to brownish hairs generally appressed, these strongly prominent, pale brownish, generally forming a striking pattern standing out against the rest of the light green blade; secondary veins 8 to 13 on each side, more or less impressed, with short accessory veins perpendicular to the main vein, tertiary venation ± scalariform, reticulation somewhat prominent on both surfaces. Inflorescence formed of axillary dichasia, 15–25 cm long, of which 8–15 cm for the peduncle, equalling or exceeding the axillant leaf, ample and multiflorous, with 6 to 8 levels of branching; axes bearing long fine brownish hairs. Flower: pedicel puberulent; calyx 1.5–2 mm long (of which 0.5 mm for the lobes), with yellowish to tawny hairs appressed or oblique; corolla 3–4–5 mm long, white to cream, lower lip often washed with violet; stamens exsert; ovary glabrous, pointed and glandular at the apex. Fruit obovoid, 10–15 × 7–10 mm, subtended by a cupuliform calyx 7–8 mm in diameter.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola (Cabinda); in Gabon: Estuaire, Ngounié, Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo, but probably more widespread. Conservation status: Widely distributed; probably Least Concern (LC)

## Ecology

forest groupings; at 0–1200 m altitude

## Vernacular names

angona (fang) ; gegwa, gégwasaga, gépa-sa-mogodo (mitsogo) ; ipata (banzabi)

## Source note

The type of V. lutea Exell shows all the diagnostic characters of V. ciliata. B : Meerts (2018, 2020a, 2020b), Moldenke (1955b), Raponda-Walker & Sillans (1961). Bibliography
