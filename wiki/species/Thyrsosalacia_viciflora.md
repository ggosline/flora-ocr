# *Thyrsosalacia viciflora* Hallé

**Genus**: [[Thyrsosalacia]]
**Family**: [[Celastraceae]]
**Protologue**: Notul. System. 16 (1-2): 132, fig. 4 (1960)

## Description

Woody climbing plant, glabrous with cylindrical branches, lacking rubber; stipules scarcely distinct, not known at the juvenile stage. Leaves opposite with petiole becoming thick, 4-5 mm long, with narrow channel. Lamina

PL. 8. – Thyrsosalacia viciflora Hallé: 1; leafy node, 2; inflorescent twig, 3; accumulation of cataphylls on an inflorescent twig, 4; bud, diam. 5.8 mm, 5; sepal 4.5 mm wide, 6; petal 10 mm long, 7.8; disk, diam. 6.2 mm; 9.10; stamen; 11, open ovary locule, 12; pistil in section, 13; locule of a knotted ovary (Le Testu 7897).

olive-green subconccolorous when dry, smooth above, elliptic, 8-20 × 3.5-10 cm, with base attenuate subacute; apex with an acumen of 7-12 mm. Midvein forming a fine crest above when dry; 5-7 pairs of well-marked secondary veins forming loops 4-5 mm from the entire margin.

Inflorescences composed of pluriflorous units arranged in indefinite thyrsoid panicles with long slender opposite branches; these rigid-looking axes are marked by spaced levels underlined by tight groups of petiolar scales; the flower-bearing parts lacking leaves are ± terminal and reach 10-40 cm in length; flowers are opposite in decussate pairs, with acute bracts of 1 mm, and grouped by 2-5 pairs at the extremities. Pedicels 5-7 mm long, at the apex of an axis segment of 4-12 mm bearing 2 sterile opposite or subopposite bracteoles. Bud globose, 5-7 mm in diameter. Flowers green with brown disk, rotate. Sepals 5, orbicular or ovate, entire or microfimbriolate, slightly unequal, 3 mm long. Petals 5, suborbicular, 7-10 × 5-9 mm, multivenate, microdenticulate and margined beneath, juxtaposed at the level of the disk margin, with narrow insertion below. Disk circular, 5-7 mm in diameter, cleft by 5 emarginations and weakly raised into a cupule. Stamens 3-5 mm with slender filament, inserted in a small perigynous groove; anther 1-1.7 mm in width and length, with two theca arranged in acute chevron, the dehiscence slits being nearly longitudinal. Pistil distinctly superior, pyramidal attenuate subulate, 3 mm in height, with punctiform stigma. Locules above the disk, each containing 2 superposed ovules.

Young fruit of fleshy type, oblong conical, with persistent floral parts. Mature fruit and seeds not known. – Pl. 8, p. 27.
