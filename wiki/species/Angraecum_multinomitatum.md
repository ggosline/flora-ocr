# *Angraecum multinomitatum* Rendle.

**Genus**: [[Angraecum]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Cat. Talbot. S. Nig. Pl. : 107 (1913)

## Description

Garay, Kew Bull. 28(3) : 511 (1973) ; FCAM 3 : 904 (2001). – Mystacidium clavatum Rolfe, FTA 7 : 172 (1897).

Leaves (3-) 7.5-8 (-10) × 1-3.5 cm, twisted at the base to lie in the same plane, oblong-obovate to obovate-elliptic. Inflorescence lax, 4-5 cm, 2-flowered. Flowers rather small, not resupinate, green, yellow-green, often tinged with orange. Dorsal sepal lanceolate, acute. Petals oblong-lanceolate to obliquely and broadly lanceolate, acuminate. Lateral sepals obliquely ovate-lanceolate, acute. Labellum 7-8.5 × 4-6 mm, broadly ovate to almost ovate-rhombiform, acuminate, cochleariform, with a prominent ridge-callus in the lower two-thirds. Spur < 12.5 mm, slightly swollen towards the middle, attenuate at both ends. – Pl. 227, p. 475.

## Distribution

Guinea, Sierra Leone, Ghana, Togo, Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon

## Ecology

altitude unknown. Epiphyte on moss-covered tree, collected 2 m above ground
