# *Halodule wrightii* Asch.

**Family**: [[Cymodoceaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Halodule]]

Sitzungsber. Ges. naturf. Freunde Berlin 1868: 19 (1868).

## Synonymy

- *Diplanthera wrightii* (Asch.) Asch., in Engl. & Prantl, *Nat. Pflanzenfam.*, Nachtr. 2–4: 37 (1897).

## Description

Herb with rhizome internodes 0.5–3.5 cm long. Intravaginal scales elliptic, 4–9 mm long. Leaf sheath 1–4 cm; blade 5–18(–22) cm × 0.3–1 mm; midrib visible and sometimes ending in a small, readily deciduous projecting point; intramarginal lateral veins inconspicuous and ending in two triangular teeth.

Male flower with pedicel 1.0–2.4 cm; anthers slender, 3.5–4 mm long, red, the upper attached 0.3–0.5 mm above the lower. Female flower red; ovary ellipsoid, obovoid or globose, compressed, 1.5–2 mm long; style 1.0–2.8 cm, lateral to terminal and persistent in fruit. Fruit globose to broadly obovoid, slightly compressed, 2–2.5 mm long.

## Distribution

Atlantic coasts of America and Africa. In Gabon it is known from a single observation in Corsico Bay, Estuaire, and is very probably present elsewhere along the coast.

## Habitat and ecology

Shallow marine water over sand or mud, sometimes in mangroves, to about 3 m depth.

## Vernacular names

- Shoal grass (English).

## Notes

The Gabon record is supported by a diagnostic photograph but no herbarium collection. Indo-Pacific plants formerly included here are assigned to *H. uninervis*. Leaf-tip form is variable, making those species extremely difficult to distinguish morphologically.
