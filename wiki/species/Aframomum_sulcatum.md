# *Aframomum sulcatum* (Oliv. et Hanb.) K. Schum.
**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Aframomum]]
## Description
Herb with short-petiolate glabrous blades c. 25 × 7 cm and leathery 4 mm ligule. Basal spikes one- or few-flowered, sessile or nearly so. Flowers mauve; calyx 4.5 cm, laterally split; spreading oval labellum and narrow triangular lateral corolla lobes.
## Source
Koechlin, J. (1964). Zingibéracées. *Flore du Gabon* 9: 62–63.
