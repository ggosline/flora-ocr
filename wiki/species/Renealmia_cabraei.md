# *Renealmia cabraei* De Wild. et Th. Dur.

**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Renealmia]]

## Description

Slender herb under 1 m. Petiole 1–4 cm; narrow lanceolate glabrous blade 25–35 × 4–5 cm. Very loose panicle to 25 cm on a 60–70 cm peduncle, usually with three separated leafless sheaths; about eight widely spaced bracts each subtend 2–3 flowers. Flowers white; capsule c. 2 × 1.2 cm.

## Distribution

Known from Cameroon and Congo; presence in Gabon considered probable. The treatment questions whether it is distinct from *R. congoensis*.

## Source
