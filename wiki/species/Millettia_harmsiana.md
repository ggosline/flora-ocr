# *Millettia harmsiana* De Wild.

**Genus**: [[Millettia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Ann. Mus. Congo Belge, Bot. sér. 5, 1 : 139 (1904)

## Description

Liane with stem reaching 40 m in length and 22 cm in diameter; branchlets dark purple, shortly pubescent, glabrescent. Stipules often persistent, ovate, 1.5 × 2 mm, with 6 to 9 nerves, yellowish-pubescent on the outside, glabrous on the inside. Leaf: petiole 3.5–8 cm, with sparse pubescence, channelled above; rachis similar, 5–11 cm; stipels subulate, somewhat curved, 1–2 mm, with yellowish-brown pubescence; leaflets 7 to 11, petiolule 4–10 mm, silky, blade oblong to elliptic or obovate, 5–15.5(–16.5) × 2.4–6 cm, base rounded to cuneate, apex with acumen of 0.5–2 cm, mucronulate or emarginate, bright golden-brown below; lateral nerves in 9 to 14 pairs, prominent below. Inflorescence terminal and axillary, in pseudopanicle of (15–)20–40 cm in length; ramifications sometimes branched, erect, then spreading, reaching 20 cm in length, with fascicles of 2 to 7 flowers on brachyblasts of 3–4 mm in length; bracts caducous, ovate, ± 1 × 1 mm. Flower: pedicel 0.5–3 mm, silky; bracteoles rounded-rhombic, ± 1.5 × 1.5 mm; calyx dark purple, silky to subglabrous, with tube of ± 3 mm in length, lobes 1–3.5 mm in length, obtuse; corolla white with green patch at the base of the standard, standard with claw of 1.5–2.7 mm, blade suborbicular, 9–11 × 8–11 mm, apex emarginate, prominent basal calluses, auriculate, wings with claw of 1.5–2.7 mm, blade elliptic-obovate, 8.5–12 × 3.5–4 mm, apex rounded, auricle 0.5–0.8 mm, keel with claw of 2–3.5 mm, blade elliptic-falcate, 8–12.5 × 3.5–6 mm, apex rounded, auricle ± 0.8 × 1 mm, broad lateral pouch; stamens unequal, staminal tube 5–9 mm in length, vexillar stamens shorter, free portion of filaments 2–4 mm, that of abaxial stamens 4–6 mm, anthers ± 1 × 0.5 mm; ovary 4–5.5 mm in length, with dense-spreading pubescence, stipe ± 1 mm, ovules 7; style 6–12 mm, straight portion pubescent, curved portion glabrous, stigma capitate. Pod and seed not seen.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, known from Ogooué-Lolo and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

primary and secondary forest; in Gabon at 140–535 m altitude, elsewhere at 370–470 m. Flowering from January to February and May. Bibliography: Hauman (1954a).
