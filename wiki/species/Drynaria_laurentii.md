# *Drynaria laurentii* (Christ) Hieronymus

**Genus**: [[Drynaria]]
**Family**: [[Polypodiaceae]]

## Description

— Hier., in Engl., Pflanzenwelt Afr. 2 : 57 (1908) (excl. fig.). — Tard., Mém. IFAN 28 : 216, pl. 43, f. 2-3 (1953). — Alston, FWTA : 48 (1959). = Polypodium propinquum var. Laurentii Christ, Ann. Mus. Congo, sér. 5, 1 : 6, t. 2 (1903).

Rhizome thick, creeping, covered with broad scales, light reddish-brown, peltate at the base, concolorous, with margins bearing, at the extreme apex only, long slender prolongations, formed of thin-walled cells, yellowish-brown content. Humus-collecting frond 13–15 cm long by 5–8 cm wide, cordate at the base, ovate, shallowly lobed, with obtuse lobes. Fertile frond petiolate, with petiole 5–15 cm long, winged at the apex or along its entire length, with lamina narrowed toward the base, 50–60 cm long by 20–30 cm wide, divided into linear lobes to the rachis, acute or rounded, with entire margins, 15–18 cm long by 2–3 cm wide. Texture thick, coriaceous. Veins raised, forming several rows of areoles containing included smaller areoles. Sori thick, rounded, in a single row along the costa.

Tropical Africa.

Epiphytic, on large branches, up to nearly 1,000 m.
