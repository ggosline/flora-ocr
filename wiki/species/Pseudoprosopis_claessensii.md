# *Pseudoprosopis claessensii* (De Wildeman) Gilbert & Boutique

**Genus**: [[Pseudoprosopis]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: FCB 3: 224 (1952); VILLIERS, BJBB 53 (3/4): 423, tab. 3 (1983)

## Synonyms

- Entada claessensii DE WILD., BJBB 7: 254 (1920).
- Piptadenia bequaerti DE WILD., Ann. Soc. Scient. Brux. 44 (2): 216 (1924).

## Description

– P. neo-claessensii DE WILD., Pl. Bequaert. 3: 314 (1925).

Woody liana. Mature stem subglabrous to glabrous, often with brown longitudinal bands alternating with lighter bands of lenticels; sometimes furrowed. Young stems puberulent. Stipules caducous, sometimes with a gland on the stipular scar. Petiole shortly puberulent to glabrous, 3.5–9 cm long; upper surface rounded or weakly canaliculate. Rachis puberulent to glabrous, 0–8.5 cm long, weakly canaliculate or flat above. Pinnae (1–) 2–3 pairs. Pinna axis glabrous to puberulent, 2.5–14.5 cm long, canaliculate or not above. Leaflets 2–4 pairs. Petiolule ± puberulent, 2–4.5 mm long, transversely wrinkled. Blade sparsely appressed-pubescent on both surfaces, brown on both surfaces, ± shiny above and matte below when dry, symmetrically or asymmetrically elliptic, broadly elliptic, elliptic-oblong, lanceolate, sometimes slightly falcate, often decreasing in size from apex to base, 5–10 × 1.7–4 cm for the upper ones, 2.8–6.5 × 1.4–2.7 cm for the lower ones; base asymmetric: proximal margin rounded to attenuate-rounded and distal margin strongly attenuate to subcuneiform; apex very distinctly acuminate-mucronate obtuse or emarginate. Midvein concave pubescent above and prominent pubescent below. Secondary veins 5–8 pairs, ascending, arched, anastomosing in an arch near the blade margin, ± pubescent at the base above and pubescent below, prominent on both surfaces. Tertiary veins in a dense prominent network on both surfaces.

Inflorescence in a terminal or axillary panicle near the apical leaves. Axes densely puberulent, yellowish-brown. Bracteole puberulent externally, narrowly triangular, arched. Pedicel puberulent, 1–2.75 mm long. Floral bud weakly obovoid with folded staminal filaments. Flowers ♢. Sepals fused campanulate, puberulent-tomentellous externally, 1–1.25 mm long, free lobes broadly triangular, 0.25–0.50 mm long, with acute apex. Petals rather sparsely puberulent to pubescent ± appressed, erect, free from one another, narrowly elliptic-lanceolate, 2.5–3.5 × 0.6–0.8 mm, apex acute. Stamens 10, free from one another, filament ± 4 mm long, weakly enlarged at the apex; anther oblong 0.75 mm long, with connectival tissue prolonged by a pedicellate caducous gland. Ovary very obscurely stipitate, hirsute, 1.5 mm long, with 6–11 ovules; style slender ± 4 mm long, with indistinct stigma.

Pod glabrous, flat, woody, dehiscent by 2 slits, oblong, 13–14 × 2 cm, attenuate at the base and asymmetrically rounded at the apex; valves coiling at maturity. Seeds 6–11, ellipsoid-quadrangular, 13 × 8 mm, areolate. - Pl. 18, p. 73.

## Type

Claessens 431, Zaïre : Katako Kombe, Forestier Central, fl. janv. 1910 (BR !)

## Discussion

This liana occurs in dense forest groupings. Its range extends from Gabon to Zaïre (Kasaï).
