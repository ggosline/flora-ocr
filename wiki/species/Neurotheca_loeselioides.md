# *Neurotheca loeselioides* (Spruce ex Progel) Baill. subsp. loeselioides

**Genus**: [[Neurotheca]]
**Family**: [[Gentianaceae]]
**Protologue**: Hist. pl. 10 : 132 (1888)

## Description

Plate 26(3–8), Figure 7(A, B) Hist. pl. 10 : 132 (1888). Octopleura loeselioides Spruce ex Progel in Mart., Fl. bras. 6(1) : 212 (1865). Annual herb, 5–35 cm tall; stem simple or branched, lilac-purple to green. Leaf: blade narrowly elliptic, 3–30 × 1–10 mm, base acute to obtuse, apex acute to obtuse, often violaceous beneath. Inflorescence axillary, with flowers solitary or geminate, arranged along the entire length of the stems and semi-spreading. Flower: pedicel 0.5–5 mm; calyx 3–7 mm long, tube 2–5 mm long, lobes triangular to narrowly triangular, 1–2.5 mm long, green, with purplish-red margins; corolla 3–6.5 mm long, blue, pale lilac to white, tube 4–5 mm long, lobes ovate, 1.5–2 mm long; stamens 1.5–3.5 mm long, anthers 0.3–0.5 mm long; ovary narrowly obovoid-oblong; style 2–3.5 mm, stigma level with the anthers. Fruit ellipsoid to narrowly ellipsoid, 2.5–3.5 mm long. Seed 0.3–0.5 × 0.25–0.4 mm.

## Distribution

Senegal, Mali, Guinea, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Benin, Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Burundi, Sudan, Uganda, Angola and Madagascar; northern part of South America; in Gabon known from the coastal basin, the Ogooué-Ivindo and the Haut-Ogooué

## Ecology

herbaceous savannas, grasslands on rocky outcrops, coastal dunes, swamps and secondary vegetation; in Gabon between 0 and 650 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1150 m

## Source note

The species was divided into two subspecies. The subspecies robusta (Hua) A.Raynal differs by its anthers 1–2 mm long, the flower exceeding 10 mm in length and the stigma exsert. It is found in West Africa up to North Cameroon.
