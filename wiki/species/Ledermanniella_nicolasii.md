# *Ledermanniella nicolasii* C.Cusset Planche 17

**Genus**: [[Ledermanniella]]
**Family**: [[Podostemaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Mus. natl. Hist. nat., sér. 4., Adansonia 6(3) : 274 (1984)

## Description

Bull. Mus. natl. Hist. nat., sér. 4., Adansonia 6(3) : 274 (1984). Plant with a basal thallose portion; stem simple or branched, 5–25 cm in length. Stipules absent. Leaves opposite the inflorescences, caducous, several times dichotomous, ± 2 cm in length, with filiform segments. Inflorescences pedunculate, placed on the same side of the stem; bracts fused at the base into a foliaceous blade, 2 or 3 times dichotomous, with filiform segments; spathe 3–4 mm in length, sometimes pedicellate, tearing at anthesis irregularly at the apex. Flower: pedicel ± 1 cm after anthesis; tepals filiform, ± 1.3 mm; androecium longer than the ovary, stamens 2, filaments 2.5–3 mm, fused for more than half their length, anther 1 × 0.75 mm, pollen in monads; ovary 2.2 × 0.8 mm, gynophore ± 0.5 mm in length; stigmas filiform, 0.6–0.7 mm. Fruit ellipsoid, with 8 ribs of which 6 very prominent, with valves not caducous.

## Distribution

Endemic to Gabon, known from a single collection in the Ogooué at Booué (Ogooué-Ivindo)

## Ecology

at ± 170 m elevation. B: Cusset (1984), Sosef et al. (2006). Bibliography
