# *Phytolacca dodecandra* L'Hérit.

**Family**: [[Phytolaccaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Phytolacca]]

## Description

Dioecious scrambling shrub 2–5 m tall. Leaves elliptic to ovate-elliptic, 5–15 cm long, variable at base and apex. Racemose inflorescences bear small unisexual flowers; the multicarpellate gynoecium matures into a fleshy, lobed berry.

## Distribution

Tropical African species recorded from Gabon.
