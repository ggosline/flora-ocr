# *Ptychopetalum petiolatum* Oliver

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Ptychopetalum]]

FTA 1:347 (1868).

## Synonymy

- *P. acuminatissimum* Engl., Notizbl. bot. Gart. Berl. 2:283 (1899)

## Description

Tree 15–20 m tall, with a smooth grey trunk and glabrous twigs. Leaves petiolate, elliptic to oblong-elliptic, 8–14 cm long and 3.5–5.5 cm wide, acuminate at the apex, cuneate to slightly rounded at the base, coriaceous and glabrous on both surfaces, with a prominent midrib and 8–10 pairs of lateral veins; petiole 5–8 mm long, glabrous.

Inflorescences axillary, small, few-flowered cymes, shorter than the leaves.

Flowers small, bisexual, fragrant. Calyx of 5 small, triangular, glabrous sepals 0.5 mm long. Petals 5, alternate with the sepals, free, lanceolate, 3–4 mm long, white or cream-coloured, margins fringed with filaments. Stamens 10, in two whorls; outer whorl opposite the petals with filaments, inner whorl opposite the sepals lacking filaments. Ovary superior, conical, 2–3 mm tall, with 4–5 locules; style short; stigma obscurely lobed. Disk present, with 5 small lobes.

Fruit a drupe 10–15 mm in diameter, globose to slightly oblong, with a single seed.

**Habit**: Tree 15–20 m  
**Leaves**: Petiolate, elliptic to oblong-elliptic, 8–14 cm × 3.5–5.5 cm, acuminate, coriaceous, glabrous  
**Inflorescence**: Axillary cymes, few-flowered  
**Flowers**: Bisexual, petals 5, 3–4 mm long, stamens 10  
**Fruit**: Drupe, globose, 10–15 mm in diameter

## Distribution

**Range**: Endemic to West-Central Africa.

**Gabon**: Known from Ogooué-Maritime (Fernan Vaz region, Gamba area), Ogooué-Ivindo (Makokou region), and Haut-Ogooué (Moanda region).

**Republic of the Congo**: Known from Sangha region.

## Habitat and ecology

Occurs in terra firma primary forest at altitudes of 500–700 m. Flowering records from October to November.

## Notes

*P. acuminatissimum* Engl. is treated here as a synonym of *P. petiolatum* Oliver based on the Flore du Gabon treatment. The distribution is restricted to two countries in the region, with altitude ranging from 500–700 m in forest habitat.
