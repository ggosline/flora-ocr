# *Vepris* sp.

**Family**: [[Rutaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Vepris]]

## Description

Shrub with trifoliolate, exceptionally bifoliolate leaves. Petiole 5–10 cm; petiolules 5–10 mm; blades elliptic, 12–18 × 5–8 cm, narrowly acuminate, papery and slightly undulate-margined, with 15–20 lateral-vein pairs. Male inflorescences are short, axillary, few-flowered panicles 10–20 mm long. Flowers have four rounded sepals, four 3 × 2 mm resin-dotted petals, eight subequal stamens and a 2 mm rudimentary 4-locular gynoecium.

## Distribution

Known from Gabon material but not identified to species in the treatment.
