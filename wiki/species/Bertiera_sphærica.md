# *Bertiera sphærica* N. Hallé, sp. nov.

**Genus**: [[Bertiera]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]

## Description

(PL. 15, p. 67)

Small pubescent shrub. Stipules subcaudate, 10–20 mm long. Petiole 6–15 mm long, villous. Blade glabrous above, villous below, elliptic, 10–15 cm long, acute at base, subacuminate at apex. Nerves 5–9 pairs, long-ascending, villous. Inflorescences spherical, multiflorous, terminal, sessile, pendulous at apex. Calyx not tubular, sepals 5, linear, 9–13 × 1–2 mm, free to the base. Ovary puberulous, 2-locular. Immature fruit green, up to 10 mm diam. Disk increasing in diameter. Seeds 1.2 mm long, 34 per fruit.

Slender shrub 1.30–1.80 m tall, with erect stem; branches horizontal, about 50 cm long; young branches strongly pubescent. Stipules 10–20 × 3–5 mm, apex acuminate and tapered, base tubular for 2–5 mm, pubescent. Petioles villous, 6–15 mm long. Blade glabrous above, finely villous below, brown when dry, elliptic, 10–15 × 3.5–6 cm, acute at base, subacuminate at apex. 7–9 pairs of secondary nerves very long-ascending, villous below as well as the marginal line.

Inflorescences spherical, multiflorous, 2.5–3.5 cm diam., sessile at the ends of the horizontal branches and oriented downward. Flowers sessile. Corollas not seen. Calyx pale green, villous, not tubular, with 5 linear sepals 9–13 × 1–2 mm. Ovary pubescent, 2-locular.

Infructescence capitate, dense, 4.5 cm diam., bristling with persistent sepals. Fruits sessile, contiguous, green, reaching 10 mm diam. Sepals spaced at the base around the disk which reaches 8 mm diam. 34 seeds arranged on 2 peltate, echinulate placentas. Typical seed of the genus, truncate-pyramidal, 1.3 mm tall.

## Type

N. Hallé et J.-F. Villiers 4744 (holotype P; isotypes BR et K), 7 km NE de Mela vers Nkan, Monts de Cristal, vieilles inflorescences 30-1-1968

## Discussion

This species of the subgenus Bertierella sect. Capitata is related although very distinct from B. globiceps K. Schum.

Dense almost intact forest from 400 to 900 m altitude.
