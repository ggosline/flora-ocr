# *Tricalysia coriacea* (Benth.) Hiern

**Genus**: [[Tricalysia]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Hiern, F.T.A. 3 : 120 (1877)

## Synonyms

- Randia coriacea Benth, Fl. Nigrit.: 387 (1847).
- Diplocrater sp., Benth. et Hook. f. Gen. Pl. 2 : 96 (1873).
- Tricalysia petiolata De Wild. Ann. Mus. Congo sér. 5, 1 : 202 (1904), est probablement synonyme.

## Description

Shrub 1 to 4 m, glabrous. Stipules broad for 1–1.5 mm, abruptly subulate for 1–3 (4) mm. Petiole 5–9 mm, narrowly sulcate-canaliculate. Blade glabrous, coriaceous or subcoriaceous, reddish-brown or olive-brown when dry, elliptic, 8–16 × 4–6.5 cm; base rounded or ± acute; apex obtuse or briefly subacuminate; margin slightly bordered. 6–9 pairs of secondary nerves; reticulation moderately evident below, indistinct above. Domatia either reduced, pilose and rare, or absent.

Inflorescences axillary 3–12-flowered very contracted, reddish-brown when dry, sessile, with imbricate bracteolar cups, truncate, pubescent internally, glabrous externally. Bud acute with contorted prefloration. Calyx with cupuliform expanded truncate limb, 2–3 mm high, glabrous externally. Corolla white with tube of 4 × 2 mm, with 5–8 ± lanceolate lobes of 5 × 2 mm. Throat pilose. Anthers 3 × 0.5 mm, attached toward the lower third, with acuminate connective; filament approximately 1 mm. Style 7 mm, ± hirsute in its upper third exsert, bilobed for 1 (2) mm. Ovary 2-locular. Axile placenta pendent bearing 3–6 (8) ovules.

Fruit globose, sessile, 6 mm in diameter, red at maturity, with cupuliform apex, with wall 1 mm thick or less, containing 2–3 (–?) seeds. Seeds ± angular 3.5–4 mm, with dark brownish testa, with hilum forming a long and thin furrow.
