# *Fagara heitzii* Aubrév. et Pellegr.

**Family**: [[Rutaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Fagara]]

## Description

Large tree to 120–150 cm diameter, lacking buttresses but sometimes strongly flared. Young trunks bear large flattened conical spines to 8–9 × 9–10 × 4–6 cm; bark is 1–2 cm thick, granular and finely orange-dotted on a yellow ground, and the wood is soft and light. Leaves cluster in terminal rosettes and reach 1 m, with 12–25 leaflet pairs; leaflets are 4–20 × 2–5 cm, leathery and distinctly crenate, with lateral veins looped close to the margin. Broad panicles reach 40 cm. Fruits are small, obliquely globose, apiculate follicles with one glossy black seed.

## Distribution

Recorded from Cameroon and Gabon.
