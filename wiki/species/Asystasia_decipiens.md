# *Asystasia decipiens* Heine

**Genus**: [[Asystasia]]
**Family**: [[Acanthaceae]]
**Protologue**: H. Heine, Kew Bull. 16 : 169 (1962); F.W.T.A. ed. 2 : 2 : 413 (1963)

## Synonyms

- Filetia africana auct. : S. Moore, Cat. Pl. Talbot S. Nigeria : 140 (1913), non Lindau, Bot. Jahrb. 20 : 41 (1894).
- Asystasia Lindaviana Hutch. et Dalz., Fl. W. Trop. Afr., ed. 1,2 : 257 (1931) « Lindauiana » (cf. note p. 138), quoad specimina Talbot 380, 985 tantum, non Filetia africana Lindau (1894).

## Description

Herbaceous plant, up to 80 cm tall, glabrous, erect, closely resembling Asystasia gangetica (L.) T. Anders., from which it is easily distinguished by its shorter calyces and corollas and greenish colour; stems dark green. Petioles 15 mm long; leaf blade ovate-elliptic, cuspidate; acumen subobtuse; approximately 7–8 pairs of lateral nerves, 6–13 cm long and 2–5 cm wide.

Inflorescences axillary and terminal, 5.5–8 cm long, generally simple, but sometimes slightly branched towards the base. Flowers arranged unilaterally in the inflorescence; bracts subulate-lanceolate, up to 2 mm long, caducous, ciliate at the margins; bracteoles similar and of the same dimensions. Pedicels pubescent-puberulent, up to 1 cm long. Calyx approximately 4.5 mm long, with free part reaching 3 mm long; sepals linear-lanceolate, base approximately 0.5 mm wide. Corolla greenish-yellowish or pale green-white (Hallé 2177), funnel-shaped, slightly bilabiate, 12–14 mm long with spread limb approximately 5.5 mm in diameter; the two posterior lobes are orbicular, approximately 3 mm long and with central part fused for approximately 2 mm, thus forming a posterior lip 4 mm long, emarginate at the apex for 1 mm; the three anterior lobes approximately 3 mm long form more or less the anterior lip of the corolla: lateral lobes 1.5 mm wide, central lobe very inflated and recurved, up to 3 mm wide, with indistinct lines and spots, purple or violet. Stamens didynamous; shorter filaments 3.5 mm, longer ones approximately 3 mm, fused in pairs for 1 mm long at the base and very slightly pubescent; anthers with lobes slightly superposed, 1.4 mm long. Ovary 1.5–2 mm long, finely pubescent; style 8–10 mm long, pubescent in its lower part; stigma slightly bilobed.

Fruit slightly pubescent, glabrescent, reaching 23 mm long; lower part stipitate cylindric for approximately 1 cm long and 1 mm in diameter; upper part swollen, 4–5 mm wide and 2 mm thick. 4 seeds semi-orbicular-angular approximately 4 mm in diameter, finely and regularly verrucose.
