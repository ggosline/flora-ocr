# *Trichoscypha rubicunda* Lecomte Planche 19

**Genus**: [[Trichoscypha]]
**Family**: [[Anacardiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Bot. France 52 : 656 (1906)

## Description

T. braunii Engl. var. regularis Lecomte, nomen in sched. Shrub to medium tree, 15–26 m tall; trunk up to 25 cm in diameter; branchlets pubescent to puberulent, glabrescens. Leaf: petiole 10–20 cm; rachis up to 42 cm; leaflets (9–)11–25, petiolule 5–10 mm, with ovate-lanceolate to oblong blade, (11–)17–25(–35) × (3–)5–7(–11) cm, rounded at base, acuminate for 1–2 cm at apex, glabrous and smooth above except the impressed midvein pubescent, sparsely pubescent to glabrous below, with 12 to 20 pairs of lateral veins. Inflorescence subterminal to axillary or sometimes below the leaves, the male at least up to 70 cm in length, the female up to 110 cm in length, pubescent to puberulent; bracts shorter than the axillary floral cluster. Male flower: pedicel 1–2 mm, pubescent; calyx 0.6–0.7 mm in length, lobed for half or more, ± glabrous; petals slightly imbricate, spreading to reflexed, ovate-elliptic, 2 mm in length, glabrous, pink or red; stamens incurved, slightly shorter than the petals, glabrous; disk 1.5–2 mm in diameter, glabrous, plane to slightly undulate. Female flower: pedicel and calyx somewhat longer than in the male flower; petals slightly imbricate, reflexed, ovate-elliptic, 2.5–3.5 mm in length; staminodes 1.5–2 mm in length; disk glabrous; pistil 1.5–2.5 mm in length, ovary globose, velutinous; stigmas 4, on a stipe of 1 mm. Fruit ovoid-ellipsoidal, 1.5–2 × 1–1.5 cm, red at maturity, with short, appressed hairs.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea (Rio Muni), Gabon and Republic of Congo; in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Ngounié, Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo and Woleu-Ntem.

## Ecology

primary and secondarized forest; in Gabon up to 500 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1200 m
