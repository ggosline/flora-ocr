## Description

Cam. : 25 : 38 (1983). TYPE : Nigeria, Benin (fl., fr.) Palisot de Beauvois s.n. (holo-P).

Liane ≤ 20 m, diameter ≤ 8 cm. Young branches: indumentum often mixed with long hairs > 1 mm.

Leaves whorled in 2 or 3; petiole ≤ 1 cm, lower part often transformed into a woody hook ≤ 4 cm; lamina elliptic to ovate or obovate, ≤ 16 × 6 cm; major lateral veins 6–11 pairs, often reddish-green beneath; base of lamina rounded to subcordate; apex shortly acuminate.

Scales absent.

Inflorescences: contracted axillary or terminal racemes; leaf subtending the inflorescence, whitish or pinkish with major veins green to reddish; bracts scarcely visible.

Flowers 4-merous, stipitate. Receptacle greenish-white to red: the lower one, 3–4.5 mm tall, covered with long non-glandular hairs and short glandular hairs; the upper one campanulate-infundibuliform, 6 × 3 mm, glabrous except for a few hairs near the base. Calyx, lobes acuminate to long-acuminate. Petals dark red, elongate-obovate, length 2–3 mm, width 1–2 mm, outer surface pubescent, inner surface glabrous. Stamens exserted 10–17 mm; filaments dark violet; anthers 0.5 mm long. Style exserted 6–12 mm, dark red.

Fruit obovoid, glabrous, often pinkish, height and diameter almost equal ≤ 2 cm; 4(–6) membranous wings; stipe 3–5 mm. – (Pl. 22, 1–3, p. 81).

## Distribution

From Gambia to Sudan to Uganda and Angola
