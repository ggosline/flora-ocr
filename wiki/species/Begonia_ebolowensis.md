# *Begonia ebolowensis* Engl. Planche 38

**Genus**: [[Begonia]]
**Family**: [[Begoniaceae]]
**Protologue**: B. lethomasiae R.Wilczek, Bull. Jard. Bot. Nat. Belg. 39(1) : 85 (1969)

## Description

in Engl. & Drude, Veg. Erde 9, Die Pflanzenw. Afr. 3(2) : 619 (1921). B. lethomasiae R.Wilczek, Bull. Jard. Bot. Nat. Belg. 39(1) : 85 (1969). Epiphyte; stem subscandent, reaching 1 m in length; young branches sparsely puberulent. Stipules caducous, narrowly triangular, 10–28 × 3–6 mm. Leaves: petiole 0.5–4.5 cm; lamina nearly symmetrical, ovate to obovate, 6–11.5 × 2.5–6 cm, obliquely obtuse or weakly cordate at base, generally obtuse at apex, margins entire. Inflorescence bisexual, 3–7(–15)-flowered; cymules with 2 lateral ♀ flowers and 1 central ♂ flower; peduncle 1–9 mm; short branches; bracts caducous. ♂ Flower: pedicel 7–17 mm; outer tepals 4–8 × 2–4 mm, inner ones 2–8 × 0.4–2 mm; androecium zygomorphic, stamens 8–13, fasciculate, column 0.3–1 mm, free parts of filaments 0–0.3 mm, anthers with longitudinal dehiscence. ♀ Flower subsessile; tepals similar to those of ♂ flowers, however the inner ones often reduced or absent; ovary 8–21 mm in length, triangular in section; styles (2–)3, free parts entire, stigmatic zone longitudinal, entire. Infructescence often with 3 fruits. Fruit fusiform, 40–60 × 5–7 mm, often slightly curved, two faces red, the third white, dehiscent by 1 or 2 longitudinal slits.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo

## Ecology

semi-deciduous forests, preferably old cocoa and oil palm plantations, also in riparian forests; with average annual precipitation of 1500–2000 mm; between 100 and 700 m altitude
