# *Dioncophyllum thollonii* Baill.

**Family**: [[Dioncophyllaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Dioncophyllum]]

Bull. Soc. Linn. Paris **2**(109): 870 (1890).

## Description

Evergreen liana or scrambling shrub to 8(–40) m long or high. Stem (3–)4–6(–8) mm in diameter; internodes to 0.5 cm in the basal rosette and (0.5–)2–3.5(–6) cm on the climbing stem; indumentum of translucent scales 0.05–0.07 mm in diameter and erect rigid hairs 0.05 mm long.

Rosette leaves with petiole reduced to a pulvinus about 5 mm long; blade oblong, 35–44 × 5.9–7.6 cm, base cuneate, apex more or less acuminate with a 10–11 mm apicule, margin entire to slightly undulate or sometimes crenate; glands scattered, about 30–50 per blade, elliptic to orbicular and 0.25 mm long; midrib thickened and raised on both surfaces; secondary veins 106–118 pairs; marginal vein distinct; tertiary veins impressed. Stem leaves with petiole 7–16 mm, plano-convex except at the basal pulvinus; blade elliptic-oblong, (7–)9.5–16 × 4.3–6.8 cm, apicule 5–15 mm; each tendril 4–10 mm with a central mucro about 1.25 mm; secondary veins 34–58(–66) pairs.

Inflorescence inserted 1.2–2 cm above the axil, 12–23 × 15–21 cm, with 4–22 flowers; peduncle 5–7 cm; bracts inconspicuous, scattered below the pedicel, narrowly ovate, about 1.25 × 0.5 mm. Flower about 5 cm in diameter, fragrant; pedicel 5–6 cm at anthesis; calyx green and glossy, with (3–)5 ovate-acuminate lobes about 1.5 × 1.5 mm, erect and valvate in bud, reflexed after anthesis; petals left-contorted in bud, spreading at anthesis, thickened, narrowly elliptic-oblong, 21–24 × 5–7 mm, yellow-white outside and white inside; filaments erect, 10 mm, flattened at base and red; anthers 5 mm, pale yellow, sagittate at base; ovary conical, about 8 × 3 mm; styles 2 mm; stigmas minutely hairy.

Fruit star-shaped; valves 7–10 × 0.3–0.5 cm, acute, bearing 1–5 seeds internally. Seed 9–12 cm in diameter including the wing; funicle 30–50 × 2 mm.

## Distribution

Gabon and southern Republic of the Congo. Fairly common in Gabon and recorded from every province except Estuaire and Woleu-Ntem.

## Habitat and ecology

Primary and secondary forest, forest edge, riverine forest, roadsides and forest islands in savanna, on sandy or loamy soil; 15–700 m.
