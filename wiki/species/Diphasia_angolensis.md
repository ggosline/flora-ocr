# *Diphasia angolensis* (Hiern) Verdoorn

**Family**: [[Rutaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Diphasia]]

## Description

Small tree to 25 m tall and 50 cm diameter, branching low. Leaves are normally trifoliolate; petiole to 12 cm and petiolules to 2 cm are puberulent and yellow-hirsute. Leaflets reach 18 × 7 cm, with 8–12 lateral-vein pairs and a densely hirsute midrib above. Dense terminal or subterminal panicles are 6–12 cm long. Fragrant white flowers are usually bisexual and 4-merous, 4–5 mm long. The fruit is mericarpic and contains an embryo with fleshy cotyledons.

## Distribution

Gabon and Angola are represented in the treatment and synonymy.
