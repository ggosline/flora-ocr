# *Microsodium punctatum* (L.) Copeland

**Genus**: [[Microsodium]]
**Family**: [[Polypodiaceae]]

## Synonyms

- Acrostichum punctatum L., Sp. Pl. ed. 2, 2 : 1524 (1763).
- Polypodium polycarpon Cav. ex Sw., Schrad. Journ. 1800, 2 : 21 (1801).
- Polypodium irioides Poir. in Lam., Encycl. 5 : 513 (1804).
- Polypodium crassinerve Schum., K. Danske Vidensk. Selsk. 4 : 227 (1829).

## Description

— Cop., Univ. Cal. Pub. Bot. 16 : 111 (1929). — Ching, Bull. Fan. Mém. Inst. 4 : 307 (1923). — Tard., Mém. IFAN 28 : 221, pl. 41, f. 3-4 (1953). — Alston, FWTA : 49 (1959).

Rhizome short, thick, bearing black, caducous, peltate scales formed of cells with wide lumen, thickened walls. Fronds approximate, lanceolate, 30-90 cm long by 20-40 cm wide, blackish brown, entire, with acute apex, base progressively tapering towards the petiole broadly winged to the base. Texture thin, firm, surfaces glabrous. Midvein very prominent on the lower surface. Lateral veins slightly raised, reaching the margin; 5-6 rows of fine areoles containing numerous included veinlets. Sori small, irregularly scattered.

Old World tropics.

Epiphytic on trunks and branches; on rocks in forest understory.
