# *Cyperus sphacelatus* Rottb. Planche 45

**Genus**: [[Cyperus]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]

## Description

Annual herb, small to medium-sized, with stems slightly swollen at the base and numerous fine roots; stems 15–75 cm × 0.7–2 mm (but thicker at the level of the sheaths), trigonous, almost glabrous. Leaf: sheath green to reddish-brown; main blades 6–50 cm × 1.5–4 mm, flat or W-shaped, scabrous on the margins and main veins. Inflorescence an anthela formed by one sessile spike and 2–10 spikes on

Spikelet linear to linear–lanceolate, 6–25 × 1.2–2.5 mm, often with a reddish-brown central band, with 8–20 flowers; glumes ovate, 2.3–2.8 mm long, light brown often with dark reddish-brown spots and a green central nerve not exceeding or barely exceeding the glume. Flower with 3 stamens; style with 3 branches. Achene obovate, 1.1–1.4 × 0.6–0.8 mm, trigonous, brown, with almost smooth or finely papillose surface.

## Distribution

widely distributed in the tropical regions of Africa, America and the Pacific; common in Gabon, found in Estuaire, Haut-Ogooué, Moyen-Ogooué, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem (14 collections)

## Ecology

in open grassy areas and usually on sandy soil; in Gabon, 0–600 m, up to 1200 m in East Africa. Taxonomic note: recognized by its annual habit and its green spikelets often variegated with dark reddish-brown. B: Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al. (2010), Hooper & Napper (1972). ibliography
