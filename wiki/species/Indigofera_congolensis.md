# *Indigofera congolensis* De Wild. & T.Durand var. congolensis

**Genus**: [[Indigofera]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Herb. Boissier., sér. 2, 1 : 11 (1900)

## Description

Annual herb, 10–70 cm in height, with a taproot; stem strongly branched, ± sparsely white-strigulose. Stipules setaceous, 2–4 mm in length. Leaf: petiole 1–8 mm; rachis 2–26 mm; stipels well developed; leaflets 3 to 11, opposite, elliptic to ± obovate, 4–14 × 2–8 mm, glabrous above, sparsely strigulose below. Inflorescence with 1 to 4 flowers; peduncle slender, 5–15 mm. Flower ± sessile; calyx 1–1.5 mm in length, sparsely strigulose, tube ± 0.5 mm in length, lobes ± 1 mm in length, acute; corolla red, standard elliptic to subrhomboidal, 4 mm in length, (sub)sessile, externally ± strigulose, especially at the apex and centre, wings and keel ± 2.5 mm in length; stamens 4.5 mm; ovary 4 mm in length; style 1 mm. Pod strongly ascending or erect, linear, 9–18 × 1–1.5 mm, with 6 to 10 seeds, glabrous or nearly so. Seed quadrangular-cylindric, rounded, ± 0.7 mm in diameter, beige to brown, ± pitted.

## Distribution

from Senegal to East Africa; in Gabon, on the Batéké Plateaux (Haut-Ogooué)

## Ecology

savanna and marshy places; in Gabon at 460–600 m, elsewhere up to 1400 m. Use: Probably good forage

## Vernacular names

ontchoutcholountschiegi (batéke)

## Source note

The species has a complicated synonymy. In the literature, it has been confused with I. geminata Baker and I. sparsa Baker (not known from Gabon). The status of var. bongensis (Baker f.) Gillett, without stipels, from Sudan, remains doubtful. Bibliography: Cronquist (1954), Gillett (1971a), Hepper (1958), Walters et al. (2011).
