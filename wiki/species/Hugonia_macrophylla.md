# *Hugonia macrophylla* Oliver

**Genus**: [[Hugonia]]
**Family**: [[Linaceae]]
**Protologue**: FTA 1 : 271 (1868)

## Description

Lianas with young stems clothed in brown hairs. Adult stems glabrous, furnished with opposite hooks. Leaves petiolate; stipules laciniate, pubescent, long-persistent, reaching 2 cm in length; petiole pubescent, 0.5–1 cm; blade pubescent on the veins on the lower surface, with scattered hairs on the upper surface, except the midvein densely pubescent, obovate, cuneate at the base, apiculate to acuminate at the apex, entire, 10–30 × 5–10 cm. Secondary veins anastomosing, ascending, prominent on the lower surface as are the veinlets.

Flowers subsessile, grouped in globose clusters among the bracts at the apex of leafy branches, the leaves very closely spaced, forming pseudowhorls there. Bracts and bracteoles similar to the stipules. Floral buds ovoid-acute but not acuminate. Sepals pubescent outside, glabrous inside, erect, oval-rounded, acute at the apex, the inner ones broader, 10 × 6–9 mm. Petals yellow, pubescent dorsally, obovate, reaching 2.5 × 1.2 cm. Stamens glabrous, alternately long and short; anthers elliptic, glabrous, about 1.5 mm long. Ovary oblong, pubescent at the apex, about 5 × 4 mm; styles pubescent; stigmas terminal, capitate.

Fruit unknown. PL. 4, 8–10, p. 21.

## Type

Thomson 78, Nigeria (holo-, K!)

## Discussion

Forests of southern Nigeria and Gabon. This species probably occurs in Cameroon and Zaïre. We associate with it a sterile specimen (A. Bouquet 979) from Congo.
