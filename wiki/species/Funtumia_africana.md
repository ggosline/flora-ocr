# *Funtumia africana* (Benth.) Stapf Planche 35 ; Figure 5(C-E)

**Genus**: [[Funtumia]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Hooker’s Icon. Pl. 27 : tab. 2694-2695 (1901)

## Description

Tree reaching 30 m in height; trunk reaching 50 cm in diameter; bark smooth, sometimes with hexagonal or suborbicular lenticels or finely fissured in both directions, brown-green to grey spotted; latex white not coagulating easily, sticky; branchlets glabrous or puberulent. Leaf: petiole 0.5–1.6 cm long, 1–4 times as long as wide, 0.5–1.6 × 0.5–1.2 cm, with slightly undulate margin and somewhat revolute, subcoriaceous to coriaceous, often puberulent on the midrib beneath; secondary nerves 6 to 13 pairs, generally parallel; domatia consisting of a tuft of straight hairs in variable density, sometimes absent. Inflorescence: Flower: pedicel 3–15 mm, glabrous or puberulent; ripe bud 8.5–22 mm long, pale green; calyx with triangular to broadly ovate lobes, 1.5–4 × 1.8–3 mm, glabrous or puberulent outside, with 5 to 50 colleters of variable form and size inside; corolla with subcylindric tube, inflated at 0.5–1 of the base, 1–4 times as long as wide, 0.8–2.5–5 mm, very pale green to cream, glabrous to puberulent outside, with recurved lobes, 0.5–1.6 times as long as the tube, 1–4 times as long as wide, 0.5–2 × 0.8–2 mm, cream, glabrous to puberulent on both surfaces, sometimes pubescent near the base inside; stamens on the inflated part of the tube, anther 0.5–1 × 0.8–1.2 mm, with appressed dorsal pubescence; pistil 4–7 mm long, ovary slightly 5-lobed and pubescent at the apex, hairiness gradually diminishing towards the base; style 1.2–3 mm. Fruit: subfusiform follicles, 8.4–32 × 1.4–8 cm, acuminate to acute or rarely subobtuse at the apex, sometimes slightly curved, with flat side (adaxial) 0.6–3.6 cm wide. Seed 3.5–7.5 × 0.2–0.6 cm, with beak 1.6–6 cm long, furnished with tufted hairs 3–9 cm pointing backwards and surrounding the seed, with basal part of beak glabrous or with a small raised nullor up to 1 cm wrinkled testulose. Distribution: Tropical Africa from Senegal to Tanzania and south to Zimbabwe and Mozambique; in Gabon recorded in all provinces except Ogooué Maritime. Ecology: Primary and secondary forest, open forest, gallery forest in Gabon, up to m altitude; elsewhere 0–1300 m.
