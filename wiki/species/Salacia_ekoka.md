# *Salacia ekoka* Louis ex Wilczek

**Genus**: [[Salacia]]
**Family**: [[Celastraceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Jard. Bot. Etat Brux. 27: 689 (1957); FCB 9: 193 (1960)

## Description

Large liane reaching 10–12 cm in diameter, containing rubber. Young branches with reddish-brown bark when dry, later greyish and lenticellate. Leaves opposite or subopposite, with caducous stipules. Petiole 6–14 mm, with straight channelside margins. Limb elliptic or oblong, brown to brownish when dry, subcoriaceous, 6–14(–16) × 2–5.5 cm, entire or confusedly subdentate, with acute base, ± acuminate apex. Secondary veins 7–11 pairs slightly ascending and then arched; reticulation moderately dense.

Inflorescences in bunches or multiflorous fascicles often spherical, 2–3 cm in diameter, on sessile or subsessile axillary glomerules. Pedicel 5.5–13 mm. Buds ovoid, about 2 × 2 mm. Small yellow flowers 3.5–6 mm in diameter, with corolla often reflexed. Sepals unequal, 0.7–1.3 mm wide, with entire or ± eroded margin. Petals ovate, 1.6–2.7 × 1.5–2 mm (rehydrated and flat), paucinerved. Disk truncate-conical, with truncate or ± bosselated apex, 1–1.9 mm in diameter and 0.5–0.7 mm thick. Stamens 1–1.8 mm, spaced apart at the base, inserted in fossettes; anther emarginate or apiculate, 0.6–0.8 mm wide, with chevron-shaped slits forming an acute or almost right angle. Locules half-sunken each containing (1)2 superposed or sometimes juxtaposed ovules. Subulate style 0.5–2 mm; stigmas punctiform, sometimes lobulate at the end of anthesis.

Fruit globose 1.5–3 cm in diameter, rugose, with 1–2 seeds whose seeds are rich in rubber. – Pl. 44, fig. 16–29, p. 155.

## Discussion

Zaire and Gabon; plant to be sought in Cameroon. In riparian inundable or swampy forest.

This species is related to S. coronata (see p. 154) and S. miegei Hallé, endemic to western Côte-d'Ivoire.

Côte-d'Ivoire, Ghana, Togo, Cameroon, Somalia, Tanzania, Zanzibar, Mozambique. ROBSON in 1966, probably for geographical reasons, rejects the synonymy of S. lomensis proposed by us in 1958 and 1962. Unfortunately his point of view is not supported by morphological arguments; he notes a relationship with S. congolensis without elaborating. Our new analyses confirm the relationship between S. lomensis and S. stuhlmanniana: the number of ovules is the same, namely 2 per locule; the sepals are unequal, eroded-dentate and similar; the disk is truncate-conical (Lock 43528, Ghana) as Pedro & Pedrogao 5468, from Mozambique; it lacks a perigynous groove like Schlieben 2225, from Tanzania. This synonymy can thus be maintained while recognizing that the vegetative differences are of at least varietal interest and even rather subspecific.

The presence of S. stuhlmanniana in Cameroon is based on the specimen Staudt 591, Johann Albrechtshöhe (Kumba), fl. Feb. (type not found of S. gilgiana Loes.). Another specimen is referred to this species, but with doubt because of the number of ovules which is 4 per locule: Mildbraed 4249, env. of Moloundou, 15.15'E, 2.30'N, 14.1.1911 (fl.), HBG! This species, observed as often riparian, is very poorly known in Cameroon; it is to be sought in Gabon.

43 ter. Salacia lucida Oliver

FTA 1: 373 (1868); LOES., Bot Jahrb. 44: 165 (1910); HUTCH. & MOSS, FWTA 1: 453 (1928); LOES., Pflanzenf., ed. 2., 20B: 223 (1942); HALLÉ, Monogr. Hippocr. Afr. occid., Thèse: 216 (1958); MÉM. IFAN 64: 220 (1962).

Glabrous liane 5–20 m in height, containing rubber; young branches slightly compressed at the nodes; cylindrical branches with smooth bark. Leaves opposite or clearly subopposite. Petiole 6–7–8 mm. Limb thin, somewhat discolorous and brown to light rusty when dry, glossy above, elliptic, 4.5–13.5 × 2–7.5 cm; base acute; apex with acumen 5–11 mm, rounded at the extremity; margin dentate-dentate. Secondary veins 7–9 pairs; fine reticulation visible only by transparency.

Inflorescences multiflorous axillary pedunculate, ± asymmetrically dichasial, the ramifications often ± alternate; the floral bouquet of 2 opposite inflorescences can measure 3–5 cm in diameter. Very small acute bracts. Pedicels 3.5–5 mm. Bud rather narrow and conical, 3 × 1.8 mm. Flower pale green, ± cream or yellowish, rusty when dry, 6 mm in diameter. Sepals 5, 0.8 mm, very unequal in width, 0.6–1.3 mm, the small outer acute, the large inner very rounded. Petals very little overlapping each other at the top of the bud, about 3.2 × 1.4 mm, wide only at the base, folded channel-wise and with ± narrowly cucullate apex. Disk frusto-conical 1 mm for a diameter at the base of 1.3 mm, subtrigonous at mid-height and subhexagonal at the level of stamen insertion, with finely guttulate papillose surface. Stamens exceeding 2 mm, filament thickened in the axis. Sagittate anther 1.2 × 0.8 mm, with acute acumen 0.2 mm, with locules parallel for more than half their length. Subulate pyramidal style trifurcate into acute stigmas at the apex. Ovary locules sunken one-third or one-half below the level of insertion of the androecium and each containing 5–7 ovules, mostly superposed.

Fruit unknown. – Pl. 46, fig. 1–9, p. 161.

Nigeria, Cameroon; to be sought in Muni and Gabon. Rare species sometimes frequenting littoral forests and mangroves.

Liane glabre contenant du caoutchouc. Bois montrant en coupe des cernes concentriques interrompus. Feuilles opposées ou subopposées. Pétiole de 3-6(-11) mm. Limbe brun roux subdiscolore et subcoriace de 5-11×2,5-4,5 cm, à base brièvement cunéiforme, à acumen court, à marge subentière ou dentée. Nervures secondaires 7-10 paires.

Fleurs axillaires en fascicules sessiles pauciflores ou jusqu'à 20-flores. Bouton ové. Pédicelle plutôt grêle de 3-8 mm. Sépales très inégaux et dentés sublaciées. Pétales ovés de 2,2-3 × 1,75-2,5 mm. Disque tronconique de 1,7 mm de diamètre, 0,6 mm d'épaisseur et faiblement étranglé à mi-hauteur, un peu lobé verticalement entre les bases des pétales. Étamines de 1-2 mm à anthère largement cordiforme plus large que longue, microapiculée ou non. Pistil pyramidal à stigmate étroitement tronqué. Loges enfoncées de plus de la moitié de leur hauteur ; 2 ovules par loge superposés.

Fruit globoïde de 2-3 cm de diamètre, lisse ou ± rugueux et tri-costé, au moins vers le sommet. – Pl. 45, fig. 24-30, p. 159.
