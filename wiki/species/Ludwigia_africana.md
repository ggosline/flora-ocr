# *Ludwigia africana* (Brenan) H.Hara

**Family**: [[Onagraceae]] · **Genus**: [[Ludwigia]]

J. Jap. Bot. **28**(10): 291 (1953).

## Description

Herb, sometimes scrambling, to 3(–4) m, with short pubescence mixed with longer erect hairs. Stems hollow, angled, reddish-brown and soon woody. Petiole 2–15 mm, swollen at base; blade ovate-elliptic, 1.5–8 × 0.5–2(–3) cm, broadly cuneate, acuminate, dark green above and grey-white beneath; lateral veins 12–17(–20) pairs. Axillary racemes rather lax.

Pedicel (2–)3–15 mm with two acute ovate-triangular bracteoles about 0.5 mm; sepals 4(–5), 2–4 mm; petals 4(–5), suborbicular, 5–6 mm in diameter, yellow to golden; stamens 8; ovary about 10 × 0.5 mm; style 2–3 mm. Fruit cylindrical or four-angled, sometimes slightly curved, 17–25 × 2 mm, puberulent, thin-walled and irregularly dehiscent. Seeds horizontal in one row, ovoid, 0.6–0.8 × 0.4–0.5 mm, within a triangular or horseshoe-shaped endocarp fragment.

## Distribution

West and Central Africa from Guinea to western Democratic Republic of the Congo. Fairly common in Gabon in Estuaire, Haut-Ogooué, Nyanga and Woleu-Ntem.

## Habitat and ecology

Sunny marshes, flooded forest, marsh grassland and stream margins; to 730 m in Gabon and 1500 m elsewhere.
