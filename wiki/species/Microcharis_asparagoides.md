# *Microcharis asparagoides* (Taub.) Schrire Planche 86

**Genus**: [[Microcharis]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bothalia 22 : 165 (1992)

## Synonyms

- Indigofera asparagoides Taub. in Engl., Pflanzenw. Ost-Afrikas C: 210 (1895).

## Description

Perennial herb with woody base, ± glabrous; stems slender, prostrate or rooting, up to 60 cm long. Stipules subulate, 0.2–1 mm long. Leaf paripinnate: petiole 2–6 mm; apex of rachis resembling a terminal leaflet; leaflets 4 to 12, opposite, with linear limb, 3–21 × 0.1–0.8 mm, sparsely strigulose. Inflorescence axillary; raceme 1.5–7 cm long, including peduncle of 1–3 cm, with 2 to 10 flowers; bracts subulate, 0.5–1 mm long. Flower: pedicel 1–5 mm; calyx 1.8–2.8 mm long, with triangular to subulate lobes, as long to 2 times longer than the tube; corolla red or purplish pink, standard 4–5 mm long, wings exceeding the standard and keel, 5–7 mm long; stamens 2–3 mm. Pod straight, 8–32 × 1–1.7 mm, with 4 to 16 seeds, ± glabrous. Seed rectangular, ± 1 mm long.

## Distribution

Gabon and Republic of Congo to Kenya, in Tanzania and Zambia; in Gabon, rare, known from Ogooué-Ivindo (Bélinga)

## Ecology

savanna, periodically marshy stations, open terrain and fallows; in Gabon around 500 m altitude, elsewhere at 500–1700 m. b: Cronquist (1954), Gillett (1971a), Schrire (1992, 2012). Bibliography
