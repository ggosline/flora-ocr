# *Zingiber officinale* Rosc.

**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Zingiber]]

## Description

Aromatic perennial with a thick branched rhizome and leafy shoots separate from the flowering shoots. Leaves distichous, narrow and ligulate. Dense spikes bear one flower per bract; tubular bidentate calyx often splits laterally, and the corolla has a broad concave posterior lobe. Lateral staminodes unite with the spreading labellum; the fertile stamen has a long connective appendage surrounding the style.

## Uses and distribution

Asian crop cultivated in Gabon for its pungent aromatic ginger rhizomes, used as condiment and medicine.
