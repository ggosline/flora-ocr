# *Afrobrunnichia erecta* (Aschers.) Hutch. et Dalz.

**Family**: [[Polygonaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Afrobrunnichia]]

## Description

Large woody liana climbing to 30 m, with glabrous striate branches and forked axillary tendrils. Leaves broadly elliptic, 6–12 × 2.5–5 cm, abruptly acuminate, with 5–7 lateral-vein pairs and ochrea reduced to an annular ridge. Loose racemes reach 20 cm. Pink flowers have a 7–8 mm 5-lobed perianth and six exserted stamens; the winged pedicel becomes 5–6 cm long in fruit. Achene slightly trigonous, c. 1 cm.

## Distribution

Forest edges and galleries from Côte d’Ivoire to Gabon; the genus extends to Angola. Gabon material includes Libreville, Mayombe-Bayaka, Haute Ngounyé and Etéké at 800 m.
