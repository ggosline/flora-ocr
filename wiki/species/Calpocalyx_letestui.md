# *Calpocalyx letestui* Pellegrin

**Genus**: [[Calpocalyx]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. bot. France 87 : 261 (1940) ; Légum. Gabon : 25 (1948) ; VILLIERS, BMNHN 6 (3) : 307 (1984)

## Description

Small tree 7 m tall with a diameter of ± 10 cm. Young twig densely velutinous pubescent reddish-brown on the ridges; glabrous or glabrescent patches between them. Stipules caducous. Petiole velutinous pubescent reddish-brown darkly, 4.5–9.6 cm long, cylindrical, apex thickened bearing a gland on its upper surface. Rachis of the pinnae velutinous pubescent reddish-brown to brown, cylindrical to furrowed above and carinate below, 12–23 cm long, glands present between the apical leaflets. Leaflets 3–4 pairs of decreasing size from apex to base. Petiolule pubescent, cylindrical to weakly canaliculate above, 5–8 mm long. Blade coriaceous, glabrous above and erect pubescent below; dark brown to blackish-brown ± lustrous above and reddish-brown below when dry; broadly elliptic, elliptic-oblong to oblanceolate (apical leaflet), 7–19.5 × 4–10 cm; base ± distinctly asymmetric: proximal margin rounded very weakly attenuate (rarely weakly cordate) and distal margin rounded to weakly cordate; apex obtuse rounded retuse mucronate. Midvein concave (at least at the base) to weakly prominent above, very strongly prominent below; pubescent on both surfaces. Secondary veins 6–12, concave to weakly prominent above and very strongly prominent below, glabrous to weakly pubescent below, ascending arched. Veinlets in dense ± scalariform network (for the more prominent ones) prominent above and very strongly prominent below.

Inflorescences in fascicled spikes axillary to the terminal leaves or panicle of fascicled spikes. Axis of the panicle strongly ribbed with velutinous reddish-brown pubescence on the ridges and glabrous or glabrescent in the furrows at the base, entirely pubescent at the apex. Subtending bract of the spikes, velutinous pubescent, arched, 1.25–2 mm long. Rachis of the spikes densely velutinous pubescent, grouped by 1–3, thick, ribbed, 3.5–7 cm long. Bracteole persistent, densely pubescent, lanceolate, 1–1.25 mm long, apex acute. Flower bud ellipsoid. Flower ♂ sessile, yellowish to brownish in the living state. Calyx cylindrical to weakly obconical, densely pubescent externally, 2.5–3 mm long, lobes free triangular, 0.50–0.75 mm long; apex acute. Petals pubescent externally, connate for 1/3 or 1/2 of the length, weakly whitish, 3–4.5 mm long, apex acute. Stamens 10, free, of variable size: 5.5–8 mm; filament cream in the living state; anther brown in the living state, oblongoid 0.5 mm long; connective prolonged by a pedicellate gland. Pistillode subsessile; ovary densely pubescent, aborted, 0.6 mm long; style absent. Flower ♀ perianth and androecium as above. Pistil substituted, ovary pubescent containing 6 ovules, style eccentric slender terminating in an acute stigma.

Fruit unknown. – Pl. 29, p. 109.

## Type

Le Testu 8265, Gabon (lecto-, iso-, P!)

## Discussion

The range of this species is currently limited to the East and to the mountainous part of the South Centre of Gabon.
