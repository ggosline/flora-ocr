# *Bulbophyllum kindtianum* De Wild., Not. Pl. Ut. et Int. Fl. Congo 2 : 309 (1904).

**Genus**: [[Bulbophyllum]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]

## Description

– Bulbophyllum mildbraedii Kraenzl., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 43 : 340 (1909).

– Bulbophyllum nudiscapum Rolfe, Kew Bull. 9 : 365 (1909).

– Bulbophyllum graciliscapum Summerh., Kew Bull. 8 : 579 (1954).

– Bulbophyllum saltatorium Lindl. var. albociliatum (Finet) J. J. Verm., Bull. J. Bot. Nat. Belg. 56: 230 (1986).

Pseudobulbs < 5 × 2 cm, conical-ovoid, 1-leaved. Leaf petiolate; 1.3-20.5 × 0.8-4.6 cm, oblong-lanceolate to ovate-elliptic or ovate, obtuse. Inflorescence erect, lax, 6.5-84 cm, 4-105-flowered. Flowers medium-sized, tepals glabrous. Sepals acute, yellowish or greenish, generally tinged with purple or brown-purple, or entirely red-purple: the dorsal oblong-lanceolate to ovate-lanceolate, the lateral ones obliquely oblong-lanceolate to oblong-ovate. Petals linear, acuminate, red-purple. Lip oblong-lanceolate to linear-lanceolate, acute, thick, greenish to red-purple, sometimes white toward the apex ornamented with long white, pink or purple hairs directed toward the base, becoming short, velvety or woolly along the adaxial margin. – Pl. 67, p. 139.

## Distribution

Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Congo (Brazzaville), Congo (Kinshasa), Uganda, Angola

## Ecology

alt. 0-750 m. Epiphyte on lianas, on canopy and on tree bark, in various forest types: primary, swamp, degraded, logged; of lowland and coastal altitude
