# *Dalbergia semiapplanata* O.Lachenaud Planche 49

**Genus**: [[Dalbergia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Symb. Bot. Ups. 38 : 188 (2016)

## Description

, nom. inval., pro parte . Liane or shrub reaching at least 2 m in height, inodorous, without spines, but sometimes with hooked prehensile branchlets; bark dark brown with pale lenticels; young branchlets with short spreading hairs, or more rarely glabrous. Stipules unknown, very caducous. Leaf imparipinnate; petiole 0.5–1.9 cm; rachis 1.5–6.7 cm, with short spreading hairs, or more rarely glabrous; leaflets 4 to 7, alternate and very unequal, with petiolule 1.5–5 mm, blade elliptic, symmetric (or sometimes slightly asymmetric on basal leaflets), 3.2–9.5 × 1.9–6.2 cm on upper leaflets and 0.8–3.7 × 0.6–3.3 cm on lower ones, rounded or acute at the base, long-acuminate at the apex, ± coriaceous, becoming brownish when dry, with the undersurface slightly glaucescent, glabrous on both faces or (generally) villous on the undersurface, with hairs ± erect and rather long (0.5–1 mm); lateral veins (4–)6 to 10, fairly fine, but more prominent than the veinlets, ascending and united in arches near the margin, veinlets in fairly dense and prominent concolorous reticulum on the undersurface. Inflorescence in a short panicle of unilateral racemes, axillary or on old branchlets, 0.7–2 cm in length, with dense golden-brown and ± spreading hairs; bracts persistent after flowering, broadly ovate, ± 0.5 × 0.5 mm, pubescent. Flower: pedicel 1–3 mm; bracteoles persistent after flowering, ovate or broadly elliptic, ± 0.5 × 0.3 mm, pubescent; calyx pale green, with short dense appressed hairs, tube 1–1.5 × 1.7–2.3 mm, teeth valvate, ± equal, triangular, acute, 0.3–0.8 mm in length; corolla white, petals with claw extending well beyond the calyx, standard bent obliquely, with claw 2–2.3 mm and elliptic limb 3.5–4 × 3 mm, with bilobed apex, wings equalling the keel, with claw 2.5–2.7 mm and elliptic or rectangular limb 3.3–4 × 1.3 mm, smooth externally, with truncate base, keel strongly curved, with claw 2.5 mm and limb 3.5 × 2–2.3 mm, with truncate base or shortly auriculate; stamens in 2 phalanges of 5, filaments ± 6 mm, united on the 4/5 of their length, straight at the base and curved in their free part; ovary ± 1.5 × 0.5 mm, glabrous, with 2 ovules and straight stipe 3–4 mm; style linear, ± 1.5 mm, with punctiform stigma. Pod with pedicel 2–3 mm and stipe 5–9 mm, woody, broadly ellipsoid, flat when young, then with central part distinctly convex, 6–7 mm in thickness, surrounded by a margin remaining flat, 4–6.3 × 2.5–3.6 cm, acute or obtuse at the apex, slightly reticulate, glabrous, with 1(–2) seed; pericarp with outer layer very thin, ± 0.1 mm in thickness, endocarp hard, ± 0.2 mm. Seed reniform, rather thick, laterally compressed, ± 30 × 20 × 5 mm at maturity.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea (Rio Muni) and Gabon; rather rare in Gabon, known from the environs of Booué (Ogooué-Ivindo) and Oyem (Woleu-Ntem)

## Ecology

river banks and dry vegetation on inselbergs; altitude not recorded in Gabon, elsewhere 500–1130 m. Flowering in March and May; immature fruits in January and July, ripe in March

## Source note

This species is very close to D. xylocarpa from which it is difficult to separate in the absence of fruits; the latter, however, has a more coastal distribution, not extending beyond Ndjolé inland. The two species have long been confused with D. monetaria which is exclusively American (Lachenaud & Maesen 2016). In the vegetative state, D. semiapplanata can also be confused with D. setifera and D. stenopetala, but its leaflets are thicker, ± glaucescent on the undersurface and with less apparent reticulation. The description of D. monetaria var. oyemensis Pellegr. is not valid, and the two specimens cited under this name belong to different species; see the note under D. pachycarpa. b: Lachenaud & Maesen (2016). ibliography
