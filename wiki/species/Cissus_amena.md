# *Cissus amena* Gilg et Brandt

**Genus**: [[Cissus]]
**Family**: [[Vitaceae]]
**Protologue**: Gilg et Brandt, Engl. Bot. Jahrb. 46 : 467, fig. 4 (1912)

## Description

Bushy, lianoid plant. Stems cylindrical, up to 5 mm in diameter, densely pubescent becoming glabrescenti; internodes short, 2-5 cm. Tendrils several times bifurcate, more or less densely pubescent; bracteoles deltoid, broadly cordate at the base, 3-4 mm long, glabrous. Stipules oblong somewhat ovate, 6-8 mm long, 3-4 mm wide, rounded at the apex, narrowly cordate at the base, glabrous or glabrescenti on the external face. Leaves simple entire. Petiole cylindrical, 2-5 cm long, slender, regularly and densely pubescent. Blade more or less ovate cordate, 6-8 cm long, 5-6 cm wide, with apex prolonged into a short and acute acumen, with cordate or subhastate base; margin presenting numerous very short teeth; upper surface bearing some curly hairs and some reddish medifixed hairs; on the lower surface blade very densely covered with a very short appressed indumentum, the veins bearing in addition some scattered medifixed hairs; venation palmatipinnate, network ladder-like; texture somewhat thick.

Inflorescences up to 12 cm long, in compound cymes of umbelliform multiflorous cymules; axes cylindrical bearing the same pubescence as the stems, with in addition scattered medifixed hairs denser at the nodes; peduncle slender, 2-6 cm long; bracts and bracteoles oblong, 1-2 mm long, the lower part thickened into a strong protuberance, glabrescenti. Flowers elongate, more or less cylindrical, 2.5-3 mm long. Pedicel cylindrical, 2-3 mm long, entirely and rather densely covered with a pilositi formed of curly hairs and medifixed hairs mixed. Calyx cupuliform, approximately 0.7 mm high, more or less loosely pubescent. Corolla cylindrical oblong, loosely or more or less densely covered with very fine puberulence; petals oblong, approximately 2.5 mm long. Stamens approximately 1.6 mm long; anthers approximately 0.5 mm long, with connective thickened into a wedge. Glands narrow and tall. Ovary approximately 1 mm high, very densely covered with long reddish medifixed hairs.

Syntypes from Cameroon: Dusen 263, Preuss 537 and Zenker 3019. Climbing shrub with flowers characteristic for their elongate form. Equatorial Africa: Cameroon and North Gabon.
