# *Cissus amena* Gilg et Brandt

**Genus**: [[Cissus]]
**Family**: [[Vitaceae]]
**Protologue**: Gilg et Brandt, Engl. Bot. Jahrb. 46 : 467, fig. 4 (1912)

## Synonyms

- Gilg et Brandt, Engl. Bot. Jahrb. 46 : 467, fig. 4 (1912). — Dewit, Fl. Congo belge et R.-U. 9 : 532 (1960).

## Description

Shrubby plant, lianoid. Stems cylindrical, up to 5 mm in diameter, densely pubescent becoming glabrescent; internodes short, 2–5 cm. Tendrils several times bifurcate, more or less densely pubescent; bracteoles deltoid, broadly cordate at the base, 3–4 mm long, glabrous. Stipules oblong somewhat oval, 6–8 mm long, 3–4 mm wide, rounded at the apex, narrowly cordate at the base, glabrous or glabrescent on the external surface. Leaves simple entire. Petiole cylindrical, 2–5 cm long, slender, regularly and densely pubescent. Blade more or less oval cordiform, 6–8 cm long, 5–6 cm wide, with apex prolonged into a short acute acumen, with cordate or subhastate base; margin bearing numerous very short teeth; upper surface bearing a few curly hairs and a few reddish medifixed hairs; on the lower surface blade very densely covered with a very short appressed indument, the veins bearing in addition a few scattered medifixed hairs; venation palmatipinnate, network ladder-like; texture somewhat thick.

Inflorescences up to 12 cm long, in compound cymes of umbelliform multiflorous cymules; axes cylindrical bearing the same pubescence as the stems, with in addition scattered medifixed hairs denser at the nodes; peduncle slender, 2–6 cm long; bracts and bracteoles oblong, 1–2 mm long, the lower part thickened into a strong protuberance, glabrescent. Flowers elongate, more or less cylindrical, 2.5–3 mm long. Pedicel cylindrical, 2–3 mm long, entirely and fairly densely covered with a pilosity formed of curly hairs and mixed medifixed hairs. Calyx cupuliform, approximately 0.7 mm high, more or less loosely pubescent. Corolla cylindrical oblong, loosely or more or less densely covered with a very fine puberulence; petals oblong, approximately 2.5 mm long. Stamens approximately 1.6 mm long; anthers approximately 0.5 mm long, with thickened wedge-shaped connective. Glands narrow and tall. Ovary approximately 1 mm high, very densely covered with long reddish medifixed hairs.

Syntypes from Cameroon: Dusen 263, Preuss 537 and Zenker 3019. Climbing shrub with flowers characteristic in their elongate form. Equatorial Africa: Cameroon and North Gabon.
