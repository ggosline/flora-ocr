# *Tricalysia atherura* N. Hallé, sp. nov.

**Genus**: [[Tricalysia]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]

## Description

Shrub 3-8 m tall. Petiole 4-13 mm, sulcate. Lamina olivaceous entirely glabrous 7-21 × 2.8-8 cm, base ± shortly acute. Lateral nerves 6-8 pairs. Inflorescences axillary few-flowered in cymes with peduncle 0.5-1 mm long, axis pubescent along two opposite ribs. Bracts cupuliform. Flowers white. Calyx uniformly pubescent throughout, margin 4.5 mm tall, 7-8 dentate. Corolla tube 12-14 mm long with throat entirely glabrous; lobes 7-8, 11-13 mm long, lanceolate-acute, at apex and margins somewhat pubescent. Stamens glabrous with anthers submedifixed, 7-9 mm long, filament 4-5 mm long. Style glabrous 20-25 mm long bilobed. Placenta peltate, ovules 6 arranged in a U-shape. Fruit golden 25-35 mm, in diameter with persistent-green calyx lamina, pericarp 3 mm thick. Seed 10 × 6 × 3 mm, hilum linear-elongate.

Shrub 3 to 8 m tall with glabrous branch tips. Stipules glabrous, enlarged to 1-2 mm, with subulate rostrum 1-3 mm. Petiole 4-13 mm, with sulcate channel. Blade olivaceous entirely glabrous elliptic 7-21 × 2.8-8 cm, base acute sometimes shortly, apex lanceolate or shortly acuminate, sometimes subobtuse. Midrib moderately sulcate; 6-8 pairs of secondary nerves; intersecondary nerves moderately conspicuous. Nerves and nerve axils entirely glabrous.

Inflorescences axillary 1-3 flowered in pedunculate cyme 0.5-1 mm. Axes puberulent along two opposite ribs. Bracts connate cupuliform moderately biapiculate. Flowers white sessile in the upper cupule, pedicellate below on 3-5 (7) mm. Calyx green approximately uniformly puberulent on the ovary and on the cupuliform limb 4-5 mm tall, 7-8 dentate, 4-6 mm in diameter. Corolla with glabrous tube 12-14 mm glabrous at the throat and inside. 7-8 lobes 11-13 × 3-5 mm, lanceolate subacute, somewhat pubescent at the apex and on the margins, overlapping to the left. 7-8 stamens glabrous with filament 4-5 mm, anther submedifixed 7-9 mm, apiculate, with 2 non-septate locules. Style glabrous 20-25 mm, with 2 stigmatic lobes 3-4 mm. Disk small. Placenta circular peltate somewhat above the centre, with 6 ovules arranged in a U-shape.

Fruit green becoming orange reaching 27-35 mm in diameter, crowned by the green calyx. Wall thick about 3 mm becoming pulpy and pink at maturity (Breteler 1933), with endocarp moulding the seeds and interstices. Seeds reddish reaching 10 × 6 × 3 mm, with hilum stretched marginally along the entire length of the testa.

## Type

N. Hallé 3405 (P), Bélinga (fl. 5 déc. 1964)

## Discussion

Occurs in Cameroon, Yaoundé region (Breteler 1933 and Letouzey 4205; the latter is somewhat more vigorous and with broader leaves than the type).

Other Gabonese material:

N. Hallé and A. Le Thomas 609, 626, Bélinga (bot. July).
