# *Saba comorensis* (Bojer) Pichon Planche 99 ; Figure 11(D)

**Genus**: [[Saba]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Mém. Inst. Franç. Afrique Noire 35 : 303 (1953)

## Description

Landolphia florida Benth. in Hook., Niger Fl. : 444 (1849). S. comorensis (Bojer) Pichon var. florida (Benth.) Pichon, Mém. Inst. Franç. Afrique Noire 35 : 309 (1953). S. florida (Benth.) Bullock, Kew Bull. 13 : 391 (1959). Liane atteignant 40 m de longueur; écorce rugueuse écailleuse souvent fissurée. Leaf: petiole 0.5–1 mm, limb 1–2 times as long as it is wide, 0.5–5 × 0.5–3 cm at summit rounded, obtuse, sometimes apiculate or briefly acuminate; secondary nerves 7 to 14 pairs, tertiary venation primarily scalariform in the basal half and elsewhere reticulate, submarginal nerve absent. Inflorescence: raceme condensed with numerous flowers; peduncle 2–6(–32) cm. Fragrant flower; pedicel 1–7 mm; calyx with lobes 1.7–4 × 1–2 mm; corolla with tube 2–3 times as long as the calyx slightly shorter than the lobes long 16–34 mm, with obovate lobes, 2–3.5 times as long as wide, long 19–41 mm, generally white with yellow or orange near the throat; stamens inserted 5.8–9.3 mm above the base of the tube; filament 1.5–2 mm; anther long 1.2–1.8 mm; ovary with

a setulose band 0.2–1 mm wide just below the apex; style 2.4–6.5 mm, pistil with head 2–3 mm long. Fruit globose to ellipsoid 8–12 × 7–10 cm orange to red; wall 1–2.4 cm thick; pulp yellowish to orange. Distribution from Senegal to Madagascar and from Ethiopia to Zimbabwe, in Gabon uniquely in the Nyanga. Ecology: gallery forests in Gabon up to 800 m altitude elsewhere up to 1200 m. Use: The fruits are edible. The latex has been used as a rubber of low minimum quality. The bark is used in the treatment of rheumatism. The plant is ornamental and can serve as a hedge. Vernacular names: m'kâmissère, andzi, ifang, mundèlè, mindumunème, bamitsogo, vingili, tsyèngi (Eshira).
