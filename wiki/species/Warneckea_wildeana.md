# *Warneckea wildeana* Jacques-Félix

**Genus**: [[Warneckea]]
**Family**: [[Melastomataceae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia, sér. 2, 19 (3) : 260, tab. 2 (1980); Fl. Cam. 24 : 170, tab. 56 (1983)

## Description

Adansonia, sér. 2, 19 (3) : 260, tab. 2 (1980); Fl. Cam. 24 : 170, tab. 56 (1983).

Small tree approximately 8 m tall; branches rounded, robust. Leaves smooth, glossy above; petiole 1 cm long; blade elliptic, up to 9 × 20 mm, cuneate on the petiole, with an acumen of 1.5 cm; 5 veins impressed above, prominent below, the main convergent ones 10 mm from the margin in the middle portion, supplemented by a pair of submarginal additional veins, coarctate; 8–10 main transversals, prominent on both faces, especially below, as well as the rather dense tertiary network.

Fasciculate cymes on the nodes of old wood, subsessile, briefly branched, 1.5 cm long and totalling about twenty flowers; bracts broadly ovate, 1–1.5 mm long, long-persistent. Flower with pedicel 5–8 mm; hypantho-calyx cupulo-patellate, approximately 4 × 2 mm; calyx sinuate or lobes shallowly incised. Petals and stamens unknown.

Fruit globose, 6 mm diameter. — Pl. 57, p. 157.

## Type

De Wilde J.J.F.E. 8686, Cameroun (WAG)

## Discussion

Species of the Cameroon-Gabonese forest, typical for its flowers almost glomerate on the nodes of old wood.
