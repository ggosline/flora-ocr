# *Buchnerodendron speciosum* Gürk

**Genus**: [[Buchnerodendron]]
**Family**: [[Flacourtiaceae]]
**Protologue**: EBJ 18 : 161, t. 6 (1893) ; WARB., in ENGL. & PRANTL, NPF 3 (6a) : 20 (1893) ; SLEUMER, EBJ 94 (3) : 291 (1974). TYPES : Mechow 562, Angola (lecto-)

## Description

WARB., in ENGL. & PRANTL, NPF 3 (6a) : 20 (1893) ; SLEUMER, EBJ 94 (3) : 291 (1974). TYPES : Mechow 562, Angola (lecto-).

– B. laurentii DE WILD., Miss. Laurent. : 411 (1907). TYPES : Laurent s. n., 24/11/1903, Zaïre (lecto-, BR, cf. SLEUMER 1974 ; isolecto-, BM) ; Laurent s.n., a. 1903, Zaïre (syn-, BR).

Shrub or small tree (7-12 m high), with hollow stem; branches greyish-reddish, tomentose when young.

Leaves with linear-acuminate, tomentose stipules; petiole 4-30 cm long, tomentose; blade broadly ovate to suborbicular, cordate at base, acuminate at apex, 10-40-(50) × 6-40 cm, dentate, papyraceous, pubescent; secondary veins 8-12 pairs.

Flowers ♂ or ♀ and ♀ (polygamous plants); pedicel 2-4 cm long, articulate, tomentose; sepals 3, ovate, 10-16 × 5-8 mm, tomentose with branched cilia on outer face, glabrous on inner face; petals 6-8, ± elliptic, 15-17 × 3-5 mm, glabrescent; stamens arranged in 2 rows, the outer (10) fused to each other by the hooked cilia of their margin, 10-14 mm long and 2 mm wide, with filaments as wide as the anthers; the inner (10) smaller, free, with flattened filaments, 2-3 mm long and 1 mm wide, anthers 4-5 mm long, apiculate; ovary tomentose, with 4 pluriovulate placentas; style entire, 8-10 mm long, pubescent at base.

Fruits: subglobose capsules, 5-6 cm in diameter, covered with prickles; seeds angular, 3-4 × 2-3 mm, ± pubescent. - Pl. 11, p. 41.
