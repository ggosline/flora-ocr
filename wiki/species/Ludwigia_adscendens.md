# *Ludwigia adscendens* subsp. *diffusa* (Forssk.) P.H.Raven

**Family**: [[Onagraceae]] · **Genus**: [[Ludwigia]]

Kew Bull. **15**(3): 476 (1962).

## Description

Perennial terrestrial or aquatic herb, glabrous to pubescent with long erect white hairs, especially when young. Terrestrial forms have reddish, node-rooting, much-branched stems and small terminally clustered leaves. Aquatic forms form mats, with simple stems or stolons 0.6–1 m long and either fascicled fusiform whitish or pink spongy floats in the leaf axils or fine elongated spongy roots. Terrestrial leaves have petioles 3–15 mm and ovate-cuneate blades 2–6 × 0.8–1.3 cm; aquatic leaves have petioles 5–20 mm and blades 3.5–10.5 × 0.9–1.4 cm; lateral veins 6–14 pairs.

Flowers solitary; pedicel 5–16 mm with two triangular bracteoles about 1 mm; sepals 5, 5–12 mm; petals 5, broadly obovate, 8–15 mm, pale yellow with a bright-yellow basal spot; stamens 10; ovary about 8 × 0.8 mm; style longer than stamens. Fruit linear to elongate-fusiform, 22–27 × 3–4 mm, ten-ribbed and subwoody. Seeds in one row per locule, ovoid, 1.1–1.3 × 0.5 mm, tightly fused into woody endocarp.

## Distribution

Nearly throughout continental Africa, Madagascar, Arabia and Iraq. In Gabon it is recorded from Ogooué-Maritime and probably occurs elsewhere.

## Habitat and ecology

Marshes, ponds, lakes, seasonally flooded plains and river margins; low altitude in Gabon, to 1900 m elsewhere.

## Notes

Aquatic forms are recognizable by their long reddish-brown stems and fine black elongated roots. Subsp. *adscendens* is Asian and differs in its white, yellow-centred petals and longer pedicels.
