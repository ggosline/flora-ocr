# *Salacia whytei* Loesener

**Genus**: [[Salacia]]
**Family**: [[Celastraceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. 44: 184 (1910); HUTCH. & MOSS, FWTA 1: 453 (1928); HALLÉ, Monogr. Hippocr. Afr. occid., Thèse: 184 (1958); MÉM. IFAN 64: 186 (1962)

## Description

1. Inflorescence with a small peduncle of 0.5–3 mm; plant rather slender not exceeding 2–3 m; leaves subdiscolorous; 2–4(–5) ovules per locule ..... 17a. var. whytei.

1'. Inflorescence with very small peduncle of 0.3–1(–1.8) mm; plant rather robust exceeding 3–15 m; leaves subdiscolorous; 4–6 ovules per locule .....

1". Inflorescence sessile; leaves olivaceous concolorous; other characters as 5b 17c. var. toussaintii.

17a. var. whytei

Slender liana often modest, 2–3 m, glabrous, devoid of rubber. Young branchlets often very slender, cylindrical or with very fine ridges; old branchlets ± rugulose subereous, brownish. Leaves opposite or subopposite. Petiole of 2–6 mm, with margins of the canal ± undulate to crisped. Lamina rather thin, brown olivaceous to olivaceous, subconcolorous, sometimes almost blackish when dry, elliptic, 6–15 × 2.5–5 cm, with base acute or ± rounded, often subobtuse, apex acuminate often attenuate with acumen somewhat oblique and apex rounded; margins subdentate to entire. Midvein prominent as fine ridge above; 5–7(9) pairs of secondary veins; fine veinlets apparent on both faces when dry.

Small axillary inflorescences uni- or pauciflorous, with basal peduncle 0.5–3 mm in length. A small number of tiny bracts grouped is more rarely replaced by a series of staggered bracts (fa. ophiurella Hallé, Bull. Mus. natn. Hist. nat. Paris 30(5): 469, 1958). Buds ovate short 2 mm. Pedicels slender 5–10 mm. Flower rotate 5–6 mm in diameter, orange, pale or ± dark, with yellowish-green calyx and green disc. Sepals ovate subequal, broad 0.9–1.2 mm, entire or eroded. Petals oblong, apex sometimes somewhat angular, 2–2.6 × 1.3–1.6 mm. Disc cushion-like briefly subcylindric or truncate-conical, 1.6–1.8 mm in diameter and 0.6–0.8 mm in thickness, ± distinctly pitted beneath the stamens (contact of locules in the bud). Filaments spaced between them, short, with adaxial pit at the base; anther with adhering locules arranged at very wide angle, broad 0.7–0.8 mm; pollen pale yellow. Pistil short pyramidal, less than 1 mm, with punctiform stigma. Locules of the ovary sunken to 2–5 ovules biseriate each.

Fruit bright red-orange, 3–4 cm in diameter, adorned with prominent ridges forming a rather tight network and containing 4–5 seeds. Seed 14 × 10 × 9 mm with white cotyledons fused into a homogeneous mass and without distinct gemule. - Pl. 25, fig. 3–7, 10, 11, 20, 24, 25, 27, 28, p. 97.

## Discussion

The species is variable and the West African forms are often less vigorous than those of equatorial Africa. In Jansen 1506, from Liberia, 2 ovules per locule were found but most often the number of ovules per locule is 4. The separation is often not very clear with the variety vermoeseniana.

Guinea, Liberia, Côte-d'Ivoire, Nigeria, Gabon, Cabinda (Gossweiler 6920, K!) and Angola (Gossweiler 4371, BM!). Probably also in Cameroon and Zaïre, given the variations of the variety vermoeseniana.

In very humid forests, often in ripicole stations, sometimes at altitude.

Nigeria (Brenan 9066), Cameroon, Gabon, Zaïre (Louis 9238!).

This variety sometimes presents galls similar to those observed on the leaves of S. cornifolia Hook. f.

17 bis. Salacia lenticellosa Loesener ex Harms

LOES., Wiss. Ergebn. Deutsch. Zentr.-Afr.-Exped. 1910–1911, 2: 77 (1922), nomen; HARMS, Notizblatt Bot. Gart. Berlin 15: 673 (1942).

Shrub of 2 m or liana reaching 25 m, glabrous, devoid of rubber. Young branchlets rapidly brownish and lenticellate, then rough and greyish. Leaves opposite or subopposite. Petiole 7–13 mm, somewhat thick, with canal pinched and finely crisped when dry. Lamina coriaceous, brown olivaceous, weakly discolorous, elliptic or oblong, 10–25 × 5–12 cm; base acute, rounded or briefly cuneiform, apex acuminate acute, margin entire coarsely bordered below or sometimes with spaced little-distinct denticules. Secondary veins 7–11(12) pairs; the network apparent is very lax below.

Axillary inflorescence pluriflorous in glomule ± divided, subsessile or on a peduncle of 1–4 mm. Bud short 2 × 3 mm. Pedicel (4)5–9.5 mm. Expanded flower not seen (7–8 mm at least). Sepals small, unequal, subobtuse, entire. Petals suborbicular 2–3 mm. Disc as a 5-lobed rim with thick edge 0.5 mm forming almost a cupule around the deep perigynous groove where the very short stamens are inserted; anther with confluent locules; fissures in barely broken transverse line, width 1 mm. Pistil short; style truncate 0.2 mm. Locules sunken to 2 ovules superposed per locule.

Fruit globoid smooth, somewhat irregular 3.5 × 4 cm, containing 1–4 seeds. - Pl. 26, p. 99.

This species is to be sought in Gabon.
