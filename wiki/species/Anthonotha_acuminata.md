# *Anthonotha acuminata* (De Wild.) Léonard

**Genus**: [[Anthonotha]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: J. Léonard, Mém. Ac. Roy. Belg. 30,2 : 218 (1954)

## Synonyms

- Macrolobium acuminatum De Wildeman, Pl. Bequert 3: 224 (1925).

## Description

Shrubs. Leaves 2–4 jugate. Rachis 4–12 cm long. Leaflets obovate or elliptic, abruptly acuminate, 3–14 cm long by 1.5–6.5 cm wide, covered beneath with appressed silvery-greyish pubescence. Secondary veins 6–11 pairs. Petiolules 3–7 mm long.

Panicles short, axes up to 8 cm, brown tomentose, composed of short racemes. Pedicels 5–8 mm. Bracteoles 8–10 mm. Sepals 4, one of which bifid. One large petal, bilobed, 8–9 mm long by 5–9 mm wide; the 4 others 2 to 2.5 mm in length. Three long fertile stamens, 6 staminodes. The calyx tube, the claw of the large petal and the base of the filaments of the stamens are silky. Ovary subsessile, tomentose.

Pods oblong, up to 25 × 4.5 cm, with transverse nerves.

## Type

Bequaert 6631 (BR)

## Discussion

Little-known species very close to A. macrophylla, perhaps merely a variety. Occurs from Gabon to Congo.
