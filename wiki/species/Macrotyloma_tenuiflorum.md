# *Macrotyloma tenuiflorum* (Micheli) Verdc. Planche 85

**Genus**: [[Macrotyloma]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull. 24 : 402 (1970)

## Description

Desmodium tenuiflorum Micheli, in T.Durand & De Wild., Bull. Soc. Roy. Bot. Belg. 36(2) : 59 (1897). Dolichos baumannii Harms, Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 26 : 313 (1899). Dolichos tenuiflorus (Micheli) R.Wilczek, Fl. Congo Belge 6 : 320 (1954). Perennial herb; stem prostrate or climbing, slender, up to 80 cm long, on a woody root. Stipules ovate-lanceolate, 2–3 × 1–1.5 mm, dark purple with pale veins. Leaf: petiole 0.4–1.3 mm; rachis almost absent; stipels linear-filiform, 1 mm; leaflets 3, with broadly elliptic to ovate or obovate blade, 1–4.5 × 0.7–3.5 cm, base rounded to broadly cuneate, apex rounded or emarginate and mucronate. Inflorescence ± sessile or on a short shoot reaching 2 cm long, in fascicles of 1 or 2(–3) flowers; bracts and bracteoles lanceolate, 1–2 mm long. Flower: pedicel 1–2 mm; calyx with scattered pubescence, tube 1.5–2 mm long, lobes triangular to triangular-lanceolate, 1–2 mm long, upper lobes connate for half their length to entirely; standard 8–12 × 5–7 mm, apex emarginate, base subauricate, white or yellowish-blue, spotted with red-purple at the base, with linear appendages 3–6 mm, wings oblanceolate, 8–10 × 2 mm, whitish, keel somewhat curved, 8–10 × 3 mm, base subauricate, apex obtuse; ovary ± 6 mm long, with 10 or 11 ovules, margin glabrous. Pod linear, 4–5.2 cm × 2–3 mm, apiculate, glabrous. Seed oblong-ellipsoid, 2.5–3 × 1.2–1.5 × 0.6 mm, reddish-brown with or without small black dots, with terminal elaiosome.

## Distribution

from Guinea and Mali to Uganda and Ethiopia, southwards to Angola; in Gabon, known from the Haut-Ogooué

## Ecology

herbaceous savannas, wooded savannas; in Gabon at 200–300 m altitude. Flowers and fruits in May. b : Mackinder et al. (2001), Verdcourt (1982), Wilczek (1954). Bibliography
