# *Fimbristylis bisumbellata* (Forssk.) Bubani Planche 88

**Genus**: [[Fimbristylis]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Dodecanthea : 30 (1850)

## Description

Annual herb, in tufts, with a slender root system; stems usually 10–25 cm × 0.5–1 mm, ± triangular, glabrous. Leaf: sheath membranous, yellowish to brown; ligule formed of a dense row of short hairs; blade flat, 1–2 mm wide, generally shorter than the stem, scabrous on the margin and main veins. Inflorescence in an open anthela, 2–6 cm wide, generally formed of 10–40 small spikelets; main branches 1–3 cm long; involucral bracts 2–5, foliaceous, the lower one generally shorter than the inflorescence. Spikelet 3–8 × 1.5 mm approximately, angular, acute; glumes 1.3–1.8 mm long, brown or golden brown, but with a green central vein with 1–3 lateral veins, prolonged by a short mucro, margin often shortly ciliate. Flower with 1 stamen; style fringed, with 2 branches. Achene obovoid, 0.6–0.7 × 0.4–0.5 mm, biconvex, lustrous pearly white, with 5–7 vertical rows of epidermal cells on each face.

## Distribution

Tropical regions of Africa and Asia; in Africa, more frequent in the dry regions of West and North Africa. Not yet found in Gabon, but its presence is probable

## Ecology

Sandy places on riverbanks; at 0–600 m altitude. Taxonomic note: Found in Gabon is an annual form of F. dichotoma that is smaller in all its parts than the perennial form and with only 7 longitudinal striae. This form covers the variations of F. bisumbellata. However, the Gabon specimens of F. bisumbellata examined belong to the annual form of F. dichotoma. B: Goetghebeur & Coudijzer (1984a), Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al. (2010), Hooper & Napper (1972), Lye (1995, 1997)
