# *Cleistanthus letouzeyi* J.Léonard

**Genus**: [[Cleistanthus]]
**Family**: [[Phyllanthaceae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia , sér. 2, 3(1) : 65, pl. 1 (1963)

## Description

Adansonia, ser. 2, 3(1): 65, pl. 1 (1963). Tree up to 18 m tall; trunk up to 25 cm in diameter; branchlets glabrous. Stipules early caducous, ovate, 3–4 mm long. Leaf glabrous; petiole 8–11(–15) mm; blade elliptic, 12–24(–30) × (4–)6–10(–13) cm, rounded to cuneate at base, apex acuminate over 1–2(–2.5) cm, papyraceous to coriaceous; lateral nerves 6–7(–9) pairs. Inflorescence racemiform, multiflorous, 5–9 cm long, puberulent-tomentellous; bracts early caducous, ovate to broadly ovate, 2–5 × 2–3 mm; bracteoles ovate-elliptic, 1–2 mm long. Flower 5-merous; pedicel 5–10(–25) mm; sepals oblong-triangular, 7–9 × 1.5–2 mm, tomentose on the outside; petals obovate-oblanceolate, 1–2.5 mm long, denticulate at apex. Staminate flower: stamens 6–7, anthers 2 mm long; androgynophore 2 mm; disk ± woolly. Pistillate flower: disk flattened, glabrous on the outside, pubescent on the inside; pistil ca. 5 mm long; ovary with appressed hairs; styles highly divided. Fruit globose, ca. 1 cm in diameter, tomentose. Seeds unknown.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea and Gabon in the provinces of Estuaire, Ngounié and Ogooué-Maritime.

## Ecology

Primary and secondarized forest; in Gabon, at 0–400 m altitude. Taxonomic note: This species resembles C. bipindensis Jabl. very closely, also described from Cameroon, but which has shorter petioles (5–9 mm), blades at least sparsely hairy below and flowers with shorter sepals. Bibliography: Breteler (2011b).
