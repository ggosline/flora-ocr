# *Afzelia pachyloba* Harms

**Genus**: [[Afzelia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Harms, Engl. Bot. Jahrb. 49 : 426 (1913)

## Synonyms

- Afzelia Zenkeri Harms I. c.: 427 (1913).
- Afzelia Brieyi De Wild., Fedde Report 13 : 369 (1914).
- Afzelia caudata Hoyle, Kew Bull.: 170 (1933).

## Description

Trees. Leaves with (5–) 7–10 pairs of opposite leaflets, oblong or oblong-lanceolate, obtuse or rounded and slightly emarginate at the apex, with obtuse or rounded base, somewhat pubescent beneath, 2 to 6 cm long, 1–2.5 cm wide. About ten secondary veins not very prominent. Rachis slender, somewhat pubescent, 15–20 cm. Stipules hairy, short. Petiolules pubescent, twisted, 2–4 mm.

Panicles 10–20 cm, tomentose. Pedicels 5–9 mm.

Bracts hairy, 5 × 3 mm, caducous. Bracteoles 2, brownish hairy, approximately 4 × 2 mm, caducous. Receptacle slender cylindrical, 1.5–2 cm long, hairy. Sepals 4 oblong, hairy, 10–12 mm long and 5–10 mm wide. Petal 1, long-clawed; claw hairy, narrow, 15–20 mm long; limb bilobed, approximately 1–1.5 × 1–2 cm glabrous, white. Stamens equal in length to the petal, hairy at the base. Ovary shortly stipitate, hairy on the margins. Style filiform, glabrous, 2–3 cm long. Ovules 12–16.

Pods woody, brown, tuberculate, obliquely elliptic, reniform, approximately 15–20 × 13 cm, with valves 2 cm thick, internally septate. Large black shiny seeds, fusiform, approximately 5.5 × 2.2 cm, with bright yellow or somewhat orange aril, cupuliform, somewhat bilobed.

## Vernacular names

Doussié

## Type

Mildbrød 5966, Kribi, Cameroun

## Discussion

Medium-sized deciduous trees, furnished at the base with small thin buttresses. Bole sometimes sinuous. Bark scaly. Wood red-brown, hard, heavy, classed among the best woods for construction, joinery and flooring (C.T.F.T. Data Sheet).

The species is distributed from southern Nigeria to the Congo.

In Gabon it is scattered, but nevertheless would be fairly common in the forests of Fernan Vaz.
