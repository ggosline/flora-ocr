# *Cleistanthus zenkeri* Jabl.

**Genus**: [[Cleistanthus]]
**Family**: [[Phyllanthaceae]]

## Description

in Engl., Pflanzenr. IV, 147.VIII: 49 (1915). Tree (3–)6–18 m tall; trunk up to 35 cm in diameter; branchlets tomentose, early glabrescent. Stipules ovate–triangular, 5–7 × 2–3 mm, pubescent. Leaf: petiole 4–10 mm; blade elliptic, (5–)8–18(–23) × 3–7(–10) cm, rounded at base, apex acuminate over 0.5–1.5 cm, coriaceous, pubescent above when very young, glabrous or often with scattered hairs below; lateral veins 5–7(–8) pairs. Inflorescence racemiform, often ± fascicled, 0.5–1.5(–2) cm long, with ≤ 10 flowers, tomentose; bracts 2–6 × 2–4 mm. Male flower 5-merous; pedicel 5–6 mm; sepals ± triangular, 4–5 × 1.5–2 mm, tomentose on the outside; petals obovate, 1.5 mm long; stamens 6–7, anthers 1–1.5 mm long; androgynophore ± hidden by the disk, about 1 mm; disk glabrous. Female flower 5-merous; pedicel 4–6 mm; sepals and petals ± identical to those of male flowers; disk glabrous to pubescent; ovary rusty, tomentellose; styles 2 or 3 times bifid. Fruit depressed-ovoid, slightly 3-lobed, 10–11 × 12 mm, pubescent. Seeds not seen.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon, Angola (Cabinda) and Democratic Republic of Congo; in Gabon, collected in the provinces of Ngounié, Nyanga and Ogooué-Lolo

## Ecology

primary and secondarized forest; in Gabon, at 0–600 m altitude. b: Breteler (2011b). Bibliography
