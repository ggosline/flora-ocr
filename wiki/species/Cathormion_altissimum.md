Medium-sized tree reaching a height of 15 m, branched low. Trunk tortuous, up to 0.80 m in diameter, able to bear spines 3-5 cm long (sometimes also on main branches) in juvenile stages. Crown spreading. Bark brownish, fissured with rhytidome peeling off in thin scales; blanchish on section. Young twig longitudinally striate, finely pubescent, with numerous whitish lenticels. Old twig glabrous, grey when fresh and blackish when dry, with numerous whitish lenticels. Terminal and axillary buds 4 mm wide, surrounded by broadly ovate scales, with yellowish external pubescence, acute apex and very prominent venation. Stipule early caducous, fleshy, finely pubescent externally, more densely so on ciliolate margins, oblong asymmetric, 10 mm long, ± folded into a channel, apex very rounded. Petiole finely ± densely yellow pubescent, 0.8-3.5 cm long, flat to canaliculate above, bearing above an elliptic, cupular gland, very prominent, in variable position. Rachis densely pubescent; canaliculate or strongly keeled at the base becoming progressively keeled with slight canalicule or not on the crest, 4.5-20 cm long; with prominent glands above between the 1-2 pairs of apical pinnae. Pinnae 5-9 pairs, elliptic oblong. Pinnae axis pubescent to ± pubescent above and pubescent below, (3.5) 5-10.5 cm long, with strong prominent crest above, bearing prominent glands between the apical leaflets (up to 11) and, exceptionally at the level of the basal leaflet pair; terminated by a caducous, fleshy, pubescent mucro, 1.25 mm long, canaliculate above. Leaflets 9-20 pairs, sessile. Lamina (except for basal leaflets) glabrous on both surfaces, coriaceous, light green on both surfaces and faintly glossy above when fresh; green to blackish-brown, mat to faintly glossy above and green to faintly greenish-brown below when dry; oblong elliptic to ± obscurely elongate triangular; reaching 18 × 8 mm; base asymmetric: proximal margin truncate auricate to rounded auricate and distal margin very attenuate oblique to attenuate auricate; apex obtuse to acute. Basal leaflets modified: adaxial leaflet very reduced, acicular or absent; abaxial leaflet normal to base ± distinctly rounded. Midvein concave flat to faintly prominent above and strongly prominent below. Secondary veins 15-20 pairs, ascending, rectilinear, prominent on both surfaces. Veinlets in rather loose to almost absent network; prominent especially below.

Inflorescences in solitary or paired capituliform spikes. Axis pubescent at apex claviform. Bracteoles absent. Floral bud obovoid. Flowers ♂, sessile, white¹, emitting a repellent odor when fresh. Basal flower with obconic to subcylindric calyx, glabrous (except sometimes a tuft of hairs in the upper 1/4), 3.25-3.50 mm long, lobes free very broadly deltoid, ± 0.25 mm long and scarcely distinct, upper margin ciliolate; corolla with connate petals, glabrous, 6-6.5 mm long, free lobes ovate 1-1.25 mm long, apex acute; numerous stamens of variable size, 8.5-12.5 mm long, connate in a tube by the filaments for 3-5 mm, connate to a disc, anther broadly oblong, ± 0.25 mm long; fleshy disc present forming a prominent fleshy zone with the base of the stamens; pistil substipitate, ovary glabrous narrowly ellipsoid 1.25-1.5 mm long, with 20-21 ovules, style 9-10.5 mm long terminated by a plateau-like stigma. Central flowers 1-2 modified; with cylindric calyx enlarged at the base, pubescence as previously, 3.5-4.5 mm long, free lobes triangular to broadly deltoid, 0.25-0.75 mm long; corolla with 5 connate petals, sparsely pubescent on the dorsal keel of the free lobes, 6-6.5 mm long, free lobes 1.25-1.5 mm long, apex acute; numerous stamens 9-11.5 mm long, connate with each other in a cylindric tube 5-5.5 mm long and to the disc, anther oblong 0.25 mm long; fleshy disc connate to the staminal filaments forming a prominent ridge; pistil substipitate, ovary glabrous, 1.25-1.50 mm long, with 21 ovules, glabrous style 10-11.5 mm long.

Legume moniliform lomentaceous, glabrous, coriaceous, spiraled or simply arched, 16 × 2 cm, reddish, brownish or black when dry; base scarcely or not stipitate; apex often apiculate. Pericarp thick, strongly compressed between the seeds and forming partitions between them; epicarp with prominent venation; mesocarp spongy; endocarp crustaceous. Sutural margins thickened; thickening often more prominent for the dorsal margin, less prominent and more undulate for the ventral. Seed light brown ± glossy, ovate to orbicular, 6.5-8 × 6-7 mm; small apical aril.

Seedling puberulent. Hypocotylary axis 4-5 cm long, with quadrangular section. Cotyledons fleshy, flattened, 0.8 × 0.6 cm, erose at the base, ± separated from the axis, early caducous. Epicotylary axis short. Leaves 1 and 2

## Discussion

HARMS described Albizia passargei from a single specimen from northern Cameroon (Passarge 164, Ngaoundéré). This specimen has been destroyed. The diagnosis remains the only means of identifying the species. However, the foliar and floral characters described agree with those found on specimens of Cathormion altissimum from Adamaoua. It is therefore appropriate to accept the synonymy between A. passargei and C. altissimum; the latter remaining alone valid because of the priority of its basionym.

Cathormion altissimum is a riparian species widely distributed throughout Africa from Senegal to Zambia. It is found in dense forest but also in forest galleries. According to Cameroonian specimens, flowering lasts essentially from November to February and fruiting from November to July.

## Uses

The wood dark yellow hard, with numerous knots, is usable in cabinetmaking.
