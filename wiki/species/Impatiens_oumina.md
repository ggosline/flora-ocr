# *Impatiens oumina* N. Hallé

**Family**: [[Balsaminaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Impatiens]]

## Description

Glabrous herb 15–20 cm. Leaves alternate, ovate-elliptic, petiole 8–32 mm; base subcordate with filiform appendages. Axillary raceme 5–11-flowered; flowers pink, pedicels 9–13 mm. Lip salver-shaped, spur 4–9 mm; wings 10 mm. Capsule about 7 mm; seeds 8–10 or more, 1 mm.

## Distribution and notes

Ngounié basin, Gabon. The treatment suggests it might be a hybrid between *I. palpebrata* and *I. filicornu*, but retains it as a species pending proof.
