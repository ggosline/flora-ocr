# *Utricularia simulans* Pilger Planche 41, Figure 14(A–D)

**Genus**: [[Utricularia]]
**Family**: [[Lentibulariaceae]]
**Protologue**: Notizbl. Königl. Bot. Gart. Berlin 6 : 194 (1914)

## Description

Terrestrial herb; rhizoids and capillary stolons, numerous at the base of the flowering stem. Leaves numerous, present at anthesis, in a rosette at the base of the flowering stem, linear-filiform, 5–15 × 0.3–0.5 mm, 1-nerved. Utricles very numerous, stipitate, ovoid, 0.2–0.3 mm in length; lateral opening, with upper lip bearing a conical straight and short appendage, with lower lip prominent and bearing 2 subulate spreading or recurved appendages. Inflorescence erect, straight, 1.5–11 cm in length, with 1 to 5 flowers; peduncle filiform, glabrous; bracts basifixed, broadly ovate, 1–1.5 mm in length, auriculate at the base, with margin deeply divided into up to 20 subulate-acute teeth. Flower: bracteoles orbicular, inserted on the distal half of the pedicel, larger than the bracts and ± 3 mm in length, with ± 11 marginal teeth; pedicel 0.5–1 mm; calyx with subequal lobes, orbicular, 3–4 mm in length, with deeply divided margins bearing 12 to 25 subulate-acute teeth; corolla 5–10 mm in length, yellow-orange, upper lip broadly oblong, 1.5–2 times longer than the upper lobe of the calyx, with saccate base, truncate to emarginate at the apex, lower lip orbicular, 3–6 mm in length, palate slightly prominent, spur cylindric, equalling ± the lower lip. Fruit globose, ± 2 mm in length, dehiscent by a single abaxial longitudinal slit with thickened margins. Seed ovoid, ± 0.2 mm in length, longitudinally striate.

## Distribution

Senegal, Guinea-Bissau, Mali, Liberia, Cameroon, Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Zambia and Angola; also in America, from Florida to Paraguay; in Gabon, observed in the Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology

swampy savannas with temporary pools on Kalahari sand; in Gabon at low altitude (30 m), elsewhere up to 1545 m. B: Taylor (1964, 1972, 1989). Bibliography
