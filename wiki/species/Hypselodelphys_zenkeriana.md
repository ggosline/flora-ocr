# *Hypselodelphys zenkeriana* (K. Schum.) M.-Redh.

**Genus**: [[Hypselodelphys]]
**Family**: [[Marantaceae]]

## Synonyms

- Trachyphrynium Zenkerianum K. Schum. Engl. Pfl. Reich, 4, 49 : 45 (1902).

## Description

— MILNE-REDHEAD, Kew Bull.: 161 (1950).

Climbing herb, branched. Leaf with petiole entirely callous, approximately 2.5 cm long. Blade linear-oblong to lanceolate, acuminate, approx. 25 × 10 cm, rounded at the base, glabrous except for more or less caducous pubescence on either side of the midvein, on the lower surface.

Inflorescence dense, in a panicle of racemes. These are enclosed at their base by a number of sterile overlapping bracts 6–10 cm long. Fertile bracts approximately 3 cm long, caducous. Corolla 15–20 mm long; fruit 1.7 cm high and 5 cm in diameter, trilobed with rounded-globose lobes, muricate.

## Type

Zenker : 1223, Cameroun (B, BM, K)

## Discussion

This species was collected in Cameroon and Congo; its presence in Gabon is very probable.
