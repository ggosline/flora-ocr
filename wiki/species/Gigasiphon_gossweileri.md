# *Gigasiphon gossweileri* (Bak. f.) Torre et Hillc.

**Genus**: [[Gigasiphon]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Torre et Hillcoat, C.F.A. 2:163 (1956)

## Synonyms

## Description

Large vine with circinate tendrils. Leaves simple, entire, ovate, with rounded or subcordate base, with 5 (–7) digitate basal nerves, with attenuate, acuminate apex, 8–11 cm by 4.5–6.5 cm, papyraceous. Petiole 1.5–3 cm.

Terminal racemes of large white flowers, or axillary flowers. Pedicel 1 cm. Calyx with narrow cylindrical tube 1.5–1.8 cm, imperceptibly prolonged by the pedicel, with 5 valvate lanceolate acute lobes, rusty-pubescent, approximately 5 cm. Petals 5, subequal, free, 6–7 × 1 cm approximately, lanceolate acute, with base abruptly narrowed into a short unguis. Stamens 10 with slender filaments, pubescent at the base, as long as the petals. Ovary pubescent at the apex of a long pubescent stipe 4–5 cm; style slender, pubescent; stigma capitate, small.

Species from Cabinda, Kasai, the Congo Basin and lower Katanga.

In Gabon it has only been collected in the Lastoursville region.
