# *Cyperus margaritaceus* Vahl Planche 39

**Genus**: [[Cyperus]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Enum. pl . 2 : 307 (1805)

## Description

Perennial herb with swollen stem bases, slender roots; stems 20–70 cm × 0.8–2 mm, trigonous, with longitudinal ridges, glabrous, base covered by firm sheaths not usually decomposing into fibres. Leaf: sheath light brown to dark brown; blade 10–45 cm × 1–5 mm, plane or rolled, scabrous on the margin and central nerve at least near the apex. Inflorescence as a solitary head, generally globose, of (1–)2–10 spikelets, 1–3 cm wide, white becoming pale brownish-red at maturity; involucral bracts 2–5, foliaceous, the largest 2–10 cm × 1–3 mm, erect or spreading. Spikelet elliptic to linear–lanceolate, 8–25 × 5–10 mm, compressed, with 10–25 flowers; glumes elliptic–lanceolate, 5–8 mm long, off-white with a pinkish-brown tint and numerous nerves on each side of the prominent central nerve, apex rather acute with the central nerve slightly exceeding it. Achene obovate, 2.2–2.8 × 1.7–2 mm, trigonous, brown to olive-green, shortly apiculate and with nearly smooth surface.

## Distribution

widely distributed in tropical and subtropical Africa; uncommon in Gabon, found in Haut-Ogooué, Ngounié, Ogooué-Ivindo and Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology

in grasslands, savanna or open wooded areas, often on rocky slopes; in Gabon, at 0–400 m altitude, up to 1600 m in East Africa. Taxonomic note: very close to C. niveus, but C. margaritaceus has fewer and less flattened spikelets, and larger fruits. B: Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al. (2010), Hooper & Napper (1972).
