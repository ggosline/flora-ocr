# *Exochaenium grande* (E.Mey.) Griseb. Planche 24

**Genus**: [[Exochaenium]]
**Family**: [[Gentianaceae]]
**Protologue**: Belmontia grandis E.Mey., Comm. pl. Afr. austr . 2 : 183 (1838)

## Description

in DC., Prodr. 9: 55 (1845). Belmontia grandis E.Mey., Comm. pl. Afr. austr. 2: 183 (1838). Sebaea grandis (E.Mey.) Steud., Nomencl. Bot., ed. 2, 2: 550 (1841). Herb 7–50 cm tall; stem simple or sometimes branched from the base. Leaves opposite; blade narrowly elliptic to elliptic, 5–40 × 2.5–13 mm, base obtuse, apex acute to acuminate. Inflorescence with solitary flower or in few-flowered cyme. Flowers heterostylous; pedicel 5–50 mm; calyx with narrowly ovate-elliptic lobes, 5–22 mm long, furnished with wings reaching 2 mm wide, with scales at the inner base; corolla 15–35 mm long, cream-yellow, orange-yellow to brownish, with tube 10–20 mm long, inflated above the ovary, lobes ovate to suborbicular, 5–20 mm long; stamens with filament 0.3–5 mm, anthers free or coherent, positioned either below the stigma, at the same height, or higher, 0.6–3.2 mm long, with apical gland, subglobose to oblongoid, 0.1–1.5 mm long and with two minute basal glands; ovary subglobose; style 4–8 mm, stigma narrowly ellipsoid. Fruit subglobose, 7–8 mm diameter. Seed 0.1–0.3 mm diameter.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Rwanda, Burundi, Ethiopia, Somalia, Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, Angola, Zambia, Malawi, Mozambique, Zimbabwe and South Africa; in Gabon, rare and known from a single collection from the Nyanga

## Ecology

savannas, xerophilous grasslands, rocky savannas, pastures; in Gabon near the sea, elsewhere up to 2250 m altitude. B: Boutique (1972), Kissling (2012), Paiva & Nogueira (1990), Sileshi (2002), Taylor Bibliography (1963)
