# *Leptoderris congolensis* (De Wild.) Dunn

**Genus**: [[Leptoderris]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1910 : 390 (1910)

## Description

, syn. nov. L. congolensis (De Wild.) Dunn var. pycnantha (Harms) Pellegr., Legum. Gabon : 208 (1949), syn. nov. Liane with stem reaching 15 cm in diameter or sarmentose shrub; branchlets puberulent. Stipules deltoid to ovate, 3–4 × 3 mm, puberulent. Leaf: petiole 4–13(–17) mm, puberulent; rachis 3–8 cm, puberulent; stipels narrowly triangular to filiform, 2–4 mm long; leaflets 3 or 5, with petiolule 4–11 mm, blade ± obovate to elliptic-oblong, 7–20 × 3–12 cm, rounded-cordate at base, strongly emarginate at apex, with 5 to 8(–9) pairs of lateral nerves, with appressed hairs on the midvein above, with short brown appressed hairs below, glabrescent. Inflorescence reaching 40 × 20 cm, brown-puberulent; bracts and bracteoles caducous, 1 mm long. Flower: pedicel ± 1 mm; calyx infundibuliform, 2.5–3.5 mm long, lobes 1–1.5 mm long, with appressed hairs; corolla 7–8 mm long, cream-yellow with red-purple spots, standard 7–8 × 3.5–4 mm, truncate at base, with claw 2 mm, wings shorter than keel, 6 × 1.5 mm, keel petals 7–8 × 2–2.5 mm; stamens as long as keel, anthers 0.5 mm long; pistil pilose. Immature pod oblong (mature pod not seen), sometimes slightly falcate, 6–9.5 × 2–3.5 cm, of which 2–4 mm for the wing, with 1(–2) seeds, with short brown appressed hairs, glabrescent.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Central African Republic, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola (Cabinda); in Gabon, known from Haut-Ogooué, Moyen-Ogooué, Nyanga, Ogooué-Lolo and Ogooué-Ivindo

## Ecology

Primary and secondarized forest; in Gabon up to 400 m elevation, elsewhere up to 650 m. Use: Plant with rotenone ichthyotoxic properties
