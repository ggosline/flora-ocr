# *Crateranthus congolensis* Lecomte

**Genus**: [[Crateranthus]]
**Family**: [[Lecythidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Mus. Natl. Hist. Nat. 26 : 70 (1920)

## Description

Shrub; branchlets with prominent lines, not winged. Leaf shortly petiolate over 3–4 mm; blade elliptic, 19–21 × 7–9 cm, rounded at the base, apex prominent, grey-green, subcoriaceous, glabrous, smooth; secondary veins 7–10 pairs. Flower: pedicel 5–6 mm; sepals 3, more or less circular; corona cupuliform, consisting of 30 folds, 2 cm long, yellowish-white, margin recurved, with acute lobes; stamens numerous, filaments up to 6–7 mm, anthers oblong, ± 2.5 mm in length, acute at the apex; ovary broad, with fine carpels, ovules numerous, anatropous. Fruit unknown.

## Distribution

endemic to Gabon, observed in the provinces of Ngounié and Ogooué-Lolo

## Ecology

dense humid forest. B: Lecomte (1920), Knuth (1939). Bibliography
