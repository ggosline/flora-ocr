# *Thecacoris stenopetala* (Müll.Arg.) Müll.Arg.

**Genus**: [[Thecacoris]]
**Family**: [[Phyllanthaceae]]
**Protologue**: T. batesii Hutch., Kew Bull. 1910 : 58 (1910)

## Description

in D.C., Prodr. 15(2): 246 (1866). T. batesii Hutch., Kew Bull. 1910: 58 (1910). Shrub 0.5–2 m high; branchlets sometimes pink to brick-red, with subappressed hairs, sometimes sparse, glabrescent. Stipules narrowly triangular to deltoid, (3–)5–7(–12) × 2–4 mm. Leaf: petiole (4–)5–12(–16) mm, with subappressed hairs; blade obovate-elliptic, (7–)13–23(–31) × (5–)7–9(–11) cm, cuneate to rounded at base, acuminate over (0.5–)1.5–2.5(–3) cm and often mucronate at apex, glabrous or with scattered hairs on the midvein beneath; midvein often pink to brick-red beneath; lateral nerves (6–)7–12(–15) pairs. Inflorescence erect, pubescent, the male ones 2–3 cm long, the female ones (2–)4–11 cm long and up to 20 cm in fruit; bracts narrowly triangular, 1–3 mm long, ciliate. Male flower: 1.5–2.5 mm in diameter; pedicel 0–1 mm, sparsely puberulent; sepals 5, broadly ovate, 1–1.5 × 1–1.5 mm, glabrous but ciliolate; petals present, ± 0.5 mm long, glabrous or with a few apical hairs; stamens 5, 1.5–2 mm long, glabrous; disc lobes with scattered hairs; pistillode obconic, ≤ 1 mm long, glabrous. Female flower 2–4 mm in diameter: pedicel 1–2 mm, sparsely puberulent; sepals 5(–6), triangular to deltoid, 1.5 mm long, ciliolate at apex; petals usually present, 0.5–1 mm long, glabrous or ciliolate at apex; annular disc, ciliolate; ovary subglobose, 2–3 mm in diameter, glabrous or rarely with some hairs. Fruit 7–10 × 10–12 mm, brownish, glabrous. Seeds subglobose, 4–5 mm in diameter, brown, shining.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, São Tomé and Príncipe, Gabon and Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, collected in the provinces of Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué and Ngounié.

## Ecology

Primary and secondarized forest; in Gabon, at 0–300 m altitude. b: Breteler (2011a), Keay (1958). Bibliography
