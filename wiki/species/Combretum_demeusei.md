# *Combretum demeusei* De Wildeman

**Genus**: [[Combretum]]
**Family**: [[Combretaceae]]
**Protologue**: Ann. Mus. Congo (Brazzaville) Bot. 5 (3) : 235, tab. 49 (1910) ; LIBEN, FZ : 48 (1968) ; Fl. Cam. 25 : 42 (1983) ; BJBB 54 : 290 (1984). TYPE : Congo (Kinshasa), Bolobo (fl.) Demeuse 472 (lecto-BR, cf. LIBEN 1983 et 1984)

## Description

Ann. Mus. Congo (Brazzaville) Bot. 5 (3) : 235, tab. 49 (1910) ; LIBEN, FZ : 48 (1968) ; Fl. Cam. 25 : 42 (1983) ; BJBB 54 : 290 (1984). TYPE : Congo (Kinshasa), Bolobo (fl.) Demeuse 472 (lecto-BR, cf. LIBEN 1983 et 1984).

Liane, length ≤ 10 m and diameter ≤ 17 cm. Presence of intraxylary phloem. Young branches lepidote and slightly puberulent (Pl. 16, 7, p. 59).

Leaves opposite; petiole 6–12 mm; lamina ovate-elliptic to obovate, ≤ 10 × ≤ 6 cm, coriaceous, abaxial surface glabrous except for scales; major lateral veins 6–8 pairs, depressed adaxially, domatia sometimes present; tertiary veins parallel; base of lamina rounded; apex rounded or shortly acuminate.

Scales brown very irregular, diameter 75–100 µm.

Inflorescences paniculate ≤ 15 cm, axillary or terminal; rachis covered with dense scales; bracts scarcely visible.

Flowers 4-merous, sessile, white and orange, fragrant. Receptacle entirely covered with reddish-brown scales; the lower 1 mm high; the upper patelliform, 2–4 mm wide including the triangular lobes of the calyx. Petals yellowish-white, spathulate, 1.5–2.5 mm long, glabrous. Stamens, length 0.7–1.1 mm and anthers 0.3 mm. Disk with puberulent margin. Style 1 mm.

Fruit, profile narrowly elliptic or ovate, ≤ 17 × 5 mm, furnished with 4(–6) triangular corky wings in cross-section, adapted for dispersal by water, entirely covered with scales; stipe ≤ 1 mm (Pl. 1 B, p. 11 ; Pl. 16, 8, p. 59).

This species does not exist in the FWTA area and its "C. demeusei" (ed. 2, 1(1) : 272) would in fact be C mildbraedii Hutch. & Dalz. (fruit wings papyraceous).
