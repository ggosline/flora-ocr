# *Begonia capillipes* Gilg Planche 37

**Genus**: [[Begonia]]
**Family**: [[Begoniaceae]]

## Description

in Engl., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 34(1) : 96 (1904). Epiphyte ; stem suffrutescent, often pendulous, reaching 2 m in length. Stipules early caducous, narrowly triangular, 8–15(–20) × 2–3 mm. Leaves : petiole (2–)5–15(–30) mm ; lamina weakly asymmetric, narrowly elliptic to linear, (2–)3–11(–14) × 0.5–2.5 cm, obliquely obtuse to cuneiform at base, acute to acuminate at apex, margin entire red ; midvein prominent on lower surface, reddish. Inflorescences unisexual ; the ♂ 8–11-flowered, peduncle 8–35 mm ; the ♀ (1–)2(–3)-flowered, peduncle 2–10 mm ; bracts caducous. ♂ Flower : pedicel 6–15 mm ; outer tepals 7–10 × 4–5.5 mm, inner ones smaller, 6–9 × 1.5–2.5 mm ; androecium nearly actinomorphic, stamens 6–10 with filaments completely fused. ♀ Flower : pedicel reduced, more or less curved and perianth bell-shaped with pieces half-open ; outer tepals 8.5–14 × 5–6.5 mm, inner ones 8–12 × 2–4.5 mm ; ovary weakly curved, 12–20 mm in length, circular in section, 1.5–2 mm in diameter ; styles 2–3(–4), free parts subulate, thickened toward base, terminal stigmatic zone, linear, entire. Infructescence with (1–)2(–3) fruits. Fruit 30–50 × 2–2.5 mm, pale pink to greenish, opening by a longitudinal fissure.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon

## Ecology

Dense ombrophilous forests ; in Cameroon notably in Biafran forest with Caesalpiniaceae ; between 260 and 1020 m altitude

## Source note

In the Chaillu Massif, at Mount Naguila near Mimongo, we have recorded between 800 m and 1020 m altitude aberrant populations marked by bright red coloration of all parts of the vegetative apparatus. The segments of the perianth, likewise, are marked with a darker red. Fruit dehiscence occurs by 2 longitudinal fissures.
