# *Ansellia africana* Lindl.

**Genus**: [[Ansellia]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Reg. 30 : tab. 12 (1844)

## Description

—Ansellia gigantea Rchb.f., Linnaea 20 : 673 (1847).

—Ansellia africana Lindl. var. nilotica Bak., Trans. Linn. Soc. 29 : 154 (1875).

– Ansellia nilotica (Bak.) N. E. Br., Lindenia 2: 36 (1886).

– Ansellia confusa N. E. Br., Lindenia 2 : 36 (1886).

—Ansellia congoensis Rodigas in N. E. Br., Lindenia 2 : 36 (1886).

— Ansellia humilis Bull., Cat. 1891 : 3 (1891).

— Ansellia gigantea Rchb. f. var. nilotica (Bak.) Summerh., Kew Bull. 9 : 462 (1937).

PL. 134. – Structure of the gynostemium of the genus Ansellia Lindl.: A, lateral view; B, upper part, view from below; C, remainder of the rostellum; D, anther, view from below; E, pollinia, various views; F, G, tegula and viscidium, various views (A-G, ex SZLACHETKO, 2003).

Pseudobulbs 10-50 cm, covered with papyraceous leaf sheaths. Leaves 8-10, 15-50 × 1.4-6.5 cm, narrowly lanceolate to lanceolate. Inflorescence up to 40-flowered. Flowers yellow, spotted with dark brown, brown, or red. Lip 11-25 × 10-20 mm, trilobed, with 2 (3) longitudinal keels running from the base to the apex; midlobe oblong to nearly orbicular, truncate, with margins more or less undulate; lateral lobes shorter than the midlobe. – Pl. 135, p. 307.

## Distribution

Guinea-Bissau, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria, Equatorial Guinea, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Gabon, Zaire, Rwanda, Burundi, Sudan, Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, Zambia, Malawi, Zimbabwe, Mozambique, Republic of South Africa

## Ecology

alt. 0-2,200 m. Terrestrial or lithophytic in open forest and wooded savanna, epiphytic on Hyphaene guineensis, Cola acuminata and Pithecelobium altissimum
