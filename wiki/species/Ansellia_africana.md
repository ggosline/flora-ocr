## Description

PL. 134. – Structure of the gynostemium of the genus Ansellia Lindl.: A, lateral view; B, upper part, ventral view; C, rostellum remainder; D, anther, ventral view; E, pollinia, various views; F, G, tegula and viscidium, various views (A-G, ex SZLACHETKO, 2003).

Pseudobulbs 10-50 cm, covered with papyraceous leaf sheaths. Leaves 8-10, 15-50 × 1.4-6.5 cm, narrowly lanceolate to lanceolate. Inflorescence up to 40-flowered. Flowers yellow, spotted with dark brown, brown or red. Labellum 11-25 × 10-20 mm, trilobed, with 2 (3) longitudinal keels extending from base to apex; median lobe oblong to almost rounded, truncate, with margins more or less undulate; lateral lobes shorter than the median lobe. – Pl. 135, p. 307.
