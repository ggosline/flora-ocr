# *Polystachya superposita* Rchb.f.

**Genus**: [[Polystachya]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Otia Bot. Hamburg. 2 : 111 (1881)

## Synonyms

- Polystachya composita Kraenzl., Rep. Sp. Nov. Regni Veg., Beih. 39 : 103 (1926).

## Description

Pseudobulbs 3.5–8 × 0.4 cm. Leaves 4–9 × 0.4–0.8 cm, linear-ligulate, very unequally and shallowly bilobed at the apex. Inflorescence 2.5–5 cm. Flowers reddish. Dorsal sepal elliptic. Petals oblong-obovate. Lateral sepals obliquely ovate-triangular, briefly apiculate. Labellum without callus, thin, delicate, trilobed just below the middle; claw progressively attenuate towards the base; median lobe elliptic-ovate, subobtuse; lateral lobes linear-falciform, subobtuse. Spur conic-saccate. – Pl. 121, p. 275.

## Distribution

Equatorial Guinea, Cameroon, Gabon

alt. 600–1,500 m. Epiphytic in montane and submontane forest

6. Sect. Polystachya

Pseudobulbs small, conic, fleshy, well developed, fasciculate, paucifoliate. Inflorescence generally branched, sometimes with secondary branches. Flowers small to minute, often fleshy. Labellum with or without callus.
