# *Polystachya superposita* Rchb.f.

**Genus**: [[Polystachya]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Otia Bot. Hamburg. 2 : 111 (1881)

## Description

– Polystachya composita Kraenzl., Rep. Sp. Nov. Regni Veg., Beih. 39 : 103 (1926).

Pseudobulbs 3.5-8 × 0.4 cm. Leaves 4-9 × 0.4-0.8 cm, linear-ligulate, very unequally and shallowly bilobed at the apex. Inflorescence 2.5-5 cm. Flowers reddish. Dorsal sepal elliptic. Petals oblong-obovate. Lateral sepals obliquely ovate-triangular, briefly apiculate. Labellum without callus, thin, fragile, trilobed just below the middle; claw gradually attenuate toward the base; median lobe elliptic-ovate, subobtuse; lateral lobes linear-falciform, subobtuse. Spur conic-sacciform. - Pl. 121, p. 275.

## Distribution

Equatorial Guinea, Cameroon, Gabon

## Ecology

alt. 600-1,500 m. Epiphytic in montane and submontane forest
