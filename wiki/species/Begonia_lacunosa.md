# *Begonia lacunosa* Warb. Planche 24

**Genus**: [[Begonia]]
**Family**: [[Begoniaceae]]
**Protologue**: B. mayombensis Irmscher, Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 81 : 181 (1961)

## Description

in Engl. & Prantl, Nat. Pflanzenfam. 3, 6a : 140 (1894). B. mayombensis Irmscher, Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 81 : 181 (1961). Plant reaching 15(–25) cm in height. Leaf peltate; petiole 1.5–11(–22.5) cm, hirsute; blade ± spreading when fresh, variously bullate, weakly to distinctly asymmetric, ovate to elliptic-ovate or sometimes broadly ovate to broadly elliptic-ovate, (3.5–)4–12(–13.5) × 2–7.5(–9) cm, apex acute to gradually attenuate or acuminate, margin denticulate to denticulate-crenulate, upper surface bearing long hairs on the prominences. Inflorescence: peduncle 1–10 cm, with 2–3 ♂ flowers and 1(–2) ♀ flowers with yellow or sometimes white tepals; bracts 2–8 mm long. ♂ Flower: tepals (5–)7–15(–18) × 7–18.5 mm; androecium with 14–28 stamens. ♀ Flower: pedicel elongating in fruit to 3.5(–5.5) mm; tepals (6.5–)7.5–17 × 8–20 mm; ovary (wings included) very broadly obovoid to broadly ellipsoid or rarely obtriangular depressed, 4–8(–10) × (2.5–)4–7.5 mm, bearing long hairs, with 3 small obovate depressed to very broadly obovate wings, 0.5–4 mm wide; styles 3. Fruit as the ovary, (3.5–)4.5–10(–12) × (3.5–)5–12 mm; rostrum absent or to 3 mm.

## Distribution

western Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, western Congo (Brazzaville), Angola (Cabinda) and western Democratic Republic of the Congo; widely distributed in Gabon

## Ecology

on flat terrain but preferring slopes, banks or rocky outcrops, in primary and secondary forests, often along streams, rivers and waterfalls; to 1000 m altitude. B: Hutchinson, Dalziel & Keay (1954), Irmscher (1925), Sosef (1994), Wilczek (1969a)
