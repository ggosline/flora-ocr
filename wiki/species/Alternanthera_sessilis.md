# *Alternanthera sessilis* (L.) R. Br.

**Family**: [[Amaranthaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Alternanthera]]

## Description

Low creeping or ascending herb with opposite leaves and small sessile axillary heads of whitish flowers. Flowers have five unequal pointed tepals, a short staminal cup and one compressed ovary; the dry indehiscent fruit contains one lenticular seed.
