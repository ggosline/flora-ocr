# *Pseudosabicea aurifodinae* N. Hallé sp. nov.

**Genus**: [[Pseudosabicea]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]

## Description

Aff. Ps. Batesii (Wernh.) N. Hallé, but differs markedly in the glabrous internodes, stipules and petioles with glabrous midrib above at the base, cauline inflorescences, glabrous subulate sepals, and longer corollas.

Sarmentose plant with woody base 16 mm diam., with semiwoody medullate non-twining stems 2–4 m long, sparsely branched, forming a bush that leans on itself and on neighbouring plants. Internodes glabrous (4) 5–20 × 0.5–0.7 cm, subligneous, glabrous. Extreme anisophylly: a small reflexed blade a few millimetres long opposite the developed leaf. Stipules glabrous in erect spathelike system with an apex on each side of the aborted leaf. Petiole glabrous 2.5–4.5 cm. Blade discolorous, with very dense and very fine woolly tomentum, white beneath, glabrous above, ovate, reaching 17–23 × 8–13.5 cm; base very unequal with one side 1–2 cm lower than the other, and strongly cordate; apex with attenuate very acute acumen and slender mucro 1–3 mm. Midvein glabrous above towards the base but ciliate towards its middle portion and glabrous below. 20–25 lateral nerves spaced less than 1 cm apart per side.

Cauline inflorescences on the lower defoliate nodes, in multiflorous sessile glomerules 12–30 mm diam. Calyx violet tubulous ca. 1–1.5 mm, with 5 white subulate glabrous lobes, ca. 3 mm, arched almost erect. Corolla white, glabrous externally, with ovoid apex in bud, with tube 9–11 × 1–1.5 mm, with internal ring of hairs inserted towards mid-length, in 5 interstaminal tufts (similar hairs but in smaller tufts exist in Ps. Batesii). 5 reflexed triangular lobes reaching in vivo 3 × 2–2.5 mm. Anthers 2 mm exceeding the throat in the tuft of divergent white hairs. Style glabrous 9 mm, with bilobed stigma. Annular disc 1 mm diam., glabrous; ovary 2-locular; placentas narrow, 1.4 mm long, densely covered with ovules.

Immature fruit 8 mm diam., green with apex tinged violet.

Type and only known material: N. Hallé and G. Cours 6137 (P), Moubigou-2, at the end of the road from Massima towards Moumba, Etéké region. In semi-lit fringing scrub with Marantaceae and large Vernonia, on lower slope.
