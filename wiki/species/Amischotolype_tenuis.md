# *Amischotolype tenuis* (C.B.Clarke) R.S.Rao

**Genus**: [[Amischotolype]]
**Family**: [[Commelinaceae]]
**Protologue**: Maharashtra Vidnyan Mandir Patrika 6(2) : 53 (1971)

## Description

Maharashtra Vidnyan Mandir Patrika 6(2) : 53 (1971). Buforrestia tenuis C.B.Clarke in A.DC. & C.DC., Monogr. phan. 3 : 233, t. 234 (1881). Forrestia tenuis (C.B.Clarke) Benth., Gen. pl. 3 : 851 (1883). Forrestia preussii K.Schum., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 24(3) : 344 (1897). Forrestia africana K.Schum. ex C.B.Clarke, Fl. trop. Afr. 8 : 77 (1901). Forrestia lescrauwaetii De Wild., Ann. Mus. Congo Belge, Bot. sér. 5, 3(1) : 53 (1909). Herb decumbent or ascending, 0.6–1 m in height, basal part rhizomatous and rooting at the nodes, internodes straight, 1.5–4(–6.5) cm, longer towards the base, shorter towards the apex. Leaves: ± 4 leaves gathered at the stem apex; sheath reaching 20 mm in length, glabrous or slightly hairy, with a few hairs on the margin and suture; pseudetiole 1–2 cm, with 2 lines of short hairs; blade broadly elliptic to elliptic, 6–12 × 4–5 cm, base cuneate to obtuse, apex shortly acuminate, upper surface smooth and glabrous, dark green, lower surface with a line of hairs towards the margin, pale greenish-violet. Inflorescence composed of 2(or 3) cincinni coiled at first, finally almost straight, of ± 4–5 cm in length, with ± 24 rather closely packed flowers per cincinnus, rarely pauciflorous towards the plant apex; peduncle reaching 3 mm; floral bracts ovate, 8–9 × 3–3.5 mm, sometimes with a few hairs on the basal margin, green or purple. Flower: sepals oblong elliptic-ovate, weakly carinate, quasi conduplicate, 5–5.5 × 2 mm, with truncate apex, cucullate with a few hairs, green; petals oblong elliptic-ovate, 5 × 1.5 mm, glabrous, white; stamens with filament longer than the petals, 7–8 mm, sinuous, anther with oblong thecae, pale yellow; ovary obovoid, 1 mm in length; style ± 4–5 mm, stigma slightly 3-lobed. Ripe fruit exposing 3 to 6 seeds. Ripe seed not seen.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea (Río Muni), Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Central African Republic and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, in the Haut-Ogooué, the Ngounié and the Ogooué-Ivindo

## Ecology

damp undergrowth; in Gabon from 85 to 750 m in altitude, elsewhere from 40 to 910 m

## Vernacular names

ako-nkok (fang)

## Source note

In the past, several African species were described - most in the genera Forrestia and Buforrestia - but they have all been placed in synonymy with A. tenuis (C.B.Clarke) R.S.Rao. Clarke (1901) mentions in particular a subglobose inflorescence and a stem with square section for F. tenuis, whereas F. africana would present a stem with round section and a linear-oblong inflorescence. It was subsequently proven that the form of the inflorescence depends on the maturation stage, and that the section of the stem is a drying artefact. Nevertheless, even separated from A. scandens, A. tenuis still exhibits strong morphological variability, and we think that this name probably covers several taxa. An in-depth study, based on a larger number of specimens, ideally associated with photographs and material preserved in alcohol, would be necessary in order to resolve this complex. B: Brenan (1968b), Cabezas et al. (2014), Clarke (1901), Raponda-Walker & Sillans (1961), Bibliography Sosef et al. (2006).
