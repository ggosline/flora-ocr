# *Dalbergia micheliana* De Wild. Planche 45

**Genus**: [[Dalbergia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: D. foenum-graecum De Wild., Études fl. Bas- Moyen-Congo 3 : 210 (1910)

## Description

Studies fl. Bas-Moyen-Congo 1: 148 (1904). D. foenum-graecum De Wild., Studies fl. Bas-Moyen-Congo 3: 210 (1910). D. bakeri var. acutifoliolata E.P.Sousa in Exell, Consp. fl. angol. 3(2): 351 (1966). Liane or sarmentose shrub 3–10 m tall, with strong persistent fenugreek-like aromatic odour on dried material, without spines and apparently without prehensile branches; bark grey to light brown, longitudinally striated, with paler lenticels; young branches with very short appressed pubescence, becoming glabrous over time. Stipules very early caducous, elliptic to narrowly lanceolate, 4.5–17 × 1.5 mm, with short appressed pubescence. Leaf imparipinnate; petiole 0.7–2.5 cm; rachis 2.1–13.5 cm, with short appressed or semi-erect pubescence; leaflets 4 to 8, alternate and unequal, with petiolules of 3–8 mm, lamina elliptic, symmetric or ± asymmetric, (3.9–)5–13.4 × (1.6–)2.4–5 cm in upper leaflets and 2.2–5 × 1.8–3 cm in lower ones, acute or obtuse at base, acuminate at apex, coriaceous to papyraceous, becoming olive-brown to blackish on dried material, with the lower surface distinctly glaucous in adult leaves (± copper-coloured in young leaves), with very short appressed pubescence on both surfaces (appearing ± glabrous to the naked eye); lateral veins 6 to 9, not prominent; venules in apparent and fairly dense network on the lower surface. Inflorescence in short panicle of unilateral racemes, axillary or on old branches, sometimes also terminal, 1.5–3.8 cm long, with short dense brown pubescence; bracts early caducous, broadly ovate, ± 1 × 0.6 mm, pubescent. Flower: pedicel 1.5–3 mm; bracteoles persistent or caducous after flowering, broadly elliptic, 1–1.5 × 0.8–1 mm, rounded at apex, pubescent; calyx pale green, with dense golden-brown appressed hairs, tube 2.5–3 × 2.5–3.5 mm, teeth valvate ± equal and triangular, acute at apex, 0.5–1.3 mm long; corolla white, petals with claw exceeding the calyx, standard bent at right angle, with claw 2–3 mm and suborbicular limb 3–3.5 × 4–5.5 mm, with apex bilobed, wings equal to or slightly exceeding the keel, with claw 2.8–3 mm and distinctly asymmetric limb 2.7–4 × 1.7–2.5 mm, slightly wrinkled transversely towards the base of the outer surface, with shortly auriculate base, keel strongly curved, with claw 2.8–3 mm and limb 3–4 × 1.7–2 mm, with strongly auriculate base; stamens in 2 phalanges of 5, filaments ± 6 mm, fused for 3/4 of their length, 4–5 straight at base and curved in their free portion; ovary ± 3 × 0.8 mm, glabrous, with 3 ovules and stipe 3.5 mm; style linear, 1.5 mm, with punctiform stigma. Pod with pedicel 1.5–2 mm and stipe 5–8 mm, papyraceous, flattened and narrowly ellipsoid, 5.6–9.6 × 1.5–2.5 cm, rounded or obtuse at both ends, reticulate over entire surface, glabrous, with 1 to 3 seeds. Seed reniform and flattened, ± 8 × 3 mm.

## Distribution

Equatorial Guinea (Rio Muni), Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola; in Gabon, appears limited to the Batéké Plateaus where it is not uncommon.

## Ecology

Gallery forests, savanna thickets, dry forests on Kalahari sands, and edge of low forest on inselbergs; at 400–480 m altitude in Gabon, 300–1120 m elsewhere. In Gabon, flowers and fruits from August to February.

## Vernacular names

okinkéné (Batéké)

## Source note

D. micheliana was long considered a synonym of D. bakeri (Cronquist 1954) or as a variety of this species (Sousa 1966). It is, however, clearly different from it (Lachenaud & Maesen 2016) by the characters indicated in the key, and also apparently by its distribution. Bibliography: Lachenaud & Maesen (2016), Sousa (1966).
