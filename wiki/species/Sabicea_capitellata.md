# *Sabicea capitellata* Benth. (Fig. 5)

**Genus**: [[Sabicea]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bentham., Hook., Fl. Nigrit. : 398 (1849)

## Synonyms

- S. trichochlamys K. Schum., Bot. Jahrb. 33 : 338 (1903).

## Description

Small liana reaching 2 m in height. Branchlets villous, with pubescence sometimes tinged with rosy-purple on young organs. Stipules suborbicular, reflexed, 3–5 mm wide. Petioles pubescent, 10–40 (… 100) mm long. Blade ± sparsely pubescent above, with clear woolly tomentum pressed beneath, 5–11 (15) × 2.5–7 (8.8) cm, base ± briefly acute or cuneate, apex acute ± distinctly acuminate; 10–16 pairs of pubescent secondary veins.

Inflorescences entirely villous with peduncle 0.5–4 (7) cm long, with first pair of bracts at least somewhat connate-fused at base, enveloping and hirsute, apex reduced to a leaflike languet 2–3 mm. A second pair of inner bracts protects the flanks of the capitulum which often bears more than 4 flowers (6–13). Flowers sessile, villous; 5 calycine lobes ensiform, 3–6 × 0.5–1 mm, green. Corolla pale green, pubescent toward the apex (hairs short ± violaceous), tube 7–10 mm long, 5 lobes spreading over 5 mm diam. Stamens inserted in the internal pubescent zone of the corolla. Style (long form) reaching 10.5 mm. Ovary very villous.

Fruit white (or red, Klaine 1532) sessile, reaching 1 cm diam., pubescent, with 5 locules in a very colorful dark blue-blackish succulent pulp.

## Discussion

The species is variable, notably in the length of the peduncles which are sometimes much reduced.

Distributed from SE Nigeria to Gabon and, according to Wernham, to Congo. Plant of savanna at forest margins.

Bakota name: Mbouboutou.
