# *Scleria goossensii* De Wild. Figure 25b

**Genus**: [[Scleria]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Rev. Zool. Afr. 14 , Suppl. Bot. : 20, f. 4 (1926)

## Description

Robust perennial herb in tufts, with short rhizome; stem 1.3–2 m × 5 mm approximately at the level of the sheaths, triangular, glabrous or weakly pilose. Leaves numerous, the lower ones reduced to pilose sheaths; ligule oblong, approximately 4–5 × 5 mm; blade 20–40 cm × 5–8 mm, plane or rolled, densely pilose at least beneath. Inflorescence composed of a terminal panicle and 4–7 lateral ones well-spaced or congested, 4–15 × 2–4 cm, much shorter than the foliaceous bracts; peduncles robust and erect, 0–8 cm, sometimes barely exceeding the sheath. Male spikelet approximately 6 mm long, brown, greenish or brown-violet, the glumes subtending either 3 staminodes or 3 stamens. Bisexual spikelet approximately 6 mm long, formed of 3–4 basal glumes 5 mm long subtending a female flower and 1–2 smaller glumes each subtending 3 stamens. Female spikelets rare. Achene spherical, approximately 2.5 (including hypogynium) × 2 mm, slightly compressed with a short mucro, white to beige, verrucose; hypogynium irregularly pleated, without lobes, brownish.

## Distribution

Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo; rare in Gabon, found only in Ogooué-Ivindo and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

In flooded or swampy terrain in forest, often along watercourses and rivers; probably at 300–500 m altitude in Gabon. Taxonomic note: recognized by its globular achenes with a hypogynium without lobes. b: De Wildeman (1926), Piérart (1953). ibliography
