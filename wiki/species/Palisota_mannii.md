# *Palisota mannii* C.B.Clarke subsp. mannii Figure 5(A, B)

**Genus**: [[Palisota]]
**Family**: [[Commelinaceae]]

## Description

in A.DC. & C.DC., Monogr. phan. 3 : 132 (1881). Perennial herb, 30–60 cm tall; stem short, unbranched, covered by the sheaths; roots thick, non-fibrous. Leaves in rosette; pseudipetiole 12–40 cm, narrowly winged, margins ciliate with rusty or tawny hairs; blade obovate to obovate-elliptic or narrowly obovate-elliptic, 26–110 × 8.5–22 cm, base narrowly cuneate, apex abruptly acuminate, margins ciliate with rusty or tawny hairs, upper surface entirely glabrous, lower surface with velvety white or brown indumentum. Inflorescences 1 or several per rosette, Figure 5(A, B) 14 × 2–3.5 cm (up to 23 × 5 cm at fruiting); peduncle 8–15(–30) cm, with 1 or several bracteiform leaves; floral bracts ovate-elliptic to narrowly ovate-elliptic, 10–14 × 2.5–5 mm, long-ciliate with rusty hairs, flowers functionally male and female, the faded ones persistent, black. Flower glabrous, white or whitish-pink, pleasantly fragrant; pedicel 7–10 mm, becoming curved or spiral in fruit; upper sepal oblong-elliptic to ovate-elliptic, 4–5 × 2–2.5 mm, the lower ones oblong-elliptic, 4–5 × 1.6–2 mm; upper petals elliptic-ovate, 4.5–5 × 2–2.5 mm, the lower one similar or slightly narrower, 4–4.5 × 2 mm; stamens unequal, the 2 upper ones with filament 1.5–1.8 mm, anther 1.2–1.4 × 0.8–1.2 mm, yellow, the lower one in central position in the male flower, with filament 3.5–4 mm, anther ± 1 × 1 mm, with dehiscent thecae, in the female flower, the lower one strongly recurved, with filament 2.5–3 mm, anther as in the male flower, but with indehiscent thecae; staminodes as long as the stamens, white; ovary ± 1 mm in diameter, densely hirsute; style 2.5–3 mm, white, stigma capitate. Fruit globose or slightly flattened, 6–11 × 6.5–13 mm, sparsely pubescent, becoming red to purple-brown at maturity; locules 3, with 0 to 2 seeds. Seed coarsely polygonal, 3.5 × 3–3.5 × 2–2.5 mm; testa radially and finely striate, granulate on all sides.

## Source note

The distinction between the typical subspecies and subspecies megalophylla is sometimes difficult to establish in Central Africa, where both are present. According to Faden (2007a), all collections of P. mannii from East Africa are to be assigned to subspecies megalophylla, the typical subspecies being absent there. B: Bidault & van der Burg (2019), Brenan (1968b), Cabezas et al. (2014), Faden (2007a, Bibliography 2012), Morton (1967), Sosef et al. (2006), Vandeweeghe et al. (2016). subsp. megalophylla (Mildbr.) Faden Kew Bull 62 : 137 (2007). P. megalophylla Mildbr., Notizbl. Bot. Gart. Berlin-Dahlem 84 : 248 (1925). Like the typical subspecies, but leaf blade on the lower surface with dense velvety indumentum, white or brown only on the main vein.
