# *Drypetes aframensis* Hutch.

**Genus**: [[Drypetes]]
**Family**: [[Putranjivaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. trop. Afr. 6(1): 682 (1912)

## Description

Plate 38

Small tree up to 12 m tall; trunk up to ±25 cm in diameter, often knotty; bark greyish-green, with fruity and slightly spicy odour; branchlets slightly furrowed, sparsely puberulent, glabrescent. Stipules not seen. Leaf: petiole (3–4.2–5.3–6.8) mm long, crinkled, glabrescent; blade elliptic to oblong-elliptic, (5–10–14.5–(20) × (2.5–)4.4–6.8–(7.1) cm, coriaceous, with oblique base, acute or slightly obtuse, with apex abruptly and shortly acuminate, with margin subentire, slightly and obscurely serrulate slightly recurved and undulate, dark green shiny above, paler below, glabrous or rarely sparsely puberulent, with simple or rarely stellate hairs; venation loosely reticulate, midvein glabrous or rarely sparsely puberulent, glabrescent, secondary veins 6 or 7, scarcely raised above, slightly elevated below, ascending, slightly looped and anastomosing well before the margin. Male inflorescence in the axils of leaves or below leaves along the branches, with up to ± 10 flowers; bracts ovate, 0.7–1.1 mm long, puberulent. Male flower: pedicel slender, (1.5–3.5–10(−12) mm, glabrous or finely and densely hairy; sepals 4, imbricate, obovate to oblong, 1.7–2.6(−4.5) × 1–1.3(−1.6) mm, with obtuse and strongly cucullate apex and shortly hairy, whitish, externally glabrous or finely hairy, internally glabrous; stamens 6 to 8(−9), mainly arranged around the margin of the disc, some penetrating the disc towards the centre, filament 2.1–2.7(−3.3) mm, glabrous or finely hairy, anther 0.5–0.8 mm long, glabrous; disc 1.8–2.2 mm in diameter, concave, strongly plicate, with irregular margin, exceeding the filaments, glabrous. Female inflorescence like the male, with up to ± 10 flowers; bracts as in males. Female flower: pedicel slender, 8–10 mm, glabrous; sepals 4, ± 2.5 × 2.4 mm, whitish; disc up to ± 2.6 mm in diameter, concave, cupuliform, glabrous; ovary subglobose, 2-locular, glabrous; style up to ± 1.1 mm, with 2 free branches, stigma 2.2–2.7(−3.2) × (4.1–4.7–5.5) mm, fan-shaped. Fruit globose, up to ± 10 × 10 mm, rugose, glabrous, pale orange at maturity, without persistent sepals or stigmas, on a pedicel reaching 6.3 mm, with (1–)2 seeds. Seed reaching 8.3 mm in length.

## Distribution

Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon: Estuaire and Ogooué-Maritime

Habitat: evergreen forests; in Gabon at 10–150 m altitude, up to 950 m elsewhere.

## Source note

This species is known from Gabon only from certain sterile collections from the west of the country. The leaves of D. aframensis have subentire margins and are fairly discolored, bright green above and paler below, and have short crinkled petioles and slightly channeled above. The sepals of this species are substantially cucullate and finely hairy in their apical quarter (the remainder being glabrous or sparsely hairy).
