# *Hymenophyllum hirsutum* (L.) Swartz

**Genus**: [[Hymenophyllum]]
**Family**: [[Hymenophyllaceae]]

## Synonyms

- Trichomanes hirsutum L., Sp. Pl. 2 : 1098 (1753). — Alston, FWTA : 32 (1959).
- Hymenophyllum ciliatum Sw., Schrad. Journ. 1800 (2) : 100 (1801). — Tard., Mém. IFAN : 38, pl. 3, f. 4-5 (1953).
- Sphaerocionium ciliatum Pr., Hym.: 34 (1843).

## Description

— Sw., Schrad. Journ. 1800 (2): 99 (1801) emend. Morton, Contrib. U.S. Nat. Herb. 29: 155 (1947).

Rhizome filiform, creeping, with fronds spaced apart. Petiole 2–3 cm long, broadly winged in the upper part. Frond blade 5–15 cm, ovate-lanceolate, bipinnatifid. Pinnae approximately 1–1.5 cm long, decurrent in a broad wing along the rachis, obtuse, divided over half their width into segments 0.1 cm, obtuse, often bifurcate, with ciliate margin, bearing stellate hairs with a basal cell with horizontal branches; rachis and costae bearing the same hairs. Sori rounded, sometimes cordate, divided almost to the base into two ciliate valves; receptacle cylindric, claviform, included.

São Thomé, Cameroon, Belgian Congo, East and South Africa, Madagascar, tropical America.
