# *Pogostemon micangensis* G.Taylor Figure 4(F)

**Genus**: [[Pogostemon]]
**Protologue**: J. Bot. 69 ( Suppl. 2) : 166 (1931)

## Description

J. Bot. 69 ( Suppl. 2) : 166 (1931). Perennial herb, prostrate or sarmentose, up to 20 cm tall ; stem quadrangular, decumbent, violaceous, rooting at the nodes and glabrous towards the base, pubescent towards the apex, with appressed hairs directed upwards. Leaf : petiole 5–15(–20) mm ; blade oval, 1–4 × 0.7–2 cm, rounded to cuneate at the base, acute at the apex, dentate, glabrous or with minute hairs on the veins of the lower surface. Inflorescence terminal, unbranched, cylindric, 3–4 × 0.7–1 cm ; verticillasters with 5 to 7 flowers, sometimes a few lower verticillasters somewhat spaced ; bracts narrowly elliptic-ovate to linear, 2 mm long, villous towards the apex ; bracteoles absent or scarcely visible. Flower : calyx funnel-shaped, becoming urceolate in fruit, 2–3 mm long, ± regularly 5-lobed, sparsely villous with yellowish sessile glands, lobes narrowly triangular, reaching 1 mm in length ; corolla 2–3 mm long, shortly exserted from the calyx, pink, with lobes ± 1 mm long ; stamens spreading, bearded with long moniliform hairs around the midpoint ; style with equal branches. Fruit : 4 achenes, 1 mm long, black.

## Source note

The genus Premna has long been assigned to the family Verbenaceae, subfamily Viticoideae (Briquet 1897). The assignment of Viticoideae to Labiatae has been well established since Harley et al. (2004). Very recently, phylogenetic analyses have shown that Premna should be detached from Viticoideae and constitute a new subfamily, Premnoideae (Li et al. 2016). Premna is the only genus of this subfamily that is represented in Africa. The taxonomy of the genus Premna is one of the most difficult in the family Labiatae (Li et al. 2016) ; pending a revision for Africa, our treatment follows that of Huber et al. (1963).
