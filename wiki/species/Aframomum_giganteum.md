# *Aframomum giganteum* (Oliv. et Hanb.) K. Schum.
**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Aframomum]]
## Description
Very large herb to 6 m or more, with 3 cm-thick rhizome and glabrous blades to 60–70 × 15–18 cm. Inflorescence axis to c. 60 cm bears lateral many-flowered spikes. Fruit and seeds illustrated in Vol. 9; a puberulous-leaved variety is discussed inline.
