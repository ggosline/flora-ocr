# *Ludwigia leptocarpa* (Nutt.) H.Hara

**Family**: [[Onagraceae]] · **Genus**: [[Ludwigia]]

J. Jap. Bot. **28**(10): 292 (1953).

## Description

Erect, bushy, woody-based herb to 3 m, sometimes immersed and then bearing fusiform spongy floats or fine elongated spongy roots. Vigorous branches densely pubescent to hirsute, especially when young. Leaves subsessile; blade linear to narrowly elliptic-ovate, 3–11 × 0.8–1 cm; lateral veins 11–20 pairs. Flowers solitary; pedicel 2–10 mm; sepals 5, 5–11 × 1.5–3(–4) mm; petals 5, broadly obovate, 5–11 × 4–9 mm, yellow; stamens 10; ovary about 15 mm; style 3–4.5 mm. Fruit 18–50 × 2–4 mm, subcylindrical below, distinctly five-angled above, hirsute, with one seed row. Seed horizontal, 0.8–1.2 mm, loosely embedded in horseshoe-shaped endocarp.

## Distribution

Widespread in tropical Africa and Madagascar and in the Americas from Florida to Peru and Argentina. In Gabon it is known from Ogooué-Maritime and Ogooué-Ivindo.

## Habitat and ecology

Sandbanks, marsh and river margins, wet ground and drains; to 500 m in Gabon and 1300 m elsewhere.
