# *Combretum platypterum* (Welwitsch) Hutchinson & Dalziel

**Genus**: [[Combretum]]
**Family**: [[Combretaceae]]
**Protologue**: FWTA, ed.1 1 : 220 (1927) ; ANDREWS, AESudan : 204 (1950) ; KEAY, FWTA, ed. 2, 1(1) : 274 (1954) ; LIBEN, FZ : 22, fig 2a (1968) ; EXELL & GARCIA, CFA : 80 (1970) ; LIBEN, Fl. Cam. 25 : 27 (1983)

## Description

KEAY, FWTA, ed. 2, 1(1): 274 (1954); LIBEN, FZ: 22, fig 2a (1968); EXELL & GARCIA, CFA: 80 (1970); LIBEN, Fl. Cam. 25: 27 (1983).

– *Cacoucia platyptera* Welwitsch, Trans. Linn. Soc. London 27: 24 (1869). TYPE: Angola, Cuanza Norte, Golungo Alto, Sange (fl., fr.) Welwitsch 1752 (holo-LISU; iso-BM, BR, COI, K, M, P).

– *Cacoucia paniculata* LAWSON, FTA 2: 434 (1871). TYPE: Nigeria, Onitsha (fl.), Barter 1657 (LECTOTYPE designated here: lecto-K); Gabon, Riv. Gabon (fl.), Mann s.n. (syn-K; isosyn-P). This binomial has nothing to do with *Combretum paniculatum* VENT.

– *Combretum lawsonianum* Engler & Diels, Mon. Afr., Pfl., 3: 101, tab. 30 (1899), illegitimate name based on the same type as *Cacoucia paniculata* Lawson (1871).

Large liana ≤ 25 m, diameter ≤ 10 cm, or shrub. Bark fissured.

Leaves alternate to opposite; petiole ≤ 2 cm; blade ovate ≤ 17 × ≤ 7 cm, coriaceous; major lateral veins 6–18 pairs; base of blade rounded to subcordate; apex shortly acuminate.

Scales absent.

Inflorescences ≤ 75 cm: axillary or terminal racemes; rachis covered with appressed hairs; bracts reddish to scarlet, ≤ 25 mm long, ≤ 5 mm wide.

Flowers 5-merous, stipitate. Receptacle, exterior covered with a coating of appressed greyish hairs: the lower one 5 mm high, the upper broadly tubular, height ≤ 20 mm, width 8 mm, curved toward the interior, rounded at the base, reddish. Calyx: lobes ≤ 8 mm long. Petals red-greenish to red, length ≤ 7 mm and width ≤ 3 mm, scattered hairs on both surfaces. Stamens exserted 5–10 mm; filaments reddish; anthers dark purplish-brown, 1.2–1.5 mm long. Style exserted 5–10 mm.

Fruit, length ≤ 5 cm, diameter ≤ 4.5 cm, wings puberulent, reddish, membranous to papyraceous; stipe ≤ 13 mm. Cotyledons planoconvex.

## Distribution

from Guinea to Sudan, to Congo (Kinshasa) and Angola
