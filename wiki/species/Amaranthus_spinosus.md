# *Amaranthus spinosus* L.

**Family**: [[Amaranthaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Amaranthus]]

## Description

Erect annual, woody below, 1.5 m or taller. Paired straight strong axillary spines 8–15 mm flank each petiole. Lower inflorescences are dense axillary glomerules, upper ones spikes 8–14 cm; male flowers concentrate at spike tips. Flowers 5-tepalled. Capsule circumscissile, ovoid-conical; seed dark red or black and glossy.

## Ecology and uses

Village waste ground, often ferruginous clay; leaves eaten as a vegetable.
