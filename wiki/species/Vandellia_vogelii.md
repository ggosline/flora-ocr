# *Vandellia vogelii* (Skan) Eb.Fisch., Schäferh. & Kai Müll. Planche 53

**Genus**: [[Vandellia]]
**Family**: [[Scrophulariaceae]]
**Modern family**: [[Linderniaceae]] — *Scrophulariaceae* as circumscribed here is superseded; see [[Scrophulariaceae]]
**Protologue**: Willdenowi a 43 : 235 (2013)

## Synonyms

- Lindernia vogelii Skan, Fl. trop. Afr . 4(2) : 339 (1906).

## Description

Annual herb; stem prostrate to ascending, with numerous ramifications (paraclades), 10–25 cm in length, glabrous. Leaves sessile or shortly petiolate; limb oval to orbicular, 10–18 × 8–13 mm, acuminate at the apex, with obtusely dentate margin. Inflorescence lax, each floriferous node bearing 2 flowers. Flower: pedicel ± 8 mm; calyx 8–10 mm in length, with lobes 2–3 mm in length; corolla white to pale pink with lower lip blue, tube 5 mm in length, upper lip entire and 5 mm in length, lower lip tripartite and 5 mm in length; filaments of anterior (abaxial) stamens 4–5 mm, filaments of posterior (adaxial) stamens 2 mm; ovary ovoid, 1.5 mm in length; style 7 mm. Fruit lanceolate to linear, 18–25 mm in length, more than 2 times longer than the calyx.

## Distribution

Côte d`Ivoire, Nigéria, Guinée équatoriale (Bioko) and Gabon; in Gabon, observed in l'Estuaire, l'Ogooué-Maritime and le Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology
