# *Vandellia vogelii* (Skan) Eb.Fisch., Schäferh. & Kai Müll. Planche 53

**Genus**: [[Vandellia]]
**Family**: [[Scrophulariaceae]]
**Protologue**: Willdenowi a 43 : 235 (2013)

## Description

Lindernia vogelii Skan, Fl. trop. Afr. 4(2): 339 (1906). Annual herb; stem prostrate to ascending, with numerous branches (paraclades), 10–25 cm long, glabrous. Leaves sessile or shortly petiolate; blade ovate to orbicular, 10–18 × 8–13 mm, acuminate at apex, with obtusely dentate margin. Inflorescence lax, each floriferous node bearing 2 flowers. Flower: pedicel ± 8 mm; calyx 8–10 mm long, with lobes 2–3 mm long; corolla white to pale pink with lower lip blue, tube 5 mm long, upper lip entire and 5 mm long, lower lip tripartite and 5 mm long; filaments of anterior (abaxial) stamens 4–5 mm, filaments of posterior (adaxial) stamens 2 mm; ovary ovoid, 1.5 mm long; style 7 mm. Fruit lanceolate to linear, 18–25 mm long, more than 2 times longer than the calyx.

## Distribution

Ivory Coast, Nigeria, Equatorial Guinea (Bioko) and Gabon; in Gabon, observed in Estuaire, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem
