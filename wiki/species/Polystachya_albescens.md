# *Polystachya albescens* Ridl.

**Genus**: [[Polystachya]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bol. Soc. Brot. 5 : 199 (1888)

## Description

Stems 10-50 × 0.3-0.5 cm. Leaves (8-)10-27 × 0.7-2(-2.5) cm, linear-ligulate. Inflorescence glabrous. Flowers white tinged with yellow and violet, to yellowish-green. Dorsal sepal lanceolate, acute. Petals oblanceolate. Lateral sepals obliquely triangular-ovate, acute. Labellum of highly variable shape, distinctly or obscurely trilobed, papillose or pubescent at the base in the apical half; callus obtuse; lateral lobes of variable size and shape. - Pl. 115, p. 261.

## Distribution

Equatorial Guinea, São Tomé, Príncipe, Annobón, Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Angola, Tanzania, Zambia, Malawi, Zimbabwe

Within this species, the following subspecies present in Gabon may be distinguished:

a. ssp. kraenzlinii (Rolfe) Summerh.

Leaves 5-10 cm. Inflorescence short, bracts not imbricate. Labellum obscurely 3-lobed, lateral lobes broadly rounded, the median triangular.
