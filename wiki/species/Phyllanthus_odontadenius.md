# *Phyllanthus odontadenius* Müll.Arg.

**Genus**: [[Phyllanthus]]
**Family**: [[Phyllanthaceae]]
**Protologue**: J. Bot. 2 : 331 (1864)

## Description

p.p. (as regards material from Gabon). Annual to perennial herb, up to 1 m tall, with ± woody base; lateral branchlets keeled, slightly denticulate. Stipules unequal, longer on the upper face of the branchlet, 1.5–2.5 mm long. Leaf: petiole 0.7–1.35 mm; lamina obovate to subrectangular, 10–30 × 5–12 mm, rounded at base, acute at apex, with 6–7 pairs of lateral veins. Inflorescence male, bisexual or female. Flowers monoecious. Male flower: pedicel 1–1.5 mm; sepals 6, 0.8–1.4 × 0.4–0.9 mm; disk formed of alternisepal elements; stamens 3, filaments fused into a staminal column 0.7–0.8 mm. Female flower: pedicel 2.4–3.6 mm, sepals 6, 2.1–3.1 × 0.8–1.5 mm; disk entire or slightly crenulate; ovary smooth with 3 bifid styles. Capsular fruit, up to 2.4–2.8 mm in diameter at maturity, smooth. Seeds nail-shaped spherical, 1.2–1.4 mm long, brown, dorsally ornamented with 7 or 8 longitudinal costules.

## Distribution

from Sierra Leone to Cameroon, Central African Republic, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Rwanda, Burundi, Zambia and Angola; in Gabon, widely distributed in all provinces

## Ecology

herbaceous or shrubby savanna, river banks, swamps, damp rocks; in Gabon, at 0–1000 m altitude. Taxonomic note: Brunel and Roux l.c. based the description of P. raynalii on two specimens. One is the holotype from Cameroon and the other comes from Gabon. The latter is now identified as P. odontadenius. b: Brunel (1987), Keay (1958), Radcliffe-Smith (1996b), Smith (1987). Bibliography
