# *Millettia macrophylla* Benth. Planche 95.1,2

**Genus**: [[Millettia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]

## Description

in Hook., Icon. Pl. n.s. 4 : t. 788-9 (1848). Small tree (2–)4–10 (–13) m tall; trunk up to 13.5 cm diameter; crown dome-shaped; bark greenish-brown, with cream lenticels; branchlets shortly hirsute. Stipules caducous, villous. Leaf: petiole 2–12.7 cm; rachis 40 cm, striate, channelled above, brown pubescent; stipels absent; leaflets 5 to 13, petiolule 5–8 mm, densely brown pubescent, blade obovate to oblong-elliptic, 8–19(–25) × 4–9.5(–11) cm, base rounded-cuneate, apex with acumen 0.3–2 cm, mucronate, dark green, glossy and glabrous above, dull ± greyish-green and with short golden-brown pubescence beneath, especially on the nerves; lateral nerves in (6–)10 to 12(–14) pairs, greyish above and brown prominent beneath. Inflorescence axillary, in erect pseudoracemes, 3–35 cm long; peduncle 3–15 cm, striate-channelled, lenticellate; lateral branches 0.5–8 cm long, with ± 8 flowers; bracts caducous. Flower: pedicel 6–9 mm; bracteoles at base of calyx, ovate-circular, 1–1.2 mm diameter, brown pubescent; calyx brown-red, tube 3–8 × 4–6 mm, truncate or with lobes up to 0.5 mm long; corolla purple or white, standard almost always without basal green spot, with claw ± 3 mm, limb broadly ovate to obovate, 20–21 × 15–24 mm, base broadly cuneate, apex emarginate, brown pubescent near the incision, wings white at base, with claw 3–9 mm, limb obovate, 4–10 × 5 mm, keel white at base, with claw 4–10 mm, limb 18 × 5 mm, basal sutures with cream pubescence, becoming brown; stamens 10–20 mm, with white filaments, free part curved, ± 4 mm; ovary linear-lanceolate, 14 mm long, white, with golden-brown pubescence, with 4 ovules; style 5 mm, white, glabrous, stigma pale green. Pod oblong, ± 8–15 × 2–3 cm, attenuate at base and apex, with 1 or 2(–4) seeds, sutures prominent, pale brown, silky. Seed flattened, circular, ± 10 × 9 × 2 mm, brown; hilum ± 3 mm long.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon and Equatorial Guinea; to be searched for in Gabon

## Ecology

primary and secondary forest, near mangrove; at 20–1100 m altitude. Flowering June to December; fruits September to March. b : Pellegrin (1949), Mollel & Adema (2006). Bibliography
