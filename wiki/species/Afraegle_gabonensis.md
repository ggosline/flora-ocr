# *Afraegle gabonensis* (Sw.) Engl.

**Family**: [[Rutaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Afraegle]]

## Description

Based on plants cultivated in Paris and Washington from fruit and seed sent from the Libreville region by Klaine in 1892. Adult leaves are trifoliolate; petiole 0.5–3.5 cm, lateral leaflets less than half the terminal one, and blades lanceolate or narrowly lanceolate with acute apex and deltoid base. The flowers and fruits closely resemble *A. paniculata*; the treatment questions whether the observed foliage reflects cultivation and whether the two are truly distinct.

## Uses

Hollowed fruits were used as powder measures or containers and, when fitted with a handle and filled with stones or seeds, as dance rattles.

## Distribution

Reported from Gabon, but not relocated there in wild material by the treatment.
