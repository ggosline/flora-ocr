# *Xanthocercis rabiensis* Maesen Planche 131

**Genus**: [[Xanthocercis]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Jard. Bot. Natl. Belg. 66 : 20 (1997)

## Description

Tree of ± 40 m in height; trunk up to 3 m in diameter; branchlets with grey-brown bark, with short, scattered, glabrate pubescence. Stipules linear, 3–5 mm in length. Leaf: petiole 2–3 cm; rachis 2–9 cm; stipels early caducous, up to 0.5 mm in length; leaflets (1–)5, alternate, petiolule somewhat swollen, 5–8 mm, brown, blade elliptic to obovate, 6–13 × 4–8 cm, the terminal one broadest, base cuneate, apex with acumen of 1–1.5 cm, green and glossy above, medium green below. Inflorescence in pseudoracemes with axis of 8–15 cm, branched or not; bracts triangular, 1 mm in length, brown, pubescent. Flower: pedicel slender, 1–1.5 cm; bracteoles minute, brown, pubescent; calyx cupuliform, ± 5 × 7 mm, partially split, with teeth not prominent, pale green and grey velvety; petals ovate to lanceolate, 7–10 × 3–5 mm, standard pubescent in the middle; stamens 10, ± 1 cm in length; ovary linear, 1 cm, but developing rapidly, with ± 8 ovules, pubescent. Pod knotty, with 1 or 2 seeds, moniliform if there are two, 3.5–6 × 2–2.5 cm, brown at maturity, with pale pulp. Seed rounded-rectangular, ± 3 × 2 × 1.5 cm.

## Distribution

endemic to Gabon, known from Ogooué-Maritime, Rabi-Kounga and Fernand-Vaz

## Ecology

evergreen humid forest; at 30 m altitude. Flowers in March; fruits in September. B: Détienne (1998), van der Maesen (1997). Bibliography
