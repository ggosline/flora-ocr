# *Drypetes laciniata* (Pax) Hutch.

**Genus**: [[Drypetes]]
**Family**: [[Putranjivaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. trop. Afr. 6(1): 677 (1912)

## Description

Plate 47(1-3); Figure 10(A, B)

Small tree, up to ±10 m in height, sometimes reaching ±20 m in height; trunk up to ±15 cm in diameter, frequently twisted; bark grey or brownish, smooth, lenticellate; branchlets cylindrical to slightly flattened, densely puberulent, with brownish hairs, glabrescent; buds hirsute. Stipules persistent, narrowly elliptic-ovate, (5–)7.5–16–(21) × 4–6 mm, hairy, with lateral protuberances and laciniate, (0.5–)2.5–4–(7.5) mm long. Leaf: petiole (2.2–)4–7–(8.5) mm, densely hairy; blade broadly oblong, (13–)18–21.5–(31.5) × (4.2–)5–7–(9.5) cm, papyraceous to subcoriaceous, with oblique base, acute to obtuse, with sides meeting the petiole at the same point or rarely separately, acuminate to acute at apex, with subentire margin, flat, sometimes slightly recurved, sometimes slightly undulate, dark green and shiny above, much paler green and densely puberulent below, glabrescent; venation densely reticulate, slightly impressed above, prominent below, midvein densely hairy, secondary veins 6 to 9 (to 11), ascending, looped and anastomosing well before the margin, impressed above and prominent below. Male inflorescence in the axils of leaves and below leaves on branches, with up to ±25 flowers; bracts broadly triangular to suborbicular, (0.9–)1.3–1.8 mm long, hairy. Male flower: pedicel 1.2–1.5(–4) mm, densely puberulent; sepals 4, imbricate, ovate to suborbicular, 2.8–3.2(–3.4) × (2.1–)2.5–3.2 mm, acute or obtuse and cucullate at apex, pale yellowish to brownish, densely puberulent on both faces, with golden yellowish to brownish hairs; stamens 5 to 10, in a whorl around the margin of the disk, filament 0.4–1.4 mm, anther 0.7–0.9 mm long; disk slightly convex, (0.9–)1.7–1.9(–2.3) mm in diameter, rugose, with undulate-lobate margin, protruding between the filaments, densely puberulent, with a tuft at centre; pistillode absent. Female inflorescence like the male, with up to ±12 flowers; bracts similar to those of males. Female flower: pedicel 1.7–2.5(–2.7) mm, densely puberulent; sepals 4, (3.1–)3.5–4.2(–4.8) × (3.8–)4.1–5.2(–6.4) mm, similar to those of males; disk slightly cupulate, (2.8–)3.5–4.7(–5.3) mm in diameter, with slightly undulate margin, puberulent; ovary subglobose, 2-locular, hairy; styles (0.4–)0.7–1.1(–1.3) mm, with 2 branches, partially fused, with flabellate stigma, (1.3–)1.5–1.6(–1.8) × 2.9–3.4(–3.6) mm, with erose distal margin. Fruit ellipsoid, sometimes globose, (10.5–)12–13(–14.5) × (8.2–)9.5–10 mm, brown to dark brown, with persistent sepals, densely hairy, velvety, with (1 or) 2 seeds; pedicel (1.1–)1.5–2.6(–5.3) mm, hairy. Seed (6.5–)8–9.5 mm long.

## Distribution

Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea (continental), Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Central African Republic; in Gabon: Estuaire, Haut-Ogooué, Ngounié, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo and Woleu-Ntem

Habitat: understory of evergreen forests, riverine forests, swamp forests, roadsides; in Gabon and elsewhere at 50–1000 m elevation.

## Source note

D. laciniata is a fairly hairy species generally collected as an understory tree, with laciniate stipules almost always present and generally densely hairy.
