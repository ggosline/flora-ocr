# *Alternanthera maritima* (Mart.) St-Hil.

**Family**: [[Amaranthaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Alternanthera]]

## Description

Robust prostrate coastal herb with opposite fleshy leaves and dense whitish heads. Flowers are subtended by persistent pointed bracts and have five unequal rigid tepals. Fruit dry, compressed and one-seeded.

## Distribution

Atlantic tropical shores, including Brazil and Gabon; Gabon material is coastal.

## Source

Cavaco, A. (1963). Amaranthacées. *Flore du Gabon* 7: 44–46.
