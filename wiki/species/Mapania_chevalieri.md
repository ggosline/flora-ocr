# *Mapania chevalieri* (Nelmes) Lye Figure 15a

**Genus**: [[Mapania]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Lidia 7 : 88 (2011)

## Synonyms

- Hypolytrum chevalieri Nelmes, Kew Bull. 1955 : 69 (1955).

## Description

Robust herb with a short thick rhizome 4–7 mm; central stem, solitary, 70–120 cm × 2–5 mm, triangular, green, scabrous below the inflorescence. Leaves numerous, mostly basal but some spaced along the stem, longer than the stem; sheath green; blade flat or slightly folded, strongly scabrous on the margin and main nerves, the largest 50 × 2.5 cm, gradually narrowed to an acute apex. Inflorescence in a terminal panicle, lax, about 15 × 15 cm, brown, with 5–10 main branches 0.5–5 cm, each subtending 3–6 secondary branches bearing sessile or pedunculate spikes; involucral bracts foliaceous, linear, often spreading, green, very scabrous on the margin and central nerve, apex acute, the lower one 25

a b

## Distribution

endemic to Gabon; very rare and found only in Estuaire and Ngounié (2 collections)

## Ecology

humid forests, often along rivers and watercourses; probably at less than 100 m altitude only. Taxonomic note: very close to M. afro-orientalis, but differs in its larger cylindrical spikes (9–18 mm in length versus 5–8 mm) and achenes entirely wrinkled. It is distinguished from M. polystachya by its narrower blades and non-spotted spicoid bracts. b: Nelmes (1955a). Bibliography
