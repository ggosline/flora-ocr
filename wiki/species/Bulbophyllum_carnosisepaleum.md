# *Bulbophyllum carnosisepaleum* J.J. Verm.

**Genus**: [[Bulbophyllum]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: BJBB 56 : 236 (1986)

## Description

– TYPE: Bruneel s.n., Congo (Kinshasa), holo-BR. – FAC 2: 363 (1992); FCAM 2: 467 (2001).

Pseudobulbs 0.9–1.5 × 0.4–1 cm, orbicular to ellipsoid, unifoliate. Leaf petiolate 4–8.5 × 0.8–1.6 cm, linear to linear-lanceolate, obtuse. Inflorescence rather dense, 4.8–7 cm, rachis glabrous, somewhat zigzag, 3–12-flowered. Flowers small, greenish, entirely or most often tinged with red-purple. Sepals acuminate to cuspidate, thick with fine abaxial papillae; dorsal sepal ovate to oblong-ovate; lateral sepals obliquely triangular. Petals linear to linear-lanceolate, subacute to obtuse, falcate or not, rather thin, glabrous. Lip glabrous, thick, erect, broadly oblong to oblong, with margins irregularly crenulate toward the rounded apex. – Pl. 94, p. 189.

## Distribution

Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroon, Gabon, Congo (Kinshasa), Uganda

## Ecology

epiphytic; lowland to montane forest; collected on thick branch of an Aucoumea klaineana

MATERIAL EXAMINED (Map 35):

Arends et al. 329, Waka, forest exploitation trail, near the locality "la Falaise", small valley, in closed forest on fairly steep slope, 1° 18' S, 10° 57' E (fl., Sept.), WAG!.
