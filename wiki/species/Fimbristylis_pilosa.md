# *Fimbristylis pilosa* (Poir.) Vahl Planche 96

**Genus**: [[Fimbristylis]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Enum. pl. 2 : 290 (1805)

## Description

Perennial herb, tufted, with a short woody rhizome or more rarely, annual herb with a weak root system; stems 25–60 cm × 0.5–1 mm, compressed above, angular below, very scabrous at least below the umbel, with rounded ribs, base covered by remains of old leaves. Leaves numerous; sheath hairy, brownish near the throat; ligule formed of a row of close hairs; blade 15–20 cm × 1–3 mm, flat or canaliculate, densely hairy especially along the margin. Inflorescence in simple or compound umbel with 3–12 spikelets; main involucral bract foliaceous, 0.5–4 cm long, hairy, shorter than the umbel. Spikelet ovoid, 5–12 × 3–4 mm, with numerous glumes arranged in spiral; glumes 2.5–3 × 3 mm, reddish-brown with the margin and central nerve paler, glabrous or with a few scattered hairs, with shortly ciliate margin, central nerve terminating at the obtuse apex or sometimes slightly exceeding it. Flower: style very compressed, ciliate, with 2 branches. Achene approximately obovate, 1.2–1.7 × 1–1.4 mm, biconvex, brown, surface with 18–25 longitudinal striae on each side, but the young achenes much paler and almost smooth.

## Distribution

widely distributed in the tropical regions of West Africa, reaching Gabon, the Democratic Republic of the Congo and East Africa; rare in Gabon, found only in Estuaire and Ogooué-Maritime (2 collections)

## Ecology

in humid depressions of grasslands, roadsides, at the edge of swamps; in Gabon, towards sea level, but up to 1140 m in Uganda. Taxonomic note: very close to F. dichotoma, but differs by its wider spikelets and its achenes with longitudinal striae much more numerous, but less prominent. B: Goetghebeur & Coudijzer (1984a), Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al. (2010), Hooper & Napper (1972)
