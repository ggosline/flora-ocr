# *Tricalysia anomala* E. A. Bruce

**Genus**: [[Tricalysia]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Protologue**: E. A. Bruce, Kew Bull.: 485 (1936)

## Description

Shrub ± sarmentose. Internodes velutinous hirsute. Stipules hirsute, attenuate over 6–8 mm, abruptly enlarged at the base. Petiole 1–2 mm, hirsute. Lamina pilose above and below, oblong 4–15 × 1.8–6 cm, with rounded to subcordate base, with lanceolate or acuminate apex often terminated by a very small point. Midrib pubescent in a groove on the upper face. (5) 6–10 pairs of secondary nerves hirsute on the lower face.

Flowers white axillary in brief cymules of (1) 3-flowers. Peduncle 1–2 mm; pedicels 1–2 mm. Ovary clothed with yellow hairs (when dry). Calyx hirsute tubular over 2–3 mm, with 6–8 filiform lobes 6–10 mm long. Corolla with pubescent or nearly glabrous tube 8–12 mm long, with 6–8 narrowly acute lobes 10–13 × 2 mm, glabrous only in the distal region of the upper face and furnished with white hairs toward the throat. Anther strongly velutinous with soft hairs, very narrow, acuminate, 8–9 mm long, inserted toward the lower third; filament glabrous reaching 3–4 mm. The pollen is tricolpate sublongiaxial, subcircular in polar view; P = 24 μ, E = 21 μ; ornamentation with meshes of approximately 1 μ; endexine = ectexine = 0.8 μ. Style 15 mm long, with glabrous column, 2-lobed, pubescent on the external side, 4–5 mm long. Disk small, 1 mm diam., surrounded by a circular groove below the level of corolla insertion, the receptacle thus forming a small rim. Ovary 2-locular, each with a suborbicular placenta, subemar­ginate at the apex, with central insertion, with 5–7 ovules arranged in a U.

Fruit grey-green, globose 15–19 mm in diameter, ± pubescent, with ± persistent sepals with wall approximately 3 mm thick, 2-locular separated by a thin septum, cavernous, with yellowish inner surface. 12 seeds in a fruit (Tisserant 2453), 7 × 4 × 2.5 mm, black, with thin non-fibrous testa, finely shagreened (under magnification); seed ends somewhat attenuate; hilum of remarkable form, arched, 5 mm long, 0.3 mm wide. Albumen abundant horny, non-oleaginous; small straight embryo 3 mm.

## Type

E. M. Bruce 800 (holotype K!, isotype BR!), Uluguru, Tanganyika

## Discussion

Despite the very distinctive appearance of this species, we have not found sufficient characters to isolate it in a new genus. The disk is very little different by the fact that it is the lower ends of the anthers that impress, in the young bud, the circular groove. The fruit is too clearly similar to that of T. Elliotii to find argument in it, especially in the absence of sufficient comparative material for the seed. There remains only the pubescence of the style which presents itself in an aberrant way for a Tricalysia.

Cameroon (Mildbraed 4200, HBG!); Central African Republic (Tisserant 1892). The specimen A. Chevalier 19279, from Côte d'Ivoire, is perhaps to be referred to this species.

Central African vernacular name: Mélakema (Lissongo of the Babingas) according to Tisserant 2453.
