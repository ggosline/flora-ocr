# *Liparis tridens* Kraenzl.

**Genus**: [[Liparis]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 28 : 162 (1900)

## Description

Pseudobulbs < 1.3 × 0.7 cm, ovoid. Leaves shortly petiolate, 3–7.5 × 0.9–1.4 cm, oblong-lanceolate, acute to obtuse, margins undulate. Inflorescence 5–6.5 (10) cm, 9–15 (23)-flowered. Flowers yellowish-green. Dorsal sepal acute to obtuse, oblong-ovate; lateral sepals rhombic or obliquely oblong-ovate. Petals linear, acute or obtuse. Labellum rather delicate, fragile, glabrous, distinctly trilobed at the basal 1/3; median lobe ovate-lanceolate, apiculate, margins incurved; lateral lobes obliquely ovate-lanceolate or rhombic, acute to subacute. - Pl. 61, p. 127.

## Distribution

Côte d'Ivoire, Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Equatorial Guinea, Fernando Po, Uganda, Tanzania, Malawi

## Ecology

alt. 20–1800 m. Epiphytic on trees and tree ferns in humid places, Brachystegia, Uapaca and Anogeissus woodland, collected on mossy trunk in swamp forest 2–3 m or more above ground
