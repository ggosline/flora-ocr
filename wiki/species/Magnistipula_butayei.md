# *Magnistipula butayei* De Wildeman

**Genus**: [[Magnistipula]]
**Family**: [[Scytopetalaceae]]
**Modern family**: [[Lecythidaceae]] subfam. Scytopetaloideae — *Scytopetalaceae* as circumscribed here is superseded; see [[Scytopetalaceae]]
**Protologue**: Ann. Mus. Congo Bot. ser. 5, 2, Fl. Bas et Moyen Congo : 255 (1908). Nota : ENGLER (Pflanzenw. Afr. 3 (1) : 308, 1915) et CHEVALIER (Bull. Mus. Nat. Hist. Nat. Paris, ser. 2, 3 : 194, 1931) ont omis de citer cette espèce pour le genre Magnistipula

## Description

Note: ENGLER (Pflanzenw. Afr. 3 (1) : 308, 1915) and CHEVALIER (Bull. Mus. Nat. Hist. Nat. Paris, ser. 2, 3 : 194, 1931) omitted to cite this species for the genus Magnistipula.

Under this name, and the synonymous one of Hirtella butayei (De Wild.) Brenan, Trop. Woods 86 : 4 (1946), various "forms" are grouped together in the literature at the present time, distributed in Côte d'Ivoire, Gabon, Congo, Angola, Zaïre, Central African Republic, Tanzania, Malawi and Zambia; thus according to BRENAN, l.c., HAUMAN (BJBB 21 : 178, 1951 and FCB 2 : 41, 1952), TISSERANT & SILLANS (Not. System. 15 (2) : 211, 1956), KEAY (FWTA, ed. 2, 1 (2) : 430, 1958), AUBRÉVILLE (FFCI, ed. 2, 1 : 186, 1959), GRAHAM (FTEA, Rosacea : 58, 1960), MENDES (Consp. Fl. Angol. 4 : 18, 1970), WHITE (BJBB 46 : 281, 1976), all these "forms" having been grouped by WHITE into 8 subspecies, taking into account chorological, ecological and morphological criteria (the latter especially vegetative, the floral elements providing scarcely any reliable data). The presence of a single one of these subspecies, known from Côte d'Ivoire and Congo, is now confirmed in Cameroon and Gabon. This subspecies (sargosii) is therefore described below, the 7 other subspecies concerning other territories: bangweolensis (Tanzania, Malawi, Zambia) butayei (Zaïre, Angola), greenwayi (Tanzania, montana (Zaïre), tisserantii (Central African Republic), transitoria (Zaïre, Burundi, Tanzania, Zambia), youngii (Zaïre, Angola).
