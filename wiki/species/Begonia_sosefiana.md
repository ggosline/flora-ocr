# *Begonia sosefiana* J.J. de Wilde & Valk. Planche 14

**Genus**: [[Begonia]]
**Family**: [[Begoniaceae]]
**Protologue**: Blumea 50 : 467 (2005)

## Description

Plant reaching approximately 3 cm in height. Leaf not peltate; petiole 1–2.5 mm, without long hairs; blade elliptic to elliptic-obovate, 7–20 × 3.5–6 mm, apex acute to obtuse, margin entire, toward the apex with a few obtuse teeth, upper surface glabrous. Inflorescence: peduncle 1.5–2.5 cm, with (0–)1–2 ♂ flowers and (0–)1 ♀ flowers with yellow tepals; bracts 0.5–1.5 mm in length. ♂ Flower: tepals approximately 5 × 5–6 mm; androecium with 5–8 stamens. ♀ Flower: pedicel elongating in fruit to 4 mm; tepals 5 × 5 mm; ovary ellipsoid-ovoid to fusiform, 3–4 × 1–2.5 mm, without long hairs, not winged, sometimes 3–4-ribbed; styles 4. Fruit ellipsoid to ellipsoid-ovoid, approximately 4 × 2–2.5 mm.

## Distribution

endemic to Gabon, known from a single specimen from Mount Igoumbi in the Doudou Mountains (Ogooué-Maritime)
