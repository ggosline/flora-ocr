# *Ipomoea imperati* (Vahl) Griseb. Planche 15

**Genus**: [[Ipomoea]]
**Family**: [[Convolvulaceae]]
**Protologue**: Cat. pl. Cub. : 203 (1866)

## Synonyms

- Convolvulus imperati Vahl, Symb. bot. 1 : 17 (1790).
- Ipomoea stolonifera (Cirillo) J.F.Gmel. in L. Syst. nat. ed. 13, 2 : 345 (1791).

## Description

Perennial herb; rhizomes creeping, stoloniferous, glabrous, cylindrical, rooting at the nodes; stems erect, up to approximately 20 cm in length. Leaf: petiole channelled above, 0.5–3 cm, glabrous or sometimes with a few apical hairs above; blade highly variable in shape and dimensions, linear or oblong to ovate, 3–10 × 0.5–3.5 cm, rounded to slightly cuneate at the base, obtuse-emarginate at the apex, with entire to undulate margin; pennate venation, with 5–10 pairs of lateral veins, glabrous. Inflorescence most often uniflorous, rarely with more than one flower, glabrous; the peduncle and pedicel combined 2–7 cm long; bracteoles narrowly triangular, 2–4 mm in length. Flower: sepals subequal, elliptic, 8–10 mm in length, glabrous, mucronate; corolla infundibuliform, 3.5–4.5 cm in length, white to yellow; stamens with filaments pubescent at the base; pistil included, 12–15 mm in length, glabrous. Fruit: subglobose, 10–12 mm in length, 4-valved. Seeds 1–4, ± 8 mm in length, tomentose with undulate and brownish hairs.

## Distribution

Tropical Africa and Mediterranean countries, tropical Asia and America; in Gabon, present on maritime sands of Estuaire, Ogooué-Maritime and Nyanga
