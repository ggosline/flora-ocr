# *Pleiocarpa robusta* O.Lachenaud Planche 92

**Genus**: [[Pleiocarpa]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Candollea 77 : 39 (2022)

## Description

Shrub 0–3 m tall with white latex. Leaf glabrous; petiole 0.7–1.7 cm; blade elliptic, 9–16 × 5–9 cm, acute at base abruptly caudate at apex for ± 0.5 cm, coriaceous; secondary nerves 6 to 14 pairs well marked and visible, moderately ascending, bullate on upper face and strongly raised below, anastomosing at < 1 mm from the margin, tertiary venation forming a transversely stretched, rather dense and prominent network above on the dry state, more lax and less visible below. Inflorescence terminal or axillary in glomerule with 3–5 flowers bracteate, 3–4 × 2–3 mm. Fragrant flower; pedicel absent or reaching 1 mm; calyx with lobes 1.5–1.8 × 1.3 mm; corolla white, tube 15–16 mm long, 2–2.5 mm wide at mid-height slightly reflexed at base, 2.5 mm wide at apex, glabrous on the outside; lobes narrowly elliptic, 5–6 × 2–3 mm, laterally auriculate at base, rounded at apex, glabrous on both faces; stamens inserted at 18–20 mm below the throat with their apex reaching almost the anthers, anther narrowly ovoid, 1–1.2 × 0.4–0.5 mm; ovary with 5 uni-ovulate carpels; style ± 12 mm, reaching just below the base of the stamens. Ripe fruit orange, with 5 free mericarps, obovoid to ellipsoid, 12–15 × 8–11 mm, acute at apex, glabrous, densely covered with dome-shaped warts, sometimes the warts irregular and larger, 1 seed per mericarp. Seed ± 11 × 6.5 mm. Distribution endemic to Gabon, limited to the Moyen-Ogooué valley or found between Ndjolé and the east of Lastoursville, Moyen-Ogooué; Ogooué, Ivindo & Ogooué-Lolo. The species seems to be rare in the west of its range but relatively frequent in the east.

## Ecology

Evergreen forest, on drained soils; at 250–500 m altitude. B: Jongkind & Lachenaud (2022). BIBLIOGRAPHY
