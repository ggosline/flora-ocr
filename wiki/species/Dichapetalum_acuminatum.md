## Description

PELLEGRIN, Fl. Mayombé 1, Mém. Soc. Linn. Norm. 26 (2) : 55 (1924); HAUMAN, FZRB 7 : 309, excl. Gillet 2154 (1958). BRETELER, MLW 73 (13) : 4, 18, 28, 39-43, 55 (1973); l.c. 78 (10) : 16 (1978); l.c. 81 (10) : 82 (1981); l.c. 82 (8) : 43 (1982); AUWP 86 (3) : 2, 13, 14 (1986). TYPE : Zaïre, Mombonge, Thonner 150 (lecto-, BR ; iso-, BM, K, W, WAG).

- D. acuminatum var. laurentii DE WILD., l. c.: 222 (1911). TYPE: Zaïre, Kasa, Mogandjo, Laurent 1500 (holo-, BR.; iso-, Z).

- D. claessensii DE WILD., BJBB 3: 269 (1911); HAUMAN, FZRB 7: 310 (1958). TYPE: Zaire, Kasa, Bena Dibele, Claessens 235 (holo-, BR).

- D. tessmannii ENGL., l.c. : 569 (1912). TYPE : Equatorial Guinea, Campo, Akonango, Tessmann 894 (lecto-, K ; isolecto-, BM, photo-, WAG).

Shrub or climbing shrub, but ordinarily a liane reaching the top of the forest canopy, stem diameter ≤ 6 cm. Older branches with narrow pith, mostly glabrous or glabrescent with scattered and inconspicuous lenticels. Young branches at most vaguely lenticellate, glabrous, tomentose-puberulent, then glabrescent or bearing long hispid hairs that may be mixed with a rather tomentose indumentum.

Stipules usually very early caducous, narrowly ovate to subulate, (1.5) 2-4 (5) mm long, glabrous or glabrescent.

Leaves. Petiole hemicylindric generally canaliculate above, (1) 2-5 (7) mm long, often with white crisp or rather hispid hairs above, sometimes entirely covered with white hairs; blade narrow and elliptic to obovate, base cuneate or sometimes rounded, apex with progressive to abrupt acumen or even caudate, (4) 6-10 (14) × (1) 2-4 (5) cm, (2) 3-4 (5) times longer than wide; acumen straight or laterally incurved, 0.5-2.5 cm long, emarginate to rounded or even acute at the tip; primary vein prominent above and secondary veins, 4-7 pairs, more prominent below; leaves usually glabrous or early glabrescent; however specimens from the western part of the range, and thus those from Gabon, often bear white hispid or short hairs (either a mixture of both), on the upper surface of the primary vein; lower surface with white hispid hairs often present on the lateral veins (some hairs sometimes also between the veins); glands absent above, small, scattered and inconspicuous below.

Inflorescence in a lax and pedunculate cyme, (3) 15-50 (60)-flowered, branched ≤ 6 times, 1.5-5 cm long, puberulent-tomentose; peduncle 0.5-2.5 cm long; bracts and bracteoles deltoid-ovate to ovate-oblong 0.5-2 mm. Pedicels puberulent-tomentose, lower portion 2-8 (10) mm long, upper portion 0.5-1 (1.5) mm.

Flowers. Sepals reflexed, very shortly united at the base, ovate-elliptic to obovate-elliptic, 2-3 (4) × 1-1.5 mm, apex rounded to acute; external face covered with an appressed puberulent tomentum (the part covered in the bud is villous); internal pubescence similar but only in the upper region most often. Petals erect or spreading or bent below the lobes (sometimes reflexed), spatulate, (2.5) 3.5-4.5 (5) mm long, cleft over (0.5) 1-≤1.5 mm, glabrous (sometimes with rare hairs with external villous indumentum below the lobes), lobes concave to apex rounded or acute. Stamens erect (3.5) 4.5-7 mm long, glabrous; anthers globular to reniform, approximately 0.5 mm long. Staminodes rather flattened subquadrate, free or united to each other, ≥ 0.5 × 0.5 mm, truncate-emarginate or lobulate at the apex, glabrous or bearing some woolly hairs on the inner side. Pistil (3) 4-5 (7) mm long; ovary 3-locular, subspherical, with dense woolly hairs; ovules with or without small fimbrillate obturator; style glabrous or lower part woolly; apex terminated by 3 lobes 1 mm long, but in general much shorter.

Fruits ovo-ellipsoid to broadly ellipsoid or subspherical, 2-3 cm × ≈ (1.5-3 × 1-2 cm), 1-3-seeded; exo- and mesocarp 2-3 mm thick, dehiscent together from the apex in 1-3 slits; external face covered by a dense and caducous pale brown indumentum of aciculate barbed and irritating hairs ≤ 1 mm long; outer layer of endocarp bony, rugose on surface; inner layer fibrous finely striated. Seeds subellipsoid, 14-17 × 10-12 mm; testa brown, glabrous, strongly nerved, fibrous. - Pl. 4, p. 37.
