# *Salacia diplasia* Hallé, sp. nov.

**Genus**: [[Salacia]]
**Family**: [[Celastraceae]]

## Description

S. dimidiae Hallé valde affinis. Shrub erect glabrous, juvenile branches 4-alate. Leaves opposite sessile or subsessile with thickened petiole, cordate at base, acuminate, 19-32 × 7-13 cm, 9-15 nerved on both sides. Inflorescences axillary sessile, conglomerate or ramulous pluriflorous or multiflorous. Buds subglobose; pedicel slender 10-20 mm long. Flower pallid 10-17 mm in diam., sepals ± unequal suborbicular refracted; disk red, cupuliform in bud, then expanded. Stamens distant, anthers broad. Ovary lageniform, stigma capitate, ovules 4-6 in each locule. Fruit elongate acute. Inhabits Gabon.

## Type

Hallé 2204, Gabon (holo-, P !)

## Discussion

Etymology of the name: diplasia means double in size while dimidia (see p. 102) is half.

Shrub erect 1-2 m in height, devoid of rubber, glabrous, with juvenile leaves rosé-brown in vivo; young stems olivaceous, sublisse, 4-alate. Cylindrical wood 15 mm in diameter with regular and very fine concentric striations; bark grayish with whitish longitudinal cracks. Leaves opposite. Petiole swollen, about 1 cm, adherent along its entire length to the distinctly cordate base of the blade which is thus sessile. Blade olive discolorous when dry, elliptic to obovate oblong, 19-32 × 7-13 cm, subdenticulate to entire, acuminate; midvein prominent and relief above; 9-15 pairs of secondary nerves, the upper ones often the only ones regularly arched from the base; intersecondary nervilles apparent and almost transversely subparallel.

Inflorescences reduced, axillary with 2-6(-8) flowers on a bracteolate glomerule sometimes slightly tiered but sessile. Buds subglobose 3 × 4 mm. Pedicel 4-7 mm, slender, articulate slightly above its base; the persistent part exceeds the small acute bracts by about double. Flower 9-15 mm in diameter; calyx 7-8 mm in diameter with unequal sepals 3-4 mm wide, suborbicular, thin, veined and salmon-colored. Petals pale salmon to orange-rose, orbicular slightly attenuate at base, 4-5 × 4-5 mm, with flabellate venation. Disk reddish in flared cupule with irregularly crenate margin, 2.8-4 mm in diameter and 0.5 mm in height. Stamens reaching 2-3 mm, inserted in spaced fashion in small pits of a slightly distinct perigynous furrow; anther 1.2-1.4 mm wide, with 2 locules in very open chevron and apically confluent. Pistil 1-2.4 mm high, lageniform, with superior locules and capitate stigma. 2 superposed ovules per locule, rather pendulous.

Fruit elongate (4)6-8 × (1.2)5-2 cm, smooth orange or scarlet red at the acute attenuate extremities, more or less constricted between the seeds or seed groups of which the number reaches 6.
