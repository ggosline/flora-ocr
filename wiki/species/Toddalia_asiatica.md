# *Toddalia asiatica* (L.) Lamk.

**Family**: [[Rutaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Toddalia]]

## Description

Scrambling shrub or liana whose young branches bear small downward-curved spines. Leaves trifoliolate; petiole 1–3 cm and sessile leaflets 2–7 × 1–3 cm, elliptic, obovate or oblanceolate, slightly crenate. Inflorescences are panicles of fascicles, often densely rusty-pubescent. Flowers unisexual, 5-merous, corolla c. 2 mm. Red globose drupes c. 8 mm diameter have a gland-rich fleshy mesocarp and 5–7 one-seeded locules.

## Uses

The macerated bark was reported as a remedy for gonorrhoea and in Fang ritual washing; the spiny liana was also regarded as protective against witchcraft.
