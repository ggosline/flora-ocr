# *Strophanthus congoensis* Franch. Planche 101 ; Figure 11(G)

**Genus**: [[Strophanthus]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: J. Bot. (Morot) 7 : 302, 324 (1893)

## Description

J. Bot. (Morot) 7 : 302, 324 (1893). Liane reaching 30 m in height, deciduous; white latex; branches glabrous. Leaf: petiole and blade elliptic of 0.5–2 cm, rounded to cuneate at base, obtuse to acuminate at apex, glabrous; secondary nerves 3 to 5(6) pairs, curved, forming an angle of 40–60° with the median nerve. Inflorescence rather condensed with 3–8 flowers, glabrous in all its parts; bracts 1.5–5 × 1–1.5 mm. Flower: pedicel 2–15 mm; calyx glabrous, erect, with 5 petals equal or nearly so, 0.5–1 × 0.5–1 mm, acute at apex, glabrous; corolla with tube 11–19 mm long, white, becoming whitish-reddish, widening at 20–33% of its length into an upper cupuliform part, 5–12 mm wide at the throat, glabrous on the outside, puberulent on the inside, crown with subulate lobes, 3–7.5 mm long, fleshy, violaceous, with corolla lobes oval, 3–8 × 3–7 mm, white becoming whitish-reddish, abruptly constricted into yellow tails 14–42 × 1 mm, glabrous on both surfaces or puberulent on the inside near the base; stamens included, anther 4.2–5.2 mm long; style 4.3–7 mm. Fruit: follicles divergent at 0.5–2 cm, narrowed to an obtuse apex, exocarp thick and hard, slightly sulcate, glabrous, densely lenticellate. Seed 11–17 × 4 mm, pubescent; beak glabrous for 2–9 mm, bearing a coma for 15–40 mm of its length, erect or spreading, 30–80 mm long. Distribution: Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Democratic Republic of the Congo, in Gabon, Nyanga, Ogooué, Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo and Ogooué-Maritime. Ecology: primary and secondary forest in Gabon, up to 730 m altitude, elsewhere up to 800 m. B: Beentje (1982, 2021). BIBLIOGRAPHY
