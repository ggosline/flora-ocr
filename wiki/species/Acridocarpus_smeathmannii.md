# *Acridocarpus smeathmannii* (De Candolle) Guillemin & Perrottet

**Genus**: [[Acridocarpus]]
**Family**: [[Malpighiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. Senegamb. Tent. 1 : 124 (1831)

## Description

(var. α). — NIEDENZU, Pflanzenr., Malpighiaceae : 274 (1928), excl. syn. A. chevalieri SPRAGUE et var. staudtii ENGL. — KEAY, FWTA ed. 2, 1 : 352 (1958). — WILCZEK, Fl. Congo belge 7 : 232 (1958). — LAUNERT, FTEA : 5 (1968).

— Heteropteris smeathmannii DC., Prodr. 1 : 592 (1824).

— Anomalopteris spicata G. Don, Gen. Hist. 1 : 647 (1831).

— Acridocarpus goossensii De Wild., Plant. Bequaert. 1:592 (1924).

Shrubs 1 m tall or lianes 3–7 m tall, branchlets clothed with short hairs; stem striate, nearly glabrous, lenticellate. Leaves penninerved; stipules absent; petiole canaliculate, glabrous, 3–7 mm; lamina glabrous, elliptic, oblong to obovate, bearing 2–4 glands at the base, more often truncate at the base, abruptly acuminate at the apex, 5–12 (–17) × 2–7 cm; secondary nerves anastomosing, always more or less arched, prominent on the lower surface only.

Racemes pubescent, terminal, sometimes grouped in panicles; bracts erect, pubescent, triangular-acute, 1–1.5 mm long; bracteoles similar but smaller, glandular. Pedicels 9–20 mm, pubescent. Calyx with 2–3 glands, sepals pubescent, rounded at the apex, 2–3 mm long. Petals yellow, ⊥ unguiculate, with limb crenate, 9–14 × 10 mm. Stamens 5–8 mm long; anthers acute, slightly hairy at the apex. Ovary pubescent, 2–3 mm long; styles curved, 8–12 mm long, glabrous.

Fruits red, with 2 (–3) samaras, 3–5 cm long. PL. 4, 1–3, p. 21.
