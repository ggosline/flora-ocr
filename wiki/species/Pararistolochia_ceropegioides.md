# *Pararistolochia ceropegioides* (S.Moore) Hutch. & Dalziel Planche 2

**Genus**: [[Pararistolochia]]
**Family**: [[Aristolochiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Kew. Bull. 1928 : 24 (1928)

## Description

Slender liane; stem 3–5 mm in diameter. Leaf: petiole slender, 20–25 mm; lamina ovate to ovate-elliptic, 5.5–11 × 2–6 cm, base obtuse to slightly cordate, apex acute to slightly acuminate. Inflorescence: rachis 2–3 cm, with 3–5 flowers. Flower: pedicel (without ovary) (5–)8–15 mm; perianth pubérulent within, cream white to orange-brown, utricle 0.5–1.2(–2) cm long, brownish-green, with 2 circular or slightly ovate lateral depressions, tube 2–3.5 cm long, brown, lobes 3, equal, spreading, triangular but caudate, 0.5–3 cm long, orange to golden-yellow but reddish-brown acumen; gynosteme 3–5 mm long, with 6 brown stamens and 6 stigmatic lobes; ovary 3–8 mm long. Fruit short, 4–12.5 × 2.5–3 cm, brown, longitudinally and transversely ribbed, stellate in section. Seed 3 mm long, brown.

## Distribution

Cameroon and Gabon; in Gabon widely distributed but rather rare

## Ecology

evergreen or semi-deciduous lowland to submontane forest; in Gabon, at 0–700 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1100 m. B: Hutchinson & Dalziel (1928), Poncy (1978). Bibliography
