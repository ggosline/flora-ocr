# *Cyperus odoratus* L. Planche 50

**Genus**: [[Cyperus]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Sp. pl. 1 : 46 (1753)

## Synonyms

- Torulinium odoratum (L.) Hooper, Kew Bull. 26 : 579 (1972).

## Description

Robust, perennial herb, sometimes short-lived, of highly variable size; stems 50–100 × 0.6–1.2 cm, swollen at the base, trigonous, scabrous particularly below the inflorescence. Leaf: sheath red-violet; blade 10–100 cm × 4–20 mm, V- or W-shaped in section, distinctly scabrous on the margin and on the central nerve at least near the apex. Inflorescence in a loose compound anthela, 10–30 cm wide, composed of one to a few sessile spikes and of 2–8 pedunculate spikes or groups of 2–5 spikes, on peduncles 2–25 cm long; involucral bracts numerous, distinctly exceeding the anthela and 1–2 cm wide; spikes cylindric, 1–2.5 cm long with the numerous spikelets spreading. Spikelet linear-cylindric, 10–23 × 0.8–1 mm, with 6–15 flowers, with a corky rachis disarticulating above each glume at maturity; glumes elliptic, 2–3.5 mm long, brown to yellowish-brown, central nerve trinerved and green with apex rounded or with a mucro 0.3 mm long. Flower: style with 3 stigmas. Achene narrowly ellipsoid, 1.2–1.8 × 0.4–0.7 mm, reddish-brown to blackish, finely papillose.

## Distribution

West coast of Africa, tropical America and the Pacific; very rare in Gabon or poorly known, found only in Ogooué-Maritime, near PortGentil (1 collection)

## Ecology

Marshes of coastal dunes, lagoon margins; at low altitude. Taxonomic note: recognized by its robust habit, large inflorescence and cylindric spikelets disarticulating at the base of the glumes. B: Hooper & Napper (1972). Bibliography CYPERUS L. subgenus KYLLINGA (Rottb.) Valck.Sur. Cyperus: 42 (1898). Kyllinga Rottb., Descr. icon. rar. pl.: 12, t. 4 (1773). Perennial herbs or more rarely annuals; stems 5–80 cm long, triangular. Leaves usually with well-developed blade. Anatomy of stems and leaves of chlorocyperoid type (that is to say with C4-type anatomy).
