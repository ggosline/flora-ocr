**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Mém. Inst. Franç. Afrique Noire 35 : 286, pl. 18.4 (1953)

Leaf glabrous: petiole 4–15 mm; lamina generally elliptic to narrowly elliptic, sometimes ovate or oblong, 7.5–15 × 1.5–5 cm, cuneate or sometimes rounded at the base, acuminate at the apex; secondary nerves 10 to 25 pairs, ± straight, forming an angle of 60–90° with the midvein; tertiary venation reticulate. Inflorescence 9–25 × 4–15 cm, with 4 to 8 cymes; peduncle 4–14.5 cm, pubescent; bracts persistent; sepals reaching 3 mm in length, densely pubescent. Flower: pedicel ± 2.5 mm; calyx with lobes 0.6–3.5 mm long, densely pubescent on the outside, sometimes bearing colleters at the base and alternating with them; corolla white, sometimes tinged with pink, 19–25 mm long in bud and forming an oblong head representing 20–40% of its length, glabrous or slightly pubescent on the outside, with tube slender, not thickened above the anthers, 17–44 mm long, contracted at the base and widened at the level of the anthers, over 2 mm fairly abruptly widened at the throat, internally with a pilose band between the anthers and extending upward, throat glabrous, with narrowly elliptic to oblong lobes representing 0.2–0.4 of the tube length, 1.5–4 times as long as wide, 3–7 × 1–2 mm, glabrous on both faces; stamens inserted 1–3 mm from the base of the tube, anther 1.6–2.3 mm long; pistil 4.2–6.5 mm long, with densely pubescent ovary; style glabrous. Fruit subglobose, 2–5 cm in diameter, yellow to red, with 2 to 10 seeds. Seed ellipsoid, 8–14 mm long.

## Distribution

from Guinea to Burundi and Angola; in Gabon: Estuaire, Haut-Ogooué, Nyanga, Ogooué, Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem. Ecology: costal thicket, woodland and forest edge in Gabon, up to 650 m elevation; elsewhere up to 1200 m. B: Pichon (1953), Vonk et al. (1994). BIBLIOGRAPHY
