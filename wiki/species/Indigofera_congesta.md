# *Indigofera congesta* Welw. ex Baker

**Genus**: [[Indigofera]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]

## Description

in Oliv., Fl. trop. Afr. 2 : 70 (1871). Perennial herb, 0.15–1 m tall; stems numerous and strongly branched, puberulent. Stipules 4–8 mm long. Leaf: petiole 4–15 mm; stipels absent; leaflets 3 to 7, opposite, with blade narrowly elliptic to elliptic, 6–20 × 2–7 mm, mucronate, strigose or puberulent on both surfaces. Inflorescence in small dense capitules, axillary or sometimes terminal, subsessile; bracts 3-foliolate, attenuate at apex, distinctly hairy like the calyx lobes, white or brown. Flower: pedicel up to 2 mm; calyx 3.5–5 mm long, tube almost absent, lobes narrow, rigid; corolla orange or red, ± equalling the calyx, standard sessile, mucronate, the back puberulent. Pod ovoid, 2–4.5 mm long, with 1(–2) seeds. Seed subcircular, laterally flattened, ± 1 mm in diameter, olive-green to brown.

## Distribution

West tropical Africa, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Angola, Uganda and Tanzania; in Gabon, known from the Moyen-Ogooué and Nyanga

## Ecology

savannas, often in rocky places; in Gabon at low altitude, elsewhere up to 1700 m. b : Cronquist (1954), Gillett (1971a), Pellegrin (1949). Bibliography
