# *Margaritaria discoidea* (Baill.) G.L.Webster Planche 16

**Genus**: [[Margaritaria]]
**Family**: [[Phyllanthaceae]]
**Protologue**: J. Arnold Arbor. 60 : 433 (1979)

## Synonyms

- Phyllanthus discoideus (Baill.) Müll.Arg., Linnaea 32 : 51 (1863).
- Cicca discoidea Baill., Adansonia 1 : 85 (1860).

## Description

Tree up to 35 m tall and 60 cm diameter; branches mostly lenticellate, glabrous. Stipules early caducous, narrowly triangular, (2–)5–13 × (1–)2–3 mm, glabrous, scars distinct. Leaf: petiole canaliculate above, (4–)5–8(–10) mm, glabrous; lamina elliptic or sometimes oblong to obovate, (4–)7–10(–16) × (2–)3–4(–5.5) cm, cuneate to rounded or sometimes obtuse at the base, acute to briefly acuminate and sometimes mucronate at the apex, glabrous; lateral veins (9–)10–14 pairs, rather fine. Male flower: pedicel 3–4 mm, glabrous; sepals ± elliptic, 1–1.5 mm long, glabrous; stamens free, ± 1.5 mm long; disk ± flat, ± lobed. Female flower: pedicel 1–1.5 mm; pistil 1–1.5 mm long; styles ± 1 mm. Fruit (2–)3–4-lobed, 7–10 mm diameter, glabrous. Seeds ± 2 per locule, crowded, (2.5–)4–5 × (2–)3–4 mm, smooth, bluish.

## Distribution

Common species and well distributed in tropical Africa as in Gabon

## Ecology

Secondary forest, forest regrowth, fallow; in Gabon, up to 560 m altitude

D. Female flower. – E. Infructescence. – F. Fruit. – G. Fruit showing the persistent calyx. – H. Fruit showing the styles. (A, B, D: Breteler 6843; C: Louis 211; E – H: Bissiengou et al. 480). Drawing by H. de Vries, NCB Naturalis (section NHN) ©.

Uses: The wood is used as timber for the construction of houses and boats, but also used as firewood and for charcoal production. Medicinal use is limited. A decoction of bark would bring about a more or less prolonged cessation of menstruation. In poultices, the pulp of young leaves incorporated into palm oil would cause the abortion of boils and abscesses.

## Vernacular names

bushveld peacock-berry, common pheasant-berry (angl.).
