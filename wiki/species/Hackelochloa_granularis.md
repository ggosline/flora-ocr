# *Hackelochloa granularis* (L.) O. Kuntze. (f. p. 135).

**Genus**: [[Hackelochloa]]
**Family**: [[Gramineae]]

## Synonyms

- Manisuris granularis Sw.
- M. polystachya Beauv.
- Cenchrus granularis L.

## Description

Erect herb, glabrous or pubescent; hairs with tuberculate base. Leaf sheaths more or less compressed, hirsute at the junction with the blade. Ligule short, membranous and ciliate. Blade linear-lanceolate, cordate and amplexicaul at the base, 4 to 12 cm long and 8 to 15 mm wide, ciliate on the margins and more or less hirsute at the base. Central vein white and flattened on the upper surface. Racemes 1 to 3 cm long, glabrous, greenish-yellow, more or less distinctly pedunculate. Rachis articles (formed by the fusion of the article and pedicel) 1 to 1.5 mm long; the pedicel is marked by two longitudinal furrows. Sessile spikelet 2 to 3 mm long, including the conical callus. Lower glume with numerous fine veins, visible from the inside. Upper glume of the same length, with 3 veins. Lemmas of the 2 flowers obtuse, broadly oval. Pedicellate spikelet slightly longer or as long as the sessile; lower glume keeled, asymmetrical, smooth or slightly scabrous, with 5 to 7 well-marked green veins; upper glume naviculate, with scabrous keel, 5 to 7 veins. The flowers of the pedicellate spikelet are often reduced, or even suppressed. This species is most often a plant of fallow land or waste ground. It would perhaps present some fodder interest, but it is never very abundant.

## Specimens examined

Thollon : 792, Plaines de Lopé, Ogooué.
