# *Heterotis buettneriana* (Cogniaux ex Buettner) Jacques-Félix

**Genus**: [[Heterotis]]
**Family**: [[Melastomataceae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia, sér. 2, 20 (4) : 418 (1981); Fl. Cam. 24 : 40 (1983)

## Description

Adansonia, ser. 2, 20 (4) : 418 (1981); Fl. Cam. 24 : 40 (1983).

— Osbeckia buettneriana Cogn. ex Buett., Verh. Bot. Ver. Brandenb. 31 : 95 (1890).

Slender herb, spreading to 0.60 m, with erect flowering branches up to 0.30 m; branches 4-angled, strigillous to setulose, especially on the angles. Leaves membranaceous; petiole ± 1 cm, strigillous; blade oval, rounded, then wedge-shaped at the base of the petiole, subacute at the apex, ± 2 × 3 cm, rarely larger under the inflorescences and up to 3 × 5 cm, sometimes of variable size on the same specimens, setulose on both faces; 3-(5)-nerved, tertiary venation scarcely visible; margins entire.

Flowers terminal, solitary, or 2 to 3 in a cyme with short internodes; bracts poorly differentiated, or small and caducous; pedicel 1–2 mm; hypanthium campanulate, 4 × 6 mm, with mixed indumentum of simple bristles and slender, stellato-setulose emergences; the latter more numerous and longer toward the apex and up to 2 mm for the intersepalary ones; sepals triangular-oblong, 0.5 × 2–3 mm, abruptly caudate by a subapical filiform and stellate emergence. Petals pink, obovate, 0.6–1 cm long. Stamens subequal, anther 2.5 mm; pedoconnective very short, with a 2-lobed obtuse appendage; filament 3 mm. Ovary with convex apex, pubescent, with ± distinct collar; style 6 mm.

Fruit ellipsoid, 3–5 × 6–10 mm; emergences partly marcescent or caducous; capsule included, 5-ribbed. Seeds oblong, 0.8 mm long, surface smooth or foveolate, half-covered by the aril. — Pl. 8, p. 27.

## Type

Buettner 23, Congo (B, delet.); Thollon 444, Gabon (P!)

## Discussion

Homorstaminous species, whose coherent range occupies the western forest of Zaire, Gabon, and Cameroon.
