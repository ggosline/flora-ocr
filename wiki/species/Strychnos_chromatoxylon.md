# *Strychnos chromatoxylon* Leeuwenberg

**Genus**: [[Strychnos]]
**Family**: [[Loganiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Meded. Landbouwh. 69 (1) : 80, fig. 10, carte 9 (1969)

## Description

Large liana reaching a length of 30 m and a height of 20 m or more; stem 3–5 cm in diameter or more; bark pale gray, with some large lenticels, thick, outer bark orange-brown in section; wood pale yellow, with large islands of whitish or yellowish bark and becoming reddish when the wood is freshly cut; branchlets pale grayish-brown, lenticellate, cylindric; twigs glabrous, not lenticellate, sulcate when dry like the branchlets; tendrils in 1–3 pairs.

Leaves with glabrous petiole, 5–11 mm long, blade coriaceous or papyraceous, concolorous, elliptic, 5–15 × 2.5–6 cm, acuminate or, for shaded leaves, caudate at the apex, cuneate or rounded at the base, glabrous on both surfaces; 4–6 pairs of secondary nerves, the basal pair not longer than the others, as generally in the genus Strychnos; tertiary venation reticulate, prominent below.

Inflorescences axillary, contracted (?); peduncle, branches and pedicels finely pubescent. Flowers 5-merous. Sepals narrowly triangular, subulate, glabrous. Corolla glabrous outside. Stamens inserted at the throat of the corolla (?); anther cordate, bearded (?). Pistil glabrous (?).

Fruits large, depressed-globose, 6–6.5 × 6–7 cm, subcordate at the base, rugose and containing ± 5 seeds; wall very hard, 5 mm thick; pulp fibrous, abundant. Seeds flat, obliquely elliptic, ± 1.5–2 cm in diameter, scabrous; testa thick. Pl. 22, p. 131.

## Type

Breteler 2161, Cameroun

## Discussion

Cameroon, Central African Republic. Fairly dry forests with Sterculiaceae and Ulmaceae. Altitude 400–650 m.
