# *Tribulus terrestris* L.

**Family**: [[Zygophyllaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Tribulus]]

## Description

Diffuse prostrate herb, often woody at the base, whitish-villous to glabrescent. Leaves opposite but unequal within each pair, 2–6 cm, paripinnate with 3–10 pairs of opposite leaflets. Flowers solitary, axillary, yellow, with five sepals, five petals and ten stamens; the five antisepalous stamens bear basal gland-like appendages. Ovary hirsute, 5-lobed and 5-locular, each locule with 3–5 ovules. Fruit divides into five spiny mericarps, each usually with two large and two small spines and 2–5 one-seeded compartments.

## Distribution and notes

Pantropical and warm-temperate. Its occurrence in Gabon is doubtful: Thollon 663 is labelled “Acra 11/86” and may have been collected at Accra, Ghana, rather than at an unidentified Gabon–Congo locality.
