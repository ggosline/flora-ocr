# *Baissea campanulata* (K.Schum.) de Kruif Planche 18

**Genus**: [[Baissea]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Agric. Univ. Wageningen Papers 85(2) : 40 (1985)

## Description

Agric. Univ. Wageningen Papers 85(2) : 40 (1985). Liane reaching 35 m in height; branchlets pubescent to glabrous. Leaf: petiole 1–5 mm, pubescent or puberulent; limb obovate to oblong—sometimes as long as wide—0.5–1.7–8.1 cm, (sub)cordate to rounded at the base, acuminate or apiculate, rarely obtuse, at the apex, glabrous above, glabrous or puberulent below; secondary nerves 4 to 10 pairs, the 2nd to 5th pair incurved toward the margin, pubescent or glabrous above, pubescent or glabrescent and often reddish below, tertiary venation scalariform; domatia present. Inflorescence axillary and sometimes also terminal, condensed or the terminal rather lax, in panicles pauciflorous to pluriflorous with dense or reddish or reddish-brown pilosity; peduncle 1–1 mm. Flower fragrant; pedicel 1.3–4.5 mm; calyx with lobes 2–3.5 mm long, variously pubescent on the exterior, pubescent to almost glabrous on the interior, without or with small or fine colleters at the base; corolla generally yellow, sometimes red when aged, sometimes with spots or red stripes inside the tube, with funnel-shaped tube to linear, detail with domatia (× 2). – Baissea erythrosticta: 4. Calyx lobe, inner surface with colleters (× 8). (1: Breteler 2058; 2, 3: Le Testu 7913; 4: Bos 6377). Drawing by Yuen Fang Tan, Naturalis Biodiversity Center (©), reproduced from van Dilst (1995).

cylindrical, 2.2–5.5 mm long, with dense and clear pubescence on the exterior, except at the base, tufts of hairs on the interior; lobes ovate to ribbon-shaped—sometimes as long as the tube—sometimes as long as wide—0.5–1.7 ± mm, sometimes auriculate at the base, partially pubescent on the exterior, glabrous on the interior, except at the apex; stamens inserted near the base of the tube, included, anther 1.6–2.8 mm long, pubescent on the exterior; ovary glabrous, sometimes with a few hairs at the apex of one or both carpels. Fruit: follicles ± 50 × ± 0.7 cm, glossy, glabrous. Distribution from Côte d'Ivoire to Democratic Republic of the Congo. In Gabon: Ogooué, Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo and Woleu-Ntem. Ecology: primary and secondary forest, often near watercourses. In Gabon up to m altitude. B: van Dilst (1995), Jongkind & Lachenaud (2022). BIBLIOGRAPHY
