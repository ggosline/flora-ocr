# *Dicliptera ocymoides* (Lam.) Juss., Ann. Mus. Paris 9 : 268 (1807).

**Genus**: [[Dicliptera]]
**Family**: [[Acanthaceae]]

## Synonyms

- Dicliptera maculata Nees var. senegambica Nees, DC. Prodr. II : 485 (1847).

## Description

≡ Diclioptera senegambica (Nees) Benoist ex Tisserant, Mém. Inst. Et. Centrafric. 2 : 15 (1950).

1-8 : Dicliptera obanensis S. Moore.

9-7 : Dicliptera Elliotii C. B. Clarke.

Perennial plant, erect, reaching 1 m high, with quadrangular sub-6-angular stems, somewhat decumbent and ascending, with slightly swollen nodes bearing hairy roots; indumentum quite variable, some forms can have stems and internodes striated-canaliculate 11-8 cm long towards the middle of the plant. Petiole 2-4 cm long, more or less hairy or glabrescent. Leaf blade oval, acuminate-cuspidate, hairy, pubescent or glabrescent, 5-8 × 3-4 cm; base broad, rounded, but somewhat decurrent into the petiole; acumen elongate. 6 pairs of lateral veins, in fairly constant number, ascending, curved and anastomosing at a distance of 0.5-1 mm from the margins.

Inflorescences in pseudofascicles of verticillate appearance, axillary and terminal, very condensed and almost sessile; those at the apex approach one another and, especially in large specimens, give the impression of a large subpyramidal spike; partial inflorescences composed of approximately 5-8 involucres, of which 1-3 can be sterile; the small lateral inflorescences, as well as those at the apex, are borne by leaves much smaller than ordinary leaves, 11 × 2 mm at maximum; involucres containing 1-3 flowers, of which frequently only one develops; bracts lanceolate, 10 × 2.5 mm, acuminate and bearing a spine 0.5-1 (2) mm long, ciliate at the margins, with white hairs 1-2 mm erect at right angles, trinerved; there are 3-4 pairs of fine pinnate veins between the three main veins. The bract is translucent or hyaline between the margins and the dark green veins. Bracteoles lanceolate-acuminate, hyaline, transparent, naviculate, 7 × 1-1.5 mm, ciliate at the margins and on the back, with a terminal awn approximately 3 mm long. Calyx 5 mm long, with lanceolate-filiform sepals, the posterior one larger, 5 × 0.5 mm, the others 4 × 0.2 mm, hyaline, ciliate at the margins and on the back. Corolla purple, approximately 2 cm long, glabrous, with a tube 1 cm long, and with rather divergent lips; lower lip 8 × 1.5-2 mm somewhat trilobate; upper lip approximately 10 × 2 mm. Ovary glabrous, fusiform-elliptic, 1.2 mm long, with 4 ovules. Style 18-20 mm long, glabrous; stigma bilabiate.
