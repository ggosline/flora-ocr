# *Nothospondias staudtii* Engl.

**Family**: [[Simaroubaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Nothospondias]]

## Synonymy

- *Nothospondias Talbotii* S. Moore (1913)

## Description

Dioecious small to medium tree, to 25 m. Leaves clustered at branch ends, imparipinnate, with 9–21 pairs of leaflets; rachis 0.5–1.2 m. Leaflets opposite or alternate, obliquely oblong-elliptic, blunt-acuminate, asymmetric at the base, 10–16 × 4–6.5 cm; blade glabrous and membranous; about six pairs of arched lateral veins joined near the margin, with a loose network of nearly parallel transverse veinlets conspicuous beneath; petiolules about 5 mm.

Panicles large and lax, to 70 cm, axes puberulous. Male flowers on 2–4 mm pedicels. Calyx cup-shaped, about 1.5 mm high, with four ciliate teeth. Petals 4, oblong-obovate, 4–6 mm, yellow, imbricate in bud. Stamens 8 in two unequal whorls of four, 4–7 mm; anthers basifixed. Disc glabrous and ovoid, topped by a four-lobed abortive ovary. Drupes 2–2.5 × 1.3–1.8 cm, yellow-orange when ripe; endocarp very tough.

## Distribution

From Côte d’Ivoire to Gabon and Mayombe. In Gabon recorded at Libreville, Ganda, Tchibanga and Lastoursville.

## Specimens examined

Holotype: Staudt 746. **Gabon** — Klaine 3312 (Libreville); Le Testu 1439 (Ganda, Mayombe), 1662 (Tchibanga), 7572 (Lastoursville).
