# *Pleiocarpa rostrata* Benth. Planche 93 ; Figure 9(G)

**Genus**: [[Pleiocarpa]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Hooker’s Icon. Pl. 12 : 71, t. 1182 (1876)

## Synonyms

- Carpodinopsis rostrata (Benth.) Pichon, Bol. Soc. Brot. sér. 2, 27 : 147 (1953).

## Description

Shrub or small tree reaching 5 m in height, with semi-spreading habit. Leaves opposite; petiole strictly elliptic to narrowly elliptic or obovate, often as long as wide, 10.6–28 × 3.8–11.3 cm, acute or decurrent at the base on the petiole, acuminate, rarely obtuse, at the apex; secondary veins 7 to 12 pairs, oblique, very prominent below, forming an angle of ± with the midvein and uniting in a submarginal nerve along the entire limb or only in the upper half. Inflorescence axillary, rarely terminal, ramified, 5–8 × 3–5 cm with bracteate flowers, minute, squamiform bracts. Flower. Calyx 0.5–1 mm, calyx with 4 lobes, 4 times as long as wide, 0.3–0.5 × 1.2–3 mm; corolla white, cream to pale green, 13–37 mm long in the mature bud, forming a relatively broad oblong head, 5–6 of the length of the bud, rounded at the apex; tube subcylindric, 10–14 mm long, 0.5–1.5 mm wide at the base, narrowed on 0.5–2 mm of width below the insertion of the stamens, enlarged again on 0.5–3.5 mm at the level of the anthers, yellow at the narrow throat, with lobes spreading to recurved, obovate to elliptic, 4–5 times as long as the tube, 3–5 times as long as wide, 7–11 × 4–15 mm; stamens included, with apex reaching 3–8 mm below the throat, inserted above the middle of the tube, anther 1–2 times as long as wide, 0.5–1 × 1–2 mm, pistil 10–13.8 mm long, with the apex situated 1–5 mm below the stamens, ovary with 3 to 5 (or 6) free carpels; style 6.5–11 mm. Fruit with 3 to 5 free mericarps, narrowly ellipsoid to ovoid, slightly gibbous, 10–15 × 8–11 × 5–8 mm, orange-brown or bright orange, waxy, smooth to rugose, with 1 to 5 seeds. Seed 14–17 × 8–11 × 4–5 mm, orange, smooth.

Distribution. Nigeria, Cameroon and Gabon; in Gabon known from a single flowering specimen from Ogooué Lolo. Ecology. In forest undergrowth in Gabon, at m of altitude, elsewhere up to m.

## Source note

The main distribution range of P. rostrata is situated in Nigeria and Cameroon; the only collection from Gabon, Le Testu 8871, equally cited by Omino, is geographically quite distant from the rest. B: Jongkind & Lachenaud (2022), Omino (1996). BIBLIOGRAPHY
