# *Cnestis macrantha* Baillon

**Genus**: [[Cnestis]]
**Family**: [[Connaraceae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia, sér. 1, 7 : 242 (1867) ; BAKER, FTA 1 : 462 (1868) ; SCHELLENBERG, PR 103 (4, 127) : 34 (1938) ; HEPPER, FWTA, ed. 2, 1 (2) : 743 (1958) ; LEMMENS, in BRETELER (éd.), AUWP 89 (6) : 202, fig. 90-91 (1989). TYPE : Gabon, s.l., Duparquet 53 (lecto-P)

## Description

SCHELLENBERG, PR 103 (4, 127) : 34 (1938) ; HEPPER, FWTA, ed. 2, 1 (2) : 743 (1958) ; LEMMENS, in BRETELER (ed.), AUWP 89 (6) : 202, fig. 90-91 (1989). TYPE : Gabon, s.l., Duparquet 53 (lecto-P).

Liane or lianescent shrub. Branchlets cylindrical, young branchlets often somewhat angular with dense brownish-yellowish pubescence.

Leaves 10-17-jugate; petiole 0.5-4.5 cm long, rachis 11-22 cm, dense brownish-yellowish pubescence. Leaflets finely papyraceous, the lateral ones ± opposite, elliptic or (narrowly) oblong, 1-5 × 1-1.5 cm, with rounded, truncate or rarely subcordate and slightly unequal base, the terminal leaflet obovate, oblong or elliptic, 2.5-3.5 × 1-1.5 cm, rounded or cuneate at the base; all leaflets obtuse or truncate, more rarely retuse or subacute, pubescence sparse above, dense with long hairs below; midvein impressed above, prominent and densely pubescent below; lateral veins 5-8 pairs; tertiary veins indistinct; petiolules very short ≤ 1 mm, pubescent.

Inflorescences in panicles or racemes, 1 per axil, at the apex of young branchlets (often with axillary leaves reduced or very young, hence the appearance of a pseudo-terminal compound inflorescence), 5-24 cm long, ≤ 40-flowered, with dense brownish-yellowish hairs and short brown glandular hairs. Bracts subulate or lanceolate, 3-5 mm long, generally curved. Pedicels articulate 3-7 mm below the calyx, dense pubescence.

Flowers: sepals never reflexed, in imbricate prefloration, narrowly oblong or narrowly obovate, 5-6.5 × 1.5-2.5 mm, obtuse or rounded, outer face with dense pilosity accompanied by numerous glandular hairs, inner face pubescent. Petals in imbricate prefloration, obovate to obcordate, 2-3 × 1.3-2 mm, cuneate at the base, truncate or retuse at the apex, with some hairs on the lower half of the external side, glabrous inside, veins generally indistinct. Stamens united at the base, subequal, 1.5-2.2 mm long, anthers ≈ 0.4 × ≈ 0.3 mm. Pistil: carpels 4-5 mm long; ovaries ≈ 1 mm long, brown pubescence; styles straight or curved in their upper part, pubescent at the base; stigmas oblique, ≤ clearly 2-lobed.

Fruits: mature follicles 1-4 per flower, narrowly ovoid or narrowly ellipsoidal, somewhat oblique, 4-6 × 1-1.5 cm, rounded at the base; beak blunt and broad, 15-20 mm long, indistinctly marked; pericarp with short red hairs and some longer yellowish ones on the external face while the internal face bears long yellowish hairs.

Seeds ovoid, 10-20 × 5-8 mm, sarcotesta 2-5 mm long. - Pl. 15, p. 55.

## Source note

In a small pocket attached to the Paris specimen Mann 2235, one finds parts of leaves and flowers of a specimen of C. macrantha. On this pocket, in the hand of BAILLON: "Cnestis, Duparquet 53, Gabon". SCHELLENBERG (1938) chose Duparquet 53 as lectotype. He supposed that this specimen had been collected at Calabar in Nigeria, probably because all the other specimens at his disposal, including Mann 2235, came from Nigeria. DUPARQUET never went to that country; he collected instead in Gabon, as BAILLON had correctly noted. The collection Breteler & Lemmens 8344 thus provides additional evidence of the presence of C. macrantha in Gabon

Breteler & Lemmens 8344, Env. de Libreville, Forêt de la Mondah (st, Oct.), WAG. Duparquet 53, type, s.l. (fl., -), P.
