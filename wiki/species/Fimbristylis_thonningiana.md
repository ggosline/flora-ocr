## Also treated in vol 44

**Pages**: 154-154 · **Source**: `sources/Cyperaceae_vol44_liteparse`

F. microcarya sensu Goetghebeur & Coudijzer (1984a), non F.Muell. (1859). Annual herb, slender, in tufts with ± numerous crowded stems; stems 20–50 cm × 0.5 mm approximately, distinctly compressed, green, longitudinally ribbed, glabrous below, scabrous under the inflorescence, new stems produced inside the sheaths. Leaves 1–3 basal and usually 3 cauline; sheath green to straw-yellow with the margins membranous paler and finer; blade longer towards the upper sheath, 10–20 cm × 1–2 mm, flat, finely scabrous particularly on the margin. Inflorescence in a loose anthela of 3–7 cm × 2–4 cm, formed of a basal sessile spikelet surrounded by 3–10 primary rays each bearing a sessile spikelet surrounded by 2–6 pedunculate spikelets or by new groups of spikelets of 3rd and 4th orders; involucral bracts green and foliaceous, the lower 2–6 cm × 1–2 mm, distinctly scabrous at least near the apex. Spikelet 4–5 × 1–1.2 mm at flowering, but up to 8 × 1.5 mm at fruiting, light brown, with 10–20 flowers; glumes oval–triangular, 1.5–2 × 1 mm, glabrous, light brown with a thick nerve ending in a short mucro. Flower: style 1.5–2 mm including the 3 branches 0.5–1 mm long. Achene 0.6–0.8 × 0.5 mm approximately, grey, with scattered protuberances and cells horizontally elongated prominent.

### Distribution

Sierra Leone, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria and Gabon; rare in Gabon, found only in the Estuaire and Ogooué-Maritime (2 collections)

### Ecology

in humid depressions of grasslands; at 0–300 m altitude. Taxonomic note: Goetghebeur & Coudijzer (1984a) consider that this species is the same as the South Asian species, F. microcarya F.Muell. They may be right, but F. thonningiana has longer glumes (1.5–2 mm, instead of 1–1.2 mm) and a longer style; we have therefore retained the name.
