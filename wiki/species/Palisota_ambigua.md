# *Palisota ambigua* (P.Beauv.) C.B.Clarke Figure 3(F, G)

**Genus**: [[Palisota]]
**Family**: [[Commelinaceae]]
**Protologue**: Commelina ambigua P.Beauv., Fl. Oware 1 : 26, t. 15 (1805)

## Description

in A.DC. & C.DC., Monogr. phan. 3 : 131 (1881). Commelina ambigua P.Beauv., Fl. Oware 1 : 26, t. 15 (1805). Aneilema ambiguum (P.Beauv.) Loudon, Hort. brit. : 15 (1830), as "ambigua". P. thyrsiflora Benth. in Hook., Niger Fl. : 544 (1849), nom. illeg. P. prionostachys C.B.Clarke in A.DC. & C.DC., Monogr. phan. 3 : 134 (1881). P. congolana Hua, Bull. Soc. Bot. France 41 (Sess. Extraord.) : 52 (1895). P. plagiocarpa Hua, Bull. Soc. Bot. France 41 (Sess. Extraord.) : 52 (1895). P. micrantha K.Schum. ex C.B.Clarke in Dyer, Fl. trop. Afr. 8 : 31 (1901). Perennial herb, 0.3–1(–2.5) m tall; stem erect, branched, densely tufted, with internodes of ± 10–15 cm. Leaves in whorls; pseudopetiole 2–3.5 cm, with reddish-brown pubescence; blade obovate-elliptic, rarely linear-obovate (see Notes below), (4–)12–18(–25) × 3.5–6(–9) cm, base cuneate, apex abruptly acuminate, margins brown-reddish, ciliate, coriaceous, bright green above, grayish-green and with appressed woolly or scaly pubescence below. Inflorescences one to several on the terminal whorl, in spike or raceme, straight, lax, erect, up to 25 cm long including up to 12 cm for the floral portion, axes purple, violet or more rarely white, with 20 to 30 lateral cincinni ± 2–3 cm long, with ± 20 flowers, elongating, becoming erect and slightly swollen at fruiting, with dark zigzag scars. Flower concolorous with the inflorescence axis; pedicel 2–3 mm; sepals elliptic, 2.5–3 × 1.5(–2) mm, subequal, the upper one shorter and broader; petals subequal to the sepals but narrower; stamens with filament 2 mm, the 2 upper ones with anther slightly narrowing toward the apex, ± 1 × 0.5 mm, flattened and pressed against the petals, with longitudinal slits, the lower one with robust anther, 0.5 mm long, distinctly curved and fist-shaped; staminodes ± 2 mm long, composed of a filament and long fan-shaped hairs; ovary subglobose, ± 1 mm in diameter, glabrous; style 2 mm, stigma penicillate. Fruits 1 or 2 per cincinnus, ovoid to globose, at first shiny white, with a violet spot at the apex extending into 6 descending rays, becoming dark purple and waxy at maturity and often somewhat irregular. Seed irregularly conical, 2 mm in diameter, ridged with ± 15 irregular crests, blackish; caruncle in a round depression.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea (Río Muni), Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola; in Gabon in nine provinces

## Ecology

secondary forests and forest margins, very common; in Gabon up to 1020 m altitude, elsewhere 60 to 850 m. Flowering and fruiting observed throughout the year

## Source note

Specimens Parmentier 2592 and Sosef 662 (Mont Iboundji, Boussimbi) have atypical leaves that are very narrowly lanceolate, ± 13–20 × 2(–3.5) cm, long-acuminate. The fruits are reported as "purple, brown, shiny". Despite these differences from the typical form of P. ambigua, the lack of additional specimens prevents us from concluding that these specimens represent two different taxa.
