# *Celosia trigyna* L.

**Family**: [[Amaranthaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Celosia]]

## Description

Erect or trailing annual, normally c. 50 cm but to 2 m. Ovate to ovate-elliptic leaves are often red-tinged, 2.5–6 × 2.5–4 cm. Small white or pink 1–2 mm flowers form cymules in simple spikes or panicles. Style divides into usually three red papillose stigmatic branches. Capsule subglobose, with about six dark glossy seeds.
