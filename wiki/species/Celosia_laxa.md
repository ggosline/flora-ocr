# *Celosia laxa* Schumach. et Thonn.

**Family**: [[Amaranthaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Celosia]]

## Description

Scrambling shrub 1–2 m, hooked into surrounding bushes. Leaves ovate to lanceolate, 3–9 × 2.5–4.5 cm, acuminate. White or straw-white terminal and axillary inflorescences are usually compact branched spikes 2–5 cm long. Perianth 4–5 mm; ovary with 6–8 ovules. Capsule ovoid, 3 mm, with about five glossy black lenticular seeds.
