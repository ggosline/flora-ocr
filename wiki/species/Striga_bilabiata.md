# *Striga bilabiata* (Thunb.) Kuntze subsp. bilabiata Planche 56(F)

**Genus**: [[Striga]]
**Family**: [[Scrophulariaceae]]
**Protologue**: Revis. gen. pl. 3(2) : 240 (1898)

## Description

Revis. gen. pl. 3(2) : 240 (1898). Perennial herb, hemiparasitic, 7–40 cm tall, densely pubescent; stem quadrangular, canaliculate, simple or little branched. Leaves opposite, sessile; blade narrowly triangular-ovate, 10–20 × 1–2.5 mm, with entire margin. Inflorescence in dense spike. Flower sessile or subsessile; bracteoles 3–4 mm long; calyx with 5 ridges (nerves), 4–5 × 1.4–1.5 mm, teeth subulate, 1.5–1.8 mm long, fifth tooth very small; corolla rose-purple, glandular, tube 12 mm long, curved and swollen in the upper part, upper lip emarginate and 5 × 3–4 mm, lower lip tripartite, with lobes 5–5.5 mm long. Fruit ovoid, 3.5 × 2 mm.

## Distribution

Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Rwanda, Burundi, Zambia, Zimbabwe, Malawi, Mozambique, Botswana, Angola, Namibia and South Africa; in Gabon, observed in Haut-Ogooué

## Ecology

rocky savannas; in Gabon, at 600 m, in East Africa, up to 1700 m. Taxonomic note: Mohamed et al. (2001) distinguish six subspecies of S. bilabiata which can be identified by the width of the bracts and the shape of the lobes of the lower lip (bracts wider than the leaves, lobes obtuse: subsp. bilabiata, subsp. jaegeri Hepper; bracts narrower than the leaves, lobes acute: subsp. linearifolia (Schumach. & Thonn.) Mohamed, subsp. ledermannii (Pilg.) Hepper, subsp. rowlandii (Engl.) Hepper, subsp. barteri (Engl.) Hepper). In Gabon, only subsp. bilabiata has been collected. The species in the broad sense is distributed from Senegal to the Cape. B: Mielcarek (1996), Mohamed et al. (2001), Philcox (1990). Bibliography
