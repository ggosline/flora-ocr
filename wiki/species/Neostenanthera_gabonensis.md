## Description

Journ. of Bot. 73, Suppl. Polypet., Add.: 6 (1935). — Pellegrin, Bull. Soc. Bot. Fr., Mém. 31: 56 (1949). — Keay, F.W.T.A., ed. 2, 1: 42 (1954). — Walker et Sillans, Pl. Ut. Gab. 69 (1961).

Shrub 4 to 6 m tall, with grey bark, somewhat rough, yellowish-white wood; branchlets glabrous, twigs puberulent to glabrescent. Leaves with petiole 6–15 mm long, channelled above, appressed-puberulent; lamina papyraceous, elliptic-oblong to oblanceolate, 10–22 cm long, 3.5–9 cm wide, cuneiform to obtuse at the base, ± longly acuminate at the apex; upper face green, puberulent to glabrescent, lower face glaucous, puberulent. Midvein impressed above, prominent below. Lateral nerves 7–11 pairs, obliquely ascending, not united in loops near the margin. Reticulum scarcely visible.

Flowers yellow, solitary, extra-axillary, pendulous; pedicel 3–6 cm long, slender, enlarged at the apex, puberulent. Bracteole minute, inserted towards the lower half of the pedicel. Sepals very small, broadly triangular-ovate, 1–1.5 mm in diameter, acute, fused at the base, appressed-pubescent on both faces. Outer petals lanceolate, 15–30 mm long, 3–4.5 mm wide, enlarged and concave at the base, densely appressed-puberulent on both faces except at the inner base; inner petals triangular-ovate, 6–10 mm long, 3–4 mm wide, concave, contiguous at the base, cucullate at the apex, glabrous outside except on a median line densely appressed-pubescent, tomentose inside. Stamens numerous on 3–4 cycles, subsessile, oblong, about 2 mm long, 0.6–0.7 mm wide, thecae linear extrorse, locellate transversely; connectives triangular-ovate, puberulent. Carpels numerous, about 2.5 mm long, 0.5 mm wide, ovaries cylindrical, pubescent, styles linear, 0.5 mm long, glabrous.

Fruits with slender pedicel, 3–6 cm long, tomentellous, enlarged at the apex; mericarps numerous, orange at maturity, narrowly ellipsoid to fusiform, 1.7–3 cm long, 0.5–1.2 cm wide, apiculate at the apex; pericarp ribbed longitudinally, puberulent; stipites slender, 0.5–2.5 cm long, tomentellous. Seeds ellipsoid-elongate to fusiform, 1.5–1.7 cm long, 0.5–0.6 cm wide, with 5 longitudinal ribs; testa reddish-brown, thin.

## Type

Soyaux I17, Sibang,Gabon, fr. (holotype B; isotypes P!, K!)

## Discussion

Species closely related to N. myristicifolia (Engl. et Diels) Exell, characterized by the fusiform-ribbed form of the mericarps, and its inner petals marked by an external median pubescent line.

It is found in dense humid forest, in Liberia, Ivory Coast, Ghana, Gabon and Angola. Certain collections from the Crystal Mountains region suggest that it could be found in Cameroon.
