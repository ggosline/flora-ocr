# *Neuropeltis aenea* R.D.Good

**Genus**: [[Neuropeltis]]
**Family**: [[Convolvulaceae]]
**Protologue**: J. Bot. 67, suppl. 2 : 114 (1929)

## Description

Liane, at least up to 30 m in height; stem up to 12 cm in diameter; branchlets, petioles, leaf blades beneath and inflorescences with fine, appressed, reddish-copper indumentum, finely combed in appearance. Leaf: petiole 5–10(–15) mm, narrowly channelled above; blade ± ovate, 5–9 × 2–5 cm, rounded to cordate at base and acuminate apex over 5–10 mm and mucronate over 1–2 mm, glabrous or with ± scattered, appressed hairs above, with persistent indumentum beneath; lateral nerves, 7–11 pairs, tertiary nerves prominent above. Inflorescence in terminal panicle and in axillary raceme and/or panicle; bract as long as calyx. Flower: pedicel 2–3 mm; calyx 2 mm in length, with broadly elliptic sepals; corolla ± 5 mm in length, with spreading lobes and puberulous on the outside; stamens 6–8 mm in length, glabrous, anthers 1 mm in length; pistil 3–5 mm in length, glabrous. Fruit (immature) ellipsoid to obovoid, 5 × 4 mm, glabrous; bract enlarged, ± circular, membranous, 3–4 cm, ± sparsely pubescent. Mature seeds unknown.

## Distribution

Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo (Mayombe), Angola (Cabinda); in Gabon, it is only reported from the province of Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology

primary and secondarized forest; in Gabon, at low altitude, elsewhere, up to approximately 300 m. B: Breteler (2010), Lejoly & Lisowski (1984). Bibliography
