# *Ledermanniella bifurcata* (Engl.) C.Cusset Planche 14

**Genus**: [[Ledermanniella]]
**Family**: [[Podostemaceae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia , sér. 2, 14(2) : 273 (1974)

## Synonyms

- Inversodicraea bifurcata Engl., Veg. Erde 9, Pflanzenw. Afr. 3(1) : 273 (1915).

## Description

Plant with basal part thallose, ribbon-like, ± 1 mm wide, branched; shoots subsessile or with branched stems (rarely simple). Leaves distichous, entire or 1–2-dichotomous, 0.7–2.5 cm in length, with linear segments. Inflorescence subsessile, bearing (2–)4 to 6 flowers terminal or axillary at the branching forks; spathe sessile to long-pedicellate, ovoid, 1.8–2 mm in length, tearing irregularly at the apex at anthesis. Flower: pedicel 8–10 mm after anthesis; tepals linear, 0.2–0.4 mm; androecium longer than the ovary, stamens 2, filaments fused for more than half their length, anther oblong, 0.8 × 0.4 mm, pollen in monads; ovary 1.3–1.5 × 0.8 mm, gynophore 0.2 mm in length; stigmas linear, 0.6–0.7 mm.

## Distribution

South Cameroon, Gabon and southern Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, known from Estuaire (Kinguélé) and Ogooué-Lolo

## Ecology

in Gabon at 75–225 m altitude. B: Cusset (1984, 1987a), Engler (1926), Hall (1971), Sosef et al. (2006). Bibliography
