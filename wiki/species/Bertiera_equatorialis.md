# *Bertiera equatorialis* N. Hallé

**Genus**: [[Bertiera]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Notulae System., 16 : 286 (1960)

## Description

Shrub 80 cm to 1.50 m, erect. Shoot apexes with ± sparse or hairy pubescence. Stipules pubescent, 6–20 × 2.5–5 mm, narrow at the base and connate for 3–5 mm. Petioles pubescent, 3–7 mm long. Lamina ± pubescent above, subconcolorous, somewhat glossy when dry, 5–12 × 2–5.5 cm; base acute or ± rounded, apex acute to acuminate. Margins and veins pubescent. 5–8 pairs of secondary veins. Veinlets finely striolate.

Terminal inflorescences erect, 3–6 cm long, pubescent. Rachis green, fractured and lacking bracts; alternate cymes slender, sinuous or contorted in appearance, dichotomous, bearing a subulate bract at the apex of the peduncle; 3 to 10 flowers per branch appearing often as unilaterally superposed, but sometimes also as alternate. Bud very acute, hairy. Flowers sessile, 6–9 mm long. Calyx green with 5 subulate, pubescent sepals, somewhat unequal, 0.6–1.5 mm long. Corolla white, 6.5 mm, with lobes opening flat (8 mm diam.), pubescent externally. Internal pubescence widespread from the base of the anther lobes where it forms a ring. Anthers very acute, exceeding 2 mm. Pollen white. Style reaching 5 mm long. Disk cylindrical, 0.8 mm diam. and 0.6 mm high.

Fruit succulent, spherical, bright red, ca. 10 mm diam. Seeds 1.1 mm.

## Type

N. Hallé 794 (P), du Gabon

## Discussion

Degraded ± dense forest or semi-deciduous forest.

Cameroon, Spanish Guinea (Tessmann 729), Congo (Kouilou, Brazzaville), and Cabinda (Gossweiler 7162 BM!), Gabon.
