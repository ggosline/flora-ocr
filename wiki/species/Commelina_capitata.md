# *Commelina capitata* Benth. Figure 2(A, B)

**Genus**: [[Commelina]]
**Family**: [[Commelinaceae]]

## Description

in Hook., Niger Fl. : 541 (1849). Perennial herb; fibrous roots; stems solitary or in tufts, creeping or erect, ± 20–60(–245) cm, strongly branched, glabrous, glabrescent or puberulent. Leaves distichous; sheath closed, except if separated by a lateral branch, 1–3 cm long, puberulent, ciliate with long reddish hairs at the apex; pseudipetiole short; blade narrowly oval-oblong to oval, 4.5–15.5 × (1.5–)2–5 cm, base distinctly oblique, with one side cuneate, the other rounded, apex acuminate to attenuate, margin generally glabrous, upper surface glabrous or sparsely puberulent on the midvein, lower surface puberulent, sometimes limited to the midvein. Inflorescence with spathes in fascicles, subsessile; peduncle hidden, 3.5–7 mm, glabrous or puberulent, those of faded or fertilized flowers emerging distinctly from the spathe; spathe non or slightly falcate at the apex, 1.3–2.6 × 0.8–1.3(–1.7) cm, apex acute or obtuse, dorsal margin somewhat saccate at the base, free margins or fused up to 7 mm from the base, ciliate, surface puberulent, often sparsely hirsute with long reddish hairs, pale green, whitish at the base; spathe with 2 cincinni, the upper with a single male or bisexual flower, the lower 3- to 6-flowered. Flower 15–17 mm in diameter; sepals greenish-white, the upper ± 4 mm long, the lower ± 5 × 3 mm, fused for about ¾ of their length, forming a cup; upper petals oval-cordate, 8–14 × 9–11 mm, of which ± 4 mm for the claw, yellow, pale yellow or greenish-white, the lower ± 6 × 1 mm, yellow to translucent white; stamens with yellow pollen, the lateral with filament of ± 6 mm, anther ± 1 mm long, the median with filament of ± 5 mm, anther ± 1.5 mm long; staminodes 3, with reduced antherodes; style ± 7 mm, stigma capitate. Fruit 2-locular, oblong-ellipsoid to ellipsoid, (6–)8–9.3 × (3.5–)4.8–6.5 mm, on a stipe of ± 15 mm, persistent, white then pale green, distinctly exceeding the spathe, apex rounded to truncate or rarely slightly emarginate. Seed transversely ellipsoid, compressed dorso-ventrally, 3.5–4.5 × 1.7–2.3 mm; testa medium brown, dorsal face foveate to foveolate, entirely covered by a fleshy orange-yellow to cream matte aril, forming a mid-dorsal longitudinal crest, ventral face free, smooth.

## Distribution

Senegal, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Ivory Coast, Ghana, Benin, Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Rwanda, Burundi, Uganda and Angola; in Gabon in nine provinces

## Ecology

evergreen forest, forest margins and trails, swamp forest, secondary forest, rarely in dry forest or scrubland; in Gabon from 40 to 950 m altitude, from 1050 to 2600 m in East Africa, elsewhere from 5 to 2000 m. Flowers opening from 8 a.m. to 11 a.m. in the morning (West Africa). Flowering is observed throughout the year, except October and December (East Africa)

## Source note

C. africana L. is another species with yellow flowers, fairly widespread in Africa. This species lacks the reddish pubescence (notably on the margins of the spathes and/or on the sheaths), and the spathes are solitary, whereas the spathes of C. capitata are generally clustered at the stem apex. C. africana has not yet been recorded from Gabon. B: Brenan (1968b), Brunel et al. (1984), van der Burg (2006), Cabezas et al. (2014), Faden (2012), Sosef et al. (2006), Vandeweghe et al. (2016), Vanden Berghen (1988)
