# *Ravenala madagascariensis* Sonn.

**Family**: [[Strelitziaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Ravenala]]

## Description

Tree-like plant with a trunk reaching about 30 m. Long-petiolate leaves, blades 2 m or longer, form a single distichous fan at the stem apex. Lateral inflorescence with about ten distichous bracts, each enclosing numerous bisexual white flowers. Perianth segments free in two whorls; six stamens; ovary 3-locular and many-ovuled; capsule loculicidal.

## Distribution

Native to Madagascar and cultivated in Gabon as the traveller’s tree. Overlapping petiole bases retain water.
