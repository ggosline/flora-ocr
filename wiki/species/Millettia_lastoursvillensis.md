# *Millettia lastoursvillensis* Pellegr.

**Genus**: [[Millettia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Bot. France 95 : 260 (1948)

## Description

M. breteleri Adomou, ined., in sched. (see Note). Liane, reaching 20 m; stem slightly ribboned; branchlets with appressed pubescence, with whitish to brownish lenticels, circular and linear, horizontally arranged on flattened portions; exudate viscous, becoming reddish. Stipules generally caducous, ovate, slightly to distinctly falcate, peltate, 10–15 × 2–3 mm, ± silky with brown hairs on the outside, pubescent on the veins on the inside. Leaf: petiole 1.5–6 cm, channelled above, with a pubescent pulvinus 4–7 mm in length; rachis 3–9.5 cm, channelled above; stipels subulate, 2.5–5 mm, with spreading pubescence; leaflets (5–)7 to 9, petiolule 4–8 mm, with dense pubescence, blade obovate to elliptic-obovate, (4–)7–16 × (2–)3.5–7 cm, base attenuate to rounded or somewhat cordate, apex with an acumen 0.6–2 cm in length and mucronate, chartaceous to coriaceous, glabrous above, densely appressed-pubescent to ± silky below, dull; lateral veins very prominent, in 8 to 12 pairs. Inflorescence in terminal or sometimes axillary panicles, 25–80 cm in length, often with units of 2 opposite flowers, densely brown-reddish tomentose; peduncle (1–)3–6 cm; lateral branchlets (4–)10–40 cm in length; bracts caducous, falcate, peltate, 10–14 × 2–3 mm, densely brown-pubescent on the outside, pubescent on the inside with 7 or 8 veins; bracts of the branchlets ovate, 4–4.5 × 1.5–2 mm, pubescent on the outside, glabrous on the inside. Flower: pedicel 4–9 mm, pubescent; bracteoles caducous, at the base of the calyx, ± elliptic, 3–5 × 1–1.5 mm, pubescent; calyx 10–18 mm in length, tube 4–6 mm in length, with yellowish-brown pubescence on the outside, glabrous on the inside, upper lobes connate, but sometimes weakly torn, lower lobes 7–10 × 3–4 mm; corolla red to purple, with yellowish veinlets, the base yellowish, standard suborbicular to obovate, 19–20 × 16–17 mm, with a claw of 3 mm, apex rounded or sometimes emarginate, auriculate, with prominent basal calluses, wings elliptic, 19–20 × 5–5.5 mm, auricle ± 0.5 mm, with a claw of ± 4 mm, without lateral pouch, keel with elliptic-falciform limb, 19–20 × 7–8 mm, apex rounded, auricle ± 1 mm in length, with a claw of 5 mm, lateral pouch broad; stamens monadelphous, tube 13–14 mm in length, free portion 5–6 mm in length, anthers medifixed; disk striate, with 10 lobes; ovary 12–13 mm in length, pubescent, with 7 or 8 ovules; style 5–7 mm, the base pubescent. Pod flattened, linear to obovate or elliptic, often constricted between the seeds, 10–20 × 1.8–3.3 cm, base attenuate to rounded, apex pointed, brown velutinous pubescence. Mature seed not seen.

## Distribution

subendemicto Gabon, also in the Republic of the Congo (environs of Brazzaville); in Gabon, known from Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

primary and secondary forest, climbing on large trees, often on margins and roadsides; at 200–500 m altitude. Flowers and fruits from September to December

## Source note

The length of the lower lobes of the calyx is exaggerated by Pellegrin (1949), being only 7–10 mm in well-elongated buds, and not 13–15 mm. The manuscript name M. breteleri Adomou is mentioned here, as this name has been used quite frequently for some time, in data banks and on specimens annotated by this author during his revision.
