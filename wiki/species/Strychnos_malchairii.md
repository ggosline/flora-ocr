# *Strychnos malchairii* De Wildeman

**Genus**: [[Strychnos]]
**Family**: [[Loganiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Jard. Bot. Brux. 5 : 44 (1915) (« malchairi »)

## Description

("malchairi"). — DUVIGNEAUD, Bull. Soc. Roy. Bot. Belg. 85: 30, fig. 10 B (1952) ("id."). — LEEUWENBERG, l. c.: 174, fig. 24, map 28.

Large liana reaching a height of 8–18 m; stem 1–8 cm in diameter; bark pale brown, with some lenticels; wood pale brownish; branches somewhat lenticellate; small branches glabrous, non-lenticellate, cylindrical, not furrowed; tendrils solitary.

Leaves with glabrous petiole, 2–7 mm long; blade opaque beneath, coriaceous or subcoriaceous, even fresh, elliptic, narrowly elliptic or narrowly ovate, 4–13 × 1.5–6 (–8) cm, acuminate at apex, cuneate or rounded at base, glabrous on both faces; one pair of secondary nerves curved along the margin; tertiary venation reticulate, prominent on both faces.

Inflorescences axillary and exceptionally also terminal, 1–3 × 1–1.5 cm, pauciflorous, fairly contracted, but generally distinctly pedunculate; peduncle, branches and pedicels glabrous or with sparse pubescence. Flowers 4–5-merous, even within a single inflorescence, often sessile. Sepals yellow or green, fused to about half their height, ovate or broadly ovate, 1–1.2 × 0.8–1.2 mm, acute or obtuse, glabrous on both faces or with fine pubescence externally. Corolla, in mature bud, 2.8–3 mm long, white, pale yellow or orange, distinctly thicker toward the apex, externally glabrous or with fine puberulence, internally with coarse curved hairs at the apex of the lobes, for the rest glabrous or with fine puberulence when the corolla is also puberulent externally; tube broad, 0.8–1 mm long; lobes thick, narrowly triangular, 1.8–2.2 × 0.8–1 mm, acute, suberect. Stamens exserted, inserted at the corolla throat, with glabrous filament, ± of similar length to the anther; anther oblong, glabrous or with some small hairs at the base. Pistil glabrous, 1.6–2 mm long; ovary ovoid, 1 × 0.7–1 mm, 2-locular; style short, 0.6–1 mm long; stigma indistinctly bilobed or capitate; 5 ovules per locule.

Fruits orange, small, subglobose or ellipsoid, 14–21 × 10–16 mm and containing one seed; wall thin. Seed ellipsoid, 12–15 × 9.5–11 × 8.5–10 mm, smooth, not depressed; testa very thin, adhering to the pulp. Pl. 20, 8–14, p. 129.

## Type

Malchair 506, Likimi, Equateur, Congo-Kinshasa (holotype : BR)

## Discussion

Congo-Kinshasa and possibly also Gabon. Dense forests or gallery forests. Altitude 0–1,000 m.

GABONESE MATERIAL (possibly belonging to this species):

Hallé N. & Le Thomas 521, Bélinga (ster.).
