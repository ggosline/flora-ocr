Liane reaching the summit of the tallest trees, lianescent shrubs or shrub. Trunk at least 6 cm in diameter, bark grey rather smooth. Woody cylinder almost entirely divided into several sectors by radial intrusions of the bark. Branchlets glabrescant with age, often grooved and with 5 rows of rather prominent lenticels. Young branchlets often hollow clothed with a somewhat tomentose, dense, hirsute or even hispid indumentum, quite persistent and generally brown.

Stipules, 1-8 (12) mm long, simple or divided at the base into 2(3) unequal subulate segments, indumentum similar to that of the branchlets.

Leaves. Petiole subcylindric, 1-6 (12) mm long, same pubescence as the branchlets. Blade papyraceous, elliptic, obovate-elliptic or obovate; base often oblique and cordate, subcordate, obtuse or rounded; apex acute or shortly acuminate, rarely rounded and mucronate, 1.5-2.5 (3.5) times longer than wide, 2.5-6-14 (19) × 1.5-3-6 (8) cm; primary veins II, 6-10 (13) on each side of vein I, rather little visible and generally impressed above, prominent below; upper surface with subaperssed or erect, hirsute or tomentose indumentum, very often brownish, generally very early caducous except on vein I and the primary veins; similar indumentum to the lower surface but generally more persistent and mixed with an arachnoid pubescence tightly appressed, loose or dense, caducous or persistent of white hairs, which often give the lower surface a grey or even white appearance; glands absent or little visible.

Inflorescence 0.5-1-2 (3.5) cm long, 5-10-30 (70)-flowered, in contracted cyme, same indumentum as the branchlets. Peduncle 1-2-10 (17) mm long. Bracts and bracteoles subulate of very variable length 1-9 mm, often incurved. Pedicels 1-3 (5) mm long, upper portion 0-1 mm.

Flowers. Sepals erect sometimes slightly spreading, almost free or slightly connate at the base, ovate-triangular to oblong-elliptic, ovate-elliptic or oblong-triangular, 3.5-4-5 (6) × 1-1.5-2.5 mm, usually concave, apex acute, subacute, rounded or obtuse; external surface with appressed to scabrous pubescence, parts covered in bud generally provided with a woolly tomentum sometimes extending to the entire sepal and then often mixed with stiff hairs; internal surface with silky pubescence appressed especially in the upper portion. Petals erect, somewhat longer than the sepals, oblong to narrowly obovate-elliptic, increasingly narrowed toward the base, 4-4.5-6.5 (7.5) × 1-1.5-2.5 mm, cleft for 0.5-1-2 (2.5) mm, glabrous on both surfaces; external surface exceptionally with rare hairs below the cleft; internal surface distinctly keeled, base shortly united to the filaments, rarely free; lobes concave, apex rounded. Stamens erect, generally somewhat shorter than the petals at most equal, 4-6-7 mm long, glabrous, exceptionally with a few hairs near the base on the internal side; anthers 1 mm long, connective prominent. Staminodes subquadrate < 1 mm, of variable form and size, apex obtuse or bilobed, internal surface woolly rarely glabrous or nearly so. Pistil straight, 4.5-5-7 (7.5) mm long; ovary subspheric, densely woolly, 3 (4)-locular; style glabrous to entirely woolly, apex with 3 (4) short lobes; obturator often present.

Fruit velvety yellow to orange at maturity, diameter ≤ 3 cm, composed of 1-3 (4) mericarps with an apicule of ± 5 mm and a thick short basal stipe; mericarp subspheric to ovoid, obovoid or ellipsoidal, generally somewhat laterally compressed, 13-20 (35) mm long, diameter 9-15 mm, often slightly keeled at the dorso-superior portion; exocarp rather smooth at least when fresh, velvety rust-brown to pale brown, hairs often caducous and urticating; mesocarp pulpy fibrous, yellow-orange at maturity, thickness with the exocarp ≤ 4 mm; endocarp bony, thickness at most 0.5 mm, external surface muriculate, the internal one being smooth shiny and very finely striate. Seed subspheric to ellipsoidal, 8-17 mm long, diameter 7-11 mm; testa fairly hard, dark brown-black, smooth or somewhat reticulate, generally shiny, thickness reaching 0.5 mm; cotyledons subplane-convex.
