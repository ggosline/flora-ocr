# *Uapaca staudii* Pax Planche 33F,G

**Genus**: [[Uapaca]]
**Family**: [[Phyllanthaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot . Jahrb . Syst . 23 : 522 (1897)

## Description

Tree up to 26 m tall; trunk up to 30 cm in diameter, with stilt roots at least up to 4 m tall; branchlets puberulent-tomentose, rapidly glabrescent. Stipules foliaceous, mostly hood-shaped, (5–)8–15(–20) mm long, rounded at the apex, puberulent to glabrescent. Leaf: petiole cylindric, 1–3.5 cm, puberulent, glabrescent; lamina (4.5–)8–20(–24) times longer than the petiole, narrowly obovate, 18–35(–62) × 6–15 cm, narrowed towards a ± rounded base, acute to acuminate over 0.5–1.5 cm or rounded to obtuse at the apex, coriaceous, puberulent when young, glabrous or with some scattered hairs on the midvein beneath; lateral nerves 7–13(–15) pairs, obscure above, prominent below. Inflorescence: peduncle (15–)20–30(–45) mm, glabrous; involucral bracts ± 12, slightly concave, 10–15 × 3.5–9 mm, glabrous. Male flower: calyx gamosepalous, lobed, 1.5–2.5 mm long, glabrous; petals absent; stamens 3–4 mm long, glabrous, anthers ± 0.5 mm long; pistillode cyathiform, ± equalling the calyx, glabrous. Female flower unknown. Fruit with peduncle 5–12 mm, globose to ellipsoid, often slightly lobed, 2–3 × 2–3 cm, sparsely pubescent, lenticellate.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea and Gabon where the species has been collected in Moyen-Ogooué, Ngounié, Nyanga, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

riparian and swampy forest; in Gabon, at 0–500 m altitude. Bibliography: Breteler (2012b), De Wildeman (1936), Keay (1958), Pax & Hoffmann (1922)
