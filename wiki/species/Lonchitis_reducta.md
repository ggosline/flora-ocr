# *Lonchitis reducta* C. Christensen

**Genus**: [[Lonchitis]]
**Family**: [[Dennstaedtiaceae]]

## Description

— C. Chr. in Fedde, Repert 9 : 370 (1911). — Tard., Mém. IFAN 28 : 81 (1953) (pp.). — Alston, FWTA : 34 (1959).

Rhizome erect, covered with reddish hairs, with fronds in dense rosettes. Petiole 10 to 15 cm, stramineous, slender, covered with long whitish, soft, erect hairs. Lamina with oval outline, 25-30 cm long by 15 cm wide, pinnate, pinnatifid at the apex; 5-6 pairs of opposite pinnae, spaced approximately 5 cm apart, with lanceolate outline, the lower ones slightly reduced, the middle ones 7-10 cm long by 3-4 cm wide, sessile or very shortly petiolulate, with truncate or subcordate base, acute apex, scarcely attenuate, margins lobed, with rounded, entire lobes. Upper pinnae confluent, adnate. Entire plant covered with soft, whitish hairs. Veins copiously anastomosing, forming several rows of areoles between the costa and the margin. Sori short, situated at the bottom of the sinuses. Paraphyses with recurved apex.

Guinea, Ghana, Fernando-Po; reported for the first time from Gabon.

Shady and humid rocks, understory.

## Specimens examined

N. Hallé : 2408, Abanga, monts de Cristal.
