# *Leptoderris macrothyrsa* (Harms) Dunn

**Genus**: [[Leptoderris]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1910 : 391 (1910)

## Synonyms

- Lonchocarpus macrothyrsus Harms, Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 26 : 300 (1899).

## Description

Large liana; stem exceeding 10 cm in diameter; branches lenticellate; branchlets puberulent, glabrescent, the same indumentum on stipules, petioles, rachis, petiolules and stipels. Stipules narrowly triangular, 6–8 × 3 mm, ± striate. Leaf: petiole 4.5–12.5 cm; rachis 4.5–10 cm; stipels narrowly triangular to subulate, 2–5 mm long; leaflets 5 to 7, with petiolule 6–9 mm, limb obovate-elliptic, (6–)10–15(–19) × 4–9 cm, rounded at the base, 5–10 mm acuminate at the apex, generally with mucro 2–3 mm, papyraceous, glabrous above, with short, scattered and appressed hairs below; lateral veins in 6 to 9 pairs. Inflorescence at least up to 40 cm long, brown-tomentose; bracts and bracteoles narrowly elliptic, 2.5–5 mm long. Flower: pedicel 1–3 mm; calyx campanulate, 5–5.5 mm long, with teeth 1 mm long, with short and appressed hairs on both faces; corolla 12–15 mm long, white, petals ciliolate at the apex, with claw 2–3 mm, wings distinctly shorter than the keel; stamens ± as long as the keel, anthers 1 mm long; pistil as long as the stamens, with brown and appressed hairs. Pod and seed unknown.

## Distribution

Cameroon and Gabon; in Gabon, known from a single collection from Ogooué-Ivindo in the Mékambo region

## Ecology

primary and secondarized forest; in Gabon at 550 m altitude. b: Pellegrin (1949). ibliography
