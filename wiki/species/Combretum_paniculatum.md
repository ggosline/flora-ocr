## Description

KEAY, FWTA, ed. 2, 1(1): 273 (1954); LIBEN, FZ: 34 (1968); EXELL & GARCIA, CFA: 69 (1970); WICKENS, FTEA: 52 (1973); EXELL, Fl. Zamb. 4: 149 (1978); LIBEN, Fl. Cam.: 25: 37 (1983). TYPE: Senegal, s.l. (fl., fr. juv.), Roussillon 60 in Herb. Jussieu 13632 (holo-P-JU; IDC microfiche).

– C. ramosissimum ENGLER & DIELS, Mon. Afr., Pfl., 3: 72, tab. 21d (1899). TYPE: Sierra Leone, Afzelius s.n. (syn-B dele). ; Bioko, Mann 203 (syn-B dele.; iso-K, P); Gabon, Soyaux 86 (syn-B dele.; iso-K); Cameroon, Zenker 210 (syn-B dele.); Cameroon, Zenker & Staudt 177 (syn-B dele.; LECTOTYPE designated here: -K; iso-BM).

Liane ≤ 20 m. Flowering at the appearance of young leaves. Young branchlets puberulent.

Leaves alternate to opposite; petiole ≤ 3 cm, lower part sometimes transformed into a lignified hook ≤ 1.5 cm; blade ovate to elliptic, ≤ 30 × ≤ 14 cm; major lateral veins 4–9 pairs; base of blade rounded to subcordate; apex rounded to acuminate.

Scales absent.

Inflorescences paniculate ≤ 14 cm, terminal or axillary. Bracts 2–10 mm long, lanceolate.

Flowers 4-merous, stipitate, protogynous, fragrant. Lower receptacle 3–6 mm tall, upper receptacle long-campanulate, 3–7 mm tall, 2–4 mm wide, pale green to reddish, subglabrous to entirely covered with appressed hairs. Calyx, lobes short deltoid. Petals ovate, 2–3 mm long and wide, margin ciliate with very small red glandular hairs. Stamens exsert 5–12 mm; filaments red; anthers 0.9 mm long, dark red. Style exsert 3–7 mm, red.

Fruit subcircular to oblong-elliptic in profile, ≤ 3 × ≤ 3 cm, membranaceous wings; stipe 4–10 mm.

## Distribution

from Gambia to Ethiopia and South Africa

## Ecology

in rainforest and riparian forests in savanna region; altitude ≤ 2400 m; common in certain localities
