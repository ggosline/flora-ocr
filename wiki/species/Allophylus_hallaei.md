# *Allophylus hallaei* R. Fouilloy, sp. nov.

**Genus**: [[Allophylus]]
**Family**: [[Sapindaceae]]

## Description

Climbing shrub or tree; branches terete subtomentellous, bark lenticellate punctate and lineate; leaves 3-foliolate, petiole 5–6(–10) cm long channelled above tomentellous; below convex glabrous; middle leaflet lanceolate 14–15(–24) × 6–8 cm, base long-cuneate attenuate into long petiolules (15–25 mm); lateral leaflets somewhat smaller 10–12(–17) × 5–7 cm shortly petiolulate (3–4 mm), all with rather long acuminate apex (1–2 cm), chartaceous, entire, lateral nerves (9–10 pairs) oblique toward apex mucronate (0.1 mm); nerves on both sides very densely reticulate; midvein above puberulous; below in the axil of nerves barbate. Thyrses simple or 1-branched, 4–7 cm long, densely cincinnate stipitate (1–2 cm) pluriflorous (bearing 6–8 flowers). Flowers (2 mm) pedicels 2 mm long, sepals scarcely pilose at very base; petals subglabrous, 8–10 hairs, disk and stamens glabrous; ovary bi-trilocular pilose; fruit cocci 1 cm long, 7 mm wide yellow pyriform glabrous.

TYPUS: N. Hallé & J.-F. Villiers 4391, Mbel sur riv. Kono, Gabon (fl., janv.) (holo-, P!).

Slender shrub, sarmentose or erect (2–3 m) with subtomentellous branches. Leaves with petiole channelled above (channel tomentellous), 5–6 (–10) cm, with petiolule 15–30 mm long, tomentellous above, blade glabrous concolorous, membranous, 14–15 (–24) × 6–8 cm, base gradually narrowed and apex acuminate (1–2 cm); 9–10 pairs of nerves reaching the margin in a mucro (0.1 mm); midvein tomentellous above; domatia 1 mm; lateral leaflets equalling three-quarters of the terminal ones.

Inflorescences, 4–7 cm, dense (5 flowers per cm), simple or with one branch; axis puberulent (hairs 0.1 mm); stipitate cymules (1–2 mm), with 6–8 flowers; pedicels 1.5–2 mm, articulate at base; buds 1.5 mm, glabrous; sepals nerved, translucent; petals white with yellow thickening bearing 8–10 short hairs; filaments and disk glabrous; 2–3 carpels. Fruit pyriform, yellow, 10 × 7 mm. PL. 3, 7–13, p. 33.

## Specimens examined

Hallé N. & Villiers 4314, bord du Komo près Mbel (fr., janv.) arbuste, pétiotule 6-9 mm; 4391, Mbel, sur riv. Kono (fl., janv.); 4738, Mts de cristal entre Nkam et Méla (fr., janv.) (arbuste 3 m); 4823, Nkam (fr., fév.).

REMARQUE : Le spécimen Hallé N. & Le Thomas 151 récolté avec fleurs et fruits jaunes sur un petit arbre de 10 m près des mines de fer de Bélinga (Gabon), le 25 juillet 1966, diffère des plantes ci-dessus par un limbe entier à ± 7 paires de nervures bouclées, sans mucron, plus petit (10 × 5 cm), des fruits subsphériques de 5 mm de diamètre; des grappes de 12-14 cm portant souvent 1-2 rameaux de 2-3 cm. A retrouver en fleurs pour une meilleure connaissance.
