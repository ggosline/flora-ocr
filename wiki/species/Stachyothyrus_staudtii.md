# *Stachyothyrus staudtii* Harms

**Genus**: [[Stachyothyrus]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Harms, Engl. Bot. Jahrb. 26 : 277 (1899)

## Description

Medium-sized trees. Leaves glabrous, bipinnate. Rachis 10–15 cm, cylindrical. 2–3 pairs of opposite leaflets, the lower pair shorter than the upper. Leaflets opposite, lanceolate, somewhat arcuate, acuminate acute, obtuse at the base, coriaceous, 8–15 × 4–8 cm. Midvein prominent beneath. Secondary veins 3–4 pairs, united in arcs near the margins. Network well-meshed with finely prominent veinlets beneath.

Panicles of racemes 20–30 cm long, glabrous. Bracts minute. Flowers white, sessile. Calyx glabrous. Tube 2–3 mm. Lobes semiorbicular with ciliate margins, 1.5 mm. Petals 5, oblong, with rounded apex, 7–8 × 3 mm. Stamens 10, unequal; the 5 larger ones as long as the petals, the 5 others distinctly smaller. Anthers oblong, mucronulate. Ovary glabrous.

Pod flat, woody, lanceolate, with 2–3 compressed seeds.

## Type

Staudt 423, Lolodorf, Cameroun

## Discussion

Medium-sized or small trees of primary forest, with base very fluted. Trunk irregular, sinuous, tortuous. Bark hard, granulate.
