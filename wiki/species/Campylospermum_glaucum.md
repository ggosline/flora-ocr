# *Campylospermum glaucum* (Tiegh.) Farron Planche 14

**Genus**: [[Campylospermum]]
**Family**: [[Ochnaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Jard. Bot. État Bruxelles 35 : 397 (1965)

## Description

Exomicrum glaucum Tiegh., Ann. Sci. Nat., sér. 8, Bot. 16 : 340 (Dec. 1902). Ouratea brachybotrys Gilg, Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 33 : 270 (Mar. 1903). Small tree up to 8 m tall; branches with whitish bark. Stipules persistent, triangular, 3–4 mm long. Leaf: petiole 5–7(–10) mm; blade narrowly elliptic-obovate to narrowly elliptic, (7–)11–20(–23) × (2.5–)4–5(–7) cm, ratio 3–3.8, cuneate or sometimes rounded at base, acute to acuminate or sometimes tapered at apex, papyraceous, flat, blue-green to greyish above, with margin serrulate to subentire; midvein nearly plane to prominent on upper surface, prominent on lower surface, lateral veins in (7–)10 to 18 pairs, plane to slightly prominent on both surfaces, tertiary venation scalariform, perpendicular to midvein, distinct on both surfaces. Inflorescence terminal, branched, dense, main axis (1–)2–4(–6) cm; gemmate scales slightly persistent; racemes 1 to 3, (1–)2–3 cm; cymules distant by 2–5 mm, 1- to 6(–8)-flowered; bracts persistent, sometimes leaving an apparent scar, triangular, 1–2 mm long. Flower: pedicel 6–10(–12) mm, articulate at 2–4 mm from base; sepals ovate, 5–7 × 1.5–3 mm in flower, 9–10 × 3–5 mm in fruit; petals obovate, 6–7 × 3–4 mm, cuneate to truncate at base, apex emarginate; stamens: anthers 3.5–6 mm long; style persistent in fruit, 4–5 mm. Fruit: receptacle 3 × 5 mm; drupelets 1 or 2 well-developed per receptacle, ellipsoid to slightly reniform, 7–10 × 4–5 mm; cotyledons incumbent, unequal with small external cotyledon.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon, southern Central African Republic, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon and Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, fairly common, but not found in the Cristal Mountains and in the Chaillu Massif

## Ecology

primary and secondary forest, open forest, swamp forest or periodically inundated forest, riparian forest and forest margins; in Gabon, at 50–650 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1100 m

## Vernacular names

avom. Note taxonomique : C . glaucum est caractérisée par la surface supérieure du limbe qui est vert bleuâtre à grisâtre. Ce caractère rappelle les feuilles de C . glaucifolium qui est, par contre, facilement distinguable par des inflorescences longues, lâches et pendantes. b : Bissiengou (2014), Farron (1963, 1968, 1985). ibliographie
