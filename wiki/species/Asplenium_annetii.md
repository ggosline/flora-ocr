# *Asplenium annetii* (Jeanpert) Alston

**Genus**: [[Asplenium]]
**Family**: [[Aspleniaceae]]

## Synonyms

- Diplazium Annetii Jeanpert, Bull. Soc. Bot. Fr. 68 : 326 (1921).

## Description

— Alston, Bol. Soc. Esp. Hist. Nat. 49 : 193 (1951); FWTA : 56 (1959). — Tard., Mém. IFAN 28 : 178 (1953).

Rhizome creeping, with leaves in rosette, bearing lanceolate scales, formed at the centre of thickened cells, and, on the margins, of thin cells. Petiole 30 cm long, dark brown, bearing scales. Frond fresh green (teste Hallé), 40-45 cm long by 20-25 cm wide, pinnate. 3-5 pairs of lateral pinnae, opposite at the base, subalternate higher up, spaced 5-6 cm apart, making an angle of 80° with the rachis, the lower ones slightly shorter, subsessile, decurrent on the petiole, the middle ones 13-17 cm long by 4 cm wide, oblong, shortly petiolate, with subequal bases, apex very abruptly narrowed into a filiform point, 1.5 cm long, proliferous; margins undulate. Terminal pinna similar to the lateral ones, but slightly larger, decurrent at the base. Rachis and costae slightly fibrillous. Veins simple or bifurcate from the base. Sori close to the costa, reaching approximately 0.5 cm from the margin.

## Type

Annet 19, Mangelès, Cameroun (P)

## Discussion

Cameroon, Fernando-Po.

Dark and humid forests, stream margins.
