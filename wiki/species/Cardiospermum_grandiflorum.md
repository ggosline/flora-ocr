# *Cardiospermum grandiflorum* Swartz

**Genus**: [[Cardiospermum]]
**Family**: [[Sapindaceae]]
**Protologue**: Prodr. 3 : 64 (1788); Fl. Indes Occ. 2 : 698 (1800)

## Synonyms

- Cardiospermum caillei CHEV., Expl. Bot. A.O.F. 5 : 148 (1920); FWTA, ed. 1, 1 : 503 (1928).

## Description

fa. grandiflorum

Sarmentose shrubs reaching 8 m. Leaves biternate ± pubescent, 15–20 cm, papyraceous, dentate, without stipules.

Inflorescences with 5 or more branches, equalling leaves in length. Capsules 6.5 cm long and 3.5 cm wide. Seeds 5–7 mm in diameter, hilum 1.5–2 mm. Style 2 mm. PL. 1, 10–18, p. 15.

fa. genuinum Radlkofer

Stems, lower surface of leaf blade and fruits subtomentose; sometimes fruits glabrous. American form.

fa. elegans (Kunth) Radlkofer

— Cardiospermum elegans KUNTH, in H.B.K., Nov. Gen. Sp. 5 : 99 (1821).

Stems, leaves, fruits glabriusculous; Afro-American form.

## Discussion

The form elegans has been collected in Guinée, Libéria, Côte d'Ivoire, Congo, Zaïre, Angola. To be sought in Gabon.

The form hirsutum is known from Guinée, Sierra Leone, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Dahomey, Nigeria, République Centrafricaine, Congo. To be sought in Gabon.

Use: In the Bertoua region, pregnant women make necklaces with the flexible stems.
