# *Strychnos icaja* Baillon

**Genus**: [[Strychnos]]
**Family**: [[Loganiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia 12 : 368 (1879)

## Description

— FWTA ed. 2, 2 : 43. — LEEUWENBERG, l.c. : 133, fig. 19, map 20.

— S. kipapa GILG, Notizbl. Bot. Gart. Berlin 2 : 256 (1899); in ENGLER, Bot. Jahrb. 28 : 118 (1899). — FTA 4 (1) : 521.

— S. dewevrei GILG in ENGLER, Bot. Jahrb. 28 : 119 (1899). — DE WILDEMAN, Ann. Mus. Congo ser. 5, 1 : 175 (1904).

— ? S. brachyura GILG in ENGLER, Bot. Jahrb. 28 : 119 (1899). — FTA 4 (1) : 521.

— S. pusilliflora S. Moore in Cat. Talbot's Nig. Pl. : 70 (1913). — FWTA ed. 1, 2 : 24.

— S. mildbraedii GILG in MILDBRAED, Wiss. Ergebn. deutsch. Zentr.-Afr. Exped. 1907-08, 2 : 531, t. 73 A-D (1914).

— S. dundusanensis DE WILD., Bull. Jard. Bot. Brux. 5 : 48 (1915).

— S. venulosa Hutch. & M. B. Moss in FWTA ed. 1, 2 : 24; Kew Bull. : 334 (1937).

Liane reaching a height of 20–40 m and a length of 20–100 m; stem 4–15 cm in diameter; bark pale grey to dark brown, with large lenticels, rather thin; wood cream; branchlets often ramified in an umbel, lenticellate or not; small branchlets glabrous, non-lenticellate, cylindrical, furrowed or not when dry; tendrils solitary, generally located in the branching umbel of the branchlets.

Leaves with glabrous petiole, 4–12 mm long; blade usually dark green and glossy on both surfaces, coriaceous or chartaceous in the sun, papyraceous or thin-coriaceous in shade even when fresh, elliptic, narrowly elliptic, narrowly ovate, or sometimes ovate, 5–15 × 2–7 cm (on young plants in forest understory up to 23 × 13 cm), acuminate, apiculate or, in shade, caudate at the apex, cuneate or rounded at the base, glabrous on both surfaces; one pair of secondary nerves curved along the margin and reaching the apex; tertiary venation reticulate, prominent on both surfaces in dried leaves.

Inflorescences axillary, several grouped together, lax, few- or multiflorous, 3–7 × 2–6 cm; peduncle and branches slender, glabrous as are the pedicels. Flowers 4-merous. Sepals pale green, fused for 2/5 of their length, broadly ovate to suborbicular, 0.4–1 × 0.4–1 mm, rounded, obtuse or subtuncate, scarcely or not ciliate, glabrous on both surfaces. Corolla, in the mature bud, 2.2–2.5 mm long, yellowish-green or yellowish-white, glabrous on both surfaces or pubescent within at the base of the lobes; tube short, broad; lobes oblong to almost ovate, 2.5 times longer than the tube, 1.6–1.8 × 1–1.2 mm, acute, spreading. Stamens exserted, inserted at the corolla throat, with short filament, 0.5–1 times longer than the anther; anther suborbicular, glabrous. Pistil glabrous, 1–1.4 mm long; ovary globulous, 0.5–0.6 mm long, 2-locular; style short, 0.4–0.8 mm long; stigma small, capitate; 3–9 ovules per locule.

Immature fruits dark green then dark yellow, small, tender, subglobulous, 2.5–3 cm in diameter and containing one seed; wall thin. Seed ellipsoid, 16–21 × 15–20 × 9–15 mm; testa woolly, caducous, adhering to the pulp. Pl. 29, 6–10, p. 136.
