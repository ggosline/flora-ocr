# *Begonia mildbraedii* Gilg Planche 27

**Genus**: [[Begonia]]
**Family**: [[Begoniaceae]]

## Description

in Mildbr., Wiss. Erg. Zweit. Deut. Zentr.-Afr. Exp., Bot. : 574 (1913). Plant reaching 20(–30) cm in height. Leaf peltate; petiole 2–15(–18) cm, hirsute; blade ± spreading when fresh, not bullate, asymmetric, circular to broadly elliptic-ovate or rarely elliptic-ovate, (2.5–)4–12.5(–15) × (2–)2.5–10 cm, apex rounded to acuminate or rarely acuminate, margin entire to weakly undulate, upper surface without long hairs; tertiary veins without sclerenchymatous sheath and thus invisible when dry. Inflorescence: peduncle (1–)1.5–7 cm, with 2–3(–4) ♂ flowers and 1–2(–3) ♀ flowers with yellow tepals; bracts (1–)1.5–4 mm long. ♂ Flower: tepals 5–12(–14) × 4.5–11(–13) mm; androecium with (12–)15–28 stamens. ♀ Flower: pedicel elongating in fruit to 4.5 mm; tepals (4–)5–12(–14) × (4.5–)5–13(–14) mm;

## Distribution

disjunct, rare in Ivory Coast and Ghana, then in southeastern Cameroon, Gabon, Congo (Brazzaville) and Democratic Republic of the Congo; widely distributed in Gabon

## Ecology

often on slopes, talus or rocky faces, but also along forest trails, in primary and secondary forests, often along watercourses and rivers; 100–950 m altitude. B : Irmscher (1925), Sosef (1994), Wilczek (1969a; as B. quadrialata ). IBLIOGRAPHY
