# *Cyperus congensis* C.B.Clarke Planche 24

**Genus**: [[Cyperus]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]

## Description

in Durand & Schinz, Études fl. Congo : 285 (1896). Perennial herb, fairly robust, with the base of stems thickened, woody and covered by fibrous remains of old leaf sheaths; stems 40–70 cm × 1.5–3.5 mm, trigonous, glabrous, leafy for 10–20 cm at the lower part. Leaf: sheath green to pale brown at the top and dark reddish-brown near the base; blade 20–30 cm × 3–7 mm, plane. Inflorescence in a broad anthela 3–20 cm, composed of 1–3 sessile spikes and usually 2–10 spikes or groups of spikes on rays 1–15 cm long; spikes of rather irregular size and form, often with spikelets crowded on a short axis; involucral bracts 3–8, foliaceous, erect or spreading, the principal one 10–30 cm long. Spikelet linear, 5–25 × 1.5–2 mm, reddish-brown, with 5–40 very spaced flowers, the rachis not detaching at maturity; glumes oval–elliptic, 1.8–2.2 mm long, obtuse, reddish-brown with a trinervate midvein greenish or straw-yellow terminating below the apex. Flower with 3 stamens; style with 3 branches. Achene elliptic, 1.7–1.9 × 0.5–0.6 mm, trigonous, greyish-black, with almost smooth surface with isodiametric cells.

## Distribution

widely distributed in West and Central Africa, from Senegal to Gabon and the Democratic Republic of Congo; rare in Gabon, found only in the Ngounié and Middle-Ogooué

## Ecology

in seasonally humid habitats such as grassy savannas and river margins; in Gabon, at 0–100 m altitude, up to 1600 m elsewhere. Taxonomic note: is identified by its narrow spikelets with very spaced flowers. B: Haines & Lye (1983), Hooper & Napper (1972). Bibliography
