# *Dorstenia lujae* De Wildeman

**Genus**: [[Dorstenia]]
**Family**: [[Moraceae]]
**Protologue**: Pl. Nov. Herb. Then. 1: 221 (1907); RENDLE in HUTCH. & RENDLE, FTA 6 (2): 54 (1916); DE WILD., Pl. Bequaert. 6: 45 (1932); HAUMAN, FCB 1: 64 (1948)

## Description

RENDLE in HUTCH. & RENDLE, FTA 6 (2): 54 (1916); DE WILD., Pl. Bequaert. 6: 45 (1932); HAUMAN, FCB 1: 64 (1948).

Herbs with thick rhizomes approximately 5 mm. Stems ± ascending, 40–120 cm tall; base woody, glabrous or with short, scattered, white uncinate hairs; leafy part either with only short, white, uncinate to curved, scattered to dense hairs, or with curved to uncinate hairs, mixed with larger yellowish-white, curved to straight hairs; internodes 0.5–7 cm long and 2–5 mm thick; nodes sometimes swollen. Leaves almost distichous. Lamina elliptic to oblong or obovate, sometimes narrowing towards the base, (3–) 7–20 × (1.5–) 3–8 cm, chartaceous; apex acute or gradually to abruptly acuminate or even cuspidate; base cuneate or subobtuse to subcordate; margin sinuate, sometimes with 1–4 (–8) coarse acute teeth towards the apex; upper surface glabrous; lower surface subglabrous, or especially on the nerves, sparsely to densely puberulent with only short uncinate hairs, or with short uncinate hairs and some larger ones; sometimes upper and lower surfaces punctate as if by glands; midvein and secondary nerves slightly impressed above, slightly to very prominent below; 6–14 pairs of secondary nerves. Petiole 5–35 mm long and approximately 1.5 mm thick, with indumentum similar to that of the stem. Stipules either absent or small (up to 0.75 mm) and caducous, or 1–6 mm long, subulate or narrowly triangular, with a few small hairs, subpersistent.

Inflorescences solitary or in pairs, greenish to purpurascent. Peduncle 10–50 (–90) mm long, approximately 1 mm in diameter, with small white uncinate hairs, scattered to dense, mixed with larger and longer hairs, scattered to dense. Receptacle discoid and flat, sometimes convex after anthesis, rarely turbinate; floriferous surface suborbicular to multiangular, 7–20 (–25) mm in diameter; margin (0.5–) 1–2 (–3) mm wide, bearing numerous appendages 1–30 (–45) × 0.25–1 mm, slightly enlarged at the apex; primary appendages approximately 10–15, larger or (almost) as large as the diameter of the receptacle, sometimes with 1 or 2 small teeth and rarely also 1 or 2 small appendages towards the base; between these primary appendages, 2–6 secondary appendages of variable length; upper surface of margin and appendages glabrous or with small white uncinate hairs scattered to rather dense; lower surface of receptacle and appendages almost glabrous or with small uncinate (yellowish–) white hairs rather dense, sometimes mixed with longer and larger hairs, especially on the distinct ribs of primary appendages. Flowers numerous; perianth 2–3-lobed, approximately 0.75 mm in diameter; stamens 2–3, with filaments approximately 0.5 mm long and anthers approximately 0.25 × 0.2 mm with rather narrow connective. Female flowers, approximately 8, with perianth approximately 0.3 mm tall and 0.4 mm in diameter; style 0.25–0.75 mm long; stigmas 2, linear, 0.25–0.5 mm long, ± spreading.

Pyrene approximately 2 mm in diameter, smooth.

## Type

Luja 177S, Zaire, Sankuru (holo.-, BR)

## Discussion

In this species 2 ± distinct varieties can be recognized based on the variability of stipules, the length of the peduncle and the form of the receptacular appendages.

The specimens listed below of D. lujae could not be placed in either of these varieties. These specimens are not very similar to each other.

## Specimens examined

Hallé N. 1346, 1379, 37 km SE Makokou, Mboundou (fl., fév.). Le Testu 9365, env. de Woleu-Ntem (fl., nov.).

CLÉ DES VARIÉTÉS

1. Appendices primaires sans dents ou petits appendices à leur base ; presque aussi grands que le diamètre du réceptacle ; stipules subpersistantes, longues de 1-6 mm var. lujae.

1'. Appendices primaires avec 1-2 dents et parfois aussi 1-2 petits appendices à leur base ; plus grands que le diamètre du réceptacle ; stipules caduques, longues de 1 mm au plus ..... var. batesii.
