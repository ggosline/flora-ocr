# *Memecylon viride* Hutchinson & Dalziel

**Genus**: [[Memecylon]]
**Family**: [[Melastomataceae]]
**Protologue**: FWTA, ed. 1, 1 : 214 (1927), nom.; Kew Bull. 1928 : 123, descr.; JACQ.-FÉL., Fl. Cam. 24 : 138, tab. 46 (1983)

## Description

, nom.; Kew Bull. 1928 : 123, descr.; JACQ.-FÉL., Fl. Cam. 24 : 138, tab. 46 (1983).

Small tree 4 to 6 m tall; branches terete. Leaves dull, opaque; petiole 1 to 2 mm; limb oblong to elliptic-oblong, caudate-acuminate, 3–5 × 10–14 cm, cuneate at the petiole, acumen linear, up to 2 cm; midvein finely prominent beneath; 10 to 12 main transverse veins, spaced 3 to 4 mm apart, only visible by transparency, the converging veins obscure, 0.5–1 mm from the margin.

Cymes solitary or geminate on adult foliate nodes, compact, capitate, 1 × 1 cm, with a stipe of 2 × 4 mm and 8 × 12 flowers directly umbellate, or in 3 sessile heads forming a single capitulum; bracts 0.2 mm, triangular-acute, caducous. Flowers with slender pedicel 2.5 to 3 mm, floral bud ovoid 1.5 × 2 mm; hypanthium-calyx broadly cupuliform, 2 × 1 mm, truncate to 4-sinuate. Corolla white or violaceous, conical obtuse and as tall as the hypanthium in the bud; petals membranous, ± 2 × 2 mm. Anther 1.5 × 0.8 mm; thecae slightly convex; connective conical-acute, moderately saddled by the gland briefly elliptic, scarcely evident, median or somewhat anterior; filament 1.8 to 2 mm; epigynous chamber shallow, 8 cross-septa, scarcely developed; style 2.5–3 mm. 6 to 8 ovules.

Fruit globular, or somewhat depressed, 10 × 8–10 mm. — Pl. 46, p. 127.

## Type

Talbot 496, Nigeria (BM)

## Discussion

Recognized by its globular inflorescences of delicately pedicellate flowers.

## Specimens examined

sect. Afzeliana Jacques-Félix

Adansonia, sér. 2, 17 (4) : 423 (1978); Fl. Cam. 24 : 138 (1983).

Ces espèces sont davantage inféodées au milieu forestier humide que les précédentes. Elles sont caractérisées par leurs fruits ellipsoïdes, bleuâtres, et par leurs étamines à connectif généralement obtus et fortement ensellé par la glande. Leur connaissance est également très imparfaite.
