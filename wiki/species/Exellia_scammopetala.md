# *Exellia scammopetala* (Exell) Boutique

## Description

Liane 12 to 15 m tall and 3–5 cm in diameter. Branchlets pubescent-appressed, tawny, to glabrescent becoming glabrous. Leaves with petiole 3–5 mm long, channelled above, tawny pubescent to glabrescent; blade papyraceous to subcoriaceous, elliptic-oblong to obovate-oblong, (5) 9–22 cm long, (2.5) 3.5–8.5 cm wide, rounded at base, attenuate or ± abruptly and longly acuminate at apex; upper surface glossy, glabrous except on the midvein, lower surface glaucous, appressed-pubescent. Midvein prominent below. Lateral veins 8–12 pairs, obliquely ascending, terminating near the margin, impressed above, prominent below; veinlets scarcely visible.

Flowers white, axillary or extra-axillary, solitary or geminate; pedicel 7–15 mm long, puberulent, furnished with a basal bracteole, ovate, approximately 1 mm long, tawny pubescent. Sepals green-brown, broadly ovate, 1 mm long, ± 2 mm wide, ± connate at base, acute at apex, tawny pubescent. Petals fleshy, the outer ones broadly ovate to suborbicular, 7–10 mm in diameter, greenish-brown and tomentellous outside, white and puberulent inside; the inner ones white on both surfaces, spatulate, triangular-ovate and concave in the upper part prolonged by a thick claw, 4–7 mm long, 3–4 mm wide. Stamens 15, on two whorls, arranged in groups of 5 opposite the outer petals, approximately 1.5 mm long, filaments short, thecae lateral unequal, connectives enlarged and truncate above the anthers. Carpels 3, cylindric, 2–2.5 mm long, hairy, brown, stigmas sessile, conical, short and glabrous. Ovules 15–20, biseriate.

Fruits with pedicel 1.3–1.8 cm long, puberulent, with persistent sepals; mericarps 1–3, sessile, globose, green, black when dry, 1.5–3 cm in diameter, verrucose, puberulent. Pericarp hard, 2.5 mm thick. Seeds 14, flattened in a half-moon shape, biseriate, 1–2 cm long, 0.5–0.8 cm wide.

## Discussion

Liane of dense humid forest distributed in Gabon, Congo-Brazzaville, Congo-Kinshasa and Angola. The flowers are extremely fragile.

Monkeys eat the fruits.

Species of the understory in dense humid or semi-deciduous forest, distributed in southern Cameroon, Spanish Guinea and Gabon. Particularly remarkable for the silvery aspect of its leaves.
