# *Remirea maritima* Aubl. Planche 117

**Genus**: [[Remirea]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Hist. pl. Guiane 1 : 45, t. 16 (1775)

## Synonyms

- Cyperus pedunculatus (R.Br.) Kern, Acta Bot. Neerl. 7 : 798 (1958).

## Description

Herb slightly succulent, with long (more than 50 cm) creeping rhizomes 1–3 mm thick and usually bearing spaced stems; stem erect, 5–20 cm × 1–2 mm, triangular, glabrous. Leaves crowded; blade thick, 3–5 mm wide at base, canaliculate, with scabrousmargin at least near the stiff and acute apex. Inflorescence irregular, reddish-brown at maturity, consisting of 3–7 spikes, subsessile among the upper leaves; spikes ovoid, 7–15 × 6–10 mm. Spikelet 4–5 × 1.5–2 mm, with 1 flower, sessile, about 30 per spike, falling as a single unit. Flower with glumes broadly oval and strongly concave, with numerous nerves, the 3 lower glumes 2–3 mm long, sterile, the fourth 3.5–4.5 mm long, enveloping the single flower. Achene ellipsoid to linear–oblong, approximately 2.5 × 0.7–0.8 mm, brown to blackish.

## Distribution

very widespread in tropical regions; common in Gabon, along the coast, found in Estuaire, Nyanga and Ogooué-Maritime (15 collections)

## Ecology

dunes and sandy shores; near sea level

## Vernacular names

beachstar (angl.)

## Uses

Plants are capable of stabilizing dunes and coastal sands. b: Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al. (2010), Hooper & Napper (1972), bibliography Sosef & van der Maesen (1997)
