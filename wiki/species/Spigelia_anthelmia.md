# *Spigelia anthelmia* Linné

**Genus**: [[Spigelia]]
**Family**: [[Loganiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Sp. Pl.: 149 (1753)

## Description

— PROGEL, l.c. : 262. — L. B. SMITH, l.c. : 98. — LEEUWENBERG, l.c. : 461, fig. 1. — FWTA ed. 2, 2 : 45.

Annual herb, 20–50 cm tall; stem and branches terminated by a whorl of 4 leaves and a few inflorescences.

Corolla lilac, pink, or white, or tube white and lobes pale pink, with or without 5 pairs of longitudinal lines inside, at the base of the lobes, 8–17 mm long, glabrous on both surfaces.

Capsules green, composed of two ellipsoid parts, with an impressed line in the middle, distinctly tuberculate, scabrous; the base remains naviculate or almost fusiform. Seeds distinctly tuberculate. Pl. 14, p. 43.

## Type

herb. Linné 201.2, Hortus Uppsala (lectotype : LINN)

## Discussion

Tropical America; introduced as a naturalized plant in tropical Africa.

## Specimens examined
