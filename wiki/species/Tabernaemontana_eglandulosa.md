# *Tabernaemontana eglandulosa* Stapf Planche 113 ; Figure 13(A, B)

**Genus**: [[Tabernaemontana]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1894: 24 (1894)

## Description

Gabunia eglandulosa (Stapf) Stapf, Fl. trop. Afr. 4(1) : 138 (1902). Liane reaching 40 m in height; branchlets glabrous. Leaves with one pair equal or slightly unequal; petiole of 4–8 mm, glabrous, ochreous, visible; limb elliptic to narrowly elliptic of 8–18 × 3–7 cm, cuneate to rounded at the base, acuminate at the apex, glabrous on both faces or slightly pubescent below, with minute scattered black dots below; secondary nerves 4 to 10 pairs, curved, generally visible. Inflorescence sometimes ramified terminal with 2–4 stamens × 3–5. Calyx and pistil × 3–5. Pistil × 3–5. Anthers seen ventral and dorsal (× 4). (1 : Bos 4191 ; 2-6 : Leeuwenberg 11028 ). Drawing by Piet Kostense, Naturalis Biodiversity Center (©), reproduced from Leeuwenberg (1991).

Condensed with 2–8 flowers pedicellate of 2–4 mm, glabrous, bracteate, much smaller than the calyx lobes. Flower with sweet perfume; pedicel of 3–10 mm, glabrous; calyx with lobes of 2.5–7 × 2–3 mm, ciliate, otherwise glabrous, without colleters inside; corolla white, often with tube partially greenish to be button purplish nearly cylindric and with obtuse head, broadly ovoid or subglobulous, sometimes the length of the button, the exterior glabrous or pubescent at the base of the part of the lobes not covered in button, the interior slightly hairy up to the throat or the base of the lobes; tube subcylindric of 45–60 mm × 2.5–3.5 mm, reflected on 3–4 mm of width at the level of the insertion of the stamens, widening near the throat, often twisted at the base; lobes obliquely elliptic, more or less curved of 8–12 × 3–5 mm, equaled and curved back later; stamens deeply included, inserted near the base of the tube, sessile anther, narrowly triangular, long of about 7 mm, sagittate at the base, acuminate at the apex, glabrous; pistil long of 4–5 mm, glabrous, with apex near the middle of the anthers; ovary with carpels barely connate at the base. Fruit: mericarps free or sometimes united at the base, ovoid or subglobulous, of 3–8 × 2–7 cm, rounded or with two obtuse angles, yellow or orange, with several seeds, with exocarp often more than 1 cm in thickness. Seed long of 9–12 mm, with 7 or 8 shallow longitudinal striae, with minute honeycomb structure. Distribution: Benin, Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Bioko and Rio Muni, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo and northern Angola in Gabon. Estuary, Ngounié, Ogooué, Ivindo, Ogooué, Lolo, Ogooué, Maritime and Woleu-Ntem. Ecology: Primary and secondary forest in Gabon up to 600 m of altitude, elsewhere up to 800 m.

## Source note

In the revision of Tabernaemontana (Leeuwenberg 1991), T. eglandulosa included T. brachypoda and T. crispiflora. The two species are here considered as distinct. B : Boiteau & Allorge (1981), Leeuwenberg (1991), Stapf (1902). BIBLIOGRAPHY
