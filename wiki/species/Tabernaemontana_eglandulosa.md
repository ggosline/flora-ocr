## Description

Condensed with flowers pedicellate of ± mm glabrous bracteate much smaller than the lobes of the calyx. Flower with sweet fragrance; pedicel of 3–10 mm, glabrous; calyx with lobes of 2.5–7 × 2–3 mm, ciliate, otherwise glabrous, without colleters within; corolla white, often with tube partially greenish at bud, nearly cylindric and with obtuse head broadly ovoid or subglobulous, twice the length of the bud; the exteriority glabrous or pubescent; the base of the lobe portion uncovered at bud; the interior sparsely hairy up to the throat or the base of the lobes; tube subcylindric of 45–60 mm × 2.5–3.5 mm, reflected on ± mm width at the level of the insertion of the stamens, widening near the throat, often twisted at the base; lobes obliquely elliptic more or less curved of ± to ± mm, petalled and later recurved; stamens deeply included, inserted near the base of the tube, anther sessile, narrowly triangular, long of ± 7 mm, sagittate at the base, acuminate at the apex, glabrous; pistil long of 4–5 mm, glabrous, with apex near the middle of the anthers; ovary with carpels barely connate at the base. Fruit: mericarps free or sometimes united at the base, ovoid or subglobulous, of 3–8 × 2–7 cm, rounded or with two obtuse angles, yellow or orange, with several seeds, with exocarp often more than 1 cm thick. Seed long of 9–12 mm, with 7 or 8 shallow longitudinal striae, with tiny honeycomb-like structure. Distribution: Benin, Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Bioko and Rio Muni, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo and the north of Angola; in Gabon: Estuaire, Ngounié, Ogooué, Ivindo, Ogooué, Lolo, Ogooué, Maritime and Woleu-Ntem. Ecology: primary and secondary forest in Gabon up to m of altitude elsewhere up to m.

## Source note

In the revision of Tabernaemontana (Leeuwenberg 1991), T. eglandulosa included T. brachypoda and T. crispiflora. The two species are here considered as distinct. B: Boiteau & Allorge (1981), Leeuwenberg (1991), Stapf (1902). BIBLIOGRAPHY
