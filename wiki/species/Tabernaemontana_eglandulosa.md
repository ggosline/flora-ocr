# *Tabernaemontana eglandulosa* Stapf Planche 113 ; Figure 13(A, B)

**Genus**: [[Tabernaemontana]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1894: 24 (1894)

## Synonyms

- Gabunia eglandulosa (Stapf) Stapf, Fl. trop. Afr. 4(1) : 138 (1902).

## Description

Liane attaining 40 m in height; branchlets glabrous. Leaves of one pair equal or slightly unequal; petiole of 3–8 mm, glabre, ochre-visible; limb elliptic to narrowly elliptic of 8–16 × 3–8 cm, cuneate at base, acuminate at apex, glabrous on both faces or slightly pubescent beneath, with minute scattered black dots beneath; secondary nerves 4 to 10 pairs, curved, generally visible. Inflorescence sometimes ramified terminal with stamens 3–5 × calyx and pistil 3–5 × anthers viewed ventral and dorsal (× 4). (1: Bos 4191; 2-6: Leeuwenberg 11028). Drawing by Piet Kostense, Naturalis Biodiversity Center (©), reproduced from Leeuwenberg (1991).

Condensed with 3–8 flowers pedicellate of 2–4 mm, glabrous, bracteate, much smaller than the calyx lobes. Flower with sweet fragrance; pedicel of 3–10 mm, glabrous; calyx with lobes of 2.5–7 × 2–3 mm, ciliate, otherwise glabrous, without colleteers inside; corolla white, often with tube partially green at the bud nearly cylindrical and with obtuse head, broadly ovoid or subglobulous, sometimes 1.5 times the length of the bud; the exterior glabrous or pubescent at the base of the part of the lobes not covered in bud; the interior slightly pilose up to the throat or the base of the lobes; tube subcylindrical of 45–60 mm × 2.5–3.5 mm, reflexed on 3–5 mm width at the level of the insertion of the stamens, widening near the throat, often twisted at the base; lobes obliquely elliptic, more or less curved of 14–21 × 8–12 mm, staminate and recurved later; stamens deeply included, inserted near the base of the tube, anther sessile, narrowly triangular, long of ± 7 mm, sagittate at base, acuminate at apex, glabrous; pistil long of 4–5 mm, glabrous, with apex near the middle of the anthers; ovary with carpels barely connate at base. Fruit: mericarps free or sometimes united at base, ovoid or subglobulous, of 3–8 × 2–7 cm, rounded or with two obtuse angles, yellow or orange, with several seeds, with exocarp often more than 1 cm thick. Seed long of 9–12 mm, with 7 or 8 faint longitudinal striae, with minute honeycomb structure. Distribution: Benin, Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Bioko and Rio Muni, Gabon, Republic of Congo, Democratic Republic of Congo and northern Angola; in Gabon: Estuaire, Ngounié, Ogooué, Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem. Ecology: Primary and secondary forest in Gabon up to 800 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1 m.

## Source note

In the revision of Tabernaemontana (Leeuwenberg 1991), T. eglandulosa included T. brachypoda and T. crispifolia. The two species are here considered as distinct. B: Boiteau & Allorge (1981), Leeuwenberg (1991), Stapf (1902). BIBLIOGRAPHY
