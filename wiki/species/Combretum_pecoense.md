# *Combretum pecoense* Exell

**Genus**: [[Combretum]]
**Family**: [[Combretaceae]]
**Protologue**: Journal of Botany 66, Supplement Polypet.: 165 (1928); EXELL & GARCIA, CFA: 54 (1970). TYPE: Angola, Congo (Kinshasa), Peco (fl., fr., juv.) Gossweiler 8991 (holo-BM; iso-K, LISJC)

## Description

TYPE: Angola, Congo (Kinshasa), Peco (fl., fr., juv.) Gossweiler 8991 (holo-BM; iso-K, LISJC).

– C. youngii Exell, Journal of Bot. 72: 42 (1934); LIBEN, FZ: 44 (1968), syn. nov. TYPE: Congo (Kinshasa), Mangoa R, Dilolo, Lulua (fl.) Young 196 (holo-BM).

– C. excelsum KEAY, Kew Bull.: 131 (1952); KEAY, FWTA, ed. 2, 1(1): 270 (1954), syn. nov. TYPE: Nigeria, Cross River North FR, environ 22 km to the E. of Ikom (fl., fr.) Keay FHI 28147 (holo-K; iso-B, BM, BR, P).

Liane ≤ 30 m.

Leaves opposite; petiole ≤ 1 cm; blade lanceolate ≤ 17 × ≤ 7 cm; midvein depressed above; major lateral nerves 7–12 pairs, nerve III parallel, base of blade cordate to subcordate; apex acuminate. Young leaves reddish.

Scales circular, diameter 40–50 µm.

Inflorescences in axillary spikes, rachis ≤ 20 cm; velvety; bracts scarcely visible.

Flowers 4-merous, sessile, fragrant. Receptacle pale greenish: the lower one quadrangular, height 1.5–3 mm, the upper one subpatellifom, height 2 mm, width 4 mm. Calyx: lobes deltoid, acute, length 1.5 mm, both faces pubescent. Petals whitish, subcircular, diameter 1.5 mm, exterior puberulent. Stamens, 1 mm long; anthers yellow, 0.5 mm long. Disk papillose, glabrous. Style 1.5 mm.

Fruit winged, pubescent. - Pl. 19, 4, 5, p. 77.

Three species are brought together here, which differ only in leaf shape and indumentum; each is represented by one or two collections. The material from Gabon, quite homogeneous, differs from that of other countries only in narrower leaves. No fruiting material is known from Gabon.

## Distribution

from Nigeria, Gabon, Congo (Kinshasa), and Angola
