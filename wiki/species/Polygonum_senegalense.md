# *Polygonum senegalense* Meisn.

**Family**: [[Polygonaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Polygonum]]

## Description

Glabrous erect perennial whose 2–3 m stems trail in water and root at lower nodes. Petiolate ovate-lanceolate leaves 15–33 × 3–7 cm are long-acuminate, with bristles on the midrib and margin; ochrea truncate and eciliate. Two to five cylindrical spikes are 4.5–9 cm. Flowers pink, 3 mm, with 5–7 stamens and two styles. Achene compressed, nearly orbicular and glossy.
