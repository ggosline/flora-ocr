# *Bolbitis acrostichoides* (Afz. ex Sw.) Ching

**Genus**: [[Bolbitis]]
**Family**: [[Lomariopsidaceae]]

## Synonyms

- Hemionitis acrostichoides Afz. ex Sw., Schrad. Journ. 1800 (2) : 17 (1801).
- Acrostichum Afzelli Carr., Cat. Welw. 2 (2) : 277 (1901).
- Leptochilus longiflagellatus Bon., Not. Ptér. 4 : 68 (1917).
- Campium longiflagellatum (Bon.) C. Chr., Cat. Pl. Madag.: 32 (1932).

## Description

— Ching in C. Chr., Ind. Suppl. 3 : 47 (1934). — Tard., Mém. IFAN 28 : 115, pl. 19, f. 4 (1953). — Alston, FWTA : 68 (1959).

Rhizome creeping, with fronds crowded together, bearing lanceolate, peltate scales formed of cells with brown walls and pale lumen. Stipe stramineous, dull, canaliculate, 20-30 cm long, bearing scales only at the extreme base. Lamina 20-30 cm long by 20-25 cm, deltoid-lanceolate, pinnate. Terminal pinna similar to the lateral ones, lanceolate, bearing a bud beneath the very attenuate apex, sometimes very elongately proliferous, reaching 10-15 cm long. Lateral pinnae 7-10 pairs, alternate, free, shortly petiolulate, linear-lanceolate, 10-15 cm long by 2-5 cm wide, with cuneate or truncate bases, apex longly acuminate, margins entire or irregularly sinuate-dentate. Texture coriaceous. Veins raised on the lower surface, the lateral ones very prominent, the veinlets forming between them three or four rows of irregular areoles, without included veinlets; costal areoles distinct. Sterile frond of similar size, with pinnae 0.5 cm wide.

From Guinea to Congo, Fernando Po, Angola, Uganda, Madagascar.

Roadsides or even forest streams up to 1,000 m.

## Specimens examined

N. Hallé : 2300, Abanga, monts de Cristal.
