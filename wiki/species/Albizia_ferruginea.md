Tree reaching a height of 45 m. Bole 18 m tall for a diameter of 95 cm, lacking buttresses. Bark dark grey, rough, fissured, very scaly, with rhytidome detaching in irregular plates; inner bark fairly thick, yellowish to brownish, granular and brittle, exuding fairly abundantly a brownish to reddish gum. Branches spreading horizontally. Crown dome-shaped. Mature branchlets glabrous, dark brown to blackish with lighter reddish-brown lenticels. Young branchlets sparsely puberulous reddish. Petiole sparsely puberulous reddish, 2.5–4.5 cm long; upper surface sometimes canaliculate, bearing approximately at mid-length a pleated conical gland, very prominent. Rachis reddish puberulous, 2.5–6 cm long; upper surface flat or canaliculate ending in a prominent conical gland. Pinnae 3–7 (9) pairs, oblong elliptic. Pinna axis reddish puberulous, 6.3–18.8 cm long; upper surface ± keeled bearing 2–3 glands at the level of insertion of terminal leaflets. Leaflets 6–20 pairs, subpetiolulate. Lamina appressed-pubescent on both surfaces; matt brown-green above and paler below on dried material; elliptic to oblong elliptic, reaching 2.5 × 1 cm; base asymmetric: proximal edge rounded and distal edge attenuate; apex obtuse shortly apiculate. Primary midvein pubescent on both surfaces; weakly prominent to concave above and strongly prominent below. Secondary veins alternate 6–7 pairs, pubescent and prominent on both surfaces. Tertiary veins in a network with large meshes, weakly prominent on both surfaces.

Inflorescences in capituliform spikes axillary, solitary or fasciculate. Peduncle reddish puberulous, 6.5–7.8 cm long. Bract densely pubescent on both surfaces, elliptic to lanceolate. Bracteole pubescent on both surfaces, lanceolate sometimes symmetric, 4–6 mm long, base narrowly tapered and apex acute. Floral bud ellipsoid. Flowers ♠, white to greenish-white on dried material. Pedicel pubescent, 1.5 mm long. Basal flower with calyx densely pubescent externally and on the inner face of the lobes; narrowly obconical, ± 6 mm long; lobes free with acute apex. Corolla pubescent on the upper 1/2, ± 10–11 mm long, with petals fused into a tube for ± 6 mm; free lobes narrowly lanceolate, 4–5 mm long, apex acute. Stamens 4.5–5.5 cm long; united in a tube 5–6 mm long; anther oblong ± 0.25 mm long. Pistil very shortly stipitate; ovary glabrous to weakly shortly partially pubescent, 2.5 mm long, containing 6 ovules; style slender ± 30 mm long. Apical flower sessile. Calyx cylindrical, pubescent, ± 6.5 mm long. Corolla 11 mm long, as above. Stamens 4 cm long, fused in a tube for 1.8 cm. Pistil absent.

Legume glabrous (sometimes glabrate on the base and edges), brown to yellowish reddish on dried material, oblong, 15–24 × 3–5 cm; base weakly stipitate or rounded; lateral face with moderately to weakly dense venation slightly prominent, perpendicular to the sutural edges; apex apiculate. Seeds orbicular to elliptic 7–10 × 4.5–8 mm; areole elliptic oblong, central.

Seedling with hypocotyl 4–5 cm long; pubescent especially at the apex. Cotyledons yellowish-greenish with violet-edged on living material, fleshy, rounded, 1.2 × 0.9 cm, flattened, early caducous. Epicotyl with reddish pubescence, 1 cm long. Leaves 1 and 2 opposite: one pinnate compound leaf with 5–6 pairs of leaflets and one bipinnate compound leaf with 2 pairs of pinnae with 4–5 pairs of leaflets. Petiole, rachis, lower face of lamina and stipels pubescent. Leaflets (basal pair often reduced to one leaflet or to stipels), elliptic, 1.2 × 0.5 cm, base asymmetric rounded (According to G. DE LA MENSBRUGE, GPEACI, 1966). – Pl. 37, p. 149.

## Type

Leprieur s.n., Gambie : Albreda (holo-, G)

## Discussion

The pubescence of the leaves, which normally constitutes a reliable diagnostic character with A. coriaria Welw. ex Oliv., can sometimes be notably reduced. It then becomes very difficult to distinguish these two species. The colour of the flowers, of the fruits; the inner bark surface then permit identification.

The specimen Letouzey 1274 from Cameroon is a young plant of A. ferruginea found near specimen Letouzey 1273. It presents a vegetative apparatus very different from that of adults with leaflets of decreasing size from apex to base, hirsute pubescence on the entire foliar apparatus; stipels at the base of the pinnae, and fewer glands.

The range of this species extends from Senegal to Uganda to the east and to Angola to the south. It is an essence of semi-deciduous dense forest that penetrates into evergreen dense forest on the occasion of clearings.
