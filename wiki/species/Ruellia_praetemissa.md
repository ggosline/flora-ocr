# *Ruellia praetemissa* Schweinf. ex Lindau

**Genus**: [[Ruellia]]
**Family**: [[Acanthaceae]]
**Protologue**: Schweinfurth ex Lindau, Bot. Jahrb. 20 : 15 (1894)

## Synonyms

- Ruellia patula auct., Burkill, l. c. (1899), quoad specimen Brass (Sierra-Leone); Hutch. et Dalz., F. W. T. A., ed. 1,2 : 246 (1931), non Jacq., Misc. Bot. 2 : 358 (1781).

## Description

Herbaceous plant, prostrate, fairly branched; stems subquadrangular, hairy with small curled and appressed hairs, fairly dense; internodes 2–4 cm long. Petioles 1–2 cm long; the leaves of small specimens and at the shoot apices are frequently more or less subsessile. Leaf blade ovate-acuminate, rounded at the base, with obtuse or rounded acumen, 3–8.5 × 1.7 × 4 cm (maximum size in the type); upper surface dark green or somewhat greyish, lower surface lighter; blade very sparsely hairy on both surfaces (short, thick and translucent hairs; these hairs are absolutely absent in the type specimens of R. patula Jacq.). 6–7 lateral nerves on each side (4–5 in R. patula Jacq.), anastomosing at a distance of 1–2 mm from the margins.

Flowers axillary, solitary, nearly subsessile (the Testu 5075); pedicels up to 5 mm long (in the typical specimens 2–2.5 cm); the flowers are inserted on small axillary branchlets whose leaves are much smaller than those of the main stem. Bracteoles elliptic, $ 8 \times 2.5 $ mm (Le Testu 5075), and in the typical specimens $ 8–17 \times 5–8 $ mm, hairy, especially in the lower part and at the margins, with the same hairs as those on the leaves. Calyx 5.5 mm long, with sepals 0.5 mm wide, narrowly lanceolate, acuminate, glabrous, fused for a length of 1 mm at the base. Corolla white (Le Testu 5075) approximately 13 mm long; the throat at anthesis about the same diameter; corolla lobes $ 3 \times 3 $ mm (Lindau indicated, for the two syntypes, Schweinfurth 2155 and 3789, $ 7 \times 7 $ mm). Filaments of the stamens attached to the middle of the corolla tube, fused two by two at the base for about one-third of their length; long filaments with free part 4 mm, and in the short filaments, 3 mm (Schweinfurth 2155 and 3789, 6 and 3 mm). Anthers 1.7 mm long, linear. Disk very short, approximately 0.7 mm high. Ovary approximately 1 mm long, glabrous; style 10 mm long, provided with a few very sparse small hairs; stigma with one large lobe $ 10 \times 0.5 $ mm, spoon-shaped, the other lobe very reduced and almost invisible.

Capsule fusiform 10 × 3 mm, glabrous, acuminate (Schweinfurth 2155 and 3789, 14 × 4 mm). Approximately 8 round seeds, flattened and approximately 2.5 mm in diameter (Lindau's type; Le Testu 5075 has only a single fruit, open and devoid of seeds).

## Type

Schweinfurth 2155, 3754, 3789 (région des sources de Ghasal, Sudan) (B, syntypes détruits; P, iso-syntypes!)

## Discussion

The distribution of this critical species is insufficiently known; according to the results of the latest revision (Heine, 1963) it occurs in Sudan, Ubangi, Chari, Senegal, Mali, Sierra Leone, Ivory Coast, Dahomey, Cameroon and Gabon.
