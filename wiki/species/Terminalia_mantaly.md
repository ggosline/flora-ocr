## Description

Ann. Mus. Col. Marseille, ser 7, 1(1) : 24 (1953) ; PERRIER DE LA BÁTHIE, Fl. Madag. 151 : 50 (1954) ; CAPURON, Bull. Mus. Nat. Hist. Paris 3(191) : 99 (1973) ; LIBEN, Fl. Cam. 25 : 79 (1983) ; BJBB 54 : 290 (1984). TYPE : Madagascar, plateau de l'Ankarana, Perrier 1322 (holo-P). LIBEN (1983) in selecting Grève 30 as lectotype of this species, ignored the revision of Terminalia from Madagascar by CAPURON (1973) where the discovery of the holotype of Terminalia mantaly is explicit.

Trees, height ≤ 20 m.

Leaves on spur-shaped shoots at the extremity of lateral branches and in the axil of long zigzag shoots; petiole ≤ 3 mm; lamina obovate cuneiform, ≤ 7 × ≤ 3 cm, almost glabrous; major veins 5–6 pairs, domatia with short hairs; base of lamina attenuate; apex emarginate.

Flowers 5-merous, sessile. Receptacle inferior almost or entirely absent on male flowers. Calyx: lobes triangular, much longer than wide. Stamens 4.5 mm long.

Fruit ellipsoid, 1.8 cm long, without wings or ridges but superior receptacle persistent. – Pl. 31, EH, p. 105.

## Distribution

native to Madagascar, cultivated as an ornamental tree in most countries of tropical Africa, and very probably also in Gabon, although no collection from that country could be found in the herbaria

## Ecology

near watercourses and lakes
