# *Bulbophyllum cocoinum* Lindl.

**Genus**: [[Bulbophyllum]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Reg. 23 : Pl. 1964 (1837)

## Description

– Bulbophyllum andongense Rchb. f., Flora 48: 184 (1865).

– Bulbophyllum vitiense Rolfe, Kew Bull. 79: 5 (1893).

– Bulbophyllum brevidenticulatum De Wild., Bull. J. Bot. Brux. 5: 179 (1916).

Pseudobulbs 2–5 × 1–2.7 cm, ovoid to narrowly ovoid, 1-leaved. Leaf petiolate, 9–27 × 1.2–3.5 cm, lanceolate, sometimes broadly linear, acuminate to acute. Inflorescence 9–38 cm, rather dense or lax, rachis glabrous, 15–150-flowered. Flowers small, white. Sepals glabrous, often pink toward the apex, narrowly triangular acute; dorsal sepal, sometimes ovate-linear to broadly linear-lanceolate and subacute; lateral sepals sometimes broadly linear-lanceolate and acuminate. Petals narrowly oblong to oblong-obovate, obtuse to acute, irregularly denticulate and finely papillose at the apex. Lip glabrous, white or creamy, recurved, ovate to oblong-ovate, obtuse, rather thin, with basal thickening and short papillae near the margins. – Pl. 95, p. 191.

## Distribution

Sierra Leone, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Gabon, Angola, Congo (Kinshasa), Uganda

## Ecology

alt. 0–2000 m. Epiphyte on tree branch; lowland to submontane forest; primary forest periodically flooded by Gilbertiodendron
