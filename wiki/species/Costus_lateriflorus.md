# *Costus lateriflorus* Bak.
**Family in Vol. 9**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Current placement**: Costaceae · **Genus**: [[Costus]]
## Description
Possibly scrambling herb with broad glabrous lanceolate leaves 12–17 × 6–8 cm and a thin tubular ligule at least 2 cm long. The dense lateral raceme has pink flowers and a 4.5 cm yellow, red-spotted labellum with crisped-wavy margins.
## Distribution
Known only from its type, Mann 1629 from Corisco Bay, Río Muni (Equatorial Guinea); no Gabon occurrence is asserted by the treatment.
