# *Diospyros discolor* Willd., Spec. Plant. 4 : 1108 (1805).

**Genus**: [[Diospyros]]
**Family**: [[Ebenaceae]]

## Description

— D. Blancoi A. DC., Prodr. 8 : 237 (1844).

Species native to the Philippines, with edible fruits and also yielding ebony wood ("camagon"), characterized by its large oblong leaves (22 × 9 cm), with rounded base, acute apex, with numerous lateral nerves, covered beneath with a bed of long appressed irritating setae, bearing glands aligned on either side of the midvein, by its inflorescences apparently terminal, by its flowers with calyx and pubescent corolla deeply lobed into 4–5 lobes, its 24–28 glabrous stamens and by its densely hairy fruit, globose, 8–12 cm in diameter, containing 4–6 seeds, with calyx little accrescent.

Specimen: Chalot s. n., ann. 1907.

2. Diospyros Ebenum Koenig, Physiogr. Salsk. Handl. 1 : 176 (1776).

Species native to India, Malaysia and Indo-Malaysia, yielding prized ebony wood ("Ceylon ebony"), characterized by its small oblong leaves (9 × 3 cm), with cuneate base, obtuse apex, with 6–8 pairs of ascending lateral nerves little prominent, puberulent beneath, bearing numerous scattered glands, by its small pedunculate puberulent inflorescences, by its flowers in conical bud, with calyx and corolla

clearly lobed into 4 lobes, its 16–32 glabrous stamens and by its glabrous fruit, globose, 2.5 cm in diameter, with calyx rather accrescent.

Specimens: Chalot 26 and 50, ann. 1899; Klaine 2009, ann. 1900.

EXCLUDED SPECIES

1) Diospyros oblongicarpa Gürke, Bot. Jahrb. 43 : 200 (1909).

This taxon, described from the specimen Zenker 3471 from Cameroon, was mentioned by error under the name of Maba oblongicarpa Gürke in Supplement 4 of the Index Kewensis (p. 146, 1913). According to MILDBRAED, Notizbl. Bot. Gart. Berl. 9 : 1055 (1926), it is in reality Isolona Zenkeri Engl. (Annonaceae) also known from Gabon.

2) Diospyros pachylla Gürke, Bot. Jahrb. 46 : 152 (1911).

, synonym of Rhaptopetalum pachyphyllum (Gürke) Engl. (Scytopetalaceae), species represented in Cameroon and Gabon.

3) Diospyros rosea Gürke in Engler, Wiss. Ergebn. Deutsche Zentral-Afrika Exped. 2 : 525 (1913).

, synonym of Rhaptopetalum roseum (Gürke) Engl. (Scytopetalaceae), species which appears to be represented in Cameroon.
