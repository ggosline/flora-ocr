# *Hypoestes aristata* (Vahl) Soland. ex R. et S.

**Genus**: [[Hypoestes]]
**Family**: [[Acanthaceae]]
**Protologue**: Solander ex Roem. et Schult., Syst. Veg. 1 : 140 (1817)

- Hypoestes insularis T. Anders., Journ. Linn. Soc. Bot. 7 : 49 (1863). — C. B. Clarke, l. c. (1900).

## Description

≡ Justicia aristata Vahl, Symb. bot. 2 : 2 (1791).

≡ Hypoestes aristata var. Barteri (T. Anders.) R. Benoist, var. insularis (T. Anders.) R. Benoist, var. kikuyensis R. Benoist, var. Letestui R. Benoist, and var. Staudtii (Lindau) R. Benoist, l. c.: 244 (1942).

Annual plant, erect, 0.60–1 m tall, with indumentum quite variable, ± glabrous in Gabon specimens; stems green, obtusely quadrangular, somewhat swollen below the nodes. Petioles 1–3 cm long. Leaf blade ovate-acuminate, 10–15 × 3–6 cm, entire at the margins, acuminate at the base, dark green above, somewhat paler below. Approximately 7 pairs of lateral veins.

Flowers in rather dense axillary and terminal fascicles; the terminal inflorescences form, in some individuals, a ± spiciform pyramid reaching up to 30 cm long; the fascicles are composed of 1–3 flowers of which one or two are sterile and much smaller than the fertile flowers. Bracts triangular-lanceolate, subulate, 1–1.5 (3) mm long and 0.5–0.8 mm wide at the base; outer bracteoles lanceolate, longly acuminate, concave, 12–16 × 1–2 mm, somewhat united at the base, with acumen longly attenuate into an awn; inner bracteoles lanceolate-subulate, acuminate, 2 × 0.5–0.8 mm. The sterile flowers are accompanied by inner bracteoles much smaller which correspond to the dimensions of their calyx. Calyx diaphanous-hyaline, 7 mm long, with segments united in their lower half, and with free portion acuminate-subulate. Corolla approximately 2.5 cm long, rose-purple, glandular-pubescent on the exterior, with tube approximately 12 mm, very narrow, infundibuliform with opening approximately 2 mm in diameter; lips as long as or longer than the tube; the flowers are resupinate: the upper lip becomes the lower lip, it is linear-lanceolate, acuminate, striped with white, 12 × 2 mm; the lower lip becomes the upper lip, it is elliptic-oblong, with three ovate-acuminate lobes 2 mm long, with lateral lobes striped, and central lobe somewhat ciliate, with dark purple spots. Stamens longly exserted, 20 mm long, with flattened filaments, finely and sparsely pubescent on the lower two-thirds; anthers with one oval locule, 1.5 × 0.5 mm. Style 25 mm long, glabrous.

Fruit glabrous 12 × 2 mm, acuminate. Seed lenticular 2 × 1.6 mm, with smooth testa.

## Type

v. Bülow s. n. (C, holotype de Vahl) « Caput bonae spei ». Fréquent, en Afrique tropicale et australe; connu sous de nombreux noms. La séparation de six variétés (var. macrophylla, insularis, kikuyensis, Letestui, Staudtii, et Barteri) a été proposée par R. Benoist, mais elle semble artificielle et impossible à justifier vis-à-vis du polymorphismisme de cette espèce
