# *Acioa mannii* (Oliver) Engler

**Genus**: [[Acioa]]
**Family**: [[Scytopetalaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. 26 : 381 (1899); Pflanzenw. Afr. 3 (1) : 311 (1915); DE WILD., BJBB 7 : 210 (1920); KEAY, FWTA, ed. 2, 1 (2) : 433 (1958)

## Description

Pflanzenw. Afr. 3 (1) : 311 (1915); DE WILD., BJBB 7 : 210 (1920); KEAY, FWTA, ed. 2, 1 (2) : 433 (1958).

— Griffonia mannii OLIV., FTA 2 : 372 (1871).

Scandent shrub, 4–6 m high. Traces of whitish arachnoid tomentum on young branchlets, these rapidly glabrous and sparsely lenticellate. Stipules asymmetrically ovate with acute apex, 3–4 × 6 mm, ascending venation clearly marked, margin sometimes slightly denticulate. Petiole 8 mm, tomentellous then glabrescent. Limb elliptic, 5–9 × 12–18(–20) cm, sometimes somewhat oblong (6 × 18 cm), sometimes somewhat obovate; base and apex broadly acute to obtuse, acumen elongate (1 cm); lateral veins 6–7(–10) pairs, inclined at 45° to the midvein, curved ascending towards the margin; limb glabrous; 2 large circular glands towards the end of the base of the limb beneath, a few small glands higher up beneath the base of the limb, similarly beneath the apex and acumen.

Inflorescences cymose terminal and axillary-subterminal, 8–12 cm high, open, branched 1, 2 (3) times, entire inflorescence externally puberulent; peduncle 15–60 mm, sometimes bearing deeply tricuspid bracteoles with median tooth longest and furnished externally with a few circular glands; primary axes 10–20 mm, secondary axes 5–10 mm; portion of pedicels below the articulation 15–25 mm. Bracts and bracteoles broadly ovate with acute apex, 2–3 mm and 1 mm high respectively, margin sometimes slightly denticulate; bracteoles alternate, spaced 1–3 mm apart, attached near the middle of the portion of the pedicel below the articulation, the tip of the latter thus extending well beyond the upper bracteole. Pedicel of the articulated flower 2–8 mm. Receptacle 14–20 mm, gibbous at the base; sepals relatively narrow, 6–7 mm high, puberulent on the parts exposed externally in the bud and sometimes here furnished with a lateral circular gland near the base; petals broadly obovate with broadly acute to subcordate base, 4 mm high; androecium 30–40(–50) mm, ca. 30 filaments.

Infructescence and fruit unknown. — PL. 15, p. 51.

## Type

Mann 1427, Fernando Po (holo-, K; iso-, P!)

## Discussion

PHYTOGEOGRAPHIC NOTES:

Acioa mannii (Oliv.) Engl. is known only from Macias Nguema (formerly Fernando Po) and the surroundings of Mount Cameroon.

## Specimens examined

Babute 9, Bay Manya, 15 km SW Kumba (fl., févr.) (FHO). Maitland 467, près Buea, 1000 m (fl., déc., janv., P, ou mars, K); s.n. (467?), ibid. (fl., déc., janv.) (K). Staudt 513, près du lac, Johann-Albrechtshöhe (actuel Kumba) (fl., janv.); 888, ibid. (fl., mars) (K). Winkler 1104, Sonje-Pflamung (fl., févr.) (cit. in FWTA: 433).
