# *Adenia cynanchifolia* (Benth.) Harms Planche 25.2, 27

**Genus**: [[Adenia]]
**Family**: [[Passifloraceae]]

## Description

in Engl. & Prantl., Nat. Pflanzenfam. 3, 6a, Nachtr. 1 : 255 (1897). Herbaceous to woody vine, reaching 10 m in length. Stipules rapidly caducous or absent, reduced to a minute lacerate line (max. 0.5 mm in length). Leaf with petiole 0.5–4(–8) cm; lamina ovate-elliptic to oblong-ovate, 3.5–13 (–18) × 1–8.5 cm, cordate at base, acute to long-acuminate at apex, papyraceous, concolorous, midvein more pronounced than lateral veins; gland at base of lamina simple spatulate, laminar glands minute, very numerous, scattered; tendrils sterile simple to 2–3-fid, up to 15 cm. Inflorescence arranged at apex of lateral branches, generally without tendrils, the male one with up to 20 flowers, the female one with 1–3 flowers; peduncle 0.5–2.5 cm; bracts and bracteoles narrowly triangular, 0.5–1 mm in length, acute at apex. Male flower with pedicel 0.5–8 mm and stipe 1.5–3 mm, ± campanulate, 5–6 mm in length; hypanthium cupuliform, shallow, 0.5–1 mm in height, partitions inconspicuous; sepals free, oblong, 4.5–5 mm in length, subacute at apex, clearly punctate; petals obovate-oblong, 5–6.5 mm in length, ± obtuse at apex, with finely denticulate margins, sparsely punctate; corona absent or little developed and with 5 fleshy cuculliform organs, 0.3–0.5 mm in height, hypanthium glands absent; stamens inserted at base of hypanthium, filaments 2–2.5 mm, connate along almost entire length, anthers 2.5 mm, dark yellow to orange; pistillode 0.5 mm in height. Female flower as male flower, but with pedicel 1–6 mm and stipe 0.5–1 mm, approximately 6 mm in length; hypanthium patelliform, 0.5 mm in height, partitions absent; sepals 5–5.5 mm in length; petals narrowly obovate-oblong, 4.5 mm in length, greenish white, with yellowish margins, punctate at apex; corona formed of 5 fleshy cuculliform organs approximately 0.5 mm in height, hypanthium glands absent; staminodes approximately 0.5 mm; ovary ellipsoid, 2 × 1.3 mm, densely punctate, gynophore 0.5–0.8 mm; styles 0.5 mm, stigmas sessile, subreniform, 1–1.5 mm in diameter, lacerate-papillose. Fruit 1–3 per inflorescence, ellipsoid, 1.5–2 cm in length, with scarious-coriaceous pericarp, smooth to slightly hairy at apex, sometimes with numerous small punctations; gynophore 1–2 mm. Seeds numerous per capsule, 4 × 3 mm, elliptic, glossy black, with a light brown line on one side, surrounded by a transparent and orangeish aril.

Ruth van Crevel, Naturalis Biodiversity Center ©.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea (Bioko), Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Democratic Republic of the Congo; common in Gabon, present in almost the entire country (not yet observed in Estuaire and Nyanga)

## Ecology

Ombrophilous forests, forest galleries, forest regrowth, savannas; in Gabon, up to 900 m elevation, elsewhere up to 1050 m. Flowers from September to June; fruits observed in February, March, July and November. b : De Wilde (1971), Hutchinson & Dalziel (1954), Robyns (1995). Bibliography
