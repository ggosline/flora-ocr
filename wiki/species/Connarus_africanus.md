Liane or lianescent shrub. Branches cylindric lenticellate, young branches with brown pubescence, soon glabrescent.

Leaves trifoliolate, rarely upper leaves unifoliolate; petioles 2.5-14 cm long and rachis 1.5-4 cm long, with brown pubescence initially. Leaflets papyraceous, the lateral ones opposite, ovate or elliptic, (4-)6.5-25 × 2-9.5 cm, rounded at base, acuminate ≤3 cm, glabrous on both surfaces (sometimes slightly pubescent initially) ; midvein impressed above and prominent below with (3-)4-7 pairs of lateral veins, venation III reticulate generally distinct on both surfaces ; petiolules 5-7 mm long, often wrinkled, glabrous.

Inflorescences in panicles ≤35 cm long, ≤50-flowered, with dense brown pubescence. Bracts, as well as reduced leaves at the base of the inflorescences, ovate, ≤1.5 mm long. Pedicels short, articulate 0-1.5 mm below the calyx, with brown pubescence.

Flowers. Sepals in imbricate prefloration, ovate, thin or somewhat fleshy at base, (2.1-2.7-4 × (1-1.2-2 mm, obtuse to rounded, rarely acute at apex, ± flat, external brown pubescence, interior glabrous or pubescent, punctate with glands, often indistinctly ≤7-nerved. Petals generally coherent (rarely completely free), in imbricate prefloration, narrowly elliptic to narrowly obovate, 5.3-7.5 × 1-2 mm, cuneate at base, obtuse to rounded at apex, exterior glabrous or with scattered pubescence, interior glabrous or with glandular hairs, generally punctate with glands near apex, 3-7-nerved sometimes indistinctly. Stamens, 0.5-1.2 mm, united at base ; long stamens 2.6-3.9 mm or 5-7.9 mm, filaments with stipitate glands, anthers 0.3-0.5 × 0.2-0.3 mm ; short stamens 1.2-2.7 mm, filaments devoid of glands, anthers generally somewhat smaller but apparently fertile. Pistil 2.5-3.9 mm or 5-7 mm long ; ovary 1-1.5 mm long, with brown pubescence ; style pubescent at base and with stipitate glands ; stigmas oblique, ± 2-lobed, sometimes distinctly papillose.

Fruit: follicle narrowly obovoid, slightly oblique, scarcely compressed, 4.3-6.2 × (1.2)-1.5-2.4 cm, with stipe (2)-3-5(-7) mm long ; apex acute or with an erect mucro ≤2 mm long ; pericarp

fairly thin, glabrous and without visible nerves but surface slightly striate, interior glandular.

Seed (ob)ovoid, 17-28 × 10-16 × 7-10 mm, sarcotesta 10-18 mm long enveloping the basal part of the seed ; radicle dorsal, 1/4-1/2 of the seed length from the apex. - Pl. 20, p. 71.

Germination hypogeal.

## Type

Smeathman s.n., Sierra Leone

## Distribution

From southern Senegal to Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea and São Tomé

## Ecology

Ombrophilous forests, often near rivers, sometimes in savanna groves

This species has not yet been collected in Gabon but its presence there is very probable.
