# *Macropodiella heteromorpha* (Baillon) C.Cusset Planche 22

**Genus**: [[Macropodiella]]
**Family**: [[Podostemaceae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia, sér. 2, 17(3) : 298 (1978)

## Synonyms

- Sphaerothylax heteromorpha Baill., Bull. Mens. Soc. Linn. Paris 110 : 876 (1890).
- Macropodiella mildbraedii Engl., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 60(5) : 466 (1926).

## Description

Plant with simple or rarely branched stem, 20–50 cm in length. Leaves regularly spaced from base to apex, deeply divided into linear to filiform lobes, up to 25 mm in length. Inflorescence with solitary flowers or grouped by 2 or 3 in the leaf axils; spathelles obovoid, 3.5–4.5 mm in length, tearing at anthesis irregularly at the apex into 4 or 5 teeth; pedicel 1.5–2 cm after anthesis; tepals acicular, 0.3–0.6 mm; stamens (2–)3, filaments fused for ± ⅔ of their length, anther 0.7–0.8 × 0.2–0.4 mm; ovary ellipsoid, gynophore 0.2–1.6 mm in length; stigmas crested, sessile. Seed 0.16 × 0.09 mm.

## Distribution

Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroun and Gabon; in Gabon, known only from Ogooué (OgoouéIvindo), but according to Walker and Sillans (1961), the species also grows at the Samba Mavotsi falls (Empress Eugénie Falls) in the Ngounié at Fougamou (prov. Ngounié). Uses: According to Raponda-Walker & Sillans (1961), the plants are sold in the market of Brazzaville (Republic of the Congo) for eating.
