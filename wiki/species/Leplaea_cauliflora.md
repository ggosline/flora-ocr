# *Leplaea cauliflora* E.J.M.Koenen & J.J.de Wilde Planche 11

**Genus**: [[Leplaea]]
**Family**: [[Meliaceae]]
**Protologue**: Pl. Ecol. Evol. 145(2) : 216 (2012)

## Description

Pl. Ecol. Evol. 145(2) : 216 (2012). Small tree, often monocaulous, 3–5 m tall; bark orange-pink, fragrant. Leaves clustered at the stem apex or toward the branch extremities; petiole 8–22 cm; rachis (12–)18–35 cm; petiolule 5–10 mm, reaching 20–35 mm on the terminal leaflet; leaflets 9–11(–15), opposite to subopposite or rarely alternate, narrowly rectangular to narrowly ovate or elliptic, 12–35.5 × 4–9 cm, the upper ones usually the largest, with cuneate to narrowly cuneate base, acute to almost caudate apex, glabrous; venation prominent, 9–19 pairs of lateral nerves and well-marked veinule network, sometimes scalariform on both surfaces. Inflorescences in very short fascicles, directly on the trunk, approximately 1.5 cm long, puberulent. Functionally male flower: very fragrant; pedicel articulate, 2–3 mm, bracteolate; calyx more or less deeply 3–4-dentate, 1–2 mm tall, puberulent; petals ovate to rectangular or narrowly rectangular, 7.5–8.5 × 3.5–4.5 mm, externally puberulent margin; staminal tube 5–6.5 mm long, with weakly 8–10-lobed or -dentate border, glabrous, anthers 7–12; gynoecium 5.5–6.5 mm long, including the gynophore 0.5 mm long, glabrous. Functionally female flower very similar, with broadly ovoid ovary, weakly sulcate, 2.5 × 2 mm, glabrous. Infructescence usually limited to 1 or 2 cauliflorous fruits. Fruit on a robust stipe reaching 5 mm, globose or weakly 2–4-lobed, 3–3.5 cm in diameter, shortly apiculate, glabrous, orange-red at maturity. Seeds 1–4; cotyledons oblique.

## Distribution

Cameroon and Gabon; in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo and Woleu-Ntem
