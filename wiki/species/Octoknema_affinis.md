# *Octoknema affinis* Pierre

**Family**: [[Octoknemaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Octoknema]]

Bull. Soc. Linn. Paris **2**: 1290 (1897). — Van Tieghem, Bull. Soc. Bot. **19**: 45 (1905). — Mildbraed, Pflanzenfam., ed. 2, **16b**: 45 (1935). — J. Louis & J. Léonard, Fl. Congo b. et R.U. **1**: 290–292, tab. 30 (1948).

## Description

**Habit**: tree approximately 20 m tall with cylindrical bole 50 cm in diameter. Bark grey, scaly. Young branchlets pubescent with stellate hairs.

**Leaves**: petiole pubescent with stellate hairs, 1–4 cm long, swollen at apex, longitudinally striate. Blade glabrous on both faces or very sparsely pubescent (hairs often worn at apex, stellate, in depressions of the blade), yellowish-green to greyish-green on upper face, darker green on lower face, oblong, oblong-elliptic or elliptic-ovate, 12–34 × 5–12 cm; base rounded or attenuate, apex acute acuminate. Midrib concave or weakly concave above, prominent and ± pubescent beneath. Secondary nerves 6–9 pairs, ascending, arcuate, anastomosing 1 mm from the blade margin, concave above, prominent beneath. Nervules forming a fairly loose network, clearly visible on upper face, prominent on lower face.

**Male inflorescence**: many-flowered raceme, 2.5–9 cm long.

**Male flowers**: pentamerous, yellowish, 5 × 2.5 mm. Petals pubescent outside (stellate hairs) and pubescent inside (simple hairs), fused and reflexed perpendicularly, broadly ovate, with obtuse apex. Stamens 5, epipetalous, free; filament broad, inserted at the base of the disc; anthers introrse with 2 basifixed locules. Disc present. Pistillode with truncate style.

**Female inflorescence**: axillary few-flowered raceme, 0.5–2 cm long.

**Female flowers**: pentamerous, white, 5 × 2.5 mm. Pedicel short. Calyx absent. Petals fused, pubescent outside (stellate hairs), glabrous inside, lanceolate, reflexed, with obtuse apex. Staminodes 5, epipetalous, 1 mm long, free. Disc very attenuate or absent. Ovary inferior with 3 uniovulate locules. Style short, broad, conical, wrinkled, terminating in a stigma with 5 digitations, sometimes bifid.

**Fruit**: drupe ovoid, broadly pubescent. Exocarp orange. Mesocarp ± thick. Endocarp woody, emitting 8–10 meridional lamellae towards the albumen, reducing to 5 towards the apex; external face with 8–10 ribs. Albumen abundant. Embryo apical.

## Holotype

*Klaine 2471*, Gabon (P!).

## Distribution

**Range**: exclusively Guinean, from Cameroon to Zaire (Democratic Republic of the Congo), localized along river margins in forests and strictly linked to forest galleries in savanna.

**Gabon**: Ogooué-Ivindo (Libreville); Ogooué-Lolo (Lastoursville).

## Habitat and ecology

Riverine forest and forest gallery in savanna. Heliophilous but tolerating shade readily; can flower and fruit under tree canopy.

## Notes

**Authority and status (2011).** The authority is **Pierre ex van Tieghem (1905)**, not Pierre alone: Villiers cites it as *O. affinis* Pierre and also, wrongly, as the type species of the genus — the type is *O. klaineana* Pierre. Gosline & Malécot (2011) confirm the species in Gabon near Libreville, and note that Central African material has more often than not been labelled *O. affinis* while in fact covering several taxa; the recognition of that confusion prompted the revision.

Revision: Gosline G. & Malécot V. (2011). A monograph of Octoknema (Octoknemaceae — Olacaceae s.l.). Kew Bulletin 66: 367–404.


*O. winkleri* Engl. and *O. aruwimiensis* Milbr. are treated as synonyms of *O. affinis*. The treatment confirms that types of *O. winkleri* and *O. affinis* do not differ substantially.
