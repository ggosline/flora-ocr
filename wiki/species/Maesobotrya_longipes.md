# *Maesobotrya longipes* Hutch. Planche 13

**Genus**: [[Maesobotrya]]
**Family**: [[Phyllanthaceae]]

## Description

in Dyer, Fl. trop. Afr. 6(1.4) : 670 (1912). Shrub 2.5–4 m tall; trunk up to 5 cm diameter; branchlets pubérulent, rapidly glabrescent. Stipules early caducous, narrowly triangular, 2–4 mm long. Leaf: petiole (2–)5–9 cm, glabrescent; blade ± narrowly elliptic to oblong, (6–)15–31 × (3–)6–10 cm, obtuse to rounded at base, acuminate over 1.5–2 cm, entire-margined, ± glabrous, with small glands fairly numerous, ± scattered, ≤ 0.2 mm diameter; lateral nerves 8–11 pairs. Inflorescence on trunk, on old branches, more rarely at the same time in leaf axils, fasciculate or not, pubérulent. Male flower: pedicel 1.5–2 mm, ± glabrous; sepals 4–5(–6), often unequal, ± oblong, 1–1.5 mm long, glabrous or only ciliate; stamens 4–6, 2–3 mm long; disk lobed, sparsely hairy. Female flower: pedicel 1–2 mm, pubérulent to glabrous; sepals ≤ 1 mm long, glabrous or only ciliolate; ovary 2–3-locular, about 2 mm long, glabrous or slightly pubescent with ± appressed hairs. Fruit ellipsoid to globose, 5–11 × 4–7 mm, ± 3-ribbed, glabrous or with some hairs, red. Seeds 1–3; sarcotesta white.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Central African Republic, Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, recorded from Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

primary and secondarized forest; in Gabon, at 0–550 m altitude. Taxonomic note: M. longipes resembles closely M. pynaertii (De Wild.) Pax, from the Democratic Republic of the Congo, an earlier species dating from 1910. Apart from its different habitat (periodically flooded forest), M. pynaertii possesses large glands with pilose margins on the underside of its leaves. b: Léonard (1994). Bibliography
