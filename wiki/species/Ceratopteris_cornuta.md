# *Ceratopteris cornuta* (Beauv.) Leprieur

**Genus**: [[Ceratopteris]]
**Family**: [[Adiantaceae]]

## Synonyms

- Pteris cornuta P. Beauvois, Fl. Oware 1 : 63, t. 37, f. 2 (1809).
- Ceratopteris Gaudichaudii Brünner, Reise Senegamb.: 127 (1838).

## Description

— Lepr., Ann. Sc. Nat. sér. I, 19 : 103, t. 4 A (1830). — Tard., Mém. IFAN 28 : 31 t. 2 f. 3 (1953). — Alston, FWTA : 38 (1959).

Aquatic ferns, annual. Fronds dimorphous, irregularly pinnate, the sterile ones broader. Herbaceous texture. Veins anastomosing. Sporangia situated along the veins, protected by the reflexed margin of the lamina, bearing 30 to 70 thickened cells.

## Type

Palisot de Beauvois, royaume d'Oware, S Nigeria (P). Du Sénégal au Moyen Congo, Zanzibar, Madagascar

## Discussion

In the waters of marshes, lakes and rivers, rooting in mud.

## Specimens examined

Griffon du Bellay : 6, sans loc. N. Hallé : 1537, Port-Gentil; 1801, Ayem, 10 km SW de Ndjolé. Walker : sans n., Fernan-Vaz.
