# *Iridosma le-testui* (Pellegr.) Aubr. et Pellegr.

**Family**: [[Simaroubaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Iridosma]]

## Synonymy

- *Mannia Le-Testui* Pellegr.

## Description

Tree. Leaves glabrous, imparipinnate, with 11 pairs of leaflets; rachis to 60 cm. Leaflets subsessile, opposite or subopposite, oblong, shortly acuminate, asymmetric and rounded at the base, 8–15 × about 6 cm, coriaceous; 10–15 pairs of lateral veins prominent beneath; veinlets weak.

Panicles lax, branches puberulous; bracts lanceolate, hairy, about 5 mm. Bisexual flowers in spaced fascicles; pedicels pubescent, about 3 mm. Calyx pubescent, irregularly undulate, about 2 mm high. Petals (7–)8, oblong, hairy on both faces, valvate with inflexed margins in bud. Stamens 12–13, nearly as long as the petals; filaments 6–6.5 mm with a hairy basal ligule; anthers small, elliptic, versatile, 0.6 mm. Disc hairy. Carpels 4, free and hairy; four twisted styles fused into a glabrous style, stigma 4-pointed. Fruit unknown.

## Distribution

Known only from the type collection at Péca, Haute-Ngounié, Gabon.

## Habitat and ecology

The type was flowering in April. Le Testu noted that the flowers were strongly fragrant, with the scent of iris.

## Specimens examined

**Gabon** — Le Testu 6488 (holotype, Péca, Haute-Ngounié).
