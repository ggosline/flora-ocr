# *Baphia buettneri* Harms

**Genus**: [[Baphia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 26 : 281 (1899)

## Description

Small tree reaching 15 m in height; trunk up to 17 cm in diameter; branchlets glabrous or glabrescent. Stipules oblanceolate to obovate, 2–6 mm long, ciliate. Leaf: petiole with contiguous pulvinus, (4–)5–7 mm, glabrous; leaflet with ± elliptic blade, (5–)6–10(–14) × 2–5(–6) cm, ± rounded at base, apex acuminate for 0.5–1.5 cm, glabrous; lateral veins in 5 to 8(–9) pairs. Inflorescence axillary, in fascicle or raceme, up to 4-flowered and ± 2 cm long, sparsely pubescent; bracts 1–2 mm long. Flower: bracteoles at apex of pedicel, ± reniform, 0.5 mm long, ciliate; pedicel 0–6(–7) mm, glabrous; calyx spathate, ± 12 × 12 mm, glabrous except for a few short apical hairs; standard elliptic, 15 × 10 mm, wings narrowly elliptic, ± falcate, 14–16 × 5 mm, scarcely clawed, keel petals slightly falcate, 13 × 4–5 mm, with claw 1 mm; stamens 8–12 mm long, anthers 2 mm long; pistil ± 12 mm long, ovary velvety, with 6 ovules. Pod oblong, 6–12 × 2–2.5 cm, rounded at base, sparsely pubescent, glabrescent. Seed not seen.

## Distribution

Gabon and Republic of Congo; in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Ogooué-Ivindo and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

primary and secondarized forest; at 0–500 m altitude
