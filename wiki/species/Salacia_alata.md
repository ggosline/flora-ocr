# *Salacia alata* De Wildeman

**Genus**: [[Salacia]]
**Family**: [[Celastraceae]]
**Protologue**: Mission Laurent 1 : 241 (1906) ; HALLÉ, Mon. Hippocr. Afr. occid., Thèse : 180 (1958) ; Mém. IFAN 64 : 183 (1962) ; WILCZEK, FCB 9 : 222 (1960)

## Description

1. Wings of internodes straight or weakly undulate ..... 24a. var. alata. 1'. Wings of internodes undulate or crisp ..... 24b. var. superba.

24a. var. alata

Liane 3–30 m tall, glabrous and devoid of rubber. Internodes microrugulose with 4–5 straight, wide wings 0.5–2 mm broad. Leaves opposite or subopposite, more rarely alternate. Petiole 4–10 mm, with undulate margins of the channel. Lamina olivaceous above, often rust-coloured below, elliptic 6–26 × 2.5–8 cm, with acute base or, on the largest leaves, obtuse or rounded, with moderately acuminate apex, with regular and numerous marginal teeth. Midvein prominently keeled above; (6)–8–12(–15) pairs of secondary nerves; tertiary nerves moderately distinct.

Inflorescences axillary in few- to many-flowered glomerules. Flowers rotate green, yellow, yellow-orange or salmon, 6–8.5 mm in diameter. Pedicel (3)–6–11 mm, smooth or micropapillose. Bud ovate 2.5–3.5 mm broad. Sepals slightly unequal, obtuse or rounded, entire or ± denticulate, 1.5–2 mm broad. Petals briefly oblong and ± angular, arched on the upper face, 3–4 × 2.5–3.5 mm. Disc subpentagonal, in a fleshy crown with slight subcupuliform or patelliforn rim, marginate (papillose zone ± distinct), 2–3 mm in diameter and without a perigynous groove. Stamens spaced at the base, 1–2 mm long, curling back on themselves. Anther with adjacent locules arranged at an obtuse angle, 0.5–1 mm broad. Pistil pyramidal attenuate, 1.3–2 mm tall, with punctiform or trilobate stigma. Locules more than half-immersed, each containing 4–5(6) ovules.

Fruit orange-red or coral, spherical, 3–5 cm in diameter, adorned with a ± dense network of small ridges or crisp wrinkles, with pericarp 2 mm thick when dry, with ± numerous seeds. Seed $ 18 \times 15 \times 8 $ mm, with cotyledons broadly united all around. – Pl. 32, fig. 17, 19, 21, 23, 25, p. 119.

24b. var. superba Hallé

Bull. Mus. natn. Hist. nat. Paris 30 (5): 469 (1958); Mém. IFAN 64: 184 (1962).

Shrub 3–5 m or liane exceeding 5–20 m. Wood exceeding 5–6 cm in diameter is adorned in cross-section with concentric rings rather regularly spaced 3–5 mm apart. Internodes remarkably winged with undulate wings 2–3 mm broad. Base of lamina obtuse, rounded to subcordate. Lamina often large between 14 and 28 cm in length, with 10–15 pairs of secondary nerves.

Inflorescences many- to multiflorous forming up to spherical clusters 3–4 cm in diameter. Flowers green, yellow-green, or ochreous orange becoming reddish at the end of anthesis; floral characters little different from the typical variety but with sepals more distinctly denticulate to ciliate, and ovules numbering 4–6(7) per locule.

Fruits 4–6 cm in diameter, with relief sometimes submuriculate, sometimes rather ridged. – Pl. 32, fig. 16, 18, 20, 22, 24, 26, 27, p. 119; pl. 41, fig. 14, p. 143.

Very humid forests, often in ripicole stations, in Nigeria (Brennan 8393, van Meer 1298), Cameroon and Gabon.
