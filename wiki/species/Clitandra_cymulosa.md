# *Clitandra cymulosa* Benth. Planche 26 ; Figure 4(B, C)

**Genus**: [[Clitandra]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: C. arnoldiana De Wild., Compt. Rend. Hebd. Séances Acad. Sci. 136 : 400 (1903)

## Description

in Hook., Niger Fl . : 445 (1849). C. arnoldiana De Wild., Compt. Rend. Hebd. Séances Acad. Sci. 136 : 400 (1903). Large liana, reaching 40 m in height; branches with dense lenticels; branchlets glabrous, with few or no lenticels. Leaf: petiole 5–12 mm, glabrous or with scattered glandular hairs; blade oblong to elliptic, 2–6 times as long as wide, 5–35 × 2–12 cm, cuneate at the base, acuminate at the apex, glabrous above, with scattered glandular hairs below, especially on the midvein and near the base; secondary veins 6 to 16 pairs, curved towards the margin, tertiary venation reticulate. Inflorescence with 1–25 flowers, 1.5–9.5 cm long; axes 1–25 mm, pubescent. Flower strongly fragrant; calyx with lobes 1–2.4 times as long as wide, 1–3 × 1–2 mm, ciliate; corolla white, rosy or yellowish, 4–7 mm long in the mature bud, tube 3.1–6.4 mm long at the insertion level of the stamens, 0.6–2 mm wide at the throat, entirely hairy on the exterior or only in the lower part; lobes 2–4 times as long as the tube, 1.1–5.2 × 0.5–1.8 mm, rounded at the apex, hairy to entirely glabrous; stamens inserted 1–3 mm from the base of the tube, anther 0.3–0.5 mm long, apiculate at the apex, with a few hairs; pistil 1.5–3 mm long, ovary with a ring of short hairs at the apex; style 0.2–0.4 mm, glabrous. Fruit globose to pyriform, up to 4.5 cm in diameter, yellow or orange; exocarp with brown lenticels or not, with a sclerified layer with 2–4 grains surrounded by a fibrous, red, acid pulp. Seed ovoid, 15–35 mm long, blackish; testa thin, smooth; endosperm 2 mm thick. Distribution from Guinea to Tanzania and south to Angola, in Gabon: Estuary, Haut-Ogooué, Ngounié, Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo and Woleu-Ntem. Ecology: primary or secondary forest in Gabon, up to 800 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1200 m. Use: The fruits, very acidic, are edible. The latex produces black rubber and it is also used as an intestinal enema.
