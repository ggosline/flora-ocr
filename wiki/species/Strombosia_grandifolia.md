# *Strombosia grandifolia* Hooker fil.

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Strombosia]]

Niger. Fl.: 258 (1849).

## Description

**Habit**: tree or shrub 4–20(–30) m tall. Trunk usually 5–6 m high and 10–60(–80) cm in diameter. Bark smooth, slightly rough, grey-brown, red-brown or greenish, exfoliating in oblong plates. Inner bark thin and rough, longitudinally striate. Crown broad, formed by 3–4 large branches with irregular branching. Young branchlets grey-green or pale green, weakly longitudinally striate. Older branchlets almost entirely smooth, brownish. Internodes 2–2.5 cm long.

**Leaves**: petiole glabrous, thick, nearly cylindrical, canaliculate above, finely striate below, 0.8–3 cm long. Blade papyraceous, glabrous on both faces, dark green or grey-green and shiny on the upper face, dull on the lower face, oblong-lanceolate, ovate, oblong-elliptic, sometimes elliptic or oblong; margins recurved, base cuneiform, obtuse or even rounded; apex acuminate, apiculate and mucronulate. Midrib concave above, prominent below. Secondary nerves 4–7 pairs, ascending, arcuate, with poorly visible anastomoses, concave above, prominent below. Nervules fine, arcuate, parallel to one another, spaced at most 2 mm apart, anastomosing with one another, weakly prominent on the upper face, prominent on the lower face, paler below.

**Inflorescence**: flowers grouped in multiflowered axillary fascicles (5–30 flowers) on a persistent grey-whitish cushion formed by bracteoles. Pedicel finely longitudinally striate, 2–4 mm long. Bracteoles ovate, membranous, ciliolate on the upper margin.

**Flowers**: flower bud ovoid to globose, 2 × 1.5–2 mm. Flowers creamy-white or greenish. Sepals fused at the base, deltoid, ciliolate on the upper margin, apex obtuse. Petals glabrous on the exterior, pubescent on the upper third of the inner face, valvate, veined with red, oblong-elliptic, 1.5 × 0.5 mm; apex acute, reflexed and papillose. Stamens 5, epipetalous, with filiform filaments fused along their entire length to the petals; anthers with 2 locules with ± lateral longitudinal dehiscence slits. Disc forming an indistinct cap above the ovary, ± pentalobate. Ovary semi-inferior, ovoid or subglobose, with 3–4 locules each containing 1 pendulous ovule from an erect central placenta. Style short and channelled, glabrous, stout, terminating in a stigma with 3–5 papillose lobes.

**Fruit**: fruit pedicel strongly accrescent, nearly completely surrounding the drupe except at the apex. Apical depression surrounded by the remains of 5 sepals and containing the style remains. Drupe ovoid or ellipsoid, 1–1.3 × 0.7–2 cm. Exocarp black, granulous. Endocarp hard, obovoid, with horny albumen and depressed apex. Embryo solitary, apical, with thick radicle.

**Seedling**: young seedling presents a characteristic swelling at the base of the hypocotyl. Cotyledonary leaves have a petiole less than 1 cm long.

## Phenology

Flowers practically year-round, with maximum flowering from December to March and July to August.

## Habitat and ecology

Tree of the lower and middle storeys of evergreen rainforest. Frequent around lakes and along riverbanks, where it has the appearance of a shrub or small tree. Collected to 1100 m altitude.

## Distribution

**Range**: from Dahomey to Zaire (Democratic Republic of the Congo).

**Gabon**: Libreville area and surroundings (Ogooué-Maritime); Lastoursville, Tchibanga, and Dibadiba (Ogooué-Ivindo).

## Vernacular names

*Boulou*: edipbazoa, mbangmbazoa, mbazoa mbazoa. *Bakwiri*: ifondo, wofondo. *Bipinde*: mbasa. *Yaoundé*: mbazog. *Fang*: attévo, mbazoge (general); esonesoua, essonoussoua, n'sone-esoue (Lake Ayem area); ezipt, ezypt (Libreville area). *Bayaka, N'komi*: kamba, mogameba, nkameba.

## Uses and properties

Wood and sapwood are differentiated. Heartwood is violet-red with fine grain, very homogeneous; sapwood is paler and thin. The wood provides good building material, easy to saw and very durable. Used for railroad ties and tool handles.

## Notes

*S. grandifolia* shows wide variation in leaf form and dimensions. Fruit size can differ considerably between individual trees. The holotype is Vogel 156 from Fernando-Po.
