# *Renealmia africana* (K. Schum.) Benth.

**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Renealmia]]

## Description

Large-tufted herb to 2 m. Petiole to 7–8 cm; narrow glabrous blade to 60 × 10 cm. Rather dense panicle 30–40 cm on a grey sheathed peduncle to 30 cm; rachis red and finely pubescent. Secondary clusters subsessile, 3–4-flowered. Pedicels and funnel-shaped 6–9 mm calyx red-orange; labellum white, pink-striped. Capsule black, elongate-elliptic, 9–10 mm; aril orange.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Congo and Angola.
