# *Polygonum acuminatum* H. B. et K.

**Family**: [[Polygonaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Polygonum]]

## Description

Robust, little-branched herb to 1.5 m. Nearly sessile lanceolate leaves 9–22 × 1–3 cm are long-attenuate below, acuminate and setose-pubescent; ochrea appressed-setose with a ciliate, stiff-bristled rim. Two to four dense cylindrical spikes per stem, 4–10 × 0.6–1 cm. Pink flowers have 4–5 perianth lobes and two styles. Achene lenticular, 2 mm, black, smooth and glossy.
