# *Histopteris incisa* (Thbg.) J. Smith (Pr. XII)

**Genus**: [[Histopteris]]
**Family**: [[Dennstaedtiaceae]]

## Synonyms

- Pteris incisa Thunberg, Proc. Fil. Cap.: 17 (1800).

## Description

— J. Sm., Hist. Fil.: 295 (1875). — Tard., Mém. IFAN 28 : 67 (1953). — Alston, FWTA : 34 (1959).

Rhizome slender, elongately creeping, scaly. Frond glabrous, with indefinite growth, capable of reaching several metres long, often climbing, 2-3 pinnate. Pinnae opposite, sessile, with pinnules opposite, sessile, 2-7 cm long, acute apex, margins lobed, the first pinnules of each pinna adnate to the rachis, reduced, stipuliform. Rachis often glaucous. Veins anastomosing or free.

Tropical species. Lit places or stream margins.

## Specimens examined

N. Hallé : 2294, Abanga, monts de Cristal. Nicklès : sans n., sans loc.
