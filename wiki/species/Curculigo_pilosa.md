# *Curculigo pilosa* (Schumacher & Thonning) Engler

**Family**: [[Hypoxidaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Curculigo]]

Veg. Erde 9 (2): 353 (1908).

## Synonymy

- *Gethyllis pilosa* Schumacher & Thonning, Beskr. Guin. Pl.: 172 (1827)
- *Curculigo gallabatensis* Schweinf. ex Baker, Trans. Linn. Soc. London, ser. 2, 1: 266 (1878)

## Description

Rhizome elongate up to 15 cm, approximately 1–2 cm in diameter, bearing persistent brown fibrous leaf bases, whitish cataphylls and leaves. Leaves subpetiolate with sheathing bases 3 cm wide and extending up to 50 cm, with prominent veins, folded longitudinally; fine pubescence with weak white hairs.

**Inflorescence**: Floral scape reaching 5 cm. Flowers developed before the leaves.

**Flowers**: Tepals bright yellow, 10–25 × 3–6 mm, the outer slightly broader than the inner; abaxial pubescence. Filaments 2–3 mm long; anthers up to 2 times as long. Style 8–10 mm. Underground ovary approximately 2 cm long with an apical beak 3–10 cm, completely surrounded by 2 involucral bracts, 2–5 cm, the outer much broader than the inner.

**Fruit**: Capsule narrowly adapted to the seeds, 2–4 × 0.5–1 cm.

**Seed**: Seeds ellipsoid, approximately 3 mm long.

Most of the plant is covered with long flexible hairs.

## Distribution

**Range**: Widely distributed, especially in the Sudanian domain of the Sudano-Zambezian savanna areas.

**Gabon**: Nyanga province (Tchibanga, Moanda-Franceville area).

**Ghana**: Type locality (non vidi).

## Habitat and ecology

Occurs in semi-wooded and wooded savannas and appears to be favoured by fire.

## Notes

The type specimen from Thonning (Ghana) was not seen by the author. The species is noted in the treatment as being widely distributed in the Sudanian savanna domain, though the material examined here comes only from Gabon.
