# *Oricia gabonensis* Pierre

**Family**: [[Rutaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Oricia]]

## Description

Tree with compound leaves and separate male and female inflorescences. Flowers are 4-merous. The fruit divides into mericarps, commonly with three abortive units; each stone contains a strongly unequal-cotyledoned embryo. The treatment illustrates flowering and fruiting material collected by Klaine.

## Distribution

Gabon.
