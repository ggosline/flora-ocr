# *Inversodicraea paulsitae* (C.Cusset) Cheek Planche 12

**Genus**: [[Inversodicraea]]
**Family**: [[Podostemaceae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull. 72(4) : 55, 2 (2016)

## Description

Kew Bull. 72(4) : 55, 2 (2016). Ledermanniella paulsitae C.Cusset, Bull. Mus. natl. Hist. nat., sér. 4., Adansonia 5(4) : 378 (1983). Plant with a ribbon-like thalloid basal portion, 0.8–1 cm wide; stem erect, generally unbranched, 10–15(–20) × 0.5–0.6 cm, floating, fleshy, without foliaceous scales; short branchlets arranged along the stem, grouped in 3 to 6, 2–3 mm long, covered with foliaceous scales. Foliaceous scales densely imbricate, tridigitate, 1–1.5 × ± 0.3–0.4 mm. Leaves towards the apex of the stem, 4–6 cm long, several times pinnate, segments 0.8–1 cm long. Inflorescence: bracts pinnate with dichotomous segments, 4–5 cm long, caducous; spathe solitary at the apex of the branchlets, sessile, obovoid to subglobose, 1.2–1.5 × 0.8–1 mm, not exceeding or scarcely exceeding the foliaceous scales, tearing irregularly at the apex at anthesis. Flower: pedicel 4–5 mm after anthesis; tepals acicular, 0.8 mm; androecium equal or more often longer than the ovary, stamens 2, filaments ± 2 mm, fused for about ⅓ of their length, anther 0.8–0.9 × 0.3 mm, pollen in monads; ovary 1.8–2 × 0.7–1 mm, gynophore ± 0.6 mm long; stigmas filiform, 0.7–0.8 mm. Seed 0.4 × 0.2 mm.

## Distribution

Gabon and Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, known from the Ngounié

## Ecology

at 350–700 m altitude. B: Cheek et al. (2017), Cusset (1983), Sosef et al. (2006). Bibliography
