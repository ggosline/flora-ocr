# *Klainedoxa gabonensis* Pierre

**Family**: [[Irvingiaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Klainedoxa]]

## Description

Very large tree to 1.5 m diameter, trunk cylindrical with high thin buttresses. Branchlets reddish-brown, angular or flattened and geniculate at nodes, with annular stipule scars; stipules very acute, wine-red. Leaves alternate, simple, glabrous and coriaceous; typical blades broadly ovate to suborbicular, base obtuse or rounded, apex rounded or obtuse with a very short broad acumen, usually 10–12 × 6–7 cm, to 18 × 10 cm; petiole 4–6 mm. The var. *microphylla* has elliptic leaves about 5 × 3 cm, obtuse at both ends, petiole about 3 mm. Venation a dense uniform reticulum, conspicuous on both faces.

Flowers on glabrous 2–2.5 mm pedicels; sepals 5, ovate, strongly convex, 1.5–2.5 mm; petals 5, elliptic, deeply concave, about 3.5 mm; stamens 10, filaments 4.5–5.5 mm; disc glabrous; ovary 5-locular, each locule one-ovuled; style folded in bud. Fruit green, flattened-globose and slightly 5-lobed, to 12 cm diameter and 5 cm high, with fibrous mesocarp and five thick stones, each one-seeded.

## Distribution and ecology

In a broad sense widespread across Guineo-Congolian Africa from Guinea-Bissau to South Sudan and Uganda, south to Cabinda. Large- and small-leaved forms occur in Gabon. Bark grey and rather smooth, flaking in strips; slash gamboge to reddish-brown. Wood extremely heavy and hard.

## Specimens examined

The treatment lists extensive Gabon material from Libreville, Achouka, Oyem, Cap Estérias, Mayombe, Ndjolé, Kanda, Etoughi, Fernan Vaz, Mondah and the Ogooué. Holotype: Klaine 188, Libreville. Var. *microphylla* holotype: Le Testu 1823, Mayombe.

## Vernacular names

*eveuss* (Fang); *nkondjo*, *evel*, *moughoma nzaghari* (Bayaka).
