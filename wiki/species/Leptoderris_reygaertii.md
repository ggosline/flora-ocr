# *Leptoderris reygaertii* De Wild. Planche 76

**Genus**: [[Leptoderris]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Jard. Bot. État Bruxelles 7 : 238 (1920)

## Description

Liane; branchlets with puberulent hairs mixed with long hairs, becoming glabrous fairly early. Stipules ± persistent, narrowly triangular, 11–19 × 2–3 mm, striate. Leaf: petiole ± cylindric, 4–4.5 cm, glabrescent; rachis 4–9 cm, channelled above; stipels (3–)4–7 mm long; leaflets 9 to 11, with petiolule 2–4 mm, lamina ± narrowly obovate-elliptic, 3–10 × 1–3 cm, rounded to cordate at base, acute to rounded and with a mucro up to 5 mm at apex, ± glabrous below (young leaves completely covered with long appressed hairs, becoming glabrous fairly early); lateral veins in 6 to 9 pairs, fairly fine, slightly apparent above, prominent below. Inflorescence reaching 40 cm long; bracts and bracteoles narrowly triangular, 3–6 mm long. Flower: pedicel ± 1 mm; calyx ± campanulate, 5 mm long, with appressed hairs on both faces, lobes 1.5–2 mm long; corolla 7–8 mm long, white, petals of the same length, ciliolate at apex, with claw ± 2 mm; stamens ± as long as the keel, anthers ± 0.25 mm long; pistil with appressed hairs. Pod and seed unknown.

## Distribution

Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, known from a single collection from the Lastoursville region in Ogooué-Lolo

## Ecology

primary or secondarized forest; in Gabon at approximately 300 m altitude. b: Hauman (1954d). Bibliography
