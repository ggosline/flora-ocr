# *Aniseia martinicensis* (Jacq.) Choisy Planche 1

**Genus**: [[Aniseia]]
**Family**: [[Convolvulaceae]]
**Protologue**: Mém. Soc. Phys. Genève 8 : 66 (1838)

## Synonyms

- Convolvulus martinicensis Jacq., Select. stirp. amer. hist. : 26, t.17 (1763).

## Description

Climbing and/or creeping herb; branchlets sparsely pubescent, glabrescent, sometimes rooting in wet environments. Leaf: petiole 0.5–2 cm, sparsely pubescent; blade narrowly elliptic, sometimes ovate, 2.5–6(–7) × 0.5–2.5 cm, cuneate (to rounded) at base, obtuse and shortly mucronate at apex, with 5–7 pairs of lateral nerves, glabrous or with sparse marginal hairs. Inflorescence generally one-flowered, with ± sparse, short, ± appressed hairs; peduncle up to 4.5 cm long; bracteoles at summit of peduncle, 0.5–5 mm below calyx, narrowly triangular, 2 × 1 mm. Flower: pedicel 0–5 mm; sepals ovate, 10–15 mm long, slightly accrescent in fruit to 22 mm, pubescent without; corolla white, approximately 2 cm long and 2.5 cm wide, scarcely lobed, puberulent on median bands without; stamens: filaments approximately 4 mm, with enlarged base covered in glandular hairs, anthers erect; ovary ovoid, glabrous; style glabrous. Fruit ovoid, approximately 15 mm long, glabrous. Seeds approximately 4.5 mm in diameter, sparsely hairy.

## Distribution

pantropical; in Gabon, known from Ogooué-Maritime, Nyanga, Moyen-Ogooué, Ngounié and Ogooué-Ivindo

## Ecology

river and lake margins, wet sand banks, open forests on sand; in Gabon, at 100 m altitude, elsewhere in Africa, up to 700 m. B: Deroin (2001), Heine (1963), Lejoly & Lisowski (1993). Bibliography
