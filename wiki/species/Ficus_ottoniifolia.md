Epiphytic shrubs (stranglers), trees up to 15 m, or climbing plants, sometimes (?) the main branches with lateral branches subverticillate, young branches hollow. Leafy twigs approximately 2-10 mm thick, glabrous or very minutely puberulent, grayish to brown when dry, rhytidome often exfoliating on older parts. Leaves arranged in spirals. Blade often greenish when dry, elliptic to oblong most often widest at or below the middle, 3-) 8-22 cm long, (1.5-) 3-9 cm wide, papyraceous to subcoriaceous, apex acuminate, often abruptly so, base subacute to rounded, margin entire, lower surface sparsely puberulent to nearly glabrous; veins coarsely prominent to plane, the midvein often plane to coarsely concave above, veins ± prominent below; 5-10 pairs of lateral veins, including 1 (-2) pairs of basal veins, lateral veins fused in a loop 1-2 mm from the blade margin; glandular spot most often visible. Petiole 2-9 cm long, 1-2 mm thick, very minutely puberulent to nearly glabrous. Stipules free, 0.5-2 cm long, sparsely puberulent to glabrous, deciduous.

Figs up to 10 together in the leaf axils, on (small) spurs up to 4 cm long, approximately 1.5 cm thick below the leaves or lower on the trunk; peduncle 1-2.5 cm long, approximately 1 mm thick, puberulent; basal bracts 2, puberulent, the upper parts deciduous, leaving an oblique disc formed by the fused persistent parts; receptacle subglobose to ellipsoid, often ± narrowed at the base, 2-2.5 cm high when fresh, 2-2.5 (-3) cm when dry, 2-2.5 (-3) cm in diameter when fresh and 1-1.5 cm when dry, puberulent, pale orange-green or pale yellow with pale (green) spots or tinged reddish at maturity, wall approximately 1 mm thick when dry, ostiole small, bilabiate.

Pistillate flowers with a 3 (-4) -partite perianth, up to 2 mm high; seed flowers sessile or some or many of these with a pedicel reaching 2 mm, style approximately 2-2.5 mm long; gall flowers most often with a pedicel reaching 2 mm, style approximately 2 mm long. Fruits ellipsoid to subovoid, 2-2.5 mm long; gall fruits obovoid or ellipsoid and stipitate, 2.5-3.5 mm long. Staminate flowers sessile or pedicellate up to 1 mm, perianth approximately 3 mm high, tri- (tetra-) fid to partite, filament approximately 2.5 mm long, or sometimes up to 4.5 mm, sometimes flattened, anther 0.8-1 mm long. Interflorals bracts up to 2 mm long. Sometimes bisexual flowers present. - Pl. 59, p. 203.

Pollinators: Agaon camerunensis WIEBES (Gabon, Cameroun), also in F. polita?; Agaon bekiliensis bispinosum WIEBES (Gabon), Agaon bekiliensis was described from Madagascar.

This species (including F. ulugurensis WARB. and F. lukanda FICALHO) extends from Gambia to Tanzania and southward to northern Zambia and Angola, passing through Cameroun and Gabon; it is also present in Fernando Po. It is found in various types of ± open forests, often in secondary forests and forest galleries, at altitudes up to 1,650 m.

It can be subdivided into several subspecies. Material from Gabon and the synonymy belong to the subspecies ottonifolia.
