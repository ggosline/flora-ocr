# *Erythrina tholloniana* Hua Planche 65.11–13

**Genus**: [[Erythrina]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Mens. Soc. Linn. Paris, n.s. 1 : 53 (1898)

## Description

Tree 4–20 m tall; trunk with smooth bark, short spines; branchlets slightly spiny, spines straight or hooked. Stipules oblong-lanceolate, 6–10 × 2–4 mm. Leaf: petiole unarmed, 13–20(–32) cm; rachis 6–12 cm; stipels as small auricles of 1 cm; leaflets with petiolule of 8–12 mm, the lateral ones with broadly ovate or subrounded lamina, 8–24 × 10–27 cm, slightly oblique, papyraceous, with ochraceous pubescence, then glabrous, the terminal one with deltoid lamina, with obtuse or rounded apex and rounded to cordate base; lateral nerves 6 to 9 on each side, alternate. Inflorescence robust, 30 cm long, with numerous tightly packed flowers, barely pubescent. Flower: pedicel 2–5 mm; calyx spatulate, tube subcylindrical, falcate, 15–21 mm long, split nearly to the base, glabrescent, reddish, with 5 semi-circular lobes, ± equal, 1.5–2.5 mm long, fleshy; corolla reddish, emerging from the calyx slit, standard conduplicate, 4–5 × 1.5–2 cm, wings 12 mm long, keel with free petals, 8 mm long; stamens diadelphous (9 + 1), exserted, glabrous, the vexillar one fused for 15–18 mm; ovary lanceolate, with fine and dense pubescence; style glabrous. Pod moniliform with very deep constrictions, more or less spiralled, coriaceous, glabrous and shiny, with 7 to 12 articles, each article ± 2 cm in diameter and ± 5 mm thick.

Erythrina tholloniana: 11. Flower. – 12. Pod. – 13. Seed. (1–10: Wieringa et al. 3545; 11–13: Gillet 1606). Drawing by Hans de Vries (1–10) (11–13 by unknown artist), Naturalis Biodiversity Center (©) and Meise Botanic Garden (©).

Seed ellipsoid, 1.5 × 8–10 mm, dark brown or vermilion red; hilum elliptic, central, 1 cm or more, black.

## Distribution

Equatorial Guinea (Bioko), Gabon, Republic of the Congo (doubtful) and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, very rare, known only from Ogooué-Ivindo (Lopé Reserve)

## Ecology

secondary bush; in Gabon at ± 200 m altitude, elsewhere up to 500 m

## Source note

Species absent from the Check-list of vascular plants of Gabon (Sosef et al. 2006); Raponda-Walker & Sillans (1961) mention it without comment. Thollon's type was collected in the border region between Gabon and the Republic of the Congo. Bibliography: Majot-Rochez & Duvigneaud (1954), Pellegrin (1949), Raponda-Walker & Sillans (1961)
