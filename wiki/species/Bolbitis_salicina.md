# *Bolbitis salicina* (Hk.) Ching

**Genus**: [[Bolbitis]]
**Family**: [[Lomariopsidaceae]]

## Synonyms

- Acrostichum salicinum Hk., Sp. Fil. 5 : 265 (1864).
- Leptochilus diversibasis Bon., Not. Pter. 13 : 216 (1924).

## Description

— Ching, in C. Chr., Ind., suppl. 3 : 50 (1934). — Tard., Mém. IFAN 28 : 116, pl. 19, f. 5 (1953). — Alston, FWTA : 69 (1959).

Rhizome shortly creeping, with closely-spaced fronds, bearing lanceolate scales, emarginate at the base, formed of cells with dark-walled, colourless lumen. Stipe stramineous, canaliculate, glabrous, 8–14 cm long. Frond lanceolate, 18–20 cm long by approximately 10 cm broad, pinnate. Terminal pinna similar to the lateral ones, sometimes proliferous at the apex, decurrent on the rachis narrowly winged at its extreme apex. Lateral pinnae, 7–17 pairs, free (except sometimes the upper pair), alternate, the lower ones shortly petiolate, linear-lanceolate, 7–8 cm long by 0.5–1 cm broad, with a prolonged attenuate apex, bases symmetrical, oblique. Texture subcoriaceous. Veins forming one or two rows of areoles in addition to the costal areoles. Fertile frond of the same size as the sterile one, with pinnae longly petiolate.

## Type

Barter, Sierra-Leone (K)

## Discussion

From Guinea to Congo, Fernando Po.

Forest between 1000–1300 m, exondated rocks.

Le Testu : 1547, Ibanga; 1589, Midounga.
