# *Callichilia inaequalis* Stapf Planche 24

**Genus**: [[Callichilia]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. trop. Afr. 4(1) : 132 (1902)

## Description

Fl. trop. Afr. 4(1): 132 (1902). Liane or small shrub, reaching 10 m in length. Leaf: petiole reaching 4(–7) mm; leaflets obovate to oblong-obovate ± as long as wide or slightly longer, 4.5–13 × 2–8 cm, rounded to subcordate at the base, rather abruptly acuminate at the apex, with acumen reaching 12 mm, dark green and glossy above, paler beneath; lateral nerves in 5 to 14 pairs. Inflorescences in pairs or rarely solitary; peduncle 1–18(–23) cm; bracts few to numerous, at the apex of the peduncle or covering its upper part. Flower fragrant; pedicel 3–19 mm; calyx with unequal lobes, narrowly oblong to ovate, 1–3.3 times as long as wide, 5–11 × 3–6 mm, acute at the apex, at the interior near the base with 2 collateral biseriately arranged colleters; corolla in bud 0.9–1.3 times as long as the calyx, 6–9 cm long, corolla tube creamy and lobes white like snow; tube cylindrical below the insertion of the stamens, infundibuliform above, 1.5–2 times as long as the calyx, 1.8–2.2 times as long as the lobes, long 23–36 mm, at the interior with pilose striae 0.5–1 mm; lobes covering towards the left, 1–1.5 times as long as wide, long 20–38(–42) mm; stamens inserted from the sixteenth to one-third from the base of the tube, dark yellow, anther long 4–6 mm; style 14–19 mm. Fruit with carpels reaching 5 cm in length, yellow to orange, with beak reaching 1 cm.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon and Gabon; in Gabon only known from the Estuaire

## Ecology

forest edge, secondary forest; in Gabon and elsewhere at 0–200 m altitude. B: Beentje (1978). BIBLIOGRAPHY
