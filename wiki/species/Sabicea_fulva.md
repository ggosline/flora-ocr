# *Sabicea fulva* Wernh.

**Genus**: [[Sabicea]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Wernham, Monogr. Sabicea : 66 (1914)

## Description

Small vine exceeding 2 m. Internodes velutinous-fulvous 3–12 × 0.15–0.2 cm. Stipules reflexed, ovate, 4–9 mm long, with acute or obtuse apex, glabrous above. Petiole velutinous 3–11 (20) mm. Lamina ovate, discolorous, 4–10 × 2–4.5 (5.5) cm, pubescent-velutinous above and below; base rounded to ± cordate; apex ± attenuate, sublanceolate to subcaudate. 6–8 (9) pairs of secondary veins; reticulation visible when dry, moderately fine.

Inflorescences sessile or subsessile, often triflorous with peduncle 0–2 mm, pubescent-velutinous. Young main bracteate involucre naviculate-fused, 6–13 mm from one end to the other, with acute apices, with long fulvous or blonde hairs. Secondary involucres ± developed (the type presents 3 pairs of secondary bracts, one pair central pair fused at the base) or absent (N. Hallé 1953) (intermediate case in Le Testu 5096). Calyx green, villous, tubular for 1–2 mm, with 5 lobes attenuate-lanceolate, acute, 4–6 mm long. Corolla white, 17 (when dry) – 27 (maximum in vivo) mm long, with tube glabrous in the lower half, with numerous ascending white hairs above, with 5 suberect lobes 3–5 mm long, glabrous on the inner face; internal pilosity of the tube consisting of beaded tapering hairs, approximately 1 mm, ascending at the level and between the anthers; smaller, twisted and less dense hairs below. Anthers 2–3 × 0.5–0.7 mm, micro-apiculate in the type, or micro-emarginate at the apex (N. H. 1953 and Le T. 5096), medifix, split below the insertion. Style approximately 15 mm, with 5 narrow lobes of ± 5 mm. Disc 1–1.2 mm and 0.7 mm thick. Ovary pubescent. 5 elliptic placentae 1–1.6 mm long, with narrow insertion limited to approximately the upper 2/3, bearing ovules imbricate toward the base over the entire surface except for a bare oval external area of the upper region.

Mature fruit blood-red, succulent, reaching 11–13 mm in diam., pubescent, with pale red pulp, with green erect sepals. Seeds brown, angular, approximately 0.7 mm long. Germinations obtained in 12 and 29 days from seeds of ripe fruits. Small epigeal seedling with 2 ovate leaves 0.8 mm long.

## Discussion

Species of moderate size flowering in shrubby roadside margins. Known only from Cameroon and Gabon.

Bakota name: Mbouboutou.

N. Hallé: 1073, Makokou (fl. and fr. Feb.); 1953, 2003, Ayem region, SW of Ndjolé (fl. May); 2756, 2794, 2863, 3002, 3036, Bélinga, between the Ivindo and 980 m alt. (fl. fr. Oct.–Nov.).

N. Hallé and G. Cours: 6139, Etéké region (fl. May); 6161, between Mimongo and Mbigou (fl. May).

Le Testu: 5096, Pagha, forest of the Echiras (fl. Nov.).

6 bis. S. fulva Wernh. v. clipeolaria N. Hallé var. nov. (Fig. 3 p. 178)

From the type, it differs in 5–7-flowered inflorescences, convex non-contracted involucres, and two prefoliar bracts on each flower.

Sarmentose. Internodes velutinous, fulvous. Fine and sparse pubescence on the upper surface of the lamina; the latter rather ample, reaching 6.2 cm wide; apex broader than previously and subacuminate. 8–10 pairs of secondary veins. Inflorescence with 5–7 flowers and subnull peduncle 1–3 (4) mm. Lower involucre larger than that of the typical form; the upper central involucre is fused at the base of the two pieces. Each flower bears two prefoliar bracts. Corolla 18 mm long. Placenta inserted over almost its entire length; ovules imbricate toward the base; bare area observed.
