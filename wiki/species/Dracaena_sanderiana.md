# *Dracaena sanderiana* Sander ex Mast. Figure 5(A, B)

**Genus**: [[Dracaena]]
**Family**: [[Dracaenaceae]]
**Protologue**: Pleomele sanderiana (Sander ex Mast.) N.E.Br., Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1914 : 279 (1914)

## Description

Gard. Chron., sér. 3, 11 : 731 (4 June 1892). D. poggei Engl., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 15 : 478 (10 June 1892). Pleomele sanderiana (Sander ex Mast.) N.E.Br., Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1914 : 279 (1914). D. vanderystii De Wild., Bull. Jard. Bot. État Bruxelles 5 : 7 (1915). Herb reaching 2 m in height, forming mats; stem simple, base semi-woody; roots bright orange. Leaves spiral, uniformly spaced 2–3 cm apart, leaving the stem visible between the sheaths or cataphylls; sheath embracing the stem over clearly more than its circumference, over a length of > 1.5 cm; pseudpetiole shorter than the blade, channeled, green, the base passing gradually into the sheath; blade narrowly oblong-narrowly ovate to elliptic-oblong, up to 29 × 6 cm, base rounded, apex acuminate, curved toward the ground, with a mucro reaching 1 cm, papyraceous-coriaceous, smooth, upper surface medium or dark green uniform (with white longitudinal bands and pale green in cultivated plants), lower surface paler. Inflorescence erect, in a thyrsoid spicate panicle, up to 20 cm in length, with smooth axes, green, with flowers restricted to the distal 5–7 cm; bracts rapidly caducous, narrowly ovate, up to 6 × 0.6 cm, becoming smaller toward the apex; fascicles with 1 to 3 flowers, each subtended by rapidly caducous bracts, narrowly triangular, ± 1 mm in length, scarious. Flower subsessile, fragrant; perianth 40–50 mm in length, white to cream, tinged with violet at the base and apex, with lobes shorter than the tube, reflexed; stamens inserted near the throat, as long as the lobes, filament filiform, white, anther 4 mm in length, cream to grey; ovary ovoid, up to 3 × 2 mm; style exserted up to 5 mm, white, stigma slightly 3-lobed, ± 0.8 mm in diameter. Fruit subsessile, entire to 2- or 3-lobed, ± 14 × 11 mm, orange-red to red, with ellipsoid, divergent lobes. Seed spheric to ellipsoid, ± 10 × 6 mm.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola; in Gabon, known from the Ogooué-Ivindo

## Ecology

very humid stations in forest, near or in rivers or marshes; in Gabon at 110 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1020 m. Flowering year-round. Use: Cultivated throughout the world (also in Gabon) as an ornamental plant and it is also said to bring good luck, hence its English name 'lucky bamboo'. The cultivars 'Borinquensis' and 'Viridis' are sold at flower markets in Libreville (Damen, pers. obs.)

## Vernacular names

dragonnier de Sander (fr.) ; lucky bamboo (angl.). B : Baker (1898), Damen et al. (2018), Mwachala (2005). i B liographie
