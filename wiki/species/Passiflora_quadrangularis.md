# *Passiflora quadrangularis* L.

**Genus**: [[Passiflora]]
**Family**: [[Passifloraceae]]
**Protologue**: Syst. Nat., ed. 10 : 1248 (1759)

## Description

Diagnostic characters: Perennial vine, reaching 15 m in height; branchlets angular with 4 wings. Leaf: blade entire, penninerved. Flower up to 10 cm in diameter; petals 3–4.5 cm in length, red-purple on the inside; corona with filaments often longer than the petals, forming alternating white and purple "bands".

## Distribution

Native to tropical America, naturalized in all tropics; mentioned by Raponda-Walker & Sillans (1961) for Gabon, but its presence can only be confirmed by collections. Uses: In Gabon, the seed aril is used to prepare a fruit juice; its thick pulp is used to make preserves. Fresh roots are reputed to be poisonous; they are recommended as an emetic (Raponda-Walker & Sillans, 1961).
