# *Eleocharis mutata* (L.) Roem. & Schult. Planche 84

**Genus**: [[Eleocharis]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Syst. Veg. éd. 15, 2 : 155 (1817)

## Description

et Amoen. Acad. 5 : 391 (1759). Robust, perennial herb, tufted; stems 40–75 cm × 2–6 mm, triangular. Leaf: sheath violet or brown, terminating in a narrow blade, approximately 5 mm long, triangular with attenuate apex. Inflorescence in a single spikelet; bracts 2, small, with membranous margin. Spikelet 20–50 mm × 3–5 mm, with closely packed glumes, orbicular to obovate, 3.2–4 mm long, weakly nerved, with margin having a narrow translucent border and darker internal zone. Flower: perianth with 6–7 bristles furnished with numerous retrorsely barbed setae, all as long as or longer than the achene; stamens 3; style with 3 branches. Achene obovate, 1.5–1.8 × 1.1–1.3 mm, terminating in a crater-shaped rim, surface with longitudinal ribs and less pronounced transverse bars; triangular appendage, 0.5 mm long, narrow.

## Distribution

widely distributed in the tropical regions of Africa and America, infrequent in Africa; in Gabon, found only in the Estuaire and Ogooué-Maritime (5 collections)

## Ecology

in mangroves or freshwater or brackish swamps; in Gabon, at sea level or at low altitude, up to 1000 m in East Africa. Taxonomic note: very similar to E. acutangula, but it has more numerous glumes and rather different achenes. B: Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al. (2010), Hooper & Napper (1972). ibliography
