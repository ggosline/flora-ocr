# *Eleocharis mutata* (L.) Roem. & Schult. Planche 84

**Genus**: [[Eleocharis]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Syst. Veg. éd. 15, 2 : 155 (1817)

## Synonyms

- Scirpus mutatus L., Pl. Jamaic. Pug. : 6 (1759) et Amoen. Acad. 5 : 391 (1759).

## Description

Robust, perennial herb, in tufts; stems 40–75 cm × 2–6 mm, triangular. Leaf: sheath violet or brown, terminating in a narrow blade, approximately 5 mm long, triangular with attenuated apex. Inflorescence in a single spikelet; bracts 2, small, with membranous margin. Spikelet 20–50 mm × 3–5 mm, with closely packed, orbicular to obovate glumes, 3.2–4 mm long, weakly nerved, with margin bearing a narrow translucent border and an internal darker zone. Flower: perianth with 6–7 bristles bearing numerous retrorsely barbed hairs, all as long as or longer than the achene; stamens 3; style with 3 branches. Achene obovate, 1.5–1.8 × 1.1–1.3 mm, terminating in a crater-shaped rim, with surface bearing longitudinal ribs and less prominent transverse bars; triangular appendage, 0.5 mm long, narrow.

## Distribution

Widely distributed in tropical regions of Africa and America, uncommon in Africa; in Gabon, found only in Estuaire and Ogooué-Maritime (5 collections)

## Ecology

In mangroves or marshes of fresh or brackish water; in Gabon, at sea level or at low altitude, up to 1000 m in East Africa. Taxonomic note: closely resembles E. acutangula, but has more numerous glumes and rather different achenes. B: Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al. (2010), Hooper & Napper (1972). Bibliography
