# *Costus phaeotrichus* Loes.
**Family in Vol. 9**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Current placement**: Costaceae · **Genus**: [[Costus]]
## Description
Small herb densely clothed in brown hairs on rhizome, sheaths, petioles and both leaf surfaces; blades 10–16 × 4–9 cm. A short separate stalk carries a few-flowered pink-bracted spike. Each bract subtends two flowers; corolla white and labellum c. 7 cm, fringed, pink with a median yellow band.
## Distribution
Forest understorey in Cameroon and Gabon. Type: Ledermann 945, Cameroon. Gabon: Le Testu 9059, Nzoghouloumou; Hallé 2237 from Abanga was referred here only with doubt.
