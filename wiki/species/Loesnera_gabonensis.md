# *Loesnera gabonensis* Pellegrin

**Genus**: [[Loesenera]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Pellegrin, Bull. Soc. Bot. Fr. 89 : 245 (1942); Lég. Gabon; 85 (1948)

## Description

Small trees. Leaves with one pair of opposite, oblong-lanceolate leaflets, somewhat falcate, obtusely acuminate, rounded or obtuse at the base, 8.5–12.5 cm long, 3–4.5 cm wide, very finely papillose beneath yellowish-brown. Midvein dividing the lamina unequally. Secondary veins about a dozen pairs, anastomosing in arches not far from the margin, finely prominent beneath and united in a loose network of veinlets. Petiole glabrous, 5–15 mm. Petiolules twisted, articulate, approximately 5 mm.

Terminal and axillary racemes. Axes hairy, silky, yellow, 6–8 cm, marked with scars of fallen flowers. Bracts ovate, glabrous, striped with prominent longitudinal veinlets, 5 × 6 mm, soon caducous. Pedicels very short. Bracteoles 2, opposite, lanceolate acute, hairy externally, 6–7 mm long, 3 mm wide, embracing the young bud but not valvate. Flowers pink. Calyx in a narrow tube 7 mm; lobes 4, imbricate at first then reflexed, lanceolate, 6 mm long, hairy externally, glabrous internally. Petals 3, inserted at the edge of the tube, free, 1 cm long, 3 mm wide, obovate and attenuated into a long and narrow claw. Stamens 10, free, exserted, inserted on the edge of the tube. Ovary hairy, long-stipitate, the stipe being fused to the wall of the receptacle over part of its length. Ovules 2.

Young fruits oblong, velvety, yellowish-brown, with a wrinkled surface.

## Discussion

Swamp tree, known only from the upper Ngounié. Endemic to Gabon.
