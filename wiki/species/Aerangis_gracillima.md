# *Aerangis gracillima* (Kraenzl.) Arends & Stewart

**Genus**: [[Aerangis]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Lindleyana 4(1) : 24 (1989) ; FCAM 3 : 832 (2001)

## Description

Lindleyana 4(1): 24 (1989); FCAM 3: 832 (2001). – BASIONYM: Angraecum gracillimum Kraenzl., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 17: 59 (1893). – TYPE: Preuss 459, Cameroon, holo-B, del.

– Barombia gracillima (Kraenzl.) Schltr., Die Orchideen: 600 (1914).

Stem 3–8 cm. Leaves 6–14, 11–24 × 2.5–6 cm, narrowly obovate, lanceolate, oblong-lanceolate, slightly falcate, coriaceous. Inflorescence lax, < 80 cm, < 12-flowered. Flowers large, greenish-white or white, the apical half of the perianth segments and the lip somewhat ferruginous. Dorsal sepal linear-oblanceolate to very narrowly oblanceolate, acuminate. Petals and lateral sepals narrowly linear-lanceolate, oblique, acuminate. Lip 35–45 × 4–6 mm, linear-lanceolate or narrowly elliptic-obovate, acuminate. Spur 150–250 mm, filiform, pendent, slightly swollen at the apex, obtuse. – Pl. 194, p. 417.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon

## Ecology

alt. 470–800 m. Epiphyte on tree trunk at 2.5–3 m height, in very shaded forest receiving 180–220 cm annual rainfall
