# *Azolla africana* Desvaux

**Genus**: [[Azolla]]
**Family**: [[Azollaceae]]

## Synonyms

- A. pinnata var. africana (Desv.) Baker, Ferns Allies : 138 (1887).
- A. guineensis Schumann, K. Danske Vidensk. Selsk. 4 : 236 (1827).

## Description

— Desv., Ann. Soc. Linn. Paris 6 : 178 (1827). — Tard., Mém. IFAN 50 : 19, pl. 3, 3-5 (1957). — Alston, FWTA : 27 (1959).

Fasciculate roots. Horizontal stems, with numerous branches. Leaf lobes reddish, ovate, not scarious. Macrospores bearing numerous floats, finely granular. Microsporangium with several massulae.

## Type

Desvaux, Sénégal (P). Sénégal, Gambie, Sierra-Leone, Côté-d'Ivoire, Ghana, Togo, Dahomey, Nigeria, Cameroun, Congo, Angola
