# *Chlorophytum stolzii* (K.Krause) Kativu

**Genus**: [[Chlorophytum]]
**Family**: [[Anthericaceae]]
**Protologue**: Nordic J. Bot. 13 : 64 (1993)

## Synonyms

- Chlorophytum asphodeloides C.H.Wright (1906).
- Albuca stolzii K.Krause, Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 57 : 237 (1921).
- Anthericum welwitschii Marais & Reilly, Kew Bull . 32 : 657 (1978).

## Description

Robust plant, 1.5–2.5 m in height; cormiform rhizome; roots several, narrow, bearing at their extremity elongated tubers. Leaves (sub)distichous, not petiolate; blade linear, attaining 80 × 2 cm, glabrous, sheathing at the base, the latter papyraceous. Inflorescence (peduncle included) attaining 2.5 m in length, provided with bracts over almost its entire length, unbranched or with 1–2 branches at the base, rachis weakly angulate, glabrous; peduncle 1.5 cm in diameter at the base, glabrous; bracts ovate-lanceolate 4–5 mm in length, glabrous, with subulate apex. Flowers 1–5 per node; pedicel articulate in the lower half, attaining 7 mm in fruit; tepals 12–25 mm in length, white, 3-nerved; stamens as long as the perianth, filaments dilate in the middle, shorter than the anthers; style curved, exsert. Fruit obovoid, approximately 9 mm in length, nearly rounded in transverse section, transversely striate. Seeds irregularly folded, approximately 2 mm in diameter.

## Distribution

Gabon, Angola, Burundi, République Démocratique du Congo, Malawi, Mozambique, Tanzanie and Zambia. In Gabon, known only from Haut-Ogooué, near the border with Congo (Brazzaville).

## Ecology

in open arboreous vegetation, savannas, dry zones and stream banks, on heavy soils; in Gabon at approximately 400 m in altitude. B: Kativu et al. (2008), Nordal et al. (1997). ibliography
