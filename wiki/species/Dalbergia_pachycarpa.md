# *Dalbergia pachycarpa* (De Wild. & T.Durand) Uhlrich ex De Wild.

**Genus**: [[Dalbergia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Pl. Bequaert. 3 : 408 (1925)

## Description

, nom. inval., pro parte . Liana or shrub 2–10 m tall, reaching 10 cm in diameter, inodorous, unarmed, but sometimes with hook-like prehensile branchlets; bark dark brown to blackish, with lenticels often inconspicuous; young branchlets shortly pubescent to glabrescent. Stipules early caducous, elliptic to lanceolate ± falciform, 3–7.5 × 1–2 mm, obtuse, rather thick, with short and dense appressed pubescence. Leaf imparipinnate; petiole 0.6–4.3 cm; rachis 1.5–11 cm, shortly pubescent to glabrescent; leaflets 5 to 10, alternate and distinctly unequal, with petiolule 2–7 mm, blade elliptic (or ± obovate in the terminal leaflet), symmetric, 3.7–14.5 × 1.5–7.3 cm in the upper leaflets and 2.4–8 × 1.5–4.3 cm in the lower ones, rounded or obtuse at base, acuminate at apex, rather coriaceous, olive-green to dark brownish when dry, upper surface glabrous, lower surface with short appressed hairs (0.1–0.4 mm) and unidirectional, of highly variable density; lateral veins 5 to 12, generally well visible, reaching almost to the blade margin, veinlets in dense and prominent reticulation. Inflorescence in a dense panicle of unilateral racemes, axillary and terminal on the leafy branchlets, 0.8–5 cm long, with short and dense golden-brown hairs; bracts caducous after flowering, broadly ovate to elliptic, 0.7–1.5 × 0.3–0.8 mm, pubescent. Flower: pedicel 1.5–6.5 mm; bracteoles caducous after flowering, broadly elliptic, 0.5–1 × 0.3–0.6 mm, pubescent; calyx with dense and golden-brown appressed hairs, tube 1.7–2.5 × 1.5–2.5 mm, truncate or with short and ± equal valvate teeth, triangular, < 0.5 mm long; corolla white or slightly pink, petals with claw exceeding the calyx, generally rather long, standard obliquely bent, with claw 2–2.7 mm and suborbicular limb 3–4 × 3–4.5 mm, with rounded base and bilobed apex, wings equal to or slightly exceeding the keel, with claw 2–3 mm and ± rectangular limb 3–4 × 1.3–1.8 mm, smooth externally, with rounded or truncate base, keel distinctly curved, with claw 2–3 mm and limb 3–4 × 1.7–2 mm, with truncate or slightly auriculate base; stamens in 2 phalanges of 5, filaments 4–5 mm, united for 2/3 to 3/4 of their length, straight at base and curved in their free part; ovary 1.2–1.5 × 0.5 mm, glabrous or with dense appressed hairs, with 1 or 2 ovules and straight stipe 2.5–3.7 mm; style linear, 1.3 mm, with punctate stigma. Pod with pedicel 2–5 mm and stipe 4–10 mm, woody, discoid or shortly ellipsoid, very thick with margin generally thinned and sometimes recurved, 2–4.8 × 2.2–4.3 × 0.7–1.7 cm, ± bumpy, generally densely pubescent with felted brown hairs, more rarely glabrous, with 1 seed; pericarp 0.7–5 mm thick, with outer layer rather hard formed of cork tissue reinforced with thick woody fibers, endocarp shortly pubescent on the inner surface. Seed reniform, very thick, ± 27 × 12 × 7 mm.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Central African Republic, Equatorial Guinea (Bioko), Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola; widely distributed in Gabon, recorded in most provinces, except Haut-Ogooué and Moyen-Ogooué

## Ecology

secondary forests, forest galleries, river or marsh edges, fallows and recolonization thickets in savanna; up to 600 m altitude in Gabon, elsewhere up to 1400 m. In Gabon, flowering from May to August (once in February) and fruiting from October to February

## Source note

The pods of D. pachycarpa are rather variable in shape and thickness; they are generally pubescent, rarely glabrous in certain specimens from the Ivindo basin. The pubescence of the lower surface of the leaflets varies from rather dense to very sparse. D. pachycarpa is very close to D. semiapplanata and D. xylocarpa, from which it is sometimes difficult to distinguish in the absence of fruits. It is even closer to D. heudelotii Stapf and could be only a subspecies of it. D. heudelotii is restricted to West Africa (records from Central Africa are erroneous and mostly refer to D. pachycarpa) and differs from D. pachycarpa by its smaller and more asymmetric fruits, with rather soft cork-like pericarp. The description of D. monetaria var. oyemensis Pellegr. is not valid in the absence of a Latin diagnosis. The two specimens cited under this name by Pellegrin belong, one to D. pachycarpa, the other to D. semiapplanata (Lachenaud & Maesen 2016). b : Cronquist (1954), Pellegrin (1949). ibliography
