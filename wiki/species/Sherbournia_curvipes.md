# *Sherbournia curvipes* (Wernh.) N. Hallé, comb. nov.

**Genus**: [[Sherbournia]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]

## Synonyms

- Randia curvipes Wernham, Journ. Bot. 55 : 8 (1917).
- Sherbournia unguiparda N. Hallé, nomen in herb.

## Description

Liane reaching 3 to 20 m in height, with stem 1 to 2 cm diam. Young branches glabrous smooth then with reddish epidermis flaking in patches. Stipules narrowly lanceolate 8–12 × 2.5–4 mm, subobtuse at apex, puberulent with glabrous margins. Petiole 7–10 mm, moderately clothed with appressed ochraceus hairs. Leaves subcoriaceous, subconcolorous, often more russet on the lower surface; blade glabrous elliptic (7) 10–16 (19) × (3) 4–7 (8) cm; base often asymmetric, subacute, obtuse or sometimes rounded, with finely bordered margin; apex acuminate. Midvein glabrous and without relief above, sparsely pubescent with scattered appressed hairs below. 7–9 pairs of secondary veins arched, totally or nearly glabrous on the lower surface; fine reticulation not distinct.

Inflorescences (1) 2–4-flowered densely clothed with fine yellowish pubescence, with short axes (segments 2–4 mm), with deltoid acute bracts clasping at the base, 3–4 mm long. Pedicel subnull. Calyx golden yellow or bistre with fine and dense appressed pubescence, with subulate teeth 4–5 mm long, fleshy; cup-shaped calyx tube 4–5 mm high, ± wrinkled when dry; region of the ovary narrowly oblong-conical, with 10 distinct longitudinal furrows, 5–6 (10) mm in length and 2.5–3 mm in width. Corolla salmon-yellow on the outside, with 4 yellow lobes, dark violet inside (sometimes red, Thollon 4040); the corolla is entirely silky pubescent on the outside, its apex is conical in the bud; tube 2.5–3 × 1–1.5 cm, glabrous inside except for a very dense pubescent ring overhanging the fleshy disk at the lower part; lobes suborbicular 6–8 mm with apex ± acute. Anthers 12 mm, sessile, inserted somewhat below the lower third, with apex exceeding the locules by 0.8 mm, with bimucronulate base. Style 22–25 cm, with glabrous column, with fusiform stigma 10-furrowed 10 × 2.5 mm, composed of 2 acute lobes appressed together. Ovary with 2 lobes ± confluent between the two placental masses.

Fruit blue-green before maturity, mature orange, with 10 salient ribs, oblong 4 × 1.7 cm with persistent calyx, with sparsely pubescent epidermis. Seeds very numerous, light russet, 1.2–1.5 mm.

## Type

Gossweiler (BM!), Angola

## Discussion

This species is from Cameroon (Bates 1600, Biye BM!), Rio Muni (G. Tessmann 928 K!), Gabon, Congolese Mayombe (Thollon 4040), Cabinda (Gossweiler 6895 BM!) and Congo (Ouesso, Bouquet 1631; Brazzaville, A. Chevalier 4298) to Yangambi (J. Louis 1874, 2721, 3358, 6547, 10417, 11456, BR!).

Sciophilous, in primary or degraded forest.

The mature fruit would be edible; the slightly acidulous pulp is pleasant to the taste.

## Specimens examined

N. Hallé et G. Cours 6056, Moumba (fr. mai); ne diffère du type et des nombreux spécimens congolais que par son fruit un peu plus court. G. Le Testu 7164, 7882, Lastoursville (fl. janv.; fr. avr.).
