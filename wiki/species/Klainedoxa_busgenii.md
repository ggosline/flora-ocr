# *Klainedoxa büsgenii* Engl.

**Family**: [[Irvingiaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Klainedoxa]]

## Description

Large tree differing from *K. gabonensis* chiefly in its larger, often much larger, elliptic or oblong leaves, distinctly rounded to subcordate at the base, thick branchlets and stipules reaching 15 cm. Flowers and fruits do not differ from *K. gabonensis*. The treatment cautions that it may be only a variety of that species and notes that Gabonese foresters did not distinguish the two.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon and Congo. Holotype: Büsgen 474, near Edéa, Cameroon. Gabon collections: Le Testu 1200 (Ganda, Mayombe), 8046 (Mouew-agnya, upper Lolo), 8724 (Mayangha); Fleury 26545 (Adouma, Ogooué); S.F. 17 (upper Ramboué).
