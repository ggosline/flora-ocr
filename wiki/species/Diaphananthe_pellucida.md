# *Diaphananthe pellucida* (Lindl.) Schltr.

**Genus**: [[Diaphananthe]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Die Orchideen : 593 (1914) ; FTWA : 261 (1968) ; Cribb, FTEA, Orchid. 3 : 523 (1989) ; FAC 2 : 558 (1992) ; FCAM 3 : 748 (2001)

## Description

Stem < 12 cm. Leaves 15–70 × 2–9 cm, lanceolate, slightly falcate. Inflorescence 15–80 cm, 40–60-flowered. Flowers pale yellow, unscented. Dorsal sepal ovate to oblong-lanceolate, subacute. Petals linear to obliquely ovate, acute. Lateral sepals lanceolate to oblong-lanceolate, acute. Labellum 7.5–15 × 7–15(–18) mm, ovate, truncate at apex, lateral margins laciniate, single callus at the throat of the spur whose entrance is bordered on each side by a fleshy relief ridge. Spur 5–15 mm, narrowly cylindrical at the base, distinctly inflated above and gradually attenuate toward a blunt apex. – Pl. 205, p. 435.

## Distribution

Guinea, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Togo, Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Annon, Equatorial Guinea, Congo, Central African Republic, Zaire, Uganda

## Ecology

alt. 900–1,800 m. Epiphytic in dense humid forest, secondary forest, observed at 1.5–2 m above ground on Cleistanthus mildbraedii, Scorodophlaeus zenkeri, often above water.
