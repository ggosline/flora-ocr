— Sorindeia rubriflora ENGL., l.c. 46 : 338 (1911).

Tree reaching a diameter of 80 cm; base of trunk furnished with 5 concave buttresses, 10–20 cm thick and 1 m high, extending 20–50 cm from the tree; bole irregular and sinuous, 8–10 m high. Rhytidome greenish, light green-brown, dark brown, spotted with light grey; bark 1 cm thick with wine-coloured fracture spotted with very dark violet punctation. Branches tortuous forming a broad crown. Young branchlets ± angular, with 4–6 faces, longitudinally striated. Stipules pubescent externally, narrowly lanceolate and early caducous. Petiole 1–1.5 cm long, glabrous, strongly concave on the upper surface. Lamina coriaceous, brown or grey-green when dry, glabrous on both surfaces, elliptic or oblong-elliptic, 10.5–19 × 4.5–8 cm; base rounded or weakly cuneiform, apex acuminate, weakly obtuse or acute. Midvein concave above and strongly prominent glabrous below; secondary nerves 4–7 pairs, ascending, ± arched, anastomosing 2–4 mm from the lamina margin. Tertiary nerves in a dense network, parallel or subparallel and anastomosing.

Inflorescences cauline in solitary racemes or in fascicles of racemes, 14–30 cm long; axis wrinkled longitudinally, ± irregularly flattened or swollen, shortly pubescent. Pedicels very sparsely pubescent, 4 mm long. Floral bud globose. Flowers ♂ or ♀, opposite or alternate, ± perpendicular to the axis. ♂ Flowers, 9 × 5 mm. Calyx cupuliform, sepals very sparsely pubescent externally. Petals glabrous, red or reddish-violet, lanceolate, 5.5 × 2 mm, margins very finely dentate or entire, apex obtuse. Stamens 10, didynamous, in 2 whorls: 5 epipetalous stamens with filaments 1 mm long and 5 alternipetalous stamens with filaments 2 mm long; anthers ellipsoid, 1.75 mm long. Ovary reduced by abortion, shortly stipitate and conic. ♀ Flowers similar to ♂ flowers. Sepals pubescent externally, furnished with a denser tuft of hairs at the apex, acute, convex, 1 × 1.5 mm long. Petals similar to those of ♀ flowers. Ovary glabrous, green, ± lobed, with 3 uniovulate locules, base shortly stipitate and pentalobate. Style short, 0.75 mm, cylindric, terminating in 3 stigmas 1.25 mm long, weakly bifid, bent.

Drupe yellowish-green, pedicellate, globose, 5–6 cm in diameter, with apiculate apex. Endocarp 1 cm thick, very strongly lignified, muricate, lacunose. Seed shiny, triangular-ovate, concave, curved, flattened, 2 cm in diameter, with acute apex.

Seedling with cotyledons broadly obtriangular 7 × 4 cm, broadly emarginate at the apex, 5–7 primary nerves. PL. 4, p. 17.

## Type

Klaine 113, Gabon (holo-, P!)

## Discussion

The form of the vegetative apparatus, in particular the dentition of the leaves, is highly variable.

This tree is characteristic of dense humid forests. Its range extends from Liberia to Zaïre.

## Uses

The whitish-yellow or greyish wood, semi-hard and compact, would be usable in joinery. The bark contains tannin. The oily seeds are edible; an oil is extracted from them used in local cooking. A decoction of hot bark would be a remedy against kidney ailments.
