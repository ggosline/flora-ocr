# *Fagara buesgenii* Engl.

**Family**: [[Rutaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Fagara]]

## Description

Understorey shrub 0.5–2.5 m tall, sometimes described as climbing. Stem, branches, petiole, rachis, leaflet midribs and inflorescence axis bear straight, laterally compressed prickles to 8 mm; young parts and fruits have a conspicuous dark rusty or tawny indumentum. Leaves 20–75 cm long, with 3–6 pairs of opposite or subopposite leaflets and a terminal leaflet; blades range from 4.5 × 2.5 cm below to 18–25 × 9 cm above, are crenate, papery and yellow-translucent-punctate. Male inflorescences are pyramidal, to 35 cm; flowers are 4-merous. The subglobose pink-red follicle is tawny-velvety and contains one globose or two hemispherical seeds.

## Distribution

Southern Nigeria, Cameroon and Gabon.
