# *Dacryodes igaganga* Aubr. et Pellegr.

**Family**: [[Burseraceae]] · **Genus**: [[Dacryodes]]

## Description

Large tree. Leaves with 5–7 pairs of opposite leaflets; leaflets narrowly oblong to lanceolate, acuminate, asymmetrically cuneate, 7–11(–15) × 2.5–4 cm; simple hairs are caducous but minute appressed scales persist beneath. Male flowers 3-merous; pedicel 1.5 mm, sepals 3 mm; petals glandular-hairy inside; stamens 6 with glandular-hairy anthers and mixed-hairy filaments; ovary stellate-tomentose. Stone flattened-ellipsoid, rough, 2.8 × 1.8 × 1.4 cm, with an oblong apiculate shield separating two pores.

## Distribution

Abundant in Gabon’s lake region, Ngounié basin between Mouila and Lambaréné, and the Gabon estuary. Holotype: A. Chevalier 26655, Lake Oghémoué.
