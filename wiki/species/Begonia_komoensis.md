# *Begonia komoensis* Irmsch. Planche 45

**Genus**: [[Begonia]]
**Family**: [[Begoniaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 57(2) : 242 (1921)

## Description

Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 57(2) : 242 (1921). Epiphyte; stem erect or pendulous, up to 3 m in length; indumentum composed of sessile, scaly, laciniate hairs. Stipules persistent, triangular to narrowly triangular, 10–26 × 4–7 mm. Leaves: petiole 0.4–2(–3.5) cm; lamina weakly asymmetric, narrowly ovate to elliptic, (5–)7–13.5 × 2–5 cm, base obliquely obtuse, truncate or subcordate, apex acute to acuminate, margins slightly dentate; on fresh material the midrib is marked on the lower surface, with 1–3(–4) secondary veins on each side, venation indistinct on dried material. Inflorescence unisexual; the ♂ in slender bipary cymes, up to 7(–15)-flowered, peduncle (7–)10–30 mm; the ♀ (1–)3-flowered, peduncle (4–)10–25(–30) mm. Flower ♂: pedicel 7–15 mm; outer tepals elliptic, 6–10 × 3.5–9 mm, the inner narrowly obovate, 5–9 × 1.5–3.5 mm; androecium ellipsoid, actinomorphic, stamens 19–26, column 2 mm long, anthers sessile with longitudinal dehiscence. Flower ♀ sessile; lower part of the ovary constricted; tepals similar to those of ♂ flowers; ovary 15–25 mm in length, circular in section, 2 mm in diameter; styles (2–)3(–4), simple, subulate, stigmatic zone elongate, of variable shape. Infructescence with 1–3 fruits. Fruit fusiform, truncate at the apex, 25–50 × 2–4 mm, opening by a longitudinal fissure.

## Distribution

Equatorial Guinea, Gabon

## Ecology

Dense ombrophilous forests; up to 950 m altitude
