Flower white, pubescent; pedicel 6–10 mm long, clearly exceeding the flower buds, white, with white glutinous pubescence; sepals equal, oblong-elliptic, ± 5 mm long, petaloid, with white glutinous pubescence on the outside; petals oblong-elliptic, ± equal to the sepals, glabrous; stamens unequal, with white, glabrous filament, the 2 upper ones 2.5–3 mm long, with basifixed, flattened, ellipsoid anther, 0.5–0.7 mm long, yellow, often with reddish connective, the lower one 4–4.5 mm long, with dorsifixed, oblong anther, ± 0.75 mm long, apex clearly inflexed, white with the attachment point dark red; staminodes unequal, yellow, the upper one the most robust, 4 mm long, with visible filament, the 2 others smaller, with subnull filament, 3 mm long; ovary ovoid-spherical, ± 1 mm in diameter, provided with stiff hairs, 1.5 mm; style 1.5–2 mm, stigma shortly setose. Fruit and seed not seen.

## Distribution

endemic to Gabon, known from Ivindo and Ogooué-Lolo

## Ecology

mature or secondary dense forest; between 250 and 705 m altitude. Flowering in November and December

## Source note

The species differs from all others by the combination of alternate leaves, but terminating in a pseudowhorl, its terminal white densely woolly inflorescence, erect bracts and hairy flowers with very long pedicels. B : Bidault & van der Burg (2019). Bibliography
