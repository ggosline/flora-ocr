# *Oncinotis gabonica* O.Lachenaud Planche 79

**Genus**: [[Oncinotis]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Candollea 77 : 36 (2022)

## Description

Liane; branches with tawny pubescence of simple crisp hairs, approximately 0.3 mm, moderately dense. Leaf petiole ± cm, faces with the same hairs as the branches and a pair of colleters at the junction with the blade, glabrous beneath; blade narrowly obovate to narrowly elliptic, ± cm, acute at the base, abruptly acuminate at the apex, glabrous or nearly so, except for the domatia; midvein impressed above, prominent below, secondary nerves 7 to 11 pairs, strongly incurved and forming prominent loops 2–3 mm from the margin, tertiary venation dense, subparallel, prominent on the lower face; domatia in tufts of clearly visible hairs. Inflorescence axillary, 1.5–3.5 cm long, with pubescence similar to that of the branches but denser; peduncle 0.5–1.5 cm; bracts small, narrowly triangular. Flower: pedicel 1–2 mm; calyx violaceous, globose-ovate, imbricate, 1–1.2 × 0.5–0.7 mm, pubescent outside, glabrous inside; corolla violaceous outside and pale brownish-green inside, fusiform and with obtuse apex in bud, with narrowly infundibuliform tube, 4–5 × 1.8–2 mm, slightly constricted at the apex, covered outside with short hairs (except at the extreme base), villous inside from the insertion of the stamens to the throat; lobes narrowly elliptic, ± 0.7–1 mm, rounded at the apex, pubescent outside like the tube on the part exposed in bud, glabrous inside, with corona 0.5–0.8 mm long; anther 2.3 mm long; pistil ± 2.5 mm long, ovary pubescent at the apex. Fruit unknown. Distribution endemic to Gabon, known uniquely from the type locality, approximately km east of Lastoursville, in the Ogooué-Lolo.

## Ecology

evergreen secondary forest; at approximately 300 m altitude. B: Jongkind & Lachenaud (2022). BIBLIOGRAPHY
