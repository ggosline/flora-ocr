# *Inversodicraea cristata* Engl. Planche 10

**Genus**: [[Inversodicraea]]
**Family**: [[Podostemaceae]]
**Protologue**: Veg. Erde 9, Pflanzenw. Afr. 3(1) : 276 (1915)

## Description

Veg. Erde 9, Pflanzenw. Afr. 3(1) : 276 (1915). Ledermanniella cristata (Engl.) C.Cusset, Adansonia , sér. 2, 14(2) : 273 (1974). Plant with a basal part thalloid ribbon-like; stem erect, robust, dichotomous branching, 2–15 cm, entirely covered by foliate scales, scattered towards the base, becoming progressively imbricate on the branches. Foliate scales broadly elliptic to obovate, at the base of the stem 0.6–0.8 × 0.4 mm, 3-dentate, on the branches, 1.2–1.5 × 0.9–1.2 mm, 5–9(–10) dentate, with triangular teeth 0.2–0.3 mm in length. Leaves dichotomous, caducous, with linear segments. Inflorescences with solitary flowers; spatelles arranged in 2 ranks along the branching, obovoid, ± 2 × 1 mm, apiculate, tearing irregularly at the apex at anthesis. ## Distribution

Cameroon, Central African Republic, Gabon and Angola; in Gabon, known from a single specimen from the Cristal Mountains (Estuary)

## Ecology

In Gabon, at ± 150 m altitude, elsewhere up to 780 m. Bi: Cheek et al. (2017), Cusset (1983, 1987a), Sosef et al. (2006). Bibliography
