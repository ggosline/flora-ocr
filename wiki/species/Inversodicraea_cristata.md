# *Inversodicraea cristata* Engl. Planche 10

**Genus**: [[Inversodicraea]]
**Family**: [[Podostemaceae]]
**Protologue**: Veg. Erde 9, Pflanzenw. Afr. 3(1) : 276 (1915)

## Synonyms

- Ledermanniella cristata (Engl.) C.Cusset, Adansonia , sér. 2, 14(2) : 273 (1974).

## Description

Plant with a basal portion ribbon-like thalloid; stem erect, robust, dichotomous ramifications, 2–15 cm, entirely covered by foliaceous scales, scattered toward the base, becoming progressively imbricate on the branches. Foliaceous scales broadly elliptic to obovate, at the base of the stem 0.6–0.8 × 0.4 mm, 3-toothed, on the branches, 1.2–1.5 × 0.9–1.2 mm, 5–9(–10)-toothed, with triangular teeth 0.2–0.3 mm in length. Leaves dichotomous, caducous, with linear segments. Inflorescences with solitary flowers; spathels arranged in 2 rows along the ramifications, obovoid, ± 2 × 1 mm, apiculate, tearing irregularly at the apex at anthesis. ## Distribution

Cameroon, Central African Republic, Gabon and Angola; in Gabon, known from a single specimen from the Monts de Cristal (Estuaire)

## Ecology

In Gabon, at ± 150 m altitude, elsewhere up to 780 m. Bibl.: Cheek et al. (2017), Cusset (1983, 1987a), Sosef et al. (2006). Bibliography
