# *Cola mayimbensis* Pellegrin.

**Genus**: [[Cola]]
**Family**: [[Sterculiaceae]]
**Protologue**: PELL., Bull. Soc. Bot. Fr. 97 : 189 (1950), réf. orig. et Sterc. Gab. in Mém. S. B. Fr. : 38 (1950-1951)

## Description

, ref. orig. and Sterc. Gab. in Mém. S. B. Fr. : 38 (1950-1951). — Not to be confused with C. mayumbensis Exell, Journ. of Bot. LXV, suppl. Polypet. : 39 (1927).

Probable shrub, with shortly pubescent extremities; branchlets glabrescent with blackish bark. Scales of terminal buds and stipules scarious, pubescent, narrow and acute, with prominent midvein, 3-4 (5) mm long, caducous. Leaves alternate and appearing all arranged in the same plane, separated by short internodes of 10-15 mm. Petioles puberulent, 4-6 mm long. Blade glabrous, moderately coriaceous, 5.5-12 × 1.7-4.5 cm, elliptic, with moderately acute base, acuminate-caudate apex; 5-7 pairs of pinnate lateral veins; reticulation close and well apparent on both surfaces of the blade.

Pauciflorous glomerules axillary, sessile on successive nodes, furnished with numerous subacute bracteoles, more or less glabrous, arched and convex, approximately 1 mm long. Pedicels puberulent, slender, 5-10 mm long, articulated distinctly below their midpoint. Buds oblong.

Flowers, 1-5 per glomerule, 4-9 mm in diameter. Calyx moderately pubescent on the outside, clothed with punctiform papillae on the inside, shortly campanulate, with 5 lobes somewhat longer than the tube, with undulate fringe on the inner side of the margin. Flower ♂ with calyx 4-6 mm long, with slender androphore, glabrous, approximately 2 mm, with a ring of 8 uniseriate pollenic loges surrounding the base of a hirsute mamilla formed by aborted carpels. Flower ♀ probably opening before the others, with calyx 7-10 mm long, with lobes distinctly longer; ovary sessile surrounded basally by 8 aborted pollenic sacs, with 5 hirsute carpels 3-3.5 mm long, shortly attenuate; stigmas glabrous, recurved and sometimes irregularly fused with each other.

Fruit unknown.

Type of the species and sole known specimen: Le Testu 7765, region of Lastoursville (fl. Dec.) (P.).
