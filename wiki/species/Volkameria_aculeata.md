# *Volkameria aculeata* L.

**Genus**: [[Volkameria]]
**Protologue**: Sp. pl. : 637 (1753)

## Synonyms

- Clerodendrum aculeatum (L.) Griseb., Fl. Brit. W. Ind. 500 (1864).

## Description

Erect shrub, up to 3.5 m tall; stem slightly angular to cylindrical, solid, finely puberulent, pale brown to dark brown, with retrorse, conical and very sharp spines derived from old petioles, reaching 4 mm. Leaves opposite or more rarely ternate; petiole 2.5–10 mm, often discolorous, puberulent; blade oblong or elliptic to ovate, rarely obovate, (1.5–)2–7(–13) × 0.5–2.6 cm, cuneate to rounded at the base, rounded to acute or sometimes acuminate for up to 1.5 cm, ± glabrous or puberulent, punctate with clear or black glands below, especially near the midvein, clearly 3-veined. Inflorescence terminal or axillary, lax or dense, narrowly thyrsoid, 15(–35) × 15(–20) cm; cymes 0.5–0.8(–2) cm apart, with dense white, yellow, golden brown or brown pubescence, with 3 to 6 flowers; peduncle 0.7–4.1(–8.5) cm; bracts foliaceous, ± 3 × 1 cm; bracteoles linear, reaching 3 mm in length. Flower: pedicel 5–17 mm; calyx whitish-green or yellowish-white, puberulent, with infundibuliform tube, 2–3 mm long, up to 6 mm in fruit, with regularly spreading, triangular lobes, 3–4 × 2–3 mm, up to 4 mm wide in fruit, acuminate at apex; corolla white, green, whitish-green or yellow, pubescent, with tube 7–9(–14–27) mm long, with broadly ovate to oblong lobes, 5–6 × 4 mm, acute at apex; stamens exserted for (1–)1.5 cm. Fruit 8–12 × 7–10 mm, glossy, dark green, greyish-black at maturity.

## Distribution

native to Mexico, the West Indies and tropical South America; naturally dispersed or introduced and naturalized in Bermuda; introduced to Mali, Gambia, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Benin, Togo, Gabon and India and Australia; in Gabon: Estuaire, around Libreville

## Ecology

seashore, sandy zones; in Gabon and elsewhere at 1–10 m altitude. B: Acevedo-Rodríguez (2005), Acevedo-Rodríguez & Strong (2012), Huber et al. (1963), Bibliography Roberts (2015), Sosef et al. (2006), Yuan et al. (2010)
