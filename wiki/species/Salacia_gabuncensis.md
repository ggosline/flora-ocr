# *Salacia gabuncensis* Loesener

**Genus**: [[Salacia]]
**Family**: [[Celastraceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. 19:242 (1894); Wilczek, FCB. 9:229 (1960)

## Description

– S. volkensiana LOES. ex HARMS, Not. Bot. Gart. Berlin 15 : 675 (1942). TYPE : Tessmann 956 (iso-, K!).

Liane exceeding 6–10 m in height, devoid of rubber. Leaf blades light olivaceous when dry, concolorous and thin, (5)6–11(14) × (2)2.5–4.5(5) cm, with very fine reticulation apparent, entire (very fine teeth in the fa. volkensiana); base acute, acumen ± rostrate 8–20 mm. Petiole 4–10 mm long; channel narrow with finely undulate or straight margins. Secondary nerves 7–9 pairs.

Small axillary sessile pauciflorous glomerules. Buds ovate 1–1.9 mm in diameter; very small flowers yellow, pale yellow, pale orange or yellowish-red 4.2–5.3 mm in diameter. Pedicel slender 6.5–11 mm long. Sepals somewhat unequal, rounded, with finely denticulate glandular margin, 0.7–1.9 mm wide. Petals ovate 2.2–2.5 × 1.4–1.6 mm. Disc obtusely pentagonal, small, 1.2–1.6 mm in diameter, somewhat sinuate laterally. Perigynous groove discontinuous in 3 pits. Stamens approximately 1 mm, spaced at the base; anther reniform with confluent locules, 0.6 mm wide. Style attenuate truncate shorter than the filaments. Ovary with 3 locules (4)5–6 ovulate.

Fruit with 4–12 seeds, subglobose, 3–10 × 2.5–6 cm, pericarp somewhat bosselated, verrucose or rugose, ± apiculate but not ornamented with tortuous lamellar crests, sometimes thick (up to 9 mm in vivo); seeds 1.5–3 cm. - Pl. 23, fig. 10–20, p. 89; pl. 41, fig. 17, 18, p. 143.

## Discussion

Cameroon, Muni, Gabon and Zaïre (Lebrun 1333).

The type material is especially Gabonese and our conception of the species does not agree with that of WILCZEK. The specimen Zenker 1916 is for the moment the only one differing by its ovules, 4 only per locule. In Muni, Tessmann 956, with small lanceolate leaves merits only the rank of form: fa. volkensiana (Loes. ex Harms) Hallé, stat. nov. (see above the species synonymy).

Breteler 6957, 60 km SSW Moanda, 700 m, 17.10.1970 (fr. with galls similar to those of Klaine 683), WAG. Hallé N. & Cours 6075, W Moumba, env. Etéké, 775 m, 19.5.1963 (fl., fr.). Klaine s.n., env. Libreville, received 21.12.1897 (fr.); 683, ibid., end 11. 1896 (fr. and fr. gall.); 1828, ibid., 12.4.1900 (fl.); 2108 ter, ibid., 7 and 13.3.1901 (fl.); 2593 bis, ibid., 4.12.1901; 2770, ibid., 5.3.1902 (fl.); 2794, ibid., 12.3.1902 (fl.); 3276, ibid., 25.3.1903 (fl.). Soyaux s.n., Sibang, Riv. Munda, 1881 (fl.).

This species would be close to S. mannii: LOESENER brings it closer to S. lehmbachii and loloensis. Its size and habit are unknown.

Nous ne connaissons pas cette espèce ; la description qui suit est inspirée de la description originale.

Plante glabre et dépourvue de caoutchouc, à rameaux cylindriques très finement lenticellés. Feuilles opposées. Pétiole de 7-17 mm. Limbe discolore elliptique, ± ové ou oblong, de 12-18 × 4,2-9 cm, à base largement cunéiforme, sommet courtement acuminé et marges entières. Nervures secondaires 11-13 paires; fin réseau dense et bien apparent dessous à sec.

Fascicules floraux axillaires, sessiles, pluri- ou multiflores. Pédicelle subfiliforme de 15-20 mm. Fleur de 5-7 mm de diamètre. Sépales inégaux étalés, les extérieurs subdeltoïdes arrondis de 1 mm, les internes suborbiculaires et ± pétaloïdes atteignant 2 mm. Pétales étalés onguiculés, plus larges que longs, de 2-3,25 × 4 mm, à marge ± relevée. Disque étalé annulaire. Étamine de 1 mm ; anthère à loges confluentes en angle très obtus. Pistil bref à petit stigmate un peu capité. Ovules juxtaposés, 2 par loge.
