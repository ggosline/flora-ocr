Robust herb, with woody rhizome, 1–2 cm thick, erect or oblique; stems 1–10, lateral, arising at the axil of old lower leaves, 10–75 cm × 1–5 mm, triangular–rounded to compressed, green, very scabrous at least beneath the inflorescence, often with scabrous longitudinal ridges, the basal part covered by approximately 5 greenish, almost tubulous sheaths. Basal leaves, numerous; sheath poorly distinct, green to orange or brown; pseudo-petiole absent; blade linear, flat or folded, the largest 50–120 × 2–4 cm, with distinctly scabrous margin, gradually narrowed to an acute apex. Terminal inflorescence, in a globose head, 1–3 cm wide, composed sometimes of more than 100 crowded spikes; involucral bracts poorly developed, generally shorter than the inflorescence, brown with a large central green part, scabrous on the margin and central nerve at least towards the apex; spikes ovoid, 6–10 × 2–3 mm, brown to light brown, composed of approximately 20 crowded spicoids; spicoid bracts very crowded, elliptic, approximately 3 × 1 mm, apex rounded, light to medium brown. Flower: floral bracts 2, narrow, light reddish-brown; style with 2 branches. Achene elliptic, 2.2–3 × 1–1.4 mm, the lower part grey to brown with longitudinal ridges, upper part triangular, spongy.

## Distribution

Cameroon and Gabon; in Gabon, fairly scattered and locally common, found in Estuaire, Ogooué-Ivindo, Moyen-Ogooué, Ogooué-Maritime, Ngounié, Nyanga and Woleu-Ntem (19 collections).

swampy forest, sometimes along rivers and watercourses; at 0–600 m altitude. Taxonomic note: very close to M. pynaertii and M. secans, but differing in its very scabrous stems and its obovate achene, longitudinally ribbed at the base. b: Nelmes (1955a), Raynal (1968b). Bibliography
