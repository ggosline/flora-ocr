# *Bersama paullinioides* (Planch.) Baker

**Family**: [[Melianthaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Bersama]]

## Description

Small tree with 6–20 mm acute stipules and imparipinnate leaves 15–48 cm long, bearing 8–13 leaflet pairs; upper rachis wings 0.5–2 mm. Leaflets glabrous, elliptic or oblong, 8–15 × 2.5–5 cm, with 12–13 secondary-vein pairs. Racemes (7–)20–50 cm with about 40–80 white flowers; pedicels 6–15 mm. Sepals 4; petals 5, 15–16 mm; stamens 4(–5); ovary hirsute, 4(–5)-locular; style cylindrical. Fruit not seen in Gabon material.

## Distribution

Provisionally identified Gabon material from Lebagni, Ndingui and Coca near Lastoursville. The species limits and relationship to *B. abyssinica* and *B. nyassae* were considered unresolved.
