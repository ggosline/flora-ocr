# *Tricalysia biafrana* Hiern

**Genus**: [[Tricalysia]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Hiern, F.T.A. 3 : 122 (1877). F.W.T.A. ed. 2, 2 : 151 (1963)

## Description

a. — var. biafrana

The calycinal limb is **ample** and very briefly denticulate; the calyx is **glabrous**, notably at the base and at the level of the ovary.

## Type

Mann 7 (K!), Ambas Bay; Mann 1212 (K et iso. P!), Monts Cameroun

H. Soyaux 83 (P, holotype), Sibang (fl. 3 sept. 1879)

## Discussion

Ivory Coast, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, Central African Republic. This variety is not known from Gabon.

b. — var. fangana N. Hallé, var. nov.

Cupula calycis haud ampla sed brevior, lobis minutis, basi puberula in ovario.

Shrub 1–3 (9) m tall. Juvenile internodes glabrous, nigrescent. Stipules tapered over 2 mm. Petiole 6–9 mm, glabrous, canaliculate. Limb brownish-nigrescent, glabrous, elliptic, 6–15 (17) × 3–6.5 cm; base acute rarely almost rounded; apex acuminate. Midvein glabrous, sulcate above. 4–6 (7) pairs of secondary veins arched-ascending. Small pilose domatia, sometimes sparse.

Inflorescences nigrescent, pluriflorous, axillary, in small contracted cymes with very short axes as well as pedicels, about 1 cm long together with the corollas; axes and bracteolar cups approximately glabrous. Calyx pubescent at the base and at the level of the ovary, with a cup 1 mm tall, glabrous or moderately puberulent, with 6 small teeth of 0.2 mm. Corolla with glabrous tube 3–5 mm long, with 6 oblong lobes 4 × 1.5 mm, acute at the apex; throat ± pilose. Anther glabrous 3 × 0.4 mm; filament 1 mm. Style glabrous or with sparse hairs, 7–8 mm long, bilobed over 2.5 mm. Ovary with 2 biovulate locules.

Fruit globose, red at maturity, 6 mm in diameter when dry, nigrescent, glabrescent, containing 2–3 (-?) seeds 5 mm long, brownish.

Other Gabonese material:

N. Hallé 1058, Mboundou, SE of Makokou (fl. Feb.).

N. Hallé and J. F.-Villiers: 4400, Mbel on Komo (fl. Jan.).

Klaine 2133, 2670, 2709, 2710, 2899, environs of Libreville and Sibang (fl. Jan.–Feb.; fr. April).
