# *Impatiens gossweileri* G. M. Schulze

**Family**: [[Balsaminaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Impatiens]]

## Description

Small-leaved herb with one or two pink flowers on an axillary peduncle. Two equal opposite bracts occur at its summit. Lip boat-shaped, spur slightly swollen and no longer than the lip opening; leaf base lacks filiform appendages.

## Distribution

Mayombe in Gabon and Angola.
