# *Macroptilium lathyroides* (L.) Urb. Planche 83.8–17

**Genus**: [[Macroptilium]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Symb. antill. 9 : 457 (1928)

## Synonyms

- Phaseolus lathyroides L., Sp. pl. éd. 2 : 1018 (1763).

## Description

Annual or perennial herb; stem erect, sometimes twining (volubile) towards the apex, reaching 2 m. Stipules narrowly ovate, 5–10 × 1.5–2 mm. Leaf: petiole 1–8 cm; rachis 0.5–1.5 cm; stipels pointed, 1–3 mm long; leaflets with entire blade, elliptic to narrowly ovate, 3–8 × 1–3.5 cm, the lateral ones somewhat smaller and oblique, glabrous above, with fine pubescence below; lateral veins in 5 to 8 pairs. Inflorescence: peduncle 10–40 cm; rachis 3–35 cm; bracts pointed, ± 6 × 0.5 mm. Flower: pedicel 1–2 mm; bracteoles pointed, 1.2–4 × 0.5 mm; calyx with tube 4–5 mm long, lobes triangular, ± 1.5 × 1–2 mm; corolla red to dark red-brown, maroon, or dark purple, wings and keel sometimes green tinged with red, standard with obovate blade, 8–11 × 8–10 mm, with claw 3–6 mm, wings with elliptic blade, 9 × 5–8 mm, with claw 8–9 mm, keel with blade 7–10 × 2.5–4 mm, with claw 8–9 mm; stamens 16–18 mm, free part 3–5 mm; ovary 10–12 mm, silky; style 6–7 mm. Legume linear, reflexed, 5.5–11 × 0.25–0.4 cm, with ± 12 seeds, with fine silky pubescence. Seed oblique-oblong, 3–3.5 × 2–2.5 × 1 mm, brown or patterned brown and black; hilum central, ± 1 mm long.

## Distribution

West Indies, tropical and subtropical continental America, naturalized in tropical Africa, especially in the East and South, also in the Pacific; to be sought in Gabon

## Ecology

Coastal stations, in brush, fallows, fields, herbaceous savannas, roadsides; elsewhere up to 1100 m altitude. Use: Used as forage and green manure. Became a weed in Australia

## Vernacular names

macroptilium à fleur rouge (fr.); phasey bean, wild bushbean (angl.). b : Lewis et al. (2005), Mackinder et al. (2001), van der Maesen (2006a). ibliographie
