# *Hyperrhenia filipendula* (Hochst.) Stapf.

**Genus**: [[Hyperrhenia]]
**Family**: [[Gramineae]]
**Protologue**: STAPF, Fl. of Trop. Af. IX : 322 (1919)

## Synonyms

- Andropogon filipendulus Hochst.
- Cymbopogon filipendulus Rendle.

## Description

This species is reported from the East and South of the forest massif, but it has not yet been encountered in Gabon. It very probably exists, however, in the Franceville region. Rather similar to H. familiaris, it is distinguished from it essentially by the number of awns.
