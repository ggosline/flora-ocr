# *Albizia chinensis* (Osbeck) Merrill

**Genus**: [[Albizia]]

Tree 7 m tall ± for a diameter of 20 cm, reaching 20 m, for a diameter of 40 cm. Old branches glabrous, dark brown with brown lenticels, with fine longitudinal ridges. Young branches puberulent to pubescent or tomentose, light brown. Stipules early caducous, densely pubescent to tomentose on both faces, asymmetrically lanceolate, reaching a length of 1.5 cm; asymmetric base: petiolular margin strongly rounded and apetiolular margin very strongly cordate; apex acute. Petiole pubescent yellow, 3.5–6 cm long, upper face ± plane then canaliculate (canalicule bordered by 2 longitudinal ridges) bearing between the midpoint and the base a strongly prominent ellipsoid gland. Rachis pubescent, 12–22.5 cm long, upper face with broad keel, canaliculate bearing prominent ellipsoid to ± conical glands at the level of insertion of the 1–3 pairs of terminal pinnae. Pinnae 4–14 pairs. Pinna axis pubescent, 4–10.5 cm long, upper face keeled bearing up to 3 ellipsoid glands at the level of insertion of terminal leaflet pairs. Leaflets (10) 20–30 pairs, with basal pair reduced; with short pubescent petiolule on upper surface, 0.5–0.75 mm long. Blade glabrous above and ± pubescent below (hairs long ± appressed), weakly glaucous green above and pale green below on the living plant, blue-green above and green below on the dried material; ± oblong subulate, reaching 10 × 3 mm; asymmetric base: proximal margin rounded-truncate and distal margin weakly attenuate linear; margin ciliate; apex acute. Principal nerve strongly eccentric toward the distal margin to submarginally placed, concave above and prominent pubescent below. Secondary nerves, approximately 3, basal, radiating, not visible above, very obscurely prominent below.

Inflorescences in panicles of capituliform spikes, axillary or terminal. Panicle axis densely pubescent to tomentose yellow. Spikes fasciculate. Base of spikes surrounded by stipules as previously described and often accompanied by a reduced leaf, reduced to a pubescent axis 1–6.5 mm long, terminated by a circular gland. Spike peduncle pubescent to tomentose, 1.5–4.5 cm long. Bracteole pubescent, narrowly elliptic to linear, apex acute. Floral bud obovoid. Basal flowers sessile or subsessile. Calyx yellowish-green to yellow on the living plant, pubescent, obconical, 2.5–2.75 mm long; free lobes broadly triangular, ± 0.5 mm long, apex acute. Corolla pubescent externally becoming progressively glabrous toward the base, 5.5–6.25 mm long; free lobes ovate-triangular, 1.50–2 mm long, apex acute. Stamens white on the living plant, 25–28.5 mm long, united into non-exsert tube 3–3.80 mm long; free filaments slender; anthers oblongoid, ± 0.15 mm long. Pistil with short stipe 0.25–± 0.5 mm long; ovary with micropubescence in the upper half and some hairs toward the apex, narrowly oblong, 2–2.5 mm long, with 14 ovules; style slender reaching 34 mm, terminating in a short flattened stigma. Terminal flower solitary, sessile. Calyx pubescent ± cylindrical, 3 mm long, free lobes triangular, 0.5–0.75 mm long, apex acute. Corolla pubescent externally, progressively glabrous toward the base, distinctly obconical above the calyx, 7.5 mm long; free lobes ovate-triangular, 1.5–2 mm long, apex acute. Stamens 21 mm long, united into exsert tube 10 mm long, base enlarged. Pistil not stipitate; ovary resting on a flattened lobulate disc, with micropubescence on the upper half and some longer hairs at the apex, ellipsoid, 2 mm long, with 10 ovules; style 23 mm long.

Legume chartaceous, glabrous or glabrescent, dull brown on the dried material, 15 × 2 cm; base attenuate, sometimes stipitate; sutural margins thickened; lateral faces with very obscure or absent venation; apex obtuse. Seed elliptic, 7 × 4.5 mm; areole circular, near the micropyle, 1 mm in diameter.

## Type

Osbeck s.n., Chine, près Wampoa (non retrouvé)

## Discussion

A. chinensis is an Asian species whose range extends from India to southern China toward the East, to Ceylon, Thailand and Java toward the South. It is planted in Gabon as a shade tree in plantations.
