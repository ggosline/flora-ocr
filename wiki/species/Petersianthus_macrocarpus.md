# *Petersianthus macrocarpus* (P.Beauv.) Liben Planche 15

**Genus**: [[Petersianthus]]
**Family**: [[Lecythidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Jard. Bot. Nat. Belg. 38 : 207 (1968)

## Description

P. africanus (Welw. ex Benth.) Merrill, Philipp. J. Sci., C Bot. 11 : 201 (1916). Combretodendron africanum (Welw. ex Benth.) Exell, J. Bot. 68 : 182 (1930). Combretodendron macrocarpum (P.Beauv.) Keay in Hutch. & Dalziel, Fl. W. trop. Afr. éd. 2, 1 : 761 (1958). Tree reaching 27 m in height; trunk up to 130 cm in diameter; rhytidome regularly and longitudinally fissured or cracked, section yellowish white, bark fibrous, with unpleasant odor; crown very developed, with strong and numerous branches. Leaves glabrous; petiole (6–)17–23 mm, puberulent when young; blade obovate to elliptic, (3–)6–16 × (1–)4–7 cm, base cuneate, apex acuminate, margin denticulate, glossy above, matte below and bearing glands at the axil of 8–12 pairs of lateral veins. Inflorescence axillary, puberulent, lateral axes with peduncle reaching 4 cm and rachis 4–6 cm; bracts oblong, acute at apex, about 5 × 1.5 mm, rapidly caducous. Flower: pedicel 15–20 mm, articulate in its lower half above 2 oblong bracteoles 2–4 mm in length, acute at apex; sepals triangular-rounded, 2 × 2 mm, sparsely ciliate; petals suborbicular, 4 × 4 mm, cucullate, sparsely ciliate; stamens 2 mm long, filaments 5–6 mm, anthers with 4 short locules; disk 1 mm in diameter; ovary with 2 locules containing 10–15 ovules each; style 7–8 mm. Fruit on pedicel reaching 4 cm, fusiform, furnished with 4 wings arranged at right angles, 4.5–6 × 4–7.5 cm (including the wings). Seeds fusiform, 12–15 mm in length.

## Distribution

from Guinea to Cameroon, Central African Republic, Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola; in Gabon common and present throughout the country

## Ecology

secondary forests, dense humid forests and semi-deciduous forests; in Gabon up to 500 m altitude, elsewhere up to 700 m
