# *Magnistipula cuneatifolia* Hauman

**Genus**: [[Magnistipula]]
**Family**: [[Chrysobalanaceae]]
**Modern family**: [[Lecythidaceae]] subfam. Scytopetaloideae — *Scytopetalaceae* as circumscribed here is superseded; see [[Scytopetalaceae]]
**Protologue**: BJBB 21 : 175 (1921); WHITE, BJBB 46 : 289 (1976)

## Description

Large tree reaching 80 cm in diameter at the base. Trunk irregular, branching from 8 m in height; stout tortuous branches supporting a globose, extended crown, very leafy. Base of the trunk slightly flared, furnished with stout vertical bosses. Rhytidome yellowish-greenish with irregular, slightly prominent crests and numerous desquamation patches, numerous irregular lenticels 1-2 mm in diameter. Bark very hard, fairly thick (1 cm); oblique section with tightly packed orange-pink fibres on a pink background. Sapwood very hard, pale orange; heartwood? Branchlets glabrous; lenticels circular on young branchlets then irregular longitudinal striations. Stipules slightly falciform, ca. 1 × 6-8 mm, sparsely furnished with short appressed hairs then glabrescent, margin often subdenticulate. Petiole 5-12 mm, swollen and transversely cracked at the base. Blade oblanceolate reaching 8 (at the upper third) × 20 cm, sometimes very oblong-elliptic; base cuneate narrowly attenuate onto the petiole; apex acute with acumen reaching 12 mm in length; lateral nerves 5-6 pairs, curved ascending; blade glabrous; 2 small circular glands ± prominent near the blade margin, beneath, near the base.

Panicles tomentellate golden-brown (to the level of the sepals externally), terminal and axillary-subterminale, reaching 20-25 cm in height, with peduncle up to 5 cm, with robust primary axis, sometimes unramified, normally furnished only with a few oblique secondary axes, rarely themselves briefly ramified, often bearing at their base reduced foliaceous bracts; all ultimate axes racemiform bearing, especially towards their tip, sessile pauciflorous cymes, in general only the central flower being distinctly and more prominently pedicellate. Bracts ovate-triangular, 2-3 × 4-5 mm, fleshy at the base, often furnished laterally with one or two stout circular glands (diameter 0.5 mm); bracteoles ovate-lanceolate, 1.5 × 3 mm; pedicel of the central flower scarcely exceeding 5 mm, articulated ± distant from the base of the receptacle. Receptacle pink, obliquely campanulate curved, 6 mm high, 4-5 mm wide at the apex, furnished internally but only beneath the ovary with a dense tuft of hispid hairs directed downward; sepals 4-6 mm in height, obtuse to rounded at the apex, faintly tomentellate internally; petals white, at first ± concave-orbicular 4-5 mm high, with villous margin, then elongately obovate, 6 × 10 mm for the largest upper ones, with base broadly cuneate; stamens (6-7) united and pale violet at the base, curved, 6-9 mm long (10-12 mm? fide HAUMAN, l.c.: 176); staminodial tongue pale violet, ± 5-crenulate at the apex, 4 mm long but ± folded 1 mm from the base and coiled outward above, furnished internally at the base with hispid hairs directed downward; ovary 2.5 mm high, densely tomentellate; style curved, 8 mm long (10-12 mm? fide HAUMAN, l.c.: 176) hairy at the base.

Infructescence and fruit unknown. — PL. 24, p. 83.

## Type

## Discussion

PHYTOGEOGRAPHIC NOTES:

Magnistipula cuneatifolia Hauman is known to date only from 2 collections, one from northern Gabon, the other from Cameroon near Abong Mbang.
