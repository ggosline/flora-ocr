# *Garcinia obliqua* Sosef & Dauby Planche 6

**Genus**: [[Garcinia]]
**Family**: [[Clusiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Phytokeys 17 : 49 (2012)

## Description

Dioecious tree; trunk up to 35 cm in diameter; latex transparent; branchlets with circular or slightly angular section. Leaves opposite, glabrous: petiole (0.8 ‒ )1 ‒ 1.5( ‒ 2) cm; blade elliptic to lanceolate, (8 ‒ )9 ‒ 17( ‒ 18) × 2.5 ‒ 6 cm, with attenuate base and acuminate apex, coriaceous; lateral veins 6 ‒ 9 pairs, prominent beneath, finely prominent above, ascending evanescent; tertiary veins well visible to prominent on the lower face, finely prominent above; secretory canals, visible on the lower face, black and subparallel to the midvein. Inflorescence axillary, forming fascicles on cushions at the internode of the branchlets. Unisexual flowers, tetramerous, glabrous; pedicel 3 ‒ 6 mm; external sepals approximately 2.5 mm in length, internal ones approximately 3 mm in length, greenish; petals suborbicular, approximately 4 mm in length. Male flower: staminodial phalanges arborescent, each with 11 ‒ 19 stamens irregularly arranged, filaments free at the apex; ovary abortive or reduced. Female flower not seen. Fruit with pedicel of 12 mm (observed for one fruit), asymmetric, oblique through ovule abortion, with circular to slightly oval section, 3 ‒ 3.5 × 1.8 ‒ 2 cm, strongly wrinkled and coriaceous when dry, green with blue reflections when dry. Seed 1, obliquely ellipsoid, 2 ‒ 3 × 1.5 ‒ 2 cm, black.

## Distribution

endemic to Gabon, known from Ogooué-Ivindo and Ngounié

## Ecology

primary upland forest; at 450 ‒ 650 m altitude. B: Sosef & Dauby (2012). Bibliography
