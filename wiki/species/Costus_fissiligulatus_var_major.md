# *Costus fissiligulatus* var. *major* Gagnep.
**Family in Vol. 9**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Current placement**: Costaceae · **Genus**: [[Costus]]
## Description
Differs from typical [[Costus_fissiligulatus|*C. fissiligulatus*]] in its larger stature and leaves 20–22 × 4–6 cm.
## Distribution
Known only from Gabon. Type: Griffon du Bellay s.n.; the treatment also cites collection 352 from Etonda, collector unknown.
## Source
Koechlin, J. (1964). Zingibéracées. *Flore du Gabon* 9: 82.
