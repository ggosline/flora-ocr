# *Drypetes aylmeri* Hutch. & Dalziel

**Genus**: [[Drypetes]]
**Family**: [[Putranjivaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. W. trop. Afr. 1(2): 288 (1928)

## Description

Plate 40; Figure 7(F, G)

Tree up to ±23 m tall; trunk up to ±35 cm in diameter; bark dark-coloured, spotted with pale green; cut slightly aromatic; branchlets slightly furrowed, glabrescent, with scattered hairs; buds shortly hairy. Stipules not seen. Leaf: petiole 10–(14.8) mm, slightly wrinkled, glabrescent; blade narrowly oblong to elliptic-ovate, (12–16.5–21.5(−25) × (3.5–5.5–7(−8.5) cm, coriaceous, base oblique, acute or obtuse, shortly acuminate at apex, margin subentire, sometimes obscurely crenate or serrulate, usually recurved along the entire length, dark green and glossy above, pale green below; venation loosely reticulate, midvein yellowish, glabrous, secondary veins 6 or 7, ascending, looped and anastomosed well before the margin, impressed above and prominent below, yellowish, frequently drying blackish. Male inflorescence axillary or below the leaves along the branches, with up to ±25 flowers; bracts broadly ovate, 0.3–0.4 mm long. Male flower aromatic; pedicel slender, (2.2–2.7–4.3(−5.6) mm, puberulent; sepals 4, imbricate, suborbicular, 2–2.5 × 1.5–2.6(−3.3) mm, pale, greenish to yellowish, puberulent outside, densely puberulent inside, margin finely ciliate; stamens 5 to 10, in a whorl around the disk, filament 1–1.9 mm, anther elongate, 0.6–0.8 mm, glabrous; disk 1.2–1.9 mm in diameter, plane to slightly convex, rugulose, somewhat undulate, glabrous, with or without a conical projection in the centre 0.2 mm. Female inflorescence like the male, with up to 5(−10) flowers; bracts as in the male. Female flower: pedicel slender, (2–4–8.7) mm, puberulent; sepals 4 or 5, imbricate, suborbicular, 2.2–2.5 × 2.5–4.8 mm, pale greenish to yellowish, with fine and scattered hairs outside, densely and shortly hairy inside, margin finely ciliate; disk annular, 2.1–3.3 mm in diameter, puberulent, glabrescent; ovary globose-ellipsoidal, 1-locular, glabrous; style absent, stigma pulvinate-capitate, 1–1.3 mm in diameter, rounded. Fruit globose, 13–14 × 9.5–10.5 mm, pale yellowish-green, 1-seeded, with persistent sepals and stigmata, glabrous; pedicel 9.5–26 mm. Seed up to ±8 mm long.

## Distribution

Guinea, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Côte-d'Ivoire and Ghana; in Gabon: Estuaire, Ngounié, Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo and Woleu-Ntem

Habitat: primary and secondary evergreen forests, riparian forests; in Gabon at 390–460 m altitude, elsewhere up to 800 m.

## Source note

D. aylmeri is rarely collected in central and south-western Gabon. It shares a unilocular ovary and a pulvinate-capitate stigma with D. arborescens (sepals open), D. moliwensis (sepals imbricate, but with very robust pedicel) and D. similis (sepals open). Further collection of material is necessary to fully document its presence in Gabon; likewise the relationship with specimens from south-western Democratic Republic of the Congo should be established.
