# *Trichoscypha reygaertii* De Wild. Planche 22(D)

**Genus**: [[Trichoscypha]]
**Family**: [[Anacardiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Jard. Bot. État 4 : 368 (1914)

## Description

44 Frans J. BRETELER Shrub or tree reaching 15 m in height, with trunk up to 40 cm in diameter, or else liane with stem up to 15 cm in diameter; branchlets puberulent. Leaf: petiole 1.5–7(–14) cm, puberulent to glabrous; rachis 10–13 cm; leaflets (5–)7 to 13(–15), petiolule 6–13 mm, with blade oblong-elliptic to lanceolate, (3.5–)9–18(–25) × (1.5–)3.5–7(–10) cm, rounded to slightly cuneate at base, caudately acuminate over (1–)1.5–2(–3) cm at apex, glabrous above except for impressed midvein pubescent, sparsely puberulent to (sub)glabrous below, with (7–)9 to 14(–18) pairs of lateral veins, generally finely pustulate to granulate. Inflorescence terminal, axillary or sometimes arising below the leaves, broadly paniculate, up to 55 cm in length, pubescent to puberulent; bracts shorter than the axillary floral cluster. Male flower: pedicel 1–1.5 mm, puberulent; calyx 1 mm in length, with sepals almost free, spreading, ± deltoid, puberulent to glabrous; petals imbricate, spreading to reflexed, oval-elliptic, 1.5–2 mm in length, glabrous, white or yellow; stamens erect to incurved, 1.5–2 mm in length, glabrous; disk 1–1.5 mm in diameter, glabrous, with surface ± undulate. Female flower: pedicel, calyx and corolla as in the male flower; staminodes 1 mm in length; disk glabrous; ovary 1.5 mm in length, glabrous; stigmas 3 or 4, with stipe of 1 mm. Fruit subspherical to obovoid-ellipsoidal, 1.5–2.5 × 1.5–2 cm, smooth, glabrous.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué, Ngounié, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

primary and secondarized forest; in Gabon at 10–980 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1000 m. Uses: The fruits are edible. B: Breteler (2004), Van der Veken (1960a). Bibliography
