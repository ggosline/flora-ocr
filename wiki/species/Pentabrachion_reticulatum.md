# *Pentabrachion reticulatum* Müll.Arg. Planche 18

**Genus**: [[Pentabrachion]]
**Family**: [[Phyllanthaceae]]
**Protologue**: Flora 47 : 533 (1864)

## Description

Shrub to small tree up to 7(–20) m in height; branchlets glabrous to ± sparsely pubescent. Stipules triangular at the base, then ± subulate, 2–5 mm in length, with appressed hairs. Leaf: petiole wrinkled, 4–8(–11) mm, glabrous to sparsely pubescent; blade ovate-elliptic to oblong, 9–25(–32) × 3–10(–12) cm, cuneate to slightly cordate at the base, acumen 0.5–3(–4) cm, sparsely pubescent when very young, but glabrescent or with a few hairs on the midvein below; lateral veins (5–)7–11(–16) pairs. Inflorescence exfoliate. Male flower: pedicel 3–8(–9) mm, sparsely puberulent; sepals 4–5, spreading, elliptic, 3.5–5 mm in length; petals 5, suborbicular to obdeltoid, sometimes unguiculate, 1–1.5 mm in diameter, incised on the margins, glabrous; disk with slightly lobed margin; stamens (3–)5–7(–9), glabrous, filaments fused for 0.5–1.5 mm into an androgynophore, free part 1.5–2 mm in length; pistillode ± sessile on the androgynophore, glabrous. Female flower: pedicel 8–17 mm, up to 25 mm in fruit; sepals, petals and disk as in the male flower; ovary globose-trilobed, 2–3 mm in diameter, ± glabrous; styles 2–2.5 mm. Fruit 3-lobed, up to 10 × 18 mm, green. Seeds 5–7 mm in length, light brown.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, known from all provinces except Haut-Ogooué and Nyanga

## Ecology

primary and secondarized forest, forest regrowth; in Gabon, at 0–300 m altitude. Taxonomic note: Material of this species is easily confused with specimens of certain species of the genus Cleistanthus (for example C. gabonensis). Bibliography: Keay (1958), Léonard (1970), Pax & Hoffman (1922).
