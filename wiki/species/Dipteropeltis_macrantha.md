# *Dipteropeltis macrantha* Breteler Planche 8

**Genus**: [[Dipteropeltis]]
**Family**: [[Convolvulaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Jard. Bot. Natl. Belg. 64 : 184 (1995)

## Description

Slender liane, at least up to 10 m in length; branchlets glabrous or glabrescent, lenticellate or not, hollow. Leaf: petiole subcylindric, often wrinkled above, 5–8 mm, with short brown hairs, especially below; lamina elliptic, 5–9 × 2.5–4 cm, rounded to subcordate at base, acute to shortly acuminate over 0.5(–1) cm and generally mucronate over approximately 1 mm, papyraceous to coriaceous, ± glabrous above except the midvein impressed with short brown hairs, below with short brown hairs on the midvein, with 3–7 pairs of lateral veins. Inflorescence in axillary or terminal panicles with scattered flowers, up to 40 cm in length, hairy like the branchlets; bracts and bracteoles generally early caducous, narrowly triangular to filiform, up to 4 mm in length. Flower: pedicel 2.5–4 mm; calyx with free, erect sepals, 4–5 × 1–2.5 mm, the outer ones with base twice as wide as that of the inner sepals; corolla funnel-shaped, 10–18 mm in length, shallowly lobed, white, with short hairs on the external parts not folded in the bud and partially inside; corolla tube, 8–10 mm in length; stamens glabrous, inserted at the middle of the tube, with anthers of ± 1 mm in length, included; disc 0.5 mm thick, glabrous; pistil 6–6.5 mm in length, ovary conical, pubescent; styles approximately 3 mm in length, glabrous, stigmas included, subcapitate, 0.5 mm in diameter. Fruit ellipsoid, approximately 10 mm in length, ± as long as the inner sepals, sparsely hairy, enveloped by the two outer sepals enlarged, ± circular, deeply cordate at base, approximately 4–4.5 cm in diameter, sparsely puberulent. Mature seeds not seen.

## Distribution

endemic to Gabon, known from Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

primary and secondarized forest; at 0–600 m altitude. B: Breteler (1995). Bibliography
