# *Pandanus parvicentralis* Huynh, sp. nov.

**Family**: [[Pandanaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Pandanus]]

*Flore du Gabon* 28: 10 (1986).

## Description

**Syncarps**: Probably solitary, about 15 cm wide at maturity, triangular in cross-section; core triangular, about 5 cm on each side, hollow when dry.

**Drupes** (of specimen type): 5.7–6 cm long, about 0.9 cm wide, about 0.7 cm thick in the middle and 0.3 cm at the pileus, 5–6-angular, strongly compressed in the upper portion, swollen at the equator and shoulders; generally unilocular, free to the middle of the upper mesocarp, sometimes to the base of the peripheral endocarp; equator situated just above the base of the pileus. Pileus about 1.1 cm long, with scarcely prominent and ± irregular base; stigma sometimes bearing a spine on its back. Pileal hypodermis cells generally lignified and with little variation in dimensions (i.e., no giant cells among these lignified cells). Upper mesocarp rich in spongy material when dry, with base near the apex of the seed locule, axial fiber distinctly thicker downward than extra-axial fibers (which are very thick), slightly thicker upward; endocarp strongly shouldered (shoulders higher than axial apex), on the seed locule axis about 1.2 cm long, from the apex of the shoulders to the base of the germination tube about 1.4 cm long, on the periphery 0.5–0.8 cm long. Upper wall of seed locule slightly prominent within the upper mesocarp, nearly uniformly thickened, about 1.2 mm thick on the axis; seed locule about 0.8 × 0.4 cm, center slightly inframedian to medial, intralocular fiber about 1.9 mm wide; albumen whitish; germination tube about 3 mm long below the seed locule, entirely free from fibers protruding from the lower part of the seed locule; lower mesocarp moderately rich in spongy material when dry, its fibers in part slightly thicker than the thickest extra-axial fibers in the upper mesocarp. Other characters unknown or described below.

## Distribution

**Gabon**: Libreville, kilometre point 18.

## Habitat and ecology

Mangrove. The type was collected in March.

## Specimens examined

**Gabon**

- I.N.E.F. s.n., Libreville, p.k. 18, mangrove, 10.III.1969 (type specimen, P).

## Notes

Vegetative and male structures are unknown. The species is known in the
treatment only from the type collection and differs from the other Gabonese
species in its strongly compressed drupes c. 6 cm long, strongly shouldered
endocarp and seed-locule centre that is never supramedian.

Printed page 12 of Vol. 28 was obliterated during scanning. It contained the
continuation of this species treatment, between the description beginning on
p. 10 and the next treatment. Only isolated line fragments survived in the
OCR, so this page preserves all recoverable characters but must not be treated
as a complete transcription of the original description.
