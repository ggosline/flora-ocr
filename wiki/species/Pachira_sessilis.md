# *Pachira sessilis* Bentham

**Genus**: [[Pachira]]
**Family**: [[Bombacaceae]]
**Modern family**: [[Malvaceae]] subfam. Bombacoideae — *Bombacaceae* as circumscribed here is superseded; see [[Bombacaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Voy. Sulphur. : 70 (1884)

## Synonyms

- Bombacopsis sessile (BENTH.) PITTIER, Contr. U.S. Nat. Herb. 18 : 162 (1916); A. ROBYNS, Bull. Jard. Bot. Etat. Bruxelles 33 : 180 (1963); Ann. Miss. Bot. Gard. 51 : 41 (1964), syn. nov.
- Bombax sessile (BENTH.) BAKH., Bull. Jard. Bot. Buitenz. ser. 3, 6 : 180 (1924).
- Pachira angusta Duchassaing ex Decne., Fl. Serres 23 : 52 (1880), nom. nud.

## Description

This species is distinguished from the two others by:

— aliform buttresses 0.5–1 m high;

— apex of leaflets emarginate and mucronate most often;

— staminal column glabrous or sparsely pubescent on 5 narrow zones;

— style tomentellous hirsute at the base;

— capsule ellipsoid;

— seeds ornamented with low-relief striations.

## Type

Hinds s.n., île de Taboga, Panama (holo-, K; iso-, LE)

## Discussion

A species from Central America found in tropophilous and equatorial forests, or at their margins. It is cultivated in Africa. No specimen from Gabon has been observed.
