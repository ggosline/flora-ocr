# *Polygala lecardii* Chodat Planche 23(6), 25

**Genus**: [[Polygala]]
**Family**: [[Polygalaceae]]
**Protologue**: Mém. Soc. Phys. Genève 31(2) : 222, tab. 23, fig. 38–40 (1893)

## Description

Mém. Soc. Phys. Genève 31(2) : 222, tab. 23, fig. 38–40 (1893). Annual erect herb, up to 30 cm; stem weakly branched, filiform and glabrous. Leaves alternate, subsessile; limb linear to acicular, 3–7 × 0.5–1 mm, cuneate at the base, acute at the apex, glabrous. Inflorescence: elongated terminal racemes sparsely flowered, up to 6–7 cm in length; rachis glabrous; bracts linear, 0.7–0.8 mm in length, glabrous, early caducous; bracteoles similar, but 0.3–0.5 mm in length. Flower pink, mauve, purplish or greenish white; pedicel 0.5–1.2 mm, glabrous; upper sepal linear, 1–1.5 mm in length, glabrous; winged sepals elliptic, 2–2.5–2 × 1–1.25 mm, glabrous; lower outer sepals linear, 0.75 mm in length, glabrous, free; upper petals lanceolate, 1.5 mm in length; keel 1.5 mm in length, crest 0.5 mm in length, ± fimbriate; stamens 8. Fruit narrowly ovoid, 1.5–2 × 0.7–1 mm, acute, not winged, glabrous. Seeds fusiform, 1.5–2 × 0.5 mm, with dense appressed pubescence, the hairs covering the base; caruncle absent.

## Distribution

West and central tropical Africa: Sénégal, Guinée-Bissau, Guinée Conakry, Sierra Leone, Libéria, Côte d'Ivoire, Nigeria, Gabon, Guinée Équatoriale, Congo (Brazzaville) and République Démocratique du Congo; in Gabon: Estuaire, Ogooué-Maritime, Nyanga and Ngounié

## Ecology

gramineous formations, swampy savanna; at 0–550 m altitude. B: Keay & Taylor (1954), Paiva (1998). Bibliography
