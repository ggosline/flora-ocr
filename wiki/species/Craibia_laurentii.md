# *Craibia laurentii* (De Wild.) De Wild.

**Genus**: [[Craibia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Jard. Bot. État Bruxelles 7 : 268 (1920)

## Description

C. utilis M.B.Moss, Kew Bull. 1929: 195. C. atlantica sensu Pellegr. (non Dunn), Leg. Gabon 239 (1949), pro parte. Tree 20–30 m tall, reaching 45 cm diameter, glabrous except for the inflorescence; bark thin, smooth, brownish to grey. Leaf without stipels; leaflets 7 to 9, with wrinkled petiolule, lamina oblong to ovate or obovate, 6–14 × 3–5 cm, rounded to cuneate at base, apex abruptly acuminate, coriaceous, dark green shiny above, greyish on both faces when dry; veins scarcely prominent below. Inflorescence in terminal and axillary panicle, dense and much-branched, 5–10 cm long; peduncle glabrescent or glabrous toward base; bracts axillant, similar to bracteoles but caducous. Flower fragrant, with pedicel, bracteoles and calyx with dense, dark brown, semi-appressed pubescence; pedicel 1–2.5 mm; bracteoles suborbicular to square, 1.5 mm long, at base of calyx, spreading at anthesis; hypanthium short or absent; calyx cupulate, 3–4 mm long, scarcely toothed, lobes 1 mm or less long; corolla white, standard oblong, 12–14 × 6–8 mm, white with a green patch in the middle; stamens 8–13 mm; ovary with 4 ovules, red, almost glabrous; style glabrous. Pod slightly flattened to suborbicular, ± 2.8 × 2.2–2.4 × 0.7–1.3 cm, 1-seeded. Seed ovate, ± 17 × 13 × 5 mm, brown, smooth.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon, Ethiopia, Sudan, Kenya, Uganda, Republic of the Congo and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, known from Ogooué-Ivindo

## Ecology

gallery forest edge; in Gabon at 180–200 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1300 m. Use: In Uganda, boiled seeds are consumed and are very sweet, but after the first boiling, the water is discarded, because of its toxic properties

## Source note

Outside Gabon, pods may measure up to 12 × 3.5 cm. b: Gillett (1960a), Hauman (1954a). Bibliography
