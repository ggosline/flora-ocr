# *Rourea erythrocalyx* (Gilg ex Schellenberg) Jongkind

**Genus**: [[Rourea]]
**Family**: [[Connaraceae]]

## Description

in BRETELER (éd.), AUWP 89 (6): 334 (1989).

descr. in clavi (1910). TYPE: Zaïre, Gillet 2006 (holo-B delet.; lecto-BR).

## Type

Zaïre, Ubangi, Gugo près de Yakoma, Thonner 228 (holo-BR)

Gillet 2006, Zaïre

## Discussion

– Roureopsis thonneri (DE WILD.) SCHELLENB., EBJ 55: 447 (1919); PR 103 (4, 127):

111 (1938); TROUPIN, FCBR 3: 78 (1952); EXELL & MENDONÇA, CFA 2: 145 (1954).

Very large liana reaching up to 20 m. Branchlets puberulent.

Leaves. Petiole 1–8 cm long; rachis 2.5–12 cm; petiolules 1–3 mm long; leaflets 3–11, ovate to elliptic, glabrous or puberulent beneath, numerous mucilage cells on the upper surface forming small dots on herbarium specimens; acuminate at the apex; acumen emarginate; terminal leaflet $ 3.5–10 \times 1.5–6 $ cm; lateral leaflets $ 2.5–9 \times 1.5–4.5 $ cm, slightly asymmetric.

Inflorescences ≤6 cm long, puberulent; bracteoles close to the calyx. Pedicels articulate at the apex.

Flowers. Sepals 3–5 × 1.5 mm, almost glabrous on the inner surface. Petals 11–14 × 1–1.5, free, enrolled at least in bud. Stamens, the longer ones 1.5–4.5 mm long, the shorter ones 1–3 mm. Pistil 1.2–5 mm long; style with scattered hairs; ovary pubescent.

Fruits: follicles 1–5 per flower, 14–22 × 6–8 mm, glabrous dehiscing along a ventral suture. Sepals 13–15 × 4–7 mm beneath the fruit, reddish, finely coriaceous, exterior glabrous or with scattered hairs.

Seed with testa ≈ 1/4 fleshy, the rest glossy black. Radicle ventral. - Pl. 37, p. 119.

Cameroon, Gabon, Angola (Cabinda), Zaïre

## Ecology

ombrophilous forests; alt. 0–500 m
