# *Bertiera capitata* De Wildeman

**Genus**: [[Bertiera]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Ann. Mus. Congo, 5, 2 : 169 (1907)

## Description

Erect shrub 1.80–4 (5) m tall, with glabrous oblique branchlets; young internodes quadrangular when dry. Stipules glabrous 10–16 × 7–12 mm, connate, with apex ± acute or obtuse. Petioles glabrous 10–23 mm. Blade fragile when dry, moderately pubescent on both faces, discolorous, 12–25 × 6–12 cm; base rounded and briefly cuneate; apex acute. Midvein pubescent on upper surface; main veins with appressed pubescence on lower surface. 11–14 pairs of secondary veins. Tertiary intersecondary veins chevron-like.

Terminal inflorescences many-flowered, contracted into pseudo-capitate spherical heads 25–35 mm diam. A pair of leaves, sometimes reduced, is positioned just below the lower flowers. Bud pale green, acute acuminate. Calyx pubescent tubular for nearly 4 mm, with whitish caudate lobes 2–3 mm. Corolla white, reaching 12 mm long. Tube 6 mm long, glabrous in its narrow part, ± puberulent above; lobes ± glabrous, caudate, approximately 7 mm long. Dense cottony pubescence internally in the tube all around the anthers. The latter reach 3 mm in length, their apex fairly short. Disk cylindric.

Infructescence somewhat accrescing, showing ± clearly the contracted axes composing it. Pedicels much reduced in the flower, reaching up to 2.5–3 cm in fruits. Fruit green, globose, 1–1.5 cm diam., glabrescent, with calycine limb ± deteriorated. Seeds angular compressed, 1.3 mm long.

## Type

Marc Laurent 131 (BR!), Eala, Congo

## Discussion

Secondary forest and roadside scrub on well-drained soil.

Cameroon, Gabon, Congo, from 400 to 950 m altitude, and extending to Tanganyika and Uganda (Maitland 803, K!).
