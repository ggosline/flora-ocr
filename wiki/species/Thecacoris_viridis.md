# *Thecacoris viridis* (Müll.Arg.) Leandri ex G.L.Webster

**Genus**: [[Thecacoris]]
**Family**: [[Phyllanthaceae]]
**Protologue**: Ann. Missouri Bot. Gard. 81 : 52 (1994)

## Synonyms

- Cyathogyne viridis Müll.Arg., Flora 47 : 536 (1864).
- Cyathogyne preusii Pax, Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 23 : 521 (1897).

## Description

Herb or suffrutex, more rarely shrub, (0.2)–0.3–0.6(–1) m tall; stem often prostrate-rooting then erect; branchlets puberulent, glabrescent. Stipules triangular, simple or 2–3(–4)-lobed at base or laciniate, 1–5(–6) × 0.5–3 mm, puberulent. Leaf: petiole channelled above, (3–)6–10(–25) mm, puberulent; lamina rhombiform to elliptic or sometimes obovate, 5–18 × 2.5–7(–8.5) cm, acute to rounded or sometimes obtuse at base, acuminate for 0.5–1 cm and often mucronate at apex, margin very often dentate to undulate, ± glabrous above, puberulent and often early glabrescent on midvein and sometimes on 5–10 lateral nerves below. Inflorescence once to several times branched or sometimes simple, puberulent, the male and female flowers up to 13 cm long. Male flower white: pedicel 0–1 mm; sepals ± ovate, 1–1.5 mm long, pubescent; pistillode cyathiform, glabrous. Female flower: pedicel 1–2(–3) mm; sepals ovate, 1–1.5 mm long, pubescent; disc pubescent; ovary globose, ± pubescent to glabrous. Fruit trilobed, 3–5 × 6–8 mm, glabrous to sparsely puberulent. Seeds ovoid-ellipsoid, 2.5–3 × 1.5–2 mm, brownish, shiny.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, collected in all provinces except Haut-Ogooué

## Ecology

primary and secondarized forest, often on river margins; in Gabon, at 0–500 m altitude. Taxonomic note: The subdivision of this species into three subspecies, as treated in the Flora of Central Africa (Léonard 1995a), is not retained here. b: Breteler (2011a), Léonard (1995a). ibliography
