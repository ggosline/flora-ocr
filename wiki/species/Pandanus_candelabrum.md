# *Pandanus candelabrum* Palisot de Beauvois

**Family**: [[Pandanaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Pandanus]]

*Flore d’Oware et de Bénin*: 37–38, tabs 21–22 (1804).

## Description

**Habit**: Tree to 15 m tall. Trunk supported by stilt roots.

**Leaves**: To 2 m long and 6 cm or more wide; lateral folds unarmed. Marginal
spines antrorse, although a few lower ones may be retrorse, and generally
denser than the costal spines at the same level. Costal spines retrorse in the
lower part, sometimes with a few antrorse ones, becoming antrorse above and
continuing to the apex.

**Infructescence**: Polysyncarpic, with 3–5 ovoid syncarps c. 16 × 10 cm,
occasionally to 20 × 13 cm. Rachis c. 35 cm long, to 1.4 cm thick and slightly
curved. Bracts 9–10; upper bracts without a tail, spatulate-navicular and wholly
or partly membranous; lower bracts membranous below, to 42 × 6.5 cm, with
antrorse costal spines nearly to the blade base, unarmed lateral folds, and a
tail increasing downward to 10 cm.

**Drupes**: 3.5–4 × 0.9–1 × 0.6–0.8 cm, 5–6-angled, broadly conical in the
free part, with 1–3 stigmas but usually one. Most pileal hypodermal cells
unlignified. Base of the upper mesocarp near the roof of the seed locule;
axial fibre slightly thicker than the thickest extra-axial fibres. Endocarp c.
1.3 cm along the seed-locule axis and 0.4–0.6 cm peripherally, not or only
slightly shouldered. Seed locule c. 0.9 × 0.4 cm, its centre usually slightly
supramedian. Germination tube c. 2.9 mm below the locule, free from the lateral
fibres or sometimes fused to them only in its upper part.

**Male inflorescence**: c. 32 cm long, with 9–10 spikes to 14 × 2.2 cm. Male
flowers to 15 mm long, comprising a column with 10–12 subumbellately or
umbellately arranged stamens; filaments c. 4 mm and oblong anthers c. 1.4 ×
0.4 mm. Pollen subspherical and echinulate.

## Type

Nigeria, *Palisot de Beauvois s.n.* (lectotype G).

## Distribution

**Range documented by the treatment**: Nigeria and Cameroon.

The species is Nigerian in origin and was confirmed in Cameroon from *Letouzey
8717*, Mayo Darlé, 24 June 1967 (P), with leaf, infructescence rachis and
fruit. Its presence in Gabon was considered possible but still required
confirmation from fruiting specimens; it is therefore not recorded here as a
confirmed Gabon occurrence.

## Specimens examined

**Cameroon** — *Letouzey 8717*, Mayo Darlé, 24 June 1967 (leaf,
infructescence rachis and fruit, P).
