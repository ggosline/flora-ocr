# *Scyphosyce manniana* Baillon

**Genus**: [[Scyphosyce]]
**Family**: [[Moraceae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia, sér. 1, 11 : 293 (1875) ; HUTCH. in HUTCH. & RENDLE, FTA 6 (2) : 221 (1917) ; BERG, BJBB 47 : 285 (1977)

## Description

HUTCH. in HUTCH. & RENDLE, FTA 6 (2): 221 (1917); BERG, BJBB 47: 285 (1977).

- Cyathanthus zenkeri ENGL., Nat. Pflanzenfam., Nachtr. 1: 120 (1897). TYPE: Zenker 905, Cameroon (holo-, B.; iso-, G, K, P).

- Scyphosyce zenkeri (ENGL.) ENGL., Mon Afr. Pfl. 1, Moraceae: 31 (1898).

- Scyphosyce gilletii DE WILD., Ann. Mus. Congo, Bot. ser. 5, 1: 26 (1903); HAUMAN, FCB 1: 98 (1948). TYPE: Gillet s.n., Zaire (holo-, BR).

1. The characters of fruit and seed only according to S. manniana.

Undershrubs reaching 50 cm in height; small leafy branchlets sometimes branched, 1–4 mm thick, (rather) densely hirtellous to puberulent, whitish to brownish, with straight to curved hairs; epidermis not exfoliating. Leaves often grouped at the apex of branchlets. Blade elliptic to lanceolate, most often broadly to narrowly obovate to subpanduriform or oblanceolate, symmetrical or somewhat asymmetrical, (1.5–) 5–18 × (0.5–) 2–7 cm, chartaceous, acuminate to subcaudate, base (sub)obtuse to subcordate, at least one half auricate; margin entire or ± distinctly crenate to (sometimes coarsely) dentate toward the apex; upper surface (almost) glabrous; lower surface sparsely to rather densely puberulent to hirtellous (in part with uncinate hairs) especially on the main nerves, sometimes almost glabrous; nerves slightly prominent to plane above, ± prominent below; 6–15 pairs of secondary nerves, with or without parallel tertiary nerves. Petiole 2–12 mm long, (rather) densely hirtellous, with straight to curved hairs. Stipules subpersistent, 2–15 (–20) mm long, lanceolate to narrowly ovate, with a prominent midrib, a mucronate to (sub)aristate apex, puberulent to almost glabrous, dark brown.

Inflorescences solitary to grouped, at least initially deflexed. Receptacle 2–4 mm in diameter, cupuliform to infundibuliform, whitish to pale pinkish at anthesis; bracteoles ciliolate; peduncle 3–11 mm long; peduncle and receptacle glabrous or with scattered unicellular appressed hairs and/or pluricellular globose-capitate reddish hairs; at the base of the peduncle, bract reniform to deltoid, mucronate and ciliolate. Flowers 8 from the base of the receptacle, partially in groups of 2–3; perianth 1.5–2.5 mm high, (irregularly) (3–) 4 (–5) -lobed to (3–) 4 (–5) -fid, the upper part being puberulent or only ciliolate; pedicels reaching ca. 4 mm in length; stamens 1, 2 (3) with filaments 2–3 mm long, very sparsely to densely hairy, upper part glabrous, and anthers (0.3–) 0.5–0.8 × 0.15–0.3 mm, with rather narrow connective, sometimes apiculate. Flower 9 sessile at the base of the receptacle; perianth 2–3 mm high, with 2 free tepals, conduplicate, imbricate, sparsely puberulent to glabrous, ciliolate; sometimes a third plane tepal, lanceolate to narrowly ovate; ovary ca. 1 mm high; style 2–3 mm long; stigmas ca. 1.5 mm long, filiform to ribbon-like, very slightly puberulent.

Infructescences green, ca. 7 mm in diameter. Perianth 1–1.2 cm high in fruit (purplish to black at maturity). Fruit ca. 8–10 mm long; endocarp thin, brown to blackish, with anastomosing lighter nerves, slightly wrinkled; testa thin, fused to the endocarp; embryo with a distinct mark of the thickened portion of the testa; cotyledons equal or subequal. – Pl. 29, p. 99.

## Discussion

The species is highly variable in the shape and dimensions of the leaves and, to a lesser degree, in the indumentum of the stamen filaments. According to this variation, apparently local or regional forms of the species may be recognized.

S. manniana has an apparently isolated distribution range, extending from SE Nigeria (Calabar) to Zaire (near Kinshasa) and Angola-Cabinda passing through Cameroon and Gabon, in the undergrowth of humid forests, near watercourses, often in periodically flooded places. This gregarious species is not common but apparently locally abundant.
