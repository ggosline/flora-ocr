# *Stelechantha cauliflora* (R. Good) Brem.

**Genus**: [[Stelechantha]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bremekamp, Bot. Jahrb. 71 : 218 (1940)

## Synonyms

- Urophyllum cauliflorum R. Good, Journ. Bot. 61 : 87 (1923).
- U.(.) stelecanthum Mildbread, Wiss. Ergebn. D. Zentr. Afr. Exp. 1910-1911, 2:91 (1922), nomen.

## Description

Small shrub 2–3 m tall; wood exceeding 12 mm diam.; branchlets glabrous; internodes 3–8 cm. Stipules coriaceous glabrous, longitudinally striate, insertion somewhat or distinctly narrower than the branchlet, ovate or elliptic acute, 7–16 × (2) 3–8 mm. Petiole 7–20 mm, glabrous. Blade coriaceous, ± discolorous, olivaceous, glabrous, obovate or elliptic, 10–21 × 3–6.5 cm, acute at base; acumen ± 1 cm. 8–12 pairs of lateral veins slightly ascending, in arches often distinctly closed.

Inflorescences many-flowered cauline in glomerate clusters or small briefly pedunculate cymes, micropubérulent, ca. 2 cm long. Bud acute subacuminate 10–14 mm long, nigrescect when dry. Flower bluish or blue-violet. Calyx micropubérulent, truncate, ample, cupuliform over 2.5–3 mm in height. Corolla with tube 7–11 mm long by 3 mm wide; 5 lobes 3 mm long with rostrate apex; throat with erect pubescence at the level of the anthers, with internal pilose hairs inflexed below. 5 anthers ca. 3 × 0.5–0.6 mm, with narrow acumen ca. 0.5 mm, with filament 1–1.5 mm. Disc ca. 2 mm diam., barely convex, surface reticulate. Style glabrous 4–4.5 mm of which the stigma 2–2.5 mm, narrow and bilobed. Ovary with 2 locules separated from the apex and over more than half by a false septum. Ovular mass in deep V 1.2 mm high. The fruit would be a red berry according to Bremekamp (we do not know the source of this indication), mauve according to Bouquet; infructescences many-flowered with pedicels reaching 2 cm.

## Type

Gossweiler 7661 (type BM) et 7662, Mayumbe portugais (échantillons vus)

## Discussion

Rarely observed, this species is also from Cameroon, northern slope of the Ntem basin, and from Congo, region of watershed between the upper Ogooué and the Kouilou basin (A. Bouquet 1002).

Le Testu: 5582, between Moussanga and Nyanga-Moubigou, Haute-Ngounyé (fl. Oct.).
