# *Fagara dinklagei* Engl.

**Family**: [[Rutaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Fagara]]

## Description

Prickly woody species with pinnate, gland-dotted leaves. The unisexual flowers are 4-merous, with a thick disk and a reduced gynoecium in male flowers. Female flowers develop one or two follicles, as illustrated from Gabon and Cameroon material.

## Distribution

Cameroon and Gabon in the treatment.
