# *Begonia erectotricha* Sosef

**Genus**: [[Begonia]]
**Family**: [[Begoniaceae]]
**Protologue**: Wageningen Agric. Univ. Papers 91-4 : 98 (1992)

## Description

Plant reaching 30 cm in height. Leaf peltate; petiole 10–28 cm, hirsute; blade spread when fresh, not bullate, symmetric or slightly asymmetric, circular to broadly elliptic-obovate or broadly elliptic, 12.5–22.5 × 8.5–17 cm, apex generally briefly acuminate, upper surface distinctly pilose. Inflorescence: peduncle 1–5.5 cm with 2(–3) ♂ flowers and 1 ♀ flower with yellow tepals; bracts 1–3.5 mm long. ♂ Flower: tepals 6–10.5 × 6.5–11 mm; androecium with 28–46 stamens. ♀ Flower: pedicel elongating in fruit to 5.5 mm; tepals 7–9.5 × 6.5–13 mm; ovary (including wings) obtriangular-obovoid very depressed to very broadly obtriangular, 2.5–7.5 × 6.5–9.5 mm, glabrous or sometimes with a few long hairs, with 3–4 obtriangular-obovate very depressed to very broadly obovate wings, 1–3.5 mm wide; styles 3–4. Fruit like the ovary, but approximately 8.5 × 9–10 mm; beak up to 1.5 mm.

## Distribution

endemic to the Cristal Mountains, in northwestern Gabon; original stations reduced; rare species

## Ecology

on flat terrain or on slopes and banks, in primary or secondarized forest, often at the edge of streams and rivers; at approximately 100 m altitude. B: Sosef (1992a), Sosef (1994). BIBLIOGRAPHY
