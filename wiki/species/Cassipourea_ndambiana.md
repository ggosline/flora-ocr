# *Cassipourea ndambiana* Breteler Planche 30.5, 34

**Genus**: [[Cassipourea]]
**Family**: [[Rhizophoraceae]]
**Protologue**: Edinb. Journ. Bot. 65 : 420 (2008)

## Description

C. mezilii Floret, nomen in sched. Small tree up to 15 m tall; trunk up to 20 cm in diameter; branchlets glabrous. Stipules caducous, triangular, 4–10 mm long, with appressed hairs on the outside, glabrous on the inside, the base provided with a dense band of cerocysts. Leaf: petiole (4–)6–11(–14) mm, glabrous or glabrescent; blade elliptic, 10–14(–22) × (3–)4–6(–9) cm, 2–3 times as long as wide, base rounded or cuneate, apex acuminate with acumen 0.5–1.5 mm, coriaceous, with entire margin, both surfaces glabrous except for the midvein sparsely puberulent below; lateral veins 6–8 pairs, indistinct. Inflorescence axillary, with 2–3(–4) flowers, compact; peduncle up to 1 mm, pubescent; bracts and bracteoles ovate-triangular, 1–1.5 mm long. Flower: 5-merous; pedicel slender, (5–)7–20(–25) mm, articulate near the calyx, stipe 1–2 mm; calyx cupuliform, 7–10 mm long, persistent in fruit, the tubular part shorter than the 5 spreading lobes of (4–)5–7 mm, sparsely puberulent on the outside, densely silky on the inside; petals incurved in bud, spatulate, ± 11 × 4–5 mm, pennilobate on the upper half, lobes ribbon-like, pubescent; stamens 35–45, 3–5 mm long, with glabrous filaments, united at the base forming a collar 0.5–1 mm high; anthers 1.5–2 mm long, pilose; ovary subglobose, ± 2 mm long, with 3–5 locules; style 4 mm, persistent in fruit, stigma peltate. Immature fruit subglobose, deeply 3–5-furrowed, ± 1.5 cm in diameter, glabrous. Seeds not seen.

## Distribution

Southern Cameroon and Gabon; in Gabon, rare, known only from two localities in Ogooué-Lolo: east of Ndambi, approx. 70 km east of Latoursville (type locality) and 34 km WSW of Bambidie

## Ecology

rainforest; in Gabon, at ± 300 m altitude. Floral buds in October, fruits in November. b: Breteler (2008). Bibliography
