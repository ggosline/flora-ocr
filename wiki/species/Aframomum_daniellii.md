# *Aframomum daniellii* (Hook. f.) K. Schum.
**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Aframomum]]
## Description
Robust herb 3–4 m with reddish basal sheaths and glabrous 20–40 × 6–9 cm blades. Short basal raceme has lateral spikes of up to six flowers and red bracts. Fruit borne at the leafy-stem base.
