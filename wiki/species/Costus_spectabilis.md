# *Costus spectabilis* (Fenzl) K. Schum.
**Family in Vol. 9**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Current placement**: Costaceae · **Genus**: [[Costus]]
## Description
Stoloniferous herb with four broadly obovate leaves forming a cross-shaped rosette flat on the ground. Blades 4–15 cm across, green with a red rim, glabrous above and hairy beneath. Three or four subsessile yellow flowers arise at the rosette centre; corolla lobes are 3.5–4.5 cm and the labellum is longer. The membranous capsule is 1.5–2 cm long.
## Ecology and distribution
Frequent in savanna through humid and subhumid tropical Africa, flowering after fire at the beginning of the rains. Gabon material: Le Testu 1527, Nyanga savannas at Bouyanga; Thollon 1365, Mayumba.
