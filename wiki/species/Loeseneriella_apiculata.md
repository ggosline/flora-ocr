# *Loeseneriella apiculata* (Welwistch ex Oliver) Hallé ex Wilczek

**Genus**: [[Loeseneriella]]
**Family**: [[Celastraceae]]
**Protologue**: FCB 9: 151, tab. 19 (1960); HALLÉ, Bull. IFAN, sér. A, 21 (3): tab. 70 fig. b (1959), nom. nud.; Pollen & Spores 2 (1): 11 (1960)

## Description

HALLÉ, Bull. IFAN, sér. A, 21 (3): tab. 70 fig. b (1959), nom. nud.; Pollen & Spores 2 (1): 11 (1960).

– Hippocratea apiculata Welw. ex OLIV., FTA 1: 369 (1868); HIERN, Catal. Welw. 1: 149 (1896); EXELL & MENDONÇA, CFA 2: 22 (1954).

- H. pynaertii DE WILD., Ann. Mus. Congo b., Bot., sér. 5, 2 : 294 (1908). TYPE : Pynaert 736, BR!

Very large forest liana reaching 30 to 40 m. Bark of trunk almost smooth or somewhat rugose grey ochraceous or greyish brown, sometimes fissured lengthwise. Rubber ± abundant in the cortical parenchyma, absent in the leaves. Old wood, 18 cm in diameter, ornamented with deep furrows, tends to separate into 4 quarters. Internodes glabrous, russet, becoming lenticellate. Stipules reduced, spaced on a transverse callus. Petiole 5–9(12) mm. Blade entire, obovate or elliptic 7–18(–25) × 3–10(–12) cm, subcoriaceous, mostly russet when dry; base variable often obtuse; apex often suddenly and briefly acuminate. Secondary nerves 7–9(10) pairs; fine network prominent when dry.

Cymes ± lax and multiflorous, up to 12(–15) cm long, sometimes grouped into terminal panicles; axes puberulent. Pedicel of lower flowers long and slender, caducous above the base; the others reaching only 2–3 mm and detaching at the base. Bud narrowly pyramidal attenuate, subobtuse at the apex, 5–6.5 mm long, clothed with very fine puberulence. Flower green with white central column. Sepals acute 0.5 mm. Petals valvate at the base and very narrowly imbricate approaching the apex, 4.5–6 × 1.2–1.5 mm, glabrous only at the base of the inner face. Disk elevated, pyramidal, with 5 prominent teeth around the base. Anther transverse, 0.7 mm wide, subapically dehiscent. Style 1.5 mm. Ovary locules with 8–10 ovules each, just below the level of the androecium.

Mericarps ovoid-oblong 7–11 × 3–4 cm. Seeds 5.5 × 2 cm, almost uniformly salmon-russet; cotyledons 21–23 × 4–5 mm; funicular sheaths 4–5 × 3 mm. - Pl. 68, fig. 1–12, p. 237.

## Type

Welwitsch 1323, Angola, Golungo Alto (holo-, BM!; iso-, BR! K! P!)

## Discussion

Nigeria (Latilo FHI 56866), Cameroon, Gabon, Central African Republic (Tisserant 497), Congo (Attims 97, Descoings 9634), Zaïre, Cabinda (Gossweiler 7627) and Angola.

Dense humid forests, sometimes flooded, or forest margins, on schistose or clayey soil. Altitude noted, 650–700 m.
