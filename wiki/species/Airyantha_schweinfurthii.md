### Distribution

West and central Africa, from Côte d'Ivoire to the Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, known only from Ngounié

### Ecology

Primary and secondary forest, forest edge near the sea and lagoons, riparian forest, swamp forest; at 0–720 m altitude

### Source note

Brummitt (1968a) distinguished a subsp. schweinfurthii from central Africa and a subsp. confusa (Hutch. & Dalziel) Brummitt from West Africa and Bioko. The latter with smaller flowers and shorter pedicels. I consider that the observed differences do not well support this distinction, and both ranges are found in all countries where the species is present. Moreover, in the juvenile state, the difference is not at all discernible. Well-developed pods resemble those of Pithecellobium Mart., but these are arillate. Pods possessing a single seed are not spiral, but rather falciform. The Asian species A. borneensis (Oliv.) Brummitt is very close to A. schweinfurthii, but has pale brown pubescence and smaller bracts and bracteoles. b: Brummitt (1968a), Hepper (1958), Lewis et al. (2005), Pellegrin (1949), Toussaint (1953). ibliography
