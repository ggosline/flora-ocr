# *Begonia poculifera* Hook.f. var. poculifera Planche 35

**Genus**: [[Begonia]]
**Family**: [[Begoniaceae]]
**Protologue**: B. haullevilleana De Wild. in Ann. Mus. Congo, sér. 5 (Bot.) 2 : 320 (1908)

## Description

in Oliv., Fl. trop. Afr. 2 : 574 (1871). B. haullevilleana De Wild. in Ann. Mus. Congo, sér. 5 (Bot.) 2 : 320 (1908). Epiphytic, sometimes rupestral; stem more or less lignified at the base, erect or pendulous, reaching 200 cm in length. Stipules narrowly ovate to triangular, reaching 55 × 15 mm. Leaf: petiole 1–6 cm; blade asymmetric, distinctly falciform, obliquely ovate, 3.5–22 × 2–19 cm, 3–7-palmatinerved, rounded to cuneate at the base, acuminate to caudate at the apex, margin entire or obscurely dentate or lobed, with an indumentum of denticulate or fimbriate scaly hairs on both surfaces. Inflorescence: peduncle (0.5–)1.5–4(–7) cm; bracts broadly obovate, 1–2.5(–3) × 1–4(–5) cm. Male flower: pedicel reaching 3 cm; tepals broadly elliptic to almost circular, (4–)12–25(–29) × (3–)10–21(–28) mm, pale pink or white streaked with red; stamens 10–45. Female flower: pedicel 0.5–2 mm; tepals with free parts similar to those of male flowers, sometimes almost circular, reaching 22 mm in diameter, perianth column 5–15 mm long; ovary obovoid, 4–9 × 2–6 mm, slightly 4(–5)-sulcate, with scaly indumentum; styles 4(–5). Fruit almost sessile, obovoid, 8–23 × 5–18 mm, sulcate, whitish, often reddish-brown at the apex.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon, Bioko, Gabon, Democratic Republic of Congo, Angola, Rwanda, Burundi, Tanzania

## Ecology

Rainforests; 470–2250 m altitude. Uses: A decoction of the leaves was used by women to stimulate lactation. B: Plana et al. (2006), Wilczek (1969a), de Wilde & Arends (1980).
