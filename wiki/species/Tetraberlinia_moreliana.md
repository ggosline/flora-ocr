# *Tetraberlinia moreliana* Aubréville, sp. nov. ¹

**Genus**: [[Tetraberlinia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: A. Aubréville, Adansonia 8,2 (1968)

## Synonyms

- Tetraberlinia sp. nov., de Saint-Aubin, For. Gabon : 57 (1963).

## Description

Beniamam (Pl. 77, p. 301)

Trees. Leaves with 9-13 pairs of sessile, opposite leaflets, the first pair attached less than 1 cm from the insertion of the rachis. Large stipules bearing auricles, up to 3 cm in length; caducous. Rachis approximately 15 cm, swollen at the insertion. Leaflets lanceolate, somewhat falcate, obliquely acuminate, with asymmetric base, the anterior margin parallel to the rachis, the posterior margin rounded and auriculate, measuring up to 5-8 cm in length, by 1-2 cm in width, glabrous, membranaceous. Secondary veins inconspicuous, several of them diverging from the point of insertion on the rachis.

Inflorescences? Buds oblong, densely ferruginous velvety. Bracteoles carinate, valvate, 10-12 mm in length, by 5 mm in width, velvety on both faces. Sepals 5, very unequal: a large sepal short and very broad at the base of the large petal, pubescent, measuring 5 mm in length by 1.5 mm in height; one ovate, ciliate, 3.5 mm in height; two others, ovate, ciliate, 1.5 mm in height. One large oblong petal, very shortly unguiculate, pubescent externally and on the margins within, 9.5 mm long; 2 small petals 2.5 mm, bearing 2 small tufts of hairs towards the apex, and 2 others minute 0.75 to 1.5 mm, also bearing small tufts of hairs. Stamens 10, 1 free, and 9 with filaments 11 to 18 mm, connate for approximately 2 mm at the base. Ovary stipitate, hairy; 2-4 ovules.

Robust pod obliquely oblong, stipitate, measuring approximately 14 × 5 cm, with sutural margin winged on each side, smooth, flat, the valves marked with a weak longitudinal median ridge, rolled up after dehiscence.

## Type

de St-Aubin 1993 (= Bois C.T.F.T. 11646), Gabon (P)

## Discussion

Large tree with straight, tall, cylindrical trunk, slightly flared at the base. Bark smooth, dark grey in appearance, with fibrous section, yellowish orange becoming rapidly red, exuding at incisions an abundant opaque whitish gum. Wood ochre-pink, tender and light.

The leaves recall those of Monopetalanthus Durandii but, among other differences, the leaflets are clearly more elongately acuminate.

This species, of the type of the Gabonese Andoungs, appears rare, and until now may be considered endemic to the coastal sedimentary basin of Gabon, as far as the Kouilou to the South.

## Vernacular names

Beniamam, Andoung, Kakaka, Ngang (Fang); Okorwé (Nkomi); Dikuru (Bapunu)
