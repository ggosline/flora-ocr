# *Camoensia scandens* (Welw.) J.B.Gillett

**Genus**: [[Camoensia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull. 17 : 166 (1963)

## Description

C. maxima Welw. ex Benth., Trans. Linn. Soc. London 25(2) : 302 (1865). Scrambling shrub, reaching 3 m in height (in Gabon) or liane up to 30 m in length; climbing branches, densely yellow-tomentose when young, glabrescent. Spiny stipules, ± 5 mm in length, somewhat curved downwards. Leaf: petiole 6–16 cm, puberulent; stipels filiform, (0.3–)0.5–10 mm in length; leaflets with petiolule of 2–5 mm, limb obovate-lanceolate or oblong, the lateral ones slightly oblique, 4.5–24 × 2.5–9 cm, base cuneate, apex with acumen ± 13 mm in length, coriaceous, glabrous except for very short pubescence on the underside of the midvein. Inflorescence: raceme 5–18 cm in length; rachis tawny-tomentose; bracts caducous except at the base of the peduncle, triangular, ± 18 × 2–4 mm. Fragrant flower, with pedicel and calyx tawny-tomentose; pedicel 8–20 mm; bracteoles like the bracts, leaving a prominent scar; receptacle 4–11 cm in length; calyx with tube 2.5–5.5(–10) cm in length, lobes triangular, ± 20 × 8 mm; standard flabellate, 14–16 × 8–11 cm, white, fringed with yellow and/or with yellow patch in the middle of the apex (orange margin in bud), other petals elliptic, 10–12 × 1–2.5 cm, white; stamens white, filaments 8–12 cm, fused for 3–4 cm, anthers pale yellow; ovary ± 2 cm in length, long-stipitate, detaching at mid-height of the tube, tawny-tomentose; style 7–8 cm, pale green, brown-tomentose, stigma green. Pod elliptic, 18–22 × 2–4 cm, densely hairy, brown, with 1 to 5 seeds. Seed ovoid-flattened, 2.5 × 2 × 0.7 cm; hilum small, on the distal side.

## Distribution

Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola (Cabinda); in Gabon, known from Estuaire and Haut-Ogooué

## Ecology

Forest margins, savannas colonized by forest; in Gabon at 400–500 m elevation, elsewhere at 800 m. Use: Sometimes used as an ornamental, the flowers being enormous and very remarkable

## Source note

Toussaint (1953) mentions 11 stamens, we counted 10. Species with the largest flowers of the subfamily. b : Pellegrin (1949), Toussaint (1953). Bibliography
