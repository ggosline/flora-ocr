# *Ophioglossum rcticulatum* Linné

**Genus**: [[Ophioglossum]]
**Family**: [[Pteridophytes]]
**Protologue**: Type : Linné cite, dans Tractatus de filicibus americanus (1705), la planche 163 de Plumier qui se rapporte à une plante de St-Domingue

## Synonyms

- Ophioglossum vulgatum var. reticulatum (L.) Luerssen, Journ. Mus. Godefr., 8 : 18, t. 15, f. 115-118, t. 16, f. 128-130 (1875).

## Description

— L., Sp. 2 : 1063 (1753). — Tard., Mém. IFAN 28 : 22, pl. 1, f. 2 (1953).

Plant 5-30 cm tall, with an erect rhizome, short, bearing during one season one, rarely several, leaves. Common stem 3-16 cm long, blade 1.5 cm long by 1.2-5 cm wide, sessile or with a petiole 1 cm long, membranaceous, cordate, ovate or orbicular, often subreniform, with a cordate or truncate base, obtuse or rounded apex. Venation diffuse, forming areoles 0.5 to 1 cm long, containing contorted secondary venules. Fertile frond with a petiole 5-18 cm long; spike 1.5-5 cm long; 17-45 pairs of sporangia. Spores tetrahedral, more or less rounded, colourless, bearing reticulate thickenings.

## Type

Linné cite, dans Tractatus de filicibus americanus (1705), la planche 163 de Plumier qui se rapporte à une plante de St-Domingue

## Discussion

Pantropical. In Africa: from Sudan to Moyen Congo.

On moist and sandy soil, margins of lit paths: forms small pure patches.
