# *Aframomum sanguineum* (K. Schum.) K. Schum.
**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Aframomum]]
## Description
Herb to 5 m, blades c. 40 × 10 cm with pubescent midrib beneath. Several-flowered head or branched cluster on a 20–30 cm peduncle. Corolla and narrow labellum blood-red; fruit elongate, 8 cm. Reported common in Gabon.
## Source
Koechlin, J. (1964). Zingibéracées. *Flore du Gabon* 9: 56.
