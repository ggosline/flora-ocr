# *Salacia hispida* Blakelock

**Genus**: [[Salacia]]
**Family**: [[Celastraceae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull.: 243 (1956); HALLÉ, Monogr. Hippocr. Afr. occid., Thèse : 197 (1958); Mém. IFAN 64: 201 (1962); WILCZEK, FCB 9: 202 (1960)

## Description

Shrub up to 2 m then liana up to 12 m, and 8 cm in diameter. Bark including a thin orange layer. Branches green then grey, subcylindrical ± striated, scabrous and hispid; hairs violaceous, black when dry, up to 2 mm long. Leaves opposite to subopposite, ovate or elliptic, 4.7–14(–24.5) × 2.2–6.8(–10.5) cm. Petiole 5–10(–17) cm pubescent, canaliculate. Blade glabrous, at least above, and dark shining; paler below; margin regularly dentate; base subrounded to slightly cordate; acumen short or very short. Secondary veins 4–11 pairs ± hispid below, ± impressed above.

Inflorescences pauciflorous sessile, 1–6 flowers per axil. Pedicel glandular scabrous, pale brown, 4–7 mm; bracts minute. Flower bud ovate obtuse. Flower rotate 5–7 mm in diameter. Calyx quincuncial 3 mm in diameter with 5 subequal sepals, convex and scabrous externally, apex obtuse, approximately 1 mm. Petals 5 yellow or yellow-green ovate oblong, glabrous, with flabellate venation, 2–3.3 × 1.5–2.4 mm. Disk in a toroidal ring, neither flattened nor angular, glabrous, brown or greyish brown. Stamens 1.5 mm, orange, in chevron approximately 90°, lobes somewhat fused at the apex which is emarginate; anther 0.8 mm wide, filament yellow-green 1 mm. Style conical barely 1 mm; ovary weakly echinulate, at least from fruit set. Ovules 2–3 per locule.

Fruit globose 3.5 cm in diameter, bright red, adorned with a tight network of small ridges ± crisped; 3 seeds observed (Donis 2259, BR). – Pl. 38, fig. 1–9, p. 137.

## Type

Brenan 8916, Nigeria, Okomu, Benin (holo-, K !, iso-, BM !, P !)

## Discussion

Nigeria, Gabon, Zaire, to be sought in Cameroon. The juvenile sterile forms, subshrubby, are easily recognizable with their denticulateblade, narrowly cordate at the base, the petioles and internodes bristling with coarse black hairs tapered; when dry the median vein is prominent in a narrow ridge that is double toward the base for one or a few centimeters in length.
