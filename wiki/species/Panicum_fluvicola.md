# *Panicum fluvicola* Steud. (f. p. 67)

**Genus**: [[Panicum]]
**Family**: [[Gramineae]]
**Protologue**: Steud. Syn. Pl. Glum. I : 89 (1859)

## Description

Perennial herb, glabrous, with culms geniculate at the base, sometimes branched, capable of exceeding 50 or 60 cm in height. Leaves with striated sheaths, the upper ones shorter than the internodes, ligule ciliate, leaf blades linear, rolled, 2 mm wide (when flattened) and 10 to 20 cm long.

Panicle elongate, very much divided, reaching about twenty cm in length, branches slender, filiform. Spikelets pale green, tinged with red at their apex. Lower glume lanceolate, making 2/3 or 3/4 of the spikelet length, with 5 nerves, the lateral ones visible by transparency. Upper glume as long as the spikelet, acumen distinctly red curved backward. Lower flower male, lemma similar to the upper glume, paler, with 5 nerves. Palea acuminate, shorter. Upper flower about 2 mm long.

Species recorded from Nigeria, from Cameroon on the Logone, from Gabon and from the Lower Congo. Plant of little forage value, colonizing zones of white sand periodically flooded.
