# *Leptoderris tomentella* Harms

**Genus**: [[Leptoderris]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Repert. Spec. Nov. Regni Veg. 14 : 343 (1916)

## Description

Liane or lianescent shrub; branchlets tomentulose, the same indumentum present on the stipules, petiole, both surfaces of the leaflets, rachis, stipels, petiolules and inflorescences or sometimes with brown, stiff, appressed hairs on leaflets differing on the same branchlet. Stipules ± persistent, transversely elliptic to deltoid or triangular, 1–4 × 1.5–2 mm. Leaf: petiole subcylindric, (3–)5–11(–13) cm; rachis (1.5–)3–11(–13) cm; stipels ± triangular, 1–3 mm long; leaflets (3–)5, with petiolule 6–10 mm, blade obovate-elliptic, 5–15(–24) × 2–10(–14) cm, rounded to subcordate at base, rounded to obtuse at apex, sometimes slightly emarginate, sometimes with acumen 3–20 mm, glabrescenct above except the midrib; lateral nerves in 4 to 6 pairs. Inflorescence up to 40 cm long; bracts and bracteoles early caducous, ± circular, 1 mm diameter. Flower: pedicel 1–2 mm; calyx tubular-campanulate, 4–5 mm long, scarcely dentate, rather undulate, tomentulose externally, with lustrous appressed hairs internally; corolla 10–12(–13) mm long, whitish, blackish when dry, petals with claw 3 mm, wings shorter than keel; stamens as long as the keel, with anthers 0.8 mm long; pistil with appressed hairs. Pod elliptic-oblong, 8–10 × 2.5–3.5 cm, 1-seeded, with short, ± scattered, appressed hairs, wing 2–5 mm wide.

## Distribution

Southern Cameroon and Gabon; in Gabon, known from Estuaire and Nyanga

## Ecology

primary and secondarized forest; in Gabon up to 150 m altitude. b: Pellegrin (1949). Bibliography
