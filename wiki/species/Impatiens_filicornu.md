# *Impatiens filicornu* Hook. f.

**Family**: [[Balsaminaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Impatiens]]

## Description

Herb with alternate leaves, acute unappendaged leaf bases and pedunculate pseudumbels of rose to garnet-purple flowers. Lip shallow and cup-shaped, white; spur long, narrow, arched or sinuous; upper leaf surface glabrous; lateral petal posterior lobe obtuse and oblong.

## Distribution

West Africa from Togo through Lower Guinea to Gabon.
