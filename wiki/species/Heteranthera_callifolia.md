# *Heteranthera callifolia* Rchb. ex Kunth

**Genus**: [[Heteranthera]]
**Family**: [[Pontederiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Enum. pl. 4 : 121 (1843)

## Description

Herb reaching 50 cm, glabrous, stoloniferous; stem prostrate, rooting at the base. Stipules membranous, dark red purple. Leaf of same type; petiole up to 15(–20) cm long, channelled; blade floating or emergent, ovate to broadly ovate, 2.5–7.5 × 1.3–5 cm, shiny above, apex subobtuse to briefly acuminate, base cordate. Inflorescence 5–10 cm long, with 5–20 flowers; basal spathe enclosing a cleistogamous flower, spike sometimes reduced to this flower. Chasmogamous flower sessile; perianth white, blue or purplish, bearing glandular hairs on the outside, cylindric tube, 5–12 mm long, lobes 3.5–7 mm long; stamens: filament ± 2 mm long, anther 1.5–2 mm long, yellow; ovary ellipsoid to ovoid, 3–5 mm long; style 5–8 mm long, white. Fruit fusiform, trigonous, that from chasmogamous flowers 8–12 mm long, that from cleistogamous flowers 12–18 mm long. Seed ellipsoid to ovoid, 0.2–1 mm long.

## Distribution

Species with wide distribution throughout the entire zone of tropical Africa, southwards to the Transvaal and Namibia. In Gabon, known only from Minkébé National Park (Woleu-Ntem province).

## Ecology

Fresh or brackish waters, stagnant or slow-flowing, pools, ponds, rivers, marshes, etc.; in Gabon around 500 m altitude, elsewhere up to 2600 m. The flowers close at night. Uses: The species is sometimes cultivated as an ornamental in tropical aquariums. B: Burkill (1997), Catarino & Martins (1997), Lawalrée & Bruynseels (1981), Verdcourt (1968)
