# *Pancovia letestui* Pellegrin

**Genus**: [[Pancovia]]
**Family**: [[Sapindaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Bot. France 102 : 228 (1955)

## Description

— Chytranthus malendeensis PELLEGRIN, l.c. : 226, syn. nov.

Shrub or tree; leaves reaching 85 cm, of which 28 for the petiole with short hairs (0.1 mm), 38 for the rachis; basal leaflets 19–22 × 6.5 cm, the median ones 27–28 × 7–7.5 cm, the upper ones 25–20 × 7.5–6 cm; petiolules of approximately 1 cm brown, wrinkled, channelled or not; blade slightly discolorous, upper surface glabrous olive-green, lower surface finely glandular brownish-green; secondary veins 11–16 pairs, the midvein prominent below, longitudinally striated.

Inflorescences on old wood, rarely axillary, 15–20 cm, simple or with 1–5 branches, axes finely cannulate and rusty-pubescent, hairs 0.1 mm, bearing briefly stipitate cymules 0.5 mm, with 3–5 flowers; pedicel 2 mm, pubescent like the calyx on the outside, hairs dense 0.1 mm; sepals 4 mm, glabrous inside; 4 petals 4 mm of which 1 for the hairy claw; blade rhomboidal denticulate, glabrous; bifid scale arched, hairy below, with 2–4 teeth at the apex, fimbriate outgrowth, sometimes very reduced; 8 stamens with hairy filament. Fruit unknown. PL. 29, 6–8', p. 123.
