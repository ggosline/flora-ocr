# *Fagara welwitschii* Engl.

**Family**: [[Rutaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Fagara]]

## Description

Prickly woody plant with pinnate leaves and gland-dotted leaflets. Its small unisexual flowers are borne in branched panicles; male and female flowers are 4-merous. The follicular fruits are glandular and contain glossy dark seeds. The treatment compares its female inflorescences and flowers closely with those of *F. bouetensis* and *F. buesgenii*.

## Distribution

West-central Africa, including Gabon and Angola.
