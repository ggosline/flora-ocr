# *Trichilia zewaldae* J.J.de Wilde Planche 24

**Genus**: [[Trichilia]]
**Family**: [[Meliaceae]]
**Protologue**: Meded. Landbouwhogeschool Wageningen 68(2) : 190 (1968)

## Description

Meded. Landbouwhogeschool Wageningen 68(2) : 190 (1968). Small well-branched tree. Leaf: petiole 6–15 cm; rachis (2–)10–25(–30) cm; petiolules 4–12 mm, the terminal ones 10–20 mm; leaflets (3–)9–13, opposite to subopposite, narrowly obovate or obovate to ovate, 7–22 × 3–7 cm, cuneate or obtuse at the base, acuminate at the apex, glabrous to glabrescent on both surfaces. Inflorescence axillary to supra-axillary, 5–25 cm long, the branches up to 9 cm long; bracts rapidly caducous. Functionally male flower: pedicel up to 4 mm; bracteoles caducous; receptacle 0.6–2 mm long; calyx deeply 5-lobed, 2.5–3.5 × 4.5–6.5 mm, pubescent on the outside; petals narrowly rectangular, 8–11.5 × 2–3 mm, puberulent on the outside; staminodial crown 6.5–8.5 mm long, including the anthers, with the free part of the filaments densely villous on the inside and with distinct apical appendages, the rest glabrous on both surfaces, anthers narrowly rectangular, 1.5–2 × 0.5 mm; pistillode filiform, 5.5–7.5 mm long, pubescent, ovary underdeveloped, 3-locular. Functionally female flower unknown. Fruit obovoid-subglobose, 2–2.5 cm in diameter, 3-lobed, dehiscent by 3 valves, with a distinct rostrum 3–5 mm long, with a stipe of 2–5 mm, puberulent. Seed 19–22 × 11–16 mm; testa blackish shiny, covering the convex surface, the rest modified into fleshy red-orange sarcotesta.

## Distribution

Cameroon and Gabon; in Gabon, rare, known from a single collection in the Chaillu Massif

## Ecology

dense humid forest, primary and secondary; in Gabon, at 1020 m altitude, elsewhere at 200–600 m. Taxonomic note: In vegetative characters, this species resembles especially T. monadelpha. B: de Wilde (1968). Bibliography
