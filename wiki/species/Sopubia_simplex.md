# *Sopubia simplex* (Hochst.) Hochst. Planche 42

**Genus**: [[Sopubia]]
**Family**: [[Scrophulariaceae]]
**Protologue**: Flora 27 : 27 (1844)

## Description

Perennial herb, up to 45 cm tall, glabrous or finely pubescent; stem erect, cylindric with numerous ribs, with scattered leaves. Leaves alternate to whorled, appressed to the stem, subglabrous to scabrous; blade entire, linear, (5.5–)12–20 × 0.5–1 mm, with revolute margin; midvein prominent below. Inflorescence lax to somewhat compact. Flowers whorled: pedicel 6–13 mm; bracteoles 1.5–2 mm long; calyx 4–6 mm long, verrucose to hispid or finely pubescent, lobes triangular, 1–3 mm long, woolly at the margins and within; corolla pink to lilac or pale mauve, limb (10–)14–15 mm in diameter; stamens with anther 2.5–2.8 mm long. Fruit oblong, 4.5–5.5 × 2.5–3.5 mm.

## Distribution

Mali, Guinea-Conakry, Sierra Leone, Ghana, Togo, Nigeria, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Sudan, Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, Zambia, Zimbabwe, Mozambique and South Africa; in Gabon, observed in Ngounié and Nyanga

## Ecology

swampy savannas; in Gabon, up to 250 m altitude, in East Africa, up to 2300 m. B: Fischer (2006b), Ghazanfar et al. (2008), Mielcarek (1996), Philcox (1990)
