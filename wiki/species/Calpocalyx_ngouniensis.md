# *Calpocalyx ngouniensis* Pellegrin

**Genus**: [[Calpocalyx]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Bot. Fr. 87 : 261 (1940) ; Légum. Gabon : 25 (1948) ; VILLIERS, BMNHN 6 (3) : 308 (1984)

## Description

Tree reaching a diameter of 30 cm. Bark ± marbled gray brown. Bark section brown. Wood reddish brown. Crown dome-shaped, very dense. Branches dull brown. Branchlets pubescent with prominent lenticels. Young branchlets densely pubescent, velvety brown, ± furrowed. Stipules shortly triangular, tawny pubescent, caducous. Petiole pubescent ± hirsute; dull green above and dull brown below when fresh, ± tawny brown when dry; (1.6-2.1-6.5 cm long; cylindrical to ± weakly flattened above; apex enlarged forming a ridge below and bearing above a prominent or flat concave discoid gland; thick narrowly ovoid pubescent mucro, 2 mm long. Rachis densely tawny pubescent when dry, same color as petiole when fresh, 7.5-22.1 cm long; often asymmetrical in size on a single leaf, ± canaliculate at the base, gradually flattened transversely with ± prominent ridge above; ± prominent glands between 1-3 pairs of terminal leaflets. Leaflets 3-5 pairs. Petiolule tawny pubescent, 3-5 mm long, ± flat above. Blade blackish brown, reddish brown or gray above and reddish brown to brownish red below when dry; pubescent on both faces; broadly elliptical to narrowly elliptical (basal leaflet), lanceolate (apical leaflets), reaching 13.5 × 5 cm; base slightly asymmetrical: attenuate to attenuate-rounded, sometimes subrounded; apex distinctly shortly acuminate obtuse mucronate. Midvein concave with erect pubescence above and prominent pubescent below. Secondary veins 6-10 pairs, ascending arcuate, rather weakly prominent above, pubescent (at least at the base) on both faces, prominent below. Veinlets in moderately dense network weakly prominent above and prominent below.

Inflorescence in solitary or geminate axillary spikes, or in a terminal panicle of solitary or geminate spikes. Inflorescence axis densely brownish to tawny pubescent, ribbed. Basal bracts of spikes shortly triangular to ± elongate, densely pubescent, 3-5 mm long, the central one larger than the 2 lateral ones. Bracteoles at the base of the spike densely pubescent externally, broadly to narrowly triangular-linear, 1.75-3 mm long, apex acute. Interflorал bracteoles densely pubescent externally, narrowly triangular, curved, ± 1 mm long, apex acute to acuminate. Floral bud obovoid oblong. Flowers yellowish, yellow-green to white when fresh; sessile. Calyx cylindrical to weakly obconical truncate, pubescent externally, 2-3 mm long; lobes free triangular, 0.5 mm long, apex acute. Corolla 3.5-4.5 mm long; petals densely pubescent externally towards the apex, less densely towards the base, fused together, elliptical; free lobes ± 1.5 mm long, apex acute. Stamens 10, free; filament 5.5-7 mm long; anther broadly oblong, ± 0.5 mm long; connective prolonged by a long-pedicellate gland, flattened, bosselated or with a triangular expansion. Pistil substipitate, ovary often reduced by abortion, densely pubescent, broadly obovoid, 2 mm long containing 5 ovules; style glabrous 6 mm long, apex acute.

Fruit unknown. — Pl. 30, p. 111.

## Type

Le Testu 5513, Gabon (holo-, P)

## Discussion

The range of this species is Cameroonian-Gabonese. It was found in forest depressions or on clay-sandy soils in the Atlantic forest.

## Specimens examined

Le Testu 5513, Boudyanguila (fl., sept.) Moungazi 246, Belinga (fl.).
