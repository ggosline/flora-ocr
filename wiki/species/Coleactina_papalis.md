# *Coleactina papalis* N. Hallé

**Genus**: [[Coleactina]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]

## Description

Shrub. Young internodes pubescent, 5 mm wide. Stipules 6.5–8.5 × 2.5–3.5 cm, ovate-lanceolate, very acute, erect, outer surface moderately pilose, more distinctly pubescent at the base and along the prominent axial line; inner surface glabrous except at the base which bears dense axillary tomentum of orange hairs. Petiole 10–20 × 4 mm, broadly canaliculate, pubescent. Blade very elongately elliptic, reddish-brown and scarcely discolorous, glabrous on both surfaces, 30–50 × 10–14 cm; base acute; apex acuminate over 1–2 cm. Midvein broad, glabrous and with fine median groove on upper surface, pubescent beneath, with ochraceous appressed hairs. 16–23 pairs of secondary nerves, arcuate-ascending, pubescent beneath; fine reticulum of venules indistinct. No axillary domatia along the midvein.

Inflorescences axillary, uniflorous, opposite in a subterminal pair. Peduncle 5 mm, pubescent. Prefloral leaves 38–17 mm, forming together with their stipules of 40 × 20 mm an involucre masking the entire calyx. A second involucre protects the base of the calyx; it is a subcylindric cup, 9 mm tall, with a subtruncate margin from which emerge 7 (+3) very acute denticles, 0.5–1 mm long; this involucre is glabrous in its upper third, pubescent below, sparsely so on the exterior, densely so on the interior. Calyx densely clothed, at the level of the ovary, with ascending orange hairs 2–3 mm long, conversely glabrous and very elongately tubular for more than 2 cm in length with a diameter of 6 mm. This tube is surmounted by 7 erect, subulate lobes, moderately ciliate, 6 × 2 mm. Corolla white, clothed externally with ochraceous hairs when dry, with tube 8 × 0.5 cm; (6) 7 lobes approximately 30–40 × 9–12 mm, acutely attenuate, probably contorted in prefloration, glabrous on the upper surface. 7 sessile anthers, included at the level of the throat, very elongately sagittate, 20.5 × 1 mm, with deltoid connective projection 9 mm above the locules, sessile, attached in the upper third. Pollen 4(5)-colporate, weakly brevaxial, 24 × 28 μ. Disc a cushion 3 mm in diameter. Style filiform, pubescent only at the level of the anthers, with 2 lobes approximately 10 × 2 mm, glabrous at the apex and divergent, briefly exsert. Ovary turbinate, with two locules. Two oblong placentas covered with very numerous ovules ± arranged transversely, as in Leptactina; placental stalk thin, as tall as the placenta; mass of ovules on the placenta 3 × 1.5 mm. There appears to be an incipient supplementary septum at the base of the locule.

## Specimens examined

G. Le Testu 8356, Moughimba, SW de Koulamotou, approximativement à midistance de Mbigou (fleurs blanches, 17 septembre 1930); s. n., Poupa (fl. 7 octobre 1930) (P ex BM).
