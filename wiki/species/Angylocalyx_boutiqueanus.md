# *Angylocalyx boutiqueanus* L.Touss.

**Genus**: [[Angylocalyx]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Jard. Bot. État Bruxelles 23 : 380 (1953)

## Description

Shrub or small tree, 1–6 m tall; branchlets glabrous. Leaf: petiole 2–6 cm; rachis 2–10(–12) cm; leaflets 5 to 7, with blade ovate or rarely ovate-elliptic, 9–17 × (4–)5–9 cm, base rounded or triangular, apex ± acuminate; lateral veins in 3 to 5(–7) pairs, prominent or not. Inflorescence cauline, sometimes towards 3.5 m above ground, 1–4(–12) cm long, glabrous. Flower: receptacle ± conical or cylindric, 3–5 mm long; calyx cup-shaped, rather distinct from the receptacle but without sharp angle, 3–5 mm long, lobes rounded, approximately 0.5 × 1.5 mm; standard pink to pinkish, claw 5 mm, blade rounded or broadly ovate, 6–8 × 6 mm, other petals similar but narrower, apex slightly pointed; stamens 15 mm long; ovary lanceolate, 15 mm long, glabrous. Legume irregularly moniliform, sometimes few constrictions, reaching 18 × ± 1 cm, glabrous, pale green spotted with purple, bright orange at maturity; pericarp fleshy. Seed ellipsoid, 18–25 × 8–9 × 7–9 mm, brown; hilum ± central, 2 mm in diameter.

## Distribution

Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, known from Estuaire and Moyen-Ogooué.

## Ecology

Forest understory, secondary forest; in Gabon at 200–300 m altitude, elsewhere up to 500 m. Bibliography: Toussaint (1953), Povydysh et al. (2010), Yakovlev (1968).
