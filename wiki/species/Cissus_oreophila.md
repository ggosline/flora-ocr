# *Cissus oreophila* Gilg et Brandt

**Genus**: [[Cissus]]
**Family**: [[Vitaceae]]
**Protologue**: Gilg et Brandt, Engl. Bot. Jahrb. 46 : 471 (1912)

## Synonyms

- Gilg et Brandt, Engl. Bot. Jahrb. 46 : 471 (1912).
- Cissus clematitis A. Chevalier, Bot.: 142 (1920).
- Cissus glaucophylla Baker, F.T.A. 1 : 392 (1868), pro parte.

## Description

Herbaceous or subwoody plant. Stems cylindrical, 3–5 mm in diameter, sparsely covered with medifixed hairs denser at the nodes; internodes 4–5 cm long. Tendrils slender, bifurcate, sparsely pilose; bracteoles deltoid acute; approximately 1.5 mm long, thickened in the lower part, more or less pilose. Stipules ovate oblong, with base enlarged distinctly cordate, fairly thick with a more or less distinct protuberance in the lower part, 3–3.5 mm long and 2–2.5 mm wide, puberulent when young but becoming glabrous fairly quickly. Leaves simple entire. Petiole 4–8 cm, cylindrical, striate, sparsely pilose. Lamina ovate, more or less suborbicular, 5–9 cm long, 4.5–8 cm wide, with cordate base, with broadly cuneate and acuminate apex; margin presenting fairly numerous thick teeth; upper surface glabrescent or bearing medifixed hairs loosely arranged; lower surface presenting double piloseness: a whitish or somewhat reddish very short tomentum extremely dense covering the entire lamina and more loosely the veins, reddish medifixed hairs scattered over the lamina and veins above the indumentum; palmatipinnate venation, with 3 basal veins; texture thick.

Inflorescences 5–10 cm long, in compound cymes of dense multiflorous umbelliform cymules; axes covered with increasingly dense piloseness toward the apex; peduncle cylindrical, 2–5 cm long; bracteoles triangular, 1–1.5 mm long, presenting a more or less marked protuberance at the base, more or less densely pilose. Flowers approximately 2 mm long. Pedicel cylindrical, 2–2.5 mm long, very densely covered with medifixed hairs. Calyx cupuliform, approximately 0.5 mm in height, glabrescent or more or less entirely puberulent. Corolla obtuse to subacute at the apex, entirely

covered with very fine whitish puberulence often longer at the apex with sometimes a few reddish medifixed hairs; petals ovate deltoid, approximately 1.7–2 mm long. Stamens approximately 1.5 mm long; anthers with connective thickened into a wedge. Ovary densely pilose.

Berries ovoid or globulous, 5–6 mm long, 3.5–5 mm wide, glabrous. Seed more or less ovoid reniform, 4–5 mm long, 3–4 mm wide, approximately 3 mm thick; rostrum very short; median crest distinct very prominent; lateral lines emitting thick dorsal and ventral radial lines; testa with surface strongly and irregularly tuberculate.

Syntypes from Cameroon: Mann 1279, Deistel 175 and 390.

Large herbaceous vine with strong stems and yellowish-white flowers. In forest, forest galleries and secondary growth. Species widely distributed in West and equatorial Africa.
