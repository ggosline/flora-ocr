# *Cissus oreophila* Gilg et Brandt

**Genus**: [[Cissus]]
**Family**: [[Vitaceae]]
**Protologue**: Gilg et Brandt, Engl. Bot. Jahrb. 46 : 471 (1912)

## Synonyms

- Cissus clematitis A. Chevalier, Bot.: 142 (1920).
- Cissus glaucophylla Baker, F.T.A. 1 : 392 (1868), pro parte.

## Description

Herbaceous or subligneous plant. Stems cylindric, 3–5 mm in diameter, sparsely covered with medifixed hairs more dense at the nodes; internodes 4–5 cm long. Tendrils slender, bifurcate, sparsely pilose; bracteoles deltoid acute; approximately 1.5 mm long, thickened in the lower part, more or less pilose. Stipules oval-oblong, with base distinctly cordate and enlarged, rather thick with a more or less distinct protuberance in the lower part, 3–3.5 mm long and 2–2.5 mm wide, puberulent when young but becoming glabrous rapidly. Leaves simple entire. Petiole 4–8 cm, cylindric, striate, sparsely pilose. Blade oval, more or less suborbicular, 5–9 cm long, 4.5–8 cm wide, with cordate base, with broadly cuneate and acuminate apex; margin bearing rather numerous, thick teeth; upper surface glabrescent or bearing loosely arranged medifixed hairs; lower surface presenting a double pilositude: a whitish or somewhat reddish very short tomentum extremely dense covering the entire blade and more loosely the veins, reddish medifixed hairs scattered over the blade and veins above the indument; venation palmatipinnate, with 3 basal veins; texture thick.

Inflorescences 5–10 cm long, in compound cymes of dense multiflorous umbelliform cymules; axes covered with increasingly dense pilositude toward the apex; peduncle cylindric, 2–5 cm long; bracteoles triangular, 1–1.5 mm long, presenting a more or less marked protuberance at the base, more or less densely pilose. Flowers approximately 2 mm long. Pedicel cylindric, 2–2.5 mm long, very densely covered with medifixed hairs. Calyx cupuliform, approximately 0.5 mm in height, glabrescent or more or less entirely puberulent. Corolla obtuse to subacute at the apex, entirely covered with a very fine whitish puberulence often longer at the apex with sometimes a few reddish medifixed hairs; petals oval-deltoid, approximately 1.7–2 mm long. Stamens approximately 1.5 mm long; anthers with thickened cuneate connective. Ovary densely pilose.

Berries ovoid or globose, 5–6 mm long, 3.5–5 mm wide, glabrous. Seed more or less ovoid-reniform, 4–5 mm long, 3–4 mm wide, approximately 3 mm thick; rostrum very short; median crest sharp and very prominent; lateral lines emitting very thick dorsal and ventral radials; testa with surface strongly and irregularly tuberculate.

Syntypes from Cameroon: Mann 1279, Deistel 175 and 390.

Large herbaceous vine with robust stems and yellowish-white flowers. In forest, forest galleries and forest regrowth. Species widely distributed in West and equatorial Africa.
