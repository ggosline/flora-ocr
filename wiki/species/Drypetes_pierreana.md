# *Drypetes pierreana* Hutch., Fl. trop. Afr. 6(1): 686 (1912).

**Genus**: [[Drypetes]]
**Family**: [[Putranjivaceae]]

## Description

Tree reaching 10(–19) m in height; trunk reaching 10(–20) cm in diameter, slightly grooved, furnished with bosses bearing the inflorescences; bark whitish; branchlets thin, grooved and densely puberulent; buds densely puberulent. Stipules subpersistent, narrowly triangular to linear, 3–4 × 0.6–0.8 mm, hairy, becoming nearly glabrous. Leaf: petiole 3–4 mm, densely hairy; blade elliptic to narrowly elliptic and sometimes slightly obovate-elliptic, 9–15(–17) × 3.5–6.5 cm, papyraceous to coriaceous, cuneate at base on one side, rounded on the other, apex 0.5–2 cm long, margin furnished with teeth spaced 5–8 mm apart, 0.3–0.8 mm long and directed forward; midvein hairy beneath, particularly at the base, secondary veins 5 to 8, looping distinctly 6–9 mm from the margin. Male inflorescence cauliflorous, with 1 to 4(to 15) flowers. Male flower: pedicel slender, 5.4–11 mm; sepals 4 or 5, imbricate, ovate to orbicular 3.1–4.1 × 3–3.4 mm, glabrous except for occasional marginal cilia reaching 0.1 mm; stamens 12 to 16, at the periphery of the disk, slightly surrounded by its marginal lobes, filament 2.2–4.1 mm, white, anther 1.2 mm long, yellow then brown; disk 2.7–3.2 mm in diameter, 0.3–0.4 mm in height, glabrous. Female inflorescence cauliflorous, with 3 to 5(to 10) flowers. Female flower: pedicel 6–10 mm, glabrous; sepals 4, ovate to oblong, 3.3–3.7 × 1.9–3.6 mm, obtuse at apex, very pale yellowish-green, glabrous within and without, with short marginal cilia reaching 0.1 mm; disk cupuliform, 3.1–3.5 mm in diameter, 0.3–0.4 mm in height, encircling the base of the ovary, glabrous; ovary globose, somewhat trilobed, 0.9–1.9 × 2.4–3.2 mm, 3-locular, glabrous or hairy; style 0.5 mm, with 2 or 3 flabellate and lobed stigmas, 0.9 × 2.3 mm. Fruit ellipsoid, slightly but distinctly 3-lobed, 20–32 × 14–20 mm, generally glabrous, but sometimes with scattered hairs lost at maturity, brownish-green to yellow at maturity, with semi-persistent sepals, with (1 to)3 seeds; pedicel erect, 7.5–13.3 mm, glabrous. Seed 11.5 × 5.5 mm.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon and Gabon; in Gabon: Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué, Ngounié, Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem

Habitat: primary and secondary evergreen forests, riparian forests; in Gabon and elsewhere at 30–600 m altitude.

## Source note

The names Cyclostemon klaineanus Pierre and Drypetes klaineana (Pierre) Breteler, synonyms of D. preussii, are based on printed illustrations with analyses and drawn by Delpy. Furthermore, Breteler (2006) argued that distributed to the major herbaria, these illustrations with analyses constituted a valid publication, but van Rijckevorsel (2011) proposed adding these drawings to the list of suppressed works. This proposal was accepted and consequently, the two names are invalid.
