# *Camoensia brevicalyx* Benth. Planche 22

**Genus**: [[Camoensia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Trans. Linn. Soc. London 25(2) : 302 (1865)

## Description

Trans. Linn. Soc. London 25(2) : 302 (1865). C. laurentii De Wild., Mission Em. Laurent : 103 (1905). C. klaineana Pellegr., Lég. Gabon : 260 (1949). Liane of 30–35 m in length or shrub of 1–7 m in height; stem reaching 3 cm in diameter; branchlets pubescent-rust to glabrescent. Stipules 5 mm in length, with rounded apex. Leaf: petiole 3.5–18 cm, pubescent-rust to glabrescent; stipels absent or vestigial in small tufts of hairs; leaflets with petiolule of 5–10 mm, with blade oblanceolate to obovate, 8.5–24 × 3–9 cm, base obtuse, apex acuminate. Inflorescence: raceme 6–23 cm in length; rachis puberulent-rust; bracts caducous, triangular, ± 2 × 2 mm. Flower with pedicel and calyx puberulent-rust: pedicel 1–3 cm; bracteoles resembling the bracts; receptacle 8–13 mm in length; calyx 2.2–3 × 3.5–4 cm, with dense pubescence, with subequal teeth of 5–6 × 7–8 mm; standard flabellate, apex emarginate, blade 4.5–5 × 3.5–4 cm, white to pink or mauve spotted with yellow at the centre, other petals elliptic, 4.5–5.5 × 1.5–2 cm, mauve; stamens white but brown at the base, filaments ± 5 cm, fused at the base for 5–7 mm, anthers 5 mm in length, brown; ovary 12 mm in length, densely pubescent-tawny, dark brown, on a stipe of 18–22 mm. Pod elliptic to spatulate, reaching 15 × 5 cm, pale green with brown hairs, then almost glabrous at maturity. Seed flattened-ellipsoid to ovoid, reaching 3.5 × 1.9 × 0.7 cm, brown-chestnut, glossy; hilum oblique.

## Distribution

South-eastern Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola (Cabinda); in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué, Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo and Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology

swamp forest, stream banks, lakes and lagoons; in Gabon at 2–500 m in altitude. Bibliography: Lewis et al. (2005), Pellegrin (1949), Toussaint (1953).
