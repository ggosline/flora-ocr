# *Dalbergia setifera* Hutch. & Dalziel

**Genus**: [[Dalbergia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. W. trop. Afr., éd. 1, 1 : 374 (1928)

## Description

Odorless liane, unarmed, but sometimes bearing hooked prehensile branchlets; bark dark brownish; young branchlets hairy with long spreading hairs, 0.5–1 mm, reddish-brown when dry. Stipules very caducous, lanceolate. Leaf imparipinnate; petiole 0.5–1 cm; rachis 1–7.3 cm, densely hairy like the branchlets; leaflets 4 to 7 (or sometimes up to 13 in young plants), alternate and very unequal, petiolule 1–3 mm, limb elliptic to slightly obovate, symmetric or nearly so, 5–10 × 2.3–6 cm in the upper leaflets and 1.2–2.4 × 0.9–1.7 cm in the lower ones, rounded or obtuse at the base, acuminate and mucronulate at the apex, papyraceous, olive-green to blackish and almost concolorous when dry, upper surface glabrous except for the hairy midvein, lower surface entirely hairy, with long erect hairs similar to those of the branchlets; lateral nerves 7 to 12, rather fine, but clearly more prominent than the nervules on the lower surface, ascending and reaching almost to the limb margin, nervules in a fairly dense and prominent concolorous reticulum. Inflorescence in a raceme or in a very condensed panicle, axillary or on old branchlets (sometimes on prehensile branchlets), 0.7–2 cm in length, with dense hairs, sometimes short and appressed, sometimes longer and spreading; bracts persistent at anthesis, elliptic, 0.4–1 × 0.3–0.5 mm, pubescent. Flower: pedicel 1.5–4.5 mm; bracteoles persistent at anthesis, elliptic or narrowly ovate, 0.7–1 × 0.3–0.5 mm, pubescent; calyx pale green, with fairly dense appressed hairs, tube 2–2.5 × 1.8–2.3 mm, teeth valvate ± equal and triangular, 0.5–1 mm in length, acute or rarely rounded; corolla white, petals with claw exceeding the calyx; standard bent obliquely, with claw 2 mm and suborbicular limb 4.5 × 4.5 mm, with rounded base and bilobed apex, wings equaling or slightly exceeding the keel, with claw 2.5 mm and ± elliptic limb 4.5 × 2–2.5 mm, smooth externally, with truncate base or barely auriculate and ± thickened at the upper margin, keel strongly curved, with claw 2–2.5 mm and limb 3.7 × 2 mm, with auriculate base; stamens in 2 phalanges of 5, filaments 6.5 mm, united for 4/5 of their length, 5 straight at the base and curved in their free part; ovary 2 × 0.5 mm, longly pubescent at the margins and very shortly on the sides, with 2 ovules and straight stipe 2.5 mm; style linear, 1.5 mm, with punctiform stigma. Legume unknown.

## Distribution

Ghana, Gabon and Republic of the Congo; rare in Gabon, known from the vicinity of Makokou (Ogooué-Ivindo) and Lastoursville (Ogooué-Lolo)

## Ecology

Dense forest, sometimes along river margins; in Gabon around 470 m altitude. Flowering in September and October in Gabon

## Source note

This species is very close to D. adamii and D. stenopetala, but differs from them by the much longer pubescence of the leaves and branchlets; it also differs from D. adamii by the almost concolorous leaflets, and from D. stenopetala by the more rounded form of the standard. It also resembles the pubescent forms of D. semiapplanata, but the latter has thicker leaflets and ± glaucescent below, with less prominent nervules, and short and broad bracteoles. D. setifera has often been confused with D. dalzielii Baker f. ex Hutch. & Dalziel, notably by Pellegrin (1949) and Hawthorne & Jongkind (2006). In reality, D. dalzielii is limited to West Africa, from Ghana to Nigeria; it is very different from D. setifera (and much closer to D. rufa) by its larger flowers with narrowly elliptic standard, its stamens with filaments curved in an S shape from the base, and its leaflets obtuse or barely acute at the apex. b: Hawthorne & Jongkind (2006), Hepper (1958), Pellegrin (1949).
