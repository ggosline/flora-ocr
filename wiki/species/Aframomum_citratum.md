# *Aframomum citratum* (Pereira ex Oliv. et Hanb.) K. Schum.
**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Aframomum]]
## Description
Forest herb to 5 m, young stems red. Leaves with 2 cm petiole and bifid ligule to 25 mm. Basal many-flowered globose spike c. 7 cm across, with reddish crisped bracts. Mauve flowers large; calyx 7.5 cm, labellum 7 × 5 cm. Fruit c. 3 cm diameter.
