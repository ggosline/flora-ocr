## Distribution

of Asian origin, but brought by man to all tropics where it is often naturalized; in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Ngounié, Nyanga, Ogooué-Lolo and Woleu-Ntem, but probably present in all provinces

## Ecology

roadside and railway verges, plantations, secondary forest in transition to savanna, in scrubland, should also be found in oil palm plantations, etc.; in Gabon at 0–250 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1000 m. Flowering in June and August; fruits in October and November. Uses: Cover plant and fodder par excellence. The tubers are edible, but rarely reported for this use

## Vernacular names

kudzu tropical, puero (fr.) ; tropical kudzu (angl.) ; puero (angl. d’Australie)

## Source note

The varieties phaseoloides and subspicata (Benth.) A.N.Egan & B.Pan, with acute lateral calyx lobes, are of rather Asian distribution, but sometimes introduced elsewhere.
