# *Santiria trimera* (Oliv.) Aubréville

**Family**: [[Burseraceae]] · **Genus**: [[Santiria]]

## Description

Shrub or small to medium tree on a bundle of stilt roots, exceptionally to 20 m and 50 cm diameter. Leaves completely glabrous, with 2–3(–4) pairs of leaflets; leaflets 9–23 × 3–9 cm, variable, acuminate, base obtuse or cuneate, with 7–12 pairs of veins and wart-like points beneath. Terminal panicles very lax, to 40 cm, glabrous. Flowers yellow, 3-merous; pedicels usually 3–4 mm; petals 2.5–3.5 mm; stamens 6; disc and ovary glabrous. Fruit eccentric, flattened-ellipsoid, 2–2.5 × 1.5 cm, style scar lateral; one flattened stone with a red, laciniate-cotyledon seed.

## Distribution and ecology

Sierra Leone to Congo; common in Gabonese primary-forest understorey. Also on São Tomé, where it yields São Tomé balsam. Bark and branchlets strongly aromatic. Flowers August–October, fruits January.
