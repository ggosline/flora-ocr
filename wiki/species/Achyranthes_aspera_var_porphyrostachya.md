# *Achyranthes aspera* var. *porphyrostachya* (Wall. ex Moq.) J. D. Hooker

**Family**: [[Amaranthaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Achyranthes]]

## Description

Subshrub or climber about 1.5 m, probably introduced from India. Differs from typical *A. aspera* in its broadly obovate-elliptic leaves, widest above the middle and to 8 cm wide, cuneate and petiole-decurrent below; inflorescences are less robust, usually flexuous or pendent, with more open spikes. Leaves blacken on drying.

## Distribution

Tropical Asia and Africa. Gabon represented by Duparquet s.n.
