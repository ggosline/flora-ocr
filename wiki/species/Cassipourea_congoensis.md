# *Cassipourea congoensis* DC. Planche 29.2

**Genus**: [[Cassipourea]]
**Family**: [[Rhizophoraceae]]
**Protologue**: Prodr. 3 : 34 (1828)

## Description

Shrub (or liane ?) to small tree 8–13 m tall; trunk up to 20 cm in diameter; bark brown, with elongated lenticels, cream-coloured on cross-section, flaking, with a dark line externally, with horseradish odour; branchlets thickened at the nodes and pubescent, becoming glabrous rapidly. Stipules caducous, lanceolate to narrowly ovate, 6–8 mm long, pubescent on the outer face. Leaf: petiole 0.5–1.5(–2) mm; lamina elliptic to broadly elliptic-obovate, 6–16 × (2–)3.5–9 cm, 2.3–2.7 times longer than wide, obtuse to cuneate at the base, acuminate at the apex with acumen 1–1.5 cm, coriaceous, margin entire or shortly serrate, widely glabrous, rather shiny, lower face distinctly paler; lateral veins 7–10 pairs. Inflorescence axillary, fasciculate, dense, multiflorous, with (4–)8–10 flowers in different stages of development, sometimes 2–3 at full maturity; bracts 1–2 per flower, concave, semi-circular and pubescent, surrounding the base of the pedicel. Flower (4–)5(–6)-merous; pedicel 1.5–3 mm and stipe up to 1 mm, with appressed hairs; calyx campanulate, 4.5 mm long, greyish, lobes 4–5(–6), spreading, sublanceolate, 3 mm long, densely hairy externally, glabrescent, brownish and striate internally; petals spatulate, 6–7 mm long, smooth, white (orange when dry), fringed-laciniate in the upper 2.5 mm in ± 7 filiform lobes; stamens ± 16–20, free, glabrous, filaments 2–3 mm, anthers oblong, 0.8 mm, glabrous; disk glabrous; ovary subglobose, 1 mm in diameter, 3–4-locular, glabrous; style (1.5–)4 mm, glabrous, persistent in fruit, stigma flattened at the apex in mushroom shape. Fruit globose to obovoid, 5 mm in diameter, 3–4-locular, pale green then brown, glabrous. Seeds 1–2(–4), ellipsoid, 2.5 × 1.5 mm, dark red, topped with a pale aril.

## Distribution

from Senegal to the Central African Republic, Cameroon, Gabon and the Democratic Republic of the Congo; species fairly common in Gabon, known from Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo, Haut-Ogooué and Woleu-Ntem.

## Ecology

mixed primary forest with savannas; in Gabon, at 200–600 m altitude, elsewhere at 35–2400 m. Flowering February–April and in August, fruits in February. Taxonomic note: Species fairly variable. The species C. acuminata Liben and C. louisii Liben are mentioned in the Check-list of Vascular Plants of Gabon (Sosef et al. 2006), but considered as conspecific with C. congoensis DC. (Breteler, 2008). Due to this new taxonomic situation, the IUCN evaluation of C. acuminata as being 'endangered' is no longer valid. b: Breteler (2006, 2008), Keay (1954), Lewis (1956), Liben (1987).
