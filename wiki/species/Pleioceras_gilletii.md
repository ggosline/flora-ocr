# *Pleioceras gilletii* Stapf Planche 94 ; Figure 9(H)

**Genus**: [[Pleioceras]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. trop. Afr. 4(1) : 167 (1902)

## Description

Fl. trop. Afr. 4(1): 167 (1902). Shrub or small liane, 1–5 m tall; branchlets pubescent to glabrous. Leaf: petiole 0.5–1.5 cm, pubescent; lamina obovate or narrowly obovate, 5–15 × 2–7.5 cm, glabrous or nearly so beneath; secondary veins 6 to 9 pairs, tertiary venation scarcely visible. Inflorescence 0.5–1 × 0.5–1 cm with numerous flowers; peduncle 3–4 cm, pubescent; bracts 0.5–1 mm long. Flower: pedicel 2–5 mm, pubescent; calyx with lobes 1.5–2.5 × 0.9–2.2 mm, pubescent to sparsely pubescent externally, glabrous internally and without or with colleter; stamens to total per flower; corolla with tube long 0.5–1 × 0.5–1 mm, internally pilose towards the throat; lobes 0.5 times as long as the tube, 4.5–7 × 2.2–3 mm, yellow and puberulent to papillose internally, dark red to violet and puberulent externally; each set of appendages comprising filiform ones resembling brushes, 3.5–6.5 mm long, branched 2.5–4.5 mm from the base and glabrous, the median one oblong, 1–3.2 × 0.5–1.3 mm, adnate over half to three-quarters of its length to the corolla lobe, rounded and pubescent internally towards the apex and at the base, glabrous or partially hairy in the middle; stamens inserted 0.2–0.5 mm below the throat; filament 0.5–1 mm; anther long 0.5–1 mm; pistil long 0.5–1 mm; ovary glabrous or with a few papillae especially towards the apex. Fruit green; follicles 16.5–40 × 1–1.7 cm. Seed 12–25 × 2 × 1 mm, with hairs 20–60 mm. Distribution: Nigeria, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, and Angola; in Gabon uniquely in the Ogooué, Ivindo.

## Ecology

forest, especially along rivers; in Gabon at 480 m altitude, elsewhere at 0–500 m. B: Barink (1984), Beentje (2021). BIBLIOGRAPHY
