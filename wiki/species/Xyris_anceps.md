# *Xyris anceps* Lam. subsp. anceps

**Genus**: [[Xyris]]
**Family**: [[Xyridaceae]]
**Protologue**: Tabl. encycl. 1 : 132 (1791)

## Description

Perennial herb or sometimes annual, forming large tufts. Leaf: blade linear, 20–40 cm × 4–5 mm, attenuate to an asymmetric apex, glabrous, brownish or reddish when dry. Inflorescence: peduncle up to 1 m long, 1.5–3 mm in diameter, glabrous, furrowed when dry, flattened and bearing two longitudinal wings, especially towards the apex; spike broadly ovoid, then ovoid in fruit, reaching 15 × 11 mm; sterile bracts 4, suborbicular, 4–5 mm, bifid at the apex, pale yellowish-brown; fertile bracts 30–40, ovate or elliptic, approximately 6 × 4 mm, slightly bifid towards the apex and with a small rhombic greyish or greenish spot. Flower: lateral sepals 5 mm long, curved, with entire keel, often marked by a reddish submarginous line towards the apex; corolla yellow, tube approximately 3 mm long, the lobes oblong, approximately 4 × 2 mm; stamens 2 mm long, anthers approximately 1 mm long; staminodes three-branched, each branch fimbriate at the apex; ovary obovoid, approximately 2 × 1 mm; style with branches 1.5 mm, stigma densely branched at the apex. Fruit ellipsoid, 4 × 2 mm. Seeds numerous, broadly ellipsoid, 0.5 × 0.4 mm, bearing 12–13 longitudinal furrows.

## Distribution

Eastern coasts of Africa, from Kenya to South Africa and Madagascar, rarer along the coasts of West Africa, from Nigeria to Angola; in Gabon mainly coastal

## Ecology

herbaceous marshes near the sea; at 0–50 m altitude. B: Lewis (1981), Lock (1999, 2010). Bibliography
