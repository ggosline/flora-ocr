Tree or shrub polygamous or dioecious, up to 25(–30) m tall; bark reddish-brown, with horizontal lenticels; latex yellow; branchlets wrinkled to slightly winged longitudinally, glabrous. Inflorescences in axillary and cauline fascicles, of 5–50(–100) flowers, inserted on cushions situated at the nodes of old wood. ## Distribution

from Guinea to Cameroon, Central African Republic, Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Angola, Tanzania, Malawi and Zambia; common in Gabon and widely distributed

## Ecology

primary or secondarized forest, forest galleries, riparian forest, on sandy to clayey soil; in Gabon, up to 750 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1850 m

## Vernacular names

ipina-vina (ngowé), mpina-vina (nkomi), gbodani, ouropoupati. Note taxonomique : voir aussi sous G. ovalifolia . Cette espèce est également proche de G. staudtii Engl., du Nigéria et du Cameroun, mais qui se distingue facilement de G. smeathmannii par ses feuilles sessiles et cordées à la base .
