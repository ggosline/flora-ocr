# *Musa balbisiana* Colla

**Family**: [[Musaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Musa]]

## Description

Vigorous giant perennial herb with a pseudostem of overlapping leaf sheaths, large alternate leaves and a pendent terminal inflorescence. Wild plants are diploid (2n = 22) and bear viable seeds. In Gabon the species is not reported wild but contributes one parental genome to sterile cultivated bananas.

## Cultivated hybrids in Gabon

Triploid *M. acuminata* × *M. balbisiana* clones include plantains, whose long curved angular fruits have thick skin and starchy, little-sweet pulp and are eaten cooked. Clones with stronger *balbisiana* genomic influence include Cacambou, with thick angular cooking fruits.

## Distribution

Native to India, Myanmar and the Philippines; present in Gabon through cultivated hybrid clones.
