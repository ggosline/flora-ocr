# *Lindernia parviflora* (Roxb.) Haines Planche 37

**Genus**: [[Lindernia]]
**Family**: [[Scrophulariaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Bihar & Orissa 4 : 635 (1922)

## Description

Annual herb, reaching 10(–20) cm in height; stem erect or sometimes prostrate to ascending, simple or sparsely branched, glabrous. Leaf: lamina narrowly lanceolate to oblanceolate or ovate, extremely variable in shape and width, 2–30 × 0.5–10 mm. Inflorescence lax, each floriferous node bearing a flower opposite to a scaly bract with a rudimentary bud. Flower: pedicel reaching 11 mm; calyx 2.5–3.5 mm in length, with lobes 2–3 mm in length, glabrous; corolla white with purplish spots, tube 1.8–7 mm in length, upper lip bipartite and 1.2–4 mm in length, lower lip tripartite and 1.5–4(–5) mm in length; anterior (abaxial) stamens in the form of claviform staminodes 2 mm in length, covered with glandular hairs and papillae, filaments of posterior (adaxial) stamens 1.5 mm; ovary ovoid, 1 mm in length; style 4 mm. Fruit ovoid 2–3 × 2–3 mm. Seeds with non-alveolate albumen.

## Distribution

Senegal, Mali, Ghana, Nigeria, Gabon, Chad, Sudan, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Rwanda, Burundi, Ethiopia, Somalia, Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, Zambia, Malawi, Zimbabwe, Mozambique, Botswana, Namibia, South Africa, Madagascar, Mauritius, also in India, Sri Lanka and Vietnam; in Gabon, observed in Ogooué-Ivindo

## Ecology

lake and river margins and swamps, laterite slabs; in Gabon, at low altitude, in Rwanda up to 2000 m. B: Fischer (1992, 1995, 1999, 2006a), Fischer et al. (2013), Ghazanfar et al. ibliography (2008), Philcox (1990)
