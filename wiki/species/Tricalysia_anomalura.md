# *Tricalysia anomalura* N. Hallé, sp. nov.

**Genus**: [[Tricalysia]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]

## Description

Shrubby, much-branched, 2–5 m tall with glabrous branch tips. Stipules glabrous, expanded to 2 mm, with a subulate rostrum 1–5 mm long. Petiole 9–20 mm, with a sulcate channel. Lamina olivaceous, entirely glabrous, elliptic, 10–24 × 4.2–9.5 cm, with an acute base and an acumen 7–18 mm long. Midrib sulcate above. 7–9 pairs of secondary nerves; intersecondary nerves moderately subtransverse and weakly prominent on both faces. Nerves and nerve axils entirely glabrous.

Inflorescences axillary, 1–3-flowered, in pedunculate cymes on 1–2 mm peduncles. Axes puberulent to glabrous. Bracts deltoid, connate, naviculate. Flowers white, subsessile above the upper bracteoles. Calyx green, puberulent at the level of the ovary, nearly glabrous or glabrous on the cupule 2.5–4 mm tall, 4–5 dentate, often torn on one side. Corolla with a tube 10–15 mm, glabrous externally, villous internally; 8 lobes 12–15 × 2.5–4 mm, attenuate, very acute, glabrous with glabrous margins, overlapping to the left; throat with white hairs. 8 stamens glabrous, with filaments 4 mm long, with anthers submedifixed, multilocellate, 10 × 0.8 mm, attenuate, terminated by a small acute acumen; pollen white. Style glabrous, 20–25 mm long, with 2 stigmatic lobes attenuate, acute, 4–6 mm long. Small green disk. Placenta circular, 0.7 mm; peltate at centre, with 4 ovules: two lateral and two inferior.

Young fruit 10 mm in diameter with wall already thick, more than 2 mm, with peduncle slightly accrescent.
