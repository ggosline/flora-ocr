Liane. Branchlet glabrous or nearly so, dark brown slightly reddish when young on the dry state, paler thereafter; lenticels few on young growth, in longitudinal lighter bands on older wood; recurved prickles present. Often remains of bracts surrounding the bud around the base of young ramifications. Stipules glabrous to subglabrous, linearly triangular, 5 mm long, apex acute, early caducous. Petiole glabrous or nearly so, 2–5.2 cm long; canaliculate above after a prominent conical or disciform gland; prickles present or absent below. Rachis 4.5–13.5 cm long, canaliculate above at the base becoming keeled and sulcate, with a few hairs in the sulcus, a few glands at the level of insertion of the terminal pinnae; prickle towards the base on the lower face. Pinnae 4–15 pairs. Pinnae axis 2–7 cm long, canaliculate at the base then a fine raised line with a few hairs; lateral margins ± winged with a few hairs; keel ± present very sparsely pubescent to glabrous on the lower face. Leaflets 23–60 pairs, subsessile; 1–2 lower pairs modified and often reduced. Lamina glabrous on both faces; brown to brownish-green above and light brown to pale green below on the dry state; narrowly oblong, reaching 7 × 2 mm, base very asymmetric; proximal margin rounded-truncate and distal margin linear; margin glabrous to very sparsely winged; apex obtuse. Primary vein ± diagonal, close to the upper margin, scarcely visible above and raised below. Remainder of venation not visible.

Inflorescences in terminal panicles of capitate spikes. Inflorescence axis subglabrous, with numerous prickles. Spike-bearing peduncle ± pubescent. Bracteole pubescent externally, obovate to spatulate, 1 mm long, apex obtuse. Floral bud obovoid. Flowers ♂, sessile, white in the living state. Calyx obconic, pubescent externally, 2–2.5 mm long; lobes free broadly triangular-lanceolate, 0.5–1 mm long, margin ciliolate, apex acute. Corolla obconic, narrowed at the base, glabrous, 3–3.5 mm long; petals connate into a tube 2–2.5 mm long; free lobes ± triangular-lanceolate, margin ciliolulate, apex acute. Stamens 4.5–7.5 mm long; filaments connate at the base for 0.75 mm and to the disk; anther very broadly oblong, 0.30 mm long; connective prolonged by a small globulose pedunculate gland. Disk fleshy connate to the staminal tube and forming a fleshy ridge on its inner face. Pistil with stipe 1–1.5 mm long; ovary glabrous or with a few hairs at the apex, elliptic-oblong curved, 1 mm long, with 15 ovules; style slender 3.5–4.5 mm long.

Pod indehiscent, glabrous, blackish-brown on the dry state, 7.5–16 × 1.8–3.5 cm; sutural margins thickened; base attenuate-rounded, not stipitate; apex attenuate distinctly apiculate; transverse venation scarcely visible. Seeds about 12, blackish shining on the dry state; ellipsoid-oblong, 10–13 × 4–8 mm; longitudinal areole as long and as wide as the seed. - Pl. 33, Fig. 12–19, p. 125.

## Type

Thonning s.n., Ghana, ladofa (holo-, C ; photo-, K)

## Discussion

This species is widely distributed in regions of dense humid forest from Guinea and Sierra Leone to Sudan towards the east and Mozambique and Angola towards the south. It is most often present in forest gaps, more or less secondarized formations, or even swamp forests, and does not appear to exceed an altitude of 1450 m.
