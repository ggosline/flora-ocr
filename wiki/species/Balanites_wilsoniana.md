# *Balanites wilsoniana* Dawe et Sprague

**Family**: [[Balanitaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Balanites]]

## Description

Distorted tree to 50 m, base to 2 m wide; trunk 30–40 m, twisted and deeply fluted, crown flattened. Bark yellowish to grey-green, slash orange-yellow outside and white within, aromatic. Trunk and branches bear strong simple or forked supra-axillary spines to 10 cm. Leaves alternate with one pair of leaflets; petiole 1–2.5 cm; leaflets unequal-sided, ovate to elliptic, 3–12 × 2–8 cm, abruptly acuminate, with 5–8 weak lateral-vein pairs.

Flowers yellow-green, bisexual and 5-merous, in axillary fascicles or short umbels; pedicels 8–16 mm; sepals 4–5 mm; petals 8 × 2 mm, densely hairy inside; stamens 10; ovary 5-locular, one pendent ovule per locule. Drupe yellow, ovoid to ellipsoid, 5–12 × 4–7 cm; mesocarp fibrous, resinous and strongly scented; stone hard, fusiform and pentagonal, containing one oily seed.

## Distribution

Côte d’Ivoire to Kenya, south to Cabinda and Congo. Rare in Gabon; the sole cited Gabon specimen is Fleury-Chevalier 33672, Libreville.

## Vernacular names

*Abône* (Fang).
