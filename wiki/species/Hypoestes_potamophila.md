# *Hypoestes potamophila* Heine, sp. nov.

**Genus**: [[Hypoestes]]
**Family**: [[Acanthaceae]]

## Description

H. triflorae (Forsk.) Roem. et Schult. most closely related, differing in leaves glabrous, narrowly lanceolate, with entire margins, in looser and smaller inflorescences, long-pedunculate, with narrower, acuminate bracts and bracteoles, not rounded.

Herbaceous plant, slender, to 35 cm tall, glabrescens; stem at first more or less prostrate, creeping, rooting at the nodes, eventually ascending, in dry state subquadrangular, fuscescent; internodes 5.5-2 cm distant, slightly thickened; leaves subsessile or shortly petiolate; petioles 2-7 mm long; leaf blade glabrous, narrowly elliptic or lanceolate, acuminate, 5-7 × 0.5-1.4 cm; acumen obtuse or rounded; base much narrowed and decurrent into the petiole; above dark green in dry state or dark brown, below paler; lateral nerves on both sides approximately 6, ascending and anastomosing 0.5-1 mm near the margin, cystoliths well visible on both surfaces. Inflorescences axillary and terminal; peduncles 2-4 cm long, much elongated in the fruiting state; bracts lanceolate, acuminate, 6-8 (14) × 1-1.5 mm, accrescent in the fruiting state, pale green, glabrous but with very slightly ciliate margins and one or another longer hair, bracteoles very similar but smaller, hyaline; calyx 3.5 mm long, with segments narrowly lanceolate, 0.5-1 mm wide, hyaline; corolla white or pale violet, 12 mm long, tube cylindric, 5 mm long, throat 2.5 mm in diameter, lips very divergent, upper lip slightly trilobate (on account of resupination of the flower, the upper lip; this lip originally anterior: this resupination is one of the characters of the genus Hypoestes), lobes rounded, 0.5 mm in diameter slightly ciliate; median nerve with 2-3 lateral nerves on either side of the upper lip well visible; stamens 5 mm long, glabrous, filaments affixed in the corolla throat, anthers 1 mm long, elliptic; style glabrous, 15 mm long, stigma bilabiате; ovary glabrous, 1.1 mm long. Mature capsule 6.5 mm long, glabrous, stipitate; stipe approximately 2 mm long; mature seed lentiform, hilum slightly emarginate, densely and regularly verruculose, black, 0.8 × 1 mm in diameter, 0.3 mm thick. Habitat in Gabon.

Slender herbaceous plant, somewhat creeping and prostrate at the base, ascending-erect, up to 35 cm tall, with stem more or less quadrangular, grayish green; internodes 5.5-2 cm long; Leaves subsessile or shortly petiolate over 2-7 mm. Leaf blade glabrous, narrowly elliptic or lanceolate, 5-7 × 0.5-1.4 cm, with obtuse or rounded acumen, with base much acuminate and decurrent, fairly dark green above, paler below. Lateral nerves approximately 6 pairs, ascending and anastomosing at a distance of 0.5-1 mm from the margins.

Inflorescences axillary and terminal; peduncles 2-4 cm long much accrescent at fruiting; bracts containing approximately 3 flowers, one of which is aborted, lanceolate, acuminate, 6-8 (9) 1-1.5 mm, accrescent at fruiting, glabrous, pale green, lighter than the leaves, slightly ciliate at the margins, bearing here and there a longer hair, bracteoles resembling the bracts but smaller, very acuminate and translucent. Calyx 3.5 mm long, with segments narrowly lanceolate, hyaline. Corolla white or very pale blue-violet, opening widely in two fairly narrow large lips; tube of the corolla cylindric, approximately 5 mm long, glabrous; upper lip slightly trilobate 6 × 3.5 mm, with rounded, ciliate lobes, approximately 0.5 mm in diameter. Stamens inserted at the throat, filaments glabrous, 5 mm long; anthers elliptic, 1 mm long. Style glabrous, 11 mm; bilabiате stigma; ovary glabrous, 1.8 mm long.

Capsule 6.5 mm, glabrous, stipitate over approximately 2 mm. Seeds lenticular, slightly emarginate at the hilum, black, densely and regularly verruculose, 0.8 to 1.2 mm in diameter, and 0.3 mm in thickness.

N. Hallé and G. Cours: 6146, Mimongo, 700 m alt. (fl. 22 May 1963), herbaceous with rigid stem, prostrate at base and erect, 0.50 m in length; growing on rocks at the edge of strong current, flower very pale blue-violet opening widely two fairly narrow large lips; somewhat shaded spot.
