# *Cyclocarpa stellaris* Afzel. ex Baker Planche 33

**Genus**: [[Cyclocarpa]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. trop. Africa 2 : 151 (1871)

## Description

Herb to 20(–50) cm tall; stem branched, reddish, slender, glabrous. Stipules lanceolate, ± 3 mm long, with linear spur of 2 mm below the point of insertion. Leaf: petiole 0.5–1 cm; leaflet with petiolule of 0.5 mm, lamina oblanceolate, 4–8(–11) × 2–3(–5) mm, glabrous. Inflorescence with axis < 5 mm; bracts caducous, lanceolate, 1–2.5 mm long. Flower: bracteoles below the calyx, ± 1 mm long; corolla yellow, standard obovate-cuneiform, 3–4 mm long, wings oblong, obtuse, keel obtuse, obliquely obovate. Legume 4–5 mm in diameter, with 8 to 12 deltoid articles ± 2 mm long, flattened, finely scabrous, each article with 1 seed. Seed triangular-reniform, ± 1 mm long and wide.

## Distribution

paleotropical species; in Africa, from Guinea-Bissau to the Democratic Republic of the Congo and Burundi, southwards to Malawi and Mozambique; in Gabon, known from Haut-Ogooué, Ogooué-Maritime, Ngounié and Nyanga

## Ecology

herbaceous savannas, roadside edges, humid places, lake margins; in Gabon at 125–640 m altitude, elsewhere to 1300 m. Flowering from February to May. b : Pellegrin (1949), Verdcourt (1971a, 1971f, 2000). ibliography Gardens, Kew (©), from Verdcourt (1971f)
