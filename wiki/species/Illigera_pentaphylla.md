# *Illigera pentaphylla* Welw.

**Genus**: [[Illigera]]
**Family**: [[Hernandiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Trans. Linn. Soc. London 27, 1: 26 (1869)

## Description

Stem solid. Leaves generally with 5 leaflets (juvenile and inflorescence leaves often with 3 leaflets); petiole 5–12 cm, loosely pilose or glabrous, petiolules 0.7–1.5(–3) cm; leaflets lanceolate-elliptic or obovate to lanceolate, 4–10 × 2.5–6 cm, subcordate to obtuse at the base, acuminate at the apex, upper surface glabrous, lower surface pilose towards the base of the nerves; lateral nerves in 2(–3) pairs, arising from the lower third of the blade. Flowers: tepals with inner surface tomentulose and provided at the base with setae; outer tepals 5–7 mm in length, 3–5-nerved, inner ones 3–4 × 0.5–0.6 mm, 1-nerved; filaments of the stamens provided with lanceolate appendages 0.5–0.8 mm long. Fruit 2-winged, puberulent, the central part up to 3.5 × 0.5 cm, the 2 wings oblong, slightly striated, each up to 3.9 × 6 cm.

## Distribution

from Guinea to Ghana and Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Congo, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Angola and Uganda

## Ecology

young or old secondary forests, rarely in riparian forest or gallery forest

## Source note

It should be noted that leaflets are highly variable, because for certain leaflets, the number of pairs of nerves is sometimes very inconspicuous, which creates a risk of confusion with I. vespertilio. For certain plants, the number of leaflets varies with the distance from the base to the inflorescence: 5, far from the inflorescence and 3, closest to it.
