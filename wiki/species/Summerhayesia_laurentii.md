# *Summerhayesia laurentii* (De Wild.) P.J. Cribb

**Genus**: [[Summerhayesia]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull. 32 : 185 (1977) ; FAC 2 : 505 (1992) ; FCAM 3 : 828 (2001)

## Synonyms

- Angraecum laurentii De Wild., Not. Pl. Ut. Inter. Fl. Congo. 1 : 322 (1904).
- Aerangis laurentii De Wild., Beih. Bot. Centralbl. 36 : 118 (1918).

## Description

PL. 208. – Structure of the gynostemium of the genus Summerhayesia P. J. Cribb : A, lateral view ; B, ventral view ; C, rostellum remnant (A-C, ex SZLACHETKO, 2003).

Stem < 20 cm. Leaves 15–25 × 0.5–0.8 cm, linear to oblong, apex rounded. Inflorescence rather lax, < 50 cm, < 15-flowered. Flowers small, perianth whitish or yellowish. Dorsal sepal elliptic, apiculate. Lateral sepals similar but with oblique base. Petals elliptic, acute. Lip 7 × 3 mm, subelliptic, apiculate, without callus, thin, fragile, glabrous. Spur 60–70 mm, filiform, pendent.

## Distribution

Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Gabon, Zaïre

## Ecology

alt. 0–300 m. Epiphyte in dense forest, notably observed on Antiaris welwitschii and Scorodophlaeus zenkeri

Rostellum digitate, long, fleshy, acute. Tegula double, one on each side of the rostellum, filiform, long, narrow, fleshy, with several layers. Viscidies 2, elliptic, lamellate, small, not touching each other. Rostellum remnants digitate, acute, without visible scar of the tegula or viscidies. Pollinia 2, porate.
