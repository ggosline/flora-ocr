# *Anthonotha pellegrini* Aubréville, sp. nov. $ ^{1} $

**Genus**: [[Anthonotha]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]

## Description

Young branches, rachis and petiolules densely rusty villous. Leaves 5-7 jugate, shortly petiolate. Rachis 7-14 cm long. Leaflets oblong, acuminate mucronate, glabrous above, densely rusty villous below, 4-11 cm long, 2-3 cm wide. About twelve lateral nerves, not visible below under the pubescence.

Panicles very short, densely villous, rusty, grouped in fascicles densely flowered in the axils of fallen leaves, on one-year-old branches. Pedicels 10 mm, villous. Involucral bracteoles valvate, obovate, densely villous on the outside. Sepals 4, one of which bifid wider, 4-6 mm long. One large petal 2 cm long, unguiculate, with bilobed lamina, 1.5 cm wide. Fertile stamens 3, long exserted, with filaments villous at the base; 3 staminodes. Ovary sessile, villous, with 3-4 ovules. Fruit unknown.

## Type

Le Testu 6496 (P), Ivouta, haute Ngounié (fl. avr.)
