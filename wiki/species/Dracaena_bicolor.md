# *Dracaena bicolor* Hook. Planche 5

**Genus**: [[Dracaena]]
**Family**: [[Dracaenaceae]]
**Modern family**: **Asparagaceae** subfam. Nolinoideae — *Dracaenaceae* as circumscribed here is superseded; see [[Dracaenaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Mag. 87 : t. 5248 (1861)

## Synonyms

- Pleomele bicolor (Hook.) N.E.Br., Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1914 : 277 (1914).

## Description

Monocaulous shrub, up to 5 m tall; trunk ± 5 cm in diameter; trunk generally simple. Leaves spiral, inserted in the upper part (1–1.5 m) of the trunk, with the stem visible between the sheaths or cataphylls; sheath embracing the stem over clearly more than the stem circumference, for a length of < 1.5 cm; pseudipetiole shorter than the blade, winged, green, passing abruptly into the sheath; blade narrowly obovate to linear or narrowly obovate, reaching 80 × 15 cm, cuneate at base, broadly acuminate at apex, tapering into a subulate mucro 0.5 cm long, coriaceous, bright green to dark green above, pale waxy green below; young shoots or fallen branches may bear considerably smaller leaves. Inflorescence subcapitate, densely flowered, up to 26 cm long, axis smooth, bearing prominent bracts; ± 20 flowers per fascicle, each fascicle subtended by broadly ovate bracts, ± 2 × 1 cm, scarious; floral bracts narrowly ovate, up to 20 × 3 mm, enveloping the base of the pedicel, scarious, green to violaceous. Flower: pedicel 0–4 mm; perianth 25–30 mm long, white, slightly tinged with violet near the apex of the lobes, lobes shorter than the tube, reflexed; stamens inserted near the throat, up to 2 mm shorter than the lobes, filament filiform, white, anther 2.5 mm long, pale yellow; ovary ovoid, up to 3 × 1.5 mm; style exsert for up to 1 mm, white, stigma ± 1 mm in diameter. Fruit subspheric, up to 3 cm in diameter, orange to yellow-orange, with receptacle up to 2 mm long. Seed laterally flattened, ± 13 × 5 mm, surrounded by a white pulpy aril.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, in Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué, Ngounié, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

understory of primary forests, humid stations, often near rivers; in Gabon at 5–800 m altitude, elsewhere at 0–1300 m. Flowering recorded from January to March, July, November and December

## Source note

The species often spreads by fallen branches that root rapidly, thus forming large patches. Bibliography: Baker (1898), Bos (1984), Damen et al. (2018), Hepper (1968), Mwachala (2005, 2007), Velayos & Cabezas (2018)
