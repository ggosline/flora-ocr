## Description

HUTCHINSON & DALZIEL, FWTA, ed. 1, 1 (2): 324 (1918); EXELL & MENDONÇA, CFA 1 (2): 327, p.p. exclud. Welwitsch 4668 = D. insigne ENGL. (1951); HAUMAN, FZRB 7: 315 (1958); KEAY, FWTA, ed. 2, 1 (2): 438 (1958); BRETELER, MLW 73 (13): XX (1973); L.C. 79 (16): 47, 66 (1979); MPLW 19: 85 (1980); L.C. 82 (8): 67–70 (1982); AUWP 86 (3): 12, 13, 16, 17 (1986). TYPE: Angola, Golungo Alto, Welwitsch 4652 (holo-, G; iso-, BM, COI, K, LISU, P).

Large liane, with diameter reaching 4 cm, or lianescent shrub. Wood cylinder entire or nearly so. Bark rather smooth with poorly defined rows of scattered and rather large lenticels. Young and mature branchlets red-brown velvety to hispidulous to villous-tomentose, glabrescents with age.

Stipules entire, triangular and narrow or not, 1–3 (5) mm long, appressed pubescence.

Leaves. Petiole subcylindric, 1–3 (6) mm long, velvety to villous. Blade obovate-elliptic, sometimes ovate or oblong, (1) 1.5–2.5 times longer than wide, 5–14 (17) × (3) 4–6 (7) cm; base subcordate to obtuse, sometimes rounded to cordate; apex with acute acumen 1 cm long, to simply acute or obtuse apex and often mucronate; young leaves generally slightly scabrous above, hispidulous on the upper surface of vein I and on the entire lower face; but denser covering on this vein I and on the (6) 7–9 (10) main pairs of veins I; veins I and II often impressed above and prominent below; glands generally few only beneath the blade and very difficult to see, ± scattered but often more numerous in a narrow zone along vein I.

Inflorescences in subspherical capitula, ≤ 15-flowered, tomentose-velvety. Peduncle (2) 6–25 mm. Bracts and bracteoles generally narrow and ovate-triangular, 0.5–2 mm long; appressed brown pubescence on the outside, absent to sparse on the inside. Pedicel ≤ 2 mm, lower part ≤ 2 mm, upper part ≤ 0.5 mm.

Flowers. Sepals suberect to spreading to slightly reflexed, shortly united at their base, oblong-obovate to elliptic, (2.5) 3–4 × (0.7) 1–1.5 (2) mm; exterior villous often mixed with some stiff hairs; interior glabrous or puberulent. Petals slender, suberect (at least for the lower part), distinctly longer than the sepals, narrow and obovate, very shortly united at their base to the filaments, (3.5) 4.5–5 mm long, split for 1.5–2 mm; interior glabrous; exterior with some rather stiff hairs below the split, sometimes distributed toward the base and on the lobes which often also bear some crisp hairs; lobes concave often somewhat rolled or twisted; the proximal margins of the lobes united at their base and protruding on the inner side (Pl. 55, 5). Stamens generally suberect, slender, (6) 7–9 mm long (more than the petals), glabrous; anthers subreniform, ≤ 0.5 mm long. Staminodes narrow and oblong 1–1.5 mm, fine and glabrous, terminated by 2 horns. Pistil 3 (4)-merous, 6.5–9 mm long, glabrous or rarely with some scattered stiff hairs (ovary becoming velvety immediately after fertilization); stigma flattened to vaguely 3 (4)-lobed.

Fruits 1 (3 ?)-seeded, aborted locules not visible or rendered scarcely visible by a vague ridge, yellow-orange at maturity, subspherical, velvety, diameter ≤ 25 mm, apex most often depressed; exocarp 2–4 mm thick; mesocarp palisade-like in appearance, 1–2 mm thick; endocarp cartilaginous, smooth and shiny on the inside. Seed subellipsoidal, ≈ 10 × 7 mm; testa brown, smooth slightly shiny. – Pl. 55, p. 183.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Congo, Zaïre, Angola

## Ecology

evergreen and semi-deciduous forests

This species may present some resemblances to D. reticulatum Engl. (still unknown from Gabon); it differs from it however by its petals distinctly longer than the sepals, its glabrous pistil or rarely with some scattered stiff hairs and by the petiole 1–3 (6) mm long (see under D. ruficeps).
