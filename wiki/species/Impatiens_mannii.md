# *Impatiens mannii* Hook. f.

**Family**: [[Balsaminaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Impatiens]]

## Description

Herb with alternate medium-sized leaves and fascicled axillary violet-pink flowers. Lip shallow and boat-shaped with a straight or curved spur no longer than its opening; standard ample; lateral petals more strongly coloured, lip white.

## Distribution

Lower Guinea east to Uganda.

N. Hallé (1962). Balsaminaceae. In *Flore du Gabon* 4: 35–36.
