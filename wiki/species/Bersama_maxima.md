# *Bersama maxima* Baker

**Family**: [[Melianthaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Bersama]]

## Description

Small or large tree. Stipules 25–75 mm; leaves 30–95 cm with 6–14 leaflet pairs; rachis densely orange-pubescent and winged 2–8 mm. Leaflets sessile, largest (9–)12–26 × (3–)4–7.5 cm, base obtuse to subcordate, pubescent especially beneath. Racemes 36–80 cm with 100–200 white flowers; pedicels 10–24 mm. Sepals 4; petals 5, to 17–20 mm; stamens 4; disc glabrous; ovary pubescent, 4-locular; style tapered and pubescent. Young fruit apiculate, four-grooved and yellow-rufous-pubescent.

## Distribution

Cameroon to Angola and coastal Congo; Gabon collections from Tchibanga and Maghoungha, upper Ngounié. Type: Mann 1853, Corisco.
