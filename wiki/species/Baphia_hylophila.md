# *Baphia hylophila* Harms

**Genus**: [[Baphia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 26 : 282 (1899)

## Description

B. buettneri Harms subsp. hylophila (Harms) Soladoye, Kew Bull. 40 : 378 (1985). Tree, small to medium or sometimes shrub, reaching 18 m in height; trunk up to 20 cm in diameter; branchlets glabrous. Stipules elliptic, 3–3.5 × 1.5 mm, with ciliate-denticulate margin. Leaf: petiole with contiguous pulvini, (3–)4–8(–10) mm, glabrous; leaflet with ovate-elliptic blade, (3–)8–15(–18) × 2–6(–7) cm, rounded at base, apex acuminate for 0.5–2(–2.5) cm, papyraceous, glabrous; lateral nerves in 5 to 7(–8) pairs. Inflorescence in axillary fascicle of up to 10-flowered or in short raceme, glabrous; bracts broadly elliptic, 1–1.5 × 1.5–2 mm, striated. Flower: bracteoles situated at apex of pedicel, against calyx, transversely elliptic, ± 1 × 1.5 mm, with ciliate margin; pedicel filiform, 12–15(–25) mm, glabrous or nearly so; calyx spatulate, 8–12 × 9–10 mm, glabrous except for some apical hairs; standard ± circular to broadly obovate, 10–14 × 10–13 mm, bilobed at apex, wings obovate, 10–16 × 5–7 mm, with claw of 1–1.5 mm, keel petals falciform, 9–14 × 3–5 mm, with claw of 0.5–1 mm; stamens 8–14 mm, anthers 2–2.5 mm in length; pistil 9–13 mm in length, ovary velvety, with 5 or 6 ovules. Legume oblong, 8–15 × 1.5–3.5 cm, rounded at base, sparsely puberulous, glabrescent, with 2 or 3 seeds. Mature seed not seen.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Angola (Cabinda); in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

Primary and secondary forest; in Gabon at 50–500 m altitude, elsewhere at 0–400 m

## Source note

In Soladoye (1985), this species was treated as a subspecies of Baphia buettneri, but on the basis of the original descriptions of Harms and material collected since 1985, it is apparent here that its original rank is more appropriate. Bibliography: Pellegrin (1949), Soladoye (1985).
