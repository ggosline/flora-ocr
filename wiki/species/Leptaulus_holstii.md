# *Leptaulus holstii* (Engler) Engler

**Genus**: [[Leptaulus]]
**Family**: [[Icacinaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. 24 : 481 (1898)

## Synonyms

- Alsodeiopsis holstii Engl., Abhandl. Wiss. Ergebn. Deutsch. Zentr.-Afr. Exp. 1910-1911 2:33 (1922).

## Description

— L. oddonii (De Wild.) Engl., Pflanzenw. Afr. 3, 2: 251 (1921).

— L. gossweileri Cavaco, Bull. Mus. Paris 29: 511 (1957), syn. nov.

— Alsodeiopsis oddonii DE WILD., Ann. Mus. Congo b., Bot., ser., 5, 2: 42 (1902). — Th. & H. DURAND, Syll.: 99 (1909).

Shrub with young branchlets ± pubescent. Adult branchlets obscurely striate longitudinally. Petiole ± glabrous or pubescent, 2–5 mm long, canaliculate and striate longitudinally above, striate longitudinally below. Blade glabrous on both surfaces, dull green ± yellowish on upper surface, pale green on lower surface, elliptic to obovate, 4.5–20 × 2.5–8 cm; base attenuate or cuneate, apex obtusely acuminate. Midvein concave above, prominent and striate longitudinally below. Secondary veins ascending, arched, anastomosing 2.5–7 mm from the blade margin, weakly prominent on upper surface, prominent on lower surface. Tertiary veins in loose network prominent on lower surface.

Inflorescence in a cyme decussate with respect to the leaf, pauciflorous. Pedicel glabrous, 1–2 mm long, bearing triangular bracteoles. Flowers ♀ pentamerous, 4 × 2 mm, yellow. Sepals connate ± imbricate, glabrous on both surfaces, lanceolate, 2.5 × 1 mm. Petals glabrous, connate with each other; corolla lobes campanulate, linear, 3 mm long, furnished with a glandular claw 1–1.5 mm long; apex acute. Stamens connate to the corolla tube by the filament for almost its entire length; anther with 2 falcate locules appendiculate at the apex. Ovary glabrous to puberulent, asymmetric, 2.5 mm long; style glabrous, short, with ± globose stigma.

Drupe ovoid oblong, 10–15 × 6–8 mm, becoming bright red at maturity. Endocarp woody. PL. 14, 13–17, p. 63.

## Type

Holst s.n., Ngwelo, Tanzanie (holo-, B, delet.; iso-, K)

## Discussion

The type of Leptaulus gossweileri Cavaco shows no notable difference from specimens of L. holstii. We therefore place these two taxa in synonymy.

This species is found in rainforests or in forest galleries from Cameroon to Tanzania.
