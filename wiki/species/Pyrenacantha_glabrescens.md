# *Pyrenacantha glabrescens* (Engler) Engler

**Genus**: [[Pyrenacantha]]
**Family**: [[Icacinaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. 43 : 187 (1909)

## Synonyms

- Chlamydocarya glabrescens ENGL., Bot. Jahrb. 24 : 485 (1897).

## Description

— P. mangenotiana Miège, Bull. I.F.A.N., ser. A, 17 : 8, tab. 1, 2 (1955). — KEAY, FWTA ed. 2, 1 : 641 (1958), syn. nov.

Liana with glabrous stem, weakly ribbed, grey or pale beige, pustulate. Petiole very sparsely pubescent, 1–3 cm long; circular in section. Blade glabrous above, very sparsely pubescent or glabrous below, dark green becoming black when dry on the upper surface and dark brown ± glossy on the lower surface, elliptic, broadly elliptic or lanceolate, 8–16 × 3.5–8 cm; base rounded or attenuate, apex shortly acuminate obtuse mucronate. Midvein prominent glabrous above, prominent below. Secondary veins 3–4 pairs, very ascending, arched, anastomosing 3–4 mm from the blade margin, prominent on the upper surface, very strongly prominent on the lower surface. Tertiary veins in dense reticulate pattern prominent above, very strongly prominent below. Hydathodes absent.

Inflorescences ♂ and ♀ in solitary axillary spikes. Rachis of the ♂ inflorescence ± strongly pubescent with reddish hairs, green when fresh becoming black when dry, 11–16 cm long. Floral bud globose, pubescent. Bract fleshy, sparsely pubescent at the base, densely reddish pubescent at the apex, strongly appressed against the inflorescence rachis, lanceolate, 1 mm long. ♂ Flower tetramerous, sessile. Calyx absent. Petals free to the base, pubescent on the outer surface, glabrous on the inner surface, elliptic, 2.5 × 1.5 mm; apex obtuse. Stamens 0.75 mm long; filament short, 0.3 mm long, terminating at the apex in a collar surrounding the anthers, upper margin wavy; anther ellipsoid 0.3 mm long. Pistillode reduced to a tuft of hairs. ♀ Inflorescence rachis 4–4.5 cm long, reddish pubescent. Floral bud reddish pubescent, globose. Bract similar to that of ♂ inflorescences. ♀ Flower tetramerous, pale green, sessile. Calyx absent. Petals free, reddish pubescent on the outer surface, glabrous on the inner surface, elliptic, 2.5 × 1.5 mm; apex obtuse. Ovary superior densely reddish pubescent (uncinate hairs). Style short terminating in a digitate stigma.

Drupe 30–35 × 15–18 mm when fresh, 25–30 × 15–18 mm when dry, slightly flattened transversely, finely pubescent, ellipsoid. Fine prickles erect on the inner surface of the woody endocarp. PL. 17, p. 75.

## Type

Soyaux 302, Gabon (holo-, B, delet.; iso-, P!)

## Discussion

This type could be compared with the ♀ specimens that served for the diagnosis of Pyrenacantha mangenotiana Miège. The morphology of the vegetative organs, the inflorescence axes and the bracts of the two species does not differ notably. We therefore accept their synonymy.

This species has a very discontinuous range: Côte d'Ivoire and Gabon.
