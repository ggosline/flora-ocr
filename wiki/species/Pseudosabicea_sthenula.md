# *Pseudosabicea sthenula* N. Hallé sp. nov. (P.L. 43).

**Genus**: [[Pseudosabicea]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]

## Description

≠ Ps. medusula cf. N. Hallé in Adansonia 3 : 171, pl. 1 f. 3 (1963).

Aff. Ps. medusula (K. Schum. ex Wernham) N. Hallé, sed caulis gracilibus repentibus, nodiis omnino unifoliatis, stipulis plurifidis cingentibus, laminis ovatis vel ovalibus non obovatis, nerviis secundariis 7-10 (nec 10-12) utrinque, apice alabastri barbato, differt.

Small hairy quasi-herbaceous plant, creeping and mat-forming, several decimetres long, with slender root-bearing internodes, with flowering apices moderately erect with short internodes of ± 1 cm, with alternate leaves opposite to an embracing submembranous stipule, 9-15 mm long, with 3-9 sharp ciliate teeth. Petiole hairy 0.5-3 (4.5) cm; discolorous blade with ± scattered hairs above, with dense woolly white indumentum below, ovate or oval, 3-8 × 1.8-5 cm; base unequal obtuse to cordate; apex very briefly acute. 7-10 pairs of secondary nerves.

Hairy inflorescences sessile 1-4-flowered, inserted in the axil of the stipule opposite the leaf, and bearing two lower bracts somewhat fused to each other on the adaxial basal side. Calyx hairy (violaceous hairs) at the level of the ovary, tubular for 1 mm, then divided into 5 lobes of approx. 6 × 1 mm, ciliate. Corolla with bearded apex in the bud, with ± red tube reaching 8 × 1.5 mm in vivo, with triangular lobes 3-4 mm long, white, glabrous on the upper surface, with the throat concealed by abundant erect white hairs, with beaded structure. Stamens subsessile; medifixed, subapiculate at apex, split towards the base, white and exceeding 1 mm long, inserted at the level of the throat (short-styled form studied); pollen white. Style glabrous, white, 6 mm, including the 2(3) lipid suberect stigma lobes. Disc glabrous, in vivo pale yellow, 0.5 mm tall, somewhat sinuous at the upper level. Ovary 2-locular. Multiovular masses oval exceeding 1 mm in height.

Fruit green, sometimes slightly reddish at apex, hairy, globoid, reaching 8-10 mm diam., crowned by the sepals. Numerous small brown seeds, elongate, 0.6 mm long, ovate ± angular.

## Type

N. Hallé 1339 (P), région de Makokou, 27 fév. 1961. L'espèce la plus voisine, Ps. medusula, susceptible d'être rencontrée au Gabon, se distingue par des feuilles un peu plus grandes et obovées; probablement moins longuement traçante, elle diffère de façon remarquable par ses stipules entières et par l'une des feuilles de chaque paire qui est réduite à un vestige. Ps. medusula est connu du Cameroun et du Congo (Bas-Uele)

## Discussion

Ps. sthenula appears to be a riparian semi-shade species; it has also been found around 700-800 m, along roadsides, on illuminated clayey banks.

Material studied (only known):

N. Hallé : 1339 and 1378, Mboundou, 37 km SW of Makokou (fl. fr. v. Feb.). N. Hallé and G. Cours : 6160, road from Mimongo to Mbigou (fr. May).
