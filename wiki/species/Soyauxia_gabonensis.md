# *Soyauxia gabonensis* Oliv.

**Genus**: [[Soyauxia]]
**Family**: [[Peridiscaceae]]

## Description

in Hook., Icon. pl. sér. 3, 14 : t. 1393 (1882). Shrub or tree up to 20 m tall; trunk up to 90 cm in diameter; branchlets often angular, pubescent to ± hispidulous, generally glabrescenct. Stipules narrowly elliptic, straight or slightly falcate, 7–12 × 1.5–4 mm, with appressed hairs on the outside, glabrous on the inside. Leaf: petiole (2–)3–6(–7) mm, channelled above, with indument like the branchlets; lamina narrowly obovate-elliptic, (8–)12–22 × 3–6(–8.5) cm, rounded to shortly cuneate at the base, acuminate over (0.5–)1–2.5 cm at the apex, glabrous above except for the pubescent midvein, below with scattered hairs, ± appressed or not, more dense on the midvein and lateral nerves; lateral nerves (13–)15–18(–19) pairs. Inflorescence simple, up to 12 cm long, longer on compound racemes at the branch tips, with indument like the branchlets; peduncle 1–1.5 cm. Flower: pedicel 0–1.5 mm, with appressed hairs; sepals spreading, broadly ovate-elliptic, 3–3.5 × 2.5–3.5 mm, with appressed hairs on the outside except on the margin; petals spreading, ± elliptic, 4 × 1.5–3 mm, with short appressed hairs on the outside, glabrous on the inside; stamens approximately 6 mm long, glabrous, with anthers approximately 0.3 mm long; disk as tall as the ovary, dentate to lobulate; ovary 0.5 mm long, tomentellous; styles 4–5 mm, glabrous. Fruit oblong, 8–12 × 6–7 mm, glabrous except at the apex tomentellous. Mature seed not seen.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon and Gabon where it was collected in the Estuaire, the Ogooué-Maritime and the Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

primary and secondary forest; in Gabon, up to 250 m elevation, elsewhere, up to 500 m. B: Breteler et al. (submitted), Keay (1958). Bibliography
