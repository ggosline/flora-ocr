# *Polycoryne fernandensis* (Hiern) Keay

**Genus**: [[Polycoryne]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Protologue**: R. W. J. Keay, Bull. J. B. Brux. 28 : 34 (1958); F. W. T. A. ed. 2, 2 : 119, f. 225 (1963)

## Description

Description based exclusively on Gabonese specimens.

Very tall liane with glabrous extremities. Internodes with August (mature) subquadrangular wood 3–8 cm long and 4–9 mm wide, with brown or reddish epidermis, at first lenticellate then scaly. Oblong stipules 10–15 mm, erect, folded lengthwise into a groove, glabrous, with moderately ciliolate margin, caducous. Petiole glabrous 16–45 mm. Blade glabrous, brownish grey when dry, ovate, 8–17 × 5–10 cm; base briefly cuneate to cordate; apex with short acumen or ± rounded subtruncate. 5–7 (8) pairs of glabrous secondary veins; fine reticulation poorly distinct.

Inflorescences glabrous, multiflorous in corymbiform or ± spherical clusters, terminal sometimes on short branchlets grouped into panicles; axes of ramifications rarely exceeding a few millimetres in length. Pedicels 1–3 mm. Bud clavate with subacute conical apex. Flowers white very fragrant, with carnation scent, nigescent when dry. Calyx glabrous 5 × 1.5 mm, tubular for 2–2.5 mm, with small ± ciliolate lobes 0.5–1 mm. Corolla glabrous inside as well as outside, with tube 18–35 mm long and approximately 1 mm wide at the middle; throat funnelform reaching 3–5 mm diam.; lobes oblong 9–13 × 4–5 mm. Anthers 5 × 1 mm, with connective prominent in an apicule for nearly 0.5 mm and briefly slit at the lower extremity. Ovary turbinate 2 mm long.

Fruits spherical smooth, solitary or in small number, green before maturity, 3–5 cm diam. when dry, reaching the size of a small orange. Seed disciform ± irregular, approximately 12 mm diam.; cotyledons flattened.

## Type

Mann 22, Fernando-Po (K!, isotype P!)

## Discussion

This species is known from West Guinea as far as the Congo (Yangambi) and Cabinda; the range appears to be interrupted only in Togo and Dahomey. Specimens from Guinea differ in the size of the calyx lobes which reach 5 × 1.5 mm; they merit being described as a new variety.

P. fernandensis (Hiern) Keay var. Pobeguini N. Hallé var. nov., differs from the type in having foliaceous sepals 5 mm long, 1.5 mm wide (holotype P! : H. Pobeguin 1401, Timbo, Jan. 1907, flowers; the fruit is described as "hard yellow-green ball, with Strychnos seeds, size of large lemon". Other specimens: Pobeguin 867; A. Chev. 20539; Jacques-Félix 773.

F. Fleury 26187 (in herb. A. Chev.), Adouma on the Orimbo (fr. f. July).
