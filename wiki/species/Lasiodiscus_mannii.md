# *Lasiodiscus mannii* Hook. f. ex Hemsl.

**Family**: [[Rhamnaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Lasiodiscus]]

## Description

Shrub or small tree with alternate toothed leaves and branched axillary inflorescences. Flowers 5-merous with hooded petals enclosing the stamens and a conspicuously hairy disc. Fruit a small drupe. The Vol. 4 treatment supplies a new description based solely on Gabonese specimens and discusses substantial variation.

## Distribution

Lower Guinea from Nigeria and Cameroon through Equatorial Guinea to Gabon.
