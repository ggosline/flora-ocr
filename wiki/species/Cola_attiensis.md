# *Cola attiensis* Aubr. et Pellegr.

**Genus**: [[Cola]]
**Family**: [[Sterculiaceae]]
**Protologue**: AUBR. et PELL., Bull. S. B. Fr. 92 : 255 (1945)

## Synonyms

- Cola bodardii Pellegr. nov. syn.

## Description

Shrub exceeding 3 m in height (up to 15 cm in trunk diam. in F. F. C. I.), with very finely puberulent extremities.

Terminal bud with enveloping ovate, obtuse or somewhat acute scales, puberulent externally. Stipules very early caducous. Branchlets pale beige. Petioles puberulent then glabrous, 1 to 9 cm long, very unequal on the same branchlet. Blade subcoriaceous, glabrous, elliptic, oblong or obovate, 10–26 × 3–10 cm, with base more or less longly acute, sometimes subobtuse; apex distinctly acuminate. Lateral veins 7–15 pairs pinnate. Reticulation very apparent on both faces of the blade when dry, and particularly on the lower face.

Inflorescences in sessile glomérules densely bracteolate on the trunk or branchlets, at a certain distance from the leaves. Bracteoles ovate pubescent 1–1.5 mm long. Pedicels somewhat pubescent, slender, 2 to 13 mm long, accrescent after fruit set in the ♀ flowers. Buds subspheric 5-keeled.

Flowers pink or salmon yellow. Calyx 2.5 to 6 mm long, clothed with rust-coloured stellate hairs externally, with numerous punctiform papillae internally, very briefly campanulate at the base, divided into 5 oblong lobes with thinned margins and adorned with a puberulent line on the external side; apex of the lobes more or less obtuse. ♂ flower with narrow androphore 0.5–1.5 mm long, glabrous, bearing a corona of (8) 10 pollen sacs uniseriate (i.e. (4) 5 anthers and not (8) 10). Ovary of the ♀ flower with (4) 5 pubescent carpels, 1–2-ovulate, surrounded basally by a corona of reduced pollen sacs, shortly attenuate at the apex and terminated by 5 curved papilliferous stigmas.

Fruit composed of small follicles, one to four, divergent on the receptacle; they are globular, glabrous and indehiscent, with a narrow pedunculate base for 5–10 mm long, apiculate, containing 1 or 2 seeds 6–8 mm in diameter.

We have not observed ♂ flowers with 8 pollen sacs (= 4 stamens), nor ♀ flowers with 4 carpels; however the indications of previous authors allow us to cite these numbers.

The name Cola attiensis var. bodardii (Pellegr.) N. Hallé comb. nov., should be retained for the form with small flowers and shortly pedicellate.

Type of the species: Forest Service of the Ivory Coast no. 12 bis (= 1786 nec 1785), specimen in fruit (P.).

There exists for Gabon no ecological note concerning this species. In Ivory Coast it is a small understory tree that grows along riverbanks or in humid terrain.
