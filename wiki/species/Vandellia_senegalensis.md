# *Vandellia senegalensis* Benth. Planche 52, 54(I–K)

**Genus**: [[Vandellia]]
**Family**: [[Scrophulariaceae]]
**Protologue**: Lindernia senegalensis (Benth.) Hiern, Cat. afr. pl. 1 : 763 (1898)

## Description

in DC., Prodr. 10 : 416 (1846). Lindernia senegalensis (Benth.) Hiern, Cat. afr. pl. 1 : 763 (1898). Annual herb; stem prostrate to ascending, with numerous branches (paraclades), 13–29 cm in length, glabrous or pubescent. Leaves sessile; blade oval-triangular to obovate, 20–40 × 10–25 mm, acuminate at the apex, margin obtusely dentate. Inflorescence lax, each fertile node bearing 2 flowers. Flower: pedicel 3–4 mm; calyx 6 mm in length, with lobes 1 mm in length, glabrous or sparsely pubescent; corolla light violet with darker spots, tube 6 mm in length, upper lip bipartite and 4 mm in length, lower lip tripartite and 4–5 mm in length; anterior (abaxial) stamens with filaments 3–4 mm, filaments of posterior (adaxial) stamens 2 mm; ovary ovoid, 1.5 mm in length; style 3 mm. Fruit lanceolate to ovoid, 13–14 mm in length, more than 2 times as long as the calyx.

## Distribution

Senegal, Guinea-Bissau, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, observed in Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

primary and secondary rainforests, paths, clearings, river banks, on sandy soil; in Gabon, at 450–570 m altitude, in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, up to 850 m. B: Fischer (1992, 1995, 1999), Fischer et al. (2013), Hepper (1963). Bibliography
