# *Dracaena arborea* (Willd.) Link Planche 3

**Genus**: [[Dracaena]]
**Family**: [[Dracaenaceae]]
**Protologue**: Enum. hort. berol. alt. 1 : 341 (1821)

## Synonyms

- Pleomele arborea (Willd.) N.E.Br., Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1914 : 277 (1914).

## Description

D. arborea (Willd.) Link var. baumannii Engl., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 59, Beibl. 131: 20 (1924). Tree up to 30 m tall; trunk up to 1.75 m in diameter, with scaly bark, longitudinally fissured, without exudate; young stems and branches yellow-brown; aerial roots often produced after physical damage. Leaves spiral, clustered at the apex of stems and completely covering them, sessile; blade ensiform, up to 200 × 6 cm, gradually narrowed toward the base with a sheathing portion embracing the stem over more than ¾ of the circumference, progressively narrowed at the apex into a mucro of ± 4 mm, succulent on old specimens, green above, pale green with prominent midvein below, on young specimens narrowly obovate, up to 30 × 7 cm, coriaceous. Inflorescence pendulous, highly branched, up to 2 m in length, with smooth axes, greenish-red; bracts beneath the branches rapidly reduced from transitional leaves 15 cm in length to narrowly triangular bracts of ± 20 × 10 mm, scarious, dark green; fascicles densely aggregated at the ends of the axes, forming there a short spiciform thyrse, pluriflorous, with up to 5 flowers per fascicle; floral bracts triangular, up to 4 mm in length, scarious. Flower: pedicel filiform, up to 10 mm, with the basal persistent part up to 8 mm, giving a bristly appearance to the axes after flowering; perianth 15–23 mm in length, cream-white, with lobes longer than the tube, with apex light flesh-coloured on the outside; stamens inserted 1 mm above the throat of the tube, up to 4 mm shorter than the lobes, filament white, inflated, with acute apex, anther 2–2.5 mm in length, pale yellow; ovary bottle-shaped, up to 3 × 2 mm; style weakly exceeding the tube, white, stigma ± 1 mm in diameter. Fruit spherical or slightly obloid, up to 24 × 27 mm, smooth and shiny, orange to red, with receptacle up to 6 mm in length, much thicker than the persistent pedicel. Seed spherical, ± 15 mm in diameter.

## Distribution

Guinea, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Togo, Benin, Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, São Tomé-and-Príncipe, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Angola; in Gabon, in Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué, Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem.

## Ecology

Forests and forest margins, often on mountain ridges, near streams and villages; in Gabon at 0–600 m altitude, elsewhere at 0–2000 m. Flowering recorded in February, March, June, July, September and December.

Use: The species is planted near villages, churches and cemeteries. The bark is used for a female initiation ritual, the roots as an aphrodisiac and for purification of the urinary system.

## Vernacular names

cockade bush, tree dracaena (angl.) ; ozogoué (mpongoué) ; alénokoué (pahouin)

## Source note

The leaves of young specimens resemble those of D. fragrans. B: Baker (1898), Bos (1984), Hepper (1968), Velayos & Cabezas (2018). Bibliography
