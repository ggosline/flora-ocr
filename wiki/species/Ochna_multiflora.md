# *Ochna multiflora* DC.

**Genus**: [[Ochna]]
**Family**: [[Ochnaceae]]
**Protologue**: Ann. Mus. Paris 17 : 412, t. 3 (1811)

## Description

O. mannii Tiegh., J. Bot. (Morot) 16 : 121 (1902), non Kuntze. O. coriacea Tiegh., Ann. Sci. Nat., Bot. sér. 8, 16 : 384 (1902). O. griffoniana Tiegh., Ann. Sci. Nat., Bot. sér. 8, 16 : 384 (1902). O. klainei Tiegh., Ann. Sci. Nat., Bot. sér. 9, 5 : 177 (1907). O. rubricaulis Tiegh., Ann. Sci. Nat., Bot. sér. 9, 5 : 177 (1907). Pentochna ramosa Tiegh., Ann. Sci. Nat., Bot. sér. 9, 5 : 177 (1907). Tree or shrub reaching 15 m in height; bark smooth, thin, pale grey to dark grey and brownish-purple, scaly; branchlets dark brown, generally densely furnished with small round white lenticels but sometimes lenticels absent and branchlets smooth; buds fusiform, 12–15 × 2–4 mm, with caducous scales. Stipules linear, 10–20 mm in length, entire. Leaf: petiole 2–7 mm; blade elliptic to elliptic-oblong or elliptic-obovate, 5–16 × 3–7.5 cm, obtuse to rounded or slightly cordate at the base, obtuse to slightly acuminate at the apex, coriaceous or sometimes membranous, with margin subentire to very weakly spinulose-serrulate, subcoriaceous to coriaceous; 7 to 10 secondary nerves, prominent on both surfaces, tertiary venation densely reticulate and prominent especially beneath. Inflorescence racemiform, multiflorous, erect, with rachis 2.5–8 cm. Flower: pedicel 1.5–2.5 cm, articulate 1–5 mm from the base; sepals ovate to elliptic, 5–7 × 3–4 mm in flower, reaching 9–12 × 6–7 mm in fruit; petals obovate or elliptic to broadly obovate-elliptic, 7–10 × 3–6 mm, bright yellow; stamens numerous, filament 2–4 mm, anthers 1.5–2 mm in length, with longitudinal dehiscence; ovary with 5 to 8(–9) carpels; style 4–5 mm, with short radiating stigmas. Fruit: pedicel elongating to 2.5 cm; receptacle 4 × 6 mm; drupels reniform-flattened, 6–10 × 4–6 mm. Seed curved; cotyledons incumbent, unequal, with small internal cotyledon.

## Distribution

from the Gambia to Senegal and from Sierra Leone to Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Angola and Zambia; in Gabon, uncommon, recorded from the Estuaire, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Maritime and Haut-Ogooué (Batéké Plateaux).

## Ecology

forest galleries or periodically flooded forest, mangroves, coastal savanna, often on sandy soil; in Gabon, at 0–400 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1200 m. b : Bamps & Farron (1967), Hawthorne & Jongkind (2006), Hutchinson, Dalziel & Keay (1954), Robson (1963), Verdcourt (2005).
