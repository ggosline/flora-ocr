# *Octolobus spectabilis* Welwitsch.

**Genus**: [[Octolobus]]
**Family**: [[Sterculiaceae]]
**Protologue**: WELW., Trans. Linn. Soc. 27 : 18 t. 6 (1869), réf. orig

## Synonyms

- Oct. angustatus Hutch. nov. syn.
- Cola rolandi-principis A. Chev.

## Description

, orig. ref. — F. T. A., I : 219 (1868). — K. SCHUM. Sterc. Afr. : 99 (1900). — PELLEGR. Sterc. Gab., Mém. S. B. Fr. : 45 (1950-1951), excl. Le T. 9491 et 9004 (between Ogooué... and Bitam). — F. W. T. A. 2nd ed. I : 319 (1958). — F. F. C. I. 2nd ed. II : 294 (1959).

Small tree 4–5 m tall, with young twigs clothed in rust-colored stellate hairs. Mature branchlets glabrescent. Terminal bud scales erect, acute. Stipules very acute, long-attenuate, 5–12 mm long, generally less than 1 mm wide at base, falling relatively quickly. Petioles puberulent-glabrescent, (0.9) 1–7 cm long. Leaves entire, olivaceous or greyish in color when dry, pubescent when very young then glabrous. Lamina 5–24 (29) cm long by 1.5–10 cm wide, with base long-attenuate, acute or narrowly subacute; apex variable, acuminate or sublanceolate. Midvein distinctly raised on upper surface of lamina. 7–11 pairs of lateral veins. Fine reticulation distinct on lower surface. Flowers solitary or in few-flowered glomules on mature branchlets. Approximately 7–20 imbricate bracteoles beneath each flower, the upper ones larger, ovate-oblong, with obtuse or more or less acute apex, with ochraceus external pubescence, glabrous and striate-nerved on inner face. Bud obovate. Flowers sessile, articulate, yellowish or yellow-green. Calyx fleshy, 16–33 mm long, campanulate in its lower half, divided into 8 lobes, clothed with ochraceus hairs of more or less appressed or hirsute appearance on exterior, papilliferous on inner face. Inner margin of lobes thinned into undulate fringes. Male flower with calyx tube rather narrow, columnate androphore 5–7 mm tall, glabrous at least on upper two-thirds. 24–32 pollen sacs arranged in a cupule. Female flower with short conical pubescent androgynophore, ovary surrounded by a crown of anthers; 3 or 4 tight rows of a large number of carpels inserted on a conical receptacle. Carpels hairy, erect, free or slightly adherent to one another, each terminated by a broad, rounded stigma. Fruit composed of approximately 10–30 follicles 12–20 mm in diameter, globose, densely puberulent, pressed against one another, with pedicle-like base 3–12 mm, with apex bearing a beak 2–3 mm long. Approximately 2–6 seeds arranged in two rows, compressed against each other, with oval placental insertion approximately 6 × 3 mm, with shiny black epidermis, with two fleshy glabrous cotyledons, with gemule clothed in rust-colored hairs.

Type specimen of the species: Welwitsch 1202 (B.M., P.).

This species is widespread from Sierra Leone to the Belgian Congo and Angola. The variations observed on specimens from Gabon and Ubangi, in comparison on the one hand with those from Ivory Coast, on the other hand with the type, lead us to consider Oct. angustatus as not specifically distinct from Oct. spectabilis. Indeed, the calyx pubescence as well as the size and number of bracts are highly variable; by contrast, the characters of the fruits are very constant, and in the leaves, the venation, the texture and color of the lamina as well as the form of the base.
