# *Dorstenia ciliata* Engler

**Genus**: [[Dorstenia]]
**Family**: [[Moraceae]]

## Description

in SCHLECHT., W. Afr. Kautschuk-Exp. 1899-1900: 286 (1900), nom. nud.; Bot. Jahrb. 33: 114 (1904); RENDLE in HUTCH. & RENDLE, FTA 6 (2): 31 (1916); KEAY, FWTA, ed. 2, 1: 597 (1958).

- D. harmsiana ENGL., in SCHLECHT., l.c.: 286 (1900), nom.; l.c. 33: 115 (1904), descr.; RENDLE, l.c.: 31 (1916). TYPE: Schlechter 12888, Cameroon (holo-, B; photo, BM, K).

- D. jabassensis ENGL., Bot. Jahrb. 46: 270 (1911); RENDLE, l.c.: 42 (1916). TYPE : Ledermann 1072 Cameroon (holo-, B).

- D. jabassensis var. cuneata ENGL., l.c.: 271 (1911); RENDLE, l.c.: 42 (1916). TYPE : Ledermann 1286, Cameroon (holo-, B).

- D. asteriscus ENGL., l.c. 51: 428 (1914); RENDLE, l.c.: 36 (1916). TYPE: Ledermann 6044, Cameroon (holo-, B).

Herbs with creeping rhizomes approximately 6 mm thick. Ascending stems, 30-100 cm tall, with short uncinate hairs (yellowish), white, scattered but dense in the upper part, mixed with recurved to uncinate hairs, longer and stouter; internodes 1.5-4.5 cm long, 2.5-6 mm thick. Leaves almost distichous. Blade lanceolate to oblong to elliptic (sometimes orbicular to obcordate), sometimes narrowing towards the base, (6-) 10-26 × 3-10.5 cm, chartaceous; apex abruptly to gradually acuminate or subacute with an acute acumen; base broadly to narrowly obtuse or subcordate, sometimes cuneate; margin sinuate to slightly sinuose, sometimes with 1-3 obscure teeth towards the apex; upper face glabrous; lower face, especially on the nerves, with short uncinate white hairs, scattered to fairly dense, often mixed with somewhat longer and stouter hairs; midvein and secondary nerves slightly impressed above; midvein, secondary nerves and sometimes also the network of tertiary nerves very prominent below; 8-14 pairs of secondary nerves. Petiole 1-3.5 cm long, 1.5 mm thick, with indumentum similar to that of the stem or denser. Stipules deciduous, subulate, 1-3 mm long and approximately 0.5 mm wide at the base, with small scattered hairs.

Inflorescences solitary or in pairs, greenish. Peduncle (0.5-) 1-4 cm long and 1-2 mm in diameter, with small white uncinate hairs, dense, mixed with stouter, somewhat longer hairs. Receptacle discoid and flat but slightly convex at anthesis or after, suborbicular to multiangular, or sometimes subromboid, 8-22 mm in diameter, with margin at most 0.5 mm wide; appendages numerous, crowded, 1-30 mm long, ± claviform or even bilobed at the apex; 6-15 appendages longer than the diameter of the receptacle and arising beneath the margin; between them, 2-5 appendages much smaller and often inflexed, arising from the margin; upper face of appendages and margin with small fairly dense (yellowish-) white uncinate hairs; lower face of receptacle and appendages with small (yellowish-) white uncinate hairs, fairly dense, sometimes mixed with stouter and longer hairs, especially on the very prominent ribs of the longer appendages. Flowers ♂ numerous; perianth 2-3 (-4) -lobed, approximately 0.75 mm in diameter; stamens 2-3 with filaments 0.5 mm long and 0.1 mm in diameter and anthers approximately 0.25 × 0.2 mm, with connective as wide or less wide than the thecae; pistillode present. Flowers ♀ 20-40, regularly arranged; perianth tubular approximately 0.5 mm tall and 0.4-0.75 mm in diameter; style approximately 0.3 mm in diameter, exceeding the perianth by 0.15 mm; stigmas 2, linear, approximately 0.7 mm long, spreading or reflexed, sometimes with a few very small hairs at the apex. - Pl. 16, p. 59.

## Type

Schlechter 12891, Cameroun (holo-, B ; photo-, K)

## Discussion

This variety is known from Nigeria and several localities in NW Cameroon. Only one specimen comes from Gabon. It is found in the understory of dense humid high forests or riparian forests, up to 1,800 m altitude.

## Specimens examined
