# *Entandrophragma congoense* (De Wild.) A.Chev. Planche 3(D–F)

**Genus**: [[Entandrophragma]]
**Family**: [[Meliaceae]]
**Protologue**: Vég. Ut. Afr. Trop. Franç . 5, Bois Côte Ivoire : 204 (1909)

## Description

, p.p. Large tree reaching 45 m in height; trunk up to 90 cm in diameter, with thickened base or buttresses; bark fissured vertically producing elongated rectangular scales, brownish; heartwood pinkish, without exudate and with faint mahogany odor; terminal buds glabrescent. Leaf: petiole 6–14 cm, plane-convex at base; rachis 10–26 cm, glabrous; petiolule 1–10 mm; leaflets 10–18(–20), elliptic to narrowly rectangular or ovate or sometimes obovate, (4–)6–14 × 1.5–4.5 cm, cuneate or more rarely subrounded and asymmetric at base, acute or acuminate at apex and often with the acumen spoon-shaped (the margins folded upward), glabrous, shiny above; midvein ordinarily glabrous beneath, secondary veins (7–)9–15(–16) pairs, prominent, sometimes hairy and with domatia at the axil, veinlet network prominent. Inflorescence reaching 40 cm in length, loosely branched; axes pubescent; bracts broadly obovate, 0.7–0.8 mm in diameter. Functionally male flower: pedicel ± 1.5 mm; calyx patelliforme, 5-lobed, 0.5 mm in height, glabrescent; petals elliptic to rectangular, 4–5 × 1.5–2.5 mm, glabrous; staminal tube urceolate, 2–3 mm in length, with 10 apical lobes 0.2–0.5 mm in length, each surmounted by an elliptic anther 0.7 × 0.4 mm, filaments thin and free, 0.2 mm in length; gynoecium (pistillode), the disc-gynophore included, 3 mm in length, glabrous. ## Distribution

Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Ngounié, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

primary humid forest and old secondary evergreen and semi-deciduous forest; up to 600 m altitude. Use: timber

## Vernacular names

tiama negro, tiama noir (nom pilote) ; odyokou (mitsogho). B : Liben & Dechamps (1966), Liben (1970), Wilks & Issembé (2000). ibliographie
