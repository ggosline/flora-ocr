Leaves distichous; sheath 1–2.2 cm long, with a line densely pubescent along the suture, otherwise subglabrous to long ciliolate at the apex; pseudopetiole absent; blade narrowly oval-elliptic to oval-elliptic, 4–9 × 1.5–3.5 cm, base cuneate on one side and rounded on the other, apex acute to acuminate or sometimes mucronate, upper surface glabrous or with short pubescence, lower surface with pubescent midvein, margins scabrous toward the apex, sometimes ciliate toward the base. Inflorescence terminal, with 2 or 3(–4) sessile and imbricate spathes in clusters, sometimes on short lateral branches; spathes funnel-shaped to triangular, with partially connate margins (over 7–20 mm), 1.3–2.1 × 1–2.5 cm, slightly falcate, apex acute, glabrous on both surfaces; peduncle up to 7 mm, sparsely hairy, that of fallen flowers emerging clearly from the spathe; upper cincinnus absent, the lower 3- to 9-flowered. Flower ± 11–13 mm in diameter; upper sepal narrowly oval-elliptic, lower ones obovate, entirely connate; upper petals broadly spatulate, with semi-circular apical part, 9 × 7 mm, with claw 4 mm, white or tinged with pale blue, violet or mauve, the lower one oval, 6 × 3 mm, concave, white; stamens with filament ± 5 mm, filaments of the lateral ones slightly longer than that of the median, anther oblong, pollen yellow; staminodes 3; style ± 8 mm, stigma capitate. Fruit 3-locular, obovoid to ellipsoid, 5.5–7 × (2.5–)4–5 mm, with constricted base, 2-valved, pale yellow or greyish-brown speckled with dark brown, dorsal valve deciduous, connate with the seed, each locule with one seed. Dorsal seed ellipsoid, dorsoventrally compressed, 2.3–3.8 × 2–2.5 mm, with brown testa, ± smooth to alveolate; ventral seeds ± ellipsoid to broadly ovoid, dorsoventrally compressed, 2.5–3.3 × 1.7–2.2 mm; testa smooth to alveolate, dark brown, covered with white powder.

## Distribution

Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Ivory Coast, Ghana, Togo, Benin, Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea (Bioko), São Tomé, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Uganda; in Gabon in the Estuary, Moyen-Ogooué, Nyanga, Ogooué-Lolo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

forest trails, forest edges, marshy places; in Gabon from 25 to 500 m altitude, from 700 to 1200 m in East Africa, elsewhere from 190 to 1200 m. Flowers opening between approximately 7 and 12 o'clock (West Africa). Flowering is observed from January to May, October and November (Gabon), in August and November (Benin) and in September (East Africa)

## Source note

C. congesta is a species closely related to C. bracteosa Hassk. (not yet recorded from Gabon), but this species has sterile bracts on the peduncle. Bibliography: Brenan (1968b), Brunel et al. (1984), Cabezas et al. (2014), Faden (2012), Sosef et al. (2006), Vanden Berghen (1988)
