# *Pyrenacantha lebrunii* Boutique

**Genus**: [[Pyrenacantha]]
**Family**: [[Icacinaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. J. B. Brux. 29 : 426 (1959); Fl. Congo b. et R.U. 9 : 254 (1960)

## Description

Liane with stem densely pubescent, russet greenish, weakly twisted and ribbed. Petiole pubescent russet, ± prehensile, flattened on upper face, strongly longitudinally wrinkled, 1-2 cm long. Lamina glabrous on upper face, densely pubescent on lower face, with ± erect pubescence, grey-green on both faces, elliptic or lanceolate, 5.5-15 × 2.5-6.5 cm; base weakly auriculate or attenuate rounded, apex acuminate obtuse mucronate. Midvein prominent glabrous on upper face, prominent pubescent on lower face. Secondary veins 4-8 pairs, ascending, slightly arched, anastomosing 1-3 mm from limb margin, prominent on both faces, pubescent below. Veinlets in dense prominent reticulation on both faces. Hydathodes erect piriform on limb margin, glabrous.

Male inflorescence in fascicles of racemes. Rachis bearing bracts, pubescent, 3.5-7 cm. Male flowers pale green, tetramerous, pedicellate. Pedicel pubescent bearing a bractlet at its base or at its middle, 1-1.5 mm long. Bractlet linear or narrowly lanceolate, pubescent externally, glabrous internally. Petals free from each other, pubescent externally, glabrous internally, broadly elliptic, 2 × 1.25 mm; apex acute.

Stamens erect, filament short, fleshy, lanceolate, 0.25 mm long; anther with 2 ellipsoid locules 0.3 mm long. Pistillode reduced to a tuft of hairs. Female inflorescence in axillary raceme. Rachis pubescent 2.5-5 cm long, bearing bracts. Female flowers tetramerous, pedicellate. Pedicel pubescent, 1 mm long, bearing a bractlet. Petals oblong, 1.5-2 mm long. Ovary strongly pubescent to tomentellous.

Drupe ellipsoid globose, compressed transversely, apiculate, pubescent. Prickles inflexed or recurved at apex, on the inner face of the woody endocarp. Seed ellipsoid, compressed transversely, 10 × 8 mm.

## Type

Lebrun 3032, Angodia, District forestier central, Zaïre (BR)

## Discussion

This species occurs in forest groupings up to an altitude of 1300 m. Its range extends from Cameroon to Zaïre.
