– H. neurophyllum HOYLE, Kew Bull.: 265 (1932); KEAY, in HUTCH. & DALZIEL, FWTA, ed. 2, 1 (1): 195 (1954). TYPE : Vigne 1182, Ghana (holo-, FHO ; iso-, K).

Tree (30-35-(45) m tall) or slender shrub ; young branches covered with pale ferrugineous tomentum.

Leaves with subpersistent stipules, reniform-falciform, 15 × 13-15 mm ; petiole 4-8-(12) mm long, pubescent ; blade elliptic-oblong or obovate-elliptic, rounded to subcordate at the base, shortly acuminate or obtuse-rounded at the apex, 7-16-(25) × 4-8-(10) cm, dentate, chartaceous to subcoriaceous, pubescent especially on the midvein on the upper surface and everywhere on the lower surface, sometimes glabrescent on both surfaces ; secondary veins 8-12-(15) pairs.

Inflorescences in axillary and terminal panicles, multiflorous, reaching 25 cm long, tomentose. Flowers arranged along the slender rachis, either solitary or grouped 2-4 together in spaced glomérules ; pedicel 0.5-1 mm long ; calyx with broadly turbinate tube, as long as wide, 1 × 0.5-1 mm, shortly contracted-attenuate at the base ; lobes 5-6-(7), triangular-ovate, 1-1.5 mm long ; petals spatulate-oblong, 2-3 × 1 mm, accrescent to 8-10 × 2.5 mm in the fruiting state, tomentulose on both surfaces. - Pl. 20, 7-10, p. 65.
