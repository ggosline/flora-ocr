# *Bulbophyllum intertextum* Lindl.

**Genus**: [[Bulbophyllum]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: J. Linn. Soc., Bot. 6 : 127. (1862)

## Description

– Bulbophyllum seychellarum Rchb. f., Linnea 41 : 93 (1877).

– Bulbophyllum quintasii Rolfe, Bol. Soc. Brot. 9 : 138 (1891).

– Bulbophyllum viride Rolfe, Kew Bull. 79 : 170 (1893).

– Bulbophyllum usambarae Kraenzl., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 34 : 58 (1904).

– Bulbophyllum triaristellum Kraenzl. & Schltr., Orchis 2: 98 (1908).

– Bulbophyllum amauryae Rendle, Cat. Talb. S. Nig. Pl. : 101, 146 (1913).

– Bulbophyllum pertenue Kraenzl., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 51 : 385 (1914).

Pseudobulbs 0.4–1 × 0.3–0.7 cm, ovoid to suborbicular, 1-leaved. Leaf shortly petiolate to sessile, 0.7–10 × 0.3–1.1 cm, elliptic to linear-lanceolate, rounded or acute. Inflorescence 2–30 cm, lax, generally rigid, rachis very often zigzag, 2–14 (20)-flowered.

PL. 96. – Bulbophyllum comatum Lindl. var. inflatum (Rolfe) J. J. Verm.: A, general view; B, flower; C, floral parts; D, labellum; E, labellum and gynosteme in natural position, lateral view; F, anther; G, pollinia (A-G, ex VERMEULEN, 1987).

Flowers small, very pale yellowish to greenish or red-purple. Sepals cuspidate, glabrous to finely papillose: the dorsal ovate-lanceolate; lateral sepals obliquely triangular to ovate-triangular sometimes caudate. Petals broadly elliptic to obovate-oblong, oblique, obtuse, generally glabrous. Labellum oblong-elliptic to oblong-ovate, apex rounded, somewhat recurved, surface glabrous, papillose towards the apex, margins almost glabrous to ciliate. – Pl. 97, p. 195.

## Distribution

from Guinea to Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, São Tomé, Príncipe, Gabon, Angola, Congo (Kinshasa), Ethiopia, Kenya, Tanzania, Zambia, Malawi, Zimbabwe, Seychelles, Madagascar

## Ecology

alt. 300–600 m. Epiphytic on branch and trunk; submontane and montane lowland forest, grassland, rare in Brachystegia woodland; sometimes lithophytic
