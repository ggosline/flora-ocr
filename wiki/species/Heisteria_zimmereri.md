# *Heisteria zimmereri* Engler

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Heisteria]]

Notizbl. bot. Gart. Berl. 2: 288 (1899); Pflanzenw. Afr. 3(1): 85, tab. 50 (1915).

## Description

Shrub or small tree 4–7 m tall, reaching 15 m in hygrophilous groupings. Crown conical. Branches sinuous, furrowed and weakly winged along 2 opposite lines, very branched toward the exterior. Petiole 7–15 mm long, canaliculate above, striated below. Blade glabrous on both faces, brown or yellowish-green ± shining with a granular aspect above, paler green, matte and finely granular sometimes below, oblong, elliptic, sometimes lanceolate, 8–17 × 3–7.5 cm; base cuneiform, apex acuminate obtuse mucronate. Midrib concave on the upper face and salient, strongly striated on the lower face. Secondary nerves 5–7 pairs, ascending, arched, slightly concave above, weakly salient below. Nervilles in a loose indistinct network most often on both faces.

**Inflorescence**: Axillary fascicle with few flowers. Pedicel 3–8 mm long. Floral bud globose with valvate petals. Flower pentamerous, white or greenish. Sepals fused for 2/3 of their length, glabrous on both faces, broadly ovate, apex acute. Petals erect, glabrous externally, pubescent in the middle of the inner face, elliptic, 3.45 × 1.45 mm, strongly keeled on their external face, fleshy on the upper 2/3. Stamens 10 in 2 cycles, filament of alternipetalous stamens 2.3 mm long, enlarged at the base and filament of epipetalous stamens strongly applied against the inner face of the petals, 1.8 mm long; anther of alternipetalous stamens 0.5 × 0.3 mm, anther of epipetalous stamens suborbicular 0.5 × 0.5 mm; connective apiculate at the apex. Ovary superior 0.75 mm long, with 3 uniovulate locules. Central placenta erect. Style short conical, approximately 1 mm long, terminating in a trilobed stigma.

**Fruit**: Axillary solitary. Pedicel black slightly striated, 10–12 mm long. Calyx strongly accrescent with undulate or very weakly emarginate upper margin. Drupe ellipsoid to obovoid with yellow or brown exocarp. Seed solitary with oily albumen. Embryo apical.

## Distribution

**Range**: Guinea region, from Cameroon to Zaïre (Democratic Republic of the Congo) and Cabinda.

**Cameroon**: Known from Kribi, Makak Forest Reserve, Bidjoka, Bipindi, Mimfia, and Ebéa.

**Gabon**: Known from Moanda, Libreville, Mayumbe Forest (Nyanga and Tchibanga), Moughimba, and Sibang.

**Democratic Republic of the Congo and Cabinda**: Distribution extends through Zaïre and Cabinda.

## Habitat and ecology

Rainforest species found in dense humid evergreen forest and along watercourses. Flowering September to November; fruiting extending to February.

## Uses

The grayish-pink wood is used in carpentry; it provides house posts. The oily seed yields an oil that solidifies very rapidly. The seed is edible when roasted.

## Source

## Notes

Lectotype designated as Dinklage 861 from Cameroon, with isotypes at P and WAG. The distribution cited extends from Cameroon through Gabon to Zaïre and Cabinda, representing a wide range across the Lower Guinea region.
