# *Heisteria zimmereri* Engler

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Heisteria]]

Notizbl. bot. Gart. Berl. **2**: 288 (1899); Pflanzenw. Afr. **3(1)**: 85, tab. 50 (1915). — Pellegrin, Fl. Mayombe **1**: 63 (1924). — Sleumer, Pflanzenfam., ed. 2, **16b**: 16 (1935). — J.Louis & J.Léonard, Fl. Congo b. et R.U. **1**: 645 (1954). — Walker & Sillans, Pl. ut. Gabon: 316, tab. 44,2 (1961). — Michaud, Mém. I.F.A.N. **75**: 181, tab. 2, j–k (1966).

## Description

**Habit**: shrub or small tree 4–7 m tall, reaching 15 m in hygrophilous groupings. Crown conical. Branches sinuous, furrowed and weakly winged along 2 opposite lines, very much branched outwards.

**Leaves**: petiole 7–15 mm long, canaliculate above, striate beneath. Blade glabrous on both faces, brown or yellow-green ± glossy with a granular appearance above, lighter green, matte and finely granular sometimes beneath, oblong, elliptic, sometimes lanceolate, 8–17 × 3–7.5 cm; base cuneate, apex acuminate, obtuse, mucronate. Midrib concave on the upper face and prominent, strongly striate on the lower face. Secondary nerves in 5–7 pairs, ascending, arcuate, slightly concave above, weakly prominent beneath. Nervules forming a loose, mostly indistinct reticulum on both faces.

**Inflorescence**: axillary fascicles, few-flowered. Pedicel 3–8 mm long. Floral bud globose with valvate petals.

**Flowers**: pentamerous, white or greenish. Sepals fused for 2/3 of their length, glabrous on both faces, broadly ovate, apex acute. Petals erect, glabrous externally, pubescent in the middle of the inner face, elliptic, 3.45 × 1.45 mm, strongly keeled on their external face, fleshy on the upper 2/3. Stamens 10 in 2 cycles; filament of alternipetalous stamens 2.3 mm long, enlarged at the base and filament of epipetalous stamens strongly appressed against the inner face of the petals, 1.8 mm long; anther of alternipetalous stamens 0.5 × 0.3 mm, anther of epipetalous stamens suborbicular 0.5 × 0.5 mm; connective apiculate at the apex. Ovary superior, 0.75 mm long, with 3 uniovulate locules. Central placenta erect. Style short, conical, approximately 1 mm long, ending in a trilobed stigma.

**Fruit**: axillary, solitary. Pedicel black, slightly striate, 10–12 mm long. Calyx strongly accrescent with upper margin undulate or very weakly emarginate. Drupe ellipsoid to obovoid with yellow or brown exocarp. Seed solitary, with oleaginous albumen. Embryo apical.

## Lectotype

*Dinklage 861*, Ebéa, Cameroon (holo–, B, deleted; iso–, P!, WAG!).

## Distribution

From Cameroon to Zaire (Democratic Republic of the Congo) and Cabinda. In Gabon, collected at: Moanda (6 km road to Franceville, 1°33' S and 13°15' E); Mayumbe bayaka forest, Nyanga border, Mongonyanga; Mayumbe bayaka forest, Tchibanga; Moughimba; environment of Libreville; Sibang.

## Habitat and ecology

Dense humid evergreen forest and riverbank forest. Flowering from September to November; fruiting until February.

## Vernacular names

Nkamba (Mpongwè, Nkomi, Orungu); akamba (Bakèlè); pasi (Béséki); psè, gégamba-sa-ebeko (Apindji); gépasè, ogamba-kotè (Mitsogo); gépasè, ogamba (Bavové); igamba (Banzabi); gikamba (Bavili, Baduma); mugamba-syègi (Eshira, Bavarama, Bavungu, Bapunu); mugamba (Balumbu, Ngowé); ngós, vyas, afōng (Fang).

## Properties and uses

The greyish-pink wood is used in joinery; it provides house posts. The oleaginous seed yields an oil that solidifies very rapidly. The seed is edible after roasting.
