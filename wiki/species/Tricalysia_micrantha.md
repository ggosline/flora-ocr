# *Tricalysia micrantha* Hiern

**Genus**: [[Tricalysia]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Hiern, F.T.A. 3:122 (1877)

## Description

Small shrub 1 to 5 m tall. Juvenile branchlets slender with scarcely visible puberulence even under magnification. Stipules glabrescent with subulate acumen 1–2 mm. Petiole glabrescent, 2–5 mm long. Lamina brown or olivaceous, glabrous, somewhat coriaceous, with finely bordered margin on the lower surface, elliptic, 6–14 × 2–5 cm; base acute or sometimes obtuse; apex acuminate sometimes sublanceolate. 5–7 pairs of secondary veins entirely glabrous; fine veins scarcely distinct, sometimes indistinct. Domatia axillary with poorly ciliolate ostiole or absent.

Flowers in few- or many-flowered contracted axillary cymes. Axes pubescent, including pedicels 2 mm or less long. Small bracteolar cupules with short pubescence. Calyx pubescent, cupuliform for a height of 1 mm, with 5–6 narrow denticles reaching one quarter to one half millimetre. Corolla moderately pubescent externally, nearly glabrous internally, with 5–6 elliptic apiculate lobes 3 mm. Filament 2 mm; anther glabrous or somewhat pubescent, submedifixed, 2.5 × 0.5 mm, with indistinctly apiculate connective. Style glabrous, 2 (3)-lobed. 2 locules biovulate.

Fruit globose, 5.5–6 mm in diameter, glabrescent, red at maturity, containing 2 to 4 seeds. Seed brownish, 3–4 mm.
