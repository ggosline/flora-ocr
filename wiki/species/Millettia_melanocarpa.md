# *Millettia melanocarpa* (Hauman) Adomou, comb. et stat. nov .

**Genus**: [[Millettia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: M. gossweileri Baker f.. var . melanocarpa Hauman, Bull. Soc. Roy. Belg. 86 : 280 (1954)

## Description

Robust liane; stem cylindrical, ±3 cm in diameter; branchlets with white lenticels, silky to almost glabrous; exudate brownish-red. Stipules caducous. Leaf: petiole (2.5–)3–7 cm, channelled above, silky; rachis like the petiole, 5.5–14 cm; pulvinus 0.5–1 cm in length, silky; stipels absent; leaflets (3–)5 to 7(–9), petiolule 4–9 mm, silky, blade broadly elliptic or sometimes obovate, (4.5–)8–15 × 5–8.5 cm, base cuneate to rounded, sometimes truncate or slightly cordate, apex acuminate-mucronate, acumen 0.5–1.7 cm, glabrous above, silky below; lateral veins in (10–)15 to 25 pairs. Inflorescence in terminal panicle or sometimes in axillary pseudopanicle, 20–50 × 12–14 cm, brown silky; peduncle 4–12 cm; branches 16–32 cm; bracts caducous. Flower: pedicel 1.5–4(–5) mm, silky; bracteoles at the base of the calyx, ovate, concave, 1–1.5 × 1–1.75 mm; calyx 6–7(–8.5) mm in length, golden-brown silky, tube 3–6 mm, lobes 1–5 mm, margins white ciliate; corolla white to orange, somewhat fragrant, standard with claw 2–4 mm, blade broadly obovate-elliptic, 11–14 × 10–15 mm, apex emarginate, auricles inflexed, callositites prominent, wings with claw 2.5–4 mm, blade elliptic-obovate, 10–18 × 2.5–5 mm, keel with claw 3.5–5 mm, blade elliptic-falcate, 11–17 × 3–7 mm, apex rounded, lateral pocket prominent; stamens unequal, staminal tube 8–12 mm, free portion 1–7 mm; ovary sessile or nearly so, 4–6 mm in length, with spreading pubescence, with 4 to 6 ovules; style 8–14 mm, lower part pubescent, upper part curved and glabrous, stigma capitate. Legume oblong, flattened, 8–12 × 1.6–2.1 cm, apex rostrate, with dark purple pilosité mixed with white hairs. Seed flattened, obovoid to suborbicular, 0.8–1 × 0.6–0.8 cm, with punctate surface; hilum central, 1.5–2 mm in length.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, rare and known only from Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology

Swampy or coastal forest, near savannas; in Gabon at 2 m altitude, in the Democratic Republic of the Congo at 470 m. Flowers in May. b: Hauman (1954a). ibliography
