# *Cuervea macrophylla* (Vahl) Wilczek ex Hallé

**Genus**: [[Cuervea]]
**Family**: [[Celastraceae]]
**Protologue**: Monogr. Hippocr. Afr. occid., Thèse : 53, tab. 2 (1958); Bull. IFAN, sér. A, 21 (3) : tab. 50 (1959) ; Pollen & Spores 2 (1) : 11 (1960) ; Mém. IFAN 64 : 58 (1962) ; Wilczek, FCB 9 : 141 (1960)

## Description

Bull. IFAN, ser. A, 21 (3): tab. 50 (1959); Pollen & Spores 2 (1): 11 (1960); Mem. IFAN 64: 58 (1962); Wilczek, FCB 9: 141 (1960).

– Hippocratea macrophylla VAHL, Enum. 2: 28 (1805); DE CANDOLLE, Prodr. 1: 568 (1824); OLIVER, FTA 1: 371 (1868); LOES., Bot. Jahrb. 34: 115 (1904); A. CHEV., Expl. Bot. 1: 131 (1920); HUTCH. & MOSS. FWTA 1: 449 (1928); EXELL & MENDONÇA, CFA 2: 14 (1954); KEAY & BLAKELOCK, FWTA, ed. 2, 1 (1): 629 (1958). – Hippocratea rotundifolia HOOK.F., Fl. Nigr., London: 279 (1849).

High lianes, 5–20 m and more, with cylindrical stems. Petiole 10–20(–26) mm. Blade large, 10–27 × 7–17 cm, coriaceous; base often rounded; apex ± broad with an often short and acute acumen less than 1 cm; margin entire. Secondary veins 5–8 pairs. Reticulation dense, sometimes very apparent when dry, appearing lighter on olivaceous blade.

PL. 65. – Cuervea isangiensis (De Wild.) Hallé: 1–3, cruciform branchlets; 4, floriferous branchlet; 5, detail of leaf margin viewed from the lower face; 6, bud; 7, flower, diam. 11 mm; 8, calyx, diam. 5 mm; 9, sepal 2.2 mm wide; 10, petal 4.5 mm long; 11, young disk; 12, 13, disk, diam. 3 mm; 14, 15, stamen; 16, 17, open locules; 18, fruit valve 9 cm long; 19, seed group from a mericarp (1, Bouquet 1230, Congo; 2, de Néré 1302, Congo; 3, Letouzey 5475; 4, 5, 15, 17, Le Testu 8113; 6–14, 16, Hallé N. 2803; 18, 19, Laurent s.n. 8.2.06, Zaire).

Inflorescences lax in panicles of cymes or in cymes with peduncle 4–12 cm, rather vigorous. Pedicel 5–10(–25) mm. Buds globose, 4–6 mm in diameter. Flower 20–24 mm in diameter. Calyx green; 5 subequal sepals, 1.7–2.7 mm wide, rounded and microdenticulate. Petals white turning ochre-yellow, subunguiculate, spaced, obovate, concave in a cap shape, 7–12 × 5–7 mm. Disk 3–3.5 mm in diameter, with 5 rounded lobes. Stamens with filaments 0.5 mm, as wide as long. Pistil approximately 1 mm tall, with (4)6(–8) ovules per locule, with lobed stigma already evident in the bud.

Fruit voluminous, 15 cm in diameter with the 3 carpels. Mericarp reaching 10–11 or 11–10 cm, thickness up to 4 cm, of variable shape; receptacle up to 12 mm in diameter. Seeds 4–6 per carpel, 4–6 × 2–3 cm, weighing up to 15 g, with ribbon-like raphe 5–33 mm long, without wing. Cotyledons at least partly fused. Embryo poorly structured. - Pl. 66, p. 227.

## Type

Don s.n., Sierra Leone (holo-, BM!)

## Discussion

Guinea, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Ivory Coast, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, Muni, Gabon, Central African Republic, Congo, Zaire, Angola.

Forest species favoring galleries and stations periodically flooded with moisture; sometimes on sublittoral fringes.
