# *Zehneria palmatiloba* O.Lachenaud & H.Schaefer.

**Genus**: [[Zehneria]]
**Family**: [[Cucurbitaceae]]
**Protologue**: Phytotaxa 496(2): 172 (2021)

## Description

Plate 37

Herb apparently dioecious; stem filiform, ±0.5 mm thick. Leaf: petiole slender, 1.5–4 cm; lamina deeply 5-7-palmatifid, 2.5–6 × 2–5.5 cm, cordate at base, with unequal lobes (the basal ones smaller), obovate to rhomboidal, usually bearing 1 or 2 small lateral teeth and sometimes shortly lobed, obtuse and with a short mucro at apex. Inflorescence axillary: the male 1–3 cm long, with a filiform peduncle 0.7–2.3 cm, with 7 to 20 flowers arranged in a subumbelliform raceme; the female 3.5–7.5 cm long, racemose, with a peduncle 1.7–6 cm and with 4 to 7 flowers. Male flower: pedicel 1–3 mm; receptacle cupuliform, 0.8–1.2 × 1.2–1.8 mm; sepals deltoid, scarcely distinct; corolla divided almost to the base into 5 triangular lobes ±0.5 mm long; stamens 3, included, with filament ±0.5 mm, glabrous, with bithecate, reniform anthers, ±0.4 × 0.6 mm, papillose; rudimentary ovary convex, ±0.5 mm high, glabrous. Female flower: pedicel 8–11 mm; receptacle urceolate, ±1 × 2.3 mm; sepals deltoid, scarcely distinct; corolla divided almost to the base into 5 triangular lobes 0.8–1.2 × 0.6–1 mm; staminodes 5, linear, ±0.5 mm long; ovary ovoid, ±2 × 1 mm, with ±16 ovules; disc entire, cylindric, ±0.5 mm high, glabrous; styles 3, connate at base, ±1 mm, glabrous, with capitate stigma. Fruit globose, 8–11 cm diameter when dry, with pedicel 9–14 mm. Seed compressed, obovoid in outline, ±5 × 3 mm, whitish, ±155

## Distribution

Equatorial Guinea (Rio Muni); in Gabon: Estuaire

## Ecology

primary forest, swampy low-lying areas, sometimes along streams; in Gabon at 75 m altitude, elsewhere at ±450 m
