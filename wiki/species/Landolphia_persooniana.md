# *Landolphia persooniana* O.Lachenaud & Jongkind Planche 64

**Genus**: [[Landolphia]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Candollea 77 : 32 (2022)

## Description

Large liana; branches sparsely pilose to densely hirsute; outer bark not exfoliating. Leaf: Spirolophide ± mm hirsute petiole and elliptic of ± to ± cm rounded to slightly cordate at base abruptly acuminate to caudate over ± cm at the apex, cuticle shiny dark green above, dark green below with a clear median nerve, glabrous or with very scattered hairs on the median nerve below; secondary nerves 8 to 15 pairs, almost straight at angle almost perpendicular to the median nerve, forming well-visible arcs, arriving at 1–2.5 mm from the margin, generally several less visible nerves between two secondaries, tertiary venation visible below. Inflorescence axillary, subsessile very contracted, with 1 to flowers, long-bracteate mm. Flower fragrant; pedicel of 1–1.5 mm, with scattered short hairs; 2 bracteoles at the apex of the pedicel; bud reaching 21 mm in length; calyx to lobes imbricate, erect narrowly ovate to triangular ± to ± mm, ciliate and slightly pubescent on the outside; corolla with pale yellow-green tube and white lobes, tube long of 5.5–8 mm, slightly swollen at the level of the stamens, wide of ± mm with short ascending hairs on the outside, hairy on the inside only at the level of the stamens, throat glabrous, with lobes of 9.5–18 × 0.5–2 mm, glabrous or with very scattered hairs on the margin, white; stamens included, inserted just above the middle of the tube, anther long of ± mm, glabrous or with some hairs on the back and a tuft of hairs at the apex; pistil long of 4.5–5 mm, ovary glabrous at the base, villous at the apex; style of 2.5–2.7 mm, glabrous. Fruit unknown. Distribution endemic to Gabon, Ngounié and Nyanga.

## Ecology

forest, at 90–740 m altitude
