# *Tridactyle filifolia* (Schltr.) Schltr.

**Genus**: [[Tridactyle]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Beih. Bot. Centralbl. 36 : 144 (1918) ; CRIBB, FTEA, Orchid. 3 : 610 (1989) ; LA CROIX & al., Orchid. Malawi : 291 (1991) ; FAC 2 : 532 (1992) ; FCAM 3 : 700 (2001)

## Description

– BASIONYM: Listrostachys linearifolium De Wild., Not. Pl. Ut. Congo 1 : 149 (1903).

## Type

Schlechter 12791, Gabon, holo-B, delete., iso-K!

## Discussion

– Angraecum filifolium Schltr., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 38 : 21 (1905).

– Tridactyle linearifolia (De Wild.) Schltr., Beih. Bot. Centralbl. 36 : 146 (1918).

– Tridactyle tridentata (Harv.) Schltr. var. subulifolia Summerh., Kew Bull. 3 : 287 (1948).

Stem 10–80 cm, cylindrical. Leaves 3.5–14 × 0.03–0.1 cm, cylindrical, apiculate, grooved on the upper surface. Inflorescence 0.2–0.8 cm, 2–3-flowered. Flowers minute, dingy white. Dorsal sepal lanceolate to lanceolate-ovate. Petals linear-lanceolate. Lateral sepals obliquely ovate to oblong-ovate. Lip 1.7–3.5 × 0.8–2.5 mm, distinctly auriculate at the base, rectangular, semi-square to trapeziform, with a small triangular apicule at the centre of the apical margin. Spur 5–8.3 mm, cylindrical, curved upward at the apex. – Pl. 180, p. 391.

## Distribution

Guinea, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Togo, Nigeria, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Gabon, Congo, Zaire, Rwanda, Burundi, Ethiopia, Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, Zambia

## Ecology

alt. 850–1,600 m. Pendant epiphyte, in dense humid forest, observed on Entandrophragma cylindricum
