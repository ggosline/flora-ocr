# *Tristemma leiocalyx* Cogniaux

**Genus**: [[Tristemma]]
**Family**: [[Melastomataceae]]
**Protologue**: Mon. Phan. 7, Melast. : 1179 (1891); JACQ.-FÉL., Fl. Cam. 24 : 62, tab. 17 (1983)

## Description

Shrub trailing and rooting, or erect up to 1.20 m, glabrescent; branches slender, woody, subangular, strigillous then glabrescent. Leaves with slender, canaliculate petiole, 0.5 to 2 cm long, strigillous; blade 2–5 × 4–10 cm, ovate to ovate-lanceolate, rounded-truncate at the base and attenuate on the petiole, acute to subacuminate at the apex; bristles ± reduced to their base on both surfaces; 5 veins.

Glomerules 1–5 (often 3)-flowered, scarcely differentiated at the extremity of the branches and main stems, sessile above the last pair of normal leaves; axial bracts foliaceous; floral bracts immediately short, reaching or covering only the basal 1/3 of the flower, membranous, glabrescent on the back, ciliate. Flower small. Hypanthium 4 × 7 mm, normally glabrous; sepals triangular-oblong, 2.5 × 4 mm, glabrous or furfuraceous on the back, delicately ciliate. Petals pink or white, obovate, 5 × 9 mm. Stamens short; anther oblong, 2–3.3 mm; pedoconnective straight, 0.6–0.8 mm, anterior appendages lobed-auriculate, erect, 0.5 mm; filament 4.5 mm. Apex of ovary convex, scabrous, peristyle ridge slightly pronounced, glabrous; style 5 to 8 mm long, weakly dilated toward the top, stigma truncate.

Berry ellipsoid, 5–6 × 8–9 mm. — Pl. 15, p. 45.

## Type

Hens 13, Zaïre (holo-, G; iso-, P!, BR!)

## Discussion

Forest species widespread from Cameroon to Uganda. Well characterized by its glabrescent appearance, its pauciflorous glomerules. Prefers fresh to swampy soils.
