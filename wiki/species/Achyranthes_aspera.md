# *Achyranthes aspera* L.

**Family**: [[Amaranthaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Achyranthes]]

## Description

Variable perennial, woody below and to 3–6 m when climbing. Leaves ovate to obovate, usually 3–7 × 3–5 cm, pubescent especially beneath. Terminal spikes may reach 30 cm; mature flowers become reflexed against the axis. Five rigid pointed tepals surround five stamens alternating with fringed staminodes. Achene oblong and truncate, one-seeded.

## Distribution

Widespread through Africa, but the typical variety was explicitly listed “to be sought in Gabon”; only var. [[Achyranthes_aspera_var_porphyrostachya|*porphyrostachya*]] was documented there.
