# *Landolphia maxima* (K.Schum. ex Hallier f.) Pichon Planche 60

**Genus**: [[Landolphia]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Mém. Inst. Franç. Afrique Noire 35 : 177 (1953)

## Description

Large liana; branches glabrous. Leaf glabrous: petiole 9–42 mm; blade oval to obovate, as long as or longer than wide, 21.5–40 × 9.8–22.5 cm, attenuate to truncate at the base, acute or very shortly acuminate at the apex; secondary nerves 7 to 13 pairs, straight or curved, tertiary venation ± distinctly scalariform, main nerves impressed above. Inflorescence axillary or cauliflorous. Flower: Sepal ca. 1.5–3 mm, calyx with lobes ± as long as wide, 1.7–3.4 × 1.2–2.7 mm, often membranous at the ciliate margin, partially pilose on the outside; corolla white or cream-green with a violet base, with tube 11.5–14 mm long, 0.9–1.8 mm wide in the middle, 2–3 mm at the level of the anthers and 1.8–2.8 mm at the throat, glabrous on the outside, inside pilose between and up to ca. 2 mm below the anthers, otherwise glabrous, with lobes ± as long as wide, ± as long as the tube, 2.8–5.5 mm, partially pilose on the outside, otherwise glabrous; stamens inserted at 0.7–0.8 of the tube length, included 0.3–0.9 mm, anther 2.1–2.7 mm long, glabrous; pistil 11–12.5 mm long, ovary villous; style gradually enlarged toward the base, 8–9.2 mm, pilose at the base and glabrous near the apex. Fruit unknown. Distribution south-west of Nigeria, Cameroon, and north-west of Gabon, at Gabon known only from a sterile specimen from the environs of Libreville.

## Ecology

forest; in Gabon at low altitude, elsewhere at 0–100 m
