# *Vitex agelaeifolia* Mildbr. ex W.Piep. var. agelaeifolia Figure 5(A-C)

**Genus**: [[Vitex]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 62, Beibl. 141 (« 142 ») : 55 (1928)

## Description

Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 62, Beibl. 141 (« 142 ») : 55 (1928). V. phaseolifolia Mildbr. ex W.Piep. Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 62, Beibl . 141 ( «142 ») : 55 (1928). V. wellensii De Wild., Pl. bequaert. 5 : 19 (1929). Liana with stem reaching 5 m in length; branches strongly quadrangular, with subwinged angles, hollow (inhabited by ants), with internodes pierced by a hole at the apex. Leaf: petiole 4–15 cm, generally glabrous, generally plane on the upper surface; leaflets 3, with petiolules of (1–)5–10(–30) mm, that of the median leaflet distinctly longer than the other two, with blade broadly elliptic to ovate-elliptic or obovate-elliptic to almost circular, 6–17 × 3–10 cm, that of the median leaflet attenuate or cuneate at the base, that of the lateral leaflets obtuse to rounded or slightly cordate at the base, apex abruptly contracted into an acumen, somewhat asymmetric, entire, practically glabrous, rather coriaceous, upper surface scabrous, lower surface punctate with yellow glands, with often violaceous veins; secondary veins 4 or 5 on each side of the main vein, accompanied by shorter veins perpendicular to the main vein, reticulation prominent on both surfaces. Inflorescence formed of axillary thyrses, or sometimes grouped into a panicle at the branch extremities, 5–25 × 1–3 cm, with quadrangular glabrous to puberulent axes; each cymule with 2 to 10 flowers. Flower: pedicel 1–15 mm; calyx ± 2 mm long, cupuliform, almost truncate or with short teeth (0.5 mm), often tinged with purple; corolla twice as long as the calyx, whitish, yellowish to greenish, with yellowish glandular hairs, with geniculate or straight tube; stamens with puberulent filament; ovary glabrous. Fruit globose, ± 9 mm in diameter, orange-brown, with apiculate apex, subtended by the persistent calyx spreading.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, in Estuaire, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo, Woleu-Ntem. Conservation status: Widely distributed; probably Least Concern (LC)

## Ecology

secondary forests, forest edges, fallows; at 0–800 m altitude

## Source note

The lianescent Vitex species with thyrse inflorescence pose a taxonomic and nomenclatural problem in tropical Africa. Besides V. thyrsiflora (5 leaflets), Pieper (1928) recognizes two taxa with 3 leaflets, V. agelaeifolia and V. phaseolifolia. The latter would be distinguished by its axillary inflorescence (vs. terminal in V. agelaeifolia). In the abundant material that we examined for western tropical Africa, we observe only a single taxon with 3 leaflets, which possesses axillary inflorescences, sometimes accompanied by terminal panicles. It thus appears that V. agelaeifolia and V. phaseolifolia are synonyms. The two names were published at the same time. We did not retrieve the type of V. phaseolifolia; on the other hand, an isotype of V. agelaeifolia was found (Tessmann 289 [BM001209283]). It therefore seems preferable to retain the latter name. Var. rufula Moldenke, endemic to the Democratic Republic of the Congo, is distinguished by petioles furnished with crinkled rusty hairs.
