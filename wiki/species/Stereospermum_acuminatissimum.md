# *Stereospermum acuminatissimum* K. Schumann

**Genus**: [[Stereospermum]]
**Family**: [[Bignoniaceae]]

## Description

in ENGL. & PRANTL, Nat. Pflanzenfam. 4 (3b): 243 (1895); SPRAGUE, FTA 4 (2): 519 (1906); HEINE, FWTA, ed. 2, 2: 386 (1963); GENTRY, Fl. Cam. 27: 46, tab. 14 (1984).

Tree 6–25 (–30 according to HEINE) m tall, at least 40 cm in diameter; branchlets subcylindrical, slightly flattened at the nodes, glabrous, usually black when dry contrasting with lighter lenticels. Pseudostipules linear, about 5 mm long. Leaves with 7–21 leaflets; leaflets narrowly elliptic to oblong or narrowly ovate, acuminate, asymmetrically rounded to broadly cuneate at the base, 4–14 × 2.5 cm; entire, sparsely scaly along the midvein above, very finely scaly-punctate all over below, with conspicuous circular glands at the base; petiolules 1–2 mm long, rachis strongly angular, grooved below, articulation not prominent.

Inflorescence terminal, distinctly paniculate. Pedicels slender, 0.5–1 cm long, glabrous with 2 prominent bracteoles per bud, these 1–2 mm long and densely ciliate with multicellular hairs. Calyx tubular-campanulate, 11–14 mm long, 4–6 mm wide, bilabiate, sparsely scaly. Corolla magenta with darker lines in the throat, tubular-campanulate above a narrow basal tube, 3.5–5 cm long, 1–1.3 cm wide at the tube mouth, tube 2.5–4 cm long, lobes 0.6–1 cm long; glabrous externally except for scattered very inconspicuous circular glands, lobes clearly ciliate externally with long, crinkled, multicellular hairs; internally with flexuous multicellular hairs at the insertion of the stamens and at the base of the tube. Stamens didynamous; thecae thin, divaricate, 2 mm long. Pistil 2.2–2.4 cm long; ovary linear, strongly tetragonous, 5–9 mm long, 0.5–1 mm wide, glabrous. Disc cupular, pulvinate, 1 mm long, 1.5 mm wide.

Capsule linear, cylindrical, 41–65 cm long, 0.8–1.2 cm wide, glabrous, dark brown when dry with scattered lenticels. Seeds oblique, 0.5–1.1 cm long, 2.8–3.3 cm long, with thin hyaline membranous wings, body longitudinally folded, thick and wedge-shaped. – Pl. 10, p. 45.
