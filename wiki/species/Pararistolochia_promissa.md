# *Pararistolochia promissa* (Mast.) Keay Planche 5

**Genus**: [[Pararistolochia]]
**Family**: [[Aristolochiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Kew. Bull. 1952 : 160 (1952)

## Description

, nom. inval. Pararistolochia talbotii (S.Moore) Keay, Kew Bull. 1952 : 161 (1952). Pararistolochia tenuicauda (S.Moore) Keay, Kew Bull. 1952 : 160 (1952). Liane up to 30 m long ; stem slender, 2-lobed in transverse section. Leaf : petiole slender, 2–3.5 cm ; blade elliptic-oblong to broadly ovate, 7–15 × 3.5–9 cm, acuminate to cuneate or rounded at base, apex acuminate with an obtuse acumen 1–1.5 cm, coriaceous. Inflorescence : rachis 1–4 cm, bearing 2–3 (–5) flowers. Flower : pedicel (excluding ovary) 2.5–5 cm ; perianth with white pilosity inside, cream white or greyish to greenish or yellow, dotted with dark red to reddish-brown on the outside, utricle 1–2.5(–3) cm long, with small sub-rectangular glandular zones, tube 2.5–4(–5) cm long, lobes 3, very unequal, the 2 lateral ones more or less erect and auriculate above the tube, 4.5–8 cm long and with a ribbon-like appendage 6–20(–30) cm, lower median lobe very reduced ; gynosteme 7–12 mm long, with (8–)9 brown to yellowish stamens and (8–)9 stigmatic lobes ; ovary 9–30 mm long. Fruit cylindric, (7–)12–25 cm long, longitudinally ribbed, star-shaped in section. Seed cordate, 11 × 9 mm.

## Distribution

Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroun, République Centrafricaine, Gabon, Congo (Brazzaville) and République Démocratique du Congo ; in Gabon, widely distributed but fairly rare

## Ecology

lowland forest to montane forest, sometimes in secondarized stations ; in Gabon at 100–900 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1500 m. B : Hauman (1948), Hawthorne & Jongkind (2006), Hutchinson & Dalziel (1928), Poncy (1978)
