# *Pararistolochia promissa* (Mast.) Keay Planche 5

**Genus**: [[Pararistolochia]]
**Family**: [[Aristolochiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Kew. Bull. 1952 : 160 (1952)

## Description

Liane up to 30 m in length; stem slender, 2-lobed in transverse section. Leaf: petiole slender, 2–3.5 cm; blade elliptic-oblong to broadly ovate, 7–15 × 3.5–9 cm, acuminate to cuneate or rounded at base, apex acuminate with acumen obtuse 1–1.5 cm, coriaceous. Inflorescence: rachis 1–4 cm, bearing 2–3 (–5) flowers. Flower: pedicel (without ovary) 2.5–5 cm; perianth with white pubescence inside, cream-white or greyish to greenish or yellow, punctate with dark red to reddish-brown on outside, utricle 1–2.5(–3) cm in length, with small and sub-rectangular glandular zones, tube 2.5–4(–5) cm in length, lobes 3, very unequal, the 2 lateral ones more or less erect and auriculate above the tube, 4.5–8 cm in length and with a ribbon-like appendage 6–20(–30) cm, lower median lobe very reduced; gynostyle 7–12 mm in length, with (8–)9 brown to yellowish stamens and (8–)9 stigmatic lobes; ovary 9–30 mm in length. Fruit cylindrical, (7–)12–25 cm in length, longitudinally ribbed, star-shaped in section. Seed cordate, 11 × 9 mm.

## Distribution

Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Gabon, Congo (Brazzaville) and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, widely distributed but rather rare

## Ecology

lowland forest to montane forest, sometimes in secondarized stations; in Gabon at 100–900 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1500 m. B: Hauman (1948), Hawthorne & Jongkind (2006), Hutchinson & Dalziel (1928), Poncy (1978)
