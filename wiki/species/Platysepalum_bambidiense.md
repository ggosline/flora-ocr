# *Platysepalum bambidiense* Maesen Planche 104

**Genus**: [[Platysepalum]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Webbia 65 : 166 (2010), comme bambidiensis

## Description

'. Webbia 65 : 166 (2010), as bambidiensis Liane ; stem reaching 4 m in length ; branches 2–7 mm in diameter, with erect, brown, caducous pubescence. Stipules triangular, 3–4 × 2.5–3 mm, with enlarged base, striate with age, with brown hairs. Leaf : petiole (1–)3–6 cm ; rachis (2–)4–10 cm ; stipels setaceous, (2–)3–5 mm ; leaflets (5–)7 to 9 , with petiolule 2–4 mm, the terminal ones with obovate limb, 6–16 × 1.5–5 cm, base subcordate, apex acuminate with mucro 0–1 mm or sometimes slightly emarginate, coriaceous, with lax pubescence below, glossy green above, grey-green below , the lateral ones 3.5–13 × 1.2–4 cm ; midvein with dark brown hairs. Inflorescence in panicle reaching 25 cm in length, secondary axes reaching 6 cm , with flowers often in pairs, fairly dense on short tertiary axes, 5–10 mm, elongating with age ; bracts triangular-ovate, 1–2(–3) mm in length, striate. Flower : bracteoles broadly elliptic-ovate, 3–6 × 2–5 mm , striate, with appressed, dense, brown hairs, with clear midvein ; calyx green and purple when young, pale brown later, with whitish pubescence of long and short hairs, tube ± 6 mm in length, upper lobe broadly obovate, 15–27 × 11–30 mm , with clearly split apex, lateral lobes falcate, 10–13 × 6 mm, lower lobe lanceolate, 18 × 5 mm ; corolla white, standard broadly obovate, hooded, 24 × 25 mm , purple on the adaxial margin, base auriculate, claw curved, ± 5 mm, wings obovate, 18 × 12 mm, partly finely ribbed perpendicular to the adaxial margin, claw 3 mm, keel 18 × 12 mm, with a lateral pouch, claw ± 6 mm ; stamens white purplish , the vexillary one free, staminal tube ± 15 mm, filaments curved upward, anthers elongate, dorsifixed ; disc 1–2 mm in width, with 10 rounded lobes of ± 0.5 mm in length ; ovary ± 10 × 3 mm, with long hairs, ovules 5 ; style ± 13 mm, curved upward, pubescent to the middle. Pod (immature) oblong-elliptic , ± 4.5 × 1.2 cm, with dense golden-brown pubescence, hairs ± 0.1 mm, also loosely covered with golden-brown hairs of ± 2 mm. Seed not seen.

## Distribution

endemic to Gabon, known only from the Bambidie region, in Ogooué-Lolo

## Ecology

humid evergreen or secondary forest ; at 300 m altitude. Flowers and young fruits in January and April

## Source note

Two poorly known Congolese species and also with large flowers, P. hypoleucum Taub. and P. poggei Taub., are close to P. bambidiense , but have small bracteoles, about 2 × 2 mm. B : van der Maesen (2010). Bibliography
