# *Tarenna conferta* (Benth.) Hiern

**Genus**: [[Tarenna]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Hiern, F.T.A. 3 : 90 (1877)

## Synonyms

- Stylocoryne conferta Benth., Hook. Niger Fl. : 389 (1849).
- Tarenna Talbotii Wernham, Cat. Talb. Niger. Pl. : 44 (1913).
- Tarenna Le-Testui Pellegr., Bull. S.B. Fr. 83 : 667 (1936) syn. nov.

## Description

Shrub or small tree reaching 7–8 m in height. Young branches with fine appressed pubescence, subquadrangular in the upper part of the internodes, with reddish ochraceous epidermis becoming scaly. Stipules triangular acute, 3–4 mm long, black and shiny when dry on the outside. Petioles finely pubescent to glabrescent, 5–15 mm long. Lamina brownish when dry, rather dull in appearance on the upper side, 6–15 (18) × 2.5–7 cm, acute at base, acuminate at apex. Upper surface entirely glabrous, including the midline which appears as a narrow groove; lower surface with very sparse appressed pubescence ± scattered, more distinct on the veins. 6–11 pairs of secondary veins. Fine reticulum evident.

Inflorescences terminal multiflorous ± contracted 3–5 cm wide, with axes finely villous, generally less than 0.5–1 cm. Pedicels 1–3 mm, villous as well as the calyx, with cupuliform margin 0.5–0.7 mm tall, and with teeth briefly triangular 0.5–1 mm. Corolla twisted in bud, white, with glabrous tube 5–8 mm, with lobes 5–7 × 1.5 mm, with throat mediocrely pubescent or glabrous. Stamens with filament less than 1 mm; apiculate anther 5–7 mm. Style filiform 12–16 mm, pubescent toward the middle, with long and narrow stigmatic club. Ovary villous turbinate, 2-locular. Placentae ovoid or hemispherical bearing 6–12 ovules embedded.

Fruit glabrous, lustrous, 8 mm diam., pale-green.

## Type

Th. Vogel 23 (K!), Nun river, Sud Nigeria

## Discussion

From Guinea (Nzo) to Congo and Fernando-Po.
