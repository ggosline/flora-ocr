# *Bonamia gabonensis* Breteler Planche 2

**Genus**: [[Bonamia]]
**Family**: [[Convolvulaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Mus. Natl. Hist. Nat. , Sér. 4, Misc. 14 : 62, fig. 1 (1992)

## Description

Liane; branchlets tomentose-brownish. Leaf: petiole 3–7 mm; blade obovate-elliptic to sometimes ovate-elliptic, 8–15 × 3–6 cm, cordate at the base, acuminate over 0.5–1.5 cm and mucronate at the apex, papyraceous, glabrous above except for the midvein, sparsely tomentose below, with 7–11 pairs of lateral veins. Inflorescence cymose, axillary in the upper leaves and thyrsoid above, tomentose; bracts and bracteoles ovate to obovate-elliptic, up to 7 × 3 mm, folded longitudinally. Flower: pedicel 1.5–2 mm; sepals ± equal in length, suborbicular to broadly elliptic, 6–6.5 × 5–6 mm, concave, glabrous inside, tomentose outside; corolla narrowly campanulate, 9–10 mm in length, with golden hairs, appressed outside (glabrous at the base), lobes erect to slightly spreading, 4–5 mm in length; stamens ± as long as the corolla, filaments hairy on the part adnate to the corolla tube, anthers ellipsoid, 1.5 × 1 mm; pistil ± as long as the corolla, glabrous; styles united at the base, stigmas rounded-reniform. Fruit: shortly conical, 10–15 × 8 mm, apiculate, glabrous, with 1–3(–4) seeds. Seeds 6 × 5 mm, orange-red.

## Distribution

Republic of Congo and Gabon where it is known from a single collection from the Ngounié

## Ecology

primary or secondarized forest; in Gabon, at approximately 700 m altitude, elsewhere, around 400–500 m. B: Breteler (1992). ibliography
