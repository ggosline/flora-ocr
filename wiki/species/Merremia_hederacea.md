# *Merremia hederacea* (Burm.f.) Hallier f. Planche 21

**Genus**: [[Merremia]]
**Family**: [[Convolvulaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 18 : 118 (1893)

## Synonyms

- Evolvulus hederaceus Burm.f., Fl. Ind. : 77, t. 30, fig. 2 (1768).
- Merremia convolvulacea Hallier f., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 16 : 552 (1893).

## Description

Perennial herb with twining or prostrate stems; branchlets subcylindrical, sparsely hirsute or hirsute only at the nodes, often tuberculate. Leaf: petiole (0.5–)1–6 cm, pubescent above, often tuberculate; blade ovate, ovate to trilobed, (1.5–)2–6 × (1–)1.5–5 cm, deeply cordate at the base, obtuse to acuminate over 1–1.5 cm and mucronate at the apex, glabrous to sparsely pubescent especially on the main venation, with 6–8 pairs of lateral veins. Inflorescence in pauciflorous cymes, glabrous or with some hirsute hairs; peduncle 0.5–2 cm, up to 6 cm in fruit; bracts caducous, approximately 3 mm long. Flower: pedicel 2–5 mm; sepals elliptic to obovate, 4–6 mm long, the inner ones longer than the outer, with truncate-apiculate apex, ± glabrous; corolla subcampanulate, 6–12 mm long, yellow, glabrous except internally at the insertion of the stamen filaments; stamens 4–6 mm long, filaments flattened; pistil ± 5 mm long, glabrous; ovary ovoid, approximately 1.8 mm long. Fruit ovoid, ± 4-angled, approximately 5–6 mm in diameter, glabrous, with persistent sepals, spreading or reflexed. Seeds 4, triquetrous, 2.5–3 mm long, puberulent.

## Distribution

known from all tropical Africa, including Madagascar, Asia, Australia and the Pacific islands; in Gabon, reported from Moyen-Ogooué and Ogooué-Ivindo

## Ecology

river banks, roadsides, secondarily vegetated areas; in Gabon, up to 500 m elevation, elsewhere, up to 800 m. B: Gonçalves (1987), Heine (1963), Lejoly & Lisowski (1993), Verdcourt bibliography (1963)
