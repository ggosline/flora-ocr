# *Merremia hederacea* (Burm.f.) Hallier f. Planche 21

**Genus**: [[Merremia]]
**Family**: [[Convolvulaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 18 : 118 (1893)

## Description

Perennial herb with twining or prostrate stems; branchlets subcylindric, sparsely hirsute or hirsute only at the nodes, often tuberculate. Leaf: petiole (0.5–)1–6 cm, pubescent above, often tuberculate; blade ovate, ovate to trilobate, (1.5–)2–6 × (1–)1.5–5 cm, deeply cordate at the base, obtuse to acuminate over 1–1.5 cm and mucronate at the apex, glabrous to sparsely pubescent especially on the main venation, with 6–8 pairs of lateral veins. Inflorescence in few-flowered cymes, glabrous or with some hirsute hairs; peduncle 0.5–2 cm, up to 6 cm in fruit; bracts caducous, approximately 3 mm in length. Flower: pedicel 2–5 mm; sepals elliptic to obovate, 4–6 mm in length, the inner ones longer than the outer ones, with truncate-apiculate apex, ± glabrous; corolla subcampanulate, 6–12 mm in length, yellow, glabrous except internally at the insertion of the stamen filaments; stamens 4–6 mm in length, filaments flattened; pistil ± 5 mm in length, glabrous; ovary ovoid, approximately 1.8 mm in length. Fruit ovoid, ± 4-angular, approximately 5–6 mm in diameter, glabrous, with persistent sepals, spreading or reflexed. Seeds 4, triquetrous, 2.5–3 mm in length, puberulent.

## Distribution

known from all tropical Africa, including Madagascar, Asia, Australia and Pacific islands; in Gabon, recorded from Moyen-Ogooué and Ogooué-Ivindo

## Ecology

river banks, roadsides, secondary vegetation areas; in Gabon, up to 500 m altitude, elsewhere, up to 800 m. B: Gonçalves (1987), Heine (1963), Lejoly & Lisowski (1993), Verdcourt Bibliography (1963)
