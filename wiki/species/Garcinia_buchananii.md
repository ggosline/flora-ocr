# *Garcinia buchananii* Baker

**Genus**: [[Garcinia]]
**Family**: [[Clusiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull. 1894 : 354 (1894)

## Description

Dioecious tree, up to 15(–25) m tall; bark brownish; latex yellow; branchlets angular. Leaves opposite, glabrous; petiole (5–)10–15 mm; blade elliptic to ovate, 7–16 × 2.5–7.5 cm, cuneate to obtuse at base, long-acuminate at apex; subcoriaceous to coriaceous; venation prominent on both faces, lateral veins numerous, at insertion angle of 30–45°, secretory canals subparallel to midvein. Inflorescence axillary or terminal, in short cymes. Flowers unisexual, tetramerous; pedicel 3–6 mm; sepals suborbicular, unequal, glabrous, the inner ones 3.5–4 mm long; petals obovate, 8–10(–12) × 4–6(–10) mm, white to yellowish or orange. Male flower: disc obconical, 2 mm diameter; stamen phalanges with filaments entirely connate, broadly spathulate, 1–2 mm long, anthers 5–6 per phalange, oblong, not locellate. Female flower often solitary; stamens, if present, few, free and sterile; ovary 4-locular, 2–2.5 mm diameter, stigma sessile, obpatelliforme, 4-lobed. Fruit subglobose, 2–2.5 cm diameter, smooth, glabrous, yellow to red. Seeds 1–4, ellipsoid, 7–15 × 6–8.5 × 3–5 mm.

## Distribution

Gabon, Sudan, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Rwanda, Burundi, Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, Malawi, Zambia and Mozambique; in Gabon, known only from the Northeast (Makokou)

## Ecology

gallery forest, sclerophyll forest; in Gabon, at 500 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1800 m. Use: The fruit is edible. Bibl.: Bamps (1969, 1970a), Bamps et al. (1978), Robson (1961). Bibliography
