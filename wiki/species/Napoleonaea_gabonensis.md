# *Napoleonaea gabonensis* Liben

**Genus**: [[Napoleonaea]]
**Family**: [[Lecythidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Jard. Bot. Belg. 41 : 370 (1971)

## Description

Shrub, up to 9 m tall; branchlets glabrous, finely longitudinally striated. Leaf: petiole 3–9 mm, channelled, black; blade elliptic-oblong to elliptic-obovate, (12–)15–25(–30) × 5–11 cm, acute to obtuse at base, apex acuminate with acumen 5–18 mm long or rarely acute, underside with ± distinct glands 2–4 mm from margin; lateral veins 5–9 pairs, prominent below and obscure above, free in lower half of blade, united in arches toward apex, spaced 5–21 mm apart, tertiary veins scalariform. Inflorescence axillary or cauliflorous; bracts circular, 7–10 mm diameter, concave, emarginate at apex, with grayish pubescence. Flowers solitary or in fascicles; calyx 22–25 mm diameter, with lobes 7–12 × 6–8 mm, smooth to strongly verrucose, pubescent, with apical glands generally small; first corona 3.0–3.2 cm long; second corona microtomentulose, lobed for approximately 2/3, 1.6–2.4 cm in diameter; third corona somewhat shorter, glabrous. Fruit broadly ellipsoid, 2.2–3.2 × 1.7–2.3 cm, with persistent calyx.

## Distribution

endemic to Gabon, fairly common, observed in Estuaire and Ogooué-Ivindo (Lopé Reserve and around Booué)

## Ecology

dense humid forests, at 0–500 m altitude

## Vernacular names

alola nzak, obanniar, obanniat (Fang). Notes taxonomiques : Les feuilles de N. gabonensis diffèrent de celles de N. imperialis par la présence d’un réseau de nervures latérales libres sur la moitié inférieure du limbe et réunies en arceaux vers le sommet. La présence de cette espèce au Cameroun, mentionnée par Liben (1971), était basée sur une erreur de localité. B : Liben (1971). i B liographie
