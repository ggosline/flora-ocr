# *Tribu des* Cryptocaryées

**Genus**: [[Tribu]]
**Family**: [[Lauraceae]]

## Synonyms

- Nees von Esenbeck, Syst. Laurin : 191 (1836).

## Description

Of the twenty-five genera cited by Lemée, only one is known from Gabon: Beilschmiedia. The African genus most closely related to Beilschmiedia is characterized by fruit completely covered at maturity by the accrescent calyx; hence its name: Cryptocarya (literally, hidden nut), the type of the tribe. By contrast, the calyx is caducous in Beilschmiedia and the fruit remains naked. Since many Gabonese plants are known only from flowering specimens but not fruiting ones, the attribution to one or the other genus might seem doubtful. Fortunately, in the Malagasy and East African species of Cryptocarya, the floral pedicels bear three small tiered bracteoles, whereas all African Beilschmiedia have naked pedicels, the bracteoles occurring only at the insertion of the main branches of the inflorescence. However, the presence or absence of bracteoles on the pedicels is not a valid generic character outside Africa, because Beilschmiedia Roxburghiana Nees, from Bengal, the precise type of the genus, bears a large bracteole at mid-height of the floral pedicels. So far, no Cryptocarya has been recorded from either Gabon or Congo; the range of the genus would extend only to East Africa and southern Africa.

Trees or shrubs; leaves alternate or sub-opposite, more or less coriaceous, penninerved; inflorescences axillary in racemes or panicles of cymes; bracteoles more or less caducous. Flowers hermaphrodite with a briefly tubular perianth of 6 (rarely 8) sub-equal segments (tepals) finally caducous. Androecium with 4 cycles or whorls: the first two on the outside of 3 stamens each with introrse anthers, the 3rd cycle with extrorse or lateral anthers, sometimes sterile; the 4th cycle reduced to staminodes, but not absent. Ovary sessile, free, but more or less immersed in the receptacle, 1-locular, uniovulate, attenuate into a style; drupe almost dry, of variable shape (spherical, ovoid, pyriform), naked or surrounded by the persistent base of the calyx.

Type species: B. Roxburghiana Nees, from Bengal.

In agreement with the work of Robyns and Wilczek, the following former genera are given as synonyms or designate sections: Hufelandia (1) Nees (Plant. Laurin. sec. affin. natur. expos.: 11-21, 1833); Nesodaphne Hook. f. (Flor. Nov. Zeland. I : 217, 1855); Tylostemon Engler (Bot. Jahrb. 26 : 389, 1899); Afro-daphne Stapf. (Journ. Linn. Soc. Lond. bot. 37 : 110, 1905).

The genus Beilschmiedia thus understood comprises approximately 200 species from the intertropical regions of the entire world, of which 80 are from Africa and Madagascar. The divisions of the genus are given after Robyns and Wilczek with some minor modifications.
