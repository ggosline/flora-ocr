# *Tribu des* Cryptocaryées

**Genus**: [[Tribu]]
**Family**: [[Lauraceae]]

## Description

— Nees von Esenbeck, Syst. Laurin : 191 (1836).

Of the twenty-five genera cited by Lemée, only one is known from Gabon: Beilschmiedia. The African genus most closely related to the genus Beilschmiedia is characterized by the fruit completely covered at maturity by the accrescent calyx; hence its name: Cryptocarya (literally, hidden nut) type of the tribe. In contrast, the calyx is caducous in Beilschmiedia and the fruit remains naked. Since many Gabonese plants are known only from flowering specimens but not fruiting ones, the attribution to one or the other genus might seem doubtful. Fortunately, in the Malagasy and East African species of Cryptocarya, the floral pedicels bear three small tiered bracteoles whereas all African Beilschmiedia have naked pedicels, the bracteoles being found only at the insertion of the main branches of the inflorescence. However, the presence or absence of bracteoles on the pedicels is not a valid generic character outside Africa because Beilschmiedia Roxburghiana Nees, from Bengal, precisely the type of the genus, bears a large bracteole at mid-height of the floral pedicels. Up to now, no Cryptocarya has been reported either from Gabon or from Congo; the range of the genus would not extend beyond East Africa and South Africa.

2. BEILSCHMIEDIA $^{1}$ Nees von Esenbeck

— Wallich, Plantae asiaticae rariorae, 2: 669 (1831). — Robyns et Wilczek, Bull. Jard. Bot. Brux.: 459 (1949): 197 (1950).

(1) Genus dedicated to C. T. Beilschmied, botanist of Breslau (Silesia), friend of Nees.

(Le T 2038) stam. 1 mm; pist. 2 mm. — Vertic. : 1,1',1'', stamens of the 1<sup>st</sup> cycle, inner face, profile, outer face; 2, stamens of the 2<sup>nd</sup> cycle, inner face; 3,3',3'', stamens of the 3<sup>rd</sup> cycle, inner face, profile and outer face; 4,4',4'', staminodes of the 4<sup>th</sup> cycle, same views; 5, pist. — Note: in d, e, f, h anthers closed; in 3 and 3', one gland is cut longitudinally. 1 to 4, same scale.

Trees or shrubs; leaves alternate or sub-opposite, more or less coriaceous, penninerved; inflorescences axillary in racemes or panicles of cymes; bracteoles more or less caducous. Flowers hermaphrodite with perianth briefly tubular with 6 (rarely 8) sub-equal segments (tepals) eventually caducous. Androecium with 4 cycles or whorls: the first two on the outside each of 3 stamens with introrse anthers, the 3rd cycle with extrorse or lateral anthers, sometimes sterile; the 4th cycle reduced to the state of staminodes, but not absent. Ovary sessile, free, but more or less sunken into the receptacle, with 1 locule, uniovulate, attenuated into a style; drupe almost dry, of variable shape (spherical, ovoid, pyriform) naked or surrounded at the base by the persistent calyx.

Type species: B. Roxburghiana Nees, from Bengal.

In accordance with the work of Robyns and Wilczek, the former genera are given as synonyms or designate sections: Hufelandia (1) Nees (Plant. Laurin. sec. affin. natur. expos.: 11-21, 1833); Nesodaphne Hook. f. (Flor. Nov. Zeland. I : 217, 1855); Tylostemon Engler (Bot. Jahrb. 26 : 389, 1899); Afro-daphne Stapf. (Journ. Linn. Soc. Lond. bot. 37 : 110, 1905).

The genus Beilschmiedia thus understood comprises about 200 species from the intertropical regions of the entire world, of which 80 for Africa and Madagascar. The divisions of the genus are given after Robyns and Wilczek with some minor adjustments.
