# *Begonia thomeana* C.DC. Planche 1

**Genus**: [[Begonia]]
**Family**: [[Begoniaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bol. Soc. Brot. 10 : 123 (1893)

## Description

Plant with climbing or prostrate stem, reaching 10 m in length and 1–4 mm in diameter, branched, covered with short adventitious roots on the substrate side. Stipules persistent, triangular, 6–11 × 2–6 mm. Leaf: petiole 2–9(–10) cm; blade ovate to broadly ovate, 2–11 × 1.5–6 cm, deeply cordate or truncate at base, acuminate at apex, margins dentate, puberulent, 5–8-palmatinerved. Inflorescence erect, of 3–4 ♂ flowers and 2–3 ♀ flowers; peduncle 3–6.5 cm; bracts 3–9 × 2–6 mm; first branches 0.5–1.5 cm long with smaller bracts. ♂ Flower: pedicel 3.5–13 mm; tepals very broadly ovate, cordate at base, 12–20 mm wide; stamens 10–16, filaments unequal, 1.5–3.5 mm, anthers 2–3.5 mm long. ♀ Flower: pedicel 5–25 mm, strongly accrescent at fruiting; tepals similar to those of ♂ flowers; ovary trigonous, ellipsoid, 15 × 7 mm (without wings), furnished with 3 unequal wings; styles fused at base, divided twice, stigmas lunulate (see plate 1). Infructescence reaching 15 cm. Fruit obovoid, 30 × 35 mm (wings included). Seed narrowly rectangular, 0.9–1.3 × 0.2–0.3 mm, furnished with an appendage at both ends (see plate 1).

## Distribution

Gabon (Chaillu Massif, rare) and São Tomé

sect. FILICIBEGONIA A.DC. Prodr. 15, 1: 392 (1864). Terrestrial, herbaceous, perennial plants; stem erect, 10–60(–100) cm in height. Leaves not peltate, simple, pinnate or bipinnate; venation generally pinnate. Inflorescence axillary, bisexual, rarely unisexual, in unipara cyme, the branches strongly reduced always terminating in a ♂ flower, finally with 1(–2) ♀ flowers. Flowers with 2 white or pink tepals. ♂ Flower: androecium zygomorphic, stamens with unequal filaments, fused at their base. ♀ Flower: ovary ellipsoid to broadly obovoid, 3(–4)-winged, 3(–4)-locular; placentas central, entire; styles 3(–4), fused at base, divided ± distinctly into horseshoe shape at apex, stigmas in semi-ring (lunulate) or sometimes reduced and capitate. Infructescence sessile or pedunculate. Fruit: capsular, solitary, pendulous, pedunculate, persistently long, with 3(–4) equal or subequal wings; styles caducous. Section distributed in West and Central Africa, from Guinea to the east of the Democratic Republic of the Congo and northern Angola, comprising 8 species of which 7 in Gabon, including 3 endemic species.
