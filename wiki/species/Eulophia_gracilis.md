# *Eulophia gracilis* Lindl.

**Genus**: [[Eulophia]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Reg. 9 : tab. 742 (1823)

## Synonyms

- Eulophia preusii Kraenzl., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 17 : 54 (1893).
- Eulophia laurentiana Kraenzl. in Durand & De Wild., Bull. Soc. Roy. Bot. Belg. 38 : 60 (1899).

## Description

Leaves < 6, 20–30 × 0.7–2.5 cm. Inflorescence lax, < 150 cm, 15–20-flowered. Sepals and petals greenish, yellowish, violet-bronze green, labellum white, pale green, spur yellowish. Dorsal sepal oblong-rhombiform to elliptic-lanceolate. Petals obliquely obovate, acute. Lateral sepals obliquely oblong-ovate, acuminate. Labellum 6–7 × 5–7 mm, transversely truncate, densely covered with long fleshy papillae on the apical margin, thick crest extending from the base to the apex. Spur 3.5–8 mm, claviform, obtuse. – Pl. 145, p. 327.

## Distribution

Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroun, Gabon, Zaïre

## Ecology

Terrestrial in dense humid forest, forest gallery, riparian forest, Hevea plantation
