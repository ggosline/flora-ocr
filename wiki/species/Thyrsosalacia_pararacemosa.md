# *Thyrsosalacia pararacemosa* Hallé, sp. nov.

**Genus**: [[Thyrsosalacia]]
**Family**: [[Celastraceae]]

## Description

Highly allied to Th. racemosa (Loes. ex Harms) Hallé, but differing by a double disc, the upper one not patelliform (nor with rimose margin as in Th. vicifloram Hallé), but disciform, the lower one slightly sinuous and subcupuliform.

## Type

Jacques-Félix 2529, Cameroun (holo-, P !)

## Discussion

Shrub 0.5–1.5 m ± sarmentose, or climbing vine reaching 6 m in height, without rubber, with pleated ± fissured bark, with rather opposite branches. Petiole 5–12 mm. Leaf blade 8–23 × 3–9.5, olivaceous when dry, paler beneath, cuneate or sometimes obtuse at base, elliptic or obovate, acuminate at apex. 7–12 pairs of secondary veins.

Flowering branches ± divided, usually collected in understory; the old parts marked by very short internodes; terminal or axillary extremities in racemes with rachis bearing small acute alternate bracts, often inserted as observed when dry, on prominent ridges; lateral flowers, exactly uniflorous cymes, borne on an axis 1.2–2.5 cm long, the peduncle being furnished, beneath the pedicel, with 2 minute sterile opposite or alternate bracteoles. Bud globose, 5–6 mm in diameter. Flower rotate, waxy white, yellow or pale green, 15–22 mm in diameter. Calyx with rounded, green, markedly unequal sepals, 2 to 4 mm wide, finely fringed. Corolla with suborbicular petals 7 to 9.5 mm long, fringed margin, with very numerous fine flabellate veins. Double disc: the lower one larger, diameter 4.5–6.5 mm, somewhat sinuous and subcupuliform, orange and thinner; the upper one disciform, smaller, diameter 3.5–5.5 mm, somewhat thicker, reddish brown. Stamens with filaments 4–5 mm long, narrow and spaced, bending outward without coiling; anther as long as wide, approximately 1.5 mm in the bud, the two locules arranged at an acute angle; pollen yellow orange. Ovary pyramidal

attenuate to ± spreading stigma. 2 superposed ovules per locule, the lower descending, the upper ascending.

Juvenile fruit cucumiform acute; mature fruit ellipsoid, 30 × 22 mm, subverrucose, subtricosate toward the base, sometimes acuminate at apex, containing a seed approximately 20 × 15 mm. - Pl. 7, p. 25.

Understory plant apparently spreading chiefly vegetatively and thus forming patches ± dense and remaining very localized.

The species preceding is probably conspecific and appears to be identical in all respects except for the disc. According to LOESENER, whose diagnosis was published by HARMS, the species would have a simple and patelliform disc. Pending a new analysis of the reference materials, we prefer to retain the two species in a genus which appears to be full of surprises, and in view of the importance of such a character.
