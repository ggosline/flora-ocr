# *Coleus esculentus* (N.E.Br.) G.Taylor

**Genus**: [[Coleus]]
**Protologue**: J. Bot. 69 (suppl. 2) : 158 (1931)

## Synonyms

- Plectranthus esculentus N.E.Br., Bull. Misc. Inform, Kew 1894 : 12 (1894).

## Description

Perennial herb, sometimes woody at base, 0.6–2 m tall, aromatic, with a single stem, developing from a small tuberous and digitate rhizome; stem ascending, leafless at flowering, with wrinkled coriaceous epidermis, pubescent, sometimes densely so, with short hairs directed downward and longer hairs on the nodes, inflorescence axis with short spreading pale hairs and pale, brownish or reddish sessile glands, very rarely with small axillary bulbils. Leaves opposite or ternate, ascending; petiole 0–2 mm; blade elliptic, 3–8 × 1–3 cm, cuneate

Inflorescence axillary, loose to condensed, branched or not; cymes with 1 flower, distant (1–)2–10 mm; bracts oval to obovate, slightly hooded, 1–5 mm long. Flower: pedicel (2–)3–19 mm; calyx tubular, 4–5 mm long, and 6–9 mm in fruit, pubescent with pale brown or reddish sessile glands, upper lip obovate to elliptic, mucronate to obtuse at the apex, slightly curved upward, decurrent, lobes of the lower lip narrowly triangular, the medians longer than the laterals, curved upward with the sinus separating the two median lobes similar in shape to that separating the lateral and median lobes; corolla 10–18 mm long, yellow, tube slightly sigmoid, 6–10 mm long, upper lip generally shorter than the lower horizontal lip and 4–8 mm long; stamens with free filaments. Fruit: nutlets broadly ovate, slightly flattened, 1–1.5 mm long, brown with darker spots.

## Distribution

Tropical and southern Africa including: Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Central African Republic and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon: Haut-Ogooué. Conservation status: Widely distributed and cultivated; probably Least Concern (LC)

## Ecology

cultivated in Gabon. It is also found in grasslands, open forests of Isoberlinia, Brachystegia or Uapaca; in Gabon at ± 400 m altitude, at 800–2200 m elsewhere
