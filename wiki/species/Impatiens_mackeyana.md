# *Impatiens mackeyana* Hook. f.

**Family**: [[Balsaminaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Impatiens]]

## Description

Gabonese endemic herb with alternate narrow lanceolate leaves and axillary purple flowers. Lip deep and flared, wings broad and large; spur sinuous rather than sharply bent. Lateral sepals entire.

## Distribution

Monts de Cristal, Gabon.
