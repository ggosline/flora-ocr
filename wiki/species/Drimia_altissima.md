# *Drimia altissima* (L.f.) Ker Gawl. Planche 6

**Genus**: [[Drimia]]
**Family**: [[Hyacinthaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Mag . 27 : t. 1074 (1808)

## Synonyms

- Ornithogalum altissimum L.f.., Suppl. pl . : 199 (1782).
- Urginea altissima (L.f.) Baker, J. Linn. Soc ., Bot. 13 : 221 (1873).

## Description

Plant usually robust, (35–)50–150(–250) cm tall, generally flowers before leaves. Leaves (3–)5–10(–16) arising from the bulb; blade lanceolate, 20–50 × 2–7.5 cm, margins entire. Inflorescence: erect; raceme 30–60(–80) cm, ± dense, with 80–300(–700) flowers; floral scape 0.5–1(–2) cm in diameter; bracts lanceolate, 5–8(–14) mm long, the basal ones with a spur 1–3 mm long. Flower with pedicel spreading (0.5–)1–1.5(–3) cm, glabrous; tepals free or fused for 2 mm, spreading to revolute, oblong, 5–11(–15) mm long, white to pale green without or with a green or purple vein; filaments linear, free or attached to the tepals, white, free portion 4–7 mm long, anthers dorsifixed, pale yellow when fresh, 1–3 mm long; ovary turbinate to ovoid, 3–4 × 1.5–2 mm; style as long as the ovary. Fruit subglobose, 8–15 mm in diameter, sometimes with an emar­ginate apex. Seeds ovoid, asymmetric, 5–8 mm long.

## Distribution

widely distributed in tropical Africa, from Senegal to Kenya and South Africa. In Gabon, observed in the province of Haut Ogooué (southeast of Lékoni and in Plateaux Batéké) and in Nyanga at Tchibanga

## Ecology

savannas and open forests, dry or wet places, on sandy, clayey or rocky soils; in Gabon up to 600 m altitude, elsewhere up to 2100 m. B: Jessop (1977), Oyewole (1987), Stedje (1987). i Bibliography
