# *Ficus elasticoides* De Wildeman

**Genus**: [[Ficus]]
**Family**: [[Moraceae]]
**Protologue**: Repert. Sp. Nov. 12: 302 (1913); HUTCH. in HUTCH. & RENDLE, FTA 6 (2): 214 (1917); LEBRUN & BOUTIQUE in HAUMAN, FCB 1: 170 (1948); KEAY, FWTA, ed. 2, 1: 610 (1958); AUBRÉV., FFCI, ed. 2, 1: 92, tab. 21, fig. C (1959)

## Description

HUTCH. in HUTCH. & RENDLE, FTA 6 (2): 214 (1917); LEBRUN & BOUTIQUE in HAUMAN, FCB 1: 170 (1948); KEAY, FWTA, ed. 2, 1: 610 (1958); AUBRÉV., FFCI, ed. 2, 1: 92, tab. 21, fig. C (1959).

Trees epiphytic or terrestrial, reaching 40 m (according to collection). Leafy branchlets 2–5 mm thick, very minutely puberulent, light to dark brown when dry. Leaves arranged in spirals. Blade oblong to elliptic, 6.5–15 cm long, 2.5–8.5 cm wide, widest at or above the middle, coriaceous, apex shortly and ± clearly acuminate (to subacute), base acute to subobtuse, margin entire; both faces glabrous; midrib plane to coarsely concave above, other veins coarsely prominent; veins prominent below, especially the midrib; (12–) 15–20 pairs of primary lateral veins, often difficult to distinguish from the numerous secondary lateral veins, lateral veins looped 2–3 mm from the margin, no distinct basal vein; glandular spot not visible. Petiole 1.5–6 cm long, approximately 2–3 mm thick, glabrous, brown-red to purplish or blackish when dry. Stipules free, 1–1.5 cm long, glabrous, caducous.

Figs normally in pairs in the leaf axils; peduncle 0.5–1 cm long, 1.5–2 mm thick, very minutely puberulent; basal bracts 2 (–3), semicircular to broadly ovate, 3–7 mm long, 4–8 mm wide, basally connate, rather densely puberulent, persistent or the most numerous upper parts caducous; receptacle globose, approximately 1.5 cm in diameter, almost glabrous, ostiole prominent, normally bilabiate, occasionally trilabiate.

Pistillate flowers with 3 tepals 1.7–2.2 mm long, free, subacute in seed flowers, obtuse to subacute in gall flowers; seed flowers sessile, style 2–3 mm long. Fruits oblongoid to subovoid or ellipsoid to ovate, 1.5–2.2 mm long; gall fruits oblongoid to subovoid, 2–2.5 mm long; fruits bicolorous, the seed fruits more distinctly so as well as the gall fruits. Staminate flowers with 3 tepals 1.2–1.5 mm long, obtuse, free, filament approximately 0.3–0.4 mm long, anther 0.8–1 mm long. Interfloral bracts 2–2.5 mm long. – Pl. 55, p. 189.

Pollinator: Elisabethiella sp. (Gabon).

## Type

Sapin s.n., Zaïre, Illongonga (BR)

## Discussion

The species is known from a few collections made in Guinea-Bissau, Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroon, Gabon, Central African Republic, Zaire and Angola.

## Specimens examined

Michaloud 536, Makokou (stér.); 1022, 80 km sur route Makokou-Mekambo (stér.).
