# *Cleistanthus ngounyensis* Pellegr.

**Genus**: [[Cleistanthus]]
**Family**: [[Phyllanthaceae]]
**Protologue**: Mém. Soc. Linn. Normandie, Bot. 1(2) : 61, pl. 4 (1928)

## Description

Mém. Soc. Linn. Normandie, Bot. 1(2): 61, pl. 4 (1928). Tree 14–30 m tall; trunk up to 60 cm in diameter; branchlets with small appressed hairs, early glabrescent. Stipules early caducous, not seen. Leaf: petiole (3–)4–6(–7) mm, pubescent, early glabrescent; blade elliptic to ovate-lanceolate, 7–14(–19) × 2–4.5(–6) cm, rounded to subcordate at base, apex gradually acute to slightly acuminate over 0.5–2 cm, coriaceous, glabrous above, generally with small scattered appressed hairs below (with magnification ≥ 20×!); lateral veins 5–8 pairs. Inflorescence unisexual (dioecious species?), pauci- to multiflorous, racemiform, up to 15 cm long, pubescent, bearing fascicles with ≤ 12 flowers; bracts thin, early caducous, ± triangular, 2–4 × 2 mm; bracteoles very small, ≤ 1 mm long. Flower 5-merous; pedicel 5–8 mm; sepals ovate-elliptic, 5 × 2 mm, sparsely pubescent on the outside; petals narrowly elliptic to oblanceolate, 1–2 mm long, glabrous or with a few appressed hairs. Male flower: stamens 5, anthers 1.5–2 mm long; androgynophore distinct, 2–3 mm; disk slightly lobed, villous. Female flower: disk about 1 mm tall, villous; pistil 4 mm long; ovary trilobed, 2 mm long, villous; styles ± free, bilobed at apex. Fruit obovoid, 3-lobed, 11 × 13 mm, pubescent. Seeds unknown.

## Distribution

endemic to Gabon, known from Estuaire (Cristal Mountains) and Ngounié (Chaillu Massif)

## Ecology

primary and secondarized forest; at 500–700 m altitude. b: Breteler (2011b). Bibliography
