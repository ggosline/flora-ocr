# *Scleria vogelii* C.B.Clarke Planche 142, Figure 30

**Genus**: [[Scleria]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]

## Description

in Dyer, Fl. trop. Afr. 8 : 508 (1902). Robust, perennial herb with a branched rhizome; stem 1–2.5 m × 3–7 mm, glabrous or scabrous. Leaf: sheath with sharply-winged margins densely furnished with retrorse teeth; collar with a triangular ligule with thickened margin; blade up to 60 cm long and 1–3 cm wide, pleated, scabrous on the margin and the nerves. Inflorescence confined to the upper part of the plant, formed of a terminal panicle and 3–5 lateral ones, all subtended by foliaceous bracts; peduncle generally solitary in each sheath, erect, pubescent. Male spikelet 4–6 mm long, with light brown to reddish glumes and finely hairy especially below and on the margin. Female spikelet 7–9 mm long, with straw-coloured or pale brown glumes with streaks or dark reddish-brown spots and finely hairy especially on the margin and near the central nerve. Achene globular or slightly compressed, 2.5–3.3 mm wide, smooth, glabrous, ivory to grey at maturity; base of style somewhat thickened and usually persisting as a dark reddish-brown appendage,

## Distribution

Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon and Gabon; fairly widespread in Gabon, found in Estuaire, Ngounié, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem (9 collections)

## Ecology

swampy forest and near streams, in permanently humid terrain and usually partially shaded; in Gabon, at 0–500 m altitude. Taxonomic note: This species is not clearly separated from S. racemosa. It differs from it by its smaller achenes and should perhaps be considered as a variety or subspecies of S. racemosa or of S. verrucosa. Intermediate forms exist in Gabon (e.g. Mann 1023). b: Hooper & Napper (1972), Piérart (1953). Bibliography
