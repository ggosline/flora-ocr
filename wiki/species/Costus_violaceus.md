# *Costus violaceus* J. Koechlin
**Family in Vol. 9**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Current placement**: Costaceae · **Genus**: [[Costus]]
## Description
Erect herb with spiralled stem, narrow lanceolate leaves c. 20–25 × 4.5 cm and a thin tubular ligule 1.5–2 cm long. The terminal dense spike is c. 6 × 3 cm; each dark maroon-violet bract subtends one flower. Calyx violet, corolla yellowish white becoming pink apically, and rounded wavy-edged labellum pinkish white with a yellow throat.
## Distribution
Known from the Gabon type, N. Hallé 1543, Port-Gentil. Koechlin distinguished the new species from *C. edulis* and *C. littoralis* by its longer inflorescence and especially the colour of bracts and calyces.
