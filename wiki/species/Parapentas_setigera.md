# *Parapentas setigera* (Hiern) Verde.

**Genus**: [[Parapentas]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Protologue**: B. Verdcourt, Bull. J.B. Brux. 23 : 57 (1953)

## Synonyms

- Virecta setigera Hiern, F.T.A. 3 : 48 (1877).
- Parapentas gabonica Bremek., l.c.: 54 (1952), type Schwébisch et Thollon 348 (P!).

## Description

Herb ± creeping, rooted at nodes and internodes, ± branched. Stems reaching 0.50–1 m, pubescent, green, reddish or violaceous; young extremities hairy with tawny or ochraceus hairs when dry. Internodes 2–11 cm. Stipules with 3(5) filiform lobes less than 2 mm. Petiole 1–6(10) mm, pubescent. Limb glabrous or with scattered pubescence above, 6–40 × 3–21 mm, ovate with cuneiform base, with ± briefly lanceolate apex. Midvein pubescent to glabrous above. 4–7 pairs of oblique secondary nerves, pubescent on lower surface.

Capitoid pluriflorous inflorescences 4–10 mm diam. with staggered flowering. Buds narrowly obtuse. Calyx with (4) 5 unequal pubescent sublinear or ± ovate lobes, 1.5–4 mm long. Corolla white, pale green at base of tube, 9 mm long, 6 mm diam. at anthesis, ± pubescent externally, moderately pubescent in the tube which widens toward the throat; (4) 5 excurved lobes. ## Discussion

In forest, humid trails, muddy patches, marshy raphial areas, river margins; on ground or on rotten wood. Grows outside the darkest places, sometimes in patches or as carpets.

Material studied for Gabon (and Muni):

Guiral: s.n., Haut-Benito, 60–120 km from the sea. N. Hallé: 621, Nkoulounga (fl. June); 1091, Makokou (fl. Feb.); 1158, Zoolendé (fl. Feb.); 1355, Mboundou (fl. Feb.); 2659, km 15 SW Makokou (fl. Oct.). N. Hallé and G. Cours: 6053, Moumba, 750–800 m (fl. May). Thollon (and Schwébisch): 348, Ogooué (fl. Dec.).
