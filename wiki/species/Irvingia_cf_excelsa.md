# *Irvingia* cf. *excelsa* Mildbr.

**Family**: [[Irvingiaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Irvingia]]

## Description

Very large tree locally distinguished from *I. gabonensis*. Leaves are not separable from that species but are borne on distinctly thicker branchlets. Fruit larger, about 8 × 6.5 cm; pulp reportedly inedible, while the seeds are roasted and used for odika bread.

## Distribution and notes

Known in Gabon from the Monts de Cristal and compared with Mildbraed’s poorly described *I. excelsa* of southern Cameroon, which shares the Fang name *eweweh*. Aubréville considered it probably a distinct variety or species but retained the account as *I.* sp. cf. *excelsa* pending better material.

## Specimens examined

**Gabon** — Normand 354 (Monts de Cristal near Atogfina); Le Testu 9156 (Ndoung-olo), 9151 (Ebé).

## Vernacular names

*evengven*, *evewe*, *essou* (Fang); *muvayu* (Punu).

## Source

Aubréville A. (1962). Irvingiaceae. In *Flore du Gabon* 3: 27–28.
