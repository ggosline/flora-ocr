## Description

Shrub 2 to 5 m tall, 4–5 cm in diameter; branches glabrous, branchlets puberulent to glabrescent. Leaves with petiole 6–12 mm long, puberulent to glabrous; blade papyraceous, elliptic-oblong to obovate-oblong, 7–30 cm long, 3–12 cm wide, rounded to obtuse at base, attenuate or acute-acuminate at apex, appressed-puberulent to glabrescent above, puberulent to glabrous below. Midvein impressed on upper surface, prominent on lower surface. Lateral veins 12–17 pairs, oblique-ascending.

Flowers green becoming pale yellow, rarely solitary, usually 2–4 fascicled, extra-axillary or on leafless branches; pedicel 2–5.5 cm long, slightly enlarged toward apex, appressed-puberulent to glabrous, with a small bracteole at base. Sepals small, triangular, acute, approximately 1.5 mm long, 2 mm wide, puberulent on outside. Outer petals fleshy, thick, lanceolate, 10–30 mm long, 4–8 mm wide, enlarged and concave at base, puberulent to glabrescent on both faces; inner petals not contiguous at base, triangular-ovate, 8–10 mm long, 4–6 mm wide, acute and very thick at apex, puberulent on outside, concave and pubescent on inner base. Stamens numerous, subsessile, oblong, approximately 2 mm long, locules linear extrorse, transversely locellate, connectives ovate, developed above anthers. Carpels numerous, approximately 2.5 mm long, ovaries cylindric curved, pubescent, styles narrowly cylindric, approximately 1 mm long, stigmas scarcely enlarged, glabrous.

Fruits 2–4 cm long, puberulent, with thick pedicel; mericarps numerous, stipitate, ellipsoid to ovoid, 9–15 mm long, 5–9 mm wide, mucronate at apex; pericarp granulate, puberulent to glabrous; stipes slender, 1.5–3 cm long, articulated at base of mericarp. Seeds ellipsoid, 8–14 mm long, 4–8 mm wide.

## Discussion

Apart from the number of flowers and their position there are no differences between the original description of N. myristicifolia and N. pluriflora. However, we have noted that in either species, flowers could be solitary or geminate and that the position of the flowers of N. pluriflora, extra-axillary, was often supra-axillary, scarcely above leaf insertion, which could have been considered as axillary. We believe this is a single species whose distribution range is continuous from southern Nigeria to the Central African Republic and southward to Congo-Kinshasa.

It is a dense forest shrub, found especially in forest galleries and in swampy scrubland.
