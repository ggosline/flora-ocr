# *Cyperus brunneoalatus* (Cherm.) Lye

**Genus**: [[Cyperus]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Nordic J. Bot . 30 : (2012)

## Synonyms

- Kyllinga brunneoalata Cherm., Bull. Soc. Bot. France 83 : 492 (1936).

## Description

Perennial herb, with a horizontal rhizome of 10+ cm × 3‒4 mm and numerous stems; stems of 40‒90 cm × 1‒2 mm (wider at the sheath level), trigonous, smooth, the basal part only slightly thickened, leafy in the lower part. Leaf: lower sheaths with blades 1‒3 cm long, the upper ones with blades of 10‒30 cm × 2‒3 mm, plane with scabrous margin. Inflorescence in a globose, dense head 6‒10 mm wide; involucral bracts 3‒4, usually spreading or reflexed, the lower one 10‒20 cm long. Spikelet 4‒5 mm long, flattened, 1‒2-flowered, but generally producing only a single achene; glumes pale reddish with a salient brownish winged central nerve, slightly and shortly ciliate; lower fertile glume 3.5‒4 mm long, the upper one 4‒4.5 mm. Flower with 3 stamens with obtuse anthers; style with 2 long branches. Achene lenticular, 1.6‒1.8 mm long, brown, finely papillose.

## Distribution

endemic to Gabon, very rare or poorly known and found only in Woleu-Ntem (Abam & Alen)

## Ecology

on rocky outcrops, probably in seasonally wet shallow soils; at 400‒700 m elevation

Taxonomic note: differs from C. cristatus (Kunth) Mattf. & Kük. (Kyllinga alba Nees) by its long creeping rhizomes, its longer stems, its smaller heads, its reddish glumes and its central nerve with brown wing and less scabrous. B: Chermezon (1936). ibliography
