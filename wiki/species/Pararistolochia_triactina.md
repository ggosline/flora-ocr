# *Pararistolochia triactina* (Hook.f.) Hutch. & Dalziel Planche 6

**Genus**: [[Pararistolochia]]
**Family**: [[Aristolochiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Kew. Bull. 1928 : 24 (1928)

## Description

Pararistolochia schweinfurthii (Engl.) Hutch. & Dalziel, Kew Bull. 1928 : 24 (1928). Large liana; stem up to 3 cm in diameter, flattened or 2-lobed. Leaf: petiole 4–7 cm; blade ± broadly ovate and with undulate margins to 3- or 5-lobed, sometimes hastate, (8–)12–20 × (6–)10–17 cm, deeply cordate at the base, apex acute to acuminate, acumen up to 2 cm, coriaceous; principal veins prominent beneath. Inflorescence: rachis short and robust, up to 1.5(–5) cm, bearing (1–)2–3 flowers. Flower: pedicel (excluding the ovary) 2–4 cm; perianth pubescent within, yellowish or greenish-white becoming pinkish or pale red-purple, spotted with dark brown or purple, the lobes darker, utricle 2–4 cm long, ± globose but wider towards the apex, with 2 lateral reniform glandular zones, tube 3–7 cm, curved, lobes 3, equal, erect-patent to spreading, triangular-lanceolate, (2–)4–7 cm long, more or less conduplicate, their apex acute and bearing a tuft of white hairs or with long erect hairs in the upper half; gynosteme 9–12 mm long, with 9 stamens and 9 stigmatic lobes with elongate tapering appendages 3 × 1 mm; ovary 8–25 mm long. Fruit elongate, 20–35 × 2.5–4 cm, with marked longitudinal ribs. Seed ovate, 12 × 9 mm.

## Distribution

from Côte d'Ivoire, Benin, Nigeria, Cameroon, Chad and Sudan to Democratic Republic of the Congo, Uganda and Angola; in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Ngounié, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo and Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology

swampy or periodically inundated lowland forest, but sometimes also in drier forests and even in savanna; in Gabon at 5–750 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1200 m

Taxonomic note: In 2009, a specimen collected on the Mayombe hills bears flowers with a smaller utricle and narrower tube and lobes. It is not yet clear whether this specimen represents a new taxon or an exception within P. triactina; we therefore await more material to decide.
