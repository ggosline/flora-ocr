# *Pararistolochia triactina* (Hook.f.) Hutch. & Dalziel Planche 6

**Genus**: [[Pararistolochia]]
**Family**: [[Aristolochiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Kew. Bull. 1928 : 24 (1928)

## Synonyms

- Aristolochia triactina Hook.f., Trans. Linn. Soc. London 25 : 186 (1865).
- Aristolochia schweinfurthii Engl., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 24 : 492 (1898).
- Pararistolochia schweinfurthii (Engl.) Hutch. & Dalziel, Kew Bull. 1928 : 24 (1928).

## Description

Large liane; stem up to 3 cm in diameter, flattened or 2-lobed. Leaf: petiole 4–7 cm; blade ± broadly ovate and with undulate margins to 3- or 5-lobed, sometimes hastate, (8–)12–20 × (6–)10–17 cm, deeply cordate at base, apex acute to acuminate, acumen up to 2 cm, coriaceous; primary veins prominent beneath. Inflorescence: rachis short and robust, up to 1.5(–5) cm, bearing (1–)2–3 flowers. Flower: pedicel (without ovary) 2–4 cm; perianth pubescent within, yellowish or whitish-green becoming pinkish or pale red-purple, speckled with dark brown or purple, the lobes darker, utricle 2–4 cm in length, ± globose but wider toward apex, with 2 lateral reniform glandular zones, tube 3–7 cm, curved, lobes 3, equal, erect-oblique to spreading, triangular-lanceolate, (2–)4–7 cm in length, more or less conduplicate, their apex acute and furnished with a tuft of white hairs or with long erect hairs in the upper half; gynosteme 9–12 mm in length, with 9 stamens and 9 stigmatic lobes with elongate tapering appendages 3 × 1 mm; ovary 8–25 mm in length. Fruit elongate, 20–35 × 2.5–4 cm, with marked longitudinal ribs. Seed oval, 12 × 9 mm.

## Distribution

from Côte d'Ivoire, Benin, Nigeria, Cameroon, Chad and Sudan to Democratic Republic of Congo, Uganda and Angola; in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Ngounié, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo and Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology

swampy or periodically flooded lowland forest, but sometimes also in drier forests and even in savanna; in Gabon at 5–750 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1200 m

Taxonomic note: In 2009, a specimen collected on the Mayombe hills bears flowers with a smaller utricle and narrower tube and lobes. It is not yet clear whether this specimen represents a new taxon or an exception within P. triactina; we thus await more material to decide. B: Hauman (1948), Hawthorne & Jongkind (2006), Hutchinson & Dalziel (1928), Poncy (1978), Sinsin & van der Maesen (2006), Verdcourt (1986).
