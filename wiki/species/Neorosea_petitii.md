# *Neorosea petitii* N. Hallé, sp. nov.

**Genus**: [[Neorosea]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]

## Description

Shrubby fruit with villous internodes. Stipules tubular acuminate. Petiole puberulous 3-5 mm long. Lamina ferruginous discolorous when dry, glabrous above, puberulous below, elliptic-obovate, obtuse at base. Costa puberulous above and below. Secondary nerves 7-10 on each side, puberulous below with scarcely conspicuous pilose domatia. Black punctiform galls scattered. White puberulous flowers axillary solitary, pedicel bearing below the apex two cupules, 5-10 mm long, ochraceo-puberulous when dry. Calyx incised 2-3 lobed. Corolla 8-9-merous. Anther

Tall shrub with reddish-velutinous branches. Stipules velutinous connate at juvenile stage for about 2 mm, with subulate acumen 2 mm. Petiole pubescent 3-5 mm. Limb reddish discolorous glabrous above except for the midline, finely pubescent below, ± narrowly obovate, (2) 4-9 × 1.5-4 cm; base obtuse, apex shortly subacuminate. 7-10 pairs of secondary nerves; intersecondary fields ± riddled with small black bacterial galls 0.1-0.6 mm. Intersecondary reticulation not oriented transversely.

Flowers white ochraceo-pubescent when dry, solitary axillary, with pedicel 5-10 mm long, bearing below each apex a cupule often biappendiculate, 1-2 mm high. Calyx pubescent fused to the acute apex, reaching 3-3.5 mm in diameter and concealing the corolla in the bud, tearing into 2-3 scarious lobes at anthesis. Corolla 8-9-merous pubescent externally; lobes 5-6 × 2 mm; tube sparsely pubescent within, with white hairs evident at the throat. Anthers subsessile basifixed 4-5 × 1.2 mm, with narrow marginal locules meeting at the apex. Style 9-12 mm long, pubescent, with 2 stigmatic lobes 2 mm. 2 elliptic placentae each bearing 2 ovules spaced on the abaxial face. Fruit not seen.

Type and only known material: Le Testu 5245 (P, isotype BM), Pounga, Upper Ngounyé (26 Feb. 1925).

This species is dedicated to our Belgian friend E. Petit who was the first to foresee the necessity of grouping outside the genus Tricalysia the species with bacterial galls.
