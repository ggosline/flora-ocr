# *Thyrsosalacia nematobrachion* Loesener

**Genus**: [[Thyrsosalacia]]
**Family**: [[Celastraceae]]

## Description

in Fedde Report. 49: 226 (1940).

– Hippocratea nematobrachion LOES., mss. in herb. (BM, G, L, P).

Shrub of 0.30–0.50–2 m in height or small liana up to 4–4.5 m. Petiole 2–7 (–14) mm, thickening in older leaves. Blade olive green when dry, 5–20.5 × 2–9 cm, acute to rounded at base, apex with an acumen 6–18 mm long. 5–8 pairs of secondary nervures.

Inflorescence a terminal or axillary thyrse, rachis sometimes partly leafy, lateral cymes remarkably slender, pale green, pendulous, opposite or alternate, pauciflorous; cymules with alternate uniflorous branches very slender. Pedicel very fine, articulate clearly above the ultimate minute bracts. Bud globose, 2 mm in diameter. Flower rotaceous, 6–9 mm in diameter, pale orange, yellow or pale yellow-green. Sepals very unequal, rounded, ± fringed. Petals orbicular or elliptic, 2–3 mm long, fringed. Disk disciform, yellow-green, simple and entire. Stamens 0.3–0.7 mm long; anther wider than long; pollen pale yellow. Ovary trilobed with short style and spatulate stigma; 2 superposed ovules per locule.

Young fruit cucumiform, green and smooth, pendulous, with long slender pedicel approximately 4–7 cm. Mature fruit bright orange, smooth, without ribs, 3.9–10 × 1.9–2.7 cm, attenuate at base, rostrate at apex, with 1–3 seeds; probably only one seed developed per locule; sweet endocarpic mucilage; the largest fruits sometimes constricted towards the middle. Seed 23–30 × 11–15 mm, with very thin testa; 2 thick green cotyledons, easily separable. Embryo glabrous with well-structured plumule. — Pl. 6, p. 23.

## Discussion

Cameroon and Gabon. In humid or inundable forests, sometimes on rocky slopes up to 1,000 m.

One inflorescence showed galled flowers similar to those observed in certain Salacia (see p. 166).
