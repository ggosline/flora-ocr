# *Neonotonia wightii* (Wight & Arn.) J.A.Lackey subsp. wightii

**Genus**: [[Neonotonia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Isleya 2(1): 11 (1981)

## Description

var. longicauda (Schweinf.) J.A.Lackey Planche 99 Isleya 2(1): 11 (1981). Glycine javanica auct., non L., Sp. pl. 2: 754 (1753). Glycine micrantha A.Rich., Tent. fl. Abyss. 1: 212 (1847). Glycine javanica L. var. longicauda (Schweinf.) Baker, Fl. trop. Afr. 2: 178 (1871). Glycine javanica L. var. claessensii (De Wild.) Hauman, Fl. Congo Belge 6: 96 (1954). Glycine javanica L. subsp. micrantha (A.Rich.) F.J.Hermann, U.S. Dept. Agric. Techn. Bull. 1268: 31 (1962). Glycine wightii (Wight & Arn.) Verdc. subsp. wightii var. longicauda (Schweinf.) Verdc., Taxon 15: 36 (1966).

Slender herb with twining stem up to 5 m long; plant glabrescent to densely silky. Stipules ovate-triangular to lanceolate, 2–10 mm long. Leaf: petiole 2–12 cm; rachis (0.1–)1–2 cm; stipels subulate, 2–3 mm; leaflets with petiolule 1–2.5 mm, blade subcoriaceous, glabrescent above, white-hairy below, the terminal one ovate to obovate, (1.5–)4–15 × (1–)3–12.5 cm, base truncate-rounded to cuneate, apex acute to very weakly acuminate, the lateral ones smaller and asymmetric; lateral veins (3–)4 or 5 pairs, pale yellow. Inflorescence with 20 to 150 flowers in fascicles of 2 to 5; rachis (2–)8–60 cm, with appressed hairiness; bracts linear, 1.5–9 mm. Flower: pedicel 0.5–3.5 mm; bracteoles linear, 1.5–5 mm; calyx silky, tube 1.5–2.5 mm long, lobes lanceolate, 2–6 mm long, subequal; corolla white to yellowish, becoming orange-red with age, slightly exsert, standard suborbicular, 3.5–7.5 × 3.5–5.5 mm, with claw 1–2 mm, wings and keel oblong, ± 4.5 × 1 mm, their claw representing ± half their length; ovary ± 4.5 mm long, hairy; style ± 2 mm. Legume 1.5–3.5 cm × 2.5–5 mm, septate between 4 to 6 seeds, densely hairy. Seed quadrangular, biconvex, 2.5–4 × 1.5–3 × 0.7–1.5 mm, purple to orange-brown, finely shagreened.

## Distribution

widely distributed in the tropics; in Gabon, in the Estuaire and Ngounié

## Ecology

riparian forest, forest edge, fallow land and hedges, savanna; in Gabon between 10 and 150 m altitude, elsewhere reaching up to 2000 m. Use: Used as a cover crop and pasture. The leaves could be edible for humans. The plant has several medicinal uses

## Vernacular names

soja pérenne (fr.) ; perennial soybean (angl.) ; lekap (bandjabi)

## Source note

The species is highly variable and has been subdivided into several subspecies and varieties. In Gabon, only subsp. wightii var. longicauda occurs, but it is very poorly sampled. Subsp. pseudojavanica (Taub.) Verdc. var. pseudojavanica, with glabrous legumes, is also widely distributed in Africa and should be sought in Gabon. Bibliography: Anonymus (1975), Burkill (1995), Hauman (1954b), Pellegrin (1949), Verdcourt (1971c), Mackinder et al. (2001)
