# *Lygodium smithianum* Presl (P.L. VIII)

**Genus**: [[Lygodium]]
**Family**: [[Schizaeaceae]]

## Synonyms

- Lygodium pinnatifidum Hooker, Syn. Fil.: 438 (1868).

## Description

— Pr. ex Kuhn, Fil. Afr. : 169 (1868). — Tard. Mém. IFAN : 33 (1953).

— Alston, FWTA : 22 (1959).

Fronds spaced 12-14 cm apart, about 20 cm long, with a petiole 3 cm, imparipinnate. Pinnules alternate, 7 cm long, with a petiole 0.2-0.4 cm, not articulate, linear in outline, acute apex, base decurrent on the winged petiole, margins dentate with oblique, acute teeth, the pair of pinnules adjacent to the terminal one often adnate, decurrent on the petiole. Texture subcoriaceous; rachis of the fronds pubescent, especially in fertile fronds. Veins bi- or quadrifurcate, free. Fertile pinnules similar to sterile ones; sori 0.2 to 0.3 cm.
