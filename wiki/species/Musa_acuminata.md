# *Musa acuminata* Colla

**Family**: [[Musaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Musa]]

## Description

Large perennial herb, the apparent stem formed by tightly overlapping leaf sheaths. Leaves alternate, not distichous, with channelled petioles. The pendent terminal spike has female flowers below and male flowers above; the inferior ovary develops into a berry. The wild species is very polymorphic, usually 3–4 m tall, diploid with 2n = 22 and fertile seeded fruits.

## Cultivated forms in Gabon

Seedless parthenocarpic clones derived from *M. acuminata* include diploid sweet “figue” bananas and triploid Gros-Michel, Cavendish (“sinensis”) cultivars such as Lacatan, Poyo, Grande naine and Naine, and pink-skinned Figue-rose. Gros-Michel has a 4–8 m pseudostem and is susceptible to Panama disease; dwarf Chinese/Canary bananas have a c. 2 m pseudostem.

## Distribution

Wild in South and Southeast Asia; cultivated clones introduced throughout Gabon.
