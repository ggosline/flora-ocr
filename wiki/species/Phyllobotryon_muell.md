## Description

WARB., in ENGL. & PRANTL, NPF 3 (6a): 38 (1893); GILG, EBJ 40: 500 (1908); BAMPS, FCBR, Spermat., Flacourt. 1: 43 (1968); LETOUZEY, HALLÉ & CUSSET, Adansonia, sér. 2, 9 (4): 515 (1969); HUL, Bull. Mus. natl. Hist. nat. Paris, 4° sér., sect. B, Adansonia 13 (3-4): 157 (1991).

– Phylloclinium BAILL., BSLP 2: 870 (1890); WARB., in ENGL. & PRANTL, NPF 3 (6a): 38 (1893); GILG, EBJ 40: 502 (1908); BAMPS, FCBR, Spermat., Flacourt. 1: 44 (1968). TYPE SPECIES: P. paradoxum BAILL.

Unarmed shrubs, monocaulous, with terminal tuft of leaves, rarely branched.

Leaves stipulate, denticulate.

Inflorescences epiphyllous, in glomerules or in 1-4-fasciculate cymes; these in a single or several groups of flowers per leaf, arranged along the midvein or above 2/3 of the height of the lamina. Flowers ♂ or ♀ and ♀ (polygamous plants), rarely solitary, supported by small triangular imbricate bracts or by a large median bract; pedicel glabrous, 0.5-10 mm long; sepals 3-4, imbricate; petals 3-4, imbricate; stamens numerous (20-40); ovary glabrous, with 2-3 pluriovulate placentae; style divided into 3 stigmatic branches or 2-3-fid at the apex.

Fruits: capsules with 2-3 valves; seeds ± arillate.

TYPE SPECIES: P. spathulatum MUELL. ARG.

The genus Phyllobotryon MUELL. ARG. is divided into two subgenera (HUL, 1991): subg. Phyllobotryon and subg. Phylloclinium (BAILL.) HUL. A genus of west-central Africa, it comprises 5 species, 2 of which are present in Gabon.
