# *Ludwigia hyssopifolia* (G.Don) Exell

**Family**: [[Onagraceae]] · **Genus**: [[Ludwigia]]

Garcia de Orta **5**: 471 (1957).

## Description

Erect, freely branched herb or shrub 0.6–2 m high, glabrous except for puberulent young parts and inflorescence; stem shortly and weakly winged below petioles. Petiole flattened, 9–15 mm; blade narrowly ovate to elliptic-ovate, 1–12 × 0.4–2.1 cm, long-tapered at base, acute to subacuminate; lateral veins 11–17 pairs. Flowers numerous, solitary and subsessile; sepals 4, 2–5 mm; petals 4, 2–3 × 1–3 mm, pale to bright yellow; stamens 8; ovary about 7 × 0.5 mm; style 1–1.5 mm. Fruit straight, cylindrical and slightly broadened above, 12–22(–30) × 1–2 mm, puberulent; seeds single-rowed below and several-rowed above, 0.7–0.8 mm below and 0.4–0.5 mm above.

## Distribution

From Senegal and Cape Verde to Sudan and south to Democratic Republic of the Congo, also through Asia to Malaysia, Australia and the Pacific. Widespread in Gabon.

## Habitat and ecology

Wet ground, marshy savanna hollows, grassland, marshes and sandbanks; 5–390 m in Gabon, to 500 m elsewhere.
