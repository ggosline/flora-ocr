# *Bulbophyllum comatum* Lindl.

**Genus**: [[Bulbophyllum]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: J. Linn. Soc., Bot. 6: 128 (1862)

## Description

Pseudobulbs 0.6–4 × 0.5–2.2 cm, suborbicular to ovoid or ellipsoidal, 1-leaved. Leaf petiolate 9–27 × 1.2–5 cm, broadly oblong to linear, acuminate. Inflorescence 4.8–14 cm, rather dense, rhachis fusiform, glabrous or woolly, 25–65-flowered. Flowers not very open, reddish or green tinged with red-purple. Dorsal sepal oblong-lanceolate to ovate-lanceolate, acuminate to caudate, glabrous to papillose and with long hairs on external face; lateral sepals similar but narrower and oblique. Petals narrowly to broadly obovate, subspatulate, obtuse. Labellum dark red-purple, thick, narrowly elliptic to oblong, covered with dense hirsute hairs, apex rounded, base with 2 very small triangular lateral lobes.

Two varieties were distinguished: the typical variety not collected in Gabon is very likely there:

a. var. comatum

Ovary and sepals covered with thick tomentum of long hairs.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Equatorial Guinea

contrary to the typical variety, it is known from Gabon by three collections. – Sierra Leone, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Gabon, Rwanda
