# *Elachyptera holtzii* (Loesener ex Harms) Wilczek ex Hallé

**Genus**: [[Elachyptera]]
**Family**: [[Celastraceae]]
**Protologue**: Monogr. Hipp. Afr. Occid., These: 77 (1958); Bull. IFAN, ser. A, 21 (3): tab. 62a, fig. 1-9 (1959); Pollen & Spores 2 (1): 9 (1960); Mém. IFAN 64: 82 (1962); WILCZEK, FCB 9: 138, tab. 16 (1960)

## Description

Bull. IFAN, ser. A, 21 (3): tab. 62a, fig. 1-9 (1959); Pollen & Spores 2 (1): 9 (1960); Mém. IFAN 64: 82 (1962); WILCZEK, FCB 9: 138, tab. 16 (1960).

– Hippocratea holtzii LOES. ex HARMS, Notizbl. Bot. Gart. 15: 669 (1942); LOES., Pflanzenf., ed. 2, 20B: 216 (1942), nom. nud.

– H. aptera LOES. ex HARMS, Notizbl. Bot. Gart. 15: 669 (1942); LOES., Wiss Erg. Deut. Zent.-Afr. Exped. 2: 33 (1922), nom. nud. TYPE: Mildbread 4067, fr. (HBG !).

– H. hierniana Exell & Mendonca, Bol. Soc. Brot., ser. 2, 26: 224 (1952); CFA 2 (1): 15 (1954). TYPE: Welwitsch 1324, Angola, flowers only, fruits of Simicratea welwitschii (BM!, BR!).

Vine reaching 4–12 m in height. Wood not known. Leaves opposite or subopposite. Petiole 5–11 mm, canaliculate. Lamina oval 4–11(15) × 2.5–6(8) cm; base rounded or briefly cuneiform; apex sublanceolate or with an acumen of 5–15 mm; margins entire, sometimes obscurely denticulate toward the apex. Secondary veins (4)5–7 pairs, often long-ascending. Fine reticulum often scarcely apparent when dry.

Inflorescences many-flowered, axillary or ± paniculate terminal, 3–20 cm long, either with a simple and distinct peduncle or branched fairly low, or again in fascicles of panicles, glabrous, with axes light reddish when dry and furnished with fine wings; ramifications sometimes alternate; bracts narrow very acute persistent 1 mm. Pedicels 0.5–2.5 mm. Buds subglobose, 1–1.5 mm. Flower nearly 2 mm in diameter, 1 mm when dry, pale green or white, not spreading. Sepals denticulate micropustulous 0.4–0.5 mm. Petals fleshy subcucullate, ovate slightly denticulate about 1–2 mm long. Disk cupuliform 0.65 mm in diameter. Stamens small; anther transverse with 4 small confluent locules. Style very short; stigma punctiform. 2 ovules per locule.

Mericarps observed solitary, (25)34–50 × 20–30 × 10–18 mm, almost ovoid; tegument coriaceous rather thin, light reddish smooth on the upper surface when dry, on the interior light ochraceous reddish rugose. Seeds 2, reddish, (20)26–45 × 13–20 × 5–8 mm; wing atrophied 2)4–7 mm long; funicular tubules thick and yellowish, suborbicular in apical view with a diameter of 2–2.6 mm. - Pl. 53, fig. 1–4, p. 185; pl. 54, fig. 1–14, p. 187.

## Discussion

Cameroon, Gabon, Congo (F. Hallé 1469), Zaïre, Angola and Tanzania (Usambara). Vine of dense humid forest on firm or flooded ground, sometimes in forest gallery.
