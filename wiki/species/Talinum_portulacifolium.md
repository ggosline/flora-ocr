# *Talinum portulacifolium* (Forsk.) Asch. ex Schweinf.

**Family**: [[Portulacaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Talinum]]

## Description

Perennial herb about 1 m, woody at the base, with a taproot and angular branching stem. Nearly sessile obovate or subspatulate leaves reach 7 × 3 cm and become smaller toward the inflorescence. Flowers have two caducous sepals, usually five fugacious petals, numerous stamens and a superior one-locular ovary; capsule 3-valved with numerous seeds.

## Distribution

Intertropical and southern Africa, Arabia and India; recorded in Gabon.
