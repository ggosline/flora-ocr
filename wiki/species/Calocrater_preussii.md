# *Calocrater preussii* K.Schum. Planche 25 ; Figure 3(F-H)

**Genus**: [[Calocrater]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]

## Description

in Engl. & Prantl, Pflanzenfam. 4(2): 175 (1895). Shrub monocaulescent or ramified once, 20–75 cm tall; branches with lenticels concolorous, ramifications with lines salient alternating with leaf pairs, glabrous. Leaf: petiole absent or up to 3 mm, glabrous, limb narrowly obovate to elliptic, 2–3 times as long as broad, 10–30 × 4–11 cm, subcordate to rounded at the base, rather abruptly acuminate to caudate at the apex, papyraceous even when fresh, glabrous on both faces; lateral nerves in 8 pairs, rather straight, tertiary nervation reticulate, well visible. Inflorescence contracted, with ± flowers, pedicel reaching ± 2 mm, covered with sepaloid bracts. Flower fragrant; pedicel reaching ± 2 mm; calyx with lobes 2.5–8 × 1–3 mm, acuminate, glabrous on both faces; corolla 24–55 mm long, white, glabrous on the outside, pilose on the inside in a band 3–4 mm broad from the insertion of the stamens downward, tube 18–45 mm long, clearly enlarged just above the anthers, lobes broadly oval to subcircular, 0.2–0.33 times the length of the tube, 6–18 × 5–15 mm; stamens inserted ± mm from the base, anther glabrous, pistil glabrous, ovary abruptly constricted, one style slender, with a disciform thickening. Fruit 20–40 × 15–20 × 10–20 mm, orange or yellow, with 2 horns 5–10 mm long and acute at the apex, with 10 to 20 seeds; wall 2 mm thick; aril silvery white. Seed subglobose to ellipsoid, 6–7 mm long, dark brown. Distribution: Cameroon, Gabon and west of the Republic of the Congo to Gabon, common but not yet observed in the Haut-Ogooué. Ecology: undergrowth, forests, often on streambanks in Gabon, up to m altitude. Mt. Igumbi. B: Beentje (2021), Hallé (1965), Leeuwenberg (1995). BIBLIOGRAPHY
