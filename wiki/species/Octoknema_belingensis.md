# *Octoknema belingensis* Gosline & Malécot

**Family**: [[Octoknemaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Octoknema]]

Described as new in 2011. **Endemic to Gabon**, known only from the Bélinga mountains in Ogooué-Ivindo Province.

## Diagnosis

Compared by the authors with *O. kivuensis*, from which it differs in having
leaves **elliptic to obovate** (not narrowly elliptic to narrowly obovate) with
a **subcordate** base (not peltate), and **globose fruits to 20 × 18 mm** with
the corolla remains less than 1 mm long — against *O. kivuensis*, whose fruits
reach only 14 × 12 mm and are tipped by a hemispherical mass of calyx remains
2 × 3 mm.

## Types

Type: Gabon, Bélinga.

## Distribution

**Gabon only** — the Bélinga mountains, Ogooué-Ivindo Province (iron-mine area).
Not recorded elsewhere.

## Source

Gosline G. & Malécot V. (2011). A monograph of *Octoknema* (Octoknemaceae — Olacaceae s.l.). *Kew Bulletin* **66**: 367–404.
