# *Octoknema belingensis* Gosline & Malécot

**Family**: [[Octoknemaceae]] *sensu lato* · **Current placement**: Erythropalaceae · **Genus**: [[Octoknema]]

Kew Bull. **66**: 382 (2011). Species 4 of 14 in the monograph.

IPNI: `urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:77114307-1`

Described as new in 2011. **Endemic to Gabon**, known only from the Bélinga
mountains in Ogooué-Ivindo Province, and only from the type gathering.

## Diagnosis

*Latin diagnosis*: similar to *O. kivuensis* but differing in leaves elliptic to
obovate (not narrowly elliptic to narrowly obovate), the base subcordate (not
peltate), and fruits globose to 20 × 18 mm with corolla remains under 1 mm long
(not globose and at most 14 × 12 mm, with calyx remains forming a hemispherical
mass 2 × 3 mm at the fruit apex).

## Description

**Habit**: tree to 12 m tall, 7 cm diam. at 1.5 m. Young twigs light brown
beneath densely matted hairs. Indumentum densely floccose, hairs erect
0.3 × 0.3 mm, light brown, appearing dark brown because of their density;
semidendritic hairs present, to 1 mm long.

**Leaves**: narrowly elliptic to obovate, 15–30 × 5–10 cm; apex rounded to
acute, acumen rounded to absent, to 1 × 1 cm; base rounded. Glossy dark green
above, dull and paler green below, with occasional obscure stellate hairs on
both surfaces of the lamina. Midrib sparsely pubescent. Secondary veins in 7–9
pairs, ascending at 30° from the midrib, barely anastomosing 2 mm from the
margin, glabrous below. Tertiary veins visible, straight.

**Petiole**: 1–2.5 cm long, rounded, with a distinct apical pulvinus, densely
pubescent.

**Male inflorescence**: unknown.

**Female inflorescence**: known from fruiting material only; peduncle 10–15 mm
long; fruits in clumps of 2–4.

**Fruit**: orange-red when ripe, globose, 2 × 1.8–2 cm, densely pubescent.
Epicarp smooth; mesocarp 2–2.5 mm thick, woody when dry; endocarp 0.5 mm thick
with **9 internal laminae**.

**Seedling** (*Setten 665*, raised from *Breteler & de Wilde 589*): stem
dark-green, glabrous; hypocotyl tapered; main taproot with small secondary
radicels; germination epigeal; cotyledons 2, foliaceous, opposite, base
rounded, apex pointed, with 2 secondary nerves, glabrous.

## Types

**Holotype**: Gabon, Bélinga, iron mine exploration area, 13 Nov. 1978,
*Breteler 589* (WAG); **isotypes** B, K, MO, P, WAG.

## Distribution

**Gabon only** — the Bélinga mountains, Ogooué-Ivindo Province (iron-mine
exploration area). Map 4 in the monograph.

## Habitat and ecology

Rocky bed of a permanent creek in closed high forest; a humid locality in shade
or half-shade. 850 m elevation.

## Conservation

**CR B2ab(iii)** (IUCN 2001 criteria), assessed in the monograph. Known from a
single collection, in an area then planned for a large open-cast iron mining
project.

## Specimens examined

**Gabon**

- Ogooué-Ivindo Province: Bélinga, iron mine exploration area, 13 Nov. 1978,
  *Breteler 589* (holotype WAG; isotypes B, K, MO, P, WAG)
- Wageningen, Botanic Gardens greenhouse nr. 78-648, 10 June 1981,
  *Setten 665* (WAG) — a seedling raised from *Breteler 589*

Both gatherings trace to the same wild collection; the species is known from a
single locality.

## Notes

The description is drawn entirely from the type gathering and its greenhouse
seedling, so the male flowers are unknown and the female flowers known only in
fruit. Any further collection from Bélinga would be worth flagging.

The seedling description is unusual in the genus and comes from material raised
at Wageningen — one of the few *Octoknema* seedlings ever described.
