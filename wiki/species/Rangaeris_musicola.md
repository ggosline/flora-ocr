## Description

Stems 1–6 (–30) cm. Leaves 5–11, 6.5–20 × 0.6–2 cm, fan-shaped, ribbon-like, unequally bilobed at the apex. Inflorescence 5–25 (–42) cm, 5–15-flowered. Flowers rather small, resupinate, white then yellow-orange, spur olive-green tinged with rose or white, fragrant during the day. Dorsal sepal ovate, acute. Petals obliquely linear-lanceolate. Lateral sepals obliquely lanceolate to oblong-ovate. Lip 6.7–8.5 × 4–7 mm, ovate-cordate, very weakly trilobed; median lobe ovate-triangular; lateral lobes rounded. Spur (4–) 5.5–10 (–12) cm, filiform, pendant. – Pl. 172, p. 377.
