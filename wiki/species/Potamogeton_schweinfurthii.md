# *Potamogeton schweinfurthii* A.Benn.

**Family**: [[Potamogetonaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Potamogeton]]

In Dyer, *Flora of Tropical Africa* **8**: 220 (1901).

## Synonymy

- *Potamogeton bunyonyiensis* Denny & Lye, Kew Bull. **28**: 120 (1973).

## Description

Rhizomatous aquatic herb; stem slender, cylindrical, to 3.5 m long. Stipules 2–6 mm, acute, brownish-green tinged purple and translucent.

Submerged leaves sessile or with petiole 0–3 cm, longer in transitional leaves; blade narrowly elliptic-ovate, 4.5–26 × 0.3–2.8 cm, margin entire or denticulate, papery and glossy, bright to dark green or brownish-green. Floating leaves sometimes present; petiole 3–7 cm and concolorous at its junction with the blade; blade elliptic to ovate, 4–13 × 1–3 cm, margin entire, coriaceous to subcoriaceous, light to dark green tinged red.

Inflorescence terminal or axillary, a continuous cylindrical many-flowered spike; peduncle 3.5–25 cm, slightly swollen and broader than the stem; rachis 3–9 cm in fruit. Tepals pale brownish-green; anther yellowish-green; carpels 4. Fruit 3–4 mm long with a short blunt beak.

## Distribution

From the Azores and southern Europe through North and tropical Africa to South Africa and Madagascar, but rare or nearly absent from the forest zone. In the wiki region it is recorded from Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon and western Democratic Republic of the Congo. In Gabon it is rare and known from one Ngounié locality near the Nyanga border.

## Habitat and ecology

Flowing or standing water 0.1–1.2 m deep; at 165 m in Gabon and 0–4250 m elsewhere.

## Notes

Broad-leaved African pondweeds have long been confused. *P. schweinfurthii* closely resembles *P. nodosus*; the treatment distinguishes them anatomically by U-type stem endodermal cells in the former and O-type cells in the latter.
