# *Voacanga wieringae* Jongkind, spec. nov. Figure 16(D-H)

**Genus**: [[Voacanga]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]

## Description

Differs from the closely related V. micrantha by its larger corolla (tube 11–18 mm versus 7.7–11 mm long, lobes 14–30 × 11–17 mm versus 7–13 × 4–8 mm) and often larger leaves (5–26 cm versus 3–17 cm long).
