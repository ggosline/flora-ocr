# *Bulbostylis scabricaulis* Cherm. Planche 17

**Genus**: [[Bulbostylis]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Bot. France 68 : 419 (1922)

## Description

, non Bulbostylis collina (Kunth) C.B.Clarke (1894). Abildgaardia collina (Ridl.) Lye, Nordic J. Bot. 1 : 757 (1982). Perennial herb, fairly robust; bases of stems bulbous, remaining attached to one another; stems 15–50 cm × 0.1–1 mm, glabrous or with, in the lower part, short appressed hairs, and in the upper part, hairs 0.3 mm at most, appressed or slightly spreading, stiff, rendering the stem very scabrous. Leaf: sheath pale to dark reddish-brown or golden, with numerous flexuous hairs approximately 3 mm at the collar; blade 10–25 cm × 0.5 mm, flat or canaliculate, with numerous short hairs. Inflorescence a dense head formed of numerous crowded spikelets sessile or very shortly pedicellate; involucral bracts 0.5–3 mm long, sometimes shorter than the inflorescence, with short sheathing part. Spikelet ordinarily 5–8 × 0.5–2 mm; glumes

## Distribution

very widespread in tropical Africa, from Senegal to Kenya and southwards to Angola and Zimbabwe; rare in Gabon, found only in the Nyanga and Ogooué-Ivindo (2 collections)

## Ecology

in fairly dry scattered savannas; in Gabon, at 100–150 m altitude, up to 1900 m in East Africa. Taxonomic note: differs from B. filamentosa by the less crowded spikelets and achenes almost smooth (finely papillose in B. filamentosa).
