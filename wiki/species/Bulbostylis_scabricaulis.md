# *Bulbostylis scabricaulis* Cherm. Planche 17

**Genus**: [[Bulbostylis]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Bot. France 68 : 419 (1922)

## Synonyms

- Fimbristylis collina Ridl., Trans. Linn. Soc. London, Bot. 2 : 154 (1884), non
- Bulbostylis collina (Kunth) C.B.Clarke (1894).
- Abildgaardia collina (Ridl.) Lye, Nordic J. Bot. 1 : 757 (1982).

## Description

Perennial herb, fairly robust; bases of stems bulbous, remaining attached to each other; stems 15–50 cm × 0.1–1 mm, glabrous or with, in the lower part, short appressed hairs, and in the upper part, hairs 0.3 mm at most, appressed or slightly spreading, stiff, making the stem very scabrous. Leaf: sheath pale to dark reddish-brown or golden, with numerous flexuous hairs approximately 3 mm at the collar; blade 10–25 cm × 0.5 mm, flat or canaliculate, with numerous short hairs. Inflorescence in a dense head formed of numerous closely crowded spikelets sessile or very shortly pedicellate; involucral bracts 0.5–3 mm long, sometimes shorter than the inflorescence, with a short sheathing part. Spikelet ordinarily 5–8 × 0.5–2 mm; glumes

## Distribution

very widespread in tropical Africa, from Senegal to Kenya and southwards, to Angola and Zimbabwe; rare in Gabon, found only in Nyanga and Ogooué-Ivindo (2 collections)

## Ecology

in fairly dry open savannas; in Gabon, at 100–150 m altitude, up to 1900 m in East Africa. Taxonomic note: differs from B. filamentosa by the less crowded spikelets and achenes almost smooth (finely papillose in B. filamentosa).
