# *Aframomum mannii* (Oliv. et Hanb.) K. Schum.
**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Aframomum]]
## Description
Herb to 1.5 m; long-petiolate obovate blades c. 20 × 7 cm, abruptly caudate. Basal 2–3-flowered spikes on c. 3 cm peduncles. Violet flower with 4.5–5 cm undulate labellum. Known only from Mann 1902, Gabon.
