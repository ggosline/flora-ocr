# *Renealmia macrocolea* K. Schum.

**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Renealmia]]

## Description

Rhizomatous herb 2 m or taller. Petiole 2–3 cm or blade decurrent nearly to sheath; narrow glabrous blade to 70 × 15 cm. Rather dense panicle to 40 cm on an 8–30 cm sheathed peduncle; axis red and pubescent. Sessile secondary clusters bear 3–5 flowers. Calyx red, 7 mm; corolla white, broad labellum yellow, stamens pink. Pubescent ovary forms a red-orange ellipsoid or subglobose capsule 10–15 mm.

## Distribution

Humid forest understorey in Togo, Cameroon and Gabon.
