Stem < 100 cm. Leaves 2.5-5 × 0.7-2 cm, oblong. Inflorescence 0.5-1.5 (−2) cm, 6-10 (14)-flowered. Flowers small, resupinate, perianth whitish or greenish but labellum white. Dorsal sepal oblong or elliptic-ovate. Petals obliquely oblong-obovate, obtuse. Lateral sepals obliquely oblong-lanceolate. Labellum < 2 × 2 mm, cordate, slightly cochleariform, without callus, weakly three-lobed, median lobe acute, triangular, lateral lobes rounded. Spur 5-10 mm, pendent, oblong-claviform, obtuse. - Pl. 174, p. 379.
