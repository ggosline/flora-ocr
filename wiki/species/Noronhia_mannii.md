# *Noronhia mannii* (Soler.) Hong-Wa & Besnard Planche 7

**Genus**: [[Noronhia]]
**Family**: [[Oleaceae]]
**Protologue**: Mol. Phylogen. Evol. 67(2) : 376 (2013)

## Synonyms

- Chionanthus mannii (Soler.) Stearn, Bot. J. Linn. Soc. 80 : 199 (1980).
- Linociera mannii Soler., Bot. Centralbl. 12(46) : 17 (1891).

## Description

Tree or shrub attaining up to 5(–15?) m in height. Leaf glabrous or nearly so; petiole channelled above, 4–6 mm; blade obovate-elliptic, 5–10 × 2–4.5 cm, base attenuate, shortly acuminate at apex; main vein furrowed above, prominent below, the lateral veins in 6 to 9 pairs, discrete, sometimes 1 or 2 with domatia in crypts in the axil. Inflorescence in thyrse, up to 3 cm in length; axis pubescent; bracts triangular, small. Flower sessile or with pedicel < 1 mm; calyx with appressed hairs; corolla 7–8 mm in length. Fruit ellipsoid, 2–3 × 1.5–2 cm, glabrous, dark purple; endocarp ± 1.5 cm in length.

## Distribution

endemic to Gabon, known only from Estuaire (environs of Libreville)

## Ecology

at the margin of mangrove, above the high tide line
