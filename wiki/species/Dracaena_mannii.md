# *Dracaena mannii* Baker Figure 3(A, B)

**Genus**: [[Dracaena]]
**Family**: [[Dracaenaceae]]
**Modern family**: [[Asparagaceae]] subfam. Nolinoideae — *Dracaenaceae* as circumscribed here is superseded; see [[Dracaenaceae]]
**Protologue**: J. Bot. 12 : 164 (1874)

## Synonyms

- Draco mannii (Baker) Kuntze, Revis. gen. pl. 2 : 710 (1891) .
- Pleomele mannii (Baker) N.E.Br., Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1914 : 278 (1914).

## Description

Tree reaching 40 m in height; trunk up to 1.5 m in diameter; bark grey, longitudinally fissured, absence of resinous exudate, cut surface cream-white, weak odour. Leaves spiral, densely distributed along the branches, sometimes concentrated at the extremities, leaving the stem visible between them; sheath embracing the stem over ½ or ¾ of the circumference, over a length of < 1 cm, leaf scar lunulate; pseudipetiole absent; blade ensiform to narrowly obovate, up to 70 × 4.5 cm, base gradually narrowed, abruptly widening towards the sheath, apex gradually narrowed into a mucro of ± 2 mm, coriaceous, dark brown or black when dry, upper surface mid-green shiny, not variegated, with green margins, lower surface pale green with prominent midvein. Inflorescence erect, paniculate with numerous branches, up to 40 cm in length and 70 cm in width, with smooth axes, yellowish-green; bracts subtending the branches rapidly caducous, triangular, up to 10 × 7 mm, diminishing in size towards the apex, scarious; fascicles with up to 8 flowers, each subtended by a small caducous bract, triangular, up to 5 mm in length, scarious; floral bracts ± 1 mm in length, scarious. Flower fragrant; pedicel up to 4 mm, articulate up to 3.5 mm from the base; perianth 12–15 mm in length, yellowish-white, lobes longer than the tube, spreading to reflexed; stamens inserted near the throat, up to 1.5 mm shorter than the lobes, filament white, filiform, anther ± 2 mm in length, pale yellow; ovary oblong, up to 2 × 1 mm; style exsert up to 1 mm, white, stigma ± 0.5 mm in diameter. Fruit spherical to obloid, up to 25 mm in diameter, entire to superficially 2- or 3-lobed, orange-red; receptacle up to 4 mm in length. Seed not seen.

## Distribution

Benin, Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola; in Gabon, known from Moyen-Ogooué, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

humid primary forest, near villages and roadsides; in Gabon at 0–900 m altitude, elsewhere at 0–1150 m. Flowering recorded in April and October

B A C D E

Uses: The species is a multipurpose tree and is sometimes planted near villages. It is used in medicine for several ailments. It produces fibres, dyes, resin and the trunk is used for the production of sculptures, musical instruments, games and toys, etc.

## Vernacular names

Asparagus tree, Asparagus bush (angl.) ; alen ocpo (nom pilote)

## Source note

D. nitens, D. perrottetii and D. usambarensis are closely related to D. mannii and are even treated as synonyms by Bos (1984). Here, we treat them as distinct taxa on the basis of the characters mentioned in the key. B: Baker (1898), Bos (1984), Burkill (1985), Damen et al.
