# *Crotalaria oligostachya* Baker

**Genus**: [[Crotalaria]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]

## Description

in Oliv., Fl. trop. Afr. 2 : 41 (1871). Herb or suffrutex, annual or short-lived perennial, 0.3–0.8 m tall; stem procumbent or ascending, cylindric or slightly furrowed, ± puberulent. Stipules absent. Leaf: petiole 1–4 cm; leaflets 3, with elliptic-oblong lamina, 2.5–6(–11) × 0.6–0.8(–3) cm, base cuneate, apex acute to rounded and mucronate, loosely appressed-puberulent, undersurface whitish. Inflorescence terminal, pseudoracemose; peduncle (5–)10–24 cm; flowers concentrated towards the apex, terminated in tight buds; bracts filiform to subulate, 1–1.5 mm. Flower: bracteoles below the base of calyx, linear, 0.5–1 mm; calyx becoming truncate at base, deflexed against the pedicel in fruit, appressed-puberulent, tube 1.5–2 mm long, with triangular-acuminate lobes, ± 2 mm long; standard rounded, 5–10 mm long, yellow striped with red or purple, wings and keel 6–7.5 mm long, keel falcate, with an acute beak, curved, yellowish-green and striped with purple. Pod subsessile, cylindric, 1.6–2.4 × 0.4–0.5 cm, black at maturity, appressed-puberulent, with 20 to 34 seeds. Seed oblique-cordate, 1.5–2.5 × 1.5 × 1 mm, smooth, cream; hilum nearly central.
