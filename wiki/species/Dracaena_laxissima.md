# *Dracaena laxissima* Engl. Planche 14

**Genus**: [[Dracaena]]
**Family**: [[Dracaenaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 15 : 478 (1892)

## Description

Slender shrub, creeping to lianescent, up to 6 m in height; trunk ± 3 cm in diameter; branches generally horizontal. Leaves distichous, rather scattered, leaving the stem clearly visible between the sheaths; sheath embracing the stem over 0.7–0.9 times the circumference, over a length of < 1 cm; pseudopetiole 5–12 mm, dark green to black, with scarious wings, the base gradually passing into the sheath; blade generally elliptic, subfalcate, up to 19 × 7 cm, base cuneate to rounded, apex acuminate with a mucro reaching 3 mm, coriaceous, upper surface dark green glossy, sometimes with small scattered pale green spots, lower surface paler and more matte. Inflorescence spreading or pendulous, in a loose panicle, up to 50 cm in length, with smooth, green axes; bracts rapidly caducous, narrowly ovate-ligulate, up to 2 × 0.7 cm, becoming smaller towards the apex; flowers solitary; floral bracts triangular, up to 3 mm in length, scarious. Flower: pedicel 21 mm, articulate up to 17 mm from the base; perianth 16–20 mm in length, white tinged with red, lobes much longer than the tube, reflexed; receptacle green, obconic, up to 5 mm in length (up to 8 mm in fruit); stamens inserted 1 mm above the throat, up to 1.5 mm shorter than the lobes, filament white, swollen, with subulate apex, anther ± 2 mm in length, pale yellow; ovary oblong, up to 2.5 × 1.5 mm; style exsert for up to 1 mm, white, stigma slightly 3-lobed, ± 0.5 mm in diameter. Fruit spherical, up to 12 mm in diameter, entire to 2- or 3-lobed, red-orange; receptacle much thicker than the persistent pedicel. Seed spherical, up to ± 7 mm in diameter.

Naturalis Biodiversity Center (©), reproduced from Bos (1984).

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, São Tomé and Príncipe, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, South Sudan, Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi, Kenya, Tanzania, Malawi, Zambia and Mozambique; in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué, Ngounié, Ogooué-Lolo, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

Understory, humid places, often along water margins and in mangrove; in Gabon at 0–900 m altitude, elsewhere at 0–2500 m. Flowering recorded from November to January and in May

## Source note

In the Democratic Republic of the Congo and Tanzania a form with white or yellowish bands irregularly undulate along the transverse veins of the leaves has been observed (D. laxissima forma aureilicia Wiland & Q.Luke). It could also be present in Gabon. B: Baker (1898), Bos (1984), Damen et al. (2018), Hepper (1968), La Croix (2010), Marquet (1987), Mwachala (2005, 2007), Velayos & Cabezas (2018)
