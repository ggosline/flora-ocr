# *Callichilia bequaertii* De Wild. Planche 23 ; Figure 3(E)

**Genus**: [[Callichilia]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Pl. bequaert. 1 : 401 (1922)

## Description

Shrub reaching 4 m in height. Leaf blade of 7–30 cm on the limb elliptic to ovate–1.8 times as long as wide of 4–15 × 2–7 cm cuneate or rarely rounded at the base, acute or acuminate at the apex, with acumen acute reaching 20 mm, dark green, paler below; secondary nerves 6 to 17 pairs. Inflorescence solitary; peduncle of 5–70 mm, covered with persistent bracts, from 5–20 mm from the base; bracts of 2–7 × 1.5–5 mm. Flower fragrant; pedicel of 4–18 mm; calyx with lobes narrowly oblong to narrowly ovate–1.5 times as long as wide of 4–8 × 3–5 mm acute to rounded and mucronulate at the apex, greenish with pale dots, inside near the base with 6 to 42 colleters in 2 to 4 rows; corolla in the mature bud long of 8–16.5 cm; mature corolla white, white–greenish near the base of the tube, cylindrical tube long of 6–10.8 cm, inside with pilose crests long of 3–12 mm, with lobes overlapping to the left, 1–2 times as long as wide of 2–8 × 2–5 mm stamens inserted from the sixteenth to one-third from the base of the tube, anther long of 6.8–10.2 mm; style of 4.8–25 mm. Fruit with carpels of 20–43 × 10–20 × 10–18 mm, yellow to orange, scattered with white, with beak reaching 10 mm. Seed reaching 8 mm in length. Distribution Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola (Cabinda); in Gabon: Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo and Ogooué-Lolo. Ecology Primary and secondary forest in Gabon, up to 500 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1200 m
