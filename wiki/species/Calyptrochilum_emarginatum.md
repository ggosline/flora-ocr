# *Calyptrochilum emarginatum* (Afzel. ex Sw.) Schltr.

**Genus**: [[Calyptrochilum]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Beih. Bot. Centralbl. 36 : 84 (1918) ; FTWA : 251 (1968) ; FAC 2 : 594 (1992) ; FCAM 3 : 810 (2001)

## Synonyms

- Angraecum imbricatum Lindl., J. Linn. Soc. Bot. 6: 137 (1862), non Angraecum imbricatum (Sw.) Schltr, (1918).
- Calyptrochilum imbricatum (Lindl.) Schltr., Die Orchideen : 608 (1914).

## Description

Stem < 300 cm. Leaves oblong-elliptic. Inflorescence 3-5 cm, < 20-flowered, rachis cylindric, straight. Flowers small, resupinate, fragrant; perianth white or slightly violaceous. Dorsal sepal ovate, briefly apiculate. Petals obliquely oblong-ovate, briefly apiculate. Lateral sepals oblong, apiculate. Labellum 7-10 mm, subquadrate, entire, truncate. Spur < 10 mm, basal portion broadly cylindric, the apical third compressed and geniculate. – Pl. 190, p. 409.

## Distribution

Guinée, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroun, Gabon, République centrafricaine, Zaïre, Angola

## Ecology

alt. 0-560 m. Epiphytic on trunk and branch up to 25 m from the ground in dense humid forest, swamp of old mangrove, gallery forest and semi-deciduous forest with Celtis and Sterculiaceae, observed on Crudia harmsiana, Irvingia smithii, Dialium sp., Triplochiton adenoxylon, Chrysobalanus atocorensis
