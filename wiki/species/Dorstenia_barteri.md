# *Dorstenia barteri* Bureau

**Genus**: [[Dorstenia]]
**Family**: [[Moraceae]]

## Description

in DC., Prodr. 17:272 (1873); ENGL., Mon. Afr. Pfl. 1, Moraceae: 14 (1898).

Herbs with creeping rhizomes approximately 5 mm thick; stems 20–80 cm tall, ascending, with woody base and leafy part glabrous or with hairs (yellowish to) white, scattered to dense, sometimes mixed with shorter white hooked hairs; internodes 1–4 cm long and 2–5 mm thick. Leaves almost distichous. Blade lanceolate to elliptic, wider at the middle or below, but most often above the middle, often narrowing towards the base, (4–) 6–22 × (1.5–) 3–8.5 cm, finely chartaceous, abruptly to gradually acuminate with a rounded or acute acumen; base subobtuse or subcordate, sometimes cuneate or asymmetric and cuneate-subobtuse; margin ± sinuose, with sometimes 1 or 2 coarse teeth towards the apex; upper surface glabrous, lower surface almost glabrous or puberulent, especially on the nerves, with short white hooked hairs, scattered to quite dense, sometimes mixed with a few larger hairs; midvein and secondary veins impressed above, prominent below; 5–13 pairs of secondary veins. Petiole 0.5–1.5 (–3) cm long, approximately 1.5 mm thick, with scattered hooked hairs, mixed with longer and larger hairs, scattered or quite dense. Stipules caducous or sub-persistent, subulate, (1–) 2–9 (–15) mm long, 0.5–1 mm wide at the base, glabrous or with scattered straight to hooked hairs; stipules sometimes grouped at the apex of the stems.

Inflorescences solitary, purplish (black), greenish or yellowish. Peduncle (1–) 1.5–7 cm long, 1–2 mm in diameter, with scattered to quite dense hairs, dome-shaped, and/or small, curved to hooked or straight, sometimes mixed with longer and larger hairs. Receptacle discoid, subtriangular, multiangular or suborbicular, 20–55 mm in diameter; floriferous surface subtriangular to suborbicular, or irregularly stellate, 7.5–30 mm in diameter; margin (1–) 3–15 mm wide, (finely) chartaceous, sometimes undulate; primary appendages 3–24, linear, 7.5–60 mm long and approximately 1 mm wide, often wider at the apex; between these appendages or at their base, often several secondary appendages dentiform to linear, 0.5–7.5 × 1.2 mm; upper surface of the margin and lobes with visible radial primary ribs, most often extending from the angles of the receptacle and continuing into the primary appendages, with radial bands between them; upper surface of the margin glabrous or with small white comose-shaped hairs, scattered, or sometimes with small white hooked hairs scattered, most often on the (primary) ribs; lower surface of the receptacle and appendages with white hooked hairs scattered to quite dense, most often on the (primary) ribs, mixed with somewhat to much longer and larger hairs. Male flowers numerous; perianth with flattened apex, (2–) 3 (–4) lobed, 0.5–1 mm in diameter; (2–) 3 stamens with filaments approximately 0.5 mm long, inclined in different directions at anthesis, with anthers approximately 0.2 × 0.2 mm with connective as wide as the locules; pistillode present, sometimes well developed, up to 0.25 mm in diameter. Female flowers 4–20; perianth approximately 0.5 mm in diameter and 0.3 mm tall; style approximately 0.25 mm long; stigmas 2, linear, approximately 0.25 mm long.

Pyrene approximately 2.5 mm in diameter, smooth.

## Type

Mann 152, Fernando Po (holo-, K ; iso-, P)

## Discussion

The species is highly variable, especially in the indumentum of the stem, the leaf shape, the stipule length, the color and form of the inflorescence and the perianth lobes. Four more or less distinct varieties can be recognized. Only one is present in Gabon.

D. mogandjensis DE WILD. (Ann. Mus. Congo, Bot., sér. 5, 2: 241, 1908) appears to be very close, even probably conspecific, with D. barteri. It resembles especially the var. subtriangularis.
