# *Oxystigma mannii* (Baill.) Harms

**Genus**: [[Oxystigma]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Harms, Nat. Pflanzfam. Nachtr. 3,3 : 195 (1897)

## Synonyms

- Copaifera (?) Mannii Baill., Adans. 6 : 202 (1866).
- Hardwickia (?) Mannii (Baillon) Oliv., F.T.A. 2 : 316 (1817).

## Description

Branchlets whitish. Leaves with 1–2 pairs of opposite leaflets, oblong or elliptic-oblong, with rounded or obtusely acuminate apex, glabrous, 8–15 × 3.5–6.5 cm. Secondary veins and veinlets finely prominent beneath. Petiolules short, 2–3 mm. Rachis approximately 3 cm long.

Panicles of small white flowers with very finely pubescent axes. Pedicels 1 mm, somewhat pubescent. Sepals 5, somewhat ciliate, orbicular, petaloid, approximately 2 mm. Petals 0. Stamens (8–)10, somewhat hairy at base. Ovary hairy, stigma pointed. Ovules 1–2.

Pods thick, orbicular, slightly bilobed at apex, woody, indehiscent, approximately 6 cm in diameter by 3 cm thick.

## Vernacular names

Bossipi

## Type

Mann 754, Cameroun River (isotype P)

## Discussion

Medium-sized trees, forming stands in the back mangrove, along riverbanks, remarkable for the interlacing of their powerful root systems. The species appears to be distributed from the mangroves of southern Nigeria to those of Cameroon, Spanish Guinea, and northern Gabon.
