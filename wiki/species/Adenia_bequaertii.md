# *Adenia bequaertii* Robyns & Lawalrée subsp. occidentalis W.J.de Wilde

**Genus**: [[Adenia]]
**Family**: [[Passifloraceae]]
**Protologue**: Acta Bot. Neerl. 17 : 135 (1968)

## Description

Semi-woody liane, reaching 20 m in length; stem at base reaching 10 cm in diameter. Stipules broadly reniform, with ± lacerate margins, 0.5–1.0(–1.5) mm. Leaf with petiole 2–10 cm; blade entire, broadly ovate, 2.5–14 × 2–12 cm, generally 5-palmatinerved, base rounded to cordate, apex acute to acuminate, acumen reaching 1.5 cm long, basal gland single, spatulate or broadly spatulate, laminar glands 2, axillary on each side of the midvein; sterile tendrils, simple or 3-fid, 10–25 cm. Male inflorescence comprising up to 40 flowers, the female with 2–4 flowers; peduncle (0.5–)2–16 cm; tendrils 0, 1 or 3, 1–3 cm; bracts and bracteoles narrowly triangular, acute, with serrulate margins, 0.5–2 mm. Male flower with pedicel 2–10 mm and stipe 1.5–4 mm, ± campanulate, 6.5–13 mm in length; hypanthium cupuliform, shallow; sepals free, oblong-elliptic, 5.5–7 mm in length, obtuse to acute at apex; petals oblanceolate, 5.5–6.5 mm in length, obtuse, with serrulate-laciniate margins; corona formed of 5 cushion-shaped thickenings, hypanthial glands absent; stamens inserted at base of hypanthium, filaments ca. 1.5 mm, connate for ¾, anthers 3–5.5 mm; pistillode 0.5–1 mm. Female flower similar to male flower, but with pedicel 2–6 mm and stipe 0.5–4 mm, 5–9 mm in length; hypanthium patelliform, 0.5–0.8 mm in height, partitions absent; sepals 4.5–5 mm in length; petals linear, 3–4 mm, entire to finely serrulate at apex; staminodes 0.75 mm; corona as an obsolete ring; ovary ovoid, 2–4 × 1.2–2.5 mm, finely punctate, gynophore ca. 0.5 mm; styles entirely connate, 1–1.5 mm, stigmas sessile, reniform or sub-globose, 1–1.5 mm in diameter, papillose. Fruit

ovoid, 2–3(–5) × 1.5–2(–3.5) cm, acute at apex, with coriaceous to woody pericarp, smooth, foveolate or granulate, with gynophore 1–2 cm. Seeds 50–60 per capsule, obliquely ovoid, 4–5 × 2.5–3 mm, with 7–12 pits along a meridional line.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Central African Republic, Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola; in Gabon, known only from Makokou (Ogooué-Ivindo).

## Ecology

Hygrophilous forests, forest galleries, forest regrowth, savannas; up to 500 m altitude. Taxonomic note: This species has been divided into three subspecies. The subspecies bequaertii occurs in eastern Democratic Republic of the Congo, Rwanda, Burundi, Uganda and Kenya, while the subspecies macranthera W.J.de Wilde is endemic to the Central African Republic. b: De Wilde (1971, 1975), Robyns (1995). ibliography
