# *Strychnos malacoclados* C. H. Wright

**Genus**: [[Strychnos]]
**Family**: [[Loganiaceae]]
**Protologue**: FTA 4 (1) : 523 (1903)

## Description

FTA 4 (1) : 523 (1903). — DUVIGNEAUD, Lejeunia 11 : 61 (1947); Bull. Soc. Roy. Bot. Belg. 85 : 30 (1952). — FWTA ed. 2, 2 : 43.

— S. limbogeton WINKLER in ENGLER, Bot. Jahrb. 41 : 284 (1908).

— S. pansa S. Moore in Cat. Talbot's Nig. Pl. : 68 (1913). — FWTA ed. 1, 2 : 24.

Liana reaching 10 m in height and 10–25 m in length or more; stem 1–2 cm in diameter or more; bark pale grey, thin, smooth, farinose, lenticellate or not; wood cream-coloured; branches non-lenticellate, pale grey or brown; branchlets with ochraceus pubescence, cylindric, non-sulcate when dry; tendrils solitary.

Leaves with petiole bearing ochraceus pubescence, short, 1–3 (–5) mm in length, usually transversely wrinkled below; lamina subcoriaceous or less often coriaceous, even fresh, thinner on shade leaves, elliptic or narrowly elliptic (and for leaves borne on the main axis ovate, broadly ovate or orbicular), variable in size, 1.5–10 × 1.3–5 cm, in shade reaching up to 17.5 × 7.5 cm, acuminate or sometimes, for a few leaves, obtuse or acute at apex, cuneate, rounded or sometimes, especially for leaves borne on the central axis, subcordate at base, usually with scattered pubescence above on the midrib and below on the main nerves; 1–2 pairs of secondary nerves curved along the margin; midrib impressed above; tertiary venation reticulate.

Inflorescences axillary and sometimes also terminal, solitary, lax, few-flowered, 1–3 × 0.6–2 cm; peduncle and branches slender, pubescent as the very short pedicels. Flowers 5-merous. Sepals green, united to one-third of their height, ovate or broadly ovate, 0.5–0.8 × 0.5–0.8 mm, subacute, ciliate, with scattered pubescence outside and glabrous inside. Corolla, in mature bud, 2–2.5 (–2.8) mm in length, yellow, with scattered pubescence outside but glabrous at base, inside pilose on the lobes, especially at their base; tube short, 0.6–0.8 mm in length; lobes 1.5–3.5 times longer than the tube, ovate, 1.2–2.2 × 0.8–1.4 mm, acute, spreading. Stamens scarcely exsert, inserted at the throat of the corolla, filament glabrous, same length as the anther; anther suborbicular, glabrous. Pistil glabrous or with a few small hairs, 1.2–1.6 mm in length; ovary ovoid or globose, 0.6–1 × 0.6–0.9 mm, glabrous, 2-locular; style 0.5–0.6 mm in length, glabrous or with a few small hairs; stigma capitate; 5 ovules per locule.

Fruits orange, small, soft, ellipsoid, 18–27 × 16–18 × 15–18 mm, containing one seed, smooth-skinned and somewhat obliquely pedicellate; wall thin, c. 1 mm thick. Seed somewhat flattened, ellipsoid, 10–12 × 9–10 × 6–8 mm, not deeply depressed on one side, smooth, glabrous; testa detaching very easily. Pl. 16, 8–13, p. 125.

## Discussion

West Africa, Cameroon and Gabon. Moist places in dense and secondary forests. Altitude 0–900 m.

Bos 4632, Kribi, 12 km on road to Lolodorf (sterile) WAG; 6011, Ebéa, 36 km N Kribi (sterile) WAG.

Hanke in herb. Winkler 527, Victoria, bank of the Limbe (fl., Oct.) B (not seen, destroyed, holotype of S. limbogeton; lectotype: G, isotypes: L, MO, WAG, Z).

Leeuwenberg 6396, 3 km E of km 21 Yabassi–Douala road (fr. nearly mature, Aug.) P, WAG, YA.

Zenker 117, Lokoundjé (fl.) BR, FI, G, GH, MO, P; 4571, Bipindi (fl.) BM, BR, COI, E, FHO, G, GOET, HBG, K, L, LE, LY, M, MO, P, PR, PRE, S, W, WU, Z; Bipindi, bank of the Lokoundjé, Apr. 1906 (sterile) F.
