# *Rhynchospora corymbosa* (L.) Britt. Planche 120

**Genus**: [[Rhynchospora]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Trans. New York Acad. Sci. 11 : 84 (1892)

## Description

Perennial herb, robust, in large tufts; stem 60–250 cm × 2.5–10 mm, trigonous. Leaves numerous, very closely packed; leaf sheath closed; main blades 50–120 × 1–2 cm, coriaceous but thick and soft at the base. Inflorescence formed of a terminal corymb and several lateral ones; corymbs with a main axis of 1–6 cm, scabrous with numerous ascending to spreading branches; primary branches at most 10 cm, compressed, with scabrous margins, terminated by short branchlets bearing groups of pedunculate spikelets; bracts subtending primary branches 10–60 cm × 5–20 mm. Spikelet narrowly lanceolate, 6–10 × 1–2.5 mm, brown to orange-brown, with 6–7 glumes, with 1 (rarely more) bisexual flower at the base and usually 1 male flower at the top; glumes reddish-brown, glabrous with a narrow central vein, the lower sterile ones 2–4 mm long, the two upper ones 6–9 mm. Bisexual flower; perianth with 6 scabrous subequal bristles, 4–6 mm long; stamens 3; style long, entire or slightly bifid. Nutlet obovoid to oblanceolate, 3–4.5 × 1.5–2.5 mm, brown; conical cap 4–5 mm high, brown, with a longitudinal groove on each of the two flattened sides.

## Distribution

very widespread in tropical Africa, Asia, Australia, America and the Pacific; in Gabon, locally common, found in Estuaire, Haut-Ogooué, Ngounié, Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem (18 collections).

## Ecology

lake shores, river banks and shallow basins, tolerating semi-shaded exposure; in Gabon, at 0–600 m altitude, up to 1850 m in East Africa.

## Vernacular names

golden beaksedge, matamat (angl.). Usage : La plante entière sert comme engrais vert. Les tiges sont utilisées pour faire des tapis, des sandales et des corbeilles. Note taxonomique : R. triflora Vahl, une espèce très proche, est plus grêle, avec des feuilles plus étroites (5 mm au plus), des épillets moins nombreux et plus aigus, sur des tiges plus longues et plus grêles
