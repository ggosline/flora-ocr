# *Farquharia elliptica* Stapf Planche 34

**Genus**: [[Farquharia]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1912 : 279 (1912)

## Description

*Odavelutina* Leeuwenb., Novon 6(3): 271 (1996). Liana at least 1 m long with corky bark fissured longitudinally; large oval lenticels; branches smooth, green. Leaf glabrous, petiole 4–9 mm, blade elliptic to oval or narrowly oval, 3–7 times as long as wide, 4–12 × 1–3 cm, cuneate to decurrent at base on petiole, acuminate at apex, coriaceous; secondary veins 3 to 10 pairs hardly visible. Inflorescence thyrsoid, pubescent branchlets, nodes brown, rust-coloured, practically glabrous. Flower: calyx with oval lobes, 1.4–2.9 × 1.3–2.7 mm, pubescent outside, glabrous inside and with a single row of colleters and hairs at base; corolla white, pale green at base, pale yellow and becoming brown at anthesis, tube 10–21 × 3.8–5.6 mm, enlarged at 0.5–0.9 of the base, slightly constricted at throat, puberulent to glabrous outside, glabrous inside or sometimes pubescent just below the insertion of stamens, lobes 0.5–1 times as long as tube, 8.8–15.4 mm × 2.3–7.8 mm, puberulent or glabrous on both faces, sometimes pubescent inside near base; stamens 5.2–7.6 mm long, inserted at 0.4–0.7 of the base of tube; pistil 10–16 mm long, ovary villous with rust-brown hairs, sometimes glabrous at base; style 8–13 mm. Fruit: follicles 22–42 cm × 6–9.5 mm, puberulent with rust-brown hairs. Seed 1.5–2.5 cm long; apical coma with hairs 3–3.5 cm. Distribution: Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Central African Republic and Democratic Republic of the Congo, in Gabon, Haut-Ogooué, Ogooué, Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo and Woleu-Ntem. Ecology: Primary and secondary forest in Gabon, up to 850 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1 300 m.
