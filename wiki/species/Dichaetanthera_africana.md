# *Dichaetanthera africana* (Hooker f.) Jacques-Félix

**Genus**: [[Dichaetanthera]]
**Family**: [[Melastomataceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. bot. France 102 : 38 (1955); Fl. Cam. 24 : 55, tab. 14 (1983)

## Synonyms

- Sakersia africana Hook. F., FTA 2: 458 (1871).

## Description

Shrub to small tree, 6 to 10 m tall, scabrous or strigillous on its various parts, except on the hypanthium; branches ± 4-angular, nodes slightly thickened with bristles + longer. Leaves with petiole 1 to 2 cm, rounded with groove, densely strigillous; blade 3–6 × 8–15 cm, lanceolate to lanceolate, base rounded or broadly cuneate and apex acute; scabrous above, bristles + longer on the nerves below; nerves prominent below, + 2 submarginal ones much more slender; transverse nerves numerous and parallel; margins entire but scaberulous.

Pyramidal panicle of 4 to 6 tiers of cymes, the first nodes with foliaceous bracts ± caducous, other bracts and bracteoles navicular, ± 3 × 4 mm, finally caducous; all axes, peduncles, pedicels and bracts scabrous. Flower with pedicel 3 to 5 mm; hypanthium glabrous, ovoid-cylindric, ± 5 × 7 mm; lobes broadly triangular-obtuse to rounded, ± spreading after anthesis, glabrous to ciliolate. Petals pink or mauve, ± fleshy, 4 × 10 mm, oblong, abruptly and briefly clawed. Outer stamens with anther 7 mm, slightly curved; connective appendage 4–5 mm, compressed, appendices divergent, linear and ± spatulate at their apex, 3 mm long; filament compressed 6–7 mm; inner stamens somewhat shorter, anther more curved, 6 mm; connective appendage 3 mm, appendices 2.5 mm; filament as long, 7 mm. Ovary sparsely setulose and bristles of the apex + longer and coherent in a peri-stylar collar; style 20 mm long.

Capsule ± 7 × 10 mm, ovoid to globose, vaguely 4–8-angular when dry; calyx lobes spreading; ovary trunconic, reaching the edge of the hypanthium; valvular dehiscence lines slightly prominent; collar 4-lobed by the dehiscence. Seeds 1 mm long with the funicular appendage from the base, remarkably ornamented with a crest of linear papillae as long as themselves. — Pl. 12, p. 37.

Western forest species, from Sierra Leone to Angola (Cabinda) and Zaïre.
