# *Drypetes similis* Hutch.

**Genus**: [[Drypetes]]
**Family**: [[Putranjivaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. trop. Afr. 6(1): 679 (1912)

## Synonyms

- Sibangea similis (Hutch.) Radcl.- Sm., Kew Bull. 32(2): 481 (1978).

## Description

Small tree, up to ±10(−14) m tall; trunk up to 15 cm in diameter; branchlets cylindrical to slightly flattened, grayish, glabrous; buds with triangular-ovate scales, (6.4–6.8–8.3(−9.3) × (3.7–4.2–5.5(−6.2) mm, coriaceous, glabrescent. Stipules caducous, narrowly triangular to slightly falciform, up to ±8.2 mm long, glabrescent. Leaf: petiole (10.3–13.5–16.5(−28) mm, glabrous, distinctly channeled above, slightly wrinkled; blade broadly oblong, (22.5–27.5–35(−46) × (9.5–11.5–13.5(−15.5) cm, coriaceous, oblique and acute to slightly obtuse at the base, shortly acuminate at the apex, with subentire margin, slightly undulate, flat to recurved, smooth, glabrous on both faces, slightly glossy above, very pale yellowish-green below; venation weakly reticulate, yellowish, secondary veins (6 to)8 to 12, extending upward, slightly looped and anastomosing well before the margin, impressed above, prominent below. Male inflorescence in the axils of leaves or below the leaves along the branches, with up to ±30 flowers; bracts ovate to broadly triangular, 0.6–0.7(−1.2) mm long. Male flower fragrant; pedicel slender, (1.3–2.8–4.4(−7.8) mm, glabrous; sepals 4 or 5, not touching or scarcely touching during anthesis, narrowly oblong, (1.7–2.2–2.9(−4.3) × (0.7–1.1–1.8(−2.2) mm, obtuse at the apex, slightly and sparsely dentate, pale yellowish-green to bright yellow, glabrous on both faces, with a few small scattered marginal cilia; stamens 3 to 6, in a whorl around the margin of the disk, filament (1.7–2.1–2.5(−4.4) mm, anther (0.8–1–1.3 mm long, glabrous; disk flat to slightly convex, (1–1.4–2(−2.2) mm in diameter, glabrous, with or without a central conical projection 0.2 mm. Female inflorescence like the male, with up to ±5 flowers; bracts as in the male. Female flower: pedicel up to ±8.1 mm, glabrous; sepals 4 or 5, ±3.9 × 2 mm, yellowish, otherwise as in the male; disk annular, up to ±3 mm in diameter, glabrous; ovary globose-ellipsoidal, 1-locular, glabrous; style robust, up to ±0.9 mm, unbranched, with a pulvinate-capitate stigma, up to ±1.2 mm in diameter, rounded. Fruit ellipsoidal to ovoid, (6.5–8.5–14.5(−16) × (6–7–13(−15) mm, ivory white at maturity, glabrous, with persistent sepals and stigma, 1-seeded; pedicel (5–9–12(−13) mm, glabrous. Seed 10–10.5 mm long.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon and Gabon; in Gabon: Estuaire

Habitat: evergreen forests, riparian forests; in Gabon at 80 m altitude, elsewhere at 20–750 m.

## Source note

We studied only a single sterile Gabonese specimen of this large-leaved tree, collected in Estuaire. Its foliar morphology and its presence in Cameroon allow us to assume its presence in Gabon. D. similis has broadly oblong leaves with a robust petiole, which can reach sizes larger than any other species of the genus present in the country. They have main and secondary veins yellowish becoming often blackish when dry, as in D. aylmeri and D. moliwensis (both with imbricate sepals) and D. arborescens, all with unilocular ovaries and capitate stigmas. The flowers and fruits of D. similis resemble those of D. arborescens, but they are slightly larger and the sepals broader. The bud scales of D. similis are quite large, triangular, ovate and hard, and when present, they are useful for identification.
