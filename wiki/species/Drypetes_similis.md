# *Drypetes similis* Hutch.

**Genus**: [[Drypetes]]
**Family**: [[Putranjivaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. trop. Afr. 6(1): 679 (1912)

## Description

Sibangea similis (Hutch.) Radcl.-Sm., Kew Bull. 32(2): 481 (1978).

Small tree, up to ±10(−14) m tall; trunk up to 15 cm in diameter; branchlets cylindrical to slightly flattened, greyish, glabrous; buds with triangular-ovate scales, (6.4–6.8–8.3(−9.3) × (3.7–4.2–5.5(−6.2) mm, coriaceous, glabrescents. Stipules caducous, narrowly triangular to slightly falciform, up to ±8.2 mm long, glabrescents. Leaf: petiole (10.3–13.5–16.5(−28) mm, glabrous, distinctly channeled above, slightly wrinkled; lamina broadly oblong, (22.5–27.5–35(−46) × (9.5–11.5–13.5(−15.5) cm, coriaceous, oblique and acute to slightly obtuse at base, shortly acuminate at apex, with subentire margin, slightly undulate, flat to recurved, smooth, glabrous on both faces, slightly lustrous above, very pale yellowish-green below; venation weakly reticulate, yellowish, secondary nerves (6 to)8 to 12, extending upward, slightly looped and anastomosing well before the margin, impressed above, prominent below. Male inflorescence in the axils of leaves or below leaves along the branches, with up to ±30 flowers; bracts ovate to broadly triangular, 0.6–0.7(−1.2) mm long. Male flower fragrant; pedicel slender, (1.3–2.8–4.4(−7.8) mm, glabrous; sepals 4 or 5, not touching or scarcely touching during anthesis, narrowly oblong, (1.7–2.2–2.9(−4.3) × (0.7–1.1–1.8(−2.2) mm, obtuse at apex, slightly and sparsely dentate, pale yellowish-green to bright yellow, glabrous on both faces, with a few small scattered marginal cilia; stamens 3 to 6, in a whorl around the margin of the disc, filament (1.7–2.1–2.5(−4.4) mm, anther (0.8–1–1.3 mm long, glabrous; disc flat to slightly convex, (1–1.4–2(−2.2) mm in diameter, glabrous, with or without central conical projection 0.2 mm. Female inflorescence like the male, with up to ±5 flowers; bracts like the male ones. Female flower: pedicel up to ±8.1 mm, glabrous; sepals 4 or 5, ±3.9 × 2 mm, yellowish, otherwise as in males; disc annular, up to ±3 mm in diameter, glabrous; ovary globular-ellipsoid, 1-locular, glabrous; style robust, up to ±0.9 mm, non-branched, with pulvinate-capitate stigma, up to ±1.2 mm in diameter, rounded. Fruit ellipsoid to ovoid, (6.5–8.5–14.5(−16) × (6–7–13(−15) mm, ivory white at maturity, glabrous, with persistent sepals and stigma, 1-seeded; pedicel (5–9–12(−13) mm, glabrous. Seed 10–10.5 mm long.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon and Gabon; in Gabon: Estuaire

Habitat: evergreen forests, riparian forests; in Gabon at 80 m altitude, elsewhere at 20–750 m.

## Source note

We have studied only a single sterile Gabonese specimen of this large-leaved tree, collected in the Estuaire. Its leaf morphology and its presence in Cameroon allow us to suppose its presence in Gabon. D. similis has broadly oblong leaves with a robust petiole, capable of reaching larger sizes than any other species of the genus present in the country. They have yellowish primary and secondary veins often becoming blackish when dry, as in D. aylmeri and D. moliwensis (both with imbricate sepals) and D. arborescens, all with unilocular ovaries and capitate stigmas. The flowers and fruits of D. similis resemble those of D. arborescens, but they are slightly larger and the sepals wider. The bud scales of D. similis are rather large, triangular, ovate and hard, and when present, they are useful for identification.
