# *Begonia anisosepala* Hook.f.

**Genus**: [[Begonia]]
**Family**: [[Begoniaceae]]

## Description

Plant reaching 10 cm in height. Leaves peltate; petiole 1–9 cm, hirsute; blade spreading when fresh, not bullate, very asymmetric, reniform to broadly elliptic-ovate or broadly elliptic-obovate, (3.5–)4.5–15 × (2.5–)3–7.5 cm, apex rounded to acute, margin crenate or crenate-dentate, upper surface without or with a few long hairs. Inflorescence: peduncle 1–4.5 cm, with 2–5 ♂ flowers and 1 ♀ flower with yellow or white tepals; bracts 2–3.5 mm long. ♂ Flower: tepals 7–12 × 5.5–11 mm; androecium with 16–26 stamens. ♀ Flower: pedicel elongating in fruit to 6.5 mm; tepals 7–11 × 9–10 mm; ovary (including wings) obtriangular depressed or obtriangular-obovoid depressed, 4.5–7 × 8–13 mm, without long hairs, with 3–4 obtriangular very depressed to obovate depressed wings, 3–6 mm long; styles 3–4. Fruit as the ovary, but 4.5–10 × 8.5–16.5 mm; beak up to 1.5 mm.

## Distribution

southern Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea and northwestern Gabon (Cristal Mountains and surroundings of Ndjolé)

## Ecology

on slopes, river beds or hillsides in primary or secondarized forest, often at the edge of watercourses; 50–600 m altitude. B: Sosef (1994). BIBLIOGRAPHY
