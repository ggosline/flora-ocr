# *Aframomum letestuanum* Gagnep.
**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Aframomum]]
## Description
Often pendent herb to 3 m, with red sheaths, sessile cordate-auriculate blades 20–40 × 3–10 cm and almost no ligule. Inflorescence axis 50–100 cm bears about ten one-flowered spikes. Flowers purple-violet with broad 6 cm labellum. Known only from Gabon.
