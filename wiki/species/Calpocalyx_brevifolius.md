# *Calpocalyx brevifolius* Villiers

**Genus**: [[Calpocalyx]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: BMNHN 6 (3): 300, tab. 2 (1984)

## Description

Large tree. Old branches glabrous, blackish when dry, with brown corky longitudinal bands. Young branches puberulent, yellowish, ± flattened especially at the nodes. Petiole glabrous or sparsely pubescent, 2.2–4.5 cm long, flat to concave above, triangular in section, bearing at the apex of the upper face a prominent ellipsoid gland, fleshy ridge at the apex of the lower face. Pinnae axis sparsely pubescent (sometimes glabrous), 5–9.5 cm long, ± flattened laterally at least towards the apex, upper face plane, keeled towards the apex, bearing a ± cylindric prominent gland between the 1–3 pairs of terminal leaflets. Leaflets 4–5 pairs, the basal pair often reduced to an outer leaflet. Petiolule folded in a narrow gutter, sparsely pubescent, 2.5–3 mm long. Blade glabrous above and ± pubescent (hairs appressed, very difficult to see when dry) to glabrous below; black to blackish-brown above and brown below when dry; ovate to elliptic for the basal leaflets, and elliptic-oblong or oblanceolate for the terminal leaflets, reaching 6.5 cm in length; base weakly asymmetric, very attenuate to subcordate for the basal leaflets and attenuate for the terminal leaflets; apex progressively broadly acuminate-obtuse, apiculate, with margins folded towards the upper face. Midvein strongly concave above and prominent, sparsely pubescent below. Secondary veins ascending, rectilinear until branching; weakly prominent on both faces. Tertiary veins in a network with very loose polygonal meshes, very little or not visible on both faces. Resin network fine, blackish-brown above, concave on both faces.

1. A specimen with the same number is determined as Piptadeniastrum africanum.

Solitary axillary spike, 7–9.5 cm long. Axis flattened, ribbed. Interfloral bracteole densely pubescent, very arched, lanceolate, approximately 1 mm long, very shortly unguiculate, apex very acute. Flower bud obovoid with contorted staminal filaments. Flowers sessile. Calyx obconic, ± pubescent, 1.5–1.75 mm long, sepals fused, free lobes very broadly triangular, approximately 0.30 mm long, with thickened acute apex. Petals pubescent externally except at the base, 3–3.5 mm long, fused to each other, free lobes lanceolate, 1.25 mm long, apex obtuse or acute. Stamens 10, ± fused to each other at the base and to the petals; filaments cream, 6.5 mm long, widening towards the apex; anther oblong, 0.75 mm long, with connective prolonged by a pedicellate globose gland. Pistillode sparsely pubescent, 0.75 mm long, lacking a style. ♢ Flower, sessile. Perianth and androecium as before. Pistil shortly stipitate; ovary pubescent, ellipsoid-obovoid, 1 mm long, with 5–8 ovules; style 5–6.5 mm long.

Fruit unknown. – Pl. 27, p. 105.

## Type

Le Testu 1732, Gabon (holo-, P ; iso-, P, K, LISC)

## Discussion

C. brevifolius is currently known only from southern Gabon.

## Specimens examined

Le Testu 1732, Tchibanga (fl., mai).
