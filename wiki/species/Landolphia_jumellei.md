# *Landolphia jumellei* (Pierre ex Jum.) Pichon Planche 54

**Genus**: [[Landolphia]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Mém. Inst. Franç. Afrique Noire 35 : 196 (1953)

## Synonyms

- Carpodinus fulva Pierre ex Hallier f., Jarhb. Hamb. Wiss. Anst. 17, Beih. 3 : 108 (1900).

## Description

Large liana; branches sparsely to densely hirsute. Leaf: petiole 4–20 mm, pilose; blade obovate to broadly elliptic or broadly obovate, 1–4 times as long as wide, 4.5–15 × 2–9 cm, cuneate at base, generally acuminate at apex, pilose above, more densely so on the midrib and secondary veins, hirsute below on the midrib and secondaries and otherwise tomentose; secondary veins 5 to 13 pairs, ± curved, tertiary venation scalariform, impressed above, visible below. Inflorescence axillary, compact, with 4–8 flowers. Flower: Sepal whorl up to 6 mm, bracteate with 5 sepal lobes patent, unequal, 2–4.3 times as long as wide, 2.3–3.4 mm long, outer pilosities with long hairs in the centre and along the edge; corolla orange or yellow, with tube thickened just below the anthers and at the level of the throat, 4–5 mm long, 1.1–2.2 mm wide at the level of the anthers and 1–2.1 mm at the throat, the upper parts of the exterior tomentose or pilose, the interior pilose around the anthers, with oval to obovate lobes, 1.2–2 times as long as wide, 1.5–3.5 times as long as the tube, 2–3 × 1–2.5 mm, densely pilose with long hairs on and around the auricles, ciliate; stamens inserted at 0.45–0.6 of the tube length, anther 1–1.2 mm long; pistil 2.9–4.2 mm long, ovary pilose; style glabrous. Fruit irregularly ovoid to obovoid, 3–5 cm in diameter, red to reddish, with warts and bosses, with 3 to 15 seeds; wall 0.4–1.5 cm thick; pulp yellow or orange. Distribution: Cameroon and Gabon: Gabon, Estuaire, Ngounié, Ogooué, Ivindo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem. Ecology: primary and secondary forests up to 650 m altitude. B: Persoon et al. (1992), Pichon (1953). BIBLIOGRAPHY
