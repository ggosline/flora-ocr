# *Landolphia jumellei* (Pierre ex Jum.) Pichon Planche 54

**Genus**: [[Landolphia]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Mém. Inst. Franç. Afrique Noire 35 : 196 (1953)

## Description

Large liana; branchlets sparsely to densely hirsute. Leaf: petiole 4–20 mm, hairy; leaflets oblong-obovate or broadly elliptic to broadly obovate, 1–4 times as long as wide, 2.5–12 cm long, cuneate at base, generally acuminate at apex, hairy above, more densely so on the midvein and secondary veins, hirsute below on the midvein and secondary veins and otherwise tomentose; secondary veins 5 to 13 pairs, ± curved, tertiary venation scalariform, impressed above, visible below. Inflorescence axillary, compact, with 5–15 flowers. Flower: Sepals up to 4 mm, bracteate with 5 sepals or some lobes spreading, unequal, 2–4.3 times as long as wide, 2.3–3.4 mm long, exterior pilose with long hairs at centre and along the edge; corolla orange or yellow, with tube thickened just below the anthers and at the level of the throat, 4–5 mm long, 1.1–2.2 mm wide at the level of the anthers and 1–2.1 mm at the throat, the upper portions of the exterior tomentose or pilose, the interior pilose around the anthers, with oval to obovate lobes, 1.2–2 times as long as wide, 1.5–4 times as long as the tube, 1.5–3.2 mm, densely pilose with long hairs on and around the auricles, ciliate; stamens inserted at 0.45–0.6 of the tube length, anther 1–1.2 mm long; pistil 2.9–4.2 mm long, ovary pilose; style glabrous. Fruit irregularly ovoid to obovoid, 3–8 cm in diameter, red to reddish with warts and bosses, with 3 to 15 seeds; wall 0.4–1.5 cm thick; pulp yellow or orange. Distribution: Cameroon and Gabon: Gabon, Estuary, Ngounié, Ogooué, Ivindo, Ogooué Maritime and Woleu-Ntem. Ecology: Primary and secondary forests up to 650 m altitude. B: Persoon et al. (1992), Pichon (1953). BIBLIOGRAPHY
