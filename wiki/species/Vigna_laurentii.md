# *Vigna laurentii* De Wild.

**Genus**: [[Vigna]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Mission Laurent 1 : 122 (1905)

## Description

Vigna spec. A Verdc., Fl. trop. E. Afr., Leg.-Pap. 4(2) : 635 (1971). Trailing and/or twining herb, reaching 1 m in height. Stipules 2.5–4 mm long, pubescent, prolonged below the insertion by two unequal and rounded lobes. Leaf: petiole 1–3 cm; rachis 3–5 mm; stipels 1 mm long; leaflets with petiolule 1–2 mm, lamina oblong to oblong-lanceolate, 1.5–6 × 0.5–1.5 cm, rounded at the base, rounded-apiculate at the apex, sparsely pubescent on both surfaces. Inflorescence: peduncle slender, 2–15 cm; rachis up to 1 cm; bracts oval, 0.5–1.5 mm long. Flower: bracteoles ± 0.5 mm long; calyx sparsely puberulent, with tube ± 2 mm long, lobes triangular, 0.5–1.5 mm long; corolla blue, 5–9 mm long, glabrous, standard subsessile, obovate, 6–7 × 7–8 mm, wings oblanceolate, ± 7 × 2 mm, keel falcate, 6 mm long, obtuse; ovary with 3 to 5 ovules; style prolonged beyond the stigma by a short beak. Pod pendulous, linear, cylindric, 1.5–2 cm × 3.5–4 mm, with 4 or 5 seeds, black, glabrous. Seed irregularly ellipsoid, ± 2 × 1 mm; strophiole eccentric.

## Distribution

from Burkina Faso and Ghana to Uganda, Tanzania and Zambia; in Gabon, known from Haut-Ogooué

## Ecology

marshes and wet places, savannas; in Gabon at 420 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1200 m. Fruits in March. B: Verdcourt (1971c), Wilczek (1954). Bibliography
