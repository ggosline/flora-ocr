# *Rhynchospora perrieri* Cherm. Planche 124

**Genus**: [[Rhynchospora]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Bot. France 69 : 721 (1923)

## Description

Annual herb, slender, 10–50 cm tall; stem 2–25 cm × 0.3–1 mm, triangular, glabrous. Basal leaves without or with a short blade, of increasing length upward; sheath at most 4 cm long, brownish near base, green higher up; blade linear, flat, acuminate, flexuous, glabrous or scabrous along the margins toward the apex, the largest 10–40 cm × 0.6–2 mm. Inflorescence usually formed of 3–4 groups of 2–10 spikelets, rather dense and elongate, scarcely exceeding the leaf sheaths; primary branches with sheaths 1–2 cm long and blades 2–30 cm long. Spikelet ovoid–lanceolate, 3.5–5 × 0.7–1.5 mm, brown, 1–2 flowers; lower sterile glumes 2–2.6 mm long, the fertile ones ovate–lanceolate, 3–4.5 mm long, glabrous, apex acute. Flower: perianth absent; stamens 2, filaments 3–4 mm long, anthers 1.6–1.8 mm long; style with 2 long branches. Achene obovoid to obtriangular, 1–1.5 × 1.2–1.4 mm, strongly transversely rugose, grey to almost black; base of style capping the apex and the "shoulders" of the achene.

## Distribution

widespread but scattered in tropical Africa and Madagascar; rare or poorly known in Gabon, found only in Ogooué-Ivindo and Ogooué-Maritime (3 collections)

## Ecology

marshes and wet places, sometimes along watercourses; in Gabon and elsewhere, at 0–500 m altitude. b: Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al. (2010), Hooper & Napper (1972), Bibliography Raynal (1971), Robinson (1961a)
