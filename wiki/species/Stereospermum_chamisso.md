# *Stereospermum chamisso*

**Genus**: [[Stereospermum]]
**Family**: [[Bignoniaceae]]
**Protologue**: Linnaea 7 : 720 (1832) ; SPRAGUE, FTA 4 (2) ; 519 (1906) ; HEINE, FWTA, ed. 2, 2 : 386 (1963) ; VAN STEENIS, Fl. Malesiana, sér. 1, 8 (2) : 145 (1977) ; GENTRY, Fl. Cam. 27 : 46 (1984)

## Description

SPRAGUE, FTA 4 (2) ; 519 (1906) ; HEINE, FWTA, ed. 2, 2 : 386 (1963) ; VAN STEENIS, Fl. Malesiana, sér. 1, 8 (2) : 145 (1977) ; GENTRY, Fl. Cam. 27 : 46 (1984).

– Hieranthes RAF., Sylv. Tell.: 79 (1838).

– Dipterosperma Hassk., Flora 25, Beibl. 1 : 28 (1842).

Trees. Leaves opposite, compound, imparipinnate.

Inflorescences terminal paniculate. Calyx cupular to tubular-campanulate, subtruncate to irregularly bilabiate. Corolla lavender or clear magenta to white, tubular-campanulate above a narrowed base. Stamens didynamous, staminode very small. Ovary linear, ± tetragone, glabrous. Disc present.

Capsules linear, cylindric, with thickened septum. Seeds biwings, sunk in the depressions of the septum; seed body thick, triangular and longitudinally folded; wings hyaline membranous.

TYPE SPECIES: S. kunthianum Cham.

Approximately 19 species; 5 in Asia, 4 in continental Africa, and 10 in Madagascar. Only one is present in Gabon. Two other species are known from Zaïre and Cameroon; they are apparently restricted to drier habitats and thus may not be represented in Gabon.

Stereospermum acuminatissimum K. Schumann

in ENGL. & PRANTL, Nat. Pflanzenfam. 4 (3b): 243 (1895); SPRAGUE, FTA 4 (2): 519 (1906); HEINE, FWTA, ed. 2, 2: 386 (1963); GENTRY, Fl. Cam. 27: 46, tab. 14 (1984).

Tree 6–25 (–30 according to HEINE) m tall, at least 40 cm in diameter; branchlets subcylindric, slightly flattened at the nodes, glabrous, usually black when dry contrasting with lighter lenticels. Pseudostipules linear, approximately 5 mm long. Leaves with 7–21 leaflets; leaflets narrowly elliptic to oblong or narrowly ovate, acuminate, asymmetrically rounded to broadly cuneate at the base, 4–14 × 2.5 cm; entire, sparsely scaly along the midvein above, very finely scaly-punctate throughout below, with conspicuous circular glands at the base; petiolules 1–2 mm long, rachis strongly angular, furrowed below, articulation not prominent.

Terminal inflorescence, clearly paniculate. Pedicels slender, 0.5–1 cm long, glabrous with 2 prominent bracteoles per bud, these 1–2 mm long and densely ciliate with multicellular hairs. Calyx tubular-campanulate, 11–14 mm long, 4–6 mm wide, bilabiate sparsely scaly. Corolla magenta with darker lines in the throat, tubular-campanulate above a narrow basal tube, 3.5–5 cm long, 1–1.3 cm wide at the mouth of the tube, tube 2.5–4 cm long, lobes 0.6–1 cm long; glabrous externally except for sparse conspicuous circular glands scarcely visible, lobes distinctly ciliate externally with long, wavy, multicellular hairs; internally with flexuose multicellular hairs at the level of stamen insertion and at the base of the tube. Stamens didynamous; anthers thin, divaricate, 2 mm long. Pistil 2.2–2.4 cm long; ovary linear, strongly tetragone, 5–9 mm long, 0.5–1 mm wide, glabrous. Disc cupular pulvinate, 1 mm long, 1.5 mm wide.

Capsule linear, cylindric, 41–65 cm long, 0.8–1.2 cm wide, glabrous, dark brown when dry with scattered lenticels. Seeds biwings, 0.5–1.1 cm long, 2.8–3.3 cm long, wings thin and hyaline membranous, body longitudinally folded, thick and wedge-shaped. – Pl. 10, p. 45.
