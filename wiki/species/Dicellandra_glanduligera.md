# *Dicellandra glanduligera* (Pellegrin) Jacques-Félix

**Genus**: [[Dicellandra]]
**Family**: [[Melastomataceae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia, sér. 4, 14 (1) : 95, tab. 8 (1974)

## Synonyms

- Petalonema glanduligerum PELLEGR., Bull. Mus. natn. Hist. nat., Paris, 30 : 326 (1924).
- Neopetalonema glanduligerum (PELLEGR.) JACQ.-FÉL., Icon. Pl. Afric. 3 : tab. 60 (1955).

## Description

Low plant, furfuraceous on its young parts; stem simple, up to 0.20–0.30 m, with short internodes, at first prostrate, rooting then erect, quadrangular. Leaf with petiole 6–10 cm, furfuraceous to hirsute toward the apex; blade up to 10 × 18 cm, elliptic-oblong to elliptic-lanceolate, rounded or narrowly cordate at the base, abruptly and briefly acuminate at the apex; slightly bullate with a few scattered short hairs on the upper surface, furfuraceous on the veins on the lower surface; 3–5 (7) prominent veins beneath, the transverse ones substantially parallel, but scarcely more prominent than the reticulate tertiary veinlets; margins serrulate-ciliate.

Panicle oblong, approximately 4 cm, 10–20-flowered, with 3 to 4 pairs of briefly but distinctly pedunculate cymes; flowers glomerate with pedicel 2 to 4 mm. Flower narrowly campanulate; hypanthium obconical, 3 × 5 mm, furfuraceous; calyx spreading, 2.5 mm high, with sepal lobes broadly triangular-rounded, distinctly keeled on the back. Corolla purple or pink; petals thick, triangular, 10 mm long, widely inserted at the base, acuminate-subulate at the apex. Stamens homomorphic but slightly unequal: anther 5 mm, linear, attenuate at the apex, sessile; connective with 2 anterior tubercles and a linear spur; filament 5 mm. Ovary deeply included in the tube, then reaching two-thirds in the fruit; adnate along almost its entire length by the septa; apex truncate, papillose, with a marginal corona, membranous, ciliate-glandular, 0.8–1 mm high; style linear, 10 mm; stigma finely capitate; placentas narrowly stipitate.

Fruit (immature) 5 mm in diameter, globose, crowned by the persistent calyx; apex of the ovary concave or truncate with marginal rim. Seeds papillose. — Pl. 37, p. 99.
