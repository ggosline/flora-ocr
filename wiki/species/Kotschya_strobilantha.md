# *Kotschya strobilantha* (Welw. ex Baker) J.Dewit & Duvign. var. strobilantha Planche 73

**Genus**: [[Kotschya]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Roy. Bot. Belg. 86 : 213 (1954)

## Description

Shrub 0.75–2 m tall; branches glabrous to pubescent or hispid; stipules, bracts, bracteoles and calyx glabrous to villous. Stipules persistent, fused into a sheath 3–4 mm around the stem, ovate, 11–15 × 1–2 mm, acute to acuminate, membranous. Leaf: petiole 5–16 mm; rachis 5–15 mm, leaflets 6 to 8, ± digitate, subsessile, with falcate lamina, 9–18 × 2–4 mm, glabrous, with ciliate-denticulate to entire margin, with 3 or 4 basal nerves. Inflorescence in dense racemes 1–2.5 cm long; bracts semi-orbicular, 3–6 × 2–4 mm. Flower: pedicel 2–4 mm; bracteoles free, elliptic-falcate, 3–5 × 1–2.5 mm; calyx 3–6 mm long, upper lip distinctly emarginate, lower lip trilobed with ovate, rounded lobes; corolla blue, pink or white, standard 6–7 × 3–3.5 mm, emarginate, with a few setae in the notch. Legume with 1 or 2 articles 3–5 × 2–3 mm, the terminal one longer and obliquely acuminate, pubescent. Seed not seen.

## Distribution

Democratic Republic of Congo, Angola and Malawi; its presence in Gabon is mentioned by Raponda-Walker & Sillans (1961), but is not yet confirmed by herbarium specimens

## Ecology

shrubby savannas, open forests, water margins, sandy soils; up to 1900 m altitude. Uses: Potential ornamental plant

## Source note

In the South-East of the Democratic Republic of Congo (Katanga), there exists a slender form, a suffrutex 15–30 cm, with leaflets only 5–7 × 1–2 mm, recognized as var. kundelunguensis Dewit & Duvign. b: Dewit & Duvigneaud (1954), Raponda-Walker & Sillans (1961), Verdcourt (2000). Bibliography
