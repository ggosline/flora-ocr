# *Adenia staudtii* Harms Planche 25.4, 30

**Genus**: [[Adenia]]
**Family**: [[Passifloraceae]]

## Description

in Engl. & Prantl., Nat. Pflanzenfam. 3, 6a, Nachtr. 1 : 255 (1897). Herbaceous vine, reaching 8 m in length; stem at base reaching 4 mm in diameter. Stipules persistent, rudimentary, triangular, ± 1 mm in length. Leaf with petiole 2–11 cm; lamina reniform, up to 20 × 14 cm, entire, 3(–5)-palmatilobed, base peltate, lobes with narrowed base and rounded sinus, apex acute, papyraceous; peltate base with 0 or 2 glands 1 mm (sub-)marginal, laminar glands 0–6, sub-marginal; sterile tendrils little branched, up to 20 cm. Inflorescence: the male comprising up to 40 flowers, the female up to 10 flowers; tendril present or not; peduncle slender, up to 5 cm (male flowers) or 2.5 cm (female flowers); bracts and bracteoles triangular, 1 mm in length, acute, margin dentate. Male flower with pedicel 5–21 mm and stipe 5–7 mm, cylindrical, 15–28 × 4–7 mm, pale yellow or white with greenish base; hypanthium cupuliform, 2.5–5 mm, 5-chambered, partitions 1.5–4 mm in height; sepals connate into a tube reaching 18 mm, with lobes broadly ovate, 3–6 mm in length, ± obtuse, margins shortly laciniate; petals linear-oblanceolate, 8–12 mm in length, acute at apex, with shortly fimbriate margins; corona composed of very fine tangled filaments, 1–1.5 mm in length, hypanthium glands scaly, 1–1.5 mm in length; stamens inserted at the base of the hypanthium, filaments 10–23 mm, connate at the base for 1/2–1/3, anthers 6–7 mm; pistillode 0.5–1 mm. Female flower like the male flower, but with pedicel 5–10 mm and stipe 2–3 mm, up to 30 × 8–10 mm; hypanthium 2–4 mm, with partitions 1.5 mm in height; sepals connate into a tube reaching 20 mm, with ovate lobes 5–7 mm and ciliate margins; petals linear-filiform; corona 2 mm in length; staminodes 10–15 mm; ovary ellipsoid, 6–8 × 2.5–5 mm, gynophore 8–14 mm; styles connate for (1–)3–5 mm, with recurved branches 2–3 mm, stigmas flabelliform, deeply laciniate, 4–8 mm. Fruits 1–2(–3) per inflorescence, globose, 4–6.5 × 3–4.5 cm, with smooth, coriaceous and spongy pericarp, gynophore 1.5–3 mm. Seeds 20–45 per capsule, ovate, 8–9 × 6.5–7 mm, with 6–8 foveoles along a meridional line.

## Distribution

from Cameroon and the Central African Republic to Angola; very rare in Gabon, known only from the Lastoursville region (Ogooué-Lolo).

## Ecology

Ombrophilous forests, swamp forests, forest galleries, clearings, fallows; in Gabon at approximately 300 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1000 m. Flowers observed in August and December. b : De Wilde (1971), Robyns (1995). Bibliography
