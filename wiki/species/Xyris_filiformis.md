# *Xyris filiformis* Lam.

**Genus**: [[Xyris]]
**Family**: [[Xyridaceae]]
**Protologue**: Tabl. encycl. 1 : 133 (1791)

## Description

Annual herb, sometimes densely gregarious, highly variable in size depending on moisture conditions. Leaf: sheath reddish-brown, smooth, with glabrous margins but the rest densely rugose-papillose, widening toward the base; ligule absent; blade linear, 5–10 cm × 0.5–1 mm, flattened, papillose on the margin, apex solid, obtuse, asymmetric. Inflorescence: peduncle 16–28 cm × 0.5 mm, smooth or with weak longitudinal ridges; spike ellipsoid, becoming obotrianglular, truncate, initially 2–3 mm in diameter, then 6–8 mm at maturity; sterile bracts widely elliptic, approximately 3 × 2 mm, brown, smooth; fertile bracts comparable but wider, approximately 5 × 3 mm, with a distinct midvein from the base and 1–2 pairs of lateral veins diverging at the base and converging with the midvein below the apex, often dark with small narrowly elliptic dorsal markings. Flower: lateral sepals 4–4.5 mm long, reaching 1.5 mm wide, widening toward the apex, the keel with a few obtuse teeth; corolla blue to mauve, tube approximately 3 mm long, lobes widely obovate, 2.5–3 mm long; stamens approximately 1 mm long; ovary ellipsoid; style branched, approximately 1 mm, widened and fimbriate at the apex. Fruit obovoid, 2–3 mm long, smooth, brown. Ripe seeds ellipsoid, approximately 0.2–0.3 × 0.1 mm, with longitudinal ridges.

## Distribution

very widespread on the west coasts of Africa, from Sénégal to Gabon; in Gabon mainly on the coasts of Estuaire and Ogooué-Maritime and in the environs of Mouila
