# *Canna bidentata* Bertoloni

**Family**: [[Cannaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Canna]]

## Description

Glabrous erect rhizomatous herb. Leaf blade ovate-elliptic, slightly asymmetric below, acuminate, to about 45 × 20–25 cm. Inflorescence exceeds the leaves and is little or not branched. Flowers shortly pedicellate, red-orange; sepals 1.3–1.5 cm, petals lanceolate c. 4 cm. Two staminodes to 6 cm, expanded to 1–1.5 cm and notched apically; labellum linear. Ovary and capsule strongly muricate; very hard seeds in fruits c. 4 × 2–2.5 cm.

## Ecology and uses

Subspontaneous and frequent around villages. Reported medicinally against dysentery, worms and fever, and in magical practices.
