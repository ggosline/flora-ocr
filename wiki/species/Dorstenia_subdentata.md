# *Dorstenia subdentata* M. Hijman & C.C. Berg

**Genus**: [[Dorstenia]]
**Family**: [[Moraceae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia, sér. 2, 16 (4): 428, tab. 2 (1977)

## Description

Herbs with creeping rhizomes ca. 5 mm thick and ascending stems ca. 25 cm tall with dense, short and long hairs, white, straight to curved, or uncinate; internodes 1.5–5 cm long and 2.5–5 mm thick. Leaves spiral. Blade elliptic to oblong, widest at or above the middle, 10–21 × 3.5–8 cm, (finely) chartaceous, acuminate with acute acumen; base subobtuse; margin irregular, denticulate; upper face hairy with long, white, straight hairs, sometimes uncinate; lower face with fairly dense short and long hairs, straight to curved and white or uncinate, especially on the veins; midvein and secondary veins slightly impressed above, but plane toward the apex and even slightly prominent; veins prominent below; 7–9 pairs of secondary veins. Petiole 10–25 mm long and ca. 1 mm thick, with indumentum similar to that of the stem. Stipules persistent, subulate or narrowly triangular, 2–4 mm long and 1 mm wide at the base with straight, short, white, fairly dense hairs.

Inflorescences solitary. Peduncle 12–20 mm long and ca. 1 mm in diameter, with dense short and long hairs, white uncinate or curved. Receptacle discoid (but young ones obscurely infundibuliform). Receptacle and flowering surface plane suborbicular, (8–)10–15 mm in diameter; margin composed of ca. 25 lobes of variable shapes and sizes, obtuse, at least 1 mm in length and width; external face of receptacle and lobes with small white curved hairs or uncinate hairs, mixed with longer, larger and more scattered curved hairs. Flowers numerous, indistinct; perianth 2- or 3-lobed; with 2 or 3 stamens with filaments ca. 0.2 mm long and anthers ca. 0.1 × 0.1 mm with connective as wide as the locules. ♀ flowers ca. 16; perianth discoid, ca. 0.3 mm in diameter and 0.1 mm in height; style not exceeding the perianth; stigmas 2, spreading, ca. 0.4 mm long. – Pl. 13, p. 47.

## Type

Descoings 6517, Gabon (holo-, P)

## Discussion

The inflorescence resembles that of D. picta. D. subdentata is distinguished from it by its fairly long hairs on the upper face of the leaves. Such indumentum is found in some East African species, such as D. brownii RENDLE (Journ. Bot. 53: 299, 1915).

This species is known only from a single specimen from Gabon and a specimen from Congo (De Néré 645, Lefini, Mayama, P).

## Specimens examined

Descoings 6517, vallée de la Nyanga, galerie forestière de la Douli, 100 km S Tchibanga (fl., déc.).
