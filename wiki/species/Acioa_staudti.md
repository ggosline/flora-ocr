# *Acioa staudti* Engler

**Genus**: [[Acioa]]
**Family**: [[Scytopetalaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. 26 : 379 (1899); Pflanzenw. Afr. 3 (1) : 309 (1915); DE WILD., BJBB 7 : 207 (1920)

## Description

Pflanzenw. Afr. 3 (1) : 309 (1915); DE WILD., BJBB 7 : 207 (1920).

— A. talbotii Bak. f., Cat. Pl. Talbot. : 29 (1913); De Wild., l.c. : 206 (1920); Keay, FWTA, ed. 2, 1 (2) : 433 (1958), syn. nov.

Shrub of several meters in height sometimes or small tree (14 m fide ENGLER, l.c. : 309). Young branches covered with dense yellow-orange to greyish villosity, later glabrescencing tardily. Stipules elongate-triangular, 5-6 mm, somewhat falcate, sétulous on the exterior. Petiole 2-5 mm, villous. Leaf blade elliptic-oblong, 4-5(-7) × 8-12(-14) cm, broadly acute at the extremities, with triangular acumen ± long (0.5-1 cm), sometimes mucronate; midvein sparsely villous above then glabrescencing, villous below; lateral nerves, 8-10(-12) pairs, and nervules as well as the lower surface of the blade ± sparsely villous to glabrescencing; blade slightly scabrous above; often with a few small circular glands beneath the blade near the base and especially under the acumen, frequently remote from the midvein.

Racemes solitary terminal and axillary subterminaf, few-flowered, with axis not exceeding 3(-4.5) cm with subnull peduncle sometimes bearing a few rare deeply tricuspid bracts; peduncle, axis, bracts, pedicels and bracteoles bearing abundant villosity of long yellowish hairs. Bracts lanceolate 4 mm, glabrous internally with marginal denticulation scarcely visible to the naked eye, the teeth sometimes forming very shortly stipitate glands; portion of the pedicels below the articulation not exceeding 5 mm, bearing at the apex 2 subopposite bracteoles, glabrous internally, 1-1.5 mm in height, tapering to the extremity and (in a manner scarcely visible to the naked eye) broadly triangular toward the base, with 2-4 glandular appendages, or even stipitate glands reaching ca. 0.5 mm in height. Pedicel of the disarticulated flower 2-4 mm, scarcely distinct. Receptacle 12-20 mm, villous; sepals 6-8 mm, likewise villous for the parts exposed externally in the bud; petals white, broadly obovate with rounded to subcordate base, 5-6 mm in height; androecium 30-40-50 mm, 15-20 filaments.

Infructescence and fruit unknown. — PL. 18, p. 59.

## Type

Staudt 263, Cameroun (holo-, B, delet.; iso-, K! P!)

## Discussion

TAXONOMIC NOTES:

It appears that the approximation of Acioa talbotii Bak. f. (known from the sole type specimen, Talbot 1533, K!, P!) with A. staudtii Engl. cannot at present be called into doubt, despite secondary differences in the somewhat greater size of various elements of the Nigerian specimen; such variations are also found among the now available Gabonese specimens of the second species. DE WILDEMAN (l.c. : 206), after BAKER f., mentions for A. talbotii Bak. f.: "Floral peduncles simple very rarely forked"; this observation appears to result only from the compact structure of a simple raceme with short axis.

PHYTOGEOGRAPHIC NOTES:

Acioa staudtii Engl. is known from southeastern Nigeria, Cameroon and Gabon.
