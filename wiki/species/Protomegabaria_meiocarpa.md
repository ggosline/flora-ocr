# *Protomegabaria meiocarpa* J.Léonard Planche 25

**Genus**: [[Protomegabaria]]
**Family**: [[Phyllanthaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Jard. Bot. Nat. Belg . 64 : 60 (1995)

## Description

Tree up to 15 m tall; branchlets pubescent, quickly glabrescent. Stipules oval, 3–4 mm long, pubescent on the outer surface. Leaf: petiole (1.5–)2–7(–10) cm, pubescent to glabrescent; blade oblong-elliptic or sometimes obovate, (9–)11–28 × 5–10.5 cm, obtuse to cuneate at the base, acute to obtuse at the apex, glabrous above, glabrous beneath or often pubescent along the midvein and at the base of the lateral veins, and furnished with numerous small reddish pustules; lateral veins (10–)12–16 pairs. Inflorescence solitary to ternate, ± glabrous, the male ones 9–12 cm long, the female ones 4–6 cm, but up to 17 cm in fruit. Male flower: pedicel 1.5–2.5 cm; sepals oblong, 1.5 mm long, ciliolate at the apex; stamens 0.5–2 mm long; pistillode not lobed, 0.5 mm long, glabrous. Female flower: pedicel 2–3 mm, 7–10 mm in fruit; sepals oval, 1.5–2 mm long; ovary subglobose, 1.5–2 mm in diameter, glabrous; stigmas 1 mm long. Fruit subglobose, 14–17 × 12–14 mm, glabrous, with 6 prominent ribs. Seeds not seen.

## Distribution

known from eastern Democratic Republic of the Congo and fairly recently recorded from Gabon from two collections, one from the Cristal Mountains, the other from the southwest of Mount Pelé in the Nyanga

## Ecology

primary forest; in Gabon, at 450–600 m altitude. b: Breteler (2012c), Léonard (1995b). Bibliography
