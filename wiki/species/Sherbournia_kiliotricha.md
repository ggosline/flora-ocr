# *Sherbournia kiliotricha* N. Hallé, sp. nov.

**Genus**: [[Sherbournia]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]

## Description

(PL. 29, p. 133)

Climbing, voluble with slender internodes, hirsute, then brownish-scaly. Stipules 10-17 mm long, ± oblong, obtuse, slightly pilose. Petiole hirsute, 9 mm long. Lamina elliptic, subconcolorous, glabrous above, pilose below, base ± rounded, apex ± acute, with 7-10 pairs of secondary nerves, suddenly hirsute. Inflorescences short, pendulous, 4-7 flowered, pilose, with acute bracts. Calyx puberulous, purple, cupulate, with deltoid subobтuse teeth, 2 mm long. Corolla oblong, pink, ochraceo-pilose when dry, lobes 5, suborbicular, acute. Anthers apiculate, puberulous. Style glabrous, apex elliptic-claviform, sulcate. Disk fleshy, 4 mm diam., cupulate. Ovary villous, not sulcate when fresh.

Twining liane 2-5 m long, with young internodes slender reaching 10-24 cm in length for a width of 2-3 mm, and finely hirsute. Old internodes brown, scaly. Stipules 10-17 × 4-6 mm, elliptic or oblong, with obtuse apex, with loose pubescence. Petioles hirsute, 5-9 mm long. Blade brown, ± olivaceous above, rust ± reddish below, elliptic, 9-15 × 4-9 cm, with obtuse or rounded base, acute or subacute apex, acumen rarely distinct. Upper surface glabrous except sometimes the midvein; lower surface loosely villous with the main nerves hirsute. 7-10 pairs of secondary nerves; fine veinlets not distinct, the tertiaries at most visible on the lower surface.

Inflorescences pubescent, directed downward, 4-7 flowered, with common peduncle 2-3 mm, with small acute bracts 2-4 mm. Pedicels villous, 1-7 mm long, bearing at their apex a pair of prefloral leaves connate into a navicular form. Calyx clothed with small silvery-blonde hairs; lower part 3-4 × 2-3 mm; cylindric cup 6 mm in height and 5-6 mm in diam.; 5 triangular subobтuse lobes c. 2 mm long, 2.5-3 mm wide at base; the calycinal limb is bright incarnate pink. Corolla acute in bud, pale pink, with tube 14-20 × 6 mm, with 5 suborbicular apiculate lobes 4-6 mm, with dense ochraceus pubescence when dry, with a ring of internal hairs at the upper level of the narrow part.

Anthers 9 × 2.2 mm, finely pubescent, apiculate, inserted toward the lower third; pollen whitish. Style 18 mm long, with yellowish biapiculate stigma, 10-sulcate, elliptic, 7 × 3.5 mm, with glabrous purplish-rose column. Disk fleshy, cupuliform, purplish-rose. Ovary circular in section when fresh, containing two ovuliferous masses 3 × 1.2 mm; ovules half-embedded in the fleshy placenta. Fruit unknown.

## Type

N. Hallé 3610 (P) du Gabon, 29 déc. 1964

## Discussion

Species pendulous or sometimes shrubby in altitude thickets, known only from NE Gabon. "Thousand-hair Liane".

## Specimens examined

N. Hallé : 3610, 3621, Bélinga, Crête de Babiel et Grande Crête Sud, alt. 950-1000 m (fl. déc.).
