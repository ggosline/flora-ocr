# *Impatiens niamniamensis* Gilg

**Family**: [[Balsaminaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Impatiens]]

## Description

Succulent or slightly woody herb 0.2–1.5 m. Leaves alternate, ovate to lanceolate, 7–20 × 3–8 cm, petiole 15–90 mm with subulate glands; margins deeply crenate. Flowers 1–6 in axillary fascicles; lip a laterally compressed violet, purple or scarlet sac with a curved 4–10 mm spur; standard yellow or green, 10–12 mm; wings whitish or green, about 12 mm. Capsule to 17 mm, many-seeded.

## Distribution

Equatorial Africa from Mount Cameroon to Uganda. Gabon collections from Monts de Cristal, Makokou, Bélinga and upper Ngounié.
