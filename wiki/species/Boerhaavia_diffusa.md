# *Boerhaavia diffusa* L.

**Family**: [[Nyctaginaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Boerhaavia]]

## Description

Diffuse or prostrate branching herb with opposite unequal leaves and small flowers in slender branched inflorescences. The petaloid perianth is constricted above the ovary; the persistent lower portion encloses a ribbed one-seeded anthocarp.

## Distribution

Pantropical ruderal recorded in Gabon.
