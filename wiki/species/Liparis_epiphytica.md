# *Liparis epiphytica* Schltr.

**Genus**: [[Liparis]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 38 : 6 (1905)

## Description

– TYPE: Schlechter 12694, Congo (Brazzaville), holo-B del., iso - BR!, K!, P! - FTWA: 214 (1968); FAC 1: 280 (1984); FCAM 2: 358 (2001).

Pseudobulbs < 1.2 × 0.2–0.4 cm, ovoid, very crowded on a short rhizome. Leaves 5–11 × 0.6–0.9 mm, oblong-oblanceolate, obtuse, with a small apicule. Inflorescence 2.8–10 cm, 2–20-flowered. Flowers pale greenish-yellow. Dorsal sepal with revolute margins, linear to oblong-lanceolate, acute to subacute; lateral sepals semi-elliptic to obliquely elliptic-obovate, falcate, subobtuse. Petals filiform, subacute, margins revolute. Lip broadly obovate to obcordate, fleshy, apex rounded or slightly retuse, margins entire, basal auricles of the lip short. - Pl. 59, p. 123.

## Distribution

Côte d'Ivoire, Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Congo (Kinshasa), Rwanda, Uganda

## Ecology

alt. 300–2440 m. Epiphytic on the crown or trunk of large forest tree; collected in old-growth forest
