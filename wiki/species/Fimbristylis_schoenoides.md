# *Fimbristylis schoenoides* (Retz.) Vahl Planche 99

**Genus**: [[Fimbristylis]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Enum. pl. 2 : 286 (1805)

## Synonyms

- Scirpus schoenoides Retz., Obs. Bot. : 14 (1788).

## Description

Slender annual herb, tufted, with a weak root system, rarely with a more robust base; stems 10–50 cm long, angular. Leaf: sheath greenish;

Inflorescence usually formed of a single spikelet terminating the stem, more rarely with 1–2 additional pedunculate spikelets; involucral bracts 2, short. Spikelet ovate, 4–8 × 3–5 mm, but reaching 15 mm at fruiting; glumes about 3 mm long, very pale brown to straw yellow, glabrous, with several nerves, the central nerve reaching just the rounded apex. Flower: style with 2 ciliate branches. Achene obovoid, about 1.5 × 1.2 mm, brown, with finely reticulate surface, with isodimetric cells, base with a gynophore 0.3–0.5 mm long.

## Distribution

widely distributed in India and Malaysia, rare and perhaps introduced in Africa; rare in Gabon, found only in Estuaire, Ogooué-Ivindo and Ogooué-Maritime (3 collections)

## Ecology

on swampy soil, mainly in disturbed habitats; at low altitude

## Vernacular names

ditch fimbry (angl.)

## Discussion

Taxonomic note: very close to F. ferruginea, but easily distinguished from it by its solitary spikelet. B: Goetghebeur & Coudijzer (1984a), Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al. (2010), Hooper & Napper (1972).
