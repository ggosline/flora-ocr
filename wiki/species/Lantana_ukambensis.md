# *Lantana ukambensis* (Vatke) Verdc. Planche 37

**Genus**: [[Lantana]]
**Family**: [[Verbenaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. trop. E. Afr., Verbenac . : 43 (1992)

## Synonyms

- Lippia ukambensis Vatke, Linnaea 43 : 528 (1882).

## Description

Herb or sometimes shrub 0.2–1.5 m tall, aromatic; stem quadrangular, unarmed, pilose with long whitish hairs with tuberculate base, intermixed with very short varnished setae and scattered sessile glands. Leaves in whorls of 3 or sometimes opposite; petiole 1–5(–8) mm; blade ovate to ovate-elliptic, 1.7–10 × 0.5–5 cm, broadly cuneate to attenuate at base, acute to rounded at apex, denticulate-crenate or crenate, adaxial surface pilose with appressed hairs, rugose or flat, abaxial surface coarsely and densely velvety with hairs and glands greyish-yellow-green. Inflorescence in solitary or paired spike, conical, 0.3–5 cm long; peduncle short, generally not exceeding the base of the leaf, but elongating and becoming oblong in fruit; bracts elliptic-ovate or triangular-ovate, 8–20 × 5–13 mm, acute to cuspidate at apex, often violaceous. Flower: calyx 1–2.5 mm long, slender; corolla scarcely longer than the bracts, dark purple, crimson or magenta, sometimes with a white or yellow centre, tube 2–5 mm long, pilose outside, limb 1–3 mm in diameter, lobes rounded, 0.7–1.5 mm long. Fruit subglobose, 2–5 mm in diameter, blue or violet at maturity.

## Distribution

Native to tropical Africa, including Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Central African Republic and Angola; in Gabon, known from Haut-Ogooué

## Ecology

Open forests, grasslands, old plantations; in Gabon at approximately 550 m altitude, elsewhere at 75–2070 m
