## Description

**Habit**: shrub 0.6–2.5 m tall, with branchlets crowded at the apex. Branchlets green, with 4 longitudinal wings ± strongly prominent, ornamented with strong longitudinal striations and sometimes with small transverse striations. Petiole absent or short (1–4 mm), ± concave above, wrinkled beneath. Blade glabrous on both faces, dark glossy green or dull green on the upper face, pale dull green on the lower face, elliptic to lanceolate, sometimes obovate or sub-lanceolate, 6–12 (–16) × 2.2–7 cm; base cuneate or narrowed, apex acute, sometimes acuminate-apiculate, often mucronate. Midrib concave above, prominent beneath. Secondary nerves 6–9 pairs, ascending, arcuate, anastomosing 2–5 mm from the blade margin (the first pair or first two pairs strongly ascending to the middle of the blade), concave above, ± prominent beneath.

**Inflorescence**: short axillary raceme, few-flowered. Rachis flattened, zigzag in outline, flowers on the narrow faces of the rachis. Bracts very broadly ovate or orbicular, acuminate or apiculate at the apex, strongly keeled, ca. 1.25 × 1 mm. Floral bud 6 × 2 mm, cylindrical at the base, swollen and ovoid at the apex; petals valvate. Pedicel quadrangular. **Flowers**: pentamerous, glabrous, greenish, whitish, yellowish or pinkish, sometimes fragrant. Calyx cupuliform, with upper margin undulate or denticulate. Petals glabrous on both faces, free or very weakly fused at the base, linear, 8 × 1.5 mm, fleshy; apex acute, apiculate, reflexed outward. Stamens 3, alternipetalous, fused to the lateral edges of the petals, 6 mm long; filament fused to the edges of the petals, 5 × 0.75 mm, thickening towards the middle, narrowing at the apex and ending in a narrow, transversely wrinkled filament; anther introrse with 2 longitudinal dehiscence slits, medifixed. Staminodes 5, epipetalous, 7 mm long; filament identical to that of the stamens, 5 × 0.75 mm, ending in two lobes widened at the apex, ± narrowly juxtaposed or fused at the base. Disc thick, cupuliform, surrounding the base of the pistil. Ovary superior, conical or ovoid, furrowed, 2 mm long, with 3 incomplete uniovulate locules. Style slender, angular, 4 mm long, ending in a capitate 3-lobed stigma.

**Fruit**: drupe surrounded by the strongly accrescent calyx except at the apex, red at maturity, globose, depressed at the apex, 10 mm in diameter. Epicarp coriaceous and striate at the base. Endocarp hard and thick. **Seed**: solitary, globose and depressed at the apex, 12 × 8 mm, without integument, with granular surface. Albumen very hard. Embryo apical.

## Types

**LECTOTYPE**: *Mann s.n.*, Old Calabar, Nigeria, flowers and fruits, 1863 (holotype K; isotype P).

## Distribution

Range from Nigeria to the Democratic Republic of the Congo (lower Congo region). The species occurs in Cameroon and has been collected in Gabon at Makokou and Bélinga.

## Habitat and ecology

Dense forest, riverine and swamp forest. Grows on argillaceous-sandy, humid soils.

## Notes

Multiple synonyms have been established by Sleumer's systematic revision. The species represents a widespread element of the Guinea–Congolian rain forest block, with the Gabonese populations at its eastern limit.
