# *Olax mannii* Oliver

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Olax]]

Flora Tropical Africa 1: 349 (1868).

## Description

Shrub 0.6–2.5 m high, with branches at the apex. Branches green with four longitudinal wings ± strongly prominent, adorned with strong longitudinal striations and sometimes small transverse striations. Petiole absent or short (1–4 mm), ± concave above, wrinkled below. Blade glabrous on both surfaces, dark green shiny or dull on the upper surface, pale green dull on the lower surface, elliptic to lanceolate, sometimes obovate or lanceolate, 6–12 (–16) × 2.2–7 cm; base cuneate or narrowed, apex acute, sometimes acuminate apiculate, often mucronate. Midvein concave above, prominent below. Secondary nerves 6–9 pairs, ascending, arched, anastomosing 2–5 mm from the margin of the blade (the first pair or the first two pairs strongly ascending to the middle of the blade), concave above, ± prominent below.

**Inflorescence**: Short axillary raceme, pauciflorous. Rachis flattened, in a broken line, flowers on the narrow faces of the rachis. Bracts very broadly ovate or orbicular, acuminate or apiculate at the apex, strongly keeled, ± 1.25 × 1 mm. Floral bud ± 6 × 2 mm, cylindric at the base, swollen and ovoid at the apex; petals valvate. Pedicel quadrangular. Flower pentamerous, glabrous, greenish, whitish, yellowish or pinkish, sometimes fragrant. Calyx cupuliform, with upper margin undulate or denticulate. Petals glabrous on both surfaces, free or very weakly united at the base, linear, ± 8 × 1.5 mm, fleshy; apex acute apiculate reflected outward. Stamens 3, alternipetalous, fused to the lateral edges of the petals, 6 mm long; filament fused to the edges of the petals, ± 5 × 0.75 mm, thickening toward the middle, narrowing at the apex and terminating in a narrow filament, wrinkled transversely; anther introrse with 2 longitudinal dehiscence slits, medifixed. Staminodes 5, epipetalous, 7 mm long; filament identical to that of the stamens, ± 5 × 0.75 mm, terminating in two blades widened at the apex, ± narrowly juxtaposed or fused at the base. Disk thick cupuliform surrounding the base of the pistil. Ovary superior, conical or ovoid, furrowed, 2 mm long, with 3 incomplete uniovulate locules. Style slender, angular, 4 mm long, terminating in a capitate stigma with 3 lobes.

**Fruit**: Drupe surrounded by the calyx, strongly accrescent except at the apex, red at maturity, globulose depressed at the apex, 10 mm in diameter. Epicarp coriaceous and striated at the base. Endocarp hard and thick. Seed solitary, globulose and depressed at the apex, ± 12 × 8 mm, without integument, with granular surface. Albumen very hard. Embryo apical.

## Distribution

**Range**: From Nigeria to the lower Congo.

**Nigeria**: Old Calabar (lectotype locality).

**Cameroon**: Bityé near the Dja; Kumba, Banga, south Bakundu; Douala; Essam near Nanga Eboko; Nkolessom near Nanga Eboko; Kourou near Yoko; Nkoemvone, 12 km south Ebolowa; Mendoum, 19 km south Ambam; Adjou, 55 km west Ebolowa; Ebemwok, 55 km west Ebolowa; Johann-Albrechtshöhe (present-day Kumba).

**Gabon**: 6 km north Makokou; Bélinga; Monts de Cristal, road to Kinguélé.

**Democratic Republic of the Congo**: Bas Congo (lower Congo).

## Habitat and ecology

A plant of dense forest, growing on humid clay-sandy soils. It is a riverine and swamp plant.
