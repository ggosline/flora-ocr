# *Cleistanthus polystachyus* Hook.f. ex Planch.

**Genus**: [[Cleistanthus]]
**Family**: [[Phyllanthaceae]]
**Protologue**: C. libericus N.E.Br., J. Linn. Soc. Lond. , Bot. 37 : 113 (1905)

## Description

in Hook., Icon. Pl. 8 : t. 779 (1848). C. libericus N.E.Br., J. Linn. Soc. Lond. , Bot. 37 : 113 (1905). Shrub to small tree, 3–14 m tall; trunk up to 35 cm in diameter; branchlets tomentose, soon glabrescent or not. Stipules very early caducous, oblong to narrowly triangular, 3–11 mm long. Leaf: petiole 4–10 mm; blade oval–elliptic to obovate–elliptic, (4.5–)7–13(–17) × 2–6(–8) cm, rounded to cuneate at base, apex acuminate over (0.5–)1–1.5(–2) cm, papyraceous to coriaceous, ± glabrous above, with a few small appressed hairs, especially on the midvein below; lateral veins 4–6(–7) pairs. Inflorescence lax, racemiform, up to 5 cm long, with ≤ 20 flowers, brown-tomentose; bracts oval–triangular, 4–6 × 2–4 mm, early caducous; bracteoles about 3 × 1–2 mm. Male flower 5-merous; pedicel 4–8 mm, tomentose; sepals lanceolate, 6–8 × 1–2 mm, tomentose on the outside; petals oblanceolate, 1–2 mm long; stamens 5–8, 5–6 mm long, anthers about 2 mm long; androgynophore ≤ 1 mm; disk hairy. Female flower 4–7-merous; pedicel 4–5 mm; sepals triangular, 4–5 × 1.5–3 mm, tomentose on the outside; petals as in the male flower; disk flattened, hairy at the apex; pistil 4 mm long; ovary about 3 mm long, with appressed hairs; styles 1–2 times bifurcate. Fruit depressed-trilobed, 10–15 mm in diameter, tomentose. Seeds not seen.

## Ecology

primary and secondarized forest; in Gabon, at 0–900 m altitude. b : Breteler (2011b), Keay (1958), Léonard (1960). Bibliography
