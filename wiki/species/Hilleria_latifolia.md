# *Hilleria latifolia* (Lamk.) Walt.

**Family in Vol. 7**: [[Phytolaccaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Hilleria]]

## Description

Erect perennial herb to 1 m or subshrub to 2 m, woody below. Ovate-elliptic acuminate leaves reach 15 × 6 cm and show styloid crystals beneath. Racemes reach 14 cm; irregular white or vivid pink flowers are c. 5 mm long, normally with four stamens and a one-carpelled ovary. Fruit ovate-compressed, reticulate and blackish.

## Distribution

American species introduced into tropical Africa, Madagascar and Réunion; recorded in Gabon.
