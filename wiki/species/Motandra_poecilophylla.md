# *Motandra poecilophylla* Wernham Planche 77 ; Figure 8(H, I)

**Genus**: [[Motandra]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: J. Bot. 54 : 228 (1916)

## Description

Liane reaching 5 m in height; branches brownish-grey, without lenticels; branchlets shortly tomentose to sparsely pubescent with rust-brown hairs. Leaf: petiole 5–8 mm; blade 3–11 × 2–3.7 cm, the first two pairs of leaves on a branchlet generally smaller and rounded or emarginate at the apex; domatia consisting of a tuft of rust-brown hairs. Inflorescence rather lax, 7–27 × 2–7 cm; peduncle and axes with caducous rust-brown tomentum, short; bracts 0.5–0.9 × 0.4–1 mm, the exterior with rust-brown tomentum, the interior at the base often with 1 to 3 colleters. Flower: pedicel 2–4 mm, with rust-brown tomentum; calyx with lobes 0.7–1.1 × 0.6–1.1 mm, rust-brown, without or with 1 to 3 colleters per lobe; corolla white, 1.4–3 mm long in mature bud, tube 1.5–2 mm long, 1.2–1.5 mm in diameter at the base and 0.7–1 mm at the throat, the interior with tufts of hairs 0.7–1.2 mm from the base, lobes broadly ovate to broadly triangular, as long as the tube, as wide as or wider than long 1–1.5 × 0.7–1.2 mm, the exterior pubescent, generally only at the base, the interior glabrous; stamens inserted almost at the base of the tube, anther long, 0.5–0.7 mm, with hairs 0.2–0.4 mm at the apex; disk with lobes glabrous or pubescent at the apex; pistil long. Fruit: follicles forming an angle of 60–120° of 3–8 × 1–1.5 cm with up to 8 seeds per follicle, exocarp very finely tomentose rust-brown caducous, able to reach 1 mm in thickness; wings reaching 1 mm in width. Seed 10–14 mm long; coma 20–35 mm long. Distribution: Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, and Republic of the Congo; in Gabon: Ogooué-Maritime, Ivindo and Ogooué-Lolo. Ecology: primary and secondary forest in Gabon, up to 700 m elevation; elsewhere up to 1,100 m. B: de Kruif (1984). BIBLIOGRAPHY
