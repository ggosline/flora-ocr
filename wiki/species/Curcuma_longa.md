# *Curcuma longa* L.

**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Curcuma]]

## Description

Perennial with adjacent elliptic or cylindrical yellow aromatic rhizome tubers. Petiole to 45 cm; blade oblong-elliptic, c. 35 × 11 cm, acuminate, unequally attenuate below; ligule almost absent. Basal cylindrical spike 20 × 5 cm; pale greenish-white bracts 3.5 cm. Calyx 1 cm, corolla tube c. three times longer; yellow-centred labellum 12 mm.

## Uses and distribution

Asian crop cultivated in a few Gabon gardens. Rhizome used in curry powder, as a dye, stimulant, stomachic and diuretic.
