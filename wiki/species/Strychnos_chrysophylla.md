# *Strychnos chrysophylla* Gilg

**Genus**: [[Strychnos]]
**Family**: [[Loganiaceae]]

## Description

in ENGLER, Bot. Jahrb. 28 : 119 (1899). — FTA 4 (1) : 525. — FWTA ed. 2, 2 : 43. — LEEUWENBERG, l.c. : 82, fig. 11, carte 9.

— S. eketensis S. Moore, Journ. Bot. 52 : 29 (1914). — FWTA ed. 1, 2 : 24.

Large vine 10–25 m in height and 40–100 m in length; stem 8–15 cm in diameter; bark pale brown, thin, indistinctly lenticellate, pale yellow in section; wood pale yellow, soft, with brown bark islands; branches non-lenticellate, often grooved like the small branches glabrous, often quadrangular when fresh, and distinctly grooved when dry; tendrils in pairs.

Leaves generally large; petiole glabrous, 5–10 mm in length; blade often pale green when fresh and brownish-green when dry, coriaceous, equally when fresh, elliptic or narrowly elliptic, 4–25 × 2.5–11 cm, apiculate or acuminate at apex, cuneate or rounded at base, glabrous on both surfaces; 1–2 pairs of secondary veins curved along the margin; midvein impressed above; tertiary venation reticulate, prominent on both surfaces.

Inflorescences axillary, solitary, often large, rather lax, multiflorous, 2.5–14 × 1.5–7 cm; peduncle and branches rather thick, glabrous as the short pedicels. Flowers 5-merous. Sepals pale green, connate to one-quarter of their height, orbicular, oblong or ovate, 1.5–2 × 1–1.5 mm, rounded or obtuse, ciliate, glabrous on both surfaces. Corolla, in the mature bud, 3–4 times longer than the calyx and 4.5–6 mm in length, pale green, campanulate, rather thickened at the throat, externally glabrous or with sparse and fine pubescence on the lobes, internally with a villous ring at the throat; tube short, 1.5–2 mm in length; lobes thick, 1.8–2.3 times longer than the tube, oblong, 3–4 × 1.4–1.8 mm, acute, nearly erect. Stamens exserted, inserted at the throat of the corolla, filament 0.7–1 times longer than the anther, glabrous; anther oblong, bearded at the base with pilose hairs. Pistil pilose-pubescent, 3.2–4 mm in length; ovary ovoid, pilose-pubescent but glabrous at the base, 2–2.5 × 1.2–1.4 mm, two-celled; style short, 1.2–1.5 mm in length, pilose at the base; stigma capitate; 10–15 ovules per locule.

Fruits yellow, not very large, rather soft at maturity, globose, 3–4 cm in diameter and containing 3–6 seeds or more; wall rather thick. Seeds flattened, ± plano-convex, obliquely ovate, elliptic or oval-angular, generally irregularly curved, 22–25 × 14–20 × 5–6 mm, with thick very short hairs, erect, rather rugose; testa thick. Pl. 23, p. 132.
