# *Costus albus* A. Chev. ex J. Koechlin
**Family in Vol. 9**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Current placement**: Costaceae · **Genus**: [[Costus]]
## Description
Robust glabrous herb to 2 m with lanceolate leaves to 25 × 8 cm. A separate sheathed peduncle bears a globose spike, sometimes elongating beyond 10 cm. Each green or purple-marked bract subtends one white flower; the labellum is yellow-marked and 6–7 cm long. Exceptional spikes may terminate leafy stems.
## Distribution
Forest understorey across western and central Africa to Uganda. Type: A. Chevalier 15217, Côte d’Ivoire. Gabon: Le Testu 1218, Tchibanga.
