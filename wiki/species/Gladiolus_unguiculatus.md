# *Gladiolus unguiculatus* Bak.

**Genus**: [[Gladiolus]]
**Family**: [[Iridaceae]]
**Protologue**: J. Linn. Soc., Bot . 16 : 178 (1877)

## Description

Herb 30–60 cm tall, glabrous; corm 15–25 mm in diameter; cataphylls up to 15 cm long; stem generally simple, about 2 mm in diameter. Cauline leaves 2–3, 6–10 cm long and up to 4 mm wide; almost reduced to sheaths on the flowering stem at anthesis but sometimes developed on a sterile stem, basal leaves 2–3, linear to narrowly elliptic, up to 45 cm × 10 mm, appearing after flowering. Inflorescence erect, 10–18-flowered; bracts with insertions 10–20 mm apart, green, 10–15 mm long. Flowers zygomorphic; perianth distinctly bilabiate, yellowish to purple, with an incurved tube and flared towards the apex, 8–12 mm long; lobes unequal, the upper median one arched, obovate, 18–22 × 10–12 mm, the 5 others narrower, united for 3–4 mm of which the 3 lower ones clawed, united for 1–2 mm, bearing a ± rhomboidal macula, dark purple with a lighter margin; stamens arched beneath the dorsal tepal, exserted for 4–5 mm, filament 10–12 mm long, anther 6–8 mm long; ovary 2–3 mm long; style 15–18 mm long, stigmas 2–2.5 mm long. Fruit ellipsoid to obovoid, 12–16 mm long; valves 4–8 mm wide. Seeds subelliptic, about 7 × 4 mm.

## Distribution

Senegal, Mali, Guinea-Bissau, Guinea, Sierra Leone, Burkina Faso, Ivory Coast, Ghana, Togo, Benin, Nigeria, Cameroon, Chad, Central African Republic, Gabon, Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Angola, Sudan, Tanzania, Zambia, Malawi, Zimbabwe, Mozambique, Botswana; fairly common in Gabon.

## Ecology

Savannas and forest margins; up to 2200 m altitude. B: Baker (1892, 1898), Geerinck (2005), Goldblatt (1993, 1996a, 1996b), BIBLIOGRAPHY Hepper (1968c)
