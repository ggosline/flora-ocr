### Distribution

Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroun, République Centrafrique, Guinée Équatoriale, Gabon, Congo (Brazzaville) and Democratic Republic of the Congo; uncommon in Gabon

### Ecology

lowland forest, sometimes swampy, forest edges; in Gabon at 0–500 m altitude, elsewhere up to 700 m. Taxonomic note: The two varieties of P. macrocarpa have been united, based on their overlapping distributions and morphological characters. The only difference between the two would be the length of the flower lobes: 5–7 cm in var. macrocarpa and 10–15 cm in var. soyauxiana. The types of both names were collected around Libreville, one in flower and the other in fruit. Consequently, the length of the lobes cannot be determined in one of the two. Moreover, we observed continuous variation in lobe length and the distribution of the two varieties being also not distinct, we decided no longer to recognize them.
