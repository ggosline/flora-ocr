Flower : pedicel (without ovary) 2–4 cm ; perianth with black hairs and white apex inside, utricle 1.5–2.5 cm long, with 2 large diamond-shaped to rectangular glandular zones, tube 3–3.5 cm long, white, finely speckled with purple, lobes 3, slightly unequal, spreading, triangular-ovate, 5–15 cm long, furnished with 3 longitudinal crests at base, the 2 lateral ones somewhat larger than the lower one, internally yellow, punctate brown or purple-brown to entirely pinkish to reddish-brown ; gynosteme 8–10 mm long, with (8–)9(–10) brown stamens and (8–)9(–10) rounded stigmatic lobes ; ovary 8–20 mm long, pale green punctate with dark red. Fruit elongate, 30–53 × 3–4 cm, with more or less marked and rounded ribs.

### Distribution

Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Congo (Brazzaville) and Democratic Republic of the Congo ; uncommon in Gabon

### Ecology

lowland forest, sometimes swampy, forest edges ; in Gabon at 0–500 m elevation, elsewhere up to 700 m. Taxonomic note : The two varieties of P. macrocarpa have been united, based on their overlapping distributions and morphological characters. The only difference between the two would be the length of the flower lobes : 5–7 cm in var. macrocarpa and 10–15 cm in var. soyauxiana . The types of both names were collected around Libreville, one in flower and the other in fruit. Therefore, the length of the lobes cannot be determined in one of the two. Moreover, we have observed continuous variation in the length of the lobes and the distribution of the two varieties not being distinct either, we decided no longer to recognize them. B : Hawthorne & Jongkind (2006), Hutchinson & Dalziel (1928), Poncy (1978)
