# *Tarena precidantenna* N. Hallé

**Genus**: [[Tarenna]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Protologue**: N. Hallé, Adansonia 7 : 506 (1967)

## Synonyms

- Pavetta Warburgiana De Wild. et Th. Dur., Ann. Mus. Congo 3,1 : 27 (1901), non Tarenna Warburgiana Valeton 1924 de Nouvelle-Guinée.
- Ixora Warburgiana (De Wild. et Dur.) Bremek. (msc. BR, Dewèvre 1080).

## Description

Small shrub with cylindrical glabrous branchlets; stipules 2-5 mm, with very acute apex, glabrous. Petiole 5-25 mm, with margins of the channel applied toward the underside and furnished with a line of poorly distinct hairs. Lamina elliptic, rarely nigrescens when dry, 8-21 × 2-9 cm, with acute asymmetrical or symmetrical base. 8-11 pairs of secondary veins. Axillary domatia moderately pubescent along the midvein only.

Inflorescences loose paniculate, multiflorous; axes glabrous or somewhat pubescent. Pedicels 6-14 mm. Calyx lobes tapered, 4-5 mm long, very finely pubescent. Corolla tube glabrous, 10-13 mm long; lobes 10-12 × 2 mm, with acute apiculate apex. Anthers 2-3 mm, basifixed; filament nearly twice as long as the anther. Style c. 3 cm long, glabrous, with brief, subcapitate club 1-1.5 × 0.7 mm. Ovary 1.5-2 × 1.2 mm. Placentas triovulate.

Fruit exceeding 10 mm in diam.
