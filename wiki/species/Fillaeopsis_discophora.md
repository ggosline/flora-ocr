# *Fillaeopsis discophora* Harms

**Genus**: [[Fillaeopsis]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]

(1961); SAINT-AUBIN, For. Gabon : 103 (1963); KEAY, ONOCHIE & STANFIELD, Niger. Trees 2 : 92 (1964); GUNN, FSGM : 40, pl. p. 41 (1984); VIVIEN & FAURE, AFDAC : 290, pl. p. 291, ph. 515 (1985).

Large tree reaching 40 m with bole 65 cm in diameter. Trunk elevated, somewhat sinuous, furnished with thick low shoulders, sometimes weakly fluted. Rhytidome dark brown, sometimes blackish, peeling in vaguely rectangular plates, corky, friable. Bark section fibrous, pinkish externally, internally yellowish to light brown exuding a viscous, translucent yellow liquid, nauseating. Sapwood pale yellow. Crown well-developed, spreading. Branchlets brown, longitudinally striated with lighter lenticels. Petiole glabrous, 2.5–9.5 cm long, cylindrical, weakly longitudinally striated. Rachis (if more than one pair of pinnae), glabrous, 0–12 cm long, cylindrical. Pinnae 1–2 pairs. Pinnae axis glabrous, 6–15 cm long, carinate above. Leaflets 4–8, alternate. Petiolule glabrous, 2–4 mm long. Blade brownish-green to glossy black above and dull below on the dried plant, elliptic to ovate towards the base of the pinna, 3–10 × 1.5–5 cm, base attenuate to subrounded, apex ± longly acuminate acute or obtuse, ± distinctly mucronate. Midrib concave above and prominent below. Secondary nerves 10–14 pairs, prominent on both faces, straight or slightly arched. Veinlets in fairly dense reticulum, with ± elongate meshes, prominent on both faces.

Inflorescence axillary in panicles of dense many-flowered spikes. Bracts glabrous, narrowly triangular, 1 mm long, incurved. Floral bud globose. Flowers ♂, pentamerous, white in the living state. Sepals 5, glabrous, 1–1.5 mm long, connate for 1/2 their length, free lobes obtuse. Petals 5, spreading in the open flower, glabrous, free, elliptic to broadly elliptic, 2.5–3 × 1.25 mm; dorsal face marked by a keel, apex acute. Stamens 10, free, filaments 4–5 mm long, villous especially toward the weakly widened base, anther ellipsoid, 0.25–0.60 mm long, 2-locular with connective prolonged by a globose stipitate gland. Disk fleshy, cupular, intrastaminal, prominent. Ovary glabrous, sessile, oblong, oblong-ellipsoid, 1.5–2 mm long, arched or straight, with 8–10 ovules; style ± eccentric, 2.25 mm long, terminating in a plateau-like stigma.

Pod dehiscent, chartaceous to subwoody, very broadly elliptic-oblong to oblong, sometimes irregular, 20–60 × 10–18 cm; sutural borders with prominent beading; surfaces ± glossy with strong transverse venation and very distinct veinules externally and smooth internally with lighter seed-separation lines. Seeds transverse, thin, winged, oblong, 5–17 × 3–5.5 cm, pink, funicle very long inserted at the middle of one long face of the seed.

Seedling with short hypocotyl, hypogeal germination, first internode 8.5–12 cm long; primary leaves opposite, bipinnate with a single pair of pinnae with 2–3 alternate leaflets; subsequent leaves alternate (after GILBERT & BOUTIQUE, l.c.). – Pl. 8, Fig. 3–4, p. 37 ; Pl. 9, p. 39.

## Discussion

F. discophora is a heliophilous tropophyte of dense forested regions. Its range extends from southern Nigeria to Angola and Zaire (Yangambi region).
