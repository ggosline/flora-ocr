# *Polygala ganguelensis* Exell & Mendonça Planche 23(4)

**Genus**: [[Polygala]]
**Family**: [[Polygalaceae]]
**Protologue**: Consp. fl. angol. 1, 1 : 100 (1937)

## Description

Annual herb, up to 35–40 cm; stem erect slender, simple or weakly branched, striated, glabrescenct to glabrous. Leaves alternate: petiole 0.5 mm; blade linear to linear-lanceolate, 15–20 × 1–1.5(–2) mm, cuneate at base, acute at apex, weakly glabrescenct to glabrous. Inflorescence: simple racemes, terminal, up to 18 cm in length, glabrous, loosely flowered; bracts linear-lanceolate, 0.75–1 mm in length, persistent; bracteoles similar, but 0.5 mm in length. Flower pale yellow or rarely violet; pedicel 1.5–2 mm, glabrous; upper sepal lanceolate, 2–2.5 × 1–1.3 mm, glabrous or rarely ciliate, winged sepals obliquely elliptic, 4.5–5.5 × 2.5–3 mm, glabrous; lower external sepals lanceolate, 1.5 × 0.5–0.75 mm, glabrous, rarely ciliate, free; upper petals obliquely obovate, 1.5–2(–3.5) × 1 mm, keel 3.5–4 × 2 mm in length, crest 1 mm in length, fimbriate; stamens 8. Fruit flattened, ellipsoid, 3.5 × 2.5 mm, emarginate, very narrowly winged, glabrous, rarely ciliate. Seed ellipsoid, (1.8–)2–2.5 × 1–1.25 mm, with short silky white hairs; caruncle symmetric, reniform.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Angola and Zambia; in Gabon: Ogooué-Ivindo

## Ecology

gramineous swampy formations; in Gabon at 100–150 m altitude. B: Exell & Mendonça (1937), Paiva (1998). Bibliography
