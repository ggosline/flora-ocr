# *Cleistanthus caudatus* Pax Planche 6

**Genus**: [[Cleistanthus]]

**Protologue**: C. racemosus Pierre ex Hutch., Fl. trop. Afr. VI, 1 : 1043 (1913)

## Description

in De Wild. & Th.Dur., Ann. Mus. Congo Belge, Bot., sér. II, 1: 49 (1899). C. racemosus Pierre ex Hutch., Fl. trop. Afr. VI, 1: 1043 (1913). C. milbraedii Jabl. in Engl., Pflanzenr. Jard. Bot. État Brux. 33: 425 (1960). C. kasaiensis J.Léonard var. paulopubescens J.Léonard, Bull. Jard. Bot. État Brux. 33: 427 (1960). C. willmannianus J.Léonard, Bull. Jard. Bot. État Brux. 33: 430 (1960). Tree, rarely shrub, 9–40 m tall; trunk 15–80 cm in diameter, with very hard wood; branchlets velvety or puberulent to almost glabrous or glabrescente. Stipules early caducous, oblong to oblanceolate, 4–7 mm long, sparsely pubescent. Leaf: petiole (3–)4–10(–13) mm, pubescent to glabrous; lamina ovate-elliptic, (1.5–)2–3 times as long as wide, (5–)10–18 × (1.5–)3–6(–10) cm, cuneate to cordate at base, apex acuminate over 1–2.5(–3) cm, glabrous above or with the midvein sparsely puberulent, glabrous or with scattered appressed hairs below; lateral veins (3–)4–6(–7) pairs. Inflorescence few- to multiflowered, 1.5–12 cm long, pubescent to subglabrous. Male flower: pedicel (2–)3–10(–18) mm, glabrous to sparsely pubescent; sepals ± elliptic, 4–7 × 1.5–3 mm, ± glabrous; petals ± rudimentary, of very variable shape, 0.5–2 mm long; stamens 4–5, anthers 1.5–2 mm long; androgynophore (1.5–)2–5 mm; disc glabrous to pubescent. Female flower: pedicel 7–10 mm, glabrous to sparsely pubescent; sepals ± elliptic, 6–7 × 3 mm; petals and disc as in male flowers; ovary glabrous. Fruit ± globose-depressed, slightly trilobate, (9–)13–25 × (9–)15–20 mm, glabrous, with prominent reticulation. Seed subglobose, approximately 10 mm in diameter, smooth, shiny.

## Distribution

from the Central African Republic to Cameroon, Gabon and the Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon the species has been collected in the provinces of Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué, Ngounié, Woleu-Ntem, Ogooué-Ivindo and Ogooué-Lolo

## Ecology

primary forest and old secondary forest; at 0–500 m altitude. b: Breteler (2011b), Léonard (1960, 1962). Bibliography
