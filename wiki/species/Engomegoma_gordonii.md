# *Engomegoma gordonii* Breteler

**Family**: Strombosiaceae (in [[Olacaceae]] s.l.) · **Genus**: [[Engomegoma]]

Bot. Jahrb. Syst. **118**: 113–132 (1996).

The only species of [[Engomegoma]], described from and known in the available
protologue pages only from central Gabon.

## Description

**Habit**: Tree up to at least 35 m tall and 70 cm dbh; branches and branchlets
glabrous.

**Leaves**: ± distichous, coriaceous, smooth, dark green, wine-red when young,
glabrous. Petiole 7–12(–17) mm, shallowly canaliculate above and usually
thickened distally. Blade ovate-elliptic, 1.5–2.5 times as long as wide,
(7–)9–14(–18) × (3–)5–8 cm; base rounded and very slightly peltate or not;
apex obtusely acuminate, the acumen usually mucronate, (0.3–)0.5–1 cm long.
Nervation obscure, especially above; main lateral nerves 5–8 pairs; midrib
plane to slightly prominent above and prominent beneath. Dried leaf surface
granular; margin revolute or not.

**Inflorescence**: A glabrous fascicle of up to 15 flowers, sometimes on a
stalk up to 1 mm; bracts and bracteoles ovate-triangular, up to c. 1 mm.

**Flowers**: 4(–5)-merous; buds subglobose and yellow. Pedicel 5–8 mm,
subterete and glabrous, becoming strongly elongated in fruit. Calyx ±
cup-shaped, 0.5 mm high, shallowly 4(–5)-lobed and glabrous. Petals free,
white, firm, elliptic, 4.5–5.5 × 2–3 mm, spreading to partly reflexed at
anthesis, acute at apex, obtuse at base, glabrous outside and densely papillate
inside. Stamens 4(–5), epipetalous, glabrous, 2–2.5 mm long; anthers
subovoid-ellipsoid, 1–1.5 × 1 mm, with a very thick connective exceeding the
four separate narrow anther cells; filaments firm, up to 1 mm, adnate to the
petal at base. Ovary superior, (3–)4-angled, tapering to the apex, c. 1 mm,
glabrous, (3–)4-locular, with one pendulous ovule per locule and (3–)4 minute
apical stigmas.

**Fruit**: Obovoid-ellipsoid, 2.5–3 × 2–2.5 cm, ± smooth and glabrous, on a
2–3.5 cm stalk. Pericarp rather thin; endocarp woody, muricate outside and
smooth inside. Mature seeds unknown.

## Distribution

**Range**: Central Gabon.

**Gabon**: 5–15 km NNW of Ndjolé; 11 km south of Boué; Liyanga; south of Ayem
on the western border of Lopé-Okanda Reserve; 8 km north of Yeno; and 48 km
ESE of Mitzic.

## Habitat and ecology

Primary and old secondary rain forest at 100–700 m. Flowering specimens were
collected in November; fruits were collected in February, May, July and
December.

## Type

Gabon, 5–15 km NNW of Ndjolé, flowering November, *Breteler & Jongkind 10498*
(holotype WAG; isotypes BR, K, L, LBV, MO, P).

## Specimens examined

**Gabon** — 5–15 km NNW of Ndjolé, *Breteler & Jongkind 10498* (BR, K, L, LBV,
MO, P, WAG), type; 11 km S of Boué, *Dibata 243* (WAG); Liyanga, *Le Testu
7694* (P); S of Ayem, western border of Lopé-Okanda Reserve, *McPherson 15511*
(MO, WAG), *McPherson 15693* (MO, WAG); 8 km N of Yeno, *Wilks 1284* (WAG);
48 km ESE of Mitzic, *Wilks 1684* (WAG).

## Notes

Not in Flore du Gabon Vol 20 (1973) — the genus was described twenty-three
years later. Any future revision of the volume's key to the Gabonese
[[Olacaceae]] would need to accommodate it.

The epithet honours Gordon McPherson of the Missouri Botanical Garden, who
collected one flowering and one fruiting specimen. The project extract covers
the taxonomic content on pp. 113–117; the specialized anatomical and
palynological sections on the remaining pages are outside the wiki's scope.
