# *Engomegoma gordonii* Breteler

**Family**: Strombosiaceae (in [[Olacaceae]] s.l.) · **Genus**: [[Engomegoma]]

Bot. Jahrb. Syst. **118**: 113–132 (1996).

The only species of [[Engomegoma]], described from Gabon. A tree of wet
tropical forest, ranging from Cameroon to Gabon.
