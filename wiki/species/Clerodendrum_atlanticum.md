# *Clerodendrum atlanticum* Jongkind

**Genus**: [[Clerodendrum]]
**Protologue**: Blumea 51(2) : 306 (2006)

## Description

Blumea 51(2) : 306 (2006). Liane or shrub of ± 1 m in height; stem slender, ascending, narrowly hollow, not reported as myrmecophilous, sometimes armed with short pseudospines of 2–4(–5) mm; branchlets pilose. Leaves opposite: petiole of 0.4–2.5 cm, sparsely pilose; blade ovate-elliptic, of 4.5–15 × 2–8 cm, acute to rounded, sometimes subcordate at the base, acute to acuminate at the apex, entire, with sparse erect hairs above, with erect hairs mainly on the principal veins and with black punctations below; secondary veins in 4 or 5 pairs. Inflorescence terminal, paniculate, lax, borne by short lateral branchlets, of 10–12(–18) × 8–13(–18) cm; bracts similar to the leaves, but smaller; cymes pubescent, distant by 2.5–4.5(–12) cm; peduncle of (1–)2.5–4.5 cm. Flower: pedicel of 1.0 cm; calyx campanulate, tube of 1.5–2 × 1 cm at anthesis, with lobes of (6–)9–15(–20) × 3–7 mm, green to red-purple, externally with short dense glandular hairs and a few simple hairs, with much denser and longer hairs on the margin, internally glabrous; corolla very pale greenish-white, externally with glandular and simple hairs, tube of 20–25 × 1.5 mm, internally with reflexed hairs, with elliptic lobes, of ± 6 × 4 mm, sometimes reflexed, internally glabrous; stamens exserted by 1.5–2 cm, filament greenish-white, anther of ± 2 mm long, yellow to orange-brown or dark brown. Fruit: unknown.

## Distribution

Cameroon and Gabon; in Gabon: Estuaire. Conservation status: probably Endangered (EN); known from seven collections representing two localities

## Ecology

humid forest, forest margin, scrubland, seashore, pure sands in savanna zone, sand dunes between seashore and mangrove; in Gabon and elsewhere at ± 5 m altitude. B: Jongkind (2006). i Bibliography
