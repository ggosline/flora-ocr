# *Brassica oleracea* Linné

**Genus**: [[Brassica]]
**Family**: [[Brassicaceae]]
**Protologue**: Sp. Pl. : 667 (1753) ; CUFODONTIS, Enum. Pl. Aethiop. : 150 (1954) ; KEAY, FWTA, ed. 2, 1 (1) : 97 (1954) ; ROBYNS et BOUTIQUE, Fl. Congo 2 : 530 (1951) ; AGNEW, Upl. Kenya Wild. Fl. : 94 (1974) ; JONSELL, FCAM 21 : 6 (1980) et FTEA, Cruciferae : 6 (1982)

## Description

KEAY, FWTA, ed. 2, 1 (1) : 97 (1954) ; ROBYNS et BOUTIQUE, Fl. Congo 2 : 530 (1951) ; AGNEW, Upl. Kenya Wild. Fl. : 94 (1974) ; JONSELL, FCAM 21 : 6 (1980) et FTEA, Cruciferae : 6 (1982).

Annual or perennial plant, often subshrubby, glabrous, glaucous. Stem erect up to 3 m in height (but often much shorter) with oblique branches. Leaves fleshy, glabrous, glaucescent green or glaucous. Basal and lower cauline leaves petiolate, ± lyrate-pinnatifid, with strong prominent whitish veins. Terminal segment very large, reaching 50 × 30 cm, apex rounded, base ± cordate, margins entire, undulate crenate crisped or incised; lateral segments 1–5 pairs, little developed, margins entire or crenate. Middle cauline leaves oblong or obovate, amplexicaul, obtuse, sinuate. Upper leaves weakly amplexicaul, linear-oblong, subentire.

Corymb-like racemes with large flowers not exceeding the buds, rapidly elongating. Flower pedicels reaching a length of 8 mm and fruit pedicels 20 cm. Sepals oblong, approximately 10 mm. Petals pale yellow or white, with obovate unguiculate limb, length reaching 20 mm. Stamens with anthers approximately 4 mm long. Ovaries containing 30 to 40 ovules. Siliques oblique, linear ± torulous, 50–100 × 5 mm, sometimes borne on a gynophore, attenuate into a beak ordinarily conical reaching 6 mm in length (or filiform and much longer); valves reticulate 1.5 to 2 mm in diameter.

## Type

Europe « habitat in maritimis Angliae »

## Discussion

This highly polymorphic species comprises numerous cultivated forms. Some of these, little modified, have naturalized themselves in the cultivation zones of tropical countries. The type of the species belongs to the spontaneous form that lives on the cliffs of the Atlantic coasts of Europe.
