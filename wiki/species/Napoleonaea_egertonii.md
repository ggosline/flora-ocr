# *Napoleonaea egertonii* Bak.f.

**Genus**: [[Napoleonaea]]
**Family**: [[Lecythidaceae]]
**Protologue**: N. le-testui Pellegrin, Bull. Soc. Bot. France 82 : 434 (1936), ‘ N. Le Testui’

## Description

in Rendle et al., Cat. pl. Oban: 33, t. 4, fig. 3–4 (1913). N. le-testui Pellegrin, Bull. Soc. Bot. France 82: 434 (1936), 'N. Le Testui'. Tree up to 30 m tall; trunk up to 50 cm diameter. Leaf: petiole (3–)5–8 mm, canaliculate, black; blade elliptic-oblong to obovate, 17–32 × 7–9(–10) cm, cuneate to rounded or sometimes cordate at base, acute to acuminate at apex, generally thick and coriaceous, without marginal glands; lateral veins (5–)6–9(–11) pairs, sometimes distant from each other up to 3 cm then curved toward the margin, prominent beneath; tertiary veins scalariform, dense, undulate. Inflorescences in branched panicles, on the branches or trunk, puberulent, reaching 18 cm in length; bracts and bracteoles up to 10 mm diameter, densely puberulent. Flower with tomentose, muricate calyx, lobes 6–7 × 4–5 mm, not glandular; first corona 3–4 cm diameter, white or sometimes purple; second corona with white tomentum in the lower part, becoming gradually glabrous toward the apex; third corona 16–17 mm diameter, sparsely puberulent on the veins. Fruits slightly ellipsoid, 12–13 × 14–16 cm, spiny. Seed ± triangular in section, 6–7 × 4–5 cm.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon; rare in Gabon, known from Ogooué-Lolo and Ogooué-Ivindo provinces.

## Ecology

Primary and secondary forests; in Gabon up to 500 m altitude, elsewhere up to 900 m. Taxonomic note: The specimen Mcpherson 16743, collected east of Lastoursville, is remarkable for petioles 1–3.5 cm in length and leaves sometimes narrowly elliptic, 3.5–7 cm wide. This collection may represent a variety of N. egertonii. B: Hutchinson & Dalziel (1954), Liben (1971a). Bibliography
