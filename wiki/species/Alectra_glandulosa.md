# *Alectra glandulosa* Philcox Planche 56(A–B)

**Genus**: [[Alectra]]
**Family**: [[Scrophulariaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bol. Soc. Brot., sér. 2, 60 : 269 (1987)

## Description

Annual herb, 6–14 cm tall; stem erect or prostrate, simple or with opposite branches, hispid and reflexed hairs. Leaf sessile; blade lanceolate to oblong, (10–)15–30(–50) × 1–4 mm, cuneate at base, acuminate or obtuse at apex, dentate margin, hispid to subglabrous. Inflorescence lax, with foliaceous bracts. Flower: pedicel 1–3(–5) mm; bracteoles 6 mm long; calyx 7–8.5(–13) mm long, with 10 distinct nerves, glandular and with short white hairs, especially on the nerves, lobes 1–1.75 mm long; corolla (10–)15–19 mm long, yellow, glandular-stipitate outside, lobes 4–5 mm long, rounded; stamens subequal, filaments 3–6 mm, glabrous, anthers ± 1.5 mm long, apiculate; ovary 2–3 mm long; style 10–15 mm. Fruit oval to spherical 11 × 7 mm.

## Distribution

Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Zambia; in Gabon, observed in Ngounié and Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology

swampy savannas; in Gabon, at low altitude, in East Africa up to 1700 m. B: Mielcarek (1996), Philcox (1990). Bibliography
