# *Rourea obliquifoliolata* Gilg

**Genus**: [[Rourea]]
**Family**: [[Connaraceae]]
**Protologue**: EBJ 14: 328 (1891); JONGKIND, in BRETELER (éd.), AUWP 89 (6): 345 (1989). TYPE: Zaïre, Mukenge, Pogge 733 (holo-B delet.). NÉOTYPE: Cameroun, Bipindi, Zenker 2992 (holo-WAG; iso-B, BR, E, G, GOET, L, M, MO, Z)

## Description

JONGKIND, in BRETELER (ed.), AUWP 89 (6): 345 (1989). TYPE: Zaire, Mukenge, Pogge 733 (holo-B delet.). NEOTYPE: Cameroon, Bipindi, Zenker 2992 (holo-WAG; iso-B, BR, E, G, GOET, L, M, MO, Z).

– Roureopsis obliquifoliolata (GILG) SCHELLENBERG, PR 103 (4, 127): 108 (1938); TROUPIN, FCBR 3: 77 (1952); EXELL & MENDONÇA, CFA 2: 145 (1954); HEPPER, FWTA, ed. 2, 1 (2): 740 (1958).

Large liana reaching up to 25 m or shrub. Young branches puberulent.

Leaves. Petiole 0–0.5 mm long, rachis 3.5–28 cm, puberulent; petiolules 0.5–3 mm long; leaflets 13–41, ovate to elliptic or rhombic to reniform, puberulent on the undersurface of the midvein or glabrous, cartaceous; apex rounded to subacuminate, mucronate; terminal leaflet 1.7–9 × 1–5 cm; the lateral ones 0.7–8.5 × 0.4–3.5 cm, remarkably asymmetric.

Inflorescences ≤1.2 cm long, subspheric, often assembled in large numbers at the end of a leafy branch simulating a terminal inflorescence. Pedicel 0–1 mm long above the articulation.

Flowers. Sepals 3–6 × 1.5–2.5 mm, interior nearly glabrous. Petals 11–14 × 1.5–2 mm, strap-shaped, free, rolled in the bud. Stamens, the longer ones 2.5–7 mm long, the shorter ones 1.5–5 mm. Pistil 1.5–6 mm long; style with some hairs; ovary with hairs only on the adjacent faces of the carpels.

Fruits: follicles 1–5 per flower, 15–24 × 7–10 mm, glabrous, dehiscing along a ventral suture. Sepals below the fruit 6–8.5 × 2.5–3 mm, reddish, cartaceous to coriaceous, nearly glabrous.

Seed with testa ≈ 1/4 fleshy, the remainder thin, shiny and black. Radicle ventral. - Pl. 40, p. 125.
