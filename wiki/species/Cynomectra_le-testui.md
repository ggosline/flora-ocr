# *Cynomectra le-* Testui (Pellegr.) Léonard

**Genus**: [[Cynometra]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: J. Léonard, Bull. Jard. Bot. Brux. 21 : 382 (1951); F.C.B. 3 : 312 (1952)

## Synonyms

- Cynometra Le-Testui Pellegrin, Bull. Soc. Bot. Fr., 67 : 386 (1920) nomen.
- Hymenostegia Le-Testui Pellegrin, Not. Syst. 4 : 56 (1923); Fl. Mayumbe,

## Description

1, Mém. Soc. Linn. Norm. 16 : 107 (1924); Lég. Gabon : 87 (1949). — Bak. f. Lég. Trop. Afr. : 766 (1930).

Small trees. Branchlets lenticellate. Buds scaly, hirsute. Leaves bifoliolate. Petiole 3 mm, glabrous. Petiolules 1 mm. Leaflets oblong, asymmetrical, acuminate emarginate, glabrous, 6–10 cm long, 2.5–4.5 cm wide. Secondary veins about a dozen pairs, not very distinct from a fine reticulum, visible beneath. Presence of a prominent black gland on the lower surface of the limb, near the petiolule, sometimes inconspicuous.

Inflorescences in very small axillary racemes, resembling poorly flowered small glomérules, white.

Pedicel 4 mm, hirsute. Bracteoles 2 at mid-height, 3 mm long, somewhat hirsute. Sepals 4, nearly glabrous, 4 mm long. Petals lanceolate, narrowly clawed, glabrous, 5 × 1.5 mm. Stamens 10 with glabrous filaments. Ovary hirsute, biovulate. Fruit unknown.

Ripicole species of the Mayombé.
