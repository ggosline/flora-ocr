# *Araliopsis soyauxii* Engl.

**Family**: [[Rutaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Araliopsis]]

## Description

Tree 25–30 m tall and 60–80 cm diameter, fluted for 5–6 m; bark smooth ash-grey with corky excrescences, yellow and pink-spotted in section. Leaves have (3–)5–7 leaflets, petiole to 30 cm and petiolules 1–3(–5) cm. Blades are obovate-oblanceolate to oblong-elliptic, to 40 × 14 cm, papery to leathery, long-cuneate below and shortly acuminate. Flowers were unknown; infructescence axes and c. 5 mm fruiting pedicels are puberulent.

## Distribution

Gabon and Calabar Province in southern Nigeria.

## Vernacular names

Ayoleu (Fang).
