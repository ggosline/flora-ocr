## Description

Pflanzenwelt Afr. 3(2) : 276 (1921); Hutch. & DALZ., FWTA, ed. 1, 1 : 504 (1928).

Shrub? Leaves 3–4-jugate; leaflets 14–20 × 4.5–8 cm; petiolules 1 cm.
