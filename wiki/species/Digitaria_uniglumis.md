# *Digitaria uniglumis* (A. Rich.) Stapf v. major Stapf.

**Genus**: [[Digitaria]]
**Family**: [[Gramineae]]

## Description

Perennial plant, caespitose, exceeding 2 m tall. Leaves sometimes pubescent; ligule membranous glabrous; blades up to 60 cm long or more, and 15 mm wide.

Large inflorescence about 50 cm long. Rachises slender, scabrous on the margins; pedicels in fascicles of 3 to 6, up to 3 mm long. Spikelets about 1.5 mm long, glabrous, darker on the convex face. Upper glume hyaline, less than 0.5 mm long. Lower lemma as large as the spikelet, hyaline, 3-nerved. Palea 0.5 mm long. Upper floret shiny, dark brown to black.

The typical species is found in East Africa; the variety major is more widely distributed throughout tropical Africa. It does not appear to have very strict ecological requirements and is found in most savannas, but rarely in great abundance. It is a fair forage, used in the young state.

Recorded from Gabon by Koechlin in the savannas of the South.
