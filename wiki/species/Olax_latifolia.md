## Description

**Habit**: shrub, small tree or small arbrisseau 0.5–3 m tall. Bark greyish. Branchlets green, with strong ribs or four wings and distinct longitudinal striations between them.

**Leaves**: alternate, decussate. Petiole glabrous, 0–4 mm long, concave above, wrinkled beneath. Blade glabrous on both faces, green above, yellowish-green or greyish-green beneath, elliptic, lanceolate, sometimes sublinear or oblong, 9–23 × 2.5–10.5 cm; base ± distinctly cuneiform; apex acuminate, obtuse or acute. Midrib very concave above, prominent and longitudinally striate beneath. Secondary nerves 5–12 pairs, ascending (especially the first pair), arcuate, anastomosing 2–7 mm from the blade margin, concave above, strongly prominent beneath. Nervules forming a loose reticulum, ± prominent on both faces.

**Inflorescence**: axillary, multiflowered raceme. Rachis ± quadrangular; flowers arranged on the broad faces. Bracteoles ovate with dorsal keel and apiculate or mucronate apex. Pedicel 2 mm long, longitudinally striate. Flower bud ellipsoid, ± constricted or narrowed in its central portion, with valvate petals, 3.5 × 2.5 mm. Flower pentamerous, reddish-yellowish to reddish-orange, 5 × 4 mm.

**Calyx**: cupuliform with entire margin.

**Petals**: glabrous on both faces, oblong-elliptic, free to the base, 4.5–5 × 1.5 mm, apex apiculate and reflexed outwards.

**Stamens**: 6, epipetalous, 3.5 mm long; filament oblong, thick, 3.5 × 1 mm; anther introrse, slightly dorsifixed, ellipsoid, 1 mm long, with longitudinal dehiscence slits.

**Staminodes**: 3, alternipetalous, 4 mm long; filament similar to those of the stamens, 2.5 × 0.6 mm, terminating in 2 blades fused at the base, acute at the apex or enlarged, 1 mm long.

**Disc**: cupuliform, surrounding the base of the ovary.

**Ovary**: superior, conical, furrowed at the base, with 3 incomplete uniovulate locules. Placenta central, erect. Style 2 mm long, terminated by a capitate stigma with 3 lobes.

**Fruit**: calyx strongly accrescent, ellipsoid, surrounding the drupe except sometimes at the apex where it is subtubuliform and perforated by a pore; green when young, becoming golden then red at maturity, longitudinally striate. Drupe ellipsoid, 9–13 × 9–15 mm, orange, apiculate at the apex. Exocarp orange, shiny. Endocarp hard.

**Seed**: ellipsoid, globose and erect. Albumen abundant. Embryo apical, 0.5 mm long.

## Types

SYNTYPES: *Dinklage 178* (B, delet.); *1070* (B, delet.); *1338* (B, delet.); *Staudt 465* (B, delet.; iso., P!); *Zenker 856* (Z); all from Cameroon.

## Distribution

**Range**: West and Central Africa (Cameroon and Gabon).

**Gabon**: Ogooué-Ivindo (Makokou, Bélinga, Ivindo); Ogooué-Maritime (Lastoursville); Estuaire (Libreville region, Monts de Cristal). Also from the Komo river near Mbel and Assok, and other localities in the Monts de Cristal.

## Habitat and ecology

Forest habitats. Recorded at 700 m altitude on the plateau near Moanda.

## Vernacular names

Kafé apan, ban ofan, nom young (Bobili, Yaoundé).

## Uses and properties

Hard, solid wood. For treating colds, leaves are applied to the chest and a leaf maceration is used as a nasal wash. Seeds are eaten after roasting.

## Notes

Similar flower variations occur as in *O. staudtii* Engl. The specimen *Hallé N. 2086* presents narrowly elliptic leaves and flowers approaching those of *O. pynaertii* DE WILD.
