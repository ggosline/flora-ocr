# *Olax latifolia* Engler

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Olax]]

Notizbl. bot. Gart. Berl. 2: 284 (1899).

## Synonymy

- *Olax longifolia* Engl., Notizbl. bot. Gart. Berl. 2: 285 (1899).
- *Olax zenkeri* Engl., Notizbl. bot. Gart. Berl. 2: 284 (1899).
- *Olax tesmannii* Engl., Bot. Jahrb. 43: 161 (1909).

## Description

Shrub, small tree or small arborescent, 0.5–3 m tall. Bark greyish. Branches green with strong ribs or 4 wings, with distinct longitudinal striations between them. Petiole glabrous, 0–4 mm long, concave above, wrinkled beneath. Leaves alternate, decussate. Blade glabrous on both faces, green above, yellowish-green or greyish-green beneath, elliptic, lanceolate, sometimes spathulate or oblong, 9–23 × 2.5–10.5 cm; base ± distinctly cuneate; apex acuminate obtuse or acute. Midrib very concave above, prominent and longitudinally striated beneath. Secondary veins 5–12 pairs, ascending, particularly the first pair, arched, anastomosing at 2–7 mm from the leaf margin, concave above, strongly prominent beneath. Tertiary veins in a lax network ± prominent on both faces.

Inflorescence as a plurifloral axillary raceme. Rachis ± quadrangular; flowers arranged on the broad faces. Bracteoles oval with dorsal keel and apiculate or mucronate apex. Pedicel 2 mm long, longitudinally striated. Floral bud ellipsoid ± constricted or narrowed in its middle part, with valvate petals, 3.5 × 2.5 mm. Flower pentamerous, reddish-yellowish to orange-red, 5 × 4 mm. Calyx cupuliform with entire margin. Petals glabrous on both faces, oblong-elliptic, free to the base, 4.5–5 × 1.5 mm, apex apiculate and reflexed outward. Stamens 6 epipetalous, 3.5 mm long; filament oblong thick, 3.5 × 1 mm; anther introrse, slightly dorsifix, ellipsoid, 1 mm long, with longitudinal dehiscence slits. Staminodes 3 alternipetalous, 4 mm long; filament similar to that of stamens, 2.5 × 0.6 mm, terminating in 2 blades united at the base, acute at the apex or enlarged, 1 mm long. Cupuliform disc surrounding the base of the ovary. Ovary superior, conic, furrowed at the base, with 3 incomplete uniovulate locules. Central placenta erect. Style 2 mm long, terminated by a capitate stigma with 3 lobes.

Calyx strongly accrescent, ellipsoid, surrounding the drupe except at the apex sometimes subtubulous, pierced by a pore, green when young, becoming golden then red at maturity, longitudinally striated. Drupe ellipsoid, 9–13 × 9–15 mm, orange, apiculate at the apex. Exocarp orange, shiny. Endocarp hard. Seed ellipsoid, globose and erect. Albumen abundant. Embryo apical, 0.5 mm long.

Known from 700 m altitude in Gabon.

## Vernacular names

Cameroon: kafé apan, ban ofan (Yaoundé), nom young (Bobili).

## Properties and uses

Hard and solid wood. Against colds, leaves are applied to the chest and a maceration of leaves is taken as a nasal wash. Seeds are eaten after being roasted.
