# *Drypetes gilgiana* (Pax) Pax & K.Hoffm.

**Genus**: [[Drypetes]]
**Family**: [[Putranjivaceae]]
**Protologue**: Cyclostemon gilgianus Pax, Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 33(2): 278 (1903)

## Description

Plate 45; Figure 8(F-H)

in Engler, Pflanzenr. IV, 147, XV (Heft 81): 261 (1922).

Cyclostemon gilgianus Pax, Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 33(2): 278 (1903).

Lingelsheimia gilgiana (Pax) Hutch., Fl. trop. Afr. 6(1): 691 (1912).

Shrub reaching 8 m in height; trunk up to 5 cm in diameter; bark pale in colour; branchlets channeled, with hairs 0.2–0.3 mm; buds 1–2 mm × 0.7 mm, with dense hairs 0.4 mm or glabrous. Stipules caducous, broadly triangular, 0.7 × 0.5 mm. Leaf: petiole 0.5–1 mm on the shorter side, 1.5–2 mm on the longer side; lamina ovate to broadly elliptic, 5–9.5–12 × 2.3–4.5–5 cm, papyraceous, with asymmetric base, with one side rounded to slightly cordate, apical portion 0.8–22 mm long, margin regularly hooked-pointed spaced 5–6 mm apart, 0.5 mm long, directed forward, not recurved; secondary veins 7 or 8, with visible and slightly raised looping on both surfaces, tertiary and quaternary venation raised and visible on both surfaces, with scattered hairs 0.1–0.2 mm especially toward the base of the lower surface. Male inflorescence in the leaf axils, with 1 to 4 flowers. Male flower: pedicel slender, 6 mm, glabrous or with very short hairs 0.05 mm; sepals 4, imbricate, suborbicular, 2 × 2.5 mm, glabrous except for marginal cilia 0.1 mm; stamens 12 to 15, most obscurely arranged in a whorl around the margin of the disk, more or less inserted between the lobes of the disk, very little enclosed in the disk, filament 2.2 mm, glabrous, anther 0.9 × 0.4 mm; disk lobed and dentate, 2.8 mm in diameter, 1.2 mm in height, fleshy. Female inflorescence in the leaf axils, with 1 (or 2) flowers; bracts minute. Female flower: pedicel slender, 6–7–10, with hairs 0.05–0.07 mm; sepals 4, elliptic, 3.2 × 2.7 mm, yellowish, glabrous; ovary globose slightly depressed, 3 × 3.5 mm, glabrous; style 0.3–0.4 mm, with 2 stigmas, each with two cuneiform branches, 2 × 1 mm. Fruit solitary, globose, 1.8 × 1.6 cm, with persistent sepals, with 1 or 2 seeds; pedicel 0.8–10 mm, glabrous. Seed 12–14 × 7 × 10 mm.

## Distribution

Senegal, Guinea-Bissau, Guinea, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Benin, Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea (mainland), Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Central African Republic and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon: Estuaire, Ngounié, Ogooué-Ivindo and Ogooué-Maritime

## Source note

This species resembles D. diopa. Both species flower and fruit already at the shrub stage. However, D. gilgiana remains smaller, whereas D. diopa becomes a large tree and can reach a height of 25 m. The form of the leaves, particularly the base, is always different between the two species. The surface of dried fruits of D. diopa is much more granulate (when examined with a × 10 magnifying glass) compared to that of D. gilgiana. In Gabon, in general, D. gilgiana is found closer to the coast than D. diopa, although they overlap in certain areas
