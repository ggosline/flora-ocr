# *Strombosia scheffleri* Engler

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Strombosia]]

Bot. Jahrb. 43: 166 (1909).

## Synonymy

- *S. toroensis* S. Moore, Journ. Bot. 58: 224 (1920)
- *S. minor* Engl., Bot. Jahrb. 43: 168 (1909)

## Description

Tall tree 10–30 m high and 40–50 cm in diameter, with pendulous branches. Young branchlets green, ± two-winged along 2 opposite lines. Older branchlets brownish, almost cylindrical. Internodes 1–2(–3) cm long. Petiole glabrous, 0.9–3 cm long, channelled above, longitudinally wrinkled below. Lamina glabrous on both faces, dark green, ± shining above, pale green, dull below, oblong to ovate, 9–20 × 4–9 cm; base obtuse or rounded, sometimes attenuate, apex shortly acuminate, or shortly apiculate obtuse, or obtuse. Midvein slightly concave above, prominent, striated below. Secondary veins 6–7 pairs, ascending, arched, anastomosing 1–2 mm from the lamina margin, concave on the upper face, prominent on the lower face. Veinlets fine, subparallel to one another, 2–4 mm apart, anastomosing, scarcely visible above, prominent below.

Inflorescence a many-flowered fascicle, axillary or extra-axillary on a bract cushion. Pedicel striated, 2.5–3 mm long, surrounded at the base by ciliolate bracts. Floral bud cylindrical, 2–3 × 1.5 mm. Flower whitish-green, greenish, or yellowish-green. Sepals 0.5 mm long, fused to each other at the base, ciliolate on the margin, apex subacute. Petals valvate, glabrous externally, pubescent on the upper third of the inner face, almost entirely free, oblong, 2.5–5 × 1–1.5 mm; apex and lateral margins papillose. Stamens 5, epipetalous, 3.5 mm long; filament fused to the petal, 3 mm long; anther with 2 lobes, 0.5 mm long, with ± lateral longitudinal dehiscence slits. Ovary semi-inferior with 3 imperfect uniovulate locules; central placenta erect. Style short, 1–2 mm long, terminated by a trilobed stigma with indistinct lobes.

Fruit pedicel finely striated and strongly accrescent, surrounding almost the entire drupe except at the apex. Apical depression bordered by sepal-like lobes or teeth. Receptacle, epicarp, and mesocarp thin. Endocarp hard, light brown, with a spongy appearance, 1.5 mm thick. Seed solitary, obovoid, with abundant albumen. Embryo apical, very small.

## Distribution

**Range**: From southern Nigeria to eastern Africa, particularly abundant in the east, and Angola.

**Cameroon**: Gounté (27 km from Bertoua), Buea, Masok (18 km W), Nkogham (gorge W), Mbem (40 km ESE Nkambe, trigonometric point).

**Gabon**: Lastourville.

## Habitat and ecology

Large forest tree preferring acid and granitic soils. Flowering and fruiting dates similar to those of *S. grandifolia*.

## Uses

The wood, red or white, very hard and dense, is highly useful for carpentry.
