## Description

**Habit**: tree 10–30 m tall and 40–50 cm in diameter, with pendant branches. Young branchlets green, ± biwinged along 2 opposite lines. Older branchlets brownish, almost cylindrical. Internodes 1–2(–3) cm long.

**Leaves**: petiole glabrous, 0.9–3 cm long, canaliculate above, wrinkled longitudinally beneath. Blade glabrous on both faces, dark green, ± glossy above, pale green, matt beneath, oblong to ovate, 9–20 × 4–9 cm; base obtuse or rounded, sometimes attenuate; apex shortly acuminate, or shortly apiculate and obtuse, or obtuse. Midrib slightly concave above, prominent and striated beneath. Secondary nerves 6–7 pairs, ascending, arcuate, anastomosing 1–2 mm from the blade margin, concave on the upper face, prominent on the lower face. Nervules fine, subparallel to one another, 2–4 mm apart, anastomosing, inconspicuous above, prominent beneath.

**Inflorescence**: axillary or extra-axillary multiflorous fascicles on a bracteate cushion. Pedicel striated, 2.5–3 mm long, surrounded at the base by ciliolate bracts. Flower bud cylindrical, 2–3 × 1.5 mm. Flower whitish-green, greenish, or yellowish-green.

**Flowers**: Sepals 0.5 mm long, fused to one another at the base, ciliolate on the margin, apex subacute. Petals valvate, glabrous outside, pubescent on the upper third of the inner face, almost entirely free, oblong, 2.5–5 × 1–1.5 mm; apex and lateral margins papillose. Stamens 5, epipetalous, 3.5 mm long; filament fused to the petal, 3 mm long; anther 2-locular, 0.5 mm long, with ± lateral longitudinal dehiscence slits. Ovary semi-inferior with 3 imperfect uniovulate locules; central placenta erect. Style short, 1–2 mm long, terminated by a trilobed stigma with poorly distinct lobes.

**Fruit**: pedicel finely striated and strongly accrescent, surrounding almost the entire drupe except at the apex. Apical depression bordered by sepal-like lobes or teeth. Receptacle, epicarp, and mesocarp thin. Endocarp hard, pale brown, with a spongy appearance, 1.5 mm thick. Seed solitary, obovoid, with abundant albumen. Embryo apical, very small.

## Types

**Holotype**: *Deistel 456*, Cameroon (B).

## Distribution

**Range**: from southern Nigeria to East Africa, where it is particularly abundant, and Angola.

**Gabon**: Lastourville region (known from a single collection).

## Habitat and ecology

Forest preferring acidic and granitic soils. Flowering and fruiting dates are the same as for *S. grandifolia*.

## Gabonese material examined

- *Le Testu 7121*, Lastourville (fl., March)

## Properties and uses

The wood, red or white, very hard and dense, is highly suitable for carpentry.
