# *Thecacoris grandifolia* (Pax & K.Hoffm.) Govaerts

**Genus**: [[Thecacoris]]
**Family**: [[Phyllanthaceae]]
**Protologue**: Cyathogyne grandifolia Pax & K.Hoffm. in Engl., Pflanzenr. IV.147.XV : 41 (1922)

## Description

in Govaerts et al., World Checklist & Bibliogr. Euphorbiaceae : 1517 (2000). Cyathogyne grandifolia Pax & K.Hoffm. in Engl., Pflanzenr. IV.147.XV : 41 (1922). Shrub to small tree up to 10 m tall; trunk 10 cm diameter; branchlets pubescent, glabrescent or not. Stipules triangular, 5–11 × 2–5 mm, pubescent. Leaf: petiole 4–8 mm; blade obovate-elliptic, (10–)17–34 × (4–)6–15 cm, rounded to cuneate at base, acuminate for 1–3 cm at apex, glabrous or with a few hairs on the midrib beneath; lateral veins 8–12 pairs. Inflorescence solitary, pendulous, simple, up to 60 cm long; bracts narrowly triangular, 1.5–3 mm long. Male flower: pedicel ± 1 mm, sparsely puberulent; sepals 5(–6), ± 1 mm long, with thin margin, entire or lobulate-dentate, glabrous or with a few hairs; rudimentary petals or absent, 0.5–1 mm long, glabrous; stamens 5(–6), ± 1.5 mm long, glabrous; disc lobes pubescent; pistillode ± 0.7 mm long, glabrous. Female flower: pedicel 2–6 mm; sepals 5, 1–2 mm long, margin as in male flowers, with a few hairs outside; annular disc, pilose outside; ovary 3–4 mm diameter, pubescent; styles glabrous. Fruit with pedicel (10–)15–18 mm, 10–15 × 13–18 mm, greenish to brownish, with appressed hairs, sometimes almost glabrous at maturity. Ripe seeds not seen.

## Distribution

south-west Cameroon and north-west Gabon; in Gabon, known from the Cristal Mountains

## Ecology

primary and secondary forest; in Gabon encountered at 600 m altitude, elsewhere at 300–1300 m. b : Breteler (2011a), Govaerts (2000), Léonard (1995a), Pax & Hoffmann Bibliography (1922)
