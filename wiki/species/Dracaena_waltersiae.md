# *Dracaena waltersiae* Damen

**Genus**: [[Dracaena]]
**Family**: [[Dracaenaceae]]
**Protologue**: Blumea 63 : 47 (2018)

## Description

Shrub with lianing habit or palm-like tree, reaching 10 m in height; bark slightly fissured longitudinally, grey, without resin. Leaves distichous, distributed uniformly along the branches, internodes ± 1.5–3 cm in length, stem visible; sheath embracing the stem over 0.5–0.7 of the circumference, over a length of < 1 cm; pseudopetiole ± 2–3 mm, distinctly darker than the lamina, with scarious wings; lamina subfalcate, elliptic to narrowly obovate, up to 14 × 3 cm, base attenuate, apex acute to acuminate with a mucro of ± 1 mm, thin, coriaceous, uniform green, midvein grooved above, prominent below, secondary nerves indistinct above, prominent below. Inflorescence terminal or axillary, spiciform, sometimes with a few branches, up to 30 cm in length, with smooth axes; peduncle very bright orange; fascicles with up to 4 flowers, each subtended by a small, caducous, triangular, scarious, brown bract up to 4 mm in length; floral bracts barely visible, triangular, up to 1 mm in length. Flower with pleasant scent; pedicel 6–9 mm, articulated 5–8 mm from the base; perianth 27–28 mm in length, greenish-white, violaceous toward the apex, with lobes longer than the tube, 16–17 × 1.5 mm; stamens inserted near the throat, up to 4 mm shorter than the lobes, filament filiform, anther ± 2 × 1 mm; ovary not seen; style exsert up to 1 mm, stigma capitate, ± 0.9 mm in diameter. Fruit oblong, up to 16 × 20 mm (in herbarium), reddish-brown; receptacle ± 3 × 2 mm. Seed not seen.

## Distribution

Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Central African Republic; in Gabon, known from Haut-Ogooué

## Ecology

forest edges, shrubby savannas, inundated forests, gallery forests, dry forests on sand, Kalahari Sands; in Gabon as elsewhere at 400–620 m elevation. Flowering recorded in February and March.
