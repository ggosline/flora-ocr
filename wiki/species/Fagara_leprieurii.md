# *Fagara leprieurii* (Guill. et Perr.) Engl.

**Family**: [[Rutaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Fagara]]

## Description

Woody, prickly species with pinnate leaves, translucent-gland-dotted leaflets and paniculate inflorescences. Flowers are 4-merous and unisexual; the female gynoecium develops into one or more follicles, each containing a shining seed with a fleshy embryo. The treatment spelling in its heading is *leprierii*, while the plate and established epithet printed elsewhere are *leprieurii*.

## Distribution

West and Central tropical Africa; Gabon material is included.
