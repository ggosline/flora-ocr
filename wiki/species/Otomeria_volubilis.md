## Description

Terminal contracted inflorescences, with generally short peduncle less than 1 cm. Numerous isostylous flowers, subsessile in attractive clusters. Calyx green puberulent with 5 lobes of which 1-2 reach 7-12 × 2-3 (5) mm, the others acute small and erect. Corolla bright red or light carmine, puberulent with slender tube 22-32 × 1 mm, slightly swollen in the upper 3 mm; limb spreading 19-22 mm diam., with 5(6) ovate apiculate lobes, 4-5 mm wide; narrow throat surmounted by erect red hairs. Anthers whitish included 2.2 × 0.7 mm with internal locules somewhat shorter than the external ones; pollen white. Glabrous style exceeding the throat by ± 5 mm, with 2 pink diverging stigmatic lobes, 1.2 mm long.

Infructescence remaining contracted. Fruits oval to obovate 7-10 × 3-5 mm, longitudinally nerved, persistent degraded after seed dispersal; the latter brown, angular, ca. 0.5 mm.

## Discussion

Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Central African Republic, Congo and Uganda. Forest edges, post-cultivation scrubland. The beauty of the flowers and the abundance of flowering in this species draw the attention of horticulturists.
