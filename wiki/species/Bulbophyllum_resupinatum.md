# *Bulbophyllum resupinatum* Ridl.

**Genus**: [[Bulbophyllum]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bol. Soc. Brot. 5 : 198 (1888)

## Description

– SYNTYPE : Moller, Sao Tomé, s.n., syn – COI (chosen by Vermeulen, 1987). – FAC 2 : 354 (1992) ; FCAM 2 : 439 (2001).

Pseudobulbs 0.8–5 × 0.5–1.4 cm, ovoid to ellipsoid, 2-leaved. Leaves subsessile, 1.3–8.5 × 0.5–1.6 cm, oblong to linear-lanceolate, obtuse. Inflorescence dense or lax, 1.8–40 cm, rachis not flattened, zigzag or not, 8–60-flowered. Flowers small, dark red or sepals yellowish or greenish, spotted with purple and labellum purple with whitish base. Sepals acute, glabrous or with dark external hairs; dorsal sepal oblong-triangular to ovate-lanceolate; lateral sepals very obliquely triangular to elliptic-triangular. Petals linear, acute, straight or falcate, thin, generally glabrous. Labellum broadly ovate or triangular-ovate, apex obtuse or rounded, recurved, thick, glabrous. - Pl. 92, p. 185.

distinguished 2 varieties: one (var. filiforme) already collected in Gabon, but the typical variety is likely there:

a. var. resupinatum

Inflorescence dense < 8.6 cm. Exterior of floral parts glabrous or papillose, without dark hairs. Petals straight.
