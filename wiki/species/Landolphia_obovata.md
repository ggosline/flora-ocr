# *Landolphia obovata* O.Lachenaud & Jongkind Planche 62

**Genus**: [[Landolphia]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Candollea 77 : 30 (2022)

## Description

Large liane; branches with brown-orange lenticels, with short glossy hairs or rarely glabrous. Leaf imparipinnate, 5–9 pairs of pinnae, oblong to elliptic, 1–4 times as long as wide, 3–14.5 × 1–7.5 cm, narrowly cordate or auriculate at base, obtuse to acuminate at apex, coriaceous and dark green fresh, almost black when dry, above, on dry glabrous or weakly hairy at base of impressed midvein, below generally hairy on main veins; secondary veins 5 to 9 pairs, curved, tertiary venation reticulate to ± scalariform; glandular dots visible below, very dark on dry. Inflorescence axillary with 3–7 flowers, pedunculate reaching 5 mm, hairy, bracts of 0.5–1 × 0.3–0.5 mm, tomentose outside. Flower: pedicel reaching 4 mm, hairy; bracteoles similar to bracts, calyx with imbricate lobes of 1–1.5 × 0.7–1 mm, partly hairy outside, with basal colleters inside; corolla white to yellowish, with tube 6–9 mm long, 1–2 mm wide at base, 2–2.5 mm at level of anthers and 1.5–2 mm at throat, interior with very sparse hairs around insertion of stamens, exterior glabrous except near lobes, with lobes 1.5–2 times as long as tube, 1–1.5 times as long as wide, 9–17 × 2–4 mm, hairy at base, ciliate; stamens 1.7–2.3 mm long, inserted at 0.75–0.80 of tube length, included 0.2–0.7 mm, anther 1.5–2 mm long; pistil 6.3–7.3 mm long, ovary hairy at apex; style 3–4 mm, hairy at base or glabrous. Fruit broadly obovoid and turbinate, 8–12 × 5–8 cm, orange, smooth or slightly bosselé, bearing 1–2 cm from apex a rounded transverse crest, 1–2 cm wide and about 1 cm thick, with 5 to 15 seeds; wall 0.5–1.5 cm thick; pulp pale yellow to orange. Distribution south of Cameroon and Gabon to Gabon, Estuary, Moyen-Ogooué, Ngounié, Nyanga, Ogooué Maritime and Woleu-Ntem. Ecology primary and secondary forest, gallery forest in savannah in Gabon, up to 800 m altitude.
