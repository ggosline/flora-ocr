# *Costus lucanusianus* var. *major* K. Schum.
**Family in Vol. 9**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Current placement**: Costaceae · **Genus**: [[Costus]]
## Description
Distinguished from typical [[Costus_lucanusianus|*C. lucanusianus*]] by greater size and leaves glabrous or nearly glabrous beneath.
## Distribution
Described from Cameroon (Preuss 413). Koechlin considered its presence in Gabon probable but cited no confirmed Gabon specimen; Cameroon alone is therefore recorded in frontmatter.
## Source
Koechlin, J. (1964). Zingibéracées. *Flore du Gabon* 9: 86.
