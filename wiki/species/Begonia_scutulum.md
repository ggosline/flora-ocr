# *Begonia scutulum* Hook.f. Planche 29

**Genus**: [[Begonia]]
**Family**: [[Begoniaceae]]

## Description

in Oliv., Fl. trop. Afr. 2 : 575 (1871). Plant reaching 35(–50) cm in height. Leaves peltate; petiole (3.5–)6–38 cm, hirsute; blade spreading when fresh, not bullate, asymmetric, elliptic-ovate to broadly elliptic or broadly elliptic-ovate, 5.5–17 × 3.5–12 cm, apex obtuse to acuminate, margin entire to sinuate or irregularly and coarsely dentate, upper surface without long hairs. Inflorescence: peduncle 1.5–12 cm, with 2–3 ♂ flowers and 1(–2) ♀ flowers with yellow tepals; bracts 2–9 mm long. ♂ Flower: tepals 10–21 × 10.5–20 mm; androecium with 24–44 stamens. ♀ Flower: pedicel elongating in fruit to 2(–6) mm; tepals 12–18 × 13–20 mm; ovary obovoid to broadly obtriangular-obovoid or broadly ellipsoid, (4.5–)7.5–14.5 × (4–)6–10 mm, hirsute, wingless or with 3 small wings, obovate to obtriangular depressed, up to 3.5 mm wide; styles 3. Fruit broadly ellipsoid to broadly obtriangular-obovoid or globose, 5.5–12(–15) × (4.5–)6–11.5 mm; rostrum absent or up to 2 mm.

## Distribution

endemic to Gabon (Cristal Mountains, Chaillu Massif, Doudou Mountains); uncommon

## Ecology

on the ground or rarely on vertical rocky faces, on flat terrain or steep slopes, in primary forest or old secondary forest, often along watercourses or rapids; 250–700 m altitude. B: Irmscher (1925), Sosef (1994). BIBLIOGRAPHY
