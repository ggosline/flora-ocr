# *Strychnos ngouniensis* Pellegrin

**Genus**: [[Strychnos]]
**Family**: [[Loganiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Mus. Hist. Nat. Paris 32 : 394 (1926); Mém. Soc. Linn. Norm. n. ser. 1 : 37, fig. 8 (1928) (« ngounyensis »)

## Description

("ngounyensis"). — FWTA ed. 2, 2: 43, p. p., excl. the two syn. and all the material cited. — LEEUWENBERG, Act. Bot. Neerl. 14: 218 (1965); Meded. Landbouwh. 69 (1): 202, fig. 31, map 32 (1969).

Liane reaching a height of 20 m and a length of 30 m or more; stem 3 cm in diameter or more; bark dark brown, lenticellate; wood cream-brownish; branches non-lenticellate, cylindric; branchlets pubescent or glabrous, often furrowed when dry; tendrils in pairs.

Leaves with short petiole, often with sparse pubescence, 1–2 mm in length; blade subcoriaceous or papyraceous when dry, narrowly ovate, narrowly elliptic or oblong (and for leaves situated at the base of branches and on the main axis generally ovate, smaller and comparatively wider, or reduced to a small bract), (2–) 3.5–11 × 1.6–5.5 cm, reaching in shade up to 14 × 5.5 cm, acuminate or apiculate at apex, rarely obtuse (for leaves situated toward the base of branches), rounded, subcordate or sometimes (for leaves situated at the apex of branchlets) cuneate at base, glabrous or with a few hairs on the midvein above, pubescent beneath the primary veins or glabrous below; midvein impressed above, especially at the base; 2 pairs of secondary veins curved along the margin, the first being less distinct; tertiary venation reticulate, especially below.

Inflorescences axillary, contracted or not, few- to many-flowered, 1–3 × 1–3 cm; peduncle, branches and pedicels short or rather short, with sparse pubescence. Flowers 5-merous. Sepals fused to one third of their height, orbicular or nearly so, 0.8–1.5 mm in length, rounded or nearly so, finely ciliate, outside with short pubescence, inside glabrous and with a few dark longitudinal lines. Corolla, in the mature bud, 6–7.5 (–8.5) mm in length, white, outside with sparse and often fine pubescence, especially on the tube, inside with a broad densely pilose ring at the throat, but lobes and base of tube glabrous; tube 0.6–1 times as long as the lobes, 2.5–4 mm in length; lobes narrowly triangular, 3.5–4 (–4.5) × 1.5–1.8 mm, recurved. Stamens well exserted, inserted at the throat of the corolla, with glabrous filament, 1.1–1.7 times as long as the anther; anther elliptic, usually furnished, inside, at the base, with long rigid erect hairs. Pistil 6.5–7 (–9) mm in length, hirto-pubescent; ovary ovoid, 1.5–2 × 1 mm, with 2 locules; style glabrous at apex, 5–5.5 (–7.5) mm in length; stigma capitate; ca. 10 ovules per locule.

Fruits subglobose, ca. 3 cm in diameter and containing ca. 10 seeds; wall ca. 2 mm thick. Seeds flat, ellipsoid, rugose. Pl. 25, p. 133.

## Type

Le Testu 2303, Gabon

## Discussion

Cameroon, Gabon, Congos. Dense or secondary forests; often on river banks. Altitude 0–200 m.
