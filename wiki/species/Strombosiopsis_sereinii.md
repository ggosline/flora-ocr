# *Strombosiopsis sereinii* Breteler

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Strombosiopsis]]

Adansonia **23**(2): 303–306 (2001).

The **third** species described in [[Strombosiopsis]], and the second in Gabon
after [[Strombosiopsis_tetrandra|S. tetrandra]]. Published as *Novitates
Gabonenses* 40, with a key to the three species then recognised.

Postdates the Flore du Gabon treatment (Vol 20, 1973), which treats the genus
as monospecific.

## Notes

The genus characters are on the [[Strombosiopsis]] page: a tree whose bark
exudes a sticky red resin, with a tetramerous flower, semi-inferior ovary, and
a drupe wrapped by an accrescent pedicel and tipped by four calycine teeth.
