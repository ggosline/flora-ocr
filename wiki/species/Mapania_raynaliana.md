# *Mapania raynaliana* D.A.Simpson Planche 114

**Genus**: [[Mapania]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Revis. Mapania : 131, fig. 25 (1992)

## Description

Robust herb, with a thick rhizome 4–6 mm; stem solitary, central, 50–80 cm × 4–6 mm, trigonous, glabrous. Leaves all basal; sheath linear–lanceolate, 10–20 × 2.5–3.5 cm, green to reddish-brown, fairly thick; blade linear, plane, fairly thick, trinerved, the largest 80–110 × 4–6 cm, base gradually narrowed into sheath, apex gradually acute. Inflorescence terminal, in a globose head, 35–55 mm wide, brown, composed of numerous crowded and distinct spikes; involucral bracts 3–4, 2–50 × 1–5 cm, the lower ones foliaceous, spreading, linear to lanceolate–ovate, without pseudo-petiole, apex acute; spikes elliptic to ovate, 10–15 × 4–6 mm, composed of numerous crowded spikelets; spikelet bracts crowded, linear to linear–oblong, 6–7 × 1.2–1.4 mm, plane, brown, glabrous, apex acute, each subtending a spikelet with 2 male flowers and 1 female flower. Flower: floral bracts 4, linear, 6–7 mm long, the 2 lower ones, 0.4–0.6 mm wide, brown, keel not winged and hispid, apex acute, the 2 upper ones 0.3–0.4 mm wide, glabrous, apex truncate; style 4.8–5 mm, with 2 branches 1.5–2.3 mm. Achene obovoid, 1–1.5 mm long, lenticular, brown, tuberculate with 2 distinct lateral ribs.

## Distribution

Cameroon and Gabon; rare in Gabon, found only in the Cristal Mountains and Middle-Ogooué (2 collections)

## Ecology

Primary or secondary forest, often along rivers and streams; probably only at medium altitude (300–600 m). Taxonomic note: formerly included in M. macrantha, but differs by the head inflorescence with less crowded and more distinct spikes, and also by the shorter spikelet and floral bracts (6–7 mm in M. raynaliana and respectively 9–10 and 8–11 mm in M. macrantha). M. purpuriceps differs by its purplish inflorescence, with larger spikelet and floral bracts. Bibliography b: Simpson (1992).
