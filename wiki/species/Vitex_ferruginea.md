# *Vitex ferruginea* Schumach. & Thonn. Planche 27(1-6)

**Genus**: [[Vitex]]
**Protologue**: V. fosteri C.H.Wright, Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1908(10) : 437 (1908)

## Description

in Schumach., Beskr. Guin. pl. : 288 (1827). V. fosteri C.H.Wright, Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1908(10) : 437 (1908). Shrub or tree 5–25 m tall; young branches with dense ocre to tawny pubescence, later very shortly pubescent. Leaf: petiole 3–13 cm, with short ocre to tawny pubescence, deeply canaliculate; leaflets (3 or) 5 to 7, with petiolules 3–12 mm, canaliculate, lamina elliptic to narrowly obovate-elliptic, 5.5–11(–17) × 2–5(–6.8) cm, attenuate at base, apex distinctly acuminate, entire, upper surface nearly glabrous, usually becoming dark brown to blackish in herbarium, with main vein canaliculate, lower surface with short yellowish to ocre pubescence (hairs ± 0.3 mm) concentrated on the veins; secondary veins ± 11 to 15 (to 17) on each side, impressed above (scarcely visible in herbarium), without visible reticulation above, reticulum very fine and green, not prominent below. Inflorescence formed of axillary dichasia, generally developing before the leaves, 2–5(–10) cm long, of which 1–4 cm for the peduncle, typically much shorter than the leaf; axes with long yellowish to ocre appressed to spreading hairs; bracts narrowly obovate-elliptic, 1–2 mm wide, strongly discolorous, glabrous and dark brown to blackish on upper surface (in herbarium), tomentose with long ocre to tawny hairs on lower surface. Flower: calyx 2.5–)3–5 mm long, tomentose with dense ocre to tawny hairs, lobes 1–2 mm long, blackish within; corolla 8–12 mm long, with short ocre pubescence, cream with lower lip pale violet; stamens long-exserted, papillose, anther violet; ovary hairy at apex; style papillose. Fruit globose to obovoid, 15–20 mm in diameter, apex often apiculate and with a few hairs, subtended by the spreading calyx.

## Distribution

widely distributed in tropical Africa, from Benin to Somalia and South Africa; in Gabon: apparently uncommon, known from Ngounié and Nyanga. Conservation status: Widely distributed; probably Least Concern (LC).

## Ecology

forests of various types, degradation savannas, fallows; in Gabon at 0–600 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1200 m.

## Source note

This represents the first record of this species for the flora of Gabon. Bibliography: Cabral (2013), Hawthorne & Jongkind (2006), Huber et al. (1963), Lisowski (2009), Meerts (2020a, 2020b), Sales (2005), Verdcourt (1992).
