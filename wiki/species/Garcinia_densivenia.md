# *Garcinia densivenia* Engl.

**Genus**: [[Garcinia]]
**Family**: [[Clusiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 40 : 563 (1908)

## Description

Dioecious shrub, 3–7 m tall; latex cream-white; branchlets angular, slightly winged. Leaves opposite, glabrous; petiole (3–)5–15 mm, deeply channeled and transversely wrinkled; blade ovate to ovate-elliptic or elliptic, (4.5–)7–21 × (1.5–)2.5–9.5 cm, long-cuneate at base, acuminate at apex, subcoriaceous; main vein prominent above and acute below, lateral veins 5–12 pairs, sunken above and prominent below, reticulation very dense to indistinct (when dry) on both surfaces, secretory canals scarcely visible, and if present subparallel to the main vein. Inflorescence in axillary or terminal racemes, multiflorous, often several together; rachis tetragonal, 2–10 mm long; bracts imbricate, 1 × 1 mm, glabrous, caducous. Flowers unisexual, pentamerous; pedicel 1–7 mm, glabrous; sepals subequal, suborbicular, 1 × 1 mm, glabrous; petals suborbicular, 4–6 mm long, greenish-white. Male flower: disk 5-lobed, 2.5 mm tall; staminodial bundles 5–7 mm long, filaments free at apex, anthers 8–9 per bundle, spheroidal. Female flower: stamens much reduced; interstaminal disk glands 1 × 1 mm; ovary 3–5-locular; style with 3 or 4 sessile, flabellate stigmas. Fruit globose to distinctly lobed, 1–3 cm in diameter, smooth, glabrous, white to yellow or orange, with pedicel 0.3–1.5 cm; sepals and disk glands persistent. Seeds 3–5, ellipsoid, 1.5–2.5 × 0.9–1.2 cm, brown with white nerves.

## Distribution

Southern Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea and Gabon; poorly distributed in Gabon, known from Ogooué-Ivindo and Ogooué-Lolo.

## Ecology

Primary and secondary forest, riparian forest, littoral forest, on ridges; in Gabon and elsewhere up to 400 m altitude. B: Engler (1908), Pellegrin (1959), Sosef & Dauby (2012). Bibliography
