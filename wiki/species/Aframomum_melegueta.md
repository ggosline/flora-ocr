# *Aframomum melegueta* (Rosc.) K. Schum.
**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Aframomum]]
## Description
Herb 1.5–2 m with narrow 18–22 × 1.8–2.5 cm leaves and nearly absent ligule. Basal one-flowered spikes c. 8 cm. Pink or pale-mauve flower with yellow-throated labellum to 10 cm across. Fruit 5 cm; seeds granular, 3.5 mm. Widespread West and Central African forest species.
