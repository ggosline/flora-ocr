# *Amaranthus gracilis* Desf. ex Poir.

**Family**: [[Amaranthaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Amaranthus]]

## Description

Glabrous annual or subshrub 30 cm–1 m. Leaves ovate, rhombic or broadly triangular, 2.5–7.5 × 1.5–5 cm, usually notched at apex. Slender racemes of distinctly stalked cymules reach 12 cm. Female perianth 1.5–2 mm with three tepals. Fruit indehiscent, subglobose, apiculate, striate and minutely rough; seed dark purple and lenticular.

## Uses

Common pantropical crop weed eaten as a vegetable.
