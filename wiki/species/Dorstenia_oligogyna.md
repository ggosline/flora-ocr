# *Dorstenia oligogyna* (Pellegrin) C.C. Berg

**Genus**: [[Dorstenia]]
**Family**: [[Moraceae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia, sér. 2, 16 (4): 422 (1977)

## Synonyms

- Trymatococcus oligogyna PELLEGR., Bull. Mus. Nat. Hist. Nat. Paris, sér. 2, 1: 62 (1929).
- Craterogyne oligogyna (PELLEGR.) LANJ., Rec. Trav. Bot. Néerl. 32: 277 (1935).

## Description

Plants reaching 2.5 m in height, with erect stems, often unbranched, with a woody base, the leafy parts having 3-10 mm thickness, sparsely puberulent with stiff hairs to almost glabrous; internodes 1.5-3 mm. Leaves spiral (at least on the stem). Blade lanceolate to oblong, sometimes tending to be panduriform, generally wider in the upper half, 14-50 × 4-16 cm, chartaceous to subcoriaceous, acuminate to subacute; base truncate, obtuse, acute or attenuate, with entire or subentire margin; upper and lower surface (almost) glabrous, or often young leaves bearing scattered hairs, partially uncinate on the midvein at the lower face; veins slightly prominent above, more prominent below; midvein convex above or plane at the base of the blade; (12-) 14-22 pairs of secondary veins without clearly parallel tertiary veins. Petiole 3-4 mm in length and approximately 1-3 mm thick, sparsely puberulent to almost glabrous, ± thickened at the base. Stipules subpersistent, semi-amplexicaul, lanceolate to ovate, 8-17 mm long, coriaceous, sometimes briefly apiculate, shortly pubérulent to almost glabrous, with visible veins.

Inflorescences solitary or grouped by 2 or somewhat more; narrowly to broadly turbinate to almost discoid, 4-8 mm in diameter. Peduncle 4-10 mm long; peduncle and external face of the receptacle sparsely puberulent. Bracts ovate to reniform, approximately 1.5 mm long, almost glabrous but always ciliolate, sometimes with a few distinct veins, scattered from the base of the peduncle to the margin of the receptacle where they are arranged in approximately 2 rows. Flowers with 2-3 (-4) divided perianth, 0.5-0.7 mm high, puberulent with, at the upper part, small papilla-like hairs, claviform or oblong to globose; stamens 1-3, most often 2, with filaments 0.7-1 mm long, strongly swollen at the base and most often ± cushion-like, glabrous or nearly so, their upper part generally incurved; anthers 0.2-0.3 × 0.2-0.3 mm with connective generally wide and ± swollen. ♀ flowers solitary, the free parts of the perianth 0.6-0.8 mm high, 4-lobed, puberulent; style approximately 1 mm long; stigmas 2, 0.5-1.5 mm long.

Infructescences approximately 1 cm in diameter, broadly turbinate to subglobose. - Pl. 7, p. 31.

## Type

Le Testu 2234, Gabon, près Sindara (lecto-, P)

## Discussion

This species resembles very much in its vegetative part the West African D. djettii GUILLAUMET, a rare species. D. oligogyna is known only from the region of the Ngounyé in Gabon.
