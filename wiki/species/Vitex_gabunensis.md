# *Vitex gabunensis* Gürke var. gabunensis Planche 28

**Genus**: [[Vitex]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 18 : 167 (1893)

## Description

V. impressinervia Mildbr. ex W.Piep., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 62, Beibl. 141 ("142") : 44, 57 (1928). Shrub or small tree 0.8–4(–5) m tall; branches glabrous, solid, smooth, tinged with violet or purple when young. Leaves opposite or rarely whorled by 3, generally blackening in the herbarium; petiole (1–)9–18(–22) cm long, 2–2.5 mm thick, stiff, glabrous or sparsely puberulent; leaflets 5(to 7), with channeled petiolules, 5–20(–30) mm long, glabrous or sparsely puberulent, those of the lateral leaflets shorter (0–5 mm), blade of the median leaflet narrowly elliptic-obovate, (9–)13–28(–35) × 3–8(–12) cm, those of the lateral ones two to five times smaller, attenuate at the base, apex rather abruptly contracted into an acumen 0.5–2 cm long, with entire and recurved margin, thin but somewhat coriaceous, upper surface dark green glossy when fresh, dull and generally becoming dark greenish in the herbarium, glabrous, lower surface paler green, with glands absent or scattered and scarcely visible, glabrous including the main veins, these often tinged with purple; main and secondary veins slightly impressed above, secondary veins (5 to)7 to 15(to 18) on each side, arched, anastomosing in arches, reticulation scarcely visible, not or slightly raised above, not raised below; domatia present at the base of secondary veins, pouch-shaped or with round opening. Inflorescence formed of 1 to 4 pairs of superposed dichasia, on leafless nodes at the branch apex, fairly dense; dichasia 2–3 × 3–4 cm, peduncle 0.5–4.5 cm long, with 3 or 4 levels of branching; axes more or less flattened, shortly and sparsely puberulent; bracts linear to narrowly triangular, the lower ones reaching 8 mm in length, the floral ones 1 mm, ciliate. Flower: pedicel 1–4 mm long, ± 0.5 mm in diameter; calyx campanulate, 1.5–3 mm long, obtuse or rounded at the base, glandular at the apex, sparsely and shortly puberulent, hairs ± 0.1 mm, appressed, with triangular teeth, (0.2–)0.5(–1) mm long, generally obtuse; corolla 7–15 mm long, yellowish-green or dirty reddish-green or dull greyish-brown on the exterior, dark red-purple on the lobes on the interior, yellowish at the throat, with very short pale tawny appressed hairs, partly papillose, tube bent; stamens slightly exserted, filaments glandular; ovary flat to convex, sprinkled with a few golden globular glands at the apex, glabrous or rarely with a few scattered hairs. Fruit obovoid, 10–26 × 8–18 mm, flat or depressed at the apex, glabrous, yellowish to orange, often dotted with white; fruiting calyx almost flat to slightly concave, 7–12 mm in diameter, glabrous.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon and Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, fairly widespread (Estuaire, Ngounié, Ogooué-Lolo, Woleu-Ntem). Conservation status: Widely distributed; probably Least Concern (LC)

Cameroon and Gabon; in Gabon, widely distributed. Conservation status: Widely distributed; probably Least Concern (LC)

## Ecology

Primary humid forests, secondary forests; at 50–700 m altitude

## Source note

By its very short inflorescences, localized at the branch apex, V. gabunensis superficially resembles V. grandifolia, with which it has generally been confused until now. However, V. gabunensis, having a glabrous ovary, is certainly not related to V. grandifolia, which possesses an ovary bristly with hairs at the apex. At the vegetative stage, V. gabunensis is distinguished from V. grandifolia by its habit (it is a shrub or small tree of modest size), smaller and much less coriaceous leaflets, furnished with domatia on the lower surface, and branches not inhabited by ants. V. grandifolia can become a large tree, has leaflets without domatia and hollow branches inhabited by ants. We see no significant difference between V. gabunensis and the type specimen of V. impressinervia Mildbr. ex W.Piep. (Mildbraed 6074, iso-: HBG).
