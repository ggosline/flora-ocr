# *Cola ballayi* Cornu ex Heckel.

**Genus**: [[Cola]]
**Family**: [[Sterculiaceae]]
**Modern family**: [[Malvaceae]] s.l. — *Sterculiaceae* as circumscribed here is superseded; see [[Sterculiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Heckel, Ann. Inst. Bot. Marseille I : 101 et 104, fig. 9 (1893), réf. orig

## Synonyms

- Cola subverticillata De Wild.

## Description

, orig. ref. — Tschirch et Oesterlé, Anat. Atl. pharmac. : 347 (1900), — De Wild. Et. fl. Bas et Moy. Congo II : 306 pl. XXXI et XXXII (1908). — A. Chev. Vég. Ut. Afr. VI : 139 (1911).

Tree reaching 10 to 20 m tall, with very young branchlets slightly pubescent. Leaves very large whorled or sub-whorled by 5 to 15 leaves. Petioles very unequal, robust, 1 to 20 cm long. Blade coriaceous 20 to 30 cm long by 7.5-15 cm wide. 7 to 9 pairs of pinnate lateral nerves; the lower pair is basal. Scales of axillary buds acuminate acute.

Inflorescences robust 5-12 cm long, puberulent. Flowers large, long-pedicellate. Calyx 2-4 cm in diameter, divided into 5 (-8) lobes light pink. Ovary with 6 (-8) carpels. Follicles with 5-15 seeds each having 4-6 cotyledons of pink tint at maturity.

Type of the species: specimen that grew in the greenhouses of the Paris Museum; only the photograph given by Chevalier remains (Vég. Ut. Afr. pl. VIII (1911)), and in herbarium a slender branch, typical in the shape of its leaves but not in their arrangement.

Species widespread from Cameroon to former Belgian Congo and Ubangi.

## Vernacular names

Aboel, Gband, Mgwangh (Fang); Mwali (Mayombe); Obanga (Mitsogho); Nkasu (Kouilou Inf.)

## Discussion

Uses. The nuts of pleasant flavor are consumed but not commercialized.
