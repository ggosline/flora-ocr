# *Dacryodes le-testui* (Pellegr.) Lam

**Family**: [[Burseraceae]] · **Genus**: [[Dacryodes]]

## Description

Leaves 6-jugate; rachis 30–40 cm, densely ferruginous-pubescent; leaflets subsessile, oblong, acuminate, asymmetrically rounded at base, to 18 × 6.5 cm, with dense long simple hairs on petiolules and veins and normally no stellate hairs. Female flowers on 3 mm pedicels; sepals 6–7 mm and hairy on both faces; petals 7 mm, glandular-hairy inside; stamens six, 1.75–2 mm; disc and ovary glabrous. Fruit reported edible but otherwise unknown, leaving the species’ distinction from hairy *D. edulis* uncertain.

## Distribution

Endemic to Gabon: Mayombe Bayaka, Lastoursville and upper Ngounié. Holotype: Le Testu 2103, Manzembi.

Aubréville A. (1962). Burseraceae. In *Flore du Gabon* 3: 86–87.
