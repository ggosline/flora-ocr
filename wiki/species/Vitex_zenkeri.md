# *Vitex zenkeri* Gürke Figure 7

**Genus**: [[Vitex]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 33 : 293 (1903)

## Description

Shrub 1.5–3 m or more rarely a small tree reaching 8 m in height; branches almost glabrous, often purple in color. Leaf: petiole 4–18 cm almost glabrous; leaflets (3 to) 5 to 7, with petiolules 2–15(–20) mm, poorly distinct from the blades, with blade narrowly obovate-elliptic, 8–20(–24) × 3–7(–10) cm, long-attenuate in the lower half, abruptly acuminate at the apex, margin entire, practically glabrous, upper surface dark green, often glossy, midvein with a few scattered hairs and short papillae, lower surface remarkably discolorous, typically very pale grey-green, punctate with dark spots (glands), sometimes slightly puberulent on the veins, with midvein keeled, often tinged with mauve; secondary veins 6 to 10 (to 12) on each side, anastomosing in arches, reticulum slightly prominent or almost invisible above; domatia present. Inflorescence formed of axillary dichasia, 6–)8–25 cm long, of which (3.5–)7–16 cm for the peduncle, typically with 3 or 4 branches at certain nodes, pauciflorous; axes glabrous or sparsely puberulent, often flattened, sometimes tinged with mauve. Flower: pedicel well-developed, 2–5 mm; calyx campanulate, (2.5–)3–4 mm long, shortly puberulent with short appressed hairs not obscuring the surface, with lobes 0.5–1.5 mm long; corolla ± 8 mm long, greenish white or pale dirty yellow, spotted with mauve or pink; stamens exserted; ovary glabrous, glandular at the apex. Fruit ± 15 × 11 mm, subtended by the spreading calyx, with teeth remaining clearly visible.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, principally in the west and north of the country. Conservation status: Widely distributed; probably Least Concern (LC)

## Ecology

Dense forests; at 0–1000 m altitude

## Source note

This represents the first record of this species for the flora of Gabon. The inflorescence comprising nodes with 3 or 4 branches is a rare characteristic in the genus Vitex, and had not yet been recorded for this species. The type specimen of V. lehmbachii Gürke shows this character and is certainly conspecific. The two names were published simultaneously.

As the types of V. zenkeri were much more widely distributed, it is preferable to retain this name (Meerts 2020a). B: Huber et al. (1963), Meerts (2020a). Bibliography. Insufficiently known taxa and doubtless new species. The material collected in Gabon includes specimens that cannot be assigned with certainty to any of the species treated above. These are probably new species, which will remain to be described when sufficient material becomes available. One of the most remarkable is the following. Fruit ± 11 × 10 mm, shortly hairy at the apex. Specimens observed: van Valkenburg, Degreef, Issembé & Boussiengui 2969 (BRLU, K, WAG); Dauby et al. 2855 (BRLU).
