# *Leptoderris glabrata* (Welw. ex Baker) Dunn

**Genus**: [[Leptoderris]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1910 : 390 (1910)

## Description

, syn. nov. L. cylindrica De Wild., Bull. Jard. Bot. État Bruxelles 7 : 228 (1920), syn. nov. L. mildbraedii Harms, Notizbl. Bot. Gart. Berlin-Dahlem 9 : 294 (1925), syn. nov. L. glabrata (Welw.) Dunn var. glaberrima Hauman, Bull. Jard. Bot. État Bruxelles 24 : 227 (1954), syn. nov. Liane or lianescent shrub; branchlets puberulent to tomentellous, glabrescent. Stipules ± persistent, ± narrowly triangular, sometimes with rounded base, 3–6 × 1.5–4 mm. Leaf: petiole cylindric, 1.5–6(–12) cm, sparsely puberulent, glabrescent; rachis 2.5–10(–17) cm, angled above, pilose as on petiole; stipels ± persistent, 1–3 mm; leaflets 5 to 9, with petiolule of 3–4(–6) mm, sparsely pilose, blade obovate-elliptic, 3–10 × 2–6.5 cm, rounded to obtuse to subcordate at base, acute (sometimes shortly acuminate) to emarginate at apex, coriaceous, glabrous or with scattered hairs on midvein above, with short, scattered and glabrescent hairs below; lateral veins in 5 to 8 pairs. Inflorescence in ± terminal panicle, reaching 50 cm in length, ± densely flowered, puberulent; bracts and bracteoles caducous, narrowly triangular, 0.5–1 mm in length. Flower: pedicel 0.5–3(–4) mm; calyx funneliform, slightly curved, 3 mm in length, with teeth 0.5 mm in length, with brown and appressed hairs on both faces; corolla white, petals 5–8 mm in length, with claw 2 mm, shortly auriculate, glabrous or with a few small hairs at apex, wings distinctly shorter than keel, obliquely oblong, keel incurved, with elliptic-oblong petals; stamens 6 mm, anthers ± 0.25 mm in length; pistil with short and appressed hairs. Pod narrowly oblong-elliptic, 8–9 × 2.5–3 cm, 1-seeded, with appressed hairs; wing ± 3 mm wide.

## Distribution

from Guinea in the west to Central Africa, East Africa and Angola; in Gabon, known from Nyanga, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

primary and secondarized forest; in Gabon at 0–200 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1900 m
