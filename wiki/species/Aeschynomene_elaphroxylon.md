# *Aeschynomene elaphroxylon* (Guill. & Perr.) Taub.

**Genus**: [[Aeschynomene]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Herminiera elaphroxylon Guill. & Perr., Fl. Seneg. 1 : 201 (1832)

## Description

in Engl. & Prantl, Nat. Pflanzenfam. 3(3) : 319 (1891). Herminiera elaphroxylon Guill. & Perr., Fl. Seneg. 1 : 201 (1832). Shrub 2.5–4(–8) m tall; branches pubescent and hirsute with acute hairs 1–2 mm, especially near leaves furnished with straight to hooked spines 2–10 mm long, 1–2.5 mm wide at base, often in pairs. Stipules ± ovate, 10–13 × 6–9 mm, of which 2–4 mm for the auricle, tomentose on the outside. Leaf: petiole 2–10 mm; rachis 4–15 cm, hirsute and with small, straight or slightly hooked spines, 1–2 mm; leaflets in 10 to 20 pairs, with elliptic-oblong blade, 7–30 × 4–10 mm, with (obliquely) cordate base, apex truncate to emarginate and mucronate, nearly glabrous above, pubescent below, brown, with prominent venules in 6 to 8 pairs. Inflorescence axillary, in pseudoraceme, with 2 to 4 flowers, reaching 4.5 cm long; peduncle 1–2 cm; bracts ovate, 6–7 × 3–5 mm. Flower: pedicel 1–1.6 cm; bracteoles ovate to rhomboidal, 10–16 × 5–10 mm, pubescent abaxially; calyx pubescent and with prickles, pale green to pale brown, upper lip 19–25 mm long, lower lip 15–20 mm long; corolla bright yellow to yellow-orange, standard orbicular, 30–45 mm diameter, pubescent on the upper part, apex emarginate, wings obovate, 30–45 × 18–22 mm, pubescent, upper half sculptured (as in Crotalaria), keel with semi-obovate laminae, 25–40 × 10–20 mm, lacerate on the upper margin and united by these expansions 0.5–1.5 mm long, outer face pubescent; stamens 28–40 mm long, exceptionally the vexillary stamen free, tube of filaments divided in two, the free parts 10–15 mm long, anthers 3 × 0.8 mm, dorsifixed; ovary 20–22 mm long, with stipe 2–4 mm, somewhat pubescent, with 15 to 17 ovules; style 15–20 mm, the terminal part glabrous. Pod (5–)10–14 cm long, curved in a spiral of 2–3 cm diameter, articles 6 to 17, 6–8 × 7–9 mm, hispid-glandular, hairs very short and dense mixed with hairs or prickles 1–2 mm, the surface finely reticulate. Seed semi-orbicular, ± 4 × 4 × 1.5 mm, upper side truncate, black; hilum dark, somewhat excentric.

## Distribution

widespread in tropical Africa, including Cameroon, Central African Republic, and Democratic Republic of the Congo; expected in Gabon

## Ecology

river and lake margins, even in water, swamps, reed beds, open vegetation on sand, rare in dense forest; at 0–1600 m altitude; forming extensive stands, but also found as isolated individuals. Use: The very light wood is used as cork for fishing nets and for the manufacture of seats (in Democratic Republic of the Congo)

## Vernacular names

nzoane (fang) ; ntamba-(mpongwè) ; oranga-ranga (galoa)

## Source note

Although in herbaria Gabonese specimens of this species are lacking, Raponda-Walker & Sillans (1961) treat it (as Herminiera elaphroxylon) and mention it as common along the banks of the Ogooué. The presence in Equatorial Guinea (De la Estrella et al. 2010) also remains to be confirmed. b: De la Estrella et al. (2010), Léonard (1954a, 1954b), Raponda-Walker & Sillans (1961). ibliography
