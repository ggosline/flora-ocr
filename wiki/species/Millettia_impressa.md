# *Millettia impressa* Harms subsp. impressa

**Genus**: [[Millettia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst . 26 : 288 (1899)

## Description

Scrambling shrub; branches glabrous or with short appressed hairs. Stipules ± caducous, narrowly triangular, 2.5–6 mm long. Leaf: petiole 4–11 cm; rachis 4–8.5 cm; stipels filiform, 2–3 mm long; leaflets 7 to 9, with petiolule 2–5 mm, blade elliptic to obovate, 2.3–14.5 × 1.5–5.7 cm, rounded at the base, shortly acuminate or obtuse at the apex, coriaceous, glabrous or with very short sparse appressed hairs on the lower surface, becoming ash-green to greyish-brown when dry; nerves ± sunken on the upper surface, the lateral ones in 5 to 9 pairs, rather prominent and clearly ascending, joined 1.5–2 mm from the margin in very faint arches, nervules in dense but inconspicuous reticulum. Inflorescence axillary and terminal, in panicles of pseudoracemes, reaching 25 cm long, shortly tomentose with ferruginous hairs; brachyblasts very short, < 2(–3) mm long; bracts linear, the lower ones 3–4.5 × 0.8 mm, the upper ones 1.5–3 × 0.3–0.5 mm, pubescent, shorter than the calyces. Flower: pedicel 1.5–3 mm, puberulent; bracteoles elliptic, 0.5–1 × 0.3 mm, pubescent; calyx with short appressed hairs, tube 1.7–4 mm long, with triangular teeth 0.5–2 mm long, the upper ones fused into a bidentate lobe; corolla pale violet, standard with suborbicular to ovate limb, 7–11 × 6 mm, with claw 2 mm, densely covered with appressed hairs on the outer surface, wings oblong, 7–7.5 × 1.8 mm, with truncate base and claw 2–2.5 mm, glabrous on the outside or slightly puberulent towards the apex; keel 6–10 mm long, with claw 2 mm and short hooked basal auricle, pubescent on the outside towards the apex; stamens monadelphous, 6–11 mm long; ovary linear, with dense appressed hairs; style curved, 2 mm, glabrous, stigma minute. Pod obliquely and narrowly obovate-lanceolate, ± 6 × 1.5 cm, with 2 to 4 seeds, ferrugineous-hirsute, with reflexed apicule at the apex. Seed oblong to subrhomboidal, 11–13 × 9–10 × 4.5 mm, brown.

## Distribution

Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola (Cabinda); to be sought in Gabon

## Ecology

Savannas and forest galleries; at 10–200 m altitude

## Source note

This species has not been collected in Gabon, but its presence in the southern part of the country is plausible. The type specimen, Soyaux 99, comes from Cabinda (Angola), and not from Gabon as indicated by Pellegrin (1949). The species is represented in central Africa by subsp. impressa. The other subspecies, subsp. goetzeana (Harms) Gillett (= Millettia goetzeana Harms), is found in Tanzania and northern Mozambique; it has leaflets with rounded rather than acuminate limb, and larger flowers with standard 10–15 mm long. Bibliography: Brummitt et al. (2007), Gillett (1961), Hauman (1954a), Pellegrin (1949).
