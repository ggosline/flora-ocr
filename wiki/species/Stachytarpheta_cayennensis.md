# *Stachytarpheta cayennensis* (Rich.) Vahl Planche 39(1-8)

**Genus**: [[Stachytarpheta]]
**Family**: [[Verbenaceae]]
**Protologue**: Enum. pl. 1 : 208 (1804)

## Description

Herb or perennial subshrub, 0.4–2.5 m tall; stem subcylindrical, pilose when young, glabrescent. Leaf: petiole 1–1.5 cm; blade ovate to elliptic, 2–9 × 1.6–4.5 cm, rounded to attenuate or decurrent at base, obtuse to subacute at apex, margin distally crenate-serrate, generally blackish when dry, pilose on upper surface, glabrous or pilose on veins on lower surface. Inflorescence 20–45 cm long; spike slender, sparsely pilose; axis up to 3.5 mm thick in fruit; bracts 3.5–5 × 0.5–1 mm, narrowly triangular and narrowing to a subulate apex, ± equal to or slightly shorter than the fruiting calyx, very narrowly scarious and ± ciliate on margin. Flower: calyx ellipsoid, 4–5 mm long, with 1 to 3 peltate glands near apex, with 4 subequal teeth; corolla pale blue to lilac or white, with tube 4–6 mm long, with lobes 1.5–2 mm long; style persistent, exsert 1.5–2.5 mm from calyx after corolla fall. Fruit with mericarps 3–4 mm long, almost black at maturity.

## Distribution

native to tropical America and introduced and often naturalized in tropical and subtropical Africa, including Cameroon, São Tomé and Príncipe, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Central African Republic; in Asia and the Pacific; in Gabon: Estuaire, Haut-Ogooué, Ngounié, Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

gardens, secondary forests, roadsides, disturbed areas; in Gabon 0–585 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1200 m

## Vernacular names

petite verveine queue de rat (fr.) ; rough-leaf false vervain, snakeweed (angl.). Usage : Les feuilles sont consommées et parfois utilisées contre les fièvres ou comme médicament pour l’estomac.
