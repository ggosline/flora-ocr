# *Drypetes inaequalis* Hutch.

**Genus**: [[Drypetes]]
**Family**: [[Putranjivaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. trop. Afr. 6(1): 684 (1912)

## Description

Figure 9(D-F)

D. leonensis (Pax) Pax & K.Hoffm. in Engler, Pflanzenr. IV, 147, XV (Heft 81): 262 (1922), nom. illeg., non Pax (1909).

Shrub up to ±4 m, sometimes tree up to ±10 m tall; trunk up to ±10 cm in diameter, straight; bark brown to reddish brown; cut surface with a remarkable fruity odor; branchlets cylindrical to slightly flattened and furrowed, slender, with dense whitish to yellowish hairs, sometimes glabrescent; buds puberulent. Stipules caducous, oblong, elliptic-ovate, 5–6.5(−7) mm long, acuminate at the apex, with dentate margin toward the apex, shortly hairy, glabrescent, with lacerate base. Leaf; petiole (1−1.3−3.3(−3.9) mm, puberulent, glabrescent; blade obliquely ovate to obliquely elliptic-ovate, (4.5−6.5−8(−9.5) × (1.5−2.7−3.5(−3.9) cm, papyraceous, oblique to asymmetric and obtuse-truncate or subcordate at the base, margins meeting the petiole at the same point or separately, abruptly acuminate to caudate at the apex, crenate to serrulate margin, plane, undulate, dark green glossy above, paler green below, lower surface glabrous or puberulent; midvein glabrescent, secondary veins 4 to 6 on each side, slightly prominent above, ascending, looped and anastomosing well before the margin. Male inflorescence in the leaf axils, with 1(or 2) flowers; bracts ovate to triangular, 1.3–1.7 mm long, hairy. Male flower: pedicel slender, 1.2–1.7(−3) mm, puberulent; sepals 5, imbricate, suborbicular, 2.4–3.5(−4.9) × (2.5−2.8−3.8(−5.1) mm, obtuse at the apex, yellowish green, hairy on the outside with golden hairs, short hairs on the inside, short marginal cilia; stamens (8 to)10 to 18, arranged in a somewhat disordered fashion, most surrounded by the radial folds of the disk, filament (1.4−1.7−2.6 mm, anther 1.2−1.5(−1.7) mm long; disk slightly convex, 2.8−3.2(−3.4) mm in diameter, rugose-tuberculate, with irregular margin, densely puberulent; pistillode absent. Female inflorescence like the male, with solitary flower; bracts ±1.3 mm long, similar to males. Female flower: pedicel up to ±2.3 mm, puberulent; sepals 5, up to ±2.2 × 3.2 mm, puberulent on both faces, otherwise like the males; disk annular, up to ±3 mm in diameter, rugose-tuberculate, puberulent; ovary globose, 2-locular, hairy; style absent, stigma reniform, flabellate or obtriangular 0.7 × 1.8 mm. Fruit globose to slightly ellipsoid, (11.5−15.5−18.5(−21.5) × (9.5−13.5−16(−18) mm, orange to reddish at maturity, with persistent sepals, densely and shortly hairy with golden hairs, (1 or 2 seeds; pedicel (0.8−1−1.6(−2) mm, puberulent, glabrescent. Seed (8−10−13(−13.5) mm long.

## Distribution

Guinea, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroon, continental Equatorial Guinea and Gabon; in Gabon: Ngounié, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem

Habitat: primary or secondary evergreen forests; in Gabon at 100–950 m elevation, elsewhere up to 1,000 m.

## Source note

D. inaequalis has rarely been collected in Gabon. Its flowers share many characteristics with those of D. cinnabarina, but they are smaller and bear fewer stamens. The leaf size of D. inaequalis is, exceptionally in this genus, very consistent on the same branch. Their fruits are also different in these two species, being smaller in D. inaequalis.
