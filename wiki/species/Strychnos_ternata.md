# *Strychnos ternata* Gilg ex Leeuwenberg

**Genus**: [[Strychnos]]
**Family**: [[Loganiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Act. Bot. Ncerl. 14 : 224, fig. 2, 7-14 (1965); Meded. Landbouwh. 69 (1) : 258, fig. 13, phot. 6 et 7, carte 39

## Description

Meded. Landbouwh. 69 (1) : 258, fig. 13, phot. 6 et 7, carte 39. — (GILG in MILDBRAED, Wiss. Ergebn. zw. deutsch. Zentr.-Afr. Exped. 1910-1911, 2 : 62 (1922), nom. nud.).

Fairly large tree, 12-25 m in height; trunk 70-80 cm in diameter, with long and narrow buttresses, making it ± irregularly fluted; bark thin, pale grey-brown, somewhat scaly; wood hard, yellowish, without bark islands; sapwood paler than the heartwood; branches not spiny, indistinctly lenticellate, pale brown; branchlets often with a few hairs in the juvenile state, soon glabrous, not lenticellate, resembling the adult branches when the leaves are mature; stipular line distinct.

Leaves opposite, never ternate; petiole glabrous or with a few hairs, 5-15 mm long; blade papyraceous or coriaceous, even on fresh specimens, elliptic, oblong or ovate, 10-20 × 4-10 cm (or sometimes some smaller), apiculate, acuminate or almost caudate at the apex, rounded or cuneate at the base, glabrous above, below partially hirsute on the main veins; 2-4 pairs of secondary veins curved along the margin and impressed above.

Inflorescences terminal, umbel-like, fairly contracted, multiflorous, 5-10 × 6-9 cm; peduncle, branches and pedicels pubescent. Flowers 5-merous. Sepals pale green, connate at the base, unequal, linear, 4-6 × 0.2 mm, subulate, not ciliate, with scattered pubescence on the outside, on the inside often with a few hairs only at the base. Corolla, in the mature bud, 4.5 mm long, pale green, thin at the base, thicker towards the apex, glabrous on the outside, on the inside with a white-penicillate crown at the throat; tube urceolate, ± 2 times longer than the lobes, 3 mm long; lobes triangular, 1.5 × 0.8 mm, acute, erect. Stamens included, inserted at the base of the corolla, with filament short-pubescent, 2 times longer than the anther; anther cordate, ciliate all around with villous hairs, but less abundant at the apex. Pistil pubescent, 2 mm long; ovary ovoid, 1.8 × 1.2 mm, acuminate, 2-locular; stigma subsessile; 50 ovules per locule.

Fruits voluminous, hard, dark green, often somewhat yellowish, globose, 9.5-15 cm in diameter and containing 10-90 seeds; wall very thick, hard, irregularly bumpy on the inside, the thickness of this wall varying thus from 9 to 22 mm for the same fruit; pulp ochraceus, with the smell of rotten apple. Seeds flattened, ± plano-convex, obliquely ovate or elliptic, generally irregularly curved, 19-30 × 13-21 × 4-7 mm, smooth, with short pubescence; testa thick. Pl. 41, 7-14, p. 148.

## Discussion

South-East Cameroon. Semi-deciduous fairly dry forests with Sterculiaceae and Ulmaceae. Altitude 600-800 m.

Uses: Fruits used as mortars.

Breteler 1715, near Dimako, 28 km SW Bertoua (imm. fr., July) WAG, YA; 2196, near Gounté, km 27 road Bertoua-Bétaré Oya (fr., Dec.) BR, K, P, WAG; 2855, SE Djémiong, 45 km SW Batouri (fallen fr., tree bare, Apr.) WAG.
