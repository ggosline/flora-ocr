# *Commelina diffusa* Burm.f. subsp. diffusa Planche 6, Figure 2(G, H)

**Genus**: [[Commelina]]
**Family**: [[Commelinaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. indica : 18 (1768)

## Description

C. diffusa Burm.f. subsp. aquatica (J.K.Morton) Ogwal in Seyani & Chikuni, Proc. AETFAT Zomba Malawi 1991 1 : 417 (1994). Perennial or annual herb; fibrous roots; stem rooting at the nodes, creeping or ascending, up to 2 m tall, strongly branched, glabrous or bearing a uniseriate line of hairs below the sheath base. Leaves distichous; sheath generally closed, but often open along the creeping stem, ± 2.5 cm long, with a line of white uniseriate hairs on the margin and suture; pseudpetiole short; blade narrowly ovate-oblong to ovate, 1.5–8.5 × 0.6–2.7 cm, base oblique, cuneate to rounded, apex acute to acuminate or obtuse, sometimes mucronate, faces glabrous to pilose or rarely puberulent, sometimes scabrous above, often a paler green in the centre. Inflorescence: peduncle 0.7–2.5(–3) cm, glabrous or with a line of hairs; spathe solitary, usually slightly falcate, 0.8–4 × (0.3–)0.4–1.3 cm, base cordate, with free margins, glabrous or sparsely pubescent, apex acute or acuminate, surface generally glabrous, sometimes puberulent, often sparsely ciliolate, puberulent or pubescent; spathe with 2 cincinni, often distinctly exserted, the upper one with 1 to 5 male flowers or rarely 1 bisexual, the lower one with 3 to 7(–10) flowers generally bisexual, sometimes functionally male; bracteoles absent. Flower 10–15 mm in diameter; upper sepal 2.5–3.7 mm long, whitish-green translucent, the lower ones 2.5–4 × 1.5–2.5 mm, united for about ⅓ of their length, white-blue; upper petals broadly ovate-cordate, 4.5–9 × 4–7 mm, of which the claw 2–3 mm, bright blue or violet, the lower one cupuliform, 3–4.5 × 3–6 mm, generally concolorous; stamens with golden yellow pollen, the lateral ones with filament 4–7.5 mm, often blue, anther 0.7–1.7 mm long, the median one with filament 3.5–5.5 mm, anther 1–2.1 mm; staminodes 2 or 3, somewhat butterfly-shaped, the median one often rudimentary or absent; ovary 1–1.6 mm long; style 4–5 mm, blue or lilac, stigma capitate-deltoid. Fruit 3-locular, with 2 persistent valves, oblong-elliptic in dorsal view, (4.7–)5–6(–7) × 2.5–3 mm, slightly constricted between the 5 seeds, beaked or not, pale yellow or densely spotted with dark brown, dorsal locule indehiscent, with 1 seed, the ventral ones with 2 seeds. Seed broadly ovate to ellipsoid, compressed dorsiventrally, 1.8–3.1 × 1.5–2 mm; testa medium to dark brown, distinctly reticulate with 4 rows of ± 8 to 12 alveoles or rarely foveolate or smooth to alveolate; seed of the dorsal locule also compressed dorsiventrally or elongate-hemispherical, oblong-elliptic to elliptic or reniform, (2.4–)3.0–4.1 × 1.8–2.2 mm.

## Distribution

pantropical, but introduced in South and North America; in Gabon in the Estuary, Haut-Ogooué, Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

humid places and marshes at water's edge, clearings, roadsides, in crops such as rice, banana and in forestry, on sandy and clayey soils; in Gabon from 0 to 610 m altitude, from 40 to 2100 m in East Africa, elsewhere from 0 to 2550 m. Flowering in Gabon from November to July

## Vernacular names

essang (fang). Usage : Utilisé comme nettoyant vaginal

## Source note

In Gabon (and in West and central Africa) this is the typical subspecies. Subsp. montana J.K.Morton, present in Cameroon and Equatorial Guinea, is distinguished by its leaves without pseudpetiole, with blade base cordate to subcordate, and its smooth seeds.
