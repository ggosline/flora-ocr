# *Bacopa monnieri* (L.) Wettst. Planche 54(B)

**Genus**: [[Bacopa]]
**Family**: [[Scrophulariaceae]]
**Protologue**: Nat. Pflanzenfam. 4(3b) : 77 (1891) (comme « Bacopa monniera »)

## Description

Nat. Pflanzenfam. 4(3b) : 77 (1891) (as « Bacopa monniera »). Herpestis monnieria (L.) Kunth in Humb., Bonpl. & Kunth, Nov. gen. sp. 2 : 366 (1818). Perennial herb, reaching 10–40 cm in length; stem creeping to prostrate, much branched, glabrous, quadrangular. Leaf sessile; blade ovate to spatulate, 6–20 × 1–5 mm, obtuse to subacute at apex, with entire margins; venation palmate. Inflorescence foliaceous, each floriferous node bearing 1 flower. Flower with 2 bracteoles; pedicel 6–15 mm; calyx with 3 ovate sepals 5–6 × 2.5 mm and 2 lanceolate sepals 1.5 mm wide; corolla white to blue-violet, with tube 5 mm in length, upper lip bipartite and 3–5 mm in length, lower lip tripartite and 3–5 mm in length; anterior (abaxial) stamens with filaments 1.5–2 mm, the posterior (adaxial) ones with filaments 0.8–1 mm, anthers 0.5–0.7 mm in length. Fruit 5 × 3 mm, septicidal-septifragal.

## Distribution

pantropical species; in Gabon, collected in the Estuaire

## Ecology

ruderal zones, marshes, coastal sands; in Gabon, at 0–5 m altitude, in Madagascar, up to 500 m

## Uses

The plant has several medicinal uses. It is also cultivated as an ornamental plant for pond borders and aquariums

## Vernacular names

water hyssop (angl.). B : Aguilar (2001), Fischer (2006a), Philcox (1990). ibliographie
