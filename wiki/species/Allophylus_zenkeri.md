# *Allophylus zenkeri* Gilg ex Radlkofer

**Genus**: [[Allophylus]]
**Family**: [[Sapindaceae]]
**Protologue**: Sitzungsber. Bayer. Akad. Wiss. München 38 : 224 (1908)

## Description

Shrub, 2-3 m, to tree, 5-10 (-15) m, or even liane; limb acuminate, entire or obscurely dentate. Fruit red or yellow, pyriform, 7-8 mm. PL. 10, 1-9, p. 53.

## Type

Zenker 3203, Cameroun (holo-, B, iso-, P!)

## Discussion

Also known from Brazzaville (La Patte d'Oie), Normand D.W. 7 (fr., wood).

## Vernacular names

messii (Yaoundé)
