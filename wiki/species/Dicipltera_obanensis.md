# *Dicipltera obanensis* S. Moore

**Genus**: [[Dicliptera]]
**Family**: [[Acanthaceae]]
**Protologue**: S. Moore, Cat. Pl. Talbot S. Nigeria 90 (1913)

## Description

Annual plant (?) reaching 1.50 m tall, erect, with stem somewhat creeping at the base and ascending, ± simple, dark green, subquadrangular; internodes grooved, slightly pubescent, glabrescent. Petioles 1–3 cm long; leaves dark green; lamina ovate-acuminate, 6–13 × 2.5–5 cm, rounded at the base and equally cuneiform decurrent into the petiole; acumen elongate and rounded at the apex. 7–8 pairs of lateral veins, glabrous.

Inflorescences axillary and terminal, forming racemes approximately 1 cm long, entirely dark green, with slender, subquadrangular rachis, with internodes 5–10 mm approximately, the upper ones shorter; floral leaves rigid-subulate, approximately 4 mm long; biflorous involucres, one of the two flowers most often sterile; principal bract 14–16 × 7.5–8.5 mm; secondary bract 13 × 16 mm; glabrescent, they are often ciliate at the margins, with palmatinerved veins; bracteoles lanceolate, 2.5 × 0.5 mm, whitish and somewhat translucent. Calyx approximately 6 mm long; sepals of the same structure as the bracteoles, somewhat glandular-ciliate on the margins. Flowers clearly exceeding the involucral bracts. Corolla light pink-violet with two unequal lips, widely open, with white tube, 7 × 1.5 mm, 2.5 mm wide at the throat; upper lip 4 × 2.75 mm; lower lip 4.5 × 2.35 mm, 3-dentate. Stamens exsert; filament 2 mm; anthers 1.8 mm, with locules 0.9 mm; ovary 1.5 mm long; style glabrous 13 mm.

Capsule 7 mm long, broadly obovate, acuminate, stipitate, somewhat pubescent. 4 seeds approximately 1.5 mm in diameter, finely tuberculate, brown.

## Type

Talbot 1363, du Nigeria (BM, holotype! K, isotype!). Sierra Leone, Ghana, S. Nigeria
