# *Drypetes occidentalis* (Müll.Arg.) Hutch.

**Genus**: [[Drypetes]]
**Family**: [[Putranjivaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. trop. Afr. 6(1): 683 (1912)

## Description

Plate 49

Tree reaching 35 m in height, with orthotropic trunk and plagiotropic branches; trunk reaching 45(–60) cm in diameter, slightly fluted, with buttresses reaching 1.5 m in height, often forked on mature individuals; bark smooth, hard when cut, pale yellow, oxidising rapidly to dark orange, especially on the wood side, no mustard oil smell, but often a slight fruity odour; branchlets reddish, slightly flattened, 2 × 1.3 mm in cross-section, strongly fluted and sparsely hairy; buds sparsely hairy, with golden hairs. Stipules rapidly caducous, narrowly triangular, 6.4 × 1.3 mm, densely hairy, the scars whitish on reddish stems. Leaf: petiole 7–11 mm, densely hairy; blade narrowly elliptic, occasionally ovate, 8–14.5 × 2.8–5.5 cm, papyraceous to coriaceous, with oblique base, cuneate to broadly cuneate, acute and tapering over 9–15 mm at the apex, with margin dentate or denticulate over most of its length, with prominent teeth 1–1.4 × 7–9 mm, hooked or pointed, slightly recurved; venation glabrous except the base of the midvein on the lower face of young leaves, midvein slightly prominent above and prominent below, secondary veins 4 or 5 (or 6), curved and ascending, tertiary and quaternary venation reticulate and slightly raised on both faces, becoming obscure non-prominent on both faces of older leaves. Male inflorescence in the leaf axils and just below, with 1 to 7 flowers. Male flower: pedicel slender, 2–4 mm; sepals 5, imbricate, ovate, 2.5 × 1.5–1.7 mm, generally with marginal cilia; stamens 4 to 6, in a whorl around the disc margin, filament 2–3 mm, anther 0.4–0.6 × 0.3–0.6 mm; disc 1.8–2.4 mm in diameter. Female inflorescence in the leaf axils and on young branches just below leaves, with 1 to 5 flowers. Female flower: pedicel 6–8 mm, densely hairy; sepals 4, ovate, 4.3 × 2.8 mm, hairy outside; ovary 2.2 × 2.9 mm, 2-locular, hairy; stigmas 2-lobed, fan-shaped, 0.6 × 2.4 mm. Fruit laterally compressed, 16 × 16 × 11 mm, weakly 2-lobed, brownish-green immature, yellow at maturity, 2-locular, with persistent sepals, hairy, with persistent style remains, but generally without stigma; pedicel 9 mm, hairy. Seed 12 × 6 × 5.5 mm.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea (Bioko and Rio Muni), Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Central African Republic, Rwanda and Angola; in Gabon: Estuaire, Ngounié, Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo and Woleu-Ntem

Habitat: mixed semi-evergreen and evergreen terra firme forests, mono-dominant Gilbertiodendron dewevrei forest, rarely in submontane forest; in Gabon at 10–900 m altitude, elsewhere at 10–940 m.

## Source note

The margin of the leaf blades is distinctly serrulate, combined with the reddish hairy branchlets and petioles and the secondary veins pointing forward, it allows identification of this species with some experience. Canopy leaves can be quite coriaceous and of different colour and texture, with very obscure tertiary venation, but the teeth and secondary veins are the same as on shade leaves
