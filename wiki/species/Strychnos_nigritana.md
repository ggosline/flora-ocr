# *Strychnos nigritana* Baker

**Genus**: [[Strychnos]]
**Family**: [[Loganiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull.: 97 (1895)

## Description

— FTA 4 (1): 523. — FWTA ed. 1, 2: 24. — FWTA ed. 2, 2: 43. — LEEUWENBERG, l. c.: 204, fig. 6, carte 25.

— S. ciliicalyx GILG in SCHLECHTER, Westafr. Kautschuk-Exped. : 304 (1900), nom. nud. — GILG & BUSSE in ENGLER, Bot. Jahrb. 36 : 95 (1905).

Climbing shrub or liana reaching a length of 30 m and a height of 5-15 m or more; stem 4-13 cm in diameter; bark pale grey or pale to dark brown, lenticellate; wood cream-coloured; branchlets grey-brown, lenticellate; ultimate branchlets glabrous, lenticellate or not; tendrils in pairs. Leaves with petiole glabrous, 2-7 mm long; blade coriaceous in sun, subcoriaceous in shade, elliptic or narrowly elliptic, (3-) 6-11 (-14.5) × (1.6-) 2.5-7 (-8.5) cm, apiculate or acuminate at the apex, cuneate or rounded at the base, glabrous on both surfaces; one pair of secondary nerves curved along the margin; tertiary venation reticulate, for dried leaves prominent on both surfaces or only beneath; margin reflexed, especially for sun-exposed leaves.

Inflorescences axillary or sometimes terminal, generally several grouped together, lax, usually pauciflorous, 2-8 × 2-4 cm; peduncle, branches and pedicels glabrous. Flowers 5-merous. Sepals pale green, fused at the base, orbicular or ovate, 2.5-5 × 2-5 mm, rounded, ciliate, glabrous on the outside, on the inside with fine appressed pubescence at the base. Corolla, in the mature bud, 7.5-11 mm long, cream, yellow or pale green, often with lobes purplish at the apex, glabrous on the outside, on the inside with a ring-like brush of woolly white hairs at the throat as well as at the base of the lobes; tube cylindric or nearly so, 1-1.3 times as long as the lobes, 4-6.5 mm long; lobes narrowly triangular, 4-5 × 1.3-2 mm, thick, especially at the apex, acute, spreading. Stamens slightly exserted, inserted at the throat of the corolla, with very short glabrous filament; anther oblong, glabrous. Pistil hairy in the middle, 7-9 mm long; ovary ovoid or oblong, 3-4 × 1.5-2 mm, hairy at the apex, 2-locular; style thick, hairy at the extreme base, 4-6 mm long, stigma capitate; 50 ovules per locule.

Fruits orange or yellow, bluish-green when nearly ripe, large, hard, globose or nearly so, 4-6.5 × 4.8-6.7 cm and containing 8-45 seeds; wall thick, 1.5-3 mm thick, brittle for fresh fruits, hard for dried or unripe fruits; pulp pale orange. Seeds flattened, ± plano-convex, obliquely ovate, elliptic or ovate-angular, generally irregularly curved, 16-25 × 12-21 × 5-8 mm, with short thick erect hairs, rather rough. Pl. 18, 8-13, p. 127.

## Type

Barter 3249, Eppah, Nigeria du sud (lectotype : K, isotype : P)

## Discussion

West Africa and northern equatorial Africa. Dense or secondary forests; generally on periodically inundated banks, or forest galleries. Altitude 0-1,000 m.
