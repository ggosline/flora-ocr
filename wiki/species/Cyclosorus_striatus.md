# *Cyclosorus striatus* (Schum.) Ching (P.L. XXIV)

**Genus**: [[Cyclosorus]]
**Family**: [[Thelypteridaceae]]

## Description

— Ching, Bull. Fan. Mém. Inst. 10:249 (1941). — Tard., Mém. IFAN 28:125, pl. 22, f. 1-21 (1953). — Alston, FWTA:62 (1959).

Rhizome very long-creeping, naked, dark brown, with fronds very distant from each other. Petiole 30-50 cm long, dark brown at the base, light brown higher up, channelled, glabrous. Frond blade lanceolate, 50-60 cm long by 30-40 cm wide, bipinnate. Pinnae sessile, subopposite or slightly alternate, at right angles to the rachis, oval-lanceolate in outline, 20-30 cm long by 3-5 cm wide, narrower at the base, serrulate and shortly tapering at the apex, divided over 2/3 of their width into rounded, entire lobes with margins often rolled. Rachis glabrous. Margins ciliate. Texture subcoriaceous. Veins glabrous, raised, very prominent, 7-9 pairs per lobe, the basal pair of two contiguous lobes united into an excurrent vein. Sorus somewhat closer to the margin than to the midvein, surrounding the sinuses. Indusium small, persistent. Spores reniform, finely warty.

Tropical Africa from Sudan to Angola. Marshy grassland with various Cyperus species. Stream margins.
