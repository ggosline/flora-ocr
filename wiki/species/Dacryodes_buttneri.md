# *Dacryodes büttneri* (Engl.) Lam

**Family**: [[Burseraceae]] · **Genus**: [[Dacryodes]]

## Description

Large tree, trunk to 1.5 m diameter. Leaves 6–8-jugate; leaflets oblong-lanceolate, 12–20 × 3–4 cm, glossy above and densely covered beneath by persistent reddish appressed stellate scales. Flowers 3-merous, six stamens; disc stellate-tomentose; ovary glabrous, 2-locular. Fruit oblong-ellipsoid, about 3.5 cm, pink-violet when ripe, with one thin stone.

## Distribution and ecology

Characteristic of Gabon and also exported from Equatorial Guinea; said not to occur in Cameroon or Congo. A light-demanding old-secondary-forest species with a range roughly matching okoumé. Fruits are eaten boiled. Timber is used for plywood, joinery, flooring and furniture.

## Specimens examined

Klaine 99, 314, 1450; Fleury 26514, 26688; S.R.F.G. 1700, 1771, 1778–1780.
