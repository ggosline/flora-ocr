# *Costus nudicaulis* Bak.
**Family in Vol. 9**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Current placement**: Costaceae · **Genus**: [[Costus]]
## Description
Epiphytic herb c. 30 cm tall with tubular hairy sheaths and glabrous leaves clustered near the stem apex. A dense terminal spike c. 4 cm long has leaf-tipped bracts, each with one flower. Corolla yellow with 3.5 cm pink lobes; labellum c. 5 cm, pink with yellow markings.
## Distribution
The species is known only from its type, Mann 1033, Gabon. The treatment also mentions a hairy variety from the Brazzaville region without describing it as a separate account.
