# *Brachycorythis pubescens* Harv.

**Genus**: [[Brachycorythis]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]

* Herbarium deposit location indicated differently by error in FCAM.

Terrestrial herb. Tuberose, fleshy roots. Stem 15–80 cm, densely pubescent. Cauline leaves 2–6 × 1–2.5 cm, ovate to narrowly lanceolate, densely velvety pubescent. Inflorescence 4–35 cm, dense, < 40–50-flowered. Flowers small, pink to purple, centre often orange or yellow. Tepals often darker at apex. Sepals with dense external pubescence, the dorsal one elliptic to broadly obovate, obtuse, the lateral ones oblique, ovate or elliptic, rounded at apex, cochleariform. Petals obliquely ovate to elliptic. Labellum geniculate toward the base, broadly cuneiform or fan-shaped, base very narrow, apical region trilobed, dense pubescence on both faces. – Pl. 6, p. 23.

## Distribution

Mali, Guinea, Côte d'Ivoire, Togo, Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Congo (Brazzaville), Angola, Congo (Kinshasa), Burundi, Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, Zambia, Malawi, Mozambique, South Africa

## Ecology

alt. 500–2400 m. Fine grayish clayey soil very humid, montane grassland, edge of dambo and open woodland with Brachystegia or other open woodland
