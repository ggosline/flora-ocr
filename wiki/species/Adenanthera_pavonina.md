## Description

FZ 3 (1): 11 (1970); LIBERATO, Fl. Guiné Port., Mimosaceae: 14 (1972); FL. S. Tomé e Principe, Mimosaceae: 10 (1975); GUNN FSGM: tab. p. 51, fig. F, J-K (1984).

1. A specimen bearing the same number is determined as Calpocalyx heitzii.

Tree 6–7 m tall. Crown globose, fairly voluminous. Branchlets glabrous, dark brown, with brownish lenticels. Petiole glabrous, 2.5–11 cm long, weakly canaliculate above. Rachis glabrous, (2.5–) 9.5–30 cm long, canaliculate above. Pinnae 3–5 pairs. Pinna axis sparsely glabrescent to glabrous, 5.5–15.5 cm long, upper face carinate to pubescent canaliculate. Leaflets 9–15, alternate. Petiolule weakly pubescent above, 1–1.5 mm long, twisted. Blade glabrous appressed above and pubescent below; dark greenish-matte above and dull green below when dry; elliptic-oblong, 1.4–3.7 × 0.9–1.9 cm; base asymmetric: proximal margin attenuate and distal margin subcuneate; apex rounded. Midvein slightly pubescent and plane above; prominent below. Secondary veins ascending, rectilinear, weakly prominent on both surfaces. Tertiary veins scarcely or not visible.

Inflorescence a terminal panicle of multiflorous racemes. Raceme axis ± pubescent. Bract ± pubescent, very narrowly triangular-linear, ± 1.25 mm long, apex acute. Pedicel glabrous, 3.25–4 mm long. Flower ♂, yellow in life. Calyx campanulate, glabrous or very sparsely pubescent externally with hairs reflected toward the base, 0.75–1 mm long, lobes 5, free, very short, apex ± acute. Petals free, valvate, glabrous, narrowly lanceolate, 4–4.5 × 1 mm, reflexed in the open flower, base long-attenuate, keel sinuous on the upper 1/2 of the inner face, apex very acute. Stamens 10, some strongly appressed to the inner face of the petals, free; filament ± 4 mm long; anther oblong, 0.75 mm long, 2-locular, connective terminating in a pedicellate gland. Pistil with stipe ± 0.10–0.30 mm long; ovary glabrous, narrowly ellipsoid, ± 2.75 mm long, with 10–12 ovules; style ± 2 mm long, terminating in a tapered point.

Pod glabrous, black externally when dry, reaching 23 cm, with sinuous margin, compressed between the seeds, base attenuate stipitate, curved then becoming contorted at maturity. Seed white then red, shiny, orbicular, ± 1 × ± 1 × ± 5 mm, areole scarcely visible. — Pl. 15, p. 63.

## Type

Hermann s.n., Ceylan (BM)

## Discussion

This tree is planted in all tropical regions as an ornamental and shade plant.
