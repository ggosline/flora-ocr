# *Impatiens claeri* N. Hallé

**Family**: [[Balsaminaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Impatiens]]

## Description

Species described as new from Gabon. Leaves alternate, elliptic, characteristically laterally emarginate at the base. Flowers axillary and pink, with a deep flared lip, broad wings and a sinuous unjointed spur.

## Distribution

Gabon.
