# *Millettia mannii* Baker Planche 95.3,4 & 96.1–5

**Genus**: [[Millettia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. trop. Africa. 2 : 127 (1891)

## Synonyms

- Lonchocarpus zenkeri Taub., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 23 : 193 (1896).

## Description

M. aboensis (Hook.f.) Baker var. glabrescens Dunn, J. Linn. Soc. Bot. 41 : 214 (1912). Small tree, 2–5(–18) m tall; trunk up to 10 cm diameter; crown dome-shaped; bark brownish-greenish, with cream-coloured lenticels; branchlets shortly hirsute. Leaf: petiole 5–9 cm; rachis 20–36 cm, striated, channelled above, brown pubescent; stipels absent; leaflets (11-)13 to 25, opposite or alternate on the same leaf, petiolule 5–7 mm, densely brown pubescent, blade obovate-elliptic to elliptic-oblong, (3–)7–13(–15) × (1.6–)3–6(–9) cm, the terminal one largest, base rounded-cuneate, apex with an acumen of 0.5–1 cm, mucronate, papyraceous, dark green shiny and glabrous above, dull, ± greyish green and silky below, especially on the veins; lateral veins in 6 to 8 pairs, greyish above and more prominent below. Inflorescence axillary, in erect pseudoracemes; peduncles 15–35 cm, striated-channelled, lenticellate; lateral branches 5–10 mm; bracts caducous, 5–7 × 1–2 mm, silky. Flower: pedicel 3–4 mm, silky; bracteoles at the base of the calyx, ovate-circular, brown pubescent; calyx brownish-red, tube 5–7 × 4–6 mm, lobes 0.1–0.5 mm long;

corolla purple or white, standard with a green patch at the base, with a claw of 3–8 mm, limb broadly ovate to broadly obovate, 10–20 × 10–18 mm, base broadly cuneate, apex emarginate, brown pubescent near the incision, sometimes more ample, wings white at the base, with a claw of 3–11 mm, limb obovate to broadly oblong, 13–17 × 2–4 mm, keel white at the base, with a claw of 3–8 mm, limb 10–16 × 4–5 mm, upper suture with cream pubescence, becoming brown; stamens with white filaments, staminal tube 10–20 mm, free part curved, 3–7 mm; ovary narrowly lanceolate, 10–22 mm long, white, brown pubescent, with 3 to 8 ovules; style 3–10 mm, white to pale green, strigulose at the base, stigma pale green. Pod oblong to oblong-oblanceolate, 8–13(–15) × 1.2–2.2 cm, attenuate at base and apex, with 1 or 2(–4) seeds, green with reddish pubescence, later brown, glabrescent, sutures prominent, pubescent. Seed flattened, broadly ellipsoid, 10–13 × 9–12 × 2 mm, brown; hilum 1–5 mm long.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon and Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Haut-Ogooué, Moyen-Ogooué, Ngounié, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

Primary and secondary forest, white sands and gravels, river margins and pools, coastal forest, herbaceous vegetation; at 0–700 m altitude. Flowering throughout the year, mainly in November. Fruits from January to April and August to October. Uses: Sometimes planted in gardens as an ornamental plant

## Vernacular names

eboba (langue inconnue) ; mvakoué, vénékoué, nara (pahouin). b : Pellegrin (1949), Mollel & Adema (2006). ibliographie
