# *Pyrrosia lanceolata* (L.) Farwell

**Genus**: [[Pyrrosia]]
**Family**: [[Polypodiaceae]]

## Synonyms

- Acrostichum lanceolatum L., Sp. Pl.: 1067 (1753).
- Polypodium adnascens Hk., Syn. Fil.: 349 (1868) (pp. non Sw.).

## Description

— Farwell, Am. Midl. Nat. 12 : 245 (1931). — Tard. Mém. IFAN : 224 (1953). — Alston, FWTA : 46 (1959).

Rhizome very long-creeping, with fronds very widely spaced, bearing lanceolate, tapering scales with peltate base, margins furnished with long hyaline extensions (especially at the apex of the scale), formed of cells with thin walls, colorless lumen. Frond very shortly petiolate (petiole at most 1 cm long), lanceolate or linear-lanceolate, 6–10 cm long by 1–1.5 cm wide, cuneate at the base, with rounded apex. Texture coriaceous. Lower face bearing stellate, greyish scales with 6–8 broad, equal, obtuse branches; upper face bearing similar but deciduous scales. Fertile frond slightly longer than the sterile, reaching 10–15 cm by 0.8–1 cm wide, with lower face covered with grey scales; sori situated at the upper part of the frond, immersed in the grooves.

in Herb. Hermann (1, fol. 3) (BM). Ile du Prince, Congo, Afrique orientale, sud de l'Asie. Épiphyte sur Elaeis
