# *Faroa acaulis* R.E.Fr. Figure 6(B, C)

**Genus**: [[Faroa]]
**Family**: [[Gentianaceae]]
**Protologue**: Wiss. Ergebn. Schwed. Rhod.-Kongo-Exped. 1 : 260, f. 30 (1916)

## Description

Acaulescent herb, appressed to the ground, 0.5–1.5 cm tall. Leaves in 3 or 4 pairs, forming a rosette; petiole 0–2.5 mm; blade ovate-triangular to ovate, 5–45 × 3–22 mm, base obtuse to rounded, apex obtuse. Inflorescence in a hemispherical glomerule, 5–15 mm in diameter. Flower: pedicel 0.5–1.2 mm; calyx 1.5–4.5 mm long, with tube 0.5–1.5 mm long, lobes narrowly obovate to spatulate, 1–3 mm long, with reflexed apex; corolla 3–5 mm long, violet, blue, pink to whitish, with tube 1.5–3.5 mm long, lobes narrowly ovate-triangular, 1–3 mm long; stamens with filament 0.5–1.5 mm, each filament furnished at the base with a glandular scale, anther 0.3–0.6 mm long; ovary ellipsoid; style 0.7–2.5 mm, stigma bifid. Fruit 2–2.5 mm long. Seed 0.4–0.5 × 0.35–0.45 mm.

## Distribution

Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Rwanda, Burundi, Tanzania, Angola, Zambia and Malawi; in Gabon known only from Haut-Ogooué

## Ecology

savannas, sometimes flooded, savanna with Hymenocardia acida; in Gabon between 630 and 770 m altitude, elsewhere at 400–2200 m

## Source note

F. acaulis is the sole acaulescent species of the genus and furthermore bears a hemispherical, dense and multiflowered inflorescence. B: Boutique (1972), Paiva & Nogueira (1990), Sileshi (2002), Taylor (1973). Bibliography
