# *Stanfieldiella brachycarpa* (Gilg & Lederm. ex Mildbr.) Brenan var. brachycarpa

**Genus**: [[Stanfieldiella]]
**Family**: [[Commelinaceae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull. 14 : 285 (1960)

## Synonyms

- Buforrestia brachycarpa Gilg. & Lederm. ex Mildbr., Notizbl. Bot. Gart. Berlin-Dahlem 9 : 258 (1925).

## Description

Herb decumbent and ascending; stem 8–30 cm long, reddish, internodes with fine pubescence extending to the upper sheath. Leaves spaced on the stem, then concentrated at the apex; sheath 1–2 cm long, with a line of dense pubescence on the suture and long sparse to dense pubescence at the apex; blade narrowly ovate-elliptic, 7–14 × 2.5–4.5 cm, base acute with a few scattered long hairs and violaceous, apex acuminate to attenuate, margins glabrous, faces discolorous, the upper dark green with short purple hairs, the lower purple, generally glabrous. Inflorescence terminal, in a compound thyrse, subsessile, sometimes with 1 to 3 additional axillary thyrses, dense, wider than long, 3–4 cm in diameter, generally with up to 4 cincinni (rarely more), each with ± 5 bisexual and male flowers; peduncle (of an individual inflorescence) up to 1 cm. Flower white;

sepals narrowly elliptic-ovate to narrowly elliptic-oblong, 3.5–5 × 1–1.8 mm, as long as the fruit, pale green and bearing long hairs (± 0.75 mm) glandular, purple, shining; petals narrowly elliptic, 4–5 × 1.5–1.8 mm, glabrous, white; stamens of the male flower with filament 2.5 mm and discoidal anther with curved thecae, 1 mm in diameter, those of the bisexual flower with filament ± 5 mm, 3 with discoidal anther of ± 0.6 mm in diameter and the other 3 with more reduced anther with straight thecae, 0.4 mm long; ovary oblongoid-ovoid, ± 1 mm long; style straight, ± 1.75 mm, stigma capitate. Fruit cylindric or slightly trigonous, (4.5–)6–9(–10) × 2.25–3 mm, not exceeding or scarcely exceeding the calyx in length, glabrous or with glandular pubescence, dark brown shining, sometimes becoming dull grey-brown after dehiscence, with 2 to 4 seeds per locule. Seed verrucose.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon in Ngounié, Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo and Woleu-Ntem

Nigeria, Cameroon and Gabon; in Gabon in Moyen-Ogooué, Nyanga and Ogooué-Lolo

## Ecology

primary forest, near waterfalls, on rocky slabs in forest or inselbergs; in Gabon from 450 to 900 m altitude, elsewhere from 35 to 930 m. Flowering in February and April.
