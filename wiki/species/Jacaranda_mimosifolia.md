# *Jacaranda mimosifolia* D. Don

**Genus**: [[Jacaranda]]
**Family**: [[Bignoniaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Reg.: tab. 631 (1822); SANDWITH, Kew Bull. 1953: 455 (1954); GENTRY, Ann. Missouri Bot. Gard. 60: 803 (1973); Fl. Cam. 27: 54, tab. 17 (1984)

## Description

Tree up to at least 15 m tall and 45 cm in diameter. Branchlets cylindrical. Leaves compound bipinnate, 15-30 cm long, with 13-31 pinnae, each pinna 5-10 cm long with a winged rachis and 13-41 sessile leaflets, these 3-12 × 1-4 mm, narrowly elliptic, abruptly acuminate, cuneiform at base, papyraceous, glabrous or weakly puberulent along the midvein and margin.

Inflorescence in an open terminal panicle, with shortly puberulent branches. Calyx reduced, broadly campanulate, 5-denticulate, 1 mm long, 1.5-2 mm wide, subpuberulent at least along the margin. Corolla blue-violet with the tube white internally, tubular-campanulate above a narrowed neck that is not visibly curved and weakly widened towards the base, 3-4 cm long, 0.7-1.2 cm wide at the tube opening, lobes approximately 0.5 cm long; pubescent externally, especially towards the base, pubescent internally at the level of stamen insertion, and sparsely with long trichomes in the tube. Stamens didynamous; anthers monothécal, second theca reduced to a very small appendage, each theca 2 mm long; filaments pubescent below the middle. Staminode 2-2.5 cm long, median zone and apex glandular-pubescent. Pistil 2.2-2.5 cm long; ovary flattened cylindric, 3-3.5 mm long, 1.5-2 mm wide, glabrous, ovules 6-8 seriate in each locule. Disc short pulvinate.

PL. 12. - Jacaranda mimosifolia D. Don: 1, flowering branch × 0.5; 2, corolla inner face × 1; 3, stamen × 3; 4, fruit × 0.66; 5, seed × 1 (1-3, Dang 520, Cameroon; 4-5, Audru 3130, Senegal). (Taken from Flora of Cameroon 27: tab. 17, 1984).

Capsule orbicular compressed, often shallowly emarginate at apex and truncate to broadly but shallowly subcordate basally, 3.2-5.8 cm long, 3.7-5.5 cm wide, reddish-brown when dry, margins not undulate. Seeds thin, 0.9-1.2 × 1.1-1.7 cm, wing ± surrounding and clearly demarcated from the seed body. - Pl. 12, p. 49.

Ornamental tree very commonly cultivated in subtropical regions of the world; less commonly cultivated in tropical lowland regions. Native to northern Argentina and the adjacent part of Bolivia.

COMMON NAME: Jacaranda.

Although no collection from Gabon has been seen, it is possible that it occurs there; it is cultivated in Cameroon and elsewhere in tropical Africa.
