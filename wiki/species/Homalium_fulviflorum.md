# *Homalium fulviflorum* Sleumer

**Genus**: [[Homalium]]
**Family**: [[Flacourtiaceae]]
**Modern family**: **Salicaceae** — *Flacourtiaceae* as circumscribed here is superseded; see [[Flacourtiaceae]]
**Protologue**: BJBB 43 : 309 (1973). TYPE : Thomson 40, Gabon (holo-, iso-)

## Description

Tree (25-30-(40) m tall); branchlets glabrous.

Leaves with petiole 4-6 mm long, glabrous; blade subobovate-oblong, elliptic-oblong, cuneate at base, acuminate at apex, 7-9 × 3.5-4.5 cm, undulate-crenate, coriaceous, glabrous; secondary veins 4-5 pairs.

Inflorescences in axillary racemes, reaching 10-17 cm long, covered with light ferruginous tomentum. Flowers 5-merous; pedicel 1.5-2 mm long; calyx with tube 2 × 1.5 mm; lobes oblong-lanceolate, 3-4 × 1 mm; petals oblong-spatulate, 3-4 × 1.5 mm, accrescent to 8 × 2 mm in the fruiting state. - Pl. 24, 4-6, p. 77.
