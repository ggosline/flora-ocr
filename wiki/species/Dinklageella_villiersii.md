# *Dinklageella villiersii* Szlach. & Olsz.

**Genus**: [[Dinklageella]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: FCAM 3: 818 (2001)

## Description

Leaves numerous, 1.6-3 x 1.1 cm, oblong-ovate, equally bilobed. Inflorescence 4-flowered, peduncle 1 cm, rachis 3-3.5 cm. Flowers small to medium, resupinate, green. Dorsal sepal oblong-obovate, cochleariform, obtuse. Petals obliquely oblong-ovate, obtuse. Lateral sepals obliquely elliptic-ovate. Labellum 10-11 mm, clearly trilobed, basal part thick, fleshy, slightly cochleariform; median lobe truncate; lateral lobes 3.5 x 3 mm, elliptic-obovate, rounded, irregularly indented. Spur 25 mm, arched, narrowly cylindric, clearly swollen in the upper third. – Pl. 192, p. 413.

## Distribution

currently known only from Gabon

## Ecology

abundant epiphytic or epilithic vine, mangrove margin

Rostellum beak-shaped, long, narrow and fleshy, acute. Tegula single or double, linear, narrow and long. Viscidium single, elliptic, lamellate, lying on the upper surface of the rostellum apex. Rostellum remains acute after pollinarium withdrawal, with a groove on the upper surface. Pollinia 2, porate.
