# *Hyparrhenia cyanescens* (Chev.) Stapf.

**Genus**: [[Hyparrhenia]]
**Family**: [[Gramineae]]

## Synonyms

- Cymbopogon cyanescens Stapf.
- Andropogon cyanescens Chev.
- Andropogon hirtus Pobéguin.

## Description

and Journ. de Bot. 2e série, II : 209. — CHEV. Sudania, 35 (1911). — POBÉGUIN, Ess. Fl. Guin. fr., 216 (1906).

Perennial, up to 2 m tall. Culms glabrous, pruinose; sheaths prolonged by auricles approximately 2 mm long. Blades linear, glaucous, glabrous, able to exceed 50 cm long and 6 mm wide.

Inflorescence in large loose panicle up to 60 cm long. Spathéoles linear 5 cm long or more, peduncles eventually exceeding them slightly, curved and furnished near the apex with long hairs with tuberculate base. Racemes eventually reflexed, up to 2.5 cm long with up to 11 awns per pair, the lower sessile, the upper very shortly pedunculate, the two bases pubescent on the inner faces. Joints and pedicels approximately 3 mm long, densely ciliate. One pair of homogamous spikelets at the base of the lower raceme.

Fertile spikelets 4 to 6 mm long, bluish and red toward the apex. Lower glume more or less pubescent with 9 nerves, upper glume ciliate toward the apex. Awn of the fertile flower 2.5 to 3.5 cm long, column pubescent russet. Pedicellate spikelets approx. 6 mm long, reddish, lower glume with 11 nerves.

Species recorded in Guinea, Niger, Cameroon, Congo and by Koechlin in Gabon in the savannas of the Nyanga.

On fresh alluvial and clayey soils; good quality pasture.
