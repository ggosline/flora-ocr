## Discussion

Species of dense humid forest and forest galleries, widespread in Gabon, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Sudan, Uganda, Congo-Kinshasa, Congo-Brazzaville, Angola and Rhodesia.

It is very close to M. Louisii Boutique, from which it is difficult to differentiate without the fruit, which is spherical, rounded at apex, with woody pericarp, approximately 1 cm thick, with less pronounced ornamentation.
