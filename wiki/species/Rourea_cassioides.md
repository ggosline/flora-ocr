# *Rourea cassioides* Hiern

**Genus**: [[Rourea]]
**Family**: [[Connaraceae]]
**Protologue**: Catalogue of the african plants collected by Welwitsch en 1835-1861: 187 (1896); JONGKIND, in BRETELER (éd.), AUWP 89 (6): 321 (1989). TYPE : Angola, Cuanza Norte, Mt. Queta, Welwitsch 4627 (holo-BM; iso-C, COI, G, K)

## Description

JONGKIND, in BRETELER (ed.), AUWP 89 (6): 321 (1989). TYPE: Angola, Cuanza Norte, Mt. Queta, Welwitsch 4627 (holo-BM; iso-C, COI, G, K).

- Byrsocarpus cassioides (HIERN) SCHELLENB., Mit. Bot. Mus. Univ. Zürich 50: 41 (1910); Fed. Repert. 10: 243 (1912); PR 103 (4, 127): 148 (1938); TROUPIN, FCBR 3: 91 (1952); EXELL & MENDONÇA, CFA 2: 150 (1954).

Liane. Branches glabrous, never lenticellate; young branches puberulent.

Leaves. Petiole 2.5–4 cm long, rachis 7–35 cm, petiolules 1–2 mm; leaflets 13–31, glabrous to puberulent, cartaceous, lower surface papillose; apex rounded; terminal leaflet oblong-ovate to elliptic, 3–7 × 1–3 cm, lateral ones strongly asymmetric, elliptic to oblong, 1.4–7 × 0.8–2.5 cm.

Inflorescences 6 cm long, puberulent, in the axils of immature or reduced leaves on developed shoots. Pedicel 1–3.5 mm long above the articulation.

Flowers. Sepals 3–4 × 1.5–2.5, outer surface puberulent, inner surface almost glabrous. Petals 5–8 × 1.5–2 mm, free, apex sometimes distinctly folded in the bud. Stamens, the longer ones 3.5–5.5 mm long, the shorter ones 2.8–4.5 mm long. Pistil 1.5–4 mm long; style with large hairs; ovary pubescent.

Fruits: follicle 1 per flower, rarely more, 13–16 × 7–8 mm, symmetric, apex rounded, glabrous, dehiscent along a ventral suture. Sepals below the fruit 4–5 × 3–4 mm, hairs scattered on the outer surface.

Seed with radicle emerging at the apical extremity; testa entirely fleshy. – Pl. 34, p. 113.
