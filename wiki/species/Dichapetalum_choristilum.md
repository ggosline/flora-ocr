# *Dichapetalum choristilum* Engler

**Genus**: [[Dichapetalum]]
**Family**: [[Dichapetalaceae]]

## Description

(synonymy is listed under the varieties)

Large liane, lianes or shrub. Liane stem, diameter ≤11 cm; wood cylinder deeply lobed, lenticels in at least 5 rows on orthotropic shoots, sometimes well visible or not. Branches with dark brown to black bark, glabrous or glabrescent, lenticels well visible or not. Young branches covered with a dense pale brown indumentum initially, then glabrescent; lenticels generally very little visible when present.

Stipules rather persistent, narrowly triangular to subulate, 2–7 (10) mm long, pubescent like the young branches.

Leaves becoming black when dry. Petiole 1–11 (18) mm, subcylindric, often grooved above, covered with pale brown indumentum, later glabrescent. Lamina obovate-elliptic sometimes narrow, 1.5–2.5 (4) times longer than wide, (4) 8–18 (30) × (2) 3–8 (13) cm; base obtusely cuneate or often narrowed and becoming narrowly rounded, obtuse, obliquely cordate or subcordate; acumen most often present 1 (2.5) cm long; secondary veins 5–9 pairs; venation always prominent below and generally impressed above; but primary and secondary veins sometimes raised above; lamina generally bearing pale brown or floccose tomentum on both surfaces in the young state, sometimes very early caducous but most often more persistent below, as well as on the upper surface of the primary and secondary veins (the most important); glands, when present, scattered on both surfaces but more numerous below and difficult to see.

Inflorescences sessile or with a very short peduncle ≤ 0.5 cm, generally clearly branched, tomentose, pauci- to multi-flowered. Bracts and bracteoles minute, at most 1 mm, appressed, subtriangular, tomentose to tomentellose below, glabrous or nearly so below. Pedicel (0) 1–3 mm, tomentose to tomentellose, upper portion very short or absent.

Flowers. Sepals erect-spreading or reflexed, free or slightly connate at the base, ovate-oblong or triangular, $ \approx 1.5-3 \times 1 $ mm; external face tomentose to tomentellose; internal face with less dense pubescence to nearly glabrous, apex obtuse to acute. Petals white on fresh material, black when dry, erect to spreading, 2.5–4 (2) mm long, often split at mid-length or more 0.5–2.5 mm, glabrous or with a few hairs below the split on the external side and/or near the base on the internal side, lobes concave. Stamens becoming black when dry, erect or nearly so, (1) 3–4 (5) mm long, glabrous. Staminodes reduced, subquadrate to strongly bilobed, $ \approx 0.5 \times 0.5 $ mm, glabrous or with a few curly hairs. Pistil 2–4-merous, 1–4.5 mm long, dense woolly pubescence on the ovary and lower part of the style which may be deeply 2–4-lobed or even absent; lobes 1–3 mm long.

Fruits 1–3-lobed, 1–3-seeded, terminated by a beak or not, sometimes stipitate; monospermus fruits ovoid, length 1.5–5 cm, diameter 1.5–2 cm, orange at maturity; exocarp firm, 1–2 mm thick, densely velvety to tomentose; mesocarp 2–3 mm thick, pulpous; endocarp chartaceous, its inner surface bearing a dense indumentum, brown velvety. Seeds ellipsoid or nearly so, ≤ 15 × 10 mm; testa brown and fine.

Seedling, first leaves alternate.
