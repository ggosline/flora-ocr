# *Marantochloa sulphurea* (Bak.) J. Koech. comb. nov.

**Genus**: [[Marantochloa]]
**Family**: [[Marantaceae]]

## Synonyms

- Phrynium sulphureum Bak., F.T.A. 7 : 326 (1898).
- Clinogyne sulphurea (Bak.) K. Schum., Pfl. R. 4, 48 : 65 (1902).

## Description

Herb erect, 70 cm tall, with simple stem bearing 3 leaves at the apex. Petiole entirely callose, puberulent, 5 mm long. Blade asymmetric, oblong, caudate acuminate, rounded at the base, glabrous, reaching 16 × 6 cm, sheaths pubescent. Inflorescence formed of 2–3 erect racemes, with 3 internodes. Bracts slightly puberulent at the base, 1.4 cm long, subtending 2 to 3 biflorous cymes, with peduncle 1.2 cm long. Flowers yellow.

## Type
