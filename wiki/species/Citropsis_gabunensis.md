# *Citropsis gabunensis* (Engl.) Sw. et Kell.

**Family**: [[Rutaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Citropsis]]

## Description

Spiny shrub to about 6 m, with slender branches and spines 1–3 cm. Leathery leaves have 1, 3 or 5 sessile articulated leaflets and narrowly winged petiole and rachis segments; leaflets are c. 3–12 × 1–6 cm, crenulate and acuminate. White flowers occur in axillary pseudoracemes; pedicels 8–12 mm, petals 6–8 mm, stamens eight. Ovary generally 4-locular, each locule topped by a large oil gland.

## Distribution

Sierra Leone and Liberia, then Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, the former Belgian Congo and Angola.
