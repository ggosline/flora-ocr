# *Cyperus renschii* Boeckeler Planche 69

**Genus**: [[Cyperus]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Flora 65 : 11 (1882)

## Description

Robust perennial herb with thick woody rhizome 1–1.5 cm thick; stems 50–150 cm × 2–8 mm, triangular, scabrous to nearly glabrous with numerous large basal leaves. Leaf: leaf sheath purplish near base; blade 30–120 cm × 5–25 mm, plane, scabrous on margins and main nerves. Inflorescence in an anthela 10–20 × 15–30 cm, composed of 6–12 pseudo-umbels on branches 0.5–30 cm; prophylls at base of main branches, tubular, purple, the largest 1–8 cm long; each pseudo-umbel bearing one sessile spikelet and several groups of pedunculate spikelets, in the larger inflorescences, pseudo-umbels of third and fourth orders are also produced; involucral bracts numerous, foliaceous, spreading, the largest 25–80 cm × 8–40 mm. Spikelet ovate, 2–3 × 1–2 mm, brown, very densely packed, with 5–8 flowers; glumes ovate-lanceolate, 1.4–1.6 mm long, reddish-brown with a green central nerve usually terminating in a glabrous reflexed mucro. Flower with 3 stamens; style with 3 long branches. Achene ovate-elliptic, 0.6–0.9 × 0.4–0.6 mm, reddish-brown, with finely papillose surface.

## Distribution

widely distributed in forested regions of tropical Africa; apparently infrequent in Gabon, scattered in Ngounié, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem (11 collections)

## Ecology

in swamp forests, along forest tracks and watercourses; in Gabon, at 0–500 m altitude, up to 2100 m in East Africa
