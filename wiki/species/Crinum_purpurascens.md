# *Crinum purpurascens* Herbert

**Family**: [[Amaryllidaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Crinum]]

Amaryllid.: 250 (1837); BAK., Curt. Bot. Mag. 106: tab. 6525 (1880), FTA 7: 396 (1898); DE WILD., Ann. Mus. Congo., Bot., ser. 5, 2: 21 (1907); A. CHEV., Rev. Bot. Appliq. 30: 616 (1950); ANDREWS, Fl. Pl. Sudan 3: 287 (1956); MORTON, W. Afr. Lil. Orch.: 25 (1961); BERHAUT, Fl. Seneg.: 310 (1967); HEPPER, FWTA, ed. 2, 3: 134 (1968); AKÉ ASSI, Bull. IFAN, ser. A, 34: 526 (1972); GEERINCK, Fl. Afr. Centr.: 11 (1973).

## Synonymy

- *C. purpurascens* var. *angustilobium* DE WILD., Ann. Mus. Congo. Bot., ser. 5, 1: 18 (1903)
- *C. koutiense* A. CHEV., Etud. Fl. Afr. Centr.: 306 (1913), nom. nud.

## Description

**Bulb**: subglobose, 3–5 cm in diameter, often with a distinct neck and frequently stoloniferous from the rhizomatous portion of the bulb.

**Leaves**: present with the flowers, in a basal rosette, dark green, paler at the base, fairly fine in texture, often clasping, nearly erect, linear to ribbon-like, 20–70 (–100) cm long, 1–4 cm wide, acute, with a prominent central nerve, margins glabrous, rarely denticulate, undulate.

**Inflorescence**: flowering scape tinged with red, slender, 20–50 cm long. Bracts membranous, falling very early, subtending 2–10 subsessile flowers with a faint sweet scent. Flowers dark purple, inclined before development, colour fading later.

**Flowers**: perianth tube tinged with purple, erect at anthesis, 12–20 cm long; inner segments white, outer segments tinged with reddish, spreading or reflexed, linear, 5–9 cm long, 0.3–0.7 cm wide. Filaments purple towards the apex, curved, arranged radially, slightly shorter than the segments. Anthers dark purple to violet-black, 1.2–1.5 cm long. Style purple, exceeding the anthers.

**Fruit**: red tinged with green, distinctly veined, subglobose, 1–3 cm in diameter, with a slender beak 8–15 cm long. Seeds green, irregular, angular, up to 10 per fruit.

## Distribution

**Range**: Guineo-Congolian forests, distributed from Gambia to Sudan in the east and Angola in the south.

**Gabon**: Ogooué-Ivindo province (Zoolendé, Makokou area, Bouqué on the banks of the Ogooué).

## Habitat and ecology

Found frequently in swampy zones and at or even in rivers. Tolerates disturbed or secondary vegetation and can occur in plantations such as cocoa or banana. Distributed mainly in littoral forest and Biafran forest.

## Notes

The type specimen from HERBERT's herbarium, Fernando Pô, is noted as not seen and possibly lost. The distribution recorded in the source extends well beyond the Lower Guinea region (Gambia to Sudan and Angola), reflecting the pan-African range of this species; the Gabonese material represents the western equatorial African portion of this range.
