# *Cyphostemma ukerewense* (Gilg) Descoings var. gabonicum Descoings

**Genus**: [[Cyphostemma]]
**Family**: [[Vitaceae]]
**Protologue**: Descoings, Bull. Soc. Bot. Fr. 114 : (1967)

## Description

Herbaceous climbing vine reaching several meters in length. Cylindrical stems with short pubescence more or less dense, sometimes almost absent, and numerous fairly long glandular hairs; internodes 6–8 cm long. Cylindrical tendrils, bifurcate, glabrous or weakly pubescent and sometimes bearing glandular hairs; oblong obtuse bracts, glabrous. Deltoid oval stipules, more or less broadly cordate at the base, 5–6 mm long and wide, glabrous, with margins somewhat laciniate bearing a few glandular hairs. Leaves compound, digitate, 5-foliolate. Petiole cylindrical, deeply channeled, 3–7 cm long, densely pubescent and bearing glandular hairs longer than the pubescence; petiolules 2–6 mm long, densely pubescent and glandular. Limb of leaflets obovate or obovate-oblong, 5–7 cm long, 1.5–2.5 cm wide, cuneate at base, more or less elongately attenuate and decurrent on the petiolule, apex attenuate into a wedge terminated in a short acute acumen; margins with thick acute teeth, decurrent at the base; on the upper surface some large glandular hairs on the limb, the main veins bearing long loose pubescence and large glandular hairs; on the lower surface limb glabrous, all veins bearing loose pubescence and a few glandular hairs; venation pinnate; texture thin.

Inflorescences 10–15 cm long, in lax compound cymes, corymbiform; peduncle cylindrical, 3–6 cm long, more or less loosely pubescent and glandular; axes with short dense pubescence and rare or absent glandular hairs; bracts oblong to oblong-deltoid, 3–4 mm long, glabrous.
