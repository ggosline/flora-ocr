# *Palisota plicata* E.Bidault & Burg Planche 21, Figure 5(C-E)

**Genus**: [[Palisota]]
**Family**: [[Commelinaceae]]
**Protologue**: Candollea 74(2) : 193 (2019)

## Description

Candollea 74(2) : 193 (2019). Creeping and trailing herb; stem up to ± 30 cm in length, erect for ± 10–15 cm, glabrescent or with short brown woolly pubescence, with internodes 0.5–3.5 cm, bearing roots furnished with numerous short rootlets, covered with yellow hairs. Leaves spiral along the aerial part of the stem and pseudowhorled at the apex; sheath 7–15 mm in length, forming a whitish-green or dark mauve swelling at the base, sometimes longitudinally striate; pseudopetiole 0–5 cm; blade obovate to obovate-elliptic, 16–31 × (3.5–)5.5–10 cm, long-decurrent at the base, plicate, with margins bearing fine appressed russet pubescence, with secondary venation prominent on both surfaces, upper surface with appressed white woolly pubescence when young, glabrescent, lower surface glaucous-green to glaucous-silvery, with persistent appressed white woolly pubescence. Inflorescence strictly axillary, on the aerial part of the stem, erect, 3–5 cm in length, simple; peduncle 1–1.3 cm, with (2–)3 persistent, sheathing bracts, with margins densely covered with russet pubescence; floriferous bracts narrowly triangular, up to 1 cm in length, rapidly deciduous; cincinni with ± 6 flowers. Flower: pedicel 12–15 mm at anthesis, erect at anthesis, spiralled after; perianth distinctly reflexed, then closed; sepals equal, oblong, 5–6 × 1.5–2 mm, petaloid, white, with brown-ochre pubescence at the apex of the outer surface; petals similar to the sepals, but 4.5 × 1.5–2 mm, glabrous; stamens unequal, erect, the 2 upper ones with filament ± 3 mm, glabrous, with oblong anther, yellow, the lower one more firm, with anther with curved, reniform thecae; staminodes white; ovary ovoid to ellipsoid, 1.5 × 0.8 mm, slightly pubescent with stiff hairs; style 2.5 mm, stigma disciform, white. Fruit irregularly ovoid, 11–15 × 6–7 mm when dry, ± acuminate at the apex, bright red, with sparse pubescence. Seed globular or polygonal, ± 4 mm in diameter; testa dull, light grey, smooth with 20 to 30 rounded ribs all around the seed, the latter entirely surrounded by a brownish epidermis; caruncle distinctly concave, whitish; hilum constricted, in a slight depression, verrucose.

## Distribution

Equatorial Guinea and Gabon; in Gabon, only in the Cristal Mountains, Estuaire Province, and one specimen collected in Ngounié. Perhaps also in southern Cameroon (Bidault & van der Burg 2019)

## Ecology

Old humid forest on slopes or along river banks; from 430 to 550 m in altitude. Flowering in November; fruiting in March and November

## Source note

This species appears close to P. leewhitei and P. cristalensis by the combination of a creeping-trailing habit, a short, axillary, erect inflorescence, and roots with numerous yellow rootlets. Nevertheless, it differs from them among other things, by its longitudinally plicate leaves. Bi : Bidault & van der Burg (2019). Bibliography
