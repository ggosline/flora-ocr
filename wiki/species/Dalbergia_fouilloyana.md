# *Dalbergia fouilloyana* Pellegr.

**Genus**: [[Dalbergia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Bot. France 93(1) : 10 (1946)

## Description

Bull. Soc. Bot. France 93(1) : 10 (1946). Liane or shrub approximately 1 m tall, inodorous, without spines, but sometimes bearing hook-like prehensile branches; bark dark brown, longitudinally striated; young branches with spreading dark brown pubescence, formed of long hairs (± 1 mm) mixed with shorter hairs. Stipules early caducous, falciform, 7 × 2–3 mm, acute at apex, densely velutinous. Leaf imparipinnate; petiole 1.1–3 cm; rachis 5.3–10.5 cm, with dark brown pubescence similar to that of the branches; leaflets 7 to 18, alternate and unequal, with petiolules of 1–2 mm, blade broadly ovate, symmetrical, (1.7–)2.4–8.8 × (1–)1.6–4.5 cm in the upper leaflets and 0.7–3.3 × 0.6–2.3 cm in the lower ones, rounded or slightly cordate at base, rounded or obtuse at apex, papyraceous to slightly coriaceous, green or brownish when dry and sometimes glaucescent on the lower face, velutinous on both faces, with long erect hairs of ± 1 mm; lateral veins 4 to 6 pairs, very faintly marked and scarcely distinct from the veinlets, the latter in a concolorous rather dense network, but faintly marked. Inflorescence in panicle generally rather lax, terminal or axillary, 2.5–25 cm long, with brown pubescence similar to that of the branches; bracts caducous (generally after flowering), elliptic, 2–4 × 1–2 mm, velutinous. Flower: pedicel 1–3 mm; bracteoles caducous (generally after flowering), elliptic, 1–1.2 × 0.3–0.5 mm, velutinous; calyx dark purple, nearly glabrous to densely covered with brown hairs, tube 1.5–2 × 1.5–2 mm, becoming ± ribbed when dry, teeth imbricate, 0.5–1 mm long, ± equal and rounded or the lower one somewhat longer and acute, the upper ones connate; corolla violet with a whitish-greenish patch at the base of the standard, petals with claw shorter than the calyx, standard bent at almost a right angle and ± folded lengthwise, with short thick claw of 1 mm and ovate or suborbicular blade of 4.5–5 × 4–5 mm, with slightly auriculate base and bilobed apex, wings longer than the keel and almost entirely concealing it, with claw of 1.2–1.3 mm and very asymmetric blade of 4.8–5.2 × 3 mm, transversely wrinkled toward the base on the outer face and rounded at the upper margin, keel strongly curved, with claw of 1–1.5 mm and blade of 2.8–3.5 × 2 mm, with strongly auriculate base; stamens in 2 phalanges of 5, filaments ± 4 mm, connate to the middle or on the ⅔ and curved in C from the base; ovary 2 × 0.7 mm, pubescent at least on the margins, with 2 ovules and stipe of 1–2 mm; style linear, 1–2 mm, with punctiform stigma. Pod with pedicel of 1–2 mm and stipe of 7–13 mm, papyraceous, flattened and ellipsoid, 7–11 × 2–3.5 cm, obtuse at both ends, with ± prominent reticulation, especially when young, glabrous and glaucous, with 1(–2) seeds.

## Distribution

Equatorial Guinea (Rio Muni), Gabon and Republic of the Congo; uncommon, but fairly widely distributed in Gabon, present in Estuaire, Ngounié, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo and Ogooué-Maritime.

## Ecology

Secondary forests and forest edges, sometimes in swampy terrain; in Gabon at 0–900 m altitude. Flowering from May to February, fruiting from August to January.

## Source note

D. fouilloyana is very close to D. saxatilis, of which it could be merely a variety; it differs from it by the violet flowers, and by the dark brown pubescence of the branches, leaf rachises and inflorescences. Like its close relative, it is quite variable in the number and dimensions of the leaflets. A specimen from the Wonga-Wongué reserve (J.J.F.E. de Wilde et al. 861) is very close to D. fouilloyana, but has nearly glabrous branches and leaves with very sparse hairs. Bibliography: Pellegrin (1949).
