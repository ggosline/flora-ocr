# *Salacia oliveriana* Loesener

**Genus**: [[Salacia]]
**Family**: [[Celastraceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot Jahrb. 19 : 240 (1895) ; l.c. 44 : 167 (1910) ; Pflanzenf. 20B : 223, tab. 70, fig. H et J (1942) ; HALLÉ, Monogr. Hippocr. Afr. occid., Thèse : 215 (1958) ; Mém. IFAN 64 : 218 (1962)

## Description

l.c. 44 : 167 (1910) ; Pflanzenf. 20B : 223, tab. 70, fig. H et J (1942) ; HALLÉ, Monogr. Hippocr. Afr. occid., Thèse : 215 (1958) ; Mém. IFAN 64 : 218 (1962).

– S. oblongifolia OLIV., FTA : 1 : 374 (1868), non BLUME (1825), Java.

– S. johannis-albrechtii LOES. & WINKLER, Bot Jahrb. 41: 281 (1908); HUTCH. & MOSS, FWTA 1: 453 (1928).

– S. lucida auct. non OLIV.: A. CHEV., Expl. Bot. 1: 134 (1920).

Climbing shrub or very tall liane, glabrous, containing rubber. Wood exceeding 3 cm in diameter, ornamented in section with parenchymatous grooves that are at once radiate and concentric, thus forming woody islets. Internodes often slender, smooth, ± marked with very fine ridges when dry. Leaves opposite or subopposite, rather small. Petiole 3–8 mm, with canal margins straight or nearly so. Lamina thin, elliptic or oblong, 3–8(10) × 1–3(4) cm, acute at base, acuminate or rostrate at apex, margin dentate often somewhat indistinctly, concolorous or slightly discolorous. Secondary veins 6–8(9) pairs, slightly oblique.

Inflorescences axillary, 1–20-flowered, with peduncle 3–10 mm, sometimes briefly divided at apex. Pedicels 7–13 mm. Bud oblong, 4–6 × 2–2.8 mm. Flower almost white or green, 6–10 mm in diameter. Calyx 2 mm in diameter with 5 subequal sepals, obtusely deltoid, approximately 1 mm. Petals 5, 4–5.5 × 1.5–3 mm (when flat), somewhat reflexed. Column 4–5.5 mm long. Papilliferous disc 1–1.3 mm high for 1.2–1.5 mm in diameter at base; a glabrous crown without papillae, 0.2 mm thick, separates the disc from the androecium. Stamens 3, 3–3.5 mm, filaments spaced at base; anther elliptic, 1.2–1.4 × 0.9 mm, with very small terminal apicule, dehiscence slits parallel toward the base. Pistil subulate, 2.5–3.6 mm, with microlobulate stigmas alternating with stamens; 3 locules situated higher than the disc, elongate, with (4)5–7 ovules ± superposed in each.

Fruit the shape and size of a very large lemon, in Gabon according to GRIFFON DU BELLAY; smooth, oblong, 6–10 × 3.5 cm, red-orange and spotted in Ivory Coast as in Zaire (Breyne 3196, BR). Abundant sweet mucilage. – Pl. 46, fig. 10–18, p. 161.

## Discussion

The variety adiopodoumella Hallé (Bull. Mus. natn. Hist. nat., Paris 30 (5) : 470, 1958, type IA 1517, P !) groups collections from West Africa with pauciflorous and almost sessile inflorescences, the peduncle often reduced to 1 mm.

Ivory Coast, Cameroon, Gabon, Zaire (Gutzwiller 1589, Lukweli 12.8.1957, fr., BR ; Coûteaux 367, Eala, 14.10.1937, fr., BR).

From forest margins or the canopy, this species favors sublittoral forests, being very rare beyond.

Salacia oliveriana is related to S. lucida Oliv., but it is well distinguished by its less unequal and smoother sepals, by its less divided inflorescences, by its non-sagittate anthers, and by the elevation of its disc, which is a small androgynophore.
