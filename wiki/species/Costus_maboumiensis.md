# *Costus maboumiensis* Pellegr.
**Family in Vol. 9**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Current placement**: Costaceae · **Genus**: [[Costus]]
## Description
Herb at least 1.5 m tall with separate spiral leafy and flowering shoots. Glabrous blades reach c. 20 × 9 cm; ligule 12–15 mm and slightly hairy. The flowering stalk reaches 35 cm and bears a 4–5 cm spike. Each bract subtends one dark red-brown flower with a 4 cm yellow labellum.
## Distribution
Known only from Gabon. Type: Le Testu 1820, Maboumi, Mayombe-Bavili; also recorded at Montéti and between Kembélé and Ayumba in Haute-Ngounyé.
