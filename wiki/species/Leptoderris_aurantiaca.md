# *Leptoderris aurantiaca* Dunn

**Genus**: [[Leptoderris]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1914 : 245 (1914)

## Description

Liane to lianas-cent shrub, 2.5 to 10(–12) m in height; red latex; branchlets often perforated, with appressed or erect hairs, glabrescent or not, with the same pilose covering on the petioles and rachis. Stipules narrowly elliptic-ovate, reaching 2 × 1 cm, finely longitudinally striated, with short appressed hairs on the outside, glabrous on the inside. Leaf: petiole 4.5–11 cm; rachis 9–13 cm; stipels as long or longer than the petiolules of 3–6 mm; leaflets 7 to 9, with obovate blade, 7–15 × 3.5–7 cm, rounded at the base, acuminate for 5–15 mm at the apex, glabrous above, with ± appressed hairs below on the midvein and the (6–)7 to 10(–11) pairs of lateral nerves. Inflorescence in axillary and terminal panicles, reaching 50 cm in length, ± velvety; bracts and bracteoles narrowly triangular to oblong, 2–4 mm in length. Flower: pedicel ≤ 1 mm; calyx infundibuliform, 3–4 mm in length, velvety, lobes ± 1 mm in length; corolla 6–8 mm in length, pinkish, glabrous, standard ovate, narrow at the base, wings oblong, as long as the keel, long-clawed, keel with hooked auricles, long-clawed; stamens with anthers of 0.25–0.3 mm in length; ovary with scattered hairs; style curved. Pod elliptic-oblong to narrowly obovate, 7–10 × 2.5–4 cm, 1-seeded, with scattered, appressed hairs, glabrescent, with wing 3–4 mm wide.

## Distribution

South-East Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea and Gabon; in Gabon, known only from the environs of Libreville, in the Estuary

## Ecology

primary and secondarized forest; at 0–150 m altitude. b: Hepper (1958), Pellegrin (1949). Bibliography
