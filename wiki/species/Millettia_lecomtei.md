# *Millettia lecomtei* Dunn

**Genus**: [[Millettia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: J. Bot. 49 : 220 (1911)

## Description

Liane or shrub; branchlets yellow, tomentose, with whitish lenticels. Stipules caducous. Leaf: petiole 2–5.5 cm, channelled above, tomentose; rachis 5–13 cm; stipels absent; leaflets 7 to 9, with obovate to elliptic blade, 5–16.5 × 2–8.3 cm, base cuneate, apex acuminate-caudate with acumen 2–15 mm, glabrous above, dense, silvery, appressed pubescence below; lateral veins in 7 to 15 pairs, alternating with finer pairs, tertiary veins very weakly visible. Inflorescence terminal or rarely axillary, in large panicle, reaching 40 cm in length, with yellowish-brown tomentum; bracts ovate, 1 × 0.75 mm. Flower: pedicel 1.5–2.5 mm; bracteoles at the base of the calyx, broadly ovate to suborbicular, 1–1.5 × 1–2 mm, tomentose, margins ciliate; calyx brown velvety, tube 3–5 mm in length, upper lobes ± 1 mm in length, lower ones 3–4 mm in length; corolla white to yellowish, standard with claw 1–2 mm, blade suborbicular, 7–11 × 6–9 mm, base auricled and callous, apex emarginate, wings with claw 1–2 mm, blade oblong, 7–11 × 2–3.5 mm, appendage uncinate, keel with ovate blade, curved, 7–12 × 3–4.5 mm; stamens with staminal tube 6–9 mm in length, free part 2–4 mm; ovary lanceolate, 3–4.5 mm in length, with 4 to 7 ovules, silky; style curved, 5–10 mm, base silky, stigma capitate. Pod narrowly oblong, ± 8 × 2–2.2 cm, base rounded, apex pointed, with 2 seeds, with brown, appressed, very short pubescence. Seed flattened, orbicular, ± 1 cm in diameter, black, provided with small pores arranged in a flabellate pattern arising from the small hilum of 1 mm.

## Distribution

Gabon, Republic of Congo, Democratic Republic of Congo and Angola; in Gabon, known from Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo and Ogooué-Maritime

endemic to Gabon, known only from Nyanga

## Ecology

edge of lagoon, beach, disturbed forest; at 200–500 m altitude. Flowering from February to June. b : Hauman (1954a), Pellegrin (1949), Raponda-Walker & Sillans (1961). Liane; bark dark brown, lenticellate; branchlets pubescent to velvety. Stipules ± persistent, ovate, ± 3 × 2 mm, silky on the outside, with 9 to 11 veins. Leaf: petiole 2–6 cm, tomentose and channelled above; rachis 2–5.5 cm; stipels subulate, 3–4 mm; leaflets 7 to 9, petiolule 1.5–2 mm, blade elliptic-obovate, 5–9 × 2.5–4 cm, base rounded to cuneate, acumen short, glabrous and reticulate above, drying very pale, golden-silvery below, golden hairs initially dominant; lateral veins in 8 to 12 pairs. Inflorescence terminal and axillary, in dense pseudopanicle, 5–15 cm in length; bracts caducous, ovate, ± 2 × 1 mm, acute. Pod narrowly oblong, 10–14 × ± 2.5 cm, base attenuate, with 2 to 4 seeds, with short pubescence. Seed not seen.

gallery forest. Flowering in September

## Source note

Pellegrin (1949) mentions the presence of this species in Angola (Cabinda), but we cannot confirm it. The difference with M. chrysophylla being slight, the two may represent the same species. b : Pellegrin (1949). Bibliography
