– D. jansii BAMPS, BJBB 34 : 497 (1964) ; FCBR, Spermat., Flacourt. 1 : 6 (1968). TYPE : Jans s. n., Zaïre (holo-, BR).

Tree (10–12 m tall) or shrub; branchlets greyish to brownish, glabrous.

Leaves with petiole 5–15 mm long, glabrous; blade elliptic-oblong or obovate-oblong, cuneate to rounded at base, shortly acuminate at apex, 10–20 × 4–9 cm, subentire to slightly denticulate, subcoriaceous, glabrous; secondary veins 5–8 pairs.

Inflorescences in spiciform racemes 4–6–(8) cm long. Flowers ♂ or ♀ and ♀ (plants polygamous); pedicel short (1–1.5 mm long), glabrous, articulate on the rachis; sepals 4–5, elliptic-oblong, 6–9 × 3–5 mm, glabrous; petals 5–8, elliptic to elliptic-oblong, 5–7 × 2–2.5 mm, provided with a ± pubescent scale at the base of their inner face; stamens 20–30, with filaments 2–5 mm long, glabrous; anthers 2–3 mm long; ovary glabrous, with 3 pluriovulate placentas; style glabrous, with 3-lobed stigma or 3–4 very short branches.

Fruits: subglobose or subovoid capsules, sometimes ribbed, 2.5–4 × 2–2.5 mm, glabrous; seeds 4–5, angular. - Pl. 8, p. 31.
