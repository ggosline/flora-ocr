# *Octoknema dinklagei* Engler

**Family**: [[Octoknemaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Octoknema]]

Bot. Jahrb. **43**: 177 (1909). — Mildbraed, Pflanzenfam. ed. 2, **16b**: 45 (1935).

## Description

**Habit**: small tree with young branchlets pubescent and yellow. Old branchlets glabrous. Bark yellow. Internodes 4.5–6.5 cm long.

**Leaves**: petiole glabrous, 2.5–3 cm long, flat above, rounded beneath, apex weakly thickened. Blade glabrous on both faces, greenish to yellowish, glossy above, greyish-green or greyish beneath, elliptic or lanceolate, 15–20 × 6–8 cm; base cuneiform; apex abruptly and shortly acuminate, obtuse. Midrib weakly concave or prominent on the upper face, prominent and longitudinally wrinkled on the lower face. Secondary nerves ascending, arcuate, anastomosing 1–3 mm from the margin, prominent on both faces. Nervules in a fairly dense network, prominent on both faces.

**Inflorescence and male flowers**: unknown. Female inflorescence an axillary raceme with pubescent rachis bearing stellate hairs. Female flowers unknown.

**Fruit**: drupe glabrous, ellipsoid, c. 2 × 1.5 cm. Exocarp verrulose. Endocarp bearing 8 meridian lamellae. Seed ovoid with 8 longitudinal ridges.

## Types

**HOLOTYPE**: *Dinklage 1298*, Batanga, Cameroon (B!).

## Distribution

Known only from Cameroon.

## Notes

**Range corrected (2011).** Gosline & Malécot (2011) find this species **endemic to the coastal forest of Cameroon** between Douala and Campo. It **does not occur in Gabon**, contrary to its inclusion in the Flore du Gabon. Gabonese material named *O. dinklagei* should be re-determined against the revision — see [[Octoknema]].
