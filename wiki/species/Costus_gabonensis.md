# *Costus gabonensis* J. Koechlin
**Family in Vol. 9**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Current placement**: Costaceae · **Genus**: [[Costus]]
## Description
Herb 1–2 m tall with nearly sessile ovate-lanceolate leaves 10–20 × 5–10 cm, sparsely hairy on both faces with yellow-brown tubercle-based hairs. Ligule 10–20 mm and divided into two unequal teeth. The cylindrical terminal spike has hairy purple-tinged bracts with leafy tips; calyx does not split laterally and the labellum is yellow, 6 cm long.
## Distribution
Known only from Gabon. Type: Le Testu 2240, Tsamba, Ngounyé; also Le Testu 2069, Mounou-Yalala in the Nyanga region.
