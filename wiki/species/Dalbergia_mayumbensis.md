# *Dalbergia mayumbensis* Baker f. Planche 44

**Genus**: [[Dalbergia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Journ. Bot. 66, Suppl. 1 : 124 (1928)

## Description

, syn. nov. D. malangensis E.P.Sousa, Mem. Junta Invest. Ultramar, 2e Sér. 38 : 55 (1962), syn. nov. D. bignonae Berhaut, Fl. Sénégal, éd. 2 : 427 (1967), syn. nov. Very large liana, reaching 40 m in length and 35 cm in diameter at the base, with drooping stems forming dense tangles, inodorous, without spines, but sometimes with hook-like prehensile branchlets; bark dark brown longitudinally striated, without conspicuous lenticels, with red exudate on the cut surface; young branchlets glabrous or with dense blond hairs ± spreading and rather long. Stipules unknown, very caducous. Leaf imparipinnate; petiole 1.5–4.5 cm; rachis (4–)6.2–19 cm, with blond hairs appressed or spreading, sometimes very sparse; leaflets (12–)15 to 30, alternate and slightly unequal, with petiolule of 2–5 mm, limb generally rather narrow elliptic, symmetric, 2.4–6.3 × 0.7–2 cm in the upper leaflets, 2.3–6.8 × 0.7–2.8 cm in the median ones and 1–4.3 × 0.6–1.6 cm in the lower ones, rounded or obtuse at the base, rounded or shortly emarginate at the apex, ± coriaceous, olive-brown to greyish when dry and often glaucous on the lower surface, pubescent on both surfaces or sometimes glabrous on the upper surface, with hairs sometimes short and appressed, sometimes long and erect; lateral veins 7 to 15, little visible and scarcely more prominent than the veinlets, the latter in dense ± apparent network in young leaves, becoming lax and effaced in older leaves. Inflorescence in lax panicle, terminal and sometimes axillary, 5.5–16 cm in length, with rather dense fauve pubescence spreading or appressed; bracts early caducous, elliptic to lanceolate, 1.5–5 × 0.5–1.5 mm, pubescent at least on the margins. Seed reniform, flattened, ± 14 × 5 mm.

## Distribution

Senegal, Guinea-Bissau, Guinea, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Mali, Côte d'Ivoire, Togo, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Equatorial Guinea (Rio Muni), Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Angola and Zambia; rare in Gabon, recorded from Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué, Nyanga and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

secondary forests, riverbanks, forest galleries, open forests, margins of inselbergs; in Gabon up to 665 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1200 m. Flowering from October to December in Gabon

## Source note

D. mayumbensis is highly variable in the size and pubescence of leaflets; these have hairs sometimes appressed and very short, sometimes long and erect. In general, plants from savanna regions have larger and more hairy leaflets than those from humid forest regions, but many intermediates exist and pubescence sometimes varies within the same locality. It is therefore not possible to maintain D. noldeae and D. bignonae as separate taxa. Consequently, the species has a much more extensive range than was previously supposed, extending over the entire Guineo-Congolian region and its periphery. D. mayumbensis is very close to D. ealaensis (see this species for the differences) and to D. saxatilis, with which it is often confused. It differs from the latter by its somewhat larger flowers, with more developed and generally persistent bracteoles. The two species have identical fruits and are sometimes difficult to separate in the absence of flowers, although the leaflets of D. mayumbensis are generally more coriaceous and narrower. Some of the material cited by Cronquist (1954) under D. saxatilis belongs to D. mayumbensis. b: Adam (1971), Berhaut (1976). Pellegrin (1949), Sousa (1966). Bibliography
