# *Raphanus sativus* Linné

**Genus**: [[Raphanus]]
**Family**: [[Brassicaceae]]
**Protologue**: Trop. Crops, Dicot. 1: 96 (1968); AGNEW, Upl. Kenya Wild Fl.: 93 (1974); JONSELL, FCAM 21: 10 (1980) et FTEA, Cruciferae: 13 (1982)

## Description

Biennial herbs with thick turbinate or cylindrical root. Stem hispid at base, erect, 15–150 cm tall. Lower leaves lyrate-pinnatifid up to 15 cm long, not auriculate; lamina ± ovate longer than petiole, acute, dentate, cuneate base. Upper leaves undivided, scarcely distinctly petiolate, ± lanceolate, acute, crenate or dentate, cuneate base. Racemes ± corymbiform, loosely to densely flowered. Fruit inflorescences lax. Fruiting pedicels up to 30 mm long. Sepals lanceolate, obtuse, 5–10 mm long. Petals ± violet, white or yellow with ± dark veins, 15–20 mm long. Silique thick with spongy to corky texture, 20–90 × 7–15 mm, scarcely or not constricted between seeds which number 1 to 12. - Pl. 00, p. 00.

## Type

Herb. Linné 846.1, LINN!

## Discussion

This plant, known only in cultivation, is probably derived from hybrids of various forms of R. raphanistrum L. In tropical regions, it sometimes spreads as a weed; this is why it is described here, although we have not seen Gabonese specimens.
