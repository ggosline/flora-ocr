# *Dracaena tholloniana* Hua Planche 19

**Genus**: [[Dracaena]]
**Family**: [[Dracaenaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Hist. Nat. Autun 10 : 662 (1897)

## Synonyms

- Pleomele tholloniana (Hua) N.E.Br., Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1914 : 279 (1914).

## Description

Shrub monocaulous, up to 1 m tall; trunk generally simple; rhizome woody. Leaves equitant; sheath embracing the stem over clearly more than the circumference, over a length of > 1 cm; pseudopetiole up to 3 times the length of the blade, furrowed above, fibrous, green to violet-green, gradually passing into the sheath; blade oblong to elliptic, up to 40 × 9 cm, base rounded to cuneate, apex gradually narrowed into a mucro of 1 cm, papyraceous-coriaceous, smooth, distinctly longitudinally undulate, upper surface dark green uniform (rarely with cream longitudinal bands), lower surface paler. Inflorescence

erect, in a spiciform thyrse, up to 35 cm in length, with flowers condensed toward the apex, with smooth axes, dark green to violet-green; bracts quickly caducous, narrowly ovate, up to 3 × 0.4 cm, becoming smaller toward the apex; fascicles with 3 to 6 flowers, each subtended by bracts quickly caducous, narrowly triangular, ± 0.5–1 mm in length, scarious. Flower: pedicel 0–1 mm; perianth 8–13 mm in length, white to greenish-white, striped longitudinally with violet on the outside, with lobes longer than the tube, reflexed; stamens inserted near the throat, up to 1 mm shorter than the lobes, filament filiform, white, anther ± 0.3 mm in length, pale yellow; ovary oblong, up to 3 × 2 mm; style exsert for up to 1 mm, white, stigma ± 0.5 mm in diameter. Fruit entire to deeply 2- or 3-lobed, ± 13 × 9 mm, smooth and glossy, bright orange to red, with ellipsoid, divergent lobes. Seed ellipsoid, ± 7 × 4 mm.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola; in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué, Ngounié, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

understory, often on steep slopes; in Gabon as elsewhere at 0–1000 m altitude. Flowering recorded from December to March and in July
