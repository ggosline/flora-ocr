# *Palisota cristalensis* E.Bidault & Burg Planche 16, Figure 4(A, B)

**Genus**: [[Palisota]]
**Family**: [[Commelinaceae]]
**Protologue**: Candollea 74(2) : 183 (2019)

## Description

Candollea 74(2): 183 (2019). Creeping and decumbent herb; stem erect for ± 10–20 cm in height, covered on all its aerial parts with long reddish pubescence, spreading, with simple hairs, 3–9 mm, except on the lower face of the lamina outside the main vein, internodes 0.5–5 cm; axillary roots at first glabrous, then bearing numerous rootlets covered with yellow hairs. Leaves spiral along the aerial part of the stem; sheath 7–15 mm in length, with brown margin, forming a whitish-greenish swelling at the base; pseudopetiole 2.5–5 cm, light green; lamina obovate-elliptic to narrowly elliptic, 11–25 × 3–7.5 cm, acute to cuneate at the base, apiculate at the apex. Inflorescence strictly axillary, on the aerial part of the stems, erect, enveloped by the sheath, then by the pseudopetiole and following its axis, sometimes to the base of the lamina, 4.5–5 cm in length at anthesis, simple; peduncle erect, 1 cm, with 2 sheathing bracts; floral bracts 3 to 6, broadly to narrowly ovate, 7–15 × 2–6 mm, with acuminate apex, each supporting a sessile cincinnus with ± 4 male and bisexual flowers. Flower distinctly spreading at flowering; pedicel 12–16 mm, spiraled in fruit; sepals equal, oblong, 6 × 2 mm, petaloid, white with a pale green spot at the apex, furnished with long hairs on the outer face; petals similar to the sepals, but glabrous and white; stamens with glabrous filament and yellow anther, unequal, the two upper ones with filament 1.6–1.8 mm, anther subbasifix, oblongoid, 1.5 × 0.6–0.7 mm, the lower one more firm, with filament 2.5 mm, anther dorsifix, 2 × 2 mm, with curved, reniform thecae; staminodes white to pale yellow; ovary ovoid to ellipsoidal, 0.5 mm in diameter; style 0.8–1.7 mm, stigma truncate. Fruit irregularly ovate, slightly oblique, weakly triangular in cross-section, 19 × 12 mm, with a few scattered reddish hairs, bright red. Seed triangular-ovoid, 3.5 × 3 × 3 mm, slightly and finely ribbed with up to ± 40 irregular ribs; testa dark greyish-brown; small embryotege round, slightly concave; hilum minute, flat.

## Distribution

Equatorial Guinea (Rio Muni, rare, Ndote Reserve) and Gabon; in Gabon known only from the Monts de Cristal and from a specimen from the edges of the Remboué River, in the provinces of Estuaire and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

mature humid forest on slope, river edges and secondary forests; at 40–555 m in altitude. Flowering from October to January; fruiting in November and May. The plants form small gregarious populations of up to about ten individuals

## Source note

Despite its relative abundance in the Monts de Cristal, the species was long confused with P. satabiei (see Plate 15), although it is distinguished from it by its erect inflorescences, and by its long spreading reddish pubescence abundant on the upper face of the leaves, the stems and the inflorescences. It is in fact closer to P. leewhitei by its habit, its short inflorescence with sessile bractiferous cincinni, but is distinguished from it, apart from by its characteristic pubescence, by its more robust habit.
