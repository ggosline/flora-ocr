# *Tricalysia gossweileri* S. Moore

**Genus**: [[Tricalysia]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Spencer Moore, Journ. Linn. Soc. Bot. 37 : 305 (1906)

## Description

Slender shrub 1–4 (10) m tall. Young internodes micropuberulent to glabrous. Stipules tubular for 1 mm, tapering for 3–6 mm. Petiole (2) 3–9 mm, micropuberulent to glabrous. Blade glabrous, reddish-olivaceous, elliptic, 7–16 × 2.2–6 cm, acute at base, ± acuminate at apex. Midrib puberulent to glabrous above. 5–8 pairs of secondary veins. Nerve axils glabrous or very slightly pubescent.

Inflorescences inserted 1–2 mm above the axils, 3–12-flowered, 1 cm or less long, with subnull peduncle, axes and bracteolar cupules puberulent. Pedicels subnull, accrescent at fruiting. Calyx pale green, puberulent, tubular for 0.5–1 mm, with (4) 5 (6) minute teeth barely 0.5 mm. Bud fusiform, acute, glabrous. Corolla white with tube 2.5–3.5 × 1.2 mm, 5 lobes 3–4 × 1–1.5 mm, overlapping toward the left, throat distinctly pubescent. Filament 1.5 mm; anther 3 × 0.5 mm, inserted toward the lower third, acuminate for 0.6 mm above the locules. Style 5–6 mm, bilobed for 1–2 mm, not quite glabrous. Small annular disc. Ovary two-locular. Each placenta with 1 (2) ovule sunken at its centre.

Fruits pinkish, ± fusiform before maturity, reaching 8–10 mm in length, with persistent calyx, pedicel slender 2–3 mm above the final bracteoles, thin-walled. 1–2 seeds.
