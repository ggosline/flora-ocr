# *Pouzolzia guineensis* Benth. Planche 28

**Genus**: [[Pouzolzia]]
**Family**: [[Urticaceae]]
**Protologue**: P. golungensis Hiern, Enum . 1 : 993 (1900)

## Description

in Hook.f., Niger Fl. : 518 (1849). P. golungensis Hiern, Enum. 1 : 993 (1900). P. guineensis Benth. var. abyssinica (A. Rich.) Rendle, in D. Prain (ed.), Fl. Trop. Afr. 6(2) : 288 (1917). Annual or briefly perennial herb, 0.1–1(–2) m tall; stem, if perennial, weakly woody at the base, branched at the base or throughout, spreading or erect, pubescent in the upper parts, with fine hairs mixed with long erect hairs, rare to abundant. Stipules caducous, oval to lanceolate, 2–4 × ± 1 mm, with shortly caudate apex and ciliate margins, membranous, translucent, generally greenish. Leaves alternate, rarely subopposite in the lower parts of the stems, generally decreasing in size toward the branch apices; petiole 0.1–3(–5) cm, pubescent, sometimes with scattered long hairs mixed in; blade narrowly elliptic to ovate, 1.5–10 × 0.5–5 cm, base cuneate, apex acuminate, margin entire, chartaceous to subcoriaceous, upper surface with rare appressed rigid hairs and cystoliths, lower surface with ± abundant arachnoid pubescence (often completely absent) and long rigid hairs on the nerves; venation symmetrically triplinerved, basal pair of lateral nerves reaching the upper three-quarters, upper lateral nerves (1–)2 or 3(–4) pairs, but the ultimate ones scarcely distinct from the coarser tertiary network. Inflorescence restricted to the upper part of the stem, in glomerules with 1 or 2(–3) female flowers and up to 5(–6) male flowers, generally mixed with foliaceous bracts with broadly cordate base and rounded apex (probably reduced leaves), ± 1–2 mm long, bearing dense hooked hairs, and bearing membranous or brown, slender and indistinct bracts, up to ± 1 mm long (probably the true bracts or stipules of the cordate bracts). Male flower: pedicel ± 0.5 mm; perianth globose, apiculate, ± 1 mm in diameter, pubescent and with dense hooked hairs, 4(–5)-merous. Female flower sessile; perianth ± 1 mm long, narrowed at the apex, with indistinct tepals, but with 8(–10) ± marked longitudinal nerves, pubescent to pilose with scattered hooked hairs; ovary ± 1 mm long; stigma exserted, arched above it or rolled into a glomerule of ± 0.5 mm in diameter. Fruit 1.5–2 × 1.3 mm, pearly white to glossy brown, with perianth non-winged or rarely with two very narrow longitudinal wings.

## Ecology

wet open forest and wooded savanna, in clearings of lowland evergreen forest, sometimes on rocks; in Gabon at 70–500 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1300 m

## Source note

Léandri (1965) cited two varieties of P. guineensis for Madagascar and the Comores. Wilmot-Dear & Friis (2006) reduced P. guineensis var. madagascariensis Wedd. in DC. (1869) to a synonym of P. boiviniana (Blume) Wedd. (1857) and P. guineensis var. seyrigii Léandri (1965) to a synonym of P. weddellii Léandri (1951). After this taxonomic clarification, P. guineensis is restricted to continental Africa. P. guineensis is highly variable, from slender annuals to robust shrublets, the first form was originally called P. abyssinica and the second, P. guineensis. But the two rather occur together over their range, from west to east Africa, with the "P. abyssinica" form in wooded savannas and the "P. guineensis" form along forest edges and in forest clearings.
