# *Napoleonaea talbotii* Bak.f.

**Genus**: [[Napoleonaea]]
**Family**: [[Lecythidaceae]]
**Protologue**: N. megacarpa Bak.f. in Rendle et al. , Cat. pl. Oban : 31 (1913)

## Description

in Rendle et al., Cat. pl. Oban : 32 (1913). N. megacarpa Bak.f. in Rendle et al. , Cat. pl. Oban : 31 (1913). N. gascoignei Bak.f. in Rendle et al. , Cat. pl. Oban : 33 (1913). Shrub sometimes bushy, 3–12 m tall; branches ± quadrangular. Leaf: petiole 5–15 mm, black; blade narrowly elliptic-obovate to oblong or elliptic, (9–)15–23(–30) × 3–7(–9.5) cm, cuneate or rarely rounded at base, cuspidate at apex, acumen (1.2–)1.5–2.5(–3.9) cm, subcoriaceous, margin entire or undulate to dentate, upper surface shiny, lower surface bearing on the margin a few rare and scattered ± visible glands; lateral nerves 4–8(–10) pairs, united in arches toward the blade margin; tertiary venation scalariform. Inflorescence axillary or cauliflorous; bracts circular, 2–3 mm in diameter, glabrous. Flowers solitary or in fascicles of 5–10, with distinct pedicel; calyx smooth or ± verrucose, very shortly puberulent, not exceeding 7–9 mm in diameter, with erect and acute lobes, bearing (2–)3 glands at apex; first corona up to 45 mm in diameter, but generally smaller, cream to white; third corona fused over half. Fruit transversely ellipsoid, 2–2.5 × 4–5.5 cm, brown, with 5–10 lobes. Seed reniform, 17 × 14–15 × 9–10 mm.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroun, Gabon; in Gabon uncommon, observed in the provinces of Estuaire, Ogooué-Lolo, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ngounié and Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology

dense humid forests; in Gabon and elsewhere up to 600 m elevation. Taxonomic note: This species is close to N. egertonii by the presence of lateral nerves united in arches over the entire length of the blade. B: Hutchinson & Dalziel (1954), Knuth (1939), Liben (1971). Bibliography
