# *Psorospermum membranaceum* C.H.Wright

**Genus**: [[Psorospermum]]
**Family**: [[Hypericaceae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull. 1898 : 301 (1898)

## Description

Subshrub to shrub 0.5–3 m tall; bark scaly, brown to reddish-brown; branches reddish-brown, glabrous. Leaves opposite; petiole 4–11 mm, glabrous or sparsely puberulent; blade elliptic, sometimes elliptic-ovate to elliptic-obovate or narrowly elliptic, 5–17 × 2–8.5 cm, cuneate at base, apex acuminate, papyraceous, glabrous, lower surface with a line of black glands along the margin and often scattered over the surface which is also densely covered by small brownish spots; lateral veins 5–8, tertiary reticulum indistinct. Inflorescence terminal, with one umbellate cyme or sometimes two superposed umbellate cymes; peduncle 0–3 mm, glabrous. Flower: pedicel 3–7 mm, up to 11 mm in fruit, glabrous; sepals ovate, 3.5–4.5 × 1.5–2 mm, glabrous to puberulent, with membranous margins; petals ovate to elliptic, 7–8 × 3.5–4 mm, dirty white or yellowish-white and with several red glandular lines; stamen fascicles with 5–8 stamens 4–5 mm long, pubescent on both faces, anthers ± 0.5 mm in diameter; ovary globose, 2 mm tall, grooved, glabrous; styles 3 mm, stigmas slightly swollen. Fruit globose, apiculate at apex, 6–15 mm in diameter, orange-red to dark red. Seed ellipsoid, 8–9 × 4 mm, heterocotyledonous with sub-plano-convex cotyledons and incumbent.

## Distribution

southwestern Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea and Gabon; in Gabon especially in the northern and central part of the country (Estuaire, Woleu-Ntem, Moyen-Ogooué, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ngounié and Ogooué-Maritime)

## Ecology

primary forest and old secondary forest, on sandy or clay soil, on slopes or summits; in Gabon at 0–750 m altitude, elsewhere up to 900 m. B: Bamps (1966, 1969), Pellegrin (1959), Sosef et al. (2006). Bibliography
