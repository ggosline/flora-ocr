# *Amphiblemma soyauxii* Cogniaux

**Genus**: [[Amphiblemma]]
**Family**: [[Melastomataceae]]
**Protologue**: Mon. Phan. 7, Melast.: 1184 (1891); JACQ.-FÉL., Fl. Cam. 24 : 98, tab. 32 (1983)

## Description

Shrub erect, up to 2 m, often purple, cauliflorous; branches at first furfuraceous, then glabrescent, or with spreading hairs, glandular; warts on the nodes. Leaves ample, membranaceous; petiole 7-10 cm, furfuraceous, often with scattered glandular hairs; blade oval, 8-10 × 15-17 cm, rounded-cordate at the base, acute to acuminate at the apex; setae appressed, scattered above, rarer below, except towards the margins and on the nerves; 5 to 7 nerves, the transverse ones ± parallel; margins serrulate-ciliate.

Axillary cymes, mainly on recently defoliated nodes, subsessile, bifurcate, contracted. Flowers with pedicel 1 to 4 mm; hypanthium ellipsoid-oblong, pentagonal, 5-6 mm tall, glabrescent to pubescent, rarely with a few scattered setae towards the base, often glandular hairs on the calyx; tooth-like lobes 2 to 2.5 mm, acute to setaceous. Petals pink, obovate, asymmetric, 5 × 10 mm, ciliate-glandular towards the top. External stamens with anther 7 mm; podoconnective 4-5 mm, anterior appendage 2 mm, truncate-bidiscoid; filament 5 mm. Internal stamens with anther 4-5 mm; connective thickened with an obtuse anterior appendage and an obtuse spur; filament 4 mm; placentas sessile; style 10 mm.

Capsule ellipsoid-oblong, 5 × 10 mm, pentagonal; apex of ovary truncate, epigynous crown non-accrescent, reaching the edge of the hypanthium; loculicidal dehiscence over the entire height. Seeds 1.5 mm in total length, appendiculate below by the funicle and above by the air vesicle. — Pl. 28, fig. 5-7, p. 79.
