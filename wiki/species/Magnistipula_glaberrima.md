# *Magnistipula glaberrima* Engler

**Genus**: [[Magnistipula]]
**Family**: [[Scytopetalaceae]]
**Protologue**: Not. Bot. Gart. Mus. Berlin 6 : 35 (1913); Pflanzenw. Afr. 3 (1) : 308, tab. 198 (1915); WHITE, BJBB 46 : 290 (1976)

## Description

Pflanzenw. Afr. 3 (1) : 308, tab. 198 (1915); WHITE, BJBB 46 : 290 (1976).

— Hirtella glabrescens (ENGL.) A. CHEV., Bull. Mus. Nat. Hist. Nat. Paris, ser. 2, 3 : 195 (1931); HAUM., BJBB 21 : 184 (1951).

Small tree reaching 10–15 m in height and 30 cm in diameter. Trunk irregular, knotty here and there, somewhat winged at the base. Branches curved, spreading, with fairly dense foliage. Rhytidome yellowish-greenish mossy. Bark thin, brittle, with an ephemeral odour (as does the wood) of sugarcane; oblique section red with large darker fibres. Wood hard, brownish-red. Young branchlets glabrous. Stipules ovate or slightly cordate, up to $15 \times 18$ mm, 5–9-palmatinerved with anastomosing veins and reticulate veinlets, glabrous. Petiole 3–4(–7) mm, glabrous. Blade elliptic, 4–6(–7) × 8–12(–16) cm, rarely elliptic-oblong; base broadly acute but abruptly narrowed onto the upper part of the petiole; apex obtuse, scarcely distinctly acuminate with the acumen broadly obtuse; lateral veins 4–6(–7) pairs; blade glabrous; generally 2 small glands, rarely more, elliptic or circular above the base of the blade, between the midvein and the margin, sometimes small circular glands below the acumen.

Inflorescences cymose, terminal and axillary-subtermininal, scarcely exceeding 6(–12) cm in length, slender, 2(3) times ramified, glabrous. Bracts triangular, 1 × 2 mm, ± bearing on the margin glands with substipitate base; pedicel slender bearing, almost at its base, 2 bracteoles alternate, sometimes subopposite, likewise triangular, 0.5 × 1 mm, and glandular; pedicel measuring 2.5(–6) mm to the articulation, the latter situated at the level of the apex of the upper bracteole (or distinctly higher), the portion of the pedicel below the receptacle also reaching 2.5(–6) mm. Floral bud glabrous, approximately 5 mm, with receptacle straight, obliquely truncate and bearing the sepals in continuation. Open flower ("nacre-like yellow and violet" according to LE TESTU) reaching 6(–10) mm, half of which for the very swollen ventricose base of the receptacle, the latter bearing white hairs inside directed downward at its throat; lower and lateral sepals triangular, 2 mm, the upper one ovate concave, 2.5 mm, all ciliate on the margin and pubescent inside; petals white, orbicular (1 mm), with villose margin; stamens (6–7), slightly curved, 3–4 mm long and fused almost to mid-height; staminodial rim formed of (7–)8 triangular teeth at most 0.5 mm high; ovary ovoid scarcely exceeding $1 \times 1.5$ mm, glabrous; style curved downward, 3–4 mm, very independent from the base of the ovary.

Fruiting inflorescence and fruit unknown. — PL. 26, p. 89.

## Type

## Discussion

PHYTOGEOGRAPHIC NOTES:

Magnistipula glaberrima Engl. is known only from Cameroon, in the Atlantic forest zone with Caesalpiniaceae, and from Gabon (Upper Ngounyé).
