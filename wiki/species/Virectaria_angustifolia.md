# *Virectaria angustifolia* (Hiern) Brem.

**Genus**: [[Virectaria]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bremekamp, Ver. K. Nederl. Akad. Wetensch., Afd. Natuurk., set. 2 : 21 (1952)

## Synonyms

- Virecta angustifolia Hiern, F.T.A. 3 : 48 (1877).
- Virecta heteromera K. Schum., Bot. Jahrb. 23 : 422 (1896).
- Virectaria heteromera (K. Schum.) Bremek., l.c. (1952).

## Description

Shrub ± spreading 6–30 cm; internodes rarely exceeding 3–4 cm, finely pubescent. Stipules triangular simple, pubescent, 1–2.5 mm. Petiole pubescent 1–7 mm. Blade glabrous above or moderately hairy (N. H. 2199), narrowly elliptic, reaching 10–35 × 3–8 mm, often very small; base acute; apex narrowly obtuse. 2–4 pairs of secondary veins.

Inflorescences terminal subcontracted surrounded by leaves. Approximately 2–6 white or pale pink-lilac flowers, erect. 4 calyx lobes linear acute, 0.6–1 mm long, nearly glabrous. Corolla 5–8 mm long, with 4–6(7) glabrous lobes. Stamens 3.9 mm. Style 7 mm. Disk cylindric truncate. Ovary pubescent.

Infructescence with the pedicels somewhat accrescent. Young fruit glabrescent with sepals semi-erect. Mature fruit with a single persistent valve, 2–2.5 mm long, with margins curved so as to touch or overlap. Small prismatic seeds 0.4–0.5 mm when dry.
