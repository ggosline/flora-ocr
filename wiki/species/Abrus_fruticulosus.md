# *Abrus fruticulosus* Wall. ex Wight & Arn.

**Genus**: [[Abrus]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Prodr. fl. Ind. orient. 1 : 236 (1834)

## Description

Leaflet, lower surface (× 2). – 4. Terminal part of an inflorescence (× 4). – 5. Flower (× 1.5). – 6. Calyx with bract and bracteoles (× 5). – 7. Pod (× ¾). (1–6: Espirito Santo 3116; 7: Michel 2822). Drawing by Ike Zewald, Naturalis Biodiversity Center (©).

Climbing shrub or liana 6 m tall. Stipules small, lanceolate to linear, 2 mm long or sometimes reaching 10 mm. Leaf: petiole 0.3–3 cm, rachis 1.5–7 cm, terminated by a point 1–3 mm; leaflets in 5 to 10(–20) pairs (for Gabon 5 to 7 pairs), with limb oblong to obovate or sometimes rectangular, often oblique, 3–45(–50) × 1–15(–20) mm, base cordate to rounded or cuneate, apex acute to rounded or ± truncate-emarginate, papyraceous, glabrous to pubescent above, with sparse to dense appressed pubescence below. Inflorescence in terminal or axillary pseudoraceme; bracts reaching ½ of the calyx length. Flower: calyx with tube ± 2–4 mm long, the teeth 0.5–1 mm long; corolla 0.9–1.5 cm long, yellow or white to lilac. Pod oblong, flattened, 2–7(–9) × 0.8–1.5 cm, base rounded-cuneate, apex rounded with a beak, with 4 to 12 seeds, glabrescent to pubescent, sometimes verrucose. Seed ovoid-suborbicular, laterally flattened, 3–7 × 2–5 mm, yellowish to black, mostly glossy.

## Distribution

circumtropical; in Gabon, known from Haut-Ogooué and Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology

forest margins, savanna, as a shrub in sunny places, secondary forests; in Gabon, up to 170 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1500 m. Flowers and fruits in January and October

## Source note

Very polymorphic species with considerable synonymy, see Breteler (1960). b: Boutique (1954), Breteler (1960), Lemmens & Breteler (1999), Verdcourt (1970, 1971b). Bibliography
