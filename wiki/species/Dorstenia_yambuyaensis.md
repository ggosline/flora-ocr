# *Dorstenia yambuyaensis* De Wildeman

**Genus**: [[Dorstenia]]
**Family**: [[Moraceae]]
**Protologue**: Ann. Mus. Congo, Bot., sér. 5, 2: 241 (1908); sér. 5, 3: 66, fig. 2, 3, tab. 5 (1909); Pl. Bequaert. 6: 72 (1932); N.E. Br., Curt. Bot. Mag. 141: tab. 8616 (1915); RENDLE in HUTCH & RENDLE, FTA 6 (2): 56 (1916); HAUMAN, FCB 1: 64 (1948)

## Description

sér. 5, 3: 66, fig. 2, 3, tab. 5 (1909); Pl. Bequaert. 6: 72 (1932); N.E. Br., Curt. Bot. Mag. 141: tab. 8616 (1915); RENDLE in HUTCH & RENDLE, FTA 6 (2): 56 (1916); HAUMAN, FCB 1: 64 (1948).

- Dorstenia goossensi DE WILD., l.c.: 33 (1932); HAUMAN, l.c. 1: 65 (1948). TYPE: Van Goossens 1584, Zaïre, environ Bikoro, lac Toumba (holo-, BR).

Herbs with creeping and descending rhizomes, stems 15-40 cm tall, lower parts woody, sparsely puberulent, leafy parts with fairly long white hairs, dense spreading, internodes 0.5-2.5 cm long, 2-4 mm thick. Leaves arranged in spirals. Blade oblong or elliptic, occasionally deeply and distinctly lobed, widest at or above the midpoint, 5-13 (-16) × 2-7 cm, chartaceous, apex acute to shortly acuminate, base acute or subobtuse to subcordate, margin undulate to coarsely dentate, glabrous above, very small to short white spreading hairs, scattered to dense especially on the veins below; midvein and secondary veins weakly concave above, weakly prominent below, 6-10 pairs of secondary veins. Petiole 3-15 mm long, approximately 1.5 mm thick, with fairly dense short and long white spreading hairs. Stipules persistent, subulate, 2-6 mm long, approximately 0.5 mm wide, with scattered short hairs.

Inflorescences solitary, whitish or yellowish-green. Peduncle slender, (1-) 2.5-6 cm long, 1-2 mm thick, puberulent with short white hairs mixed with longer spreading horizontal hairs. Receptacle discoid and flat to coarsely convex, suborbicular to multiangular, flowering face multiangular or stellately lobed, 13-20 mm in diameter, margin slightly papyraceous, 2-8 mm thick, with radiant ribs becoming numerous thin linear appendages, widened at the base and pectinate, approximately 6 cm long, very long up to approximately 12 cm; appendages widened and pectinate in the lower 1/3 or 1/2, between numerous shorter appendages of variable length, widened and pectinate in the lower 1/5 or 1/4; upper surface of the margin and appendages almost glabrous, lower surface of the receptacle and appendages with very small white hairs mixed with longer and rougher rather horizontal hairs especially on the visible ribs of the longer appendages. Flowers ♂ numerous, spaced, approximately 0.7 mm in diameter, perianth bi- or trilobed; stamens 2-3, filaments approximately 0.4 mm long, anthers approximately 0.2 mm long and 0.1 mm wide, connective rather narrow; pistillode present. Flowers ♀ 10-20, regularly distributed on the flowering face, perianth tubular, approximately 0.4 mm in diameter; style approximately 0.4 mm long, stigmas 2, linear, approximately 0.3 mm long, reflexed.

Pyrene subglobose, approximately 2.5 mm in diameter, smooth. - Pl. 23, p. 75.

## Type

Laurent M. 1997, Zaïre, Yambuya (lecto-, BR)

## Discussion

This species is morphologically close to D. barteri var. multiradiata, but differs in the remarkable inflorescences with their long gracefully fringed appendages.

This species is known from Zaïre, Gabon, and southwestern Cameroon. It occurs in the shaded parts of the forest floor.
