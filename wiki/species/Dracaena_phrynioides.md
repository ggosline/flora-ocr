# *Dracaena phrynioides* Hook. Planche 18, Figure 4(E)

**Genus**: [[Dracaena]]
**Family**: [[Dracaenaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Mag. 88 : t. 5352 (1862)

## Description

Pleomele phrynioides (Hook.) N.E.Br., Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1914 : 278 (1914). Shrub up to 1 m tall; trunk solitary, generally simple, up to ± 15 cm tall, visible only in the basal 5 cm in older specimens; roots orange. Leaves: up to 6, arranged in a rosette, concealing the stem by their base, uniformly distributed and overlapping; sheath embracing the stem for more than the circumference, over a length of ± 1 cm; pseudetiole erect, up to 65 cm long, cylindric, channelled above, dark green, base passing abruptly to the sheath; blade horizontal, arched, conduplicate, narrowly ovate to ovate, up to 30 × 11 cm, base rounded, apex recurved, acute-acuminate, mucronate for ± 10 mm, papery-coriaceous, upper surface dark green shiny, lower surface pale green matte, generally variegated with transverse green spots, sometimes with a small yellow centre, midvein prominent below for more than half the blade length, veins parallel and veinlets obscure. Inflorescence nodding, capitate, up to 10 cm long, with smooth, green axes, capitule bracteate, up to 4 cm diameter; bracts caducous, broadly ovate to circular, up to 15 × 10 mm, dark brown to violet, scarious; fascicles with up to 3 flowers; floral bracts narrowly ovate, up to 15 × 3 mm, scarious, grey to violet. Flower sessile; perianth ± 22 mm long, white tinged with pink toward the apex, with lobes much shorter than the tube, patent; stamens inserted near the throat, up to 2 mm shorter than the lobes, filament cylindric, swollen with a subulate apex, white, anther ± 2 mm long; ovary oblong, up to 2.5 × 1.5 mm; style up to 1 mm shorter than the lobes, white, stigma slightly 3-lobed, ± 0.5 mm diameter. Fruit subsessile, entire to 2- or 3-lobed, 12 × 9 mm, orange-red to dark red, lobes ovoid, distinctly cornute. Seed trapezoidal, ± 8 × 4 × 8 mm.

## Distribution

Guinea, Liberia, Ivory Coast, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea and Gabon; in Gabon, known from Estuaire and Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology

forest understory, riverbanks; in Gabon at 0–150 m altitude, elsewhere at 0–1500 m. Flowering not recorded in Gabon. In West Africa, flowers are found from March to October. Use: D. phrynioides is a magic plant of women. It is planted around the enclosure reserved for the secret ceremonies of Ndèmbè. B: Baker (1898), Bos (1984), Hepper (1968), Mwachala (2005), Velayos & Cabezas (2018). Bibliography
