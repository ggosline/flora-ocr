# *Dracaena phrynioides* Hook. Planche 18, Figure 4(E)

**Genus**: [[Dracaena]]
**Family**: [[Dracaenaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Mag. 88 : t. 5352 (1862)

## Synonyms

- Pleomele phrynioides (Hook.) N.E.Br., Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1914 : 278 (1914).

## Description

Shrub up to 1 m in height; trunk solitary, generally simple, up to ± 15 cm in height, visible only in the basal 5 cm in older specimens; roots orange. Leaves: up to 6, arranged in rosette, concealing the stem by their base, uniformly distributed and overlapping; sheath embracing the stem for more than the circumference, over a length of ± 1 cm; pseudpetiole erect, up to 65 cm in length, cylindrical, channelled above, dark green, base passing abruptly to the sheath; blade horizontal, arched, conduplicate, narrowly oval to oval, up to 30 × 11 cm, base rounded, apex recurved, acute-acuminate, mucronate over ± 10 mm, papyraceous-coriaceous, upper surface dark green glossy, lower surface pale green matte, generally variegated with transverse green patches, sometimes with a small yellow centre, midvein prominent below over more than half the length of the blade, parallel veins and obscure veinlets. Inflorescence nodding, capitate, up to 10 cm in length, with smooth axes, green, capitule bracteate, up to 4 cm in diameter; bracts caducous, broadly oval to circular, up to 15 × 10 mm, dark brown to violet, scarious; fascicles with up to 3 flowers; floral bracts narrowly oval, up to 15 × 3 mm, scarious, grey to violaceous. Flower sessile; perianth ± 22 mm in length, white tinged with pink towards the apex, with lobes much shorter than the tube, spreading; stamens inserted near the throat, up to 2 mm shorter than the lobes, filament cylindrical, swollen with a subulate apex, white, anther ± 2 mm in length; ovary oblong, up to 2.5 × 1.5 mm; style up to 1 mm shorter than the lobes, white, stigma slightly 3-lobed, ± 0.5 mm in diameter. Fruit subsessile, entire to 2- or 3-lobed, 12 × 9 mm, orange-red to dark red, lobes ovoid, distinctly corniculate. Seed trapezoidal, ± 8 × 4 × 8 mm.

## Distribution

Guinea, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea and Gabon; in Gabon, known from Estuaire and Ogooué-Maritime.

## Ecology

Forest understory, river margins; in Gabon at 0–150 m altitude, elsewhere at 0–1500 m. Flowering not recorded in Gabon. In West Africa, flowers are found from March to October. Use: D. phrynioides is a magical plant of women. It is planted around the enclosure reserved for the secret ceremonies of the Ndèmbè.
