# *Agelaea pentagyna* (Lamarck) Baillon

**Genus**: [[Agelaea]]
**Family**: [[Connaraceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Linn. Paris 44: 345 (1882); SCHELLENBERG, PR 103 (4, 127): 89 (1938);

## Description

- Connarus pentagynus LAM., Encycl. Meth. 2 (1): 95 (1786). TYPE: Madagascar, s.l., Commerson s.n. (holo- P).

- A. trifolia (LAM.) BAILL., Adansonia, sér.1, 7: 237 (1867); SCHELLENBERG, PR 103 (4, 127): 83 (1938); HEPPER, FWTA, ed. 2, 1 (2): 745 (1958).

- Cnestis trifolia LAM., Encycl. Méth. 3 (1): 24 (1789). TYPE: Sierra Leone, s.l., Smeathmann s.n. (holo- BM).

- A. obliqua (PAL.; BEAUV.) BAILL., Adansonia, sér. 1, 7: 238 (1867); BAKER, FTA 1: 454 (1868); SCHELLENBERG, PR 103 (4, 127): 90 (1938); HEPPER, FWTA, ed. 2, 1 (2): 745 (1958).

– Cnestis obliqua PAL. BEAUV., Fl. Oware : 97 (1804). TYPE : Nigeria, Oware, Palisot de Beauvois s.n. (holo- G).

- A. hirsuta DE WILD., Ann. Mus. Congo, sér. Bot., 5 (3): 100, tab. 25 (1909); SCHELLENBERG, PR 103 (4, 127): 67 (1938); TROUPIN, FCBR 3: 100 (1952); HEPPER, FWTA, ed. 2, 1 (2): 745 (1958). TYPE: Zaïre, Lukolela, Pynaert 185 (holo-BR).

- A. conraui, SCHELLENB., EBJ 58: 207 (1923); PR 103 (4, 127): 73 (1938). TYPE: Cameroun, Bangwe, Conrau 268 (holo-B deletion); NEOTYPE: Gabon, 30 km S.E. of Doussala, Reitsma 1677 (WAG).

- A. ovalis SCHELLENB., l.c.: 218 (1923); l.c.: 87 (1938). TYPE: Annobon, Mildbraed 6606 & 6679 (all 2 syn- B delet.). NEOTYPE: Gabon, Oveng, Louis & al. 416 (holo-WAG; iso- LBV).

Large liana reaching 25 m. Interxylary phloem frequent. Branches sometimes deeply grooved; young branches glabrous to tomentose.

Leaves. Petiole 1-26 cm; rachis 0.1-9 cm; petiolules 1-9 mm. Leaflets 3, elliptic to ovate or cordate, glabrous or tomentose; 3-8 pairs of large lateral veins: those of the basal pair are equal to or longer than the following ones and originate exactly at the base of the leaflet; venation III sometimes distinctly parallel; apex acuminate; terminal leaflet 2.2-30 × 1.5-17 cm; lateral leaflets 1.8-24 × 1.4-12.5 cm, generally asymmetric – Pl. 8, p. 35.

Inflorescences ≤ 35 cm, glabrous to tomentose.

Flowers heterotristylous. Pedicel approximately 1 mm long. Sepals 2.5-5 × 1 mm, bordered with multicellular hairs, exterior velvety. Petals 3-5.5 × 1 mm, often connivent near the base, glabrous. Stamens: the large ones 2.5-6 mm long and the small ones 1-4.5 mm long. Pistil, height 2-5.5 mm differing from that of the stamens; style pubescent; ovary velvety.

Fruit: developed follicles 1-5 per flower, 15-25 × 8-16 mm, pyriform and generally not terminated by a beak, never bearing coarse protuberances.

Seed 10-15 × 5-10 mm; testa yellow to red, of which approximately one quarter is fleshy. - Pl. 9, p. 37.

## Source note

We believe that A. pentagyna forms a complex of numerous microspecies or forms apparently resulting from partial genetic isolation. In the field, one may encounter two or more of these forms, most of whose descendants (often very abundant in the forest stratum) have an appearance very similar to the parental form. Since heterostylous species encounter serious obstacles to self-fertilization, one would consequently come to describe species on differences such as those of leaf shape and/or indumentum, as SCHELLENBERG had done (1923: 200; 1938: 65), even though the flowers and fruits are all similar. The lengthening of the resulting species list would be rapid and without limit: acquisitions, particularly those from Gabon and Cameroon, would continually bring forth forms corresponding to as many "new species" as new combinations of characters, whereas these forms are often in fact bridges between already described taxa. Thus, there exist numerous forms living in partial isolation, but these correspond in our opinion only to the variations of a single and unique species: A. pentagyna (example: variation of the terminal leaflet, see Pl. 9, p. 37).
