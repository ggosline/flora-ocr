# *Combretum falcatum* (Welwitsch ex Hiern) Jongkind

**Genus**: [[Combretum]]
**Family**: [[Combretaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Mus. Nat. Hist. Paris, Adansonia 14: 261 (1993)

## Description

– Quisqualis falcata Welwitsch ex Hiern, Cat. african plants collected by Welwitsch 1(2) : 355 (1898) ; LIBEN, FZ : 83 (1968) ; EXELL & GARCIA, CFA : 86 (1970). TYPE : Angola, Cunza Norte, Golungo Alto, between Sange and Bango (fl., fr.) Welwitsch 4348 (holo-BM ; iso-BR, COI, K, LISU, M, P).

– Combretum pellegrinianum EXELL, Journal of Botany 67 : 177 (1929). TYPE : Gabon, Ossiga (fl., fr.) J. de Brazza 83 (holo-BM : iso-P).

– Quisqualis pellegriniana (Exell) Exell, l.c. 69 : 121 (1931).

– Cacoucia littorea Engler, Pflanzenwelt Ost Afrikas und der Nachbargebiete, C : 293 (1895). TYPE : Tanzania, Tanga : Holst 2061 (syn-B delet. ; iso-E, HBG, K, M, Z) ; 4034 (syn-B delet. ) ; (fl., fr.) Volkens 147 (syn-B delet. ; LECTOTYPE designated here : lecto-BR ; iso-BM, HBG, K).

– Combretum littoreum (ENGLER) ENGLER & DIELS, Mon. Afr., Pfl., 3 : 87, tab. 25d (1899).

– Quisqualis littorea (Engler) Exell, Journal of Botany 69: 121 (1931); Wickens, FTEA: 68 (1973).

– Quisqualis pellegriniana auct. non (EXELL) EXELL, l.c. 69 : 121 (1931) : KEAY in FWTA, ed. 2 1(1) : 275 (1954) quoad Talbot 498 = Griffonia physocarpa BAILLON [FABACEAE-CAES].

Liana ≤ 20 m or shrub. Young branches sometimes bearing large spreading dark red hairs.

Leaves opposite ; petiole ≤ 8 mm, its lower part sometimes transformed into a lignified hook ; blade elliptic-ovate, papyraceous, ≤ 16 × ≤ 6.5 cm ; major lateral veins 5-7 pairs ; base of blade rounded to subcordate ; apex acuminate.

Scales absent.

Inflorescences, in terminal or axillary racemes, furnished with bracts, up to ≤ 11 cm long, increasing in size during flowering and axilled by white-greenish to red leaves. Bracts ≤ 25 × 5 mm, white-greenish.

Flowers 5-merous, shortly stipitate, inclined toward the interior. Receptacle inferior 3 mm high, the superior tubular-infundibuliform, ≤ 25 mm high and ≤ 5 mm wide. Calyx : lobes triangular, apiculate. Petals ≤ 6 × ≤ 4 mm, exterior covered with short hairs, interior glabrous but furnished with papillae. Stamens exserted ≤ 1 mm, anthers 0.7 mm long. Style adnate to the wall of the upper part of the receptacle for more than half its length (Pl. 14, 4, 5, p. 51).

Fruit ≤ 3 × ≤ 2.5 cm, papyraceous wings, stipe 2 mm.
