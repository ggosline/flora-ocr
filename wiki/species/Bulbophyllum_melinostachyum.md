# *Bulbophyllum melinostachyum* Schltr.

**Genus**: [[Bulbophyllum]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 26: 342 (1899)

## Synonyms

- Bulbophyllum fuscum Lindl. var. melinostachyum (Schltr.) J. J. Verm., Bull. Jard. Bot. Nat. Belg. 56 : 240 (1986).
- Bulbophyllum obanense Rendle, Cat. Talbot S. Nig. Pl. : 101, 146 (1913).

## Description

Pseudobulbs 1-5 × 0.4-1.4 cm, ovoid to ellipsoid, 2-foliate. Leaves subsessile to shortly petiolate 1.8-11 × 0.4-2.2 cm, linear-lanceolate to elliptic-lanceolate, obtuse. Inflorescence lax, 4.5-23 cm, 6-34 (64)-flowered, rachis shortly swollen and flattened, glabrous. Flowers small. Sepals yellow, greenish to red or brownish-reddish, acute to acuminate, thin to thick, glabrous; dorsal sepal ovate to ovate-lanceolate; lateral sepals obliquely elliptic-triangular or ovate-triangular. Petals yellow to dark red, lanceolate to linear-lanceolate, subacute, straight, thin, glabrous. Lip yellow, orange or dark red, oblong to elliptic, apex rounded, very thick, glabrous. - Pl. 89, p. 179.

## Distribution

Sierra Leone, Côte d'Ivoire, Nigeria, Cameroun, Guinée équatoriale, Gabon, Congo (Kinshasa), Ouganda, Tanzanie, Zambie, Malawi, Zimbabwe, Mozambique

## Ecology

alt. 0-2100 m. Epiphytic in lowland and montane forest, secondary forest with Lophira alata, Brachystegia woodland, on branch and trunk, or lithophytic on rock covered with lichens or mosses
