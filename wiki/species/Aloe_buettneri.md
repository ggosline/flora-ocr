# *Aloe buettneri* A.Berger Figure 1

**Genus**: [[Aloe]]
**Family**: [[Aloaceae]]
**Protologue**: Engl. Bot. Jahrb. 36 : 60 (1905)

## Description

Acaulescent herb, solitary, sometimes producing young shoots basally and thus forming small groups, the base enlarged into a bulb towards the end of the growth period. Leaves in rosette, spreading, desiccated in the dry season, narrowly ovate, 35–55 × 5–10 cm, uniformly green, upper surface with dark lines, sometimes with a few whitish spots towards the base, smooth, margin with white teeth 3–4 mm long and distant 5–40 mm. Inflorescence erect, 40–100 cm tall, often with 3–5 branches; peduncle with a few sterile ovate bracts below the racemes; racemes cylindric to subcapitate, 15 × 7 cm; floral bracts narrowly ovate-acuminate, 10–25 × 4–8 mm, brownish. Flower: pedicel 20–35 mm, thickened in fruit; perianth cylindric, 35–38 mm long, constricted above the ovary, yellowish-green to dull red; outer tepals free for ± ⅓ of their length. Fruit on a robust pedicel.

## Distribution

widely distributed across West Africa, from Mali and Ghana to Nigeria; to the south, towards western Democratic Republic of the Congo, Angola and northern Namibia. In Gabon, found in Haut-Ogooué, but perhaps also in Ogooué-Maritime (see note above)

## Ecology

grasslands and wooded savanna, on sandy soils; in Gabon, at 500 m altitude, in tropical Africa, at 250 to approximately 900 m, in the southernmost part of its range, up to around 1800 m. B: Carter et al. (2011), Hepper (1968), Newton (2001). Bibliography
