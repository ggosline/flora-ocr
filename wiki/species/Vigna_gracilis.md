# *Vigna gracilis* (Guill. & Perr.) Hook.f.

**Genus**: [[Vigna]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: V. multiflora Hook.f., in Hook., Niger Fl. : 308 (1849)

## Description

in Hook., Niger Fl. : 311 (1849). V. multiflora Hook.f., in Hook., Niger Fl. : 308 (1849). V. desmodioides R.Wilczek, Bull. Jard. Bot. État Bruxelles 24 : 438 (1954). Perennial herb, climbing; stem reaching 1–2.5(–5) m in length, glabrous or pubescent. Stipules narrowly triangular, 1–5 mm in length, cordate to subauricate at the base, striate, pubescent. Leaf: petiole 1–5 cm; rhachis 3–9 mm; stipels subulate, 1 mm in length; leaflets with petiolule of 1–2 mm, blade ovate to ovate-lanceolate, rather variable, that of the terminal leaflet rarely lobed, 1.5–6 × 1–4 cm, rounded at the base, rounded to acute and scarcely mucronate at the apex, with fine pubescence on both faces or glabrous. Inflorescence: peduncle 1–15 cm; rhachis up to 15 cm in length; bracts linear, 1–2 mm in length. Flower very slightly or not scented; bracteoles subulate, 0.5–2 mm, ± pubescent; calyx pubescent, with tube 1.5–3 mm in length, lobes triangular 1–1.5 mm in length; corolla blue, 12–16 mm in length, glabrous, standard obovate, subsessile, 9–12 × 10 mm, wings oblique, ± 9 × 5 mm, keel falcate, ± 10 × 4 mm, obtuse; ovary with 5 to 8 ovules; style extended beyond the stigma by a short beak. Pod pendulous, linear, cylindrical, 2–4 cm × 3–4 mm, with 6 to 8 seeds, black at maturity, very slightly pubescent. Seed irregularly ellipsoid, ± 3–3.5 × 2 mm; hilum ± central, strophiole circular, small.

## Distribution

from Senegal to Uganda, the Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola; in Gabon, scattered throughout

## Ecology

periforest savannas, roadsides, mangrove near the beach, fallow land; in Gabon up to 400 m in altitude, elsewhere up to 1300 m. Flowers and fruits almost throughout the year

## Vernacular names

haricot sauvage (fr). B : Pasquet (2001), Pellegrin (1949), Raponda-Walker & Sillans (1961), Verdcourt (1971c), i B liographie Wilczek (1954)
