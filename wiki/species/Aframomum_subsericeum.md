# *Aframomum subsericeum* (Oliv. et Hanb.) K. Schum.
**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Aframomum]]
## Description
Rhizomatous herb to 4 m. Blades c. 35 × 6.5 cm, linear-lanceolate, densely short-felted beneath; ligule leathery, 5 mm. Two- or three-flowered spikes on an 8–10 cm peduncle. Corolla pink; labellum purple with yellow throat, c. 6 cm across. Fruit 6 × 3 cm.
## Source
Koechlin, J. (1964). Zingibéracées. *Flore du Gabon* 9: 38–39.
