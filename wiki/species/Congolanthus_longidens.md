# *Congolanthus longidens* (N.E.Br.) A.Raynal Planche 23

**Genus**: [[Congolanthus]]
**Family**: [[Gentianaceae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia , sér. 2, 8 : 56, t. 2 (1968)

## Synonyms

- Neurotheca longidens N.E.Br. in Thiselton-Dyer, Fl. Trop. Afr. 4(1) : 560 (1903).
- Neurotheca densa De Wild., Ann. Mus. Congo, Bot., sér. 5, 2 : 337 (1908).

## Description

Herb 5–100 cm in height; stem white to reddish. Leaf: blade elliptic to ovate, 2–35 × 1.3–15 mm, green or sometimes violaceous, base long-attenuate, apex obtuse. Flower: pedicel 0–1(–3) mm; calyx 3–4.5 mm in length, green, often violaceous at apex, tube 1–2 mm in length, lobes narrowly triangular, 2–2.5 mm in length, membranous and hyaline on the margins; corolla 4.5–7 mm in length, violaceous to blue or white to pink, tube 3–5 mm in length, lobes triangular, 1.5–2 mm in length; stamens: filament 0.5–4 mm, anther 0.5–0.6 mm in length; ovary ellipsoid-oblong; style 1.5–3 mm. Fruit 3–5 mm in length. Seed 0.40–0.45 × 0.35–0.45 mm.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Uganda and Tanzania; in Gabon, known only from Ogooué-Ivindo and Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology
