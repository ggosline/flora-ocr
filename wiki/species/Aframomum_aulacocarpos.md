# *Aframomum aulacocarpos* Pellegr. ex J. Koech.
**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Aframomum]]
## Description
Herb 1.5–2 m with 30–35 × 6–9 cm asymmetric blades and bifid ligule to 25 mm. Basal spike one- or few-flowered; flowers mauve. Red fruit 5–6 × 2.5 cm, diagnostic with nine strong longitudinal ribs.
