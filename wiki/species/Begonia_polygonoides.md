# *Begonia polygonoides* Hook.f. Planche 50

**Genus**: [[Begonia]]
**Family**: [[Begoniaceae]]

## Description

in Oliv., Fl. trop. Afr. 2 : 571 (1871). Epiphyte; stem suffrutescent, slender, 2–3 mm in diameter, reaching 20–80(–300) cm in length. Stipules long-persistent, narrowly triangular, 5–15(–20) × 2–5 mm. Leaves: petiole 1–6(–10) mm; blade weakly asymmetric, narrowly elliptic to ovate, obovate or oblong, 2–10 × 0.5–1.5 cm, base obliquely obtuse, apex acute to acuminate, margins nearly entire; midvein and secondary veins scarcely distinct. Inflorescence unisexual or bisexual; the ♂ (1–)7(–9)-flowered, peduncle 5–25 mm; the ♀ 1(–2)-flowered, peduncle 2–9 mm; the bisexual with 1 ♂ flower quickly caducous and 1 ♀ flower lateral, peduncle 1–5 mm. ♂ Flower: pedicel 3–11 mm; outer tepals 5–10 × 2.5–5 mm, the inner ones narrower, 4–8 × 1–2 mm; androecium ± actinomorphic, stamens 3–7, column 0.5–2 mm long, anthers sessile with longitudinal dehiscence. ♀ Flower sessile; outer tepals 7–12 × 2.5–5 mm, the inner ones 6–10 × 1–2 mm; ovary weakly curved, 10–26 × 2–2.5 mm, circular in cross-section, base generally narrowed; styles 2(–3), free parts subulate, stigmatic zone terminal, linear, entire, 1–2 mm. Infructescence with 1 or 2 fruits. Fruit (20–)30–50(–60) × 2–5 mm, opening by a longitudinal slit.

## Distribution

Guinea, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo; to be sought in Equatorial Guinea and Congo (Brazzaville)
