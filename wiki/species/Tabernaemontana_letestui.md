# *Tabernaemontana letestui* (Pellegr.) Pichon Planche 116 ; Figure 13(D)

**Genus**: [[Tabernaemontana]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Notul. Syst. (Paris) 13 : 250 (1948)

## Description

Notul. Syst. (Paris) 13: 250 (1948). Protogabunia latifolia Boiteau, Adansonia, sér. 2, 16: 278 (1976). Protogabunia letestui (Pellegr.) Boiteau, Adansonia, sér. 2, 16: 276 (1976). Shrub or small tree reaching 4 m in height; branchlets glabrous. Leaves in one pair, unequal, the smaller sometimes reaching half the length of the other and comparatively broader; petiole zero or up to 5 mm, limbless elliptic or especially at the ramifications ovate — sometimes as long as broad, 0.5–2 × 0.5–1.5 cm, cuneate or cordate at the base and often unequal, acuminate or caudate at the apex, subcoriaceous when fresh, papyraceous when dry, glabrous on both faces; secondary venation 4 to 12 pairs, tertiary venation not or scarcely visible when dry. Inflorescence 2–4 × 2–3 cm, dense with few flowers; peduncle null or reaching 3 mm, axes often marked with numerous scars of the pedicels. Flower: pedicel 1–4 mm; calyx with lobes 1.5–3 × 1–2.5 mm, glabrous, ciliate, on the inside with 2 to 4 colleters situated basally; corolla white, slender in bud, Mr with ovoid head relatively broad, 0.25 times the length of the bud and with obtuse or acute apex, glabrous on the outside, often ciliate on the lobes, partially hairy to pubescent on the inside, with tube 7–13 times as long as the calyx, 17–25 mm long, subcylindric, slightly enlarged around the anthers, 2–3 mm wide at the throat, twisted 0.5 turn at the level of the anthers, with lobes inflexed, 0.3–0.5 times the length of the tube, 1–1.5 times as long as broad, 2–3 × 1–1.5 mm; stamens inserted at 0.15–0.22 of the length of the tube, anther sessile, 4–5 mm long; pistil 5–7 mm long, glabrous, with apex ± at mid-height of the anthers. Fruit: mericarps distinct, horizontal, not recurved, obliquely ovoid, often compressed laterally when dry, 35–40 × 15–20 × 15–20 mm, rounded at the base, yellow or orange, not spotted, with 2 lateral ridges; exocarp 3 mm thick. Seed 9–10 × 4–5 × 3–3.5 mm, densely papillose and longitudinally striate. Distribution Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo, in Gabon, Estuary, Middle-Ogooué, Ngounié, Nyanga, Ogooué, Lolo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem. Ecology Undergrowth of forest in Gabon up to 700 m altitude.

Notes In the Democratic Republic of the Congo, near the border with Gabon, the type of T. bouquetii (Boiteau) Leeuwenb. was collected. This species resembles T. letestui, but differs by a distinctly longer peduncle, 5–10 mm, and by smaller flowers, corolla 10–15 mm long in bud. B: Leeuwenberg (1991). BIBLIOGRAPHY Tabernaemontana pendulflora K.Schum. Plate 117; Figure 13(E) Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 23: 225 (1896). Shrub or small tree reaching 8(–12) m in height; bark with large lenticels; wood yellowish; branchlets compressed laterally (even when fresh), glabrous. Leaves in one pair, equal or unequal, the smaller sometimes reaching half as long as the other and often broader; petiole 3–15 mm, ochre and clearly visible, limb broadly to narrowly elliptic, 0.3–0.6 times as long as broad, 5–25 × 3–15 cm, cuneate or sometimes rounded at the base or decurrent on the petiole, acuminate at the apex, subcoriaceous (even when fresh), glabrous on both faces; secondary venation 6 to 15 pairs, rather straight and abruptly curved upward near the margin. Inflorescence pendulous, 2–3 × 2–3 cm, condensed, 1 or 2 times ramified, few flowers; peduncle 1–2 mm. Flower fragrant; pedicel 2–5 mm; calyx with thick lobes, 4–5 × 3–4 mm, glabrous, ciliate, on the inside with 3 to 5 centro-basal colleters; corolla white to cream, slightly fleshy in bud, Mr with cylindric tube and subglobulous head, much broader, obtuse, 0.3–0.4 times the length of the bud, glabrous or puberulent on the outside, partially puberulent on the inside, with lobes often puberulent in the lower part, with tube 2–3.2 times the length of the calyx, 10–13 × 3–4 mm, not angular, not inflexed, twisted 0.5 turn, contracted at the throat, with lobes almost ribbon-like, 0.4–0.6 times the length of the tube, 1–1.5 times as long as broad, 5–8 × 4–5 mm; stamens inserted at mid-height of the length of the tube, apex reaching 1–4 mm below the throat, anther sessile, 5–7 mm long; pistil 6–7 mm long, glabrous. Fruit: mericarps separated, subglobose to transversely ellipsoid, 4–6 × 5–8 × 4–6 cm, pale green to glaucous, with numerous seeds; exocarp 5–15 mm thick. Seed 10–15 × 4–7 × 3–5 mm, papillose and longitudinally striate. Distribution Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Central African Republic, in Gabon, Ogooué, Ivindo, Ogooué, Lolo and Ogooué-Maritime. Ecology Undergrowth of forest in Gabon up to 1500 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1800 m.

Notes According to Leeuwenberg, the fruits can be orange, which is doubtful, for all field notes on the fruits give a color of white, greenish or glaucous. The photograph of Figure (E) shows the same color. B: Beentje (2021), Leeuwenberg (1991). BIBLIOGRAPHY
