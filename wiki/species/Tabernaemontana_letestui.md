# *Tabernaemontana letestui* (Pellegr.) Pichon Planche 116 ; Figure 13(D)

**Genus**: [[Tabernaemontana]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Notul. Syst. (Paris) 13 : 250 (1948)

## Synonyms

- Protogabunia latifolia Boiteau, Adansonia , sér. 2, 16 : 278 (1976).
- Protogabunia letestui (Pellegr.) Boiteau, Adansonia , sér. 2, 16 : 276 (1976).

## Description

Shrub or small tree reaching 4 m in height; branchlets glabrous. Leaves in one pair, unequal, the smallest up to sometimes the length of the other and comparatively broader; petiole zero or up to 5 mm, limbate, elliptic or especially at the ramifications ovate—times as long as broad, 0.5–2 × 0.8–1.2 cm, cuneate or cordate at the base and often unequal, acuminate or caudate at the apex, subcoriaceous when fresh, papyraceous when dry, glabrous on both surfaces; secondary nerves 4 to 12 pairs, tertiary venation not or scarcely visible on the dry material. Inflorescence 2–4 × 2–3 cm, dense, with few flowers; peduncle zero or reaching 3 mm, axes often marked with numerous scars of the pedicels. Flower: pedicel 1–4 mm; calyx with lobes 1.5–3 × 1–2.5 mm, glabrous, ciliate, on the inside and with 2 to 4 colleters situated basally in the middle; corolla white, slender in bud, head relatively ovoid and broad, 0.25 times the length of the bud and with apex obtuse or acute, glabrous on the outside, often ciliate on the lobes, partially hairy to pubescent on the inside, with tube 7–13 times as long as the calyx, 17–25 mm long, subcylindric, slightly enlarged around the anthers, 2–3 mm wide at the throat, twisted over 0.5 turn at the level of the anthers, to lobes inflexed, —times the length of the tube, —times as long as broad, 2–3 × 1–1.5 mm; stamens inserted at 0.15–0.22 of the length of the tube, anther sessile, 4–5 mm long; pistil 5–7 mm long, glabrous, with apex ± at mid-height of the anthers. Fruit: mericarps distinct, horizontal, not recurved, obliquely ovoid, often compressed laterally when dry, 35–40 × 15–20 × 15–20 mm, rounded at the base, yellow or orange, without spots, with 2 lateral ridges; exocarp 3 mm thick. Seed 9–10 × 4–5 × 3–3.5 mm, densely papillose and longitudinally striated. Distribution Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo at Gabon, Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué, Ngounié, Nyanga, Ogooué, Ivindo, Ogooué, Lolo, Ogooué, Maritim and Woleu, Ntem. Ecology: understory of forest in Gabon up to m altitude.

BIBLIOGRAPHY Tabernaemontana penduliflora K.Schum. Plate 117; Figure 13(E) Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 23 : 225 (1896). Shrub or small tree reaching 8(–12) m in height; bark with large lenticels; wood yellowish; branchlets compressed laterally (even when fresh), glabrous. Leaves in one pair, equal or unequal, the smallest up to times as long as the other and often broader; petiole 3–15 mm, ochreate, distinctly visible, limb broadly to narrowly elliptic, —times as long as broad, 5.5–12 × 2.5–5.5 cm, cuneate or sometimes rounded at the base or decurrent on the petiole, acuminate at the apex, subcoriaceous (even when fresh), glabrous on both surfaces; secondary nerves 6 to 15 pairs, rather straight and abruptly curved upward near the margin. Inflorescence pendulous, 2–3 × 2–3 cm, condensed, 1 or 2 times ramified, with few flowers; peduncle 1–2 mm. Flower fragrant; pedicel 2–5 mm; calyx with thick lobes, 4–5 × 3–4 mm, glabrous, ciliate, on the inside with 3 to 5 centro-basal colleters; corolla white to cream, slightly fleshy in bud, head subcylindric and subglobose, much broader, obtuse, 0.3–0.4 the length of the bud, glabrous or puberulent on the outside, partially puberulent on the inside, with lobes often puberulent in the lower part, with tube 2–3.2 times the length of the calyx, 10–13 × 3–4 mm, not angular, not reflexed, twisted over ±tour, contracted at the throat, to lobes almost ribbon-like, —times the length of the tube, —times as long as broad, 2–4 × 1–2 mm; stamens inserted at mid-height of the length of the tube, with apex reaching 1–4 mm below the throat, anther sessile, 5–7 mm long; pistil 6–7 mm long, glabrous. Fruit: mericarps separated, subglobose to transversely ellipsoidal, 4–6 × 5–8 × 4–6 cm, pale green to glaucous, with numerous seeds; exocarp 5–15 mm thick. Seed 10–15 × 4–7 × 3–5 mm, papillose and longitudinally striated. Distribution: Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Central African Republic, at Gabon, Ogooué, Ivindo, Ogooué, Lolo and Ogooué, Maritim. Ecology: understory of forest in Gabon up to m altitude, elsewhere up to m.
