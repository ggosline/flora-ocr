# *Inversodicraea gabonensis* (C.Cusset) Cheek Planche 11(1-3)

**Genus**: [[Inversodicraea]]
**Family**: [[Podostemaceae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull. 72(4) : 55, 2 (2016)

## Synonyms

- Ledermanniella gabonensis C.Cusset, Bull. Mus. natl. Hist. nat., sér. 4, Adansonia 5(4) : 368 (1983).

## Description

Plant with a basal portion thallose and foliaceus; stem erect, robust, ramified several times dichotomously, 2–3 cm in length, entirely covered by foliaceus scales, scattered towards the base, becoming progressively imbricate on the branches. Foliaceus scales at the base of the stem oval to broadly oval, ± 0.7 × 0.3–0.6 mm, entire to subentire, those of the branches oval, 0.8 × 0.3–0.4 mm, entire, with 1 to 3 lobes or irregularly lobulate. Leaves not observed. Inflorescences: spathellas solitary, arranged along the ultimate branches, ellipsoid, 2 × 0.8 mm, dehiscent at the apex; pedicel 3 mm after anthesis; tepals acicular, 0.6–0.7 mm; androecium longer than the ovary, stamens 1 or 2, filaments 3–4 mm, united for more than half their length, anthers 1.2 × 0.4 mm, pollen in monads; ovary 1.8–2 × 0.5–0.6 mm, gynophore 0.4–0.5 mm in length; stigmas linear, 0.3–0.4 mm. Seed 0.2 × 0.1 mm.

## Distribution

Gabon and Republic of the Congo; in Gabon rare and known from a single collection from Ngounié. Bi: Cheek et al. (2017), Cusset (1983), Sosef et al. (2006). Bibliography
