# *Inversodicraea gabonensis* (C.Cusset) Cheek Planche 11(1-3)

**Genus**: [[Inversodicraea]]
**Family**: [[Podostemaceae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull. 72(4) : 55, 2 (2016)

## Description

Kew Bull. 72(4): 55, 2 (2016). Ledermanniella gabonensis C.Cusset, Bull. Mus. natl. Hist. nat., sér. 4, Adansonia 5(4): 368 (1983). Plant with basal portion thallose-foliaceous; stem erect, robust, several times dichotomously branched, 2–3 cm long, entirely covered with foliaceous scales, scattered towards the base, becoming progressively imbricate on the branches. Foliaceous scales at the base of the stem oval to broadly oval, ± 0.7 × 0.3–0.6 mm, entire to subentire; those of the branches oval, 0.8 × 0.3–0.4 mm, entire, with 1 to 3 lobes or irregularly lobulate. Leaves not observed. Inflorescences: spathellas solitary, arranged along the ultimate branches, ellipsoid, 2 × 0.8 mm, dehiscent at the apex; pedicel 3 mm after anthesis; tepals acicular, 0.6–0.7 mm; androecium longer than the ovary, stamens 1 or 2, filaments 3–4 mm, fused for more than half their length, anthers 1.2 × 0.4 mm, pollen in monads; ovary 1.8–2 × 0.5–0.6 mm, gynophore 0.4–0.5 mm long; stigmas linear, 0.3–0.4 mm. Seed 0.2 × 0.1 mm.

## Distribution

Gabon and Republic of the Congo; in Gabon rare and known from a single collection from the Ngounié. Bi: Cheek et al. (2017), Cusset (1983), Sosef et al. (2006). Bibliography
