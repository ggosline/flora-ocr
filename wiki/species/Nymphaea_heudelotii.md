# *Nymphaea heudelotii* Planch. Planche 13

**Genus**: [[Nymphaea]]
**Family**: [[Nymphaeaceae]]
**Protologue**: Ann. Sci. Nat., Bot . sér. 3, 19 : 4 (1853)

## Description

, non Schumach. & Thonn. Delicate rhizomatous herb with scaly, often elongated rhizome, reaching ± 7 cm in length. Leaf with slender, glabrous petiole; lamina orbicular to broadly elliptic, 2–6(–8) × 1.7–6(–7) cm, not peltate, membranous to cartaceous, glabrous, light green to pale green on the upper surface, dark red, green spotted with violet or green on the lower surface, apex obtuse, base deeply cordate, lateral lobes acute, often very divergent but sometimes touching, margins entire; palmate venation weakly prominent on the lower surface, lateral veins 4–7, secondary veins 3–5(–6) on either side of the midvein. Flower (2.5–)4–5.5(–8) cm in diameter; pedicel very long, glabrous; sepals usually 4, slightly widened above their insertion point, oblong-lanceolate, 1–3(–4) × 0.3–1(–1.5) cm, glabrous, pale green often spotted with dark violet on the exterior, apex acute; petals 5–12(–14), oblong-lanceolate, blue-white, rose-lilac, rarely white, apex acute to obtuse; stamens 8–20(–30), connective variably prolonged; carpels 4–10. Fruit coarsely spherical, 1–1.5 cm in diameter. Seeds ovoid, 1.5 × 1 mm

## Distribution

from Senegal to Angola; in Gabon known from Nyanga (Moukalaba Doudou region) and Ogooué-Ivindo (Lope Reserve and Ivindo National Park)

## Ecology

lakes, riverbanks, swamp forests, savanna pools; in Gabon up to 200 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1850 m. B : Conard (1905), Hauman (1951), Verdcourt (1989b). i Bibliography
