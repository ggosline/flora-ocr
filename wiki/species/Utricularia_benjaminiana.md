# *Utricularia benjaminiana* Oliv. Planche 34, Figure 9(C–F)

**Genus**: [[Utricularia]]
**Family**: [[Lentibulariaceae]]
**Protologue**: J. Linn. Soc., Bot ., 4 : 176 (1860)

## Description

U. gilletii De Wild. & T.Durand, Bull. Soc. Roy. Bot. Belg. 38(2) : 40 (1900). U. villosula Stapf, in Dyer, Fl. trop. Afr. , 4(2) : 490 (1906). Aquatic herb; stolons filiform, reaching 50 cm in length or more, flexuous, ± densely pilose, with internodes 1–10 cm; rhizoids absent. Leaf with 1 or 2 primary divisions with oval outline, 1.5–4 cm in length, pinnatisect; rachis filiform or ± swollen especially in the lower half, pilose; pinnae alternate, the lower ones reaching 1.5 cm in length from the base of the rachis, several times bifurcate pseudodichotomously, with ultimate divisions capillary, sparsely setulose. Floats terminating short lateral branches, slightly swollen and pilose, whorled in groups of 4 to 9, fusiform or narrowly cylindric to ovoid or ellipsoid, 0.3–2.5 cm in length, bearing a few leaf-like segments in their distal half. Utricles few in number, often absent on certain leaves, inserted at the axil of the primary divisions of the leaves and subsequent dichotomies, long-stipitate, ovoid, 1–3 mm in length, pilose; orifice lateral, with upper lip provided with 2 simple or ± branched hairs, with lower lip naked or provided with 1 or several simple, short hairs. Inflorescence terminal on short branches of the stolons, arising at the center of the whorl of floats, erect, 3–25 cm in length, with 2 to 10 flowers, the upper ones crowded, the lower ones ± spaced, cleistogamous flowers often present at the base of the peduncle among the floats; peduncle filiform, straight or flexuous, smooth, glabrous; bracts basifixed, broadly triangular to oval, ± 1.5 mm in length. Flower: bracteoles absent; pedicel erect, capillary, 2–3 mm at anthesis, reaching 10 mm at maturity; calyx with orbicular, subequal lobes, ± 1 mm in length at anthesis, barely accrescent, membranous; corolla 10–15 mm in length, mauve or pale purple with a yellow patch on the palate or entirely white, upper lip oblong, 3–4 times as long as the upper lobe of the calyx, ± auriculate at the base, deeply divided into 2 parallel lobes, lower lip reniform, 7–10 mm wide, ± emarginate at the apex, palate provided with a narrow raised ridge, ± crenulate, spur cylindric or botuliforme, swollen, 2 to 3 times as long as the lower lip, 8–10 × 2.5–3.5 mm; corolla of cleistogamous flowers ± abortive. Fruit ellipsoid, 2.5–3.5 mm in length, circumscissile, with few seeds, sometimes only 2. Seed lenticular, ± 1 mm in diameter, with narrow and irregular wing; testa with indistinct, elongate cells.

## Distribution

Senegal, Guinea-Bissau, Mali, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Togo, Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Burundi, Uganda, Tanzania, Angola, Botswana, Namibia, South Africa (Natal), Madagascar, Central and South America (from Honduras to Brazil); in Gabon, observed in the Estuaire, the Moyen-Ogooué, the Ngounié, the Haut-Ogooué and the Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology

Still or very slow-flowing waters of temporary pools, ponds and savanna rivers; in Gabon, at low altitude (5-400 m), in West and East Africa, mainly between 0 and 1200 m

## Source note

The photograph in Vande weghe et al. (2016) shows a variation of Utricularia inflexa and not of U. benjaminiana. B: Taylor (1964, 1972, 1989), Vande weghe et al. (2016). Bibliography
