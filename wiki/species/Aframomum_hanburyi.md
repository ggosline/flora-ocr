# *Aframomum hanburyi* K. Schum.
**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Aframomum]]
## Description
Robust herb to 4 m; glabrous blades 20–35 × 4–7 cm and obliquely truncate 1 cm ligule. Basal simple spikes or short dense racemes have 5–6 flowers per branch. Flowers bright yellow; smooth fruit 6 cm.
## Source
Koechlin, J. (1964). Zingibéracées. *Flore du Gabon* 9: 58–59.
