# *Drypetes principum* (Müll.Arg.) Hutch.

**Genus**: [[Drypetes]]
**Family**: [[Putranjivaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. trop. Afr: 6(1): 684 (1912)

## Description

Plate 52(1, 2); Figure 11(A, B)

Tree up to ±20(−25) m tall; trunk up to ±30 cm in diameter, frequently twisted and knotty; bark smooth, ash-grey, with darker raised lenticels; cut surface with a sweet mustard oil odor; branchlets with fine and scattered hairs, glabrescent; buds sparsely pubérulent. Stipules caducous, triangular, (1.4–1.6–2(−2.9) mm long, pubérulent. Leaf: petiole (3.8–5.7–8.4(−11.5) mm, shortly hairy, glabrescent; blade elliptic-ovate or elliptic-obovate, (12.5–15–19(−23.5) × (4.5–6–7(−8.5) cm, papyraceous to subcoriaceous, oblique and acute or sometimes cuneate at the base, shortly acuminate at the apex, with margin subentire to sparsely and slightly crenate or serrulate, mainly in the distal half, plane to slightly recurved, frequently undulate, bright green above, paler and very sparsely and shortly hairy below, glabrescent; venation weakly reticulate, midvein glabrescent to shortly hairy, secondary veins 5 to 9, slightly impressed above, ascending, looping well before the margin. Male inflorescence in the axils of leaves and on leafless branches, with up to ±10 flowers; bracts broadly ovate to ovate, (0.4–0.6–0.9(−1.1) mm long, pubérulent. Male flower: pedicel slender, (2–2.8–4.7(−5.2) mm, with minute hairs; sepals 4, imbricate, suborbicular, (2.6–2.9–3.1(−3.8) × (2.3–2.8–3.8(−4.3) mm, slightly cucullate at the apex, yellowish to cream, densely pubérulent on both faces, with minute marginal cilia; stamens 8 to 12, in a whorl around the margin of the disk, filament (1.9–2.2–3.5(−5.2) mm, anther 0.1–1.3(−1.4) mm long, glabrous; disk plane to slightly convex, (1.5–1.7–2.7(−3.2) mm in diameter, rugose, with undulate margin, exceeding between the filaments, pubérulent, the central part often in a tuft of hairs. Female inflorescence like the male; bracts like those of the male. Female flower: pedicel (0.7–1.6–2.1(−2.7) mm, pubérulent; sepals 4, (3–3.2–4.3(−5.7) × (3.5–3.7–4.7(−6) mm, greenish to yellowish, the rest like those of the male; disk 3.2–3.5(−3.7) mm in diameter, with subentire margin, sparsely pubérulent; ovary subglobose to ellipsoid, 2-locular, densely hairy; style (0.5–1.4–2(−2.2) mm, with 2 short branches, partially fused, with 1 or 2 fan-shaped stigmas, 2.1(−2.7) × (1.9–2.2–3.2(−4.3) mm, with eroded margin. Fruit subglobose to ellipsoid, (11.5–14–16.5(−23.5) × (8.5–11–14(−17) mm, brownish or orange at maturity, with persistent sepals and stigmas, with short and dense hairs (two obvious types: minute brownish and others longer, scattered, whitish), with (1 or)2 seeds; pedicel (0.8–)2.5–4.4(−8.1) mm long, shortly hairy, glabrescent. Seed (7–)9–12(−13) mm long.

## Distribution

Guinea, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Ivory Coast, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, São Tomé and Príncipe and Gabon; in Gabon: Ogooué-Ivindo

Habitat: primary or secondary evergreen forests, riparian forests, swamps; in Gabon at 750 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1600 m.

## Source note

D. principum has been very rarely collected in Gabon (Ivindo National Park). It shares with other species, such as D. bipindensis and D. fallax, the combination: flowers arranged either in the axils of leaves or in the axils of leafless axes, four sepals, more than five stamens and fruits with two remarkable types of hairs.
