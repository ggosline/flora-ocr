# *Polystachya rhodoptera* Rchb.f.

**Genus**: [[Polystachya]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Hamb. Gartzg. 15 : 214 (1858)

## Description

Stems 50 × 0.5 cm. Leaves 5–20 × 0.3–2.5 cm, lanceolate to oblong.

Inflorescence < 10.5 cm, 8–40-flowered. Flowers white or yellow, often tinged with pink. Dorsal sepal oblong-ovate, apiculate. Petals oblong-oblanceolate, acute. Lateral sepals obliquely ovate-triangular, apiculate. Lip distinctly three-lobed just above the base, glabrous, with a thick oblong callus, glabrous, in the basal third; median lobe obovate-obtriangular, wider at the truncate apex, with a short deflexed apicule; lateral lobes linear to oblong, subobtuse, crossed above the median lobe. Spur conical. - Pl. 118, p. 269.

## Distribution

Sierra Leone, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Nigeria, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Príncipe, São Tomé, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Zaïre

## Ecology

alt. 470–980 m. Epiphytic in dense forest, on shaded branch over water, lithophytic on rocky slope between riverbank and mangrove
