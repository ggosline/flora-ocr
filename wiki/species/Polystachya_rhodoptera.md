# *Polystachya rhodoptera* Rchb.f.

**Genus**: [[Polystachya]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Hamb. Gartzg. 15 : 214 (1858)

## Synonyms

- Polystachya wahisiana De Wild., Belg. Colon. 1 : 183 (1904).

## Description

Stems 50 x 0.5 cm. Leaves 5-20 x 0.3-2.5 cm, lanceolate to oblong.

Inflorescence < 10.5 cm, 8-40-flowered. Flowers white or yellow, often tinged with pink. Dorsal sepal oblong-ovate, apiculate. Petals oblong-oblanceolate, acute. Lateral sepals obliquely ovate-triangular, apiculate. Lip distinctly trilobed just above the base, glabrous, with a thick oblong callus, glabrous, in the basal third; median lobe obovate-obtriangular, wider at the truncate apex, with a short deflexed apicule; lateral lobes linear to oblong, subobtuse, crossed above the median lobe. Spur conical. - Pl. 118, p. 269.

## Distribution

Sierra Leone, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Nigeria, Cameroun, République centrafricaine, Principe, Sao Tomé, Guinée équatoriale, Gabon, Zaïre

## Ecology

alt. 470-980 m. Epiphyte in dense forest, on shaded branch over water, lithophyte on rocky slope between the shore and mangrove
