# *Utricularia capillacea* Willd. Planche 35, Figure 10(D)

**Genus**: [[Utricularia]]
**Family**: [[Lentibulariaceae]]
**Protologue**: Sp. pl. , éd. 4, 1(1) : 113 (1797)

## Description

Sp. pl., ed. 4, 1(1): 113 (1797). U. scandens Benj., Linnaea 20: 309 (1847). U. scandens Benj. subsp. schweinfurthii (Stapf) P.Taylor, Taxon 12: 294 (1963).

Pocock 925; 7: Matton 2; 8-11, 13-15: Deighton 5973). Drawing by P. Taylor, Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew (©), reproduced with permission from Taylor (1989).

Terrestrial herb; rhizoids and capillary stolons, few at the base of the flowering stem. Leaves few, opposite on branches on the stolons, linear, reaching 10 × 1 mm, 1-nerved. Utricles scattered on the stolons and leaves, shortly stipitate, spherical, 0.6–1 mm in diameter, glandular; basal orifice, with upper lip bearing 2 simple and subulate appendages, with lower lip furnished with a single shorter appendage truncate or shortly bifid. Inflorescence erect or twining, 3–35 cm long, with 1 to 8 flowers spaced apart, alternating with sterile bracts; peduncle filiform, glabrous; bracts basifixed, broadly ovate-deltoid, 1–1.5 mm long, with acute or acuminate apex, without veins, at least 4 times as wide as the bracteoles. Flower: bracteoles very narrowly elliptic-ovate, ± 1 mm long; flowering pedicel up to 3 times as long as the calyx, narrowly winged; calyx with broadly ovate lobes, decurrent, 2.5–3 mm long at anthesis, reaching 5 mm long at maturity, slightly unequal, the upper longer, acute to acuminate at the apex, the lower shortly 2- or 3-dentate; corolla reaching 15 mm long, yellow, with upper lip as long or longer and wider than the upper calyx lobe, oblong, ± emarginate at the apex, lower lip orbicular, rounded and entire or 2 or 3-crenate at the apex, palate prominent and usually with 2 to 4 humps, spur subulate, acute, ± curved. Fruit oblong-ovoid, compressed dorsiventrally, 2–2.5 mm long, dehiscent by a longitudinal abaxial slit with thickened margins. Seed ovoid, ± 0.2 mm long, smooth, with prominent hilum; testa with distinct cells, elongate, much longer than wide.

## Distribution

Guinea, Ivory Coast, Nigeria, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ethiopia, Tanzania, Mozambique, Malawi, Zimbabwe, Zambia, Angola, South Africa (Transvaal), Madagascar, India, Sri Lanka, Thailand, Vietnam, China, Malaysia, New Guinea; in Gabon, observed in the Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué and Ogooué-Ivindo

## Ecology

swampy savannas, humid rocks in forest; in Gabon, at 310–540 m altitude, in West and East Africa, mostly between 0 and 2300 m. B: Fleischmann (2012a), Taylor (1964, 1972, 1989). Bibliography
