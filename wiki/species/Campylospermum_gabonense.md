# *Campylospermum gabonense* Biss.

**Genus**: [[Campylospermum]]
**Family**: [[Ochnaceae]]
**Protologue**: Blumea 58 : 4 (2013) (comme “ gabonensis ”)

## Description

(as "gabonensis"). subsp. gabonense Tree or shrub reaching 7 m in height; branches with brownish bark. Stipules persistent, narrowly triangular, 7–15 mm long. Leaf: petiole 3–7 mm, channelled above; lamina narrowly elliptic to narrowly elliptic-obovate, 16–30(–35) × 6–10(–11) cm, ratio 2.7–4.5, cuneate to attenuate at base, apex acuminate or sometimes acute, coriaceous to strongly coriaceous, flat or rarely bullate above, margin serrulate or sometimes entire toward base, rarely entirely entire; primary vein flat on upper surface, prominent on lower surface, lateral veins in 14 to 27 pairs, prominent on both surfaces, tertiary venation scalariform perpendicular to primary vein, indistinct above, slightly distinct below. Inflorescence terminal, non-ramified, ascending, compact, main axis 3–13 cm; peduncle robust; gemmate scales persistent; cymules distant 3–5(–10) mm apart, 4- to 8-flowered; bracts persistent, triangular, 2–3 mm long. Flower: pedicel 5–15 mm, articulate 3–9 mm from base; sepals ovate, 7–9 × 2–3 mm in flower, 9–10 × 3–4 mm in fruit; petals obovate, 10–15 × 5–9 mm, cuneate at base, rounded at apex; stamens: anthers 5–7 mm long; style 6–7 mm. Fruit: receptacle 3–4 mm wide; drupels 1 or 2 well developed per receptacle, ellipsoid, approximately 7 × 5 mm; cotyledons incumbent, unequal with small external cotyledon.
