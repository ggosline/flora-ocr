# *Andropogon patris* Robyns.

**Genus**: [[Andropogon]]
**Family**: [[Gramineae]]

## Synonyms

- A. aureus Vanderyst.
- A. perligulatus Chiov. nec Stapf.
- A. gayanus De Wild. nec Kunth.

## Description

Robyns, Bull. Jard. Bot. Etat, Brux. VII : 225 (1922-31). — Fl. Agrost. C.B. et R.U., I : 137 (1929). — Chiov., Nuov. Giorn. Bot. Ital. 26 : 74 (1919).

Perennial grass, caespitose, up to 1.5 m tall. Leaf sheaths provided with auricles 3 to 6 mm long, fused to the ligule. Blades linear, 15 to 30 cm long and 3 to 12 mm wide, more or less pubescent.

Panicle about forty centimetres long, with few racemes, $ 3\ \text{to}\ 8\ \text{cm} $ long. Articles and pedicels of $ 2.5\ \text{mm} $, ciliate on the margins. Sessile spikelets flattened laterally, $ 5\ \text{mm} $ long, with lower glume truncate, deeply depressed on the back. Upper glume $ 2\ \text{to}\ 3\ \text{mm} $ long, shortly subulate. Fertile lemma hyaline, bifid to the middle, ciliate; geniculate awn $ 1.5\ \text{to}\ 2\ \text{cm} $ long.

Pedicellate spikelets lanceolate, 4 to 5 mm long, more or less tinged with red, male or neuter. Lower glume acuminate, with 7 or 9 nerves, ciliate on the margins; upper glume membranous, acuminate, somewhat shorter. Lemmas acuminate, glabrous.

This species was previously reported from the Belgian Congo, in the Kassai and the Lake Moero region where it occurs on swampy sandy soils.

It was found by J. Koechlin in Gabon, near Libreville, in the savannas of Ovendo where it forms stands in certain low-lying areas.

It is a forage grass of average value, usable only in the young state.
