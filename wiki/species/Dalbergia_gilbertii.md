# *Dalbergia gilbertii* Cronquist Planche 38

**Genus**: [[Dalbergia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Jard. Bot. État 22 : 216 (1952)

## Description

Woody liane reaching 5 cm in diameter, inodorous, provided with long straight spines (1.8–8 cm in length) on the trunk and old branches, sometimes also provided with hooked prehensile branchlets; bark dark brown with small pale lenticels; young branches with sparse and early caducous strigillose pubescence. Stipules very early caducous, linear, ± 3 × 0.2 mm, with sparse strigillous hairs. Leaf imparipinnate; petiole 1.5–3.4 cm; rachis 2.3–8 cm, with short and sparse strigillose pubescence; leaflets 4 to 9, alternate and distinctly unequal, with petiolule of 1–4 mm, lamina broadly elliptic, symmetric, 3.8–8 × 1.8–4.5 cm in the upper leaflets and 1.6–4.4 × 1.3–3 cm in the lower ones, acute or rounded at the base, rounded or obtuse at the apex (sometimes shortly acuminate in young leaves), thin, olive green to blackish and almost concolorous when dry, sparsely hairy, the upper surface with long hairs of ± 1 mm directed in all directions and rather rapidly caducous, the lower surface with shorter appressed hairs (0.3–0.5 mm) and persistent; lateral nerves 6 to 10, scarcely prominent and barely more raised than the veinlets, veinlets in apparent dark network on the lower surface, with meshes of ± 0.5 mm. Inflorescence in a short and rather lax panicle, axillary or on old branches, 2–7 cm in length, with short and appressed strigillous hairs; bracts caducous, narrowly lanceolate, ± 1.2 × 0.2 mm, pubescent on the outside. Flower: pedicel 1–2.5 cm; bracteoles caducous, linear, ± 1 × 0.1 mm, pubescent on the outside; calyx pale green, with very short and sparse appressed hairs, tube 2.3–3.3 × 2–3 mm, teeth imbricate and slightly unequal, the upper ones rounded and almost entirely connate, 0.8–1 mm long, the lateral ones acute or obtuse, 0.8–1 mm, the lower one acute, 1–1.5 mm; corolla white, petals with claw shorter than the calyx, standard slightly and very obliquely bent, with claw of 1.5–2 mm and limb suborbicular to elliptic of 2.5–3.3 × 2.5 mm, with shortly auriculate base, wings nearly equaling the keel, with claw of 1.7–2 mm and limb broadly obovate of 2.5–2.8 × 1.8 mm, rounded or truncate at the base, transversely wrinkled toward the base on the outer surface and provided with a strong internal thickening at the upper margin, keel weakly curved, with claw of 1.8–2 mm and limb of 2.8–3.2 × 1.7 mm, with shortly auriculate base and provided with a pocket at the upper margin; stamens 10, monadelphous, filaments ± 4 mm, connate for ¾ of their length, straight at the base and curved in their free part; ovary 1.5–2.3 × 0.5–0.7 mm, pubescent at least on the margins, with 2 ovules and curved stipe of 2.5–3 mm; style linear, 1–1.5 mm, with punctiform stigma. Pod with pedicel of 2–3 mm and stipe of 5–6 mm, papyraceous, flattened and narrowly ellipsoid, 6–10 × 1.3–1.8 cm, acute at the base and rounded at the apex, with plane or slightly concave reticulation and without differentiated zone around the seeds, glabrous and slightly glaucous, with 1 or 2 seeds. Seed reniform, flattened, 8 × 4.5 mm.

## Distribution

Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo (Mayombe); in Gabon, reported only from Ogooué-Ivindo, in the vicinity of Koumaméyong and Makokou where it is locally common

## Ecology

old secondary forest; in Gabon around 475 m elevation. Flowers in March and April

## Source note

This species is reported here for the first time from Gabon. It is close to D. hostilis but differs from it by the fewer and generally larger leaflets, larger flowers, petals with much longer claw, androecium with 10 stamens instead of 9, the elongate linear style, and glaucous pods (Lachenaud & Maesen 2016). b: Cronquist (1954), Lachenaud & Maesen (2016).
