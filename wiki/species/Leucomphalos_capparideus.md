# *Leucomphalos capparideus* Benth. ex Planch. Planche 80

**Genus**: [[Leucomphalos]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Hooker’s Icon. Pl. : t.784 (1848)

## Description

Medium-sized liana, or sometimes small tree (?); branchlets glabrous, sometimes with scattered hairs when young. Stipules early caducous, triangular, 0.5–1.5 mm in length. Leaf: petiole (0.3–)0.5–6(–11) cm, ± glabrous; leaflet with blade oval-elliptic to oblong, (5–)9–20(–29) × (1.5–)3–7(–12) cm, rounded to obtuse at base, (0.3–)0.5–2.5(–3.5) cm, acuminate at apex, glabrous, ± papyraceous; lateral nerves in (5–)6 to 9(–13) pairs. Inflorescence axillary or terminal, in panicle or raceme, with short appressed hairs; bracts and bracteoles broadly oval-triangular, 0.5–1 mm in length. Flower: pedicel 5–12 mm, ± glabrous; calyx opening by two longitudinal slits, glabrous; corolla with subequal petals, standard 7–10.5 × 4–7 mm, with claw of 0–1 mm, the other petals lanceolate-elliptic, 8–11 × 2.5–4 mm, with claw of 1 mm; stamens 11(–12), anthers 4–6 mm in length, longer than the filaments; pistil 6–7 mm in length, ovary stipitate, 2–3 mm in length, with 8 to 13 ovules, sparsely hairy. Pod subellipsoid, 2–2.5 × 1.2–2.5 × 1–1.8 cm, with 1(–4) seeds, glabrous, scarcely nerved, ± pink at maturity. Seed subellipsoid, 14–20 × 10–13 × 7–9 mm, red and black; strophiole white.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea and Gabon; in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué, Ngounié, Ogooué-Ivindo and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

primary and secondarized forest; in Gabon at 15–760 m altitude, elsewhere up to 880 m. b: Breteler (1994a), Pellegrin (1949). ibliography
