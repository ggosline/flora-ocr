# *Panicum phragmitoides* Stapf. ex A. Chev.

**Genus**: [[Panicum]]
**Family**: [[Gramineae]]
**Protologue**: STAPF, F.T.A. IX : 677 (1920), ex CHEVAL. Sudania : 74-76 (1911)

## Synonyms

- P. coloratum Dur. et De Wild.
- P. trypheron v. giganteum Rendle.

## Description

, ex CHEVAL. Sudania : 74-76 (1911). — DUR. et DE WILD. Mat. Fl. Congo, I : 44 (1924). — RENDLE, Cat. Afr. Pl. Welw. II : 181 (1899).

Perennial herb, in robust tufts, reaching 2 meters in height and more. Leaves with very long sheaths, generally glabrous, ciliate ligule, linear blades, averaging 50 × 1.5 cm.

Large panicle divided to the 5th or 6th degree, lower primary branches more or less whorled. Spikelets often tinged with purple. Glumes acuminate, the lower making 2/3 of the spikelet, with 5 nerves, the upper as long as the spikelet, with 7 nerves. Lower flower male, lemma with 9 nerves, contrasting with the glumes by its pale tint, palea of equal length.

Common herb throughout humid or semi-humid tropical Africa. Species of fallows or savanna, providing mediocre forage and little favored by cattle. Often highly invasive in degraded pastures.

Recorded from Gabon by Koechlin in the savannas of the south of the country.
