# *Neuropeltis acuminata* (P.Beauv.) Benth. Planche 23

**Genus**: [[Neuropeltis]]
**Family**: [[Convolvulaceae]]
**Protologue**: Porana acuminata P.Beauv., Fl. Oware 1 : 66, tab. 39 (1805)

## Description

in Hook., Niger Fl. : 469 (1849). Porana acuminata P.Beauv., Fl. Oware 1 : 66, tab. 39 (1805). Large liana or sometimes shrub, up to 40 m in length and reaching at least 15 cm in diameter, with numerous medullary rays, white, rapidly turning brown; branchlets pubescent to puberulent, glabrescing or not. Leaf: petiole 5–15(–25) mm, narrowly canaliculate above, ± pubescent to puberulent; lamina elliptic or sometimes ovate, (3–)5–10(–12) × (2–)3–6 cm, cuneate to rounded at the base, acute to acuminate over 5–15 mm and mucronate over 1–3 mm at the apex, with fine hairs, appressed on both surfaces, early glabrescing especially above, 7–10 pairs of lateral nerves. Inflorescence with brown pubescence; bracts ± as long as the calyx and with same indumentum; bracteoles absent or very poorly developed. Flower: pedicel 1.5–2(–4) mm, hairy; calyx 1.5–3 mm in length, with brown pubescence; corolla 3–8 mm in length, glabrous inside, sparsely pubescent on the lobes refracted outside; stamens as long as or longer than the corolla, 4–9 mm in length, glabrous, anthers 1–1.5(–2) mm in length; pistil (3–)4–7.5 mm in length, ovary pubescent; style glabrous or sparsely pubescent. Fruit ovoid, 7–8 × 5 mm, sparsely hairy, at least at the apex, rarely glabrous; bract enlarged, papyraceous, suborbicular, 4–6 cm in diameter, cordate at the base and acute to emarginate at the apex. Seeds 1(–2), black, finely pustulate.

## Distribution

Sierra Leone, Liberia, Ivory Coast, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Angola (Cabinda); in Gabon, known from all provinces except Estuaire and Haut-Ogooué

## Ecology

primary and secondarised forest; in Gabon, up to 650 m altitude, elsewhere, up to 1500 m

## Uses

In Gabon, the leaves are eaten as a vegetable. Taxonomic note: Flowering collections of this species appear with corollas of (5–)6–8 mm in length and others with smaller corollas, of 3–4(–5) mm in length, often accompanied by narrower leaves. This phenomenon is present throughout its range of distribution. Study of this variation has not led to the discovery of an associated character. There is therefore no reason, at least for now, to formally recognize it as a taxon. B: Breteler (2010), Burkill (1985), Heine (1963), Lejoly & Lisowski (1984). Bibliography
