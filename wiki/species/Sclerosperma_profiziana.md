# *Sclerosperma profiziana* Valk. & Sunderl. Planche 25

**Genus**: [[Sclerosperma]]
**Family**: [[Arecaceae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull. 63(1) : 82 (2008)

## Description

Kew Bull. 63(1) : 82 (2008). S. gilletii Profizi in Harley & J.Dransf., Grana 42 : 3, 6 (2003), nom. inval. S. mabondo De Wild. in Renier, Fl. Kwango 1 : 83 (1948), nom. inval. Acaulescent palm or with stem up to 2 m. Leaf entire; sheath up to 40 cm long; petiole 0.6–1(–4) m; rachis 1.5–2(–4.5) m; blade narrowly obovate, 20–60(–140) cm long, cuneate at base, deeply bifid at apex where the rachis continues into a fine fibre, upper surface dark green, lower surface glaucous with small dark scales along the veins. Inflorescence: peduncle up to 15 cm, elliptic in cross-section, densely tomentose; prophyll up to 23 cm long; peduncular bract up to 45 cm long; rachis up to 25 cm at anthesis. Male flower: sepals 5 × 6 mm; petals obovate to elliptic, 5–8 × 11–15 mm; ± 100 stamens. Fruits up to 30 per infructescence, on a rachis 6–10 cm long, globose to obovoid, 3–3.5 × 3.8–4 cm. Seed globose to obovoid, 3 × 3.5 cm.

## Distribution

Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, a single collection from southern Nyanga (Ndindi). Conservation status: Least Concern (LC)

## Ecology

in periodically flooded forest zones, swampy areas or along rivers; in Gabon, at low altitude, elsewhere up to 480 m. B: Bourobou Bourobou et al. (2016), van Valkenburg et al. (2007, 2008). Bibliography
