# *Bulbophyllum fuerstenbergianum* (De Wild.) De Wild.

**Genus**: [[Bulbophyllum]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Pl. Bequaert. 1 : 86 (1921) ; FTWA : 239 (1968) ; FCAM 2 : 417 (2001)

## Description

– TYPE: Fuerstenberg s.n., s.loc., Afr. occidentale, holo-BR, iso-K!.

– Bulbophyllum scaberulum (Rolfe) Bolus var. fuerstenbergianum (De Wild.) J. J. Verm., Bull. J. Bot. Nat. Belg. 56: 234 (1986).

Pseudobulbs 1.8–9 × 0.8–3.6 cm, generally narrowly ovoid to ellipsoid, 2-leaved. Leaves petiolate, 3–28 × 7–6 cm, elliptic, oblong-lanceolate to linear, acute to obtuse. Inflorescence lax, 6–55 cm, <100-flowered, rhachis limbate. Flowers small, glabrous, yellowish to greenish tinged with purple. Sepals acute: the dorsal one narrowly oblong to broadly lanceolate with very often involute margins. Petals obliquely oblong to broadly lanceolate, apex acute, not thickened, the lateral ones obliquely elliptic-triangular. Lower part of lip transversely elliptic, apical region oblong to ligulate, obtuse, recurved, fairly thick in the centre. - Pl. 75, p. 154.

Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Congo (Kinshasa)?

Epiphyte in lowland and montane forest
