# *Xyris imitatrix* Malme

**Genus**: [[Xyris]]
**Family**: [[Xyridaceae]]
**Protologue**: Ark. Bot. 22A, 4 : 2 (1928)

## Description

Perennial herb in spreading tufts. Leaves strongly flabellate; sheath ⅓–¼ of limb length; limb linear, 10–30 cm × 4–5 mm, falcate-incurved at acute apex, striate when dry, smooth or obscurely papillose, glabrous on both surfaces, margins furnished with long and flexuous hairs. Inflorescence: peduncle 30–65 cm × 1 mm, fistulose, bearing 6–8 longitudinal ridges, brownish and glossy towards the base; spike pauciflorous, obovoid or ellipsoid, 8–10(–12) × 4–5(–6) mm; inflorescence bracts entire, without keel, rounded at apex, the lower ones 3–4 mm long, ovate, furnished with a well-marked dorsal spot; the median ones obovate to suborbicular, 5–6 × 4–5 mm, convex, reddish-brown, semi-opaque, dorsal spot 2–2.5 mm long, ovate, greyish-green. Flower: lateral sepals approximately 3 × 0.5 mm, free, subhyaline, narrowly ovate, acute; keel with narrow wing, sparsely lacerate-ciliate; corolla yellow, 3–4.5 mm long, lobes ovate, irregularly denticulate at apex; stamens 2.2–3.5 mm long; style 1.5–2.2 mm long, trifid. Fruit narrowly ellipsoid, approximately 3.5 mm long; pericarp very thin, whitish. Seed oblong-cylindric, approximately 1 mm long.

## Distribution

Gabon, Congo (Brazzaville), Democratic Republic of the Congo, Angola and Zambia; in Gabon in Estuaire, Loango National Park and the Lambaréné region

## Ecology

swampy clearings in dense humid forests (ésobés, baïs), also in marshes and humid grasslands; in Gabon at 0–100 m altitude, elsewhere in Africa, reaching 1300 m. Bibliography: Lock (2010).
