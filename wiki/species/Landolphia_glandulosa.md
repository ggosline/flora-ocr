# *Landolphia glandulosa* (Pellegr.) Pichon Planche 53(8)

**Genus**: [[Landolphia]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Mém. Inst. Franç. Afrique Noire 35 : 170 (1953)

## Synonyms

- Carpodinus glandulosa Pellegr., Bull. Mus. Natl. Hist. Nat. , sér.1, 31 : 465 (1925).

## Description

Liane ; branches lenticellate, glabrous. Leaf glabrous ; petiole 3–7 mm ; limb obovate, acute at base, acute or shortly acuminate at apex ; midvein impressed above, secondary veins 4 to 7 pairs, curved, tertiary venation not apparent. Inflorescence axillary with ± flowers. Flower : pedicel 1–2 mm ; bracteoles 2–3 × 2–4 mm, villous outside ; calyx with 4 lobes ± mm, slightly keeled, distinctly imbricate, villous outside, with colleters inside at base ; corolla cream, tube ± 11 mm long, glabrous outside, glabrescent inside, throat glabrous, with lobes 10–11 × 3 mm, ciliate, otherwise glabrous ; stamens inserted toward apex of tube, included, anther 2 mm long ; ovary villous ; style 7 mm. Fruit ellipsoid ± cm on the axis, white to several-seeded, yellowish pulp. Distribution endemic to Gabon, known only from three specimens from Estuaire and Nyanga. Ecology probably in evergreen lowland forests but no precise ecological information is available on the specimens. Notes. The flower description is principally based on the work of Pellegrin. Inflorescence axillary, sometimes also terminal, 1–7 cm long, with short but well-visible branches, with ± flowers ; peduncle ± mm, glabrous or sparsely pubescent except occasionally in the form of a tendril and much longer, bracts sepal-like.
