# *Landolphia glandulosa* (Pellegr.) Pichon Planche 53(8)

**Genus**: [[Landolphia]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Mém. Inst. Franç. Afrique Noire 35 : 170 (1953)

## Description

Liane; branches lenticellate, glabrous. Leaf glabrous; petiole 3–7 mm; blade obovate, acute at base, acute or shortly acuminate at apex; midvein impressed above, secondary veins 4 to 7 pairs, curved, tertiary venation not apparent. Inflorescence axillary with flowers. Flower: pedicel 1–2 mm; bracteoles 2–3 × 2–4 mm, villous on the outside; calyx with 4 lobes, clearly keeled, distinctly imbricate, villous on the outside, ciliate, with colleters inside at the base; corolla cream, tube ± 11 mm long, glabrous on the outside, glabrescent on the inside, throat glabrous, with lobes 10–11 × 3 mm, ciliate, otherwise glabrous; stamens inserted near the apex of the tube, included, anther 2 mm long; ovary villous; style 7 mm. Fruit ellipsoid, ± cm on the dry fruit, whitish, to several seeds, yellowish pulp. Distribution endemic to Gabon, known only from three specimens from the Estuary and the Nyanga. Ecology probably in evergreen lowland forests but no precise ecological information is available on the specimens. Notes: The flower description is principally based on the work of Pellegrin.
