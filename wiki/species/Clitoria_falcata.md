# *Clitoria falcata* Lam. Planche 25.1–3

**Genus**: [[Clitoria]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Encycl. 2(1) : 51 (1786)

## Description

Encycl. 2(1) : 51 (1786). C. rubiginosa Juss. ex Pers., Syn. pl. 2(2) : 303 (1807). Climbing herb, perennial; stems slender, glabrous to pubescent. Stipules lanceolate to ovate, 3–5 mm long. Leaf: petiole 1.5–4.5 cm, slightly pubescent; rachis 0.6–1.8 cm; stipels lanceolate, 3–5 mm long, striate; leaflets 3, with petiolule 1–2.5 mm, blade ovate-elliptic, 1.8–7.5 × 0.8–3.5 cm, rounded at base, apex obtuse, glabrous above, slightly to densely pubescent and pale green below; secondary veins in 5 pairs. Inflorescence with 1 to 4 flowers on a peduncle 4–15 cm; bracts 1–2 mm long. Flower sometimes cleistogamous: bracteoles at base of calyx, ovate-attenuate, 4–12(–17) × 2–5 mm; calyx with tube 3–4 mm long, teeth oblong-triangular, 4–5 mm long; corolla white, marked with purple, standard 3–4(–5) cm long; stamens with staminal column 20–27 mm, anthers 1–1.2 mm long; ovary shortly hairy. Pod stipitate, oblong-elliptic, inflated, ± straight, 3–4 × 0.8–1.1 cm, with aristate apex, puberulent, with 4 to 8 seeds, with 2 longitudinal ribs. Seed ellipsoid to subglobose, 3–4(–4.5) × 2–3(–4) mm, reddish-brown to greyish-green.

## Distribution

Native to tropical America, introduced elsewhere in tropical regions; in Africa, West and East; in Gabon, known only from Ogooué-Ivindo

## Ecology

Thickets on sandy soils, swampy places; in Gabon at 200 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1200 m

## Source note

Recorded only once in Gabon. b : Fantz (1980), Verdcourt (1971c). Bibliography
