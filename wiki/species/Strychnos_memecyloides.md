# *Strychnos memecyloides* S. Moore

**Genus**: [[Strychnos]]
**Family**: [[Loganiaceae]]

## Description

in Cat. Talbot's Nig. Pl. : 69 (1913). — FWTA ed. 1, 2 : 24, p. p., excl. syn. S. talbotiae S. Moore, Chevalier 17994 et Talbot 2077. — non A. CHEVALIER, Expl. Bot. Afr. Occ. Fr. 1 : 443 (1920).

— S. memecyloides var. effusior S. Moore, l. c. : 70.

Liane reaching a length of 10-40 m and a height of 5-20 m or more; stem 3-10 cm in diameter; bark pale brown, with large lenticels, thin; wood pale ochraceous; branchlets non-lenticellate; small branchlets brown-pubescent, scarcely or not sulcate when dry; tendrils in pairs.

Leaves with short petiole, brown-pubescent, 2-3 mm long; blade coriaceous or, especially in shade, papyraceous, elliptic, narrowly elliptic or narrowly ovate, (7.5-) 12-22 × 3.5-10 cm or sometimes, for a few leaves, smaller, distinctly acuminate at the apex, cuneate or rounded at the base, glabrous above, with sparse pubescence beneath the main nerves; one pair of secondary nerves curved along the margin; midvein and main secondary nerves, at least at the base, impressed above; tertiary venation reticulate, prominent beneath.

Inflorescences axillary or terminal, or both at once, contracted or lax, short, 1.5-8 × 1.5-3 cm; infructescences often distinctly longer, reaching up to 9 × 4 cm, multiflorous; peduncle, branches and pedicels very pale green, brown-pubescent. Flowers 5-merous. Sepals very pale green, connate at the base, ovate to suborbicular, 1.2-2 × 1.2-1.5 mm, obtuse or rounded, finely ciliate, glabrous or with sparse pubescence on the outside, glabrous or with appressed pubescence at the base on the inside. Corolla, in mature bud, 3-4 mm long, very pale green and with sparse to glabrous pubescence on the outside, white and with a pilous ring at the throat on the inside; tube 1.2-1.5 mm long; lobes 1.6-1.7 times longer than the tube, narrowly triangular, 2-2.5 × 0.9-1.2 mm, acute, spreading. Stamens scarcely exserted, inserted at the throat of the corolla, with short filament; anthers ovate or nearly so, barbate with some pilose hairs at the base. Pistil pilose, 2-2.2 mm long; ovary completely glabrous or only at the base, broadly ovoid, 1-1.2 × 0.8-1.2 mm, 2-locular; style pilose, 1-1.2 mm long; stigma capitate; 10-12 ovules per locule.

Fruits rather small, ellipsoid, laterally compressed, 2-4 × 1.8-3 × 1.5-2.7 cm, containing 1-2 seeds; wall thin. Seed flattened, ± plano-convex, obliquely elliptic, 22-23 × 14-16 × 5-6 mm, with very short and dense pubescence, rather smooth. Pl. 34, 6-10, p. 141.
