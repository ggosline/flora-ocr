# *Ledermanniella sanagaensis* C.Cusset Planche 19(5–7)

**Genus**: [[Ledermanniella]]
**Family**: [[Podostemaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Mus. natl. Hist. nat., sér. 4, Adansonia 6(3) : 256 (1984)

## Description

Bull. Mus. natl. Hist. nat., sér. 4, Adansonia 6(3) : 256 (1984). Plant with a basal thalloid foliaceous part, bearing acaulescent shoots or stems well developed, 0.5–3 cm in length. Stipules present. Leaves distichous, imbricate, simple, with an amplexicaul base, linear, 2–4 mm, with an acute apex, with the blade shorter than the expanded flower. Inflorescence with terminal flowers, solitary or grouped by 2; spathe ovoid. Flower: pedicel ± 3.5 mm before anthesis; tepals 0.4 mm in length; stamens 2(–3), filaments connate, anther 1 × 0.6 mm, pollen in monads; ovary 2 × 1 mm, gynophore 0.4 mm in length; stigmas linear, 0.2 mm before anthesis. Fruit with caducous valves.

## Distribution

Cameroon (Sanaga River) and Gabon; in Gabon, recently collected for the first time on the Mbé River (Cristal Mountains, in the Estuaire). B: Cusset (1984, 1987a). Bibliography
