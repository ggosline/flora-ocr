# *Rumex abyssinicus* Jacq.

**Family**: [[Polygonaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Rumex]]

## Description

Rhizomatous perennial with annual erect, hollow, reddish glabrous stem to 3.5 m. Leaves triangular or ovate, hastate at base, to 20 × 15 cm; lower petiole 10 cm, ochrea 18 mm. Much-branched pyramidal terminal inflorescence 20–30 cm. Inner three perianth segments become 6 × 5 mm, cordate and strongly reticulate in fruit. Achene brownish, 3 mm.

## Ecology and uses

Common in cultivation and on mountain summits near 2,000 m. Leaves are eaten as sorrel.
