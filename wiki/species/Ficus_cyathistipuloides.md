# *Ficus cyathistipuloides* De Wildeman

**Genus**: [[Ficus]]
**Family**: [[Moraceae]]
**Protologue**: Repert. Sp. Nov. 12: 194 (1913); HUTCH. in HUTCH. & RENDLE, FTA 6 (2): 206 (1917); LEBRUN & BOUTIQUE in HAUMAN, FCB 1: 172 (1948)

## Description

HUTCH. in HUTCH. & RENDLE, FTA 6 (2): 206 (1917); LEBRUN & BOUTIQUE in HAUMAN, FCB 1: 172 (1948).

Trees. Leafy branchlets thick 1.5–3.5 mm, glabrous or (sparsely) puberulent to hirtellous, always densely white puberulent to shortly pubescent on the scars, dark grey to blackish when dry with lenticels usually visible, solid. Leaves arranged in spirals. Blade oblong to elliptic or to lanceolate, 4.5–15.5 cm long, 2–6 cm wide, coriaceous, apex shortly and abruptly acute (to subacute), base acute to obtuse or sometimes rounded, margin entire; both surfaces glabrous; midvein concave and other veins (almost) flat above, venation ± prominent below; 6–10 pairs of lateral veins, no distinct basal veins; glandular spot at the base of the midvein visible. Petiole 0.5–2 cm long, 1–2 mm thick, glabrous, periderm usually detaching in scale-like discoid pieces. Stipules free, 0.5–2 cm long, puberulent to shortly pubescent, caducous or subpersistent.

Figs solitary in the leaf axils; peduncle 0.3–0.9 cm long, ca. 1.5 mm thick, puberulent to almost glabrous; basal bracts 2, broadly ovate to almost circular, 3–5 mm long, glabrous or almost; receptacle globose to ellipsoid or to ovoid, ca. 3.5 cm in diameter when fresh, 2–2.5 cm when dry, with a stipe 3–8 mm long, glabrous or sparsely puberulent in its lower part, ostiole very prominent, bilabiate.

Pistillate flowers with 3 (–4) unequal tepals, 1–2.5 mm long, acute, free; seed flowers sessile or subsessile, style 3–4 mm long; gall flowers subsessile or pedicellate up to 4 mm, style 1.5–2 mm long. Fruits probably ellipsoid to ovoid; gall fruits probably ellipsoid to oblongoid or subobovoide and ± stipitate; fruits distinctly bicolorous. Staminate flowers few, subsessile or pedicellate up to 1 mm, perianth with 3 unequal tepals, 1–2 mm long, acute, free, anther subsessile, ca. 1.5 mm long, ± distinctly apiculate. Interfloral bracts 3–4 mm long. – Pl. 72, p. 237.

## Type

Seret 794, Zaïre, Djanga (holo-, BR)

## Discussion

The figs of F. cyathistipuloides resemble those of F. cyathistipula, but the wall is not thick and spongy as in F. cyathistipula. The subpersistent stipules are often lost in herbarium specimens.

It is known from a few collections made in Gabon, Zaïre and Côte d'Ivoire.

## Specimens examined

Chevalier 4332, cap Lopez (stér.); 26841, près poste Kango, riv. Komo (stér.). Michaloud 485, 499, Makokou (fig., nov.).
