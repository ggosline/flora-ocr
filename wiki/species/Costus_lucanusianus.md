# *Costus lucanusianus* J. Braun et K. Schum.
**Family in Vol. 9**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Current placement**: Costaceae · **Genus**: [[Costus]]
## Description
Robust glabrous-stemmed herb 2–3 m tall. Blades 20–25 × 6–10 cm, usually densely silver-silky beneath; the very short ligule is marked below by a complete ciliate collar. Terminal globose to ellipsoid spikes have green, red-edged bracts, each with two flowers. Corolla white; labellum white, red-edged with a yellow median line; seeds black with white arils.
## Distribution and uses
Forest understorey across western and central Africa. The treatment records numerous Gabon vernacular names and medicinal or magical uses, but its Gabon specimen list is lost at the page break in the OCR.
