# *Impatiens zenkeri* Warb.

**Family**: [[Balsaminaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Impatiens]]

## Description

Herb with alternate elliptic leaves and axillary mauve or violet flowers. Lip deep and flared, wings broad; spur sinuous. Separated from *I. mackeyana* by broader leaves and toothed lateral sepals.

## Distribution

Cameroon and Gabon.
