# *Plagiocladus diandrus* (Pax) Jean F.Brunel Planche 23

**Genus**: [[Plagiocladus]]
**Family**: [[Phyllanthaceae]]
**Protologue**: Gen. Phyllanthus Afr. Intertrop. Madag. : 260 (1987)

## Synonyms

- Phyllanthus diandrus Pax, Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 33(2) : 276 (1903).
- Lingelsheimia longipedicellata J.Léonard, Bull. Jard. Bot. État Bruxelles 32 : 515 (1962).

## Description

Shrub to small tree, up to 12 m tall, glabrous; trunk up to 12 cm in diameter. Stipules obliquely ovate, 1–2 mm long, cordate at base, with ± hyaline and often lobed margin. Leaf: petiole channeled above, 1–3(–4) mm; lamina obovate-elliptic to oblong, sometimes suborbicular, (3–)8–21 × (1.5–)2.5–7.5 cm, rounded to cuneate at base, acuminate over (0.5–)1–2.5 cm at apex, papyraceous to coriaceous; primary lateral veins (5–)6–7(–9) pairs. Inflorescence: male flowers separated from female flowers, either on different branches or on different parts of the same branch, males below, females toward the apex. Male flower ± erect; pedicel capillary, up to ± 10 mm; sepals spreading, ± elliptic, 1.5 mm long; disk ± hexagonal; stamens ± 0.3 mm long, with anthers ± as long as the filaments. Female flower pendulous, ± 5 mm in diameter; pedicel filiform, up to 4.5 cm and 7 cm in fruit; sepals ± connate at base, spreading, elliptic, 1–1.5 mm long; disk hexagonal, ± 3 mm in diameter; ovary ± trilobed; styles 6, ± free or with bifurcate style. Fruit trilobed, 8–13 × 5–8 mm. Seeds ± obovoid to ellipsoid, 5–6 mm long, light brown.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon and Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, frequently encountered in all provinces except in Haut-Ogooué

## Ecology

Primary and secondarized forest, forest regrowth; in Gabon, up to 550 m altitude. Taxonomic note: Contrary to the description of Brunel (1987), the species is monoecious and not dioecious. b: Breteler (1991). ibliography
