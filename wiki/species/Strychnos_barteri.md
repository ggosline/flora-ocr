# *Strychnos barteri* Solereder

**Genus**: [[Strychnos]]
**Family**: [[Loganiaceae]]

## Description

in ENGLER, Bot. Jahrb. 17 : 556 (1893). — OLIVIER in HOOKER, ICON. 23 : t. 2284 (1894). — FTA 4 (1) : 523. — FWTA ed. 1, 2 : 24. — FWTA ed. 2, 2 : 44. — LEEUWENBERG, 1. c. : 65, fig. 6, carte 5.

Large liane 30–100 m in length and reaching a height of 10–40 m; stem 3–16 cm in diameter; bark dark brown, with large lenticels; branches non-lenticellate, sometimes slightly furrowed when dry; branchlets glabrous, cylindrical, non-furrowed; tendrils in 1–3 pairs.

Leaves with petiole glabrous, 4–10 mm in length; blade of flowering branches shiny above, scarcely shiny and slightly paler below, often black on dried specimens and becoming ± grey below, coriaceous even in the fresh state, elliptic, narrowly elliptic or narrowly obovate, 3–10 × 1.5–6 cm, rounded, apiculate, or briefly acuminate at the apex, cuneate at the base, glabrous on both faces; one pair of secondary nerves curved along the margin and often a second less distinct one; tertiary venation reticulate, prominent on both faces. The shade-form leaves differ by a blade often much larger, but variable in shape and size, subcoriaceous or papyraceous, reaching up to approximately 20 × 8 cm, acuminate to caudate at the apex, cuneate or rounded at the base. The shade-form leaves situated at the base of the branch, on the main axis, have a suborbicular blade, 3 × 2.5–7 × 6 cm, cordate or subcordate at the base, rounded to acuminate at the apex.

Inflorescences axillary, several together, contracted or exceptionally non-contracted, $1–3 \times 1–2$ cm, pauciflorous; peduncle, branches and pedicels glabrous, short. Flowers tetramerous. Sepals connate, to about one-quarter of their height, ovate to suborbicular, $1–1.5 \times 0.7–1.1$ mm, acute, obtuse, or rounded, finely ciliate, glabrous on both faces. Corolla, in the mature bud, 4–6 times longer than the calyx, 5.8–7 mm in length, white, glabrous on the outside, pilose on the inside towards the base of the lobes and in the tube; tube approximately 2 times longer than the calyx, 0.9–1.5 times longer than the lobes, 2.8–4.5 mm in length; lobes oblong, $2.5–3.2 \times 0.9–1.3$ mm, recurved. Stamens well exserted, inserted at the

throat of the corolla, filament glabrous or sparsely pubescent, approximately 2 times longer than the anther, elongate during flowering; anther oblong, glabrous. Pistil glabrous, 4–6 mm in length; ovary globose or ovoid, $1 \times 1$ mm, with two locules; style 3.5 mm in length, slender; stigma weakly bilobed; 10 ovules per locule.

Fruits orange, obliquely ellipsoid or obovoid, $20 \times 16 \times 15$ to $32 \times 22 \times 22$ mm, with one or several seeds; walls thin, fairly hard. Seed obliquely ellipsoid, $13 \times 10 \times 4–20 \times 14 \times 10$ mm, marked on one side by a deep depression and the other furnished with a boss surrounded by a furrow and appearing papillose; false papillae composed of short, curved hairs. Pl. 18, 1–7, p. 127.

## Type

Barter 1246, Onitsha, Nigeria du sud (holotype : B, non vu, détruit; lectotype : K, isotypes : GH, LE, P)

## Discussion

West Africa. Dense and secondary forests, also in mountains; generally on periodically inundated riverbanks. Altitude 0–1300 m.

## Specimens examined

Leeuwenberg 6910, 20 km W Victoria (stér.) P, WAG; 6960 et 6961, 3 km S Victoria, Bimbia (fr. desséché et plantules, oct.) P, WAG, YA.
