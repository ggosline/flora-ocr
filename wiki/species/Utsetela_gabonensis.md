# *Utsetela gabonensis* Pellegrin

**Genus**: [[Utsetela]]
**Family**: [[Moraceae]]
**Protologue**: Mém. Soc. Linn. Norm., n. sér., 1 (3): 81 (1928); Bull. Soc. bot. Fr. 75: 673 (1928); BERG, BJBB 47: 291 (1977)

## Description

Mem. Soc. Linn. Norm., n. ser., 1 (3): 81 (1928); Bull. Soc. bot. Fr. 75: 673 (1928); BERG, BJBB 47: 291 (1977).

Shrubs (branched or unbranched) reaching 2.5 m in height (or more?) white latex, small leafy branchlets 1–3.5 mm thick, puberulent with uncinate retrorse hairs white to brown. Blade elliptic to lanceolate, most often wider at the middle or above, symmetric or slightly asymmetric, (2–) 3–19 × (0.5–) 1–8 cm, chartaceous, acuminate to caudate or subacute; base acute to obtuse; margin (weakly) sinuate, but most often coarsely dentate to lobed toward the apex; blade with scattered hairs on the main veins and margin, glabrescent; midvein prominent above, the others slightly prominent to plane, or slightly impressed; veins prominent below; 3–8 pairs of secondary veins, the lower pair forming acute angles with the midvein, the other pairs arising from 1/3 of the blade with less acute angles, with or without a few parallel tertiary veins. Petioles (0.5–) 1–5 mm long, with fairly dense uncinate hairs. Stipules subpersistent, 2–5 mm long, ovate to deltoid, subaristrate, with a prominent midvein, sparsely puberulent.

Inflorescences most often solitary or in pairs, or sometimes more, at the leaf axils, 3–4 mm in diameter, subsessile or pedunculate. Peduncle reaching 2 cm in length, puberulent with straight and uncinate hairs, white to brownish. Flowers several to many, most of them grouped 2–3 together; perianth 1–2 mm high, tubular, often ± constricted toward the base, irregularly 2–4-lobed to 2–4-fid, partially puberulent with uncinate hairs; stamens (1) 2 (3), free or united at the base, with filaments 1–4.5 mm long, slender, and anthers 0.4–0.8 (–1.0) × 0.2–0.5 mm, apiculate or not, with rather broad connective. Female flower unique (or more?); perianth ca. 3 mm long, tubular, 4-toothed, partially puberulent with uncinate hairs; style 2–3 mm long; stigmas 1–4 mm long. Bracts up to 7–8, in 2–3 rows, with straight, relatively long, brownish hairs and uncinate hairs white to brownish; the 3–4 outer bracts deltoid to ovate, large (up to ca. 3.5 mm in length),

the inner ones narrowly ovate to lanceolate or linear, generally much smaller.

Infructescences subglobose, 6–9 cm in diameter. Peduncle reaching 3.5 mm in length. Receptacle densely puberulent with uncinate hairs; staminal filaments of the male flowers persistent, often reflexed. Stone ca. 8 × 6–7 mm. Seed ca. 7 × 5–6 mm. – Pl. 30, p. 103.

## Type

Le Testu 1930, Gabon (holo-, P)

## Discussion

U. gabonensis is known from a few localities in Gabon, from a single one in Congo (near Sounda, lower Kouilou) and from a single locality in Zaïre (Kivu, terr. Pansi, Lumina). It is a forest understory species.

Breteler 6562, near Lastoursville (fl., Sept.). Fleury in Chevalier 26159, env. Adouma, on the Orimbo, tributary of the Ogooué (fl., July). Le Testu 1660, 1930, Tchibanga, Nyanga region (fl., Nov., Dec.); 5808, Boungoung (fl., and fr., Dec.); 8012, Lastoursville region, Latimba (fl., Apr.); s.n., Lastoursville region (fl., Mar.); s.n., Lastoursville region, source of the Ogooué (fl., Apr.). Trilles 38, riv. Udzémé (fr., May).
