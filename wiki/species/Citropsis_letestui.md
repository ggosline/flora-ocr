# *Citropsis letestui* Pellegr.

**Family**: [[Rutaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Citropsis]]

## Description

Known from a single Gabon specimen. It differs from *C. gabunensis* chiefly in normally having three, sometimes five, leaflets, an unwinged petiole and rachis (petiole to 12–15 cm in five-foliolate leaves), and pedicels about 5 mm long. Letouzey considered it possibly only an extreme form or variety of *C. gabunensis*.

## Distribution

Gabon: Ndougou in the Ngounyé valley.

## Specimens examined

Le Testu 2286 (possibly also 2286 bis).
