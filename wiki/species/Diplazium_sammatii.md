# *Diplazium sammatii* (Kühn) C. Chr. (P.L. XX)

## Description

— C. Christensen, Ind. Fil.: 238 (1905).

Rhizome erect, with fronds in tufts, covered with lanceolate scales, formed of cells with darkened walls, margins furnished with recurved prolongations. Petiole thick, blackish at the base and bearing scales, reddish-brown higher up, quadrangular, 15-30 cm long. Lamina with lanceolate outline, 40-50 cm long by 25-30 cm wide, pinnate. Pinnae subopposite at the base, alternate higher up, subsessile or very shortly petiolate, making an almost right angle with the rachis, 13-15 cm long by 2-2.5 cm wide, with subequal base, truncate, apex long-tapering, margin serrulate, with acute teeth, oblong, especially prominent towards the apex. Terminal pinna lanceolate, similar to the lateral ones, sometimes lobed at the base, the two upper pinnae adnate. Plant glabrous. Texture subcoriaceous. Veins bi- or trifurcate. Sori elongate, diplazioid, almost all of the same thickness, arising near the costa and reaching almost to the margin. Indusium brown, thin.

From Sudan to former Belgian Congo. Shaded forests, in humid zone.
