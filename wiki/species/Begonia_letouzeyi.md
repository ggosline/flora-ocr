# *Begonia letouzeyi* Sosef Planche 8

**Genus**: [[Begonia]]
**Family**: [[Begoniaceae]]
**Protologue**: Wageningen Agric. Univ. Papers 94-1 : 162 (1994)

## Description

Plant 10–27 cm in height. Leaf peltate; petiole 10–26 cm, hirsute; lamina broadly ovate or broadly elliptic-ovate to elliptic or elliptic-ovate, (3–)5–17.5 × (1.5–)2.5–11 cm, apex attenuate, acuminate or caudate, margin often with a few poorly marked or coarse teeth in the upper half, upper surface generally densely hirsute. Inflorescence: peduncle 1.5–10 cm, with (1–)2–3 ♂ flowers and 1 ♀ flower with yellow tepals; bracts (1.5–)2.5–8 mm. ♂ Flower: tepals (5.5–)8.5–17.5 × (5.5–)8.5–18(–28) mm; androecium with 14–39 stamens. ♀ Flower: pedicel elongating in fruit to 5 mm; tepals (6–)8–22 × (6–)9–20(–28) mm; ovary globose to broadly obovate or broadly ovate to elliptic, (2.5–)4–9 × (2–)2.5–5 mm, sparsely to densely hirsute, with (3–)4 wings 0.1–2 mm in width; styles (3–)4. Fruit globose to ellipsoid, 4.5–10 × 2.5–9 mm.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon (scattered and rare), Congo (Brazzaville; Mayombe)

## Ecology

Primary and secondarized lowland and submontane forests, on slopes, rocks or mossy trunks, often along watercourses and rapids; 350–900 m altitude. B: Sosef (1994). BIBLIOGRAPHY
