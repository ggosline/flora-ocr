# *Illigera verspertilio* (Benth.) Baker f.

**Genus**: [[Illigera]]
**Family**: [[Hernandiaceae]]
**Protologue**: J. Bot. 63: 175 (1925)

## Synonyms

- Dioscorea verspertilio Benth. in Hook., Niger Fl. : 538 (1849).

## Description

Stem solid. Leaves with 3 leaflets; petiole 3–9 cm, loosely pilose or glabrous, petiolules 0.6–2 cm; leaflets obovate (those of the inflorescence leaves oblong-ovate), 3.5–9.0 × 2–7 cm, cuneate or obtuse at the base, rounded or obtuse or very shortly acuminate at the apex, upper surface glabrous, lower surface sparsely pilose toward the base of the nerves; lateral nerves 1(–2) pairs, arising from the lower third of the blade. Flowers: tepals with tomentellous inner surface and furnished at the base with setae, outer tepals 5–6 mm in length, 3–5-nerved, the inner ones 5 × 0.4–0.5 mm, 1-nerved; filaments of the stamens furnished with lanceolate appendages reaching 1 mm in length. Fruit 3-winged, puberulent, central part reaching up to 3 cm in length, the 2 major wings 3.0–4.2 cm long, the minor one up to 0.7 cm.

## Distribution

of Sierra Leone, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroon, Gabon and the Central African Republic

## Ecology

secondary forests. B: Keay (1952), Hutchinson & Dalziel (1954), Kubitzki (1969). BIBLIOGRAPHY
