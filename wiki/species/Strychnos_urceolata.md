# *Strychnos urceolata* Leeuwenberg

**Genus**: [[Strychnos]]
**Family**: [[Loganiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Act. Bot. Neerl. 14 : 226, fig. 2, 1-6 (1965); Meded. Landbouwh. 69 (1) : 265, fig. 43, carte 43 (1969)

## Description

Large liana 10–40 m tall and reaching a length of 80 m or more; stem 3–18 cm in diameter; bark dark brown, with large lenticels; wood pale yellowish; branchlets non-lenticellate; small branchlets brown-pubescent; tendrils solitary at the axils of ordinary leaves.

Leaves with brown-pubescent petiole, 2–4 mm long; lamina subcoriaceous, elliptic, narrowly elliptic, ovate or narrowly ovate, 3–7.5 (–11.5) × 1.5–3 (–4.5) cm, acuminate at apex, cuneate or rounded at base (and, for leaves situated on the main axis, sometimes subcordate), often bearing scattered pubescence on both faces of the midvein and on the secondary veins below, often bearded below at the axils of the second pair of main secondary veins, these departing 0.5–1 cm approximately above the base and reaching almost the apex, the first pair, less distinct, departing from the base; tertiary venation indistinct.

Inflorescences terminal and often also axillary, lax, multiflorous, 8–15 × 5–10 cm; peduncle, branches and pedicels brown-pubescent. Flowers 5-merous, pleasantly scented. Sepals pale green, fused to approximately one-third of their height, triangular, 0.6–1 × 0.6–1 mm, acute, ciliate, pubescent on the outside, glabrous on the inside. Corolla, in the mature bud, 2–3 × 2 mm, pale grey-green, urceolate, bearing fine pubescence on both faces and pilose on the inside at the base of the lobes; tube 0.7–1 mm long; lobes 1.5–2 times longer than the tube, narrowly triangular, 1.2–2 × 1–1.2 mm, acute, suberect. Stamens slightly exserted, inserted at the throat of the corolla, with glabrous filament, almost

the same length as the anther; anther triangular to ovate, ciliate all around or, most often, only at the base. Pistil glabrous, 1.3–1.6 mm long; ovary depressed-globose, 0.7–0.8 × 1 mm, 2-locular; style 0.6–0.7 mm long; stigma capitate; 8–10 ovules per locule.

Fruits orange, small, soft, ellipsoid, 12–14 × 10–12 mm and containing one seed; wall thin. Seed ellipsoid, flattened, 11 × 9 × 3 mm, biconvex, with short and dense pubescence, fairly rugose; testa thick, not adhering to the pulp. Pl. 41, 1–6, p. 148.
