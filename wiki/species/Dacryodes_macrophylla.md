# *Dacryodes macrophylla* (Oliv.) Lam

**Family**: [[Burseraceae]] · **Genus**: [[Dacryodes]]

## Description

Leaves usually with three pairs of large elliptic or ovate-oblong leaflets, 10–20 × 6–12 cm, base obtuse or rounded, apex blunt-acuminate; petiolules stout, 1–2 cm; blades apparently glabrous but with minute scales beneath. Female flowers finely tomentose, 3-merous; sepals 4–5 mm; petals suborbicular; six stamens; disc glabrous. Fruit ellipsoid; endocarp illustrated in Plate XIV.

## Distribution

Western Central African forest, including Gabon. The large, few leaflets distinguish it from most Gabonese *Dacryodes*.
