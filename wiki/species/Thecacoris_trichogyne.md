# *Thecacoris trichogyne* Müll.Arg. Planche 29

**Genus**: [[Thecacoris]]
**Family**: [[Phyllanthaceae]]
**Protologue**: T. lenifolia J.Léonard, Bull. Jard. Bot. État Bruxelles 17 : 253 (1945)

## Description

in Seem., J. Bot. 2: 328 (1864). T. lenifolia J.Léonard, Bull. Jard. Bot. État Bruxelles 17: 253 (1945). Shrub (1–)2–4 m tall; branchlets pubescent to villous. Stipules narrowly triangular, (2–)6–18(–30) mm long, pilose like the branchlets on the external face. Leaf: petiole 5–10 mm, villous; blade obovate-elliptic, (3–)10–20(–35) × (1.5–)5–8(–15) cm, cuneate to obtuse or rounded at the base, acuminate for 0.5–1.5(–2) cm and mucronulate or rarely obtuse at the apex, glabrous above except (rarely) pubescent on the midvein, puberulent or pubescent to glabrous below; lateral veins 5–10(–16) pairs. Inflorescence solitary or few-fascicled, pendulous, with pubescent axis, the male ones in a dense spike 3–11 cm long, the female ones in a spiciform raceme 6–15(–30) cm long and 6–40 cm in fruit; bracts oval to narrowly elliptic, 2–6(–12) × 1–3 mm. Male flower 5(–6)-merous, 2–2.5 mm in diameter, sessile to subsessile; sepals 5–6, ± oval, 1–1.5 × 1 mm, ± ciliolate; petals often present, ± 1 mm long or minute; stamens 2–3 mm long; disk pubescent; pistillode ± 1 mm long, ± glabrous. Female flower ± 3 mm long; pedicel 1–3 mm, but 6–20 mm in fruit, pubescent; sepals ± oval, 1.5–2 × 1–1.5 mm, ciliate; petals as in male flowers; disk pubescent; ovary 2 mm long, pubescent to villous. Fruit (5–)6–8 × (7–)9–11 mm, brownish, pubescent. Seeds ± globose or orange-segment-shaped, 3–4 × 2.5–3.5 mm, black.

## Distribution

Cameroon, São Tomé and Príncipe, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Zambia; in Gabon, known from Moyen-Ogooué, Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem.

## Ecology

Primary and secondarized forest, gallery forest; in Gabon, at 0–600 m altitude. b: Léonard (1995a). Bibliography
