# *Oncinotis pontyi* Dubard Planche 78(4), 83

**Genus**: [[Oncinotis]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Notul. Syst. (Paris) 2 : 201 (1912)

## Description

Notul. Syst. (Paris) 2 : 201 (1912). Liane reaching 50 m in height; branches glabrous or slightly pubescent. Leaf: petiole 7–22 mm, glabrous or rarely slightly pubescent, with colleters – often paired – along the upper face; limb obovate, sometimes also as long as wide, 4–18 × 1.4–10 cm, cuneate at base, acuminate or often emarginate at apex, subcoriaceous to coriaceous, glabrous or rarely puberulent on the midvein, shiny above, dull and distinctly paler below; secondary veins 3 to 10 pairs, tertiary venation scarcely visible; domatia generally formed of pits with ciliate margins, sometimes absent. Inflorescence 3–11.5 × 1.5–8.5 cm; peduncle and axes with reddish-brown pubescence; bracts 0.6–1.6 mm long. Flower: pedicel 2–3.5 mm; calyx with lobes 0.8–2.1 × 0.6–1.2 mm, puberulent reddish-brown outside; corolla greenish-yellow or white, tube cylindrical, weakly retracted towards base and apex – sometimes also as long as the calyx lobes, 1.4–2 mm, pubescent outside, with tufts of hairs inside, with narrowly ovate lobes, ± as long as the tube – ± as long as wide, 0.5–1.5 mm, puberulent or glabrous outside, with a crown 0.4–0.8 mm long; anther 1.8–2.1 mm long; pistil 2–2.8 mm long. Fruit: follicles narrowly cylindrical, often slightly recurved or subrect, forming an angle of – , ±–± cm, exocarp up to mm thick, most often very hard, slightly siliconed on the external face, with greyish to reddish-brown pubescence, glabrescent. Distribution from Guinea to Cameroon, Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Central African Republic, South Sudan and Uganda; in Gabon: Ngounié, Ogooué, Ivindo and Ogooué-Lolo.
