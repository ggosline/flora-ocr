# *Millettia aboensis* (Hook.f.) Baker Planche 94

**Genus**: [[Millettia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. trop. Africa 2 : 130 (1871)

## Description

Tree reaching 10 m in height; trunk up to 13.5 cm in diameter; crown dome-shaped; bark with vertical lenticels; branchlets velutinous. Stipules narrowly ovate, 6–9 × 1–3 mm, silky. Leaf: petiole 3.5–8 cm; rachis 13–20 cm, brown velutinous; stipels absent; leaflets 11 to 19, petiolule 5–10 mm, densely brown pubescent, lamina ovate, 8–19 × 3–5 cm, base rounded-cuneate, apex with an acumen of ± 0.2–0.6 cm, brown strigulose above, brown velutinous below; lateral nerves in 9 to 11 pairs. Inflorescence in erect pseudoracemes, 16–54 cm in length; peduncles 3–16 cm; lateral branches 0.5–8 cm in length, with ± 12 flowers; bracts ovate-oblong, ± 6 × 2 mm. Flower: pedicel 6–10 mm; bracteoles at the base of the calyx, caducous, ovate, ± 4 × 2 mm, brown pubescent; calyx almost truncate, brown velutinous, tube 7–8 × 6–8 mm, lobes 0.1–0.5 mm in length; corolla purple, standard with claw of ± 3 mm, limb ovate to obovate, 20–24 × 10–15 mm, base broadly cuneate, apex emarginate, with golden-brown pubescence near the incision, wings with claw of 6–12 mm, limb oblique-obovate, 13–18 × 5 mm, keel with claw of 6–12 mm, limb 12–16 × 5–6 mm, ventral sutures with golden pubescence; stamens monadelphous, filaments white, 12–23 mm, free part curved, 5–7 mm; ovary narrowly lanceolate, 14 mm in length, silky, with 6 ovules; style 4–8 mm, glabrous, white. Pod narrowly oblong, 9–15 × 2.2 cm, base and apex attenuate, with rather prominent sutures, dark brown, velutinous. Seed orbicular, flattened, ± 18 × 16 × 3 mm, brown, furnished with flabelliform incisions arising from the hilum of ± 3 mm.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon and Gabon (no locality)

## Ecology

secondary forest, agricultural land; at 50–140 m altitude
