# *Bulbophyllum schinzianum* Kraenzl.

**Genus**: [[Bulbophyllum]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. R. Bot. Belg. 38 : 57 (1899)

## Description

– TYPE: Laurent s.n., Congo (Kinshasa), holo-BR – FTWA: 236 (1968); FAC 2: 312 (1992); FCAM 2: 391 (2001).

Pseudobulbs 1–6 × 0.8–3 cm, orbicular to narrowly ovoid, 1-leaved. Leaf petiolate, 7.5–32 × 1.4–6.5 cm, oblong, rounded to subacute. Inflorescence dense to lax, 28–102 cm, rachis not thickened, < 100-flowered. Flowers medium-sized, glabrous. Sepals yellowish or greenish, sometimes brownish, generally spotted with (or entirely) red-purple, the dorsal one oblong-ovate to elliptic-lanceolate, acute, the lateral ones obliquely oblong to linear-lanceolate, subacute. Petals red-purple, linear to filiform, acuminate. Lip yellowish-green tinged with red-purple, rather thick, oblong-lanceolate to narrowly obovate, subacute to obtuse, margins covered with short brown hairs on adaxial face, hairs 4–8 mm long on abaxial face. – Pl. 70, p. 145.

## Distribution

Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Congo (Kinshasa)

## Ecology

alt. 0–800 m. Epiphytic in the crown and on bark of trees of primary forest and exploited forest, collected on a Dacryodes sp.
