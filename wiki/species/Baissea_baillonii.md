# *Baissea baillonii* Hua Planche 16

**Genus**: [[Baissea]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Linn. Paris , sér. 2, 1 : 8 (1898)

## Description

Liane attaining 5 m in height; branches densely pubescent or puberulent. Leaf: petiole 0.6–3.2 mm, pubescent; lamina deltoid to narrowly oblong, 1–4 times as long as wide, 1.5–7.5 × 1–2.2 cm, cordate or sometimes rounded at base, acuminate or sometimes mucronate, rarely obtuse at apex, glabrescent on both faces, ciliate, pubescent at apex; secondary nerves 5 to 10 pairs, pubescent or puberulent on both faces, tertiary venation scalariform; domatia present. Inflorescence axillary and often also terminal, long, in cymose panicles few- to pluriflorous, densely or sparsely pubescent; peduncle 4–21 mm. Flower fragrant: pedicel 2.1–4.9 mm; calyx with lobes 1–3.5 times as long as wide, finely pubescent externally, ciliate; generally with 5 small internal colleters at base; corolla white, sometimes with yellowish or purplish throat, with tube slightly funnel-shaped or ± campanulate, 1.3–3.4 times as long as calyx, long, puberulent externally, hairy within at interior, otherwise glabrous; lobes broadly ovate to ovate, times as long as tube, times as wide, 0.5–2 cm lightly auriculed at base, narrowly acuminate at apex, pubescence variable and fine externally, glabrous at interior, diversely ciliate; stamens inserted 0.8–1.8 mm from base of tube, anther long 1.7–2 mm; ovary diversely pubescent to glabrescent at apex. Fruit: follicles ± 70–100 × 2 cm, finely ribbed, glabrescent, shining. Distribution from Guinea to the Republic of the Congo, in Gabon, uniquely absent from the Ogooué Maritime. Ecology: primary and secondary forest in Gabon up to m altitude, elsewhere up to m. B: van Dilst (1995). BIBLIOGRAPHY
