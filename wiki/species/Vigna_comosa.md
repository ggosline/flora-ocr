# *Vigna comosa* Baker subsp. comosa

**Genus**: [[Vigna]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: V. micrantha Harms, Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 26 : 311 (1899)

## Description

in Oliv., Fl. trop. Afr. 2 : 202 (1871). V. micrantha Harms, Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 26 : 311 (1899). V. ntemensis Pellegrin, Bull. Soc. Bot. France 93 : 223 (1946). Perennial herb, climbing, pubescent. Stipules filiform, 1–2 mm, pubescent, with truncate base and not prolonged beneath the insertion. Leaf: petiole 7–25 mm; rachis 2–10 mm; stipels ± 1 mm long, obtuse; leaflets with petiolule ± 1 mm, blade oval to lanceolate, lobed (especially the lateral ones) or not, (1–)2–4 × 0.7–2 cm, cuneate at the base, rounded to subacute and mucronate at the apex, densely puberulent to glabrescent on both faces. Inflorescence: peduncle 2–15 cm; rachis 1.5–2 cm; bracts deltoid, 2–3 mm long. Flower: bracteoles subulate, 3–4 mm, longer than the calyx; calyx pubescent to glabrous, with tube ± 2 mm long, lobes triangular, 0.5–1.5 mm long; corolla yellow, reaching 12 mm long, standard orbicular, 9–12 × 10 mm, biauricate, wings oblanceolate, ± 9 × 5 mm, keel falcate, 9 × 4 mm, obtuse; ovary with 2 to 6 ovules; style prolonged beyond the stigma by a short beak. Pod pendulous, cylindrical to slightly flattened, 10–25 × 4 mm, with a curved beak, with (1–)2 or 3 seeds, sparsely pubescent. Seed 3.5 × 2–3.5 mm, with eccentric strophiole.

## Distribution

from Guinea to Uganda, southwards to Mozambique and Angola; in Gabon, known from Haut-Ogooué

## Ecology

herbaceous savannas on sand, on rocks; in Gabon at 0–500 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1100 m. Flowers in March, fruits in June and July

## Vernacular names

haricot sauvage (fr)

## Source note

In our area, this is subsp. comosa, the other subspecies, subsp. abercornensis Verdc., is found in Zambia. B: Botton & Hallé (1958), Pasquet (2001), Verdcourt (1971c), Wilczek (1954). Bibliography
