# *Platycoryne guingangae* (Rchb.f.) Rolfe

**Genus**: [[Platycoryne]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: FTA 7 : 258 (1898) ; FAC 1 : 156 (1984) ; La Croix & Cribb, Fl., Zambes., Orchid. 1 : 154 (1995) ; FCAM 1 : 208 (1998)

## Synonyms

- Habenaria pogeana Kraenzl., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 16: 207 (1892).
- Platycoryne poggeana (Kraenzl.) Rolfe, FTA 7 : 258 (1898).

## Description

Stem 20–60 cm, glabrous. Leaves generally spreading along the stem but sometimes forming a rosette at the base, 1.5–5 × 0.4–0.8 cm, linear to narrowly lanceolate, acute. Inflorescence 2–4.5 cm, 1–6 (15)-flowered. Flowers orange, glabrous. Dorsal sepal broadly ovate to almost semi-orbicular; lateral sepals obliquely lanceolate to oblong ovate-lanceolate. Petals acute, linear-lanceolate to ovate-lanceolate with a small acute and filiform basal lobule. Lip rather fleshy, clearly 3-partite:

PL. 23. – Structure of the gynostemium of the genus Platycoryne Rchb.f.: A, lateral view; B, rostellum and stigma spread, anterior view (A & B, ex SZLACHETKO & RUTKOWSKI, 2000).

midlobe the largest, ligulate to linear-lanceolate, subobtuse to acute; lateral lobes filiform, acute, pendant. Spur narrowly cylindrical, slightly swollen towards the apex or equal along its entire length. – Pl. 24, p. 55.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon, Congo (Brazzaville), Central African Republic, Congo (Kinshasa), Angola, Zambia

## Ecology

alt. 80–1500 m. Terrestrial herb on greyish moist humus, sandy soil, humid marsh, humid to very humid savanna, sandy grassland (with Pennisetum)

II. TROPIDIOIDEAE (Pfitzer) Szlach.

Fragment Flor. Geobot., Suppl. 3: 35 (1995).

Veil of "Calanthe" type. Stem bambusiform, woody, often branched. Leaves spiral, convolute, folded. Inflorescence terminal or lateral. Flowers resupinate or not, tubular or sacciform at the base. Lip fixed. Gynostemium erect, slender to stout. Staminodes wing-like, fused to the style below the stigma, or rudimentary or entirely reduced. Stigma transversely elliptic, sometimes horizontal. Rostellum elongated like a beak, straight, massive, acute. Viscidium simple, oblong, thick. Hamulus thick and fleshy, formed of two layers. Rest of the rostellum bidentate. Anther narrowly cordate to oblong, mobile. Connective fleshy, forming a thick swelling above the locules. Pollinia sectile.

This group comprises 3 genera, only one of which occurs in Africa.
