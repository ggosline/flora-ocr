## Description

in Engler, Monogr. Afr. Pflanzenf. 6: 89, tab. 29 B (1901). – Pellegrin, Bull. Soc. Bot. Fr. 94: 386 (1947). – Keay, F.W.T.A., ed. 2, I 1: 54 (1954). – Keay et al., Nigerian trees 1: 61 (1960). – Aubreville, F.F.C.I., ed. 2, I: 152, tab. 42 (1959).

Scrambling shrub or small tree reaching 9–10 m in height, with glabrous branches. Leaves with petiole 2–4 mm long; blade papyraceous, elliptic to elliptic-obovate, 6–20 cm long, 2.5–7.5 cm wide, rounded to cuneate at the base, shortly acuminate at the apex. Lateral veins 8–15 pairs, prominent on both surfaces; reticulum loose, clearly visible.

Flowers solitary, extra-axillary to opposite the leaves; pedicel slender, 2–4.5 cm long, bearing towards the upper half a membranous, lanceolate acute, crisped bract, ± 1 cm long, 4.5–5 mm wide, glabrous. Sepals reflexed, lanceolate crisped, 1–2 cm long, 4–6 mm wide at the base. External petals greenish spotted with red, lanceolate-linear, crisped, very narrow at the apex, 4–8 cm long, 1.5–2 cm wide at the base; internal petals pink and white, 1.6–2.5 cm long, with claw as long as the blade, 2 mm wide, blade triangular-ovate, crisped, 1–1.3 cm wide, sparsely pubescent within. Receptacle planar-convex. Stamens sessile, 1 mm long, thecae linear extrorse, connectives truncate above the anthers. Ovary 1.5 mm long.

Fruits with pedicel 3.5–7 cm long; syncarp ovoid, 4–5.5 cm long, 3.5–4 cm wide, with thin pericarp, adorned with 6–7 longitudinal ribs, regular and narrow. Seeds oily, elliptic, slightly compressed and curved, 1–1.2 cm long, 6–7 mm wide, with thin, coriaceous testa, surrounded by a circular ridge slightly prominent.

## Type

Preuss, entre Isongo ct Mowange, Cameroun (holotype B)

## Discussion

Species of humid dense forest, uncommon, distributed from Sierra Leone to Gabon.
