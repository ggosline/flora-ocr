# *Ledermanniella pusilla* (Warming) C.Cusset Planche 18

**Genus**: [[Ledermanniella]]
**Family**: [[Podostemaceae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia , sér. 2, 14(2) : 274 (1974)

## Synonyms

- Inversodicraea pusilla Engl., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 60(5) : 461 (1926).

## Description

Plant with a basal thallose foliate part; stem simple, up to ± 1 cm in length. Stipules present. Leaves distichous, entire at the base of the stem, then 1 or 2 times dichotomous, 0.6–6 cm in length, with linear segments. Inflorescence with terminal flowers, solitary in

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, known from Ngounié and Ogooué-Ivindo
