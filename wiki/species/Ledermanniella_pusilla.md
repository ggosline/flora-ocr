# *Ledermanniella pusilla* (Warming) C.Cusset Planche 18

**Genus**: [[Ledermanniella]]
**Family**: [[Podostemaceae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia , sér. 2, 14(2) : 274 (1974)

## Description

Adansonia, ser. 2, 14(2): 274 (1974). Inversodicraea pusilla Engl., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 60(5): 461 (1926). Plant with a basal thalloid foliate portion; stem simple, up to ± 1 cm in length. Stipules present. Leaves distichous, entire at the base of the stem, then 1 or 2 times dichotomous, 0.6–6 cm in length, with linear segments. Inflorescence with terminal flowers, solitary in subacaulous plants, grouped in 6 or 7 in well-developed individuals; spathe ± long-pedicellate, ellipsoid to obovoid, 1.5–3 mm in length, sometimes mucronate at the apex, sometimes slightly compressed in the middle. Flower: pedicel 5–10 mm after anthesis; tepals filiform, 0.5–0.75 mm; androecium longer than the ovary, stamens 2, filaments 3–4 mm, fused for half their length, anther 1 × 0.4 mm, pollen in dyads; ovary 1.5 × 0.8 mm, gynophore 0.8–1 mm in length; stigmas linear, 0.5–0.6 mm. Fruit with caducous valves.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, known from Ngounié and Ogooué-Ivindo

## Ecology

in Gabon at 150–200 m elevation. B: Cusset (1984, 1987a), Sosef et al. (2006). Bibliography
