# *Pierrodendron africanum* (Hook. f.) Little

**Family**: [[Simaroubaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Pierrodendron]]

## Synonymy

- *Mannia africana* Hook. f. (1862)
- *Pierrodendron grandifolium* Engl. (1907)

## Description

Small tree to 15 m. Leaves glabrous, to 1 m, with 5–15 pairs of subopposite leaflets and a cylindrical rachis. Leaflets oblong-elliptic, rounded and asymmetric at the base, rounded above with an abrupt short thick acumen, 10–30 × 3–9 cm, coriaceous; 8–10 pairs of secondary veins joined by veinlets into a characteristic loose network slightly impressed beneath; petiolules about 3 mm.

Racemes to 40 cm, bearing red-violet flowers. Pedicels 6–7 mm; buds oblong. Calyx glabrous, with five lobes about 1 mm. Petals 5, oblong, contorted in bud, about 1 cm, red. Stamens 15(–18), red; anthers large, oblong, about as long as or slightly longer than the 3–3.5 mm filaments; filaments with a hairy basal ligule. Disc annular and glabrous. Carpels 5, glabrous; style short. Drupes mango-like, about 7 × 4.5 cm, with a short apical point.

## Distribution

From southern Nigeria to Angola and the Congo. Apparently uncommon in Gabon, where the treatment records it only near Libreville.

## Specimens examined

Holotype: Mann, Nun River and Old Calabar. **Gabon** — Klaine 274 (Libreville).
