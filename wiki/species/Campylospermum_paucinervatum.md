# *Campylospermum paucinervatum* Sosef

**Genus**: [[Campylospermum]]
**Family**: [[Ochnaceae]]
**Protologue**: Blumea 52 : 15 (2007)

## Description

Shrub reaching 2 m in height; branches with whitish bark. Stipules caducous, narrowly triangular, 3–5 mm in length. Leaf: petiole 0–2 mm; lamina narrowly elliptic or sometimes very narrowly elliptic 3–9.5(–13) × 0.7–2(–3) cm, ratio (2.5–)3.5–8, attenuate at base, acute to slightly acuminate at apex, apiculate at tip, coriaceous, margin entire on lower third or half, distal part serrulate with spaced teeth; midvein prominent on both faces, lateral veins in 0 to 2(–5) pairs, prominent on both faces, branching at lower part and strongly arched toward upper part, forming a prominent submarginal vein, tertiary veins scalariform, perpendicular to midvein, forming a series of numerous reticulate veinlets that connect to midvein, distinct on both faces. Inflorescence terminal, branched, lax, main axis 2–5(–10) cm; racemes 1 to 3, reaching 3 cm, with persistent bracts at apex; gemma scales absent or few; persistent bracts, triangular, ± 1.5 mm in length; cymules distant 3–5 mm, 1- or 2-flowered. Flower: pedicel 7–12 mm, articulate 1–3 mm from base; sepals elliptic-ovate, ± 5 × 2 mm in flower, 7 × 3 mm in fruit; petals broadly spatulate, 6–8 × 3.5–5 mm, attenuate at base, rounded at apex, stamens: anthers ± 4 mm in length; style ± 4 mm. Fruit: receptacle ± globose, 3–5 mm in width; drupels 1 to 3 well developed per receptacle, ± reniform, 5–6 mm in diameter; cotyledons incumbent, unequal with small external cotyledon.

## Distribution

Gabon, Republic of the Congo and northwestern Angola (Cabinda); in Gabon, only along the coast of the southern half of the country (Ogooué-Maritime and Nyanga).

## Ecology

Forest, forest edge, coastal scrub; on white sand; at 10–40 m in altitude. b: Bissiengou (2014), Sosef et al. (2007). Bibliography
