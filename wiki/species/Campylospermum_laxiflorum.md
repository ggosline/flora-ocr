# *Campylospermum laxiflorum* (De Wild. & T.Durand) Tiegh. Planche 16

**Genus**: [[Campylospermum]]
**Family**: [[Ochnaceae]]
**Protologue**: Ouratea laxiflora De Wild. & T.Durand, Bull. Soc. Roy. Bot. Belgique 38(2) : 33 (1899)

## Description

J. Bot. (Morot) 16 : 197 (June 1902). Ouratea laxiflora De Wild. & T.Durand, Bull. Soc. Roy. Bot. Belgique 38(2) : 33 (1899). Ouratea macrobotrys Gilg, Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 33 : 268 (1903). Ouratea ituriensis Gilg & Mildbr., Wiss. Erg. Deut. Zentr.-Afr. Exped., Bot. 2 : 558 (1913). Ouratea flamignii De Wild., Rev. Zool. Afr. 7, Suppl. Bot . : B50 (1920). Ouratea bracteato-pedunculata De Wild., Ann. Soc. Sci. Bruxelles, sér. B 48, Compt. Rend . : 26 (1928). Ouratea dubiosa De Wild., Ann. Soc. Sci. Bruxelles , sér. B 48, compt. rend . : 28 (1928). Ouratea leroyana (Tiegh.) Keay, Kew Bull. 1953 : 81 (1953). Tree reaching 10 m in height; trunk up to 10 cm in diameter; branches with whitish-brown bark. Stipules persistent, narrowly triangular, (3–)4–10 mm long. Leaf: petiole 2–7 mm; blade elliptic-obovate or elliptic or sometimes narrowly elliptic-obovate to narrowly elliptic, (5–)13–23(–32) × (1.5–)6–9(–11) cm, ratio 2–3(–4.5), narrowly cuneate to rounded at base, apex gradually acuminate, papyraceous to subcoriaceous, flat or slightly bullate, bearing on the dry state minute tubercles above, margin serrulate; primary vein prominent on both faces, lateral veins in 7 to 18 pairs, prominent above but often in a groove, prominent below, tertiary venation scalariform, perpendicular to the primary vein, forming a dense network, distinct on both faces. Inflorescence terminal, branched, lax, main axis (5–)11–35(–50) cm; gemmate scales persistent, triangular, 5–10 mm long; racemes (1–)2 to 8, (4–)7–21(–23) cm, spreading; cymules distant by 1–2 cm, 1- to 12-flowered. Flower: pedicel (7–)10–18(–20) mm, articulate at 1–5(–7) mm from the base; sepals ovate, 5–7 × 2–3 mm in flower, 5–8 × 2.5–4 mm in fruit; petals obovate, 7–12 × 5–10 mm, attenuate at base, emarginate at apex; stamens: anthers 4–6 mm long; style 3–5 mm. Fruit: receptacle up to 6 × 5 mm; drupels 1 to 3 well developed per receptacle, ellipsoid, 4–10 × 4–6 mm; cotyledons incumbent, unequal with small external cotyledon.

## Distribution

from Senegal to Ghana and from southern Nigeria to the Democratic Republic of the Congo and northern Angola (Malanje); in Gabon, very common throughout the country

## Ecology

primary and secondary forest, on slopes, in swampy or inundated forest, riparian forest, on sandy soil; in Gabon, up to 1000 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1500 m. Taxonomic note: This species shares certain characteristics with C. reticulatum, notably the tertiary venation forming a dense network. But C. laxiflorum possesses minute tubercles on the blade often slightly bullate and persistent stipules (see also the note under C. reticulatum).
