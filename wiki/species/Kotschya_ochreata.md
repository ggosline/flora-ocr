# *Kotschya ochreata* (Taub.) J.Dewit & Duvign. var. ochreata

**Genus**: [[Kotschya]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Roy. Bot. Belg. 86 : 213 (1954)

## Synonyms

- Smithia ochreata Taub., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 23(1-2) : 191 (1896).
- Smithia bilingensis Micheli ex Pellegr., Fl. Mayombe, Mém. Soc. Linn. Normandie 27(2) : 87 (1924).

## Description

Shrub 0.8–3 m tall; branches, stipules, bracts, bracteoles and calyces pubescent. Stipules persistent, clasping the stem, lanceolate, 8–10 × 3–4 mm, membranous. Leaf: petiole 8–10 mm; rachis 50–65 mm; leaflets 20 to 50, in pairs mostly alternate, with linear-oblong blade, 10–12 × 2–3 mm, glabrous on both surfaces, ciliate, with 4 or 5 basal nerves. Inflorescence: subsessile racemes, sometimes condensed, 2–4 cm long; bracts oval, slightly falcate, 3–5 × 3 mm. Flower: pedicel 4–5 mm; bracteoles free, oval-falcate, ± 5 × 3 mm; calyx 3–5 mm long, lobes oval, rounded to square, subequal; corolla violaceous, standard obovate, 5–6 × 2–3 mm, emarginate, with a few setae in the notch, with a small appendage above the claw, wings 5 mm long, keel petals 6 × 1 mm, with unilateral appendage; stamens in tube ± 3 mm long; ovary silky, with 2 ovules. Legume with 2 articles ± 1.5 × 1.5 mm, hairy. Seed not seen.

## Distribution

from Sierra Leone to the Democratic Republic of the Congo and in Angola (Cabinda); in Gabon, known from Haut-Ogooué

## Ecology

waterside edges, marshy scrubland; in Gabon at approximately 500 m altitude, elsewhere at 0–2050 m

## Source note

Var. longipetala Hepper differs from the typical variety by the longer corolla. b: Dewit & Duvigneaud (1954), Hepper (1958), Walters et al. (2011). ibliography
