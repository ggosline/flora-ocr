# *Sabicea pilosa* Hiern (P.L. 34)

**Genus**: [[Sabicea]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Hiern, F.T.A. 3 : 76 (1877)

## Description

Slender vine creeping, or twining and climbing, exceeding 4 m in height. Internodes hairy then glabrescent reaching 15–24 cm, or very short on some small lateral branches. Stipules ± deflexed, glabrous above, subtriangular ovate 4–9 mm wide, ± acute. Petiole rather slender 7–40(60) mm, bristly with fine hairs. Blade subconcolorous pilose or hairy on both surfaces, elliptic, 7–14(17) × 2.5–6.5 cm; base acute, rarely somewhat obtuse; apex ± acuminate, sometimes sublanceolate. 11–15 pairs of secondary veins distinctly pubescent beneath.

PL. 34. — Sabicea speciosa K. Schum. (Letouzey 5275): 1, inflorescence. — S. pilosa Hiern: 2, flowering branch (N. Hallé 1685); 3, inflorescence; 4, stamen, and 5, style and disk (Zenker 2053); 6, fruiting inflorescence; 7, section of mature fruit; 8, seed (6 to 8, N. H. 1685).

Inflorescences flesh-red appearing often at ground level on nodes of creeping radiciferous defoliated stems, sometimes on ascending leafy or leafless stems, more rarely axillary on young elevated leafy apices. Peduncle pubescent ± slender 3–10 mm long. Involucre ± naviculate composed of 2 red thin ovate ± acute bracts, 7–15 mm long, moderately pilose, somewhat united-connate at the base. Other colored bracts surround the flowers. Sepals thin, ample, erect, flesh-red, 7–17 × 2.5–6 mm, lanceolate and pilose. Corolla pale yellow 20–25(30) mm long, pubescent, with short lobes ca. 2–2.5 mm. Stamens sessile inserted toward the lower third of the two thecae, 3–4 mm long, not apiculate, situated at the top of the tube. Style narrowly 5-lobed for 5–6 mm. Ovary very hairy. Annular disk 2 mm in diam.

Mature fruit bright red or pale pink spherical, 11–15 mm in diam., hairy, with 5 narrow compartments spaced in the white spongy pulp. Numerous small light brown angular seeds, 0.5 mm long.

## Type

G. Mann 1866, 1º lat. N, baie de l'île Corisco, récolté en 1862 (P! ex K)

## Discussion

Species known from Ivory Coast (F. Hallé 47 and 176), Cameroon and Gabon.

Very humid forest or secondary grassland.

Remedy for certain wounds according to Le Roy.

Gabonese material (or bordering):

Dinklage: 548, Sibang (Feb.) HBG.

Guiral: s.n., exploration of the Benito.

d'Ayem, 10 km SW of Ndjolé (fl. and fr. Apr.).

N. Hallé and G. Cours: 6063, Moumba, Etéké region (j. fr. May).

Jolly: 79, Boulou, env. of Libreville (fl. Feb.).

Mgr. Le Roy: s.n., Gabon.

G. Mann: 1866 (type, cf. above).

Soyaux: 220, Mondah, fide Wernh. (not seen).
