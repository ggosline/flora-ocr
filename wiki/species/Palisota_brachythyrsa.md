# *Palisota brachythyrsa* Mildbr. Figure 3(J)

**Genus**: [[Palisota]]
**Family**: [[Commelinaceae]]
**Protologue**: Notizbl. Bot. Gart. Berlin-Dahlem 9 : 251 (1925)

## Description

Perennial herb, erect, sometimes climbing to 3.5 m tall; stem branched at the apex, up to 3 cm diameter at the base, glossy, bright green or brownish-green, with scattered or sometimes dense reddish-brown pubescence. Leaves in whorls; pseudopetioles short, bordered by reddish-brown cilia; blade obovate-elliptic, 9–20 × 2.5–6 cm, base cuneate, apex abruptly acuminate, margins ciliate with reddish-brown hairs, upper surface glabrous, bright green, lower surface pale olive coloured, densely tomentose and covered with silvery brownish silky hairs. Inflorescence terminal, solitary; peduncle 2.5–3.5 cm; thyrse subspheric or broadly oblongoid, (1.5–)3.5–5 × (1–)1.5–3 cm, giving a disorganized impression, with some cincinni spreading and others, more mature, erect, glabrous, with scars of fallen flowers in brown or blackish spots arranged in lines then in zigzag (see also under Notes). Flower pale pink to violet; pedicel ± 2 mm; sepals glabrescent, purple, sometimes white, rounded at the apex, the upper one broadly ovate, ± 3 × 2 mm, the lateral ones oblong, 3–4 × 1.5 mm; petals distinctly longer than the sepals, 5 × 1.5 mm, rounded at the apex; stamens with filament 2 mm, one with strongly curved anther, 0.75 mm long, fist-shaped, the other 2 with flattened anther pressed against the petal, slightly narrowing towards the apex, ± 1 × 0.5 mm, with longitudinal slits; staminodes ± 2 mm long, composed of a filament with long fan-shaped hairs; ovary ovoid, ± 1.5 × 0.75 mm; style 3 mm, stigma penicillate. Fruit subglobose to ellipsoid, ± 8–12 × 8 mm, glossy, white and bearing at the apex a violet spot extending into 6 descending striae, then coral-red matte at maturity, with 5 to 7 seeds. Seed subconical, ribbed, 2.5–3 mm diameter, smooth, light brown, irregularly and weakly ± 8-ridged; hilum and embryotegium minute.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Central African Republic; in Gabon rare, in the Estuary, Upper Ogooué, Nyanga and Ogooué-Ivindo

## Ecology

gallery forests, mature forests; in Gabon as elsewhere from 400 to 970 m altitude. Flowering before 4 p.m. (Central African Republic). Flowering in January, February, May, and October to December; fruiting in January, May, August, and October to December

## Source note

The species is close to P. ambigua, highly variable, ranging from slender with slight mauve pubescence to robust and covered with erect rust-coloured hairs. An important difference appears to be the fruit colour: while the mature fruits of P. ambigua are dark purple and waxy, the mature fruits of P. brachythyrsa are always indicated as bright red or coral-red. This species also seems close to P. fadenii by its terminal and subglobose inflorescence and its bright red fruits, nevertheless this latter is distinguished from it by its hirsute inflorescence, wider, more massive, and with numerous cincinni.
