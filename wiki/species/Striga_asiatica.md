# *Striga asiatica* (L.) Kuntze Planche 43(A–B), 56(C)

**Genus**: [[Striga]]
**Family**: [[Scrophulariaceae]]
**Protologue**: Revis. gen. pl. 2 : 466 (1891)

## Synonyms

- Buchnera asiatica L., Sp. pl . 2 : 630 (1753).

## Description

Annual herb, hemiparasitic, (5–)10–15(–30) cm in height, densely hispid; stem quadrangular, channeled, simple or sparingly branched. Leaves opposite or sometimes alternate in the inflorescence, sessile; blade narrowly triangular-ovate, 8–16 × 0.5–0.9 mm, with entire margin. Inflorescence in lax spike. Flower sessile or subsessile; bracteoles < 2 mm in length; calyx with 10 crests (nerves), 6 × 2.5 mm, teeth 2 mm in length, acute; corolla dark red or rarely yellow, tube 6–9 mm in length, very slender, curved near the limb, upper lip emarginate, 1–1.5 mm in length, lower lip tripartite, lobes 4 mm in length. Fruit ovoid, 7 × 2 mm.

## Distribution

paleotropical species, very widespread in Africa and Asia; in Gabon, observed in Haut-Ogooué, Nyanga and Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology

cultivations, degraded savannas, rocky outcrops, fallows; harmful parasite of rice and maize; in Gabon, up to 500 m in altitude, in East Africa, up to 2480 m. Taxonomic note: The taxa of the S. asiatica complex, distinguished by Mohamed et al. (2001), are not accepted here, their distinction seeming artificial to us. According to us, S. lutea and S. hirsuta represent forms of the variable S. asiatica
