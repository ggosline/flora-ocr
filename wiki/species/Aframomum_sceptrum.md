# *Aframomum sceptrum* (Oliv. et Hanb.) K. Schum.
**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Aframomum]]
## Description
Herb to 2 m; blades c. 25 × 6 cm, glabrous, with bifid membranous ligule to 2 cm. A 3–4-flowered spike stands on a peduncle sometimes 30 cm. Pale-mauve flowers have a 6 cm crisped labellum with yellow throat. Fruit 6.5–7.5 cm.
