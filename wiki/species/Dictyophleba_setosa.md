# *Dictyophleba setosa* B.de Hoogh Planche 32 ; Figure 4(G)

**Genus**: [[Dictyophleba]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Jard. Bot. Natl. Belg. 59 : 220 (1989)

## Description

Liane reaching 30 m in height; branchlets hirsute with brown-red hairs. Stipules distinct, divided into linear segments, 0.5–2 cm long. Leaf: petiole 5–20 mm; limb narrowly obovate or sometimes narrowly elliptic to elliptic of ± to ± cm, cuneate at apex, more or less scabrous on the midvein and often also on the major veins above, scabrous to hirsute below; secondary veins 9 to 13 pairs. Inflorescence of ± to ± cm; cymes alternate, setulous, with several flowers; peduncle of ± cm scabrous or hirsute; bracts laciniate. Flower: pedicel 1–1.5 mm; bracteoles 1 to 3, 3.3–7.5 mm long, strigose; calyx with narrowly oblong lobes, 6.2–7.3 times as long as wide, 5.1–6.2 mm long, with strong erect hairs; corolla 9.5–16.5 mm long, white to cream-yellow, pale at apex in bud; tube ± times as long as calyx, ± mm long; lobes ± as long as wide, ± mm long, with marginal hairs of ± mm; stamens with anther 1.1–1.2 mm long; style 0.7–1.2 mm. Fruit 1.5–5 cm in diameter. Seed ovoid, ± mm long, angular. Distribution Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea and Gabon; in Gabon, Moyen-Ogooué, Ngounié
