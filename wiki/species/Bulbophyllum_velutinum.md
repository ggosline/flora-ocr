## Description

Pseudobulbs 0.9-4.2 × 1 cm, narrowly ovoid to subfusiform, 2-leaved. Leaves shortly petiolate 2-15.5 × 0.5-2 cm, narrowly lanceolate to oblanceolate, subacute to subobtuse. Inflorescence 3-28 cm, rachis subcylindric, <40-flowered. Flowers small to minute. Dorsal sepal yellowish or greenish, with yellow, orange or red marginal thickenings, acute to obtuse, obovate-lanceolate above a short ribbon-like basal part, external face generally covered with dark hairs; lateral sepals often partly red, obliquely triangular, acute, adaxial face finely papillose to sparsely pubescent. Petals linear-lanceolate, falcate, acute. Lip yellow or dark red (sometimes entire flower entirely red) broadly ovate, obtuse, recurved, thick, glabrous. – Pl. 84, p. 171.

## Distribution

Sierra Leone, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroun, Guinée équatoriale, Sao Tomé, Principe, Annobon, Gabon, Congo (Kinshasa)

NB. VERMEULEN (1987) distinguished two entities within B. falcatum var. velutinum, but did not name them. Their flowers are identical but the rachis of one is straight, clearly widened and flattened, while that of the other is pendant and cylindric: the latter occurs in Nigeria, Cameroon, Guinée Équatoriale, Principe, Gabon and Congo (Kinshasa).
