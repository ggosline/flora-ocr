Herbaceous plant 0.8-3 m tall; stem pubescent, strongly ribbed. Stipules very narrowly deltoid, pubescent, with acute apex. Petiole pubescent, canaliculate above, 1.5-3.5 cm long. Blade pinnatifid, (1)-3-5 lobed, glabrous above and pubescent below, bearing on both surfaces brown-coloured glands in the form of droplets very abundant on the upper face, each lobe narrowly elliptic, 5-12 × 0.4-1.8 cm, base of lateral lobes sometimes asymmetric, lateral margins toothed (except for the entire basal lobes) and apex acute ± mucronate. Venation palmate, 3-5 main veins weakly depressed above and prominent below; secondary veins, 7-16 pairs depending on the lobes, ascending, slightly arcuate, weakly prominent on both faces. Veinlets obscurely scalariform, indistinct above and very faintly distinct below.

Male inflorescences in complex racemes or panicles, axillary or terminal, many-flowered, 3.5-4 cm long; axes pubescent. Filaments pinkish, fleshy, perpendicular opposite to the flowers, pubescent, 3-4 mm long. Male floral bud ellipsoid, 3 mm long, with imbricate sepals. Bracts lanceolate, pubescent externally, apex acute. Male flowers pentamerous, actinomorphic, apetalous. Sepals free from one another, pubescent externally and glabrous internally, papyraceous and perpendicular transparent, elliptic, 3 × 1.5 mm, apex acute or obtuse. Stamens 5, episepals, 3 mm long; filaments slender, short, 0.5 mm long; anther of 2 locules with longitudinal dehiscence, 2.5 mm long. Ovary absent. Female inflorescences in spikes, axillary or terminal; very numerous bracts, narrowly lanceolate to lanceolate, ranging from 0.5 to 1.5 cm in size, pubescent externally. Female flower zygomorphic, monomerous, apetalous. Sepal 1, very asymmetric, rolled, pubescent externally and glabrous internally, 3 mm long. Ovary superior, glabrous, flattened, broadly ellipsoid or orbicular, 1 mm in diameter; 2 stigmas free, rose-reddish, bearing strong papillae sometimes uncinate, flattened, 5.5 mm long, far exceeding the sepal.

Achene ± enclosed in the persistent calyx, 4 × 3 mm, flattened transversely, marked by 2 lateral ribs. Seed broadly ellipsoid, keeled dorsally and inflated ventrally. PL. 14, p. 57.

## Type

s. c. in herb. Linné 1177 (2), s.l. (holo-, LINN)

## Discussion

This species occurs under very varied climates ranging from temperate to tropical zones. In Gabon, it is cultivated or subspontaneous.

## Vernacular names

## Uses

The entire plant is intoxicating and narcotic. The glands of the stems and leaves contain a resinous substance containing two volatile essences: cannabene accompanied by a hydride of cannabene and hashischine. In Gabon, the flowering tops and those already in the process of fruiting as well as the leaves are smoked alone or mixed with tobacco. They cause torpor and pleasant sensations but, sometimes also, hallucinations or furious delirium. The narcotic flowering tops have a particular odour
