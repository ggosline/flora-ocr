# *Calanthe sylvatica* (Thouars) Lindl.

**Genus**: [[Calanthe]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Gen. Sp. Orchid. Pl. : 250 (1833) ; FTWA : 226 (1968) ; FAC 1 : 264 (1984) ; FCAM 2 : 330 (2001)

## Description

– BASIONYM: Centrosis sylvatica Thouars, Hist. Orchid. (Fl., Iles Austr. Afr.): tab. gen., fig. 35, 36 (1822). – TYPE: Du Petit Thouars s.n., Ile Maurice, holo-P! – Calanthe natalensis (Rchb. f.) Rchb. f., Bonplandia 4: 322 (1856).

– Calanthe corymbosa Lindl., J. Linn. Soc., Bot. 6: 129 (1862).

– Calanthe sanderiana Rolfe, Gard. Chron., ser. 3: 396 (1892).

– Calanthe delphinioides Kraenzl., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 17: 55 (1893).

– Calanthe volkensii Rolfe, FTA 7: 46 (1897).

– Calanthe neglecta Schltr., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 53: 570 (1915).

Br.: A, lateral view; B, anterior view; C, view of the base, labellum removed; D, anterior view, anther removed; E, remainder of the rostellum, anterior view; F, pollinarium, various aspects; G, natural position of the viscidium and caudicles in the rostellum, anterior view (A-G, ex SZLACHETKO & MARGOŃSKA, 2001).

– Calanthe stolzii Schltr., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 53: 569 (1915).

– Calanthe schliebenii Mansf., Notizbl. Bot. Gart. Berlin 11: 808 (1933).

Pseudobulbs cylindro-conical. Leaves 15-45 × 5-13 cm, lanceolate to oblong-elliptic, acute. Inflorescence < 72 cm, erect, axis very pubescent, 10-25-flowered. Flowers white, rose-purple, labellum darker or purple with white to orange callus. Dorsal sepal acute, pubescent on external surface, ovate-lanceolate to broadly lanceolate; lateral sepals obliquely ovate-lanceolate or triangular-lanceolate. Petals lanceolate to oblanceolate, glabrous. Labellum three-lobed from the base, lateral lobes small, sometimes rudimentary, semi-orbicular to obliquely ovate, recurved upward in natural position, median lobe flabellate to obovate, emarginate, callus fleshy verruculose with 3 ridges of which the longest is that of the centre. Spur pendant, generally swollen near the middle. – Pl. 51, p. 109.

## Distribution

widely distributed in Africa: Guinea, Sierra Leone, Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Fernando Po, Sao Tome, Annobon, Gabon, Congo (Brazzaville), Congo (Kinshasa), Rwanda, Burundi, Angola, Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, Zambia, Malawi, Zimbabwe, Mozambique, South Africa, Swaziland, Madagascar, Mascarene Islands, Comoros, Reunion, tropical Asia

## Ecology

alt. 900-1700 m. Herbaceous terrestrial in the shade of evergreen and riparian forest, often near watercourses and in floodable places
