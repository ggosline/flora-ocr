# *Baissea leonensis* Benth. Planche 20 ; Figure 3(A-C)

**Genus**: [[Baissea]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Hook., Niger Fl. : 452 (1849)

## Description

, syn. nov. Liana reaching 40 m in height; branches puberulent or glabrescent. Leaf: petiole 3–22 mm, puberulent, glabrescent, with or without colleters above; blade obovate to narrowly oblong, 1–6 times as long as wide, 1.5–15 × 0.8–6 cm, cuneate or sometimes rounded or more rarely decurrent at base, acute to apiculate at apex; secondary nerves (6)10 to 17 pairs, often red beneath, the largest puberulent or glabrous on both faces, tertiary nervation scalariform, forming most often right angles with the midvein; domatia present. Inflorescence axillary and also terminal, lax to sometimes condensed, cymes pluriflorous, peduncle 3–8 mm with dense pubescence. Scented flower; pedicel 1–5 mm; calyx with lobes 1–3.4 mm long, variously puberulent on the outside, glabrous on the inside or sometimes puberulent at the apex, with 3 to 6 colleters (especially 5) at the base; corolla with funnel-shaped or sometimes slightly campanulate tube, 2.1–4.2 mm long, white, sometimes tinged or streaked with purple on the outside, with reddish streaks on the inside, with very short blackish or dark red pubescence on the outside, with tufts of hairs on the inside, otherwise glabrous, with narrowly ovate or oblong lobes, sometimes ribbon-like, 1–4.2 times as long as the tube, 1.5–6 times as long as wide, 1–7 × 0.5–2 mm, white, often tinged or streaked with purple, with very short irregular pubescence, dark brown or dark reddish-brown on the outer right edge, the left edge glabrous, glabrous on the inside; stamens inserted 0.7–1.2 mm above the base of the tube, with the apex 0.7–1.2 mm below the throat, anther 2–3 mm long; ovary with variable dark brown pubescence, rarely glabrescent. Fruit: follicles 15–105 cm × 4–14 mm, slightly puberulent. Distribution from Senegal to Uganda and to Angola, in Gabon, Estuary, Middle Ogooué or Ngounié, Nyanga, Ogooué, Ivindo, Ogooué, Lolo, Ogooué, Maritime and Woleu Ntem. Ecology Primary and secondary forest in Gabon, from sea level to 750 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1500 m (Ayanabo, Gabon).

## Source note

The characters used by van Dilst (1995) to separate B. longipetiolata from B. leonensis are not robust. The type of B. dichotoma Stapf – a synonym of B. leonensis in van Dilst (1995) – is almost equivalent to that of B. longipetiolata for the length of the petiole and the form of the flowers. The "pits" in the corolla tube of B. longipetiolata can also be found in certain other specimens of B. leonensis, and the length of the corolla lobes can vary in B. leonensis, even in a single inflorescence. Consequently, B. longipetiolata is included here in B. leonensis. B: van Dilst (1995). BIBLIOGRAPHY
