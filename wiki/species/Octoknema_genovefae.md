# *Octoknema genovefae* Villiers

**Family**: [[Octoknemaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Octoknema]]

Bull. I.F.A.N. **34** (1): 5, tab. I (1972).

## Description

**Habit**: shrub 2 m tall with erect stem. Crown spreading horizontally. Old branchlets with pale yellow, longitudinally wrinkled and weakly verrucose rhytidome. Young branchlets densely pubescent with dark brown stellate hairs.

**Leaves**: petiole very densely pubescent, cylindrical, 1.2–2.5 cm long, thickened at apex. Blade sparsely pubescent on upper face, densely pubescent on lower face, dark blackish-green above, light brown ± reddish matt or shiny beneath, elliptic to oblong-elliptic, sometimes ± lanceolate, 26–14 × 5–11 cm; base rounded, peltate; apex obtuse, acuminate. Midrib concave on upper face, prominent and pubescent on lower face. Secondary nerves 8–11 pairs, ascending, weakly arcuate, anastomosing 0.5–1.5 mm from the margin, concave above, prominent and pubescent beneath. Nervules forming dense reticulum, prominent on both faces.

**Male inflorescence**: lax raceme of fascicles, axillary, solitary, pendant. Rachis approximately 30 cm long, densely pubescent with stellate hairs. Fascicles of 2–4 flowers. Male flower bud globose, 1.75 mm in diameter, petals valvate. Male flowers pentamerous, actinomorphic. Pedicel 1.25 mm long, pubescent. Sepals absent. Petals pubescent externally, glabrous internally, broadly ovate, 1.5 mm long, apex obtuse and papillose internally. Stamens 5, epipetalous, 0.75 mm long; filament short, 0.25 mm long; anther 0.75 mm long. Ovary reduced through abortion. Style with 5 grooves, apex truncate, 0.5 × 0.5 mm.

**Female inflorescence**: short spike, 2 cm long. Female flower bud globose, densely pubescent with stellate hairs. Sepals absent. Petals 5, free from each other, densely pubescent externally, glabrous internally, elliptic, 1 × 0.5 mm, fleshy, apex obtuse, weakly papillose on inner face. Staminodes 5, claviform. Ovary semi-inferior, glabrous; style terminating in a digitate stigma.

**Fruit**: ellipsoid drupe, pubescent externally with stellate hairs, yellow-green becoming brilliant red at maturity, apex apiculate. Endocarp woody, emitting 10 short laminae on its inner face, tapering or disappearing at the fruit ends. Seed ellipsoid with 10 strong longitudinal furrows.

## Types

**Holotype**: *Letouzey 4074*, south of Zingui, 40 km ESE Kribi, Cameroon (P).

## Distribution

Known from Cameroon and the Republic of the Congo. To be sought in Gabon.

## Notes

**Epithet and range corrected (2011).** Villiers published this species as *O. genovefae* (1972); the Vol 20 heading was read by OCR as "genovefa" and the page has been renamed. More importantly, Gosline & Malécot (2011) give the range as **Cameroon only** — river basins from Yaoundé to the coast, possibly also Rio Muni — so it **does not occur in Gabon**, contrary to its inclusion in the Flore du Gabon. See [[Octoknema]].

Revision: Gosline G. & Malécot V. (2011). A monograph of Octoknema (Octoknemaceae — Olacaceae s.l.). Kew Bulletin 66: 367–404.


*De Wilde 2814* differs from other specimens in having less dense pubescence on the lower face.

Vernacular name: *nom avulu* (Yaoundé).
