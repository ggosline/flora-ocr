# *Cyperus polystachyos* Rottb. Planche 76

**Genus**: [[Cyperus]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Descr. pl. rar . : 39 (1772)

## Description

Pycreus polystachyos (Rottb.) P.Beauv., Fl. Oware 2 : 48, t. 86, fig. 2 (1816). Perennial herb, caespitose, with short woody rhizome, rarely with thin stolons; young plants resembling annuals; stems (8–)20–80 cm × (0.5–)1–3 mm, trigonous, glabrous. Leaf: sheath purple; blade 5–50 cm × 1.5–5 mm, plane or canaliculate, scabrous on the margin and central nerve at least near the apex. Inflorescence a contracted anthela formed of sessile fascicles with numerous crowded spikelets, or a looser anthela with ± numerous groups of pedunculate fascicles arranged in a brush-like manner, or more rarely inflorescence with spikelets loosely arranged; fascicles often of 10–30 spikelets; involucral bracts 3–7, foliaceous, spreading, the largest 10–35 cm × 2–5 mm. Spikelet linear–lanceolate, 5–20 × 0.8–2.2 mm, yellowish-brown to brown, with rachis straight or zigzagged; glumes ovate, 1.5–2.2 mm, pale brown, yellowish-brown, golden or pale reddish-brown; central nerve usually with 1–3 longitudinal ridges, sometimes green, ending at the acute apex or slightly exceeding it. Flower with 2–3 stamens; style with 2 branches. Achene oblong to cylindric, 0.8–1.2 × 0.3–0.6 mm, pale becoming greyish-brown to dark metallic grey at maturity, finely papillose.

## Distribution

pantropical; in Gabon common along the coast, in Estuaire and Ogooué-Maritime and rare or poorly known elsewhere (11 collections)

## Ecology

seasonally flooded herbaceous places, lake shores and marshes, often bordering mangroves and lagoons and also in disturbed areas; in Gabon, at less than 100 m altitude, up to 2400 m in East Africa

## Vernacular names

bunchy flatsedge (angl.). Note taxonomique : très variable, particulièrement dans la taille et la densité des épillets. Elle est facilement identifiée par son inflorescence en pinceau. La variété laxiflora Benth. a des anthèles moins denses et non en pinceau, mais elle n’a pas encore été trouvée au Gabon. B : Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al. (2010), Hooper & Napper (1972), ibliographie Lye (1997)
