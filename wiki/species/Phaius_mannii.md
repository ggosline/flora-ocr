# *Phaius mannii* Rchb.f.

**Genus**: [[Phaius]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Otia Bot. Hamburg. 2 : 118 (1878)

## Description

Leaves 15–21 × 5–7 cm, elliptic-lanceolate to broadly lanceolate, acute. Inflorescence < 33 cm, axis glabrous, 5–10-flowered. Flowers pinkish or reddish, labellum whitish. Tepals glabrous. Dorsal sepal acute, oblong to narrowly lanceolate; lateral sepals obliquely lanceolate to oblong-lanceolate. Petals obliquely lanceolate to oblong-obovate, acute. Labellum channelled, very papillose at the base, apical half clearly trilobed; median lobe oblong to elliptic, subacute, somewhat undulate along the margins; lateral lobes rhombic to triangular, acute, shorter than the median lobe. Spur gradually attenuate towards the acute apex. – Pl. 53, p. 113.

## Distribution

Congo (Kinshasa), Gabon

## Ecology

alt. 0–750 m. Herbaceous terrestrial plant growing in the shade of forest
