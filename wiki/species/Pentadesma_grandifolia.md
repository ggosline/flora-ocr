# *Pentadesma grandifolia* Baker f. Planche 11

**Genus**: [[Pentadesma]]
**Family**: [[Clusiaceae]]
**Protologue**: P. exelliana Staner, Bull. Jard. Bot. État Bruxelles 13 : 151 (1934)

## Description

in Rendle et al., Cat. Pl. Oban: 8 (1913). P. exelliana Staner, Bull. Jard. Bot. État Bruxelles 13: 151 (1934). P. reyndersii Spirlet, Bull. Jard. Bot. État Bruxelles 29: 356 (1959). Tree up to 30 m tall; trunk up to 80 cm in diameter; bark rough, greenish or greyish, spotted with white, green or black, inner bark reddish or reddish-brown; branchlets angular or longitudinally furrowed. Leaf: petiole 1–2 cm; blade oblong to elliptic or obovate or sometimes oblanceolate, (5–)7–28 × (2–)3–9 cm, cuneate at base, obtuse to shortly acuminate at apex, coriaceous, with black glandular punctations below; midvein prominent below, lateral veins numerous, indistinct above, with 2–4 parallel secretory canals between each pair. Inflorescence with 1–5 flowers. Flower inclined, cupuliform; pedicel 1–3 cm; sepals unequal to subequal, the outer ones oval-oblong, 1.5–4(–5) × 1.5–2.5(–3) cm, the inner ones oblong, (3–)4–5(–6) × 2–3(–4) cm; petals similar to inner sepals, white or cream; staminodial phalanges with filaments 2–3 cm fused for 2–15 mm, anthers 8–15(–20) mm in length; discal glands pyramidal, 3–4 mm in height and width; ovary (3–)5(–6)-locular, globose, about 1 cm in diameter, with 2 series of 2–6 ovules per locule; style up to 2 cm, the lobes spreading and up to 5 mm in length. Fruit conical to ovoid, 5–7 × 4.5–6 cm, ridged, brown. Seeds 1–4 per fruit, subglobose to angular, 3–5 × 2–3 cm, light brown.

## Distribution

southern Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Rwanda; in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Haut-Ogooué, Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

primary and secondary forest, riparian forest or forest galleries, also in submontane forests on rocky ridges; in Gabon, at 50–750 m altitude, elsewhere up to 2100 m

## Uses

The wood is of good quality. As in P. butyracea, a vegetable fat is extracted from the seeds which serves as cooking fat. Taxonomic note: We have followed here the view of Meer (1965), considering P. exelliana and P. reyndersii as synonyms of P. grandifolia. We observed, as he did, that the length of the outer sepals is variable during flower development, whereas Bamps (1970a) uses this character as a primary distinction between the species. There is furthermore confusion concerning the number of ovules per locule, also used as a principal character for distinguishing between these taxa.
