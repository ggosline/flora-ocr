# *Didymosalpinx konguensis* (Hiern) Keay

**Genus**: [[Didymosalpinx]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Protologue**: R. W. J. Keay, Bull. J. B. Brux. 28 : 62 (1958)

## Synonyms

- Gardenia konguensis Hiern, F.T.A. 3 : 104 (1877).

## Description

Shrub 1 to 3 m tall. Lateral branches with internodes 3–7 cm long, and nodes enlarged, flattened, convex above, slightly concave or with a small pit on the side facing downward. Stipules deltoid acute, 3 to 5 mm tall. Petiole 5–10 mm, distinctly canaliculate. Blade varnished olivaceous concolorous, narrowly elliptic oblong, 10–20 × 2.5–5 cm, acute at base, very acutely acuminate at apex, margins slightly bordered. 7–12 pairs of secondary veins distinctly raised as well as the midvein on the upper face. No axillary domatia.

Flowers subter­minal solitary. Pedicels 10–15 mm. Ovary ca. 6 × 2.5 mm; calycinal limb tubular for 2–3 mm, with triangular acute teeth 3–5 × 2 mm. Corolla with tube 60 × 25 mm, lobes ca. 15 × 10 mm.

Fruit fusiform smooth, orange, 52 × 19 mm, pentagonal toward the apex, and crowned by the calycinal limb 11 mm long, lobed for 7 mm. Endocarp varnished and pale yellow inside when dry. 8 (11) seeds per fruit, 10–15 mm long, 3–5 mm thick.

## Type

Mann 1784 (holotype K!, isotype P), 1 $ ^{o} $ lat. N, Rio Muni

## Discussion

This species is known only from two collections from the Cristal Mountains; it is very close to D. abbeokutæ (Hiern) Keay, the type of the genus, widespread from Guinea to Cameroon and the Congo (Central Forest District and Ubangi-Uele). The distinguishing characters between these two species are rather weak.

## Specimens examined

N. Hallé 757, La Nkoulounga (fr. juill.)
