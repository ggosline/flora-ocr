# *Habenaria genuflexa* Rendle

**Genus**: [[Habenaria]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: J. Bot. 33 : 279 (1895)

## Synonyms

- Habenaria confusa Rolfe, FTA 7 : 241 (1898).
- Habenaria stenoloba Schltr., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 38 : 1 (1905).
- Habenaria anaphysema sensu Summerh., FWTA, ed. 1, 2: 412 (1936) non Rchb. f.

## Description

Stem 20–100 cm, glabrous. Leaves 7–29 × 0.5–2 (2.5) cm, linear or narrowly lanceolate, acute. Inflorescence 5–29 cm, rather lax, 6–40-flowered. Flowers small, resupinate, green, petals and column white. Dorsal sepal narrowly elliptic or oblong, concave, obtuse or shortly apiculate; lateral sepals obliquely obovate-oblong to obovate-elliptic, with a short lateral apicule. Petals bipartite nearly from the base. Labellum tripartite from an undivided base, finely papillose, base slender, apex thickened; median lobe linear, lateral lobes linear, acute, curved upward. Spur inclined, geniculate near the middle, swollen and twisted 1 or 2 times above the apical 1/3–1/2, apex obtuse. – Pl. 17, p. 44.

## Distribution

Senegal, Guinea, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Côté d'Ivoire, Ghana, Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon, Congo (Brazzaville), Central African Republic, Sudan, Congo (Kinshasa), Burundi, Angola, Uganda, Tanzania, Zambia

## Ecology

alt. 80–2100 m. Terrestrial herb on pure sand or fine greyish clay and humid soil, in savanna and humid or inundable short grassland
