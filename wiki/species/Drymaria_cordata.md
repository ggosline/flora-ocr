# *Drymaria cordata* (L.) Willd. ex Roem. et Schult.

**Family**: [[Caryophyllaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Drymaria]]

## Description

Slender glabrescent annual 20–35 cm, initially prostrate then erect, rooting sporadically from shoots. Opposite broadly ovate leaves are cordate below, 1.5–2.5 × 1.5–2 cm, with laciniate c. 1 mm stipules. Loose long-peduncled cymes bear small green flowers; five 3 mm sepals surround smaller white bifid petals and included stamens. Capsule c. 2 mm, 3-valved, with three or sometimes one flattened brownish seed.

## Ecology and uses

Humid forest understorey, cultivation margins and villages. Pantropical. Le Testu reported use against leprosy.
