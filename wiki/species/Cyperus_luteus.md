# *Cyperus luteus* Boeckeler Planche 37

**Genus**: [[Cyperus]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Linnaea 38 : 370 (1874)

## Synonyms

- Mariscus foliosus C.B.Clarke in Dyer, Fl. trop. Afr. 8 : 399 (1902).

## Description

Perennial herb, rather robust, with stems thickened at the base and arising from a short or poorly distinct rhizome; stems 30‒100 cm × 1‒6 mm, trigonous, glabrous. Leaves all near the base; sheath green or pale violet at the apex, dark violet near the base; main blades 20‒50 cm × 3‒10 mm, plane, scabrous at least on the margin and principal veins. Inflorescence as a simple anthela, broad up to approximately 20 cm, formed of one sessile spike and 1‒11 spikes on rays 0.5‒11 cm long (sometimes all spikes subsessile); involucral bracts 5‒9, foliaceous, erect or spreading, the principal one 15‒40 cm × 3‒8 mm; spikes 1‒3 × 1‒3 cm, with 10‒30 spikelets spreading. Spikelet linear to linear–lanceolate, 5‒17 × 1‒2 mm, with 5‒10 flowers, falling entire at maturity; glumes ovate to lanceolate–elliptic, 3.5‒6 mm long, green with or without a golden-brown or reddish tint and an indistinct central nerve. Flower: with 3 stamens; style with 3 branches. Achene linear–ellipsoid, 2.5‒3.5 × 0.6‒0.9 mm, triangular, grey to brown, with smooth or finely papillose surface.

## Distribution

widely distributed in tropical Africa and Madagascar; apparently rare in Gabon, found only in Ogooué-Maritime

## Ecology

in grasslands and open forests, often at forest margins and in secondary forests, also a weed of cultivated land and roadsides; at 0‒2200 m altitude. Taxonomic note: close to C. cyperoides, but this is often a larger plant with broader leaves, larger spikes and longer glumes. Bibliography: Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al. (2010), Hooper & Napper (1972).
