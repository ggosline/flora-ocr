# *Opilia congolana* Baillon

**Genus**: [[Opilia]]
**Family**: [[Opiliaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Linn. Paris 2 : 872 (1890)

## Synonyms

- Urobotrya minutiflora STAPF, Journ. Linn. Soc. 37 : 90 (1904).

## Description

— O. minutiflora (Stapf) Engl., Bot. Jahrb. 43 : 172 (1909).

— U. angustifolia STAPF, l. c.

— O. angustifolia (Stapf) Engl., l. c. : 171 (1909). — SLEUMER, Pflanzenfam., ed. 2, 16 b : 38 (1935).

— U. stapfiana STAPF, l. c.

— O. stapfiana (Hutch. & Dalz.). SLEUMER, l. c.

— O. macrocarpa Pierre & Engl., Bot. Jahrb. 43 : 172 (1909). — SLEUMER, l. c.

Shrub 1–3 m tall with branchlets longitudinally striated. Stem brown or yellowish-green. Petiole short, 5–10 mm long, flattened on the upper surface, wrinkled on the lower surface. Blade glabrous on both surfaces, yellow-green or greyish-brown above, green or yellowish-green beneath, oblong, broadly oblong, elliptic or narrowly lanceolate, 3.5–8 × 10–22 cm, cuneate or attenuate at the base; apex acuminate-obtuse. Primary vein prominent on the upper surface, prominent-grooved on the lower surface. Secondary veins ascending, slightly arcuate, with anastomoses scarcely visible, prominent on both surfaces. Tertiary veins in a very loose reticulum, very slightly prominent on the upper surface.

Inflorescence in a raceme of fascicles, axillary, equal to or exceeding the leaf length. Fascicle of 2–4 flowers. Floral bud globose, pedicellate. Pedicel 5–11 mm long. Flower pentamerous, yellow. Petals pubescent on the outer surface, reflexed, free from one another, oblong-lanceolate to lanceolate, obtuse at the apex. Stamens epipetalous, free from one another, gynobasic; filament slender, 6–8 mm long; anthers ellipsoid with 2 longitudinal dehiscence slits, medifix. Disk present as ± connate glandular lobes. Ovary conical, one-locular, containing one ovule pendant from an erect central placenta. Stigma truncate.

Drupe ellipsoid, yellowish-red when fresh, swollen at the base, 1.5–2 × 1.5 cm. Fruiting pedicel 2 cm long. Endocarp woody, longitudinally grooved. PL. 37, 7–14, p. 171.

## Type

Thollon s. n., Congo (P!)

This species occurs from Sierra Leone to Congo.
