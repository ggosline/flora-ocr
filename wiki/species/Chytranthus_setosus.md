# *Chytranthus setosus* Radlkofer

**Genus**: [[Chytranthus]]
**Family**: [[Sapindaceae]]
**Protologue**: Sitzungsber. Bayer. Akad. Wiss. München 38 : 240 (1890); in Engl., Pflanzenreich, Sapindaceae : 785 (1932); Keay, FWTA, ed. 2, 1 : 717 (1958); Hauman, Fl. Congo 9 : 352 (1960)

## Description

in Engl., Pflanzenreich, Sapindaceae : 785 (1932); Keay, FWTA, ed. 2, 1 : 717 (1958); Hauman, Fl. Congo 9 : 352 (1960).

Monocaulous shrub 2.5–4 m tall; trunk reaching 10 cm in diameter; apex pubescent; upper internode 10–18 mm in diameter. Leaves 80 cm with 4–6 pairs of leaflets; petiole 20–45 cm; rachis pubescent and longitudinally striated; petiolules opposite or subopposite, pubescent, 7–13 mm long; leaflets oblong or ± narrowly obovate, 20–40 × 8–15 cm, the upper ones larger; blade discolorous, often brown-russet olivaceous when dry; base ± obtuse; apex acuminate; midvein pubescent above; yellowish hairs on the veins and blade on the lower surface; 18–22 pairs of secondary veins; blade sometimes slightly embossed between them.

Male and female inflorescences appearing to arise from different plants; the male ones are erect, only 5–6 cm long, whitish-green, with flowers 5–6 mm in diameter; 5 sepals; 5 pubescent petals, 4 × 2.2 mm, with a relatively short ligule slightly emarginate at the apex, without appendicle, base narrowed into a claw; crescent-shaped fleshy disc, subpentagonal, completed by a remarkable fleshy lobe isolated between its two ends; an annular disc has even once been observed; 8 stamens of the same length as the petals, equal to one another, with pubescent filament; anther 1.5 × 0.8 mm, pubescent hirsute on the margins of the locules.

Female inflorescences ± pendulous, 10–25 cm long, ochraceus-green; rachis grooved, densely puberulent. Flowers exceeding 7 mm in diameter; sepals and petals as before; disc similar but the crescent is larger and narrower; 8 stamens appearing devoid of pollen; gynoecium about 8 × 4 mm, hirsute with 8 strong grooves; 8 uniovulate locules; stigma capitate.

Fruit 5–6 cm in diameter when dry, pubescent, with 8 (–9) alate ribs separated by grooves; each wing is slightly widened toward the apex of the fruit; the latter is conical and grooved, the base attenuate. Seed compressed, 2 × 1.2 cm, the greatest width situated toward the apex of the fruit; hilum 1 cm long and narrow. PL. 25, p. 107.

## Discussion

The figure by ENGLER (Pflanzenwelt Afr. 3, 2 : 247, 1921) reproduced by RADLKOFER presents some elements that appear doubtful to us. Detail H shows an ovary with 8 ribs and 10 locules, whereas normally a rib covers only one locule. The fruit J is ovoid in outline (and not obovoid), its section K presents 6 very thick ribs and 6 locules (and not 8 alate ribs and 8 locules); the conspecificity of this fruit appears doubtful to us. Moreover, the remarkable character of the bipartite or sometimes annular disc does not appear to have been described hitherto; it is, however, clearly visible in the figure by N. HALLÉ in AKÉ ASSI (Et. Flor. Côté d'Ivoire, Paris 1963, tab. 8).

Ivory Coast (N. Hallé 532 bis), Nigeria, Cameroon, Central African Republic (Tisserant 1990; Eaux et Forêts 3117), Zaire as far as Yangambi and Mayombe. In ombrophilous forests. To be sought in Gabon where the species has never yet been collected.
