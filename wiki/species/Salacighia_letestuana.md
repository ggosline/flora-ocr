# *Salacighia letestuana* (Pellegrin) Blakelock

## Description

PL. 5. - Salacighia letestuana (Pellegrin) Blakelock : 1, sterile branch ; 2, floriferous branch ; 3, 4, buds, diam. 3.5 and 4.9 mm ; 5, flower, diam. 11 mm ; 6, petal 6.2 mm long ; 7, detail of petal margin ; 8, androecium in young bud, disc 3.2 mm diam. ; 9, flower without petals ; 10, 11, disc, diam. 4.1 mm ; 12, disc seen from below (perianth removed) ; 13-16, stamens ; 17-18, opened locule and ovary section ; 19-21, fruits, diam. 20-35 mm ; 22, fruit section (1, 3, 9, 11-14, 16-18, Le Testu 1907 ; 2, 20, Tisserant 1006, Centrafrique ; 4, 6-8, 10, 15, Letouzey 10585 ; 5, 21, 22, Hallé N. 554, Côté-d'Ivoire ; 19, Hallé N. & Villiers J.-F. 4934).

Large liana exceeding 10 or even 30 m ; wood up to more than 10 cm in diameter. Branches reddish-brown, rough, lenticellate. Leaves opposite or ± alternate, with petiole 7-15 mm. Blade elliptic 7-18 × 3-9 cm, subcoriaceous, bordered, entire (or denticulate in seedlings) ; base cuneate or obtuse ; apex often with an acumen 3-10 mm. Secondary veins 5-8 pairs, very prominent when dry especially beneath ; veinlets flabellate and prominent.

Floriferous branches without leaves, 60 cm or more long, bearing ± pendulous fertile branches, sometimes very long, 140 cm, with numerous pauci- to multiflorous glomérules. Buds large, diameter 4-5 mm, truncate at base, subacute apiculate at apex. Pedicel 6-20 mm. Flower fragrant, whitish-yellow or pale green. Calyx tearing into 2-3 irregular lobes. Petals (4-)5(-7) suborbicular or somewhat oblong 4-6 mm with fimbriate margin, inserted on a short claw. Disc green or pale cream, in a solid subpentagonal cushion, 2-4 mm in diameter, with 6 apical pits. Stamens 3(4) distinctly exceeding the pistil before dehiscence ; pollen orange. Ovary 3(4)-locular ; 2 superposed ovules per locule.

Fruit globose, smooth or bosselated, sometimes with 3(4) longitudinal furrows, 1.8-3.5 cm in diameter, red-orange at maturity, with pedicel at most 2 cm long. Seeds 1-6 subglobose, approximately 10 × 7 mm. - Pl. 5, p. 19.

## Type

Le Testu 1907, Gabon (holo-, P!; iso-, BR!)

## Discussion

From Côté-d'Ivoire to Zaïre (Kivu), Centrafrique and Cabinda, the distribution being interrupted from Ghana to Dahomey. This species is scattered and found here and there, in dry or wet dense forest, in rocky station or in raphiale, at various altitudes from 10 to 1 000 m.
