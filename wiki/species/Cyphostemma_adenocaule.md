# *Cyphostemma adenocaule* (Steud.) Descoings

**Genus**: [[Cyphostemma]]
**Family**: [[Vitaceae]]
**Protologue**: Descoings, Not. Syst. 16 : 120 (1960); Natural. monspel. 18 : 218 (1967). —

## Synonyms

- Cissus adenocaulis Steud. ex A. Rich., Fl. Abyss. I : III (1847).
- Vitis adenocaulis (Steud. ex A. Rich.) Miq. Ann. Mus. Bot. Lugd. Bat. 1:79 (1864).
- Vitis tenuicaulis (Hook. f.) Bak., F.T.A. I : 404 (1868), pro parte.

## Description

Herbaceous climbing vine, with strongly swollen rootstock and tuberous roots. Stems cylindrical, 1.5-2 mm in diameter, glabrous or glabrescent, sometimes with scattered glandular hairs; internodes 3-6 cm long. Tendrils cylindrical striated, glabrous or very sparsely pubescent, bifurcate several times; bracts oval oblong, obtuse, 0.7-1 mm long, glabrous. Stipules oval oblong, somewhat falciform, more or less gradually attenuate at apex and acute, 6-8 mm long, 1.5-3 mm wide, glabrescent or somewhat pubescent on the margins and toward the apex. Leaves compound pedalate 5-foliolate. Petiole and petiolules more or less cylindrical, glabrescent or more or less pubescent with sometimes scattered glandular hairs; petiole and median petiolule 1.5-4.5 cm long; petiolules of lateral leaflets 0.6-1 cm long. long, 1-4 cm wide, oval to obovate, more or less elliptic, with cuneate, rounded or subcordate base, often oblique, apex obtuse or cuneate, briefly acuminate and acute; lateral leaflets progressively smaller; margins with few crenulations terminated by a short point; limb glabrous on both faces, primary veins glabrous, glabrescent or weakly pubescent; venation pinnate; texture thin.

Inflorescences 2-15 cm long, in lax many-flowered cymes; peduncle 1.5-6 cm long; axes cylindrical, glabrescent or more or less densely pubescent, sometimes with a few glandular hairs; bracts and bracteoles oval oblong to deltoid, 0.2-0.6 mm long, glabrous or pubescent. Flowers 3-3.5 mm long. Pedicel cylindrical, 1-2 mm long, more or less densely pubescent. Calyx cupuliform, 0.4 mm high, glabrous to pubescent. Corolla long conical then widely flared in the upper part, rounded or somewhat flattened at apex, glabrous to pubescent; petals oblong, about 3 mm long and 0.5 mm wide. Stamens 2-2.5 mm long; anthers 0.5 mm long. Ovary generally densely pubescent.

Berries glabrous, ovoid, about 6 mm long and 4.5 mm wide. Seed ovoid reniform, 4-4.5 mm long and about 3.5 mm wide and thick; rostrum triangular; median crest somewhat prominent; lateral lines scarcely distinct; testa entirely and finely echinulate on surface.

Type from Abyssinia: Schimper 1703.

The trichome, as concerns both hairs and glands, is often highly variable from one specimen to another. This has led to the subdivision of the species into several varieties: it is the variety adenocaule which is represented in Gabon.

Slender vine, with yellowish-green flowers, rather mesophile; generally fairly common in savannas, forest regrowth and fallow lands. Very widely distributed throughout tropical Africa.
