# *Phyllobotryon bracteatum* (Lecomte) Hul

**Genus**: [[Phyllobotryon]]
**Family**: [[Flacourtiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Mus. natl. Hist. nat. Paris, 4 ᵉ sér., sect. B, Adansonia 13 (3-4): 162 (1991)

## Description

Bull. Mus. natl. Hist. nat. Paris, 4th ser., sect. B, Adansonia 13 (3-4): 162 (1991).

– Phylloclinium bracteatum LECOMTE, Bull. Mus. Nat. Hist. Nat. Paris 24 : 58 (1918) ; GILG, in ENGL. & PRANTL, NPF 21 : 430 (1925). TYPE : Le Testu 2182, Gabon (holo-). – P. bracteatum var. coriaceum LECOMTE, l. c. : 61 (1918). TYPE : Leroy 11, Gabon (holo-, P! ; iso-, P!).

Shrub (2–4 m tall) monocaulous; branches greyish, glabrous.

Leaves with linear, glabrous stipules, sometimes caducous; petiole 8–10 mm long, glabrous; blade obovate, attenuate for a long distance towards the base, acuminate at the apex, 20–40 × 5–12, presenting loose dentition with curved teeth, glabrous; secondary nerves 12–15 pairs.

Flowers ♂ or ♀ and ♀ (polygamous plants), grouped in a few-flowered cyme, generally situated at the base of the acumen of the blade; lateral bracts narrowly ovate to linear, 2–3 mm long, median bract ovate-acuminate, very large (6–8 × 3.5–4 cm), covering the flowers; pedicel 1–6 mm long, glabrous; sepals 3–4, ovate, 2–4 × 1.5–2.5 mm, ciliate; petals 3–4, oblong-ovate, 8–14 × 3–4 mm, ciliate; stamens numerous (20–40), filament 3–4 mm long, glabrous; ovary glabrous, with 2–3 pluriovulate placentas; style 2–(3)-fid at the apex.

Fruits: ovoid capsules, 8–12 × 6–10 mm, generally 2-valved; seeds weakly arillate. – Pl. 6, 9–17, p. 25.
