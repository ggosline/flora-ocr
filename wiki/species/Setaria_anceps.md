# *Setaria anceps* Stapf.

**Genus**: [[Setaria]]
**Family**: [[Gramineae]]
**Protologue**: STAPF, F.T.A. IX : 793 (1930)

## Synonyms

- S. aurea A. Chev.
- S. aurea s.-sp. palustris Vanderyst.

## Description

— FRANCH. Contr. Fl. Congo Fr. : 45 (p. p.) (1896). — VANDERYST, Bull. Agr. C. Belge XVI : 683 (1925).

Perennial herb, approximately 1 m tall in general, in dense tufts; culms slightly geniculate at the base, arising from a short rhizome. Plant remarkable for its culms and sheaths strongly flattened, keeled and flabellate at the base. Leaf blades often folded longitudinally, reaching about thirty centimeters long.

Inflorescences in dense spikes, sometimes exceeding 25 cm long, and 4 to 5 mm in diameter, excluding the hairs. Spikelets solitary or in 2 or 3. Lower glume most often with 3 nerves and not reaching half the length of the spikelet, the upper one slightly longer and with 5 nerves. Lower flower male, the size of the spikelet, lemma with 5 nerves, palea of the same length. Fertile lemma often tinged brown-red, transversely rugose.

Species known from the Gold Coast to East Africa, and in the Congo. Plant of marshy or inundable places, little favored by livestock.
