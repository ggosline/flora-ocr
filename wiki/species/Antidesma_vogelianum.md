# *Antidesma vogelianum* Müll.Arg. Planche 2

**Genus**: [[Antidesma]]
**Family**: [[Phyllanthaceae]]
**Protologue**: Flora 47 : 529 (1864)

## Description

Shrub to tree 1.5–8(–20) m tall; trunk up to 10(–25) cm in diameter; branchlets with short hairs, ± appressed. Stipules narrowly triangular, often slightly falcate, 4–11 × 1–2 mm, pilose like the branchlets. Leaf: petiole almost terete, 2–7 mm; lamina obovate-elliptic, (3–)7–20(–26) × (1.5–)4–8 cm, cuneate to rounded at base, apex acuminate over (0.5–)1–2.5 cm, papyraceous to coriaceous, glabrous or sometimes with a few scattered hairs below and generally with pilose or glabrous domatia; lateral veins (4–)7–10(–12) pairs. Inflorescence generally axillary, sometimes 2 or 3 on terminal branches, ± sparsely pubescent, the male ones 7–11 cm long, the female ones 5–11 cm long, 10–30 cm in fruit; bracts ≤ 1 mm long, ciliate. Male flower: calyx 0.5–1 mm long, sparsely pilose; disk surrounded by stiff hairs. Female flower shortly pedicellate. Fruit ellipsoid, slightly flattened, glabrous, sometimes ± pubescent, red then black at maturity.

## Distribution

from Nigeria eastwards to Sudan, Uganda and Tanzania and southwards to Angola and Zimbabwe; in Gabon collected in all provinces

## Ecology
