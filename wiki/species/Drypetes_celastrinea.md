# *Drypetes celastrinea* Pax & K.Hoffm.

**Genus**: [[Drypetes]]
**Family**: [[Putranjivaceae]]

## Description

in Engler, Pflanzenr. IV, 147, XV (Heft 81): 253 (1922).

Tree up to ±20 m tall; trunk up to ±30 cm in diameter; branchlets slightly sulcate, densely puberulent with whitish or golden-yellowish hairs, then glabrescent; buds puberulent. Stipules caducous, triangular-narrowly elliptic-ovate, 1.5–2.3 mm long, densely puberulent. Leaf: petiole (3.7–)4.5–8(−10) mm, densely puberulent; lamina elliptic-ovate, (4.4–)6.5–9(−15) × (2.1–)2.5–4(−7) cm, subcoriaceous, oblique, acute, or sometimes slightly obtuse at the base, abruptly acuminate at the apex, with margin subentire and crenulate or serrulate toward the distal third, sometimes undulate, plane to slightly recurved, finely and sparsely ciliate near the base and dark green above, pale green and finely and slightly hairy below, glabrescent; venation densely reticulate, slightly prominent above and below, midvein shortly hairy at the base below, glabrescent, secondary veins 4 to 7 on each side, ascending, slightly looped and anastomosing well before the margin. Male inflorescence in the leaf axils or below the leaves, with up to ±11 flowers; bracts ovate to broadly ovate, 0.8–1(−1.3) mm long, densely puberulent. Male flower remarkably fragrant; pedicel slender, 1.8–3.2 mm, densely and shortly hirsute; sepals 4, imbricate, ovate to oblong, 1.3–1.6 × 1.4–1.8 mm, obtuse at the apex, yellowish-green, densely puberulent on the outside, sparsely hairy on the inside, with marginal cilia scarcely distinct from the other hairs; stamens 4(or 5), arranged in a whorl around the edge of the disk, filament 1–1.5 mm, anther 0.5–0.8 mm long, glabrous; disk 0.9–1.1 mm in diameter, plane to slightly concave, somewhat undulate, slightly prominent between the filaments, glabrous. Female inflorescence as the male, with solitary flowers; bracts as in the male. Female flower: pedicel as in the male, 2.6–3.9 mm; sepals 4, 2–3.1 × 2.8–3 mm, similar to those of the males; disk 2.4–2.9 mm in diameter, cupuliform, glabrous; ovary globose, 2-locular, densely puberulent; style 1.2–1.4 mm, unbranched or with 2 branches, partially fused, with fan-shaped stigma, 0.2–0.8 × 1.2–2.5 mm, slightly bifid. Fruit globose, up to ±14.5–16 × 13.5–14 mm, dark-coloured at maturity, without persistent sepals and stigmas, densely puberulent, glabrescent, with (1 or)2 seeds; pedicel up to ±10.5–12 mm. Seed up to ±8.3 mm long.

## Distribution

Cameroon and Gabon; in Gabon: Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Lolo and Woleu-Ntem

Habitat: Inselbergs and forest margins, generally in rocky soils; in Gabon at 500–700 m altitude, elsewhere up to 900 m.

## Source note

D. celastrinea is a species rarely collected in Gabon. It has branchlets densely puberulent with generally golden-yellowish hairs, and its discolorous leaves are also visibly hairy on the veins and petioles. The male flowers of D. celastrinea are densely hairy, even hirsute, smaller than those of D. umbricola and with shorter pedicels than in D. klainei
