# *Rhabdophyllum welwitschii* Tiegh. Planche 25

**Genus**: [[Rhabdophyllum]]
**Family**: [[Ochnaceae]]
**Protologue**: Ann. Sci. Nat., Bot., sér. 8, 16 : 322 (1902)

## Description

Rhabdophyllum leptoneurum (Gilg) Tiegh., Ann. Sci. Nat., Bot., sér. 8, 18: 34 (1903). Rhabdophyllum coronatum Tiegh., Ann. Sci. Nat., sér. 9, 5: 168 (1907). Rhabdophyllum gracile Tiegh., Ann. Sci. Nat., sér. 9, 5: 168 (1907). Ouratea welwitschii (Tiegh.) Exell, J. Bot. 65, Suppl. 1: 58 (1927). Suffrutex to tree up to 10 m tall; stems slender. Stipules caducous. Leaf: petiole 2–4 mm; blade narrowly elliptic to narrowly elliptic-obovate, (4–)5–12(–14.5) × (1.5–)2–4(–4.5) cm, cuneate or attenuate at base, acute to acuminate or rarely acuminate-caudate at apex, papyraceous, margin slightly undulate to weakly serrulate; secondary veins not hierarchized, not or scarcely prominent on both surfaces. Inflorescence with axis 1–6 cm, in false umbel; cymules elongate, often grouped at apex of inflorescence axis, 1- to 5-flowered; bracts at base of inflorescence non-persistent. Flower: pedicel 6–15 mm; sepals narrowly elliptic in flower, slightly widened, accrescent and rounded at apex in fruit, 6–8 × 2–3 mm; petals 6–7 mm long. Fruit with pedicel, receptacle and sepals red; mericarps more or less globose, red, becoming black at maturity.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Central African Republic, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Angola and Zambia; in Gabon, rare and known from the environs of Libreville and the southeast

## Ecology

primary and secondary forest, forest edges, fallows and shrubby savannas, on upland or stations sometimes periodically flooded; in Gabon, up to 600 m elevation, elsewhere up to 2000 m. b: Bamps & Farron (1967), Farron (1965, 1985), Robson (1963), Sosef Bibliography (2008)
