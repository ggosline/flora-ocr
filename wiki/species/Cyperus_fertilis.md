# *Cyperus fertilis* Boeckeler Planche 65

**Genus**: [[Cyperus]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 5 : 90 (1884)

## Description

Annual or perennial herb, in tufts with a slightly thickened base; stems 2–20 cm × 1–2 mm, triquetrous with longitudinal ridges, glabrous, with 5–10 crowded basal leaves. Leaf: sheath purplish near the base; blade 5–12 cm × 5–25 mm, plane, lanceolate–elliptic, finely scabrous on the margins and main veins. Inflorescence with 2–10 primary rays of unequal length (2–40 cm), triquetrous or flattened and bearing 1–3 spikelets, the rays sometimes arched and proliferous; involucral bracts 5–10, foliaceous, elliptic, spreading, the longest 5–20 cm × 0.7–2 cm. Spikelet ovate–elliptic, 5–10 × 3–5 mm, light brown, flattened, with 5–10 flowers; glumes ovate–lanceolate, 2–2.5 mm long, light reddish-brown with a green vein terminating below the rounded apex. Flower: style with 3 long branches. Achene obovate–elliptic, approximately 1.2 × 0.8 mm, reddish-brown, strongly triangular; surface smooth to finely papillose.

## Distribution

widely distributed in the forest regions of West and Central Africa; locally common in Gabon, in Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem, also present in Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué and Ngounié (38 collections)

## Ecology

in swampy forests, along forest trails and watercourses; in Gabon, at 0–900 m altitude, up to 2100 m elsewhere. Taxonomic note: It is the only African species of Cyperus with blades wider in the middle, that is to say elliptic and not linear. B: Haines & Lye (1983), Hooper & Napper (1972). Bibliography
