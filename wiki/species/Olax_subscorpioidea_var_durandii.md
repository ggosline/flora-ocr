# *Olax subscorpioidea* var. *durandii* (Engler) Michaud

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Olax]]

Mém. I.F.A.N. **75**: 212 (1966). — *O. durandii* Engl., Notizbl. bot. Gart. Berl. **2**: 286 (1899). — De Wild., Ann. Mus. Congo b., Bot., ser. 4, **1**: 176 (1903); ser. 5, **3**: 70 (1909).

*O. chariensis* A. Chev. l.c., pro parte.

## Description

This variety differs from the type by its larger flowers (petals 6–7 mm long) and its style exceeding 4 mm in length.

## Types

**Holotype**: *Verdick 526*, plateau de Lukafu, Zaïre (BR).

## Distribution

Known from the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Source

Villiers J.-F. (1973) Olacacées. In: Letouzey R. (ed.), *Flore du Gabon*, Volume 20: 120–121. Muséum national d'Histoire naturelle, Paris.
