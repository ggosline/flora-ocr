# *Olax subscorpioidea* var. *durandii* (Engler) Michaud

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Olax]] · **Species**: [[Olax subscorpioidea]]

Mém. I.F.A.N. 75: 212 (1966).

## Synonymy

- *Olax durandii* Engl., Notizbl. bot. Gart. Berl. 2: 286 (1899)
- De Wild., Ann. Mus. Congo b., Bot., ser. 4, 1: 176 (1903); ser. 5, 3: 70 (1909)
- *Olax chariensis* A. Chev. *pro parte*

## Description

This variety differs from the type by its larger flowers (petals 6–7 mm long) and its style exceeding 4 mm in length.

## Distribution

**Holotype**: Verdick 526, plateau de Lukafu, Democratic Republic of the Congo (BR).

## Specimens examined

**Cameroon**
- Letouzey 2555, Boubala near Tibati (flowers, December)

## Source

Flore du Gabon, Volume 20: 120–121.

## Notes

The holotype is from the Democratic Republic of the Congo (Zaïre), but only Cameroonian material is recorded as examined in this treatment. The relationship to *Olax chariensis* A. Chev. is noted as partial (*pro parte*), indicating possible taxonomic complexity.
