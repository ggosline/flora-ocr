# *Costus ligularis* Bak.
**Family in Vol. 9**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Current placement**: Costaceae · **Genus**: [[Costus]]
## Description
Somewhat succulent herb from a few decimetres to 1.5 m, with broadly lanceolate leaves hairy on both faces and a membranous bifid ligule to 3.5 cm. The small ovoid spike is terminal or borne separately at rhizome level. Each reddish hairy bract subtends one pink-violet flower; the labellum is 7–8 cm with deeply fringed margins.
## Taxonomy and distribution
Type: Mann 1628, Río Muni. Koechlin united variable Gabon plants previously split as *C. fimbriatus* and the unpublished *C. ngouniensis*, noting that living material was needed to resolve this complex. Reported only from Río Muni and Gabon; numerous Gabon vernacular names and magical uses are recorded.
