# *Portulaca oleracea* L.

**Family**: [[Portulacaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Portulaca]]

## Description

Glabrous fleshy annual, diffuse or ascending, 10–30 cm long. Leaves alternate or subopposite, nearly sessile and subspatulate, 1–3 cm × c. 7 mm. Pink flowers in groups of 3–5 have two keeled sepals, five delicate 4–5 mm petals, 6–12 stamens and a style with 3–6 branches. Capsule circumscissile; seeds black and minutely tuberculate.

## Distribution

Cosmopolitan ruderal of warm regions; recorded in Gabon.
