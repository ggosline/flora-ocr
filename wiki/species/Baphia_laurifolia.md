# *Baphia laurifolia* Baill.

**Genus**: [[Baphia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia 6 : 212 (1866)

## Description

Tree, small to medium, sometimes shrub, occasionally described as liana or lianescent, reaching 20 m in height; trunk up to 35(–50?) cm in diameter; branchlets pubescent-ferruginous, glabrescent. Stipules ovate-triangular, 2–3.5 × 1–1.5 mm. Leaf: petiole with contiguous or shortly separated pulvini, (4–)6–9(–11) mm, indumentum as on the branchlets; leaflet with blade ± elliptic, (3–)8–12(–17) × (1.5–)3–5(–6.5) cm, ± rounded at base, apex acuminate over 0.5–1.5(–2) cm, coriaceous to papyraceous, glabrous above, glabrous below or with a few appressed hairs, especially on the midvein; lateral veins in (5–)6 to 8(–9) pairs. Inflorescence in axillary fascicles, with 1 to 5 flowers, sometimes united in terminal pseudoracemes, pubescent-ferruginous; bracts transversely elliptic, 1.5–2 × 3–4 mm. Flower: bracteoles opposite, situated at the apex of the pedicel against the calyx, transversely elliptic, 0.5–1 × 2 mm; pedicel 8–16 mm, pubescent-ferruginous; calyx spathate, 11–5 × 13–15 mm, pubescent-ferruginous, sometimes glabrous except at the apex; standard ± circular, 13–17(–22) × 13–17(–20) mm, wings obovate, 13–21 × 8–12 mm, with claw 0.5–1 mm, keel petals 13–20 × 6–7 mm, glabrous, with claw 0.5–1 mm; stamens 7–15 mm, anthers 2–3.5 mm in length; pistil 12–18 mm in length, ovary velutinous, 6-ovulate. Pod 7–15 × 1.5–3 cm, sparsely pubescent, with 1 or 2 seeds. Seed lenticular, 12–13 mm in diameter, dark brown.

## Distribution

South-East Nigeria, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, known from all provinces except Nyanga and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

primary and secondarized forest; in Gabon up to 500 m altitude, elsewhere up to 600 m. Use: The wood is used as kitchen utensils, firewood for cooking and as a dyewood
