# *Amphibemma cuneatum* Jacques-Félix

**Genus**: [[Amphibemma]]
**Family**: [[Melastomataceae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia, sér. 2, 13 (4) : 454, tab. 10 (1973)

## Description

Shrublet branched, woody, glabrescent, 0.30 m tall; branchlets slender, with short internodes, furfuraceous, with a few slender setae, then early glabrous. Leaves with slender petiole, 3 cm long, furfuraceous, sometimes with a few bristly setae; blade lanceolate, up to 1.7 × 8 cm, cuneate on the petiole, with attenuate and obtuse acumen; a few setae very scattered on both faces, or glabrous above; 3 nerves, the transverse ones parallel, spaced; margins entire with a few cilia.

Terminal cymes, uni- or paucifloral; hypanthium-calyx narrowly obconic, 3 × 4 mm, attenuate on the pedicel of 3 mm; setae spreading, glandular, 1 to 1.5 mm long, very scattered; lobes broadly triangular-acute, 1.5–2 × 1.2 mm. Petals pink, 11 mm long, with a few glandular hairs toward the apex. Outer stamens with anther 5 mm; androconnective 5 mm long, thickened toward the lower third, appendage 1.2 mm long, thick, truncate; filament 5 mm. Inner stamens not known. Ovary deeply included; style 10 to 12 mm.

Fruit obconic, 5 mm long; ovary reaching the margin of the hypanthium; calyx marcescent and glandular hairs long persistent. — Pl. 32, p. 89.

## Type

Hallé N. & Villiers J.-F. 4710, Gabon (P.)

## Discussion

Species related to A. setosum, distinguished from it immediately by its 3-nerved leaves.
