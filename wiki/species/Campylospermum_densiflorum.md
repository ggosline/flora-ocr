# *Campylospermum densiflorum* (De Wild. & T.Durand) Farron

**Genus**: [[Campylospermum]]
**Family**: [[Ochnaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Jard. Bot. État Bruxelles 35 : 394 (1965)

## Description

Ouratea reticulata (P.Beauv.) Engl. ex Gilg var. schweinfurthii Engl., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 17 : 81 (1893). Ouratea coriacea De Wild. & T.Durand, Ann. Mus. Congo, Bot. , ser. 3, 1 : 36 (1901). Ouratea densiflora De Wild. & T.Durand, Ann. Mus. Congo, Bot. , ser. 3, 1 : 37 (1901). Ouratea laevis De Wild. & T.Durand var. ipamuensis De Wild., Pl. Bequaert. 4 : 482 (1929). Campylospermum katangense Farron, Bull. Jard. Bot. État Bruxelles 35 : 397 (1965). Gomphia coriacea (Tiegh.) Lye, Lidia 4(3) : 91 (1998). Gomphia densiflora (De Wild. & T.Durand) Verdc., Fl. trop. E. Africa : 50 (2005). Tree, erect or rarely climbing, reaching 20 m in height; trunk up to 8 cm in diameter; branches with dark brown bark. Stipules caducous, triangular, (2–)4–9 mm in length. Leaf: petiole 5–12 mm, stout; blade narrowly elliptic to narrowly elliptic-obovate or sometimes elliptic-obovate, (10–)19–32(–36) × (2.5–)7–9(–12) cm, ratio (2.5–)3–4, rounded to cuneate at base, obtuse to acuminate at apex, coriaceous, with margin regularly serrulate; midrib plane to ± canaliculate towards the base on upper surface, prominent on lower surface, lateral veins in 10–15 pairs, generally indistinct above, distinct below, tertiary venation scalariform, indistinct above, distinct to fairly distinct below. Inflorescence terminal or sometimes axillary, branched, fairly dense, main axis (5–)10–26(–32) cm, more or less angular, robust; racemes 2 to 7, (3–)6–23(–30) cm, ascending; gemmary scales absent or sparse; bracts caducous, sometimes foliaceous, elliptic, 5–8 × 2–3 cm; cymules distant by 0.5–1 cm, 1- to 9(–16)-flowered. Flower: pedicel 7–15 mm, articulate at (2–)4–5(–7) mm from the base; sepals ovate to elliptic, 6–7 × 3–4 mm in flower, 8–11 × 4–5 mm in fruit, erect-spreading; petals obovate, 6–11 × 4–8 mm, unguiculate or not at base, apex rounded; stamens: anthers 4–6 mm in length; style 5–7 mm. Fruit: receptacle 6 × 5 mm; 3 or 4 well-developed drupelets per receptacle, ellipsoid, 7–10 × 4–6 mm; cotyledons incumbent, unequal with small external cotyledon.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Central African Republic, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, northeastern Angola, southern Sudan, Uganda, Kenya, Burundi, Tanzania and northern Zambia; in Gabon, rare and known only from Estuaire and Haut-Ogooué

## Ecology

primary and secondary forest, gallery forest, dry forest, savanna, montane forest, swamp forest, along rivers and streams, near waterfalls; on silvery soil; in Gabon, at 100–500 m altitude, elsewhere up to 2000 m. Taxonomic note: C. densiflorum resembles C. dybovskii, but differs by the presence of lateral veins distinct but not prominent on the lower surface and a well-branched inflorescence with angular axes. Bibliography: Bamps & Farron (1967), Bissiengou (2014), Farron (1963, 1985), Robson (1963), Verdcourt (2005)
