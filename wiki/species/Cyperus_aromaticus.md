# *Cyperus aromaticus* (Ridl.) Mattf. & Kük. Planche 51

**Genus**: [[Cyperus]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Kyllinga aromatica Ridl, Trans. Linn. Soc . London, Bot. 2 : 146 (1884)

## Description

in Engl. & Diels, Pflanzenr . IV. 20(101) : 581 (1936). Kyllinga aromatica Ridl, Trans. Linn. Soc . London, Bot. 2 : 146 (1884). Kyllinga polyphylla Kunth, Enum. pl . 2 : 134 (1837). non C. polyphyllus Vahl (1805). Kyllinga erecta Schumach. var. polyphylla (Kunth) Hooper, Kew Bull . 26 : 580 (1972). Robust herb, perennial, with creeping rhizome and crowded stems; rhizome approximately 5 mm thick (scales included); scales fairly thick, pale brown to dark violet or blackish, less than 10 mm long; stems 25–90 cm × 1–3 mm (wider at sheath level), the basal part usually covered with naked violaceous sheaths. Leaf: upper sheath exceeding blade 3–15 cm × 2–6 mm . Inflorescence in an irregular to globose hemispherical head, with one central spike and usually several smaller lateral spikes; involucral bracts 5–8, generally long and spreading, the main one 6–15 cm long. Spikelet 3–4 mm long, with 1–2 flowers, but producing only a single achene; glumes yellowish or pale yellow with greenish central nerve and often with dark brown spots or streaks especially near the central nerve, with 3–5 ribs on each side of the central nerve. Achene obovoid, 1.2–1.5 mm long, brown to blackish.

## Distribution

widespread in tropical and southern Africa, including Madagascar and the Mascarene Islands; rare in Gabon or overlooked, found in Estuary, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem (Minkébé Massif) (5 collections)

## Ecology

in seasonally humid grasslands and rocky habitats; in Gabon, around 900 m altitude, up to 1250 m elsewhere
