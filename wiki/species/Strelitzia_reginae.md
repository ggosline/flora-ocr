# *Strelitzia reginae* Banks

**Family**: [[Strelitziaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Strelitzia]]

## Description

Rhizomatous perennial with distichous, long-petiolate large leaves. Flowers are borne in dense spikes within large coloured bracts. The three outer tepals are free and bright orange; two anterior inner tepals unite into a blue sagittate structure enclosing five linear anthers, while the remaining inner tepal is smaller and concave. Ovary 3-locular; capsule 3-valved and loculicidal.

## Distribution

Native to the Cape region of South Africa and cultivated ornamentally in Gabon.
