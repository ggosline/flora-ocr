# *Tristemma demeusei* De Wildeman

**Genus**: [[Tristemma]]
**Family**: [[Melastomataceae]]
**Protologue**: Anns Mus. Congo 5 (1) : 299 (1906); JACQ.-FÉL., Fl. Cam. 24 : 72, tab. 21 (1983)

## Description

Anns Mus. Congo 5 (1) : 299 (1906); JACQ.-FÉL., Fl. Cam. 24 : 72, tab. 21 (1983).

Shrub trailing, rooting, or erect up to 0.40 m; branches slender, subangular, finely strigillous to hirsute, especially on the angles, with longer setae on the nodes, then rounded and glabrescent. Leaves small, with slender petiole 0.5 to 2 cm long, strigillous to hirsute, especially on the inner face ± channeled; limb 1–3 × 2–8 cm, elliptic-lanceolate, cuneate on the petiole, obscurely acuminate-obtuse at the apex; regularly strigillous above; setae more scattered, softer and longer below, especially on the 3 to 5 finely prominent nerves.

Flowers typically solitary or in threes; variably sessile or exserted beyond the last pair of normal leaves; axial bracts ± foliaceous, lanceolate, nerved, setulose, longer than the flower; 1 to 2 pairs of floral bracts membranous, broadly ovate, obtuse, glabrous on the back, ciliate, covering only the 1/3 or half-height of the flower; hypanthium ovo-ellipsoid, bearing near the apex a membrano-ciliate ring generally complete, membrane 0.3 to 0.5 mm, flexuous setae 2 to 3 mm; sometimes a few underlying fringes, ciliate without membrane; sepals broadly triangular-oblong, 2–3 × 3–4 mm, distinctly ciliate. Petals pink or white, obovate, 4–5 × 6–8 mm. Stamens small; anther oblong, 2.5–4 mm; pedoconnective 0.3 mm, anterior lobes linear-obtuse, erect, ± equal to the pedoconnective; filament 3–4 mm. Ovary with convex apex, setulose, apical setae slightly overtopping the poorly pronounced peristyle rim; style 6–7 mm, slightly thickened toward the top, stigma discoid.

Fruit ovo-globose 6–9 × 7–10 mm. — Pl. 18, p. 51.

## Type

Demeuse 76, Zaïre (BR)

## Discussion

Small western forest species, variable in habit, indumentum, flower color, etc. The reduced form, which can be observed on ravined clayey soil, corresponds to T. monanthum GILG ex ENGL., which we placed in synonymy in the Flora of Cameroon, but whose status should be studied in the field.

## Specimens examined

Hallé N. 1467, Makokou, pente argileuse en forêt dégradée (mars). Le Testu 8911, Kemboma, ENE Makokou (sept.).
