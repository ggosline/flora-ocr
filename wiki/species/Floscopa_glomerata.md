# *Floscopa glomerata* (Willd. ex Schult. & Schult.f.) Hassk. Planche 8

**Genus**: [[Floscopa]]
**Family**: [[Commelinaceae]]
**Protologue**: Commelin. ind. : 166 (1870)

## Synonyms

- Tradescantia glomerata Willd. ex Schult. & Schult.f., Syst. 7 : 1175 (1830).

## Description

F. rivularis (A.Rich.) C.B.Clarke in A.DC. & C.DC., Monogr. phan. 3 : 267 (1881). Perennial or annual herb, reaching (10–)20–60(–100) cm in height; roots thin, fibrous, situated at basal nodes; rhizomes or underground stems sometimes present; stem creeping-ascending, generally unbranched or rarely sparsely branched, up to 2 m in length, covered by sheaths. Leaf: sheath 0.3–2 cm, with one pubescent line on the suture, ciliate at the apex; pseudetiole absent; blade narrowly ovate-oblong to very narrowly elliptic-ovate, (2–)3–10(–15) × 0.6–2 cm, base cordate-amplexicaul, apex acute to acuminate, margins generally distinctly undulate, sometimes ciliolate at the base, remainder scabrous, sometimes purplish, surfaces glabrous or upper surface scabrous to sparsely pubescent and lower surface pubescent or puberulent, sometimes scabrous on the midvein. Inflorescence terminal, in a ± dense compound thyrse, (1–)1.5–5(–8) × (1–)1.5–5(–9) cm, densely glandular-pubescent; sometimes with reduced inflorescences at the apex of lateral branches; cincinni up to 4.5 cm in length, floriferous almost to the base, all directed and curved upward, with distichous flowers; bracteoles of irregular shapes, 0.5–1.3 cm in length, scarious. Flower: pedicel 1.4–3(–3.8) mm, with glandular pubescence; sepals elliptic to ovate, 2–3 × 1.5–2 mm (up to 4.7 mm in length at fruiting), with external glandular pubescence, purplish to violet or rarely white; petals purplish, mauve or white, glabrous, the upper ones elliptic to ovate, 2–3.5 × 1.5–2 mm, the lower one oblong, 2.5–3 × 0.4–0.75 mm; lower stamens with filament connate at the base, 3–3.5 mm in length, anther oblong, 0.5–0.6 × 0.5–1 mm, dark blue, the upper ones with filament 2.5–3.2 mm, anther bilobed, 0.5–0.6 × 0.7–1 mm, yellow; ovary 0.7–0.8 mm in diameter, with stipe reaching 0.6 mm; style (2.5–)4.5 mm, with apex reflexed, stigma punctiform. Fruit elliptic, 1.5–2.8 × 1.9–3 mm, sometimes apiculate, ochreous, lustrous, with stipe reaching 0.8 mm or rarely subsessile. Seed ellipsoid to globose, 0.8–1.4(–1.6) × 0.7–1.1(–1.4) mm; testa dark brown to almost black, rarely pale grey, with 12 to 18(–20) slight or pronounced ribs, rarely smooth, farinose with white to ochreous granules.

## Distribution

Senegal, Gambia, Guinea, Guinea Bissau, Burkina Faso, Mali, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Togo, Benin, Nigeria, Cameroon, São Tomé & Principe, Equatorial Guinea (Bioko and Río Muni), Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Central African Republic, Sudan, Ethiopia, Rwanda, Burundi, Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, Angola, Zambia, Malawi, Zimbabwe, Mozambique, Namibia and Madagascar; in Gabon in the Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

wet grasslands, marshes; in Gabon up to 485 m altitude, elsewhere from 280 to 2550 m. Flowering in May. B: Brenan (1968b), Cabezas et al. (2014), Faden (2012), Sosef et al. (2006). Bibliography
