# *Buchnera paucidentata* Engl. ex Skan Planche 56(D–E)

**Genus**: [[Buchnera]]
**Family**: [[Scrophulariaceae]]

## Description

in Hemsl. & Skan in Dyer, Fl. trop. Afr. 4(2): 381 (1906). Annual herb, scabrous and rigid, 20–30 cm in height; stem erect, rounded. Cauline leaves, sessile; limb of basal leaves obovate to oblanceolate, 15–50 × 7–30 mm, obtuse at apex, with crenate margin. Inflorescence capitate, 1.5–2 cm in length, furnished with lanceolate or ovate-lanceolate to ovate bracts, 7 × 2.5 mm, acute at apex. Flower subsessile to sessile; calyx tubular, 3.8–5 mm in length, pilose, with faintly distinct veins, with 5 unequal teeth 1.8–3 mm in length; corolla mauve to violet-blue, tube 10 mm in length, lobes subequal, 5–6 × 2 mm. Fruit ovoid-cylindric, 3.5–3.8 × 2.8–3 mm, obtuse.

## Distribution

widespread in Cameroon, Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, observed in the Haut-Ogooué (Batéké Plateaus)

## Ecology

humid savannas; in Gabon, at 640 m altitude, in Democratic Republic of the Congo (Katanga), up to 1200 m. B: Mielcarek (1996), Walters et al. (2011). Bibliography
