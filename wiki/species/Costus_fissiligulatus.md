# *Costus fissiligulatus* Gagnep.
**Family in Vol. 9**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Current placement**: Costaceae · **Genus**: [[Costus]]
## Description
Slender herb a few decimetres tall with spiralled stem, asymmetrical lanceolate blades 10–15 × 4–6 cm and a thin, laterally split, more or less bilobed ligule to 4 cm. The terminal spike has bracts prolonged into reflexed leafy tips. Flowers are pink and yellow; the labellum reaches 7 cm and the ovary is hairy.
## Distribution
Known from Gabon and Cameroon. Type: “Costus Gabon” 693, cultivated at the Muséum. Gabon material includes Mayumba, Kango and several unlocalized early collections; numerous vernacular names are recorded.
