# *Cissus dinklagei* Gilg et Brandt

**Genus**: [[Cissus]]
**Family**: [[Vitaceae]]
**Protologue**: Gilg et Brandt, Engl. Bot. Jahrb. 46 : 476 (1912)

## Synonyms

- Gilg et Brandt, Engl. Bot. Jahrb. 46 : 476 (1912). — Dewit, Fl. Congo belge et R.-U. 9 : 414 (1960).

## Description

Woody liane reaching 25-30 m in length and 5 cm in diameter. Branches cylindrical, with loose pilose indumentum denser on the nodes; internodes 1.5-3 cm long. Tendrils generally simple, glabrous; bracteoles deltoid, approximately 0.5 mm long and wide, glabrous. Stipules quickly caducous, oblong deltoid, 4-5 mm long and 2.5-3.5 mm wide, obtuse or rounded at the apex, weakly pubescent. Leaves simple entire. Petiole cylindrical, channeled, 1-4 cm long, loosely pubescent, more densely so at the base. Blade oval more or less deltoid or elliptic, 5-10 cm long, 3-6 cm wide, with truncate, rounded or subcordate base, acuminate apex; margin entire not denticulate, somewhat revolute on the dried state toward the lower surface; leaf glabrous, or weakly pilose on the lower surface on the blade or on the base of the primary vein; domatia glabrous or somewhat ciliate in the angle of primary and secondary veins on the lower surface; venation pinnate; texture subcoriaceous.

Inflorescences 4-10 cm long, in regular cymes composed of umbelliform cymules; peduncle 3-5 cm long, loosely pubescent or glabrescent; axes glabrous in their lower parts becoming increasingly densely puberulent toward the apex; bracteoles broadly ovate deltoid, approximately 1 mm long, thick, glabrous or somewhat puberulent. Flowers 3-4 mm long. Pedicel cylindrical, 4-7 mm long, densely puberulent. Calyx cupuliform, approximately 1 mm in height, very thick, entirely puberulent. Corolla densely puberulent ovoid, conical at the apex; petals oval obtuse, very thick, fleshy in the upper part. Stamens 2 mm long; anthers oval, approximately 0.5 mm long. Ovary glabrous.

Berries ovoid 3 cm long and 2 cm wide, glabrous. Seed ovoid oblong, 15-17 mm long, 10-12 mm wide, and 6-7 mm thick; rostrum long, thick; median line very prominent, thick; lateral lines neat regular, emitting a large number of prominent radial lines; seed coat with smooth surface.

Syntypes from Gabon: Dinklage 555 and Mildbraed 3216.

Plant characteristic for its leaves with entire margin. In dense rainforest. Range of distribution covering equatorial Africa, from Cameroon to Angola.
