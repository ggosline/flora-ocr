# *Chestis uncata* Lemmens

**Genus**: [[Cnestis]]
**Family**: [[Connaraceae]]

## Description

in BRETELER (ed.), AUWP 89 (6) : 228, fig. 106-109 (1989). TYPE : Gabon, km 25 Libreville-Kango, Breteler & Lemmens 8340 (holo-WAG ; iso-LBV).

Liane or lianescent shrub. Young and old branches cylindrical, with dense brown pubescence at first, then glabrescent.

Leaves 6-10-jugate; petiole 0.5-4 cm long, rachis 7.5-23 cm, with brown pubescence, then ± glabrescent. Leaflets strongly papyraceous, the lateral ones alternate or sometimes opposite, ovate to narrowly oblong, (0.5-1), 5-7 × (0.5-1)-2.5 cm, with cordate and unequal or subequal base, the terminal leaflet ovate, obovate or elliptic, 4.5-7.5 × 2-3.5 cm, subcordate, rounded or somewhat cuneate at the base; all leaflets obtuse or indistinctly acuminate, upper surface glabrous (except on the midvein), lower surface bearing crisp hairs; midvein impressed above, prominent below; lateral veins 5-10 pairs; tertiary veins reticulate, prominent, distinct on both surfaces; petiolules 0-1 mm long, pubescent.

Inflorescences: fascicles (rarely short racemes), 3-10-flowered on knotty swellings of old branches. Bracts ovate to subulate, 1-2 mm long, partially connate on these swellings. Pedicels not articulate, 3-7 mm, dense pubescence.

Flowers heterodistylous. Sepals, valvate prefloration, then reflexed, subulate, narrowly oblong to sublinear, 3-4.5 × 0.5-0.8 mm, obtuse or acute, pubescent on the outer side, glabrous inside, clearly 1-3-nerved. Petals in valvate to narrowly imbricate prefloration, narrowly oblong to sublinear, 5-7 × 0.7-1 mm, base rounded or cuneate, apex obtuse or subacute, glabrous, generally clearly 3-nerved. Stamens united at the base, of subequal or different length in the two whorls (long-staminate flower), 1-1.6 or 3.1-4.3 mm long, anthers ≈ 0.2 × ≈ 0.2 mm. Pistil: carpels 1.1 or 2.8-4.4 mm long; ovaries ≈ 0.5 mm long, brown-yellowish pubescence; styles ± straight, pubescent at their base; stigmas ± prominent and oblique, 2-lobed.

Fruits: follicles 1 (-2) per flower, uncinate 2-3 × 1 cm, cuneate, with a basal stipe 5-10 mm long; beak broad, strongly reflexed toward the ventral face of the follicle, 10-13 mm long; pericarp with very short spiniform hairs, red and others long, urticant and easily caducous on the outer face, the inner face bearing long yellowish hairs.

Seeds ovoid, 13 × 7 mm, sarcotesta 6 mm long. – Pl. 18, p. 63.

## Source note

C. uncata has fasciculate flowers, exactly like C. urens. It differs from it by its typically uncinate fruits (beak strongly reflexed) and fewer and larger leaflets, glabrous on the upper surface. These are therefore the differences borne by the leaves that allow identification of material whether fertile or sterile.
