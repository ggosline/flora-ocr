# *Panicum fulgens* Stapf.

**Genus**: [[Panicum]]
**Family**: [[Gramineae]]
**Protologue**: STAPF, F.T.A. IX : 668 (1920)

## Description

Perennial plant, in loose tufts about fifty centimetres high. Culms erect, geniculate and sometimes branched at the base. Leaves generally glabrous, ligule ciliate, blades about fifteen centimetres long.

Panicle divided to the $ 3^{\circ} $ or $ 4^{\circ} $ degree, about twelve centimetres long. Spikelets globose, $ 1 \ \text{to } 2 \ \text{mm} $ in diameter, glabrous, with a metallic sheen. Lower glume narrow, with 3 nerves, about half the length of the spikelet or slightly more. The upper glume with 5 nerves, as large as the spikelet and very similar to the lemma of the lower flower which is male. Fertile lemmas white, papyraceous, verrucose.

Species recorded from Nigeria, Uganda, Oubangui and Congo. Fairly good forage species but of poor development. Lives in savanna among tufts of large Grasses.
