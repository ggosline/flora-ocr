# *Bulbostylis coleotricha* (A.Rich.) C.B.Clarke Planche 8

**Genus**: [[Bulbostylis]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fimbristylis coleotricha Hochst. ex A.Rich, Tent. fl. abyss. 2 : 506 (1850)

## Description

in Durand & Schinz, Consp. fl. afric. 5 : 613 (1894). Fimbristylis coleotricha Hochst. ex A.Rich, Tent. fl. abyss. 2 : 506 (1850). Abildgaardia coleotricha (A.Rich.) Lye, Bot. Not. 127 : 495 (1974). Annual herb, in tufts, generally 8–25 cm tall, with stems, leaves and spikelets hairy; stems deeply grooved, 0.3–0.5 mm wide, with pale striae and furnished with stiff hairs, 0.1–0.2 mm. Leaf: collar of leaf sheaths with numerous fine white hairs 3–10 mm; blade channelled, 0.2–0.3 mm wide, grooved and densely covered with stiff white hairs. Inflorescence in an umbelliform anthela; main involucral bracts far exceeding the upper spikelets, other bracts and spikelets narrow, lanceolate, long-attenuate at the apex and bordered with fine white hairs 1–6 mm. Spikelet ovoid, approximately 3 × 1.5–2 mm (excluding awns), with several basal glumes furnished with long awns with ciliate margins and upper glumes shorter, with shorter awns and shorter marginal hairs; glumes pale brown to dark brown, often with a mealy surface; central nerve and awns with short stiff white hairs. Achene triangular, approximately 1 mm long, very pale yellow, capped by the dark brown base of the style; surface with small papillae at maturity.

## Distribution

Tropical Africa, from Senegal to Ethiopia and southward to Angola and Tanzania; rare in Gabon, found in Woleu-Ntem (2 collections)

## Ecology

in dry grasslands on inselbergs; in Gabon, at 500 m altitude, up to 1200 m in East Africa. Taxonomic note: The pale and ovoid spikelets, each furnished with a fascicle of long awns, are characteristic. B: Goetghebeur & Coudijzer (1984b), Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al. (2010), Hooper & Napper (1972), Lye (1997)
