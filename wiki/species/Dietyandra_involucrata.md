# *Dietyandra involucrata* (Hook. f.) Hiern

**Genus**: [[Dictyandra]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Hiern, F.T.A. 3 : 86 (1877)

## Synonyms

- Leptactina involucrata Hook. f., Ic. Pl., t. 1092 (1871).
- Leptactina enosmia vel euosmia K. Schum. nomen.

## Description

(PL. 20, p. 89)

Shrub 4–8 m tall, exceeding 10 cm in diameter. Internodes glabrous, compressed and furrowed beneath the stipules. Stipules ample, orbicular, refracted, glabrous, 5–20 mm wide. Petiole (0.5) 1–4 cm, micropubérulent or glabrous. Leaves often unequal toward the flowering extremities. Blade elliptic, 8–22 (35) × 4–11 (20) cm, glabrous or finely pubérulent above, especially at the principal nerve axils; base acute; apex attenuate acuminate. Nerves ± pubescent on the lower face; axils with finely hairy domatia. 7–12 (15) pairs of secondary nerves; intersecondary nerves prominent, fairly regularly spaced and angled at the midpoint.

Inflorescences at the extremities of lateral branches, multiflorous, with axes finely pubérulent or glabrous, compressed when dry. Bracts linear, 3–10 mm. Pedicels 0.5–5 mm, glabrous or ± pubescent. Calyx with 5 lobes contorted at the juvenile stage, then ± spaced lanceolate, 8–10 × 2–3 mm, erect. Corolla white, 4 cm in diameter, pubescent externally, with a slender tube 6–8 cm in length and 2–3 mm wide; lobes attenuate acute, 15–25 × 3–4 mm, glabrous above. Anthers medifixed, semi-exsert, inserted scarcely below the upper level of the throat, 10–15 × 1 mm; locules numerous, 0.5–1 mm. Style glabrous, scarcely exceeding the throat by ca. 3–5 mm, filiform, terminated by two lobes 4–8 mm, very narrow and remaining appressed. Ovary with 2 locules, multiovulate, and peltate placentas.

Fruit ovoid, ca. 16–10 mm, crowned by the erect sepals reaching up to 12 × 4 mm, with black surface longitudinally wrinkled when dry. More than 375 seeds in a fruit; seeds black, shiny, prismatic, 1.5 mm.

## Type

Mann 2156 (K!), Ambas Bay, Cameroun

## Discussion

The species is distributed from Sierra Leone and Upper Guinea (Macenta) to the Central African Republic (Bangui). Forest margins, secondary forests.
