# *Limnophila barteri* Skan Planche 34

**Genus**: [[Limnophila]]
**Family**: [[Scrophulariaceae]]

## Description

in Oliv., Fl. trop. Afr. 4(2): 317 (1906).

Annual herb, semi-aquatic, reaching 17–65 cm in height; stem erect, simple or sparingly branched, aerial portion up to 40 cm long, pubescent, quadrangular, submerged portion up to 20 cm long, glabrous to subglabrous. Aerial leaves opposite, limb ovate-elliptic, 7–19 × 6–12 mm, slightly amplexicaul at the base, acuminate at the apex, margin obtusely to narrowly crenate-dentate; submerged leaves pinnatifid, 14–18 mm long, with capillary segments. Inflorescence lax. Flower: bracteoles 2, 2 mm long; calyx 2–2.3 mm long, with lobes 2 mm long, with small round glands; corolla up to 3–4 mm long, white or pink with purplish spots, with white spot on the lower lip, tube 3 mm long, upper lip bipartite or emarginate and 1–1.3 mm long, lower lip tripartite and 1–1.2 mm long; anterior (abaxial) stamens with filaments 0.8 mm, those of posterior (adaxial) stamens 0.6 mm; ovary 1 mm long; style 2 mm. Fruit septicide-septifragous and loculicidal, ovoid 2.3 × 2 mm.

## Distribution

Senegal, Guinea-Bissau, Guinea-Conakry, Mali, Côte d'Ivoire, Burkina Faso, Nigeria, Chad, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Tanzania; in Gabon, observed in the Ogooué-Ivindo

## Ecology

marshes and small pools; in Gabon, at 100 m elevation, in East Africa, up to 1000 m. B: Fischer (1999), Ghazanfar et al. (2008), Hepper (1963), Mielcarek (1996), Bibliography Philcox (1970, 1990)
