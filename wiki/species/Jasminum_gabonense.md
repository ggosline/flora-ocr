# *Jasminum gabonense* Pierre, nomen (voir Notes) .

**Genus**: [[Jasminum]]
**Family**: [[Oleaceae]]

## Description

Slender liane or lianescent shrub; if liane, with stem at least up to 15 m in length and 1 cm in diameter; branchlets puberulent. Leaves simple (unifoliolate), opposite or subopposite; petiole straight or geniculate, subcylindric, canaliculate above, (3–)4–8(–9) mm, puberulent; blade obovate-elliptic, (3–)7–8(–12) × (1.5–)3–5 cm, rounded to slightly cuneate at base, acute to acuminate over 0.5 cm and sometimes mucronate at apex, papyraceous to coriaceous, pubescent on the midvein above and sometimes with scattered hairs elsewhere, glabrous beneath, but puberulent on the midvein and generally on the main lateral veins, sometimes entirely puberulent, with margin revolute to enrolled; lateral veins in 4 to 7 pairs; domatia very conspicuous. Inflorescence axillary and/or terminal, cymose, lax, with 1 to 7 flowers, puberulent; peduncle 3–40 mm; bracts filiform, 2–8 mm. Flower very fragrant; pedicel (12–)15–20(–30) mm, puberulent to glabrous; calyx with tube (1.5–)2–4 mm in length, with 6 to 11 ± subulate lobes (3–)6–8 mm in length, glabrous to shortly hirsute; corolla white, glabrous, with tube 12–19 mm in length, with 8 or 9 lobes spreading to slightly reflexed, narrowly lanceolate-oblong, (10–)12–17(–23) mm in length, sometimes with small scattered hairs at apex; stamens: anthers 3–5 mm in length, almost sessile, inserted on the upper part of the corolla tube; ovary ± 1 mm in length, slightly bilobed at apex, glabrous; style as long as or slightly longer than the corolla tube or distinctly shorter (heterostylous flowers) (see plate 2). Fruit unknown.

## Distribution

Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, known from all provinces except Moyen Ogooué and Ngounié

## Ecology

secondary forest, coastal savanna; in Gabon up to 700 m altitude, elsewhere up to 500 m

## Source note

The name J. gabonense Pierre exists on the label of the Klaine 1190 collection at P, but was never formally published. Since this name has entered several databases, it is included here to show its status. Bi: Breteler (2002), Liben (1973). Bibliography
