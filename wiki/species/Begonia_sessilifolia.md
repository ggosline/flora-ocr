# *Begonia sessilifolia* Hook.f.

**Genus**: [[Begonia]]
**Family**: [[Begoniaceae]]

## Description

in Oliv., Flora trop. Afr. 2 : 577 (1871). Herb 30–60 cm tall, glabrescent, hirsute when young; stem scarcely branched. Stipules fairly rapidly caducous, ovate to rectangular, 6–13 × 3–9 mm, glabrous, margin dentate-ciliate in upper third. Leaf: petiole 2–20 mm; lamina obovate to elliptic, 4–17 × 2.5–8 cm, asymmetric, base oblique, on one side cuneate or rounded, on the other often auriculate, apex acuminate, margin entire somewhat sinuate and crenate-denticulate toward apex, glabrous, lower surface with nervures bristling with ± dense hirsute hairs. Inflorescence with 3–9 ♂ flowers and 1 ♀ flower; peduncle 3–6(–12) mm, curved; bracts small fairly persistent, narrowly ovate to rectangular, 4–8 × 1–2.5 mm, acute or with 2 or 3 ciliate teeth at apex. ♂ Flower: pedicel 5–7 mm, glabrous; tepals circular to broadly ovate, 4–7 × 2.5–5 mm, white, base sometimes reddish; stamens 14–17. ♀ Flower: pedicel reaching 5 mm; tepals 5.5–12 × 4–8 mm; ovary ellipsoid, 6–13 × 5–9 mm, including the 3 wings; styles 3, 3–6 mm long, stigmas capitate. Fruit solitary, on a peduncle 5–20 mm, circular to broadly obovate, 9–22 mm in diameter, with 3 subequal wings, rostrate for 3 mm.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea (including Bioko), Gabon

## Ecology

primary dense humid forests on slopes, often at the edge of watercourses, up to 750 m altitude. Use: eaten as a vegetable

## Source note

highly variable taxon, presumed to hybridize with B. macrocarpa and B. minutifolia. B: Irmscher (1925). BIBLIOGRAPHY sect. LOASIBEGONIA A.DC. Prodr. 15, 1 : 389 (1864). Terrestrial plant, perennial, rhizomatous; stem herbaceous often ascending. Leaf peltate or not, simple; lamina with upper surface plane or rarely bullate; venation palmate, rarely palmate-pinnate. Inflorescence bisexual, in unipary cyme, the branches strongly reduced, the basal ones bearing (1–)2–4(–12) ♂ flowers, finally 1(–3) ♀ flowers at the apex; bracts often persistent. Flower with 2 ± circular tepals yellow or sometimes white, with or without red spot at base. ♂ Flower: androecium zygomorphic; stamens with unequal filaments, connate at base, anthers dehiscing by 2 longitudinal slits. ♀ Flower: ovary 3–4-locular, 3–4-winged, sometimes wingless, narrowly elliptic or rarely very broadly obovoid, with translucent pericarp when fresh; axile placentation, with one placenta per locule; styles 3 or 4, connate at base, bifid at apex with branches bearing reniform stigmatic patches. Infructescence on an erect and always straight peduncle. Fruit solitary, erect, fleshy, finally dry with thin pericarp, indehiscent. Section distributed in West and central Africa, from Guinea to Rwanda and Burundi and northern Angola, currently comprising 20 species of which 9 in Gabon. B: Doorenbos, Sosef & de Wilde (1998), Irmscher (1925), Sosef (1994), BIBLIOGRAPHY Tebbitt (2005)
