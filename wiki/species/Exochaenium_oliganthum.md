# *Exochaenium oliganthum* (Gilg) Kissling Planche 25 ; Figure 6(A)

**Genus**: [[Exochaenium]]
**Family**: [[Gentianaceae]]
**Protologue**: Syst. Bot. 37(1) : 249 (2012)

## Synonyms

- Belmontia oligantha Gilg, Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 26 : 102 (1899).
- Sebaea oligantha (Gilg) Schinz, Bull. Herb. Boissier , sér 2, 6 : 736 (1906).

## Description

Herb mycoheterotrophic ("saprophytic") and without chlorophyll, 2–7 cm tall; stem simple to somewhat branched, whitish to reddish. Leaves opposite to more or less alternate, sessile; blade triangular to broadly triangular, 1.5–2.5 × 1.2–1.3 mm, apex acute. Inflorescence with solitary flower or rarely in 2-flowered cyme, the aerial with normal flowers, the basal hidden in the litter and with cleistogamous flowers. Flowers heterostylous; pedicel 2–25 mm; calyx with narrowly triangular-elliptic to linear lobes, 3–4 mm long, keeled and narrowly winged, with scales at the inner base; corolla 5–8 mm long, white to pale mauve, yellow at the throat, with tube 3–4.5 mm long, lobes narrowly elliptic to narrowly obovate, 2–3.5 mm long; stamens included, free from one another in the brevistylous flowers, but anthers fused into a ring in the longistylous flowers, filament 0.7–1.5 mm in the brevistylous flowers and < 0.2 mm in the longistylous flowers, anther with an oblongoid apical gland, 0.3–0.8 mm long and with two tiny basal glands; ovary globose; style 1–2 mm in the brevistylous flowers and 3.5–4 mm in the longistylous flowers, stigma narrowly oblongoid. Cleistogamous flower similar to the normal flower, but reduced. Fruit globose, 2–2.5 mm in diameter, or globose-depressed and ± 1.5 × 1.7–2 mm. Seed 0.1–0.25 mm in diameter.

## Distribution

Senegal, Guinea-Bissau, Guinea, Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroon, Gabon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Burundi, Sudan, Ethiopia, Uganda, Tanzania, Angola and Zambia; in Gabon known only from Estuaire

## Ecology

primary forests; in Gabon between 0 and 200 m altitude, elsewhere up to 1250 m

## Source note

E. oliganthum, studied in detail by Raynal (1967b), is a very peculiar species due to the absence of chlorophyll, heterostyly and basal cleistogamous flowers. B: Boutique (1972), Kissling (2012), Raynal (1967b), Sileshi (2002). Bibliography
