# *Fagara macrophylla* (Oliv.) Engl.

**Family**: [[Rutaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Fagara]]

## Description

A polymorphic, usually prickly forest tree with very large imparipinnate leaves. The treatment emphasizes the variable leaflet outline and armature and the difficulty of separating young plants from *F. tessmannii*. Leaflets of *F. macrophylla* are generally sessile or nearly so and less strongly asymmetric than in that species. Male and female flowers are 5-merous; the fruit separates into gland-dotted follicles with glossy dark seeds.

## Distribution

Widespread in tropical West and Central Africa, extending east to Sudan and south to Angola.
