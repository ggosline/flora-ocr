# *Stachyanthus zenkeri* Engler

**Genus**: [[Stachyanthus]]
**Family**: [[Icacinaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. 24 : 487, tab. 8, E-K (1898)

## Synonyms

- Neostachyanthus zenkeri (ENGL.) EXELL & MENDONÇA, 1. c. — KEAY, FWTA, ed. 2, 1 : 643 (1958).

## Description

Vine 7–8 m long, with young branches pubescent-hirsute, slightly yellowish. Leaves petiolate. Petiole pubescent-hirsute, 7–17 mm long. Blade glabrous above, appressed-pubescent below, light green or yellowish-green on the upper surface, greyish-green on the lower surface, elliptic to oblanceolate to elliptic-oblong, 10–23 × 5–10 cm; base rounded, apex acuminate mucronate. Midvein prominent pubescent above, pubescent nearly hirsute below. Secondary veins 5–8 pairs, ascending strongly arched, anastomosing 3 mm from the blade margin, prominent on both surfaces, pubescent below. Tertiary veins forming a fairly dense network prominent on both surfaces.

Inflorescence in raceme or spiciform raceme in both sexes. Racemes grouped on old wood on cushions, along the defoliate branches; rachis pubescent, 5–15 cm long. Bracts 0.5 mm long. Male flowers greenish, pedicellate (pedicel 0.5 mm long), hexamerous, sometimes pentamerous. Petals sparsely pubescent externally, glabrous internally, linear-oblong, reflexed. Stamens with slender filament 1 mm long; anther sagittate with 2 locules. Ovary reduced by abortion, conical pubescent. Female flowers similar to male flowers, without stamens. Ovary ellipsoid with flattened apex, 1.5–2 mm long; stigma discoid.

Drupe ellipsoid, flattened with 4 prominent ribs, 3 × 1.8 × 1.2 cm. Endocarp tuberculate. Seed ellipsoid, dark brown.

## Type

Zenker 1093, Cameroun (holo-, B, deletion.; iso-, P!)

## Discussion

This species has a range extending from southern Nigeria to Zaïre and Cabinda. It has not yet been collected in Gabon where it should be sought.

## Specimens examined

Leeuwenberg 6914, 18 km Bota, W Victoria, entre Batokke et Bakingeli, base du Mont Cameroun (fl. ♂, oct.). Zenker 1093, 4914, Bipindi (fl. ♂, s:pt.).
