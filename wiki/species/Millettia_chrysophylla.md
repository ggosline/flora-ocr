# *Millettia chrysophylla* Dunn

**Genus**: [[Millettia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: J. Bot. 49 : 220 (1911)

## Description

Liane or tree; stem reaching 15 m in length; branchlets lenticellate. Stipules caducous, triangular, 2–3 × 2–3 mm, sparsely pubescent. Leaf 20–30 cm in length; petiole 3–8 cm, glabrescent; rachis 6–11 cm; stipels subulate, 1–2(–4) mm, often caducous; leaflets 7 to 9, with petiolule of (3–)5–8 mm, blade ovate-oblong, 8–14 × 3–6(–9) cm, base rounded, acumen short or sometimes 1–2 cm, glabrous above, silky, golden or silvery below; lateral veins prominent, in 10 to 18(–22) pairs. Inflorescence terminal or axillary, paniculate 8–25(–40) cm in length, lax or dense; peduncle 1–6.5 cm; lateral branchlets velvety, 1–6 cm, scars of fallen flowers prominent; bracts caducous, ovate. Flower: pedicel 1.5–3 mm; bracteoles ovate-rhomboidal, 1 mm in length; calyx narrow, yellowish-brown, very silky, tube 4.5–5.5 mm in length, lobes obtuse, 1.2–2.2 mm in length; corolla cream-white, standard suborbicular, 14–17 mm in length, base sagittate, with hooked auricles, with claw 3–3.5 mm, wings oblong, 14–15.5 mm in length, transversely wrinkled, with claw 2.5–5 mm, keel ovate-oblong, obtuse, 13–15 mm in length, with claw 4–5 mm; ovary linear, 6–7 mm in length, with 4 to 6 ovules, silky; style 7–10 mm, pubescent, except at apex. Legume oblong-lanceolate, 12 × 2–2.5 cm, base ± attenuate, apex mucronate, yellowish-brown, silky, with 4 or 5 seeds. Seed oblong, flattened, ± 1.5 × 1 cm, dark brown.

## Distribution

from Guinea to the Central African Republic, Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola; in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Haut-Ogooué and Ogooué-Lolo

## Ecology

Primary and secondary forest, forest edges in humid stations; in Gabon at approximately 300–500 m in altitude, elsewhere up to 1200 m. Flowers and fruits March to May. Use: The branchlets have been used as rope for huts (Sierra Leone). b: Pellegrin (1949). Bibliography
