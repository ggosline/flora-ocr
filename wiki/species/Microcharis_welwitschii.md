# *Microcharis welwitschii* (Baker) Schrire

**Genus**: [[Microcharis]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bothalia 22 : 167 (1992)

## Synonyms

- Indigofera welwitschii Baker, Fl. trop. Africa 2 : 84 (1871).

## Description

, non N.E.Br. (1903), nom. illeg. Slender annual herb or rarely perennial, 10–40 cm tall; stem branched, erect to ascending or sometimes prostrate; with dark indumentum, strigulose-hirsute on stem and leaves. Stipules subulate to narrowly triangular, 1.5–5 mm long. Leaf: leaflets (1–)3 to 5(–7), the lateral ones opposite, the terminal one shortly petiolulate, with elliptic-oblong to elliptic blade, 7–22 × 2–5(–9) mm, apex acute, sometimes the leaflets fairly unequal in size. Inflorescence racemose, 1.5–10 cm long, with 3 to 15 flowers. Flower: pedicel slender, 1–4 mm; calyx 1–2.5 mm long, sparsely hirsute, lobes equalling to up to 2 times longer than the tube; corolla pink, red or orange, standard elliptic to rhomboid-oval, 3–4 mm long, keel without lateral appendages, shorter than the standard and wings, with beak 0.5–1.5 mm; stamens 2–2.5 mm. Pod ± curved, 15–35 × 1–1.5 mm, with 8 to 15 seeds, sparsely puberulent. Seed square, ± 1.5 × 1 × 1 mm, orange-brown to dark brown; hilum along the short side.

## Distribution

from Ghana to Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon and as far as Sudan, Tanzania, Malawi, Zambia and Angola; in Gabon, known from Ogooué-Ivindo and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

savanna, wet grassland margin, granitic rocks, on shallow soils and in crevices; in Gabon at 460–690 m altitude, elsewhere at 1050–2000 m. b : Gillett (1971a), Schrire (1992, 2012). ibliography
