# *Baissea welwitschii* (Baill.) Stapf ex Hiern Planche 17(1)

**Genus**: [[Baissea]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Cat. afr. pl. 1 : 675 (1896)

## Description

Vine reaching at least 10 m in height; branches smooth, glabrous or pubescent, but rapidly glabrescent. Leaf: petiole 2–10 mm, with well-visible colleters above and axillary colleters, limb elliptic to oblong 0.5–2 cm, cuneate to acute at base, acuminate at apex, papyraceous, glabrous or puberulent on both faces; secondary nerves 5 to 10 pairs, curved toward the margin, tertiary venation scalariform, forming right angles with the midvein; domatia present or absent. Inflorescence axillary and also terminal, lax; peduncle 7–30 mm, glabrous or pubescent. Flower fragrant; pedicel 2–5 mm; calyx with oval lobes, 1–2 mm long, glabrous or pubescent on the outside, 1 or 2 colleters at the inner base near the margin of each lobe; corolla whitish, funnel-shaped, tube 3–7 mm long, streaked with red on the inside, the outside generally hairy in pits above or just behind the apex of the calyx lobes, otherwise glabrous, the inside glabrous above the stamens, with oval to oblong lobes, 6–16 mm long, glabrous, ± ciliate; stamens inserted near the base of the tube, included, anther 2–3.5 mm long, pubescent dorsally; ovary pubescent or sometimes glabrescent at the apex. Fruit: follicles 35.5–51 cm long, glabrous, often shiny. Distribution from Cameroon to Angola in the south, also in the Democratic Republic of the Congo and in the Central African Republic, in Gabon, Ngounié and Ogooué, Lolo. Ecology: primary and secondary forest in Gabon, at m altitude elsewhere up to m.

## Source note

In van Dilst's revision (1995: 158), a note mentions "3 extremes" of B. welwitschii. These extremes are equivalent to the names placed in synonymy with B. welwitschii in this same publication. They are again accepted here as 3 distinct species: B. calophylla, B. erythrosticta and B. welwitschii s.s. B: Stapf (1902), van Dilst (1995). BIBLIOGRAPHY
