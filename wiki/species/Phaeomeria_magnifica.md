# *Phaeomeria magnifica* (Rosc.) K. Schum.
**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Phaeomeria]]
## Description
Large rhizomatous herb with blades to 70 × 15 cm and a blunt leathery ligule c. 1.5 cm long. The separate flowering stalk reaches 1 m and bears a dense spike to 12 cm; outer bracts are fleshy, waxy and red to pink. Flowers have 3 cm tubular bracteoles, a slightly shorter calyx, red corolla and red, white-edged oblong-lanceolate labellum. Fruits are pubescent obconic capsules c. 2 cm across, crowded in the infructescence.
## Distribution and status
An Indonesian species introduced to Gabon and very commonly cultivated in gardens; known as rose de porcelaine. It is now usually called *Etlingera elatior*.
