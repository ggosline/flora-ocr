# *Campylospermum dybovskii* Tiegh. Planche 8

**Genus**: [[Campylospermum]]
**Family**: [[Ochnaceae]]
**Protologue**: Ouratea gentilii De Wild., Rev. Zool. Afr., Bot. 7 : B53 (1920)

## Description

Ann. Sci. Nat., sér. 8, Bot. 16 : 298 (Dec. 1902). Ouratea gentilii De Wild., Rev. Zool. Afr., Bot. 7 : B53 (1920). Ouratea latepaniculata De Wild., Rev. Zool. Afr., Bot. 7 : B56 (1920). Ouratea malelaensis De Wild., Pl. Bequaert. 4 : 497 (1929). Ouratea dybovskii (Tiegh.) Aké Assi, Candollea 55(2) : 281 (2000). Tree reaching 25 m in height; branch with dark brown bark. Stipules caducous, leaving a well-visible scar at the base of the petiole, triangular, approximately 7 mm in length. Leaf: petiole 2–10 mm, stout, canaliculate above; blade narrowly elliptic to narrowly elliptic-obovate, (11–)16–32(–40) × (3–)5–8(–11) cm, ratio 3–5, cuneate to rounded at the base, apex acute to acuminate, coriaceous, with margin entire to slightly serrulate towards the apex; primary vein plane to slightly prominent on the upper surface, prominent on the lower surface, lateral veins in 9 to 19(–26) pairs, not or slightly prominent above, prominent below, tertiary veins scalariform, perpendicular to the lateral veins or to the primary vein, indistinct above, distinct below. Inflorescence terminal, lax, the main axis 12–30(–40) cm, generally zigzag; gemmate scales absent; racemes 2 to 7, generally spreading, 11–19(–27) cm, rarely branched; bracts caducous, sometimes foliaceous, elliptic, 3–6 × 0.5–2 cm, present at the base of the peduncle; cymules distant by 0.5–3 cm, 1- to 4(–7)-flowered. Flower: pedicel 5–12 mm, articulate at 1–4 mm from the base; sepals narrowly ovate to narrowly elliptic, (6–)7–8 × 2–4 mm in flower, 12–13 × 5–8 mm in fruit, covering the drupeoles, coriaceous; petals obovate, (8–)12–19 × (5–)6–8 mm, truncate at the base, rounded at the apex; stamens: anthers 5–6 mm in length; style 4–5 mm. Fruit: receptacle 4–5 mm in height; 1 to 5 well-developed drupeoles per receptacle, broadly reniform, 7–8 × 6–7 mm; cotyledons accumbent, equal.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo and Angola (Zaïre province); in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué, Haut-Ogooué, Ogooué-Ivindo and Ogooué-Maritime.

## Ecology

primary or secondary forest, gallery forest, swamp forest or flooded forest; on laterite quarries, on white and sandy soil; in Gabon, at 0–450 m altitude, elsewhere, up to 630 m. Uses: The roots are ground with seeds of Aframomum melegueta and eaten to remedy impotence (Neuwinger, 2000). Taxonomic note: This species is very close to C. calanthum and C. densiflorum (see note under C. densiflorum). The leaves of the first are generally bullate, but in case of doubt, one may compare the stipules and gemmate scales (both persistent in C. calanthum and stipules caducous, leaving a distinct scar, but gemmate scales absent, in C. dybovskii). The differences with C. densiflorum are found in the key. b: Bamps & Farron (1967), Bissiengou (2014), Farron (1963, 1968, 1985). Bibliography
