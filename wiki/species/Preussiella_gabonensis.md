# *Preussiella gabonensis* Jacques-Félix

**Genus**: [[Preussiella]]
**Family**: [[Melastomataceae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia, sér. 2, 16 (4) : 412, tab. 3 (1977)

## Description

Shrub branched, 1.20 m tall; branchlets subquadrangular, thickened at the nodes. Leaves subsessile, petiole robust, 2 to 3 mm long; blade narrowly elliptic, narrowly cordate at the petiole, acute to vaguely acuminate at the apex; 3 well-visible nerves and 2 submarginal nerves slightly inconspicuous, the transverse ones are oblique and reticulate; margins entire.

Inflorescences terminal and axillary on the final nodes, borne on a short and robust peduncle 2 to 10 mm long, with 2 foliaceous and ciliate bracts, 5 × 10 mm; normally composed of 4 verticillate unipara cymes, and an axial cyme, simple or bifurcate; rarely a second time verticillate; each cyme reunites only 2 to 3 flowers spaced apart. Flower with pedicel 5 mm; hypanthium obconic, 10 mm long; calyx spreading, lobes 2.5 × 0.8 mm, rounded at the margin, keeled-mucronate on the back, with some short capitate hairs. Petals pink, asymmetric, apiculate, 7 × 15 mm. Stamens homomorphous and practically of the same dimensions on the 2 whorls; anthers oblong, 4 mm; connective filament 1 mm long, abruptly curved upward, with a small dorsal boss on the curve, prolonged by an anterior linear appendage, slightly spatulate, 1.2 to 1.6 mm long; filament 4 to 4.5 mm. Ovary with concave apex, peripheral margin acute, without membranous crown; style linear 12 mm, slightly sigmoid; stigma punctiform.

Developed fruit unknown. — Pl. 26, p. 73.

## Type

Le Testu 2198, Gabon (P)

## Discussion

According to LE TESTU, this Preussiella is terrestrial. However, it should also be sought as an epiphyte, for it is surprising that this spectacular plant has been collected only once.
