# *Duranta erecta* L. Planche 36

**Genus**: [[Duranta]]
**Family**: [[Verbenaceae]]
**Protologue**: Sp. pl. : 637 (1753)

## Description

Shrub or tree, often spiny, 1.8–6 m tall; branchlets quadrangular or cylindric, pubescent when young; spines paired, (sub)axillary, 0.8–2.7 cm, straight or slightly curved. Leaves opposite or in whorls of 3 or 4; petiole 0.5–1.5 cm, slender, hairy; lamina ovate-elliptic to elliptic or obovate, 1.5–8 × 1–4.5 cm, cuneate to rounded at the base, acute or obtuse at the apex, entire or distally dentate, glabrous or hairy on both surfaces. Inflorescence 3–16 cm long, with numerous flowers; bracts and bracteoles linear, 1–2 mm, those of the lower flowers generally larger and foliaceous. Flower: calyx 3–6 mm long, ± pubescent on both surfaces, with triangular teeth not reaching 2 mm, mucronate; corolla lilac to blue-violet or white, with tube longer than the calyx, incurved, hairy outside, glandular inside, with lobes oblong to orbicular or broadly triangular, 3–6 × 2.5–3.5 mm, glandular on both surfaces; stamens glandular, ± 1.2 mm long; style shorter than the corolla tube, with capitate stigma. Fruit globose or obpyriform, 5–11 mm in diameter, yellow to orange, shiny, completely enclosed by the accrescent calyx whose apex forms an incurved beak; pyrenes 3.5–5.5 × 2.5–3.5 mm. Seed obovoid, 2.5–3 × 1–1.5 mm, white, smooth.

## Distribution

Native to (sub)tropical America and introduced and often naturalized in (sub)tropical Africa, including Angola, the Central African Republic and São Tomé and Príncipe, in Asia and in the Pacific; in Gabon, known from Ogooué-Lolo

## Ecology

Gardens, roadsides, scrub, riverside thickets, occasionally in forests; in Gabon at 250 m altitude, elsewhere at 50–2000 m. The plant can become invasive. Use: D. erecta is cultivated as an ornamental plant, often as hedges. The plant is suspected to be poisonous and its juice is toxic to mosquitoes

## Vernacular names

fleur vanille (fr.) ; golden dewdrops (angl.). Bi : Burkill (2000), Fernandes & Diniz (2005), Latham et al. (2021), Sosef et. al. (2006), B liographie Verdcourt (1992)
