# *Dipcadi viride* (L.) Moench.

**Genus**: [[Dipcadi]]
**Family**: [[Hyacinthaceae]]
**Protologue**: Suppl. Meth. : 267 (1802)

## Description

D. tacazzeanum (Hochst. ex A.Rich.) Baker, J. Linn. Soc., Bot. 11 : 400 (1871). D. filifolium Baker, J. Bot. 16 : 322 (1878). D. arenarium Baker, Fl. Trop. Afr. 7 : 520 (1898). D. hockii De Wild., Repert. Spec. Nov. Regni Veg. 11 : 517 (1913). Plant 15–120(–200) cm tall; bulb 1–5 cm in diameter. Leaves 1–5(–15), linear to broadly lanceolate, elongating during flowering, up to 50(–100) cm × 1–15(–31) mm, margins sometimes with minute teeth. Inflorescence unilateral; flowering scape smooth, with 4–35 flowers; bracts lanceolate, 2–30 mm long, caducous. Flowers with pedicel 2–11 mm, up to 18 mm in fruit; tepals yellowish-green, green or brown, 8–17 mm long, outer ones spreading, 10–17 mm long with a caudate appendage extended 1–22 mm, fused for ± 1/3 of their length, inner ones 8–14 mm long, with a prominence at the apex; stamens with filament 1.5–7 mm, anther 3–6 mm long; ovary turbinate, 3–7 × 2 mm; style as long as the ovary, stigma briefly 3-lobed. Fruit oblong, 5–14 × 9–14 mm, trigonous, with truncate apex. Seeds 4–7 mm in diameter, broadly elliptic-obovate to suborbicular.

## Distribution

species with a wide distribution throughout tropical Africa extending to southern Africa; in Gabon, observed only on the Batéké Plateaus

## Ecology

herbaceous and shrubby savannas, often on shallow soils or in rocky places; in Gabon between 600 and 700 m altitude, elsewhere from 75 to 2400 m. Taxonomic notes: very variable species, which explains the high number of synonyms. The taxonomic status of the different forms remains highly problematic.
