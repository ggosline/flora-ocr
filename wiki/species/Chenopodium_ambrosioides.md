# *Chenopodium ambrosioides* L.

**Family in Vol. 7**: [[Chenopodiaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Chenopodium]]

## Description

Strongly aromatic annual or short-lived perennial herb with alternate, usually toothed leaves and numerous small green flowers in axillary and terminal clusters. Flowers have five tepals and stamens; the one-seeded fruit remains enclosed by the perianth and contains a dark, lens-shaped seed.

## Distribution

Cosmopolitan in warm regions and recorded as the sole Gabon species of the genus.
