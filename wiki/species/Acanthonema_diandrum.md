# *Acanthonema diandrum* (Engler) B.L. Burtt

**Genus**: [[Acanthonema]]
**Family**: [[Bignoniaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Mus. natn. Hist. nat. Paris, 4° sér., 3, section B, Adansonia, 4: 416 (1982); Fl. Cam. 27: 21 (1984)

## Synonyms

- Carolofritschia diandra Engl., Bot. Jahrb. 26: 362 (1899).

## Description

Herb unileafy. Leaf (accrescent cotyledon), variable in size, 12 × 2.5 cm to 15 × 17 cm, otherwise as in A. strigosum.

Inflorescence with peduncle 1–2 cm long. Pedicels 5–10 cm long, with a mixture of glandular and non-glandular hairs. Calyx with similar pubescence, 3 mm long. Corolla 10–12 mm long. Filaments 3–4 mm long. Ovary 2 mm long; style 5 mm long; stigma perhaps relatively more deeply lobed than in A. strigosum.

ISOSYNTYPES: Staudt 118, Cameroun (BM); Zenker 1381, Cameroun, Bipindi (BM, E, P).

Apart from the various measurements given above, A. diandrum is very similar to A. strigosum and there is neither ecological nor geographical separation between them. However, the flowers of A. diandrum appear to be fully fertile and hermaphrodite; thus so far, despite limited evidence, they must be accepted as distinct.

GESNERIACEAE

The identification of this species with Carolofritschia diandra ENGL. rests only on the corolla size given in ENGLER's original description. The original types were destroyed in Berlin and all isosyntypes found so far are without corolla; consequently no lectotype was chosen.
