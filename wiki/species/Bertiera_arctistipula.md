# *Bertiera arctistipula* N. Hallé, sp. nov.

**Genus**: [[Bertiera]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]

## Description

(PL. 13, p. 63)

Shrub ca. 2 m tall. Stipules narrow, sheathing, 12-21 mm long. Petiole 6-12 mm long. Lamina subnigrescent above, glabrous, beneath puberulous, oblong-elliptic, 15-27 cm long, acute at base, acute-lanceolate at apex. Nerves 5-9 pairs, pubescent beneath. Inflorescences racemose with peduncle 3-5.5 cm long, in contracted cymes 8-12-flowered. Flowers sessile, white; calyx puberulous, shortly cupulate, with scarcely conspicuous lobes. Corolla puberulous, 19-21 mm long, 1 mm wide, throat narrow, 6-7 mm long, lobes attenuate, 4 mm long. Anthers 4 mm long, strongly included.

Shrub 1.80-2.20 m. Young stem with dense appressed ochraceous pubescence. Stipules slender, acute, 12-21 × 3-5 mm, pubescent. Petioles 6-12 mm with appressed hairs. Lamina dark brown-blackish when dry, glabrous above, finely puberulent on the lower surface, 15-27 × 3.5-8 cm, acute at base, lanceolate at apex, with attenuate, very acute acumen. Midvein and margins pubescent. 7-8 pairs of spaced secondary nerves.

Terminal pendulous inflorescences 5-13 cm long, with peduncle 3-5.5 cm. Rachis pubescent bearing short contracted cymes of ca. 8-12 sessile flowers. Buds slender and green, with narrowly cylindrical head. Flowers white, reflexed upward. Calyx pubescent with cupule 1.5 mm high, margin scarcely lobed. Corolla densely pubescent, 19-21 mm long, tube 1 mm wide when dry; corolla throat 6-7 mm long; lobes 4 × 1.5 mm, attenuate; internal hairs erect in tufts at the base of the lobes. Anthers deeply included, exceeding 4 mm in length. Style 13 mm.

## Type

N. Hallé et J.-F. Villiers 4660 (P), chutes de Kinguélé, Monts de Cristal; fl. 21-1-1968

## Discussion

This species of the subgenus Bertierella is close to B. iturensis K. Krause and B. retrofracta K. Schum.; from both it is distinguished by its more slender corollas, more deeply lobed, and by stipules more extensively sheathing. From B. iturensis it further differs by the size of its leaves, by the elongation of the capitate portion of the floral bud; from B. retrofracta it finally differs by its subnigrescent laminae, puberulent beneath, with fewer secondary nerves, by its longer petioles, by its inflorescences less contracted in length.

Dense, nearly intact, very humid forest on steep slope.

## Specimens examined

N. Hallé et J.-F. Villiers : 4660, type; 4747, NE de Méla vers Nkan, Monts de Cristal ± 900 m (fl. janv.).
