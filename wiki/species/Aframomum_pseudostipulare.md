# *Aframomum pseudostipulare* Loes. et Mildbr. ex J. Koechlin
**Family**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Aframomum]]
## Description
Herb to 1.5 m with narrow 18–22 × 1.5–2.5 cm blades and bifid 1 cm ligule. Subsessile basal spike; mauve to pink-violet flowers with yellow-throated circular crisped labellum. Distinguished from *A. stipulatum* by narrower leaves and flooded-forest ecology.
