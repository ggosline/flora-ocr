# *Cyperus flavescens* L. Planche 72

**Genus**: [[Cyperus]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Sp. pl. 1 : 46 (1753)

## Description

Pycreus flavescens (L.) Rchb., Fl. germ. excurs . : 72 (1830). Annual herb, slender or fairly robust, with fibrous roots; stems 5–50 cm × 0.3–2.5 mm, triangular to almost rounded, smooth. Leaf: sheath light brown to reddish or purplish; blade 5–30 cm × 1–3.5 mm, flat and rather soft, scabrous on the margin and midvein at least near the apex. Inflorescence in a dense globular fascicle of sessile spikelets or more frequently with a central head and 1–6 pedunculate heads of spikelets; heads with 15–40 digitately arranged spikelets; involucral bracts 1–4, foliaceous, the largest 3–15 cm × 1–3 mm. Spikelet linear–lanceolate, 5–18(–25 with the naked rachis) × 1.5–3.5 mm, pale yellowish brown or with a reddish-brown tinge; glumes ovate, 1.5–3 mm long, yellowish to reddish-brown, with or without pale margin and a greenish midvein often bearing 3 longitudinal ribs. Flower with 3 stamens; style with 2 branches. Achene obovate, 0.7–1 × 0.5–0.8 mm, with a short apicule, biconvex, distinctly transversely ridged or rarely almost smooth, with elongated superficial cells.

## Distribution

Pantropical, but also present in subtropical and temperate regions; rare or overlooked in Gabon, found only on the coast in Estuaire and Ogooué-Maritime (3 collections)

## Ecology

seasonally humid grasslands, lake margins and marshes, often in swamps and ditches; in Gabon, at 0–100 m altitude, up to 2500 m elsewhere. Taxonomic note: easily recognized by its annual habit and its achenes distinctly transversely ridged. It is highly variable, particularly in the size of spikelets, glumes and achenes. Many varieties and subspecies have been described, but in Gabon, only the typical form is known. Bibliography: Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al. (2010), Hooper & Napper (1972), Lye (1997)
