# *Cyperus flavescens* L. Planche 72

**Genus**: [[Cyperus]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Sp. pl. 1 : 46 (1753)

## Synonyms

- Pycreus flavescens (L.) Rchb., Fl. germ. excurs . : 72 (1830).

## Description

Annual herb, slender or fairly robust, with fibrous roots; stems 5–50 cm × 0.3–2.5 mm, triangular to almost rounded, smooth. Leaf: sheath pale brown to reddish or purplish; blade 5–30 cm × 1–3.5 mm, plane and rather soft, scabrous on the margin and central nerve at least near the apex. Inflorescence in a dense globose fascicle of sessile spikelets or more frequently with a central head and 1–6 pedunculate heads of spikelets; heads with 15–40 digitately arranged spikelets; involucral bracts 1–4, foliaceous, the largest 3–15 cm × 1–3 mm. Spikelet linear–lanceolate, 5–18(–25 with naked rachis) × 1.5–3.5 mm, pale yellowish-brown or with a reddish-brown tint; glumes ovate, 1.5–3 mm long, yellowish to reddish-brown, with or without pale border and a greenish median nerve often presenting 3 longitudinal ridges. Flower with 3 stamens; style with 2 branches. Achene obovate, 0.7–1 × 0.5–0.8 mm, with a short apicule, biconvex, distinctly ridged transversely or rarely almost smooth, with elongated superficial cells.

## Distribution

Pantropical, but also present in subtropical and temperate regions; rare or poorly known in Gabon, found only on the coast in Estuaire and Ogooué-Maritime (3 collections).

## Ecology

Seasonally wet meadows, lake shores and marshes, often in swamps and ditches; in Gabon, at 0–100 m altitude, up to 2500 m elsewhere. Taxonomic note: easily recognized by its annual habit and achenes distinctly ridged transversely. It is highly variable, particularly in spikelet, glume and achene size. Many varieties and subspecies have been described, but in Gabon only the typical form is known. B: Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al. (2010), Hooper & Napper (1972), Bibliography Lye (1997)
