# *Indigofera suffruticosa* Mill.

**Genus**: [[Indigofera]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Gard. Dict., ed. 8 : Indigofera 2 (1768)

## Description

Shrub 1–3 m tall, much branched; branchlets straight, striate, finely pubescent. Stipules setaceous, 2–3 mm. Leaf 5–10 cm long; petiole 12–25 mm; stipels absent; leaflets 13 to 21, opposite, with oblong or obovate blade, 20–30 × 6–13 mm, base cuneate or attenuate, apex rounded to acute, mucronulate, darkening when dry, sparsely (whitish-)pubescent above, pubescent below. Inflorescence in axillary racemes; raceme moderately dense, 2–9 cm long. Flower: pedicel 0.5–1 mm; calyx campanulate, silvery externally, tube ± 1 mm long, upper teeth deltoid, ± 1 mm long, lower teeth somewhat longer; corolla fugacious, ± (3–)4–5 mm long, scarcely opening, densely pubescent, standard yellowish-brown, wings pink. Pod reflexed, arched, subquadrangular, 1–2 cm × 2–4 mm, with 6 to 8 seeds, finely strigulose to glabrous. Seed rectangular-rounded, ± 2.5 × 1.5 × 1 mm, brown; hilum central.

## Distribution

of American origin, now distributed in intertropical zones; in Africa, known from West and Central Africa; in Gabon, observed in the Estuaire and Ogooué-Ivindo

## Ecology

herbaceous and wooded savannas, river margins, cultivated soils and fallows; at 0–1500 m altitude. Flowers in March. Use: Cultivated elsewhere, on a small scale for indigo dye. Bibliography: Amshoff (1939), Cronquist (1954), Lewis et al. (2005), Pellegrin (1949), Sunarno (1997).
