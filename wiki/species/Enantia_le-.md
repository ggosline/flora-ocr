# *Enantia le-* Testui Le Thomas (P1. 56, p. 307)

**Genus**: [[Enantia]]
**Family**: [[Annonaceae]]
**Protologue**: Adansonia,ser.2,2 : 3o6,tab.1 (1962)

## Description

Tree with blackish-brown branchlets, glabrous; branchlets densely appressed-pubescent rusty. Internodes 0.8-3 cm long.

Leaf buds short, rusty villous. Leaves with very thick petiole, 4-8 mm long, rusty pubescent; lamina subcoriaceous, broadly elliptic, 7-10-21 cm long, 3-4.5-8 cm wide, obtuse to rounded at the base, sub-obtuse, acute or slightly acuminate at the apex; upper surface grey-green, glabrous, lower surface olivaceous, pubescent with stellate blonde hairs, sometimes interspersed with rare simple or geminate hairs. Midvein impressed above, very prominent below and rusty pubescent; lateral nerves 9-13 pairs.

Flowers with thick pedicel, 4-7 mm long, rusty tomentose. Bracteoles 2, reduced, broadly triangular-ovate, concave, apiculate at the apex, 3-4 mm in diameter, caducous, densely rusty pubescent on the exterior, with simple hairs, 0.5-1 mm long. Floral bud conical, obtuse at the apex, 1 cm long, densely pubescent with long rusty hairs. Sepals lanceolate acute, 7-9 mm long, 3-4 mm wide, densely rusty pubescent on the exterior, glabrous on the interior. Petals fleshy, broadly ovate, 1.2-1.8 cm long, 0.7-1.1 cm wide, obtuse at the apex, concave at the base, rusty silky on the exterior, glabrous on the interior. Stamens subsessile, 2.5-3 mm long, 0.8 mm wide, connectives truncate-enlarged above the anthers. Carpels 2 mm long, ovary swollen, lageniform, densely pubescent, capitate stigma.

Fruit unknown.

## Type

Le Testu 8432, Ikembélé,région de Lastoursville (holotype P!; isotype BM!)

## Discussion

Species still insufficiently known, to be sought. It is characterized by the stellate pubescence of its vegetative elements whereas all the floral parts have a dense pubescence of long simple hairs.

Small tree of dense humid lowland forest.

25. PSEUDARTABOTRYS Pellegrin

Bull. Muséum Paris: 656 (1920); Fl. Mayombe, Mém. Soc. Linn. Normand. 26: 15 (1924); Bull. Soc. Bot. Fr. 94: 257 (1947).—R. E. Fries in Engler et Prantl, Nat. Pflanzenf., ed. 2, 17a, 2: 124 (1959).

Liane with indumentum of simple hairs. Flowers, generally solitary, with arched pedicel, not thickened. Sepals 3, valvate, free. Petals 6, biseriate, valvate, free, subequal, deltoid and concave at the base, connivate around the stamens and carpels, flat, long and narrowly linear above. Stamens numerous, prismatic, thecae linear extrorse, connectives prolonged above the anthers. Carpels numerous, immersed in the convex receptacle, very thick, connate; styles filiform, caducous. Ovules solitary, basal, erect.

Type species: Pseudartabotrys Le-Testui Pellegrin.

Monotypic genus, endemic to Gabon. Related by the form of petals to the genus Artabotrys and even more to the Asiatic genus Cyathocalyx Champ., whose petals are very similar, and flowers are opposite the leaves; the number of carpels there is however much more reduced, 1-6, and the more numerous ovules are inserted laterally.

The genus Pseudartabotrys is characterized by the gynoecium whose numerous carpels are immersed in the receptacle and connate, as in some Annona L.
