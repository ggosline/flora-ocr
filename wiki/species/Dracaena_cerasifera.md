# *Dracaena cerasifera* Hua Planche 9, Figure 2(C)

**Genus**: [[Dracaena]]
**Family**: [[Dracaenaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Hist. Nat. Autun 10 : 663 (1897)

## Description

Pleomele cerasifera (Hua) N.E.Br., Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1914 : 277 (1914). D. scoparia A.Chev. ex Hutch., Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1939 : 247 (1939) . Shrub or small tree, up to 6 m tall; trunk simple or branched, up to 30 cm diameter, grey with scaly bark, yellow-brown and smooth when young; leafy stems to the branch tips, presence of rapidly growing shoots with foliaceous cataphylls. Leaves spiral, often pseudoverticillate; pseudopetiole absent; blade obovate to narrowly obovate, up to 20 × 3–8 cm, with decurrent to narrowly cuneate base, embracing the stem over half its circumference, apex acute to shortly acuminate with a mucro reaching 2 mm, coriaceous, upper surface green, lower surface pale green, with median nerve prominent over half its length. Inflorescence pendulous, generally half the thickness of the supporting stem, loosely paniculate, up to 50 cm long, with branches reaching 25 cm; basal bracts foliaceous, primary branch bracts triangular-narrowly ovate, up to 2 × 1 cm, decreasing in size toward the apex; fascicles with 1 to 3 flowers; bracts subtending the fascicles, triangular-narrowly ovate, up to 2 mm long, scarious. Flower: pedicel slender, up to 18 mm; perianth 18–23 mm long, with lobes longer than the tube, reflexed, greenish-white with longitudinal striae, purple outside; stamens inserted near the throat, up to 3 mm shorter than the lobes, filament claviform, swollen, apex subulate, white, anther ± 3 mm long, pale yellow; ovary oblong, up to 3 × 1 mm; style exsert up to 1 mm, white, stigma ± 1 mm diameter. Fruit spherical to obloid, ± 28 × 30 mm, entire to slightly 2- or 3-lobed, bright orange, smooth and glossy; receptacle up to 12 mm long, much thicker than the persistent pedicel. Seed irregularly spherical, up to 15 mm diameter.

## Distribution

Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon and Republic of Congo; in Gabon present in Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué, Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

understory of primary and secondary forests, often deeply shaded; in Gabon, as elsewhere at 0–800 m altitude. Flowering recorded from November to February.
