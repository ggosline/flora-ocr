# *Dracaena cerasifera* Hua Planche 9, Figure 2(C)

**Genus**: [[Dracaena]]
**Family**: [[Dracaenaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Hist. Nat. Autun 10 : 663 (1897)

## Synonyms

- Pleomele cerasifera (Hua) N.E.Br., Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1914 : 277 (1914).

## Description

Shrub or small tree, up to 6 m tall; trunk simple or branched, up to 30 cm in diameter, grey with scaly bark, yellow-brown and smooth when young; leafy stems extending to the branch tips, presence of rapidly growing shoots with foliaceous cataphylls. Leaves spiralled, often pseudoverticillate; pseudpetiole absent; lamina obovate to narrowly obovate, up to 20 × 3–8 cm, with decurrent to narrowly cuneate base, embracing the stem over half its circumference, apex acute to shortly acuminate with a mucro reaching 2 mm, coriaceous, upper surface green, lower surface pale green, with prominent midvein over half its length. Inflorescence pendulous, generally half the thickness of the supporting stem, loosely paniculate, up to 50 cm long, with branches reaching 25 cm; basal bracts foliaceous, bracts of primary branches triangular-narrowly ovate, up to 2 × 1 cm, diminishing in size towards the apex; fascicles with 1 to 3 flowers; bracts subtending the fascicles, triangular-narrowly ovate, up to 2 mm long, scarious. Flower: pedicel slender, up to 18 mm; perianth 18–23 mm long, with lobes longer than the tube, reflexed, yellowish-white with longitudinal striae, purple outside; stamens inserted near the throat, up to 3 mm shorter than the lobes, filament claviform, swollen, with subulate apex, white, anther ± 3 mm long, pale yellow; ovary oblong, up to 3 × 1 mm; style exserted up to 1 mm, white, stigma ± 1 mm in diameter. Fruit spherical to obloid, ± 28 × 30 mm, entire to slightly 2- or 3-lobed, bright orange, smooth and glossy; receptacle up to 12 mm long, much thicker than the persistent pedicel. Seed irregularly spherical, up to 15 mm in diameter.

## Distribution

Liberia, Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Cameroun, République centrafricaine, Guinée équatoriale, Gabon and République du Congo; in Gabon present in Estuaire, Moyen-Ogooué, Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

Understory of primary and secondary forests, often deeply shaded; in Gabon, as elsewhere at 0–800 m altitude. Flowering recorded from November to February. B: Baker (1898), Bos (1984), Damen et al. (2018), Figueiredo & Smith (2008), Hepper (1968), Mwachala (2005, 2007), Velayos & Cabezas (2018)
