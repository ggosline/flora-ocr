# *Hetaeria heterosepala* (Rchb.f.) Summerh.

**Genus**: [[Hetaeria]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull. 5 : 207 (1934) ; FTWA : 210 (1968) ; FCAM 1 : 262 (1998)

## Synonyms

- Zeuxine heterosepala (Rchb.f.) Geerinck, BJBB 50: 120, fig. 2 (1980); FAC 1: 21 (1984).
- Zeuxine commelinoids Chev., Ex Fig. Bot. Afr. Occ. Fr. : 619 (1920).

## Description

Stem 12-20 cm, upper part glandular. Leaves 3.5-5.5 × 1.2-1.7 cm longer than the inflorescence, arranged along the stem, obliquely oblong-ovate, acute, base semi-cordate. Inflorescence 1.5-2 cm, 7-10-flowered. Flowers green and white, sometimes tinged with purple on the exterior. Perianth parts fine, fragile. Sepals ovate, obtuse, with a sacciform base, glandular on the external face. Petals falciform, wider in the middle, subacute. Labellum sessile, contracted above the middle: hypochile deeply cochleiform, with two internal digitate processes; epichile strongly deflexed, the 2 lateral lobes very large, ± obovate. – Pl. 32, p. 72.

## Distribution

Liberia, Côté d'Ivoire, Cameroon, Gabon, Sao Tome, Congo (Kinshasa), Tanzania

## Ecology

alt. 650-900 m. Terrestrial herb in dense humid lowland and foothill mountain forest
