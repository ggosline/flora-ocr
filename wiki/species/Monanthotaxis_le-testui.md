## Description

Not. Syst. 14 : 75 (1950); Bull. Soc. Bot. Fr., Mem. 31 : 53 (1949).

var. Le-Testui

Slender liane with branchlets densely pubescent rusty, ± hirsute. Leaves with petiole 3–5 mm long, densely rusty pubescent; blade papyraceous, elliptic-oblong to obovate-oblong, 4–10 cm long, 2–4 cm wide, rounded at the base, obtusely attenuate to rounded at the apex; upper surface glabrous except on the midvein, lower surface glaucous densely pubescent with soft, ± tangled, rusty hairs. Midvein prominent beneath, densely villous; lateral veins 6–12 pairs, obliquely ascending; reticulum lax.

Inflorescences extra-axillary, in lax panicles, 3–5-flowered, with long slender rachis, 4–11 cm long, puberulent. Pedicel slender, 2–4 cm long, puberulent; bracts small, linear, about 1 mm long, villous. Sepals broadly triangular-ovate, about 1.5 mm long, 2 mm wide, connate at the base, ± acute at the apex, rusty pubescent on the outside. Petals fleshy, subequal, elliptic-ovate, 6–8 mm long, 3–4 mm wide, pubescent on the outside, puberulent and not impressed by contact with the anthers in the bud on the inside. Floral bud with an empty space between the petals and the reproductive organs. Stamens 12–17, in two cycles, cuneiform, all fertile, 1.5 mm long; filaments longer than the anthers, 0.9–1 mm long, thecae extrorse, connectives thickened, papillose, truncate above the anthers. Carpels 7–10, 1.7–2 mm long, ovaries oblong, villous, styles very short but distinct, glabrous, stigmas bilobed, glabrous. Ovules 2–4, lateral, ascending.

Fruit unknown.

Type and only material studied: Le Testu 7845, region of Lastoursville, fl., January (holotype P!).

var. Hallei Le Thomas, comb. et stat. nov. (pl. 46, p. 261)

This variety differs essentially from the typical variety by the form of its stamens shorter and more stocky, 1 mm long, with filament clearly shorter than the anthers, 0.2–0.3 mm only, with connectives thicker toward the outside; the stigmas are sessile; the petals more fleshy and slightly shorter than in var. Le-Testui, are impressed on the inside by the stamens with which they are in contact in the bud.

Infructescences with rachis very slender as in the inflorescence, pulled downward by the weight of the fruits; pedicel slender, elongating in the fruit, 2.5–4 cm long, glabrescent; mericarps red at maturity, cylindric, contracted between the seeds; 1–2 articles ellipsoid, 0.8–1 cm long, 0.5–0.6 cm wide, puberulent, the last apiculate; stipes 5–7 mm long. Seeds 1–2, ellipsoid-oblong, whitish, with regularly ruminate albumen.

## Discussion

We have no information on the ecology of var. Le-Testui; on the other hand all specimens of var. Hallei were collected in ridge scrub, around 900–1000 m altitude, in northern Gabon. It is very often associated with Popowia ferruginea (Oliver) Engl. et Diels, whose vegetative apparatus is extremely similar.

The small white flowers, with very fleshy petals, are very fragile; after their opening, they close again very rapidly, taking on the appearance of buds.
