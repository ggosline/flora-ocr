# *Laccosperma secundiflorum* (P.Beauv.) Küntze Planche 13

**Genus**: [[Laccosperma]]
**Family**: [[Arecaceae]]
**Protologue**: Revis. gen. pl. 2 : 729 (1891)

## Description

Ancistrophyllum secundiflorum (P.Beauv.) H.Wendl. in De Kerchove de Denterghem, Palmiers : 230 (1878), nom. illeg . Ancistrophyllum laurentii De Wild., Bull. Jard. Bot. État Bruxelles 5 : 148 (1916), nom. illeg . Ancistrophyllum majus Burret, Notizbl. Bot. Gart. Berlin-Dahlem 15 : 747 (1942), nom. illeg . Laccosperma laurentii (De Wild.) J.Dransf., Kew Bull. 37 : 456 (1982). Laccosperma majus (Burret) J.Dransf., Kew Bull. 37 : 456 (1982). Neoancistrophyllum secundiflorum (P.Beauv.) Rauschert, Taxon 31 : 557 (1982). Neoancistrophyllum laurentii (De Wild.) Rauschert, Taxon 31 : 557 (1982). Neoancistrophyllum majus (Burret) Rauschert, Taxon 31 : 557 (1982). Robust climber; stem to 50 m, 20–25 mm in diameter. Leaf: sheath moderately to weakly armed with spines, more weakly armed on the apical portion; ocrea 25–35 cm long, armed as the sheath, particularly towards the apex; petiole 30–60 cm, concave above, rounded below, with brown pubescence, margins armed with unevenly spaced spines to 1.8 cm, black, pointing in all directions; rachis to 1.2(–1.5) m, hexagonal in cross-section, becoming trapezoidal then rounded distally, armed as the petiole, spines progressively shorter and more sparse distally; blade with 25 to 40 pairs of horizontal leaflets, evenly spaced, sigmoidal, 35–45 × 3–8 cm, apex very finely acuminate, margins armed with robust spines 1–2 mm, pointing forwards; cirrus to 1.5(–1.8) m, armed on the lower surface with unevenly spaced, curved spines, with sparse brown pubescence, with spine-like leaflets 3–4 cm. Inflorescences 6 to 10, produced simultaneously in the distal portion of the stem; peduncle 15–20 cm; prophyll 8–10 cm; rachis 25–35 cm, perpendicular to the main axis; bracts at the base of the rachis 2.5–3 cm long; flower branches pendulous, 15–25 cm, densely covered with imbricate bracts of ± 7 mm long, with triangular apex. Flower 1–1.2 × 0.3–0.35 mm at anthesis; calyx 5–5.5 × 3–3.5 mm, with tube 1.5–2 mm long, lobes ± 4.5 × 3 mm, with apex rounded to triangular; corolla with tube 1 mm long, lobes ± 9 × 2 mm, with acuminate apex, cream-white; stamens with filament 4 mm, anther 3 mm long; ovary 1 mm in diameter; stigma to 5 mm long. Fruit ovoid, 1.8–2 × 1.3–1.5 cm, with 18 to 22 rows of scales, monospermous. Seed ovoid, 1–1.2 × 0.8–1.2 × 0.5–0.7 mm, smooth, slightly flattened on one side.

## Distribution

Widely distributed in tropical Africa, from Senegal to Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, present in Haut-Ogooué, Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo and Ogooué-Maritime. Conservation status: Least Concern (LC)

## Ecology

Mature humid forests, more rarely in secondary forests, common on periodically flooded or swampy soils; in Gabon between 10 and 500 m elevation, elsewhere between 50 and 500 m
