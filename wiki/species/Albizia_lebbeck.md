Tree 2.5–15 m. Bark grey to pale brown, rugose. Crown rounded. Mature branchlets ± angular, glabrous to glabrescent, dark brown to yellowish or greyish when dry, with paler lenticels. Young branchlets ± angular, appressed-pubescent to sparsely appressed-pubescent, dark brown, with a few lenticels. Stipules very densely pubescent, very narrowly elliptic, 4–5 mm long, apex acute; very early caducous. Petiole glabrous to sparsely pubescent, 2–10 cm long; upper surface plane to ± canaliculate towards the apex, bearing between the base and the midpoint an ellipsoid gland prominent to ± prominent. Rachis ± pubescent, 1.8–17 cm long; upper surface canaliculate bordered by 2 longitudinal ridges, ending or not with a gland. Pinnae (1–) 2–4 pairs, decreasing in size from the apex to the base. Pinnae axis sparsely pubescent to pubescent above, 3.2–14 cm long; upper surface keeled bearing up to 5 glands at the insertion level of terminal leaflets. Leaflets 3–11 pairs, with a pubescent petiolule, 1–1.5 mm long. Blade glabrous on both surfaces or very sparsely pubescent below (very young leaves with blade densely pubescent below, less abundantly above); mid-green above and pale green below on the living plant; greenish, grey-green to slightly greenish blackish above and green, grey-green to slightly greenish blackish below when dry; oblong to oblanceolate for the apical ones, sometimes slightly falcate, reaching 4.7 × 2.6 cm; base asymmetric: proximal margin broadly rounded to cordate and distal margin attenuate; apex very broadly obtuse apiculate or retuse. Midvein eccentric towards the distal margin, glabrous on both surfaces or sparsely pubescent below, prominent on both surfaces. Secondary veins ascending, rectilinear, prominent on both surfaces; 3 basal secondary veins divergent, one dominant in the proximal half. Veinlets in a dense prominent reticulation on both surfaces.

Inflorescences in capitate spikes axillary fasciculate or grouped in a terminal panicle. Spike peduncle sparsely to densely pubescent, angular, 2–12.5 cm long. Bractlet pubescent externally, spatulate, 2.5 mm long, apex acute. Floral bud obovate. Basal flower ♀, with a pubescent pedicel 1.5–5 mm long. Calyx narrowly obconic, densely shortly pubescent setose externally, yellowish-green on the living plant, 2.60–4.15 mm long; lobes triangular, 0.30–1 mm long, apex acute. Corolla ± obconic glabrous or pubescent setose or pilose on the lobes, yellowish-green on the living plant, 7–8.50 mm long; lobes free deltoid-lanceolate, 1.25–2.5 mm long, apex acute. Stamens yellow to white on the living plant, 21.5–34 mm long; fused into a non-exsert tube 3–6 mm long; anther broadly oblong, 0.20 mm long, with a verruculose connective. Pistil substipitate; ovary glabrous, ellipsoid, 1.75–2.5 mm long, containing 8–12 ovules; style slender 18–33.5 mm long. Apical flower sessile. Calyx cylindric to truncate-conical, very shortly pubescent setose, 5–5.5 mm long; lobes free to 6 unequal, triangular, 0.50–0.80 mm long, apex acute. Corolla ± cylindric, entirely glabrous or pubescent on the lobes, 11–11.5 mm long; lobes free triangular-ovate, 1.50–2 mm long, apex acute. Stamens 21.5–25.5 mm long, fused into a tube generally exsert or subexsert, 10.5–13.5 mm long; anther broadly oblong, 0.20 mm long, with a strongly verruculose connective. Disc fleshy intrastaminal fused to the base of the staminal tube, 0.50 mm high, lobulate. Pistil sessile to subsessile (stipe 0.25 mm long); ovary glabrous, ellipsoid, 1.50–2 mm long, containing 9–12 ovules; style slender 11–27.5 mm long.

Pod dehiscent, glabrous, straw-yellow and brown at the seed level when dry, elliptic to elliptic-oblong, (7.5) 11–29.5 × 3–6.5 cm; base attenuate non-stipitate; sutural margins thickened; lateral faces with very effaced venation or concave except at the seed positions where it is prominent and reticulate; apex attenuate or obtuse, apiculate or acute acuminate. Seeds (1) 4–12, matt brown, oblong, oblong-elliptic, orbicular, broadly ovate, 9–11 × 7.5–9 mm; central areole, oblong, 4.5–7.5 × 2–6.5 mm. - Pl. 38, p. 153.

## Type

Herb. Linnaeus 1228.16, Egypte (LINN)

## Discussion

This species, native to Asia, is widely distributed in all tropical regions, particularly in Africa where it can naturalize.

## Uses

It is used as an ornamental and shade plant.
