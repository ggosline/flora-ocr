# *Dacryodes heterotricha* (Pellegr.) Lam

**Family**: [[Burseraceae]] · **Genus**: [[Dacryodes]]

## Description

Tree over 25 m and 1 m diameter, with buttresses to 1 m. Leaves 7–8-jugate; rachis 30–40 cm, ferruginous-tomentose; leaflets ovate-oblong, acuminate, base obtuse, 7–8 × 2.5–5.5 cm, retaining branched reddish stellate hairs beneath and on the upper midrib. Male flowers 3-merous with six stamens and glabrous disc. Fruit olive-shaped, blue-grey-white, about 2.8 × 1.6 cm; stone hard with a narrowly oblong shield; fruit edible.

## Distribution

South of the Ogooué through Mayombe to Balali country in Congo. Holotype: Le Testu 2007, Nyanga/Tchibanga, Gabon.
