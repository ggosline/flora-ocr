# *Adhatoda le-* Testui (R. Benoist) Heine, comb. nov.

**Genus**: [[Adhatoda]]
**Family**: [[Acanthaceae]]

## Description

≡ Rungia Le-Testui R. Benoist, in Pellegrin, Mém. Soc. Linn. Norm. N. S., Bot., 1 (3): 48 (1928).

Erect subshrub, with young branches covered in brownish-russet pubescence; internodes short, 12–15 mm. Petioles 7–15 mm. Leaf blade glabrous, 10–30 × 5.5–11 cm, obovate, attenuate for a long distance but not decurrent towards the base which is distinctly rounded; apex obtuse or rounded. 10 pairs of secondary nerves covered with brown pubescence on the lower surface.

Inflorescences in the form of spikes, axillary and terminal, 9–17 cm long, tetragonal and distichous, with peduncle 12–15 cm long; sterile bracts sessile, imbricate broadly ovate-acuminate, 12.5 × 10.5 mm, quinquiplinerved, obtuse; flowering bracts ovate-lanceolate, 12.5 × 6.5 mm, subtriplinerved obtuse at the apex; these bracts are all covered with very fine and regular pubescence, very pale brown with dark and well-marked nerves. Bracteoles 1 mm long or less. Sepals 5, subequal, 7 × 1.9 mm, lanceolate acute very finely and regularly pubescent on both faces and slightly ciliate. Corolla 15 mm long, white, slightly pubescent on the exterior and along the principal nerves on the interior; glabrous otherwise on the interior; the tube subcylindric, scarcely enlarged in its upper part; posterior lip bilobed, approximately 6 × 6 mm; anterior lip tripartite to the middle, somewhat longer than the posterior lip; filaments of the stamens pubescent, 9 mm long; anther lobes glabrous, inserted at different levels, 2 mm long; the lower lobe is furnished with a spur 0.7 mm long, the upper lobe with a much smaller spur 0.3 mm in length. Ovary densely pubescent, 2.5 mm tall; disk little developed, approximately 0.7 mm tall. Style pubescent, slender 10 mm long.

Fruit finely pubescent, with indehiscent septum, 2.5 cm long, stipitate, 4 mm in width and thickness. Seeds ellipsoid, 4 × 3.5 × 2 mm, with finely verrucose-granulate testa.

## Source note

— The case of Adhatoda Le-Testui is a good example of the difficulties in delimiting genera of Acanthaceae in the sense of C.B. Clarke: the species in question possesses at once characters of Justicia, Rungia and Adhatoda. R. Benoist had described this species in the absence of fruit (these have perfectly indehiscent septa) and had considered it, rightly so, by the majority of its other morphological characters, as a species of the genus Rungia Nees. It is nonetheless very clearly, in the delimitation of C.B. Clarke accepted here, a species of the genus Adhatoda. The most closely related species is Adhatoda Engleriana (Lindau) C.B. Clarke (≡ Justicia Engleriana Lindau), from East Africa; closely related to it are also Adhatoda Vasica Nees (≡ Justicia Adhatoda L., type species of the genus Adhatoda Mill.), from the East Indies, Adhatoda Schimperiana Nees (≡ Justicia Schimperiana (Nees) T. Anders., East Africa, Central Africa, Ethiopia, etc., as well as Justicia Pseudorungia Lindau, also from East and Central Africa.
