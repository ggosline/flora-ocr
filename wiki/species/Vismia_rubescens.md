# *Vismia rubescens* Oliv. var. rubescens

**Genus**: [[Vismia]]
**Family**: [[Hypericaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. trop. Afr. 1 : 160 (1868)

## Description

Liane or sarmentose shrub, up to 25 m in length; branchlets dark reddish-brown, pubescent when young. Leaf: petiole 5–15 mm, pubescent to glabrescent; lamina elliptic to ovate, 4–17 × 2.5–8 cm, rounded to subcordate at base, acute to weakly acuminate at apex, papyraceous to coriaceous, upper surface glabrous to glabrescent, lower surface glabrous to pubescent with stellate hairs and black glandular punctations; lateral veins 7–12 pairs. Inflorescence terminal; cymose panicles multiflorous, longer than the axillant leaves. Flower: pedicel 1.5–4 mm, pubescent; sepals ovate-oblong, 2–2.5 × 1 mm, pale green, with glandular punctations, pubescent on exterior; petals ± erect, oblong, 3–4 × 1–2 mm, greenish-white, puberulent on exterior at base and with a few black punctations toward apex; staminodial bundles with 3–5(–6) stamens, 2 mm in length, glabrous, anthers spherical, 0.2 mm in length; ovary glabrous, dotted with black glands, with 2 series of 3 ovules per locule; styles 2 mm in length, pubescent, stigmas scarcely swollen. Fruit ovoid to subglobose, 3–7 × 3–6 mm, orange, with black glandular punctations. Seed ellipsoid, 2.5 × 0.5 mm, without black glands.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon common and present in all provinces
