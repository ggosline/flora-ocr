# *Vismia rubescens* Oliv. var. rubescens

**Genus**: [[Vismia]]
**Family**: [[Hypericaceae]]
**Protologue**: Fl. trop. Afr. 1 : 160 (1868)

## Synonyms

- Harungana lebruniana Spirlet, Bull. Jard. Bot. État Bruxelles 29 : 320 (1959).

## Description

Liane or sarmentose shrub, up to 25 m in length; branches dark reddish-brown, pubescent when young. Leaf: petiole 5–15 mm, pubescent to glabrescent; blade elliptic to ovate, 4–17 × 2.5–8 cm, rounded to subcordate at base, acute to weakly acuminate at apex, papyraceous to coriaceous, upper surface glabrous to glabrescent, lower surface glabrous to pubescent with stellate hairs and black glandular punctations; lateral veins 7–12 pairs. Inflorescence terminal; cymose panicles, many-flowered, longer than the axillary leaves. Flower: pedicel 1.5–4 mm, pubescent; sepals ovate-oblong, 2–2.5 × 1 mm, pale green, with glandular punctations, pubescent externally; petals ± erect, oblong, 3–4 × 1–2 mm, greenish-white, pubescent to puberulent externally at base and with some black punctations toward the apex; staminodial phalanges with 3–5(–6) stamens, 2 mm in length, glabrous, anthers spherical, 0.2 mm in length; ovary glabrous, dotted with black glands, with 2 series of 3 ovules per locule; styles 2 mm in length, pubescent, stigmas barely swollen. Fruit ovoid to subglobose, 3–7 × 3–6 mm, orange, with black glandular punctations. Seed ellipsoid, 2.5 × 0.5 mm, without black glands.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon common and present in all provinces
