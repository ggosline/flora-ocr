# *Cyperus inselbergensis* Lye Figure 6b

**Genus**: [[Cyperus]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Nordic J. Bot . 30 : (2012)

## Description

Perennial herb with short slender rhizome producing many stems 15–50 cm × 0.3–0.8 mm, green, triangular to almost winged at apex, glabrous, with weak longitudinal ribs, leafy only toward the base for 10 cm; Leaf: upper sheaths green, lower ones purplish, all glabrous; largest leaf blades up to 25 cm × 1–2 mm, flat or folded (when dry), densely scabrous on margin and midvein. Inflorescence globose to hemispherical head, 3–5 mm wide, white, composed of numerous crowded spikelets; involucral bracts usually 3–4, foliaceous, spreading, the largest 3–10 cm long. Spikelet ovate–lanceolate, 1.7–2 × 0.7–0.9 mm, strongly compressed, bisexual, one-flowered; glumes ovate–lanceolate, the largest 1.5–1.8 mm long, white with purplish spots and a green midvein ending in a short straight or curved mucro, winged keel, scabrous. Flower: style with 2 branches. Achene ellipsoid to obovoid, approximately 1 × 0.7 mm, flattened, pale to medium brown, finely papillose.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea and Gabon; common in Gabon, in the Woleu-Ntem, particularly around Oyem, not found in the other provinces (8 collections)

## Ecology

seasonally wet grasslands and shallow soils of inselbergs; in Gabon, towards 500–700 m altitude

Taxonomic note: close to C. triceps (Kyllinga tenuifolia), but distinguished by its simple spherical inflorescence (not compound), its small spikelets 1.7–2 mm long (not 2–2.5 mm), its glumes with scabrous winged keel and the achene 1 mm long (not 1.5 mm). B: Lye (2012c). Bibliography
