# *Pyrenacantha acuminata* Engler

**Genus**: [[Pyrenacantha]]
**Family**: [[Icacinaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. 24 : 483 (1898)

## Synonyms

- Chlamydocarya tenuis ENGL., l.c. : 486 (1898).

## Description

— P. vogeliana auct. non Baill. : Chev., Expl. Bot. Afr. occ. : 128, pro parte (1920).

Dioecious liane with ± flattened stem, shortly pubescent, longitudinally striated. Petiole ± twisted, pubescent, longitudinally striated on both faces, 0.5–1.5 cm long. Blade glabrous on the upper face, densely pubescent with appressed hairs parallel on the lower face, green or grey-green above, pale dull green below, elliptic or lanceolate, 7.3–16 × 2.3–6 cm; base attenuate or cordately subauriculed, apex acuminate obtuse mucronate. Midvein prominent ± pubescent on both faces. Secondary nerves 6–7 pairs, ascending arcuate, anastomosing 2–6 mm from the margin of the blade, prominent on both faces, pubescent on the lower face. Tertiary veins in loose reticulate network prominent on both faces. Hydathodes conical or piriform, very prominent, glabrous, terminating the secondary nerves.

Male inflorescence in a fascicle of multiflorous racemes 1–5 cm long. Rachis pubescent bearing bracts. Male flowers tetramerous, green, 1.5 × 1 mm. Pedicel pubescent 2.5 mm long, bearing bracteoles. Petals free or slightly connate at the base, pubescent on the exterior, glabrous on the interior, broadly elliptic, 1.5 × 0.75 mm; apex acute or subacute. Stamens free, erect, gynobasic, 0.5 mm long; filament broad and very short, equal to one-third of the stamen; anther with 2 ellipsoid locules. Pistillode reduced to a tuft of very small hairs. Female inflorescence in a ± supra-axillary multiflorous spike. Rachis pubescent bearing bracts. Female flower tetramerous, green, 2.5 × 1.5 mm, sessile. Petals free or slightly connate at the base, pubescent on the exterior, glabrous on the interior, broadly elliptic, 2.5 × 1.25 mm; apex acute or subacute. Pistil 1.25 mm long; ovary broadly pubescent with reddish-orange erect hairs; style short and broad, scarcely visible, terminating in a stigma with several white digitate lobes.

Drupe broadly ovoid pubescent, transversely flattened; rostrum obtuse equal to one-third of the length of the fruit. Prickles ± reflexed at the apex on the inner face of the woody endocarp. PL. 18, 12–23, p. 77.

## Type

Zenker 1752, Cameroun (Z)

## Discussion

This species occurs in rainforests that are flooded or subject to flooding from Sierra Leone to Yangambi in Zaïre.
