# *Alysicarpus rugosus* (Willd.) DC.

**Genus**: [[Alysicarpus]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Prodr. 2 : 353 (1825)

## Description

Annual or perennial herb, ± suffrutescent, robust, 0.3–1 (–2) m in height; stem erect to ascending or prostrate, pubescent to almost glabrous. Stipules lanceolate, 5–30 × 1–3 mm. Leaf: petiole 2–17 mm; stipels 0.5–1.5 mm; leaflet with petiolule 0.5–1.5 mm, lamina oval-elliptic to narrowly lanceolate, 1.5–12 × 0.3–2.5 cm, base subcordate to rounded to truncate or cuneate, apex acute, mucronate, glabrous and reticulate above, appressed-hairy below, margin sometimes ciliate. Inflorescence in pseudoracemes or loose panicles, 3–20 cm in length, with flowers in pairs, internodes shorter than the flowers; bracts caducous, oval-acuminate, 5–10 × 1.5–3 mm, glabrous. Flower: pedicel 2–5 mm, puberulent; bracteoles linear, 1–2.5 mm; calyx glabrous to pubescent, tube 1–1.5 mm in length, lobes imbricate, lanceolate, 5–8 × 0.5 mm, acuminate, yellowish-violet, rounded or subcordate at base, apex with white to brownish cilia; petals white to pink or red-violet, standard 6–7 × 3–4 mm, wings 5–5.5 × 1–1.5 mm, keel 5–6.5 mm in length, often greenish; ovary linear, 3 mm in length, style ± 3 mm. Legume 5–10 × 2–2.5 mm, constricted between 3 to 6 articles 1–1.5 × 2–2.5 mm, puberulent, with prominent and anastomosed reticulations, green becoming black, distal article equal to the others but pointed. Seed oblique-quadrangular, ± 1.5–2 × 1.5 × 0.8 mm, olive-brown; hilum almost central, ± 0.2 mm in length.

## Distribution

Widely distributed in tropical Africa, in Madagascar and in tropical Asia; in Gabon, known from the Ngounié

## Ecology

Shrubby savannas, marshy and rocky savannas, ruderal stations, fallows; in Gabon at 170 m altitude, elsewhere at 0–2500 m. Uses: The plant produces good forage. Leaves and roots have medicinal properties against fever and cough and the plant is used in various African countries
