# *Baissea congensis* (Good) Jongkind Figure 2(C-F)

**Genus**: [[Baissea]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Candollea 77 : 22 (2022)

## Synonyms

- Zygodia congensis Good, J. Bot. 67 (suppl. 2) : 89 (1929).

## Description

Vine reaching 35 m in height; branchlets densely and shortly tomentose. Leaf: petiole of mm pubescent and provided with colleters above; limb oblong to elliptic, 11–14 × 2.5–6 cm, rounded at the base, acuminate at the apex, upper surface of the midvein with colleters and some hairs at the base, secondary nerves one pair, tertiary venation scalariform; domatia hairy. Inflorescence axillary and sometimes also terminal; bracts as long, but wider than the calyx lobes, densely pubescent on the outside; peduncle 0.3–2.5 cm. Flower: pedicel of ± mm; calyx with elliptic lobes of 2–3 × 1–1.5 mm, widening from the base, densely pubescent on the outside, glabrous on the inside, ciliate, minute colleters or absent; corolla white and reddish, with urceolate tube, 4–5 mm long, glabrous on the outside except for a few patches of small hairs, glabrous or with tufts of hairs towards the throat on the inside, with lobes 2.5–6 × 1.5–2 mm, ciliate, hairy on the outside and glabrous on the inside; stamens inserted near the base of the tube, included, anther ± 2 mm long; ovary glabrous. Fruit: follicles at least 80 cm long.

## Distribution

Gabon and Angola (Cabinda); in Gabon: Ngounié and Nyanga. Ecology: primary and secondary rainforest in Gabon up to m altitude.

## Source note

In the revision by van Dilst (1995), B. congensis was included in B. campanulata (under Zygodia congensis). B: Jongkind & Lachenaud (2022). BIBLIOGRAPHY
