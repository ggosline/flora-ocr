# *Asystasia lindaviana* Hutch. et Dalz.

**Genus**: [[Asystasia]]
**Family**: [[Acanthaceae]]
**Protologue**: Hutchinson et Dalziel, F.W.T.A. ed. 1,2 : 257 (1931) « Lindauiana¹ »

## Description

« Lindauiana¹ ». ≡ Filetia africana Lindau, Bot. Jahrb. 20 : 41 (1894), et in Engler et Prantl,

1. This orthography is not in accord with the Latinization of the name Lindau which becomes "Lindavius", not "Lindavius"; it is modified here in accord with note 6 art. 73 International Code of Botanical Nomenclature, Montreal, 1961. See also the epithet of Mendoncia Lindaviana (Gilg) R. Benoist (p. 68).

Shrub 1 m tall, glabrous, with cylindrical stems. Petioles 0.5–2 cm; leaf blade ovate, up to 18 × 7 cm, obtuse or slightly cordate at the base.

Inflorescence terminal racemose, up to 8 cm long; bracts filiform 2–4 mm long, bracteoles similar up to 2 mm long. Flowers in small axillary and terminal fascicles; peduncles and pedicels up to 4 mm. Calyx 4 mm long, glandular, with equal filiform lobes. Corolla zygomorphic, pale pink, up to 9 mm long, 1.5 mm in diameter, briefly pubescent and glandular; posterior lip 5 × 2 mm, two-lobed; anterior lip 4 × 4, with lobes 1 × 1, with the central lobe somewhat wider. 4 stamens didynamous; outer filaments 3 mm long; inner filaments short 2 mm; anthers with superposed thecae 2–3 mm long. Ovary 1.5 mm, glabrous; locules with two ovules; annular disk about 0.5 mm tall; fruit unknown.

Type from Cameroon: Buchholz s. nº, Jenssoki, Quaqua (B, holotype destroyed).

Material studied (Gabon and Cameroon):

Annet: 499, Edea, forest region and waterfalls of the Sanaga, Cameroon (fl. 4–10 July 1918).

G.L. Bates: 495, Mabam, Como River, Gabon (fl. July 1896), 2–4 ft. high. Brenan: 9412, S. Bakundu Forest Reserve, Kumba, Cameroon (fl. March 1948).

J. A. Raynal: 9657, Ukoamvone (12 km S Ebolowa), cacao station, forest at clearing boundary, right bank of the Seing, Cameroon, height 80–100 cm, small flowers, mixed green and violet (4 March 1963).

R. Schlechter: 12759, Ngoko River, Cameroon.
