# *Ledebouria camerooniana* (Baker) Speta Planche 7

**Genus**: [[Ledebouria]]
**Family**: [[Hyacinthaceae]]
**Modern family**: [[Asparagaceae]] subfam. Scilloideae — *Hyacinthaceae* as circumscribed here is superseded; see [[Hyacinthaceae]]
**Protologue**: Phyton (Horn) 38(1) : 105 (1998)

## Synonyms

- Scilla camerooniana Baker, Refug. Bot. 3 : App. 9 (1870).

## Description

Plant (9–)15–35 cm tall; bulb 1.5–3.5 cm in diameter, tunic brown to dirty white. Leaves sessile; blade lanceolate to oblanceolate, (6–)10–30(–35) × 0.6–3.5 cm, unspotted, margins entire, apex obtuse. Inflorescence slender; floral scape erect to curved, 0.5–1(–2) mm in diameter, glabrous; raceme 3–10(–20) cm, generally rather lax; bracts (0–)1–2, small, broadened and truncate at apex. Flower 3–5 mm in diameter; pedicel 3–11 mm, glabrous; tepals ± 6 × 1–1.5 mm, pale green or tinged with violet at apex; stamens with linear filament, generally violet or sometimes white, anther 0.5–1 mm long, yellow; ovary ± globose, lobed, ± 1 × 1 mm, green; style generally longer than ovary, 1.5–2 mm, white to violet. Fruit subglobose to broadly obovoid, distinctly 3-lobed, up to 3.5 × 6 mm. Seeds 3–3.5 mm in diameter.

## Distribution

Benin, Cameroon, Gabon; in Gabon collected north of Libreville and at Lastoursville

## Ecology

in coastal savannas and pond margins, on dry and wet soils; in Gabon at 0–250 m altitude, elsewhere up to 700 m. Taxonomic note: The distinction with the closely related species, L. sudanica (A.Chev.) Burg, is not evident and the two may well represent the same species. B: van der Burg (2006), Speta (1998a).
