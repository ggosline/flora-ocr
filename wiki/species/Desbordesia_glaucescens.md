# *Desbordesia glaucescens* (Engl.) van Tiegh.

**Family**: [[Irvingiaceae]] · **Genus**: [[Desbordesia]]

## Description

Very large tree to 50 m, with thin wing-like branched buttresses rising over 10 m. Branchlets glabrous with reddish lenticels; stipules small and caducous. Blade ovate-oblong to obovate-oblong-elliptic, shortly acuminate, base asymmetric and acute or obtuse, 10–12 × 4–5 cm, glabrous, dark glossy green above and glaucous matt beneath; about eight pairs of ascending secondary veins and a double veinlet network; petiole 8 mm, twisted and flattened above.

Axillary panicles near branch ends, about 10 cm. Flowers yellowish-white, glabrous, in small terminal groups; pedicels 2.5–4 mm. Calyx campanulate; sepals about 1 mm; petals elliptic, veined, about 2.5 mm; stamens 10, filaments 3–4 mm; disc papillose and uneven; ovary flat, style bent from the base. Fruit a flat membranous winged samara, 12–14 × 3.5–4 cm, containing 1–2 flat seeds 3.5 × 1 cm.

## Distribution and ecology

Abundant from Cameroon to Mayombe and throughout Gabonese inland forest, but reported absent from littoral okoumé–ozouga forest. It forms alep–miama forest with *Calpocalyx heitzii*. Flowers July–October. Seeds are used for odika fat; wood very hard.

## Specimens examined

Holotype: Klaine 1580, Libreville. The treatment lists numerous Gabon collections by Klaine, Fleury and Le Testu from Lake Zilé, Kango, Libreville, Dabiliba, Massanga, Tchibanga, Mayombe, Dyouzou, Poungui, Moupéyou, Lastoursville, Nkā and Nidzie.

## Vernacular names

*alep*, *alo*, *allô* (Fang).
