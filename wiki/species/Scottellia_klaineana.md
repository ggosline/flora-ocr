– S. klaineana PIERRE var. mimfiensis (GILG) PELLEGR., Mém. Soc. Bot. Fr.: 108 (1952); SLEUMER, Blumea 20 : 281 (1972).

– S. klaineana PIERRE var. kamerunensis (GILG) PELLEGR., Mém. Soc. Bot. Fr. : 109 (1952).

Tree (10–30 m tall); branchlets greyish, glabrous.

Leaves with petiole 0.5–2 cm long, glabrous; blade oblong-obovate to elliptic-obovate, cuneate or rounded at base, acuminate at apex, 5–14 × 2–6 cm, entire to crenulate, coriaceous, glabrous; secondary nerves 5–8 pairs.

Inflorescences in racemes grouped into pseudopanicles, axillary. Flowers ♀; pedicel 3–5–(8) mm long, glabrous or ± pubescent; sepals 5, elliptic-ovate, 3–4 × 2 mm, ciliate; petals 5, oblong-ovate to elliptic-obovate, 5 × 2 mm, ciliate and bearing a densely hairy scale; stamens 5, with filament 1.5–2.5 mm long, glabrous; anthers horseshoe-shaped, 2–2.5 mm long; ovary ± ridged, glabrous, with 3 pluriovulate placentas; style terminated by 3 curved stigmatic branches.

Fruits: subglobose capsules, small (6–7 mm diameter), with grumose surface; seeds 1–3, angular or subglobose. - Pl. 9, p. 33.
