# *Ancistrorhynchus capitatus* (Lindl.) Summerh.

**Genus**: [[Ancistrorhynchus]]
**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bot. Mus. Le afl. Harvard Univ. 11 : 205 (1944) ; FTWA : 272 (1968) ; CRIBB, FTEA, Orchid. 3 : 588 (1989) ; FAC 2 : 572 (1992) ; FCAM 3 : 774 (2001)

## Synonyms

- Angorchis capitata (Lindl.) O. Kuntze, Rev. Gen. Pl. 2 : 651 (1891).

## Description

Stem 1–10 cm. Leaves 3–8, 15–32 × 1.1–2.1 cm, linear, coriaceous, unequally bilobed. Inflorescence capitate, multiflorous, < 1.5 (–4) cm. Flowers small, white, with a green patch on the labellum. Dorsal sepal oblong-ovate, elliptic, obtuse, papillose on the outer apical half. Petals oblong-oblanceolate, falcate, obtuse. Lateral sepals oblong-ovate, oblique, subobtuse, sparsely papillose on the outer apical half. Labellum 4.5–5.7 × 4–5 mm, elliptic or ovate, concave at the base, rounded, margins recurved upwards and undulate. Spur 7–8.5 mm, narrowly cylindrical, slightly incurved, clavate at the apex, obtuse. – Pl. 150, p. 339.

## Distribution

Sierra Leone, Liberia, Togo, Nigeria, Cameroun, Guinée équatoriale, Gabon, République centrafricaine, Zaïre, Ouganda

## Ecology

alt. 1 100–1 300 m. Epiphyte in evergreen forest, periodically inundated forest with Brachystegia eurycoma and Garcinia, observed at 3 m above the ground, on Gilbertiodendron dewevrei
