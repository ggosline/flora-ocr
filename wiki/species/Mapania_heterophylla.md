# *Mapania heterophylla* (Boeckeler) Lye Figure 15b

**Genus**: [[Mapania]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Lidia 7 : 88 (2011)

## Synonyms

- Hypolytrum heterophyllum Boeckeler, Beitr. Cyper . 1 : 22 (1888).

## Description

H. nemoreum (Vahl) Spreng. var. minus Cherm., Bull. Soc. Bot. France 77 : 276 (1930). Slender herb, with solitary stems at the apex of fine stolons ; central stem , 30– 90 cm × 1–3 mm, triangular, smooth below, strongly scabrous above, green. Leaves 3–10, mostly basal except 2–3 disposed toward the upper part of the stem, shorter or longer than the stem ; sheath green ; blade flat or slightly folded, trinerved, the largest 20–90 × 0.5–2.2 cm , with margins scabrous to finely dentate at least near the apex, gradually narrowed to an acute apex. Inflorescence a terminal panicle, lax, 2–18 × 2–13 cm , pale brown to dark brown, with 1–15 primary rays at most 5 cm long, each subtending short secondary branches or spikes ; involucral bracts often

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo ; rare in Gabon, found in Ngounié and Ogooué-Ivindo (3 collections)

## Ecology

humid forests, sometimes along rivers and streams ; in Gabon, at 0–200 m altitude, up to 500 m in DRC. Taxonomic note : recognized by its lax terminal panicle bearing spikes 3.5–5 mm long and its small achenes 1.5–2 mm long. b : Nelmes (1955a). Bibliography
