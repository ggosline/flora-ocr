# *Pauridiantha micrantha* (Hiern) Brem. (Fig. 8)

**Genus**: [[Pauridiantha]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bremekamp, Bot. Jahrb. 71 : 213 (1940)

## Synonyms

- Urophyllum micranthum Hiern, F.T.A. 3 : 74 (1877).

## Description

A very slender shrub 1.50 (2.50) m in height; the stem exceeding 1 cm in diam.; branches plagiotropic regularly arranged; internodes with short fairly dense pubescence and 2(3) cm long. Stipules tapered 4-6 mm long, not glabrous. Petiole 2.5-4 mm, pubescent. Blade greyish-olivaceous when dry ovate-oblong, 5-9 × 1.6-2.6 cm, glabrous above except for a pubescent midline; base subacute to nearly rounded; apex lanceolate subacuminate, mucronulate over ± 0.5 mm. 9-10 pairs of secondary veins arched, all moderately ascending, weakly pubescent below. No visible domatia.

Inflorescences contracted axillary sessile 2-8-flowered. Bud ovate, moderately pubescent, reaching in vivo 3-4.5 mm. Flower subsessile. Calyx without cupuliform rim, with 5 denticles reaching 0.7 (0.8) mm long. Corolla expanded barely 5 mm in diam., pale yellow. Throat masked by white hairs where the anthers are hidden. Anther mediodorsifix yellow, under 1 mm, with acute salient connective; filament 0.5 mm. Style pale green, 2 mm (short-styled form studied), with pubescent column 1 mm, with oblong stigma, bilobed at apex. Disc convex yellow about 1 mm in diam., glabrous. Ovary with 2 locules. Fruit glabrescent exceeding 3 mm in diam., green before maturity, with small sepals erect or ± arched introverse. Seeds globose, 0.4 mm in diam., ornamented with a reticulum not marked.

## Type

G. Mann 1735, 10 lat. N, Monts de Cristal, récolté en 1862 (P! ex K)

## Discussion

Species related, according to Bremekamp, to P. multiflora K. Schum. (= Urophyllum stenophyllum K. Krause), from Cameroon.

Material studied (only other one known):

N. Hallé: 848, Atok-Fina, Cristal Mountains, slope towards 350-400 m alt. (fl. and fr. fully in August).
