# *Amaranthus hybridus* L.

**Genus**: [[Amaranthus]]
**Family**: [[Amaranthaceae]]
**Protologue**: L. Sp. Pl. ed. I : 990 (1753)

## Synonyms

- A. cruentus L.
- A. paniculatus L.
- A. caudatus sensu auct. plur., non L.

## Description

Species represented in our flora by the following subspecies:

Subsp. cruentus (L.) Thell. var. paniculatus (L.) Thell.

p. p. non L. — HUTCH. et DALZ., F.W.T.A.ed. 1, 1: 125 (1927) p. p., non L.

Annual herb reaching 1.50 m in height, with erect, angular, glabrescent stem. Leaves oval to elliptic, attenuate at the base, obtuse at the apex, tinged with purple beneath (in vivo), 5-20 cm long and 2-8 cm wide, with well-marked veins, with petioles 2-14 cm long.

Spikes hispid, formed of small contracted cymes approximated together, reaching 6 cm long, grouped into a paniculate inflorescence reaching up to 40 cm long and 12 cm wide; terminal spike more or less recurved. Flowers unisexual, purple (in vivo), 5-merous; bracts and bracteoles longer than the perianth, aristate. Tepals of ♂ flowers 2.5 cm long, oblong-acute; tepals of ♀ flowers 1.5-2 mm long accrescent, the outer ones spatulate, the inner ones elliptic. Stamens scarcely longer than the perianth. Ovary oblong, almost imperceptible in ♂ flowers; stigmas 3-4. Fruit exceeding the perianth, with transverse dehiscence; seeds circular, blackish, 1 mm diam.

The type of the variety is American.

Inhabits cultivated lands in temperate and warm regions of the globe. The leaves are eaten as a vegetable.
