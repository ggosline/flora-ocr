# *Telecantha cauliflora* (R. Good) Brem.

**Genus**: [[Stelechantha]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Protologue**: Bremekamp, Bot. Jahrb. 71 : 218 (1940)

## Synonyms

- Urophyllum cauliflorum R. Good, Journ. Bot. 61 : 87 (1923).
- U.(.) stelecanthum Mildbread, Wiss. Ergebn. D. Zentr. Afr. Exp. 1910-1911, 2:91 (1922), nomen.

## Description

Small shrub 2–3 m tall; wood exceeding 12 mm in diameter; branches glabrous; internodes 3–8 cm. Stipules coriaceous glabrous, longitudinally striate, insertion slightly or distinctly narrower than the branch, ovate or elliptic acute, 7–16 × (2) 3–8 mm. Petiole 7–20 mm, glabrous. Blade coriaceous, ± discolorous, olivaceous, glabrous, obovate or elliptic, 10–21 × 3–6.5 cm, acute at base; acumen ± 1 cm. 8–12 pairs of lateral veins slightly ascending, in arches often distinctly closed.

Inflorescences many-flowered cauline in glomerules or small briefly pedunculate cymes, micropubescent, about 2 cm long. Bud acute subacuminate 10–14 mm long, nigrescent when dry. Flower bluish or blue-violet. Calyx micropubescent, truncate, ample, cupuliform 2.5–3 mm in height. Corolla with tube 7–11 mm long by 3 mm wide; 5 lobes 3 mm long with rostrate apex; throat with erect pubescence at the level of anthers, with internal hair inflexed below. 5 anthers about 3 × 0.5–0.6 mm, with narrow acumen about 0.5 mm, with filament 1–1.5 mm. Disc about 2 mm in diameter, barely convex, with wrinkled surface. Glabrous style 4–4.5 mm including the stigma 2–2.5 mm, narrow and bilobed. Ovary with 2 locules separated from the apex downward for more than half their length by a false septum. Ovular mass in deep V 1.2 mm high. The fruit would be a red berry according to Bremekamp (we do not know the source of this indication), mauve according to Bouquet; many-flowered fruiting inflorescences with pedicels reaching 2 cm.

## Type

Gossweiler 7661 (type BM) et 7662, Mayumbe portugais (échantillons vus)

## Discussion

Rarely observed, this species is also from Cameroon, northern slope of the Ntem basin, and from Congo, in the watershed region between the upper Ogooué and the Kouilou basin (A. Bouquet 1002).

Le Testu: 5582, between Moussanga and Nyanga-Moubigou, Haute-Ngounyé (fl. Oct.).
