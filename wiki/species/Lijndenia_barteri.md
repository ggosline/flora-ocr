# *Lijndenia barteri* (Hooker f.) Bremer

**Genus**: [[Lijndenia]]
**Family**: [[Melastomataceae]]
**Protologue**: Nord. J. Bot. 2 : 124 (1982); Jacq.-Fél., Fl. Cam. 24 : 174 (1983)

## Synonyms

- Memecylon barteri Hook. f., FTA 2 : 462 (1871).
- Spathandra barteri (Hook. f.) JacQ.-Fél., Adansonia, sér. 2, 18 (2) : 226 (1978).

## Description

Shrub to small tree 8 to 10 m; branches rounded. Leaves subcoriaceous; petiole 5 to 10 mm; blade varying from narrowly elliptic-lanceolate and 3 × 8 cm, to broadly elliptic and up to 7 × 14 cm, base rounded, or broadly wedge-shaped and cuneate on the petiole; acumen short, obtuse; upper surface granulate; 3 nerves, prominent beneath; 2 submarginal finer ones, arching upward, sometimes inconspicuous; the transverse ones few in number, oblique, ± bifurcate or reticulate, scarcely visible.

1 to 5 cymes per axil, on the leafy and leafless nodes; varying from 1 to 4 cm in total length, with stipe 0.5 to 2 cm; 2 to 3 times divided; bracts oval, ± cucullate, persistent on all the articulations (sometimes caducous on the lowest), including immediately below the flowers

1. Glomerate in the species of Madagascar and Asia.

which are sessile. Flowers subglobose or obovoid in bud; hypantho-calyx campanulate, ± 2.5 × 2 mm; sepal lobes developed, semi-oval to triangular, with thin margin, imbricate and overlapping widely in the still young bud. Petals white, subcircular or broadly semi-oval, unguiculate, up to 2 × 2 mm. Stamens with anther 1.2 × 1 mm; connective short, conical-obtuse, not saddled, gland very reduced and situated toward the apex, or absent; filament slender, flexuous, 2 to 4.5 mm; epigynous chamber deep, without membranous septa; style linear, 2.8 to 3.5 mm long, stigma truncate to discoid. Ovary somewhat prominent at the center, occupying only one-third or one-half the height of the hypantho-calyx; ovary locule 2-ovulate.

Fruit with pedicel 1 mm, globose, 5 mm in diameter, calyx lobes applied to the ovary. — Pl. 51, p. 143.

## Type

Barter 2152, Nigeria (holo-, K!)

## Discussion

Well-characterized species. Occurs from Senegal (Casamance) to Zaïre and, probably, as far as Mozambique. In Gabon, is to be sought in caducous thickets rather than in evergreen forest.

## Specimens examined

Klaine 3263, Libreville (mars).
