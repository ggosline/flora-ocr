# *Aneilema umbrosum* (Vahl) Kunth subsp. umbrosum Planche 4, Figure 1(E, F)

**Genus**: [[Aneilema]]
**Family**: [[Commelinaceae]]
**Protologue**: Enum. pl. 4 : 71 (1843)

## Description

A. nigritanum (C.B.Clarke) Hutch., Fl. W. trop. Afr. 2 : 312 (1936) . Perennial herb, decumbent, rooting at the nodes, with fibrous roots; stem strongly branched, ascending, reaching ± 30 cm in height, puberulent with minute hooked hairs. Leaves spiral; sheath reaching 1.2 cm in length, puberulent with stiff spreading hairs, apex with long reddish hairs; pseudpetiole 2–10 mm, with long reddish hairs; lamina lanceolate, 5–10 × 2–3(–3.5) cm (pseudpetiole included), base cuneate to rounded, apex acute, margins ± undulate, scabrous toward the apex, glabrescent to pilose. Inflorescence terminal, sometimes also subterminal, in a loose spiciform thyrse, broadly ovoid, 1.5–3 × 2.5–5 cm, with 5 to 8 branches bearing up to ± 30 cincinni and numerous bracteoles; axes puberulent or glabrous; peduncle 1–2.5 cm, with a bract at mid-length or at the base; cincinni reaching 2.5 cm in length, with up to 11 bisexual flowers. Flower ± 10 mm in diameter; bracteoles cupuliform, perfoliate, 1–1.5 mm in length, glandular below the apex, glabrous with scattered minute hooked hairs; pedicel 3–7.5 mm, erect at fruiting, puberulent with hooked hairs toward the apex; sepals oval to elliptic, 3–4 mm in length, green with red-purple margins toward the base, concave, apex cucullate, glandular below the apex, generally sparsely puberulent; upper petals oval, ± 5 × 3 mm with a claw of ± 1.5 mm, white, very rarely pale blue, the lower oval-elliptic, slightly concave, apex slightly cupuliform, ± 3 × 1.5 mm, greenish white; stamens with anther of ± 0.4 × 0.4–0.5 mm, with yellow pollen, the lateral ones with a filament of ± 2 mm, slightly sigmoid, bearded on the upper half, the median with a filament of ± 1.8 mm; staminodes 2 or 3, the median absent or rudimentary, filament ± 2 mm, antherodia bilobed, yellow; ovary ± 1 × 1 mm, glabrous; style arcuate-descending, ± 3 mm, stigma capitate. Fruit 2-locular, oblong-ellipsoid, 4–5 × 2.5–3.5 mm, bivalvate, glabrous, smooth, white, locule with (1–)2 seeds. Seed ovoid to transversely ellipsoid, 1.8–2.2 × 1.4–1.7 mm; testa coarsely foveolate-reticulate, light brown, orange-brown, pinkish grey or grey.

## Distribution

Ivory Coast, Ghana, Togo, Benin, Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea (Bioko and Río Muni), Gabon, Republic of the Congo and Central African Republic; in Gabon in Haut-Ogooué, Ngounié, Ogooué-Lolo, Ogooué-Ivindo and Woleu-Ntem

Senegal, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Ivory Coast, Ghana, Togo, Benin, Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea (Bioko and Río Muni), Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Central African Republic, Sudan, Uganda and Tanzania, also in tropical America; in Gabon in Nyanga, Ogooué-Ivindo and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

in forest, humid shaded places; in Gabon from 220 to 750 m altitude, elsewhere from 20 to 1860 m. Flowering observed in May and from September to January; flowers opening in the afternoon

evergreen forest, swamp forest, humid soils; in Gabon from 75 to 1000 m altitude, from 1050 to 1500 m in East Africa, elsewhere from 90 to 410 m. Flowering from February to May and from September to December; flowers closing around 14:00. B : Brenan (1968b), Cabezas et al. (2014), Faden (2012), Morton (1966), Vande weghe et al. (2016)

## Source note

A. umbrosum is an extremely variable species morphologically and in chromosome number (Faden 2012). Morton (1967) and Brenan (1968b) recognized two subspecies. In West Africa, intermediates between the two have been noted (Morton 1966). B : Brenan (1968b), van der Burg (2006), Faden (2012), Morton (1966, 1967), Vande weghe et al. (2016). subsp. ovato-oblongum (P.Beauv.) J.K.Morton J. Linn. Soc. Bot. 59 : 461 (1966). A. ovato-oblongum P.Beauv., Fl. Oware 2 : 72 (1818), pl. 104(1) (1819). A. umbrosum (Vahl) Kunth var. ovato-oblongum (P.Beauv.) Brenan, Kew Bull. 7 : 200 (1952). Like the preceding, but often less robust. Leaf: sheath and pseudpetiole with short pubescence of hooked hairs; lamina oval to oval-lanceolate, glabrous or finely ciliate, ± 2 times smaller than that of subspecies umbrosum. Inflorescence often less wide, less branched (4 to 6 branches) and with fewer cincinni (up to 8 per branch) and the branches with fewer bracteoles (approximately 4 to 6 per branch). Flower 6–7 mm in diameter, opening in the morning and closing around 14:00. Seed 1.5–1.7 mm in length
