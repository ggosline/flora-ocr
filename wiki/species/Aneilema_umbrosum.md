# *Aneilema umbrosum* (Vahl) Kunth subsp. umbrosum Planche 4, Figure 1(E, F)

**Genus**: [[Aneilema]]
**Family**: [[Commelinaceae]]
**Protologue**: Enum. pl. 4 : 71 (1843)

## Synonyms

- Commelina umbrosa Vahl, Enum. pl. 2 : 179 (1806).

## Description

A. nigritanum (C.B.Clarke) Hutch., Fl. W. trop. Afr. 2 : 312 (1936). Perennial herb, decumbent, rooting at the nodes, with fibrous roots; stem strongly branched, ascending, reaching ± 30 cm in height, puberulent with minute hooked hairs. Leaves spiral; sheath reaching 1.2 cm in length, puberulent with stiff spreading hairs, apex with long reddish hairs; pseudpetiole 2–10 mm, with long reddish hairs; blade lanceolate, 5–10 × 2–3(–3.5) cm (pseudpetiole included), base cuneiform to rounded, apex acute, margins ± undulate, scabrous toward the apex, glabrescent to pilose. Inflorescence terminal, sometimes also subterminal, in a loose spiciform thyrse, broadly ovoid, 1.5–3 × 2.5–5 cm, with 5 to 8 branches bearing up to ± 30 cincinni and numerous bracteoles; axes puberulent or glabrous; peduncle 1–2.5 cm, with bract at mid-length or at the base; cincinni reaching 2.5 cm in length, with up to 11 bisexual flowers. Flower ± 10 mm in diameter; bracteoles cupuliform, perfoliate, 1–1.5 mm in length, glandular below the apex, glabrous with scattered minute hooked hairs; pedicel 3–7.5 mm, erect at fruiting, puberulent with hooked hairs toward the apex; sepals oval to elliptic, 3–4 mm in length, green with red-purple margins toward the base, concave, apex cucullate, glandular below the apex, generally sparsely puberulent; upper petals oval, ± 5 × 3 mm with claw ± 1.5 mm, white, very rarely pale blue, the lower one oval-elliptic, slightly concave, apex slightly cupuliform, ± 3 × 1.5 mm, whitish-green; stamens with anther ± 0.4 × 0.4–0.5 mm, with yellow pollen, the lateral ones with filament ± 2 mm, slightly sigmoid, bearded on the upper half, the median one with filament ± 1.8 mm; staminodes 2 or 3, the median one absent or rudimentary, filament ± 2 mm, antherodelobilobate, yellow; ovary ± 1 × 1 mm, glabrous; style arcuate-descending, ± 3 mm, stigma capitate. Fruit 2-locular, oblong-ellipsoidal, 4–5 × 2.5–3.5 mm, bivalvate, glabrous, smooth, white, locule with (1–)2 seeds. Seed ovoid to transversally ellipsoidal, 1.8–2.2 × 1.4–1.7 mm; testa coarsely foveolate-reticulate, light brown, orange-brown, pinkish-gray or gray.

## Source note

A. umbrosum is an extremely variable species both morphologically and in chromosome number (Faden 2012). Morton (1967) and Brenan (1968b) recognized two subspecies. In West Africa, intermediates between the two have been noted (Morton 1966). Bi: Brenan (1968b), van der Burg (2006), Faden (2012), Morton (1966, 1967), Vande weghe et al. (2016). subsp. ovato-oblongum (P.Beauv.) J.K.Morton J. Linn. Soc. Bot. 59 : 461 (1966). A. ovato-oblongum P.Beauv., Fl. Oware 2 : 72 (1818), pl. 104(1) (1819). A. umbrosum (Vahl) Kunth var. ovato-oblongum (P.Beauv.) Brenan, Kew Bull. 7 : 200 (1952). Like the preceding, but often less robust. Leaf: sheath and pseudpetiole with short pubescence with hooked hairs; blade oval to oval-lanceolate, glabrous or finely ciliate, ± 2 times smaller than that of subspecies umbrosum. Inflorescence often less broad, less branched (4 to 6 branches) and with fewer cincinni (up to 8 per branch) and the branches with fewer bracteoles (about 4 to 6 per branch). Flower 6–7 mm in diameter, opening in the morning and closing around 2 p.m. Seed 1.5–1.7 mm in length
