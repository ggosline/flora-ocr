# *Clerodendrum volubile* P.Beauv.

**Genus**: [[Clerodendrum]]
**Protologue**: Fl. Oware 1 : 52 (1806)

## Description

Twining vine or ± erect shrub, 1–4.5 m tall; stem cylindrical, solid, striate, brown to reddish brown, glabrous, with pseudospines 2–8 mm. Leaves generally opposite or subopposite; petiole 0.3–2.4(–3) cm; blade narrowly elliptic-ovate to ovate or elliptic to ± oblong, (5–)8–11(–15.5) × (2–)4–6(–7.8) cm, rounded to ± cuneate at base, long-acuminate with very acute acumen 0.5–1.5 cm at apex, entire or obscurely undulate at margin, glabrous, 3-nerved at base. Inflorescence terminal, paniculate, lax or sometimes somewhat condensed, 8–15 × 7–15 cm; cymes with 12 to 20 flowers, distant (0.2–)0.4–1.8(–2.1) cm apart; peduncle 1.2–2.8(–4) cm, with short dense pubescence; foliaceous bracts, 3.5–11 × 1–4.5 cm; linear bracteoles, 3–6 mm long. Flower: pedicel 4–12 mm; calyx with tube 4 mm long, ± 9 × 10 mm and striate in fruit, creamy greenish, dark green at base, often yellowish when dry, with broadly triangular lobes, 2–3 × 2–3 mm, reaching 4 mm wide in fruit, apiculate; corolla yellow-green, whitish greenish or cream, with pale yellow throat, tube 5–6 mm long, shortly pubescent and glandular, with oblong-ovate lobes, 2–4 × 1–2 mm; stamens exsert 1–1.4 cm. Fruit subglobose, 8 × 8–10 mm, 2- to 4-lobed, glossy, dark green.

## Distribution

from Senegal to Nigeria, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea (Rio Muni), Gabon, on the islands of the Gulf of Guinea, Republic of the Congo, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Central African Republic, Tanzania and Angola (including Cabinda); in Gabon: Nyanga, Ogooué-Maritime and Woleu-Ntem. Conservation status: widely distributed and preliminarily assigned to Least Concern (LC).

## Ecology

forests; in Gabon at 600 m altitude, elsewhere up to 900 m

## Vernacular names

bódji-bu-kungi (bapunu). B : Huber et al. (1963), Raponda-Walker & Sillans (1961), Verdcourt (1992). i B liographie
