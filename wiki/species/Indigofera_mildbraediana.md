# *Indigofera mildbraediana* J.B.Gillett

**Genus**: [[Indigofera]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Kew Bull. 10 : 575 (1956)

## Description

Subshrub up to ± 1.5 m in height, with silvery pubescence, hairs 0.2–0.3 mm, appressed; branchlets striated. Stipules triangular, 1 mm in length. Leaf: petiole 3–4 mm; rachis reaching 2.5 cm, prolonged 1–2 mm beyond the upper leaflets; stipels absent; leaflets 9 to 13, opposite, with oblong blade, 10–15 × 2–4 mm, base cuneate, apex apiculate. Inflorescence in pseudoracemes 12–20 mm in length, with 6 to 12(–20) flowers; peduncle 1–3 mm; bracts triangular, 1 mm in length. Flower: pedicel erect, 1 mm; calyx 2–3 mm in length, deltoid teeth, ± 2 times as long as the tube; corolla pink, ± 3 mm in length, standard oval, 2.5 mm in length, apex acute, mucronulate, wings linear, obtuse, 0.7 mm in width, keel obtuse, 3 × 1.5 mm; ovary 1 mm in length, pubescent, 2-ovulate; style 2.5 mm, glabrous. Pod erect, oblong, 3–5 × 2–2.5 × 1.5–2 mm, with (1–)2 seeds, densely strigose. Seed rectangular-rounded, almost 1 mm in diameter.

## Distribution

Nigeria, Cameroon, Tanzania, Sudan, Central African Republic, Republic of the Congo and Angola. The presence of this species in Gabon was mentioned by Gillett (1971), but it remains to be verified.

## Ecology

wooded savanas; at 100–1200 m altitude

## Source note

This species was treated by Pellegrin (1949) as "I. subulifera (Welw.) Tisser.". The true I. subulifera Welw. ex Baker is an annual of 50 cm, from the Democratic Republic of the Congo, East Africa and Angola. b: Gillett (1971a), Pellegrin (1949). Bibliography
