# *Peperomia molleri* C.DC. subsp. molleri Planche 25.1

**Genus**: [[Peperomia]]
**Family**: [[Piperaceae]]
**Protologue**: P. stolzii C.DC., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 57 : 19 (1920)

## Description

in Henriques, Bol. Soc. Brot. 10 : 154 (1892). P. stolzii C.DC., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 57 : 19 (1920). Herb generally terrestrial or lithophytic, up to 40 cm tall, generally glabrous. Leaves alternate; petiole 2–8 mm; blade broadly ovate-elliptic (width/length ratio > ½), 1–3 × 1–2.5 cm, with rounded base, with acute-apiculate to subcuspidated apex, with 3 to 5(–7) palmate veins. Inflorescence in spikes, 1 to 2 per node, 1–15 cm × ≤ 1 mm, green; peduncle 5–30 mm. Fruit globose, up to 1 mm in diameter, papillose.

## Distribution

Tropical and equatorial Africa, Madagascar; in Gabon, known from Estuaire, Ngounié, Ogooué-Ivindo and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

terrestrial in forest, epiphytic on shaded trunks, lithophytic on humid rock faces or on rocks outcropping in forest, dense lowland or montane forest, inselbergs, saxicolous forest, submontane forest; in Gabon at 300–950 m altitude, elsewhere up to 2100 m
