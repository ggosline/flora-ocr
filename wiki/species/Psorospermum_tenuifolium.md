# *Psorospermum tenuifolium* Hook.f.

**Genus**: [[Psorospermum]]
**Family**: [[Hypericaceae]]
**Protologue**: Icon. Pl. 8 : t. 771 (1848)

## Description

Shrub to small tree reaching 16 m in height; trunk up to 25 cm in diameter; branchlets glabrous or pubescent when young. Leaves opposite; petiole (2–)4–10 mm, glabrous except for an axillary tuft of rusty hairs; lamina elliptic to obovate, 2–14 × 1–6 cm, attenuate or cuneate at base, apex rounded, obtuse or acuminate, herbaceous to papyraceous, lower surface greyish-green to roseate, glabrous or with the midvein sparsely pubescent, generally with numerous black glandular punctations, sometimes only on the margins and towards the apex; lateral veins 6–10 pairs, tertiary network lax, scarcely visible. Inflorescence terminal, with corymbiform cymes, few- to many-flowered; peduncle 1–2 cm, pubescent to glabrous. Flower: pedicel 3–8 mm, pubescent to glabrous; sepals ovate, 2.5–4 × 1–1.5 mm, pubescent to glabrous externally, ciliolulate, green to reddish; petals obovate, 4–6 × 2–3 mm, white to yellowish-white, internally villous and with red-brown to dark red-purple glandular lines; stamen phalanges with 5–9 stamens of 3 mm, glabrous or pubescent on the dorsal face, anthers 0.2 mm in diameter; ovary globose, 1 mm in diameter, glabrous; styles 2 mm, stigmas capitate. Fruit globose to ovoid, 7–9 × 6–9 mm, red to red-brown or dark red. Seed ellipsoid, 3–4 × 2–2.5 mm; cotyledons convolute.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Angola, Zambia; widely distributed in Gabon

## Ecology

primary and secondary forest, forest galleries, thickets, coastal forest, swamp forest and savannas, on ridges and rocky summits or plateaus; in Gabon 5–1000 m, elsewhere up to 1125 m. B: Bamps (1966, 1970), Robson (1961). i B liography
