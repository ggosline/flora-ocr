UNIDENTIFIED MATERIAL

About 10 km South-West of Ndjolé, North of Lake Ayem, N. HALLE collected under the number 1727 a specimen which has not been able to be identified thus far. This collection comes from a shrub; it has unifoliolate leaves and young fruits with remains of sepals, petals and stamens. This is undoubtedly a plant belonging to the Connarraceae. The leaves accord well with the genus Hemandradenia, as do the remains of the calyx and corolla. The origin of this collection is also compatible with the distribution of this genus. However, two characters do not correspond: the 10 apparently fertile stamens (10 large filaments, at least in this collection) and the form of the young fruit glabrescent. This fruit, strongly cuneate at both ends, corresponds better to that of other genera, such as Vismianthus and Ellipanthus. Mature fruits and/or complete flowers would at least be necessary in order to decide on the taxonomic position of this specimen, which must most probably belong to a new species.
