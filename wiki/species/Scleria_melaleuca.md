# *Scleria melaleuca* Schltdl. & Cham. Planche 135, Figure 27b

**Genus**: [[Scleria]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Linnaea 6 : 29 (1831)

## Description

S. pterota C.Presl, Isis (Oken) 21 : 268 (1826), nom. nud. Perennial herb, robust; rhizome creeping, short, 3–4 mm thick with very short internodes masked by the swollen bases of the stems; stem 30–100 cm × 2–3 mm, usually smooth, but sometimes slightly scabrid above on the angles. Leaves numerous; sheath glabrous or pubescent, narrowly winged, the basal ones without lamina; ligule at most 6 mm long, reddish, hairy; lamina shorter or longer than the stem, 5–11 mm wide, scabrid on the margin and main veins. Inflorescence composed of terminal and lateral panicles; lateral panicles solitary at the nodes, 2–8 × 1–2 cm; peduncles short, scarcely or not exceeding the sheaths. Male spikelet 4–5 mm long, with glumes glabrous, straw-yellow generally with reddish striae or spots 5 mm long. Female spikelet with glumes usually curved at the apex. Achene subspherical, 2.2–2.5 × 2–2.3 mm, smooth, glabrous and shiny on the upper surface, with small tufts of whitish hairs on the side, white with or without dark apex; hypogynium with 3 lobes about 1 mm long, rounded or ovate.

## Distribution

widespread in tropical Africa, Madagascar and South America; very rare in Gabon, found only in the Lopé, Ogooué-Ivindo (1 collection)

## Ecology

humid wooded zones; in Gabon, at 100 m altitude, at 0–750 m in East Africa. Taxonomic note: easily recognized by its spherical smooth achenes shiny on the upper surface, with small tufts of whitish hairs on the side and with a distinctly trilobed hypogynium. The other Gabonese species with spherical achenes, S. mikawana, has an achene with a lacunose surface. Bibliography: Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al. (2010), Hooper & Napper (1972), Piérart (1953)
