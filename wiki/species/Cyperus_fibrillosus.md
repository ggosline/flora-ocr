# *Cyperus fibrillosus* Kük. var. scaettae (Cherm.) Kük. Planche 71

**Genus**: [[Cyperus]]
**Family**: [[Cyperaceae]]
**Protologue**: Pycreus scaettae Cherm., Bull. Jard. Bot. État Bruxelles 13 : 278 (1935)

## Description

in Engl. & Diels, Pflanzenr. IV. 20(101) : 348 (1936). Pycreus scaettae Cherm., Bull. Jard. Bot. État Bruxelles 13 : 278 (1935). Perennial herb, in dense tufts, with a short rhizome and numerous stems and leaves; stems 10–20 cm × 0.4–0.7 mm, trigonous, smooth, stiff, the lower part swollen and surrounded by numerous dark brown fibrous remains. Leaves numerous, basal or sub-basal; blade 5–16 cm long, very narrow (<1 mm), glabrous. Inflorescence in a compact capitate anthela of crowded spikelets or more rarely, a slightly loose anthela with some fascicles of spikelets shortly pedunculate; fascicle with 4–7 spikelets; involucral bracts 1–3, foliaceous, erect or spreading, the largest 2–7 cm × 0.7–0.9 mm, glabrous. Spikelet linear-lanceolate, 10–25 × 2–3 mm, white to greenish white; glumes ovate, 2–3 mm long, dull white with a pale green central nerve, often presenting 3 longitudinal ribs and terminating in the rounded apex or slightly surpassing it. Flower: style with 2 long branches. Achene obovate, 0.7–0.9 × 0.5–0.7 mm, with a short apicule, biconvex, reddish brown to greyish, with ± distinct transverse wrinkles, sometimes finely papillose.

## Distribution

scattered in tropical Africa; uncommon in Gabon, found in Haut-Ogooué, Ngounié, Ogooué-Ivindo and Ogooué-Lolo (6 collections)

## Ecology

marshes and river banks, also on the margins of running water; in Gabon, at 100–600 m altitude, up to 1100 m in East Africa. Taxonomic note: recognized by its whitish capitate inflorescences, its filiform leaves and its stems swollen at the base and covered by the fibrous remains of old sheaths. The typical variety has a darker brown inflorescence. B: Haines & Lye (1983), Hoenselaar et al. (2010), Hooper & Napper (1972). Bibliography
