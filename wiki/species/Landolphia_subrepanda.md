# *Landolphia subrepanda* (K.Schum.) Pichon Planche 69 ; Figure 7(F)

**Genus**: [[Landolphia]]
**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Protologue**: Mém. Inst. Franç. Afrique Noire 35 : 184 (1953)

## Description

Carpodinus trichanthera Pierre ex Stapf, Fl. trop. Afr. 4(1): 82 (1902). L. subrepanda (K.Schum.) Pichon var. latifolia Pichon, Mém. Inst. Franç. Afrique Noire 35: 186 (1953). Large liana; branches generally distinctly hairy. Leaf: petiole 3–15 mm; blade narrowly elliptic to oblong or obovate to narrowly obovate, 2–4(–6) times as long as wide, 4–17(–32) × 1.5–6.5(–12.5) cm, auriculate to obtuse or sometimes acute at base, ± distinctly acuminate at apex, glabrous on both faces or hairy below at base; secondary veins 8 pairs, nearly straight or slightly curved with angles often different from the midvein, sometimes 1 or 2 veins less visible between two secondaries, tertiary venation reticulate, generally scarcely visible below. Inflorescence axillary, with 1 to 20 flowers; peduncle reaching 4 mm, bracteate throughout the peduncle and pedicel. Flower slightly fragrant; pedicel reaching 2 mm; calyx with 4 or 5 lobes, 1–1.9 times as long as wide, 0.9–1.6 × 0.7–1 mm, ciliate margin, hairy on the outside. Corolla white or greenish, lower face 3–5 mm; branch and tendril 4–5 mm (× —). Flower longitudinal section (× —). Basal part of a corolla lobe, inner face (× 6). – 7–9. Stamen, ventral, dorsal and lateral views; embryo (× 2). (1, 5–11: Bos 5430; 2: Bos 6649; 3, 4: W.J. de Wilde et al. 3888; 10: Bos 5430 & Breteler 6992; 11–13: Leeuwenberg 10563). Drawing by Ike Zewald, Naturalis Biodiversity Center, reproduced from Persoon et al. (1992).

Longitudinal section and with seeds 5–8 mm (× —). Seed 3.5–4 mm (× —). Same longitudinal section drawing showing. Le Testu 7554; 2: Le Testu 6353; 3, 12, 14: J.J.F.E. de Wilde et al. 603; 4–10: Breteler & de Wilde 7648; 11, 13: Breteler 1757; 15, 16: Klaine 152). Drawing by Wil Wessel-Brand, Naturalis Biodiversity Center (©), reproduced from Persoon et al. (1992).

Yellowish, with tube 5–8.2 mm long, 0.9–1.5 mm wide in the middle, 1.7–2.2 mm at the level of the anthers and 1.1–1.9 mm at the throat, glabrous on the outside at the base, higher up pubescent or hairy, the inside partially hairy including the throat, with narrowly ovate or ovate to obovate lobes, 1.7–5 times as long as wide, 0.4–0.9 times as long as the tube, 2.8–7 × 1.4–2.2 mm, hairy but sparse or glabrous towards the apex on the outside; stamens inserted at ± 0.6 of the tube length, at the throat level or included up to 1 mm, anther 1.3–2.5 mm long, hairy on the outside; pistil 5–7.2 mm long, ovary hairy, hairiness longer and denser towards the apex; style 3–4.6 mm, glabrous or hairy at the base. Fruit subglobuleux or ovoid to obovoid, 2.5–4 cm, yellowish, orange or reddish, often with reddish longitudinal bands, with 8 to 22 seeds; wall 0.3–1 cm thick. Distribution southeast of Nigeria, Cameroon, Gabon and Republic of the Congo; in Gabon: Estuary, Haut-Ogooué, Moyen-Ogooué, Ngounié, Nyanga, Ogooué, Ivindo and Ogooué-Lolo. Ecology primary and secondary forest, forest patches in savanna; in Gabon, up to 650 m altitude; elsewhere up to 900 m. B: Persoon et al. (1992), Pichon (1953). BIBLIOGRAPHY Landolphia uniflora (Stapf) Pichon Plate 70 Mém. Inst. Franç. Afrique Noire 35: 178 (1953). Liana; branches with pale lenticels, pubescent or glabrous. Leaf: petiole 4–18 mm; blade narrowly elliptic to elliptic or oblong to obovate, sometimes ovate, 2–4 times as long as wide, 5–21 × 1.5–8 cm, auriculate, acute or attenuate at base, acuminate at apex, hairy on the midvein and lower secondary veins, otherwise glabrous; secondary veins 7 to 14 pairs, strongly incurved, distinctly looping by joining their neighbors, tertiary venation visible below. Inflorescence axillary, with 10–30 flowers. Flower: pedicel 1.5–5 mm; bracteoles on the sides, generally in pairs, the distal ones sepaloid; calyx with 4 or 5 lobes, ovate to obovate, 1–2 times as long as wide, 0.9–1.2 × 0.8–1 mm, hairy or pubescent on the outside; corolla white, purple or violet, with tube 13–18 mm long, 2.5–3.1 mm wide at the base, 3.4–3.9 mm at the level of the anthers and 3.3–3.7 mm at the throat, glabrous (on the lower 2–4 mm) to pubescent or tomentose up to the throat on the outside, hairy on the inside among and up to 1.5 mm below the anthers; lobes 0.9–1.6 times as long as wide, 0.9–1.6 times as long as the tube, 13.5–23.5 × 4–5.5 mm, hairy on the inside on the auricles towards the edge, otherwise glabrous; stamens inserted at 0.7–0.9 of the tube length, included (0.1–)1–1.2 mm, anther 2.5–2.9 mm long; pistil 10–14.5 mm long, ovary villous; style 6–10 mm, villous at the base to glabrous in the upper 2–3 mm. Fruit oblong to ovoid, 5–8 × 2.5–3.5 cm, yellow, with 3–6 seeds.

## Distribution

Southeast of Nigeria, Cameroon and Gabon; in Gabon: Estuary and Ngounié, known only from two stations. Ecology forest in Gabon and elsewhere, up to 650 m altitude. B: Persoon et al. (1992), Pichon (1953). BIBLIOGRAPHY
