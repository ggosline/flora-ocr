# *Pauridiantha floribunda* (K. Sch. et Kr.) Brem.

**Genus**: [[Pauridiantha]]
**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]

## Synonyms

- Urophyllum floribundum K. Schum. et K. Krause, Bot. Jahrb. 39 : 523 (1907).
- Urophyllum Klaineanum Pierre msc. in herb.

## Description

Bremekar : 168

Small tree reaching 1.5 to 10 m in height and up to 15–30 cm in diam.; young shoots puberulent. Internodes glabrescent 4–7 cm. Stipules ovate-acute 8–15 × 4–9 (11) mm, not glabrous, caducous. Petiole 4–11 mm. Blade oblong discolorous, ± glossy and entirely glabrous above, 11–20 × 3–6 cm; base acute to rounded; apex ± lanceolate or acuminate. 14–19 pairs of secondary nervures arched and ascending, ± puberulent below. Fine reticulation often clearly impressed on the upper surface. Numerous small pubescent domatia along the secondary nervures.

Inflorescences axillary multiflorous (4) 8–14 cm long, with finely puberulent peduncle 6–8.5 cm, interrupted towards the middle or somewhat below by a pair of linear prefoliations.

First bifurcation without bracts; branching ± lax in dichasial cyme. Pedicel 2–3 (8) mm, pubescent. Bud ovate to oblong-subtruncate, 4–5 mm long. Flowers white or whitish. Calyx with 5-toothed cup, glabrous, 1–1.5 mm high. Corolla with tube c. 3 mm, often pubescent towards the base of the lobes; 5 lobes somewhat fleshy c. 2 × 1 mm; internal pubescence evident. Anthers apiculate 1.5–1.8 × 0.6 mm, exserted in the short-styled form, medidorsifixed. Style pubescent except at the base, 2.5 mm long with a fusiform stigma in the short-styled flower, or 4–4.5 mm with a capitate stigma in the long-styled flower. Disc fleshy convex pubescent exceeding 1 mm in diam. Ovary with two locules. In each locule a multilobulate bilobed placenta constricted and compressed in its lower median part and 0.6–0.8 mm high.

Fruit black globulous 5–8 mm in diam. Numerous brownish oval seeds, crested-foveolate-submuriculate, 1.1 mm long.

## Type

Zenker 2378 (P! ex B), Bipindi, Cameroun

## Discussion

The species is known from southern Nigeria, Cameroon, São Thomé, Prince Island and Gabon.

## Vernacular names

Boléso, Bolsou (Fang, d'après Périquet et Gauchotte)

## Uses

Excellent firewood (according to Fleury who notes for the Douala region a tree reaching 25 m in height and 50 cm in diam.)

Gabonese material (used for the description):

Aubréville: G 130, NE of Kougouleu, Monts de Cristal (fl. Dec.),

Gauchotte and Guillery: 1226 S.R.F., Lake Oguémoué (young bud Dec.).

R.P. Klaine: 31, 83, 175, 343, 1487, Libreville region (fl. Dec.; fr. Apr.).

J. Morel: 74 S.R.F., Mondah reserve (young bud Oct.).

L. Périquet: 49, Abénélan, Monts de Cristal (fr. Mar.).

R. P. Trilles: 17 (probably Monts de Cristal).
