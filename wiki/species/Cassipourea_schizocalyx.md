# *Cassipourea schizocalyx* C.H.Wright

**Genus**: [[Cassipourea]]
**Family**: [[Rhizophoraceae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1901 : 122 (1901)

## Synonyms

- Dactylopetalum kamerunense Engl., Bot. Jahrb. Syst . 40 : 55 (1907).
- Weihea kamerunensis (Engl.) Engl., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 54 : 363 (1917).

## Description

W. sericea (Engl.) Engl., Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 54 : 363 (1917). C. kamerunensis (Engl.) Alston, Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1925 : 264 (1925). C. sericea (Engl.) Alston, Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1925 : 263 (1925) .

Shrub or small tree up to 12 m tall; branches initially pubescent, then glabrous. Stipules caducous towards anthesis, elongate triangular to ovate, 1–1.5 cm long, with appressed hairs or more rarely glabrous. Leaf: petiole 0.7–1.2 cm, pubescent; blade elliptic, 6–11(–14) × 2.5–7(–9.5) cm, (1.9–)2.8–3.5 times longer than wide, base cuneate, apex elongate acuminate with acumen 2–2.5 cm, subcoriaceous, with entire margin, glabrous on both surfaces except for pubescent veins below; lateral veins 4–7 pairs, not uniting in arcs before the margin. Inflorescence axillary, fasciculate, 1–3-flowered, flowers at different stages of development; pedicels supported by 1–2 minute bracts. Flower 4–5-merous; pedicel slender, 1–1.8 cm, pubescent, stipe ± 1 mm, pubescent; calyx stellate, persistent in fruit, with 5 clearly free and spreading lobes, elongate ovate, 7 mm long, slightly pubescent on the external face, densely silky on the internal face; petals obovate-spatulate, ± 6 mm long, glabrous, white, with ± 15 irregular ribbons 4 mm; stamens 30–35, glabrous, placed outside an annular disk, filaments 2–2.5 mm, anthers narrowly oblong, 1.5–2 mm long, white or dark brown, with some long and flexuous hairs; ovary subglobose, with 3 locules densely tomentose, opening by 3 slits, each with a single seed; style 3 mm, glabrous, persistent in fruit, stigma patelliforme. Fruit ovoid-ellipsoid, with 3(–4) locules, 13–17 × 7–8(–14) mm, distinctly verrucose, yellowish. Seeds not seen.

## Distribution

Cameroon, Gabon and Democratic Republic of the Congo; in Gabon, fairly common species, known from Estuaire, Ngounié and Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

humid forests, flooded forests, along rivers; in Gabon, at (30–)200–600 m and in Cameroon at 120 m altitude. Flowering in (January, May) September–December, fruits from September to January. b : Breteler (2008). ibliography
