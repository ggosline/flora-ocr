# *Millettia oyemensis* Pellegr.

**Genus**: [[Millettia]]
**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Bot. France 93 : 111 (1946)

## Description

Large liana; branchlets lenticellate; buds flattened, lanceolate. Stipules caducous, lanceolate, ± 5 × 2 mm. Leaf rather thin, up to 15 cm in length; petiole 2–3 cm; rachis 5–6 cm, glabrescent, striate; stipels persistent, linear, ± 2 mm; leaflets 9 to 11, petiolule 3–4 mm, hairy, blade lanceolate, 3–5 × 1.5–1.8 cm, base obtuse, apex acuminate, glabrous above except for the main vein, rare appressed hairs below; lateral veins in 15 or 16 pairs, reticulation prominent. Inflorescence terminal, in a robust, dense and multiflorous panicle, 8–10 cm in length; branchlets racemiform; bracts ovate, ± 1 mm in length. Flower ± 15 mm in length; pedicel slender, ± 4 mm; bracteoles caducous, minute, 0.5 mm, velvety; calyx 4 mm in length, lobes obtuse, 1.5–2 mm, velvety; corolla white, glabrous, standard obovate, apex emarginate or not, auriculate and with prominent callousities at the base, with claw 4–5 mm, wings oblong, auriculate at the base, gibbous and ± callose, with claw 5 mm, keel broad, obtuse, with claw 5 mm; stamens monadelphous; ovary linear, ± 1 cm in length, hairy, multiovulate; style glabrous, incurved. Pod and seed unknown.

## Distribution

endemic to Gabon, in the Woleu-Ntem

## Ecology

forest. Flowering in March

## Source note

Known only from the type specimen; no recent collections. b: Pellegrin (1949). Bibliography
