# Gleicheniaceae

**Authority**: Gaudichaud
**Genera in region**: 1 · **Species in region**: 1

## Diagnosis

Terrestrial ferns, with pinnate or multiply pinnate fronds, of coriaceous texture, with free veins. Sori dorsal, sporangia few in number, subsessile, with complete annulus, median, with vertical dehiscence, lacking an indusium.

Type species: Gleichenia polypodioides (L.) Sm.

Family of approximately 130 species, chiefly distributed in the Southern Hemisphere and in tropical regions. A single genus in Africa, Gleichenia, recently divided into six. We follow Holttum's opinion in considering only these divisions as subgenera. A single one in Gabon (s.-gen. Dicranopteris), with a single species.
