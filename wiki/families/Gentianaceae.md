# Gentianaceae

**Authority**: Juss.
**Order**: Gentianales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Gentianacées*

Eight genera and 11 species — small herbs of savanna, marsh and seasonally wet
ground. Shares [[vol54]] with [[Lentibulariaceae]] and [[Dracaenaceae]].

## Notes

**The modern family is much larger here than this treatment shows**, because
***Anthocleista* now belongs to Gentianaceae.** Those eight species are treated
in [[vol19]] under [[Loganiaceae]] — the large-leaved pachycaul trees of swamp
and secondary forest. On current classification they are the most conspicuous
gentianaceous plants in the region, and they are not in this volume. The two
pages need reading together.

***Voyria* is mycoheterotrophic** — wholly without chlorophyll, deriving its
carbon from arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi rather than from photosynthesis. The
plants are small, pale pink or yellow, and appear briefly on the forest floor.
*Voyria* is otherwise a Neotropical genus; the African species is the single
exception, a striking disjunction.

*Congolanthus*, *Oreonesion* and *Schinziella* are narrowly African, and
*Exochaenium* was segregated from *Sebaea* in recent work — older African
literature will have those three species under *Sebaea*.
