# Myrtaceae

**Authority**: Juss.
**Order**: Myrtales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Myrtacées*

Five genera and 26 species, sharing [[vol11]] with [[Thymeleaceae]]. Two native
genera and three introduced.

## Notes

**Africa is poor in Myrtaceae**, and this treatment shows it: 22 of the 26
species belong to just *Eugenia* and *Syzygium*, and the remaining three genera
are all introductions. The family's great radiations are Australasian and
Neotropical; the African representation is a modest offshoot.

*Eugenia* and *Syzygium* are notoriously difficult to separate and have been
recircumscribed repeatedly — the distinction rests largely on inflorescence
position and embryo structure. Species assignments in a 1960s treatment should be
expected to have moved.

*Psidium guajava* is naturalised and invasive in parts of the region;
*Eucalyptus* appears here through plantation forestry rather than as a
naturalised element.

This family is the subject of the interactive-key dataset built for
`vol11_myrtaceae.keys.json` in the app.
