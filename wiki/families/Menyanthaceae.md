# Menyanthaceae

**Authority**: Dumort.
**Order**: Asterales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Menyanthacées*

One genus, three species — floating-leaved aquatics of still and slow water.

## Genera in region

| Genus | Species |
|-------|---------|
| [[Nymphoides]] | 3 |

## Notes

***Nymphoides* looks like a small water lily and is not one.** The floating,
cordate leaves closely resemble [[Nymphaeaceae]], with which the genus shares
ponds and backwaters throughout the region, but Menyanthaceae belong to
**Asterales** — the daisy and bellflower order, among the most derived of
eudicots — while water lilies sit near the base of the flowering plants
altogether. The two are about as distant as any pair of aquatic plants could be,
and the resemblance is purely a convergence on the floating-leaf habit.

The five-parted, fringed white or yellow flowers held above the water are the
reliable distinction in the field.
