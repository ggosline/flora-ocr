# Hydroleaceae

**Authority**: R.Br. ex Edwards · **Order**: Solanales · **FR**: *Hydroléacées*

One genus, one species — *Hydrolea palustris*.

## Notes

*Hydrolea* was formerly placed in Hydrophyllaceae, a family now largely absorbed
into Boraginaceae; molecular data separated it as **Hydroleaceae** and placed it
in **Solanales**, beside [[Sphenocleaceae]] — the two small families having
shared a history of uncertain placement and now being near neighbours.

The plants are marsh and pond-margin herbs, often with blue flowers and spiny
stems, and belong to the same seasonally flooded habitat as *Sphenoclea*.
