## Notes

***Thesium* species are root hemiparasites** — green and photosynthetic, but
attached to host roots by haustoria from which they draw water and minerals. They
are inconspicuous savanna and grassland herbs, easily overlooked and probably
under-collected in the region.

The family belongs to **Santalales**, the parasitic order also holding
[[Olacaceae]], [[Opiliaceae]] and [[Octoknemaceae]] ([[vol20]]) and the
mistletoes. Santalaceae in the modern broad sense absorbs Viscaceae, so the
mistletoe genera *Viscum* and *Phragmanthera* belong with it — none is treated
in this volume.

*Santalum album*, the sandalwood of commerce, is the family's best-known member
and is Indo-Australian, absent from Africa.
