# Aspidiaceae

**Authority**: S. F. Gray
**Genera in region**: 3 · **Species in region**: 10

## Diagnosis

Terrestrial or climbing ferns, with rhizome scaly at apex, scales not clathrate, brown or black. Petiole not articulate, presenting 3–7 vascular bundles. Lamina pinnate-multipinnate, often developed basiscopic. Pinnules sometimes inequilateral or dimidiate (Didymochlaena). Costae and costulae bearing a sulcus on the adaxial surface (Dryopteris) or not (Tectaria). Venation free or anastomosing. Presence of multicellular hairs in the sulci of the rachis, costae and costulae (Ctenitis, Lastreopsis). Sori subapical on the veins, or at the meeting of several veins, superficial or sunken (Didymochlaena). Indusium reniform or peltate, or absent. Sporangium with stalk formed of several rows of cells, never bearing hairs, but sometimes with glands. No paraphyses.

Family very diversely circumscribed by different pteridologists. We employ here the term Aspidiaceae in the sense defined by Alston (loc. cit.), for whom this family comprises the Aspidiaceae + Didymochanaceae of Ching (loc. cit.), and the Dennstaedtiaceae, subfam. Dryopteridaceae + Tectarioideae of Holltum (loc. cit.).

This family comprises a large number of species distributed throughout the world.
