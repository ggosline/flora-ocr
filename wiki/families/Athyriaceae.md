# Athyriaceae

**Authority**: Alston
**Genera in region**: 2 · **Species in region**: 3

## Diagnosis

Terrestrial ferns, with scaly rhizome, scales entire or dentate, formed of thin-walled cells. Petiole bearing 2 vascular cords, united in a U shape at the apex. Rachis channeled at the apex, naked or scaly, not bearing multicellular hairs. Blade simple to bipinnate. Costa channeled, the grooves of the costulae interrupted at the junction with that of the costa. Veins generally free. Sori rounded, J-shaped, or horseshoe-shaped, indusium curved, rarely caducous. Sporangia with elongated stalk, annulus with 14–16 thickened cells. Spores monolete with perispore.

Approximately 650 species, worldwide; a single genus in Gabon.
