# Acanthaceae

**Authority**: Juss.
**Order**: Lamiales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Acanthacées*

The source gives **32 genera and 80 species**; the segmented treatment yields 30
genera and 81 species. Largely herbs and subshrubs of forest floor and
disturbed ground, with some lianas ([[Thunbergia]], [[Mendoncia]]).

## Notes

**Three genera here are no longer Acanthaceae, or sit at its margin.**
[[Thomandersia]] (4 species) is now placed in its own family
**Thomandersiaceae**; [[Mendoncia]] belongs to Mendoncioideae, sometimes raised
to Mendonciaceae; and [[Nelsonia]] and [[Elytraria]] are Nelsonioideae, a
lineage that has been excluded from Acanthaceae s.s. in some treatments. Worth
checking against a current source — the placements have moved more than once.
