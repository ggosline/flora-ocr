# Amaranthaceae

## Diagnosis

Herbs or subshrubs with simple exstipulate leaves and small flowers grouped into glomerules, spikes, heads or panicles. Flowers have scarious tepals, stamens often united at the base, and a superior one-locular ovary; fruits are dry and usually one-seeded, though *Celosia* capsules contain several seeds.
