## Notes

***Sciaphila* is mycoheterotrophic**, like [[Afrothismia]] and *Voyria*, but
arrived there independently — Triuridaceae are Pandanales, [[Thismiaceae]] are
Dioscoreales, and *Voyria* is a eudicot in [[Gentianaceae]]. Three unrelated
lineages in the same forest have each abandoned photosynthesis for the same
fungal carbon source.

The plants are slender, pale, few-centimetre stems bearing tiny unisexual
flowers, appearing on deeply shaded leaf litter and easily mistaken for fungi or
overlooked entirely. Records are sparse everywhere they occur, and the single
species here almost certainly understates the genus's presence in the region.

Triuridaceae are of interest for another reason: the family has one of the more
contested fossil records in angiosperm palaeobotany, and its reduced morphology
long made its placement among monocots difficult to resolve.
