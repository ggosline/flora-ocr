# Meliaceae

**Authority**: Juss.
**Order**: Sapindales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Méliacées*

Twelve genera and 35 species — modest in number and **the single most
commercially important family in the corpus**. The African mahoganies and their
relatives are almost all here.

## Notes

***Entandrophragma* and *Khaya* are the economic core.** *Entandrophragma
cylindricum* (sapele), *E. utile* (utile/sipo) and *E. candollei* are among the
most valuable timbers exported from Central Africa, and *Khaya* is the true
African mahogany. Several are now of conservation concern through logging
pressure — worth checking current IUCN and CITES status, which has moved for
these genera in recent years.

**The volume uses a recent generic classification.** The presence of
[[Leplaea]] and [[Neoguarea]] is diagnostic: both were segregated from *Guarea*
in a 2012 revision of the African species. Their appearance here means vol 47
follows post-2012 generic limits, so older African floras will list these species
under *Guarea*. This is one of the volumes where Flore du Gabon is ahead of the
regional literature rather than behind it.

*Turraeanthus africanus* is avodiré, a pale decorative timber; *Lovoa
trichilioides* is dibétou, traded as African walnut.
