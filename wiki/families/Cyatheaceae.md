# Cyatheaceae

**Authority**: Schott
**Genera in region**: 1 · **Species in region**: 2

## Diagnosis

Tree ferns, with erect rhizome, forming a trunk covered with persistent bases of petioles and adventitious roots, generally bearing scales, rarely hairs (not in African forms). Fronds of large size; base of petiole and frond bearing characteristic scales or hairs; lamina bipinnate-tripinnatifid. Veins pinnate, simple or forked. Sori on veins near the margin, rounded, with globose indusium when young, ± absent later. Sporangium with complete annulus, oblique, with horizontal dehiscence.

Reichenbach (Conspect : 37, 1828) considered CYATHEACEAE as a subdivision of POLYPODIACEAE.

Copeland distinguishes 7 genera, of which 2 are monospecific and localized to Central America. Holttum cites 9 genera belonging to 4 subfamilies. The most widespread genus is the genus Cyathea.
