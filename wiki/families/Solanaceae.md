## Notes

**This is largely a treatment of introduced plants.** Solanaceae is
overwhelmingly a New World family — *Capsicum* (chillies), *Nicotiana* (tobacco),
*Brugmansia* (angel's trumpets) and *Physalis* are all Neotropical in origin and
present here through cultivation and escape. Along with [[Cucurbitaceae]], this
page documents agricultural introduction as much as native flora.

*Solanum* is the exception: the genus is genuinely diverse in Africa, and the
twelve species here include native forest and savanna elements alongside
cultivated and weedy ones. It is one of the largest genera of flowering plants
worldwide and remains taxonomically demanding.

*Schwenckia* is the most straightforwardly native genus in the treatment.

The family's sister group is [[Convolvulaceae]], also in Solanales and also in
this corpus ([[vol46]]).
