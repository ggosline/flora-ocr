**Authority**: Engl. & Gilg

## Notes

Pandaceae is a small family of tropical African and Asian trees and shrubs,
another of the segregates carved out of Euphorbiaceae s.l. — like
[[Phyllanthaceae]] and [[Putranjivaceae]] in this corpus — and confirmed as
distinct in Malpighiales.

***Panda oleosa*** is a Central African forest tree whose seeds yield an edible
oil and are widely gathered; the kernels are hard-shelled and a known food of
forest elephants and primates, which disperse them.

*Microdesmis* is a genus of forest understorey shrubs, dioecious and often
locally common — one of the more frequently encountered small trees of
Guineo-Congolian forest.
