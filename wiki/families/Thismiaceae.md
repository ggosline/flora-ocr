**Authority**: J.Agardh

**Name in source (FR)**: *Thismiacées*

## Notes

***Afrothismia* is fully mycoheterotrophic** — it has no chlorophyll, no
functional leaves, and no capacity for photosynthesis. It obtains all its carbon
from arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi, which it parasitises, and lives almost
entirely underground, emerging only to flower and fruit. The plants are a few
centimetres tall, curiously shaped and often brightly coloured.

**These are among the most threatened plants in the region.** *Afrothismia*
species are typically known from one or a handful of forest sites in Cameroon and
Gabon, depend on both a specific fungal partner and intact forest soil, and
cannot persist through disturbance. Several described species have not been
refound and some are considered extinct. Any conservation reading of this wiki
should place *Afrothismia* alongside [[Podostemaceae]] at the top of the list.

The corpus holds three independent mycoheterotrophic lineages — this one,
[[Triuridaceae]] (*Sciaphila*), and *Voyria* in [[Gentianaceae]] — plus the
holoparasitic [[Balanophoraceae]] and [[Apodanthaceae]]. Loss of photosynthesis
has happened repeatedly and independently in this flora.
