# Sapindaceae

**Authority**: Juss.
**Order**: Sapindales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Sapindacées*

Twenty-three genera and 96 species (with 5 infraspecific taxa) in [[vol23]],
edited by C. Cusset. Trees, shrubs and lianas, well represented in Gabonese
forest.

## Notes

*Blighia sapida* (ackee) and *Paullinia* are the economically familiar members;
*Allophylus* is a notoriously difficult genus whose species limits have been
revised repeatedly since 1973, so the 22 species here should be treated as a
1973 circumscription rather than a current one.

**OCR artefacts**: the segmenter picks up several tribal and mangled names from
the generic key as if they were genera — "Lepisanthee", "Melicoccae",
"Schleichere", "Haplocelum", "Dodoneee", "Doratoxylee" (for Lepisanthes,
Melicocceae, Schleichera, Haplocoelum, Dodonaeeae, Doratoxyleae). They are
excluded from the table above; a naive count of the source gives 29 rather than
23 genera.
