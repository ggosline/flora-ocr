# Gesneriaceae

**Authority**: Rich. & Juss.
**Order**: Lamiales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Gesnériacées*

Three genera and four species — small herbs of shaded, humid forest, often on
rock. Shares [[vol27]] with [[Bignoniaceae]].

## Notes

**African Gesneriaceae are a small remnant of a mainly Asian and Neotropical
family.** The vol 12 [[Rubiaceae]] general chapter, in discussing the order,
notes the family's division into Gesnerioideae and Cyrtandroideae; the African
genera belong to the latter, the group that also contains *Streptocarpus* and
*Saintpaulia* — the African violets — neither of which reaches Lower Guinea, both
being East African.

*Acanthonema* is endemic to West and Central African forest, and *Trachystigma*
likewise narrowly African. *Epithema* is the one genus here with a wide
palaeotropical range, extending to Asia.

These are plants of deep shade on wet rock and stream banks — a habitat shared
with [[Podostemaceae]] and [[Begoniaceae]], and one that is poorly collected in
the region, so the species count here is likely to be an underestimate.
