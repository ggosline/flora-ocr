# Rhizophoraceae

**Authority**: Pers.
**Order**: Malpighiales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Rhizophoracées*

Three genera and 14 species — **the principal mangrove family of the region**,
together with a larger inland forest genus.

## Notes

***Rhizophora* is the structural genus of West African mangrove** — stilt-rooted
trees forming the seaward fringe along the Atlantic coast from Senegal to Angola,
with Gabon holding some of the most extensive stands. The genus is **viviparous**:
the seed germinates while still attached to the parent, producing the long
dagger-shaped propagule that drops and plants itself.

The corpus covers the region's mangrove across three families, which is worth
assembling: *Rhizophora* here; *Laguncularia* and *Conocarpus* in
[[Combretaceae]] ([[vol35]]); and *Avicennia* in [[Avicenniaceae]] ([[vol22]]).
Mangrove is among the most threatened habitat in Lower Guinea, so these pages
have practical value together.

Most of the family's species here are not mangrove at all: *Cassipourea* is a
genus of inland forest trees and shrubs, several used medicinally, with 10 of the
14 species.

The family's closest relative is Erythroxylaceae — the coca family — also
represented in this corpus, in [[vol21]].
