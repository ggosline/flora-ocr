## Notes

**This volume treats Malvaceae in the narrow sense** — subfamily Malvoideae only.
In current classification Malvaceae is much larger, absorbing Sterculiaceae,
Tiliaceae and Bombacaceae, so the modern family also includes everything on the
[[Sterculiaceae]] page ([[vol02]]) and [[Bombacaceae]] ([[vol22]]). Anyone
assembling a regional Malvaceae from this corpus must combine all three
treatments.

**Mostly economic and weedy plants.** *Gossypium* is cotton; *Abelmoschus
esculentus* is okra; *Hibiscus sabdariffa* is roselle, and *H. cannabinus* kenaf.
*Sida*, *Urena* and *Wissadula* are largely weeds of disturbed ground, several
pantropical. This is a treatment dominated by cultivation and disturbance rather
than by forest flora.

*Hibiscus* as traditionally circumscribed is polyphyletic and has been split in
recent work, with segregates including *Talipariti* and others; the twelve
species here may not all remain in *Hibiscus*.
