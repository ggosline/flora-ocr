# Melianthaceae

**Order**: Geraniales (APG IV placement)

## Diagnosis

Trees, shrubs or subshrubs with alternate imparipinnate leaves and connate stipules. Flowers are somewhat zygomorphic in long racemes, with five sepals, five free petals, a unilateral or annular extrastaminal disc, 4–5(–6) stamens and a 4–5-locular superior ovary. Fruit is a woody loculicidal capsule with albuminous seeds.
