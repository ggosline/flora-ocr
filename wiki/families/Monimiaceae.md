# Monimiaceae

**Authority**: Juss. · **Order**: Laurales · **FR**: *Monimiacées*

One genus, two species — *Glossocalyx*. Completes [[vol10]] with
[[Lauraceae]] and [[Myristicaceae]].

## Notes

**Monimiaceae are a southern-hemisphere family** centred on Australasia,
Madagascar and South America, with a thin African representation — *Glossocalyx*
is West and Central African and one of very few members on the continent. The
distribution is a classic Gondwanan pattern.

The plants are aromatic shrubs and small trees with opposite leaves and flowers
borne on a cup-shaped receptacle that encloses the carpels — a floral form shared
with [[Lauraceae]] and characteristic of **Laurales**. *Glossocalyx* leaves are
strongly scented when crushed and used medicinally.

[[vol10]] is a coherent volume: all three of its families are magnoliids, and
two of them are Laurales.
