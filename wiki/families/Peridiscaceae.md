## Notes

***Soyauxia* has had an unusually unsettled history.** It has been placed in
Flacourtiaceae, in Medusandraceae and in its own family, and molecular work
eventually put it in **Peridiscaceae** — a small family of four genera, otherwise
Neotropical (*Peridiscus*, *Whittonia*) and African (*Medusandra*), placed in
**Saxifragales**.

That placement is the interesting part: Saxifragales is otherwise a
predominantly temperate group — saxifrages, currants, witch hazels, stonecrops —
and Peridiscaceae are its only substantial tropical rainforest element, sister to
the rest of the order. A pair of unremarkable Gabonese forest trees turns out to
occupy a deep and isolated position in the rosid tree.
