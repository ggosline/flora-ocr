# Onagraceae

**Authority**: Juss. (1789) · **Order**: Myrtales

## Diagnosis

Mostly hygrophilous terrestrial or aquatic herbs and subshrubs. Leaves are simple and lack stipules. Flowers are typically 4-merous, bisexual and actinomorphic, with the calyx tube adnate to an inferior ovary, free rapidly deciduous petals and an epigynous disc. Fruits are usually many-seeded capsules; seeds lack endosperm and may be free or enclosed by endocarp fragments.

## Distribution

Cosmopolitan, with greatest diversity in temperate parts of the Northern Hemisphere. The family comprises about 22 genera and 650 species. Tropical Africa has three genera and about 35 species; Gabon is represented in this treatment by eight taxa of [[Ludwigia]].
