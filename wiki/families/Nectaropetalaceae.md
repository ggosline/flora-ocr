# Nectaropetalaceae

**Authority**: Exell & Mendonça · **Order**: Malpighiales

## Notes

**Nectaropetalaceae is not maintained**; its genera — *Nectaropetalum*,
*Pinacopodium* — are included in **[[Erythroxylaceae]]**, the coca family, which
is treated separately in the same volume. [[vol21]] therefore holds two
family-level treatments that are now one family, alongside seven others.

That volume is the corpus's most instructive single case of shifting family
limits: of its nine families, three ([[Linaceae]]/[[Ixonanthaceae]] and this one)
have been merged or re-merged in current classification.
