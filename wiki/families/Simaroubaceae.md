# Simaroubaceae

**Order**: Sapindales (APG IV placement)

## Diagnosis

Trees and shrubs with alternate, exstipulate, usually imparipinnate leaves. Flowers bisexual, unisexual or polygamous, 4- or 5-merous, with a small cup-shaped calyx, free petals and a conspicuous disc. In most Gabonese genera the staminal filaments bear a hairy basal ligule. Carpels are usually free but closely appressed, their styles united; fruits are one-seeded drupaceous mericarps, often reduced to a single developed carpel. Bitter bark and roots are characteristic and sometimes used as febrifuges.

## Distribution

Six genera and six species were recorded from Gabon, each genus represented there by one species. *Odyendyea*, *Pierrodendron*, *Iridosma* and *Nothospondias* are restricted to Guineo-Congolian forest; *Hannoa* also reaches the Sudanian–Zambezian region, while *Quassia* is otherwise Neotropical. The treatment additionally flags *Harrisonia abyssinica*, *Brucea antidysenterica* and *B. guineensis* as possible regional or Gabonese candidates.

## Key to the treated genera

1. Staminal filaments with a woolly basal ligule.
   1. Carpels free but appressed; styles united; fruit apocarpous.
      1. Petals imbricate in bud.
         1. Calyx cup-shaped with 4–5 very short obtuse lobes; petals 4–5; stamens 8–10; disc cushion-shaped; carpels 4 — [[Odyendyea]].
         2. Calyx enclosing the bud then tearing into three irregular segments; flowers 5-merous; stamens 10; carpels 5; disc annular — [[Hannoa]].
      2. Petals contorted in bud; calyx regularly lobed; flowers 5-merous.
         1. Disc annular; flowers violet; stamens 10–15(–18), anthers large and oblong; fruits mango-like — [[Pierrodendron]].
         2. Disc cushion-shaped; flowers white; stamens 10; fruits small and ribbed — [[Quassia]].
      3. Petals (7–)8, valvate with inflexed hairy margins; stamens 12–13; anthers small — [[Iridosma]].
   2. Carpels and styles fused — *Harrisonia* (not recorded from Gabon in the treatment).
2. Staminal filaments without basal ligules; carpels free; flowers 4-merous.
   1. Stamens 4; stigmas 4, sessile — *Brucea* (not recorded from Gabon in the treatment).
   2. Stamens 8 in two whorls; style short — [[Nothospondias]].
