# Celastraceae

**Authority**: R.Br.
**Order**: Celastrales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Célastracées*

**Vol 29 covers only the Hippocrateoideae** — the volume's own title is
*Celastraceae – Hippocrateoideae* — which is why the treatment is entirely
lianas and scrambling shrubs, with *Salacia* accounting for well over half the
species. The rest of the family is treated in [[vol22]], among the composite
volume's six families.

*Salacia* with 47 species is one of the largest single genera anywhere in the
corpus, comparable to *Ficus* in [[Moraceae]].

## Notes

**This treatment records a merger.** Hippocrateaceae was long kept as a family
of its own; by 1986 it is being treated as a subfamily of Celastraceae, which is
the placement current classifications retain. The volume title preserves the
transition explicitly.
