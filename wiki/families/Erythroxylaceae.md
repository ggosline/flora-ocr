# Erythroxylaceae

**Authority**: Kunth
**Order**: Malpighiales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Érythroxylacées*

Two genera, three species — one of the nine families in [[vol21]].

| [[Erythroxylum]] | 2 |

## Notes

**This is the coca family.** *Erythroxylum coca* and *E. novogranatense* are
Andean and absent here, but the genus is pantropical and the African species
belong to the same lineage. The tropane alkaloids that make the South American
species significant are not distributed evenly through the genus, and the African
taxa are not a source of them.

*Aneulophus* is a small genus restricted to West and Central African forest.

The family's sister group is [[Rhizophoraceae]], the mangrove family, also in
this corpus ([[vol47]]) — an unexpected pairing that molecular data has made
firm.
