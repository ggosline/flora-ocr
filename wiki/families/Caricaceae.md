# Caricaceae

**Authority**: Dumort. · **Order**: Brassicales · **FR**: *Caricacées*

## Notes

**Papaya is present entirely as a cultivated and escaped plant.** The species is
Neotropical in origin and is now grown throughout the tropics; there are no
native Caricaceae in Africa, the family's diversity being American, with a small
East African outlier genus (*Cylicomorpha*) not recorded here.

**Caricaceae belong to Brassicales and produce glucosinolates** — the mustard-oil
compounds — which is why papaya latex is enzymatically active and why the leaves
and seeds have a pungent taste. That makes this the fourth mustard-oil lineage in
the corpus, with [[Brassicaceae]], [[Capparidaceae]]/[[Pentadiplandraceae]] and,
convergently from another order, [[Putranjivaceae]].

The latex is also the source of **papain**, used commercially as a meat
tenderiser and in brewing.
