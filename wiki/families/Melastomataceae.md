# Melastomataceae

**Authority**: Juss.
**Order**: Myrtales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Mélastomatacées*

Eighteen genera and 80 species, by **H. Jacques-Félix**, the specialist on
African Melastomataceae, edited by J.-F. Villiers.

## Diagnosis

Leaves opposite, sometimes whorled, always simple, entire or serrate, without
stipules or glands; venation **acrodromous** — the primary veins running from
the leaf base towards the apex in arcs rather than pinnately. That venation is
the family's most reliable field character in the region.

## Notes

**Four genera here belong to the Memecylon group** — [[Memecylon]] (15 species,
the largest in the treatment), [[Warneckea]], [[Spathandra]] and [[Lijndenia]].
This lineage has been treated as a separate family **Memecylaceae**, and is now
usually the subfamily **Olisbeoideae** within Melastomataceae. They differ from
the rest of the family in habit and, notably, in lacking the acrodromous
venation that characterises it. Anyone using the family diagnosis above should
expect the Memecylon group not to fit it.

**Taxonomic novelty published here**: *Memecylon salicifolium* Jacq.-Fél., sp.
nov.
