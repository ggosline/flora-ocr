# Dracaenaceae

**Authority**: Salisb.
**Order**: Asparagales
**Modern placement**: **Asparagaceae, subfamily Nolinoideae**
**Name in source (FR)**: *Dracénacées*

Two genera, 33 species — and in current classification, **one genus in one
family, neither of which is the name on this volume**.

## Genera in region

| Genus | Species | Modern status |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| [[Dracaena]] | 32 | Asparagaceae; now also absorbs *Sansevieria* |
| [[Sansevieria]] | 1 | **sunk into *Dracaena*** |

## Notes

**Two changes stack here.** First, Dracaenaceae is not maintained as a family —
APG places these genera in a broadly circumscribed **Asparagaceae**, in
subfamily **Nolinoideae** (the group also holding *Ruscus*, *Convallaria* and
*Polygonatum*). Second, ***Sansevieria* has been sunk into *Dracaena***, on
molecular evidence that *Sansevieria* is nested within it. So the single
*Sansevieria* species here is now a *Dracaena*, and the volume's two genera are
one.

*Dracaena* with 32 species is the substance of the treatment — forest shrubs and
small trees, several of them widely grown as ornamentals, and a genus of real
taxonomic difficulty in the region.
