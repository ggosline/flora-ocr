## Notes

**The aquatic and weedy genera of the old Scrophulariaceae.** Plantaginaceae, once the plantains alone, was recircumscribed to take in the *Veronica*, *Digitalis* and *Bacopa* lineages of Scrophulariaceae s.l. The three genera here are all plants of open wet ground.

*Bacopa monnieri* is grown worldwide as an aquarium plant and used in Ayurvedic medicine; *Scoparia dulcis* is a pantropical weed.
