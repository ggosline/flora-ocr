## Notes

**The duckweeds are Araceae.** Lemnaceae was kept as a family of its own for as long as it was judged on morphology: the plants are reduced to a floating thallus of a few millimetres, with no distinguishable stem or leaf, and often no roots. Molecular data place them firmly inside Araceae, as subfam. Lemnoideae, sister to *Pistia* and the rest of the aroids.

This flora's Araceae proper are treated elsewhere; only the duckweed genus reaches this page through [[Lemnaceae]].
