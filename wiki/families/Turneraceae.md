## Notes

**Turneraceae is included in [[Passifloraceae]]** in current classification, as
subfamily Turneroideae — so this page and the six-genus Passifloraceae treatment
in [[vol46]] are one family. *Paropsia*, on that page, has historically been
shuffled between the two, which is a fair indication of how close they are.

*Stapfiella* is a small African genus of forest herbs and subshrubs. The family's
better-known member is *Turnera diffusa*, damiana, a Central American species
traded medicinally and absent here.
