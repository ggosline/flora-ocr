# Putranjivaceae

**Authority**: Meisn.
**Order**: Malpighiales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Putranjivacées*

**One genus, 30 species** — the whole treatment is [[Drypetes]]. Another
Euphorbiaceae s.l. segregate adopted by the modern volumes, alongside
[[Phyllanthaceae]] in [[vol43]].

## Notes

**Putranjivaceae produce mustard oils** — glucosinolates, the pungent compounds
otherwise essentially confined to **Brassicales** (cabbages, capers,
*Pentadiplandra*). Their occurrence in a Malpighiales family is one of the more
striking cases of convergent biochemistry in flowering plants, and it was a
long-standing puzzle before molecular data confirmed the family really does sit
in Malpighiales rather than near Brassicales.

That gives this corpus two independent glucosinolate lineages: Putranjivaceae
here, and [[Pentadiplandraceae]] ([[vol20]]) within Brassicales proper.

*Drypetes* is dioecious, and the species are notoriously hard to determine from
sterile or single-sex material — 30 species in the region makes this one of the
more demanding genera in the flora.
