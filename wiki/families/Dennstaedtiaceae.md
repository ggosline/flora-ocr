# Dennstaedtiaceae

**Authority**: Ching
**Genera in region**: 6 · **Species in region**: 8

## Diagnosis

Rhizome creeping, bearing hairs, solenostelic. Frond of large size, multipinnate, often bearing aerophores or nectaries at the base of the pinnae. Venation free or anastomosing. Costa channelled, the groove of the lateral axes decurrent on that of the main rachis. Sori marginal or submarginal, punctiform or elongate. Indusium absent, or cup-shaped, or linear, the recurved margin of the leaf sometimes forming a false indusium. Sporangium shortly pedicellate, not arising quite all at the same time (intermediate position), often mixed with paraphyses.

This family has been very differently defined. The most recent work, that of Alston, includes part of the genera formerly placed in the PTERIDEAE (cf. Tardieu, Flore de Madagascar, 5° fam. I : 5, 1958). Holttum, on the other hand, made of DENNSTAEDTIACEAE an enormous family comprising more than 400 species and which he divides into a dozen subfamilies. We prefer to consider it in a more restricted sense.

1. Sori submarginal, not elongate; indusium cup-shaped, opening outward..... 1. Microlepia.

1'. Sori marginal, elongate, ± continuous along the margins.

2. Indusium double ..... 2. Pteridium.

2'. Indusium simple formed only by the reflexed margin of the lamina, opening inward.

3. Surfaces glabrous; costal pinnules short, stipuliform, sori most often going around the segments.. 3. Histopteris.

3'. Surfaces most often hirsute, sori often confined to the sinuses; costal pinnules not stipuliform.

4. Venation reticulate ..... 4. Lonchitis.

4'. Venation free ..... 5. Anisosorus.
