# Oleaceae

**Authority**: Hoffmanns. & Link
**Order**: Lamiales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Oléacées*

Two genera and 11 species — a small treatment, sharing [[vol51]] with
[[Urticaceae]] and [[Podostemaceae]].

## Notes

***Noronhia* is where the African *Chionanthus* went.** The continental African
and Malagasy species formerly placed in *Chionanthus* have been transferred to
**Noronhia**, a genus previously thought to be Madagascar-endemic, on molecular
evidence that *Chionanthus* s.l. was polyphyletic. Anyone consulting older
African floras — including most of the regional literature — will find these four
species under *Chionanthus*, so this is the practical name change to know for
this page.

*Jasminum* comprises the jasmines: scrambling shrubs and lianas, some native,
some grown ornamentally and escaping.

The family is otherwise familiar from temperate genera absent here — *Olea*,
*Fraxinus*, *Syringa*, *Ligustrum* — which is why a family of nearly 700 species
contributes so little to a Guineo-Congolian flora.
