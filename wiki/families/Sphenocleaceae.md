## Notes

***Sphenoclea zeylanica* is one of the principal weeds of irrigated rice** across
the palaeotropics — an erect, hollow-stemmed marsh annual that colonises flooded
fields and competes directly with the crop. Its agricultural significance is out
of all proportion to the family's size, and it is a target of rice-weed
management programmes throughout Africa and Asia.

The family holds a single genus and its placement was long uncertain; it now sits
in **Solanales**, near [[Hydroleaceae]] — another small family in this corpus
that was extracted from the same uncertainty.
