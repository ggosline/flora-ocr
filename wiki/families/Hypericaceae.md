## Notes

**Hypericaceae was segregated from Guttiferae/[[Clusiaceae]]**, with which it
shares yellow latex and opposite leaves; current classification keeps the two as
separate families within Malpighiales, alongside Calophyllaceae — so this corpus
holds all three fragments of the old Guttiferae, in [[vol42]] and [[vol45]].

*Vismia* is a shrub and small tree genus of forest clearings and regrowth,
distinctive for its **orange-red latex**, which stains and is used as a dye and
medicinally. The genus spans tropical Africa and the Neotropics, another of the
amphi-Atlantic distributions that recur through this flora.
