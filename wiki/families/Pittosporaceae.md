# Pittosporaceae

**Authority**: R.Br. · **Order**: Apiales · **FR**: *Pittosporacées*

## Notes

**Pittosporaceae is an Australasian family** whose single widespread genus
reaches Africa; *Pittosporum viridiflorum* and relatives are montane and
submontane trees and shrubs, so the family belongs to the higher ground at the
margins of this region rather than to lowland forest.

The family sits in **Apiales**, alongside [[Apiaceae]] and Araliaceae — a
placement that is unobvious from the woody habit and sticky, resinous seeds, but
well supported. It is the woody sister group to the rest of the order.
