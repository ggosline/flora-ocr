## Notes

**The parasitic genera of the old Scrophulariaceae.** Scrophulariaceae s.l. proved to be several unrelated lineages, and the root-parasitic ones form a clade with the broomrapes -- *Orobanche* itself -- which had been kept apart as a family of their own. All seven genera here are hemiparasites, green plants that draw water and nutrients from a host root through a haustorium.

*Striga*, the witchweeds, are the most consequential: obligate parasites of grasses, and among the worst constraints on cereal yields across the African savannas.
