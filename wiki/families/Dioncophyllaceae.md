# Dioncophyllaceae

**Authority**: Airy Shaw (1952) · **Order**: Caryophyllales

## Diagnosis

Climbing shrubs or lianas without stipules. Rosette and climbing-stem leaves differ, the latter bearing paired apical tendrils separated by a mucro. Bisexual actinomorphic flowers have 3–5 sepals, five contorted petals and 10 or 25–32 stamens. The rapidly dehiscent capsule releases a few very large discoid seeds with broad peripheral wings.

## Distribution

Tropical African family with a disjunction between Upper Guinea and Gabon–Republic of the Congo. It contains three monotypic genera; [[Dioncophyllum]] occurs in the wiki region.
