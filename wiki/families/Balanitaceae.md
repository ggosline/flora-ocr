# Balanitaceae

**Order**: Zygophyllales · **Current placement**: Zygophyllaceae

## Diagnosis

The monogeneric family used in Vol. 6 comprises spiny trees and shrubs with alternate bifoliolate leaves, one-ovuled ovary locules and one-seeded drupes. The treatment discusses its historically uncertain placement; modern classifications generally include *Balanites* in Zygophyllaceae.

## Genus

- [[Balanites]] — one rare species in Gabon.
