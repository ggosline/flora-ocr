# Hydrocharitaceae

**Authority**: Juss. · **Order**: Alismatales · **FR**: *Hydrocharitacées*

Two genera, two species — submerged and floating freshwater aquatics. Shares
[[vol50]] with [[Piperaceae]].

## Notes

**Hydrocharitaceae is the family that includes the seagrasses** *Thalassia*,
*Enhalus* and *Halophila* — flowering plants that have returned fully to the sea
and pollinate underwater. Those genera are not in this treatment, but the family
provides the region's marine angiosperms alongside [[Cymodoceaceae]], and any
seagrass survey of the Gabonese coast concerns these two families.

The freshwater genera here are ordinary pond and slow-river plants: *Ottelia* is
rosette-forming and submerged, *Hydrocharis* floating.

The order **Alismatales** contains almost all the world's truly aquatic monocots,
including [[Potamogetonaceae]], [[Ruppiaceae]], [[Cymodoceaceae]],
[[Alismataceae]] and — after its absorption — [[Lemnaceae]], all of which are in
this corpus.
