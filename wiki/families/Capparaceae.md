## Notes

**Capparidaceae in the strict sense.** Capparidaceae s.l. was found to be three lineages rather than one: the caper family proper, the *Cleome* group ([[Cleomaceae]]), and the mustards ([[Brassicaceae]]), which are nested with them. The name in current use for the caper family is Capparaceae; this flora spells it Capparidaceae, an older orthography for the same circumscription minus the *Cleome* genera.

*Ritchiea*, *Euadenia* and *Buchholzia* are West-Central African forest shrubs and small trees. *Buchholzia coriacea* is widely used medicinally, and its seeds are traded as "wooden pepper".
