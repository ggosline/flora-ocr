# Dichapetalaceae

**Authority**: Baill.
**Order**: Malpighiales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Dichapétalacées*

**Two genera, 60 species** — the most lopsided treatment in the corpus, with
*Dichapetalum* holding 53 of them. Trees, shrubs and lianas.

## Notes

**Dichapetalaceae are notoriously toxic.** The family is the classic source of
**fluoroacetate** — the same compound as the rodenticide "1080" — accumulated in
the leaves and seeds. *Dichapetalum cymosum*, the southern African *gifblaar*, is
the best-known case and a significant cause of livestock poisoning; the toxin was
first characterised from this family. Whether the Gabonese species accumulate it
to the same degree is not something the 1991 treatment addresses, and it would be
worth checking before repeating the generalisation about the region's taxa.
