# Connaraceae

**Authority**: R.Br.
**Order**: Oxalidales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Connaracées*

Seven genera and 37 species — lianas and scrambling shrubs, a characteristic
component of forest edge and secondary growth.

## Notes

**Connaraceae are strongly heterostylous**, and the treatment discusses this at
the family level — the OCR segmenter mistakes the French heading *hétérostylie*
for a genus name, "Heterostylie", which is why a naive count gives eight genera
rather than seven.

*Manotes* is sometimes subsumed in *Rourea*; current treatments differ, and the
four species here may be found under either name.

The family sits in **Oxalidales**, beside Oxalidaceae and Cunoniaceae — a
placement from molecular data that is not obvious from the morphology, the
follicular fruit with an arillate seed having historically suggested an alliance
with Leguminosae.

*Jollydora* is an unusual cauliflorous shrub genus endemic to West and Central
African forest.
