# Octoknemaceae

**Order**: Santalales (APG IV placement)
**Name in source (FR)**: *Octoknémacées*

A small family **endemic to Africa**, of two genera. The treatment's own header
records 2 genera and 5 species, of which 4 species occur in Cameroon, and states
1 genus and 2 species for Gabon — but it then treats four *Octoknema* species
and *Okoubaka* with Gabonese material, so that header count is inconsistent with
the body.

## Diagnosis

Trees or shrubs with simple, alternate or subopposite, entire, exstipulate,
petiolate leaves. Inflorescences racemes or panicles, axillary or cauline;
plants monoecious or dioecious. Flowers small, pubescent on the outer face,
penta- or tetramerous. Stamens epipetalous, free, with a short broad filament,
replaced by staminodes in female flowers. Disc present. Ovary **inferior**, with
one or several uniovulate locules; ovule pendulous; style short, the stigma
3–5-lobed. Drupe one-seeded, the endocarp in *Octoknema* emitting **8–10
meridian plates** towards the albumen. Embryo small.

Those internal endocarp plates give the family its name and are its most
distinctive character.

## Key to the genera

1. Inflorescence an axillary raceme; drupe 1.5–3 cm; endocarp emitting
   digitations on its inner face; ovary plurilocular; pubescence stellate →
   **1. [[Octoknema]]**
1'. Inflorescence a cauline panicle; drupe 9–15 cm; endocarp smooth within;
   ovary unilocular; pubescence of simple, forked and stellate hairs →
   **2. [[Okoubaka]]**

## Changes since the Flore (post-1973)

- [[Octoknema]] was monographed by Gosline & Malécot (2011, Kew Bull. 66:
  367–404), who recognise **14 species** against Villiers' 6, including six new
  ones. The genus page records this; the species accounts here are still
  Villiers' concepts and need reconciling with the revision.

## Notes

**Circumscription.** Later classifications sink *Octoknemaceae* into
**Erythropalaceae** within Santalales. The wiki keeps Villiers'
circumscription, since that is what the treatment documents.

**Source directory.** This family shares a directory with [[Olacaceae]],
[[Opiliaceae]] and [[Pentadiplandraceae]]: OCR truncated its heading to
`OCTOKNEMACE`, so the OCR-time splitter never separated it.
