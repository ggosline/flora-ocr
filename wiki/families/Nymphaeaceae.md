## Notes

**This is the most phylogenetically ancient family in the corpus.**
Nymphaeales are part of the **ANA grade** — the earliest-diverging lineages of
flowering plants, branching below both monocots and eudicots, with only
Amborellales diverging earlier. Every other family in this wiki sits above them
on the tree. For a page describing four pond plants, that is worth stating
plainly.

Their aquatic habit is therefore ancient rather than derived from any of the
other aquatic groups here — [[Podostemaceae]], [[Hydrocharitaceae]],
[[Pontederiaceae]] and [[Ceratophyllaceae]] each acquired it independently.

*Nymphaea lotus* and relatives are widely used across Africa — rhizomes and
seeds are eaten, and the flowers carry considerable cultural significance.
