# Typhaceae

**Authority**: Juss. · **Order**: Poales · **FR**: *Typhacées*

One genus, one species — *Typha*, the cattails or bulrushes.

| Genus | Species |

## Notes

***Typha* forms dense monospecific stands** in marshes, lake margins and
irrigation channels, spreading clonally by rhizome, and is a significant
management problem where nutrient enrichment or altered hydrology lets it expand
— in the Sahel it has choked large areas of the Senegal River system following
dam construction, with consequences for fisheries, navigation and malaria
vectors.

The plants are used throughout the region for matting, thatch and basketry, and
the rhizomes are edible.

Typhaceae in the modern circumscription also includes *Sparganium*, formerly
Sparganiaceae, which does not occur here.
