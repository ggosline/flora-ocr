# Clusiaceae

**Authority**: Lindl.
**Also known as**: Guttiferae (the conserved alternative name)
**Order**: Malpighiales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Clusiacées*

Seven genera and 23 species — trees with yellow or white latex, sharing
[[vol45]] with [[Malvaceae]].

## Notes

**Three genera here now sit in Calophyllaceae.** Clusiaceae s.l. was found to be
non-monophyletic, and *Calophyllum*, *Mammea* and *Endodesmia* — along with
*Kielmeyera* and relatives — were separated as **Calophyllaceae**. Clusiaceae
s.s. retains *Garcinia*, *Allanblackia*, *Pentadesma* and *Symphonia*, which is
the bulk of this treatment.

**Two genera are of commercial interest for their seed fats.**
*Allanblackia* seeds yield a hard oil that has been developed as a
sustainably-sourced substitute for palm and dairy fats, with organised smallholder
collection across West and Central Africa; *Pentadesma butyracea* is the
tallow tree, its seed fat long used locally. Both are non-timber forest products
of real regional economic significance.

*Garcinia* — 14 species, the largest genus here — includes the mangosteen
relatives, and several African species are chewed as chewing-sticks or used
medicinally. *Symphonia globulifera* is remarkable for its transatlantic
distribution, occurring in both African and American tropical forest.
