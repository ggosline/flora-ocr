## Notes

**The cyanogenic half of the old Flacourtiaceae.** When Flacourtiaceae was dismantled, the tribes producing cyanogenic glycosides -- Pangieae and their allies -- went to Achariaceae rather than to [[Salicaceae]], which took the rest. The family also holds *Hydnocarpus*, the Asian source of chaulmoogra oil.

All five genera here are West-Central African forest shrubs and small trees. *Scottellia* is a timber genus, traded as odoko.
