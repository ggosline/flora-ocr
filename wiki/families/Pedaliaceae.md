# Pedaliaceae

**Authority**: R.Br.
**Order**: Lamiales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Pédaliacées*

One genus, three species — but the genus is *Sesamum*.

## Notes

***Sesamum indicum* is sesame**, one of the oldest oilseed crops in cultivation
and a significant one across the African savanna zone. The wild African
*Sesamum* species are of interest as crop relatives — Africa is a centre of
diversity for the genus, and the wild taxa carry variation relevant to breeding
for disease resistance and drought tolerance.

Pedaliaceae are otherwise best known for *Harpagophytum*, devil's claw, a
southern African genus widely traded medicinally and absent from this region;
and for the family's characteristic mucilaginous foliage and hooked or horned
fruits.
