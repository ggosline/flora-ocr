# Eriocaulaceae

**Authority**: Martinov
**Order**: Poales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Ériocaulacées*

Four genera and 11 species — the pipeworts, small rosette herbs of seasonally
wet ground. Shares [[vol48]] with [[Ochnaceae]] and [[Scrophulariaceae]].

## Notes

**Eriocaulaceae are a habitat-specialist family of high conservation interest.**
They grow in shallow seasonal water, seepage, damp sand and — importantly for
this region — the thin soils and drainage tracks of **inselbergs**, the granite
domes scattered through Lower Guinea. These habitats are naturally fragmented,
which drives narrow endemism: many African pipeworts are restricted to a single
massif or a few outcrops.

That makes them, with [[Podostemaceae]], one of the two groups in this corpus
where a small treatment conceals disproportionate extinction risk, and where the
threats are specific — quarrying, altered drainage, and the trampling of seepage
communities rather than logging.

*Mesanthemum* is largely African; *Syngonanthus* and *Paepalanthus* are centred
in the Neotropics, especially the Brazilian *campos rupestres*, and the African
species are outliers of those radiations — an ecological parallel between
inselbergs and quartzite mountains on either side of the Atlantic.

*Afrotrilepis*, in [[Cyperaceae]], occupies the same inselberg habitat and is
worth reading alongside this page.
