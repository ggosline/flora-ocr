## Notes

**Salicaceae now holds most of the old Flacourtiaceae.** The family was for a long time the willows and poplars alone, temperate and absent from this region. Molecular work in the 1990s found that the greater part of Flacourtiaceae -- the tribes with salicoid teeth and without cyanogenic glycosides -- belongs with them, and the family was recircumscribed to take them in. Everything on this page reached it that way; no *Salix* or *Populus* is native to Gabon.

*Homalium* and *Casearia* are large pantropical genera of forest trees, *Homalium* a timber genus in the region. *Oncoba* is the largest genus here, and *Phyllobotryon* is remarkable for its epiphyllous inflorescences, borne along the midvein of the leaf.
