# Flagellariaceae

**Order**: Poales (APG IV placement)  
**Name in source (FR)**: *Flagellariacées*

## Diagnosis

Erect or creeping plants with alternate sheathing leaves; sheaths closed or
split, and the leaf sometimes ending in a tendril. Inflorescence a terminal
panicle. Flowers hermaphrodite or plants dioecious. Perianth of 6 imbricate
tepals in two whorls. Stamens 6, alternating with the tepals; anthers introrse,
opening by longitudinal slits. Fruit an indehiscent drupe with one or several
seeds.

## Distribution

The family comprises three genera distributed in Africa, Australia and the
Indo-Malayan region. A single genus occurs in West Africa and Gabon.
