## Notes

**Leeaceae is now included in [[Vitaceae]]**, as subfamily Leeoideae, with *Leea*
resolving as sister to the rest of the family. [[vol14]] treats the two side by
side, which now reads as a single family split across two chapters — and
presumably reflects the compilers recognising the affinity even while keeping the
families separate.

*Leea* differs from the rest of Vitaceae in being an erect shrub or small tree
rather than a tendril climber, and in lacking tendrils altogether. That habit
difference was the main basis for family rank; molecular data show it sits inside
the grape family regardless.
