# Lomariopsidaceae

**Authority**: Alston
**Genera in region**: 2 · **Species in region**: 9

## Diagnosis

Terrestrial or epiphytic ferns, with creeping or climbing rhizome, of dorsiventral structure, bearing cordately based scales. Petioles non-articulate or articulate, with vascular cords arranged in a U. Fronds simple or pinnate, with pinnae decurrent on the rachis. Veins free or anastomosing; areoles without included veinlets. Fertile frond acrostichoid.

1. Veins free..... 1. Lomariopsis.
