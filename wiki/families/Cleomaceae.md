## Notes

**The *Cleome* group, between the capers and the mustards.** *Cleome* and its relatives sit between [[Capparaceae]] and [[Brassicaceae]] and are recognised as a family of their own. They are herbs, not the shrubs and trees of the caper family, and share the mustard oils of the order.

*Cleome rutidosperma* and *C. spinosa* are pantropical weeds of cultivation; the flora treats four species, one introduced.
