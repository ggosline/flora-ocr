# Polygonaceae

Herbs or woody lianas with alternate leaves and a stem-sheathing ochrea, small flowers with a persistent or accrescent perianth, and one-seeded achenes. Vol. 7 treats [[Afrobrunnichia]], [[Rumex]] and [[Polygonum]].
