## Notes

**The family has a single genus of three species, split between West/Central
Africa and Malesia** — one species here, the others in Borneo, Sumatra and New
Guinea. It is one of a set of African–Southeast Asian disjunctions that recur in
this flora, alongside [[Pandaceae]] and [[Ixonanthaceae]], and reflects an old
palaeotropical forest flora since fragmented by aridification and continental
movement.

*Ctenolophon* was long placed in [[Linaceae]] and is now recognised as its own
family in Malpighiales. Its fossil pollen is well known and widespread, showing
the genus once had a much larger range than the relictual one it holds today.

This page and [[Ctenolophonaceae]]'s presence in the corpus exist because of the
family-split fix: the treatment's heading was truncated to `# CTENOLOPHONACEA`
by the scan and was invisible to the ingest until that was repaired.
