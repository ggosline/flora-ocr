# Phytolaccaceae

Herbs or shrubs with alternate exstipulate leaves, racemose flowers and berry-like or dry fruits. Vol. 7 treats [[Phytolacca]] and [[Hilleria]]; the latter is now generally placed in Petiveriaceae.
