# Musaceae

## Diagnosis

Giant herbs with pseudostems formed from overlapping petiole bases, alternate non-distichous leaves and large pendent terminal inflorescences. Flowers are functionally unisexual, with five fertile stamens in male flowers; the inferior 3-locular ovary forms an indehiscent berry.

## Gabon treatment

No wild species is recorded. Vol. 9 discusses cultivated clones derived from [[Musa_acuminata|*Musa acuminata*]] and hybrids between it and [[Musa_balbisiana|*M. balbisiana*]]. Plantains are of major dietary importance in Gabon.
