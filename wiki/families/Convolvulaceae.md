## Notes

***Ipomoea batatas*, the sweet potato, is the family's economic centre** — a
crop of major importance across the region, and the reason the genus turns up in
cultivation as well as in wild vegetation.

***Merremia* has been split.** Recent work divides the genus among *Distimake*,
*Camonea*, *Xenostegia* and others, retaining *Merremia* in a much narrower
sense, so the two species here should be checked against current names.

*Neuropeltis* and *Calycobolus* are the distinctively forest genera — woody
lianas rather than the herbaceous twiners the family is usually associated with.
*Neuropeltis* has a conspicuously accrescent bract subtending the fruit.

The family's closest relative is [[Solanaceae]], also in Solanales and also
treated in this corpus ([[vol55]]).
