# Balsaminaceae

**Order**: Ericales (APG IV placement)

## Diagnosis

Succulent herbs with simple exstipulate leaves and strongly zygomorphic, resupinate flowers. The lower sepal is enlarged into a coloured spurred lip; paired petals form wings; five stamens surround the ovary. Capsules split elastically into five coiling valves, throwing the small exalbuminous seeds.

## Genus in Gabon

- [[Impatiens]] — 16 species in the treatment, concentrated in very wet forest, waterfalls, torrents and river margins.
