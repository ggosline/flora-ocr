# Hyacinthaceae

**Authority**: Batsch ex Borkh.
**Order**: Asparagales
**Modern placement**: **Asparagaceae, subfamily Scilloideae**
**Name in source (FR)**: *Hyacinthacées*

Four genera, one species each — bulbous herbs of savanna and seasonally dry
ground.

## Notes

**Hyacinthaceae is not maintained as a family** in APG, which places these
genera in a broadly circumscribed **Asparagaceae**, subfamily **Scilloideae**.
That is the same family that absorbs [[Dracaenaceae]] ([[vol54]]) and
[[Anthericaceae]] — so this corpus holds several separately-named treatments
that now belong to one family, and a modern regional Asparagaceae must be
assembled from all of them.

*Drimia* includes species with strongly cardiotoxic bulbs — the group containing
the squills, used historically as rat poison and in traditional medicine, and a
cause of livestock deaths.

Bulbous monocots are poorly represented in the wet forest of Lower Guinea; this
family belongs to the drier savanna margins of the region.
