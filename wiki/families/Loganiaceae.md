# Loganiaceae

**Authority**: R.Br. ex Mart.
**Order**: Gentianales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Loganiacées*

Seven genera and 59 species, edited by N. Hallé. With [[Rubiaceae]], the source
treats Loganiaceae as one of the only two essentially tropical families of its
order — a framing set out in the Rubiaceae general-characters chapter of
[[vol12]].

***Strychnos* holds 43 of the 59 species** — lianas and small trees, and the
source of **strychnine** and of the *Strychnos* curare alkaloids. It is among the
most pharmacologically consequential genera in the flora.

## Notes

**Loganiaceae as circumscribed here has been dismantled.** The traditional broad
family was polyphyletic, and its genera are now distributed across several
families in Gentianales and Lamiales: *Strychnos* and *Spigelia* remain in
Loganiaceae s.s.; *Anthocleista* goes to **Gentianaceae**; *Nuxia* to
**Stilbaceae**; *Mostuea* to **Gelsemiaceae**; and *Mitreola* to Loganiaceae s.s.
or Mitreolaceae depending on the treatment. So five of the seven genera here may
sit outside the family in a modern source. These placements should be verified
against a current reference before being relied on.

*Anthocleista* — large-leaved pachycaul trees of swamp and secondary forest — is
ecologically conspicuous in the region and worth its own page.
