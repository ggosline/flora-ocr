# Hernandiaceae

**Authority**: Blume

## Notes

*Illigera* comprises **twining lianas**, climbing by coiling petioles rather than
by tendrils or hooks. The family belongs to **Laurales**, the magnoliid order
also holding [[Lauraceae]] and [[Monimiaceae]] ([[vol10]]), and shares their
aromatic tissues — but not their arborescent habit, which makes *Illigera*
something of an outlier among the region's magnoliids.

Hernandiaceae are otherwise known for *Hernandia* itself, a genus of coastal
trees with inflated, lantern-like fruits adapted to sea dispersal, absent from
this treatment.
