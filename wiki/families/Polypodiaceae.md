# Polypodiaceae

**Authority**: R. Br
**Genera in region**: 10 · **Species in region**: 10

## Diagnosis

Ferns generally epiphytic, rarely terrestrial, with rhizome containing a ring of vascular bundles. Scales mostly peltate. Fronds in 2 rows at the upper part of the rhizome, and articulate (except Stenochlaena). Frond entire or pinnate, with reticulate venation, areoles with included veinlets. Sori without indusium (sometimes bearing flattened paraphyses), rounded, or elongate, sometimes sunken in cavities, or acrostichoid. Sporangia with long stalk, and 12–16 cells in U-shape.

The term POLYPODIACEAE is used here in the sense given it by Alston (loc. cit).

About a thousand species, especially tropical and subtropical, formerly placed in a single poorly defined genus, Polypodium.
