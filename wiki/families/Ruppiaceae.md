# Ruppiaceae

**Authority**: Horan. (1834) · **Order**: Alismatales

## Diagnosis

Glabrous submerged or floating herbs, usually of marine or brackish water. Filiform one-veined leaves have sheathing bases and intravaginal scales. Tiny bisexual flowers have no perianth or two minute tepals, two sessile anthers and several free superior one-ovulate ovaries. The asymmetric, beaked, indehiscent drupaceous fruits are dispersed by waterbirds.

## Distribution

Cosmopolitan in marine and brackish waters. The family contains only [[Ruppia]], with about ten species globally and one documented in Gabon.
