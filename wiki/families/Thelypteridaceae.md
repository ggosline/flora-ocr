# Thelypteridaceae

**Authority**: Ching
**Genera in region**: 1 · **Species in region**: 3

## Diagnosis

Terrestrial ferns, with creeping or erect rhizome, covered with loose, thick scales, bearing unicellular hairs on the margin or surface. Petioles having, at the base, two vascular cords, united in a U at the apex. Blade pinnate-bipinnate, bearing, most often, unicellular hairs as well as the petiole. Costa and veins covered with long unicellular hairs, soft, on the upper surface, rarely naked. Venation pinnate, with free veins, the lower ones of 2 neighboring groups sometimes united into an excurrent vein, terminating in the sinus bearing a translucent membrane. Sori superficial, dorsal on the veins, rounded or crescent-shaped, with indusium generally reniform, often hirsute and fugacious. Sporangia glabrous or bearing hairs, with short stalk, annulus of 14 cells.

About 800 species, tropical and subtropical. A single genus in Gabon, Cyclosorus.
