# Combretaceae

**Authority**: R.Br.
**Order**: Myrtales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Combrétacées*

Six genera and 54 species (1999). Lianas, trees and shrubs, spanning forest,
savanna and — distinctively — mangrove.

***Combretum* holds 45 of the 54 species**, mostly lianas and scrambling shrubs.

## Notes

**The family contributes two of the region's mangrove species.**
*Laguncularia racemosa* (white mangrove) and *Conocarpus erectus* (button
mangrove) are both monospecific here and both components of Atlantic-coast
mangrove, alongside *Avicennia* ([[Avicenniaceae]], [[vol22]]) and
Rhizophoraceae. Their presence makes Combretaceae one of the few families in the
corpus spanning closed forest, savanna and tidal habitats.

*Terminalia superba* (limba/fraké) and *T. ivorensis* are major regional
timbers. *Strephonema* is an African endemic genus sometimes placed in its own
subfamily, sister to the rest of the family.
