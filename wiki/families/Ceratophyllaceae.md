# Ceratophyllaceae

**Authority**: Gray (1822) · **Order**: Ceratophyllales

## Diagnosis

Rootless, monoecious aquatic herbs with branched axes and whorls of filiform, repeatedly dichotomous leaves. Male and female flowers occur separately in the leaf axils. The superior one-ovulate ovary develops into a variably flattened, winged or spiny achene whose ornamentation is taxonomically important.

## Distribution

Cosmopolitan except Antarctica, in fresh water. The family contains the single genus [[Ceratophyllum]]. One species is confirmed in Gabon and a second occurs in Cameroon and is expected in Gabon.
