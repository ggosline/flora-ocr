## Notes

**Iridaceae is overwhelmingly a southern African family**, with the Cape Floristic
Region holding a very large share of its roughly 2,000 species, including several
hundred *Gladiolus*. Three species in Lower Guinea is a thin northern outlier of
that radiation, and the family's near-absence here is as informative as its
presence — these are corm-bearing plants of open, seasonally dry ground, a habitat
type that is marginal in a wet-forest region.

The garden gladioli are hybrids derived largely from South African species, not
from the tropical ones treated here.
