# Commelinaceae

**Authority**: Mirb.
**Order**: Commelinales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Commélinacées*

Twelve genera and 50 species — succulent-stemmed herbs of forest floor, marsh
and disturbed ground, with mucilaginous sap and sheathing leaf bases.

***Palisota* holds 21 of the 50 species** — an African genus, robust and often
almost woody at the base, and the dominant commelinaceous element of
Guineo-Congolian forest floor.

## Notes

*Palisota* is morphologically atypical for the family and is placed in its own
tribe, Palisoteae, resolving as sister to the rest of subfamily Commelinoideae in
molecular analyses. Its concentration here — nearly half the regional species —
makes Lower Guinea a centre of diversity for the genus.

*Commelina* is pantropical and includes several widespread weeds; *Cyanotis*,
*Murdannia* and *Tricarpelema* are largely palaeotropical genera reaching their
western limit in the region.
