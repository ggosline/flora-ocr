## Notes

**Cannabaceae has been substantially enlarged**, and this corpus splits it across
two volumes under two family names. The narrow family of *Cannabis* and *Humulus*
now also takes in the tropical genera formerly placed in Ulmaceae — *Celtis*,
*Trema*, *Aphananthe*, *Parasponia* — leaving Ulmaceae s.s. as a temperate family
absent from the region.

So the region's real Cannabaceae is this one-species treatment **plus** the whole
of [[Ulmaceae]] in [[vol58]]: six species in three genera, of which *Celtis* is a
significant forest timber and *Trema orientalis* one of the most widespread
pioneer trees in the tropics.

*Cannabis sativa* itself is present as a cultivated and escaped plant, not as a
native element.
