# Scytopetalaceae

**Authority**: Engl.
**Order**: Ericales
**Modern placement**: **Lecythidaceae, subfamily Scytopetaloideae**
**Name in source (FR)**: *Scytopétalacées*

Five genera and 16 species, **entirely endemic to tropical Africa** — one of the
few families in the corpus with no representation outside the continent. Shares
[[vol24]] with [[Chrysobalanaceae]].

## Notes

**Scytopetalaceae is now the African arm of Lecythidaceae** — the Brazil-nut
family — as subfamily **Scytopetaloideae**. Lecythidaceae itself is treated
separately in this corpus, in [[vol42]], so the two pages together make up the
modern family for the region.

The group is a genuine African forest endemic radiation, centred on Lower Guinea:
all five genera and the great majority of species occur within the region, and
*Pierrina* and *Brazzeia* are narrowly restricted. That makes this small family
disproportionately important for regional endemism, and worth attention in any
conservation assessment drawn from the wiki.
