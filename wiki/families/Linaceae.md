# Linaceae

**Authority**: DC. ex Perleb
**Order**: Malpighiales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Linacées*

One genus, nine species — all *Hugonia*. One of the nine families in [[vol21]].

| [[Hugonia]] | 9 |

## Notes

***Hugonia* climbs by hooks** — paired, woody, recurved structures derived from
modified inflorescence axes, positioned below the leaves and used to hold the
liana in the canopy. It is an unusual climbing mechanism, distinct from tendrils
or twining, and makes the genus recognisable in sterile material.

The genus is sometimes segregated with its relatives as **Hugoniaceae**, or
placed as subfamily Hugonioideae within Linaceae; current treatments generally
keep it in a broadly circumscribed Linaceae.

The family's familiar member, *Linum usitatissimum* — flax and linseed — is
temperate and absent from the region, so this treatment shows Linaceae in an
entirely unfamiliar guise: forest lianas rather than slender herbs.
