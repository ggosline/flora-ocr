# Lecythidaceae

**Authority**: A.Rich.
**Order**: Ericales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Lécythidacées*

Four genera and nine species — the Brazil-nut family, in its African guise.

## Notes

**The modern family is larger than this page**, because [[Scytopetalaceae]]
([[vol24]]) is now Lecythidaceae subfamily Scytopetaloideae. The two treatments
together make up the region's Lecythidaceae — five more genera and sixteen more
species on that page.

***Napoleonaea* has one of the most remarkable flowers in the African flora** —
sessile on the trunk and branches, with a complex triple corona of staminodial
rings in place of a conventional corolla, giving a structure more like a sea
anemone than a typical flower. The genus is West and Central African and its
pollination is still not well understood.

*Combretodendron macrocarpum* and *Petersianthus macrocarpus* are the same
species under two names; *Petersianthus* is correct, and the tree — **essia** or
abalé — is a significant regional timber. The source uses both, so both appear
in the OCR text.
