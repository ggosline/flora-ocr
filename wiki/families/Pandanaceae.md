# Pandanaceae

**Order**: Pandanales (APG IV placement)  
**Name in source (FR)**: *Pandanacées*

## Diagnosis

Dioecious trees, shrubs or lianas with woody, annular-scarred stems and often
adventitious stilt or climbing roots. Leaves simple, spirally arranged,
toothed on the margins and beneath the midrib, with double lateral folds.
Female spikes comprise many free or united ovaries; male flowers have free or
variously column-associated stamens. Fruits drupes or berries.

## Distribution

Three genera occur from Africa through the Indo-Pacific. Only [[Pandanus]],
the largest genus with c. 700 species, occurs in Africa. The treatment
documents four species within the wiki's region: three confirmed in Gabon and
[[Pandanus_candelabrum|*P. candelabrum*]] confirmed in Nigeria and Cameroon.
The genus is associated particularly with wet or swampy ground.

## Treatment

### Vol 28 (1986)

Huynh describes *P. parvicentralis* and *P. gabonensis* as new, treats the
poorly known *P. teuszii*, and documents *P. candelabrum* from Nigeria and
Cameroon while noting that its presence in Gabon required confirmation. Five
plates document drupe anatomy, leaves, habit and infructescences. Printed p. 12
was obliterated in the available scan, leaving the latter part of the *P.
parvicentralis* treatment incomplete.
