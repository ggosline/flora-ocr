## Notes

**This is one of the most recently erected families in the corpus.**
Centroplacaceae was recognised only in the 2000s, on molecular evidence, and
holds two genera: *Centroplacus*, West and Central African, and *Bhesa*, Asian —
another Africa–Malesia pairing like [[Ctenolophonaceae]].

*Centroplacus* had previously been shuffled between Euphorbiaceae, Pandaceae and
Flacourtiaceae without settling anywhere, which is a common history for the small
Malpighiales segregates in this flora. Its appearance as a family in [[vol57]]
means that volume follows a current classification.
