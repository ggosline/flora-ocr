# Rhamnaceae

**Order**: Rosales (APG IV placement)

## Diagnosis

Trees, shrubs or lianas with simple leaves, small mostly 5-merous flowers, stamens opposite and often enclosed by hooded petals, and a conspicuous disc. Fruits vary from drupes in *Maesopsis* and *Lasiodiscus*, to a long-winged samara in *Ventilago* and a three-winged schizocarp in *Gouania*.
