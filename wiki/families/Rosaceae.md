## Notes

**Rosaceae is a large temperate family that barely enters lowland tropical
Africa.** Some 3,000 species worldwide — roses, apples, pears, cherries, plums,
almonds, strawberries — and two brambles reach this flora. Its near-absence is a
straightforward consequence of climate: the family's centre of diversity is the
northern temperate zone, and its tropical representation is mostly montane.

*Rubus* here belongs to forest edge and higher ground; the genus is notoriously
difficult taxonomically everywhere it occurs, through apomixis and hybridisation,
so two species should be treated as a working figure.

[[Chrysobalanaceae]] in this corpus was long placed in Rosales next to Rosaceae
on floral grounds and has since moved to Malpighiales, so the resemblance
between the two families in this flora is misleading.
