# Amaryllidaceae

**Order**: Asparagales (APG IV placement)  
**Name in source (FR)**: *Amaryllidacées*

## Diagnosis

Bulbous or rhizomatous herbs with basal or distichous leaves. Leafless scape
bears an umbel-like inflorescence subtended by an involucre. Flowers showy,
bisexual, trimerous and usually regular; tepals 6, free or united into a tube;
stamens 6; ovary inferior and 3-locular. Fruit a capsule or berry.

## Treatment

Nordal treats seven indigenous species in two genera. A probable hybrid is
also documented as *Crinum* sp. A. An appendix discusses cultivated exotic
genera but these are not counted as members of the native flora.
