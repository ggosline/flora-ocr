# Rutaceae

**Authority**: Juss. · **Order**: Sapindales

## Diagnosis

Trees, shrubs or lianas, often prickly, with alternate simple or compound leaves characteristically dotted with translucent aromatic oil glands. Flowers are usually small, 4- or 5-merous, with a conspicuous disk; fruits in the Gabon treatment range from follicles and mericarps to drupes and citrus-like berries.

## Treatment

### Vol 6 (1963)

**Author**: R. Letouzey · **Pages**: 3–109  
**Source**: `sources/Rutaceae_vol6_paddle`

The treatment includes keys and extensive comparison with taxa in neighbouring West and Central African territories. Parenthesized sections on *Toddaliopsis*, *Oriciopsis*, *Teclea*, *Clausena* and *Balsamocitrus* concern species not accepted as Gabon accounts; a concluding section lists cultivated introduced *Citrus*.
