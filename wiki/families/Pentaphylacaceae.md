## Notes

**Ternstroemiaceae is part of Pentaphylacaceae.** The *Ternstroemia* group was at times placed in Theaceae and at times kept as a family of its own; it is now included in Pentaphylacaceae, a small Ericalean family of trees and shrubs.

*Ternstroemia* is pantropical, and reaches Gabon with a single montane species.
