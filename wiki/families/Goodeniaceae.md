# Goodeniaceae

**Authority**: R.Br. · **Order**: Asterales · **FR**: *Goodéniacées*

One genus, one species — *Scaevola*.

## Notes

**Goodeniaceae is essentially an Australian family**, with the great majority of
its species there. *Scaevola* is the exception: *S. taccada* (formerly *S.
plumieri* in African literature) is a pantropical strand shrub, dispersed by
buoyant, sea-tolerant fruits, and occurs on sandy coasts throughout the tropics
including the Gabonese seaboard.

The flowers are distinctive — the corolla is split to the base on one side and
spread into a fan, giving the "half-flower" that the genus name refers to.

It is one of a small set of pantropical strand plants in this flora whose
distribution is set by ocean currents rather than by continental history — a
different pattern from the Gondwanan disjunctions of [[Humiriaceae]] and
[[Lepidobotryaceae]].
