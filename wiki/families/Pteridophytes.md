# Pteridophytes

Not a family. Vol. 8 of the Flore du Gabon treats the ferns and fern allies as
one block under this heading, and its lower divisions -- Lycopodiales,
Ophioglossales, Marattiales and the rest -- are printed as ordinal headings
rather than as family treatments. Genera that arrived under the block heading
rather than under a family of their own are gathered here, so nothing in the
fern material points at a family page that does not exist.

## Genera placed here

These reached the wiki with `family: Pteridophytes` because no family
treatment was segmented around them. The modern family is given where it is
unambiguous; it is not stated by the source.
