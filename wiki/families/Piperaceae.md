# Piperaceae

**Authority**: Giseke
**Order**: Piperales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Pipéracées*

Two genera and 10 species — magnoliid herbs and climbers with the family's
characteristic swollen nodes and dense spicate inflorescences.

## Notes

***Piper guineense* is West African pepper** — ashanti or uziza pepper — a
climbing native species whose fruits and leaves are widely used as a spice and
medicinally across the region. It is the local counterpart of *P. nigrum*, the
Asian black pepper, and was traded to Europe before the latter displaced it.

*Peperomia* — succulent epiphytes and rock plants — is one of the largest genera
of flowering plants worldwide, with well over a thousand species, so seven in the
region is a modest showing. Several are grown as houseplants.

Piperaceae belong to the **magnoliids**, outside both monocots and eudicots, and
share [[vol50]] with [[Hydrocharitaceae]]. Their nearest relatives in this corpus
are [[Aristolochiaceae]] (also Piperales) and the Magnoliales families of
[[vol10]] — [[Lauraceae]], [[Myristicaceae]] and [[Monimiaceae]].
