## Notes

***Bixa orellana* is annatto** — the seeds' orange-red arils are the source of
the food colouring **E160b**, used worldwide in cheese, butter and confectionery,
and one of the few natural colourants of major commercial scale. The pigment is
bixin, a carotenoid.

The species is Neotropical in origin and present in Africa through cultivation
and escape; it was carried across the Atlantic early and is now naturalised
widely. It is also long used as a body paint and dye — the source of the name
*roucou* and, indirectly, of "Redskin" applied to Amazonian peoples who used it.
