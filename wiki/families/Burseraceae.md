# Burseraceae

**Order**: Sapindales (APG IV placement)

## Diagnosis

Resinous trees or shrubs with alternate imparipinnate leaves and small 3- or 5-merous flowers. Stamens usually twice as numerous as petals and associated with a disc; ovaries 2–5-locular. *Aucoumea* has a dry five-valved capsule with winged seeds, while *Dacryodes*, *Canarium* and *Santiria* have drupes with diagnostic hard endocarps.

## Key

1. Fruit capsular with five winged seeds; flowers 5-merous; disc extrastaminal; (9–)10 stamens — [[Aucoumea]].
2. Fruit drupaceous.
   1. Stellate hairs present; flowers tomentose; endocarp with a shield-like mark — [[Dacryodes]].
   2. Stellate hairs absent.
      1. Large tree; leaves with 8–15 leaflet pairs and reduced basal lamellae; stone thick and 3-locular — [[Canarium]].
      2. Shrub or stilt-rooted tree; leaves with 2–4 leaflet pairs; fruit flattened and eccentric — [[Santiria]].
