# Scrophulariaceae

**Authority**: Juss.
**Order**: Lamiales
**Modern placement**: **dismantled — almost nothing here remains in
Scrophulariaceae s.s.**
**Name in source (FR)**: *Scrophulariacées*

Fifteen genera and 33 species. Of all the families in this corpus, **this is the
one whose circumscription has changed most drastically**.

## Notes

**Scrophulariaceae s.l. was one of the great polyphyletic assemblages**, and
molecular work broke it apart across Lamiales. On this page **not one genus stays
in Scrophulariaceae as now circumscribed** — the family retains *Scrophularia*,
*Verbascum*, *Buddleja* and relatives, none of which appear in this treatment.
The genera here fall into three groups:

- The **root hemiparasites** — *Striga*, *Alectra*, *Buchnera*, *Cycnium*,
  *Rhamphicarpa*, *Sopubia*, *Micrargeria* — go to **Orobanchaceae**, now
  circumscribed to hold all the parasitic Lamiales.
- The **Lindernia group** goes to **Linderniaceae**, a family erected for it.
- *Bacopa*, *Limnophila* and *Scoparia* go to **Plantaginaceae**.

***Striga* is the most economically damaging plant genus in African
agriculture.** The witchweeds parasitise sorghum, millet, maize and upland rice,
and cause losses across the Sahel and savanna zones on a scale no other genus in
this corpus approaches. Four species are recorded here.

*Craterostigma* is a **resurrection plant** genus, desiccation-tolerant and much
studied for that reason.
