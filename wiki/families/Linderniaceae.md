## Notes

**A second segregate of the old Scrophulariaceae.** The *Lindernia* group -- small herbs of wet ground and seasonally flooded rock -- sits apart from both the parasitic clade in [[Orobanchaceae]] and the *Bacopa* group in [[Plantaginaceae]], and is now recognised as its own family.

*Craterostigma* is the African resurrection-plant genus: it dries to a brittle crisp on shallow soil over rock and revives within hours of rain.
