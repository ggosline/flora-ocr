# Ternstroemiaceae

**Authority**: Mirb. · **Order**: Ericales
**Modern placement**: **Pentaphylacaceae** · **FR**: *Ternstroemiacées*

One genus, one species — *Ternstroemia*.

## Notes

*Ternstroemia* was long placed in Theaceae — the tea family — and then
segregated as Ternstroemiaceae. Current classification includes it in
**Pentaphylacaceae**, in Ericales, leaving Theaceae s.s. as a smaller family
centred on *Camellia*. So the name on this page has been superseded twice over,
and a modern source will list the species under Pentaphylacaceae.
