# Potamogetonaceae

**Authority**: Bercht. & J.Presl (1823) · **Order**: Alismatales

## Diagnosis

Glabrous annual or perennial aquatic herbs rooted in fresh or brackish substrates, with or without rhizomes. Leaves are alternate or subopposite, simple, sessile or petiolate, and submerged and floating leaves may differ. Flowers are bisexual or unisexual, with 3–4 tepals, 1–4 stamens and 1–8 free or basally united carpels. Fruits are apocarpous, indehiscent or opening by a lid; the embryo is curved.

## Distribution

The family has five genera and about 85 species. Three genera occur in tropical Africa; [[Potamogeton]] is the only genus recorded in Gabon. One species is confirmed there, while two lowland Cameroonian species may occur.
