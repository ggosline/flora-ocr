# Ulmaceae

**Authority**: Mirb.

**nothing on this page remains in the family named on it**.

## Notes

**Ulmaceae s.l. was split in two.** The elm family in the strict sense —
*Ulmus*, *Zelkova*, *Planera* — is temperate and absent from this region
entirely. The tropical genera formerly placed with it, *Celtis*, *Trema*,
*Aphananthe*, *Parasponia* and *Pteroceltis*, were found to belong instead with
*Cannabis* and *Humulus*, and are now **Cannabaceae**. So both genera in this
treatment, and therefore the whole of Gabon's "Ulmaceae", are Cannabaceae on
current classification.

The corpus already holds a [[Cannabaceae]] treatment in [[vol22]]; a modern
regional account combines the two.

***Celtis* is a significant forest timber genus** in the region — *C. mildbraedii*,
*C. adolfi-friderici* and *C. zenkeri* are traded, often as "ohia" or African
celtis. *Trema orientalis* is a fast-growing pioneer of clearings and roadsides,
one of the most widespread secondary-forest trees in the tropics.
