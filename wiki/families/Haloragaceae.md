# Haloragaceae

**Authority**: R.Br. (1814) · **Order**: Saxifragales

## Diagnosis

Small trees, shrubs or terrestrial and aquatic herbs, usually associated with fresh water. Leaves lack stipules and vary from entire to deeply divided. Flowers are 2–4-merous, bisexual or unisexual; the inferior ovary forms a nutlet, schizocarp or indehiscent fruit, often externally tuberculate, ribbed or winged.

## Distribution

Worldwide, with greatest diversity in Australia. Tropical Africa has *Laurembergia* and *Myriophyllum*. [[Laurembergia_tetrandra]] is confirmed in Gabon; *Myriophyllum spicatum* is recorded from Cameroon and could reach Gabon.
