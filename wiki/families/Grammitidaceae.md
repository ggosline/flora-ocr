# Grammitidaceae

**Authority**: Ching
**Genera in region**: 2 · **Species in region**: 2

## Diagnosis

Ferns, often epiphytic, small in size, with short, erect or creeping, solenostelic rhizomes bearing stiff scales that themselves bear hairs or sori. Fronds not articulate, with petioles often bearing stiff, unicellular hairs. Lamina entire to bipinnatifid with entire lobes, often pubescent, with monocellular, stiff hairs. Veins free in the African genera. Sori exindusiate, rounded or elliptic and superficial. Sporangia glabrous with pedicel formed of a single row of cells or bearing stiff hairs. Spores tetrahedral.

Formerly placed in Polypodium from which they differ by their anatomy, their scales and their spores.

Approximately 400 tropical species.

1. One vein, simple or bifurcate, per segment, and a single superficial sorus..... 1. Xiphopteris.

1'. Each segment contains pinnate veins; several sori per segment..... 2. Ctenopteris.
