**Authority**: Rich. ex DC.

corpus of a treatment whose family name a reader will not find in any current

## Notes

**Flacourtiaceae was found to be polyphyletic and has been abandoned.** Its
genera fall into two unrelated groups within Malpighiales. The larger part —
including *Homalium*, *Casearia*, *Flacourtia*, *Dovyalis* and *Oncoba* — belongs
with **Salicaceae**, the willow family, a placement that seems startling until
one notes that the salicoid leaf teeth and the floral structure agree. The
**cyanogenic** genera, which produce cyclopentenyl cyanogenic glycosides —
*Buchnerodendron*, *Dasylepis*, *Poggea*, *Trichostephanus* — go to
**Achariaceae**, the family that also contains *Hydnocarpus*, source of
chaulmoogra oil.

The practical consequence for this wiki: **every genus on this page is now
filed under a family name that does not appear in [[vol34]]**. The table above
is the mapping.

*Homalium* is a substantial timber genus in the region.
