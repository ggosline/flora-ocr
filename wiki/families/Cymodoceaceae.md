# Cymodoceaceae

**Authority**: Vines (1895) · **Order**: Alismatales

## Diagnosis

Glabrous perennial dioecious marine herbs with creeping rhizomes and intravaginal scales. Linear parallel-veined leaves have sheathing bases, auricles and an apical ligule. Flowers lack a perianth and are pollinated underwater; female flowers have two free one-ovulate ovaries, and the fruit is an indehiscent one-seeded nut.

## Distribution

Tropical, subtropical and sometimes Mediterranean seas. The family contains five genera and 16 species globally; [[Halodule]] and one species are documented in Gabon.
