# Lindsaeaceae

**Authority**: Ching
**Genera in region**: 1 · **Species in region**: 1

## Diagnosis

Terrestrial or epiphytic plants, with creeping, scaly rhizome, scales sometimes piliform. Frond articulate, pinnate-bipinnate, glabrous. Venation free, rarely loosely reticulate, without included veinlets.

Sori fused into a marginal coenosorus, opening outward, sometimes with a "false indusium" formed by the reflexed margin of the lamina. Sporangium with a stalk formed of 3 rows of cells, annulus formed of 10 cells. Spores without perispore. No paraphyses.

Tropical and subtropical regions.

A single genus in Gabon.
