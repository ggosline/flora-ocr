## Notes

**Split from Clusiaceae s.l.** Clusiaceae in the broad sense held three lineages; the *Calophyllum* group was raised to a family of its own, while *Garcinia*, *Allanblackia*, *Pentadesma* and *Symphonia* stayed in [[Clusiaceae]].

*Calophyllum* is a pantropical strand and forest timber genus; *Mammea* includes the mamey apple. *Endodesmia* is a monotypic West-Central African forest tree.
