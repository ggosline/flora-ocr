# Urticaceae

**Authority**: Juss.
**Order**: Rosales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Urticacées*

Six genera and 16 species as treated — herbs, shrubs and small trees, several
with stinging hairs.

## Notes

**This page understates the family, because Cecropiaceae now belongs here.**
[[Myrianthus]], [[Musanga]] and [[Cecropia]] are treated in [[vol26]] under
[[Moraceae]] — the volume is titled *Moracées (incl. Cécropiacées)* — but
Cecropiaceae has since been sunk into **Urticaceae**, not Moraceae. A modern
regional Urticaceae therefore comprises this treatment *plus* those three genera.

That matters ecologically as much as taxonomically: ***Musanga cecropioides***,
the umbrella tree, is the characteristic pioneer of Guineo-Congolian forest
regrowth, and on current classification it is the most conspicuous urticaceous
plant in the region — yet it appears nowhere in the volume that bears this
family's name.

*Urera* and *Laportea* carry stinging hairs; *Laportea* in particular includes
species with a painful sting. *Boehmeria nivea* is ramie, a fibre crop.
