# Phyllanthaceae

**Authority**: Martynov
**Order**: Malpighiales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Phyllanthacées*

Sixteen genera and 65 species in a modern Margraf volume (2012). **The family
name itself is the point of interest**: Phyllanthaceae is a segregate of
Euphorbiaceae s.l., recognised by APG, and vol 43 is one of the volumes where
Flore du Gabon adopts the modern circumscription rather than the traditional
broad Euphorbiaceae. A reader looking for these genera in older African floras
will find them under Euphorbiaceae.

## Notes

*Uapaca* is ecologically prominent in the region — stilt-rooted trees of
riverine and swamp forest, and a genus sometimes segregated as Uapacaceae.
