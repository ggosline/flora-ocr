## Notes

**Lobeliaceae is now subfamily Lobelioideae within Campanulaceae**, which is also
treated separately in this corpus ([[Campanulaceae]], [[vol40]]) — the two pages
are one family on current classification, and both are in the same volume.

***Dielsantha* is a monotypic genus endemic to West and Central Africa** —
*D. filiformis*, a slender herb of forest streamsides — and the most
floristically notable plant on this page.

*Hippobroma longiflora* is Neotropical in origin, now a pantropical weed, and
strongly poisonous; its latex is a serious eye irritant.

Lobelioideae are the group that produced the giant lobelias of the East African
mountains, though none occurs in Lower Guinea.
