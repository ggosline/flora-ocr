## Notes

***Cleome* now belongs to its own family, Cleomaceae.** Capparaceae s.l. proved
paraphyletic with respect to Brassicaceae, and the modern arrangement splits the
core Brassicales into three: **Capparaceae**, **Cleomaceae** and
**Brassicaceae** — with Cleomaceae sister to Brassicaceae. All three are
represented in this corpus, and two of them share [[vol30]], which makes that
volume a compact illustration of the split.

All these families produce **glucosinolates** (mustard oils), the biochemical
signature of Brassicales — shared here with [[Pentadiplandraceae]] and,
convergently and from an unrelated order, [[Putranjivaceae]].

*Buchholzia coriacea* — the "wooden banana" — has widely used medicinal seeds in
West and Central Africa.
