# Olacaceae

**Authority**: R.Br.
**Order**: Santalales (APG IV placement)
**Name in source (FR)**: *Olacacées*

A family of 23 genera, almost exclusively tropical. Eleven are represented in
tropical and subtropical Africa, and **all eleven occur in Gabon**, with 21
species — of which 10 genera and 20 species also reach Cameroon.

## Diagnosis

Trees, shrubs or lianas, monoecious or dioecious. Leaves alternate, entire,
exstipulate, simple, sometimes decussate, exceptionally opposite.
Inflorescences racemes, fascicles or solitary flowers, axillary or
extra-axillary. Flowers hermaphrodite, male or female, actinomorphic, tetra-,
penta- or rarely hexamerous, small. Calyx small and cupuliform in many genera,
sometimes absent. Petals valvate, free or united. Glands sometimes present
between the petals and the stamens. Stamens introrse, epipetalous or
alternipetalous, variable in number by genus, free or fused to the petals;
anthers 2-locular, opening by longitudinal slits or by valves. Disc often
present. Ovary superior to inferior, with 1–5 locules, each containing one
ovule pendulous from an erect central placenta; style ± long, ending in a
3-lobed stigma. Drupe one-seeded, often surrounded by the accrescent pedicel,
calyx or corolla. Embryo small and apical.

The recurring theme of the family in Gabon is that **some organ becomes
accrescent and wraps the fruit** — the disc in [[Anacolosa]], the calyx in
[[Olax]], [[Heisteria]], [[Aptandra]] and [[Ongokea]], the pedicel in
[[Strombosia]] and [[Strombosiopsis]]. Which organ, and how it closes over the
fruit, carries most of the generic key.

## Distribution

Pantropical, with 23 genera almost entirely confined to the tropics. Eleven
genera occur in tropical and subtropical Africa, all of them present in Gabon.

## Field characters

Villiers gathers a set of vegetative characters that assist determination
without flowers or fruit.

**Habit** — Liana: *Anacolosa*. Shrub: *Ximenia*, *Olax*, *Heisteria*,
*Aptandra*. Tree: *Strombosia*, *Heisteria*, *Ptychopetalum*, *Coula*,
*Diogoa*, *Strombosiopsis*, *Ongokea*.

**Bark slash** — pink: *Heisteria*; red: *Strombosiopsis*; whitish:
*Strombosia*; pinkish-red or reddish-brown: certain *Strombosia*; yellowish to
ochre turning reddish-brown on drying: *Ongokea*; grey-brown to orange with
orange-brown to blackish spots or dots: *Diogoa*.

**Branchlet form** — 2-winged: *Heisteria*, *Strombosia*, *Ptychopetalum*,
*Strombosiopsis*, *Ongokea*. 4-winged: *Olax*.

**Miscellaneous** — sticky reddish exudate: *Strombosiopsis*; white latex:
*Heisteria*; spines: *Ximenia*; hooks: *Anacolosa*; secondary nerves running to
the blade margin: *Coula*; russet pubescence: *Coula*.

## Treatments

### Vol 20 (1973)

**Author**: Jean-F. Villiers

Villiers treats the family broadly, in the pre-APG circumscription, covering

flowers, one on fruits — plus the list of vegetative field characters
reproduced above, which is unusually practical for a flora of this period.

## Notes

**Circumscription.** This treatment uses *Olacaceae* in a broad sense. Molecular
work since has dismembered the family, and most of the genera treated here are
now referred elsewhere within Santalales: [[Coula]] to Coulaceae; [[Heisteria]]
to Erythropalaceae; [[Strombosia]], [[Strombosiopsis]] and [[Diogoa]] to
Strombosiaceae; [[Anacolosa]], [[Aptandra]] and [[Ongokea]] to Aptandraceae;
[[Ximenia]] to Ximeniaceae. Only [[Olax]] and its immediate relatives remain in
*Olacaceae* s.str. The wiki keeps Villiers' circumscription, since that is what
the treatment documents, and records the modern placement on each genus page.

**Other families in the same source directory.** `Olacaceae_vol20_paddle`
physically contains four family treatments, because the OCR splitter could not
see the headings its own OCR had truncated: Olacaceae, [[Pentadiplandraceae]],
[[Opiliaceae]] and [[Octoknemaceae]]. Only Olacaceae is ingested here.
