# Schizaeaceae

**Authority**: Kaulfuss
**Genera in region**: 1 · **Species in region**: 2

## Diagnosis

Terrestrial ferns, with creeping or erect rhizome. Rachis with indefinite growth, or dichotomous fronds, with free venation. Sporangia on narrow lobes, protected by a false indusium, or without indusium, with a complete, distal annulus, opening vertically. Gametophyte cordate, symmetric or not.

Comprises 4 genera, apparently fairly distant from each other: Schizaea, Lygodium, Anemia, Mohria. The first 2 are pantropical, the genus Anemia is almost exclusively American. Mohria is South African and Malagasy. One genus only in Gabon: Lygodium.
