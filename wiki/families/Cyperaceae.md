# Cyperaceae

**Authority**: Juss.
**Order**: Poales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Cypéracées*

**The largest single-volume treatment in the corpus** — 182 species in 15 genera,
in a modern Margraf volume (2012). Sedges of marsh, savanna, forest floor and
disturbed ground; with Gramineae parked for later, this is the principal
graminoid treatment in the wiki.

***Cyperus* alone accounts for 75 species** — 41% of the family here, and one of
the largest genera anywhere in the corpus alongside *Salacia* (47) and *Ficus*
(41). *Mapania* (23) is the characteristic forest-floor sedge genus of the
Guineo-Congolian region.

## Notes

*Afrotrilepis* is of particular interest: a genus of the Cyperaceae with a woody,
fire-resistant habit, growing on bare inselberg rock — an unusual ecology for the
family and a characteristic element of West and Central African granite outcrops.
