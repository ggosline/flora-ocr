# Moraceae

**Authority**: Gaudich.
**Order**: Rosales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Moracées (incl. Cécropiacées)*

Thirteen genera and 74 species, with 16 infraspecific taxa — an unusually high
proportion, reflecting the detailed varietal treatment of *Dorstenia* and
*Ficus*. Written by **C.C. Berg**, M.E.E. Hijman and J.C.A. Weerdenburg; Berg
was the leading Moraceae specialist of the period, and this is among the more
authoritative treatments in the corpus.

## Notes

**The volume explicitly includes Cecropiaceae**, titled *Moracées (incl.
Cécropiacées)*. [[Myrianthus]], [[Musanga]] and [[Cecropia]] are treated here
under that heading. Cecropiaceae has since been **sunk into Urticaceae**, not
Moraceae — so those three genera are now in [[Urticaceae]], and a modern search
for *Musanga cecropioides* will not find it under Moraceae. This is the single
most likely thing to mislead a reader of this treatment.

*Musanga cecropioides* is the characteristic pioneer of Guineo-Congolian forest
regrowth, and *Milicia excelsa* (iroko) one of the region's most exploited
timbers — both worth their own pages.

**Taxonomic novelties published here**: *Dorstenia dinklagei* var. *reducta* and
var. *brieyi*, both new combinations by Hijman.
