## Notes

**Crassulaceae give their name to CAM photosynthesis** — Crassulacean Acid
Metabolism, in which stomata open at night to fix CO₂ as malic acid and close by
day, drastically reducing water loss. It was first characterised in this family
and has since been found in many unrelated succulent and epiphytic lineages,
including [[Cactaceae]] and numerous orchids.

*Kalanchoe* is centred on Madagascar and eastern Africa; two species in a
wet-forest region is a marginal showing, and they belong to rocky and seasonally
dry habitats. Several species reproduce by plantlets formed on the leaf margins,
which has made some of them persistent garden escapes worldwide.

The family sits in **Saxifragales**, alongside [[Peridiscaceae]] and
[[Haloragaceae]] in this corpus — an order otherwise mostly temperate.
