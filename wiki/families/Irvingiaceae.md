# Irvingiaceae

**Order**: Malpighiales (APG IV placement)

## Diagnosis

Large humid-forest trees with alternate simple entire leaves and caducous tapering stipules that enclose terminal buds and leave conspicuous annular scars. Flowers have five free sepals, five free reflexed petals, ten stamens inserted below a thick disc, and a two- or five-locular one-ovuled ovary. Fruits range from multi-stoned drupes in *Klainedoxa*, through one-stoned drupes in *Irvingia*, to long-winged samaras in *Desbordesia*. Wood is exceptionally heavy and hard.

## Key

1. Ovary 5-locular; style erect; fruit a flattened lobed drupe with (2–)5 stones; stipules 10 cm or longer — [[Klainedoxa]].
2. Ovary flat and 2-locular.
   1. Style erect; fruit an ellipsoid compressed one-stoned drupe; stipules to 3 cm — [[Irvingia]].
   2. Style bent from the base; fruit a long-winged 1–2-seeded samara; stipules about 1 cm and very caducous — [[Desbordesia]].
