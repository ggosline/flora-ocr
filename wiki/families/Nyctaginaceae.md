# Nyctaginaceae

Herbs or woody climbers with simple leaves, petaloid perianths and one-seeded fruits enclosed by the persistent perianth base. Vol. 7 treats native or naturalized [[Boerhaavia]] and cultivated [[Bougainvillea]].
