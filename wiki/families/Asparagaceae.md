## Notes

**Two of this flora's families are subfamilies here.** Asparagaceae in the APG sense is a broad family assembled from several small ones. [[Dracaenaceae]] became subfam. Nolinoideae and [[Hyacinthaceae]] subfam. Scilloideae; both are represented in Gabon.

*Dracaena* is by far the largest genus in the region, and now includes *Sansevieria*, which molecular work showed to be nested within it -- the mother-in-law's tongue of cultivation is a *Dracaena* on current classification, though this flora keeps the two apart. The Scilloideae genera are bulbous herbs of savanna and rock.
