# Cucurbitaceae

**Authority**: Juss.
**Order**: Cucurbitales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Cucurbitacées*

Eighteen genera and 42 species — tendrillar climbers of forest edge and
cultivation, and the family with the highest proportion of cultivated and
introduced taxa in the corpus.

## Notes

**Much of the family here is cultivated rather than wild.** *Cucurbita*
(pumpkins and squashes) is Neotropical in origin; *Citrullus* (watermelon),
*Lagenaria* (bottle gourd), *Luffa*, *Cucumis* and *Momordica charantia* are all
crops of long cultivation. *Cayaponia* is Neotropical. A regional flora of this
family therefore documents agricultural history as much as native vegetation.

***Telfairia occidentalis* is a significant West African crop** — the fluted
pumpkin or ugu, grown for its leaves and oil-rich seeds, and one of the more
important indigenous leaf vegetables of the region.

*Bambekea*, *Cogniauxia* and *Ruthalicia* are African forest endemics and the
most floristically interesting genera in the treatment.
