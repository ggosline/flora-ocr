# Vittariaceae

**Authority**: (Pr.) Ching
**Genera in region**: 2 · **Species in region**: 2

## Diagnosis

Epiphytic ferns, with creeping or erect rhizome, with clathrate scales. Fronds often pendulous, glabrous, simple, linear, oval, oboval, or orbicular, with reticulate venation, areoles without included veinlets. Sori elongate along the veins, exindusiate; paraphyses numerous. Sporangium with vertical annulus formed of 12–16 cells, with short stalk. Spores without perispore.

The two genera are distinguished as follows:

1. Sori forming a network along the veins; frond elliptic to suborbicular..... 1. Antrophyum.

1'. Sori marginal; frond linear..... 2. Vittaria.
