# Chrysobalanaceae

**Authority**: R.Br.
**Order**: Malpighiales (the source's period placed the family in Rosales)

## Notes

**The African *Acioa* are now *Dactyladenia*.** *Acioa* in the strict sense is
Neotropical; Prance and F. White transferred the African species to
**Dactyladenia**, so all 13 species in this treatment — the largest genus in the
volume — carry different names in current sources. This is the most consequential
name change on the page.

**The family has moved order.** Chrysobalanaceae was traditionally placed in
Rosales, near Rosaceae, on the strength of its perigynous flowers; molecular data
place it firmly in **Malpighiales**, close to Euphorbiaceae and Trigoniaceae. The
resemblance to Rosaceae is convergent.

*Licania* has since been extensively split in the Neotropics, though this does
not affect the single African species here. *Chrysobalanus icaco* is
pantropical on sandy coasts and is the type of the family.
