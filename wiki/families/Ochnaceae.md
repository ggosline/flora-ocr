# Ochnaceae

**Authority**: DC.
**Order**: Malpighiales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Ochnacées*

Seven genera and 57 species, by **Marc S.M. Sosef** (Botanic Garden Meise) and
**Pulchérie Bissiengou** — a modern Margraf treatment.

*Campylospermum* holds well over half the species. Many of its members were
formerly placed in *Ouratea*, so older African literature will have them under
that name.

## Notes

**Two monospecific genera here carry disproportionate economic weight.**
*Lophira alata* is **azobé** (ekki), one of the hardest and most exploited
timbers in West and Central Africa, used for marine and heavy construction work;
and *Testulea gabonensis* — **izombé** — is a Gabonese near-endemic and a
commercial timber. A family of otherwise modest shrubs and small trees is
therefore of real forestry significance in the region.

*Lophira* is sometimes segregated as Lophiraceae, and the Sauvagesia group as
Sauvagesiaceae; current treatments generally keep both within a broadly
circumscribed Ochnaceae.
