## Notes

***Smilax* is a monocot liana** — prickly, climbing by paired tendrils derived
from stipules, with net-veined leaves that look conspicuously dicot-like. Along
with Dioscoreaceae and *Gnetum* ([[Gnetaceae]]), it is one of the few climbers
in this flora whose broad reticulate venation misleads on first sight.

The genus is the source of **sarsaparilla**, though the commercial article comes
mainly from Neotropical species; African *Smilax* roots are used similarly in
local medicine.

Smilacaceae sit in **Liliales**, with [[Colchicaceae]] in this corpus.
