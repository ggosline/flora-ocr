## Notes

**The family has two genera in the world and they are on opposite sides of the
Atlantic.** *Lepidobotrys staudtii* is African; its only relative,
*Ruptiliocarpon caracolito*, is Central and South American and was not described
until 1993 — a large canopy tree that had escaped formal description entirely.
The pairing is one of the more striking amphi-Atlantic disjunctions among
flowering plants, and it went unrecognised for as long as the Neotropical half
was unknown.

The family sits in **Celastrales**, sister to the rest of the order, so despite
holding two species it represents a deep and isolated lineage.
