# Hypoxidaceae

**Order**: Asparagales (APG IV placement)  
**Name in source (FR)**: *Hypoxidacées*

## Diagnosis

Perennial herbs with solid bulbs or rhizomes and contractile roots. Leaves
radical, usually prominently veined and long-hairy. Flowers bisexual,
actinomorphic, with 6 free tepals yellow adaxially, and 6 stamens; ovary
inferior and 3-locular. Fruit a loculicidal capsule in [[Hypoxis]] or succulent
and indehiscent in [[Curculigo]].

| Genus | Species | Fruit |
|-------|---------|-------|
