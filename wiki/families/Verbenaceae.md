## Notes

**Verbenaceae was drastically reduced by the same work that enlarged Lamiaceae.**
Most of the woody genera long placed here — *Clerodendrum*, *Vitex*, *Premna*,
*Gmelina*, *Tectona* (teak) — are now **Lamiaceae**. The genera remaining in this
treatment are among those that genuinely stay in Verbenaceae, so unusually for
this corpus the page needs no redirection table; but a reader looking for
*Clerodendrum* or *Vitex* in the region should look to the volume's Labiatae
treatment and to Lamiaceae, not here.

***Lantana camara* is one of the world's worst invasive plants** — Neotropical in
origin, introduced ornamentally, now naturalised across the African tropics,
forming dense thickets that suppress regeneration and are toxic to livestock. It
appears here as a naturalised species rather than a native one.

*Stachytarpheta*, likewise Neotropical, is a widespread weed of roadsides and
clearings and the most species-rich genus in the treatment — again through
introduction.
