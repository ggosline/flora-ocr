# Vitaceae

**Authority**: Juss.
**Order**: Vitales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Vitacées*

Three genera and 21 species — tendril-climbing lianas, sharing [[vol14]] with
[[Leeaceae]].

## Notes

***Cissus* holds 16 of the 21 species** — succulent-stemmed to woody lianas,
climbing by leaf-opposed tendrils, and one of the characteristic liana genera of
Guineo-Congolian forest and forest edge.

**[[Leeaceae]], treated separately in the same volume, is now included in
Vitaceae** by most current classifications, as subfamily Leeoideae — *Leea* being
sister to the rest of the family. So vol 14's two families are now one, which
also explains why the volume paired them.

Vitaceae sits in its own order, **Vitales**, near the base of the rosids —
neither in Rhamnales, where older systems put it, nor close to any other family
in this corpus.

*Cayratia* has since been split, with several species moved to *Causonis*; the
single species here should be checked.
