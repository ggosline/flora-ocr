# Ixonanthaceae

**Authority**: Planch. ex Miq.
**Order**: Malpighiales
**Name in source (FR)**: *Ixonanthacées*

One genus, four species — the last of the nine families in [[vol21]].

## Genera in region

| Genus | Species |
|-------|---------|
| [[Ochthocosmus]] | 4 |

## Notes

Ixonanthaceae is a small pantropical family, long included within
[[Linaceae]] — with which it shares [[vol21]] — and separated on the basis of
its multi-ovulate locules and distinct wood anatomy. Its position in Malpighiales,
near Linaceae and Irvingiaceae, is now well established.

*Ochthocosmus* comprises trees and shrubs of forest and riverine habitats. The
African species have sometimes been segregated as *Phyllocosmus*, and older
regional literature may use that name for these four species.
