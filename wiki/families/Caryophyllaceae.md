# Caryophyllaceae

Mostly herbs with opposite simple leaves, cymose inflorescences, usually 5-merous flowers, a superior ovary and capsular or achene fruits. Vol. 7 records only [[Drymaria]] and [[Drymaria_cordata|*D. cordata*]] in Gabon.
