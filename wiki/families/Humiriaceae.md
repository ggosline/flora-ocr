# Humiriaceae

**Authority**: A.Juss. · **Order**: Malpighiales · **FR**: *Humiriacées*

One genus, one species — *Sacoglottis gabonensis*, in the nine-family [[vol21]].

## Notes

**Humiriaceae is a Neotropical family with exactly one African species**, and
this is it. Some fifty species occur in South and Central America; *Sacoglottis
gabonensis* alone reaches West and Central Africa, from Sierra Leone to Angola.
The disjunction is old — this is a Gondwanan relict rather than a recent
dispersal — and it makes a one-line entry in a Gabonese flora biogeographically
significant.

The tree is common in coastal and riverine forest. **Its bark is added to palm
wine** across West Africa, both as a preservative and for the bitter flavour and
reputed stimulant effect, and the timber is used locally.
