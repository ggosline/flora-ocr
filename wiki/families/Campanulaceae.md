## Notes

**This page and [[Lobeliaceae]] are one family**, and both are in [[vol40]].
Lobelioideae is now a subfamily of Campanulaceae, so the region's real
Campanulaceae is *Wahlenbergia* plus *Lobelia*, *Dielsantha* and *Hippobroma* —
four species in four genera across two chapters of the same volume.

*Wahlenbergia* is a largely southern African and Australasian genus of slender
bellflower herbs; its single species here belongs to open, higher ground rather
than to forest.
