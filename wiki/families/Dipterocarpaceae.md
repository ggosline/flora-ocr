## Notes

**This is the family that dominates Southeast Asian rainforest, reduced to a
single African species.** In Borneo, Sumatra and the Malay Peninsula
Dipterocarpaceae supply the great majority of canopy emergents and of the timber
trade; in Africa the family is represented only by the small subfamily
**Monotoideae** — *Monotes*, *Marquesia* and relatives — mostly in miombo
woodland rather than wet forest.

The contrast is one of the sharpest floristic differences between the world's
two great palaeotropical rainforest regions, and it is part of why West and
Central African forest has a different structure and timber profile from Asian
forest of comparable rainfall. That a Gabonese flora records a single species is
the whole point of the page.

African dipterocarp genera have been reshuffled repeatedly; the name used in the
source should be checked against a current treatment of Monotoideae.
