# *Hypoxis* L.

**Family**: [[Hypoxidaceae]]

Solid globose to elongate bulb with roots in an equatorial zone; leaves
linear. Flowers in a corymb, rarely solitary. Tepals not separated from the
ovary by a beak. Fruit hairy and loculicidally dehiscent; testa papillose;
strophiole absent.
