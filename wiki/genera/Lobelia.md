# *Lobelia* L.

**Family**: [[Lobeliaceae]]
**Modern family**: [[Campanulaceae]] subfam. Lobelioideae — *Lobeliaceae* as circumscribed here is superseded; see [[Lobeliaceae]]
**Authority**: L.
**Protologue**: Sp. pl . 2 : 929 (1753)

## Diagnosis

Herbs, shrubs, subshrubs, or giant rosette plants. Leaves alternate. Flowers in racemes or solitary and axillary; calyx with 5 lobes, often unequal; corolla with dorsal fissure almost or to the base, generally 2-bilabiate with the upper lip 2-lobed and the lower 3-lobed; stamens 5, free from the corolla or slightly adnate towards their base, anthers fused into a tube, the two lower ones or all bearing hairs at their apex, the two lower ones slightly shorter than the others; ovary 2-locular; stigma with 2 short and broad lobes, surrounded by a ring of hairs. Fruit with dehiscence in 2 apical valves, or sometimes indehiscent. Seeds generally numerous.

Genus comprising 400 species distributed throughout the world, most in tropical and subtropical regions; in Gabon, only one species.

Taxonomic notes: Recent phylogenetic analyses based on molecular data indicate that the two genera Dielsantha and Hippobroma are grouped within Lobelia sensu lato. Consequently, changes in the delimitation of these genera may be expected.
