# *Utricularia* L.

**Family**: [[Lentibulariaceae]]
**Authority**: L.
**Protologue**: Sp. pl. 1 : 18 (1753)

## Diagnosis

Carnivorous herbs, annual or perennial, terrestrial, epiphytic or aquatic; rhizoids (stems transformed to function as roots; absent in Gabon) generally arising at the base of the floral scapes, filiform; stolons also arising at the base of the floral scapes, generally short and delicate, but sometimes developing into fleshy tubers in terrestrial and epiphytic species, more robust and longer in aquatic species. Leaves in rosette at the base of the floral scapes (U. andongensis, U. simulans), alternate, opposite or whorled on the stolons, erect or capillary, entire, linear, orbicular or peltate or ± pseudodichotomously divided into capillary segments (aquatic species). Floats (spongious leaves modified at the base of the stem) present in floating aquatic species. Utricles (traps) globose or ovoid, stipitate, with a basal, lateral or terminal orifice (opposite the petiole), furnished externally with two lips bearing various appendages.

Inflorescences racemose, often elongate, rarely short and subcapitate; peduncle simple, sometimes branched, erect or twining (coiling around a support), generally filiform, glabrous, sometimes papillose, glandular or hairy; bracts persistent, basifixed or decurrent below their point of insertion or peltate, the lower ones sterile, scale-like. Flowers: bracteoles 0 or 2, sometimes ± united with the bract, inserted with the bract at the base of the pedicel, rarely at the apex of the pedicel; pedicel cylindric, flattened or ± winged, often reflexed or curved at maturity; calyx with 2 sepals ± equal or sometimes very unequal, free or sometimes ± united at the base, rarely dentate or fimbriate; corolla glabrous, glandular or hairy, with closed or sometimes open throat, upper lip ± erect and with limb entire, emarginate or bilobed, lower lip larger, furnished with a spur or rarely with a basal sac, with palate prominent and gibbous, with limb spreading or deflexed, entire, emarginate or ± deeply 2- to 5-lobed; stamens with filaments straight or curved, twisted, sometimes winged, anthers with theca ± confluent; ovary spheric to ovoid, placenta basal or central, ± fleshy, ovules 2 to numerous; style short, often indistinct, persistent. Fruits ± spheric, dehiscent by longitudinal slits, by pores, circumscissile or indehiscent, with 1 to numerous seeds. Seeds

## Key to the species

**1.** Terrestrial or epiphytic herb; leaf entire and subulate, linear or spatulate (but often detached and absent from the base of peduncles on herbarium specimens); bracteoles present, sometimes absent, but in that case bracts medifixed → couplet **2**

**1′.** Aquatic herb; leaf divided into capillary segments; bracteoles absent; bracts basifixed → couplet **11**

**2.** Bracteoles absent; bracts medifixed; flower with yellow corolla, sometimes cleistogamous → [[Utricularia_subulata\|*U. subulata*]]

**2′.** Bracteoles present; flower with yellow or blue-violet corolla, generally not cleistogamous → 3 B A C

**3.** Bracts and bracteoles basifixed or slightly decurrent below their point of insertion 4

**3′.** Bracts and bracteoles medifixed → couplet **10**

**4.** Bracteoles deeply fimbriate-dentate, inserted at the apex of the pedicel → [[Utricularia_simulans\|*U. simulans*]]

**4′.** Bracteoles entire or 3-dentate at the apex, inserted at the base of the pedicel → couplet **5**

**5.** Bracts at most 2 times as wide as the bracteoles; pedicel cylindric, ordinarily shorter than the fruiting calyx; corolla white or lilac with a yellow patch on the palate → couplet **6**

**5′.** Bracts at least 4 times as wide as the bracteoles; pedicel flattened or winged and as long or longer than the fruiting calyx; corolla blue-violet, mauve or yellow → couplet **7**

**6.** Leaf peltate, orbicular; bracts and bracteoles slightly decurrent below their point of insertion; lower calyx lobe with acute apex, slightly bifid; calyx pubescent or glabrous → [[Utricularia_pubescens\|*U. pubescens*]]

**6′.** Leaf not peltate, narrowly obovate to narrowly spatulate; bracts and bracteoles basifixed; lower calyx lobe with obtuse or rounded apex; calyx always glabrous → [[Utricularia_arenaria\|*U. arenaria*]]

**7.** Seed spheric, with testa having isodiametral cells; corolla generally blue-violet or mauve → couplet **8**

**7′.** Seed ovoid, with testa having elongate cells; corolla yellow → couplet **9**

**8.** Corolla 12–30 mm long, with upper lip longer and 2 to 3 times as wide as the upper calyx lobe → [[Utricularia_spiralis\|*U. spiralis*]]

**8′.** Corolla < 10 mm long, with upper lip not exceeding or scarcely exceeding the upper calyx lobe in length and width → [[Utricularia_tortilis\|*U. tortilis*]]

**9.** Seed smooth; leaves 1-nerved, few, opposite on branches on the stolons, linear, reaching 10 mm long; corolla reaching 15 mm long → [[Utricularia_capillacea\|*U. capillacea*]]

**9′.** Seed verrucose; leaves 3- to plurinerved, in rosette, almost always present at anthesis, reaching 60 mm long; corolla < 10 mm long → [[Utricularia_andongensis\|*U. andongensis*]]

**10.** Plant epiphytic (not in Gabon) or lithophytic, erect; corolla with lower lip 5-lobed; seed ovoid, glochidiate; leaves reniform, always present at anthesis → [[Utricularia_striatula\|*U. striatula*]]

**10′.** Plant terrestrial, twining; corolla with lower lip subentire; seed narrowly cylindric, papillose; leaves linear, generally absent at anthesis → [[Utricularia_appendiculata\|*U. appendiculata*]]

**11.** Inflorescence furnished at the base or above the base of the peduncle with a whorl of inflated floats; corolla yellow, pink or white marked with purple or red lines → couplet **12**

**11′.** Inflorescence lacking a whorl of inflated floats; corolla yellow → couplet **14**

**12.** Stolons and utricles hairy; corolla mauve or pale purple, marked with purple or red lines and with a yellow patch on the palate, or completely white, with spur inflated, longer than the lower lip; calyx much smaller than the fruit → [[Utricularia_benjaminiana\|*U. benjaminiana*]]

**12′.** Stolons and utricles glabrous or sometimes with a few hairs; corolla yellow or white, with spur shorter or ± the same length as the lower lip; calyx as large or larger than the fruit → couplet **13**

**13.** Corolla yellow; floats narrowly ellipsoidal, 2 to 4 times as long as wide; scales of the leaf bases divided into hispid linear segments; fruiting calyx with lobes reflexed exposing the fruit; seed 2 to 3 times as wide as long → [[Utricularia_stellaris\|*U. stellaris*]]

**13′.** Corolla white or yellow, marked with variable red or purple lines; floats cylindric-fusiform, 6 to 8 times longer than wide; scales of the leaf bases denticulate; fruiting calyx with lobes erect hiding the fruit; seed about as long as wide → [[Utricularia_inflexa\|*U. inflexa*]]

**14.** Utricle inserted at the fork of the leaf segment → [[Utricularia_reflexa\|*U. reflexa*]]

**14′.** Utricle inserted laterally on the leaf segment → couplet **15**

**15.** Leaf divided abundantly into numerous capillary segments, the ultimate ones setulose; stolon flattened, with internodes 3–10 cm; inflorescence 3- to 20-flowered; seed with narrow and entire wing → [[Utricularia_foliosa\|*U. foliosa*]]

**15′.** Leaf divided 2 or 3 times only, the ultimate segments not setulose; stolon cylindric, with internodes reaching 2 cm; inflorescence 1- to 6-flowered → [[Utricularia_gibba\|*U. gibba*]]
