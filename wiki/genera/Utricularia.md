# *Utricularia* L.

**Family**: [[Lentibulariaceae]]
**Authority**: L.
**Protologue**: Sp. pl. 1 : 18 (1753)

## Diagnosis

Carnivorous herbs, annual or perennial, terrestrial, epiphytic or aquatic; rhizoids (stems transformed to function as roots; absent in Gabon) generally arising at the base of the floral scapes, filiform; stolons also arising at the base of the floral scapes, generally short and delicate, but sometimes developing into fleshy tubers in terrestrial and epiphytic species, more robust and longer in aquatic species. Leaves in rosette at the base of the floral scapes (U. andongensis, U. simulans), alternate, opposite or whorled on the stolons, erect or capillary, entire, linear, orbicular or peltate or ± pseudodichotomously divided into capillary segments (aquatic species). Floats (spongious leaves modified at the base of the stem) present in floating aquatic species. Utricles (traps) globose or ovoid, stipitate, with a basal, lateral or terminal orifice (opposite the petiole), furnished externally with two lips bearing various appendages.

Inflorescences racemose, often elongate, rarely short and subcapitate; peduncle simple, sometimes branched, erect or twining (coiling around a support), generally filiform, glabrous, sometimes papillose, glandular or hairy; bracts persistent, basifixed or decurrent below their point of insertion or peltate, the lower ones sterile, scale-like. Flowers: bracteoles 0 or 2, sometimes ± united with the bract, inserted with the bract at the base of the pedicel, rarely at the apex of the pedicel; pedicel cylindric, flattened or ± winged, often reflexed or curved at maturity; calyx with 2 sepals ± equal or sometimes very unequal, free or sometimes ± united at the base, rarely dentate or fimbriate; corolla glabrous, glandular or hairy, with closed or sometimes open throat, upper lip ± erect and with limb entire, emarginate or bilobed, lower lip larger, furnished with a spur or rarely with a basal sac, with palate prominent and gibbous, with limb spreading or deflexed, entire, emarginate or ± deeply 2- to 5-lobed; stamens with filaments straight or curved, twisted, sometimes winged, anthers with theca ± confluent; ovary spheric to ovoid, placenta basal or central, ± fleshy, ovules 2 to numerous; style short, often indistinct, persistent. Fruits ± spheric, dehiscent by longitudinal slits, by pores, circumscissile or indehiscent, with 1 to numerous seeds. Seeds
