# *Milicia* Sim

**Family**: [[Moraceae]]
**Authority**: Sim
**Protologue**: For. Fl. For. Port. E. Afr.: 97 (1909)

## Diagnosis

Dioecious trees. Leaves distichous; blade with pinnate venation. Stipules free, not quite amplexicaul.

Inflorescences generally solitary in the leaf axils or on defoliate nodes at the base of fertile branches; inflorescences spicate, pedunculate. Flowers numerous packed in alternating longitudinal rows and rows of bracts attached basally; presence of a sterile abaxial groove. Perianth of staminate flowers quadripartite, with imbricate segments; stamens 4, inflexed, flexibly curved outward at anthesis, with introrse anthers; pistillode quadrangular or flattened and bidentate. Perianth of pistillate flowers quadripartite with decussate-imbricate segments, of the same form 2 by 2; ovary free; stigmas 2, one of the 2 generally ± reduced or absent.

Perianth enlarged and somewhat thickened in fruit. Fruit stipitate and slightly compressed; exocarp thin, fleshy, thicker at the base and on the seed side, indehiscent; endocarp crustaceous with a small woody stopper toward the hilum; testa thin with an ovate vascularized zone slightly thickened toward the hilum; albumen present; embryo curved; cotyledons small, flat, equal, not enclosing the long radicle.

TYPE SPECIES: Milicia excelsa (Welw.) C.C. Berg.

The genus Milicia comprises 2 taxonomically closely related species: the widely distributed species M. excelsa and the West African species M. regia (A. CHEV.) C.C. BERG. These two species known as Chlorophora excelsa and C. regia were included in the large genus Maclura by CORNER (1962). Additional studies on Maclura and its affinities (publications in preparation) prove that the two species constitute a separate genus.
