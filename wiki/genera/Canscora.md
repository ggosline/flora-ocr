# *Canscora* Lam.

**Family**: [[Gentianaceae]]
**Authority**: Lam.
**Protologue**: Encycl. 1 : 601 (1785)

## Diagnosis

Annual herbs, erect; stems quadrangular, often strongly 4-winged. Leaves opposite, sessile; lamina with 3 principal veins. Inflorescences axillary, in dichasial cymes. Flowers zygomorphic, 4-merous; calyx with cylindrical tube, much longer than the lobes, lobes winged on the back, without scales at the internal base; corolla funnel-shaped, with 2 lobes smaller than the 2 others; stamens 4, exserted, inserted at different heights, unequal, one fertile, three sterile, anthers sagittate; ovary 1-locular; stigma 2-lobed. Fruit cylindrical. Seeds cubic to rectangular; testa reticulate, with polygonal cells.

Tropical genus comprising nine species from tropical Asia, Australia and two in Africa; a single species in Gabon.
