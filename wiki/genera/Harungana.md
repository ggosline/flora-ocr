# *Harungana* Lam.

**Family**: [[Hypericaceae]]
**Authority**: Lam.
**Protologue**: Tab. encycl. 2, 4 : t. 645 (1796)

## Diagnosis

Shrubs or trees; orange latex. Leaves opposite, without stipules; lamina with translucent glandular punctuations and stellate hairs. Inflorescences terminal, cymose.

Flowers: sepals 5, persistent, glandular; petals 5, caducous, pubescent on the inner face, glandular; staminal phalanges 5, with (3–)4 stamens, filaments partially free, staminodes fleshy, scale-shaped, alternating with the phalanges; ovary 5-locular, with 2–4 ovules per locule; styles 5, free or ± connate at the base. Fruits drupaceous, with 5 crustaceous pyrenes fused into a spherical mass ornamented with numerous black glands and longitudinal lines and surrounded by a fleshy envelope. Seeds ellipsoidal, with isocotylous embryo; cotyledons plano-convex, accumbent.

Genus with 2 species from tropical Africa, Madagascar and the Mascarenes; in Gabon a single species.

Bi: Bamps (1966, 1970), Milne-Redhead (1953).

Shrub or evergreen tree, up to 12(–27) m tall; latex yellow-orange or reddish; bark scaly; young branches densely pubescent, then glabrescent. Leaf: petiole 2–27(–35) mm, pubescent; lamina triangular-ovate to ovate-elliptic or rarely narrowly ovate-elliptic, 5–22(–29) × 3–12(–15) cm, broadly cuneate to subcordate at the base, acute to shortly acuminate at the apex, dark green glossy and glabrous to glabrescent above, often brownish beneath and with tomentose or glabrescent indumentum; lateral veins 10–16(–25) pairs. Inflorescence many-flowered, reaching 20 × 25(–30) cm in diameter; peduncle 2.5–9 cm, tomentose to glabrescent. Flower with sweet scent; pedicel 2–3 mm, tomentose; sepals ovate-elliptic, ± 2 × 1 mm, tomentose externally, with lines or glandular punctuations internally; petals ovate-elliptic, 3–4 × 1–1.5 mm, white to greenish-white, long-pubescent within, with 2–4 black glands towards the apex; staminodes ± 0.5 mm in height; phalanges 3 mm in length, anthers ovoid, 0.3 mm in length; ovary spherical, glabrous, with several black longitudinal glandular lines; styles 1–2 mm in length, stigmas capitate. Fruit globose, 3–6 mm in diameter, yellow to orange or red; pericarp crustaceous. Seed 2 × 0.5 mm, finely reticulate, reddish-brown.
