# *Xanthocercis* Baill.

**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Authority**: Baill.
**Protologue**: Adansonia 9 : 203 (1870)

## Diagnosis

Large tree, in flower with the leaves. Stipules linear. Leaves imparipinnate; stipels absent; leaflets (1–)5 to 11(–13), alternate or subopposite, the lateral ones with blade slightly oblique at the base. Inflorescences in terminal or axillary pseudoracemes, often grouped in panicles; bracts caducous, small. Flowers: bracteoles 2, caducous; calyx campanulate, pubescent, with small or absent teeth; petals 5, free, pink or whitish, narrow, clawed, the standard somewhat broader than the others, similar, not forming a keel; stamens 10, diadelphous, similar, the vexillary one united at the base, anthers ellipsoid, medifixed; disk present; ovary shortly stipitate, pubescent; style glabrous, stigma not enlarged. Legumes drupaceous, indehiscent, ellipsoid if with a single seed, cylindric and moniliform with two or three, with fibrous pulp. Seeds large, reniform to subrectangular, black; hilum central, bosselated.

Genus of 3 species, one in southern Africa, a second in Madagascar and the last endemic to Gabon.

Notes: Genus related to Angylocalyx, in the tribe Sophoreae (Lewis et al., 2005).

B: Brummitt (2007b), Lewis et al. (2005), Povydysh et al. (2010), van der Maesen (1997).
