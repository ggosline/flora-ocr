# *Arachis* L.

**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Authority**: L.
**Protologue**: Sp. pl. 2 :741 (1753)

## Diagnosis

Annual or perennial herbs, rather low, often trailing. Stipules linear-lanceolate, acute, nerved, ciliate, with base ± long adnate to the petiole. Leaves paripinnate; petiole flattened above, with ciliate margins; stipels absent; leaflets (3–)4(–5), opposite; lateral veins parallel and numerous. Inflorescences axillary, in few-flowered spikes or solitary flowers, upper flowers sterile, the lower ones somewhat smaller and fertile. Flower: bracteoles absent; calyx with very slender tube, standard rounded, unguiculate, wings oblong, free, keel incurved; stamens (8–)10, monadelphous, filaments alternately short and long; ovary with 2 to 4 ovules, at first ± sessile, then producing a long gynophore (stipe) after fertilization. Legumes penetrating the soil, woody, torulous to cylindrical, with 1 to 3(–4) seeds, with reticulate valves. Seeds ellipsoid; cotyledons oleaginous and rich in proteins.

Genus of 69 species, distributed especially in South America, of which one is cultivated in all tropical and subtropical regions, including Gabon.

Notes: The wild species of South America have underground legumes with one-seeded articles separated by long constrictions.
