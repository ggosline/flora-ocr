# *Salvinia* Adanson

**Family**: [[Pteridophytes]]
**Authority**: Adanson

## Diagnosis

Small aquatic ferns, floating. Floating stem, bearing at each node 2 rows of dorsal leaves, opposite in pairs, spreading, of unequal size, with entire lamina, folded into a channel along the principal vein, bearing papillose hairs and a submerged organ (submerged leaf of earlier authors); this organ is in reality a system composed of axes of roots and fertile leaves bearing the sori, arising in pairs along the filaments; the macrosorus contains several megasporangia with a single megaspore; the microsporangia contain 32 or 64 microspores.

Type species: Salvinia natans (L.) Adanson.

Approximately 10 species, from tropical Africa and America especially. One in intertropical Africa.
