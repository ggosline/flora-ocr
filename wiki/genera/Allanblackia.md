# *Allanblackia* Oliv.

**Family**: [[Clusiaceae]]
**Authority**: Oliv.

## Diagnosis

in Benth. & Hook.f, Gen. pl. 1 : 980 (1867).

Dioecious trees, with yellow to transparent latex; branchlets fistulose. Stipules present, caducous. Leaves opposite; blade provided with secretory canals visible on the lower surface. Inflorescences terminal, in racemes or panicles with strongly reduced secondary axes, sometimes one to few pseudoaxillary flowers on short lateral branchlets. Flowers unisexual, glabrous; sepals 5, free, imbricate, unequal; petals 5, imbricate. Male flowers: stamens numerous, grouped in 5 phalanges, anthers subsessile, arranged in several superposed ranks on the inner surface or on both surfaces of the phalange; disk stellate, consisting of 5 glands alternating with the phalanges; ovary absent. Female flowers: rudimentary staminal phalanges, alternating with 5 free discal glands; ovary with 5 incomplete locules and 5 parietal placentas, ovules 2–16 per locule, arranged in 2 ranks; stigma peltate, sessile or subsessile. Fruit: a berry. Seeds arillate, rich in fatty substances.

Genus comprising about ten species from tropical Africa, three of which are represented in Gabon.

B: Bamps (1969, 1970a), Bamps et al. (1978), Stevens (2007).
