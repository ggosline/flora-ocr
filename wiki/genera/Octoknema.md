# *Octoknema* Pierre

**Family**: [[Octoknemaceae]]

Bull. Soc. Linn. Paris **2**: 1290 (1897).

Type species: ***O. klaineana*** Pierre.

<!-- Vol 20 gives the type species as O. affinis Pierre. Gosline & Malecot
(2011: 377) state that Villiers cites this wrongly: Pierre described the genus
on a single species, O. klaineana, which is therefore the type. The authority
for O. affinis is also van Tieghem, not Pierre. -->

Villiers recognises 6 very closely allied species, four of them in Cameroon and
Gabon, and stresses how narrowly they are separated — his key runs almost
entirely on leaf pubescence and leaf-base shape.

**Revised in 2011.** Gosline & Malécot (Kew Bull. 66: 367–404) monograph the
genus and recognise **14 species**, six of them new, plus four poorly known taxa
treated as *Octoknema* sp. A–D. Their account supersedes Villiers on every
point that follows.

**Gabon has six species, not four.** Villiers listed *O. genovefae*,
*O. klaineana*, *O. dinklagei* and *O. affinis* for Cameroon and Gabon jointly.
Under the revision:

- ***O. dinklagei*** is **endemic to coastal Cameroon** between Douala and
  Campo — it does not occur in Gabon.
- ***O. genovefae*** is **Cameroonian** (river basins from Yaoundé to the coast,
  possibly Rio Muni) — it does not occur in Gabon.
- ***O. affinis*** Tiegh. and ***O. klaineana*** Pierre do occur in Gabon.
- Gabon additionally has ***O. aruwimiensis*** Mildbr. and the three species new
  in 2011: ***O. belingensis***, ***O. chailluensis*** and ***O. ogoouensis***.

Species A–D are Cameroonian, Congolese and West African; none is Gabonese.

## Diagnosis

Tree or shrubs with pubescent young branchlets. Blade with stellate pubescence
or glabrous. Inflorescence a many-flowered raceme or a raceme of fascicles.
Male flowers pentamerous, ± gamopetalous and **without a calyx**. Stamens with
longitudinal dehiscence slits. Pistillode present. Female flowers pentamerous,
gamopetalous and without a calyx. Staminodes 5, epipetalous. Disc reduced.
Ovary inferior, tri- or quadrilocular with 1 ovule per locule. Style thick, the
stigma forming a plate. Drupe ovoid to subglobose, 1.5–3 cm. Endocarp woody,
emitting digitations on its inner face. Embryo apical or subapical.

## Key to the Gabonese species

1. Blade densely pubescent on the lower face
    - 2\. Blade sparsely pubescent above; leaf base peltate; male inflorescence
      a raceme of spaced panicles → **[[Octoknema_genovefa|1. O. genovefa]]**
    - 2'. Blade glabrous above; leaf base rounded; male inflorescence a dense
      raceme of fascicles → **[[Octoknema_klaineana|2. O. klaineana]]**
1'. Blade glabrous or very sparsely pubescent on the lower face
    - 3\. Midrib, fruit and branchlets glabrous; leaf base cuneate; petiole
      glabrous → **[[Octoknema_dinklagei|3. O. dinklagei]]**
    - 3'. Midrib, fruit and branchlets pubescent; leaf base attenuate or
      rounded; petiole pubescent → **[[Octoknema_affinis|4. O. affinis]]**

## Notes

Villiers records that Hutchinson's *Families of Flowering Plants* publishes a
figure, drawn by the author, of an "*Octoknema gabonensis* Pierre". He could
find no trace of that binomial in Pierre's publications on the Octoknemaceae,
and concludes the plate undoubtedly represents
[[Octoknema_klaineana|O. klaineana]] Pierre.

Later classifications place *Octoknema* in **Erythropalaceae**.
