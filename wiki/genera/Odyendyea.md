# Odyendyea

**Authority**: (Pierre) Engl. (1896) · **Family**: [[Simaroubaceae]]

## Diagnosis

Trees with glabrous imparipinnate leaves. Flowers usually 4-merous; petals imbricate, stamens twice as numerous as the petals and furnished with woolly basal ligules; disc cushion-shaped; four carpels free but appressed, their styles united. Fruit a large drupe with a thick, longitudinally crested stone.
