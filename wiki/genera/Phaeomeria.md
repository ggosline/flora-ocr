# Phaeomeria
Perennial rhizomatous herbs with separate leafy and flowering shoots, dense heads enclosed by brightly coloured waxy bracts, and clustered berry-like fruits. The genus is now generally included in *Etlingera*.
## Species treated
[[Phaeomeria_magnifica|*P. magnifica*]] — an Indonesian ornamental introduced and widely cultivated in Gabon.
