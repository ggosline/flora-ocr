# *Guaduella* Franch.

**Family**: [[Gramineae]]
**Authority**: Franch.
**Protologue**: FRANCHET, Bull. Soc. Linn. Paris : 676 (1887)

## Diagnosis

Herbaceous plants with leafy culms often leafy only toward the apex, the base of the stem surrounded by aphyllous sheaths. Leaves oval-lanceolate to linear-lanceolate, contracted into a petiole at the base. Inflorescences terminal, on leafy culms or on distinct aphyllous culms, in racemes or more or less divided panicles. Spikelets flattened with numerous flowers, the 1 to 3 lower ones male, the others hermaphrodite. Two glumes generally pubescent, at the base of the spikelet, the lower one generally shorter. Lower glumelle coriaceous, with numerous veins. Upper glumelle thin, bicarinate with winged keels, generally pubescent; 3 oval lodicules, truncate at the apex. Six stamens with free filaments. Ovary oblong, pubescent, styles distinct almost to the base. About 8 species, most from Gabon and Cameroon, one distributed as far as Liberia. A number of species are known so far only from Cameroon; their presence in Gabon is however probable, which is why they will be mentioned in the key below. 1. Leafy stems and fertile stems distinct.

2. Sterile culms with 1 or 2 leaves only; fertile culms generally with only 3 spikelets, each with 8 flowers..... G. Ledermannii Pilger (Cameroon). 2'. More numerous leaves and spikelets at the apex of the culms.

3. Short and dense inflorescence, spikelets with 4 or 5 flowers; palea longer than the lemma..... G. densiflora Pilger (Cameroon). 3'. Spiciform inflorescence elongated, the lower branches alone ramified; spikelets with numerous flowers; lemmas and paleas of the same length, or the former slightly longer. 1. G. foliosa.

1'. Inflorescence at the apex of the leafy culms.

4. Leaves very elongated, 5 times as long as wide or more; inflorescence elongated with distant spikelets..... 2. G. longifolia. 4'. Leaves oval, elliptic or more or less elongated, but less than above. 5. Leaves few, 2 or 3 (sometimes up to 5), densely grouped at the apex of the culms; inflorescence paniculate relatively dense; spikelets generally 3 to 4 cm long..... 3. G. marantifolia 6. Leaves 15 to 20 cm long and ± 8 cm wide, most often in pairs, attenuate at the base..... var. Duparqueti. 6'. Leaves 8 to 10 cm long, generally in threes, sometimes up to 5, rounded, to subcordate at the base. var. brevifolia. 5'. Leaves more numerous, distributed along the culms, or more or less grouped toward the apex.
