# Impatiens

**Authority**: Riv. ex L. (1735) · **Family**: [[Balsaminaceae]]

## Diagnosis

Succulent terrestrial or epiphytic herbs with alternate or whorled toothed leaves. Strongly zygomorphic flowers have two small lateral sepals, a large spurred lower sepal forming the lip, a dorsal standard and paired lateral wings. Five stamens form a cap over the ovary. The fusiform capsule opens elastically into five coiling valves and projects its seeds.
