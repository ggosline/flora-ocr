# *Strombosia* Blume

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]] *sensu lato* · **Current placement**: Strombosiaceae

Bijdr.: 1154 (1826).

**Synonyms**: *Lavallea* Baill., Adansonia, sér. 1, **2**: 361 (1862);
*Lavalleopsis* Van Tiegh., Bull. Soc. Bot. Fr. **43**: 565 (1896);
*Comoneura* Pierre (= *Cosm(i)oneuron* Pierre), Bull. Soc. Linn. Paris **2**:
1293 (1897).

Type species: *S. javanica* Blume.

A tropical genus of 15 species: 8 in Africa and 7 in tropical Asia. **All the
African species have an inferior or semi-inferior ovary, whereas that of the
Asian species is superior** — a clean geographical split noted by Villiers.
Four species occur in Gabon.

## Diagnosis

Tall trees. Young branchlets winged along 2 opposite lines. Petiole strongly
wrinkled above. Blade glossy or matt above. Inflorescence a few- to
several-flowered fascicle. Pedicel widened towards the apex. Flowers female,
pentamerous. Calyx of 5 membranous, fused sepals, ciliolate on the upper
margin. Petals united in bud, free in the open flower, glabrous within,
pubescent on the upper third of the inner face, papillose at the apex. Stamens
5, epipetalous, fused to the petals by the filament; anther 2-locular. Disc
present. Ovary semi-inferior or superior, with 1–5 uniovulate locules. Placenta
central, erect, with pendulous ovules. Fruit with a **strongly accrescent
pedicel** enclosing it almost to the apex; the apical depression bordered by
the remains of the calyx forming 5 teeth or lobes. Seed solitary, the albumen
starchy. Embryo small, apical.

**2.** Upper surface of limb with granular aspect when dry → [[Strombosia_pustulata\|*S. pustulata*]] ^k2

**3.** Upper surface matt; nervilles indistinct → var. pustulata. ^k3

**3′.** Upper surface shiny; nervilles distinct → var. lucida.

**2′.** Upper surface of limb smooth.

**4.** Floral bud ellipsoid; pedicel striate; nervilles clearly distinct below → [[Strombosia_scheffleri\|*S. scheffleri*]] ^k4

**4′.** Floral bud globular; pedicel pustular; nervilles most often indistinct → [[Strombosia_zenkeri\|*S. zenkeri*]]
