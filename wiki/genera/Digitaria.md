# *Digitaria* Hall.

**Family**: [[Gramineae]]
**Authority**: Hall.
**Protologue**: Hall., Hist. Stirp. II : 244 (1768)

## Diagnosis

Perennial or annual herbs with linear leaves.

Racemes digitate or densely paniculate at the apex of the culms, unilateral. Spikelets more or less imbricate, generally appressed-pubescent, lanceolate, plano-convex. Rachis of racemes flattened or triquetrous. Lower glume very small or suppressed, the upper one of variable size. Lower flower neuter, lemma similar to the upper glume or generally larger with 5 or 7 nerves. Palea reduced; two lodicules. Upper flower hermaphrodite; lemma and palea chartaceous, the first with 3 nerves, with its margins folded over the palea which has 2 nerves. Two lodicules, 3 stamens, distinct styles.

Genus comprising more than one hundred species, of which 5 have been recorded so far in Gabon.
