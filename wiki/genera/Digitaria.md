# *Digitaria* Hall.

**Family**: [[Gramineae]]
**Authority**: Hall.
**Protologue**: Hall., Hist. Stirp. II : 244 (1768)

## Diagnosis

Perennial or annual herbs with linear leaves.

Racemes digitate or densely paniculate at the apex of the culms, unilateral. Spikelets more or less imbricate, generally appressed-pubescent, lanceolate, plano-convex. Rachis of the racemes flattened or triquetrous.

Lower glume very small or suppressed, the upper one of variable size. Lower flower sterile, lemma similar to the upper glume or generally larger with 5 or 7 nerves. Palea reduced; two lodicules. Upper flower hermaphrodite; lemma and palea coriaceous, the former with 3 nerves, with its margins folded over the palea which has 2 nerves. Two lodicules, 3 stamens, distinct styles.

Genus comprising more than one hundred species, of which 5 have been reported so far from Gabon.

1. Pedicels of the spikelets bearing at their apex bristles as long as the spikelets; lower glume suppressed, upper glume very reduced, paniculate racemes numerous and up to 25 cm long; tall savanna grass..... 1. D. uniglumis var. major.

1'. Pedicels without long hairs; lower glume present, more or less reduced; upper glume well developed, racemes digitate or sub-digitate.

2. Rachis of the racemes with triangular section, with wings or lateral margins more or less developed.

3. Upper glume with 3 nerves, shorter than the spikelet (2/3 approx.), bearing longitudinal lines of silvery hairs; racemes variable in number and length, usually 5 to 10; plant very common in fallow lands and waste ground.....

2. D. horizontalis.
