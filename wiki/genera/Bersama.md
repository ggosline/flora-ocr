# Bersama

**Authority**: Fresen. (1837) · **Family**: [[Melianthaceae]]

## Diagnosis

African trees and shrubs with winged imparipinnate leaf rachises, long racemes, five unequal clawed petals, a unilateral disc, 4–5 partly united stamens and a 4(–5)-locular ovary. Capsules have thick persistent valves and arillate seeds.

## Species

- [[Bersama_paullinioides|B. paullinioides]] — leaflets glabrous beneath; rachis wings 0–2 mm.
- [[Bersama_maxima|B. maxima]] — leaflets pubescent beneath; rachis wings 2–8 mm.

The treatment regards African *Bersama* taxonomy as unresolved and deliberately retains two Gabonese species pending revision.
