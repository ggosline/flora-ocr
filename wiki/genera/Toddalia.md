# Toddalia

**Family**: [[Rutaceae]]

## Diagnosis

Spiny scrambling shrubs or lianas with trifoliolate leaves, small unisexual 5-merous flowers and red globose drupes with several one-seeded locules.

- [[Toddalia_asiatica|*T. asiatica*]]
