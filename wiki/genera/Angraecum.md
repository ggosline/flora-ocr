# *Angraecum* Bory

**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Authority**: Bory
**Protologue**: Voy. Iles Afr. 1 : 359 (1804)

## Diagnosis

Stems short or elongate, branched or unbranched. Leaves flattened dorsiventrally or compressed laterally, imbricate at the base or well-spaced, thin or fleshy, coriaceous. Inflorescence erect to spreading or pendulous, 1–multi-flowered. Flowers minute to large, resupinate or not. Sepals and petals subsimilar. Labellum simple, generally entire, with or without a callus, furnished with a spur. Rostellum deeply notched, short, dome-shaped. Viscidies double, oblong, thin, fragile. Tegula double, linear. Rostellum deeply notched after withdrawal of the pollinium. – Pl. 216, p. 453.

Genus comprising approximately 200 species, in tropical Africa, Madagascar and adjacent islands and in Sri Lanka. 13 species exist in Gabon.

The key to sections was taken for this work from the study of Garay (1973).

## Key to the species

**1.** Inflorescence few- to many-flowered, in a raceme or panicle, rarely uniflorous, by arrested development → 6. sect. Afrangraecum

**1′.** Inflorescence generally uniflorous, sometimes 2-flowered, flowers appearing either simultaneously or successively → couplet **2**

**2.** Peduncle very short, scarcely developed → couplet **3**

**2′.** Peduncle well developed → couplet **4**

**3.** Leaves elongate with a distinct blade, or linear but never laterally compressed, more or less loosely distichous

**3′.** Leaves very short, laterally compressed, densely imbricate → 2. sect. Dolabrifolia

**4.** Peduncle shorter than the internodes → 3. sect. Conchoglossum (A. angustipetalum)

**4′.** Peduncle generally longer than the internodes → couplet **5**

**5.** Flowers small, apical part of the spur more or less clavate

**5′.** Flowers medium or large, apical part of the spur filiform → 5. sect. Arachnangraecum

**1.** Sect. Pectinaria (Bentham) Schltr. Beih. Bot. Central. 36 : 157 (1918). Stem elongate, loosely leafy. Leaves alternate, fleshy, elongate, linear or with a well-defined lamina, but never imbricate. Inflorescence generally 1-flowered. Peduncle very short or scarcely developed. Flower small.

**1.** Spur narrowly cylindrical, acute or subacute, as long as pedicel and ovary → 52.2. A. pungens.

**1′.** Spur clavate or obovoid, distinctly swollen at the apex, obtuse, shorter than pedicel and ovary → 52.1. A. gabonense.
