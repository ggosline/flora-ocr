# *Olax* L.

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]] *sensu lato* · **Current placement**: Olacaceae s.str.

Fl. Zeylan.: 14, tab. 34 (1747).

**Synonyms**: *Fissilia* Comm. ex Juss., Gen.: 260 (1789); *Pseudaleia* (or
*Pseudaliea*) Du Pet.-Thou., Gen. Nov. Madag.: 15 (1806); *Pseudaleioides* Du
Pet.-Thou., l.c.; *Spermaxyrum* Labill., Nov. Holl. Pl. Spec. **2**: 84, tab.
233 (1806); *Roxburghia* Koen. ex Roxb., Fl. ed. Carey **1**: 168 (1820);
*Lopadocalyx* Klotzsch in Lehman, Pl. Preiss. **1**: 178 (1844); *Drebbelia*
Zoll., Naturk. Tijdschrift, sér. 4, **14**(3): 159 (1857).

Type species: *O. zeylanica* L.

A palaeotropical genus of about fifty species: 37 in Africa, 15 in Asia, the
remainder in Madagascar. Six species occur in Cameroon and Gabon — the largest
genus of Olacaceae in the Flore du Gabon area.

## Diagnosis

Small trees or shrubs. Branchlets angular, ± 4-winged. Leaves sometimes
decussate, always glabrous; blade of variable size. Secondary nerves
anastomosing well in from the margin. Inflorescences axillary, simple racemes,
solitary or fascicled, or the flowers solitary. Rachis ± flattened, 4-winged,
rectangular in section. Flowers female, glabrous, pentamerous. Calyx small,
cupuliform. Petals 3–6, free from one another or united by the stamens.
Androecium of 8–10 stamens together with staminodes, variable in number by
species. Filaments ± fused to the petals, linear, flattened or thick.
Staminodes larger than the stamens, terminated by 2 blades. Ovary ellipsoid or
ovoid, glabrous, superior, sometimes slightly inferior, with 1–3 uniovulate
locules. Placenta central. Style short or long, ending in a capitate, trilobed
stigma. Drupe one-seeded, oblong or globose, free or surrounded by the
accrescent calyx. Albumen oily. Embryo apical.

## Key to the Gabonese species

1. Leaves alternate
    - 2\. Stamens alternipetalous; flowers on the narrow faces of the rachis
        - 3\. Calyx not accrescent; raceme many-flowered; petiole canaliculate
          above; bark striate and dotted with whitish points; endocarp thin,
          crustaceous → **[[Olax_gambecola|1. O. gambecola]]**
        - 3'. Calyx accrescent; raceme few-flowered; petiole ± concave above;
          bark transversely striate; endocarp thick and hard →
          **[[Olax_mannii|2. O. mannii]]**
    - 2'. Stamens epipetalous; flowers on the broad faces of the rachis →
      **[[Olax_subscorpioidea|3. O. subscorpioidea]]**
        - 4\. Petals less than 6 mm long; style less than 4 mm long →
          **[[Olax_subscorpioidea_var_subscorpioidea|var. subscorpioidea]]**
        - 4'. Petals 6–7 mm long; style more than 4 mm long →
          **[[Olax_subscorpioidea_var_durandii|var. durandii]]**
1'. Leaves decussate
    - 5\. Staminodes epipetalous; petiole absent; bark transversely wrinkled and
      longitudinally striate → **[[Olax_staudtii|4. O. staudtii]]**
    - 5'. Staminodes alternipetalous; petiole 0–4 mm long; bark longitudinally
      wrinkled
        - 6\. Ovary 1-locular; petiole 2 mm long; flower bud ellipsoid; 5
          stamens → **[[Olax_triplinervia|5. O. triplinervia]]**
        - 6'. Ovary with 3 incomplete locules; petiole 4 mm long; flower bud
          ellipsoid, constricted in the middle; 6 stamens →
          **[[Olax_latifolia|6. O. latifolia]]**

## Treatments

### Vol 20 (1973)

**Source**: `sources/Olacaceae_vol20_paddle`

Genus 3 of the family. Villiers keys the species first on leaf arrangement
(alternate vs decussate), then on whether the stamens are alternipetalous or
epipetalous and on which faces of the flattened rachis the flowers are borne.

## Notes

*Olax* is the type genus of the family and is retained in *Olacaceae* in the
narrow sense by later classifications, unlike most of the other genera treated
in this volume.
