# *Keita* Cheek

**Family**: Aptandraceae (tentative) — within [[Olacaceae]] s.l.

Kew Bull. **79**: 317–332 (2024).

A genus of **two species**, erected for the two continental African taxa
previously placed in *Anacolosa*. Established after the Flore du Gabon
treatment, so it appears nowhere in Vol 20: what Villiers treated as
[[Anacolosa]] *uncifera* is now [[Keita_uncifera|Keita uncifera]].

## Diagnosis

The two continental African species differ from the remaining, Asian species of
[[Anacolosa]] in numerous architectural, floral and vegetative characters. They
are **unique within Olacaceae s.l. in being climbers** rather than shrubs or
trees. Climbing is achieved by perennial hook-like structures formed by a
combination of five separate traits not found elsewhere in the group.

Villiers had already noticed the diagnostic feature without drawing the generic
conclusion: his key to the Gabonese Olacaceae separates the plant at the first
couplet as the only liana bearing circinate hooks.

## Species

| Species | Distribution | In Gabon |
|---------|--------------|----------|
| [[Keita_uncifera\|K. uncifera]] (J.Louis & Boutique) Cheek | Gabon, Republic of the Congo, DRC | yes |
| *K. deniseae* Cheek | Republic of Guinea (Simandou), submontane forest | no |

*K. deniseae* is assessed as Endangered and is not known from Gabon; it has no
page here.

## Placement

Cheek et al. place *Keita* tentatively in **Aptandraceae**, noting that
confirmation requires molecular study. Under the broad, pre-APG circumscription
used by Vol 20 it falls within [[Olacaceae]] s.l.

## Notes

This is the first post-Flore addendum ingested for [[Olacaceae]]. It changes the
Gabonese genus list: *Anacolosa* is no longer represented in Gabon, and *Keita*
takes its place.
