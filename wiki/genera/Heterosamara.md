# *Heterosamara* Kuntze

**Family**: [[Polygalaceae]]
**Authority**: Kuntze
**Protologue**: Rev. Gen . Pl. 1 : 47 (1891)

## Diagnosis

Herbs perennial or annual, sometimes epiphytic, shrublets or rarely shrubs.

Leaves generally alternate, sometimes pseudo-verticillate or pseudo-basal, without stipules, distinctly petiolate; blade broadly elliptic to ovate-lanceolate or oblong-elliptic to oblong-lanceolate, membranous. Flowers in terminal or pseudo-lateral racemes; bracts and bracteoles caducous; sepals 5, unequal, caducous, free or sometimes the 2 lower external ones connate, oval to suborbicular, the upper (posterior) one larger and naviculate or oval, the two inner ones larger and petaloid (winged sepals); petals 5, caducous, 3 connate, the lower one keel-shaped, with a lobed crest, lobes entire, sometimes foliaceous (rarely absent), the 2 upper ones connate to the staminal tube; stamens 8, connate in a tube split, pollen heteropolar; ovary 2-locular, with 1 ovule per locule; stigma two-branched. Fruit capsular, flattened and winged. Seeds 2, pilose, generally with a 3-lobed or 3-branched caruncle (rarely absent), 3 membranous appendages or sometimes with an appendage (strophiole) opposite to the caruncle.

Genus comprising 18 species from tropical Africa and tropical and subtropical Asia.

It has been divided into two sections: Heterosamara with seed glabrous or with short hairs or more rarely villous and bearing a caruncle, and Villosperma (C.Y.Wu & S.K.Chen) Paiva & P.Silveira with seed densely hirsute-villous, without caruncle. The two Gabonese species belong to section Heterosamara.

Bi: Paiva (1998), Castro et al. (2007).
