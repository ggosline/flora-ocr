Small trees or monocaulous shrubs polygamous or dioecious, probably not monoecious. Leaves often large, pluri- or multifoliolate, paripinnate, without stipules.

Inflorescences cauline ± scattered along the trunk or grouped around its base, in spiciform racemes approximately 5–50(–80) cm long; rachis bracts moderate and sometimes recaulescent, bearing at their axils brief sessile or pedunculate cymes, most often short and contracted. The raceme very rarely presents branching toward its base.

Flowers zygomorphic, often only 3–6 per small cyme, sometimes all ± oriented to the same side of the raceme; calyx ± urceolate, clothed with felted hairs on the exterior and interior, with (4–) 5 lobes narrowly quincuncial in bud, short (1/2 to 1/4 of the calyx) and opening little, ± coloured pale green, white, cream, brown, brownish, pink or red; 4 or 5, rarely 6 white or yellowish petals inconspicuous at anthesis, ± pubescent, base sometimes narrowed into a claw, apex spatulate sometimes crenulate, furnished on the inner face with a ligule, emarginate or not, forming a sort of pocket whose axis is sometimes ± united; an appendage or appendicule, digitate or ± laminate, sometimes appears on the abaxial face of the ligule; 6–15 stamens with narrow, hairy, slightly subulate filament at the apex; anther with 2 introrse lobes separated by a longitudinal groove, dorsifixed between the lower third and lower fifth of its height; thecae glabrous or pubescent; connective emarginate at the base and sometimes slightly at the apex, plane or convex, sometimes provided with a prominent glandular boss dorsally or apically; extrastaminal disc yellow, fleshy, crescent-shaped with sinuous outline, fleshy, convex or depressed at the middle, rarely accompanied by a satellite (Ch. setosus), very exceptionally annular; ovary hirsute or glabrous, with 3–9 uniovulate locules, outline ± lobed, reduced by abortion in the flower called ♂; style developed sometimes curved in the ♀ flower; stigma not or scarcely distinctly lobed.

Fruit fleshy, succulent at maturity, green, yellow, orange or ± purplish, adorned with ribs: either one rib per locule, or additionally with meridional intercalary ribs ± prominent.
