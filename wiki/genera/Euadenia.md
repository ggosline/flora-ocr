# *Euadenia* Oliver

**Authority**: Oliver

## Diagnosis

– Pteropetalum PAX, Bot. Jahrb. 14: 295 (1891); in ENGL., Nat. Pflanzenfam., ed. 1, 3 (2): 229 (1891).

Shrubs ± tall or small trees reaching 5 m in height, sparsely branched, stems and sometimes petioles furnished with fine, scattered pubescence, glabrous elsewhere. Lenticels numerous and clearly visible. Axillary buds solitary. Leaves alternate (1-) 3-foliolate and long-petiolate, sometimes simple and short-petiolate, deciduous or evergreen. Leaflets membranous or papyraceous, olive-green above, paler and sometimes reddish-tinged beneath, stipitate bases equal or unequal; median leaflet different from the lateral ones, symmetric, obovate, acute or cuspidate, base attenuate or cuneate; lateral leaflets asymmetric, outer half ovate, inner half obovate, base acute and asymmetric. Lateral veins 5–6, slightly prominent on both surfaces or depressed above, anastomosing near the margin; venules indistinctly marked; petiolules 2–7 mm long, base scarcely or not articulate. Petioles of compound leaves long and slender, those of simple leaves short; canaliculate on the upper surface, glabrous or furnished with fine, scattered pubescence, not articulate at the base, nor decurrent on the branchlet. Stipules free, very inconspicuous, setaceous, membranous, drying rapidly.

Inflorescence in short or elongate racemes, rigidly erect at the apex of the main shoots or leafy axillary branches; floral bracts simple reaching 1 cm in length, narrowly lanceolate or subulate, sometimes spatulate or filiform, falling early, generally before anthesis; stipules of floral bracts free and inconspicuous. Flowers zygomorphic, symmetric, distinctly bilateral, bud opening prematurely. Receptacle reduced club-shaped. Sepals 4, imbricate, with free base, lanceolate long-attenuate, yellowish-green, slightly spreading at anthesis, deciduous. Petals 4, with free base, alternating with sepals, yellow or greenish, deciduous, in 2 pairs distinctly dimorphic: the upper pair ascending much the most developed, reaching 7.5–8.5 × 0.8–2.2 cm; the lower pair relatively insignificant, spreading horizontally, shorter than the sepals reaching 25 × 1–2 mm. Androecium comprising fertile stamens and deciduous staminodes; stamens 4–5 (–7), disposed in the ventral part of the flower, equal or nearly so; filaments long and slender, inclined, free or connate at the base; anthers narrowly oblong, reaching 4 mm, basifixed.
