# *Ancistrocladus* Wall.

**Family**: [[Ancistrocladaceae]]

Numer. List : 1052 (1829), nom. cons.

The sole genus of the family [[Ancistrocladaceae]], with about 16 species in
the evergreen forests of tropical Africa, southern India and Sri Lanka, and
Southeast Asia (through Hainan to Sumatra). Four species are recorded from
Gabon.

## Diagnosis

Juvenile leaves grouped in terminal rosettes; adult leaves differentiated into
those arranged along the main stems and those at the tips of the branchlets,
sometimes themselves grouped in terminal rosettes. Secondary nervation
brochidodromous (pinnate, with the secondary nerves forming loops); tertiary
nervation irregularly areolate, often with **glands or large craters at the
junctions of groups of nervules**, visible on both faces of the leaf but
especially on the upper.

Inflorescences subsessile to pedunculate; bracts subtending the axes and
pedicels often with one or several abaxial glands. Flowers actinomorphic or
with unequal sepals; pedicels absent or — more usually — present and articulate
at the apex; sepals imbricate, equal or with the 3 outer larger, sometimes
with 1 to several abaxial glands; petals with imbricate, convolute, or
intermediate aestivation; stamens arranged in one or two whorls, the filaments
equal or of two lengths; anther thecae usually separated by a well-developed
connective.

Fruits with the accrescent sepals subequal and coriaceous to chartaceous, or
unequal, papery and aliform. Seeds with a hard endosperm.

## Key to the species

- **1.** Leaves at the apex of branchlets with blade very narrowly obovate-elliptic to narrowly obovate-elliptic, with ratio (L/w) = 3.5–15.5, in the adult state with large craters ordinarily oblong, of (0.5–)2–4 × 0.5–1 mm; bracts at the beginning of anthesis narrowly ovate-elliptic to narrowly obovate-elliptic and prolonged at the base, at the end of anthesis becoming cochleariform to claviform by the enlargement of the abaxial gland and the incurving of the apex → [[Ancistrocladus_letestui\|*A. letestui*]]
- **1′.** Leaves at the apex of branchlets with blade elliptic to narrowly obovate-elliptic, obovate or broadly elliptic, with ratio (L/w) = 2–4.8, in the adult state with large craters circular to ovate, of 0.2–1 mm in diameter; bracts at the beginning of anthesis narrowly ovate-elliptic to ovate or triangular, not prolonged at the base, not becoming cochleariform to claviform at the end of anthesis .. 2
  - **2.** Adult leaves with blade furnished with large circular to ovate craters of 0.3–1 mm in diameter; sepals unequal in flower, the 3 largest of 3.5–5.1 × 1.4–2.5 mm, the 2 smallest of 3.2–4.2 × 2–2.9 mm; petals oblong-ovate, of ± 8.1 × 3.2 mm; sepals papyraceous and very unequal in fruit, the 3 largest of 28–42 × 13–16 mm, the 2 smallest of 5–16 × 4–6 mm → [[Ancistrocladus_guineensis\|*A. guineensis*]]
  - **2′.** Adult leaves with blade furnished with large circular craters of 0.2–0.5 mm in diameter; sepals subequal in flower, of 4–6 × 2–3 mm; petals suborbicular to obovate, of 2–4 × 2–3.5 mm; sepals thickened-chartaceous or coriaceous to ligneous and subequal to unequal in fruit, of 5–10 × 3–8 mm → couplet **3**
    - **3.** Adult leaves with blade subcoriaceous to coriaceous, those at the apex of branchlets truncate to subacute and sometimes abruptly acuminate at the apex; pedicel rather robust; petals obovate, of ± 4 × 3–3.5 mm; styles 3; fruit turbinate, of 8–15 × 10–20 mm (without the wings) → [[Ancistrocladus_congolensis\|*A. congolensis*]]
    - **3′.** Adult leaves with blade chartaceous to subcoriaceous, those at the apex of branchlets ordinarily acute to acuminate at the apex, sometimes obtuse to rounded; pedicel slender; petals suborbicular, of 2–3 × 2–3 mm; style 1; fruit patelliforme, of 4–5 × 7–8 mm (without the wings) → [[Ancistrocladus_ealaensis\|*A. ealaensis*]]

## Treatments

### Vol 60 (2022) — Gereau R.E. & Walters G.M.

Source: `sources/Ancistrocladaceae_vol60_liteparse` · pp. 2–7 of the family
treatment.

The first comprehensive Gabonese treatment in the Margraf series. Treats all
four species; provides a key, full descriptions, distributions, and ecology;
illustrates *A. congolensis* and *A. ealaensis* together (Planche 1, Figure 1)
and *A. guineensis* with the classic Trevithick / Hutchinson, Dalziel & Keay
plate (Planche 2). No plate is provided for *A. letestui*.
