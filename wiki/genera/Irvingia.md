# Irvingia

**Authority**: Hook. f. (1860) · **Family**: [[Irvingiaceae]]

## Diagnosis

Trees with simple alternate leaves, caducous stipules leaving annular scars, and a characteristic double network of veinlets. Flowers small, usually 5-merous, with ten stamens around a thick disc and a two-locular ovary. Fruits are compressed fibrous drupes with one thick stone and one oily seed.
