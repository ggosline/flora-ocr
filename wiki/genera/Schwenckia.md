# *Schwenckia* L.

**Family**: [[Solanaceae]]
**Authority**: L.
**Protologue**: Gen. pl., éd. 6 : 567 (1764)

## Diagnosis

Herbs annual or perennial, or sometimes small shrubs, unarmed; stems glabrous or pubescent with simple hairs, glandular or not. Leaves alternate or fasciculate, simple, petiolate or sessile; blade with entire margins. Inflorescences axillary or terminal, in dense racemes or loose panicles of cymes, with 1 to numerous flowers. Flowers bisexual, 5-merous, most often erect, actinomorphic or slightly zygomorphic; calyx tubular to campanulate, with often unequal lobes; corolla narrowly tubular, tube often enlarged in the upper part, with short lobes, generally alternating with 5 small claviform appendices, with valvate-conduplicate aestivation; stamens 5 and didynamous or reduced to 2 and accompanied by 2 or 3 staminodes, filaments fused to the tube, filiform or flattened, glabrous or pubescent, anthers basifixed, ovoid to ellipsoid, dehiscing by longitudinal slits; disc annular or cupuliform; ovary 2-locular, glabrous; style filiform, glabrous, with capitate or discoid stigma. Fruits: capsules spherical to ovoid, smooth to rugose, with septicidal dehiscence by 2 valves, with numerous seeds. Seeds sometimes flattened on one side, with reticulate testa; embryo straight.

Genus of approximately 20 species, distributed in Central and South America as well as in the Antilles; a single species, S. americana, introduced and widely naturalized throughout tropical America and Africa.
