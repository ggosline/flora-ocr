# *Hewittia* Wight & Arnott

**Family**: [[Convolvulaceae]]
**Authority**: Wight & Arnott
**Protologue**: Madras J. Lit. Sci. 5 : 22 (1837)

## Diagnosis

Creeping and/or climbing herbs. Leaves: blade simple, lobed or not, cordate at base. Inflorescences axillary, pedunculate, one- to few-flowered. Flowers: sepals unequal, the outer ones larger than the inner and ± foliaceus, accrescent in fruit; corolla yellowish; stamens and pistil included; pollen grains smooth; ovary pubescent, imperfectly unilocular, 4-ovulate; style 1, filiform, stigma bilobate. Fruits with up to 4 seeds. Monotypic genus, paleotropical.

Bibliography: Demissew (2006), Gonçalves (1987), Lejoly & Lisowski (1993), Thulin (2006), Verdcourt (1963).
