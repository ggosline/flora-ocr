# *Heisteria* Jacq.

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]] *sensu lato* · **Current placement**: Erythropalaceae

Enum. Pl. Carib.: 4 (1760).

**Synonyms**: *Rhaptostylum* Humb. & Bonpl., Pl. Aequin. **2**: 139, tab. 125
(1808); *Hesiod(i)a* Vell., Fl. Flum. **4**: 176 (1825); *Acrolobus* Klotzsch,
Verhandl. Akad. Wiss. Berl.: 236, tab. 3 (1856); *Raptostylus* Post & O.Ktze.,
Lexicon Gen. Phaner. **4**: 477 (1904); *Phanerocalyx* S.Moore, Journ. Bot.
**59**: 244 (1921).

Type species: *H. coccinea* Jacq.

An amphi-Atlantic genus. America holds about forty species; Africa has only
three, all of which occur in Cameroon and Gabon.

## Diagnosis

Glabrous trees or shrubs. Branchlets winged along 2 opposite lines. Leaf blade
of variable shape. Inflorescence a few- to several-flowered fascicle. Flowers
female, pentamerous, small, pedicellate. Calyx glabrous, cupuliform, with an
entire or undulate margin. Petals valvate, free except at the base, pubescent
on the inner face. Stamens 10 in 2 whorls — large alternipetalous stamens and
small epipetalous ones; anther introrse with longitudinal dehiscence slits.
Pistil superior; ovary globose, 3-locular, the locules uniovulate. Placenta
central. Style ending in a 3-lobed stigma. Drupe surrounded by the **strongly
accrescent calyx**, its upper margin entire or notched. Cotyledons foliaceous.

The strongly accrescent calyx forming a collar around the fruit, together with
2 stamens per petal (alternipetalous and epipetalous), separates *Heisteria*
from [[Strombosia]], where a single epipetalous stamen faces each petal and it
is the **pedicel** that becomes accrescent.

## Key to the Gabonese species

1. Blade little granular or smooth; nervules apparent; accrescent calyx with
   the upper margin lobed or notched
    - 2\. Calyx notched almost to the base; calyx lobes longer than broad;
      pedicel short (1–3 mm) → **[[Heisteria_parvifolia|1. H. parvifolia]]**
    - 2'. Calyx weakly notched; lobes equal to 1/3 the length of the calyx and
      broader than long; pedicel long (3–7 mm) →
      **[[Heisteria_trillesiana|2. H. trillesiana]]**
1'. Blade granular on the upper face; nervules little or not apparent;
   accrescent calyx little or not notched →
   **[[Heisteria_zimmereri|3. H. zimmereri]]**
