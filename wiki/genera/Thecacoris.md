# *Thecacoris* A.Juss.

**Family**: [[Phyllanthaceae]]
**Authority**: A.Juss.
**Protologue**: Euphorb . gen . : 12, t. 1, fig. 1 (1824)

## Diagnosis

Shrubs or small trees. Stipules entire. Leaves with short petiole; blade with (sub)entire margin, not glandular. Inflorescences: axillary, sometimes (sub)terminal, in racemes or spikes solitary or fasciculate, or sometimes branched; bracts persistent, each bearing one flower. Flowers monoecious, small, 5(–6)-merous; sepals free or ± connate at base, imbricate; petals rudimentary or absent. Male flowers with short to absent pedicel; stamens 5(–6), opposite the sepals, exsert; disc glands alternating with sepals, glabrous or pilose; pistillode present. Female flowers sometimes with staminodes; disc annular, glabrous or pilose; ovary 3(–4)-locular, with 2 ovules per locule; styles 3(–4), distinct, bilobed. Fruits capsular, septicidal, globose-depressed, 3(–4)–lobed; pedicel distinctly accrescent in fruit. Seeds 1–2 per locule, ± subglobose to ± ellipsoid, lustrous at maturity.

African genus of approximately 16 species, 5 of which occur in Madagascar. On the continent, 12 species are present (one shared with Madagascar), the great majority inhabiting rainforests of West and Central Africa; seven species in Gabon and one poorly known species.

Bibliography: Breteler (2011a), Leandri (1958), Léonard (1995a), Pax & Hoffmann (1922).
