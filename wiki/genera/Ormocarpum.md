# *Ormocarpum* P.Beauv., nom. cons.

**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Authority**: P.Beauv., nom. cons.
**Protologue**: Fl. Oware 1 : 95 (1807)

## Diagnosis

Shrubs or small trees, often covered with glandular hairs with swollen base mixed with normal hairs. Stipules persistent, generally small, scarious. Leaves imparipinnate; stipelles absent; leaflets 1 to numerous, alternate, blade with entire or finely denticulate margin. Inflorescences axillary or on leafless branchlets, in loose racemes or pseudoracemes; bracts persistent, very small. Flowers: bracteoles 2, persistent;

calyx with 5 lobes, the 2 upper ones shortly connate; corolla caducous, standard suborbicular to broadly elliptic-obovate, clawed, provided with 2 calluses on the inner surface at the level of the claw, wings obliquely ovate, with narrow claws, provided unilaterally with series of imbricate transverse pouches, keel broad, incurved, as long as the wings or slightly longer; stamens connate for ½ to ⅔ of their length in 2 phalanges of 5 or in a tube split unilaterally, anthers equal; ovary stipitate, multiovulate; style curved, stigma minute, terminal. Pods stipitate, ± moniliform, straight to arched, flattened, disintegrating into oblong articles, indehiscent, with surface longitudinally striated, often papillose or muricate, sometimes with stiff and short setae.

Genus of approximately 18 species, of which 15 in Africa, including one species well distributed also in Asia, 2 species in Madagascar, and one in Asia and in the Pacific as far as Fiji and northern Australia; in Gabon, 4 species.

B: Gillett (1966, 1971b), Léonard (1954a, 1954b), Lewis et al. (2005), Pellegrin (1949).
