# *Pinacopodium* Exell & Mendonça

**Family**: [[Nectaropetalaceae]]
**Authority**: Exell & Mendonça
**Protologue**: Bol. Soc. Brot., ser. 2, 25 : 105 (1951)

## Diagnosis

Tree. Leaves alternate, simple, entire, petiolate; stipules extra-petiolar, early deciduous.

Flowers grouped in subumbelliform cymes, pedunculate. Calyx campanulate with 5 segments. Petals 5, free, with nectariferous appendages scarcely distinct on the ventral surface. Stamens 10–12 (15?) with filaments united at the base into a very short tube. Ovary 2-locular with 1 pendulous ovule per locule; style very short, stigma capitate, bilobed.

Fruit unknown.

TYPE SPECIES: P. congolense (S. Moore) Exell & Mendonça.
