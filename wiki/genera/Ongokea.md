# *Ongokea* Pierre

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]] *sensu lato* · **Current placement**: Aptandraceae

Bull. Soc. Linn. Paris **2**: 770 (1897).

A monospecific genus, represented in Cameroon and Gabon by
[[Ongokea_gore|O. gore]] — the *angueuk*, source of isano oil. The generic name
is itself Gabonese in origin.

## Diagnosis

Glabrous tree. Branchlets winged. Inflorescence an axillary panicle of
many-flowered umbels. Flowers hermaphrodite, male or female, tetra- or
pentamerous. Calyx small, cupuliform, toothed. Petals valvate, ± linear. Glands
fleshy, alternipetalous, between the petals and the stamens. Stamens
epipetalous, united into a tube; anthers opening by annular dehiscence slits.
Ovary superior. Style filiform, ending in a pointed stigma. Drupe surrounded by
the accrescent calyx, which **opens by 2–3 valves at maturity**. Endocarp hard
and lignified. Seed solitary.

See [[Aptandra]] for the characters separating the two staminal-tube genera.
