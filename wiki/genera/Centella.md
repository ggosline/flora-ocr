# *Centella* L.

**Family**: [[Apiaceae]]
**Authority**: L.
**Protologue**: Sp. pl ., ed. 2, 2 : 1393 (1763)

## Diagnosis

Herbs annual or perennial, often prostrate and rooting, sometimes subshrubs.

Leaves often grouped by (1–)2–several; blade simple, linear to broadly reniform. Inflorescences in axillary umbels, simple or compound, sessile or briefly pedunculate; involucre with 2–4 bracts. Flowers hermaphrodite or polygamous; calyx with insignificant teeth; petals inflexed, obtuse, whitish-greenish, sometimes reddish or purple; stylopodium flat or slightly concave; styles short. Fruits laterally compressed, glabrous or pilose; carpophore absent; mericarps with prominent primary and secondary ribs, anastomosing with each other in a network; pericarp often thick and coriaceous. Seeds flat.

Genus comprising approximately 50 species, most in South Africa, a few in Madagascar and Australia, and one with pantropical distribution present in Gabon.

B: Adamson (1951), Burtt (1991), Cannon (1978), Jacques-Félix (1970).
