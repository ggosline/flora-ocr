# *Anacolosa* Blume

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]]

Mus. Bot. Lugd. Bat. **1**: 250, tab. 46 (1850).

Type species: *A. frutescens* Blume.

A palaeotropical genus of 16 species: 14 in Asia, one in Madagascar and one in
Africa. The single African species, [[Anacolosa_uncifera|A. uncifera]], reaches
Gabon.

## Diagnosis

Liana or shrub. Leaves somewhat coriaceous. Inflorescence axillary,
many-flowered. Flowers small, hexamerous, actinomorphic. Calyx cupuliform,
weakly toothed. Petals fleshy, erect, papillose or pubescent on the inner face,
inserted on the disc. Disc 6-lobed. Stamens epipetalous, appressed to the
petals. Ovary adnate to the disc, with 1, 2 or 3 locules; ovule pendulous.
Calyx accrescent, enclosing the drupe; endocarp crustaceous. Seed solitary,
the albumen sometimes oily. Embryo apical.

Within the Gabonese Olacaceae, *Anacolosa* is the only liana bearing circinate
hooks, and its accrescent **disc** — not the calyx — forms the pubescent
envelope around the fruit.

## Treatments

### Vol 20 (1973)

**Source**: `sources/Olacaceae_vol20_paddle`

Treated as genus 1 of the family, keyed out on the hooked branchlets and the
strongly accrescent, pubescent disc surrounding the fruit.

## Notes

The genus is placed in *Olacaceae* throughout this treatment. Later
classifications (APG) restrict *Olacaceae* considerably and refer *Anacolosa*
to **Aptandraceae**; this is not reflected in the Vol 20 treatment.
