# *Anacolosa* Blume

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]] *sensu lato* · **Current placement**: Aptandraceae

Mus. Bot. Lugd. Bat. **1**: 250, tab. 46 (1850).

Type species: *A. frutescens* Blume.

A palaeotropical genus of 16 species: 14 in Asia, one in Madagascar and one in
Africa, as circumscribed by Vol 20.

**No longer represented in Gabon.** Cheek, Molmou, Gosline & Magassouba (2024)
removed the two continental African species from *Anacolosa* to the new genus
[[Keita]], so the plant Villiers treated here as *A. uncifera* is now
[[Keita_uncifera|Keita uncifera]] and *Anacolosa* becomes a wholly Asian and
Malagasy genus.

## Diagnosis

Liana or shrub. Leaves somewhat coriaceous. Inflorescence axillary,
many-flowered. Flowers small, hexamerous, actinomorphic. Calyx cupuliform,
weakly toothed. Petals fleshy, erect, papillose or pubescent on the inner face,
inserted on the disc. Disc 6-lobed. Stamens epipetalous, appressed to the
petals. Ovary adnate to the disc, with 1, 2 or 3 locules; ovule pendulous.
Calyx accrescent, enclosing the drupe; endocarp crustaceous. Seed solitary,
the albumen sometimes oily. Embryo apical.

Within the Gabonese Olacaceae as treated, *Anacolosa* is the only liana bearing
circinate hooks, and its accrescent **disc** — not the calyx — forms the
pubescent envelope around the fruit. That climbing habit is precisely the
character on which [[Keita]] was later segregated: it is unique in Olacaceae
s.l., the rest of *Anacolosa* being shrubs and trees.

## Notes

**Superseded for Gabon.** This page is retained because Vol 20 treats the genus,
and because the diagnosis below is the source's. For the plant itself see
[[Keita_uncifera|Keita uncifera]]; for the genus that now holds it, [[Keita]].

The genus is placed in *Olacaceae* throughout this treatment. Later
classifications (APG) restrict *Olacaceae* considerably and refer *Anacolosa*
to **Aptandraceae**; this is not reflected in the Vol 20 treatment.
