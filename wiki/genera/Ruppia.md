# Ruppia

**Authority**: L., *Sp. pl.* **1**: 127 (1753) · **Family**: [[Ruppiaceae]]

## Diagnosis

The genus has the characters of Ruppiaceae: glabrous submerged or floating herbs of saline or brackish water, with filiform one-veined leaves, tiny perianthless flowers and asymmetric beaked drupaceous fruits.
