# Tribulus

**Authority**: L. · **Family**: [[Zygophyllaceae]]

## Diagnosis

Prostrate herbs with unequal opposite paripinnate leaves, yellow 5-merous flowers and dry fruits splitting into five hard spiny mericarps.

- [[Tribulus_terrestris|T. terrestris]] — documented for comparison; its Gabon occurrence is unconfirmed.
