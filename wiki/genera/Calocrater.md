# *Calocrater* K.Schum.

**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Authority**: K.Schum.

## Diagnosis

in Engler & Prantl, 1DW3ÀDQ]HQIDP 4(2) : 175 (1895).

Shrubs with white latex. Stipules intrapetiolary, formed by enlarged ochreae. Leaves opposite, sessile or petiolate. Inflorescences apparently axillary, alternating with the leaves, shortly pedunculate. Flowers hermaphrodite, calyx with lobes almost free, equal or unequal, without colleters; corolla funnel-shaped, lobes overlapping to the left; stamens deeply included, with sessile anthers; pistil with apex situated approximately in the middle of the anthers; ovary 2-locular, syncarpous. Syncarpous fruits, with 2 horns. Seeds subglobose to ellipsoid with a deep furrow on the concave side and shallower longitudinal furrows on the other side, papillose, with a honeycomb structure, with an aril developing the grain endosperm ruminate, embryo straight, spatulate, cotyledons almost circular, deeply cordate at the base, rounded at the apex.

Monotypic genus limited to tropical Africa.
