## Diagnosis

Trees or shrubs unarmed.

Leaves entire or very slightly toothed; stipules caducous. ----Inflorescences in axillary racemes. Flowers ♂ or ♀ and ♀ (polygamous plants); sepals 4–5, connate in the lower 1/3, approximately the same length as the petals; petals 5–8, furnished with a scale at the base of their inner surface; stamens numerous (20–30); ovary glabrous or pubescent, with 2–4 pluriovulate placentas; style entire or divided into (2)–3–4 stigmatic branches. Fruits: capsules with 3 valves; seeds 1–5, angular. TYPE SPECIES: D. racemosa OLIV.

Genus of tropical Africa comprising 5 species, of which 1 for the Flora.
