Inflorescences in racemes grouped into pseudopanicles, axillary at the apex of the branches. Flowers ¥ ; sepals 5, imbricate ; petals 5, provided with a ± pubescent scale at the base of their inner surface, imbricate ; stamens 5 ; ovary glabrous to glabrescent, with 3 pluriovulate placentas ; style short with 3-fide apex. Fruits: capsules with 3 valves ; seeds few in number (1–3), arillate. TYPE SPECIES: S. leonensis OLIV.
