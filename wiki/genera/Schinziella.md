# *Schinziella* Gilg

**Family**: [[Gentianaceae]]
**Authority**: Gilg

## Diagnosis

in Engler & Prantl, Nat. Pflanzenfam. 4(2) : 74 (1895).

Perennial herbs, erect, glabrous; stems simple or sparingly branched, quadrangular to winged. Leaves opposite, sessile. Inflorescences in terminal and axillary cymes, many-flowered, long-pedunculate. Flowers actinomorphic, 4-merous; calyx campanulate, with lobes bearing 2 or 3 prominent longitudinal ridges, without scales at the internal base; corolla funnel-shaped; stamens 4, included, unequal, one large with fertile anther and three smaller ones with sterile anther; ovary 1-locular; stigma 2-lobed. Fruits oblong. Seeds cubic to rectangular, sometimes globular; testa reticulate, with polygonal cells.
