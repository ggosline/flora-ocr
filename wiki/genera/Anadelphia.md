## Key to the species

1. Rachis internodes filiform ..... A. laxatus

2. Racemes solitary, arranged in a fastigiate panicle ..... A. fastigiatus

3. Lower glume of sessile spikelets linear, laterally compressed between the pedicel and the internode, its dorsal surface furrowed with a deep median groove:

4. Upper glume of sessile spikelets and both glumes of pedicellate spikelets all awned; awns 11 mm long:

5. Annual plant; awns of the sessile spikelet 35-40 mm long . . . . . A. pseudapricus

5. Perennial and caespitose plant; awns of the sessile spikelet 20-35 mm long ..... A. chinensis

4. Glumes not awned, sometimes with a small mucro 0-2 mm long:

6. Dorso-ventral racemes with one face covered by imbricate pedicellate spikelets; leaves not auriculate ..... A. schirensis

6. Racemes not dorso-ventral; pedicellate spikelets more or less lateral; leaves auriculate ..... A. canaliculatus

3. Lower glume of sessile spikelets lanceolate and not compressed between the pedicel and the internode, its dorsal surface flattened or only depressed:

7. Leaf sheath auriculate; auricles 5-10 mm long ..... A. auriculatus

7. Leaf sheath not or shortly auriculate; auricles 0-2 mm long:

8. Pedicellate spikelets 4-4.5 mm long; lower glume of sessile spikelets with a line of hairs in the median depression ..... A. gabonensis

8. Pedicellate spikelets 5-8 mm long; lower glume of sessile spikelets glabrous (A. gayanus):
9. Pedicellate spikelets glabrous ..... A. gayanus var. polycladus
9. Pedicellate spikelets hairy ..... A. gayanus var. bisquamulatus
