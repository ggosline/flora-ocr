# *Aporrhiza* Radlkofer

**Family**: [[Sapindaceae]]
**Authority**: Radlkofer
**Protologue**: Sitzungsber. Bayer. Akad. Wiss. München 8 : 338 (1878); 9 : 478, 509 (1879); 20 : 262, 291 (1890); in ENGL., Pflanzenreich, Sapindaceae : 1134 (1933)

## Diagnosis

Trees or shrubs with rounded branchlets, glabrescent, the young ones pubescent to tomentellous. Leaves 2–11-jugate; leaflets opposite, ‡ acute at both ends, entire, coriaceous, without punctations (except A. letestui).

Inflorescences terminal or axillary, in simple or branched racemes of puberulent biporous cymules, with bracts and bracteoles. Flowers regular; calyx with 5 valvate sepals, densely tomentellous externally; 5 petals funnel-shaped in bud, then with margins folded inward in the lower part; disk in flattened regular crown, smooth or ± verrucose; stamens 6–8, with hairy filaments above and not below; anthers glabrous, exserted, introrse, cordate base, cleft; ovary with 2 locules, flattened, hairy, with papillose stigma, red, sessile; ovule unique, erect on a stout funicle.

Capsules flattened, biscutellate, surface ± bossed, stipitate with persistent calyx and accrescent style forming a point; interior of locules smooth, red. Seeds bicolored: integument yellow and soft at the base, sometimes considered as an aril, upper part hard and black tapering to a point outward; median hilum; cotyledons superposed, radicle turned upward opposite the hilum, whence the generic name.

Type species: Aporrhiza paniculata Radlkofer.
