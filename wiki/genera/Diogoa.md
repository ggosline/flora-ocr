# *Diogoa* Exell & Mendonça

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]]

Bull. Soc. Brot., sér. 2, **25**: 109 (1951).

A monospecific African genus, represented in Cameroon and Gabon by
[[Diogoa_zenkeri|D. zenkeri]].

## Diagnosis

Non-resinous tree — distinguishing it at once from [[Strombosiopsis]], whose
bark exudes a sticky red resin. Leaves with nerves prominent on the upper face.
Nervules subparallel to one another. Inflorescences fascicles or short racemes.
Pedicel short. Flower female, pentamerous. Stamens 5, epipetalous. Ovary
superior, 4–5-locular. Ovules pendulous. Drupe subglobose, marked by an
**equatorial ridge**, the pedicel accrescent up to it.

That equatorial ridge is the character on which the genus is keyed against
[[Strombosiopsis]], whose fruit instead bears 4 apical teeth or lobes.

## Notes

The species heading is printed in the OCR as *Diogo zenkeri*; the genus name is
truncated. Corrected to *Diogoa* throughout.

Later classifications refer *Diogoa* to **Strombosiaceae**; the Vol 20
treatment places it in *Olacaceae*.
