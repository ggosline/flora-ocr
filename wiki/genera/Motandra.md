# *Motandra* A.DC.

**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Authority**: A.DC.
**Protologue**: Prodr. 8 : 423 (1844)

## Diagnosis

Climbing shrubs or lianes, with white latex. Stipules absent. Leaves opposite or subopposite, without axillary colleters; petiole with glandular hairs above; secondary nerves curved towards the margin, anastomosing, with tufts of hairs (domatia) in the axils, tertiary venation scarcely visible, reticulate. Inflorescences terminal, thyrseoid, with numerous flowers.

Flowers actinomorphic or with unequal calyx lobes, calyx with imbricate lobes, without or up to 3 internal colleters at the base near the edges; corolla with tube bearing 5 deep teeth, appearing as internal thickenings from half the length to the base of the lobes, with lobes twisted and overlapping to the right in bud; stamens included, cone-shaped, inserted on a slightly thickened ring at the base of the tube; filament obscurely sigmoid; stigma annular disk with lobes alternating with the stamens; ovary semi-inferior; 2 carpels connate only at the extreme base. Fruits pendulous, composed of 2 spreading follicles, connate at their base, ± fusiform, dehiscent along an adaxial line, with acute apex and often rolled when dry; exocarp woody, with several distinct or obscure longitudinal wings except on the adaxial side; endocarp, pericarp finely parenchymatous, smooth inside.

Seeds flattened, elliptic to ovoid, glabrous, rounded at both extremities, thickened at the margin with the hilum at the base and a raphe ascending to the apex, with dense tuft of simple hairs (coma) directed towards the apex of the follicle; endosperm fleshy, in a very fine layer completely surrounding the embryo.

A genus with three species limited to West and Central Africa, all present in Gabon.

B: Beentje (2021), Endress et al. (2019), de Kruif (1984).
