# *Hugonia* Linné

**Family**: [[Linaceae]]
**Authority**: Linné
**Protologue**: Sp. Pl. ed. 1:75 (1753)

## Diagnosis

Trees, shrubs or lianes furnished with curved hooks, opposite or alternate, often arranged in pairs. Petiolate leaves, alternate, entire or crenate-dentate, penninerved; stipules generally laciniate, caducous.

1. A certain number of species not effectively collected in Gabonese territory have been cited or described, their presence in Gabon being possible or plausible. These species are immediately distinguished by their "bis" numbers.

<<<---SECTION-BREAK--->>>

Flowers grouped in axillary cymes or in terminal and lateral panicles. Sepals 5, imbricate, free, persistent. Petals 5, twisted in prefloration, caducous, darker at the base, unguiculate, free. Stamens 10, all fertile, with filaments fused at the base into a tube. Ovary with 2-5 locules each containing 2 collateral pendulous ovules; styles 2-5 free or partially fused; stigmas terminal, capitate.

Drupe with fleshy mesocarp surrounding a polyspermous nut, globose; endocarp lignified. Each partition bears within its mass a small locule or lacuna, alternating with the locules which each contain one compressed, albuminous seed. Embryo straight or slightly curved; radicle directed toward the apex of the nut.

About sixty tropical species with wide geographical distribution: Africa, Madagascar and Mascarene Islands, South-East Asia, New Caledonia and Australia.

TYPE SPECIES: Hugonia mystax L. ("myxstras"), India.
