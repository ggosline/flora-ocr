# *Pityrogramma* Link

**Family**: [[Adiantaceae]]
**Authority**: Link

## Diagnosis

Rhizome erect, with fronds in tufts, bearing narrow scales, not peltate. Petiole black, shiny. Fronds multipinnate, with segments decurrent in a wing along the channeled rachis. Veins free. Lower surface of the lamina covered with a white or yellow powder. Sori without indusia, without paraphyses, along the veins. Spores tetrahedral.

Approximately 40 species, most of them American.

Only one species in Gabon, recorded for the first time.
