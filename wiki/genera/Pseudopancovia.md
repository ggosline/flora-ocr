# *Pseudopancovia* Pellegrin

**Family**: [[Sapindaceae]]
**Authority**: Pellegrin
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Bot. France 102 : 228 (1955)

## Diagnosis

Shrubs differing from Pancovia: first, by the calyx ± bilabiate with 4-5 sepals, upper lip formed of 2-3 sepals fused for 3/4 of their length, lower lip of 2 sepals united likewise, the two lips separated to the middle of the calyx, hairy on both faces; second, by the corolla comprising 3-4 petals, the 2 median ones constituted as in Pancovia, with hairy claw, funnel-shaped limb cleft, bifid and dentate scale hairy below but bearing non-branched excrescences (they are most often branched in Pancovia), the lateral or lateral one funnel-shaped to the apex, scarcely cleft at the top, without scale or with scale, sometimes bearing inside the funnel on the ventral face an excrescencein the form of a club or irregularly lobed.

When PELLEGRIN created this new genus, only flowers with lateral petals in simple funnel shape were known, hence the specific name; it was only later that LEINFELLNER observed lateral petals with excrescences and that the study of other flowers from the Le Testu 6541 specimen showed lateral petals of Pancovia type (PL. 15, 15-15). No new material has been matched with the collections of LE TESTU.

Monotypic genus.
