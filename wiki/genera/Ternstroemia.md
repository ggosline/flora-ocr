# *Ternstroemia* Mutis ex L.f. , nom . cons .

**Family**: [[Ternstroemiaceae]]
**Modern family**: **Pentaphylacaceae** — *Ternstroemiaceae* as circumscribed here is superseded; see [[Ternstroemiaceae]]
**Authority**: Mutis ex L.f. , nom . cons .
**Protologue**: Suppl. Pl. : 39, 264 (1782)

## Diagnosis

Trees or shrubs with subopposite or whorled stems. Leaves spiral or whorled, often coriaceous, with entire or rarely crenate margin. Flowers solitary, borne below the leaves, with 2(–4) opposite or subopposite bracteoles; sepals opposite to petals; stamens numerous, filaments of the outer series often fused at the base with the petals; ovary with (1–)2(–3) locules, each locule with (1–)2(–20) pendulous ovules; style 1, with entire or lobed stigma. Fruits with irregular (often explosive) dehiscence, with 4 or sometimes 2–16 seeds. Seeds with opaque testa, smooth or sometimes plicate-rugose, fleshy.

Genus comprising about 100 species, most in tropical and subtropical America and tropical Asia; in Africa two species, one of which in Gabon.

b: Kobuski (1961), Verdcourt (1962), Weitzman et al. (2004).
