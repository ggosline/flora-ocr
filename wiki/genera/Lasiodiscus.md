# Lasiodiscus

**Authority**: Hook. f. (1862) · **Family**: [[Rhamnaceae]]

## Diagnosis

African shrubs or small trees with alternate toothed leaves, small 5-merous flowers, hooded petals and a characteristically hairy disc; fruits drupaceous.

## Key to the species

**1.** Inflorescences in ± compact or divided glomerules, subsessile or with very short peduncle of 1–5 (7) mm.

**2.** Blade olivaceous glabrous below between the main veins, with ± acute base; secondary veins (7) 8–11 pairs arched near their base; stipules 6–11 mm long, with short pubescence

**2′.** Blade rufescent finely pubescent on the entire lower face, with obtuse or rounded base; secondary veins 7–8 pairs, oblique but straight toward their base; stipules 9–12 mm long, very hairy. 2. L. contumax.

**1′.** Inflorescences dichotomously branched with peduncle of 1–10 cm.

**3.** Stamens centrifugal in the open flower; flower 6–10 mm diam.; style more than 1 mm high; pedicel 5–20 mm long; blade oblong, length more than 2.5 times the width; base of blade ± cordate or obtuse, often slightly asymmetrical → [[Lasiodiscus_mannii\|*L. mannii*]]

**3′.** Stamens centripetal in the open flower; flower 4–6 mm diam.; style less than 1 mm high; pedicel 3–6 mm long; blade ovate, length less than 2.5 times the width; base of blade symmetrical rounded to subacute. 4. L. marmoratus.
