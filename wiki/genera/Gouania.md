# Gouania

**Authority**: Jacq. (1763) · **Family**: [[Rhamnaceae]]

## Diagnosis

Climbing shrubs with tendrils, alternate toothed leaves, small 5-merous flowers and dry three-winged schizocarpic fruits.

- [[Gouania_longipetala|G. longipetala]] — the species treated for Gabon.
