## Diagnosis

Sapindaceae monospermatous shrubby or arborescent, sometimes monocaulous (Radlkofera, Chytranthus). Leaves paripinnate. Flowers regular or irregular. Fruit berry-like, indehiscent, furrowed or lobed; seed without aril. Pollen showing great resemblance in the genera Chytranthus and Pancovia; in contrast, that of Placodiscus approaches the Cupanieæ.

TYPE GENUS: Lepisanthes Blume.

The Lepisantheae unite 6 extra-African genera and 6 African genera.

1. Flowers irregular; stamens in unilateral bundle; disk cushion-shaped or crescent-shaped on one side only.

2. Calyx in a deep cup, sinuses of the teeth not reaching the middle; fruit fleshy, rounded, with ± marked ribs; flowers in racemes of cymes; monocaulous.

3. Bracts exceeding the flowers; 6–8 stamens; ovary with 6–7 locules; fruit devoid of ribs; disk red-orange..... 7. RADLKOFERA.
