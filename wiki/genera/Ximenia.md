# *Ximenia* L.

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]] *sensu lato* · **Current placement**: Ximeniaceae

Gen. Pl., ed. 1: 361 (1737).

**Synonyms**: *Heymassoli* Aubl., Pl. Gui.: 324, tab. 325 (1775); *Rottboelia*
Scop., Introd.: 2333 (1777); *Pimecaria* Raf., Alsogr. Am.: 64 (1838).

Type species: *X. americana* L.

A palaeotropical genus of about ten very closely related species. Only two
occur in Africa, and a single one — [[Ximenia_americana|X. americana]] — in
Cameroon and Gabon.

## Diagnosis

Shrubs or small trees, most often spiny. Petiole bearing a yellow-brown
pubescence on its upper part. Blade glabrous on both faces, mucronate at the
apex. Inflorescence an axillary umbelliform or corymbiform raceme. Flowers
tetramerous, female. Calyx small, not accrescent. Corolla of linear, reflexed,
exserted petals with a dense pubescence within. Stamens 8, epipetalous and
alternipetalous, with filiform filaments and linear anthers. Ovary superior,
3–4-locular. Style ending in a small stigma. Drupe ovoid or spherical, the
fruiting calyx persistent. Endocarp crustaceous. Seed solitary, the albumen
oily. Embryo apical.

In the family key *Ximenia* is separated as the spiny **shrub** whose calyx
persists without becoming accrescent, and by its umbellate inflorescence.

## Treatments

### Vol 20 (1973)

**Source**: `sources/Olacaceae_vol20_paddle`

Villiers additionally discusses *Ximenia gabonensis* Lanessan, a name published
in *Plantes utiles des colonies* and said to be abundant on the banks of the
Gabon estuary. No type was indicated and no material could be traced, so the
binomial is left unconfirmed. See the notes on
[[Ximenia_americana|X. americana]].

## Notes

Later classifications refer *Ximenia* to **Ximeniaceae**; the Vol 20 treatment
places it in *Olacaceae*.
