# *Ancistrochilus* Rolfe

**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Authority**: Rolfe
**Protologue**: In This.-Dyer. FTA 7 : 44 (1897)

## Diagnosis

Herbaceous small to medium-sized epiphyte. Pseudobulbs grouped, conical or orbicular, swollen dorsoventrally. Leaves 1 or 2, apical, shortly petiolate, thin, plicate. Inflorescence arising from the base of the pseudobulbs, arched. Flowers few in number, medium-sized, very conspicuous. Sepals subsimilar. Petals narrower and smaller than the sepals. Labellum articulate at the apex of the column foot, distinctly trilobed: median lobe linear or linear-lanceolate, the lateral ones obliquely oblong-ovate. Column foot prominent, not fused to the ovary. Gynostemium slender, arched. Pollinia 8, swollen laterally, ovoid, unequal in form and size, each with prominent apical caudicles. Rostellum recumbent, triangular, channeled, without viscidium. - Pl. 48, p. 103.

Pollination is still unknown in this genus, which comprises 2 species widely distributed from West tropical Africa to Uganda and already collected in Gabon.

1. Median lobe of labellum 8–12 mm long, linear-lanceolate; sepals and petals 1.5–3 cm, pubescent on the outer side, pseudobulbs generally conical or pyriform ..... 1. A. rothschildianus

1'. Median lobe of labellum 16–25 mm long, very slender, almost filiform; sepals and petals 3.5–4.5 cm, glabrous, pseudobulbs generally orbicular, flattened dorsoventrally ..... 2. A. thomsonianus
