# *Schizachyrium* Nees.

**Family**: [[Gramineae]]
**Authority**: Nees.
**Protologue**: Nees, Agrost. Bras.: 331 (1829)

## Diagnosis

Annual or perennial herbs with fine culms. Inflorescence in a false panicle of racemes.

Spikelets in racemes solitary at the apex of the culms or their branches and supported by narrow, scarious or more or less herbaceous spatheoles. Articles of the raceme and pedicels swollen towards the apex and often fairly robust, detaching with the pair of spikelets they bear. Lower flower reduced to a lemma, the upper hermaphrodite in the sessile spikelet, male in the pedicellate spikelet, sometimes reduced or suppressed. Lower glume of the sessile spikelet more or less flattened on the back, bicarinate, each keel terminating in a short tooth or mucro. Upper glume naviculate, keeled at least towards the apex. Lemmas ciliolate, hyaline, lemma of the lower flower with 2 nerves, that of the upper flower bifid or bidentate, sometimes entire, prolonged in the sinus by an awn. Palea minute or absent. Two small lodicules, 3 stamens. Pedicellate spikelet generally wider and more flattened or of reduced size. Glumes membranous, the lower sometimes awned. Lemmas, when present, hyaline, ciliate, muticous.

This genus is essentially characterized by its glumes with distinctly folded margins, its solitary racemes and its awned spikelets. ## Key to the species

1'. Fertile lower lemma of the sessile spikelet shortly bidentate at the apex; lower glume flattened on the back, with fine but distinct nerves; erect perennial herbs with white silky pubescent racemes..... 4. S. thollonii.

Schizachyrium are savanna herbs, which can be found in quite diverse habitats. The ecological requirements of the different species are often quite narrow. The annual species are generally good forage plants, of reduced interest however because of their poor development and their often low density in herbaceous stands.
