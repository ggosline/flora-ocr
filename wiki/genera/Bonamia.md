# *Bonamia* Thouars, nom. cons.

**Family**: [[Convolvulaceae]]
**Authority**: Thouars, nom. cons.
**Protologue**: Hist. vég. îles France : 33, t. 8 (1804)

## Diagnosis

Lianes, sometimes sarmentose shrubs. Leaves simple, entire, petiolate. Inflorescences axillary and/or terminal, in panicles or cymes. Flowers: sepals ± equal, persistent, but not accrescent in fruit; corolla infundibuliform or ± campanulate, white, 5-lobed; stamens generally included, with filiform filaments in the free part, glabrous or partially hairy, anthers elliptic-oblong, pollen grains smooth; disk ± annular to cupuliform; ovary globose to ellipsoid, glabrous, with 2 biovulate locules; styles 2, filiform, often slightly unequal and connate at their base for a variable length, glabrous or hairy, stigmas globose or ± peltate. Fruits 2-locular, with 2–4 seeds. Seeds often orange-red, glabrous.

Pantropical genus with approximately 34 species, well represented in South America, with 10 species in continental Africa and 6 in Madagascar (one in common); in Gabon, 3 species.

B: Breteler (1992), Deroin (2001), Gonçalves (1987), Lejoly & Lisowski (1993), Verdcourt (1963).
