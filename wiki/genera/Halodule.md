# Halodule

**Authority**: Endl., *Gen. pl.* suppl. **1**: 1368 (1841) · **Family**: [[Cymodoceaceae]]

## Diagnosis

Submerged marine herbs with monopodially branching rhizomes, one or more simple roots at each node and short shoots bearing 1–4 leaves. Leaves have two intravaginal scales, a deciduous three-veined blade and a rounded, notched or toothed apex. Solitary terminal flowers are borne on short shoots; male anthers are stalked and attached at different levels, and female flowers are subsessile with simple styles. Fruits are shortly beaked, compressed and stone-walled.
