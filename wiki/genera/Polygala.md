# *Polygala* L.

**Family**: [[Polygalaceae]]
**Authority**: L.
**Protologue**: Sp. Pl. 2 : 701 (1753)

## Diagnosis

Herbs annual or perennial, shrubs, arbustes, rarely small trees (or woody lianes in Madagascar). Stipules absent. Leaves generally alternate, rarely opposite or whorled, sometimes in basal rosette, sessile or shortly petiolate; blade generally linear to lanceolate. Flowers in terminal, axillary or pseudo-lateral racemes, sometimes in spikes or corymbs, rarely solitary; sepals 5, unequal, the 3 outer persistent (caducous in a few American subgenera), the 2 outer and lower equal, free or sometimes connate, the 2 inner larger and petaloid (winged sepals); petals 3(–5), the 2 lateral generally absent or rudimentary, the 2 upper connate to the lower in a keel often fimbriate (crested) or rarely without crest; stamens 8(–10), connate in a tube split, sometimes 6 fertile and 2 staminodes; ovary 2-locular, with 1 ovule per locule; upper branch of stigma papillose, the lower smooth. Fruit capsular, 2-locular, generally winged. Seeds 2, often pilose, with silky indumentum, rarely glabrous or with glochidiate hairs, generally with a 3-lobed or 3-branched caruncle, often with 3 membranous appendages.

Cosmopolitan genus with approximately 750 species, distributed especially in tropical and subtropical regions. The genus is divided into several subgenera, the seven Gabonese species belong to the subgenus Polygala.
