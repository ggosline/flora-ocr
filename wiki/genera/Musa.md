# Musa

Giant herbs whose pseudostem consists of overlapping leaf bases. Large pendent terminal spikes bear female flowers below and male flowers above; fruits are berries. Gabon has introduced, seedless cultivated clones rather than wild *Musa*.

## Parental species discussed

- [[Musa_acuminata|*M. acuminata*]] — source of sweet diploid and triploid clones.
- [[Musa_balbisiana|*M. balbisiana*]] — contributes to plantain and other cooking-banana hybrids.

The old names *M. sapientum* and *M. paradisiaca* were formerly applied respectively to dessert bananas and plantains, but Vol. 9 follows the genome-based classification.
