# *Thomandersia* Baillon

**Family**: [[Acanthaceae]]
**Authority**: Baillon
**Protologue**: H. Baillon, in Hist. Pl. 10 : 456 (1891)

## Diagnosis

≡ Scytanthus T. Anders. ex Benth., in G. Benth. et J.D. Hooker, Gen Pl. 2 : 1093 (1876), et Hook. Ic. Pl. 13 : 8, t. 1209 (1877).

≠ Skytanthus Meyen, Reise 1:376 (1834).

$ \neq $ Scytanthus Hook., Ic. Pl. 7 : 605-606 (1844).

≠ Scytanthus Liebm., Förhandl. Skand. Naturforsk. 4 : 177 (1844).

Shrubs with ± unequal leaves in pairs (anisophylly).

Bracts subulate-acicular; bracteoles scale-like at the base of the calyx, appressed and scarcely visible. Flowers arranged in terminal or sometimes lateral racemes. Calyx regular or slightly zygomorphic due to the presence of one or two glandular bosses located in its upper (adaxial) part; 5 short and broad sepals, ovate-triangular. Corolla zygomorphic with a bilabiate limb, with 5 lobes. 4 didynamous stamens; anthers with two oblong parallel lobes, muticous, inserted at the same level. Ovary with two biovulate locules; style obtuse at the apex; stigma cylindric, subulate, or with two unequal lobes.

Capsule hardened, ± thick, not stipitate, loculicidal; 2 seeds per locule, scaly or verrucose; cotyledons contorted and folded.

Type species: Th. laurifolia (T. Anders. ex Benth.) Baill.

Six species in tropical western and central Africa.

1. Inflorescences longer than the leaves; peduncle always shorter than 1/3 of the inflorescences; corollas 11–20 mm long.

2. Inflorescences up to 25 cm long, curved, loose, slender; flowers scattered, pedicellate, very rarely subsessile; corolla glabrous except for a few very small puberulent scales on the buds, yellowish-green, slightly tinged with grey, 12–14 mm long; lower lip 6.5 mm wide; free part of the filaments finely pubescent; fruit ovate-pyramidal, acuminate at the apex, with thin wall reaching a maximum thickness of 1 mm; seed testa finely and horizontally verrucose, black.

2'. Inflorescences up to 15 cm long, erect, fairly dense; flowers sessile or subsessile; corolla white, finely and densely pubescent, slightly tinged with violet, 16–10 mm long; free part of the filaments glabrous or very weakly papillose; lower lip 11–17 mm wide; fruit irregularly elliptic-rounded, with very thick and woody wall reaching 2.5 mm thickness; seed testa scaly, brown.....2.
