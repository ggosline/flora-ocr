# *Ranalisma* Stapf

**Family**: [[Alismataceae]]
**Authority**: Stapf
**Protologue**: Hooker’s. Icon. Pl. 27 : Tab. 2652 (1901)

## Diagnosis

Perennial herbs, often stoloniferous, with slender roots. Leaves erect, long-petiolate; petiole flattened dorso-ventrally; blade ovate to linear-lanceolate. Flowering scape erect, bearing an umbel of 1–3 flowers accompanied by 2 spathiform bracts. Flowers hermaphroditic, with convex receptacle elongating after anthesis; sepals membranous, reflexed after anthesis; petals caducous; stamens (6–)9–12, with linear filament, anthers basifixed; carpels numerous, free, with 1 basal, anatropous, erect ovule; style terminal, forming a subulate beak. Achenes in a subspheric head, ± obovate (with dorsal wing), laterally compressed, bearing numerous glands, with terminal beak.
