# *Volkameria* L.

**Authority**: L.
**Protologue**: Sp. pl. 2 : 637 (1753)

## Diagnosis

(by Benedict John POLLARD)

Sp. pl. 2 : 637 (1753).

Shrub, sometimes climbing, or woody liana, rarely small tree; indumentum of simple hairs.

Leaves opposite, rarely ternate to subopposite, simple, glabrous. Inflorescences axillary or supra-axillary, sometimes terminal, lax; dichasial cymes, pedunculate, erect, lax; bracts foliaceous; bracteoles subulate or obsolete. Flowers pedicellate, resupinate, slightly asymmetric in bud; calyx actinomorphic, campanulate, with 5 small lobes or sometimes truncate, persistent, generally not accrescent in fruit, scarcely coloured; corolla 5-lobed, subactinomorphic, hypocratériform or with a lower lip turned upward due to resupination, white, sometimes pink or violet, with tube generally slender, with unequal lobes; stamens 4 (or 5), didynamous, longly exsert, anthers basifixed, with parallel thecae, opening by longitudinal slits; ovary imperfectly 4-locular, with white punctiform glands; style terminal, longly exsert, glabrous, apex shortly bifid with equal branches. Fruits a subglobose schizocarp, slightly 4-lobed, separating into 4 woody mericarps, each shaped like a quarter of a pie, with broad scars and one seed.

Genus with approximately 25 to 30 species, widely distributed in tropical regions, of which five in continental Africa and two in Gabon.

Bibl.: Leeratiwong & Chantaranothai (2014), Wearn & Mabberley (2019), Yuan et al. (2010).
