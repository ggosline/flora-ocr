# *Eleusine* Gaertn.

**Family**: [[Gramineae]]
**Authority**: Gaertn.
**Protologue**: GAERTN. Fruct. I : 7, t. I (1788)

## Diagnosis

Herbs annual or perennial with elongate linear leaves. Inflorescences in digitate or sub-digitate racemes formed of glabrous spikelets with 3 or 6 flowers, laterally flattened, sessile, imbricate and alternately arranged in 2 longitudinal series on a single side of a flattened rachis terminated by a normally developed spikelet. Axis of spikelets disarticulating above the glumes and between the flowers. These hermaphrodite; glumes similar, persistent, obtuse or mucronate, keeled, with 3 or 5 nerves. Lemmas similar with 3 well-defined nerves towards the base; paleas somewhat shorter, with 2 winged keels. Two lodicules; 3 stamens; ovary glabrous with distinct styles.

This genus of minor importance comprises about half a dozen species of which two are recorded in Gabon.
