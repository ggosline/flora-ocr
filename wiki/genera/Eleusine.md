# *Eleusine* Gaertn.

**Family**: [[Gramineae]]
**Authority**: Gaertn.
**Protologue**: GAERTN. Fruct. I : 7, t. I (1788)

## Diagnosis

Annual or perennial herbs with elongate linear leaves. Inflorescences in digitate or sub-digitate racemes formed of glabrous spikelets with 3 or 6 flowers, laterally compressed, sessile, imbricate and alternately arranged in 2 longitudinal series on one side only of a flattened rachis terminated by a normally developed spikelet. Axis of spikelets disarticulating above the glumes and between the flowers. Flowers hermaphrodite; glumes similar, persistent, obtuse or mucronate, keeled, with 3 or 5 nerves. Lemmas similar with 3 well-distinct nerves toward the base; paleae somewhat shorter, with 2 winged keels. Two lodicules; 3 stamens; ovary glabrous with distinct styles.

This genus of little importance comprises half a dozen species, two of which are recorded from Gabon.

## Key to the species

- **1.** Racemes slender, less than 1 cm wide, straight, 2.5 to 10 cm long or more; lemmas lanceolate acuminate; ruderal plant very common → [[Eleusine_indica\|*E. indica*]]
- **1′.** Racemes thick, 1 cm wide, straight or slightly curved, about 5 cm long; lemmas obtuse; formerly cultivated plant, doubtless rare today → [[Eleusine_coracana\|*E. coracana*]]
