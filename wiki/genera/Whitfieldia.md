# *Whitfieldia* Hook.

**Family**: [[Acanthaceae]]
**Authority**: Hook.
**Protologue**: Hooker, Bot. Mag. 71, t. 4155 (1845)

## Diagnosis

Shrubs or sometimes lianes. Inflorescences in racemes or panicles with large bracts and bracteoles membranous and more or less coloured. Calyx with 5 deep segments or free at the base, equal or nearly so; corolla campanulate-infundibuliform with tube hairy within, with limb 2-lipped; the upper lip with 2 lobes connate for a long distance, the lower lip with 3 lobes, 4 fertile stamens didynamous included; filaments free from the base; anthers with 2 parallel locules muticous; pollen spherical. Ovary with 2 locules and 2 ovules per locule; stigma obtuse or subcapitate. Capsule contracted into a solid stipe on the lower half, with 2-4 almost glabrous seeds.

Type species: W. lateritia Hook., described from Sierra Leone. Approximately 10 species from tropical Africa.

1. Corolla tube 3.5-4 cm long, erect, narrow, cylindric; corolla whitish-green..... 1. W. longata.

1'. Corolla tube 7-22 mm long, more or less curved, campanulate or funnel-shaped; corolla yellow, yellowish or purple, with purple or brownish-red striae more or less dark.

2. Bracteoles broadly ovate or nearly orbicular 20 × 17 mm; corolla violet purple with darker striae....

2'. Bracteoles ovate, obovate or lanceolate, not exceeding 9 × 8 mm.

3. Corolla dark red-orange with faintly marked striae along the nerves; bracteoles ovate-acuminate; calyx lobes with clearly stipitate glands; leaves reaching 28 cm in length..... 3. W. rutilans.

3'. Corolla yellow streaked with purple striae; glands on calyx lobes sessile; leaves not exceeding 20 cm in length.

4. Branches of the inflorescence glabrous; bracteoles obovate-acuminate, 9 × 7 mm, glabrous; calyx finely glandular; corolla entirely glabrous; corolla tube erect, 15 mm long. 4. W. Thollonii.

4'. Branches of the inflorescence pubescent; bracteoles oblong obtuse, 8 × 3.5 mm, densely pubescent-glandular; calyces pubescent-glandular; corolla pubescent outside and with a ring of hairs at the base; corolla tube inclined, approximately 7 mm long..... 5. W. Le-Testui.
