# *Boehmeria* Jacq.

**Family**: [[Urticaceae]]
**Authority**: Jacq.
**Protologue**: Enum. syst. pl. : 9 (1760)

## Diagnosis

Suffrutescent herbs or shrubs, monoecious or rarely dioecious; young parts generally pubescent; cystoliths punctiform. Stipules lateral, free, or rarely weakly fused in the lower half. Leaves alternate or opposite, almost equal in size and shape or sometimes, in species with opposite leaves, unequal; petiole present, often long; limb symmetric or weakly asymmetric, with cuneate to cordate base, acuminate apex, generally chartaceous to weakly fleshy, darker green above, with variable indumentum, margin dentate; venation almost symmetrically triplinerve. Inflorescences often unisexual, sometimes bisexual, with flowers in small glomerules arranged in lax panicles or in discontinuous spikes; bracts small, caducous. Male flowers (3–)4(–5)-merous; tepals with hooked hairs and sometimes with a slight dorsal thickening; pistillode present. Female flowers: perianth tubular urceolate, narrowly constricted at the apex, with 2 to 4 teeth, surrounding the ovary from which emerges the persistent and filiform stigma. Fruits with a dull grey or brown exocarp, enveloped by the perianth – sometimes partially adnate to it – membranous, persistent and accrescent.

Genus with more than 50 species, widely distributed in the tropical regions of the Old and New World, extending widely into the temperate zone of North America; in Gabon, a single indigenous species and one cultivated naturalized species.

Notes: The traditional distinction between the genera Pouzolzia and Boehmeria is based on the caducous stigma and the perianth strongly longitudinally striate in Pouzolzia. Nevertheless, studies by Wilmot-Dear & Friis (2006, 2013) have shown that the only absolute distinction is as follows: in Pouzolzia, the shiny achene detaches easily at maturity from the persistent perianth; in Boehmeria, the dull achene is long enveloped within the – and often adnate to – persistent perianth. These differences are correlated with differences in the anatomy of the external layers of the achene and of the fruiting perianth (Kravtsova 2009, Kravtsova et al. 2000, 2003).

B: Friis (1993), Kravtsova (2009), Kravtsova et al. (2000, 2003), Wilmot-Dear & Friis (2006, 2013).
