# Dioncophyllum

**Authority**: Baill., Bull. Soc. Linn. Paris **2**(109): 870 (1890) · **Family**: [[Dioncophyllaceae]]

## Diagnosis

Climbing shrub or liana with cylindrical stems, minute peltate scales and erect simple hairs. Leaves are dimorphic: basal rosette leaves lack tendrils, while climbing-stem leaves bear a pair of apical tendrils separated by a mucro. Open elongated inflorescences carry white disc-shaped flowers. Fruits open into five spreading narrow valves; each valve bears 3–5 large, broadly winged seeds on elongating funicles.
