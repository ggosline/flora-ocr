# *Cyrtococcum* Stapf.

**Family**: [[Gramineae]]
**Authority**: Stapf.
**Protologue**: STAPF, F.T.A. IX : 745 (1920)

## Diagnosis

Perennial herbs with decumbent or creeping base, leaves linear to lanceolate.

Spikelets asymmetric, laterally flattened, arranged in panicles and generally lax. Glumes membranous, with 3 or 5 nerves, lower floret neuter, with lemma similar to the upper glume; palea with 2 nerves, sometimes absent. Upper floret hermaphrodite; lemma narrowly navicular, coriaceous, with margins narrowly enrolled, 5 nerves scarcely visible. Palea of similar size and texture. Two lodicules; 3 stamens; styles distinct.
