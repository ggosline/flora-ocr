# *Aeollanthus* Mart. ex Spreng.

**Authority**: Mart. ex Spreng.
**Protologue**: Syst. veg. 2 : 678 (1825)

## Diagnosis

Subshrubs or aromatic herbs, often fleshy. Leaves simple, opposite or alternate, petiolate or sessile. Inflorescences thyrsoid; cymes variously arranged, sessile, 1-flowered, appearing as spiciform racemes arranged in panicles; bracts generally discrete, sometimes with an abaxial glandular structure. Flowers sessile; calyx with tubular base, apex flattened dorso-ventrally, ± straight, circumscissile near the base in fruit, bilabiate, with 2 or 5 lobes, lips unequal, pressed against each other, the upper lip with 3 teeth or truncate, at apex sometimes folded back onto the lower lip in fruit, the lower lip with 2 teeth or truncate; corolla strongly bilabiate, 5-lobed, white, pink or blue, upper lip erect or ascending, 4-lobed with median lobes exceeding the lateral ones, horizontal and concave (elsewhere, not in Gabon, sometimes hooded and becoming reflexed to explosively release the stamens but without a line of thickened tissue at the base), tube curved in the middle or towards the base, with sides parallel at the base, dilating towards the throat, sometimes dorsally gibbous; 4 stamens, declined, free, attached to the corolla at the level of the throat, held on or within the lower lip of the corolla, anthers reniform to orbicular, dorsifixed, with equal thecae; disk 4-lobed, with anterior lobe larger; ovary deeply 4-lobed; style gynobasic, apex bifid, with equal, subulate lobes. Fruits with 4 achenes, glabrous, with small scars, mucilaginous when wet.

Genus with a little more than 40 species in tropical Africa, generally on rocks or thin soils, with a single species widespread in Gabon.

B: Harley et al. (2004), Paton et al. (2009, 2013, 2018), Ryding (1986).
