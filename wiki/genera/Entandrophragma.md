# *Entandrophragma* C.DC.

**Family**: [[Meliaceae]]
**Authority**: C.DC.
**Protologue**: Bull. Herb. Boissier 2 : 582, t. 21 (1894)

## Diagnosis

Trees dioecious, of large size, without or with buttresses or flying buttresses. Leaves in plumes at the extremity of the branches, compound, paripinnate; leaflets opposite or sometimes alternate and the leaf apparently imparipinnate, (2–)4–11(–13) pairs. Inflorescences axillary, paniculate. Flowers 5-merous, functionally unisexual, but with vestiges of the opposite sex well developed; calyx cupuliform, lobed or dentate; petals free, obovate; staminal tube cylindric or urceolate, with lobed margin or deeply divided into 10 laninae, anthers (or antheroid) 10, inserted at the apex of the tube on the lobes or on the laninae; disc-gynophore cushion-shaped cupuliform, at the base fused with the gynoecium; ovary conical, 5-locular, with 4–10 ovules per locule; style with discoid stigma, slightly exceeding the anthers. Fruits capsular, pendulous, fusiform, claviform or cylindric, woody, with basal or apical dehiscence into 5 valves, exposing within the columella (central axis) 5-angular. Seeds directly implanted on the faces of the columella, flattened, with a terminal membranous wing directed toward the base of the capsule.

Genus comprising 10 species limited to continental Africa, of which 5 in Gabon.

Bibliography: Aubréville (1959), Mabberley (2011), Pennington & Styles (1975), Stainer & Gilbert (1958).

**2′.** Leaflets asymmetrical, often falcate, with secondary nerves more spaced and curved; staminal tube entire → [[Entandrophragma_utile\|*E. utile*]]

**3.** Apex of leaflets often with a spoon-shaped acumen (the margins of the mucro folded towards the upper surface); petals mostly glabrous externally; capsule with basal dehiscence → [[#^k4|4]] ^k3

**3′.** Apex of leaflets never mucronate-cuspidate, nor with margins recurved upwards; petals slightly pubescent externally; capsule with apical dehiscence → [[#^k5|5]]

**4′.** Leaflet with midrib on the lower surface glabrous on its flanks; network of veinlets prominent; sometimes with domatia in tufts of hairs at the intersection of the nerves; staminal tube 2–3 mm in length → [[Entandrophragma_congoense\|*E. congoense*]]

**5.** Secondary nerves 6–10 pairs; network of veinlets prominent on both surfaces; staminal tube 2 mm in length → [[Entandrophragma_cylindricum\|*E. cylindricum*]] ^k5

**5′.** Secondary nerves 6–15(–20) pairs; network of veinlets prominent or not; staminal tube more than 2 mm in length → [[#^k6|6]]
