# *Microcharis* Benth.

**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Authority**: Benth.
**Protologue**: Trans. Linn. Soc. London 25 : 297 (1865)

## Diagnosis

Annual or sometimes perennial herbs; indumentum with hyaline hairs, normally with 2 equal branches, vesicular glands sometimes spread on the stems. Stipules subulate to deltoid or oblique-lanceolate to broadly ovate-cordate. Leaves simple, unifoliolate or imparipinnate or rarely paripinnate; stipels absent; leaflets 1 to 9(–13), opposite, blade with visible veins below. Inflorescences axillary or terminal, in often large, open racemes, with 3 to 15(–30) flowers; bracts persistent. Flowers: pedicel (1–)2–6 mm, often spreading; calyx ± half the length of the stamens, the lobes as long or longer than the tube; corolla orange to purple, glabrous, standard narrow at the base, divergent at the claw, wings twisted away from the keel, keel often with lateral appendices, apex furnished with a prolonged rostrum resembling a staminode; stamens diadelphous (9 + 1), with apex curved upward, the vexillary one normally sterile, anthers glabrous, those of the short stamens with hyaline scales at the base; style ⅓ as long as the ovary, thickened and compressed laterally at the articulation, stigma oblique, discoid. Pods erect and at an angle to the pedicel, linear, straight or curved, compressed laterally, with (5–)10 to 30 seeds separated by thin septa, sutures generally enlarged. Seeds quadrangular, brown.

Genus with approximately 33 species from Africa, Madagascar and Arabia; in Gabon, 3 species.

Notes: Previously considered as a subgenus of Indigofera, but reestablished by Schrire (1992).

Bibliography: Gillett (1958, 1971a), Lewis et al. (2005), Schrire (1992, 2012).
