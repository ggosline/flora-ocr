# *Strombosiopsis* Engl.

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]]

Pflanzenfam. ed. 1, **1**: 148 (1897).

A monospecific African genus, found throughout the dense forests from Nigeria
to Angola. Its single species,
[[Strombosiopsis_tetrandra|S. tetrandra]], occurs in Gabon.

## Diagnosis

Tree whose bark secretes a sticky red resin. Leaves with secondary nerves
distinct on the lower face; nervules parallel and distinct beneath.
Inflorescence a short, many-flowered axillary or extra-axillary raceme. Flower
female, actinomorphic, tetramerous. Petals apiculate, fleshy. Stamens 4, the
filament short and broad; anther with longitudinal dehiscence slits. Ovary
semi-inferior, 4-locular. Placentation central. Drupe with an accrescent
pedicel. Remains of the calyx forming 4 teeth at the apex of the fruit.

In the family key *Strombosiopsis* is reached by its tetramerous flower,
semi-inferior to inferior ovary, ellipsoid flower bud, and a fruit terminated
by 4 teeth or lobes — separating it from [[Diogoa]], whose flower is
pentamerous, the ovary superior, the bud globose, and the fruit marked by a
median ridge.
