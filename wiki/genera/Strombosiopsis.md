# *Strombosiopsis* Engl.

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]] *sensu lato* · **Current placement**: Strombosiaceae

Pflanzenfam. ed. 1, **1**: 148 (1897).

An African genus. Vol 20 treats it as **monospecific**, with
[[Strombosiopsis_tetrandra|S. tetrandra]] found throughout the dense forests
from Nigeria to Angola. That is no longer current: Breteler (2001) described
[[Strombosiopsis_sereinii|S. sereinii]] from Gabon as the **third** species of
the genus, the second being a Liberian species he described the same year. Two
of the three therefore occur in Gabon.

## Diagnosis

Tree whose bark secretes a sticky red resin. Leaves with secondary nerves
distinct on the lower face; nervules parallel and distinct beneath.
Inflorescence a short, many-flowered axillary or extra-axillary raceme. Flower
female, actinomorphic, tetramerous. Petals apiculate, fleshy. Stamens 4, the
filament short and broad; anther with longitudinal dehiscence slits. Ovary
semi-inferior, 4-locular. Placentation central. Drupe with an accrescent
pedicel. Remains of the calyx forming 4 teeth at the apex of the fruit.

In the family key *Strombosiopsis* is reached by its tetramerous flower,
semi-inferior to inferior ovary, ellipsoid flower bud, and a fruit terminated
by 4 teeth or lobes — separating it from [[Diogoa]], whose flower is
pentamerous, the ovary superior, the bud globose, and the fruit marked by a
median ridge.

## Notes

**Post-Flore addition.** Breteler (2001, *Novitates Gabonenses* 40) described
[[Strombosiopsis_sereinii|S. sereinii]] from Gabon and provided a key to the
three species then recognised. Vol 20's statement that the genus is
monospecific is therefore superseded.

Later classifications refer *Strombosiopsis* to **Strombosiaceae**; the Vol 20
treatment places it in *Olacaceae*.
