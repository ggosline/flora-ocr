# *Triplisomeris* Aubréville et Pellegrin

**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Authority**: Aubréville et Pellegrin
**Protologue**: A. Aubréville et F. Pellegrin, Bull. Soc. Bot. Fr. 104 : 497 (1957)

## Diagnosis

Shrubs. Very long pendulous inflorescences, very shortly branched. Bracteoles valvate. Calyx with 4 sepals, one of which ± bilobed. Three large subequal petals, 2 others rudimentary. Fertile stamens 3, longly exserted; staminodes present. Velvety brown pods, transversely wrinkled, with thin valves. The fruits of the type species $ T.\ explicans $ open on the tree; the heavy seeds fall and the rolled valves after dehiscence remain attached to the branches.

Type species: Triplisomeris explicans (Baill.) Aubr. et Pellegr., Guinea.

This genus marks a transition between the genus Isomacrolobium, with (4-) 5 (-6) subequal petals and the genus Anthonotha with a single large longly unguiculate petal.

It comprises one Guinean species, T. explicans, distributed from Fouta Djalon in Guinea to western Côte d'Ivoire, and another Liberian one, T. ernae. A 3rd species, poorly known, is described from Gabon, T. triplisomeris.

1. Triplisomeris triplisomeris (Pellegrin) Aubr. et Pellegr. (Pl. 45, p. 193)

A. Aubréville et F. Pellegrin, Bull. Soc. Bot. Fr. 104 : 497 (1957).

= Macrolobium triplisomere Pellegrin, Bull. Soc. Bot. Fr. 88 : 508 (1941); Lég. Gabon : 53 (1948).

= Anthonotha triplisomeris (Pellegrin) Léonard, Mém. Ac. Roy. Belg. 30,2 : 226 (1957).

Shrubs. Branches glabrous. Leaves with 2 pairs of leaflets. Stipules caducous. Rachis 6-8 cm. Leaflets oblong, attenuate then acute at the base, attenuate then acuminate at the apex, 9-15 cm in length by 4-5 cm in width, glabrous. Lateral nerves, 5 pairs, arched and ascending.

Inflorescences pendulous; slender axes 30 cm and more, velvety, bearing on very short branches 1 cm, or directly, fascicles of white flowers. Pedicels 1 cm, velvety. Bracteoles 8 mm long, tawny velvety on both faces. Calyx with 4 oblong lobes 7 mm in length, one of them broader and emarginate at the apex, glabrous with ciliate margins; tube 2 mm. Petals 3, subequal but asymmetric. The median petal, obovate, narrow, attenuate into a long unguis, 15 × 5 mm; the 2 lateral ones similar, but slightly deflected on either side of the axial petal; two petals absent or rudimentary. Fertile stamens 3. Ovary hairy, shortly stipitate, 8-9 ovules.

Holotype: Le Testu 5792, upper Ngounié, Gabon (P).

Shrubby species known only from the type from central Gabon.
