# *Adenanthera* Linné

**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Authority**: Linné
**Protologue**: Sp. Pl.: 384 (1753)

## Diagnosis

- Gonsii ADANS., Fam. 2: 318 (1763).

- Pongelion ADANS., Fam. 2: 319 (1763). – Stachychrysum BoJ., Hort. Maurit.: 114 (1837). Tree with bipinnate, multifoliolate leaves. Inflorescences elongate, in spikes or panicles, terminal. Flowers ♢ or polygamous, pentamerous, white or yellow. Calyx campanulate. Petals free or united to each other. Stamens 10, free, anthers with connective prolonged by a caducous gland. Ovary sessile. Legume linear, curved or falciform, flattened or turgid around the seeds, with 2 valves, often rolled at maturity, divided between the seeds by a septum. Seeds thin with hard seed coats, red or bicoloured, enclosed in a thin pulp. TYPE SPECIES: A. pavonina L.

This genus contains 11 species from tropical and subtropical regions from Asia to northern Australia and Polynesia. In Africa one introduced and known species.
