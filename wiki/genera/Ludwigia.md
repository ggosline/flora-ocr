# Ludwigia

**Authority**: L., *Sp. pl.* **1**: 118 (1753) · **Family**: [[Onagraceae]]

## Diagnosis

Usually hygrophilous herbs, sometimes woody at base, often producing aerating roots in wet habitats. Leaves are simple and usually alternate. Axillary flowers are solitary or in short racemes, generally 4- or 5-merous, with yellow petals and an inferior ovary. The elongated capsule contains numerous seeds, free or enclosed by endocarp fragments.

## Key to the species

- **1.** Stems bearing either fusiform, spongy floats (terrestrial forms), or fine, elongated spongy roots forming clusters becoming black when dry (aquatic forms); sepals 5(–6) → couplet **2**
- **1′.** Absence of stems bearing either fusiform floats or fine, elongated roots; sepals 4(–5) → couplet **3**
  - **2.** Seeds firmly embedded in coherent woody endocarp, pendulous, appearing as bosses in the capsule wall, distant by ± 1.5 mm; narrow wings of the sinuses between the sepals not extending onto the ovary; blade with 6 to 12 secondary veins on each side of the main vein; petals pale yellow with bright yellow basal spot → L. adscendens subsp. diffusa
  - **2′.** Seeds vaguely integrated into horseshoe-shaped pieces of endocarp, horizontal, appearing as bosses in the capsule wall distant by ± 0.5 mm; narrow wings of the sinuses between the sepals extending to the upper part of the ovary; blade with 11 to 20 secondary veins on each side of the main vein; petals bright yellow → [[Ludwigia_leptocarpa\|*L. leptocarpa*]]
    - **3.** Ovary/fruit with multiseriate ovules/seeds in each locule → couplet **4**
    - **3′.** Ovary/fruit with uniseriate ovules/seeds in each locule, sometimes uniseriate in the basal half and multiseriate toward the apex → couplet **6**
      - **4.** Plant pubescent to hirsute; petals emarginate at the apex; ovary/fruit puberulent to hirsute, at least on the sutures → couplet **5**
      - **4′.** Plant glabrous; petals rounded at the apex; ovary/fruit glabrous to glabrescent → [[Ludwigia_erecta\|*L. erecta*]]
        - **5.** Seed with raphé (part of the funicle adnate to the seed body) swollen and ± as wide as the seed; fruit puberulent; blade with lower surface light green → [[Ludwigia_octovalvis\|*L. octovalvis*]]
        - **5′.** Seed with raphé not swollen, distinctly narrower than the seed; fruit hirsute; blade with lower surface glaucescent, greenish-grey → L. stenorraphe subsp. stenorrhaphe
          - **6.** Plant glabrous or only the leaf margin and median vein of young leaves covered with very short hairs; petals 1–3.5 mm long; flowers and fruits subsessile or with pedicel reaching up to 3 mm, bearing bractéoles reduced to reddish glands → couplet **7**
          - **6′.** Plant covered with pubescence of short hairs mixed with longer erect hairs; petals 5–6 mm long; flowers and fruit with pedicel of (2–)3–15 mm, bearing 2 oval bractéoles → [[Ludwigia_africana\|*L. africana*]]
            - **7.** Plant glabrous except on the leaf margin and median vein of young leaves covered with very short hairs; stamens 4; ovary/fruit with uniseriate ovules/seeds in each locule → [[Ludwigia_abyssinica\|*L. abyssinica*]]
            - **7′.** Plant glabrous; stamens 8; ovary/fruit with uniseriate ovules/seeds in the basal half and multiseriate toward the apex of each locule → [[Ludwigia_hyssopifolia\|*L. hyssopifolia*]]
