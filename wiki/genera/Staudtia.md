# *Staudtia* Warb.

**Family**: [[Myristicaceae]]
**Authority**: Warb.

## Diagnosis

Trees with subcoriaceous, glabrous, petiolate leaves, the blade oval-elliptic with 5–10 pairs of secondary veins. Flowers dioecious, sessile, the male ones infundibuliform, the female ones subglobose with 3–4 lobes: filaments fused into a column bearing 3–4 linear anthers equalling the filaments in length. Stigma sessile; fruit with fleshy pericarp, with seed provided with a thick woody testa, entire aril, albumen not ruminate.

Stapf (F.T.A. 6, 1:156) gives the key to the 3 species of Warburg:

## Key to the species


**1′.** Petiole with narrow and deep upper canal; fruit not stipitate → S. gabonensis. PL. XXV. — 1 to 7, Staudtia gabonensis Warb.: 1, leaf of 11 cm; 1', young fruits of 1 cm (N. Hallé 1819); 2, branchlet with capitules 5', diam. 5 mm; 3, capitule 5 in half-section; 3', fl. 5; 3', section, 3', top of androecium (Le Testu 1895); 4, capitule 5 diam. 15 mm, fl. knotted; 5, half-section; 5', fl. 5; 5", its section; 5", hairs of the ovary and pedicel (SRFK 2362); 6, fr. of 24 mm (Le Testu 4570); 7, seed of 18 mm in the aril, dorsal face; 7', rapheal side; 7", section (Klaine 565bis after Pierre St. Niohue). — 8 to 11, Pycnanthus Marchalianus Ghesq.; 8, folded leaf, of 15 cm, lower face; 9, large fruit: 35 mm long; 9, seed in the aril (Letouz 5059); 10, fl. 8 and its section 10'; 11, fl. 5 and its section 11'; 11" fl. with 4 sepals (after Gilbert and Troupin, Fl. du Congo b.). Hutchinson and Dalziel (F.W.T.A.: 60) place S. gabonensis and S. stipitata in synonymy, retaining only the latter binome. The key is thus reduced to:

**1.** Fruit 2-3.5 cm long by 1.4-1.8 cm wide, with peduncle 2-8 mm. ^k1

**1.** S. stipitata = S. gabonensis.

**1′.** Fruit 5 cm long by 3-5 cm wide, with peduncle 2-4 mm. Gilbert and Troupin (Flore du Congo Belge: 394) attribute to S. gabonensis non-stipitate fruits as does Stapf but contrary to Warburg's description. The herbarium of the Muséum de Paris does not possess types of these species because the Griffon du Bellay specimen no. 5 that served Baillon to establish the diagnosis of Myristica Niohue (Baillon, Adansonia 9: 79) = Pycnanthus Niohue (Baill) Warb. is a mixture of leaves of Staudtia gabonensis and fruits of Pycnanthus. The seed Duparquet 74 given as second type by Baillon does not constitute material of great taxonomic value. As about twenty specimens of Staudtia grouped in the Muséum de Paris show fruiting peduncles varying from 2 to 8 mm long, and as no fruit corresponds to the description of that of St. kamerunensis Warb., only St. gabonensis will be discussed here.
