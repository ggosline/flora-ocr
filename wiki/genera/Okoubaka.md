# *Okoubaka* Pellegr. & Normand

**Family**: [[Octoknemaceae]] *sensu lato* · **Current placement**: Santalaceae

Bull. Soc. Bot. Fr. **93**: 139 (1946).

A genus of a single species and one variety. The generic name is West African
in origin.

## Diagnosis

Monoecious trees. Young branchlets with a russet pubescence of simple, stellate
and forked hairs. Leaves alternate, sometimes subopposite or opposite. Petiole
articulate. Inflorescence racemes or panicles, solitary or fascicled, borne on
the **old branchlets and branches** and pendulous. Male and female flowers
shortly pedicellate, pentamerous. Disc cupular. Petals erect. Stamens
epipetalous. Ovary inferior, unilocular, with 3–4 ovules. Placenta central,
erect. Drupe one-seeded, sessile, **9–15 cm**. Mesocarp fleshy and fibrous.
Endocarp furrowed, smooth within. Seeds fused on one side to the endocarp.

The cauline inflorescence and the very large drupe separate *Okoubaka* at once
from [[Octoknema]], whose racemes are axillary, whose drupe is 1.5–3 cm, and
whose endocarp bears internal digitations.

## Notes

Later classifications place *Okoubaka* in **Santalaceae** or, following the
sinking of Octoknemaceae, in **Erythropalaceae**; the Vol 20 treatment keeps it
in *Octoknemaceae*.
