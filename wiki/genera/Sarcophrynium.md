# *Sarcophrynium* K. Schum.

**Family**: [[Marantaceae]]
**Authority**: K. Schum.

## Diagnosis

Erect herbs, glabrous or pubescent in tufts formed of a certain number of distichous leaves with sheaths tightly imbricated at the base of a central axis terminated by a leaf and bearing the inflorescence laterally. Leaves with elliptic limb more or less elongated, often slightly cordate at the base and somewhat asymmetric. Callus more or less swollen but passing to the petiole without articulation marked by a furrow. Passage to the limb continuous on the lower surface but marked on the upper surface by a V-shaped step.

Inflorescences spiciform or paniculate with more or less long internodes bearing persistent bracts axilling fascicles of 4 or 5 biflorous cymes, each furnished with its adaxial spathebracts. Pedicels of the flowers very slightly unequal, each bearing a fleshy, acuminate, smooth bracteole. Calyx with free sepals, petals fused into a tube at the base. Two external petaloid staminodes, obovate. Ovary trilocular, glabrous; fruits fleshy, indehiscent, with weakly marked sutures. Seeds with section roughly rhombic or hexagonal, without an aril.

Type species: Sarcophrynium prionogonium (K. Schum.) K. Schum.

This genus comprises 4 or 5 species, all from the forest regions of tropical Africa.
