# Vepris

**Family**: [[Rutaceae]]

## Diagnosis

Unarmed shrubs or trees with 1–3-foliolate leaves and oil-gland-dotted blades. Flowers are 4-merous; the unidentified Gabon material has eight stamens.

## Taxon treated

- [[Vepris_sp|*Vepris* sp.]] — known only from male material in Vol. 6.
