# *Stolzia* Schltr.

**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Authority**: Schltr.
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. Syst. 53 : 564 (1915)

## Diagnosis

Small epiphyte or rarely lithophyte. Pseudobulbs asymmetric, fleshy, 1–2-foliate. Leaves fleshy or coriaceous, erect to spreading. Inflorescence terminal, sometimes lateral, erect, 1–n-flowered. Flowers small, resupinate, swollen at the base. Sepals and petals dissimilar. Lateral sepals united at the base. Labellum entire, curved, transverse section Y-shaped, articulate at the foot of the column which is prominent, long, stout and slightly curved upward. Pollinia 8, claviform, unequal in size, each with an apical sticky caudicle. Rostellum truncate, short, sub-erect to declined. Viscidium single, relatively large, thick, fleshy. – Pl. 54, p. 115.

No information on pollination in this genus, but the color, size, and floral structure indicate that it is ensured by the same group of pollinators as in Bulbophyllum.

Genus of 15 species from tropical Africa of which 1 concerns Gabon.
