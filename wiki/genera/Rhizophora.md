# *Rhizophora* L.

**Family**: [[Rhizophoraceae]]
**Authority**: L.
**Protologue**: Sp. pl. 1 : 443 (1753)

## Diagnosis

Trees or shrubs of mangroves with stilt roots. Stipules well developed.

Leaves opposite, coriaceous, lower surface scattered with very small subereous protuberances. Inflorescences in the form of bipar cymes or sometimes uniflorous. Flowers 4-merous, supported by partially fused bracteoles (bracteolar cup); calyx with fleshy, persistent sepals; petals delicate; stamens 8–16, (sub)sessile, with multilocular anthers, dehiscing longitudinally by a ventral flap; disk thick; ovary semi-inferior, 2-locular, with axillary placentation, 2 ovules per locule; style

---

simple with ± bilobed stigma. Fruit ovoid-pyriform, generally with a single developed seed. Seeds germinating on the tree, the hypocotyl piercing the apex of the fruit; cotyledons fused into a tube remaining attached to the fruit after liberation of the seedling, the latter bearing stipules that protect the terminal bud.

Pantropical genus comprising about ten species, of which 3 in West Africa, as well as in Gabon.

Taxonomic note: The 3 species of Gabon are part of a species complex and appear to interbreed with one another, R. harrisonii being the hybrid between R. mangle and R. racemosa. They grow in the tidal zone, along a salinity gradient; from marine salt water to brackish water inland (Keay 1953, Savory 1953, Breteler 1969). It is there that R. racemosa is found, giving the largest trees of the genus in Gabon.

Bibliography

b: Breteler (1969, 2006), Keay (1953, 1954), Lewis (1956), Liben (1987).

## Key to the species

**1.** Inflorescences uniflorous or in the form of bipinnate cymes with 1–2 bifurcations, resulting in 1, 2 or 4 flowers → [[Rhizophora_mangle\|*R. mangle*]]

**1′.** Inflorescences in the form of multiflorous bipinnate cymes with (3–)4–5 bifurcations and more than 8 (normally ± 32) flowers → couplet **2**

**2.** Inflorescences subglobose, with 5–6 bifurcations at ± right angle; floral buds ellipsoid, the apex straight; tree able to reach 20–25 m in height → [[Rhizophora_racemosa\|*R. racemosa*]]

**2′.** Inflorescences subumbelliform, with (3–)4–6 bifurcations at an angle of 30–60°; floral buds narrowly ovoid, the apex often slightly curved; small tree or shrub up to 5 m in height → [[Rhizophora_harrisonii\|*R. harrisonii*]]
