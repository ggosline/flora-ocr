# *Haumaniastrum* P.A.Duvign. & Plancke

**Authority**: P.A.Duvign. & Plancke
**Protologue**: Biol. Jaarb. 27 : 224 (1959)

## Diagnosis

Herbs annual or perennial, sometimes woody at base, or suffrutex with stems borne by a corky trunk, a woody rhizome or a taproot, with simple hairs; stems quadrangular or rounded-quadrangular. Leaves opposite, whorled by three or four, sometimes in a rosette at the base of annual stems; upper leaves subtending the floriferous heads, of form similar to the lower ones, but often coloured, sometimes very caudate at the apex. Inflorescences condensed into capitules arranged in a corymb or a loose or dense panicle; bracts small, often coloured at the base, imbricate, concealing the pedicels and calyces, subtending condensed cymes of (1 to)3(to 5) flowers; bracteoles absent. Flowers very shortly pedicellate; calyx bilabiate, curved upward in fruit, dorsi-ventrally flattened, opening slightly at the throat during maturation of the achenes, upper lip entire to 3-toothed, cucullate or plane, lower lip entire to 2-toothed, shorter, equal to or more rarely longer than the upper one; corolla short, bilabiate, with straight tube but dorsally gibbous in the middle, dilating toward the throat, upper lip 3- or 4-lobed, lower lip horizontal, entire, slightly concave; stamens 4, declined, slightly exserted from the throat, filaments of the upper pair dilated at the base, attached to the corolla ± at the level of the midpoint, swollen at the base, shortly pubescent, lower pair fixed at the throat, anthers reniform to orbicular, dorsifixed, with equal thecae; disk 4-lobed, with lower lobe larger; ovary glabrous, deeply 4-lobed; style gynobasic, apex bifid with linear lobes. Fruits: achenes finely longitudinally striate, with small scars.

Genus with 35 species in tropical Africa and Madagascar; one in Gabon.

B: Duvigneaud & Plancke (1959), Morton (1963), Paton (1997a).
