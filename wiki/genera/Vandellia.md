# *Vandellia* L.

**Family**: [[Scrophulariaceae]]
**Modern family**: **Linderniaceae** — *Scrophulariaceae* as circumscribed here is superseded; see [[Scrophulariaceae]]
**Authority**: L.
**Protologue**: Mant. pl. 1 : 12 (1767)

## Diagnosis

Herbs annual or perennial; stem ascending or prostrate (also erect outside Gabon), with numerous ramifications (paraclades) or little branched, glabrous or pubescent, quadrangular. Leaves opposite, cauline, sessile or shortly petiolate; limb orbicular to ovate or lanceolate, with margin dentate, crenate or entire; venation pinnate. Inflorescences terminal or sometimes axillary, in lax or dense raceme, each floriferous node bearing 1 or 2 flowers. Flowers pedicellate; calyx with 5 equal lobes; corolla with upper lip entire or bipartite, lower lip tripartite; stamens 4, the anterior (abaxial) ones with filaments incurved in Z-shape and forming geniculate appendages covered with glandular hairs and papillae, filaments of the posterior (adaxial) stamens straight, anthers with 2 equal thecae; ovary glabrous; stigma bilobed. Fruits capsular, septicide-septifragous. Seeds with alveolate albumen, with small round cavities formed by the endothelium (bothriospermy).

## Key to the species

- **1.** Corolla cream-white with small violet spots; capsule 8–10(–12) mm in length, exceeding the calyx by one-third of its length → [[Vandellia_diffusa\|*V. diffusa*]]
- **1′.** Corolla pale violet or pale pink with lower lip blue; capsule 13–25 mm in length, at least 2 times longer than the calyx → couplet **2**
  - **2.** Limb obovate to ovate-triangular, 20–40 × 10–25 mm; corolla pale violet; capsule 13–14 mm in length → [[Vandellia_senegalensis\|*V. senegalensis*]]
  - **2′.** Limb ovate to orbicular, 10–18 × 8–13 mm; corolla pale pink with lower lip blue; capsule 18–25 mm in length → [[Vandellia_vogelii\|*V. vogelii*]]
