# Quassia

**Authority**: L., *Species Plantarum*, ed. 2: 553 (1762) · **Family**: [[Simaroubaceae]]

## Diagnosis

Shrubs or small trees with imparipinnate leaves and a partly winged rachis. Flowers 5-merous; petals contorted; stamens 10 with hairy basal ligules; disc cushion-shaped; five free carpels with united styles. The African species has white flowers and small ribbed fruits.
