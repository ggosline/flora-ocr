# *Crinum* L.

**Family**: [[Amaryllidaceae]]

Bulbous herbs with linear to strap-shaped leaves and a solid scape. Involucral
bracts 2; flowers fewer than 15, white or white marked with red, with a long
perianth tube. Fruit irregular and indehiscent; seeds numerous, greenish and
irregularly shaped.

## Species treated

- [[Crinum_purpurascens|*C. purpurascens*]]
- [[Crinum_natans|*C. natans*]]
- [[Crinum_jagus|*C. jagus*]]
- [[Crinum_zeylanicum|*C. zeylanicum*]]

## Probable hybrid: *Crinum* sp. A

A probable *C. jagus* × *C. natans* hybrid is described separately but not
included in the four accepted-species count. Bulb globose, c. 5 cm diameter;
leaves in a basal rosette, firm and almost succulent, erect, canaliculate, c.
40 × 2–2.5 cm. Scape 25–45 cm; bracts subtending 1–2 sessile, pleasantly
scented flowers. Perianth tube greenish, erect, 12–14 cm; segments pure white
with greenish apex, narrowly lanceolate, 6–7 × 1.5–2.5 cm. Fruit green,
subglobose, with a 3–4 cm beak.

Plants from small islands at the mouth of the Nyong in Cameroon (*Nordal 978*)
combine characters of *C. jagus* and *C. natans*. Similar Gabon material is
*Davies J.N. 326* (K), previously misidentified as *C. biflorum*.
