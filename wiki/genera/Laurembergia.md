# Laurembergia

**Authority**: P.J.Bergius, *Descr. Pl. Cap.*: 350 (1767) · **Family**: [[Haloragaceae]]

## Diagnosis

Semi-terrestrial or aquatic herbs with prostrate or ascending stems rooting at the lower nodes. Leaves are usually opposite and entire or toothed. Axillary fascicles contain combinations of bisexual, male and female flowers; the four-locular ovary develops only one ovule. The fruit is a one-seeded nutlet, often with four or eight tuberculate ribs.
