# *Securidaca* L., nom. cons.

**Family**: [[Polygalaceae]]
**Authority**: L., nom. cons.
**Protologue**: Syst. Nat. , éd. 10, 2 : 1151, 1155 (1759)

## Diagnosis

Trees or small shrubs, generally sarmentose, or lianes. Stipules glandular or absent. Leaves alternate. Flowers axillary or in terminal racemes or in panicles; bracts and bracteoles small, caducous; sepals 5, unequal, free, the 2 inner (lateral) ones larger and petaloid (winged sepals); petals 3 (–5), and then the 2 lateral ones rudimentary, the lower one keel-shaped, without crest or appendages; stamens 8, partially connate in an attenuate sheath; ovary with 2 locules, one aborted, with 1 ovule per locule. Fruit samaroid, 1-locular, with one wing, sometimes with a second rudimentary one. Seed 1, without caruncle or endosperm.

Genus with approximately 80 species, most in America, approximately 8 in Indo-Malesia and two in Africa, one of which in Gabon.

B: Johnson (1955).
