# Costus
**Family in Vol. 9**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Current placement**: Costaceae

Non-aromatic rhizomatous herbs with closed leaf sheaths, usually spirally arranged leaves, cone-like spikes, a large showy labellum and three-locular capsular fruit. The treatment recognises 17 species in Gabon and separately describes two varieties.

## Ecology
Most are herbs of forest understorey, regrowth and wet places; some are epiphytes. *C. spectabilis* is the exceptional savanna species.
