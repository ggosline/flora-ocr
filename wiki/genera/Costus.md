# Costus
**Family in Vol. 9**: [[Zingiberaceae]] · **Current placement**: Costaceae

Non-aromatic rhizomatous herbs with closed leaf sheaths, usually spirally arranged leaves, cone-like spikes, a large showy labellum and three-locular capsular fruit. The treatment recognises 17 species in Gabon and separately describes two varieties.

## Key to the species

**1.** Acaulescent plants; 4 leaves in rosette pressed to the ground; yellow flowers → [[Costus_spectabilis\|*C. spectabilis*]]

**1′.** Caulescent plants, erect or creeping, with one or several leaves.

**2.** A single leaf; creeping stems, white flowers. 2. C. Englerianus.

**2′.** Several leaves distributed along the entire stem or grouped towards the apex; erect stems.

**3.** Inflorescence on a stem distinct from the leafy stem.

**4.** Robust plants, leafy stems 1 m tall or more, spikes with numerous flowers, plants glabrous.

**5.** White flowers more or less spotted with pink or yellow.

**6.** Bracts each axilling a single flower, white, with labellum spotted with yellow → [[Costus_albus\|*C. albus*]]

**6′.** Bracts each axilling two flowers, white with labellum spotted with pink → [[Costus_maculatus\|*C. maculatus*]]

**5′.** Flowers of reddish-brown tint, with yellow labellum, each bract axilling a single flower → [[Costus_maboumiensis\|*C. maboumiensis*]]

**4′.** Slender plants, leafy stems a few decimetres tall, variously pubescent; inflorescences subsessile with few flowers.

**7.** Ligule membranous, bilobed, reaching several centimetres long; leaves green, pubescent on both faces; flowers with labellum with fimbriate margins and pink-tinged. → [[Costus_ligularis\|*C. ligularis*]]

**7′.** Ligule truncate, up to 5 mm long.

**8.** Leaves glabrous or sparsely pubescent on the upper face, slightly appressed-pubescent and violaceous on the lower face; flowers with labellum not fimbriate, pink and white tinged → [[Costus_tappenbeckianus\|*C. tappenbeckianus*]]

**8′.** Both faces of the blade as well as the sheaths and petioles brown-pubescent; ligule short; labellum with fimbriate margins pink and yellow tinged → 8. C. phaeotrichus. Inflorescences and leaves borne on the same stem.

**9.** Lateral inflorescences; ligule narrowly and elongately tubular; membranous, reddish.

**10.** Leaves narrowly white-pink with purple macules → [[Costus_letestui\|*C. letestui*]]

**10′.** Leaves broadly lanceolate (12–17 × 6–8 cm); labellum yellow with red macules → [[Costus_lateriflorus\|*C. lateriflorus*]]

**9′.** Terminal inflorescences.

**11.** Bracts all prolonged by a foliaceous appendage.

**12.** Leaves grouped at the apex of the stem; plants glabrous with pink flowers → [[Costus_nudicaulis\|*C. nudicaulis*]]

**12′.** Leaves distributed along the entire length of the stem.

**13.** Slender plants, a few decimetres tall; bracts glabrous or nearly so; flowers with pink and yellow labellum; ligule membranous, long, bilobed.

**14.** Leaves 10 to 15 cm long → [[Costus_fissiligatus\|*C. fissiligatus*]]

**14′.** Leaves 20 to 22 cm long. → 12 bis C. fissiligatus var. major.

**13′.** Robust plants, several metres tall; bracts distinctly pubescent.

**15.** Calyx splitting laterally; labellum pink. 13. C. Dewevrei.

**15′.** Calyx not splitting laterally, labellum yellow → [[Costus_gabonensis\|*C. gabonensis*]]

**11′.** Bracts devoid of foliaceous appendages, except sometimes those at the base of inflorescences.

**16.** Herb up to 1.5 m tall, pubescent; ligule membranous, bilobed; bracts each axilling one flower, the lower ones prolonged by a foliaceous appendage → [[Costus_ligularis\|*C. ligularis*]]

**16′.** Robust herbs reaching several metres tall; ligule short, not membranous.

**17.** Bracts each axilling two flowers; bracts green more or less bordered with red.

**18.** Separation sheath-ligule marked by a ciliate crown entirely surrounding the sheath; calyx distinctly longer than the bracts; corolla white, labellum white with red margins and with a yellow median line.

**19.** Leaves silvery-pubescent on the lower face.

**19′.** Leaves glabrous or nearly so on the lower face. ……15 bis. C. lucanusianus var. major.

**18′.** Separation sheath-ligule marked by more or less distinct thickening, but not ciliate and not entirely surrounding the sheath; calyx shorter or as long as the bracts; leaves glabrous or nearly so, flowers as above → [[Costus_afer\|*C. afer*]]

**17′.** Bracts each axilling a single flower; bracts brightly coloured violet-purple → [[Costus_violaceus\|*C. violaceus*]]

## Ecology
Most are herbs of forest understorey, regrowth and wet places; some are epiphytes. *C. spectabilis* is the exceptional savanna species.
