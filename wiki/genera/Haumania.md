# *Haumania* J. Léonard

**Family**: [[Marantaceae]]
**Authority**: J. Léonard

## Diagnosis

Climbing plants, branched, with stems sometimes spiny. Leaves with petiole long-sheathing at the base, imbricate, alternate. Callous and non-callous portions of the petiole continuous, not separated by an annular furrow. Transition from petiole to midvein marked by a prominent beak on the upper surface, but continuous on the lower surface of the leaf. Lamina ovate-oblong, broadly rounded at the base, more or less longly acuminate, symmetric or nearly so with respect to the midvein.

Inflorescences in racemes of cymes, supported at the base by long aphyllous sheaths. Main axis zigzag, bearing at each node a large abaxial bract more or less persistent. Each bract encloses a fascicle of 3 to 6 biflorous cymes each borne by an adaxial spathe-bract. Common peduncle of cymes short, floral pedicels lacking bracteoles. Sepals petaloid, corolla tubular at the base; staminodes petaloid. Ovary with three uniovulate locules, densely hairy.

Capsular fruit, indehiscent, tuberculate. Seeds without aril. Perisperm canal in median cross-section U-shaped or H-shaped.

Type species: Haumania Liebrechtsiana (De Wild. et Th. Dur.) J. Léonard. This genus currently comprises three species, localized in the two Congos, Gabon, Cameroon and Spanish Guinea. Haumania Leonardiana has been recorded thus far only in the central Congolese basin. The two other species have been collected in Gabon.
