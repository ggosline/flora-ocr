# *Gilletiodendron* Vermoesen

**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Authority**: Vermoesen
**Protologue**: Vermoesen, Man. Ess. For. Congo Belge : 85 (1923)

## Diagnosis

Leaflets alternate, sometimes subalternate; punctate with ± translucent dots; secondary veins generally traced to a marginal vein. Buds scaly. Panicles. Bracteoles small, caducous, not enveloping the bud. Flowers distichous. Receptacle cupuliform, very short. Sepals 4, subvalvate, reflexed. Petals 5, equal. Stamens 10, free. Ovary glabrous, borne on a short stipe inserted at the base of the receptacle. Ovules 2-5. Fruits dehiscent, with valves sometimes verrucose.

Type species: G. Mildbraedii (Harms) Vermoesen.

5 species: one that may be considered a relic of the sandstones of Kita (Mali), G. glandulosum (Port.) Léonard, in Sudanese wooded savanna regions.

One species from Cameroon is known only from the description of the type destroyed in Berlin, G. Escherichii (Harms) Léonard.

One species exists in northern Côte d'Ivoire which is rather closer to G. kisantuence than to G. Pierreanum; it is known so far only from sterile specimens.

1. Leaflets numerous (14-28), small, 1-4 cm long by 0.5-1.5 cm wide …… G. Mildbraedii.

1'. Leaflets 4-12, 1.5-9 × 0.6-4 cm.

2. 6-8 leaflets; buds glabrous except at the apex..... 1. G. Pierreanum.

2'. 8-12 leaflets; buds pubescent..... 2. G. Kisantuense.
