# *Cyclocotyla* Stapf

**Family**: [[Apocynaceae]]
**Authority**: Stapf
**Protologue**: Bull. Misc. Inform. Kew 1908 : 259 (1908)

## Diagnosis

Lianes with white latex, without tendrils. Leaves opposite; petiole with colleters at the axil. Inflorescences terminal and axillary, thyrsoid, with numerous dichasial flowers.

Flowers 5-merous, actinomorphic except for unequal calyx lobes; calyx with lobes bearing rows of colleters inside; corolla with lobes inflexed at the tip, deformed and overlapping to the left in bud; stamens inserted almost at the base of the corolla tube, with anthers sessile, introrse, completely fertile; disk annular, weakly lobed with lobes alternating, 5 larger and 5 smaller; ovary syncarpous, 2-locular, ovules 2 to 6, in 1 or 2 series; pistil with basal part of the style elliptic with longitudinal striae, apical part laterally compressed, bifid in the open flower. Fruits as berries with two seeds. Seeds oblong; endosperm completely surrounding the embryo; embryo spatulate, much shorter than the seed.

Monotypic genus, limited to tropical Africa.

B: Beentje (2021), Endress et al. (2019), van der Ploeg (1985).
