# *Petersianthus* Merrill.

**Family**: [[Lecythidaceae]]
**Authority**: Merrill.
**Protologue**: Philipp. J. Sci., C Bot. 11 (3) : 200 (1916)

## Diagnosis

Trees with deciduous foliage; trunk not or slightly enlarged at the base, reaching large sizes. Leaves clustered at the apex of the branchlets. Inflorescences in terminal panicles. Flowers actinomorphic, 4-merous; sepals 4, imbricate; petals 4; stamens numerous, connate at the base, all fertile, anthers basifixed; disk present; ovary inferior, 2- or 4-locular, with axile placentation; style filiform, with small stigma. Fruits capsular, 4-winged, indehiscent. Seeds with folded cotyledons and long radicle.

Genus comprising two species, one endemic to the Philippines, the other in Africa, from Guinea to Angola.
