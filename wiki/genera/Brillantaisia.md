# *Brillantaisia* P. Beauv.

**Family**: [[Acanthaceae]]
**Authority**: P. Beauv.
**Protologue**: Palisot de Beauvois, Fl. Oware 2 : 67, t.C, fig. 2 (1818)

## Diagnosis

Herbaceous plants or undershrubs. Petiole winged. Leaves simple, cordate.

Inflorescences in panicles, racemes or spikes furnished with bracts; flowers red or violet. Calyx with 5 sepals, the upper one longer than the others. Corolla with short, thick tube, with transverse fold, with bilabiate limb, with upper lip falciform bifid; lower lip trifid. 2 stamens, or 4 stamens of which the anterior ones sterile; staminodes shorter, fertile anthers with 2 parallel locules, muticous. Annular disk. Ovary multilocular; style not exsert with 2 unequal lobes.

---

Linear capsule, tetragonous, with 2 oligospermatous locules. 6-8 seeds per fruit.

Type species: B. ovariensis P. Beauv., from Nigeria.

Approximately 20 species from Madagascar and tropical Africa.

## Key to the species

- **1.** Plants absolutely glabrous except for the pedicels and calyces, rarely the peduncles; leaf lamina obovate-acuminate, entire, obtusely crenate; petiole winged → [[Brillantaisia_soyauxii\|*B. soyauxii*]]
- **1′.** Plants regularly hairy, especially the stems and inflorescences; the leaves and lower parts of the plant are ± glabrescent in some species:
  - **2.** Leaf lamina lanceolate, or ovate-lanceolate, entire or slightly crenate, long-decurrent into the petiole → [[Brillantaisia_lancifolia\|*B. lancifolia*]]
  - **2′.** Leaf lamina ± broadly ovate; leaves petiolate.
    - **3.** Leaf lamina entire or obscurely crenate.
      - **4.** Leaf lamina rounded at the base, variable, broadly ovate, acuminate, hairy on both surfaces; petiole not or very slightly winged, hairy; panicle lax; calyx segments linear, 8-10 mm; with some glandular hairs; corolla violet.
      - **4′.** Leaf lamina rounded and decurrent into the winged petiole; inflorescence spicate; corolla white → [[Brillantaisia_verruculosa\|*B. verruculosa*]]
    - **3′.** Leaf lamina denticulate, narrowed at the base and decurrent into the winged petiole.
      - **5.** Petiole briefly, or sometimes scarcely winged toward the base of the lamina; base of lamina rounded or truncate → [[Brillantaisia_vogeliana\|*B. vogeliana*]]
      - **5′.** Petiole winged ± to the base; leaf lamina, especially the lower ones, long-cuneate at the base → [[Brillantaisia_patula\|*B. patula*]]
