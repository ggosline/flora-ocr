# *Ancistrorhynchus* Finet

**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Authority**: Finet
**Protologue**: Bull. Soc. Bot. Fr. 54 (Mem. 9) : 44 (1907)

## Diagnosis

Stem short, thick. Leaves suberect, spreading to recurved, ligulate or linear-lanceolate, sometimes gradually attenuated toward the apex, unequally bilobed at the apex. Inflorescence almost sessile, dense, generally globose or ellipsoid. Flowers small, resupinate. Sepals and petals dissimilar. Labellum entire or trilobed, without callus, with a spur. Spur straight or S-shaped. Rostellum large, fairly fleshy, elongate, parallel to the stigma in the basal half, then abruptly incurved. Viscidium unique, fairly large, lanceolate, lamellate. Tegula double, lamellate. Rostellum tridentate after withdrawal of the
