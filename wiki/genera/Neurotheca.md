# *Neurotheca* Salisb. ex Benth. & Hook.f.

**Family**: [[Gentianaceae]]
**Authority**: Salisb. ex Benth. & Hook.f.
**Protologue**: Gen. pl. 2 : 812 (1876)

## Diagnosis

Annual or perennial herbs, erect, glabrous; stems simple or branched, quadrangular.

Leaves opposite, sessile. Inflorescences terminal or axillary, in corymbiform cymes or with solitary or geminate flowers. Flowers actinomorphic, 4-merous; calyx with tube bearing 8 prominent longitudinal ribs, lobes traversed by transverse nerves, reflexed, with scales at the internal base; corolla funnel-shaped, lobes ± erect; stamens 4, included, unequal to subequal, inserted on the corolla tube, prolonged downward by two wings decurrent on the corolla tube; ovary 1-locular; stigma 2-lobed.

Fruits ellipsoid to narrowly ellipsoid. Seeds angular to globose; testa reticulate, with polygonal cells.

Genus comprising three species from tropical Africa and Madagascar, one of which also reaches northern South America; two species in Gabon.
