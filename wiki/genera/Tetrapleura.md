# *Tetrapleura* Bentham

**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Authority**: Bentham

## Diagnosis

in Hook. Journ. Bot., 4:345 (1841).

Unarmed trees. Leaves bipinnate with pinnae alternate or opposite. Leaflets alternate, with asymmetric base. Inflorescences in axillary racemes, solitary or geminate. Flowers ♢, pentamerous, pedicellate. Calyx gamosepalous with 5 teeth. Corolla with valvate petals, free. Stamens 10, free from each other, with connective prolonged by a caducous pedicellate gland. Ovary pluriovulate. Style filiform. Legume oblong, indehiscent, woody, black to blackish purple, septate between the seeds, with valves laterally winged (cross-section of fruit cruciform). TYPE SPECIES: T. tetrapleura (Schumach & Thonn.) Taub. (Adenanthera tetrapleura Schumach. & Thonn.).

This genus is endemic to Africa. It comprises two species. Only one is present in Gabon.
