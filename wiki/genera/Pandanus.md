# *Pandanus* Parkinson

**Authority**: Parkinson, *Journal of a Voyage to the South Seas*: 46 (1773)  
**Family**: [[Pandanaceae]]

## Diagnosis

Spiny trees with erect, candelabra-branched stems and adventitious roots.
Leaves linear-ensiform, coriaceous and gathered at branch tips, with toothed
margins, a keeled and toothed midrib, and paired lateral folds. Plants
dioecious. Female spikes mature into ± three-angled syncarps of sessile drupes;
male flowers consist of a column bearing several stamens. Perianth absent.

## Uses and vernacular names

Dried leaves are used for mats and bags for carrying salted or smoked fish.
Hollowed trunk sections submerged in coastal lagoons are used to catch
catfish. Vol. 28 records numerous Gabonese names, including *nkuwu* (Mpongwe),
*iguwu* (Galoa, Nkomi, Orungu), *akua* and *akubeghe* (Fang), and variants of
*fubu*, *kubu* and *gubu* in other languages.
