# *Eragrostis* Beauv.

**Family**: [[Gramineae]]
**Authority**: Beauv.
**Protologue**: Beauv. Fl. Cap. VII : 594 (1900)

## Diagnosis

Perennial or annual grasses with narrow leaves, ligules reduced to a fringe of hairs. Inflorescence in a lax or contracted and spiciform panicle.

Spikelets laterally flattened, pedicellate; rachis caducous above the glumes and between each flower, or persistent. Flowers 2 to very numerous, hermaphrodite, or the upper ones more or less reduced. Glumes equal or unequal, membranous, with 1 nerve only, often caducous. Lemmas more or less imbricate, ovate to lanceolate, entire, muticous, with 3 nerves, generally glabrous. Paleas of similar size or smaller, bicarinate, persistent or caducous. Two lodicules; generally 3 stamens; ovary glabrous with distinct styles. Seed falling surrounded by both glumelles, or only with the lemma.

An important genus represented by very numerous species, some of which are valuable forages. Eight species are recorded from Gabon; these are for the most part ruderals.
