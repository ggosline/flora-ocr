# *Mangifera* L.

**Family**: [[Anacardiaceae]]
**Authority**: L.
**Protologue**: Sp. pl. 1 : 200 (1753)

## Diagnosis

### Trees polygamous (with male and bisexual flowers on the same plant). Leaves alternate,

simple, pinnately veined. Inflorescences in terminal and/or subterminal panicles. Flowers unisexual, 4- or 5-merous, with imbricate prefloration; sepals free or nearly so; stamens 5(–12) of which 1 or 2(–5) fertile, the others sterile; disc present; ovary subglobose, 1-locular; style 1, eccentric. Fruits drupaceous, ovoid to subreniform, with fleshy mesocarp, with 1 seed, endocarp woody. Seeds oblong-ovoid, ± compressed, with thick cotyledons, plano-convex.

Genus of Asia and Oceania, grouping ± 40 species of which one cultivated in almost all tropical and sometimes subtropical countries.

B: Mukherji (1949), Pell et al. (2011), Van der Veken (1960a).
