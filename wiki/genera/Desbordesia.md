# Desbordesia

**Authority**: Pierre ex Van Tiegh. (1905) · **Family**: [[Irvingiaceae]]

## Diagnosis

Monotypic Cameroon–Gabon forest-tree genus. It differs from *Irvingia* in its style bent from the base and long, flat, winged fruits containing one or two seeds rather than fleshy drupes.
