# Pierrodendron

**Authority**: Engl. (1907) · **Family**: [[Simaroubaceae]]

## Diagnosis

Small African trees with very large imparipinnate leaves to 1 m and large racemes of red-violet flowers. Petals 5 and contorted; stamens 10–18 with hairy basal ligules and large oblong anthers; disc annular; carpels 5. Fruits large and mango-like.
