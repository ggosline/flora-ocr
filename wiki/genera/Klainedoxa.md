# Klainedoxa

**Authority**: Pierre (1896) · **Family**: [[Irvingiaceae]]

## Diagnosis

Very large Guineo-Congolian trees with long falcate stipules, simple coriaceous leaves and a dense reticulate venation equally visible on both faces. Flowers have five sepals, five reflexed petals, ten stamens, a thick disc and a five-locular ovary. Fruits are flattened, weakly five-lobed drupes containing up to five stones.
