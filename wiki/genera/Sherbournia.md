# *Sherbournia* G. Don

**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Authority**: G. Don
**Protologue**: G. Don in Loudon, Encycl. of Pl., Suppl. 2 : 1303 (diagnosis) et 1322 nec 1323 (1855)

## Diagnosis

Woody vines ± twining, sometimes shrubby. Young shoots ± pubescent. Stipules elliptic or ± lanceolate, erect, ± early caducous. Petiole channelled. Blade pubescent or not, papyraceous to subcoriaceous, acuminate or not. Secondary nerves 5-17 pairs in number, rarely glabrous on the lower face. Domatia absent.

Inflorescences terminal pubescent appearing unilaterally axillary, of 1-16 flowers pentamerous on a small shrub of short axes furnished with ± embracing and ± caducous bracteoles. Pedicel (0.5) 1 to 10 mm long (up to 30 mm in Bouquet 1201), bearing in most cases at its apex a pair of prefloral leaves similar to the bracts, connate at the level of the flower articulation. Ovary narrowly obconic, pubescent, in cross-section sometimes circular, sometimes 10-nerved furrowed lengthwise (do not confuse with drying wrinkles on herbarium specimens). Calycine cup variable, rarely very reduced (1-3 mm in Sh. buccularia), often as high as wide or about 6-12 mm; the calycine lobes are of two types, either ± short tooth-like, or ± developed laminate, imbricate or not at anthesis and overlapping toward the right or toward the left. Prefloration twisted: the calyx not always in the same direction as the

---

corolla. Corolla white, yellow, pink, red or violaceous ± spotted, more coloured inside, with a tube 14-30 mm in length, campanulate, narrow only in about the lower quarter; the broad part, pubescent externally, presents at the base a thick ring of closely-packed hairs closing access to the disc; corolla lobes overlapping toward the left, suborbicular with apex acute or not. Anthers sessile not exceeding the throat, with punctiform insertion situated toward the lower third; connective spreading dorsally, with pubescent or glabrous margins, with acute apex exceeding the locules, with base split or not; the longitudinal thecae 4 in number are exceptionally multilocellate by small transverse partitions (Sh. Batesii); pollen in simple grains. Disc in thick fleshy ring, sometimes somewhat cupuliform, sometimes ornamented inside with contact impressions of the base of the stigmatic club in the juvenile bud. Style glabrous at the column sometimes weakly thickened toward the base, with stigmatic club included, thick, ± fusiform, with 10 pollen-collecting furrows, and formed of two lobes adhering to the apex which is acute. Ovary with 2 (3) locules; placentas multilobed in oblong masses inserted by a blade-like stalk occupying the greater part of the axile line of each locule.
