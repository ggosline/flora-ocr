# *Cayratia* Jussieu

**Family**: [[Vitaceae]]
**Authority**: Jussieu
**Protologue**: Jussieu, Dict. Class. Hist. nat. 4 : 346 (1823)

## Diagnosis

Sarmentose lianes, slender or robust, herbaceous or more or less woody; aerial apparatus annual or partially perennial; root system sometimes tuberous. Tendrils always present, often multiply branched, furnished with bracts. Stipules present, sometimes very rapidly caducous. Leaves compound, 3–5 foliolate, pinnate or pedate, petiolate, with denticulate margins.

Plants dioecious, dioecious-polygamous or hermaphroditic. Inflorescences opposite the leaves or axillary, pedunculate, multi-flowered, in irregular corymbiform cymes often of large size. Flowers tetramerous, hermaphroditic or unisexual, small, pedicellate. Calyx cupuliform, entire or with more or less distinct valvate lobes. Corolla globose, ovoid or subspheric, rounded in the upper part; petals valvate, more or less distinctly hooded internally, spreading or reflexed at the end of anthesis and separately caducous. Stamens inserted on the receptacle at the base of the disc: filaments erect between the lobes of the disc: anthers introrse dorsifixed; connective thickened or not. Disc entire, forming a complete ring with 4 rounded lobes. Ovary included in the disc, with two locules separated by a thick entire partition; two oblong ovules per locule; style thick cylindro-conical; stigma poorly distinct, entire, discoid. Male flower with greatly atrophied non-functional gynoecium; style reduced, slender, not exceeding the disc. Sterile female flowers with short slender staminodes.

Fleshy berries with 2–4 seeds. Seeds deltoid, more or less cordiform attenuate into a beak at the lower part, ventrally with 2 broad shallow pits, dorsally convex presenting a median chalazal macule, short, generally salient; surface smooth or diversely crusted; transverse section T-shaped¹.

Type species: C. pedata (Lam.) Juss. ex Gagnep.
