# Fagara

**Family**: [[Rutaceae]]

## Diagnosis

Prickly shrubs or trees with alternate pinnate leaves and oil-gland-dotted leaflets. Small, usually unisexual 4- or 5-merous flowers occur in panicles; fruit consists of one or more follicles with glossy dark seeds. The treatment uses *Fagara* in the broad African sense now often included in *Zanthoxylum*.

## Key to the species

Subgabonese species in parentheses.
- **1.** Leaflets pubescent, at least on the underside of the midvein; flowers tetramerous.
  - **2.** Inflorescences or infructescences in corymbiform panicles; 11 to 19 leaflets with non-entire margin; 2 carpels; erect shrub → (10. F. pilosiuscula).
  - **2′.** Inflorescences or infructescences not in corymbiform panicles.
    - **3.** Petiolules > 1 mm; 1 carpel; tree or shrub. (11. F. rubescens).
    - **3′.** Petiolules ≤ 1 mm.
      - **4.** More than 13 leaflets; 2 carpels; fruits glabrous; liane or lianescent shrub → (12. F. viridis).
      - **4′.** Fewer than 13 leaflets; 1 carpel; fruits pubescent.
        - **5.** Leaflets pubescent above the blade; erect or lianescent shrub → [[Fagara_buesgenii\|*F. buesgenii*]]
        - **5′.** Leaflets glabrous above the blade; tree or shrub → [[Fagara_bouetensis\|*F. bouetensis*]]
- **1′.** Leaflets glabrous, even on the underside of the midvein; flowers tetramerous or pentamerous.
  - **6.** More than 25 leaflets; flowers pentamerous, 1 carpel; trees or shrubs.
    - **7.** Petiolules > 1 mm.
      - **8.** Blade margin entire.
        - **9.** Base of blade nearly symmetrical; rachis rather distinctly prickly → [[Fagara_macrophylla\|*F. macrophylla*]]
        - **9′.** Base of blade asymmetrical; rachis sparingly prickly → [[Fagara_tessmannii\|*F. tessmannii*]]
      - **8′.** Blade margin non-entire → [[Fagara_tessmannii\|*F. tessmannii*]]
    - **7′.** Petiolules ≤ 1 mm.
      - **10.** Blade margin entire; lateral veins ± fleeing near the margin → [[Fagara_macrophylla\|*F. macrophylla*]]
      - **10′.** Blade margin non-entire; lateral veins anastomosing in distinct loops near the margin → [[Fagara_heitzii\|*F. heitzii*]]
  - **6′.** Fewer than 25 leaflets.
    - **11.** Leaflets with entire margin; blade with well-visible translucent punctations accompanied by rusty punctations on the lower surface; flowers pentamerous, 1 carpel; tree or shrub of ripicole forest or periodically inundated.
    - **11′.** Characters above not associated.
      - **12.** Petiolules > 1 mm.
        - **13.** More than 18 pairs of lateral veins; leaves often paripinnate through abortion; flowers pentamerous, 1 carpel.
          - **14.** Blade symmetrical or subsymmetrical; erect or lianescent tree or shrub → [[Fagara_claessensii\|*F. claessensii*]]
          - **14′.** Blade distinctly asymmetrical; tree or shrub → (14. F. lemairei).
        - **13′.** Fewer than 18 pairs of lateral veins.
          - **15.** Inflorescences or infructescences with secondary axes in racemes; translucent punctations in the blade with dark yellow content (reddish on dry specimen), of regular size; flowers tetramerous, 1 carpel; erect or lianescent tree or shrub → *F. leprieur*
          - **15′.** Inflorescences or infructescences with secondary axes branched; translucent punctations in the blade with light yellow content, of ± irregular size.
            - **16.** More than 11 leaflets; flowers pentamerous, 1 carpel; tree or shrub → [[Fagara_tessmannii\|*F. tessmannii*]]
            - **16′.** Fewer than 11 leaflets; flowers tetramerous, 2 carpels; liane or lianescent shrub → (15. F. poggei).
      - **12′.** Petiolules ≤ 1 mm; flowers tetramerous.
        - **17.** Normal leaf more than 30 cm; 1 carpel; tree or shrub → [[Fagara_welwitschii\|*F. welwitschii*]]
        - **17′.** Normal leaf fewer than 25 cm; 2 carpels; liane or erect or lianescent shrub → [[Fagara_dinklagei\|*F. dinklagei*]]
