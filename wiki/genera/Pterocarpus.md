# *Pterocarpus* Jacq., nom. cons.

**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Authority**: Jacq., nom. cons.
**Protologue**: Select. stirp. amer. hist. : 283 (1763)

## Diagnosis

Trees; trunk sometimes with buttresses; bark with red exudate. Stipules caducous. Leaves: stipelles absent; leaflets 5 to 13 (elsewhere (1–)3–35), alternate. Inflorescences terminal or axillary, in panicles or panicle of pseudoracemes or sometimes in racemes, with numerous flowers. Flowers: bracteoles 2, caducous; calyx funnel-shaped to campanulate, with 5 short teeth, the upper ones ± connate; corolla orange to yellow, petals clawed, standard orbicular, wings obliquely obovate to oblong, keel of the same size or smaller; stamens 10, monadelphous or sometimes diadelphous (9 + 1), separating easily into two bundles, anthers dorsifixed, versatile; disc absent; ovary sessile or shortly stipitate, with 2 to 6 ovules; style short and straight to slightly curved, stigma terminal, minute. Pods distinctly flattened, indehiscent, orbicular to ovate, ± oblique, woody to papyraceous, the centre with 1 or 2(–3) seeds, sometimes spinose or reticulate, surrounded by a wing often papyraceous, broad, thin, with style remains terminal or lateral and often extending to 4/5 or 5/6 of the circumference. Seeds reniform to reniform-oblong, flattened, smooth; hilum small.

Pantropical genus with approximately 40 species, with the greatest diversity in continental Africa, six species in Asia and Madagascar, and 11 species in the Neotropics; in Gabon, 4 species.

Notes: The species P. osun Craib, described from Nigeria, is reported from Cameroon and thus incorporated in the key below; also reported for Equatorial Guinea, although it has not been found there again.

The flat fruits are dispersed by wind or float on water.

Bibliography: Hauman (1954d), Lewis et al. (2005), Pellegrin (1949), Polhill (1971b), Rojo (1972).
