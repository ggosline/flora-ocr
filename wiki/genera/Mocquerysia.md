B, Adansonia, 13 (3-4) : 156 (1991). Shrubs unarmed, monocaulous. Leaves stipulate, dentate. Inflorescences epiphyllous in cymes generally uniflorous, arranged in 2–6 groups of flowers per leaf, distributed along the petiole or in the lower 1/2 of the limb. Flowers ♀ ; bracts narrowly ovate ; sepals 5, imbricate ; petals 5, imbricate ; stamens 5 ; ovary pubescent, with 2–3 pluriovulate placentae ; style simple, entire. Fruits : capsules with 3 valves. TYPE SPECIES : M. multiflora HUA.

Genus of tropical western-central Africa comprising a single species, present in Gabon.
