# Canna

Large American rhizomatous herbs with distichous leaves and colourful zygomorphic flowers. The androecium is reduced to half of one fertile stamen plus petaloid staminodes; the inferior ovary produces a usually warty capsule.

- [[Canna_bidentata|*C. bidentata*]] — subspontaneous in Gabon.
