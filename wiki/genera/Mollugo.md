# Mollugo

**Family in Vol. 7**: [[Aizoaceae]] · **Current placement**: Molluginaceae

Annual or perennial herbs with alternate, pseudo-whorled or basal leaves, 5-merous bisexual flowers and a 3–5-valved loculicidal capsule containing many seeds.
