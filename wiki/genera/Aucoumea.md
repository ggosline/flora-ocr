# Aucoumea

**Authority**: Pierre (1896) · **Family**: [[Burseraceae]]

## Diagnosis

Monotypic western Central African tree genus distinguished by 5-merous flowers, an extrastaminal glandular disc and dry five-valved capsules bearing five winged seeds, rather than the drupes of the other genera in the treatment.

## Species

- [[Aucoumea_klaineana|A. klaineana]] — okoumé; Equatorial Guinea, Gabon and adjacent Congo.
