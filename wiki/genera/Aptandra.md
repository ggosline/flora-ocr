# *Aptandra* Miers

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]]

Ann. and Magaz. Nat. Hist., sér. 7, **2**: 201 (1851).

Type species: *A. spruceana* Miers.

A genus of 4 species, 3 in tropical America and 1 in Africa. Villiers notes a
consistent difference in breeding system between the two groups: **the American
species are hermaphrodite, the African species dioecious.**

## Diagnosis

Small trees or shrubs, monoecious or dioecious. Leaves mucronulate.
Inflorescence a ± compound axillary panicle. Flower hermaphrodite, male or
female, tetramerous. Calyx small, cupuliform. Petals valvate, linear,
apiculate. Glands 4, thick, alternipetalous. Stamens 4, epipetalous, extrorse;
filaments fused into a tube around the pistil; anthers fused into a ring, the
dehiscence slits annular. Ovary superior, slightly compressed, bilocular; one
ovule per locule. Style filiform, ending in a globose stigma. Calyx **strongly
accrescent** around the drupe. Seed solitary, the albumen fleshy and oily.

*Aptandra* and [[Ongokea]] are the two Gabonese genera whose stamens are united
into a staminal tube. They are separated by the branchlets (cylindrical in
*Aptandra*, 2-winged or compressed in *Ongokea*), the inflorescence (a simple
panicle vs a raceme of corymbs), and the fruiting calyx — a **pink collar** in
*Aptandra*, against a green coriaceous envelope opening by 2–3 valves in
*Ongokea*.

## Notes

Later classifications refer *Aptandra* to **Aptandraceae**, of which it is the
type genus; the Vol 20 treatment places it in *Olacaceae*.
