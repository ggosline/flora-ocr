# *Flagellaria* L.

**Authority**: L., *Species Plantarum*: 333 (1753)  
**Family**: [[Flagellariaceae]]

**Type species**: *Flagellaria indica* L.

## Diagnosis

Climbing plants with sheathing leaves and a ± lanceolate blade ending in a
spiralled tendril. Inflorescence a terminal panicle; flowers hermaphrodite and
actinomorphic. Perianth of two tepal whorls; stamens 6, introrse. Pistil
superior; ovary 3-locular, with one ovule in each locule; style trifid. Drupe
one-seeded, rarely three-seeded. Embryo small; endosperm mealy.

The genus is palaeotropical and comprises eight species. Two occur in Africa,
of which one is recorded from Gabon.
