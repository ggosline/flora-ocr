# *Hymenodictyon* Walli

**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Authority**: Walli
**Protologue**: Wall, in Roxb. Fl. Ind. ed. Carey 2:148 (1824)

## Diagnosis

Small trees or shrubs sometimes bushy or even epiphytic. Interpetiolular stipules entire, ± caducous. Leaves opposite, petiolate. Presence of axillary domatia.

---

Inflorescences spiciform in terminal racemes sometimes branched. Robust rachis bearing a large number of small 1–3-flowered cymes inserted at the axil of small bracts. The lower pair of bracts is remarkably developed into stiff, pale green or somewhat coloured leaves. Pentamerous flowers of dull colours. Flowering more delayed at the apex of the raceme. Ovary subglobose. Calyx not tubular with subequal linear lobes. Corolla valvate with short lobes, glabrous tube inside. Stamens basifixed, included. Annular disc reduced. Style glabrous, much longer than the corolla, exporting pollen onto its capitate stigma. Ovary with 2 locules. Numerous ovules imbricate, ascending.

Fruit capsular, loculeicidal, opening from above, with valves ± persistent after seed dispersal. Numerous elongate seeds, with margins or membranous alate extremities. Wing split on the hilum side. Albumen fleshy; embryo small.

Type of the genus: Hymenodictyon excelsum Wall. from Malesia.

About twenty tropical species from Asia, Indonesia, Madagascar and Africa. Two Gabonese species. A third, H. pachyantha K. Krause, known from Côte-d'Ivoire to S.E. Cameroon, to be sought in Gabon.

### KEY TO THE HYMENODICTYON

1. Fruit less than 2 cm long; corolla swollen clavate in bud.

2. Fine leaf venation well apparent; underside of leaf ± pubescent, at least the axillary domatia alone pubescent; limb elliptic rather broad; seeds less than 2 times longer than broad.

2'. Fine venation not apparent; underside of leaf entirely glabrous; limb obovate rather narrow; seed more than 3 times longer than broad ..... 2. H. biafranum.

1'. Fruit more than 2.5 cm long; corolla abruptly capitate in bud ..... H. pachyantha.
