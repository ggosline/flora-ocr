# *Hymenodictyon* Walli

**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Authority**: Walli
**Protologue**: Wall, in Roxb. Fl. Ind. ed. Carey 2:148 (1824)

## Diagnosis

Small trees or shrubs sometimes bushy or even epiphytic. Interpetiolular stipules entire, ± caducous. Leaves opposite, petiolate. Presence of axillary domatia.

---

Inflorescences spiciform in terminal racemes sometimes branched. Robust rachis bearing a large number of small 1–3-flowered cymes inserted at the axil of small bracts. The lower pair of bracts is remarkably developed into stiff, pale green or somewhat coloured leaves. Pentamerous flowers of dull colours. Flowering more delayed at the apex of the raceme. Ovary subglobose. Calyx not tubular with subequal linear lobes. Corolla valvate with short lobes, glabrous tube inside. Stamens basifixed, included. Annular disc reduced. Style glabrous, much longer than the corolla, exporting pollen onto its capitate stigma. Ovary with 2 locules. Numerous ovules imbricate, ascending.

Fruit capsular, loculeicidal, opening from above, with valves ± persistent after seed dispersal. Numerous elongate seeds, with margins or membranous alate extremities. Wing split on the hilum side. Albumen fleshy; embryo small.

Type of the genus: Hymenodictyon excelsum Wall. from Malesia.

About twenty tropical species from Asia, Indonesia, Madagascar and Africa. Two Gabonese species. A third, H. pachyantha K. Krause, known from Côte-d'Ivoire to S.E. Cameroon, to be sought in Gabon.

Shrub or small branching tree 4–8 m; trunk diameter exceeding 10 cm; wood soft; branchlets very brittle. Stipules triangular acute 8–12 mm long, curved, with glandular margin. Petiole 7–40 mm, glabrous or puberulent. Blade rather thin, glabrous above, puberulent or ± glabrous on the lower surface with at least the axillary domatia puberulent, 5–18 × 2–8 cm; base acute or cuneate; apex often broad bearing a small to medium triangular acumen. 7–9 pairs of secondary veins puberulent or glabrous. Fine venules in well-apparent reticulate pattern on the lower surface of dry blade. Inflorescences erect 6–15(20) cm plus peduncle 2–6 cm, 2.5–3 cm wide at anthesis. Lower bracts with petiole 3.5–5 cm and blade 6–9 × 1.7–3.5 cm, pale green or ± reddish. Rachis and pedicels puberulent as well as the ovary. 5(6) calyx lobes narrowly attenuate, ± puberulent, 1 mm long or less. Corolla tubular attenuate toward the base, 4–7 × 1.5–3 mm, puberulent to glabrous, with short triangular lobes, yellowish ± tinged with vinous pink. Filaments 1 mm inserted toward the middle of the tube. Anther 2 × 0.9 mm, with acute apex of connective. Pollen yellow. Style slender 8–10 mm with capitate stigma 1 mm in vivo. Fruit 11–15 mm, with pedicel 6–9 mm; valves brownish with lenticels. Seeds light reddish-brown 7 × 2.5 mm, with marginal wing. Type : Schimper 277 (P!), Abyssinia. Variable species, principally in the pubescence of its leaves and in its habit. It grows on ± denuded and ± rocky heights. Vast distribution from Fouta-Djallon to East Africa and Angola. Gabonese material (described above) : N. Hallé : 3080, Bélinga, alt. 975 m, in shrubby forest on rocky slope (fl. fr. Nov.). Le Testu : 8625, Mont Iboundji, alt. 900–1,000 m (fl. Dec.); 8953, Kemboma (fl. Oct.); 9008, Koum rock between Oyem and Bitam (fl. Mar.).
