# *Alysicarpus* Neck. ex Desv.

**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Authority**: Neck. ex Desv.
**Protologue**: J. Bot. Agric., sér. 2, 1 : 120 (1813)

## Diagnosis

Herbs ± woody at the base; stems partially prostrate. Stipules amplexicaul, scarious.

Leaves unifoliolate; stipelles present. Inflorescences axillary and terminal, in pseudo-racemes or panicles. Flowers: bracteoles absent; calyx persistent, scarious, with long lobes, the 2 upper ones connate, the 3 lower ones equal; corolla with oblong standard, adhering to the obtuse keel, scarcely incurved; stamens 10, diadelphous, the upper one free, anthers uniform; ovary sessile or nearly so, pluriovulate; style filiform, incurved at the apex, stigma capitate.

Pod subcylindric, indehiscent, articulate; articles (2–)4 to 8, monospermous, separating at maturity, scarcely longer than wide.

Genus of 25 to 30 species, of which 10 in Africa, the others in Asia and Australia; in Gabon, two species, and one to be sought.

Notes: A. glumaceus (Vahl) DC. is known from West Africa, Cameroon and Democratic Republic of the Congo included. It is to be sought in Gabon, which is why it appears in the key below.

b: Adema (2003), Léonard (1954a), Lewis et al. (2005), Pellegrin (1949), Verdcourt (1971d).
