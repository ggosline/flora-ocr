# Canarium

**Authority**: L. · **Family**: [[Burseraceae]]

## Diagnosis

Large dioecious resinous trees with leaves clustered at branch ends, a caducous pair of reduced basal leaflets, 3-merous flowers and a stipitate cup-shaped disc. The ellipsoid drupe has a massive three-locular, three-ridged stone.

## Species

- [[Canarium_schweinfurthii|C. schweinfurthii]] — widespread tropical African secondary-forest tree.
