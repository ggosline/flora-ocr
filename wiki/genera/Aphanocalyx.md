# *Aphanocalyx* Oliver

**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Authority**: Oliver
**Protologue**: Oliver, Hook. Ic. Pl. 21 : 53, t. 1066 (1870)

## Diagnosis

Trees. Leaves with one pair of subsessile leaflets, with two longly ascending nerves, the inner nerve being submarginous or marginal. Very short petiole. Floriferous buds squamose. Bracteoles sepaloid, valvate. Flowers with very short receptacle. Sepals 0. Petal 1 (-2) well developed. Woody pods with winged suture on the edges, marked with a few transverse nerves.

Type species: A. cynometroides Oliv.

Genus very close to Monopetalanthus, from which it differs only in its pods lacking longitudinal nerves. The unijugate leaves with their very particular asymmetry allow the 3 species of the genus to be easily distinguished. However, two Congolese species with unijugate leaves whose principal nerve is also marginal have been placed in Monopetalanthus, M. Richardsiae Léon. and M. Trapnellii Léon., which emphasizes the approximation between the 2 genera.

Small trees. A. cynometroides and A. marginernervatus exist from Cameroon to Gabon and Congo; A. djumænsis is Congolese.

## Key to the species

**1.** Leaflets with inner submarginal vein, distant from the margin by 5-20 mm. → *A. cynometroides*

**1′.** Leaflets with inner marginal vein.

**2.** Base of leaflets bearing 1-3 glands on the blade; Congolese species. A. djumænsis.

**2′.** Base of leaflets without glands → *A. marginervatus*

**1.** Aphanocalyx cynometroides Oliver (Pl. 68, p. 275) Oliver, Hook. Ic. Pl. 1066 (1867-71). — Pellegrin, Lég. Gabon : 73 (1948). Leaves with one pair of opposite, sessile, glabrous leaflets. Blade asymmetric, acuminate, attenuate at the base, obovate-oblanceolate. The main vein, straight or slightly inflected toward the interior at the apex, situated 5-20 mm from the inner margin. 2-3 basilar nerves are long-ascending. Petiole short, 3-9 mm. Stipules caducous. Small dense racemes 2-5 cm long. Floral bud scaly, with striate scales. Pedicels 2-4 mm. Bracteoles valvate, ferruginous tomentose, 5-7 mm long. One large petal 6-8 mm. Stamens 10, 7-10 mm in length. Ovary hirsute. Pods oblong, 4-5 × 2-2.5 cm, velvety brown, without longitudinal vein. Type: Mann 1810, 10 lat. N. GABONESE MATERIAL STUDIED: J. Eckendorff: 118, région du Djoua (fl. sept.).
