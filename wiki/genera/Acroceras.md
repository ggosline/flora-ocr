# *Acroceras* Stapf.

**Family**: [[Gramineae]]
**Authority**: Stapf.
**Protologue**: STAPF, F.T.A. IX : 627 (1934)

## Diagnosis

Perennial herbs, extensively creeping and rooting at the base, leaves linear-lanceolate, amplexicaul at the base, tessellate venation. Inflorescences paniculate with spiciform branches. Spikelets oblong, glumes similar, membranous, terminated by a small thickened extension of the dorsal keel, the lower shorter with 3 nerves, the upper with 5 nerves. Lower floret male or neuter, lemma similar to the upper glume, palea of the same size, hyaline, two-keeled. Upper floret hermaphrodite, lemma coriaceous, with enrolled margins, 5-nerved, terminated like the glumes; palea similar in size and texture, with 2 keels, each terminated by a thickened appendage curved backward; 2 fleshy lodicules; 3 stamens, styles distinct. One species is recorded from Gabon:

1. Spikelets keeled and crested at the apex; blade base rounded or amplexicaul .. A. zizanioides
