# Oricia

**Family**: [[Rutaceae]]

## Diagnosis

Woody rutaceous plants with compound leaves, 4-merous flowers and mericarpic fruits. The circumscription is uncertain around the polyembryonic, five-foliolate *O. lecomteana*.
