# *Cryptosepalum* Bentham

**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Authority**: Bentham
**Protologue**: Bentham in Benth. et Hook. f., Gen. Plant. 1: 584 (1865)

## Diagnosis

Trees or shrubs or undershrubs forming thickets or trailing woody plants, with a single leaflet or numerous leaflets. Petaloid bracteoles covering the bud. Receptacle short. Sepals minute or absent. A single well-developed petal (0-3). Fertile stamens 3-6 (-8); sometimes filiform staminodes. Ovary stipe fused to the receptacle wall. Fruits flat, small, smooth, with 2 woody valves.

Type species: C. tetraphyllum (Hook. f.) Benth., Trans. Linn. Soc. 25: 315 (1865), West Africa.

Genus at once Guineo-Congolian and Zambezian. Two species occur in West Africa: C. tetraphyllum (riparian) and C. minutifolium. In the Cameroon-Gabonese forest from southern Nigeria, the following have been described: C. diphyllum (Nigeria), C. Staudtii, C. Pellegrinianum, C. congolanum, the latter two being riparian. Finally, a greater number have been recognized in the open forests and dry dense forests south of the Congo humid dense forest, some being probably only geographic varieties (Rhodesia, Kasai, Katanga, Tanganyika, Angola). Several are very important physiognomically in the semi-arid Zambezian region, sometimes forming continuous mats or thickets over large areas C. maravense, C. pseudotaxus.

1. Leaves with a single leaflet.

2. Leaflet rounded or slightly cordate at the base; basal nerves slightly ascending; presence of a pair of glands at the apex of the petiole..... I. C. congolanum.
