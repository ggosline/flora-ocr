# *Combretum*

**Family**: [[Combretaceae]]
**Protologue**: Iter Hispan. Append. : 308 (1758)

## Diagnosis

Shrubby lianes with large tortuous lianes (in Gabon) bearing scales (Pl. 2, $ A_{3} $ $ B_{2} $ $ C_{2} $ $ D_{2} $, $ E_{2} $, p. 15) or stipitate glands present.

Leaves alternate to whorled. Lower part of petiole often transformed into a lignified spine.

Inflorescences terminal and/or axillary, in racemes and/or in panicles. Bracts and/or axillant leaves often shiny and coloured.

Flowers bisexual, 4 or 5 merous, regular or slightly zygomorphic, distinctly divided into a lower receptacle and an upper receptacle itself very often subdivided into a lower part and an upper part, connected to each other by a stricture situated at the level of the disc. Petals present or absent, with an indumentum or glabrous. Stamens, 2 times the number of calyx lobes; when the upper receptacle

---

is long, the stamens are generally clearly arranged in 2 cycles, the innermost being opposite to the calyx lobes. Style free or partly attached to the upper receptacle.

Fruit dry (at least in Gabon) with 4 or 5 wings (most species), sessile or stipitate (Pl. 1: A, B, C, D, p. 11), sometimes adapted for dispersal by water. Cotyledons of various shapes.

For several species, the fruit remains unknown, either totally (C. adrianii, clarensis, gabonense, ivanii, njoleense, polyanthum), or only from Gabon. It is therefore impossible to build a key to the species by fruits. Thus, the following table at least allows one to distinguish 4 groups of species by the main fruit characters:

1. Fruits with polyhedral or stellate cross-section, either fruits with narrow, woody or corky wings: C. auriculatum, carringtonianum, conchipetalum (Pl. 1 C, p. 11), demeusei (Pl. 1 B, p. 11), indicum, marginatum (Pl. 1 A, p. 11).

1'. Fruits with papyraceous to coriaceous wings, whose width is generally several times that of the central part of the fruit.

2. Fruits with 5 wings: C. bracteatum, cinnabarinum, comosum, falcatum, inflatum, latialatum, mannii, platypterum.

2'. Fruits with 4 wings.

3. Ripe fruits, diameter ≤ 2.5 cm: C. cinereopetalum, exellii, fuscum, homalioides, longipilosum, mucronatum (Pl. 1 E, p. 11), oudenhovenii, paniculatum, paucinervium, rabiense, racemosum, scandens, sordidum, erosum.

3'. Ripe fruits, diameter distinctly > 2.5 cm: C. aphanopetalum, cuspidatum (Pl. 1D, p. 11), echirense, esteriense, mortehanii, multinervium, oyemense, paniculatum, paradoxum, pecoense, rabiense, robustum, sordidum.
