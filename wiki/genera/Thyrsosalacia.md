# *Thyrsosalacia* Loesener

**Family**: [[Celastraceae]]
**Authority**: Loesener

## Diagnosis

in Fedde Repert. 49 : 226 (1940) ; Pflanzenf., ed. 2, 20 B : 198 (1942) ; HALLÉ, Notul. Syst. 16 : 131 (1960).

Shrubs or lianas with opposite branches, opposite leaves, entirely glabrous and lacking rubber. Lamina entire, somewhat margined beneath when dry, acuminate.

Inflorescences sometimes contracted and appearing axillary, sometimes developed into terminal racemes or thyrses, with rachis sometimes bearing a pair of leaves and capable of elongation, occasionally with groups of decussate perular scales marking successive flowerings; laterally the inflorescence comprises biparious cymules with few flowers or uniflorous. Flowers as in Salacia, with pentamerous perianth, trimerous androcecium and protandrous; ovary trilocular with 2 superposed ovules per locule.

Fruit as in Salacia but with more complete septa. Seeds with separated cotyledons and manifestly structured embryo.

TYPE SPECIES : Thyrsosalacia nematobrachion Loes.

Etymology: from thyrsos, name of the staff decorated with vines and crowned with a pine cone which is an attribute of Dionysus, the Greek god of the vine and wine; certain roughly pyramidal branched inflorescences are called thyrses; for salacia, see p. 30.

EXCLUDED SPECIES : Thyrsosalacia longipes (Oliv.) Loes., synonym of Salacia longipes (Oliv.) Hallé.

## Key to the species

**1.** Disk entire less than 2 mm in diameter; lateral cymes extremely slender, triflorous with alternate branches; abscission limit of the pedicel situated clearly beyond the upper bracteole → 1. T. nematobrachion. 1' Disk more than 4 mm in diameter

**2.** Disk double, the lower one somewhat larger, with weakly lobed margin

**2.** T. pararacemosa.

**3.** Entire disk patelliform 2 bis. T. racemosa. 3' Disk scarcely cupuliform, with margin emarginate opposite each petal → [[Thyrsosalacia_viciflora\|*T. viciflora*]]
