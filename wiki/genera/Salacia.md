# *Salacia* Linné

**Family**: [[Celastraceae]]
**Authority**: Linné
**Protologue**: Mant. Pl. Alt. : 159, 293 (oct. 1771) : OLIVER, FTA 1 : 372 (1868) ; LOES., Pflanzenf., ed. 2, 20B : 217 (1942) ; WILCZEK, FCB 9 : 181 (1960) ; HALLÉ, MÉM. IFAN 64 : 151 (1962) ; DING HOU, Fl. Males., ser. 1, 6 (3) : 404 (1964) ; ROBSON, FZ 2, (2) : 391 (1966)

## Diagnosis

Woody lianes ± tall or shrubs most often glabrous. Old wood cylindrical in solid mass (notably in shrubs) or divided by concentric parenchymatous lacunae ± scalloped or discontinuous, or sometimes by radiating furrows (S. nitida). Branches alternate arising from the axil of scales inserted in spiral on elongate orthotropic axes. Internodes rarely hispid or pubescent, cylindrical or marked with ridges, sometimes with wings. Plagiotropic lateral branches sometimes prehensile. Presence or absence of rubber (petiole, leaf blade, floral pedicel). Leaves opposite decussate, rarely alternate and spiral (seedlings and a few non Cameroon-Gabonese species). Petiole canaliculate. Blade simple, entire or toothed, often acuminate. Stipules reduced inserted on the branch, inconspicuous and ± caducous.

Inflorescences axillary, rarely cauline, often fasciculate on a glomerule; sometimes uni- or pauciflorous cymes, or pluriflorous ± branched and ± pedunculate. Floral bud pedicellate, globular, ovoid or oblong, sometimes short and broad, with double quinuncial perianth. Flowers protandrous, green or coloured, whitish, yellow, orange, red, brown or dark violet. Calyx with (4)5 equal or unequal lobes. Corolla rotate, more rarely erect, 2–20 mm in diameter; 5 petals orbicular, ovate or oblong, sessile or subunguiculate, thin or somewhat fleshy, sometimes subangular, subequal and marked with pits. Extrastaminal fleshy nectariferous disc, annular disciform or truncoconical, sometimes globular, subcylindric or pentagonal, more rarely cupular or borne on an androgynophore, sometimes marked like the petals with pits due to compression of the anthers during prefloration. Stamens (2)3 intradiscal, inserted or not in a perigynous groove; filament ribbonlike attenuate curving outward; anther subtransverse or in chevron ± acute, extrorse, divided into 2 distinct or ± confluent lobes, with connective rarely somewhat prominent. Pollen in monad tricolporate with reticulate exine. Ovary ± immersed in the disc, conic subpyramidal at the base of the style, with rather thick wall with (2)3 locules. Style attenuate short or long, with terminal punctiform stigma rarely somewhat capitate. Ovules (1)2–11 per locule, axile, horizontal or somewhat ascending, more rarely somewhat descending, 2-seriate or superposed.

Fruits bacciform globular, cucumiform, pyriform or oblong, 1–12 cm long, with 3 inconspicuous locules, the pulpy septa being ± degraded at maturity. Pericarp ± coriaceous but fleshy, glabrous, smooth or ornamented with folds, ridges, warts or protuberances, green then yellow or orange-red at maturity. Seeds 1–24 weighing approximately 1–6 grams, enveloped in a sweet mucilaginous endocarpic pulp and edible. Testa thin, adherent, brownish, unornamented but ± striated by the fibrovasculature expansion linked to the chalaza. Cotyledons massive, free or often ± fused. Embryo with inconspicuous structures or very reduced.
