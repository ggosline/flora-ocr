# Hannoa

**Authority**: Planch., *London Journal of Botany* 5: 566 (1846) · **Family**: [[Simaroubaceae]]

## Diagnosis

African trees with imparipinnate leaves. The calyx encloses the bud and later tears into three irregular segments; petals 5, imbricate; stamens 10 with hairy basal ligules; disc annular; five carpels free but contiguous, styles united. Fruits are small drupes.
