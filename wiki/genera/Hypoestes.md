# *Hypoestes* Soland. ex R. Br.

**Family**: [[Acanthaceae]]
**Authority**: Soland. ex R. Br.
**Protologue**: Solander ex R. Brown, Prodr. Fl. Nov. Holl.: 474 (1810)

## Diagnosis

Shrubs or herbaceous plants with entire or dentate leaves.

Inflorescences axillary or terminal, in spikes or capitules arranged along the branches, more rarely in loose panicles. Flowers solitary, or several at a time, surrounded by an involucre of 2 outer connate bracteoles and accompanied by 2 inner bracteoles generally exceeding the calyx. Flowers more or less reddish or pink in the species where this character is noted. Calyx short, dry or diaphanous, with 5 deep subequal narrow or setiform segments. Corolla resupinate with a thin tube or gradually flared, straight or curved upward; limb with 2 lips, the upper takes the lower position, it is erect, entire or (Gabonese species) somewhat bilobed; the lower, in the upper position, is wider, spreading or reflexed, ± trilobed. 2 stamens inserted below the throat, included or exserted; anthers dorsifixed with 1 locule, obtuse, wide and fleshy after dehiscence;

---

no staminode. Disk cupuliform; style filiform bifid at the apex.

Capsule compressed toward the base; placentas adnate to the valves.

4 seeds or fewer, ± orbicular, generally covered with warts.

Type species: H. floribunda R. Br., described from Australia.

According to H. Melchior (Syllabus, ed. 12, 2: 560, 1964) approximately 150 paleotropical species (a large part in Madagascar). This number seems liable to considerable reduction. In 1900, C.B. Clarke had indicated 60 species.

1. Partial inflorescences unilateral; outer bracteoles fused in their lower part and forming a small tube; lower lip (originally upper) rhomboidal, with ribs distinctly angular toward the base, long cuspidate and acuminate at the apex..... 1. H. verticillaris.

1'. Partial inflorescences non-unilateral; outer bracteoles free or only briefly fused at the base, never forming a small tube; lower lip (originally upper) ovate or ovate-lanceolate, rounded at the apex.

2. Leaves ovate, 10–15 × 3–6 cm; inflorescences ± condensed, short, subsessile, giving the impression of fairly dense whorls; bracts very long acuminate; acumen resembling awns of Gramineae; flowers pink or rose-purple; seed smooth..... 2. H. arist

2'. Leaves narrowly elliptic or lanceolate, 5–7 × 0.5–1.4 cm; inflorescences long pedunculate; bracts lanceolate, acuminate, but without elongate point; flowers white or blue-violet; seed regularly verruculate..... 3. H. potamophila.
