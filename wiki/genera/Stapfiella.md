# *Stapfiella* Gilg

**Family**: [[Turneraceae]]
**Modern family**: [[Passifloraceae]] subfam. Turneroideae — *Turneraceae* as circumscribed here is superseded; see [[Turneraceae]]
**Authority**: Gilg

## Diagnosis

in Mildbr., Wiss. Erg. Deut. Zentr.-Afr. Exped., Bot. : 571 (1913).

Evergreen shrubs; hairs simple, sometimes glandular. Leaves petiolate; stipules very small or absent; blade elliptic, often glandular-punctate, margin serrulate to crenulate. Inflorescences terminal or axillary, in panicles or racemes. Flowers: pedicels without bracteoles; receptacle very short; sepals each with a nectary at the base; petals white or yellow; anthers basifixed; ovary with 1 basal ovule; styles with fimbriate stigma. Fruits claviform. Seed solitary, straight, umbonate at the apex; testa longitudinally striate-reticulate.

Genus with six species, restricted to Central and East Africa; in Gabon a single species.

B: Arbo (2007), Fernandes (1978), Lewis (1953), Robyns (1967).
