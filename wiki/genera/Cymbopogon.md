# *Cymbopogon* Spreng.

**Family**: [[Gramineae]]
**Authority**: Spreng.
**Protologue**: SPRENG. Pug. II : 14 (1815)

## Diagnosis

Perennial tufted herbs, generally aromatic when crushed. Inflorescence in a very dense composite panicle. Racemes geminate, formed of pairs of heterogamous spikelets except for the lower pair of one or both racemes which is homogamous and male. Rachis articles and pedicels linear, often cupulate or auriculate at the apex. Spikelets with 2 flowers, the lower reduced to a lemma, the upper hermaphrodite in the sessile spikelets, male or sometimes sterile in the pedicellate spikelets.

Fertile spikelets dorsally compressed; lower glume flattened or more or less depressed on the back, bicarinate; upper glume navicular, keeled toward the apex, with one nerve. Lemma of the lower flower hyaline, entire, with two nerves. Fertile lemma bifid, awned in the sinus. Palea absent; two small lodicules, glabrous; 3 stamens. Pedicellate spikelets generally little different in form, but never depressed on the back. Glumes awnless. One lemma in the lower flower, hyaline, with two nerves; upper flower most often male, or suppressed.

This genus is distinguished from the preceding one by the great density of its inflorescence.

Two species are found in Gabon, but both cultivated.

## Key to the species

1. Sessile spikelets awned; lower glume of the sessile spikelet with a median groove ..... C. densiflorus
