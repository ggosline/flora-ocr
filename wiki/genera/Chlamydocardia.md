# *Chlamydocardia* Lindau

**Family**: [[Acanthaceae]]
**Authority**: Lindau
**Protologue**: Lindau, Bot. Jahrb. 20 : 39 (1894)

## Diagnosis

Herbaceous plants with ± ovate acuminate leaves.

Inflorescences axillary in short spikes, bracts not opposite in pairs, large, obcordate, stipitate, bracteoles present. Calyx with 5 equal segments; corolla small, with cylindrical tube, somewhat enlarged towards the apex, with bilabiate limb; upper lip slightly emarginate; lower lip with three rather long lobes, 2 fertile stamens; anthers with 2 locules inserted at somewhat different heights, obtuse, without appendage. Stigma subcapitate.

Capsule narrowed at the base and briefly stipitate, with 4 seeds.

Type species: Chl. Buettneri Lindau.

Two species in tropical Africa, from Nigeria to Congo ex belgce; a third species, Chl. lanciformis Lindau (Wiss. Ergebn. Deutsch. Zentral-Afrika-Exp. 1907/08 2 : 300, t. 32, 1911) was described from the Béni region, eastern Congo ex-Belgian; its taxonomic position within the genus Chlamydocardia does not seem entirely certain and would probably need revision.

Plants described under the name Chlamydocardia (?) nuda C.B. Clarke (F.T.A. 5 : 235, 1900) do not belong to this genus; see Justicia tigrina Heine, p. 219).
