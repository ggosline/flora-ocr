# *Hoslundia* Vahl

**Authority**: Vahl
**Protologue**: Enum. pl . 1 : 212 (1804)

## Diagnosis

Woody herbs or shrubs, erect or climbing. Leaves opposite or ternate, generally petiolate. Inflorescences terminal, lax or dense, branched into panicles; cymes reduced to a single flower giving a racemiform appearance; bracts much smaller than the leaves; bracteoles absent. Flowers pedicellate; calyx shortly tubular, with 5 ± equal, linear to deltoid lobes; calyx becoming globose in fruit, fleshy, brightly colored and with throat closed by lobes applied one over the other at least at their base; corolla bilabiate, white, greenish or yellow, tube straight, with ± parallel sides, dilating toward the throat and very slightly gibbous dorsally, upper lip 4-lobed, lower lip horizontal, concave, not thickened and articulate at the base, not reflexed at anthesis, enclosing the filaments of the lower stamens; stamens 4, declined, upper pair sterile, inappendiculate and short, included in the tube, fixed to the corolla above the middle, lower pair shortly exsert, fixed to the corolla at the level of the throat, anthers reniform to orbicular, dorsifixed, with equal thecae; disk 4-lobed, with lower lobe larger; ovary glabrous, deeply 4-lobed; style gynobasic, apex slightly claviform, with erect, appressed and equal branches. Fruits: 4 achenes, broadly ovoid, with a small basal scar, finely wrinkled, with small scars, producing a small quantity of mucilage when wet.

Genus with one species widespread in tropical and southern Africa.

B: Paton et al. (2013).
