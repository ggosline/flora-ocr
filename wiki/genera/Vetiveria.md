# *Vetiveria* Thouars ex Virey.

**Family**: [[Gramineae]]
**Authority**: Thouars ex Virey.

## Diagnosis

VIREY Journ. de Pharm. 1º Sér. XIII : 499.

Perennial herbs, rhizomatous; panicles erect, formed of whorls of numerous racemes. Joints and pedicels thin, slightly enlarged toward the apex. Lower flower reduced to a sterile lemma, the upper hermaphroditic in the sessile spikelet, male in the pedicellate one. Sessile spikelet awned or not. Lower glume coriaceous, rounded on the back, generally awnless; the upper navicular, keeled in its upper part, awnless or more or less subulate. Lemmas hyaline; lemma of the upper flower bifid, awnless or awned. Palea reduced; two lodicules, 3 stamens. Pedicellate spikelets dorsally compressed; glumes thinner than in the sessile spikelet and, like the lemmas, awnless.

A certain number of species are known from tropical Africa. In Gabon one encounters V. zizanoides, originating from Asia and which is frequently cultivated in villages.

68.1 Vetiveria zizanioides (L.) Nash Fl. Gab.: 148; FACB 1: 99; FWTA: 470; Gram. Cam.: 408; Gram. CB.: 276.
