# *Pseudosabicea* N. Hallé

**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Authority**: N. Hallé
**Protologue**: N. Hallé, Adansonia, 3 : 168-177 (1963)

## Diagnosis

Lianescent or sarmentose plants fairly robust or creeping and then sometimes slender. Anisophylly sometimes extreme or strong, often noticeable, rarely absent. Leaves rarely glabrous beneath. Inflorescences heterostylous by distinct plants, pluriflorous or multiflorous, rarely pauciflorous, always axillary (sometimes on defoliate nodes), lax and paniculate or contracted, sometimes sessile. Recaulescence of bracts generalized in the upper ramifications of lax inflorescences; each pedicel has its bract. Calyx with 5 lobes always well developed. Corolla ± whitish becoming rust-brown during or towards the end of anthesis. Lobes short triangular ± pubescent externally at least towards the apex, rarely entirely glabrous. Throat ± pubescent with moniliform hairs. Anthers medifixed at the apex of the connective not distinct or very slightly prominent, with cleft base. Style pale green or white, narrow, bifurcate and bearing two stigmas facing each other and ± dilated laterally. Disk glabrous less than 1 mm in height. Ovary with 2 locules. Placentas peltate, rounded or oblong, always at least somewhat emarginate at the apex, with generally dense ovule covering.

Infructescences often ± accrescent; fruits fleshy whitish or greenish, more rarely red or black, with pulp most often uncolored. Seeds ± angular approximately 0.5 mm long, almost smooth, with extremely fine ornamentation.

Type species: Pseudosabicea mitisphaera N. Hallé.

Twelve species, all from humid intertropical Africa, 10 known between Bipindi and Brazzaville, 8 in Gabon.

### PSEUDOSABICEA: KEY TO SPECIES

1. Anisophylly strong or extreme: leaf base very clearly asymmetric; nerves rather less well clothed than the limb on the lower surface.

2. Plants more than 0.50 m in height, with fairly vigorous and ± sarmentose internodes.

3. Calyx teeth glabrous and subulate; corolla tube 7–11 mm long; bud glabrous at apex; leaves more than 13 cm long..... 1. Ps. aurifodinae.

3'. Calyx teeth pubescent or ± felted externally; corolla tube less than 4 mm long.
