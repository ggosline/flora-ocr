# Citropsis

**Family**: [[Rutaceae]]

## Diagnosis

Spiny shrubs or small trees related to *Citrus*, with articulated 1–5-foliolate leaves, axillary flowers with usually eight stamens, and berry-like glandular fruits with few large seeds.
