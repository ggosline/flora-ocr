# *Tricalysia* A. Rich. ex DC.

**Family**: [[Rubiaceae]]
**Authority**: A. Rich. ex DC.
**Protologue**: De Candolle, Prodr. 4 : 445 (1830)

## Diagnosis

Trees, shrubs or small shrubs with the habit of Coffea: main stem orthotropic, branches plagiotropic ± oblique or horizontal. Petiole 1–13 mm. Lamina with 4–15 pairs of secondary veins. Leaves devoid of bacterial galls, with or without domatia sometimes pubescent, sometimes scarcely ciliolate.

Flowers (1–2) 3–12 (40) per inflorescence, in small fascicles or axillary cymes with axes bearing cupules or bracteate involucres. Common peduncle short to absent, generally solitary and exactly axillary, supra-axillary in T. concolor, in pairs along two sides in T. pangolina. Calyx with cupular rim 0.2–5 mm high, with 4–8 lobes 0.1–3 mm long (6–10 mm in T. anomala). Corolla 4–8-merous with tube 2–14 mm long; throat often distinctly pilose, rarely glabrous (T. atherura, T. potamogala). Anthers exserted, rarely semi-included, medifixed or submedifixed, sometimes ± versatile, glabrous or ± pubescent, with narrow and often apiculate connective, with 2 thecae exceptionally multilocellate (T. anomalura). Filament not very short, 1–10 mm. Pollen tricolpate 12–20 μ, circular in polar view, exine reticulate. Style glabrous or pubescent. Stigma bilobed with elongate lobes 1–5 mm, sometimes ± attenuate. Ovary 2-locular. Placenta fleshy axile pendulous, with (1) 2–12 (16) ovules weakly immersed. (Pl. 64, p. 279).

Fruit globose rarely somewhat elongate (T. Gossweileri), exceptionally ribbed (T. sp. C), 3–10 mm in diameter or up to 15–35 mm in certain species; in the latter case the fruit wall may exceed 3 mm in thickness. Seeds finely rugose, brown or blackish, ± angular or sometimes hemispherical, (1) 2–30 per fruit, 2.5–5 mm long. Hilum scar distinctly prolonged by a narrow sulcus (raphe?).

Type species: Tricalysia angolensis A. Rich. (syn. Tr. benguellensis Welw., Tr. griseiflora K. Schum., Tr. niamniamensis Schweinf. ex Hiern); holotype of Richard, P!.
