# *Asystasia* Bl.

**Family**: [[Acanthaceae]]
**Authority**: Bl.
**Protologue**: Blume, Bijdr.: 796 (1826)

## Diagnosis

Shrubs or herbaceous plants, sometimes sarmentose.

Inflorescences axillary or terminal in spiciform racemes with pedicels, furnished with short bracts and subulate-lanceolate bracteoles. Flowers small, pedicellate. Calyx with 5 equal sepals; corolla more or less bilabiate, almost funnel-shaped; limb with 5 subequal segments, the two upper ones forming a sort of upper lip, equal, somewhat oval, smaller than the central lower lobe, lateral lobes lanceolate-elliptic; central lower lobe orbicular, slightly concave. 4 didynamous, fertile, included stamens, fixed to the corolla, filaments connate by pairs at the base, anthers bilocular, locules more or less superposed, parallel, appendiculate or callose at the base. Disc present, not bilobed; ovary with 2 locules, 2 ovules per locule, style simple, stigma capitate or more or less bilobed, with subequal lobes.

---

Capsule long-stipitate, subtetragone, apiculate, bilocular, dehiscent loculicidally into 2 valves. 4 seeds discoidal-angular, with verrucose testa.

Type species: A. nemorum Nees, = A. intrusa (Forsk.) Bl. p. p., from India.

Approximately 70 species from the tropics of the Old World; A. gangetica (L.) T. Anders., recently introduced and naturalized in South America (Léonard, 1953).

For the six Gabonese species recognized here, the authors originally admitted five genera then considered as distinct: the genus Asystasia, and the genera Dicentranthera T. Anders., Filetia Miq., Isochoriste Miq. and Styasasia S. Moore. The position of Asystasia macrophylla (T. Anders.) Lindau, originally described as Dicentranthera macrophylla T. Anders., was already studied and elucidated in 1895 by G. Lindau; it would be superfluous to speak of it here. But two other cases need commentary, given that the species which concern them are treated, side by side, for the first time in a Flora of Africa, as representatives of the genus Asystasia.

F.A.W. Miquel created, in his Fl. Ind. Bat. 2: 822 (1858), two new monotypic genera, classified by him in "Tribus VIII Gendarusseae Nees ap. DC. Prodr. (11: 302, 1847) partim". Asystasia Bl. was classified by Miquel in "Tribus IV Ruellieae Nees in Wall. Pl. As. rar. 3: 75 (l. c. 792–793, 1858)". He had described Isochoriste Miq., for the species I. javanica Miq. and Filetia Miq., for the species F. costulata Miq. Miquel distinguished these two genera as follows:

4 didynamous stamens; fruit null; capsule with 4 seeds in the upper part.

Posterior lip bilobed; stigma capitellate..... Isochoriste.
