# *Atroxima* Stapf

**Family**: [[Polygalaceae]]
**Authority**: Stapf
**Protologue**: J. Linn. Soc., Bot. 37 : 85 (1905)

## Diagnosis

Woody lianes or lianescent shrubs, glabrous. Stipules generally absent, if present, then glandular. Leaves alternate. Flowers in axillary racemes; bracts and bracteoles small, generally long-persistent; sepals 5, unequal, the two inner ones somewhat larger; petals 5, adnate to the staminal tube, of comparable size, but somewhat different in form, the lower one concave (keeled), ovate-triangular and abruptly narrowed into a claw, the others narrowly elliptic; fertile stamens 4–5, filaments entirely or almost entirely connate into a tube; staminodes 0–3; ovary 3-locular, with 1 ovule per locule. Fruit drupaceous, ± lobed, with 1–3 seeds. Seeds without caruncle, covered with erect fleshy hairs, disappearing on the dry fruit.

Genus with two species restricted to West and Central Africa, one in Gabon.

B: Breteler & Smissaert-Houwing (1977).
