# Ceratophyllum

**Authority**: L., *Sp. pl.* **2**: 992 (1753) · **Family**: [[Ceratophyllaceae]]

## Diagnosis

Rootless submerged or floating freshwater herbs with branched stems and whorled, filiform, repeatedly dichotomous leaves. Male and female flowers occur at different nodes; the one-seeded fruit varies in flattening, surface ornamentation and apical and basal spines.

## Key to the species

**1.** Leaves with blade 2-dichotomous, rarely 1- or 3-dichotomous (on the lower parts of the plant), with often numerous, prominent marginal teeth; mature fruit weakly flattened laterally, with ± smooth surface, with long apical spine and 2 long basal spines or barely prominent → [[Ceratophyllum_demersum\|*C. demersum*]]

**1′.** Leaves with blade, at least the majority on all parts of the plant, 3- or 4-dichotomous, with few small marginal teeth; mature fruit with ± marked lateral flattening forming a ridge or marginal longitudinal wing, with strongly papillose or verrucose surface, with long or very short apical spine, and with or without basal and marginal spines → [[Ceratophyllum_submersum\|*C. submersum*]]

## Treatment

### Vol 52 (2018)

**Authors**: Archange Boupoya & Ehoarn Bidault · **Pages**: 9–13 · **Source**: `sources/Ceratophyllaceae_vol52_liteparse`

The treatment follows Wilmot-Dear in recognizing two cosmopolitan species and several infraspecific taxa.

## Notes

Only *C. demersum* is currently documented from Gabon; sparse collecting and the presence of *C. submersum* in Cameroon make the latter a plausible addition.
