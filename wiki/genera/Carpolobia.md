# *Carpolobia* G.Don

**Family**: [[Polygalaceae]]
**Authority**: G.Don
**Protologue**: Gen. Hist . 1 : 349, 370 (1831)

## Diagnosis

Shrubs or small trees, vegetative parts ± pubescent. Stipules generally absent, if present then formed of a small dark glandular spot or foliaceous and conico-truncate. Leaves alternate. Flowers in axillary racemes; bracts and bracteoles small, generally persistent; sepals 4–5, unequal, the two inner ones somewhat larger; petals 5, adnate to the staminal tube, subequal in length, weakly to strongly different in form, and then the lower one (keel) concave-galéate; fertile stamens 4–5, filaments adnate into a tube for half their length; staminodes 0–3; ovary 3-locular, with 1 ovule per locule. Fruit drupaceous, ± lobed, with 1–3 seeds. Seeds without caruncle, densely pilose.

Genus comprising five species currently confined to tropical Africa, three of which occur in Gabon.

B: Breteler (2010); Breteler & Smissaert-Houwing (1977).
