# *Corymborkis* Thouars

**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Authority**: Thouars
**Protologue**: Nouv. Bull. Sci. Soc. Philom., Paris 1 : 318 (1809) [= «Corymbis» Thouars, Hist. Partic. Orchid., tab. 37, 38. (1822)]

## Diagnosis

Terrestrial herb. Rhizome short. Stem semi-woody < 2 m. Leaves acute, spiral, petiole short. Inflorescence sometimes branched. Sepals and petals free, generally very open, size and form similar. Tepals of equal length, labellum contracted or widened near the apex, without or with a small callus. Gynostemium erect or somewhat curved, slender. Staminodes aliform, fleshy. Rostellum elongate, canaliculate, fleshy, acute. Viscidium elongate with unique tegula, ribbon-like, somewhat fleshy. Anther straight, oblong, acute.

No information on the pollination of Corymborkis: RASMUSSEN (1977) estimated that most species displayed floral structures suitable for pollination by Sphingidae: white or whitish-green flowers, intense and sweet fragrance, labellum orientable in any direction, clearly playing the role of signal and also landing platform; the lower narrow and hollow part of the labellum forms a tube that functions as a spur; it probably contains nectaries and perhaps constitutes a nectar reservoir.

Genus comprising six species widely distributed in neo-

and palaeotropical areas, of which 2 in Africa (one in Gabon).
