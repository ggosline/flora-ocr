# *Thysanolaena* Nees.

**Family**: [[Gramineae]]
**Authority**: Nees.
**Protologue**: Nees. Edimb. New Phil. Journ. XVIII : 180 (1835)

## Diagnosis

Tall perennial herbs with ribbon-like leaves. Loose panicles of numerous small, awnless, deciduous spikelets. Glumes reaching approximately half the length of the spikelet, with 0 or 1 nerve. 2 or 3 flowers, the lower one neutral and reduced to its lemma, the others hermaphrodite or the upper one more or less reduced or absent. Sterile lemma as long as the spikelet, membranous, with 1 or 3 nerves. Fertile lemmas acuminate, with 3 nerves, ciliate on the margins. Paleas shorter, bicarinate. Two lodicules; 2 stamens; styles distinct. One species is reported by Franchet from Gabon based on a specimen from Griffon du Bellay (without precise locality) which we have not relocated. ---
