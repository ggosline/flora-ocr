# *Coula* Baill.

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]]

Adansonia, sér. 1, **3**: 64 (1863).

A monospecific genus endemic to tropical Africa. Its single species is
[[Coula_edulis|C. edulis]], the *noisetier du Gabon*.

## Diagnosis

Tree with coriaceous leaves whose **secondary nerves reach the margin of the
blade** — an unusual character among the Gabonese Olacaceae, and the one on
which the genus is keyed. Young parts covered with russet hairs. Inflorescence
a many-flowered axillary raceme, sometimes a fascicle of racemes. Flowers tetra-
or pentamerous, with an indistinct cupuliform calyx. Petals free. Stamens free,
3–4 times as numerous as the petals, alternipetalous and epipetalous, the
filaments strongly appressed against the inner face of the petals; dehiscence
slits longitudinal. Ovary superior, 3-locular, the locules uniovulate. Style
short, ending in a trilobed stigma. Drupe glabrous, one-seeded.
