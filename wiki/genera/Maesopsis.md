# Maesopsis

**Authority**: Engl. (1895) · **Family**: [[Rhamnaceae]]

## Diagnosis

Probably monotypic African tree genus with gland-toothed leaves bearing acarodomatia, axillary cymes, hooded petals enclosing the stamens, a one-ovuled ovary and fleshy one-seeded drupes.

## Taxon treated

- [[Maesopsis_eminii_subsp_berchemioides|M. eminii subsp. berchemioides]] — western African subspecies.
