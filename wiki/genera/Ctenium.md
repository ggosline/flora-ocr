# *Ctenium* Panz.

**Family**: [[Gramineae]]
**Authority**: Panz.
**Protologue**: Panz. Denkschr. Acad. Muench.: 288, t. 13 (1813)

## Diagnosis

Herbs generally perennial in dense tufts, with narrow leaves.

Inflorescence in linear, one-sided racemes, solitary or digitate by 2 or 3. Spikelets densely biseriate along a flattened rachis, disarticulating above the glumes, with 3 or 4 flowers, the 2 lower ones neutral or the second male, the third hermaphrodite; the fourth male, neutral or more or less reduced. Glumes unequal, the lower one persistent, keeled, with one nerve, the upper one longer, with 2 or 3 nerves, with a stiff hair arising from the middle of one of the lateral nerves. Lemmas with 3 nerves, obtuse, awned just below the apex, glabrous or ciliate along the nerves. Paleas somewhat shorter, with 2 nerves or bicarinate. Two lodicules; 3 stamens in hermaphrodite flowers, 2 in male flowers. Ovary glabrous with distinct styles. About ten species in Africa and America, one species recorded in Gabon.
