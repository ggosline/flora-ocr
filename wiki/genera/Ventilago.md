# Ventilago

**Authority**: Gaertn. (1788) · **Family**: [[Rhamnaceae]]

## Diagnosis

Woody climbers with alternate leaves, small 5-merous flowers and one-seeded fruits extended into a long terminal wing.

- [[Ventilago_africana|V. africana]] — the single African species treated.
