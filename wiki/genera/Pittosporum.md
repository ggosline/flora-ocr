# *Pittosporum* Banks ex Gaertn.

**Family**: [[Pittosporaceae]]
**Authority**: Banks ex Gaertn.
**Protologue**: Fruct. 1 : 286, t. 59 (1788), nom. cons

## Diagnosis

Small or medium-sized trees or non-scandent shrubs, unarmed, aromatic. Leaves alternate or sometimes pseudo-whorled, often clustered at the apex of the branches; blade with entire margin or (in Africa) serrate or dentate. Inflorescence terminal or (outside Africa) axillary, in a raceme or panicle with partial inflorescences umbelliform or corymbiform or (outside Africa) sometimes solitary flowers. Flowers bisexual or more often functionally unisexual, (in Africa) greenish or yellowish, often with a sweet odour; sepals variously connate; petals rarely connate at the base, often spreading or revolute; stamens erect, with dorsifixed anthers; ovary (in Africa) 2-carpellate and with 2 placentas; style linear, glabrous, with truncate stigma. Fruits: capsules opening with 2 spreading, coriaceous or woody valves. Seeds 4–8 per valve, in 2 rows, irregularly angular, red or black, with 2–5 cotyledons. Genus of 100–160 species, distributed from the Macaronesian islands to South Africa, tropical and temperate Asia, and Australia, across the Pacific to eastern Polynesia and Hawaii; in Gabon, a single species.
