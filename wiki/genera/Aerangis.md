# *Aerangis* Rchb.f.

**Family**: [[Orchidaceae]]
**Authority**: Rchb.f.
**Protologue**: Flora 48 : 190 (1865)

## Diagnosis

Stem shortened, short. Leaves distichous, spreading, unequally bilobed, lobes generally obtuse or rounded. Inflorescence elongate, generally pendulous, few- to multiflore, often lax. Flowers resupinate, small to medium-sized, generally white or pale-coloured. Sepals and petals similar or subsimilar. Labellum generally petaloid, without callus, simple, entire. Spur filiform, pendulous, much longer than the pedicel and ovary. Rostellum elongate, digitate, rather fleshy, obtuse, sometimes more or less curved upwards near the middle. Viscidium unique, rather small. Tegula unique, linear, lamellate. Rostellum remnants acute, canaliculate. – Pl. 193, p. 415.

Genus of approximately 50 species in Africa, Madagascar, Comoros and Sri Lanka; approximately half of them are present on the African continent.
