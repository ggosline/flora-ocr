# Achyranthes

Perennial herbs or climbers with opposite leaves and elongating spikes whose stiff, pointed flowers become reflexed in fruit.

- [[Achyranthes_aspera|*A. aspera*]] — typical variety to be sought in Gabon.
- [[Achyranthes_aspera_var_porphyrostachya|*A. aspera* var. *porphyrostachya*]] — documented in Gabon.
