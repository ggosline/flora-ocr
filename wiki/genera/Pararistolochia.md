# *Pararistolochia* Hutch. & Dalziel

**Family**: [[Aristolochiaceae]]
**Authority**: Hutch. & Dalziel
**Protologue**: Fl. W. trop. Afr. 1 : 75 (1927)

## Diagnosis

Lianes with flattened stems; bark finely longitudinally striated. Leaves alternate, without pseudo-stipules: limb entire or 3- or 5-lobed, palmatinerved with 3(-5) strong basal nerves. Inflorescence cauliflorous, composed of fascicles of (1-)2-3 axes often contracted and zigzag, each bearing a uniparus cyme or a raceme; bracts reduced, coriaceous. Flowers pedicellate, zygomorphic, perianth with a swollen and dissymmetric utricle with 2 glandular zones inside, followed by a more or less curved tube, distal part divided into 3 (or rarely 6) zygomorphic lobes (one sometimes greatly reduced) or arranged radially, broadly to narrowly triangular, ribbed or with pendant appendages or still connate at the apex (South-East Asian species); stamens 6-24, filaments adnate to the style and forming a gynosteme bearing the anthers arranged in a crown; ovary inferior, elongate, with 6 locules; stigma with 6-24 lobes. Drupaceous fruit, indehiscent, woody. Seeds one series per locule, triangular to subcordate, with two fine longitudinal ridges.

Genus with disjunct distribution between Africa and Papua New Guinea and Australia (24 species). In Africa, the genus comprises 12 species distributed from Sierra Leone to Uganda in the East and Angola in the South; in Gabon six species.

---

Taxonomic note: very close to the genus Aristolochia, and sometimes included within it, but which is distinguished especially by the indehiscent and woody fruits, the absence of pseudo-stipules and the position of the glandular zones on the utricles.

## Key to the species

- **1.** Flower with 3 distinct lobes → couplet **2**
- **1′.** Flower with 2 distinct lobes (sometimes the third reduced), either with ribbon-like appendages, or with the upper lobe incised → couplet **5**
  - **2.** Flower with equal lobes; fruit generally longitudinally ribbed (but still unknown in one species) → couplet **3**
  - **2′.** Flower with 2 lateral lobes somewhat larger than the lower one; fruit with ribs more or less marked, but rounded → [[Pararistolochia_macrocarpa\|*P. macrocarpa*]]
