# *Ptychopetalum* Benth.

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]] *sensu lato* · **Current placement**: Olacaceae s.str.

London Journ. Bot. **2**: 376 (1843).

**Synonyms**: *Athesiandra* Miers ex Benth. & Hook.f., Gen. Pl. **1**: 346
(1862); *Anisandra* Planch. & Oliv., FTA **1**: 348 (1868).

Type species: *P. olacoides* Benth.

A genus of 4 species — 2 in America and 2 in Africa. Only one,
[[Ptychopetalum_petiolatum|P. petiolatum]], is represented in Cameroon and
Gabon, in two varieties.

## Diagnosis

Tree or shrub with ± winged and ± striate branchlets. Blade elliptic, oblong or
oblanceolate, glabrous, acuminate. Secondary nerves anastomosing well in from
the margin. Inflorescence a short, few-flowered axillary raceme. Flower bud
constricted about the middle. Flower female, pentamerous, actinomorphic. Calyx
absent or discoid. Corolla with the petals almost entirely free from one
another, pubescent within. Stamens 6–8, free, epipetalous. Ovary free,
superior, 1-locular; ovules pendulous. Drupe with a hard or crustaceous
endocarp. Embryo apical.

In the family key *Ptychopetalum* is separated from [[Coula]] by having 2
stamens per petal, all epipetalous, secondary nerves anastomosing well in from
the margin, and 2-winged branchlets — against *Coula*'s 4 stamens per petal, an
alternipetalous staminal whorl, secondary nerves running to the margin, and
cylindrical branchlets.

## Key to the species

- **1.** Flowers solitary on the inflorescence axis → P. petiolatum var. petiolatum.
- **1′.** Flowers grouped in fascicles on the inflorescence axis.. P. petiolatum var. paniculatum.

## Notes

Two American species of the genus, notably *P. olacoides*, are the source of
the stimulant known as *muira puama*; the Vol 20 treatment does not discuss
this.

Later classifications refer *Ptychopetalum* to **Olacaceae** s.str. or to
**Aptandraceae** depending on the author; the Vol 20 treatment places it in
*Olacaceae*.
