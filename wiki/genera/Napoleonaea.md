# *Napoleonaea* P.Beauv.

**Family**: [[Lecythidaceae]]
**Authority**: P.Beauv.
**Protologue**: Napoléone : 1, pl. 1 (1804)

## Diagnosis

Shrubs or rarely suffrutex; branches often slightly angular to subwinged.

Leaves: blade with margin more or less undulate or sometimes slightly dentate, lower surface provided with marginal or submargineal glands. Flowers axillary or cauline, solitary or sometimes in fascicles or panicles, actinomorphic, 5-merous, subsessile or sometimes very shortly pedicellate, subtended by a series of small imbricate and distichous bracts bearing 2 glands; calyx valvate, with 5(–6) lobes often provided at the apex with 2(–3) marginal glands; first staminodial corona rotacean, plicate, formed of 30–35 connate segments, underscored by as many pubescent external nerves; second staminodial corona consisting of 60–70 linear segments free or connate at the base; third staminodial corona with 30–40 segments free only toward their apex; staminal corona connate in its lower part to the preceding, comprising 20 pieces simply involute, 10 fertile and 10 sterile, anthers monotheca; annular disc; inferior ovary, 5-locular, with axile placentation and 4 ovules per locule; thick style, angular, peltate stigma, pentagonal. Fruits ± drupaceous, smooth or spiny, containing up to about twenty seeds. Seeds exalbuminous, ± reniform with fairly marked ventral sinus.

Genus exclusively African comprising 8–10 species, ranging from Senegal to the Central African Republic and the Democratic Republic of the Congo to Angola and Zambia. In Gabon, there are five species. However, N. septentrionalis has been mentioned for Gabon, but this was based on confusion with N. vogelii. But, as it is not impossible that N. septentrionalis is present in Gabon, it appears in the key below.
