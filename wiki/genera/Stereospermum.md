# *Stereospermum* Chamisso

**Family**: [[Bignoniaceae]]
**Authority**: Chamisso
**Protologue**: Linnaea 7 : 720 (1832) ; SPRAGUE, FTA 4 (2) ; 519 (1906) ; HEINE, FWTA, ed. 2, 2 : 386 (1963) ; VAN STEENIS, Fl. Malesiana, sér. 1, 8 (2) : 145 (1977) ; GENTRY, Fl. Cam. 27 : 46 (1984)

## Diagnosis

Trees. Leaves opposite, compound, imparipinnate.

Inflorescences paniculate, terminal. Calyx cupular to tubular-campanulate, subtruncate to irregularly bilabiate. Corolla lavender or light magenta to white, tubular-campanulate above a narrowed base. Stamens didynamous, staminode very small. Ovary linear, ± tetragonous, glabrous. Disc present. Capsules linear, cylindric, with thickened septum. Seeds biwinged, sunken into the depressions of the septum; seed body thick, triangular and folded longitudinally; wings hyaline membranous. TYPE SPECIES: S. kunthianum Cham.

Approximately 19 species; 5 in Asia, 4 in continental Africa, and 10 in Madagascar. Only one is present in Gabon. Two other species are known from Zaïre and Cameroon; they are apparently limited to drier habitats and thus may not be represented in Gabon. ---
