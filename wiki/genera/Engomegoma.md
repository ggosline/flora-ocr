# *Engomegoma* Breteler

**Family**: [[Olacaceae]] *sensu lato* · **Current placement**: Strombosiaceae

Bot. Jahrb. Syst. **118**: 113–132 (1996).

A **monotypic genus endemic to western Central Africa**, described from Gabon as
*Novitates Gabonenses* 27. Its single species is
[[Engomegoma_gordonii|E. gordonii]].

Postdates the Flore du Gabon treatment (Vol 20, 1973) by twenty-three years, so
it appears nowhere in Villiers' account and is absent from that volume's key to
the genera.

## Placement

Described in *Olacaceae* in the broad sense. Under the segregate
classification now generally followed it belongs to **Strombosiaceae**,
alongside [[Strombosia]], [[Strombosiopsis]] and [[Diogoa]].
