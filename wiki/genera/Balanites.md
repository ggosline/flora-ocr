# Balanites

**Authority**: Delile · **Family**: [[Balanitaceae]]

## Diagnosis

Spiny trees or shrubs with alternate bifoliolate leaves, 5-merous flowers, a thick disc, five one-ovuled ovary locules, and one-seeded drupes with a hard stone.

- [[Balanites_wilsoniana|B. wilsoniana]] — the single Gabonese species.
