# Afraegle

**Family**: [[Rutaceae]]

## Diagnosis

Spiny shrubs or trees with trifoliolate leaves, few-flowered axillary panicles, large fragrant 4-merous flowers with numerous stamens, and large hard-rinded fruits. The status of *A. gabonensis* relative to *A. paniculata* was unresolved in Vol. 6.

## Species discussed

- [[Afraegle_gabonensis|*A. gabonensis*]] — based on cultivated descendants of Gabon seed; status uncertain.
- [[Afraegle_paniculata|*A. paniculata*]] — regional species, not confirmed from Gabon.
