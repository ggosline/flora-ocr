# *Brazzeia* Baillon

Trees or small trees, cauliflorous and ramiflorous. Branchlets cylindrical but corky and glabrous, or bearing 2 ribs with intercalary groove and puberulent. Bundle of cataphylls intrapetiolary, conical flattened, reaching 1.5 mm in length, erect against the branchlet; at the branchlet tip main axis generally as persistent linear stump and sympodial axis with cataphylls at the base, the outer ones broadly triangular and often reduced, the inner ones triangular elongate and caducous, these cataphylls glabrous or papillose. Leaves with very short petiole; blade ± coriaceous or membranous papyraceous, elliptic, ± oval or obovate, very often with slight inequality of the two lateral halves, of reduced dimensions generally not exceeding 6 × 12 cm, acuminate, with entire or denticulate margin, with 3–7 pairs of lateral nerves anastomosing at the margin at 2–5 mm from the edge.

Inflorescences of solitary flowers or up to 10 flowers pseudofasciculate in a raceme with subnull or null axis, on trunks and large branches; pedicels bearing minute bracteoles at the base. Calyx broadly patelliform or cupuliform very open, with entire or festooned margin, capable of tearing irregularly; corolla pink ± dark, smooth in the floral bud, spreading into (2-)3–5 lobes at anthesis; stamens numerous, on 4 concentric whorls, with filaments elongate coalescent at the base and anthers short oblong with poricidal dehiscence; ovary dome-shaped ± tabular, 4–8 locules pluriovulate, style filiform, stigma somewhat capitate and somewhat lobed.

Capsules subglobose, sometimes distinctly acuminate, red at maturity, unilocular, with sometimes late dehiscence, opening into (4-)5–6(-8) valves; several seeds (up to 15) tunicate and agglomerated into a spherical mass; albumen non-ruminate.

Type species: Brazzeia congoensis Baillon.

This African genus comprises only two species in central Africa, present in Cameroon and Gabon (one with a variety in this territory); these species occurring elsewhere only in Macias Nguema (former Fernando Po), Equatorial Guinea, Central African Republic, Congo and Zaïre. The third species of the genus (Brazzeia longipedicellata Verdc.) is recorded only in Uganda and Zaïre, in submontane forests between 800 and 1500 m; it could be sought in the submontane forests of western Cameroon.
