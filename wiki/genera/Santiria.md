# Santiria

**Authority**: Blume (1850) · **Family**: [[Burseraceae]]

## Diagnosis

Aromatic shrubs or trees, the regional species supported by stilt roots. Leaves imparipinnate and glabrous; flowers yellow, 3-merous and glabrous in lax terminal panicles; fruit a flattened eccentric drupe with a lateral style scar.

## Species

- [[Santiria_trimera|S. trimera]] — Sierra Leone to Congo and São Tomé.
