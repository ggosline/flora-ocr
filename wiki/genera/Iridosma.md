# Iridosma

**Authority**: Aubr. et Pellegr. · **Family**: [[Simaroubaceae]]

## Diagnosis

Monotypic tree genus with imparipinnate leaves and paniculate bisexual flowers. Petals (7–)8, valvate with inflexed hairy margins; stamens 12–13, their filaments bearing long-haired basal ligules; anthers small, elliptic and versatile. Four free one-ovuled carpels sit on a thick disc; style single, stigma 4-lobed. Fruit unknown.

## Treatments

### Vol 3 (1962)

The genus was established for the former *Mannia Le-Testui* and named for the flowers’ iris-like scent.
