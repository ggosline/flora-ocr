# *Vigna* Savi

**Family**: [[Leguminosae]] (Papilionoideae)
**Authority**: Savi — Nuov. Giorn. Lett., sér. 3, 8: 113 (1834)
**Synonym**: *Voandzeia* Thouars (1806)
**Treatment with**: Rémy S. Pasquet

Sixteen species — **the most agriculturally important genus in the corpus.**

## Diagnosis

Herbs, mostly climbing and/or trailing, sometimes with a fusiform tuberous root.
Stipules persistent, generally prolonged below the point of insertion (except in
*V. comosa*).

## Key to the species

- **1.** Flowers at ground level; pods maturing underground; cultivated plant (to be sought in Gabon) → V. subterranea var. subterranea
- **1′.** Flowers clearly above ground; pods maturing above ground; wild or cultivated plant → couplet **2**
  - **2.** Stipules linear, truncate at the base; limb of lateral leaflets sometimes lobed, fairly small, (1–)2–4 cm in length → V. comosa subsp. comosa
  - **2′.** Stipules prolonged below the insertion point by one or two lobes; if limb of lateral leaflets lobed, then more than 4 cm in length → couplet **3**
    - **3.** Stipules prolonged below the insertion point by a single lobe → couplet **4**
    - **3′.** Stipules bilobed at the base → couplet **9**
      - **4.** Cultivated plant; flowers yellowish to yellow, asymmetric, 6–12 mm in length, keel with a long, curved or spiral beak, with left petal bearing a spur → couplet **5**
      - **4′.** Cultivated or wild plant; flowers with pink or rarely white petals and ± spotted with violet, practically symmetric, ≥ 15 mm in length; leaflet with ± hastate limb; keel with a short beak, with left petal lacking a spur ( V. unguiculata ) → couplet **6**
        - **5.** Stipules lanceolate, narrow; corolla bright yellow, keel rostrum bright yellow; pod erect; seed with developed strophiole → V. mungo var. mungo
        - **5′.** Stipules ovate, broad; corolla pale yellow, keel rostrum tinged with violet; pod spreading; seed without strophiole (to be sought in Gabon) → V. radiata var. radiata

          - **6′.** Wild plant; pods dehiscent, 6–11 cm in length → couplet **7**
            - **7.** Flowers fragrant, 23–30 mm in length; calyx lobes 4–14 mm in length → V. unguiculata subsp. letouzeyi
            - **7′.** Flowers slightly fragrant or not fragrant, 15–23 mm in length; calyx lobes 1–5 mm in length → couplet **8**

              - **8′.** Ovary with 12 to 18 ovules; seeds 3.5–5 × 2.5–3 mm (to be sought in Gabon) → V. unguiculata subsp. unguiculata var. spontanea

                - **9′.** Leaflets with limb having different tertiary venation → couplet **10**
                  - **10.** Leaflets with limb having secondary veins straight, perpendicular to the main vein → [[Vigna_multinervis\|*V. multinervis*]]

                    - **11′.** Flower practically symmetric, with yellow or blue corolla → couplet **13**
                      - **12.** Flower 16–23 mm in length; calyx pubescent, with lobes 6–20 mm in length; keel with a single spur supporting the left wing and a long curved beak; style with a short beak; ovary with ≥ 15 ovules; pod terminated by a straight beak . V. vexillata
                      - **12′.** Flower 9–15 mm in length; calyx glabrous or sparsely puberulent, with lobes 0.5–1.5 mm in length; keel with short beak lacking a spur; style with a long beak curved forward; ovary with 9 to 11 ovules; pod terminated by a curved beak (to be sought in Gabon) → [[Vigna_nigritia\|*V. nigritia*]]
                        - **13.** Blue corolla → couplet **14**
                        - **13′.** Yellow corolla → couplet **16**
                        - **14.** Flowers 5–8 mm in length; inflorescence with rachis up to 1 cm; ovary with 3 to 5 ovules; pod 1.5–2 cm in length → [[Vigna_laurentii\|*V. laurentii*]]
                        - **14′.** Flowers ≥ 7 mm in length; inflorescence with rachis 1–15 cm; ovary with ≥ 5 ovules; pod 2–4.5 cm in length → couplet **15**
                        - **15.** Stem with ferruginous pubescence; stipules sagittate, 5–9 mm in length; seed with eccentric strophiole → [[Vigna_racemosa\|*V. racemosa*]]
                        - **15′.** Stem glabrous or pubescent; stipules narrowly triangular, cordate to subauricular at the base, 1–5 mm in length; seed with central strophiole → [[Vigna_gracilis\|*V. gracilis*]]

                        - **16′.** Outer (dorsal surface) of standard glabrous → couplet **17**
                        - **17.** Flower 6–9 mm in length; standard deeply emarginate at the apex; keel with long rostrum; pod perpendicular to the inflorescence axis, 4–5 cm in length; seed with hilum linear for almost its entire length → [[Vigna_juruana\|*V. juruana*]]

## Species in region

*V. comosa*, *V. gracilis*, *V. juruana*, *V. laurentii*, *V. luteola*,
*V. multinervis*, *V. mungo*, *V. nigritia*, *V. oblongifolia*, *V. pubigera*,
*V. racemosa*, *V. radiata*, *V. reticulata*, *V. subterranea*,
*V. unguiculata*, *V. vexillata* — 16 in all.

## Notes

**Four of these sixteen species are crops, and two were domesticated in Africa.**

***V. unguiculata* is the cowpea** (niébé, black-eyed pea) — domesticated in
Africa and the most important indigenous grain legume of the continent, grown
across the savanna zone for seed, pods and fodder, and central to nitrogen
supply in smallholder rotations.

***V. subterranea* is the Bambara groundnut** — likewise African in origin, and
long treated as the separate genus *Voandzeia*, as this treatment's synonymy
records. It ripens its pods underground like the peanut, tolerates poor soils and
drought, and is a significant food crop that has attracted renewed attention as a
climate-resilient legume.

*V. mungo* (black gram) and *V. radiata* (mung bean) are Asian domesticates
present through cultivation.

Because the wild African *Vigna* are the crop wild relatives of cowpea and
Bambara groundnut, **the non-crop species on this page carry real breeding
value** — a point that gives a regional flora treatment direct agricultural
relevance. That the treatment was written with Rémy Pasquet, a *Vigna* crop-genetic
specialist, reflects this.
