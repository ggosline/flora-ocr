# *Lasianthera* Palisot de Beauvois

**Family**: [[Icacinaceae]]
**Authority**: Palisot de Beauvois
**Protologue**: Fl. Oware et Benin 1:85 (1804)

## Diagnosis

Tree or shrub. Leaves alternate, glabrous and acuminate. Inflorescence formed by an umbel of opposite-leafed glomerules. Flowers ¥, gamopetalous. Stamens free and pubescent. Disc unilateral. Stigma small. Drupe rostrate, keeled and curved.

This monotypic genus is endemic to tropical Africa.

## Key to the species

**1.** Leaves large: 9–20 × 3–7 cm, elliptic to oblong → L. africana var. africana.

**1′.** Leaves small: 3.5–8 × 0.9–2 cm, narrowly elliptic → L. africana var. microphylla.
