# *Heteranthoecia* Stapf.

**Family**: [[Gramineae]]
**Authority**: Stapf.
**Protologue**: STAPF, Hook. Ic. Pl. XXX t. 2927 (1911)

## Diagnosis

Annual herbs with creeping base. Leaves lanceolate.

Narrow panicle formed of a succession of small unilateral spiciform racemes. Spikelets with asymmetrical profile, arranged in two series on the flattened abaxial surface of the spike rachis. Glumes similar, or the lower one somewhat shorter; with 4 or 7 nerves. Lower floret hermaphrodite, exceeding the glumes, slightly pubescent at the base and on the margins; lemma papyraceous with 5 nerves, palea of equal length, bifid, with 2 nerves. Two lodicules; three stamens; styles distinct. Upper floret female, smaller, dorsally flattened, pubescent, glumelles similar to those of the lower floret. Two lodicules; stamens greatly reduced or suppressed; styles free.

Monotypic genus apparently distributed throughout the greater part of tropical humid and semi-humid Africa.
