# Dacryodes

**Authority**: Vahl (1810) · **Family**: [[Burseraceae]]

## Diagnosis

Resinous trees with imparipinnate leaves and predominantly 3-merous unisexual flowers: three sepals, three petals, six stamens and a two-locular ovary. The Gabon species are separated chiefly by leaflet number and shape, simple versus stellate or scaly hairs, and the form and shield-like markings of the endocarp.
