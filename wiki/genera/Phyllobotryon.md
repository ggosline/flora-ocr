## Diagnosis

Unarmed shrubs, monocaulous, with a terminal crown of leaves, rarely branched. Leaves stipulate, denticulate.

Inflorescences epiphyllous, in glomerules or in 1-4-fascicled cymes; these in a single or several groups of flowers per leaf, arranged along the midvein or above 2/3 of the height of the lamina. Flowers ♂ or ♀ and ♀ (polygamous plants), rarely solitary, supported by small imbricate triangular bracts or by a large median bract; pedicel glabrous, 0.5-10 mm long; sepals 3-4, imbricate; petals 3-4, imbricate; stamens numerous (20-40); ovary glabrous, with 2-3 pluri-ovulate placentas; style divided into 3 stigmatic branches or 2-3-fid at the apex. Fruits: capsules with 2-3 valves; seeds ± arillate.

TYPE SPECIES: P. spathulatum MUELL. ARG.

The genus Phyllobotryon MUELL. ARG. is divided into two subgenera (HUL, 1991): subg. Phyllobotryon and subg. Phylloclinium (BAILL.) HUL. A genus of West-Central Africa, it comprises 5 species, of which 2 are present in Gabon.
