# *Dicellandra* Hooker f.

**Family**: [[Melastomataceae]]
**Authority**: Hooker f.

## Diagnosis

in BENTH. & HOOK., Gen. Pl. 1 : 757 (1867); JACQ.-FÉL., Adansonia, sér. 2, 14 (1) : 77-98 (1974); Fl. Cam. 24 : 106 (1983).

Shrubs normally with a single main stem and sympodial growth; sprawling, erect, or climbing; leaves ample and petiolate.

Cymes or glomerules grouped in inflorescences often many-flowered. Flower 5-merous; calyx short; corolla exposed. Stamens long and subulate, in a perigynous position in the bud; in 2 whorls generally dimorphic: the outer ones longer, with 2 free anterior appendages and a spur; the inner ones shorter, connective not prolonged and appendages shorter. Ovary deeply included, connected to the hypanthium by the interstaminal septa; apex concave with a membranous epigynous crown; style linear, stigma finely capitate. Placentas stipitate-cymbifor throughout nearly the entire height of the locule.

Fruit ellipsoid, apex of ovary truncate, tuberculate, or depressed; epigynous crown non-accrescent, often resorbed; dehiscence irregular. Seeds obconic, hilum punctiform, raphe dilated, arillate, beaked laterally upward. Chromosome number (D. barteri) : n = 32-34 (FAVARGER).

TYPE SPECIES : Dicellandra barteri Hook. f., Nigeria.
