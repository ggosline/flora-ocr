# Nothospondias

**Authority**: Engl., *Botanische Jahrbücher* 36: 216 (1905) · **Family**: [[Simaroubaceae]]

## Diagnosis

Dioecious trees with exceptionally large imparipinnate leaves clustered at branch ends and large panicles. Flowers 4-merous; stamens 8 in two unequal whorls and lacking basal ligules. The gynoecium consists of four free carpels, of which one or two develop into yellow-orange drupes with a very hard endocarp.
