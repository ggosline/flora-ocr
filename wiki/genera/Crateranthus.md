# *Crateranthus* Bak.f.

**Family**: [[Lecythidaceae]]
**Authority**: Bak.f.

## Diagnosis

in Rendle et al., Cat. pl. Oban: 35, t. 5 (1913).

Trees or shrubs; branches angular to strongly winged. Leaves sessile or shortly petiolate; blade coriaceous, glabrous, lower surface with dark brown glandular spots; secondary veins forming a crenate intramarginal vein. Flowers solitary, axillary, actinomorphic, with several glabrous and coriaceous bracts in 2 ranks; sepals (2–)3, imbricate, persistent; petals absent; staminodial crown strongly plicate, cream or purple; stamens in 8–10 whorls, free, inserted on the crown; disc present; ovary semi-inferior, with 3–5 locules, pendulous ovules, 15–18 per locule; style filiform, with acute stigma. Fruits drupaceous, indehiscent.

Genus comprising 2 species in Africa, from Nigeria to the Democratic Republic of the Congo, and Gabon.
