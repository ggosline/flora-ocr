# *Pseudoprosopis* Harms

**Family**: [[Leguminosae]]
**Authority**: Harms
**Protologue**: Bot. Jahrb. 33:152 (1902)

## Diagnosis

Shrubs sometimes sarmentose, or lianes. Gland often present on the stipular scar. Leaflet sessile or petiolulate. Inflorescences in racemes shortly pedunculate, solitary fasciculate, sometimes paniculate. Flowers ♀, pentamerous, pedicellate. Calyx cupular. Petals 5, free valvate. Stamens 10, with filaments free or fused at the base; anther with apical gland. Ovary shortly stipitate, with approximately 10 ovules. Legume woody, dehiscent with 2 slits. Seeds orbicular to elliptic quadrangular, exalbuminous. TYPE SPECIES: P. fischeri (Taub.) Harms (Prosopis fischeri Taub.)

This genus contains 7 species in tropical Africa; of which 2 are presented in Gabon.

1. Leaflet subsessile (petiolule long of ± 0.5 mm); blade 1.35–2.3 × 0.35–1 cm; axillary hook often present; no acumen …… 1. P. gilletii. 1'. Leaflet petiolulate (petiolule long of 2–4.5 mm); blade 5–10 × 1.7–4 cm; no axillary hook; acumen distinct …… 2. P. claessensii.
