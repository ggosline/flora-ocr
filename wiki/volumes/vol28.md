## Source limitations

Printed page 12 was obliterated in the available scan. This removes part of
the [[Pandanus_parvicentralis|*Pandanus parvicentralis*]] treatment; its wiki
description contains all recoverable text but is necessarily incomplete.

Although Vol. 28 is a Gabon flora, this wiki has a wider Lower Guinea and
western Central African scope. Taxa documented by the treatments from other
in-scope countries are therefore retained. In particular,
[[Pandanus_candelabrum|*P. candelabrum*]] is included from confirmed Nigerian
and Cameroonian records even though Huynh considered its occurrence in Gabon
unconfirmed.
